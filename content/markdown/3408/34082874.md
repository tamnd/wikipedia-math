# Edward Fraenkel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Fraenkel"}

**Ludwig Edward Fraenkel** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o3] (28 May 1927 – 27 April 2019)[^o4][^o5] was a German-born British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and professor at the [University of Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bath).[^o6] He was the son of classicist [Eduard Fraenkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduard_Fraenkel).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Fraenkel earned an undergraduate degree (in 1947) and a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree (in 1948) from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in the area of Aeronautical Engineering.[^o8] His thesis was on the design of nozzles for supersonic wind tunnels.[^o9]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 1989, he was awarded the [Senior Whitehead Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Whitehead_Prize).[^o11] He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1993.
