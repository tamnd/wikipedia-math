# Bobby Wilson (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bobby Wilson"}

**Bobby Wilson** is a mathematician and associate professor at the University of Washington.[^o3]

:::

## Professional career and research {#Professional_career_and_research}

Wilson obtained his Ph.D. at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 2015 under the supervision of [Wilhelm Schlag](wikipedia://en/60517925). He was an undergraduate at [Morehouse College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morehouse_College).[^o6] He was a CLE Moore Instructor at MIT 2015–2018.[^o7] He was twice an [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Postdoctoral Fellow (New Challenges in PDE and Harmonic Analysis) and will participate in the MSRI program Mathematical problems in fluid dynamics in 2021.[^o8] His research "has been primarily concerned with questions concerning structure theory of measures and the dynamics of dispersive evolutionary equations."[^o9]

## Honors {#Honors}

In 2020, he was awarded one of three [Karen EDGE Fellowships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EDGE_Foundation)[^o9] and he was profiled in [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006).[^o11]
