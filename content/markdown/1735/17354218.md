# Ravindra Khattree

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ravindra Khattree"}

**Ravindra Khattree** (born 1959) is an Indian-American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and a distinguished [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685)[^o3] at [Oakland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland_University) and a co-director of the Center for Data Science and Big Data Analytics/ Institute for Data Science at the same university. His contribution to the Fountain–Khattree–Peddada Theorem in [Pitman measure of closeness](wikipedia://en/36291595) is one of the important results of his work.[^o4] Khattree is the coauthor of two books and has coedited two volumes. He has served as an associate editor of the *[Communications in Statistics](wikipedia://en/20150587)* journal and the editor of the *Interstat* online journal. He was Chief editor of *Journal of Statistics and Applications* for more than ten years. He is an elected fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o5]

:::

Khattree was born in [Uttar Pradesh, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uttar_Pradesh,_India). He attended the [Ewing Christian College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ewing_Christian_College)-[Allahabad University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allahabad_University) and the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418). In 1985, he earned a doctorate from the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) with [Calyampudi Radhakrishna Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calyampudi_Radhakrishna_Rao) as his advisor and immediately joined the North Dakota State University. He became a faculty member at Oakland University in 1991. He was the biostatistics group leader in the Biomedical Research and Informatics Center and a professor of biostatistics in the College of Human Medicine, Michigan State University during 2005–2006. He worked as a senior research scientist at US [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) with assignment at the Radiation Effects Research Foundation (formerly known as [Atomic Bomb Casualty Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_Bomb_Casualty_Commission)), Hiroshima during 2010–2011.[^o5]

Prior to joining Oakland University, he had been a faculty member at the [North Dakota State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Dakota_State_University), [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University) and also worked at BFGoodrich Chemical Group. He is the paternal grandson of [Binda Prasad Khattri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binda_Prasad_Khattri).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2002, Khattree received the Young Researcher Award from the [International Indian Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Indian_Statistical_Association).[^o7] Khattree was honored with fellowship in the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2003 and became an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2004.[^o8] He is also a recipient of Oakland University Research Excellence Award (2008).
