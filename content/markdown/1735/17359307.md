# Paratopological group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paratopological group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **paratopological group** is a [topological semigroup](wikipedia://en/17359213) that is algebraically a [group](wikipedia://en/19447).[^o2] In other words, it is a group *G* with a topology such that the group's product operation is a [continuous function](wikipedia://en/6122) from *G* × *G* to *G*. This differs from the definition of a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) in that the group inverse is not required to be continuous.

:::

As with topological groups, some authors require the topology to be [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637).[^o3]

[Compact](wikipedia://en/6042) paratopological groups are automatically topological groups.
