# Hilary Priestley

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Hilary Priestley"}

**Hilary Ann Priestley** is a British mathematician. She is a professor at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and a Fellow of [St Anne's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Anne's_College,_Oxford), where she has been Tutor in Mathematics since 1972.[^o4]

:::

Hilary Priestley introduced ordered [separable topological spaces](wikipedia://en/27855); such topological spaces are now usually called [Priestley spaces](wikipedia://en/26599382) in her honour.[^o5] The term "[Priestley duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priestley_duality)" is also used for her application of these spaces in the [representation theory of distributive lattices](wikipedia://en/310883#representation_theory).[^o6]
