# 318 (number)

Numbers

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="318"}

**318** (**three hundred \[and\] eighteen**) is the natural number following 317 and preceding 319. It has significance in [Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism) and [Christianity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christianity).

:::

## In Mathematics {#In_Mathematics}

The number 318 has the proper divisors 1, 2, 3, 6, 53, 106, and 159. It is an [abundant](wikipedia://en/321831) number,[^o4] a [sphenic number](wikipedia://en/245203),[^o5] and a [nontotient](wikipedia://en/748422).[^o6] There are 318 posets with 6 unlabeled elements.

## In Religion {#In_Religion}

The number 318 holds some significance in Judeo-Christian traditions and history. In the [Talmud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talmud), scholars interpret the number as a literal reference to the faithful servant, [Eliezer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eliezer). Using [gematria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gematria) (a Jewish alphanumeric code), the [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew) letters in Eliezer’s name add up to 318.[^o9]

In [Greek numerals](wikipedia://en/166697), 318 is written as τιη. The letter Tau (τ) is interpreted as resembling the cross and the letters Iota (ι) and Eta (η) represent [Jesus Christ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_Christ) as the first two letters in his name in Greek, Ἰησοῦς[^o10]

In early [Christian history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_history), the [First Council of Nicaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Council_of_Nicaea) is traditionally recorded as having exactly 318 bishops in attendance.[^o11] Additionally, in [Genesis 14](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genesis_14), Abram (later [Abraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham)) leads a rescue mission for his nephew [Lot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lot_%28biblical_person%29) with exactly 318 trained men.[^o12]

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

318 is a predicted [magic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_number_%28physics%29) for [neutrons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutrons).[^o14][^o15][^o16]
