# Hildreth–Lu estimation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hildreth–Lu estimation"}

**Hildreth–Lu estimation**, named for [Clifford Hildreth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_Hildreth) and [John Y. Lu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Y._Lu),[^o2] is a method for adjusting a [linear model](wikipedia://en/17904) in response to the presence of [serial correlation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serial_correlation) in the [error term](wikipedia://en/4229275). It is an iterative procedure related to the [Cochrane–Orcutt estimation](wikipedia://en/4229239).

:::

The idea is to repeatedly apply [ordinary least squares](wikipedia://en/1651906) to

$$y_t - \rho y_{t-1} = \alpha(1-\rho)+(X_t - \rho X_{t-1})\beta + e_t \,$$

for different values of $\rho$ between −1 and 1. From all these auxiliary regressions, one selects the pair *(α, β)* that yields the smallest [residual sum of squares](wikipedia://en/2473303).
