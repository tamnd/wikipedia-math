# Andrey Markov Jr.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrey Markov Jr."}

**Andrey Andreyevich Markov** (Андре́й Андре́евич Ма́рков; 22 September 1903, [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg) – 11 October 1979, [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), the son of the Russian mathematician [Andrey Markov Sr](wikipedia://en/915), and one of the key founders of the Russian school of [constructive mathematics](wikipedia://en/21059) and logic. He made outstanding contributions to various areas of mathematics, including [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358).[^o2][^o3]

:::

His name is in particular associated with [Markov's principle](wikipedia://en/14920509) and [Markov's rule](wikipedia://en/14920509#Markov's_rule) in mathematical logic, [Markov's theorem](wikipedia://en/56349373) in [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008) and [Markov algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_algorithm) in [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392).

An important result that he proved in 1947 was that the [word problem for semigroups](wikipedia://en/2452154#The_word_problem_for_semi-Thue_systems) was unsolvable; [Emil Leon Post](wikipedia://en/362382) obtained the same result independently at about the same time.

In 1953 he became a member of the [Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_the_Soviet_Union).

In 1960, Markov obtained fundamental results showing that the classification of four-dimensional [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) is [undecidable](wikipedia://en/15631055): no general algorithm exists for distinguishing two arbitrary manifolds with four or more dimensions. This is because four-dimensional manifolds have sufficient flexibility to allow us to embed any algorithm within their structure. Hence, classifying all four-manifolds would imply a solution to Turing's [halting problem](wikipedia://en/21391870). Embedding implies failure to create a correspondence between algorithms and indexing (naturally uncountably infinite, but even larger) of the four-manifolds structure. Failure is in Cantor's sense. Indexing is in Godel's sense.

His doctoral students include [Boris Kushner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Kushner_%28mathematician%29), [Gennady Makanin](wikipedia://en/67731944), and [Nikolai Shanin](wikipedia://en/43523988).
