# Sylvestre Gallot

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvestre Gallot"}

**Sylvestre F. L. Gallot** (born January 29, 1948, in [Bazoches-lès-Bray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bazoches-lès-Bray))[^o3][^o4] is a French mathematician, specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He is an emeritus professor at the Institut Fourier of the [Université Grenoble Alpes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Grenoble_Alpes), in the Geometry and Topology section.[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Sylvestre Gallot received his doctorate from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) (Paris 7) with thesis under the direction of [Marcel Berger](wikipedia://en/12149363). Gallot worked during the early 1980s at the University of Savoie, then at the [École Normale Supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure_de_Lyon) and the University of Grenoble (Institut Fourier).

His research deals with [isoperimetric inequalities](wikipedia://en/326182) in [Riemann geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_geometry), rigidity issues, and the [Laplace operator](wikipedia://en/174706) spectrum on [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652). With Gérard Besson and Pierre Bérard, he discovered, in 1985, a form of isoperimetric inequality in Riemannian manifolds with a lower bound involving the diameter and [Ricci curvature](wikipedia://en/276582).[^o8] In 1995, he discovered with Gérard Besson and Gilles Courtois, a Chebyshev inequality for the minimal entropy of locally symmetrical spaces of negative curvature; the inequality gives a new and simpler proof of the [Mostow rigidity theorem](wikipedia://en/2782164).[^o9][^o10] The result of Besson, Courtois, and Gallo is called *minimal entropy rigidity*.[^o11]

In 1998 he was an invited speaker with talk *Curvature decreasing maps are volume decreasing* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o12]
