# Henri Brinkman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henri Brinkman"}

**Henri Coenraad Brinkman** ([Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), 30 March 1908 – [Delft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft), 11 February 1961)[^o2] was a Dutch mathematician and physicist. He was a professor at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen).[^o3]

:::

The dimensionless [Brinkman number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brinkman_number) is named after him.[^o4]
