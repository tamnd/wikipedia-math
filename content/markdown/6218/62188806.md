# GAMA Platform

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="GAMA Platform"}

**GAMA**[^o5][^o6] (**G**IS **A**gent-based **M**odeling **A**rchitecture) is a [simulation platform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation_software) with a complete modelling and simulation [integrated development environment (IDE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrated_development_environment) for writing and experimenting spatially explicit [agent-based models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent-based_model).[^o7][^o9]

:::

## About {#About}

The GAMA Platform is [agent-based modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agent-based_model) software that was originally (2007–2010) developed by the Vietnamese-French research team MSI (located at [IFI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_la_Francophonie_pour_l'Informatique), Hanoi, and part of the [IRD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_de_recherche_pour_le_développement) - [SU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) International Research Unit UMMISCO). It is now developed by an international consortium of academic and industrial partners led by [UMMISCO](http://ummisco.ird.fr) (2022-01-23). <https://web.archive.org/web/20220123013159/http://www.ummisco.ird.fr/>, including [INRAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_national_de_la_recherche_agronomique), the [University of Toulouse 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse_1_University_Capitole), the [University of Rouen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rouen), the [University of Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud), the [University of Can Tho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Can_Tho_University), Vietnam, the [National University of Hanoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_Hanoi), [EDF R&D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Électricité_de_France), [CEA LISC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Alternative_Energies_and_Atomic_Energy_Commission), and [MIT Media Lab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Media_Lab).[^o9]

GAMA was designed to allow [domain experts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subject-matter_expert) without a programming background to model phenomena from their field of expertise.[^o10]

The GAMA environment enables exploration of [emergent phenomena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergent_phenomena). It comes with a models library including examples from several domains, such as [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology), and [system dynamics](wikipedia://en/153208).[^o11] The GAMA simulation panel allows exploration by modifying switches, sliders, choosers, inputs, and other user interface elements that the modeler chooses to make available.[^o12]

## Technical foundation {#Technical_foundation}

GAMA Platform is [free and open-source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software), released under a [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License) (GPL3).[^o14] It is written in [Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Java_%28programming_language%29) and runs on the [Java virtual machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Java_virtual_machine) (JVM).[^o15] All core components and extensions are written in Java, but end users do not need to work in Java at all if they use a published build of the platform; instead, they would write all models using GAML (described below).

### Multiple application domains {#Multiple_application_domains}

GAMA was developed with a very general approach and can be used for many application domains.[^o7] GAMA is mostly present in applications domains like transport,[^o17][^o18][^o29][^o31][^o32] urban planning,[^o29][^o31][^o32] disaster response,[^o19] epidemiology,[^o20][^o33][^o34] analysis of multirobot systems,[^o21][^o22] and the environment,[^o29][^o31][^o32] with special emphasis on analyses that use [GIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographic_information_system) data.[^o23][^o24]

### High-level agent-based language {#High-level_agent-based_language}

GAML (**GA**ma **M**odeling **L**anguage) is the dedicated language used in GAMA. It is an agent-based language, that provides the possibility to build a model with several paradigms of modeling.[^o7]

This high-level language was inspired by [Smalltalk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smalltalk) and [Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Java_%28programming_language%29), GAMA has been developed to be used by non-computer scientists.[^o7]

### User interface {#User_interface}

Modelers may use many visual representations for the same model, in order to highlight a certain aspect of a simulation. These include 2D/3D displays, with basic control of lighting, textures, and cameras. Standard charts such as series plots may also be constructed.[^o7]

## Project examples {#Project_examples}

The developers maintain a community-sourced list of scientific projects that use GAMA.[^o28]

Some of the larger efforts include:

- Hoan Kiem Air:[^o29] Agent based modeling and simulation of the urban management on traffic and air pollution through tangible interface.
- Proxymix:[^o30] Visualization tool about the influence of spatial configuration on human collaboration.
- CityScope Champs-Elysées:[^o31] An interactive platform to improve decision-making related to the revitalization of the Champs Élysées.
- ESCAPE:[^o32] A Multi-modal Urban Traffic Agent-Based Framework to Study Individual Response to Catastrophic Events.
- COMOKIT:[^o33][^o34] Generic model of public policies to contain the spread of COVID-19 epidemics in a city, validated on the basis of different case studies.

## Users {#Users}

Several academic institutions teach modeling and simulation courses based on GAMA. It is taught in the Urban Simulation class at the Potsdam University of Applied Sciences,[^o36] and at the University of Salzburg.[^o37] It is also used and taught annually at the Multi-platform International Summer School on Agent-Based Modelling & Simulation.[^o38]
