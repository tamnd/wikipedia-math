# Véronique Cortier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Véronique Cortier"}

*Image: Véronique Cortier.jpg.* Véronique Cortier in Nancy (France) in 2017

**Véronique Cortier** is a French mathematician and computer scientist specializing in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). Her research has applied [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) in the formal verification of [cryptographic protocols](wikipedia://en/591994), and has included the development of secure [electronic voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_voting) systems. She has also contributed to the public dissemination of knowledge about cryptography through a sequence of posts on the *binaire* blog of *[Le Monde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Monde)*. She is a director of research with [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS), associated with the Laboratoire Lorrain de Recherche en Informatique et ses Applications (LORIA) at the [University of Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lorraine) in [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Cortier studied mathematics and computer science at the [École normale supérieure de Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_Paris-Saclay) from 1997 until 2001, earning a master's degree and completing her [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation). She remained at Cachan for her doctoral studies, completing a Ph.D. in 2003 with the dissertation *Automatic Verification of Cryptographic Protocols* supervised by Hubert Comon. She joined the French [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) (CNRS) in 2003, completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2009, and became a director of research with CNRS in 2010.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Cortier was the 2003 winner of the Gilles Kahn Prize of the Société informatique de France for the best French dissertation in computer science. She also won a second dissertation prize, from *[Le Monde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Monde)*. In 2015 she became the second woman to win the [INRIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INRIA) and [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) Young Researcher Award for her work on [Belenios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belenios), a secure electronic voting system. In 2022 she won the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal).
