# Stephen Shing-Toung Yau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Shing-Toung Yau"}

**Stephen Shing-Toung Yau** (; born 1952) is a Chinese-American mathematician. He is a Distinguished Professor Emeritus at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago), and currently teaches at [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University). He is a Fellow of the [Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers) and the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Shing-Toung Yau was born in 1952 in [British Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Hong_Kong),[^o4] with his [ancestral home](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancestral_home_%28Chinese%29) in [Jiaoling County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiaoling_County), [Guangdong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangdong), China. He is the younger brother of [Fields Medalist](wikipedia://en/10859) [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137).[^o5]

After graduating from the [Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong), he studied mathematics at the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook), where he learnt after [Henry Laufer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Laufer)[^o4] and earned his M.A. in 1974 and Ph.D. in 1976.[^o6][^o7]

He was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) from 1976 to 1977 and from 1981 to 1982, and was a Benjamin Pierce Assistant Professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 1977 to 1980. He subsequently taught at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) for more than 30 years. He was named the UIC Distinguished Professor in 2005.[^o7]

From 2002 to 2011, Yau also served as the Zi-Jiang Professor at [East China Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Normal_University) in [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai), as well as Director of the university's Institute of Mathematics.[^o6] After retiring from UIC in 2012, he joined [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) as a full-time professor.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Among Yau's research interests are [bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214), [complex algebraic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_algebraic_geometry), singularities theory, and nonlinear filtering.[^o7] He published nearly 300 papers and established the "Yau algebra" and the "Yau number".[^o9] He served as Chairman of the IEEE International Conference on Control and Information and co-founded the *Journal of Algebraic Geometry* in 1991. He founded the journal *Communications in Information and Systems* in 2000, and has served as Editor-in-Chief since its inception.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

Yau was awarded the [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1980 and the [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 2000. He was elected an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) in 2003 and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013.[^o7]
