# James Serrin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Serrin"}

**James Burton Serrin** (1 November 192623 August 2012) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and a professor at [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He graduated from [Evanston Township High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanston_Township_High_School) in 1944. He then studied engineering and science at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 1944 to 1946 before transferring [Western Michigan College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Michigan_College), where he a Bachelor of Arts degree in 1947. He received his doctorate from [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) in 1951 under the supervision of [David Gilbarg](wikipedia://en/31519090).[^o5] From 1954 till 1995, he was on the faculty of the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).[^o6]

## Work {#Work}

He is known for his contributions to [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918), [nonlinear](wikipedia://en/146103) analysis,[^o9] and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).[^o10][^o11][^o12]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1980.
