# Steven Roman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steven Roman"}

**Steven Roman** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently Emeritus Professor of Mathematics at [California State University, Fullerton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_Fullerton) and Visiting Professor of Mathematics at [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine). He is one of the main developers of [umbral calculus](wikipedia://en/214124). He has written about 40 books on mathematics and [computer programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming).

:::

Professor Roman's books have been translated into Portuguese, French, Korean, Chinese, Russian, Polish, Bulgarian, Czech and Spanish.
