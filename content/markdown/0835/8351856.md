# Florent Bureau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Florent Bureau"}

**Florent-Joseph Bureau** (1906–1999) was a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) mathematician born in [Jemeppe-sur-Sambre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jemeppe-sur-Sambre).[^o2] He was a professor at the Faculty of Sciences of the [University of Liège](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liège). He worked on algebraic and differential geometry and the theory of analytical functions. In 1952, he was awarded the [Francqui Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francqui_Prize) on [Exact Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exact_sciences).

:::
