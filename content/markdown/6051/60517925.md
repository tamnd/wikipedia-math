# Wilhelm Schlag

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Schlag"}

**Wilhelm Schlag** (born May 2, 1969) is a mathematician and Phillips Professor of Mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University).[^o2] He is known for his work in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Schlag obtained his PhD at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1996 under the supervision of [Thomas Wolff](wikipedia://en/1099744). Since then, he has held positions at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) where he was H. J. Livingston Professor of Mathematics before moving to Yale University in 2018.[^o4] He has done extensive work in [Fourier Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_Analysis), [Spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) and [dispersive](wikipedia://en/8477417) partial differential equations.[^o5] Schlag is one of the [managing editors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of [Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae).[^o6]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow),[^o8] 2009
- Invited Speaker, [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), 2014
