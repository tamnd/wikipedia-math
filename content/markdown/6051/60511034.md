# Moshe Goldberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moshe Goldberg"}

**Moshe Goldberg** (משה גולדברג; born 1945) is an Israeli mathematician. He is a professor emeritus of mathematics at the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Moshe Goldberg was born and raised in [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv). His parents, Gad and Rachel Raya Goldberg, [immigrated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aliyah) from [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) and [Lithuania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania) to Palestine shortly after Hitler became Germany's chancellor in 1933. After completing his undergraduate studies, Goldberg served in the [Israel Defense Forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Defense_Forces) for three years. Released at the rank of captain, he resumed his studies, earning his Ph.D. from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 1973 under the supervision of Saul Abarbanel.[^o5]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

After a postdoctoral position at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA), Goldberg joined the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion) in 1979, and in due course became the Ruth and Samuel Jaffe Professor of Mathematics.

Goldberg began his scientific career in [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924). He then turned to other topics, including [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [hyperbolic](wikipedia://en/1575825) and [parabolic](wikipedia://en/7466971) partial differential systems, [linear](wikipedia://en/18422) and [Multilinear algebra](wikipedia://en/243946), [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), and various types of [algebras](wikipedia://en/191788).[^o8]

He held visiting positions at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) (Caltech), UCLA, [University of California Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara), and [Université Paris Dauphine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris_Dauphine) (Paris 9).[^o7]

Goldberg published over 80 research papers.[^o8][^o9] In 2013 he retired as [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o10]
