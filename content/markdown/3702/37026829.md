# Lidinoid

*Image: Lidinoid surface.jpg.* Lidinoid in a unit cell.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lidinoid"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **lidinoid** is a [triply periodic](wikipedia://en/31526142) [minimal surface](wikipedia://en/276734). The name comes from its Swedish discoverer Sven Lidin (who called it the HG surface).[^o2]

:::

It has many similarities to the [gyroid](wikipedia://en/3905163), and just as the gyroid is the unique embedded member of the [associate family](wikipedia://en/37016612) of the [Schwarz P surface](wikipedia://en/36763333#Schwarz_P_.28.22Primitive.22.29) the lidinoid is the unique embedded member of the associate family of a [Schwarz H surface](wikipedia://en/36763333#Schwarz_H_.28.22Hexagonal.22.29).[^o3] It belongs to [space group](wikipedia://en/463721) 230(Ia3d).

The Lidinoid can be approximated as a [level set](wikipedia://en/559622):[^o4]

$$\begin{aligned} (1/2)[&\sin(2x) \cos(y)\sin(z)\\ + &\sin(2y)\cos(z) \sin(x)\\ + &\sin(2z)\cos(x) \sin(y)]\\ -& (1/2)[\cos(2x)\cos(2y)\\ + &\cos(2y)\cos(2z)\\ + &\cos(2z)\cos(2x)] + 0.15 = 0 \end{aligned}$$
