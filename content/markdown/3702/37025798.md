# David Nualart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Nualart"}

**David Nualart** (born 21 March 1951)[^o3] is a Spanish mathematician working in the field of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), in particular on aspects of [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) and [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis). He is retired as Black-Babcock Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nualart obtained his PhD titled "Contribución al estudio de la integral estocástica" in 1975 at the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona) under the supervision of Francesc d'Assís Sales Vallès. After positions at the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona) and the [Polytechnique University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnique_University_of_Barcelona) he took up a professorship at the [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas) and was the Black-Babcock Distinguished Professor in its Mathematics Department from 2012 to 2022.[^o3] He retired in 2022.[^o6]

He was the Chief Editor of [Electronic Communications in Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Communications_in_Probability) from 2006 to 2008.[^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

He has been elected a Fellow of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics in 1997. He received a Doctor Honoris Causa by the [Université Blaise Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Blaise_Pascal) of Clermond-Ferrand in 1998. He received the Prize IBERDROLA de Ciencia y Tecnologia in 1999. He has been a Corresponding Member of the Real Academia de Ciencias Exactas Fisicas y Naturales of Madrid since 2003. He has been a member of the Reial Academia de Ciencies i Arts of Barcelona since 2003. He received the Research Prize of the Real Academia de Ciencias de Madrid in 1991.[^o3]

In March 2011 the International Conference on [Malliavin Calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malliavin_Calculus) and Stochastic Analysis in honor of David Nualart took place at [University of Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kansas). The results were published in 2013 as a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift), *Malliavin Calculus and Stochastic Analysis: A Festschrift in Honor of David Nualart*.[^o8]

He was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to Malliavin calculus, stochastic PDE's, and fractional Brownian motion".[^o9]
