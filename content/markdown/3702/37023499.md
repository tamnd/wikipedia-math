# Jean Chazy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Chazy"}

**Jean François Chazy** (15 August 1882, [Villefranche-sur-Saône](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villefranche-sur-Saône) – 9 March 1955, [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989)) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer).

*Image: Jean Chazy.jpg.*

:::

## Life {#Life}

Chazy was the son of a small provincial manufacturer and studied mathematics at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) with completion of the [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in 1905. He received his doctorate in 1910 with thesis *Équations différentielles du troisième ordre et d’ordre supérieur don't l’intégrale générale a ses points critiques fixes*. In 1911 he was *[maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)* for mechanics in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble) and then in [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille). In World War I he served in the artillery and became famous for accurately predicting the location of the [German siege gun which bombarded Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Gun).[^o3] After the war he was again professor in the *Faculté des Sciences de Lille* (which later became the [Lille University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille_University_of_Science_and_Technology)). Simultaneously he taught at the *Institut industriel du Nord* ([École Centrale de Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Centrale_de_Lille)). In 1923 he was *maître de conférences* at the *[École centrale des arts et manufactures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Centrale_Paris)* in Paris (as well as examiner at the *École polytechnique*). In 1924 he became professor for mechanics and later for celestial mechanics at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), where he retired in 1953 as professor emeritus.

## Work {#Work}

He worked on celestial mechanics and especially on the [three-body problem](wikipedia://en/1208420) and the [perihelion precession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apsidal_precession) of [Mercury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercury_%28planet%29)'s orbit. The problem of explaining Mercury's orbit was solved by [Albert Einstein](wikipedia://en/736)'s [general relativity theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_relativity_theory).

## Honors {#Honors}

In 1922 Chazy was awarded the [Valz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valz_Prize) from the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) for his papers on the [three-body problem](wikipedia://en/1208420).[^o6] He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1924 at Toronto[^o7] and in 1928 at Bologna. In 1937 he was elected to the *[Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences)* in the *Astronomie* section. He was also a member of the Romanian Academy of Sciences and a member of the Belgian Academy of Sciences. In 1934 he was president of the *[Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France)*. Since 1952 he was an official member of the *[Bureau des Longitudes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_des_Longitudes)*. He was made a commander of the *[Légion d'honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour)*.
