# Offered load

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Offered load"}

In the mathematical theory of probability, **offered load** is a concept in queuing theory. The offered load is a measure of traffic in a queue. The offered load is given by [Little's law](wikipedia://en/184920): the arrival rate into the queue (symbolized with [λ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lambda)) multiplied by the mean holding time (symbolized by [τ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tau)), equals the average amount of time spent by items in the queue. Offered load is expressed in [Erlang units](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlang_unit) or [call-seconds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call-second) per hour,[^o2] a dimensionless measure.

:::
