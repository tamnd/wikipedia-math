# Daya-Nand Verma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daya-Nand Verma"}

**Daya-Nand Verma** (25 June 1933, [Varanasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varanasi) – 10 June 2012, [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai)[^o2]) was a mathematician at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) during the period 1968-1993. The construction of [Verma modules](wikipedia://en/2975234) appears in his Ph.D. thesis as a student of [Nathan Jacobson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nathan_Jacobson) at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University).[^o2]

:::

## Select publications {#Select_publications}

- Verma, Daya-Nand. (1968). "Structure of certain induced representations of complex semisimple Lie algebras, Yale Univ., dissertation, 1966". Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. Volume 74, Number 1. p. 160–166.
- Verma, Daya-Nand. (1971). "Mobius inversion for the Bruhat ordering on a Weyl Group". Ann. Sci. Ecole Norm. Sup. 4e Serie, t.4. p. 393–398.
- J.E.Humphreys and D.N.Verma. (1973). "Projective Modules for finite Chevalley Groups". Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. Volume 79. p. 467–468.
- Verma, Daya-Nand. (1975). "Role of Affine Weyl groups in the representation theory of Chevalley groups and their Lie algebras in Lie Groups and their Representations, ed. I.M.Gelfand". Halsted, New York. p. 653–705.
