# Mauro Picone

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mauro Picone"}

**Mauro Picone** (2 May 1885 – 11 April 1977) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is known for the [Picone identity](wikipedia://en/10163390), the [Sturm-Picone comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sturm-Picone_comparison_theorem) and being the founder of the [Istituto per le Applicazioni del Calcolo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_per_le_Applicazioni_del_Calcolo), presently named after him, the first [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) institute ever founded.[^o3] He was also an outstanding teacher of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396): some of the best Italian mathematicians were among his pupils.
