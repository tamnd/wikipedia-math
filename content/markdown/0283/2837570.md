# Godunov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Godunov"}

**Godunov** (Годунов) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) surname.

:::

**Godunov** can refer to the following:

- House of Godunov, two [tsars of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar#Russia) and their kin:
  - [Tsar Boris Fyodorovich Godunov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Godunov) (1552–1605), a regent of Russia from 1584 to 1598 and then tsar from 1598 to 1605
  - [Tsar Fyodor Borisovich Godunov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feodor_II_of_Russia) (1589–1605), son of tsar Boris Godunov, ruled less than a year as Feodor II after his father's death in 1605; murdered in June the same year
  - [Xenia Borisovna Godunova (later the Nun Olga)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsarevna_Xenia_Borisovna_of_Russia) (1582–1622), daughter of tsar Boris Godunov
  - [Irina Feodorovna Godunova (later the Nun Alexandra)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irina_Godunova) (1557–1603), wife of tsar Feodor I Ioannovich and sister of tsar Boris Godunov
- [Sergei Konstantinovich Godunov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_K._Godunov) (1929–2023), a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) born [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who contributed to the [finite volume method](wikipedia://en/345017) (FVM)
  - [Godunov's scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godunov's_scheme), a mathematical method suggested by Sergei Godunov in 1959
  - [Godunov's theorem](wikipedia://en/5678057), a theorem proven by Sergei Godunov in 1954
- [Aleksandr Borisovich Godunov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Godunov) (1949–1995), a Russian ballet dancer and actor
- [Petr Ivanovich Godunov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petr_Ivanovich_Godunov)(?–1670), a Siberian governor
  - [Godunov map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godunov_map), commissioned by Alexis of Russia on 15 November 1667 and named after Petr Ivanovich Godunov
- [*Godunov*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godunov_%28TV_series%29), a Russian television drama TV series, centered upon the fate of the Godunov family
