# Tychonoff cube

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tychonoff cube"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), more specifically in [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649), the **Tychonoff cube** is the generalization of the [unit cube](wikipedia://en/407763) from the [product](wikipedia://en/24910) of a finite number of [unit intervals](wikipedia://en/49324) to the product of an infinite, even [uncountable](wikipedia://en/32248) number of unit intervals. The Tychonoff cube is named after [Andrey Tychonoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrey_Tychonoff), who first considered the arbitrary product of [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) and who proved in the 1930s that the Tychonoff cube is [compact](wikipedia://en/6042). Tychonoff later generalized this to the product of collections of arbitrary compact spaces. This result is now known as [Tychonoff's theorem](wikipedia://en/82739) and is considered one of the most important results in general topology.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $I$ denote the [unit interval](wikipedia://en/49324) $[0,1]$. Given a [cardinal number](wikipedia://en/6173) $\kappa \geq \aleph_0$, we define a Tychonoff cube of [weight](wikipedia://en/59220#Weight_and_character) $\kappa$ as the space $I^\kappa$ with the [product topology](wikipedia://en/24910), i.e. the product $\prod_{s\in S} I_s$ where $\kappa$ is the [cardinality](wikipedia://en/6174) of $S$ and, for all $s\in S$, $I_s = I$.

The [Hilbert cube](wikipedia://en/216389), $I^{\aleph_0}$, is a special case of a Tychonoff cube.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840) is assumed throughout.

- The Tychonoff cube is compact.
- Given a [cardinal number](wikipedia://en/6173) $\lambda \leq \kappa$, the space $I^\lambda$ is [embeddable](wikipedia://en/52119) in $I^\kappa$.
- The Tychonoff cube $I^\kappa$ is a [universal space](wikipedia://en/47882363) for every [compact space](wikipedia://en/6042) of [weight](wikipedia://en/59220#Weight_and_character) $\kappa \geq \aleph_0$.
- The Tychonoff cube $I^\kappa$ is a [universal space](wikipedia://en/47882363) for every [Tychonoff space](wikipedia://en/30554) of [weight](wikipedia://en/59220#Weight_and_character) $\kappa \geq \aleph_0$.
- The [character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_%28topology%29) of $x\in I^\kappa$ is $\kappa$.
- All [Tychonoff spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tychonoff_spaces) are [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to a subspace of a Tychonoff cube (known as the Embedding Theorem in some texts).

:::
