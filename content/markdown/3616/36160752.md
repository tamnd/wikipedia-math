# Morley centers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Morley centers"}

In [plane geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_geometry), the **Morley centers** are two special points associated with a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654). Both of them are [triangle centers](wikipedia://en/12401488). One of them called **first Morley center**[^o2] (or simply, the **Morley center**[^o3] ) is designated as X(356) in [Clark Kimberling](wikipedia://en/25536421)'s [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593), while the other point called **second Morley center**[^o2] (or the **1st Morley–Taylor–Marr Center**[^o3]) is designated as X(357). The two points are also related to [Morley's trisector theorem](wikipedia://en/1096323) which was discovered by [Frank Morley](wikipedia://en/589167) in around 1899.

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let $\triangle DEF$ be the triangle formed by the intersections of the adjacent [angle trisectors](wikipedia://en/91111) of triangle $\triangle ABC$. $\triangle DEF$ is called the *Morley triangle* of $\triangle ABC$. [Morley's trisector theorem](wikipedia://en/1096323) states that the Morley triangle of any triangle is always an [equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/173285).

### First Morley center {#First_Morley_center}

Let $\triangle DEF$ be the Morley triangle of $\triangle ABC$. The [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926) of $\triangle DEF$ is called the *first Morley center* of $\triangle ABC$.[^o2][^o7]

### Second Morley center {#Second_Morley_center}

Let $\triangle DEF$ be the Morley triangle of $\triangle ABC$. Then, the lines $AD, BE, CF$ are concurrent. The point of concurrence is called the *second Morley center* of triangle $\triangle ABC$.[^o2][^o7]

:::

## Trilinear coordinates {#Trilinear_coordinates}

### First Morley center {#First_Morley_center}

The [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) of the first Morley center of triangle $\triangle ABC$ are [^o2]

$$\cos \tfrac{A}{3} + 2 \cos \tfrac{B}{3} \cos \tfrac{C}{3} : \cos \tfrac{B}{3} + 2 \cos \tfrac{C}{3} \cos \tfrac{A}{3} : \cos \tfrac{C}{3} + 2 \cos \tfrac{A}{3} \cos \tfrac{B}{3}$$

### Second Morley center {#Second_Morley_center}

The trilinear coordinates of the second Morley center are

$$\sec \tfrac{A}{3} : \sec \tfrac{B}{3} : \sec \tfrac{C}{3}$$
