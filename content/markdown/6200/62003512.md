# Dirk Kroese

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dirk Kroese"}

**Dirk Pieter Kroese** (born 1963) is a Dutch-Australian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), and Emeritus Professor at the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland). He is known for several significant contributions to [applied probability](wikipedia://en/366719), [kernel density estimation](wikipedia://en/2090057), [Monte Carlo methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo_methods) and [rare-event simulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rare_event_sampling). He is, with [Reuven Rubinstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reuven_Rubinstein), a pioneer of the [Cross-Entropy (CE) method](wikipedia://en/5767980).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in Wapenveld (municipality of [Heerde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heerde)), Dirk Kroese received his MSc ([Netherlands Ingenieur (ir) degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer's_degree)) in 1986 and his Ph.D. (cum laude) in 1990, both from the Department of Applied Mathematics at the [University of Twente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Twente). His dissertation was entitled *Stochastic Models in Reliability*. His PhD advisors were Joseph H. A. de Smit and Wilbert C. M. Kallenberg.[^o4] Part of his PhD research was carried out at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the guidance of [Erhan Çınlar](wikipedia://en/32911231). He has held teaching and research positions at [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) (1986), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (1988–1989), the [University of Twente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Twente) (1991–1998), the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) (1997), and the [University of Adelaide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Adelaide) (1998–2000). Since 2000 he has been working at the [University of Queensland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Queensland), where he became a full professor in 2010.[^o5]

## Work {#Work}

Kroese's work spans a wide range of topics in applied probability and mathematical statistics, including telecommunication networks, [reliability engineering](wikipedia://en/1724836), point processes, kernel density estimation, Monte Carlo methods, rare-event simulation, cross-entropy methods, randomized optimization, and machine learning. He is a Chief Investigator of the [Australian Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Research_Council) Centre of Excellence in Mathematical and Statistical Frontiers (ACEMS).[^o7] He has over 140 peer-reviewed publications,[^o8] including 10 books.[^o9]
