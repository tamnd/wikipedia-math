# Doug Lind

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Doug Lind"}

**Doug Lind** is an American mathematician specializing in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). He is a professor emeritus at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington).[^o3] Lind was named as one of the inaugural fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013.[^o4] He is a board member of [Spectra](wikipedia://en/61935783), an association for LGBT mathematicians.[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Lind received his PhD from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1973. His advisor was [Donald Samuel Ornstein](wikipedia://en/9605775) and the title of his dissertation was *Locally Compact Measure Preserving Flows*.[^o7]
