# Narutaka Ozawa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Narutaka Ozawa"}

**Narutaka Ozawa** (born 1974) is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) and [discrete groups](wikipedia://en/468536). He has been a professor at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) since 2013. He earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1997 from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) and a Ph.D. in mathematics in 2000 from the same institution. One year later he received a Ph.D. in mathematics from [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University). He was selected for one of the prestigious [Sloan Research Fellowships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 2005[^o3] and was an invited speaker at the 2006 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid where he gave a talk on "Amenable actions and Applications".[^o4] He has won numerous prizes including the Mathematical Society of Japan (MSJ) Spring Prize and the Japan Society for the Promotion of Science (JSPS) Prize. Before becoming a full professor at Kyoto University in 2013, he was an associate professor at the University of Tokyo and at [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o5]
