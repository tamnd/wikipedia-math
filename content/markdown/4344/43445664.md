::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated square antiprism"}

The **truncated square antiprism** one in an infinite series of truncated antiprisms, constructed as a [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) [square antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860045). It has 18 faces, 2 octagons, 8 hexagons, and 8 squares.

:::

## Gyroelongated triamond square bicupola {#Gyroelongated_triamond_square_bicupola}

If the hexagons are folded, it can be constructed by regular polygons. Or each folded hexagon can be replaced by two [triamonds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triamond), adding 8 edges (56), and 4 faces (32). This form is called a *gyroelongated triamond square bicupola*.[^o9]
