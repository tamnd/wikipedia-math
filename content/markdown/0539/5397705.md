# Wolfgang Heinrich Johannes Fuchs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Heinrich Johannes Fuchs"}

**Wolfgang Heinrich Johannes Fuchs** (May 19, 1915 – February 24, 1997) was a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). His main area of research was [Nevanlinna theory](wikipedia://en/393135).

:::

Fuchs received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1941 from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), under the direction of [Albert Ingham](wikipedia://en/5380138).[^o3] He joined the faculty of [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1950 and spent the rest of his career there.
