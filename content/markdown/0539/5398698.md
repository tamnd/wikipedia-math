# Karl Prachar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Prachar"}

*Image: Karl Prachar.jpg.* **Karl Prachar**, Wien 1967

**Karl Prachar** (; 1924 – November 27, 1994) was an Austrian mathematician who worked in the area of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513). He is known for his much acclaimed book on the distribution of the [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666), *Primzahlverteilung* (Springer Verlag, 1957).

:::

Prachar received his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1947 from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna).
