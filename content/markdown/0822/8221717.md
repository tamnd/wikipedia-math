# Information-based complexity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Information-based complexity"}

**Information-based complexity** (**IBC**) studies optimal [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms) and [computational complexity](wikipedia://en/2230) for the continuous problems that arise in [physical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_science), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering), and [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).

:::
