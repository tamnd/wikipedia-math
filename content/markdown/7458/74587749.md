# Joseph Teran

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Teran"}

**Joseph M. Teran** is an American professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis).[^o3] His research considers numerical methods for [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) based on [classical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_physics), including applications in [virtual surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surgery_simulator) and movie [special effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_effects).[^o4]

:::

He played a role in simulating snow and ice in the film [*Frozen*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frozen_%282013_film%29), collaborating with Disney's animators.[^o5]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After graduating from the University of California, Davis, in 2000, Teran completed a Ph.D. at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 2005.[^o3] His dissertation, *Novel Finite Element Algorithms With Applications To Skeletal Muscle Simulation*, was supervised by [Ronald Fedkiw](wikipedia://en/6583046).

He was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) from 2005 to 2007, and a faculty member at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) from 2007 to 2020. In 2020 he returned to the University of California, Davis as a professor of mathematics.[^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Teran's accolades include:

- 2018: Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o9]
- 2012: Distinguished Alumni Scholar in Computer Science at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University)[^o10]
- 2011: [Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Early_Career_Award_for_Scientists_and_Engineers)[^o11]
- 2010: Young Investigator Award from the [Office of Naval Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Naval_Research)[^o12]
- 2008: Recognized as one of the "20 Best Brains Under 40" by [Discover Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discover_Magazine)[^o13]
