# Marco Antei

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marco Antei"}

Antei **Marco Antei** (born 1978, [Sanremo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanremo)) is an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy) mathematician and [LGBT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LGBT_community)+ activist.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Antei was awarded his PhD in mathematics in 2008 from the [University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille)[^o4] under the supervision of Michel Emsalem. He later worked at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), the [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST) in [Daejeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daejeon), the [Ben-Gurion University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev) in [Beersheba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beersheba), the [Côte d'Azur University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Côte_d'Azur_University) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice) before joining the [University of Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Costa_Rica).[^o5] He has been lecturer at [Lucerne University of Applied Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucerne_University_of_Applied_Sciences_and_Arts) since 2022. Antei studies within the field of geometry. His areas of interest in research focus on algebraic and arithmetic geometry, and applications. He particularly studies the [fundamental group scheme](wikipedia://en/27509612), [torsors](wikipedia://en/41910974) and their connections.

## The fundamental group scheme {#The_fundamental_group_scheme}

The existence of the fundamental group scheme was conjectured by [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042), while the first proof of its existence is due, for schemes defined over fields, to [Madhav Nori](wikipedia://en/71398482).[^o7][^o8][^o9] Antei, Michel Emsalem and Carlo Gasbarri proved the existence of the [fundamental group scheme](wikipedia://en/27509612#Over_a_Dedekind_scheme) $\pi_1(X,x)$ for [schemes](wikipedia://en/364754) defined over Dedekind schemes and they also defined, and proved the existence of, the *quasi-finite fundamental group scheme* $\pi^{\text{qf}}(X,x)$.[^o10][^o11]

## Award {#Award}

In 2020, Antei received the *Innovating professor* award at the [University of Costa Rica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Costa_Rica), for being able to move from in presence classes to virtual classes, at the beginning of the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic), in the best possible way. The awarded professors have been selected by the students of the [UCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Costa_Rica).[^o13][^o14]

## Activism {#Activism}

Antei stands out for his commitment to the fight for the rights of the LGBT+ community. In particular, in 2015 he conceived and co-founded the first LGBT+ association in the [province of Imperia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Imperia), M.I.A. (*Movimento Imperiese Arcobaleno*), the Arcigay Imperia local committee, affiliated with Italy's largest LGBT+ association, [Arcigay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcigay). He served as president until 2018,[^o16][^o17] and then again from January 2023.[^o18] He definitively left the office in December 2025. During his first term, he also co-organized in November 2016 the first [Transgender Day of Remembrance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transgender_Day_of_Remembrance) in the city of [Sanremo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanremo). In 2020, he took part in a remote meeting with [European Commission President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_the_European_Commission) [Ursula von der Leyen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ursula_von_der_Leyen), in which the rights of LGBT+ people within the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) were discussed, with particular emphasis on the situations in Hungary and Poland.[^o19][^o20] During his final term, the association under his presidency, in collaboration with Agedo Genova, created the *Premio Queer*, an award unofficially linked to the [Sanremo Music Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanremo_Music_Festival).[^o21]
