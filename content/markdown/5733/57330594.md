# Michael Rotkowitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Rotkowitz"}

**Michael Charles Rotkowitz** (born 1974) is an applied mathematician best known for his work in [decentralized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralized_control) [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039). He was a professor at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), where he held appointments in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) and the Institute for Systems Research (ISR), and was also affiliated with the Applied Mathematics & Statistics, and Scientific Computation Program (AMSC).

:::

Rotkowitz took leave from his university position to work as a staff research scientist with [Lyft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyft) Marketplace Labs in [San Francisco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco), subsequently worked with [Amazon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_%28company%29) Transportation Services’ Middle Mile team, and now works with [Instacart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instacart).

Born in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), Rotkowitz grew up in [Old Bridge Township, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Old_Bridge_Township,_New_Jersey).[^o3]

## Education {#Education}

Rotkowitz received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in mathematical and computational science, an [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), and an [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [aeronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautics) and [astronautics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronautics), all from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Rotkowitz was the postdoctoral fellow in networked embedded control at the [Royal Institute of Technology (KTH)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institute_of_Technology) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) from 2005 to 2006, and a research fellow with the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University) in [Canberra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canberra), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia) from 2006 to 2008. From 2008 to 2011 he served as a research fellow, senior research fellow, and [Queen Elizabeth II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Elizabeth_II) Fellow at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne). From 2012 to 2019 he was assistant professor in the Department of Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE) and the Institute for Systems Research (ISR), and was also affiliated with the Applied Mathematics & Statistics, and Scientific Computation Program (AMSC), taking leave in 2018 to work in the tech industry.

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE) [George S. Axelby Outstanding Paper Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_S._Axelby_Outstanding_Paper_Award) (2007) for "A Characterization of Convex Problems in Decentralized Control"[^o7]
- [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Control and Systems Theory Prize (2011) Conferred: "for Contributions to the theory of optimal controller synthesis for decentralized systems subject to information and control constraints."[^o8][^o9]
- [National Science Foundation (NSF) CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards) (2014)
- [Presidential Early Career Award for Scientists and Engineers (PECASE)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Early_Career_Award_for_Scientists_and_Engineers)[^o10]
