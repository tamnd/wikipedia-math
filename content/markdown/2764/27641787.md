# Gottfried Köthe

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gottfried Köthe"}

**Gottfried Maria Hugo Köthe** (25 December 1905 – 30 April 1989) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

## Scientific career {#Scientific_career}

In 1923 Köthe enrolled in the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz). He started studying [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), but switched to mathematics a year later after meeting the [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) [Alfred Kastil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Kastil). In 1927 he submitted his thesis *Beiträge zu Finslers Grundlegung der Mengenlehre* ("Contributions to Finsler's foundations of set theory") and was awarded a doctorate. After spending a year in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) working with [Paul Finsler](wikipedia://en/27007280), Köthe received a fellowship to visit the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), where he attended the lectures of [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) and [Bartel van der Waerden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartel_van_der_Waerden) on the emerging subject of abstract algebra. He began working in [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424) and in 1930 published the [Köthe conjecture](wikipedia://en/3100105) stating that a sum of two left [nil ideals](wikipedia://en/3100140) in an arbitrary [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) is a nil ideal. By a recommendation of Emmy Noether, he was appointed an assistant of [Otto Toeplitz](wikipedia://en/547682) in [Bonn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn_University) in 1929–1930. During this time he began transition to functional analysis. He continued scientific collaboration with Toeplitz for several years afterward.

Köthe's [Habilitationsschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitationsschrift), *Schiefkörper unendlichen Ranges über dem Zentrum* ("Skew fields of infinite rank over the center"), was accepted in 1931. He became Privatdozent at [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) under [Heinrich Behnke](wikipedia://en/22922678). During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) he was involved in coding work. In 1946 he was appointed the director of the Mathematics Institute at the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz) and he served as a dean (1948–1950) and a rector of the university (1954–1956). In 1957 he became the founding director of the Institute for Applied Mathematics at the [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg) and served as a rector of the university (1960–1961).

Köthe's best known work has been in the theory of [topological vector spaces](wikipedia://en/45752). In 1960, volume 1 of his seminal monograph *Topologische lineare Räume* was published (the second edition was translated into English in 1969). It was not until 1979 that volume 2 appeared, this time written in English. He also made contributions to the theory of [lattices](wikipedia://en/469304).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1928 in Bologna,[^o5] in 1932 in Zurich, and in 1936 in Oslo
- Heidelberg Academy of Sciences (1960)
- Gauss medal, Brunswick Academy of Sciences (1963)
- [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina), Halle (1968)
- Honorary degrees from [University of Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montpellier) (1965), [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) (1980), [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz) (1981) and [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University) (1981).
