# Paul Biran

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Biran"}

**Paul Ian Biran** (פאול בירן; born 25 February 1969) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He holds a chair at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). His research interests include [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Born in [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) in 1969, Biran's family moved to Israel in 1971. He attended [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), where he earned his Bachelor's degree in 1994 and Ph.D. in 1997[^o3] under supervision of [Leonid Polterovich](wikipedia://en/32556386) (thesis: *Geometry of Symplectic Packing*).

## Career {#Career}

From 1997 to 1999, Biran was a "Szego Assistant Professor" at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). At [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), he was a lecturer from 1997 to 2001, a senior lecturer from 2001 to 2005, an associate professor in 2005, and a full professor in 2008. In 2009, Biran became a full professor of mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

Biran was awarded the [Oberwolfach Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach_Prize) in 2003, the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) in 2004, and the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize) in 2006. In 2013 he became a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o7]
