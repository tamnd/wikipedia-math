# Freddy Delbaen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Freddy Delbaen"}

**Freddy Delbaen** (born 21 November 1946 in [Duffel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duffel), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium)) is a Belgian-Swiss mathematician. He is professor emeritus of [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics) at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o3]

:::

Delbaen made fundamental contributions to the mathematical theory of [arbitrage](wikipedia://en/3162) including proving, together with [Walter Schachermayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Schachermayer), a general version of the [fundamental theorem of asset pricing](wikipedia://en/442967).[^o4] He also introduced in a jointly written paper the notion of the [risk measure](wikipedia://en/12498127).[^o5]

His research includes topics in [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [actuarial mathematics](wikipedia://en/189373).

## Life {#Life}

Delbaen was born in 1946 in Duffel in the province of [Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antwerp).[^o3] He studied mathematics at the [Free University of Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit_Brussel) and received his doctorate there in 1971 under the supervision of [Lucien Waelbroeck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucien_Waelbroeck).[^o7]

From 1971 to 1995 he was a professor at the Free University of Brussels and at the [University of Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Antwerp). In 1995, Delbaen became a full professor at the ETH Zurich, remaining there until his retirement in 2008. He is still a professor emeritus at ETH and, since 2011, also a guest lecturer at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich).[^o3]

Delbaen is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) since 2011[^o8] and the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) since 2013.[^o9] He is also a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) since 2020.[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Together with Walter Schachermayer, he proved a general form of the fundamental theorem of asset pricing for (locally) bounded [semimartingales](wikipedia://en/9653775), replacing the condition of "no arbitrage" with the term [no free lunch with vanishing risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/No_free_lunch_with_vanishing_risk) (NFLVR).[^o12] The two also proved a version for unbounded price processes.[^o13]

In a joint paper with P. Artzner, J. M. Eber and [D. Heath](wikipedia://en/48356165), he introduced the concept of (coherent) risk measure on a finite probability space.[^o14] Delbaen later generalized the concept to general probability spaces.[^o15]
