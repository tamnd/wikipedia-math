# Antonio Collalto (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antonio Collalto"}

*Image: Antonio Collalto.jpg.* Antonio Collalto

**Antonio Collalto** (21 or 22 April 1765, [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice) – 16 July 1820, [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padua)) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was from a modest and otherwise unrecorded family. According to [Emmanuele Antonio Cicogna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuele_Antonio_Cicogna), his surname did not indicate a connection with the [house of Collalto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Collalto) but instead his status as a Jewish convert to Catholicism in his youth. He was unable to study at the [Patriarchal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriarchate_of_Venice) Seminary of Venice, run by the [Somaschi Fathers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somaschi_Fathers), and gained a scientific education from the physicist [Vincenzo Miotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincenzo_Miotti). He completed his studies and then became a priest. In 1795 he became professor of maths and physics in the public schools of Venice. In 1806 he became professor of 'introduzione al calcolo sublime' (introductory sublime [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176)) and [geodesy](wikipedia://en/12608) at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua), but was sacked when the [Austrian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Empire) took back control in 1814 for his reputation as a [Jacobin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobin). From 1815 onwards he was a member of the [Accademia nazionale delle scienze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_nazionale_delle_scienze).

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- *Metodo analitico per conoscere la fallacia di alcune dimostrazioni* (*Analytical method to know the fallacy of some demonstrations*), 1792.
- *Discorso sul metodo di studiare le matematiche* (*Discourse on how to study mathematics*), 1793.
- *Identità del calcolo differenziale con quello delle serie ovvero il metodo degli infinitamente piccoli di leibnizio spiegato e dimostrato colla teria delle funzioni di Lagrange* (*Identification of [differential calculus](wikipedia://en/50416) with series calculus or the infinitely small [Leibnitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibnitz) method explained and demonstrated with the theories of [Lagrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrange) functions*), 1802. (digitized version edited by the [BEIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biblioteca_europea_di_informazione_e_cultura))
- *Dell'istruzione teorico-pratica degli ingegneri* (*On the theoretical and practical training of engineers*), 1804.
- *Geometria analitica a due coordinate* (*Analytic geometry to two coordinates*), 1806.
- *Nuove lezioni di geometria analitica a tre coordinate* (*New analytical geometry lessons to three coordinates*), 1809.
- *Nuovo saggio di poliendrometria analitica* (*A new essay on analytical [polyendrometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyendrometry)*), 1810.
