# Indicator (statistics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Indicator"}

In statistics and [research design](wikipedia://en/3345681), an **indicator** is an observed value of a [variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable_%28statistics%29), or in other words "a sign of a presence or absence of the concept being studied".[^o2] Just like each color indicates in a traffic lights the change in the movement.

:::

For example, if a variable is [religiosity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religiosity), and a [unit of analysis](wikipedia://en/4145703) is an individual, then that one of potentially more numerous indicators of that individual's religiosity would be whether they attend religious services; others - how often, or whether they donate money to religious organizations.[^o2]

Numerous indicators can be aggregated into an [index](wikipedia://en/43558650).[^o3]

The complexity of biological systems makes evaluating them a challenge. [Bioindicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioindicator), such as [indicator bacteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indicator_bacteria), are [ecological indicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_indicator), sometimes requiring further consideration of [environmental indicators](wikipedia://en/4951662). In [public health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_health) study, one relies on [health indicators](wikipedia://en/38853390). In a given locality, [community indicators](wikipedia://en/22963407) inform planners, while the [design quality indicator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Design_quality_indicator) can be the basis of building permits. Assessment of social conditions relies on [sustainability indicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sustainability_indicators) or the [genuine progress indicator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genuine_progress_indicator). Standard measurements are given in the [OECD Main Economic Indicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OECD_Main_Economic_Indicators). A famous popular science individual psychological assessment is the [Myers–Briggs Type Indicator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myers–Briggs_Type_Indicator).
