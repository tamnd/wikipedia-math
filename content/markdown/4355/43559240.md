# Distinguished limit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Distinguished limit"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **distinguished limit** is an appropriately chosen scale factor used in the [method of matched asymptotic expansions](wikipedia://en/4265892).
