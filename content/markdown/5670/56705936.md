# Margaret Cheney (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret Cheney"}

**Margaret Cheney** (born 1955) is an American mathematician whose research involves [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956). She is Yates Chair and Professor of Mathematics at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Cheney graduated from [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College) in 1976, with a double major in mathematics and physics. She completed her Ph.D. in 1982 at [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington). Her dissertation, *Quantum Mechanical Scattering and Inverse Scattering in Two Dimensions*, was supervised by [Roger G. Newton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_G._Newton).

After postdoctoral study at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), Cheney took a faculty position at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) in 1984, and moved to the [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) in 1988. In 2012 she moved again to Colorado State University as Yates Chair.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2000, Cheney became the inaugural [Lise Meitner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lise_Meitner) Visiting Professor at [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University).

Cheney was elected as a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) in 2009 "for contributions to inverse problems in acoustics and electromagnetic theory". In 2012, Oberlin College gave her an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate).
