# Sylvie Boldo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvie Boldo"}

**Sylvie Boldo** is a French mathematician and computer scientist. Her research combines [automated theorem proving](wikipedia://en/2546) and [computer arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_arithmetic), focusing on the formal verification of [floating-point arithmetic](wikipedia://en/11376) operations and of [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) based on them. She is a director of research for the [French Institute for Research in Computer Science and Automation](wikipedia://en/318413) (INRIA), affiliated with the Formal Methods Laboratory at [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University) and the INRIA Saclay-Île-de-France Research Centre, where she co-leads the Toccata project for formally verified programs, certified tools and numerical computations. She is also the founding jury president for the French [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in computer science.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Boldo completed her Ph.D. at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon) in 2004, and has been affiliated with INRIA Saclay since 2005. She completed her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in 2014, with the habilitation thesis *Deductive Formal Verification: How To Make Your Floating-Point Programs Behave*.

In 2021, France began offering an *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* in computer science, and selected Boldo as the founding president of its jury.

## Books {#Books}

Boldo is the author of books including:

- *Computer Arithmetic and Formal Proofs: Verifying Floating-point Algorithms with the Coq System* (with Guillaume Melquiond, ISTE Press / Elsevier, 2017)
- *Une introduction à la science informatique pour les enseignants de la discipline en lycée* (with Dowek, Archambault, Baccelli, Bouhinou, Cegielski, Clausen, Guessarian, Lopes, Mounier, Nguyen, Quessette, Rasse, Rozoy, Timsit, Viéville, and Vincent, CRDP Paris, 2011)
