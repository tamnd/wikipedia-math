# Joseph Mazur

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Mazur"}

*Image: JosephMazur2017a.jpg.* Joseph Mazur at a launch of the Italian translation of his book *Fluke* in Milano, Italy - October 24, 2017

**Joseph C. Mazur** (born in the [Bronx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronx) in 1942) is Professor Emeritus of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Marlboro College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlboro_College), in [Marlboro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlboro,_Vermont), [Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont).

:::

He holds a B.S. from [Pratt Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pratt_Institute), where he first studied [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture). He spent his junior year in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), studying mathematics in classes with [Claude Chevalley](wikipedia://en/366040) and [Roger Godement](wikipedia://en/971810) and returned to Pratt to earn a B.S. in mathematics. From there he went directly to [M.I.T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.I.T) to receive his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics ([algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997)) in 1972. He has held a visiting scholar position at M.I.T and several visiting professor positions at The Mathematics Institute of the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick).

In 2006 he was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) for work on mathematical narrative. In 2008 he was awarded a Bellagio Fellowship from the Rockefeller Foundation, and in 2009 was elected to Fellow of the [Vermont Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences). In 2011, 2013, and 2019 he was awarded Bogliasco Fellowships.

Since 1972 he has taught all areas of mathematics, its [history](wikipedia://en/14220) and [philosophy](wikipedia://en/46439). He has authored many educational software programs, including Explorations in [Calculus](wikipedia://en/5176), the first interactive, multimedia CD package of simulations for calculus. He is the author of several mathematics books that have been translated into more than a dozen languages. He is also interested in [history of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science). He is the brother of [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679).
