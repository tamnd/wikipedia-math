# L. Mahadevan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="L. Mahadevan"}

Mahadevan**Lakshminarayanan Mahadevan** is an Indian-American [scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientist). He is currently the Lola England de Valpine Professor of Applied Mathematics, Organismic and Evolutionary Biology and Physics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). His work centers around understanding the organization of matter in space and time (that is, how it is shaped and how it flows, particularly at the scale observable by the unaided senses, in both physical and biological systems). Mahadevan is a 2009 [MacArthur Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacArthur_Fellows_Program) and an elected member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) as well as the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Mahadevan graduated from the [Indian Institute of Technology, Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology,_Madras), and then received an M.S. from the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), and an M.S. and Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1995.

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

He started his independent career on the faculty at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1996. In 2000, he was elected the inaugural [Schlumberger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schlumberger) Professor of Complex Physical Systems in the [Department of Applied Mathematics and Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Applied_Mathematics_and_Theoretical_Physics), and a professorial fellow of [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), the first Indian to be appointed professor to the Faculty of Mathematics there. He has been at Harvard since 2003.

Since 2017, he and his wife Amala Mahadevan have been faculty deans of [Mather House](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mather_House_%28Harvard_University%29), one of twelve residential houses at Harvard College.
