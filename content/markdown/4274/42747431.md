In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **dodecahedral-icosahedral honeycomb** is a uniform [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654), constructed from [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407), [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), and [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023) cells, in a [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).
