::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Tetrahedral-cubic honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **tetrahedron-cube honeycomb** is a compact uniform [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654), constructed from [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), and [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) cells, in a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring Coxeter diagram, , and is named by its two regular cells.
