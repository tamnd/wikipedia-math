::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Hyperbolic tetrahedral-octahedral honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **tetrahedron-octahedron honeycomb** is a compact uniform [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654), constructed from [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) and [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) cells, in a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).

:::

It represents a [semiregular honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_honeycomb) as defined by all regular cells, although from the Wythoff construction, rectified tetrahedral r{3,3}, becomes the regular [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) {3,4}.
