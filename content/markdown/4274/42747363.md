In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **cubic-octahedral honeycomb** is a compact uniform [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654), constructed from [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635), and [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) cells, in a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). It has a single-ring [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), , and is named by its two regular cells.
