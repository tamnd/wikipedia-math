# March Boedihardjo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="March Boedihardjo"}

[(沈) Boedihardjo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%28沈%29_Boedihardjo)

**March Tian Boedihardjo** () is a Hong Kong mathematician. He is a former [child prodigy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_prodigy) of ethnic [Hokkien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoklo_people) descent with ancestry from [Anxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anxi_County), [Quanzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quanzhou), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China).[^o3] As of 2023, he is an assistant professor of mathematics at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Boedihardjo was born to an ethnic Chinese family in Hong Kong, with family roots in [Anxi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anxi_County), China. Boedihardjo moved to the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) in 2005, when his older brother Horatio began studying at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o8]

Boedihardjo finished his [A-level exams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advanced_Level_%28UK%29) in Britain at the age of nine years and three months, after attending [Greene's College Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greene's_Tutorial_College).[^o6][^o7] He also gained 8 [GCSEs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GCSE).[^o8] He was accepted at [Hong Kong Baptist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Baptist_University), making him the youngest ever university student in Hong Kong. He commenced his university studies in September 2007.[^o9] The university tailored a special 5-year curriculum programme for Boedihardjo which he criticized as being too easy and unstimulating on the first day.[^o10][^o11] He obtained A−'s and B+'s in most of his mathematics courses in his first year, which got him on the Dean's List.[^o12] He was conferred a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in Mathematical Science and a [Master of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Philosophy) in Mathematics after completing his programme one year early in 2011.[^o13][^o14][^o15][^o16]

After graduating from Hong Kong Baptist University, Boedihardjo studied at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) as a visiting scholar and then as a PhD student.[^o16][^o17] In 2017, Boediharjo took up the position of [assistant adjunct professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunct_professors_in_the_United_States) at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) on a three-year contract,[^o18] a position he held until 2020.[^o19] He was a visiting assistant professor at [UC Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Irvine) from 2021 to 2022 before starting a [postdoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) at ETH Zurich, a position he held until 2023. He is currently an assistant professor of mathematics at Michigan State University.
