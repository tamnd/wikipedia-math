# Weakly normal subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weakly normal subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) $H$ of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) $G$ is said to be **weakly normal** if whenever $H^g \leq N_G(H)$, we have $g \in N_G(H)$.

:::

Every [pronormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/3593621) is weakly normal.
