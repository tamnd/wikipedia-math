# Notices of the American Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Notices of the American Mathematical Society"}

The ***Notices of the American Mathematical Society*** is the membership [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS), published monthly except for a combined June/July issue. Founded in 1953, it serves as the official magazine of record for the society and is distributed to approximately 30,000 AMS members worldwide, one-third of whom reside outside the United States. The AMS describes the *Notices* as one of "the world's most widely read journal aimed at professional mathematicians".[^o3]

:::

## Content {#Content}

Each issue of the *Notices* contains one or two expository articles describing current developments in mathematical research, written by professional mathematicians.[^o5] The journal also carries articles on the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), and professional issues facing mathematicians, along with reviews of books, plays, movies, and other artistic and cultural works involving mathematics.[^o5] Regular columns include *Math Reviews News*, *Washington Update* on policy matters affecting mathematicians, and *Short Stories* exploring lesser-known mathematical results.[^o6] The *Early Career* section provides advice to graduate students, postdocs, and junior mathematicians.[^o6]

Articles are [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) by an editorial board of mathematical experts.[^o3] The cover regularly features [mathematical visualizations](wikipedia://en/25948250).[^o3] Each issue since January 1995 is available in its entirety on the journal's website.[^o3]

## Editors {#Editors}

The current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief), Mark C. Wilson, began his term with the January 2025 issue.[^o3] He succeeded [Erica Flapan](wikipedia://en/38305559), who served as editor from 2019 to 2024.[^o6] Previous editors include Frank Morgan (2003–2018).[^o8]
