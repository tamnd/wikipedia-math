# Ore extension

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ore extension"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially in the area of [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) known as [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424), an **Ore extension**, named after [Øystein Ore](wikipedia://en/1643733), is a special type of a [ring extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_extension) whose properties are relatively well understood. Elements of a Ore extension are called **Ore polynomials**.

:::

Ore extensions appear in several natural contexts, including skew and differential [polynomial rings](wikipedia://en/373065), [group algebras](wikipedia://en/349223) of [polycyclic groups](wikipedia://en/4000598), [universal enveloping algebras](wikipedia://en/363551) of [solvable Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/2966308), and [coordinate rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinate_ring) of [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *R* is a (not necessarily [commutative](wikipedia://en/61346)) [ring](wikipedia://en/48404), $\sigma \colon R \to R$ is a [ring homomorphism](wikipedia://en/26411), and $\delta\colon R\to R$ is a ***σ*-derivation** of *R*, which means that $\delta$ is a [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/12396) of [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974) satisfying

$$\delta(r_1 r_2) = \sigma(r_1)\delta(r_2)+\delta(r_1)r_2.$$

Then the **Ore extension** $R[x;\sigma,\delta]$, also called a **skew polynomial ring**, is the [noncommutative ring](wikipedia://en/24062516) obtained by giving the [ring of polynomials](wikipedia://en/373065) $R[x]$ a new multiplication, subject to the identity

$$x r = \sigma(r)x + \delta(r).$$

If *δ* = 0 (i.e., is the zero map) then the Ore extension is denoted *R*\[*x*; *σ*\]. If *σ* = 1 (i.e., the [identity map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_map)) then the Ore extension is denoted *R*\[*x*, *δ* \] and is called a **differential polynomial ring**.

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The [Weyl algebras](wikipedia://en/491095) are Ore extensions, with *R* any commutative [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065), *σ* the identity ring [endomorphism](wikipedia://en/9569), and *δ* the [polynomial derivative](wikipedia://en/3832917). [Ore algebras](wikipedia://en/44311819) are a class of iterated Ore extensions under suitable constraints that permit to develop a noncommutative extension of the theory of [Gröbner bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gröbner_bases).

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- An Ore extension of a [domain](wikipedia://en/487627) is a domain.
- An Ore extension of a [skew field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skew_field) is a non-commutative [principal ideal domain](wikipedia://en/25039).
- If *σ* is an [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) and *R* is a left [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) then the Ore extension *R*\[*λ*; *σ*, *δ* \] is also left Noetherian.

:::

## Elements {#Elements}

An element *f* of an Ore ring *R* is called

- **twosided**[^o9] (or **invariant**[^o10] ), if *R·f = f·R*, and
- **central**, if *g·f = f·g* for all *g* in *R*.
