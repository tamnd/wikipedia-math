# Outline of actuarial science

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of actuarial science"}

The following [outline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outline_%28list%29) is provided as an overview of and topical guide to actuarial science:

:::

**[Actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373)** – discipline that applies [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) and [statistical](wikipedia://en/26685) methods to [assess risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_assessment) in the [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance) and [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance) industries.

## What type of thing is actuarial science? {#What_type_of_thing_is_actuarial_science?}

Actuarial science can be described as all of the following:

- An [academic discipline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_discipline) –
- A branch of [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science) –
  - An [applied science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_science) –
    - A subdiscipline of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) –
