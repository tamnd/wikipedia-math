# Elliptic complex

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elliptic complex"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in particular in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), an **elliptic complex** generalizes the notion of an [elliptic operator](wikipedia://en/550137) to sequences. Elliptic complexes isolate those features common to the [de Rham complex](wikipedia://en/221530) and the [Dolbeault complex](wikipedia://en/3189601) which are essential for performing [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590). They also arise in connection with the [Atiyah-Singer index theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atiyah-Singer_index_theorem) and [Atiyah-Bott fixed point theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atiyah-Bott_fixed_point_theorem).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

If *E*<sub>0</sub>, *E*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *E*<sub>*k*</sub> are [vector bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_bundles) on a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) *M* (usually taken to be compact), then a **differential complex** is a sequence

$$\Gamma(E_0) \stackrel{P_1}{\longrightarrow} \Gamma(E_1) \stackrel{P_2}{\longrightarrow} \ldots \stackrel{P_k}{\longrightarrow} \Gamma(E_k)$$

of [differential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_operators) between the [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) of sections of the *E*<sub>*i*</sub> such that *P*<sub>*i*+1</sub> $\circ$ *P*<sub>*i*</sub>=0. A differential complex with first order operators is **elliptic** if the sequence of [symbols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbol_of_a_differential_operator)

$$0 \rightarrow \pi^*E_0 \stackrel{\sigma(P_1)}{\longrightarrow} \pi^*E_1 \stackrel{\sigma(P_2)}{\longrightarrow} \ldots \stackrel{\sigma(P_k)}{\longrightarrow} \pi^*E_k \rightarrow 0$$

is [exact](wikipedia://en/138215) outside of the zero section. Here π is the projection of the [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600) *T\*M* to *M*, and π\* is the [pullback](wikipedia://en/1665392) of a vector bundle.
