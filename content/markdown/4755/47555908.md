# Anthony James Merrill Spencer

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Anthony James Merrill Spencer"}

**Anthony James Merrill Spencer** (23 August 1929 — 26 January 2008) [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o2] was an applied mathematician whose main field of research was in understanding and predicting the mechanical behaviour of advanced materials.

:::

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Spencer was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in 1987](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_1987).[^o2]
