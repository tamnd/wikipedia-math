# Advanced statistics in basketball

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Advanced statistics in basketball"}

**Advanced statistics**, also known as **analytics** or **APBRmetrics**, in [basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basketball) refers to the analysis of [basketball statistics](wikipedia://en/4262263) using objective evidence.[^o2] APBRmetrics takes its name from the acronym APBR, which stands for the Association for Professional Basketball Research.[^o3] According to *The Sporting News*, the APBRmetrics message board was "the birthplace of basketball analytics".[^o4]

:::

Advanced basketball statistics include [effective field goal percentage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effective_field_goal_percentage) (eFG%), [true shooting percentage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_shooting_percentage) (TS%), on-court/off-court [plus–minus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plus–minus_%28sports%29#Basketball), [adjusted plus-minus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjusted_Plus_Minus) (APM), real plus/minus (RPM), [player efficiency rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Player_efficiency_rating) (PER), [offense efficiency rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offense_efficiency_rating), [offensive rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Offensive_rating), [defensive rating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defensive_rating), [similarity score](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Similarity_score), [tendex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tendex), and [player tracking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Player_tracking_%28National_Basketball_Association%29).[^o5] A more complete explanation of basketball analytics is available in "A Starting Point for Analyzing Basketball Statistics" in the *Journal of Quantitative Analysis in Sports*.[^o6]

## Notable basketball analytics practitioners {#Notable_basketball_analytics_practitioners}

The field of basketball analytics practitioners includes, but is not limited to, the following individuals:

- [Martin Manley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Manley) wrote Basketball Heaven. Created Player Efficiency metric and many other advanced metrics.
- [John Hollinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Hollinger) authored four books in the *Pro Basketball Forecast/Prospectus* series and was a regular columnist for ESPN Insider. He is a former vice president of basketball operations for the [Memphis Grizzlies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis_Grizzlies).
- Justin Kubatko created and maintained the website Basketball-Reference.com, the professional basketball arm of [Sports Reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_Reference) LLC, until 2013.[^o8] During Kubatko's tenure, Sports Reference was named one of the 50 best websites of 2010 by *[Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_%28magazine%29)* magazine.[^o9]
- [Dean Oliver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_Oliver_%28statistician%29), "one of the godfathers of NBA analytics",[^o4] is a former [NCAA Division III](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_III) player and assistant coach at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech_Beavers). He is also a scout who has consulted with the Seattle SuperSonics, and served in the front office of the [Denver Nuggets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_Nuggets) and the Sacramento Kings.
