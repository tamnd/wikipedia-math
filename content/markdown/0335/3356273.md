# George Andrews (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George Andrews"}

**George Eyre Andrews** (born December 4, 1938)[^o9] is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He is currently an Evan Pugh Professor of Mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o12][^o4] He did his undergraduate studies at [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University)[^o12] and received his PhD in 1964 at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) where his advisor was [Hans Rademacher](wikipedia://en/1864916).[^o9]

During 2008–2009 he was president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Andrews's contributions include several monographs and over 250 research and popular articles on [q-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q-series), [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and applications.[^o16][^o8] He is considered to be the world's leading expert in the theory of [integer partitions](wikipedia://en/104790).[^o9][^o10] In 1976 he discovered [Ramanujan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan)'s [Lost Notebook](wikipedia://en/6034957).[^o12] He is interested in mathematical pedagogy.[^o12]

His book *The Theory of Partitions* is the standard reference on the subject of [integer partitions](wikipedia://en/104790).[^o9]

He has advanced mathematics in the theories of partitions and q-series. His work at the interface of number theory and combinatorics has also led to many important applications in physics.[^o18]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2003 Andrews was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o12] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1997.[^o13] In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o14] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o15]

He was given honorary doctorates from the [University of Parma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Parma) in 1998, the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida) in 2002, the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) in 2004, [SASTRA University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_University) in [Kumbakonam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumbakonam), India in 2012, and [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) in 2014.[^o17][^o18]
