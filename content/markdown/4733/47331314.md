# Abdias Treu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abdias Treu"}

**Abdias Treu** (sometimes spelled **Trew**) (29 July 1597 – 12 April 1669) was a German mathematician and academic. He was the professor of mathematics and physical science at the [University of Altdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Altdorf) from 1636-1669. He is best known for his contributions to the field of [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy).[^o2] He also contributed writings on the mathematical nature of [music theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_theory).[^o3] He is the grandfather of physician and botanist Christoph Jacob Treu.[^o4]

:::
