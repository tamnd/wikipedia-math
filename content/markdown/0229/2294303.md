# Antoni Palluth

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Antoni Palluth"}

**Antoni Palluth** (11 May 1900, [Pobiedziska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pobiedziska), [Province of Posen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Posen) – 18 April 1944) was a founder of the [AVA Radio Company](wikipedia://en/24405736). The company built communications equipment for the Polish military; the work included not only radios but also cryptographic equipment.

:::

Palluth was involved with the German section (*BS-4*) of the [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) [General Staff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Staff)'s [interbellum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interbellum) [Cipher Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biuro_Szyfrów). He helped teach courses on cryptanalysis, and was involved with building equipment to break the German [Enigma machine](wikipedia://en/9256).

## Life {#Life}

Palluth was a [civil-engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineering) graduate of the [Warsaw Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Polytechnic). In January 1929, he was one of the instructors in a [cryptology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptology) course organized by the Cipher Bureau, at [Poznań University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poznań_University), which was attended by selected [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) students. The students included future [Cipher Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biuro_Szyfrów) civilian employees [Marian Rejewski](wikipedia://en/4349420), [Jerzy Różycki](wikipedia://en/197851) and [Henryk Zygalski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henryk_Zygalski).[^o4]

In the 1930s, Palluth was one of the four directors of the [AVA Radio Company](wikipedia://en/24405736) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), which produced cryptologic equipment designed by the Cipher Bureau.[^o5]

In March 1943, while attempting to cross the border from [German-occupied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) into [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain), Palluth was captured by the Germans along with the [Cipher Bureau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biuro_Szyfrów)'s chief, Lt. Col. [Gwido Langer](wikipedia://en/2919669), its German section's chief, Major [Maksymilian Ciężki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maksymilian_Ciężki), and civilians [Edward Fokczyński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Fokczyński) and [Kazimierz Gaca](wikipedia://en/80076340).[^o6]

Palluth died during an Allied air raid at the German [Sachsenhausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachsenhausen_%28detention_camp%29) [concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentration_camp).[^o6]
