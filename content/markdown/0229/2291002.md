# Yiannis N. Moschovakis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yiannis N. Moschovakis"}

**Yiannis Nicolas Moschovakis** (Γιάννης Μοσχοβάκης; born January 18, 1938) is a [set theorist](wikipedia://en/27553), [descriptive set theorist](wikipedia://en/396619), and [recursion (computability) theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory), at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA).

:::

His book *Descriptive Set Theory* (North-Holland) is the primary reference for the subject. He is especially associated with the development of the [effective](wikipedia://en/3244136), or [lightface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lightface_pointclass), version of descriptive set theory, and he is known for the [Moschovakis coding lemma](wikipedia://en/54225729) that is named after him.

## Biography {#Biography}

Moschovakis earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1963 under the direction of [Stephen Kleene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Kleene), with a dissertation entitled *Recursive Analysis*. In 2015, he was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to mathematical logic, especially set theory and [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414), and for exposition".[^o4]

For many years, he has split his time between UCLA and the [University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Athens) (he retired from the latter in July 2005).

Moschovakis is married to [Joan Moschovakis](wikipedia://en/47640968), with whom he gave the 2014 [Lindström](wikipedia://en/28868368) Lectures at the [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg).[^o5]
