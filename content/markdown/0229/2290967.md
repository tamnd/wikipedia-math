# John R. Steel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John R. Steel"}

*Image: John R Steel.jpg.* John Steel in 2004

**John Robert Steel** (born October 30, 1948) is an American [set theorist at](wikipedia://en/27553) the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) (formerly at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles)). He has made many contributions to the theory of [inner models](wikipedia://en/1118686) and [determinacy](wikipedia://en/2596030). With [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985), he proved [projective determinacy](wikipedia://en/1236592),[^o2] assuming the existence of sufficiently [large cardinals](wikipedia://en/383644). He earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Logic & the Methodology of Science at Berkeley in 1977 under the joint supervision of John West Addison Jr. and [Stephen G. Simpson](wikipedia://en/50451674).

:::

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1988, the Association for Symbolic Logic awarded him, [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985), and [W. Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071) the Karp Prize for their work on the consistency of determinacy relative to large cardinals. In 2015, the European Set Theory Society awarded him and [Ronald Jensen](wikipedia://en/3785996) the [Hausdorff Medal](wikipedia://en/62036856) for their paper "K without the measurable".

In 2012, Steel held the [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431) titled *The hereditarily ordinal definable sets in models of determinacy.*
