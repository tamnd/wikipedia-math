# Alexander S. Kechris

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander S. Kechris"}

**Alexander Sotirios Kechris** (Αλέξανδρος Σωτήριος Κεχρής; born March 23, 1946) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Kechris' main research interests are in mathematical logic and its interaction with other areas of mathematics. He has made extensive contributions to the definability theory of the continuum and its connections and applications to [classical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_analysis), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [topological dynamics](wikipedia://en/16379713), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858).

Kechris earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at UCLA in 1972 under the direction of [Yiannis N. Moschovakis](wikipedia://en/2291002), with a dissertation titled *[Projective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_set) [Ordinals](wikipedia://en/26547932) and [Countable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Countable) [Analytical Sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytical_set)*. He was a C.L.E. Moore Instructor at MIT during 1972-1974 and has been at the California Institute of Technology since 1974. During his academic career he advised 27 PhD students and sponsored 23 [postdoctoral researchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher).

## Honors {#Honors}

- 1978 - A.P. Sloan Foundation Fellow.[^o5]
- 1986 - Invited Speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians in Berkeley (Mathematical Logic & Foundations)[^o6]
- 1987 - Honorary Doctoral Degree from the University of Athens, Greece.
- 1998 - Visiting Miller Research Professor, U.C. Berkeley.[^o7]
- 1998 - [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431) (Current Trends in Descriptive Set Theory).[^o8]
- 2002 - J.S. Guggenheim Memorial Foundation Fellow.[^o9]
- 2003 - Received the [Karp Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karp_Prize), along with [Gregory Hjorth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Hjorth) for work on Borel equivalence relations, in particular for their results on turbulence and countable [Borel equivalence relations](wikipedia://en/2123692)[^o10]
- 2004 - Tarski Lectures (New Connections Between Logic, Ramsey Theory and Topological Dynamics)[^o11]
- 2012 - Inaugural Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12]
- 2017 - Trjitzinsky Memorial Lectures, University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign (A descriptive set theoretic approach to problems in harmonic analysis, ergodic theory and combinatorics).[^o13]
- 2018 - Rademacher Lectures, University of Pennsylvania (A descriptive set theoretic approach to problems in harmonic analysis, ergodic theory and combinatorics).[^o14]
- 2024 - Brin Mathematics Research Center Distinguished Lecture, University of Maryland (Orbit equivalence relations and the compact action realization problem).[^o15]
