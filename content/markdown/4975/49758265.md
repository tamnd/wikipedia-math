# Why Johnny Can't Add

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Why Johnny Can't Add"}

***Why Johnny Can't Add: The Failure of the New Math*** is a 1973 book by [Morris Kline](wikipedia://en/826363), in which the author severely criticized the teaching practices characteristic of the "[New Math](wikipedia://en/404988)" fashion for school teaching, which were based on [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394)'s approach to mathematical research and [mathematics education in the United States](wikipedia://en/24836125).[^o3][^o4] Reactions were immediate, and the book became a best seller in its genre and was translated into many languages.[^o5]

:::
