# Discrete Mathematics (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discrete Mathematics"}

***Discrete Mathematics*** is a biweekly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) in the broad area of [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), and their applications. It was established in 1971 and is published by [North-Holland Publishing Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North-Holland_Publishing_Company). It publishes both short notes, full length contributions, as well as [survey articles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survey_article). In addition, the journal publishes a number of special issues each year dedicated to a particular topic. Although originally it published articles in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) and [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language), it now allows only [English language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) articles. The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Douglas West](wikipedia://en/22813653) ([University of Illinois, Urbana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois,_Urbana)).

:::

## History {#History}

The journal was established in 1971. The first article it published was written by [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), who went on to publish a total of 84 papers in the journal.
