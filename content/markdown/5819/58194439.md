# Vincent Pilloni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vincent Pilloni"}

**Vincent Pilloni** is a French mathematician, specializing in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177) and the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Pilloni studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) and received his doctorate in 2009 from [Université Sorbonne Paris Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Sorbonne_Paris_Nord) with thesis advisor [Jacques Tilouine](wikipedia://en/63270336) and thesis *Arithmétique des variétés de Siegel*.[^o5]

His research deals with, among other topics, the question of how the [modularity theorem](wikipedia://en/174475) for [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225) over the rational numbers (which led to the proof of [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953)) can be extended to [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152). With George Boxer, [Frank Calegari](wikipedia://en/63299027) and [Toby Gee](wikipedia://en/58462234), he proved that all abelian surfaces and genus two curves over totally real fields are potentially modular and satisfy the [Hasse-Weil conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse-Weil_conjecture).[^o6]

Pilloni is a *Chargé de recherche* of [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University) based at the Institut de mathématique d'Orsay.

In 2018 he was an invited speaker, with Fabrizio Andreatta and Adrian Iovita, at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.[^o7] In 2018 Pilloni received the [Prix Élie Cartan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Élie_Cartan). In 2021 he was awarded the [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195).[^o8]
