# Jun-Muk Hwang

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Jun-Muk Hwang"}

**Jun-Muk Hwang** (; born 27 October 1963) is a South Korean mathematician, specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and complex [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o4]

:::

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Hwang is the eldest son of *[gayageum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayageum)* musician [Hwang Byungki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hwang_Byungki) and novelist [Han Malsook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Han_Malsook).[^o6]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hwang studied physics at [Seoul National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_National_University) for his bachelors before studying physics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). In 1993, he completed his PhD under the direction of [Yum-Tong Siu](wikipedia://en/6085925) with thesis *Global nondeformability of the complex hyper quadric*.[^o9] In the following years he held positions at the University of Notre Dame, the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), and Seoul National University. Since 1999, he was a professor at the [Korea Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/16984).[^o4] He was in 2006 an invited speaker with talk *Rigidity of rational homogeneous spaces* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid)[^o10] and in 2014 a plenary speaker with talk *Mori geometry meets Cartan geometry: Varieties of minimal rational tangents* at the ICM in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul).[^o11]

With his collaborator [Ngaiming Mok](wikipedia://en/52300670), he has developed the theory of varieties of minimal rational tangents, which combines methods of algebraic geometry and differential geometry in the study of rational curves on algebraic varieties. He has applied this theory to settle a number of problems on algebraic varieties covered by rational curves.[^o4]

In 2020, he was the founding director of the Center for Complex Geometry at the [Institute for Basic Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Basic_Science).[^o12] In 2023, he was selected to be on the committee for the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285).[^o13][^o14]
