# International Workshop on Operator Theory and its Applications

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Workshop on Operator Theory and its Applications"}

**International Workshop on Operator Theory and its Applications (IWOTA)** was started in 1981[^o3] to bring together [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) and [engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) working in [operator theoretic](wikipedia://en/712675) side of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and its applications to related fields.[^o4][^o5] These include:

:::

- [Differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations) and [Integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_equations)
- [Complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [Harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147)
- [Linear system](wikipedia://en/722503) and [Control theory](wikipedia://en/7039)
- [Mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)
- [Signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324)
- [Numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506)

The other major branch of [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), [Operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebras) (C\* and von Neumann Algebras), is not heavily represented at IWOTA and has its own conferences.

IWOTA gathers leading experts from all over the world for an intense exchange of new results, information and opinions, and for tracing the future developments in the field. The IWOTA meetings provide opportunities for participants (including young [researchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research)) to present their own work in invited and contributed talks, to interact with other [researchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research) from around the globe, and to broaden their knowledge of the field.[^o6] In addition, IWOTA emphasizes cross-disciplinary interaction among [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902), [electrical engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) and [mathematical physicists](wikipedia://en/173416). In the even years, the IWOTA workshop is a satellite meeting to the biennial International Symposium on the Mathematical Theory of Networks and Systems (MTNS).[^o7] From the humble beginnings in the early 80's,[^o3] the IWOTA workshops grew to become one of the largest continuing conferences attended by the community of researchers in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675).[^o8]

## History of IWOTA {#History_of_IWOTA}

### First IWOTA Meeting {#First_IWOTA_Meeting}

The International Workshop on Operator Theory and its Applications was started on August 1, 1981,[^o3] adjacent to the International Symposium on Mathematical Theory of Networks and Systems (MTNS)[^o7] with goal of exposing [operator theorists](wikipedia://en/712675), even [pure](wikipedia://en/457210) theorists, to recent developments in [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) (especially *[H-infinity methods in control theory](wikipedia://en/314366)*) which had a significant intersection with [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675). [Israel Gohberg](wikipedia://en/19685973) was the visionary and driving force of IWOTA[^o11] and president of the IWOTA Steering Committee. From the beginning, J. W. Helton and M. A. Kaashoek served as vice presidents of the steering committee.

### West Meets East {#West_Meets_East}

Besides the excitement of mathematical discovery over the decades at IWOTA, there was great excitement when the [curtain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Curtain) between [Soviet bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_bloc) and Western operator theorists fell. Until 1990, these two collections of extremely strong mathematicians seldom met due to the tight [restrictions on travel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_rights_in_the_Soviet_Union#Freedom_of_movement) from and in the [communist countries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_state). When the [curtain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Curtain) dropped, the western mathematicians knew the classic Soviet papers but had a spotty knowledge of much of what else their counterparts were doing.[^o13] [Gohberg](wikipedia://en/19685973) was one of the operator theorists who knew both sides and he guided IWOTA, a western institution, in bringing (and funding) many prominent [FSU bloc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-Soviet_states) operator theorists to speak at the meetings. As the IWOTA programs demonstrate,[^o14] this significantly accelerated the cultures' mutual assimilation.[^o15]

### IWOTA Proceedings {#IWOTA_Proceedings}

[Proceedings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings) of the IWOTA workshops appear in the [*Springer*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) / [*Birkhäuser Verlag*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser) book series *Operator Theory: Advances and Applications (OTAA)*[^o18] (founder: [Israel Gohberg](wikipedia://en/19685973)). While [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) conference [proceedings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings) often are handed to participants as they arrive and contain short papers on each conference talk, the IWOTA [proceedings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings) follow [mathematics conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_conference) tradition and contain a modest number of papers and are published several years after the conference.

## The IWOTA Steering Committee {#The_IWOTA_Steering_Committee}

IWOTA is directed by a steering committee which chooses the site for the next meeting, elects the chief local organizer(s) and insures the appearance of the enduring themes of IWOTA. The sub-themes of an IWOTA workshop and the lecturers are chosen by the local organizing committee after hearing the steering committee's board. The board consists of its vice presidents: [Joseph A. Ball](wikipedia://en/59125027), J. William Helton (Chair, 2018-present), Sanne ter Horst, Igor Klep, [Christiane Tretter](wikipedia://en/63227596), [Irene Sabadini](wikipedia://en/68030598), Victor Vinnikov and Hugo J. Woerdeman. In addition, past chief organizers who remain active in IWOTA are members of the steering committee. The board governs IWOTA with consultation and the consent of the full steering committee. Honorary members of the steering committee, elected in 2016, are: [Israel Gohberg](wikipedia://en/19685973) (deceased in 2009), Leiba Rodman (deceased in 2015), Tsuyoshi Ando, [Harry Dym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Dym) (deceased in 2024), [Ciprian Foias](wikipedia://en/28465086) (deceased in 2020), Heinz Langer (deceased in 2024), Nikolai Nikolski. Honorary member of the steering committee, elected in 2024, is: [Rien Kaashoek](wikipedia://en/40502851) (deceased in 2024; Chair of Vice Presidents 2009-2018).

## Israel Gohberg ILAS-IWOTA Lecture {#Israel_Gohberg_ILAS-IWOTA_Lecture}

The Israel Gohberg ILAS-IWOTA Lecture was introduced in August 2016 and honors the legacy of [Israel Gohberg](wikipedia://en/19685973), whose research crossed borders between operator theory, linear algebra, and related fields. This lecture is in collaboration with the [International Linear Algebra Society](wikipedia://en/63337149) (ILAS).[^o40] This series of lectures will be delivered at IWOTA and ILAS Conferences, in different years, in the approximate ratio two-thirds at IWOTA and one-third at ILAS. The first three lectures will take place at IWOTA Lancaster UK 2021, ILAS 2022, and IWOTA 2024. Donations for the Israel Gohberg ILAS-IWOTA Lecture Fund are most welcome and can be submitted via the ILAS donation form.[^o41] Donations are tax deductible in the United States.
