# Tamar Schlick

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tamar Schlick"}

**Tamar Schlick** is an American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) who works as a professor of chemistry, mathematics, and computer science at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). Her research involves developing and applying tools for modeling and simulating [biomolecules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomolecule).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Schlick did her undergraduate studies at [Wayne State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_State_University), graduating in 1982 with a B.S. in mathematics.[^o2] She continued her graduate studies at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at New York University, completing a Ph.D. in applied mathematics in 1987 under the supervision of [Charles S. Peskin](wikipedia://en/12367630).[^o2]

After postdoctoral studies at NYU and the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), she returned as a faculty member to NYU in 1989.[^o2]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She is a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (2004), [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society) (2005), [Biophysical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biophysical_Society) (2012), and [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (2012).[^o2][^o6]
