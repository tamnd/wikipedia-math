# Lisa Fauci

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lisa Fauci"}

**Lisa J. Fauci** (born September 21, 1960) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who applies [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924) to biological processes such as [sperm motility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sperm_motility) and [phytoplankton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phytoplankton) dynamics.[^o3] More generally, her research interests include [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing), and [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology). She is the Pendergraft Nola Lee Haynes Professor of Mathematics at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University),[^o4] and was president of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (2019–2020).[^o5] She was elected to the National Academy of Sciences in 2023.[^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Fauci was born in [Brooklyn, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn,_New_York).[^o8] She did her [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate) studies at [Pace University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pace_University), where she was encouraged to continue in mathematics by her mentor there, Michael Bernkopf.[^o9] After earning a B.S. in mathematics in 1981,[^o10] she went on to graduate studies at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), completing a master's degree in 1984 and her doctorate in 1986, under the supervision of [Charles S. Peskin](wikipedia://en/12367630).[^o10][^o11]

## Career {#Career}

Fauci has been at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) since 1986.[^o8][^o10]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2012 Fauci became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) "for contributions to computational biofluid dynamics and applications."[^o14] From 2014 to 2016, Fauci served as a Council Member at Large for the American Mathematical Society.[^o15] In 2016 she was selected as the annual Sonia Kovalevsky Lecturer by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o16] In 2018, she became a [Fellow of the American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Physical_Society).[^o17]

In 2019 Fauci was elected fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science[^o18] (AAAS). She was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2020 Class, for "contributions to computational fluid dynamics and applications, and for service to the applied mathematical community".[^o19]

Fauci was elected to the 2023 class of fellows of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) "for her vision of advancing women in the mathematical sciences; for executing that vision by encouraging women to pursue graduate studies and providing sustained mentorship throughout their careers; and for opening pathways for the broader inclusion of women through her leadership in international organizations such as SIAM."[^o20] She was elected to the National Academy of Sciences in 2023.[^o6] In November 2023, Fauci was a Tulane 2023 Hall of Fame winner. [^o21] She is a speaker of the IBS Biomedical Mathematics Group.[^o22]
