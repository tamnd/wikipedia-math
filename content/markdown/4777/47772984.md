# Thaleia Zariphopoulou

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thaleia Zariphopoulou"}

**Thaleia Zariphopoulou** (born 1962) is a Greek-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680). She is the chair in Mathematics and the V. H. Neuhaus Centennial Professor of Finance at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).[^o2]

:::

Zariphopoulou earned a B.S. in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) from the [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens) in 1984.[^o2] She then went to [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) for graduate studies in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and earned her master's degree in 1985 and her Ph.D. degree in 1989 under the supervision of [Wendell Fleming](wikipedia://en/35262774).[^o2] She was an assistant professor at [Worcester Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_Polytechnic_Institute) and an associate professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), before she moved to the University of Texas at Austin in 1999. She was the first holder of the Man Chair of [Quantitative Finance](wikipedia://en/33589680) at the Oxford-Man Institute from 2009 to 2012.[^o2]

In 2012 she became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) "for contributions to [stochastic control](wikipedia://en/17058216) and financial mathematics".[^o4] She was an invited speaker at the 2014 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842),[^o5] and at the conference [Dynamics, Equations and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamics,_Equations_and_Applications) in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków) in 2019.[^o6]

Her husband is [Panagiotis E. Souganidis](wikipedia://en/62325203),[^o7] the Charles H. Swift Distinguished Service Professor in Mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).
