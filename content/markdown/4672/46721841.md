# Jon T. Pitts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jon T. Pitts"}

**Jon T. Pitts** (1948–2024)[^o2] was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474) and [variational calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_calculus). He was a professor at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University).

:::

Pitts was born on 10 January 1948.[^o3] Pitts obtained his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1974 under the supervision of [Frederick Almgren, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Almgren), with the thesis *Every Compact Three-Dimensional Manifold Contains Two-Dimensional Minimal Submanifolds*.[^o5]

He received a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 1981.[^o6]

The [Almgren–Pitts min-max theory](wikipedia://en/46587298) is named after his teacher and him.[^o7]

Pitts died on 30 May 2024.[^o3][^o8]
