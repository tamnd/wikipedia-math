# Fielden Professor of Pure Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fielden Professor of Pure Mathematics"}

The **Fielden Chair** of Pure Mathematics is an endowed professorial position in the [School of Mathematics, University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Mathematics,_University_of_Manchester), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England).

:::

## History {#History}

In 1870 Samuel Fielden, a wealthy mill owner from [Todmorden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todmorden), donated £150 to Owens College (as the [Victoria University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Manchester) was then called) for the teaching of evening classes and a further £3000 for the development of natural sciences at the college. From 1877 this supported the **Fielden Lecturer**, subsequently to become the **Fielden Reader** with the appointment of L. J. Mordell in 1922 and then **Fielden Professor** in 1923. [Alex Wilkie](wikipedia://en/10343119) FRS was appointed to the post in 2007.[^o3]

## Holders {#Holders}

Previous holders of the Fielden Chair (and lectureship) are:[^o5]

- A. T. Bentley (1876–1880) Lecturer in Pure Mathematics
- J. E. A. Steggall (1880–1883) Lecturer in Pure Mathematics
- R. F. Gwyther (1883–1907) Lecturer in Mathematics
- F. T. Swanwick (1907–1912) Lecturer in Mathematics
- H. R. Hasse (1912–1918) Lecturer in Mathematics
- [George Henry Livens](wikipedia://en/26784306) (1920–1922) Lecturer in Mathematics[^o6]
- [Louis Mordell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Mordell) (1923–1945)
- [Max Newman](wikipedia://en/19867) (1945–1964)
- [Frank Adams](wikipedia://en/1223959) (1964–1971)
- [Ian G. Macdonald](wikipedia://en/3100378) (1972–1976)
- Norman Blackburn (1978–1994)
- [Mark Pollicott](wikipedia://en/14732052) (1996–2004)
- [Alex Wilkie](wikipedia://en/10343119) (2007–)
- [Radha Kessar](wikipedia://en/57412986) (2024–)[^o7]

## Related chairs {#Related_chairs}

The other endowed chairs in mathematics at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) are the [Beyer Chair of Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyer_Chair_of_Applied_Mathematics), the [Sir Horace Lamb Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Horace_Lamb_Chair) and the [Richardson Chair of Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richardson_Chair_of_Applied_Mathematics).
