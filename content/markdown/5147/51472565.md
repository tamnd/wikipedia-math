# Barnabé Brisson (engineer)

Barnabé Brisson (ingénieur)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Barnabé Brisson"}

**Barnabé Brisson** (11 October 1777 – 25 September 1828) was a French engineer and mathematician. He collaborated in the construction of the [Canal de Saint-Quentin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canal_de_Saint-Quentin) and other navigable waterways connected to the [Escaut River](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escaut_River). He also authored a number of academic publications on the canal systems.

:::
