# Gérard Biau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gérard Biau"}

*Image: Gérard Biau.jpg.*

**Gérard Biau** is a French professor whose contributions are specialized in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488).

:::

## Career {#Career}

He studied at [Mines Paris – PSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mines_Paris_–_PSL) and obtained his PhD under the supervision of [Alain Berlinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Berlinet) at [Montpellier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montpellier).

After getting his [accreditation to supervise research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation_to_Supervise_Research) in 2003, he was appointed as full professor at [Montpellier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montpellier) in 2004. In 2007, he joined the Laboratoire de Probabilités, Statistique et Modélisation of [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University). Gérard Biau is a founding member of SCAI, the Sorbonne Center for Artificial Intelligence of [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University),[^o3] which he directed from 2019 to 2026.

He was a junior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France)[^o4] from 2012 to 2017, and served as the President of the [French Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/60209643)[^o5] from 2015 to 2018. In 2018, he was awarded the Michel-Monpetit Prize.[^o6] In 2024, he was appointed as a senior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France),[^o4] and elected as a permanent member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5][^o7]

## Research {#Research}

His work focuses on the study of the statistical properties of [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence)[^o9] algorithms: [random forests](wikipedia://en/1363880),[^o10] [functional data analysis](wikipedia://en/2141368),[^o11] [gradient boosting](wikipedia://en/26649339),[^o12] [k-nearest neighbors algorithm](wikipedia://en/1775388),[^o13] [Generative Adversarial Networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wasserstein_GAN),[^o14] [recurrent neural networks](wikipedia://en/1706303),[^o15] and, more recently, [physics-informed machine learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics-informed_neural_networks).[^o16][^o17]

He is one of the three authors of the teaching book *Mathématiques et statistique pour les sciences de la nature*,[^o18] and co-author, with [Luc Devroye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luc_Devroye), of the monograph *Lectures on the Nearest Neighbor Method*.[^o19]

Gérard Biau is an associate editor of the statistical journals [*International Statistical Review*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (since 2009),[^o20] *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313)* (2017-2025),[^o21] *[Biometrika](wikipedia://en/1734178)* (since 2018),[^o22] and *[The Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768)* (since 2019).[^o23]

- 2018 : Prix Michel-Monpetit[^o26]
