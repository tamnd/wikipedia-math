# Helffer–Sjöstrand formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Helffer–Sjöstrand formula"}

The **Helffer–Sjöstrand formula** is a mathematical tool used in [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) to represent functions of [self-adjoint operators](wikipedia://en/187408). Named after [Bernard Helffer](wikipedia://en/56549449) and [Johannes Sjöstrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Sjöstrand), this formula provides a way to calculate functions of operators without requiring the operator to have a simple or explicitly known spectrum. It is especially useful in [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), [condensed matter physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensed_matter_physics), and other areas where understanding the properties of operators related to energy or observables is important.[^o2]

:::

## Background {#Background}

If $f \in C_c^\infty (\mathbb{R})$, then we can find a function $\tilde f \in C_c^\infty (\mathbb{C})$ such that $\tilde{f}|_{\mathbb{R}} = f$, and for each $N \ge 0$, there exists a $C_N > 0$ such that

$$|\bar{\partial} \tilde{f}| \leq C_N |\operatorname{Im} z|^N.$$

Such a function $\tilde{f}$ is called an almost analytic extension of $f$.[^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement cue=named-heading name="The formula"}

## The formula {#The_formula}

If $f \in C_c^\infty(\mathbb{R})$ and $A$ is a self-adjoint operator on a Hilbert space, then

$f(A) = \frac{1}{\pi} \int_{\mathbb{C}} \bar{\partial} \tilde{f}(z) (z - A)^{-1} \, dx \, dy$[^o7]

where $\tilde{f}$ is an almost analytic extension of $f$, and $\bar{\partial}_z := \frac{1}{2}(\partial_{Re(z)} + i\partial_{Im(z)})$.

:::
