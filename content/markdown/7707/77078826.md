# Alexander Mielke

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Mielke"}

*Image: AlexanderMielke.jpg.* Alexander Mielke

**Alexander Mielke** (born 14 September 1958.[^o2]) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the areas of [nonlinear partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_partial_differential_equations) and applied [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).[^o3][^o4] He is a professor of applied analysis at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) and heads the research group on partial differential equations at the [Weierstrass Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute).[^o5]

:::

## Education and Career {#Education_and_Career}

Mielke received his PhD from the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart) in 1984 under the supervision of Klaus Kirchgässner, and his thesis was titled *Stationary Solutions of the Euler Equation in Channels of Variable Depth*.[^o7][^o8] He was a postdoctoral researcher at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) under the supervision of [Philip Holmes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Holmes) between 1986 and 1987, and in 1990, he [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitated) at the University of Stuttgart on the topic of *Hamiltonian and Lagrangian flows on center manifolds with applications to elliptic variational problems* and was appointed professor at the [University of Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hannover) in 1992.[^o9][^o10] In 1999, he was appointed at the Institute for Analysis, Dynamics, and Modeling at the University of Stuttgart. He has been a full professor at the Humboldt University of Berlin since 2004 and Head of the Research Group *Partial differential equations* at the [Weierstrass Institute for Applied Analysis and Stochastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute_for_Applied_Analysis_and_Stochastics) in Berlin.

### Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

- [Richard von Mises Prize](wikipedia://en/56348140) of the [Society of Applied Mathematics and Mechanics (GAMM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik), 1989.[^o12]
- [Heinz Maier-Leibnitz-Preis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinz_Maier-Leibnitz-Preis) awarded by the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation), 1989.[^o13]
- [ISIMM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISIMM) Senior prize, 2024.[^o14]
