# Dehn's lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dehn's lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Dehn's lemma** asserts that a [piecewise-linear map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piecewise-linear_map) of a [disk](wikipedia://en/329542) into a [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257), with the map's [singularity](wikipedia://en/54240) set in the disk's [interior](wikipedia://en/55610), implies the existence of another piecewise-linear map of the disk which is an [embedding](wikipedia://en/52119) and is identical to the original on the [boundary](wikipedia://en/72824) of the disk.

:::

This theorem was thought to be proven by (txt), but (txt, page 260) found a gap in the proof. The status of Dehn's lemma remained in doubt until (txt) using work by [Johansson](wikipedia://en/26269027) (1938) proved it using his "tower construction". He also generalized the theorem to the [loop theorem](wikipedia://en/4350138) and [sphere theorem](wikipedia://en/4375347).

## Tower construction {#Tower_construction}

Papakyriakopoulos proved Dehn's lemma using a [tower](wikipedia://en/31124859) of [covering spaces](wikipedia://en/250323). Soon afterwards (txt) gave a substantially simpler proof, proving a more powerful result. Their proof used Papakyriakopoulos' tower construction, but with double covers, as follows:

- Step 1: Repeatedly take a connected double cover of a [regular neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_neighborhood) of the image of the disk to produce a tower of spaces, each a connected double cover of the one below it. The map from the disk can be lifted to all stages of this tower. Each double cover simplifies the singularities of the embedding of the disk, so it is only possible to take a finite number of such double covers, and the top level of this tower has no connected double covers.
- Step 2. If the 3-manifold has no connected double covers then all its boundary components are 2-spheres. In particular the top level of the tower has this property, and in this case it is easy to modify the map from the disk so that it is an embedding.
- Step 3. The embedding of the disk can now be pushed down the tower of double covers one step at a time, by cutting and pasting the 2-disk.
