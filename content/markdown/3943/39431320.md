# Charles Weibel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Weibel"}

*Image: Weibel 2004.jpg.* Charles A. Weibel at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 2004

**Charles Alexander Weibel** (born October 28, 1950, in [Terre Haute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terre_Haute), [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana)) is an American mathematician working on [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410).

:::

Weibel studied physics and mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), earning bachelor's degrees in both subjects in 1972. He was awarded a master's degree by the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1973 and achieved his doctorate in 1977 under the supervision of [Richard Swan](wikipedia://en/18384954) (*Homotopy in Algebraic K-Theory*). From 1970 to 1976 he was an "Operations Research Analyst" at [Standard Oil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_Oil) of Indiana, and from 1977 to 1978 was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). In 1978 he became an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania). In 1980 he became an assistant professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), where he was promoted to professor in 1989.

He joined [Vladimir Voevodsky](wikipedia://en/104482) and [Markus Rost](wikipedia://en/6842771) in proving the ([motivic](wikipedia://en/809314)) [Bloch–Kato conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloch–Kato_conjecture) (2009).[^o2] It is a generalization of the [Milnor conjecture](wikipedia://en/2914126) of algebraic K-theory, which was proved by Voevodsky in the 1990s. He was a visiting professor in 1992 at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) and 1993 at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). Since 1983 he has been an editor of the *[Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Pure_and_Applied_Algebra)*. He helped found the *K-theory Foundation* in 2010, and has been a managing editor of the *[Annals of K-theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annals_of_K-theory)* since 2014. In 2014, he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
