# Benedikt Löwe

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Benedikt Löwe"}

**Benedikt Löwe**

(born 1972) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065) working at the universities of [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) and [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o3] He is known for his work on [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358), as well as for initiating the interdisciplinary conference series *Foundations of the Formal Sciences* (FotFS; 1999–2013) and *[Computability in Europe](wikipedia://en/22521356)* (CiE; since 2005).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Löwe studied mathematics and philosophy at the universities of [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg), [Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen), [HU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin), and [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). In 2001, he completed his PhD entitled *Blackwell Determinacy* about [determinacy](wikipedia://en/2596030) under supervision of [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985) and [Ronald Björn Jensen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Björn_Jensen). He worked at the [Institute for Logic, Language and Computation](wikipedia://en/1351034) of the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) from 2003 to 2023. In 2009, he was appointed professor for mathematical logic and interdisciplinary applications of logic at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg)[^o7] and is also an extraordinary fellow at [Churchill College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Churchill_College) of the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o8] Löwe was Managing Editor of the journal *Mathematical Logic Quarterly* from 2011 to 2022.[^o9]

He was the President of the [German Association for Mathematical Logic and for Basic Research in the Exact Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Association_for_Mathematical_Logic_and_for_Basic_Research_in_the_Exact_Sciences) (DVMLG) from 2012 to 2022 and the Secretary General of the *Division for Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science and Technology* of the [International Union of History and Philosophy of Science and Technology](wikipedia://en/33072433)[^o10] from 2015 to 2023. Since 2023, he is one of the Vice Presidents of the [International Council for Philosophy and Human Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Council_for_Philosophy_and_Human_Sciences) (CIPSH).[^o11]

He is a member of the [International Academy for Philosophy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Academy_for_Philosophy_of_Science),[^o12] the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) ([MAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Academia_Europaea)),[^o13] the [Akademie der Wissenschaften in Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities_in_Hamburg),[^o14] and a Fellow of the [International Science Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Science_Council) (FISC).[^o15]
