# Harish-Chandra homomorphism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harish-Chandra homomorphism"}

In mathematical representation theory, a **Harish-Chandra homomorphism** is a homomorphism from a subalgebra of the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of a semisimple Lie algebra to the universal enveloping algebra of a subalgebra. A particularly important special case is the [Harish-Chandra isomorphism](wikipedia://en/5352569) identifying the center of the universal enveloping algebra with the invariant polynomials on a Cartan subalgebra.

:::

In the case of the *K*-invariant elements of the universal enveloping algebra for a maximal compact subgroup *K*, the Harish-Chandra homomorphism was studied by (txt).
