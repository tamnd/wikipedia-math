# Gabriel Peyré

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriel Peyré"}

**Gabriel Peyré** (born 1979)[^o3] is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Most of his work lies in the field of [transportation theory](wikipedia://en/6631661). He is a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) senior researcher and a Professor in the mathematics and applications department of the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in Paris.[^o4] He was awarded the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal) in 2021.[^o5]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

His work mainly focuses on applied mathematics, in particular on the [imaging sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaging) and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) applications of [optimal transport](wikipedia://en/6631661).[^o7]

Gabriel Peyré is also the deputy director of the 3IA Paris [Artificial Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) Research Institute[^o8] as well as a member of the scientific committee of the [ENS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) center for data science.[^o9] He is also the creator of the Numerical tour of data science,[^o10] a popular online repository of Python/Matlab/Julia/R resources to teach mathematical data sciences. He is a frequent collaborator of the [INRIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) team Mokaplan.[^o11]

## Awards and distinctions {#Awards_and_distinctions}

Gabriel Peyré was awarded the Blaise Pascal Prize in 2017 from the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o13] as well as the Enrico Magenes Prize (2019) from the [Unione Matematica Italiana](wikipedia://en/11969092).[^o14] He also was an invited speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in 2020.[^o15] His research was supported by an [ERC starting grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council#Grants_offered) in 2012 and by an [ERC consolidator grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council#Grants_offered) in 2017.[^o16] In 2021, he was awarded the CNRS Silver Medal.[^o5]
