# Hopfion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hopfion"}

*Image: Hopfion.png.* Model of magnetic hopfion in a solid. B<sub>em</sub> is emergent magnetic field (orange arrows); in a hopfion, it does not align to the external magnetic field (black arrow).

A **hopfion** is a [topological soliton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_defect).[^o2][^o13][^o3][^o4] It is a stable three-dimensional localised configuration of a three-component field $\vec{n}=(n_x,n_y,n_z)$ of unit length with a knotted topological structure. They are the three-dimensional counterparts of 2D [skyrmions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skyrmions), which exhibit similar topological properties in 2D. Hopfions are widely studied in many physical systems over the last half century.[^o5]

:::

The soliton is mobile and stable: i.e. it is protected from a decay by an [energy barrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Activation_energy). It can be deformed but always conserves an integer Hopf topological invariant. It is named after the German mathematician, [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082).

A model that supports hopfions was proposed as follows:[^o2]

$$H= (\partial {\bf n})^2 + (\epsilon_{ijk}{\bf n}\cdot\partial_i {\bf n}\times \partial_j{\bf n})^2$$

The terms of higher-order derivatives are required to stabilize the hopfions.

Stable hopfions were predicted within various physical platforms, including Yang–Mills theory,[^o6] superconductivity[^o7][^o8] and magnetism.[^o9][^o10][^o11][^o4]

## Experimental observation {#Experimental_observation}

Hopfions have been observed experimentally in chiral colloidal magnetic materials,[^o13] in chiral liquid crystals,[^o14][^o15] in Ir/Co/Pt multilayers using [X-ray magnetic circular dichroism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray_magnetic_circular_dichroism)[^o16] and in the polarization of free-space monochromatic light.[^o17][^o18]

In chiral magnets, a helical-background variant of the hopfion has been theoretically predicted to occur within the spiral magnetic phase, where it was called a "heliknoton".[^o19] In recent years, the concept of a "fractional hopfion" has also emerged where not all preimages of magnetisation have a nonzero linking.[^o20][^o21]
