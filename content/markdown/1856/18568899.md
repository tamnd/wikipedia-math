# Hermann Rothe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermann Rothe"}

**Hermann Rothe** (28 December 1882 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) – 18 December 1923 in Vienna) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Rothe studied at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). He attained the [Doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in [Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) in 1909 in Vienna. Then he was assistant at the [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology), where he attained the [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1910. In 1913 Rothe married and began to teach [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the Vienna University of Technology as [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) extraordinarius, and from 1920 as Professor ordinarius. In 1923 he died after a long [disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disease).[^o2]

Rothe is known for his collaboration (1910–1912) with [Philipp Frank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philipp_Frank) on [special relativity](wikipedia://en/26962). Based on [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), they tried to derive the [Lorentz transformation](wikipedia://en/18404) without the postulate of the constancy of the [speed of light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_light).[^o3]

Furthermore, Rothe worked — outside his teaching activity — on mathematical problems like [Hermann Grassmann](wikipedia://en/99242)'s "Ausdehnungslehre" (theory of extension, or [exterior algebra](wikipedia://en/221537)).
