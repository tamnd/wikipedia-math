# Grassmann bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grassmann bundle"}

In algebraic geometry, the **Grassmann *d*-plane bundle** of a vector bundle *E* on an [algebraic scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_scheme) *X* is a scheme over *X*:

:::

$$p: G_d(E) \to X$$

such that the fiber $p^{-1}(x) = G_d(E_x)$ is the [Grassmannian](wikipedia://en/373810) of the *d*-dimensional vector subspaces of $E_x$. For example, $G_1(E) = \mathbb{P}(E)$ is the [projective bundle](wikipedia://en/35183882) of *E*. In the other direction, a Grassmann bundle is a special case of a (partial) [flag bundle](wikipedia://en/58223172). Concretely, the Grassmann bundle can be constructed as a [Quot scheme](wikipedia://en/41883481).

The Grassmann bundle of the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) is the [contact bundle](wikipedia://en/81359548).

Like the usual Grassmannian, the Grassmann bundle comes with natural vector bundles on it; namely, there are universal or [tautological subbundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tautological_subbundle) *S* and universal quotient bundle *Q* that fit into

$$0 \to S \to p^*E \to Q \to 0.$$

Specifically, if *V* is in the fiber *p*<sup>−1</sup>(*x*), then the fiber of *S* over *V* is *V* itself; thus, *S* has rank *r* = *d* = dim(*V*) and $\wedge^d S$ is the [determinant line bundle](wikipedia://en/984307). Now, by the universal property of a projective bundle, the injection $\wedge^r S \to p^* (\wedge^r E)$ corresponds to the morphism over *X*:

$$G_d(E) \to \mathbb{P}(\wedge^r E),$$

which is nothing but a family of [Plücker embeddings](wikipedia://en/1190270).

The [relative tangent bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_tangent_bundle) *T*<sub>*G*<sub>*d*</sub>(*E*)/*X*</sub> of *G*<sub>*d*</sub>(*E*) is given by[^o2]

$$T_{G_d(E)/X} = \operatorname{Hom}(S, Q) = S^{\vee} \otimes Q,$$

which morally is given by the [second fundamental form](wikipedia://en/652078). In the case *d* = 1, it is given as follows: if *V* is a finite-dimensional vector space, then for each line $l$ in *V* passing through the origin (a point of $\mathbb{P}(V)$), there is the natural identification (see [Chern class#Complex projective space](wikipedia://en/294349#Complex_projective_space) for example):

$$\operatorname{Hom}(l, V/l) = T_l \mathbb{P}(V)$$

and the above is the family-version of this identification. (The general care is a generalization of this.)

In the case *d* = 1, the early exact sequence tensored with the dual of *S* = *O*(-1) gives:

$$0 \to \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}(E)} \to p^* E \otimes \mathcal{O}_{\mathbb{P}(E)}(1) \to T_{\mathbb{P}(E)/X} \to 0,$$

which is the relative version of the [Euler sequence](wikipedia://en/21166036).
