# Albert Joseph McConnell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albert Joseph McConnell"}

*Image: Albert Joseph McConnell Grave.jpg.* Grave of Albert Joseph McConnell at Deangrange Cemetery,Co. Dublin

**Albert Joseph McConnell** (19 November 1903 – 19 November 1993) was an Irish mathematician and mathematical physicist who served as the 39th [Provost of Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provost_of_Trinity_College_Dublin) from 1952 to 1974 and a member of the [Council of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_State_%28Ireland%29) from January 1973 to June 1973. He spent his entire academic career at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin).

:::

He was born in [Ballymena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballymena), [County Antrim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Antrim), in 1903. He studied Mathematics and Philosophy at Trinity College Dublin, graduating with a B.A. in 1926. He carried out his postgraduate studies in the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) under the direction of Professor [Tullio Levi-Civita](wikipedia://en/499180) and was awarded his doctorate there in 1928. That same year, he was the official Irish delegate to the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna),[^o3] where he gave an invited address on "The Torsion of Riemannian Space"[^o4]

Upon returning to Trinity College, he was appointed Professor of Natural Philosophy (Physics) and was elected Fellow in 1930. He specialized in [tensor calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor_calculus) and published the book *Applications of the Absolute Differential Calculus* in 1931. He later co-edited *The Mathematical Papers of Sir William Rowan Hamilton: Volume 2, Dynamics* (1940).[^o5]

He was appointed Provost of Trinity College in 1952 and served for 22 years until his retirement in 1974. As he was elected for life, his retirement was voluntary. During his tenure he reformed the structures of Trinity, allowing more junior academics to hold offices such as Bursar, Senior Lecturer, and Registrar. He also oversaw the reform allowing women to be elected as Fellows and Scholars of Trinity College and to be entitled to reside on campus.[^o6]

On his retirement he was appointed by [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Ireland) [Éamon de Valera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Éamon_de_Valera) to the [Council of State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_State_%28Ireland%29).[^o6]

## Books {#Books}

- 1940 *The Mathematical Papers of Sir William Rowan Hamilton: Volume 2, Dynamics*, Cambridge, (co-edited with A. W. Conway)
- 1931 *Applications of the Absolute Differential Calculus*, Blackie & Son, reprinted by Dover in 1947 *as Applications of Tensor Analysis*
