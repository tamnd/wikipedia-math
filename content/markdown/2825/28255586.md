# Eli Maor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eli Maor"}

**Eli Maor** (אלי מאור; born 4 October 1937) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18831) and [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), best known for several books about mathematics and its history written for a popular audience.[^o9]

:::

Eli Maor received his BS at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) and his PhD at the [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) for his work on [musical acoustics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Musical_acoustics) under [Franz Ollendorff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Ollendorff).[^o3] He taught mathematics at the [University of the Negev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Negev) and the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin). From 1995 he taught the history of mathematics at [Loyola University Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_University_Chicago).[^o4] Maor was the editor of the article on [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261) for the *[Encyclopædia Britannica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopædia_Britannica)*.[^o5]

Asteroid [226861 Elimaor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/226861_Elimaor), discovered at the [Jarnac Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarnac_Observatory) in 2004, was named in his honor.[^o9] The official 226861 was published by the [Minor Planet Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_Planet_Center) on 22 July 2013 ([M.P.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minor_Planet_Circulars) 84383).[^o10]

- *The Facts on File Calculus Handbook* (Facts on File, 2003), 2005, Checkmark Books, an encyclopedia of calculus concepts geared for high school and college students
