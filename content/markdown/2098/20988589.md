# Jordan's inequality

*Image: Jordan inequality.svg.* $\frac{2}{\pi}x\leq \sin(x) \leq x\text{ for }x \in \left[0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right]$

*Image: Jordans inequality.svg.* [unit circle](wikipedia://en/27072071) with angle x and a second circle with radius $|EG|=\sin(x)$ around E. $\begin{aligned}&|DE|\leq|\widehat{DC}|\leq|\widehat{DG}|\\ \Leftrightarrow &\sin(x) \leq x \leq\tfrac{\pi}{2}\sin(x)\\ \Rightarrow &\tfrac{2}{\pi}x \leq \sin(x)\leq x \end{aligned}$

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jordan's inequality"}

In mathematics, **Jordan's inequality**, named after [Camille Jordan](wikipedia://en/355931), states that

:::

$$\frac{2}{\pi}x\leq \sin(x) \leq x\text{ for }x \in \left[0,\frac{\pi}{2}\right].$$

It can be proven through the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circles) (see drawing).[^o3]
