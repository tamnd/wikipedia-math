# Fredholm module

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fredholm module"}

In [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917), a **Fredholm module** is a mathematical structure used to quantize the [differential calculus](wikipedia://en/50416). Such a module is, up to trivial changes, the same as the **abstract elliptic operator** introduced by (Atiyah 1970).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

If *A* is an [involutive algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Involutive_algebra) over the complex numbers **C**, then a Fredholm module over *A* consists of an involutive representation of *A* on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H*, together with a [self-adjoint operator](wikipedia://en/187408) *F*, of square 1 and such that the [commutator](wikipedia://en/7193)

\[*F*, *a*\]

is a [compact operator](wikipedia://en/577441), for all *a* in *A*.

:::
