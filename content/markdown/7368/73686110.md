# Adelchi Azzalini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adelchi Azzalini"}

**Adelchi Azzalini** (born 1951) is an Italian statistician and educator. He is known for research in [likelihood inference](wikipedia://en/60224882) and multivariate statistics including the development of [skew normal distributions](wikipedia://en/16266475).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Azzalini was born in Milan and received his *[laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea)* in statistics and economics from the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) in 1975. He carried out his military service for a year before working as a research assistant at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua). In 1978, Azzalini moved to the UK and studied at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London), where he received his MSc and PhD, both in statistics, in 1979 and 1981, respectively. His PhD supervisor was [David Cox](wikipedia://en/1990771). Azzalini returned to [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padua) to take on a researcher position in 1981 and became a professor at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) in 1986 and remained there since.[^o6][^o7]

Azzalini is a fellow of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664),[^o6] a member of the [Bernoulli Society for Mathematical Statistics and Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) and served as the editor of the Bernoulli News in 2000.[^o8] In 2011, a workshop was held at the [Pontifical Catholic University of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Catholic_University_of_Chile) to honor Azzalini's contribution to the development of the skew-normal distribution.[^o9]
