# Applied Econometrics and International Development

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Applied Econometrics and International Development"}

***Applied Econometrics and International Development*** is a biannual [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) covering [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) published by the [Euro-American Association of Economic Development Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro-American_Association_of_Economic_Development_Studies). The journal was established in 2001. The [editor-in-chief](https://aeidpublications.com/editorial-board-members/) is **Adrian Cole Bennett** (Los Angeles, USA).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in [DIALNET](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DIALNET),[^o4] [EconLit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EconLit),[^o5] [International Bibliography of the Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Bibliography_of_the_Social_Sciences),[^o4] [Latindex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latindex),[^o4] and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus).[^o6]
