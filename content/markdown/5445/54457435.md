# Jónsson term

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jónsson term"}

In [universal algebra](wikipedia://en/45200), within [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **majority term**, sometimes called a **Jónsson term**, is a [term](wikipedia://en/24885593) *t* with exactly three [free variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_variable) that satisfies the [equations](wikipedia://en/9284) *t*(*x*, *x*, *y*) = *t*(*x*, *y*, *x*) = *t*(*y*, *x*, *x*) = *x*.[^o2]

:::

For example, for [lattices](wikipedia://en/469304), the term (*x* ∧ *y*) ∨ (*y* ∧ *z*) ∨ (*z* ∧ *x*) is a Jónsson term.

## Sequences of Jónsson terms {#Sequences_of_Jónsson_terms}

In general, **Jónsson terms**, more formally, a *sequence of Jónsson terms*, is a sequence of ternary terms satisfying certain related identities. Being one of the earliest discovered [Maltsev conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maltsev_conditions), a [variety](wikipedia://en/1021753) is congruence distributive if and only if it has a sequence of Jónsson terms.[^o4]

The case of a majority term is given by the special case *n=2* of a sequence of Jónsson terms.[^o5]

Jónsson terms are named after the Icelandic [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Bjarni Jónsson](wikipedia://en/3893573).
