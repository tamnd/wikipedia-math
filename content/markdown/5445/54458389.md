# Muriel Kennett Wales

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Muriel Kennett Wales"}

**Muriel Kennett Wales** (9 Jun 1913 – 8 August 2009) was an Irish-Canadian mathematician, and is believed to have been the first Irish-born woman to earn a PhD in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

She was born Muriel Kennett on 9 June 1913 in [Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belfast). In 1914, her mother moved to [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver), [British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Columbia), and soon remarried; henceforth Muriel was known by her mother's new last name, Wales.

She was first educated at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) (BA 1934, MA 1937 with the thesis *Determination of Bases for Certain Quartic Number Fields*).[^o7] In 1941 she was awarded the PhD from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) for the dissertation *Theory Of Algebraic Functions Based On The Use Of Cycles* under [Samuel Beatty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Beatty_%28mathematician%29)[^o8] (himself the first person to receive a PhD in mathematics in Canada, in 1915).[^o9]

She spent most of the 1940s working in atomic energy, in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto) and [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal), but by 1949 had retired back to Vancouver where she worked in her step-father's shipping company.[^o6]
