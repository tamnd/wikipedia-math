::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb"}

In the field of [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb** arises as one of 11 [regular paracompact honeycombs](wikipedia://en/39591384#Regular_paracompact_honeycombs) in 3-dimensional [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742). It is *paracompact* because it has [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) composed of an infinite number of faces. Each cell consists of a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) whose vertices lie on a [horosphere](wikipedia://en/13487128), a flat plane in hyperbolic space that approaches a single [ideal point](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity.

:::

The [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of the order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb is {6,3,5}. Since that of the [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) is {6,3}, this honeycomb has five such hexagonal tilings meeting at each edge. Since the Schläfli symbol of the [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) is {3,5}, the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of this honeycomb is an icosahedron. Thus, 20 hexagonal tilings meet at each vertex of this honeycomb.[^o10]

::: {#o12 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A lower-symmetry construction of index 120, \[6,(3,5)<sup>\*</sup>\], exists with regular [dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/7149361) fundamental domains, and an [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/14968) [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram) with 6 axial infinite-order (ultraparallel) branches.

:::

## Images {#Images}

The order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb is similar to the 2D hyperbolic regular paracompact [order-5 apeirogonal tiling](wikipedia://en/42309636), {∞,5}, with five [apeirogonal](wikipedia://en/5658261) faces meeting around every vertex.

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb is a [regular hyperbolic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_hyperbolic_3-space) in 3-space, and one of 11 which are paracompact.

There are [15 uniform honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_uniform_honeycombs_in_hyperbolic_space#.5B5.2C3.2C6.5D_family) in the \[6,3,5\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) family, including this regular form, and its regular dual, the [order-6 dodecahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/40291381).

The order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb has a related [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) honeycomb, represented by ↔ , with [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968) and [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) cells.

The **runcicantic order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb**, h<sub>2,3</sub>{6,3,5}, ↔ , has [trihexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2864397), [truncated icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534), [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538), and [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) facets, with a [rectangular](wikipedia://en/70048) [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).
