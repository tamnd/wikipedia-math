::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Order-6 hexagonal tiling honeycomb"}

In the field of [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-6 hexagonal tiling honeycomb** is one of 11 [regular paracompact honeycombs](wikipedia://en/39591384#Regular_paracompact_honeycombs) in 3-dimensional [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742). It is *paracompact* because it has [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) with an infinite number of faces. Each cell is a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) whose vertices lie on a [horosphere](wikipedia://en/13487128): a flat plane in hyperbolic space that approaches a single [ideal point](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity.

:::

The [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of the hexagonal tiling honeycomb is {6,3,6}. Since that of the [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) of the plane is {6,3}, this honeycomb has six such hexagonal tilings meeting at each edge. Since the Schläfli symbol of the [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) is {3,6}, the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of this honeycomb is a triangular tiling. Thus, infinitely many hexagonal tilings meet at each vertex of this honeycomb.[^o10]
