::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Order-6 dodecahedral honeycomb"}

The **order-6 dodecahedral honeycomb** is one of 11 paracompact regular [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654) in [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742). It is *paracompact* because it has [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719) composed of an infinite number of faces, with all vertices as [ideal points](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {5,3,6}, with six [ideal](wikipedia://en/64419430) [dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/7149361) cells surrounding each edge of the honeycomb. Each vertex is ideal, and surrounded by infinitely many dodecahedra. The honeycomb has a [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).

:::

A half symmetry construction exists as with alternately colored dodecahedral cells.

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The order-6 dodecahedral honeycomb is a [regular hyperbolic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_hyperbolic_3-space) in 3-space, and one of 11 which are paracompact.

There are [15 uniform honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_uniform_honeycombs_in_hyperbolic_space#.5B5.2C3.2C6.5D_family) in the \[5,3,6\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) family, including this regular form, and its regular dual, the [order-5 hexagonal tiling honeycomb](wikipedia://en/40291462).

The order-6 dodecahedral honeycomb is part of a sequence of [regular polychora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_polychora) and honeycombs with [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719):
