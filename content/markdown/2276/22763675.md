# Géza Fodor (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Géza Fodor"}

*Image: Géza Fodor (1927-1977) Hungarian mathematician.jpg.* Géza Fodor

**Géza Fodor** (6 May 1927 in [Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szeged) – 28 September 1977 in Szeged) was a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). He received a PhD from the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1954, advised by [László Kalmár](wikipedia://en/1988240) and [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).[^o2] His dissertation was titled " Investigation of the structure of set mappings".[^o2]

:::

He proved [Fodor's lemma](wikipedia://en/1679022) on [stationary sets](wikipedia://en/1679067), one of the most important, and most used results in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). He was a professor at the [Bolyai Institute of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolyai_Institute_of_Mathematics) at the [Szeged University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szeged_University). He was vice-president, then president of the [Szeged University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Szeged). He was elected a corresponding member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).
