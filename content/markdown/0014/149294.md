# Bernstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernstein"}

**Bernstein** is a common surname of German origin, meaning "[amber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amber)" (literally "burn stone"). The name is used by both Germans and Jews, although it is most common among people of [Ashkenazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi) Jewish heritage. The German pronunciation is , but in English, it is pronounced either as or .
