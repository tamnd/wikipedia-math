# List of statisticians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of statisticians"}

This **list of [statisticians](wikipedia://en/48661)** lists people who have made notable contributions to the theories or application of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), or to the related fields of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) or [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488). It includes the [founders of statistics](wikipedia://en/19095208) and others. It includes some 17th- and 18th-century [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) and [polymaths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polymath) whose work is regarded as influential in shaping the later discipline of statistics. Also included are various [actuaries](wikipedia://en/43405), [economists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist), and [demographers](wikipedia://en/45642) known for providing leadership in applying statistics to their fields.

:::
