# Matthew Cook

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matthew Cook"}

**Matthew Cook** (born February 7, 1970) is a mathematician and computer scientist who is best known for having proved that the [Rule 110](wikipedia://en/491028) [cellular automaton](wikipedia://en/54342) is [Turing-complete](wikipedia://en/30621).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Cook was born in [Morgantown, West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgantown,_West_Virginia) and grew up in [Evanston, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evanston,_Illinois). He completed his undergraduate studies at the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) and the Budapest Semesters in Mathematics program. In 1987, Cook qualified as a member of the six-person US team to the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) and won a bronze medal. In 1990, Cook went to work for [Wolfram Research](wikipedia://en/594109), makers of the computer algebra system [Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematica). He did his doctoral work in Computation and Neural Systems at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) from 1999 to 2005. He is now at the Institute of [Neuroinformatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroinformatics) at Zurich in Switzerland.

## Work with Stephen Wolfram {#Work_with_Stephen_Wolfram}

In the 1990s Cook worked as a research assistant to [Stephen Wolfram](wikipedia://en/52469), assisting with work on Wolfram's book, *[A New Kind of Science](wikipedia://en/93070)*. Among other things, he developed a proof showing that the [Rule 110](wikipedia://en/491028) cellular automaton is Turing-complete.

Cook presented his proof at the [Santa Fe Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe_Institute) conference CA98 before the publishing of Wolfram's book—an action that led Wolfram Research to accuse Cook of violating his [NDA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-disclosure_agreement) and resulted in the blocking of the publication of the proof in the conference proceedings. [^o4]

*A New Kind of Science* was released in 2002 with an outline of the proof. In 2004, Cook published his proof in Wolfram's journal *[Complex Systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_Systems_%28journal%29)*.[^o5]
