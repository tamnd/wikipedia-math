# Johannes Knoblauch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Knoblauch"}

*Image: Johannes Knoblauch.jpg.* Johannes Knoblauch

**Johannes Knoblauch** (27 August 1855, [Halle (Saale)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_%28Saale%29) – 22 July 1915, [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin)) was a German mathematician.[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Johannes Knoblauch, whose father was the physics professor [Karl Hermann Knoblauch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Knoblauch), studied law, mathematics and physics from 1872 in Halle, Heidelberg and Berlin. At the Friedrich Wilhelm University (later renamed the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin)) he studied from 1874 to 1878 and from 1880 to 1883 and received his [*Promotion*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) (Ph.D.) in 1882 and his *[Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)* in 1883.[^o4] His doctoral dissertation "Ueber die Allgemeine Wellenfläche" was supervised by [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459).[^o5] Knoblauch was a teacher for the academic year 1878–1879 at (his former school) the state [*Gymnasium*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29) in Halle and from 1879 to 1880 at Berlin's [*Gymnasium zum Grauen Kloster*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelisches_Gymnasium_zum_Grauen_Kloster). At the Friedrich Wilhelm University of Berlin, after his *Habilitation* in 1883 he was appointed [Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent). There in 1889 he was appointed professor extraordinarius and retained that academic post until his death.[^o4] From 1883 to 1885 his friend Heinrich von Stein stayed with him in the *Knoblauch-Haus* at Poststrasse 23.[^o7] In 1906/07 Knoblauch worked with [Kazimierz Żorawski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazimierz_Żorawski) on new [group theoretical](wikipedia://en/41890) methods.[^o8]

Knoblauch was a founding member of the Berlin Mathematical Society. For 13 years he was a member of the editorial board of the *[Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547)*.

*Image: St.-Marien- und St.-Nicolai-Friedhof Pankow Okt. 2016 - 21.jpg.* Gravesite

In 1884, he married Luise *née* Eyssenhardt (1865–1940).[^o4] The grave of the married couple is in the cemetery *Alter Friedhof der St.-Nikolai- und St.-Marien-Gemeinde* in the [Pankow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pankow) [borough of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boroughs_and_neighborhoods_of_Berlin).[^o9]
