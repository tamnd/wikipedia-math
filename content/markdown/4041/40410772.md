# Harold R. Parks

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Harold R. Parks"}

**Harold Raymond Parks** (born May 22, 1949) is an American mathematician and is a professor emeritus of mathematics at [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University).[^o3]

:::

Parks obtained his Ph.D. in 1974 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), under the supervision of [Frederick J. Almgren, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_J._Almgren,_Jr.) In 2012, he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

He has developed and implemented a computational technique for computing parametric [area minimizing surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734). He derived an existence and regularity theory for a class of constrained [variational problems](wikipedia://en/171882). Parks has discovered, and characterized, a type of minimal surface with surprising properties, defined in terms of the [Jacobi elliptic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_elliptic_function).
