# Arnaud Beauville

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Arnaud Beauville"}

**Arnaud Beauville** (born 10 May 1947[^o3]) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), whose research interest is [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

Beauville earned his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) in 1977, with a [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_thesis) regarding [Prym varieties](wikipedia://en/4116892) and the [Schottky problem](wikipedia://en/4125909), under supervision of [Jean-Louis Verdier](wikipedia://en/9168893).

He has been a professor at the [Université Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Sud), then Director of the Mathematics Department at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). He is currently Professor emeritus at the [Université de Nice Sophia-Antipolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Nice_Sophia-Antipolis).

Beauville was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in the summer of 1982.[^o4] He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 1986 at Berkeley. He was a member of [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394).[^o5] He has had 25 Ph.D. students, among them [Claire Voisin](wikipedia://en/5980475), [Olivier Debarre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Debarre), [Yves Laszlo](wikipedia://en/2803385).

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
