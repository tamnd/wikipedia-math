# Brauer–Suzuki theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brauer–Suzuki theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Brauer–Suzuki theorem**, proved by (Brauer Suzuki 1959), (Suzuki 1962), (Brauer 1964), states that if a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) has a [generalized quaternion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_quaternion_group) [Sylow 2-subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup) and no non-trivial [normal subgroups](wikipedia://en/21918) of [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) [order](wikipedia://en/493527), then the group has a [center](wikipedia://en/7125) of order 2. In particular, such a group cannot be [simple](wikipedia://en/47600).

:::

A generalization of the Brauer–Suzuki theorem is given by [Glauberman](wikipedia://en/9744285)'s [Z\* theorem](wikipedia://en/9757648).
