# Decomposition matrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Decomposition matrix"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and in particular [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685), a **decomposition matrix** is a [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) that results from writing the irreducible [ordinary characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_character) in terms of the irreducible [modular characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_character), where the entries of the two sets of characters are taken to be over all [conjugacy classes](wikipedia://en/49176) of elements of [order](wikipedia://en/493527) [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) to the [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) of the [field](wikipedia://en/10603). All such entries in the matrix are non-negative [integers](wikipedia://en/14563). The decomposition matrix, multiplied by its [transpose](wikipedia://en/173844), forms the [Cartan matrix](wikipedia://en/1146433), listing the composition factors of the [projective modules](wikipedia://en/364488).

:::
