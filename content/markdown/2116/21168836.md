# Appeal to loyalty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Appeal to loyalty"}

The **appeal to loyalty** is a [logical fallacy](wikipedia://en/1655450) committed when the premise of an argument uses a perceived need for [loyalty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyalty) of some sort to distract from the issue being discussed.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=inline-head name="Example"}

**Example**

B questions A's statement of x.

Anyone who questions A is disloyal.

Therefore, B is wrong.

Problem: Even if B is disloyal, that doesn't mean that B is wrong, as A is not necessarily always right.

:::
