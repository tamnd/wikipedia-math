# Lebedev–Milin inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lebedev–Milin inequality"}

In mathematics, the **Lebedev–Milin inequality** is any of several inequalities for the coefficients of the exponential of a [power series](wikipedia://en/59958), found by (txt) and (txt). It was used in the proof of the [Bieberbach conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bieberbach_conjecture), as it shows that the [Milin conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milin_conjecture) implies the [Robertson conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robertson_conjecture).

:::

They state that if

$$\sum_{k\ge 0} \beta_kz^k = \exp\left(\sum_{k\ge 1} \alpha_kz^k\right)$$

for complex numbers $\beta_k$ and $\alpha_k$, and $n$ is a positive integer, then

$$\sum_{k=0}^{\infty}|\beta_k|^2 \le \exp\left(\sum_{k=1}^\infty k|\alpha_k|^2\right),$$

$$\sum_{k=0}^{n}|\beta_k|^2 \le (n+1)\exp\left(\frac{1}{n+1}\sum_{m=1}^{n}\sum_{k=1}^m(k|\alpha_k|^2 - 1/k)\right),$$

$$|\beta_n|^2 \le \exp\left(\sum_{k=1}^n(k|\alpha_k|^2 -1/k)\right).$$

See also [exponential formula](wikipedia://en/1166579) (on exponentiation of power series).
