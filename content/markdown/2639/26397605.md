# Erdős arcsine law

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erdős arcsine law"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the **Erdős arcsine law**, named after [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) in 1969,[^o2] states that the [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) [divisors](wikipedia://en/49492) of a number have a [distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29) related to the [arcsine distribution](wikipedia://en/26400022).

:::

Specifically, say that the $j$<sup>th</sup> prime factor $p$ of a given number $n$ (in the sorted sequence of distinct prime factors) is "small" when $\log(\log(p)) < j$. Then, for any fixed parameter $u$, in the limit as $x$ goes to infinity, the proportion of the integers $n$ less than $x$ that have fewer than $u \log(\log(n))$ small prime factors converges to

$$\frac{2}{\pi} \arcsin{ \sqrt{u} }.$$
