# Robert Bixby

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Bixby"}

*Image: Robert Bixby.jpg.* Robert E. Bixby

**Robert E. Bixby** is an American mathematician, the Noah Harding Professor Emeritus of Computational and Applied Mathematics at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University).

:::

Bixby received a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) with a major in [industrial engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_engineering) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) (1968) and a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) (1972).[^o2] His dissertation, *Composition and Decomposition of Matroids and Related Topics*, concerned [matroid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid_theory) and was supervised by [Louis Billera](wikipedia://en/22444348). His doctoral students have included [Collette Coullard](wikipedia://en/61788749) at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), and [Eva K. Lee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eva_K._Lee) at Rice.

He is the President and Co-founder of Gurobi Optimization.[^o4] In 1987 he co-founded [CPLEX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CPLEX) Optimization, which was acquired by [ILOG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ILOG) in 1997.

Bixby was elected a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) in 1997 for contributions to [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555) and the development and commercialization of high-performance optimization software.[^o5][^o6][^o7] He is also a fellow of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).[^o8]
