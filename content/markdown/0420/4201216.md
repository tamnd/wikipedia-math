# Weeks manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weeks manifold"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Weeks manifold**, sometimes called the **Fomenko–Matveev–Weeks manifold**, is a closed [hyperbolic 3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1237700) obtained by (5, 2) and (5, 1) [Dehn surgeries](wikipedia://en/1240699) on the [Whitehead link](wikipedia://en/1966668). It has volume approximately equal to 0.942707… () and (txt) showed that it has the smallest volume of any closed [orientable](wikipedia://en/187446) hyperbolic 3-manifold. The manifold was independently discovered by (txt) as well as (txt).

:::

## Volume {#Volume}

Since the Weeks manifold is an [arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifold](wikipedia://en/15239331), its volume can be computed using its arithmetic data and a formula due to [Armand Borel](wikipedia://en/365672):

$$V_w = \frac{3 \cdot23^{3/2}\zeta_k(2)}{4\pi^4} = 0.942707\dots$$

where $k$ is the [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) generated by $\theta$ satisfying $\theta^3-\theta+1=0$ and $\zeta_k$ is the [Dedekind zeta function](wikipedia://en/1131243) of $k$. [^o6] Alternatively,

$$V_w = \Im(\rm{Li}_2(\theta)+\ln|\theta|\ln(1-\theta)) = 0.942707\dots$$

where $\rm{Li}_n$ is the [polylogarithm](wikipedia://en/482471) and $|x|$ is the [absolute value](wikipedia://en/991) of the complex root $\theta$ (with positive imaginary part) of the cubic.

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetries"}

## Symmetries {#Symmetries}

The Weeks manifold has symmetry group $D_6$, the [dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) of order 12. Quotients by this group and its subgroups can be used to characterize the manifold as a branched covering based on an [orbifold](wikipedia://en/293504). In particular, the quotient by the order-3 subgroup of the symmetry group has underlying set a 3-sphere and branch set a [5<sub>2</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/26643955). [^o9]

:::

## Related manifolds {#Related_manifolds}

The cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold obtained by (5, 1) Dehn surgery on the Whitehead link is the so-called sibling manifold, or sister, of the [figure-eight knot](wikipedia://en/59565) complement. The figure eight knot's complement and its sibling have the smallest volume of any orientable, cusped hyperbolic 3-manifold. Thus the Weeks manifold can be obtained by hyperbolic Dehn surgery on one of the two smallest orientable cusped hyperbolic 3-manifolds.
