# Belt transect

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Belt transect"}

**Belt transects** are used in [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), more specifically in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878), to estimate the distribution of [organisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organism) in relation to a certain area, such as the [seashore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coast) or a meadow.[^o2][^o3]

:::

The belt transect method is similar to the [line transect](wikipedia://en/17076220) method but gives information on abundance as well as presence, or absence of species.[^o4][^o5]

::: {#o7 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Method"}

## Method {#Method}

The method involves laying out a transect line and then placing quadrats over the line, starting the quadrat at the first marked point of the line.[^o8] Any consistent measurement size for the quadrat and length of the line can be chosen, depending on the species. With the quadrats applied, all the individuals of a species can be counted, and the [species abundance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Species_abundance) can be estimated.[^o8] The method is also suitable for long-term observations with a permanent installation.[^o9]

:::
