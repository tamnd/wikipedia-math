# Mazhamaṅgalaṃ Śaṅkaran Naṃpūtiri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mazhamaṅgalaṃ Śaṅkaran Naṃpūtiri"}

**Mazhamaṅgalaṃ Śaṅkaran Naṃpūtiri** (Śaṅkara of Mahiṣamaṅgalṃ) (1494–1570) was an Indian astronomer-astrologer-mathematician belonging to the [Kerala school of astronomy and mathematics](wikipedia://en/3099132) who wrote a large number of books on [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) and [astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrology) in the vernacular language [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam) in an effort to popularize these subjects among the common Malayalam speaking laypersons. He hailed from Peruvanam village in present-day [Thrissur district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thrissur_district). He spent most of his active life at [Chengannur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chengannur) with his teacher Parameśvaran Pōtti of Vāzha-māveli house.[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

Śaṅkaran Nampūtiri's son [Mazhamaṅgalam Nārāyaṇan Nampūtiri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazhamangalam_Narayanan_Namboodiri) was also a great scholar and had produced several original literary works in Malayalam, Sanskrit and mixed Malayalam-Sanskrit (called Maṇipravāḷaṃ) languages.

## Śaṅkaran Nampūtiri's works {#Śaṅkaran_Nampūtiri's_works}

Śaṅkaran Nampūtiri's is known to have composed the following works.[^o2]

1. *Gaṇitasāra* (astronomy)
1. *Candragaṇitakrama* (astronomy)
1. *Ayanacalanādigaṇitakrama* (astronomy)
1. *Jāatakakrama* (horoscopy)
1. *Praśnamāla* with *Bhāṣā* (astrological query)
1. *Ceriya Kāaladīpakaṃ* (short *Kāaladīpakaṃ*)
1. *Valiya Kāaladīpakaṃ* (long *Kāaladīpakaṃ*)
1. *Jātakasāra* in Sanskrit (astrology)
1. *Jātakasāra* in Malayalam (astrology)
1. Commentaries on *Pañjabodha*, *Laghubhāskarīya*, *Muhūrtapadavī* (all called *Bālasaṅkaraṃ*)
1. *Pañjabodhārthadarpaṇa*
