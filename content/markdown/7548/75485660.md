# Yuan Wang (control theorist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuan Wang"}

**Yuan Wang** (王沅) is a Chinese-American mathematician specializing in [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and known for her research on [input-to-state stability](wikipedia://en/39711468). She is a professor of mathematics at [Florida Atlantic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Atlantic_University), chair of the university's Department of Mathematical Sciences, and a moderator for the [arXiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArXiv) mathematical preprint repository in the areas of optimization and control (math.OC) and systems and control (cs.SY).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wang studied mathematics at [Shandong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University) in China, graduating with a bachelor's degree in 1982. She completed a Ph.D. in 1990 at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), with the dissertation *Algebraic Differential Equations and Nonlinear Control Systems* supervised by [Eduardo D. Sontag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduardo_D._Sontag).

She joined Florida Atlantic University as an assistant professor of mathematics in 1990. She was promoted to associate professor in 1995 and full professor in 2000.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Wang was named as an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) in 2013, "for contributions to stability and control of nonlinear systems".
