# Daniel P. Sanders

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel P. Sanders"}

**Daniel P. Sanders** is an American mathematician. He is known for his 1996 efficient proof ([algorithm](wikipedia://en/775)) of proving the [Four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949) (with [Neil Robertson](wikipedia://en/1575704), [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708), and [Robin Thomas](wikipedia://en/24641679)). He was a guest professor of the department of computer science at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).

:::

Sanders received his Ph.D. in algorithms, [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), and optimization from [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech) in 1993 under the guidance of professor [Robin Thomas](wikipedia://en/24641679). He was the Graph Theory Resources editor of www.graphtheory.com. Sanders is a quantitative strategist at [Renaissance Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renaissance_Technologies). He has been on the faculty of the mathematics departments of [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).
