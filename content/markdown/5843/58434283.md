# Hitoshi Kumano-Go

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hitoshi Kumano-Go"}

**Hitoshi Kumano-Go** (4 October 1935 – 24 August 1982) was a Japanese mathematician who specialized in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). He is especially recognized for his work on [pseudo-differential operators](wikipedia://en/973479) and [Fourier integral operators](wikipedia://en/10138003).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Hitoshi Kumano-go was born on 4 October 1935 in Arita, [Wakayama Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wakayama_Prefecture). After finishing high school in 1954, he studied mathematics and graduated from [Osaka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_University) in 1958. He started a doctoral work at the same university under the supervision of [Mitio Nagumo](wikipedia://en/50491692). He received his PhD in 1963. He was promoted to associate professor in 1967 and to full professorship in 1971.[^o3] From 1967 to 1969, Kumano-go had been a visiting member at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of New York University. In May 1981 he entered Osaka University Hospital where a [brain tumor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain_tumor) was discovered. Kumano-go died on 24 August 1982 in Osaka at the age of 46.[^o3]

## Work {#Work}

Kumano-Go first studied the local and global uniqueness of the solutions of the [Cauchy problem](wikipedia://en/1725027) for partial differential equations.[^o5] While at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, Kumano-Go collaborated with [Kurt Friedrichs](wikipedia://en/3626171), [Peter Lax](wikipedia://en/486365) and [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725) among others. He made major contributions to the theory of pseudo-differential operators. Its work contributed to the construction of the fundamental solution of a first order [hyperbolic partial differential equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_system).[^o3][^o6] His treatise on pseudo differential operators was first published in Japanese in 1974 and translated into English in 1981.[^o7][^o3]

Kumano-Go also made important contribution to the study of Fourier integral operators.[^o8]
