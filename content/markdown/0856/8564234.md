# Carleson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carleson"}

Carleson (disambiguation) **Carleson** is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_language) [patronymic surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic_surname). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [C. N. Carleson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._N._Carleson) (1865–1929), Swedish politician
- [Edvard Carleson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edvard_Carleson) (1820–1884), Swedish politician
- [Lennart Carleson](wikipedia://en/1669541) (born 1928), Swedish mathematician
- [Per Carleson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Per_Carleson) (1917–2004), Swedish fencer
- [Robert B. Carleson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_B._Carleson) (1931–2006), American presidential advisor
