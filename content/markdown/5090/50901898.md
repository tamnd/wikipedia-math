# Invasion percolation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Invasion percolation"}

**Invasion percolation** is a mathematical model of realistic [fluid distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_simulation) for slow immiscible fluid invasion in porous media, in [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805). It "explicitly takes into account the transport process taking place". A wetting fluid such as water takes over from a non-wetting fluid such as oil, and capillary forces are taken into account. It was introduced by Wilkinson and Willemsen (1983).[^o2]

:::

Invasion percolation proceeds in [avalanches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avalanche) or bursts, and thus exhibits a form of [intermittency](wikipedia://en/4662960). This avalanche behavior has been likened to [self-organized criticality](wikipedia://en/718855).[^o3][^o4]
