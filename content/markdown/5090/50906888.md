# Nicolao Colletti

*Image: Colletti - Dissertazioni d'algebra, 1787 - 774057.tif.* *Dissertazioni d'algebra*, title page (1787)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicolao Colletti"}

**Nicolao Colletti** (also written **Coletti**) was an 18th-century Italian mathematician and [academic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy) of the [Republic of Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Venice).

:::

Born in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice), he was a [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic) priest in the [church of San Moisè](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Moisè,_Venice)[^o2] and a professor of philosophy.

In collaboration with his brother Sebastiano, a bookseller and publisher, he began a new edition of *L'Italia Sacra* in 1717, which ended in 1722 with the 10th volume.[^o2]

In 1787 his book about mathematics *Dissertazioni d'algebra* was published in [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin).[^o3][^o4]
