# Andreas Albrecht (mathematician)

*Image: Fotothek df tg 0001418 Architektur ^ Geometrie ^ Perspektive ^ Schatten.jpg.* Zwey Bücher: Das erste von der Ohne und durch die Arithmetica gefundenen Perspectiva, Das andere von dem dazu gehörigen Schatten. (Two books: the first of the Perspectiva ... the other of the corresponding shadow)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andreas Albrecht"}

**Andreas Albrecht** ([Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg) 1586 – [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg) 1628) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) mathematician and engineer. Albrecht invented a variety of instruments, some of which are described in his treatises on architecture and perspective: *Instrument zur Architectur* ([Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg), 1622); *Eygendliche Beschreibung* (Nuremberg, 1625); and *Duo Libri. Prior de Perspectiva ... Posterior de umbra ...* (Nuremberg, 1625).[^o2]
