# LMS Journal of Computation and Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="LMS Journal of Computation and Mathematics"}

***LMS Journal of Computation and Mathematics*** was a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) online [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) covering computational aspects of mathematics published by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o3] The journal published its first article in 1998 and ceased operation in 2017. An [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) archive of the journal is maintained by [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press).[^o4]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683),[^o6] [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus),[^o7] and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH).[^o8]
