# Emmanuel Carvallo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emmanuel Carvallo"}

**Emmanuel Carvallo** (1856–1945) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Narbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narbonne).[^o3] He is notable for showing in 1897 that [bicycles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle) could be [self-stable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bicycle_and_motorcycle_dynamics#Self-stability),[^o4][^o5][^o6] for opposing [wave models](wikipedia://en/9426#Wave_model) of [X-rays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X-ray) in 1900,[^o7] and for claiming in 1912 that [Einstein's](wikipedia://en/736) [Theory of Relativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Relativity) had been proven false.[^o8]

:::
