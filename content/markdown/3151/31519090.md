# David Gilbarg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Gilbarg"}

*Image: David Gilbarg.jpg.* David Gilbarg

**David Gilbarg** (17 September 1918, [Boston, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston,_Massachusetts) – 20 April 2001, [Palo Alto, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto,_California)) was an American mathematician, and a professor emeritus at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).

:::

He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University) in 1941; his dissertation, titled *On the Structure of the group of*p*\-adic*l*\-units*, was written under the supervision of [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351).

Gilbarg was co-author, together with his student [Neil Trudinger](wikipedia://en/15173259), of the book *Elliptic Partial Differential Equations of Second Order*. Besides Trudinger, Gilbarg's doctoral students include [Jerald Ericksen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerald_Ericksen) and [James Serrin](wikipedia://en/22296309).
