# J. C. C. McKinsey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="J. C. C. McKinsey"}

**John Charles Chenoweth McKinsey** (30 April 1908 – 26 October 1953), usually cited as **J. C. C. McKinsey**, was an American mathematician known for his work on [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924) and [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636),[^o4] particularly, [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365).[^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

McKinsey received B.S. and M.S. degrees from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) and a Ph.D. degree in 1936 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). He was a Blumenthal Research Fellow at New York University from 1936 to 1937 and a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) from 1942 to 1943.[^o4] He also taught at [Montana State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montana_State_College), and in Nevada, then Oklahoma, and in 1947 he went "to a research group at [Douglas Aircraft Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_Aircraft_Corporation)" that later became the [RAND Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation).[^o3]

McKinsey worked at RAND until he was fired in 1951. The FBI considered him a [security risk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_risk) because he was a [homosexual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homosexual), in spite of the fact that he was an open homosexual who had been in a [committed relationship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committed_relationship) for years. He complained to his superior "How can anyone threaten me with disclosure when everybody already knows?"[^o9]

From 1951 he taught at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), where he was later appointed a Full Professor in the Department of Philosophy,[^o4] where he worked with [Patrick Suppes](wikipedia://en/4824664) on the axiomatic foundations of [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586).[^o3] He committed suicide[^o9] at his home in [Palo Alto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palo_Alto) in 1953.[^o4]
