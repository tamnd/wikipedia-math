# Vlada Limic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vlada Limic"}

**Vlada Limic** is a mathematician specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), the theory of [random graphs](wikipedia://en/358477), and [coalescent theory](wikipedia://en/3441831). Educated in Croatia and the US, she has worked in the US, Canada, and France, where she is a director of research for the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS), affiliated with the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Limic studied mathematics at the [University of Zagreb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zagreb) in Croatia, graduating in 1994. She continued her studies at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where she completed her doctorate in 1998. Her dissertation, *Properties of the Multiplicative Coalescent*, was supervised by [David Aldous](wikipedia://en/14785295).

After postdoctoral research at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) and [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in the US, she moved to Canada in 2002 to become an assistant professor at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia). In 2006 she moved again, to France, where she became a researcher for the CNRS, affiliated with the [University of Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Provence). In 2012 she became a director of research for the CNRS and in 2017 she became affiliated with the University of Strasbourg.

Limic was the editor for the *IMS Bulletin*, a former publication of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), from 2017 to 2019. With [Greg Lawler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greg_Lawler), she is a coauthor of the book *Random Walk: A Modern Introduction* (2010).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Limic was a 2016 recipient of the [Friedrich Wilhelm Bessel Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Wilhelm_Bessel_Research_Award) of the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation). She was a 2022 Medallion Lecturer of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics.
