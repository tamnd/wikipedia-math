# Yellow brick road (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yellow brick road"}

A **Yellow brick road** is "the unique simple closed path that solves the antisocial jogger problem for any of the [Platonic solids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonic_solids). Such a path must (1) begin and end at a single vertex of the solid, and (2) be a straight line path that may cross over edges, but may not pass through any other vertices."[^o2] The solution for the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407) was given by mathematicians [Jayadev Athreya](wikipedia://en/81081495) and David Aulicino in a paper published 11 February 2019,[^o3] and its area division was given by [Li Zhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Li_Zhou) in a paper published 11 October 2021.[^o4]

:::
