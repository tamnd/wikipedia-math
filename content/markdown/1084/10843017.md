# Ignacy Zaborowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ignacy Zaborowski"}

*Image: Logarytmy-dla-szkol-narodowych.jpg.* Book cover of *Logarytmy dlá szkół narodowych*, 1787.

**Ignacy Zaborowski** (2 November 1754–10 January 1803) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [geodesist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesist); [Piarist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piarists). He was a professor and [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the [Collegium Nobilium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collegium_Nobilium_%28Warsaw%29).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born on 2 November 1754 in [Ruthenian Voivodeship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruthenian_Voivodeship),[^o3] Zaborowski attended [Piarists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piarists) school in [Zolochiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zolochiv,_Lviv_Oblast). He joined the Piarists order and after that he taught [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in Piarists school in [Waręż](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waręż) and [Łomża](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łomża). After moving to [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), he became a teacher of mathematics and measuring, and then [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) (1799–1801) of [Collegium Nobilium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collegium_Nobilium_%28Warsaw%29). In 1777 he went to [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) for supplementary studies.[^o3]

In 1786 Zaborowski published the [textbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) *Jeometria praktyczna* ([Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_language) for *Practical geometry*), where he described methods of [geodesic](wikipedia://en/12608) measurement. The book was awarded the [Merentibus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merentibus) by [Stanisław August Poniatowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanisław_August_Poniatowski). Zaborowski was an author of *Logarytmy dlá szkół narodowych* (Polish for *[Logarithms](wikipedia://en/17860) for national schools*) written by order of [Commission of National Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komisja_Edukacji_Narodowej) and published in 1787. He also wrote *Tablice matematyczne* (Polish for *[Mathematical tables](wikipedia://en/239112)*) which was published in 1797. He translated Fulgenty Obermajer's *Arytmetyka praktyczna kieszonkowa*. He started working on the book *Geometria wiejska*, an agriculture measurement textbook, but it has not been finished.[^o3]

In 1800 he became a member of the [Society of Friends of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Society_of_Friends_of_Learning). Since 1802 he was a [provincial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provincial_superior) of [Piarists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piarists).

Zaborowski died on 10 January 1803 in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw).

## Works {#Works}

Books:

- *Jeometria praktyczna* (1786)
- *Logarytmy dlá szkół narodowych* (1787)
- *Tablice matematyczne* (1797)
- *Geometria wiejska* (never finished)

Translations:

- Obermajer, Fulgenty. *Arytmetyka praktyczna kieszonkowa, Porywcze codzienne trafiające się rachunki, przy redukcyach monet, przy kupi i przedaży, z wielką łatwością odprawująca przez taryfę, o której na samym czele kładnie się informacya*.
