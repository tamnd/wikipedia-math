# New York Number Theory Seminar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="New York Number Theory Seminar"}

**The New York Number Theory Seminar** is a research [seminar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seminar) devoted to [theory of numbers](wikipedia://en/21527) and related parts of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939).

:::

The seminar began in 1982 under the founding organizers Harvey Cohn, [David and Gregory Chudnovsky](wikipedia://en/1705790), and [Melvyn B. Nathanson](wikipedia://en/43212431). It is held at the [Graduate Center, CUNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center,_CUNY).

## Overview {#Overview}

*Image: CUNY Graduate Center by David Shankbone.jpg.* The [Graduate Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center,_CUNY), where the New York Number Theory seminars take place, is located in the former [B. Altman and Company Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._Altman_and_Company_Building) at 365 Fifth Avenue.

The New York Number Theory Seminar began in January 1982 and was originally organized by number theorists Harvey Cohn, [David and Gregory Chudnovsky](wikipedia://en/1705790), and Melvyn B. Nathanson.[^o3] Since the retirement of Cohn, Nathanson is the sole organizer.[^o3] The seminar also organizes an annual Workshop on Combinatorial and Additive Number Theory (CANT) at the [Graduate Center, CUNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_Center,_CUNY).

## Publications {#Publications}

Four volumes of the collected lecture notes of the seminar were published in the *[Lecture Notes in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47931235)* series by [Springer-Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer-Verlag).[^o3] These volumes covered the seminar from 1982 to 1988.[^o3] Three additional stand-alone books were published by Springer-Verlag under the title *Number Theory*, covering the seminar between 1989 and 2003.[^o3]
