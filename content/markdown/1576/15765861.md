# Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics"}

**Central Agency for Public Mobilization and Statistics** (**CAPMAS**; الجهاز المركزي للتعبئة العامة والإحصاء) is the official statistical agency of [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt) that collects, processes, analyzes, and disseminates statistical data and conducts the [census](wikipedia://en/6889). CAPMAS was established by a Presidential Decree 2915 in 1964 and is the official provider of data, statistics, and reports.[^o3]

:::

CAPMAS functions support state planning, decision-making and policy assessment but it has been criticized for acting as an information regulator and for failing to provide access to [researchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Researchers). Researchers must obtain a permit from CAPMAS prior to doing [research](wikipedia://en/13437523) in the country.[^o4][^o5]

## History {#History}

A *[Freedom of Information Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedom_of_information_laws_by_country)* was being considered in 2013 by the [Parliament of Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parliament_of_Egypt), to help ensure high level of transparency and disclosure in line with international [best practices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Best_practices) but some doubted it would pass and as of 2016, there is no such law for Egypt.[^o7]

The agency participated in [World Statistics Day](wikipedia://en/29272078) (in October 2015), with activities honoring senior [statisticians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statisticians), holding workshops, and with the launching of their new website.[^o8]

## Population growth {#Population_growth}

One of the more important findings, by the agency, has been the type of population growth occurring in Egypt. Since 2014, Major General Abu Bakr al-Gendy, the head of CAPMAS, said that Egypt's population has been growing, for decades, at an [unsustainable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unsustainable) rate. He said the population grows at an alarming 1 million Egyptians every six months and called this type of growth "a virus" that must be addressed.[^o10][^o11][^o12]
