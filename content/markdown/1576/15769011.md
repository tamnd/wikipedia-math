# Valentin Asmus (philosopher)

*Image: Асмус Валентин Фердинандович.tif.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valentin Asmus"}

**Valentin Ferdinandovich Asmus** (Валенти́н Фердина́ндович А́смус; December 30, 1894 – June 4, 1975) was a Russian and Soviet [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher). He was one of the small group who continued the classical European philosophical tradition through the early [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) times.[^o2] He was an independent thinker and unorthodox [Marxist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marxism),[^o3] with interests in the history of philosophy and [aesthetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aesthetics).

:::

He graduated from [St. Vladimir University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv) in 1919, then moved to [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) in 1927.[^o4] At this period he attacked the views of [William James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_James).[^o5] In the mid-1920s, he was a theorist of literary [constructivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructivism_%28philosophy_of_science%29).[^o6]

Through his wife Irina, he became a friend of [Boris Pasternak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Pasternak), from about 1931.[^o7] His major work *Marx and Bourgeois Historicism* (1933) was influenced by [György Lukács](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/György_Lukács).[^o8] At this point an opponent of [formal logic](wikipedia://en/19636), he changed position and wrote a textbook on it. There is a story of his being summoned to see [Joseph Stalin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Stalin), and required to give logic lectures to [Red Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Army) generals.[^o9]

He was Professor at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) from 1942 to 1972.[^o10] In the 1960s he edited [Plato](wikipedia://en/22954), with [Aleksei Losev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksei_Losev). Outside the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), Asmus was mostly known for his contributions to studying [Immanuel Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant).
