# Kathryn E. Hare

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kathryn E. Hare"}

**Kathryn Elizabeth Hare** (born 1959)[^o3] is a Canadian mathematician specializing in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [fractal geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal_geometry).[^o4] She was the Chair of the [Pure Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pure_Mathematics) Department at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) from 2014 to 2018.[^o5] She retired from the University of Waterloo in 2021.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hare did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), graduating in 1981.[^o4] She earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) in 1986. Her dissertation, under the supervision of John J. F. Fournier, was *Thin Sets and Strict-Two-Associatedness*, and concerned [group representation](wikipedia://en/12695) theory.[^o4]

She was an assistant professor at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta) from 1986 to 1988, before she moved back to Waterloo.[^o4]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

In 2011, the [Chalmers University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University_of_Technology) awarded her an Honorary Doctorate for her "prominent research, both in extent and depth, within classical and abstract harmonic analysis".[^o9] In 2020 she was named as a Fellow of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o10]
