# Pierre de Carcavi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre de Carcavi"}

*Image: Pierre de Carcavy LACMA AC1993.213.8.jpg.* Engraving of Pierre de Carcavy's portrait, a Gift of Mr. and Mrs. Theodore Harris

**Pierre de Carcavi** was born in about 1603, in [Lyon, France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyon,_France) and died in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) in April 1684. He was a secretary of the [National Library of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliothèque_nationale_de_France) under [Louis XIV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_XIV). Carcavi was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Carcavi is known for his correspondence with [Pierre de Fermat](wikipedia://en/7576966), [Blaise Pascal](wikipedia://en/4068), [Christiaan Huygens](wikipedia://en/42127), [Galileo Galilei](wikipedia://en/29688374), [Marin Mersenne](wikipedia://en/72947), [Evangelista Torricelli](wikipedia://en/210413) and [René Descartes](wikipedia://en/25525).[^o2]
