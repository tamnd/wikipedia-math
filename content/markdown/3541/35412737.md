# Michael Wood (cryptographer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Wood"}

**Michael Wood** is an American [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) who designed the [REDOC](wikipedia://en/1131119) [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) system.[^o2][^o3] He is also the author of *The Jesus Secret* and other books.[^o4]

:::

[^o2]: Bruce Schneier *Applied cryptography: protocols, algorithms, and source code in C* 1996 "REDOC III REDOC HI is a streamlined version of REDOC n, also designed by Michael Wood \[1615\]. It operates on an 80-bit block. The key length is variable and can be as large as 2560 bytes (20,480 bits). "
[^o3]: Thomas W. Cusick, Michael C. Wood, The REDOC II Cryptosystem, pp. 545 – 563, CRYPTO 1990.
