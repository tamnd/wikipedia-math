# List of dualities

duality

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **duality**, generally speaking, translates concepts, theorems or mathematical structures into other concepts, theorems or structures, in a one-to-one fashion, often (but not always) by means of an [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) operation: if the dual of *A* is *B*, then the dual of *B* is *A*.
