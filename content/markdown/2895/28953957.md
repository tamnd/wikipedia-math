# Special group (finite group theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Special group"}

In [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a discipline within [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), a **special group** is a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) of [prime power](wikipedia://en/1630483) [order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_a_group) that is either [elementary abelian](wikipedia://en/4843202) itself or of class 2 with its [derived group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derived_group), its [center](wikipedia://en/7125), and its [Frattini subgroup](wikipedia://en/540707) all equal and elementary abelian (Gorenstein 1980, p.183). A special group of order *p*<sup>*n*</sup> that has class 2 and whose derived group has order *p* is called an [extra special group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extra_special_group).

:::
