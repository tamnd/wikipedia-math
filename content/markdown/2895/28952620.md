# Tytus Babczyński

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tytus Babczyński"}

*Image: Das Fotoalbum für Weierstraß 2 49 (Tytus Babczyński).jpg.* Tytus Babczyński

**Titus Babczyński** (1832 – 1910) was a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people) mathematician and physicist. He graduated from the School of Fine Arts in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), then studied physics and mathematics. In 1872, he was a doctor at the [University of St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St._Petersburg). In the period (1857–1862), he was a professor of higher mathematics and mechanics at the School of Fine Arts in Warsaw and eventually the University of Warsaw (1862–1887).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Babczyński was born in Warsaw on 4 January 1832. He graduated provincial school in 1847. He then entered the School of Fine Arts in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), graduating with a degree in architecture in 1850. This same year, he moved to St. Petersberg to study mathematical sciences.[^o6]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- "On the phenomena of induction", master's dissertation between 1850-54
- "Course of Higher Algebra", 1864-65, 1865-66 at the Main School in Warsaw[^o8]
- "Differential and Integral Calculus", 1867-68
- "Introduction to Higher Dynamics", doctoral dissertation in 1872[^o6]
- "On the multiplication of symmetric algebraic, rational integer functions", Zeit. Math. Physik 17 (1872), 147–158.[^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

His master's degree dissertation "On the phenomena of induction," was awarded a gold medal at the [University of St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St._Petersburg).
