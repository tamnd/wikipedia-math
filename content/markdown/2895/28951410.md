# Dominik Szulc

*Image: POL Dominik Szulc.jpg.* Dominik Szulc

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dominik Szulc"}

**Dominik Szulc** (June 10, 1787 – December 27, 1860) was a Polish philosopher and historian. He was a significant precursor to [Polish positivism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Positivism).[^o2]

:::

In 1814, he began studies at the [University of Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vilnius). In 1818, became a teacher of Polish language in high school in Vilnius, and from 1823 a teacher of eloquence and logic in the gymnasium of Bialystok. From 1835, he taught at the gymnasium of Lublin, since 1840 in schools in Warsaw. In 1853, he retired. A member of the Kraków Scientific Society correspondence, and the Russian Geographical Society. In his works he defended the thesis of the Polish character of [Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copernicus). He believed that the discoveries of Copernicus began the development of modern civilization and science.
