# Andrzej Alexiewicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrzej Alexiewicz"}

Andrzej Alexiewicz

*Image: Andrzej Alexiewicz.jpg.* Portrait from *Kronika Miasta Poznania*, 1988

**Andrzej Alexiewicz** (11 February 1917, [Lwów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwów), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regency_Kingdom_of_Poland) – 11 July 1995) was a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people) mathematician, a disciple of the [Lwow School of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lwow_School_of_Mathematics). He obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) under [Władysław Orlicz](wikipedia://en/362718).[^o2] Alexiewicz was an expert at [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and continued and edited the work of [Stefan Banach](wikipedia://en/29646). He introduced the concept in [integration theory](wikipedia://en/15532) known as the [Alexiewicz norm](wikipedia://en/32715996).
