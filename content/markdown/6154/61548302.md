# Richard E. Barlow

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard E. Barlow"}

**Richard Eugene Barlow** (January 12, 1931 – 2024) was an American mathematician and mathematical statistician, who is considered with [Frank Proschan](wikipedia://en/81489700) as the founder of modern [reliability theory](wikipedia://en/1724836). He was a professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 1963 until his retirement in 1999.

:::

He introduced the concept of "Total Time on Test" processes in reliability theory.[^o3] He and Proschan cowrote the book *Mathematical Theory of Reliability*.

## Biography {#Biography}

Barlow received his bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Knox College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knox_College_%28Illinois%29) in 1953 and his master's degree in mathematics from the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) in 1955.[^o5] In 1961 he received his doctorate in mathematical statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) under [Samuel Karlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_Karlin). His dissertation was titled *Applications of Semi-Markov Processes to Counter and Reliability Problems*.[^o6] After graduation, he worked at the [Institute for Defense Analyses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Defense_Analyses) for a year. He was from 1963 until his retirement in 1999 a professor at UC Berkeley. While at Berkeley, he mentored several doctoral students in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476).

He was a guest scientist at the Boeing Laboratories in 1966 and at Florida State University in 1975 and 1976 (with Frank Proschan). From 1963 to 1969 he was an advisor to the [Rand Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rand_Corporation).

In addition to reliability theory, he dealt with probabilistic modeling in Bayesian statistics and statistical data analysis.

In 1991 he was awarded the [John von Neumann Theory Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann_Theory_Prize) with Frank Proschan. He is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), and the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

He is married since 1956 and has four children.

Barlow died in 2024, at the age of 92–93.[^o8]
