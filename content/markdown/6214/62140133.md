# Hans Peter Schlickewei

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Peter Schlickewei"}

*Image: Schlickewei.jpg.* Hans Peter Schlickewei, Oberwolfach 2007

**Hans Peter Schlickewei** (born 1947) is a German mathematician, specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and, in particular, the theory of [transcendental numbers](wikipedia://en/30325).

:::

Schlickewei received his doctorate in 1975 at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) under the supervision of [Theodor Schneider](wikipedia://en/10312621). Schlickewei is a professor at the [University of Marburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marburg).[^o3]

He proved in 1976 the *p*-adic generalization of the [subspace theorem](wikipedia://en/8830237) of [Wolfgang M. Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_M._Schmidt).[^o4] Schlickewei's theorem implies the [Thue-Siegel-Roth theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thue-Siegel-Roth_theorem), whose *p*-adic analogue was already proved in 1958 by David Ridout.[^o5]

In 1998, Schlickewei was an invited speaker with talk *The Subspace Theorem and Applications* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o6]
