# Maury Bramson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maury Bramson"}

**Maury Daniel Bramson** (born 1951 in New York City) is an American mathematician, specializing in probability theory and mathematical statistics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bramson grew up in the Los Angeles area and graduated with a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) after having also attended the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) and having participated in the University of California's Education Abroad Program at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). He graduated with a master's degree in statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o3] In 1977 he received his PhD from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) with thesis *Maximal Displacement of Branching Brownian Motion* under the supervision of [Harry Kesten](wikipedia://en/19816546). Bramson was an instructor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences). He was a member of the mathematical faculties of the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) and the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis) before becoming a professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).[^o3] He was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) for the academic year 1995–1996.[^o5]

Bramson's research deals with models of [interacting particle systems](wikipedia://en/37809826), [stochastic networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_network), and [branching processes](wikipedia://en/377202). The models are motivated by physical and biological science, engineering, and computer science.[^o3]

Bramson was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin in 1998.[^o6] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the class of 2015 for "contributions to stochastic processes and their applications."[^o7] He was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2017. He is also a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o3]
