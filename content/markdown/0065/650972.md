# Ordered subset expectation maximization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ordered subset expectation maximization"}

In [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033), the **ordered subset expectation maximization** (OSEM) method is an [iterative method](wikipedia://en/15237) that is used in [computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography).

:::

In applications in [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging), the OSEM method is used for [positron emission tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positron_emission_tomography) (PET), for [single-photon emission computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Single-photon_emission_computed_tomography) (SPECT), and for [X-ray computed tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computed_tomography) (X-ray CT) scans.

The OSEM method is related to the [expectation–maximization (EM) method](wikipedia://en/470752) of statistics. The OSEM method is also related to methods of [filtered back projection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filtered_back_projection).
