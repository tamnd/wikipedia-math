# The Alphabet Cipher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Alphabet Cipher"}

**"The Alphabet Cipher"** was a brief study published by [Lewis Carroll](wikipedia://en/26586083) in 1868, describing how to use the alphabet to send encrypted codes.[^o2] It was one of four ciphers he invented between 1858 and 1868, and one of two polyalphabetic ciphers he devised during that period and used to write letters to his friends.[^o3]

:::

It describes what is known as a [Vigenère cipher](wikipedia://en/69509), a well-known scheme in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). While Carroll calls this cipher "unbreakable", [Friedrich Kasiski](wikipedia://en/512712) had already published in 1863 a volume describing how to break such ciphers and [Charles Babbage](wikipedia://en/5698) had secretly found ways to break [polyalphabetic ciphers](wikipedia://en/157918) in the previous decade during the [Crimean War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crimean_War).
