# List of Indian states and union territories by GDP

*Image: Indian FY 2025-26 GSDP in ₹ Lakh Crores.png.* Indian FY 2025-26 GSDP in ₹ Lakh Crores

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Indian states and union territories by GDP"}

These are lists of Indian [states and union territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/States_of_India) by their nominal gross state domestic product (GSDP). GSDP is the sum of all value added by industries within each state or union territory and serves as a counterpart to the national [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP).[^o2] , the [Government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) accounted for about 21% of the GDP followed by [agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agriculture) with 21% and corporate sector at 12%. The remaining 48% is sourced from small [proprietorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proprietorship) and [partnership](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partnership) companies, unorganized sector and households.[^o3]

:::
