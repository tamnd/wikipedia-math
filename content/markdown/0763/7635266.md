# Krylov–Bogolyubov theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Krylov–Bogolyubov theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Krylov–Bogolyubov theorem** (also known as the **existence of invariant measures theorem**) may refer to either of the two related fundamental [theorems](wikipedia://en/30977) within the theory of [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). The theorems guarantee the existence of [invariant measures](wikipedia://en/7635201) for certain "nice" maps defined on "nice" spaces and were named after [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)-[Ukrainian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine) [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical physicists](wikipedia://en/19594028) [Nikolay Krylov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Mitrofanovich_Krylov) and [Nikolay Bogolyubov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Bogolyubov) who proved the theorems.[^o2]

:::

## Formulation of the theorems {#Formulation_of_the_theorems}

### Invariant measures for a single map {#Invariant_measures_for_a_single_map}

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem (Krylov–Bogolyubov)"}

**Theorem (Krylov–Bogolyubov)**. Let *X* be a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042), [metrizable](wikipedia://en/19738) [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) and *F* : *X* → *X* a [continuous map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_map). Then *F* admits an invariant [Borel](wikipedia://en/4322) [probability measure](wikipedia://en/45437).

That is, if Borel(*X*) denotes the [Borel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_sigma_algebra) [σ-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_algebra) generated by the collection *T* of [open subsets](wikipedia://en/39358) of *X*, then there exists a probability measure *μ* : Borel(*X*) → \[0, 1\] such that for any subset *A* ∈ Borel(*X*),

$$\mu \left( F^{-1} (A) \right) = \mu (A).$$

In terms of the [push forward](wikipedia://en/9412979), this states that

$$F_{*} (\mu) = \mu.$$

:::

### Invariant measures for a Markov process {#Invariant_measures_for_a_Markov_process}

Let *X* be a [Polish space](wikipedia://en/396622) and let $P_t, t\ge 0,$ be the transition probabilities for a time-homogeneous [Markov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process) [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) on *X*, i.e.

$$\Pr [ X_{t} \in A | X_{0} = x ] = P_{t} (x, A).$$

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem (Krylov–Bogolyubov)"}

**Theorem (Krylov–Bogolyubov)**. If there exists a point $x\in X$ for which the family of probability measures { *P*<sub>*t*</sub>(*x*, ·) | *t* > 0 } is [uniformly tight](wikipedia://en/6553354) and the semigroup (*P*<sub>*t*</sub>) satisfies the [Feller property](wikipedia://en/12429079), then there exists at least one invariant measure for (*P*<sub>*t*</sub>), i.e. a probability measure *μ* on *X* such that

$$(P_{t})_{\ast} (\mu) = \mu \mbox{ for all } t > 0.$$

:::
