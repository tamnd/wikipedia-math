# Conservation Reporting and Evaluation System

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conservation Reporting and Evaluation System"}

The **Conservation Reporting and Evaluation System** (CRES) is the system used by the [Natural Resources Conservation Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_Resources_Conservation_Service) (NRCS) in the United States to collect and compile information about staff activities, conservation program implementation, and program accomplishments.[^o2]

:::
