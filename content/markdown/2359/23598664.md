# Nate Ackerman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nate Ackerman"}

**Nathanael Leedom Ackerman** (born March 4, 1978),[^o3] known as **Nate Ackerman**, is a British-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [wrestler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrestling). He is the son of [Peter Ackerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Ackerman) and [Joanne Leedom-Ackerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_Leedom-Ackerman).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ackerman was born in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29), United States to [Joanne Leedom-Ackerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joanne_Leedom-Ackerman) and [Peter Ackerman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Ackerman). He was educated at the [American School in London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_School_in_London)[^o5] and then [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), where he graduated in June 2000.

Ackerman competed in the [2004 Summer Olympic Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2004_Summer_Olympics) as part of the [Great Britain National Team](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain_at_the_2004_Summer_Olympics).[^o6] He also competed in the [1999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1999_World_Wrestling_Championships), [2001](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2001_World_Wrestling_Championships), 2002, 2003, 2005 and 2011 World Championships.[^o7] Ackerman's best international finish was fifth at the 2011 Commonwealth Championship.[^o3]

Ackerman was a two-times winner of the [British Wrestling Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Wrestling_Championships) at welterweight in 2002 and 2004.[^o8]

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in mathematics in 2006 from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o10]
