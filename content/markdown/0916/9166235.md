# Mike Lesser

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mike Lesser"}

**Michael John Lesser** (28 September 1943 – 1 July 2015) was a [mathematical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [political activist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_activist).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

The youngest member of the [Committee of 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Committee_of_100_%28United_Kingdom%29), he was sent, aged 16, to [Wormwood Scrubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wormwood_Scrubs_%28HM_Prison%29) Prison along with most of the committee. He served two spells as contributor to London's [underground](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Underground_press) journal *[International Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Times)*.[^o4]

He was active in [May 1968 in France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/May_1968_in_France).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

In 1992 he was the co-author, with Prof A. Wuensche, of the book *The Global Dynamics Of Cellular Automata*, published in the *[Santa Fe Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Fe_Institute)'s Reference Volumes*. The book is an atlas of [emergent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence) forms evolving from the apparently chaotic product of a set of iterated logical operations.

He was assistant to the Directing Professor, P. Allen, at the Institute for Ecotechnological Research at [Cranfield University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cranfield_University). He is the co-author of several scientific papers on [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems) theory with Allen.[^o7]

He worked on [super computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_computer) at [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA)'s Goddard Jet Propulsion Lab and at the [Rutherford Appleton Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutherford_Appleton_Laboratories) in Oxon, UK.

He published papers on [autism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autism) with [Dinah Murray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dinah_Murray),[^o8] with whom he co-founded *Autism and Computing*, a non-profit organization.

In 2009 he founded *The International Times Archive*, a free archive of every page of *International Times* from its first issue in 1966 to its last in 1994.

A biographical interview may also be found in the style magazine *[Dazed & Confused](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dazed_&_Confused_%28magazine%29)*.[^o9] A more complete autobiography may be found in *[The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times).*[^o10]

## Death {#Death}

Lesser died in London in 2015 at the age of 71.[^o4] In December 2015, coroner Andrew Walker at [Barnet Coroners Court](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barnet_Coroners_Court) ruled that Lesser died from [asphyxiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asphyxiation). Evidence was given that Lesser had suffered from depression for "many years". He had a [heart bypass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_bypass) in 1999 and in June 2015 had been told that he had terminal [lung cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lung_cancer). He left a note for his wife and friends.[^o12]
