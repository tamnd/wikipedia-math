# Jean-Louis Verdier

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Louis Verdier"}

**Jean-Louis Verdier** (; 2 February 1935 – 25 August 1989) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked, under the guidance of his doctoral advisor [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042), on [derived categories](wikipedia://en/985897) and [Verdier duality](wikipedia://en/7190735). He was a close collaborator of Grothendieck, notably contributing to [SGA 4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SGA_4) his theory of [hypercovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypercovers) and anticipating the later development of [étale homotopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Étale_homotopy) by [Michael Artin](wikipedia://en/1519821) and [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679), following a suggestion he attributed to [Pierre Cartier](wikipedia://en/1470549). [Saul Lubkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saul_Lubkin)'s related theory of [rigid hypercovers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rigid_hypercover) was later taken up by [Eric Friedlander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Friedlander) in his definition of the [étale topological type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Étale_topological_type).

:::

Verdier was a student at the elite [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris, and later became director of studies there, as well as a Professor at the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-VII). For many years he directed a joint seminar at the École Normale Supérieure with [Adrien Douady](wikipedia://en/1604604). Verdier was a member of [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394).[^o3] In 1984 he was the president of the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France).

In 1976 Verdier developed a useful regularity condition on [stratified sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stratified_sets) that the Chinese-Australian mathematician [Tzee-Char Kuo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tzee-Char_Kuo) had previously shown implied the [Whitney conditions](wikipedia://en/9322242) for [subanalytic sets](wikipedia://en/28156210) (such as real or complex analytic varieties). Verdier called the condition (w) for [Whitney](wikipedia://en/391794), as at the time he thought (w) might be equivalent to Whitney's condition (b). Real algebraic examples for which the Whitney conditions (b) hold but Verdier's condition (w) fails, were constructed by [David Trotman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Trotman) who has obtained many geometric properties of (w)-regular stratifications. Work of Bernard Teissier, aided by Jean-Pierre Henry and Michel Merle at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), led to the 1982 result that Verdier's condition (w) is equivalent to the Whitney conditions for complex analytic stratifications.

Verdier later worked on the theory of [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213).[^o4]
