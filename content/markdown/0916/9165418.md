# Richard Ferrybridge

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Ferrybridge"}

**Richard Ferrybridge**[^o2] was an English [Scholastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholasticism) [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) of the fourteenth century.

:::

His works include a *Tractatus de veritate sive logica*, and the *Consequentiae*.[^o3] He is alluded to in *[The Anatomy of Melancholy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Anatomy_of_Melancholy)*.[^o4]
