# François Bruhat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="François Bruhat"}

**François Georges René Bruhat** (; 8 April 1929 – 17 July 2007) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_groups). The [Bruhat order](wikipedia://en/10163003) of a [Weyl group](wikipedia://en/296332), the [Bruhat decomposition](wikipedia://en/1139142), and the [Schwartz&ndash;Bruhat functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwartz&ndash;Bruhat_function) are named after him.

:::

He was the son of [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) (and associate director of the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) during the [occupation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_occupation_of_France_during_World_War_II)) [Georges Bruhat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Bruhat), and brother of physicist [Yvonne Choquet-Bruhat](wikipedia://en/22081008).
