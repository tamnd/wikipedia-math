# Willard D. James

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Willard D. James"}

**Willard Donald James**[^o2] (June 13, 1927 – May 20, 2025) was an American mathematician most known for his work on the [James-Stein estimator](wikipedia://en/4116806).[^o3] He graduated with a Ph.D. in mathematics from [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) in 1957[^o3][^o4] and was recruited to [California State University, Long Beach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_Long_Beach) in 1967 from which he retired as a Professor Emeritus in 1987.[^o5][^o6] James died on May 20, 2025, at the age of 97.[^o7]

:::
