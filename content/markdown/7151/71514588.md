# Approximate fibration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Approximate fibration"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a branch of mathematics, an **approximate fibration** is a sort of [fibration](wikipedia://en/316826) such that the [homotopy lifting property](wikipedia://en/522786) holds only approximately. The notion was introduced by Coram and Duvall in 1977.[^o2]

:::

A **manifold approximate fibration** is a proper approximate fibration between manifolds.[^o3] Some authors believe that manifold approximate fibrations are the "correct bundle theory for topological manifolds and singular spaces".[^o4]
