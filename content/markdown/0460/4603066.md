# Horseshoe lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horseshoe lemma"}

In [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), the **horseshoe lemma**, also called the **simultaneous resolution theorem**, is a statement relating [resolutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_resolution) of two objects $A'$ and $A''$ to resolutions of extensions of $A''$ by $A'$. It says that if an object $A$ is an extension of $A''$ by $A'$, then a resolution of $A$ can be built up [inductively](wikipedia://en/18881) with the *n*th item in the resolution equal to the [coproduct](wikipedia://en/310889) of the *n*th items in the resolutions of $A'$ and $A''$. The name of the lemma comes from the shape of the diagram illustrating the lemma's hypothesis.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Formal statement"}

## Formal statement {#Formal_statement}

Let $\mathcal{A}$ be an [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063) with [enough projectives](wikipedia://en/3017382). If

*Image: Horseshoe lemma.png.*

is a [diagram](wikipedia://en/138484) in $\mathcal{A}$ such that the column is [exact](wikipedia://en/138215) and the rows are projective resolutions of $A'$ and $A''$ respectively, then it can be completed to a commutative diagram

*Image: Horseshoe lemma conclusion.png.*

where all columns are exact, the middle row is a projective resolution of $A$, and $P_n=P'_n\oplus P''_n$ for all *n*. If $\mathcal{A}$ is an abelian category with [enough injectives](wikipedia://en/473779), the [dual](wikipedia://en/372399) statement also holds.

The lemma can be proved inductively. At each stage of the induction, the properties of projective objects are used to define maps in a projective resolution of $A$. Then the [snake lemma](wikipedia://en/87089) is invoked to show that the simultaneous resolution constructed so far has exact rows.

:::
