# Zuhair Nashed

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zuhair Nashed"}

**M. Zuhair Nashed** (born May 14, 1936, in Aleppo, Syria) is an American mathematician, working on [integral](wikipedia://en/474234) and [operator equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_equation), inverse and ill-posed problems, numerical and nonlinear [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), optimization and [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), [optimal control theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimal_control_theory), [signal analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_analysis), and [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Zuhair Nashed received his Master of Science degree in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1958, and his Doctor of Philosophy degree in mathematics, from the [University of Michigan Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan_Ann_Arbor) in 1963.[^o3]

He started his academic career in 1963 as an assistant professor at [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology), Atlanta, and was promoted to associate professor in 1965 and to full professor in 1969. He moved to the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware) in 1977 to hold the position of Professor of mathematics and electrical engineering. He moved to the [University of Central Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Florida), Orlando, in 2002, where he held the position of Professor and Chair 2002 – 2006. Since 2007 he is a professor at the University Central Florida, Orlando.

In 2012 he was inducted Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
