# Generatrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Generatrix"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **generatrix** () or **describent** is a [point](wikipedia://en/593693), [curve](wikipedia://en/89246) or [surface](wikipedia://en/4342970) that, when moved along a given path, generates a new [shape](wikipedia://en/169191).[^o2] The path directing the motion of the generatrix motion is called a **directrix** or **dirigent**.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

A [cone](wikipedia://en/782427) can be generated by moving a line (the generatrix) fixed at the future apex of the cone along a closed curve (the directrix); if that directrix is a circle perpendicular to the line connecting its center to the apex, the motion is [rotation around a fixed axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotation_around_a_fixed_axis) and the resulting shape is a circular cone.[^o6]

The generatrix of a [cylinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cylinder_%28geometry%29), a limiting case of a cone, is a line that is kept [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to some axis.

:::
