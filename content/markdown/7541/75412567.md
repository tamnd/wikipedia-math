# Alfonso Sorrentino (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alfonso Sorrentino"}

**Alfonso Sorrentino** (born 27 November 1979) is an Italian mathematician who is currently full professor of [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) at [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata) (Italy).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

His main scientific interests are in the field of [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), specifically, in the study of [Hamiltonian dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/198319) by means of [variational methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_methods_%28physics%29) (Aubry-Mather theory), [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) techniques (weak KAM theory and [Hamilton-Jacobi equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton-Jacobi_equation)) and geometric approaches ([symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954)).[^o7][^o8][^o9]

## Biography and career {#Biography_and_career}

Sorrentino was a student of [John N. Mather](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_N._Mather) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), obtaining his Ph.D. in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 2008.[^o11][^o12] After his graduation, he held the roles of junior research fellow at Fondation Sciences Mathématiques de Paris (2008–2009),[^o13] Herchel-Smith Research Fellow at [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (2009–2012),[^o14] Newton Trust Fellow of [Pembroke College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembroke_College,_Cambridge) (2009–2012), and Researcher at [Roma Tre University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roma_Tre_University) (2012–2014).[^o15][^o16]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- 2018 Guido Fubini Prize for Mathematics, Accademia delle Scienze di Torino (Italy).
- 2019 Barcelona Dynamical System Prize 2019, [Catalan Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/77808703) (Catalunya, Spain).
- 2020 International Consortium of Chinese Mathematicians (ICCM 2020) Best Paper Award (Gold Medal).
- 2023 Frontiers of Science Award, given at the International Congress of Basic Sciences, Beijing July 2023[^o18]
