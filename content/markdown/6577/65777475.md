# Gisèle Ruiz Goldstein

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gisèle Ruiz Goldstein"}

**Gisèle Ruiz Goldstein** is an American mathematician known for her research in [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), and applications of mathematics to physics and finance. Goldstein has won multiple awards; in particular, one of her papers was given the Editors' Choice Award from the editors of *[Mathematische Nachrichten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematische_Nachrichten)* in 2010, and she was the University of Memphis Faudree Chair from 2011-2014. In December 2020, *Discrete and Dynamical Systems - Series S* created a special issue in honor of Goldstein's 60th birthday.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Goldstein received her PhD from [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) in 1986. Her doctoral advisor was [Jerome Goldstein](wikipedia://en/32366276), and the title of her dissertation was *Mathematical Contributions to Thomas-Fermi Theory*.[^o5]
