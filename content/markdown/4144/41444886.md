# Homotopy group with coefficients

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Homotopy group with coefficients"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), for $i \ge 2$, the ***i*-th homotopy group with coefficients** in an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) *G* of a [based space](wikipedia://en/1236666) *X* is the [pointed set](wikipedia://en/1242444) of [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) classes of based maps from the [Moore space](wikipedia://en/2139649) of type $(G, i)$ to *X*, and is denoted by $\pi_i(X; G)$.[^o2] For $i \ge 3$, $\pi_i(X; G)$ is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447). The groups $\pi_i(X; \mathbb{Z})$ are the usual [homotopy groups](wikipedia://en/297506) of *X*.

:::
