# Charlie Reeve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charlie Reeve"}

Academics

**Charlie Lucian Reeve** is a professor of psychology in the [University of North Carolina at Charlotte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Charlotte)'s Ph.D. program in [health psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_psychology), where he is also an affiliate of the Cognitive Science Academy.[^o3]
