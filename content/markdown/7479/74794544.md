# Houman Owhadi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Houman Owhadi"}

**Houman Owhadi** is a professor of Applied and Computational Mathematics and Control and Dynamical Systems in the Computing and Mathematical Sciences department at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3] He is known for his work in statistical numerical approximation, kernel learning, and uncertainty quantification.[^o4]

:::

## Academic biography {#Academic_biography}

Owhadi studied at the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique) where he received a M.S. in Mathematics and Physics in 1994 and was a civil servant in the [Corps des ponts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corps_des_ponts) in 1997. He received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) in 2001, studying under [Gérard Ben Arous](wikipedia://en/48269909). He was a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) Research Fellow between 2001 and 2004. He joined the California Institute of Technology in 2004 and became Professor of Applied & Computational Mathematics and Control & Dynamical Systems in 2011.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Owhadi is noted for his work in the field of statistical numerical approximation, which explores the interplay between numerical approximation and statistical inference.[^o8][^o9] His work has influenced the field of [probabilistic numerics](wikipedia://en/69088046)[^o10] which combines approaches from [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) and applied mathematics.

He has done extensive work in [uncertainty quantification](wikipedia://en/5987648) and has been editor of the *Handbook of Uncertainty Quantification*[^o11] and the [SIAM/ASA Journal on Uncertainty Quantification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM/ASA_Journal_on_Uncertainty_Quantification).[^o12]

He has also worked on Gaussian processes and kernel methods, the problem of kernel learning, and numerical homogenization.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Owhadi won the EPFL doctorate award for his thesis in 2001.[^o14] He was an invited lecturer at the SIAM conference on Computational Science and Engineering in 2015[^o15] and a plenary speaker at the XVI International Conference on Hyperbolic Problems.[^o16] In 2019, he received the SIAM Germund Dahlquist Prize.[^o17] He was elected a [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) fellow in 2022 for "outstanding contributions in statistical numerical approximation, kernel learning, and uncertainty quantification".[^o18] He was elected as part of the 2024 class of Vannevar Bush Fellows, the [United States Department of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Defense)'s flagship single-investigator award for basic research.[^o19]
