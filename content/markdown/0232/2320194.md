# Dmitri Anosov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitri Anosov"}

**Dmitri Victorovich Anosov** (Дми́трий Ви́кторович Ано́сов; November 30, 1936 – August 7, 2014[^o3]) was a Russian mathematician active during the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). He is best known for his contributions to [dynamical systems theory](wikipedia://en/990632).[^o4]

:::

He was a full member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) and a laureate of the [USSR State Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize) (1976).[^o4] He was a student of [Lev Pontryagin](wikipedia://en/582075).[^o5] In 2014, he died at the age of 77.[^o6]
