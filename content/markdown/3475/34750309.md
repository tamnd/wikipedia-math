# Amie Wilkinson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amie Wilkinson"}

**Anne Marie Wilkinson** (born 1968) is an American mathematician and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o3] Her research topics include smooth [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [chaos theory](wikipedia://en/6295), and [semisimple](wikipedia://en/1337587) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).[^o3] Wilkinson, in collaboration with Christian Bonatti and Sylvain Crovisier,[^o4][^o5] partially resolved the twelfth problem on [Stephen Smale's list of mathematical problems for the 21st Century](wikipedia://en/8294746).[^o6]

:::

Wilkinson was named a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS) in 2014.[^o7] She was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o8] in 2019 and the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2021.[^o9] In 2020, she received the [Levi L. Conant Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_L._Conant_Prize) of the AMS for her overview article on the modern theory of [Lyapunov exponents](wikipedia://en/166441) and their applications to diverse areas of dynamical systems and mathematical physics.[^o10][^o11]

## Biography {#Biography}

Anne Marie Wilkinson was born to Ruth E. VanDemark and Leland Wilkinson.[^o13]

She received a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) degree in mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1989 and a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in Mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1995 under the direction of [Charles C. Pugh](wikipedia://en/55128667).[^o14] She is currently a professor of mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and was previously a professor of mathematics at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University).[^o15]

## Work {#Work}

Wilkinson's work focuses on the geometric and statistical properties of [diffeomorphisms](wikipedia://en/8564) and flows with a particular emphasis on stable [ergodicity](wikipedia://en/5456824) and partial [hyperbolicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolicity). In a series of papers with [Christian Bonatti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Bonatti) and [Sylvain Crovisier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvain_Crovisier), Wilkinson studied centralizers of diffeomorphisms[^o17][^o18] settling the [*C*<sup>1</sup>](wikipedia://en/1408000) case of the twelfth problem on [Stephen Smale's list of mathematical problems for the 21st Century](wikipedia://en/8294746).[^o19]

## Awards {#Awards}

Wilkinson was the recipient of the 2011 [Satter Prize in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satter_Prize_in_Mathematics),[^o14] in part for her work with [Keith Burns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Burns_%28mathematician%29) on stable ergodicity of partially hyperbolic systems.[^o21]

She gave an invited talk, "Dynamical Systems and Ordinary Differential Equations", in the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) 2010 in [Hyderabad, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India).[^o22]

In 2013 she became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), for "contributions to dynamical systems".[^o23] In 2019 she was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o24] In 2020 she received the [Levi L. Conant Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_L._Conant_Prize) of the AMS.[^o10] She was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2021.[^o25]

Wilkinson has been featured in articles in [Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976).[^o26][^o27] Wilkinson is a member of the Board of Advisers of [Scientific American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_American).[^o28]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Wilkinson married [Benson Farb](wikipedia://en/32518776) on December 28, 1996. They are professors in the same department.[^o13]
