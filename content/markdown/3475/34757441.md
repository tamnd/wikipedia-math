# L-stability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="L-stability"}

Within [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) regarding [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), **L-stability** is a special case of [A-stability](wikipedia://en/2091823#A-stability), a property of [Runge–Kutta methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge–Kutta_method) for solving [ordinary differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_equations). A method is L-stable if it is [A-stable](wikipedia://en/2091823#A-stability) and $\phi(z) \to 0$ as $z \to \infty$, where $\phi$ is the stability function of the method (the stability function of a Runge–Kutta method is a [rational function](wikipedia://en/361210) and thus the limit as $z \to +\infty$ is the same as the limit as $z \to -\infty$). L-stable methods are in general very good at integrating [stiff equations](wikipedia://en/2091823).

:::
