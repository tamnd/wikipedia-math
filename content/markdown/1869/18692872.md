# Decidable sublanguages of set theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Decidable sublanguages of set theory"}

In [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), various sublanguages of [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) are [decidable](wikipedia://en/913118).[^o2][^o3] They are referred to as syllogistics. Together with the operations of [set algebra](wikipedia://en/961805), decidable languages include:

:::

- Sets with monotone, additive, and multiplicative functions, but without quantifiers.[^o4]
- Sets with restricted quantifiers.[^o5]
