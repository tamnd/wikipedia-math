# Arnold Mathematical Journal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Arnold Mathematical Journal"}

The ***Arnold Mathematical Journal*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_reviewed_journal) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) established in 2014. It is organized jointly by the [Institute for Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Mathematical_Sciences_%28Stony_Brook_University%29) at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University), USA, and [Association for Mathematical Research](https://amathr.org/publications/journals/). The journal's [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Sergei Tabachnikov](wikipedia://en/45658812).[^o3] The journal is [abstracted and indexed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstracting_and_indexing) in [ZbMATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZbMATH) and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus).[^o4]

:::

The journal is named after the mathematician [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490), with the choice of name intended as "a declaration that the journal should serve to maintain and promote the scientific style characteristic for Arnold's best mathematical works."[^o5]
