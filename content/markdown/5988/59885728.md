# Huang Minqiang

[Huang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huang_%28surname%29)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Huang Minqiang"}

**Huang Minqiang** (; born October 1960) is a Chinese mathematician and computer scientist, with a specialization in [information processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_processing). He is a research professor of the 58th Research Institute of the [People's Liberation Army Strategic Support Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army_Strategic_Support_Force). He is an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) and an alternate of the [19th Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Huang was born in October 1960 in [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai). He graduated from the Department of Mathematics of [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University) in 1980, and earned his Ph.D. from the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China) in 1989.[^o4]

Huang is a research professor of the 58th Research Institute of the [People's Liberation Army Strategic Support Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/People's_Liberation_Army_Strategic_Support_Force).[^o5] His research focus is on information processing, [systems analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_analysis), and [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492).[^o4] He served as a member of the Information Assurance Expert Group of the [863 Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/863_Program).[^o6]

In 2005, Huang was elected an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4] He was elected an alternate of the [19th Central Committee of the Chinese Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/19th_Central_Committee_of_the_Chinese_Communist_Party) in 2017.[^o5]
