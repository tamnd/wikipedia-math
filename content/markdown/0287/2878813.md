# Judy A. Holdener

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Judy A. Holdener"}

**Judy Holdener** (née Newhauser) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator). She is a professor of mathematics at [Kenyon College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenyon_College). She was born in 1965. Holdener's primary interest is in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). She discovered a simpler proof of the theorem of [Touchard](wikipedia://en/2279627), which states that every [perfect number](wikipedia://en/23670) is of the form 2k, 12k+1, or 36k+9.

:::

Holdener earned her B.S. in mathematics at [Kent State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_State_University) and her M.S. and Ph.D. in mathematics at the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign). Holdener joined the faculty of Kenyon College in 1997, where she is currently the John B. McCoy Distinguished Teaching Chair.

The poem *Euler's Daughter* by award-winning South African poet [Athol Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Athol_Williams) is dedicated to Holdener in celebration of her love of mathematics and life.[^o2]
