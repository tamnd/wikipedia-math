# Wing Hung Wong

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wing Hung Wong"}

[Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_%28surname%29)

**Wing Hung Wong** (王永雄) is a Chinese-American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), [computational biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biologist), and [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) professor.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Wong graduated from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1976 with a bachelor's degree. At the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), he studied under renowned statistician [Grace Wahba](wikipedia://en/7187724), and was awarded a PhD in Statistics in 1980. After graduation, he taught at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), served as an assistant professor, associate professor, and professor. In 1994 he joined the [Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong) Department of Statistics. Since 1997, he taught and led his lab at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). In 2004, he was appointed Professor at Stanford University, and served as Head of the Department of Statistics at Stanford University in 2009. As of 2020, he is Professor of Statistics and Biomedical Data Science at Stanford.

His students include [George Tseng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Tseng) at University of Pittsburgh, Rick Chappell at [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), and [Jun S. Liu](wikipedia://en/31926604) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Wong is a fellow of [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in the United States and [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) (2010). He won the highest award in the field of Statistics [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) in 1993. In 2021 he was awarded the [COPSS Distinguished Achievement Award and Lectureship](wikipedia://en/15967717).
