# Hideyuki Matsumura

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hideyuki Matsumura"}

**Hideyuki Matsumura** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) particularly known for his textbooks[^o2][^o3] in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990). He received his Ph.D. in 1958 from [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) under the advisory of mathematician [Yasuo Akizuki](wikipedia://en/70092474).[^o4]

:::
