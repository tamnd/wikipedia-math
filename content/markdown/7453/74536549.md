# Grade (ring theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grade"}

In [commutative](wikipedia://en/245990) and [homological](wikipedia://en/139410) algebra, the **grade** of a [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/326430) [module](wikipedia://en/276410) $M$ over a [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) $R$ is a [cohomological](wikipedia://en/194060) invariant defined by vanishing of [Ext-modules](wikipedia://en/840758)[^o2]

:::

$$\textrm{grade}\,M=\textrm{grade}_R\,M=\inf\left\{i\in\mathbb{N}_0:\textrm{Ext}_R^i(M,R)\neq 0\right\}.$$

For an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) $I\triangleleft R$ the grade is defined via the [quotient ring](wikipedia://en/184635) viewed as a module over $R$

$$\textrm{grade}\,I=\textrm{grade}_R\,I=\textrm{grade}_R\,R/I=\inf\left\{i\in\mathbb{N}_0:\textrm{Ext}_R^i(R/I,R)\neq 0\right\}.$$

The grade is used to define [perfect ideals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_ideal). In general we have the inequality

$$\textrm{grade}_R\,I\leq\textrm{proj}\dim(R/I)$$

where the [projective dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_dimension) is another cohomological invariant.

The grade is tightly related to the [depth](wikipedia://en/9770927), since

$$\textrm{grade}_R\,I=\textrm{depth}_{I}(R).$$

Under the same conditions on $R, I$ and $M$ as above, one also defines the $M$-grade of $I$ as[^o3]

$$\textrm{grade}_M\,I=\inf\left\{i\in\mathbb{N}_0:\textrm{Ext}_R^i(R/I,M)\neq 0\right\}.$$

This notion is tied to the existence of maximal [$M$-sequences](wikipedia://en/505119) contained in $I$ of length $\textrm{grade}_M\,I$.
