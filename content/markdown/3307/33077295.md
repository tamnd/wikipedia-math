# Almgren regularity theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Almgren regularity theorem"}

In [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), a field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Almgren regularity theorem**, proved by (txt), states that the [singular set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_set) of a mass-minimizing surface has [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) at least 2. Almgren's proof of this was 955 pages long. Within the proof many new ideas are introduced, such as monotonicity of a *frequency function* and the use of a *center manifold* to perform a more intricate blow-up procedure.

:::

A streamlined and more accessible proof of Almgren's regularity theorem, following the same ideas as Almgren, was given by [Camillo De Lellis](wikipedia://en/39351243) and [Emanuele Spadaro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emanuele_Spadaro) in a series of three papers.[^o3]
