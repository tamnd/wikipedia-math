# Motoko Kotani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Motoko Kotani"}

*Image: Yōichirō Matsumoto and Motoko Kotani meets Yoshimasa hayashi.jpg.*

Kotani Motoko **Motoko Kotani** (小谷 元子, born 1960) is a Japanese [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969), specializing in discrete geometric analysis and [crystallography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallography), and an academic administrator. She is the executive vice president for research for Tohoku University, the former executive director of [Riken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riken), the former president of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786), and the president-elect of the [International Science Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Science_Council).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kotani graduated from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in 1983. She went to [Tokyo Metropolitan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_University) for graduate study, earning a master's degree in 1985 and completing her doctorate in 1990.

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

She was a lecturer at [Toho University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toho_University) from 1990 to 1997, and an associate professor there from 1997 to 1999, with a term as a postdoctoral researcher in Germany at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) from 1993 to 1994. In 1999, she moved to the Mathematics Institute of [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University). She was named as a distinguished professor there in 2008 and as director of the WPI Research Center, Advanced Institute for Materials Research in 2012. She became executive director of [Riken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riken) from 2017 to 2020, while continuing to hold a position as a researcher at Tohoku. In 2020 she was named executive vice president for research.

## Service {#Service}

Kotani was president of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786) from 2015 to 2016. She became president-elect of the [International Science Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Science_Council) in 2021, for a three-year term beginning in 2024. In 2022 the [Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Foreign_Affairs_%28Japan%29) named her as Science and Technology Co-Advisor to the Minister for Foreign Affairs.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Kotani was the 2005 winner of the [Saruhashi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saruhashi_Prize).
