# Venterus Mandey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Venterus Mandey"}

*Image: Venterus\_Mandey.\_Line\_engraving\_by\_R.\_White,\_1702.\_Wellcome\_V0003812.jpg.* Line engraving by [R, White](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_White_%28engraver%29) (1702)

**Venterus Mandey** (1646–1702), also called **Venturus Mandey** and **Venteri Mandey**, was an English [bricklayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bricklayer) and mathematician.[^o2]

:::
