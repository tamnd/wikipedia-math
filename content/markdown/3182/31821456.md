# Kurt Leichtweiss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kurt Leichtweiss"}

**Kurt Leichtweiß** (March 2, 1927 in [Villingen-Schwenningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villingen-Schwenningen) – June 23, 2013) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [convex](wikipedia://en/4165882) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o3][^o5][^o4]

:::

In 1944, while still in high school Leichtweiß traveled to the [Oberwolfach Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1368669) where his mathematical interests were encouraged.[^o5] He studied at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) and the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich). He was a student of [Emanuel Sperner](wikipedia://en/374404) and [Wilhelm Süss](wikipedia://en/1368716). He was then a lecturer in Freiburg and in 1963 became a professor at [TU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Berlin). From 1970 until his retirement in 1995, he was a professor at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart). In 1995, he received the [Wilhelm Blaschke Medal](wikipedia://en/728998#Legacy).

## Books {#Books}

- (with [Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998)), *Elementary Differential Geometry*, Springer, 1973.
- *Affine geometry of convex bodies*, Wiley, 1998.
- *Convex Geometry*, Springer, 1980.
- *Analytic Geometry, First Course*, Teubner, 1972.
