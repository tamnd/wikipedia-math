# Average Service Availability Index

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Average Service Availability Index"}

The **Average Service Availability Index (ASAI)**[^o2][^o3] is a [reliability index](wikipedia://en/70555846) commonly used by [electric power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electric_power) [utilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utilities). ASAI is calculated as

:::

$$\mbox{ASAI} = \frac{\sum{N_i} \times 8760 - \sum{U_i N_i}}{\sum{N_i} \times 8760}$$

where $N_i$ is the number of customers and $U_i$ is the annual outage time (in hours) for location $i$. ASAI can be represented in relation to [SAIDI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SAIDI) (when the annual SAIDI is given in hours, 8760 is the number of hours in a year)

$$\mbox{ASAI} = 1 - \frac{\mbox{SAIDI} }{8760}$$
