::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 9-cubes"}

In nine-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 9-cube** is a convex [uniform 9-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821726), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [9-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9-cube).

:::

There are 9 rectifications of the 9-cube. The zeroth is the 9-cube itself, and the 8th is the dual [9-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9-orthoplex). Vertices of the rectified 9-cube are located at the edge-centers of the 9-orthoplex. Vertices of the birectified 9-cube are located in the square face centers of the 9-cube. Vertices of the trirectified 9-orthoplex are located in the [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) cell centers of the 9-cube. Vertices of the quadrirectified 9-cube are located in the [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) centers of the 9-cube.

These polytopes are part of a family of 511 [uniform 9-polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821726) with BC<sub>9</sub> symmetry.
