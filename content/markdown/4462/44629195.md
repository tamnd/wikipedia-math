# Klingen Eisenstein series

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Klingen Eisenstein series"}

In mathematics, a **Klingen Eisenstein series** is a [Siegel modular form](wikipedia://en/7721927) of weight *k* and degree *g* depending on another Siegel cusp form *f* of weight *k* and degree *r*\<*g*, given by a series similar to an [Eisenstein series](wikipedia://en/1270458). It is a generalization of the [Siegel Eisenstein series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel_Eisenstein_series), which is the special case when the Siegel cusp form is 1. Klingen Eisenstein series is introduced by (txt).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *f* is a Siegel cusp form of degree *r* and weight *k* with *k* > *g* + *r* + 1 an even integer. The Klingen Eisenstein series is

$$\sum_{\binom{ab}{cd}\in P_r\setminus\Gamma_g} f\left(\frac{a\tau+b}{c\tau+d}\right)\det(c\tau+d)^{-k}.$$

It is a Siegel modular form of weight *k* and degree *g*. Here *P*<sub>*r*</sub> is the integral points of a certain [parabolic subgroup](wikipedia://en/588356) of the [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977), and Γ<sub>*g*</sub> is the group of integral points of the degree *g* symplectic group. The variable τ is in the [Siegel upper half plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Siegel_upper_half_plane) of degree *g*. The function *f* is originally defined only for elements of the Siegel upper half plane of degree *r*, but extended to the Siegel upper half plane of degree *g* by projecting this to the smaller Siegel upper half plane.

The cusp form *f* is the image of the Klingen Eisenstein series under the operator Φ<sup>*g*−*r*</sup>, where Φ is the [Siegel operator](wikipedia://en/44503792).
