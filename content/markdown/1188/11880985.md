# Converse nonimplication

*Image: Venn0010.svg.* [Venn diagram](wikipedia://en/61701) of $P \nleftarrow Q$(the red area is true)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Converse nonimplication"}

In [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), **converse nonimplication**[^o2] is a [logical connective](wikipedia://en/18153) which is the [negation](wikipedia://en/161019) of [converse implication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse_implication) (equivalently, the [negation](wikipedia://en/161019) of the [converse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conversion_%28logic%29) of [implication](wikipedia://en/658808)).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Converse nonimplication is notated $P \nleftarrow Q$, or $P \not \subset Q$, and is logically equivalent to $\neg (P \leftarrow Q)$ and $\neg P \wedge Q$.

### Truth table {#Truth_table}

The [truth table](wikipedia://en/25512250) of $A \nleftarrow B$.[^o6]

0

:::

::: {#o8 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Notation"}

## Notation {#Notation}

Converse nonimplication is notated $p \nleftarrow q$, which is the left arrow from [converse implication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse_implication) ($\leftarrow$), negated with a stroke ($/$).

Alternatives include

- $p \not\subset q$, which combines [converse implication's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse_implication) $\subset$, negated with a stroke ($/$).
- $p \tilde{\leftarrow} q$, which combines [converse implication's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converse_implication) left arrow ($\leftarrow$) with [negation's](wikipedia://en/161019) tilde ($\sim$).
- M*pq*, in [Bocheński notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocheński_notation)

:::

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

**falsehood-preserving**: The interpretation under which all variables are assigned a [truth value](wikipedia://en/161711) of 'false' produces a truth value of 'false' as a result of converse nonimplication

:::

## Natural language {#Natural_language}

### Grammatical {#Grammatical}

Example,

If it rains (P) then I get wet (Q), just because I am wet (Q) does not mean it is raining, in reality I went to a pool party with the co-ed staff, in my clothes (~P) and that is why I am facilitating this lecture in this state (Q).

### Rhetorical {#Rhetorical}

Q does not imply P.

### Colloquial {#Colloquial}

Not P, but Q.

\===Properties=== ====Non-associative==== $r \nleftarrow (q \nleftarrow p) = (r \nleftarrow q) \nleftarrow p$ if and only if $rp = 0$ \#s5 (In a [two-element Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/2584449) the latter condition is reduced to $r = 0$ or $p=0$). Hence in a nontrivial Boolean algebra converse nonimplication is **nonassociative**.

$$\begin{aligned} (r \nleftarrow q) \nleftarrow p &= r'q \nleftarrow p & \text{(by definition)} \\ &= (r'q)'p & \text{(by definition)} \\ &= (r + q')p & \text{(De Morgan's laws)} \\ &= (r + r'q')p & \text{(Absorption law)} \\ &= rp + r'q'p \\ &= rp + r'(q \nleftarrow p) & \text{(by definition)} \\ &= rp + r \nleftarrow (q \nleftarrow p) & \text{(by definition)} \\ \end{aligned}$$

Clearly, it is associative if and only if $rp=0$.

\====Non-commutative====

$q \nleftarrow p=p \nleftarrow q$ if and only if $q = p$ \#s6. Hence converse nonimplication is **noncommutative**.

\====Neutral and absorbing elements====

$0$ is a left [neutral element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_element) ($0 \nleftarrow p=p$) and a right [absorbing element](wikipedia://en/14206224) (${p \nleftarrow 0=0}$).

$1 \nleftarrow p=0$, $p \nleftarrow 1=p'$, and $p \nleftarrow p=0$.

Implication $q \rightarrow p$ is the dual of converse nonimplication $q \nleftarrow p$ \#s7.

## Computer science {#Computer_science}

An example for converse nonimplication in computer science can be found when performing a [right outer join](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Join_%28SQL%29#Right_outer_join) on a set of tables from a [database](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database), if records not matching the join-condition from the "left" table are being excluded.[^o17]
