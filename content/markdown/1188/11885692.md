# Biometrics (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Biometrics"}

***Biometrics*** is a [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) that publishes articles on the [application](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application_software) of statistics and mathematics to the biological sciences.[^o3] It is published by the [International Biometric Society (IBS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society).[^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

Originally published in 1945[^o6] under the title *Biometrics Bulletin*, the journal adopted the shorter title in 1947.[^o7] A notable contributor to the journal was [R.A. Fisher](wikipedia://en/140824), for whom a memorial edition was published in 1964.[^o8] In a survey of statistics researchers' opinions, it was [ranked](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranked) fifth overall among 40 statistics journals, and it was second only to the *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313)* in the ranking provided by [biometrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometrics) specialists.[^o9]
