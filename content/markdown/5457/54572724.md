# Workshop on Numerical Ranges and Numerical Radii

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Workshop on Numerical Ranges and Numerical Radii"}

**Workshop on Numerical Ranges and Numerical Radii (WONRA)** is a biennial workshop series on [numerical ranges](wikipedia://en/15581094) and numerical radii which began in 1992.

:::

## About {#About}

Numerical ranges and numerical radii are useful in the study of matrix and operator theory. These topics have applications in many subjects in pure and applied mathematics, such as [quadratic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_forms), [Banach spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_spaces), [dilation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dilation_theory), [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [quantum information science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_science).[^o3] [^o4] [^o5] [^o6] [^o7] [^o8] [^o9]

## History {#History}

In the early 1970s, numerical range workshops were organized by [Frank Bonsall](wikipedia://en/33763191) and John Duncan. More activities were started in early 1990s, including the biennial workshop series, which began in 1992, and special issues devoted to this workshop were published.[^o11] [^o12] [^o13] [^o14]
