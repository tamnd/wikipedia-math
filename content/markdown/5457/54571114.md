# Interrupted time series

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Interrupted time series"}

**Interrupted time series analysis** (**ITS**), sometimes known as **quasi-experimental time series analysis**, is a method of [statistical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_analysis) involving tracking a long-term period before and after a point of intervention to assess the intervention's effects. The [time series](wikipedia://en/406624) refers to the data over the period, while the interruption is the intervention, which is a controlled external influence or set of influences.[^o2][^o3][^o4] Effects of the intervention are evaluated by changes in the level and slope of the time series and [statistical significance](wikipedia://en/160995) of the intervention parameters.[^o5] **Interrupted time series design** is the [design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541) based on the interrupted time series approach.

:::

The method is used in various areas of research, such as:

- [political science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_science): impact of changes in laws on the behavior of people;[^o3] (e.g., [Effectiveness of sex offender registration policies in the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Effectiveness_of_sex_offender_registration_policies_in_the_United_States#Interrupted_time_series_analysis_studies))
- [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics): impact of changes in credit controls on borrowing behavior;[^o3]
- [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology): impact of experiments in income maintenance on the behavior of participants in [welfare programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welfare_program);[^o3]
- [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History): impact of major historical events on the behavior of those affected by the events;[^o3]
- [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology): impact of expressing emotional experiences on online content;[^o6]
- [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine): in medical research, [medical treatment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_treatment) is an intervention whose effect are to be studied;
- [marketing research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marketing_research): to analyze the effect of "designed market interventions" (e.g., [advertising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advertising)) on sales.[^o7]
- [environmental sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_sciences): impacts of human activities on environmental quality and ecosystem dynamics (e.g., forest logging on local climate).[^o8][^o9]
