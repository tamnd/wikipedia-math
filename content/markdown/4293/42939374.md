# Freudenthal algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Freudenthal algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), **Freudenthal algebras** are certain [Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371) constructed from [composition algebras](wikipedia://en/3861635).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *C* is a composition algebra over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *F* and *a* is a [diagonal matrix](wikipedia://en/174080) in GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*F*). A reduced Freudenthal algebra is defined to be a Jordan algebra equal to the set of 3 by 3 matrices *X* over *C* such that $\overline{X}$<sup>T</sup>*a*=*aX*. A Freudenthal algebra is any twisted form of a reduced Freudental algebra.

:::
