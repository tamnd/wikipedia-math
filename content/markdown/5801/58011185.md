# Vadim Schechtman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vadim Schechtman"}

**Vadim V. Schechtman** (Вадим Шехтман, born 8 June 1954) is a Russian mathematician who teaches in [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse).[^o3]

:::

Schechtman received in 1979 from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) his doctorate under the supervision of [Evgeny Golod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgeny_Golod). Schechtman was an academic at Moscow State University in the 1980s and at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) in the 1990s. He is a now a professor at [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sabatier_University) (Toulouse III).

His research deals with algebraic geometry and quantum groups as well as with mathematical physics.

He has collaborated with [Alexander Varchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Varchenko) and [Alexander Beilinson](wikipedia://en/10230613), among others.

Schechtman was an Invited Speaker with talk *Sur les algèbres vertex attachées aux variétés algébriques* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing in 2002.[^o5]
