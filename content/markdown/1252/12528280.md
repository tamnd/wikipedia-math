# Transferable utility

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Transferable utility"}

**Transferable utility** is a concept in [cooperative game theory](wikipedia://en/657958) and in [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics). [Utility](wikipedia://en/45479) is *transferable* if one player can losslessly transfer part of its utility to another player. Such transfers are possible if the players have a common [currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currency) that is valued equally by all. Note that being able to transfer [cash](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cash) [payoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payoff_matrix) does not imply that utility is transferable: wealthy and poor players may derive a different utility from the same amount of money.

:::

Transferable utility is assumed in many cooperative games, where the [payoffs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Payoff_matrix) are not given for individual players, but only for [coalitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coalition). In this case the assumption implies that irrespective of the division of the coalitional payoff, members of the coalition enjoy the same total utility.
