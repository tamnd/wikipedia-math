# Hans Fitting

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Fitting"}

**Hans Fitting** (13 November 1906 in München-Gladbach (now [Mönchengladbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mönchengladbach)) – 15 June 1938 in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg) (now [Kaliningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaliningrad))) was a mathematician who worked in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). He proved [Fitting's theorem](wikipedia://en/4081611) and [Fitting's lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitting's_lemma), and defined the [Fitting subgroup](wikipedia://en/1416042) in [finite group theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_group_theory) and the [Fitting decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fitting_decomposition) for [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) and [Fitting ideals](wikipedia://en/28034758) in [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424).

:::

After finishing his [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate) work in 1931, he wrote his [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) with the help of [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896), who helped him secure a [grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grant_%28money%29) from the *Notgemeinschaft der Deutschen Wissenschaften* (Emergency Society for German Sciences). He died at the age of 31 from a sudden bone disease.[^o2]
