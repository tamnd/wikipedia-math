# Venezuelan Mathematical Association

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Venezuelan Mathematical Association"}

The **Venezuelan Mathematical Association** (Spanish: Asociación Matemática Venezolana, **AMV**) is a [mathematical society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_society) founded in [Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela) in January 1990. The AMV has chapters in four Venezuelan cities, and is a member of the Unión Matemática de América Latina y el Caribe. It is recognised by the [International Mathematics Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematics_Union).

:::

## History {#History}

The precursor to the AMV was the Venezuelan Mathematical Society (Spanish: Sociedad Venezolana de Matemáticas), which was founded at the Third Venezuelan Congress of Mathematicians, held in October 1980 at the [University of Zulia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zulia) in [Maracaibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maracaibo). The society functioned poorly and soon after effectively ceased to exist.

Following several failed attempts at reactivating the society, the Venezuelan Mathematical Association was founded in January 1990. Within a year the AMV had branches in [Caracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracas), [Maracaibo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maracaibo), [Mérida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mérida,_Mérida), and [Barquisimeto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barquisimeto).

Since the mid 2010s the AMV has had difficulty fulfulling its duties, due to the ongoing [crisis in Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crisis_in_Venezuela). The AMV has been unable to physically publish its Bulletin since 2013, and struggles to pay membership fees to the [IMU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematics_Union). The crisis has also led many mathematicians to leave the country.

## Activities {#Activities}

The AMV organises the Venezuelan Mathematics Seminars (Spanish: Jornadas Matemáticas Venezolanas), the first of which were held at the First Venezuelan Congress of Mathematicians in [Mérida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mérida,_Mérida) in 1977. In May 2024 the 33rd Jornadas were held as a [hybrid event](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hybrid_event) in Caracas. The AMV also organises events to celebrate the [International Day of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Day_of_Mathematics).

## Publications {#Publications}

The AMV publishes the Bulletin of the Venezuelan Mathematical Association (Spanish: Boletín de la AMV), which is the preeminent Venezuelan mathematics journal. The journal is indexed by [zbMATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZbMATH).

## Governance {#Governance}

Each regional chapter of the AMV has one representative on the board of directors. As of 2024, the directors are:

- Caracas: Luis Angel Rodríguez (President of the AMV)
- Barquisimeto: Neptalí Romero
- Mérida: Oswaldo Araujo
- Maracaibo: Tobías Rosas

The regional chapters have autonomy to manage their own budget and activities.
