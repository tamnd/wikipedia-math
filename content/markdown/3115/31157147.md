# Demographics of Aguascalientes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Aguascalientes"}

The demographics of Aguascalientes are analysed and collected by the [National Institute of Statistics and Geography (INEG)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Statistics_and_Geography). As of 2010, the population of Aguascalientes is overwhelmingly ethnically [Mexican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexicans) and [Roman Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church). The average life expectancy in 2011 was 75.8, which is slightly above the national average. The city's population is steadily increasing with a growth rate of 2.2%

:::
