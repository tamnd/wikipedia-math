# Names of small numbers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Names of small numbers"}

This is a list of the names of small [decimal numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decimal_number) in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language).

:::
