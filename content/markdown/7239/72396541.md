# Antilimit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Antilimit"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **antilimit** is the equivalent of a [limit](wikipedia://en/26551602) for a [divergent series](wikipedia://en/876428). The concept is not necessarily unique or well-defined, but the general idea is to find a formula for a series and then evaluate it outside its [radius of convergence](wikipedia://en/61476).

:::
