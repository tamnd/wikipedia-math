# Peter Landweber

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Landweber"}

**Peter Steven Landweber** (born August 17, 1940, in Washington D. C.)[^o2] is an American mathematician working in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

Landweber studied at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) (B.S. 1960) and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (master's degree 1961), where he graduated in 1965 after studying under [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457) ([Künneth formulas](wikipedia://en/918047) for [bordism](wikipedia://en/391832) theories). He was then Assistant Professor at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) (from 1965) and at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) from 1968 to 1970. From 1967 to 1968 he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey). In 1970, he became [Associate Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), where he taught from 1974 until his retirement in 2007. From 1974 to 1975 he was a NATO fellow at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). Since 2007, he is a Professor Emeritus at Rutgers University.[^o4]

Landweber studied [complex bordism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_bordism) in algebraic topology (introducing [Landweber–Novikov algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landweber–Novikov_algebra) in the 1960s[^o5]). In the beginning of the 1970s, he proved his [exact functor theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exact_functor_theorem), which allows the construction of a [homology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_theory) from a [formal group law](wikipedia://en/480335).[^o6] In 1986 he introduced [elliptic cohomology](wikipedia://en/10295395) with [Douglas C. Ravenel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Douglas_C._Ravenel) and [Robert E. Stong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_E._Stong), which is a [generalized cohomology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_cohomology_theory) with applications to [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) and [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225).[^o7] From 1989 to 1992 he was Chairman of the Russian translation Committee of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). He is also a fellow of the society.[^o8]

Peter Landweber is the elder son of the engineer [Louis Landweber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Landweber) and the father of the molecular biologist [Laura Faye Landweber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Landweber)[^o9] (born 1967) and of the mathematician Gregory David Landweber (born 1971).
