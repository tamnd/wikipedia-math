# Procesi bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Procesi bundle"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Procesi bundles** are [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) of rank $n!$ on certain [symplectic resolutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_resolution) of quotient singularities, particularly on the [Hilbert scheme](wikipedia://en/6612581) of $n$ points in the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628).[^o10] They play a fundamental role in [geometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric) [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and were crucial in [Mark Haiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Haiman)'s proof of the [n! theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N!_theorem) and [Macdonald positivity conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald_positivity_conjecture), and were named after [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Claudio Procesi](wikipedia://en/35219117).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $H_n = \text{Hilb}^n(\mathbb{C}^2)$ denote the [Hilbert scheme](wikipedia://en/6612581) of n points in the complex plane $\mathbb{C}^2$, which provides a [resolution of singularities](wikipedia://en/9914115) of the [quotient](wikipedia://en/11526) $(\mathbb{C}^2)^n/S_n$, where $S_n$ is the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) of degree $n$. A Procesi bundle $\mathcal{P}$ on $H_n$ is a $\mathbb{C}^{\times}$-equivariant vector bundle of rank $n!$ together with an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) $\text{End}(\mathcal{P}) \cong \mathbb{C}[x,y]\#S_n$ (where $\mathbb{C}[x,y]\#S_n$ is the [smash product algebra](wikipedia://en/12842125#Hopf_module_algebras_and_the_Hopf_smash_product)) of $\mathbb{C}[V]^{S_n}$-algebras, such that $\text{Ext}^i(\mathcal{P},\mathcal{P}) = 0$ for all $i > 0$. The isomorphism ensures that each [fiber](wikipedia://en/6603892) of $\mathcal{P}$ is naturally the [regular representation](wikipedia://en/360011) of $S_n$.[^o10]

More generally, for a [finite subgroup](wikipedia://en/323707) $\Gamma \subset \text{SL}_2(\mathbb{C})$ and its [wreath product](wikipedia://en/146625) $\Gamma_n = S_n \ltimes \Gamma^n$, Procesi bundles can be defined on symplectic resolutions of $\mathbb{C}^{2n}/\Gamma_n$.[^o10]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Procesi bundles provide a derived [McKay equivalence](wikipedia://en/34080241) between the derived [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of [coherent sheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coherent_sheaves) on $H_n$ and the derived category of $S_n$-equivariant [modules](wikipedia://en/276410) over $\mathbb{C}[x,y]$. For one distinguished Procesi bundle $\mathcal{P}$, the $\Gamma_{n-1}$-invariants $\mathcal{P}^{S_{n-1}}$ coincide with the [tautological bundle](wikipedia://en/2913502) on $H_n$. On any symplectic resolution of $\mathbb{C}^{2n}/\Gamma_n$, there are exactly two normalized (meaning $\mathcal{P}^{S_n} \cong \mathcal{O}$) Procesi bundles which are [dual](wikipedia://en/609737) to each other.[^o11]

:::

## History and constructions {#History_and_constructions}

The first construction of a Procesi bundle was given by [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) mathematician [Mark Haiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Haiman) in his proof of the [n! theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N!_theorem), using intricate combinatorial methods.[^o7] Alternative constructions were later developed by [Roman Bezrukavnikov](wikipedia://en/24842390) and Dmitry Kaledin using [quantization](wikipedia://en/317018) in [positive characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_characteristic),[^o8] and by [Victor Ginzburg](wikipedia://en/14995490) using [*D*-modules](wikipedia://en/3440755) and the [Hotta-Kashiwara construction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotta-Kashiwara_construction).[^o9]

Belarusian-American mathematician [Ivan Losev](wikipedia://en/64862110) provided significant further developments in the theory of Procesi bundles, including a complete classification of Procesi bundles on [Hamiltonian reductions](wikipedia://en/3701344),[^o10] and an [inductive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Induction_%28mathematics%29) construction showing how Procesi bundles relate to nested [Hilbert schemes](wikipedia://en/6612581).[^o11] His work established that there are exactly two normalized Procesi bundles on any given symplectic resolution obtained by Hamiltonian reduction.

As a result of their use in the proof of the [n! theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N!_theorem), Procesi bundles have also found important applications in the proof of the [Macdonald positivity conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald_positivity_conjecture),[^o7] the study of [rational](wikipedia://en/865939) [Cherednik algebras](wikipedia://en/32034345) and their representations, and understanding derived equivalences for symplectic quotient singularities.[^o8][^o12]
