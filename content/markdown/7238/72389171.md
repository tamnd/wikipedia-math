# Eliane R. Rodrigues

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eliane R. Rodrigues"}

**Eliane Regina Rodrigues** is a Brazilian applied mathematician and statistician who works in Mexico as a researcher at the Institute of Mathematics of the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM). Her research involves using [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) including [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876) and [Poisson point processes](wikipedia://en/41597450) to model phenomena such as [air pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_pollution), [noise pollution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noise_pollution), the health effects of [fat taxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fat_tax), and the effectiveness of [vaccination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vaccination).

:::

## Education {#Education}

After undergraduate study in mathematics at [São Paulo State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Paulo_State_University), Rodrigues earned a master's degree in probability theory from the [University of Brasília](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brasília), both in Brazil. She completed a PhD in applied probability from [Queen Mary and Westfield College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary_and_Westfield_College) (now [Queen Mary University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary_University_of_London)) in England.

## Book {#Book}

Rodrigues is the coauthor, with Brazilian mathematician Jorge Alberto Achcar, of the book *Applications of Discrete-time Markov Chains and Poisson Processes to Air Pollution Modeling and Studies* (Springer Briefs in Mathematics, 2013).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Rodrigues is a member of the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences), and an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).
