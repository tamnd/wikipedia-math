# David Catlin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Catlin"}

*Image: Catlin david.jpg.* David Catlin, Oberwolfach 2004

**David William Catlin** (born 12 May 1952 [Rochester, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester,_Pennsylvania)) is an American mathematician who works on the theory of [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables).

:::

Catlin received in 1978 his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under [Joseph Kohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Kohn) with thesis *Boundary Behavior of Holomorphic Functions on Weakly Pseudoconvex Domains*.[^o3] He is a professor at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).

He solved a boundary behavior problem of complex analysis in several variables, on which his teacher Kohn worked in detail and which was originally formulated by [Donald Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_C._Spencer), a particular case of the [Neumann problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neumann_problem) for $\overline{\partial}$, a non-elliptic boundary value problem.[^o4][^o5]

Catlin was an Invited Speaker with talk *Regularity of solutions of the $\overline{\partial}$-Neumann problem* at the [ICM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematical_Congress) in 1986 in Berkeley. In 1989 he received the inaugural [Stefan Bergman Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Bergman_Prize).

His brother [Paul Allen Catlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Allen_Catlin) (1948–1995) also achieved fame as a mathematician, doing research on [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).
