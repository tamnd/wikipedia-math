# Stefan number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefan number"}

The **Stefan number** (**St** or **Ste**) is defined as the ratio of [sensible heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensible_heat) to [latent heat](wikipedia://en/164610). It is given by the formula

:::

$$\mathrm{Ste} = \frac{c_p \Delta T}{L},$$

where

- *c<sub>p</sub>* is the [specific heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_heat),
  - c<sub>p</sub> is the [specific heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_heat) of solid phase in the [freezing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freezing) process while c<sub>p</sub> is the [specific heat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Specific_heat) of liquid phase in the [melting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melting) process.
- ∆*T* is the [temperature](wikipedia://en/20647050) difference between phases,
- *L* is the latent heat of melting.

It is a [dimensionless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimensionless) parameter that is useful in analyzing a [Stefan problem](wikipedia://en/679186). The parameter was developed from [Josef Stefan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Stefan)'s calculations of the rate of [phase change](wikipedia://en/54423) of [water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water) into [ice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice) on the [polar ice caps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_ice_caps) and coined by G.S.H. Lock in 1969.[^o3] The problems origination is fully described by Vuik[^o4] and further commentary on its place in [Josef Stefan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Stefan)'s larger career can be found in [^o5] Same number exist for vapor to liquid phase change, [Nombre de Jakob](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nombre_de_Jakob).
