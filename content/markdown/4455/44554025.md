# Hiroshi Saito (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hiroshi Saito"}

**Hiroshi Saito** was a Japanese mathematician at the [Division of Mathematics and Mathematical Sciences, Graduate School of Science, Kyoto University](https://www.sci.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/divisions/math) who worked on [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146).[^o2] He introduced the [base change lifting](wikipedia://en/32338538) and the [Saito–Kurokawa lift](wikipedia://en/44536593).

:::
