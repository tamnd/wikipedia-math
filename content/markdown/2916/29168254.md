# Partial group algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Partial group algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **partial group algebra** is an [associative algebra](wikipedia://en/2112) related to the [partial representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_representation) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447).

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The partial group algebra $\mathbb{C}_{\text{par}}(\mathbb{Z}_4)$ is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to the direct sum:[^o4]

$$\mathbb{C}\oplus\mathbb{C}\oplus\mathbb{C}\oplus\mathbb{C}\oplus\mathbb{C}\oplus\mathbb{C}\oplus\mathbb{C}\oplus \mathrm{M}_2 \mathbb{C} \oplus \mathrm{M}_3 \mathbb{C}$$

:::
