# Nancy Spruill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nancy Spruill"}

**Nancy Lyon Spruill** is a retired American statistician and defense acquisitions analyst, formerly the director of Acquisition Resources and Analysis in the [United States Department of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Department_of_Defense). Within the Department of Defense, she is known for the "Spruill chart", a [spreadsheet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spreadsheet) for estimating the costs of weapons programs that she developed, and for threatening to sue the [United States Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Navy) in 1979 because it would not allow her, as a woman, to stay aboard one of its aircraft carriers.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Spruill is the daughter and granddaughter of federal workers. She is a 1971 graduate of the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), majoring in mathematics, and on graduating became a research assistant in the [Center for Naval Analyses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Naval_Analyses). She earned a master's degree in 1975, and completed a Ph.D. in 1980 at [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University), with the dissertation *Two statistical problems involving the bivariate normal distribution arising from legal issues*. In 1983 she moved to the office of the Secretary of Defense, eventually becoming a senior [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) analyst there. In 1993 she moved to the [Defense Mapping Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defense_Mapping_Agency), and in 1999 she returned to the office of the secretary as director of acquisition resources and analysis. She retired in 2018.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2003, she was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association). The American Statistical Association Section on Statistics in Defense and National Security gave her their Distinguished Achievement Award in 2006. She is also a recipient of the [Department of Defense Distinguished Civilian Service Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Defense_Distinguished_Civilian_Service_Award), the [Secretary of Defense Exceptional Civilian Service Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Secretary_of_Defense_Exceptional_Civilian_Service_Award), the Hammer Award, the David D. Acker "Skill in Communication" Award, and the [Presidential Rank Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidential_Rank_Awards).
