# Nikolaus Hautsch

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolaus Hautsch"}

**Nikolaus Hautsch** (born 1972) is a German [economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).[^o9] Since 2013, he has been [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finance) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna)[^o3] and since 2023, he has been serving as vice rector for infrastructure at the University of Vienna.[^o4] He is known for his work in [financial econometrics](wikipedia://en/1626747), the statistical analysis of financial [high frequency data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_frequency_data) and market microstructure analysis.[^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Hautsch was born on March 9, 1972, in [Singen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singen) ([Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany))[^o9] and grew up in [Radolfzell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radolfzell).[^o7]

He graduated with a diploma in [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) from the [University of Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz) in 1998. He earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390) in 2003 from the University of Konstanz.[^o3]

From 2004 to 2007, he was [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) and [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen)'s Department of Economics.[^o3][^o8] From 2007 to 2013 he held the Chair of Econometrics at [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin). In 2013, he joined the University of Vienna as professor of finance and statistics.[^o9]

Hautsch had visiting positions at the [University of Technology Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Technology_Sydney), the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne), the [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain), the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University), among others.[^o9]

He is elected [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the Society for Financial Econometrics,[^o9] [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) of the [Center for Financial Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_for_Financial_Studies) (CFS), [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt),[^o10] faculty member of the Vienna Graduate School of Finance,[^o11] and member of the research platform "Data Science" at the University of Vienna.[^o12]
