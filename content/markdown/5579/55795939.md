# Shelley Hurwitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shelley Hurwitz"}

**Shelley Hurwitz** is an American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878). She is the Director of Biostatistics in the Center for Clinical Investigation at [Brigham and Women's Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigham_and_Women's_Hospital), and an associate professor in the [Harvard Medical School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Medical_School).

:::

## Ethics {#Ethics}

As well as biostatistics, Hurwitz maintains an interest in [professional ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_ethics) for statisticians. She chaired the Committee on Professional Ethics of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) from 2010 to 2012, chaired the Committee on Ethical Practice of Clinical and Translational Biostatistics of the [CTSA Consortium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_and_Translational_Science_Award) from 2012 to 2014, and belongs to the Advisory Board on Ethics of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hurwitz has two master's degrees: an M.A. in psychology from [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University), earned in 1977, and an M.S. in statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), earned in 1981. She completed her Ph.D. in psychology in 1992 at [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University). Her dissertation was *Judgments of Correlation: Level of Measurement and Co-occurrence Bias*. She worked as a statistician at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) from 1981 to 1992, when she moved to the [Harvard School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_School_of_Public_Health). In 1997, she moved again, to Brigham and Women's Hospital, and at the same time took a teaching position in the [Harvard Medical School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Medical_School). She became a regular-rank faculty member at Harvard in 2005.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2011, Hurwitz was elected to the International Statistical Institute. In 2014, she became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).
