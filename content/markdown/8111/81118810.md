# An Introduction to Manifolds

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="An Introduction to Manifolds"}

***An Introduction to Manifolds*** is a mathematics textbook written by [Loring W. Tu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loring_W._Tu) providing an introduction to differential and integral calculus on [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold).[^o2] It was first published in 2008 by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) and received positive reviews.[^o3][^o4] The book is meant as a precursor to *Differential Forms in Algebraic Topology* by [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457) and Tu.[^o5][^o6]

:::
