# Wei-Yin Loh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wei-Yin Loh"}

**Wei-Yin Loh** is a Singaporean statistician.

:::

Loh obtained his Bachelor of Science in mathematics with first-class honours at the [University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880) in 1974. He remained at the University of Singapore until 1977 to complete a Master of Science in the same subject, then enrolled at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) for a PhD in statistics.[^o2] Loh completed his doctoral dissertation, "Tail-orderings on Symmetric Distributions with Statistical Applications", under the supervision of [Erich Leo Lehmann](wikipedia://en/16388476) in 1982.[^o3]

Loh is a professor of statistics at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).[^o5] He was elected a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1991,[^o2][^o6] and to an equivalent honour by the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1998.[^o2][^o7]

Loh is the father of [Po-Shen Loh](wikipedia://en/56127111), a mathematician at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) and coach of the US [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) team, [Po-Ling Loh](wikipedia://en/81227908), a statistician at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and Po-Ru Loh, an associate professor of medicine at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). His brother, Wei-Liem Loh, is a statistician at the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880).[^o8]
