# Heptagrammic-order heptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heptagrammic-order heptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **heptagrammic-order heptagonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) star-tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {7,7/2}. The vertex figure [heptagrams](wikipedia://en/180673) are {7/2},

*Image: Obtuse heptagram.svg.*

. The heptagonal faces overlap with [density](wikipedia://en/34257549) 3.

:::

## Related tilings {#Related_tilings}

It has the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as the regular [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365), {3,7}. The full set of edges coincide with the edges of a [heptakis heptagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heptakis_heptagonal_tiling).

*Image: Order-7 triangular tiling.svg.*

*Image: Heptakis heptagonal tiling.svg.*

It is related to a [Kepler-Poinsot polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-Poinsot_polyhedron), the [great dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2860441), {5,5/2}, which is polyhedron and a [density](wikipedia://en/34257549)-3 regular star-tiling on the sphere (resembling a regular icosahedron in this state, similarly to this tessellation resembling the order-7 triangular tiling):

*Image: Great dodecahedron tiling.svg.*
