# Peter B. Gilkey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter B. Gilkey"}

**Peter Belden Gilkey** (born February 27, 1946, in Utica, New York)[^o2] is an American mathematician, working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [global analysis](wikipedia://en/42027498).

:::

Gilkey graduated from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) with a master 's degree in 1967 and received a doctoral degree in 1972 from the Harvard University under the supervision of [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725) (*Curvature and the Eigenvalues of the Laplacian for Geometrical Elliptic Complexes*).[^o3] From 1971 to 1972 he was an instructor in computer science at the [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) and from 1972 to 1974 was a lecturer at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). From 1974 to 1980 he was assistant professor at Princeton University, he spent one year at U.S.C., and in 1981 he became associate professor and in 1985 professor at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon).

He wrote a textbook on the [Atiyah–Singer index theorem](wikipedia://en/324752). In 1975 he was [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellow). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]

Gilkey retired from the University of Oregon in June 2021 and is now a Professor Emeritus.
