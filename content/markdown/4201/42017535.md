# Graham Everest

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Graham Everest"}

**Graham Robert Everest** (14 December 1957 in [Southwick, West Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwick,_West_Sussex) – 30 July 2010) was a British mathematician working on [arithmetic dynamics](wikipedia://en/17584701) and [recursive equations](wikipedia://en/146806) in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Everest studied at Bedford College (now [Royal Holloway College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Holloway_College)) of the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) where he completed a Ph.D. in 1983 under the supervision of [Colin J. Bushnell](wikipedia://en/41171782) of [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) (*The distribution of normal integral generators in tame extensions of Q.*)[^o3] He joined the faculty of the [University of East Anglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_East_Anglia) in 1983 as a lecturer and spent his academic career there.

He was ordained a priest in the [Church of England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Church_of_England) in 2006. He died of prostate cancer on 30 July 2010, leaving behind his wife and three children.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1983 he became a member of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097). In 2012 he was awarded the [Lester Randolph Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_Randolph_Ford_Award) jointly with [Thomas Ward](wikipedia://en/59433452) for their work in [diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109).[^o8]

- With [Alf van der Poorten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alf_van_der_Poorten), [Thomas Ward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Ward_%28Mathematician%29), and [Igor Shparlinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Igor_Shparlinski): *[Recurrence sequences](https://bookstore.ams.org/surv-104)*, [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) 2003.[^o11]
