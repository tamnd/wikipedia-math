# Order-7 heptagrammic tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-7 heptagrammic tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-7 heptagrammic tiling** is a tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291) by overlapping [heptagrams](wikipedia://en/180673).

:::

## Description {#Description}

This tiling is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) star-tiling, and has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {7/2,7}. The heptagrams forming the tiling are of type {7/2},

*Image: Obtuse heptagram.svg.*

. The overlapping heptagrams subdivide the hyperbolic plane into isosceles triangles, 14 of which form each heptagram.

Each point of the hyperbolic plane that does not lie on a heptagram edge belongs to the central heptagon of one heptagram, and is in one of the points of exactly one other heptagram. The [winding number](wikipedia://en/86113) of each heptagram around its points is one, and the winding number around the central heptagon is two, so adding these two numbers together, each point of the plane is surrounded three times; that is, the [density](wikipedia://en/34257549) of the tiling is 3.

In the Euclidean plane, a heptagram of type {7/2} would have angles of 3$\pi$/7 at its vertices, but in the hyperbolic plane heptagrams can have the sharper vertex angle 2$\pi$/7 that is needed to make exactly seven other heptagrams meet up at the center of each heptagram of the tiling.

## Related tilings {#Related_tilings}

It has the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as the regular [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365), {3,7}. The full set of edges coincide with the edges of a [heptakis heptagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heptakis_heptagonal_tiling). The valance 6 vertices in this tiling are false-vertices in the [heptagrammic](wikipedia://en/180673) one caused by crossed edges.

*Image: Heptakis heptagonal tiling.svg.*

It is related to a [Kepler-Poinsot polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-Poinsot_polyhedron), the [small stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029843), {5/2,5}, which is polyhedron and a [density](wikipedia://en/34257549)-3 regular star-tiling on the sphere:
