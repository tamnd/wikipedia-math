# Number Theory Foundation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Number Theory Foundation"}

Organizations

The **Number Theory Foundation** (**NTF**) is a [non-profit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-profit) organization based in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) which supports research and conferences in the field of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), with a particular focus on computational aspects and explicit methods.

:::

The NTF funds the [Selfridge prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selfridge_prize) awarded at each [Algorithmic Number Theory Symposium (ANTS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANTS_%28conference%29) and is a regular supporter of several conferences and organizations in number theory, including the [Canadian Number Theory Association (CNTA)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Number_Theory_Association), Women in Numbers (WIN), and the [West Coast Number Theory (WCNT)](wikipedia://en/37083491) conference.

## History {#History}

The NTF was created in 1999 via a grant from [John Selfridge](wikipedia://en/995084) with an initial board of directors including [Paul Bateman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bateman), [John Brillhart](wikipedia://en/11176492), Richard Blecksmith, [Brian Conrey](wikipedia://en/15375152), [Ronald Graham](wikipedia://en/240694), [Richard Guy](wikipedia://en/816308), [Carl Pomerance](wikipedia://en/2134159), John Selfridge, [Sam Wagstaff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_S._Wagstaff_Jr.), and [Hugh Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_C._Williams). Carl Pomerance served as President of the foundation for its first two decades and was succeeded by [Andrew Sutherland](wikipedia://en/63111112) in 2019.
