# Quantitative analysis of behavior

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantitative analysis of behavior"}

**Quantitative analysis of behavior** is the application of mathematical models to the [experimental analysis of behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_analysis_of_behavior). Its aim is to describe and/or predict relations between varying levels of independent environmental variables and dependent behavioral variables. The field was founded by [Richard Herrnstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Herrnstein) in 1961 when he introduced the [matching law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matching_law) to quantify the behavior of organisms working on concurrent schedules of reinforcement.

:::

The field has integrated models from [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [zoology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoology), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), [political science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_science) (including [voter behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voter_behavior)) and psychology, especially [mathematical psychology](wikipedia://en/2081516) of which it is a branch. The field is represented by the [Society for Quantitative Analysis of Behavior](wikipedia://en/12846728). Quantitative analysis of behavior addresses the following topics among others: [behavioral economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_economics), [behavioral momentum](wikipedia://en/12728577), [connectionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connectionist) systems or [neural networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_networks), [integration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_integration), [hyperbolic discounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_discounting) including the [delay reduction hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delay_reduction_hypothesis), [foraging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foraging), [hunting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hunting), [errorless learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Errorless_learning), [creativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creativity), [learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning), and the [Rescorla-Wagner model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rescorla-Wagner_model), matching law, [melioration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melioration_theory), [scalar expectancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scalar_expectancy), [signal detection](wikipedia://en/1156527), neural [hysteresis](wikipedia://en/147003), and [reinforcement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reinforcement) control.
