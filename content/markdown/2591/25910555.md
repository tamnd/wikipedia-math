# Demonic composition

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demonic composition"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **demonic composition** is an operation on [binary relations](wikipedia://en/3931) that is similar to the ordinary [composition of relations](wikipedia://en/7472170) but is robust to refinement of the relations into ([partial](wikipedia://en/23577)) functions or [injective relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_relation).

:::

Unlike ordinary composition of relations, demonic composition is not [associative](wikipedia://en/1335).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose $R$ is a [binary relation](wikipedia://en/3931) between $X$ and $Y$ and $S$ is a relation between $Y$ and $Z.$ Their **right demonic composition** $R \textbf{;}^{\to} S$ is a relation between $X$ and $Z.$ Its graph is defined as

$$\{(x, z) \ : \ x \mathrel{(S \circ R)} z \text{ and for all } y \in Y \ (x \mathrel{R} y \text{ implies } y \mathrel{S} z)\}.$$

Conversely, their **left demonic composition** $R \textbf{;}^{\leftarrow} S$ is defined by

$$\{(x, z) \ : \ x \mathrel{(S \circ R)} z \text{ and for all } y \in Y \ (y \mathrel{S} z \text{ implies } x \mathrel{R} y)\}.$$

:::
