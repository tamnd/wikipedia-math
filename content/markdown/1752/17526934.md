# Christoph Zenger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christoph Zenger"}

*Image: Christoph Zenger 1.jpeg.* Christoph Zenger

**Christoph Zenger** (born 10 August 1940) is a German mathematician.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Born in [Lindau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lindau), Zenger studied physics at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) and did a doctorate in mathematics (theory of normed vector spaces) in 1967. In 1973, he did his habilitation in mathematics and in 1977, he became professor of mathematics at [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) (TUM). In 1980, he accepted an offer of a full professorship from the [University of the Bundeswehr Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Bundeswehr_Munich). In 1982, he returned to TUM to fill a chair in computer science. In 2000, Zenger was selected as a member of the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o3] He retired in 2005, but is still active in research.

The most important of Zenger's scientific achievements is the discovery of how to harness approximation on [sparse grids](wikipedia://en/646088) for the efficient solution of [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/550137) in higher dimensions.[^o3]

Beside his substantial scientific work, Zenger will be remembered for his contributions to contemporary wisdom, the most notable of which have been collected in the volume *Löwenzähnchen am Bachesrand*.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}
