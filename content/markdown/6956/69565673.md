# John Etnyre

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Etnyre"}

**John Boyd Etnyre** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology),[^o2] and his research fields include [contact geometry](wikipedia://en/476398), [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774). He earned his Ph.D. in 1996 from the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), Austin under the supervision of [Robert Gompf](wikipedia://en/42024874).[^o3] Etnyre was a postdoctoral scholar at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) from 1997 to 2001.[^o4] He was a faculty member at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) prior to joining the faculty at the Georgia Institute of Technology.[^o4]

:::

In 2013, Etnyre was in the Inaugural Class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5] He received a National Science Foundation CAREER grant award in 2003,[^o6] and in 2015-2016, he was a [Simons Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Fellow) in Mathematics.[^o7] Etnyre serves as a Principal Editor for the journal of [Algebraic & Geometric Topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_&_Geometric_Topology).[^o8]
