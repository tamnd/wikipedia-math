# Emma Zhang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emma Zhang"}

**Emma Jingfei Zhang** is a Chinese-American statistician whose research analyzes the statistics of [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_system) including [networks](wikipedia://en/1704711), [tensors](wikipedia://en/29965), and [point processes](wikipedia://en/2147685), and their applications in understanding [social networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_network), [gene interactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_interaction_network), and the [brain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain). She is Goizueta Foundation Term Associate Professor of Information Systems & Operations Management at [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Zhang studied mathematics at [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University), graduating in 2009. She completed her Ph.D. in statistics in 2014 at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign). Her dissertation, *Statistical Inference on Network Data*, was supervised by [Yuguo Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuguo_Chen).

She joined the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami) as an assistant professor of [management science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_science) in 2014, was promoted to associate professor in 2020, and added a secondary appointment as associate professor of public health sciences in Miami's Miller School of Medicine in 2022. In 2023 she moved to the Department of Information Systems & Operations Management in the [Goizueta Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goizueta_Business_School) of Emory University, with a secondary appointment in the Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics of the [Rollins School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollins_School_of_Public_Health).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Zhang became an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2023. In 2025 she was named as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).
