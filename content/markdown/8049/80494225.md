# Alejandro C. Frery

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alejandro C. Frery"}

**Alejandro C. Frery** (born 21 February 1960) is an Argentine-born [computational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing) expert. He is a full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of Statistics and Data Science at the School of Mathematics and Statistics, [Victoria University of Wellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Wellington), New Zealand. His research focuses on statistical computing, stochastic modeling, and the analysis of [synthetic aperture radar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthetic_aperture_radar) (SAR) and polarimetric SAR (PolSAR) imagery.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Frery was born in Mendoza, Argentina, in 1960. He received a B.Sc. in Electronic and Electrical Engineering from [Universidad de Mendoza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_de_Mendoza) in 1983. M.Sc. degree in [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) ([Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685)) from the [Instituto de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_de_Matemática_Pura_e_Aplicada) in 1990. Ph.D. degree in Applied Computing from the [Instituto Nacional de Pesquisas Espaciais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Nacional_de_Pesquisas_Espaciais), São José dos Campos, Brazil, in 1993.[^o4]

## Research and contributions {#Research_and_contributions}

Frery's research intersects computational statistics and image processing, with significant work in:

- Statistical modeling of SAR/PolSAR data[^o6]
- Entropy and complexity measures in remote sensing[^o7]
- Information-theoretic image classification[^o8]
- Image filtering and clustering in remote sensing application[^o9]

## Honors and roles {#Honors_and_roles}

- IEEE GRSS Distinguished Lecturer (2015)[^o11]
- [Editor-in-Chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-Chief) of the *[IEEE Geoscience and Remote Sensing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Geoscience_and_Remote_Sensing_Letters)* (2014-2018)[^o12]
- Huashan Scholar at [Xidian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xidian_University), Xi’an, China (2019-2020)
- [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) (2025)[^o13]
