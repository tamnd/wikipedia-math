# Stephen Drury (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Drury"}

**Stephen William Drury** is an Anglo-Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).[^o2] He specializes in [mathematical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Analysis), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [linear algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_Algebra).[^o3] He received his doctorate from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1970 under the supervision of [Nicholas Varopoulos](wikipedia://en/37551761)[^o4] and completed his postdoctoral training at the [Faculté des sciences d'Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud), France. He was recruited to McGill by Professor [Carl Herz](wikipedia://en/42020168) in 1972.

:::

Among other contributions, he solved the [Sidon set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidon_set) union problem,[^o5][^o6] worked on restrictions of [Fourier](wikipedia://en/52247) and [Radon](wikipedia://en/503378) transforms to curves,[^o7] and generalized [von Neumann's inequality](wikipedia://en/16244706).[^o8] In [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), the Drury–Arveson space is named after [William Arveson](wikipedia://en/659727) and him.[^o9]

His research now pertains to the interplay between [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) theory and harmonic analysis and their applications to [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).
