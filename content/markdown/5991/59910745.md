# 10,000,000,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="10,000,000,000"}

**10,000,000,000** (**ten billion**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 9,999,999,999 and preceding 10,000,000,001.

:::

In [scientific notation](wikipedia://en/59715), it is written as 10<sup>10</sup>. It can be also written in [tetration](wikipedia://en/482379) notation as <sup>2</sup>10.

10,000,000,000 is also the [fifth power](wikipedia://en/40150949) of [100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_%28number%29) and also the [square](wikipedia://en/659942) of [100,000](wikipedia://en/1462080).
