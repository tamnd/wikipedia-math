# Charles Radin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Radin"}

**Charles Lewis Radin** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work on [aperiodic tilings](wikipedia://en/868145) and in particular for defining the [pinwheel tiling](wikipedia://en/9402045) and, with [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807), the [quaquaversal tiling](wikipedia://en/9401706).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Radin did his undergraduate studies at [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York), graduating in 1965,[^o4] and then did his graduate studies at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester), earning a Ph.D. in 1970 under the supervision of Gérard Emch.[^o4][^o5] Since 1976 he has been on the faculty of the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
