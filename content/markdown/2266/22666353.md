# Ringel–Hall algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ringel–Hall algebra"}

In mathematics, a **Ringel–Hall algebra** is a generalization of the [Hall algebra](wikipedia://en/7611764), studied by (txt). It has a basis of equivalence classes of objects of an [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063), and the structure constants for this basis are related to the numbers of extensions of objects in the category.

:::
