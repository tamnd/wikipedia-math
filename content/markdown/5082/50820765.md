# Butler group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Butler group"}

In mathematics, a **Butler group** is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) that is the [image](wikipedia://en/579311) of a completely decomposable [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) of finite [rank](wikipedia://en/244541). They were introduced by (txt).

:::
