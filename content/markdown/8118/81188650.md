# Richard Bertram (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Bertram"}

**Richard Bertram** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in the field of biomathematics. He is an [endowed professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endowed_professor) and head of the biomathematics program at Florida State University and a fellow of the [Society for Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Mathematical_Biology) (SMB).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Bertram earned his Bachelors degree and Ph.D at Florida State University. He completed his Ph.D in 1993 under supervisor Jeronimo Magnan.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

In 2010 Bertram was named director of the Florida State University biomathematics program.[^o5] Beginning in 2014, he spent ten years as deputy editor for *[Mathematical Biosciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Biosciences)*, a peer-reviewed [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier). In 2017, the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) awarded Bertram the Outstanding Paper Prize for a paper with Theodore Vo and Martin Wechselberger.[^o6][^o7]

In 2023, he became a fellow of the [SMB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Mathematical_Biology)[^o8] and the following year he was elected to the society's Board of Directors.[^o9]
