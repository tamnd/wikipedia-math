# Proxy (statistics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Proxy"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), a **proxy** or **proxy variable** is a [variable](wikipedia://en/3728109) that serves in place of an unobservable or immeasurable variable, while not being otherwise relevant in its own right.[^o2] In order for a variable to be a good proxy, it must have a close [correlation](wikipedia://en/157057), not necessarily linear, with the variable of interest. This correlation might be either positive or negative.

:::

Proxy variable must relate to an unobserved variable, must correlate with disturbance, and must not correlate with [regressors](wikipedia://en/437701) once the disturbance is controlled for.

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

In [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_science), **proxy measurements** are often required to stand in for variables that cannot be directly measured. This process of standing in is also known as [operationalization](wikipedia://en/747363). Per-capita [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP) is often used as a proxy for measures of [standard of living](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_of_living) or [quality of life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_of_life). Montgomery *et al.* examine several proxies used, and point out limitations with each, stating "In poor countries, no single empirical measure can be expected to display all of the facets of the concept of income. Our judgment is that consumption per adult is the best measure among those collected in cross-sectional surveys."[^o5]

Frost lists several examples of proxy variables:[^o6]

| Proxy variable | Unobserved variable |
|---|---|
| [Tree ring width](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dendrochronology) | [Historical climate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historical_climatology) |
| [GDP per capita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP_per_capita) | [Quality of life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_of_life) |
| [Body mass index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_mass_index) | [Body fat percentage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_fat_percentage) |
| [Years of education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education_level) | [Intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence) |

| [Height growth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_height#Determinants_of_growth) | [Hormone levels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Growth_hormone#Function) |
