# Claudio Baiocchi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Claudio Baiocchi"}

**Claudio Baiocchi** (August 20, 1940 – December 14, 2020) was an Italian mathematician. He was a professor at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) and since the 1990s he was a professor of mathematical higher analysis at the [Sapienza University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University).

:::

He worked on partial differential equations and the calculus of variations. In 1971 he applied his mathematical methods to a free boundary problem in the filtration of liquids through porous media with applications in civil engineering (by using the Baiocchi transform).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

His later research dealt with, among other topics, the [Collatz problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collatz_problem),[^o6] [cellular automata](wikipedia://en/54342) and [Turing machines](wikipedia://en/30403).[^o7]

In 1970 Baiocchi received the [Caccioppoli Prize](wikipedia://en/23538352). In 1974 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver). He was elected to the [Accademia dei XL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_XL)[^o8] and the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).
