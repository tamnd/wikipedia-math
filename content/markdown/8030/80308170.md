# Alexander Dunn (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Dunn"}

**Alexander Jason Dunn** is an Australian mathematician who works in analytic number theory. He has been an assistant professor at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech) since 2023. Previously, he was a postdoc at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) for three years, mentored by [Maksym Radziwill](wikipedia://en/53780399).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

Dunn received his [doctorate degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 2020 from the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) under the supervision of Scott Ahlgren and [Alexandru Zaharescu](wikipedia://en/57322145). In 2024, he won the [SASTRA Ramanujan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_Ramanujan_Prize) in mathematics, citing his "several breakthroughs in the study of modular forms, half-integral weight forms, metaplectic forms and their connections to prime numbers and integer partitions."[^o5]
