# Karl Becker (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Becker"}

**Karl Becker** (2 October 1823 – 20 June 1896) was a German statistician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Becker was born in Strohausen, [Rodenkirchen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodenkirchen_%28Stadland%29) in what is now [Stadland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stadland). In 1842, he received a commission in the army, and was also an instructor in the [Oldenburg Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oldenburg_Military_Academy). He fought in the [First Schleswig War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Schleswig_War) against [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark), and rose to be a captain. In 1855, he organized the statistical bureau of [Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Oldenburg), of which he was director until 1872. He edited *Statistische Nachrichten über das Grossherzogthum Oldenburg* (Statistical reports for the Grand Duchy of Oldenburg; 1857–72). He then became director of the statistical office of the [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire), where he remained until his death in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin). In this capacity, he edited the *Monatsheft zur Statistik des Deutschen Reichs* (Statistical Monthly for the German Empire) and the *Statistischen Jahrbuches* (Statistical Yearbook). His writings include *Zur Berechnung von Sterbetafeln an die Bevölkerungstatistik zu stellende Anforderungen* (1874).
