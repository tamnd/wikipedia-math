# Leon Bankoff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leon Bankoff"}

**Leon Bankoff** (December 13, 1908 – February 16, 1997), born in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29), was an American [dentist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dentist). As an [amateur mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_amateur_mathematicians) he constructed the [Bankoff circle](wikipedia://en/16857243). He was also an [Esperantist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperantist).

:::

## Life {#Life}

*Image: Bankoff Circle.svg.* The Bankoff Circle

After a visit to the [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York), Bankoff studied dentistry at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). Later, he moved to [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles), [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), where he taught at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California); while there, he completed his studies. He practiced over 60 years as a dentist in [Beverly Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly_Hills). Many of his patients were celebrities.[^o3]

Along with Bankoff's interest in dentistry were the [piano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piano) and the [guitar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guitar). He was fluent in [Esperanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esperanto), created artistic sculptures, and was interested in the progressive development of computer technology. Above all, he was a specialist in the mathematical world and highly respected as an expert in the field of [flat geometry](wikipedia://en/13005617). Since the 1940s, he lectured and published many articles as a co-author. Bankoff collaborated with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) in a mathematics paper and therefore has an [Erdős number 1](wikipedia://en/14403539#.231).[^o4]

From 1968 to 1981, Bankoff was the editor of the *Problem Department* of [Pi Mu Epsilon](wikipedia://en/1565972) Journals, where he was responsible for the publication of some 300 top problems in the area of [plane geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), particularly [Morley's trisector theorem](wikipedia://en/1096323), and the [arbelos](wikipedia://en/887197) of [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844).[^o3] Among his discoveries with the arbelos was the [Bankoff circle](wikipedia://en/16857243), which is equal in area to [Archimedes' twin circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes'_twin_circles).[^o5]
