# Homicide statistics by gender

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Homicide statistics by gender"}

At a global level, men represent both the majority of victims and the majority of perpetrators of homicide. According to the 2023 [UNODC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Office_on_Drugs_and_Crime) Global Study on Homicide, in 2021, at a global level, 81% of homicide victims were men.[^o2] In 2021, males accounted for most homicide victims in all jurisdictions except in [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria), the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic), [Iceland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iceland), [Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvia), [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway), [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia) and [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland), where females were slightly more likely to be homicide victims.[^o3] The global homicide rate was 9.3 per 100,000 males and 2.2 per 100,000 females; and 90% of homicide suspects brought into formal contact with the police were men.[^o3] According to the most recent data from [World Bank Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Bank_Group), the highest rate per 100,000 inhabitants for female homicide victims is in [Central African Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_African_Republic), [Antigua and Barbuda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antigua_and_Barbuda) and [Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamaica),[^o4] and the highest rate per 100,000 inhabitants for male homicide victims is in [US Virgin Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Virgin_Islands), [Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamaica) and [Venezuela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuela).[^o4] In 2020, the average percentage of women as homicide victims among [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) countries was 37%, with Latvia having the largest percentage of women homicide victims at 60%.[^o5]

:::

The pattern of males constituting both the majority of perpetrators and of victims has been also observed in studies recording lethal violence in [chimpanzees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chimpanzees).[^o6][^o7]
