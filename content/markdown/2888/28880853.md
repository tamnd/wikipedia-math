# Walter Brown (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Brown"}

**Walter Brown** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (29 April 1886, [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow) – 14 April 1957, [Marandellas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marandellas), Rhodesia) was a Scottish mathematician and engineer.

:::

## Life {#Life}

The younger son of Hugh A. Brown, a headmaster in [Paisley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paisley,_Renfrewshire), Walter was educated at [Allan Glen's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Glen's_School) and then studied at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow) (BSc Hons Mathematics and Physics 1907; and BSc Pure Science 1910). He began his career as a teacher at Allan Glen's. Brown became a member of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) in March 1911.[^o3]

In 1914 he took up the post of Lecturer in Engineering at [Hong Kong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_University). He was soon promoted to become Professor in Pure and Applied Mathematics, a post he held from 1918 to 1946.[^o4]

In 1920 he was elected an Associate Member of the [Institution of Electrical Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institution_of_Electrical_Engineers). In 1923 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were [Andrew Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Gray_%28physicist%29), [George Alexander Gibson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Alexander_Gibson), [John Walter Gregory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Walter_Gregory), [John Gordon Gray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Gordon_Gray) and [Dugald McQuistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dugald_McQuistan).[^o5]

He was President of the [Hong Kong Philharmonic Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_Philharmonic_Society_%281895–1941%29), and a member of the Hong Kong English Association, the Hong Kong Sino-British Association, and the Hong Kong Institute of Engineers and Shipbuilders.

A member of the [Royal Naval Volunteer Reserve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Naval_Volunteer_Reserve), Brown was captured when Hong Kong surrendered to the Japanese, and he was held as a [Prisoner of War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner_of_War) at [Stanley Camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Camp) (1941–45). He organised study groups in the internment camp, and helped attend to the medical needs of the prisoners.[^o6]

Returning to Scotland after the war, he taught civil and mechanical engineering at the [Royal Technical College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Technical_College) in Glasgow (1946–47), and mathematics at the University of Glasgow (1947–48).

He travelled extensively and died in [Marandellas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marandellas) in [Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhodesia) (now [Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe)) in April 1957.[^o5]
