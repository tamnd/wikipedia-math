# Lattès map

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lattès map"}

In mathematics, a **Lattès map** is a [rational map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_map) *f* = Θ*L*Θ<sup>−1</sup> from the [complex sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_sphere) to itself such that Θ is a [holomorphic map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_map) from a [complex torus](wikipedia://en/3055042) to the complex sphere and *L* is an affine map *z* → *az* + *b* from the complex torus to itself.

:::

Lattès maps are named after French mathematician [Samuel Lattès](wikipedia://en/58829872), who wrote about them in 1918.
