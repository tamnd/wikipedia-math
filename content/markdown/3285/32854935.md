# Boris Moishezon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Moishezon"}

**Boris Gershevich Moishezon** (Борис Гершевич Мойшезон) (October 26, 1937 – August 25, 1993) was a Soviet mathematician. He left the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) in 1972 for [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv), and in 1977 moved to [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), where he was a professor of mathematics until his death sixteen years later. He was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow) in 1983.

:::

A resident of [Leonia, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonia,_New_Jersey), Moishezon died at [Holy Name Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_Name_Medical_Center) in [Teaneck, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaneck,_New_Jersey) on August 25, 1993, due to a [heart attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myocardial_infarction) he suffered while jogging.[^o2]
