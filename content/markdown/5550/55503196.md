# Karen Kafadar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karen Kafadar"}

**Karen Kafadar** is an American statistician. She is Commonwealth Professor of Statistics at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia), and chair of the statistics department there. She was editor-in-chief of *[Technometrics](wikipedia://en/5854084)* from 1999 to 2001, and was president of the [International Association for Statistical Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_for_Statistical_Computing) for 2011–2013. In 2017 she was elected president of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) for the 2019 term.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kafadar earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics and a master's degree in statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), both in 1975. She completed her PhD in statistics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1979 under the supervision of [John Tukey](wikipedia://en/415974); her dissertation was *Robust Confidence Intervals for the One- and Two- Sample Problem*. Before moving to the University of Virginia in 2014, Kafadar was Rudy Professor of Statistics at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University). She has also worked for the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology), [Hewlett Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett_Packard), the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute), the [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver), and [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Kafadar is a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the American Statistical Association (since 1994) and the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (2012), and an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (2007). In 2023 Kafadar was recipient of the [American Statistical Association's Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224).
