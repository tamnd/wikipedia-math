# Gail F. Burrill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gail F. Burrill"}

**Gail F. Burrill** is a [mathematics educator](wikipedia://en/326471) who was president of the [National Council of Teachers of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1561691) (NCTM) from 1996 to 1998. She works as a specialist in the Program in Mathematics Education at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University).

:::

Burrill worked for nearly 30 years as a high school mathematics teacher; she is also the author of multiple textbooks, and has pushed to include statistics in the curriculum.

She won the [Presidential Award for Excellence in Teaching Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President's_Education_Awards_Program) in 1985, and the lifetime achievement award of the NCTM in 2012. She was also given an honorary doctorate by the [Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose-Hulman_Institute_of_Technology) in 2000. In 1994, she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).
