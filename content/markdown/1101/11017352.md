# Hypertranscendental function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hypertranscendental function"}

A **hypertranscendental function** or **transcendentally transcendental function** is a [transcendental](wikipedia://en/297021) [analytic function](wikipedia://en/61478) which is not the solution of an [algebraic differential equation](wikipedia://en/9501827) with coefficients in $\mathbb{Z}$ (the [integers](wikipedia://en/14563)) and with algebraic [initial conditions](wikipedia://en/1186804).

:::

## History {#History}

The term 'transcendentally transcendental' was introduced by [E. H. Moore](wikipedia://en/1532860) in 1896; the term 'hypertranscendental' was introduced by [D. D. Morduhai-Boltovskoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._D._Morduhai-Boltovskoi) in 1914.[^o3][^o4]

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

One standard definition (there are slight variants) defines solutions of [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) of the form

$$F\left(x, y, y', \cdots, y^{(n)} \right) = 0,$$

where $F$ is a polynomial with constant coefficients, as *algebraically transcendental* or *differentially algebraic*. Transcendental functions which are not *algebraically transcendental* are *transcendentally transcendental*. [Hölder's theorem](wikipedia://en/4883158) shows that the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316) is in this category.[^o7][^o8][^o9]

Hypertranscendental functions usually arise as the solutions to [functional equations](wikipedia://en/364377), for example the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316).

:::

::: {#o11 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

### Hypertranscendental functions {#Hypertranscendental_functions}

- The zeta functions of [algebraic number fields](wikipedia://en/28730822), in particular, the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809)
- The [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316) (*cf.* [Hölder's theorem](wikipedia://en/4883158))

### Transcendental but not hypertranscendental functions {#Transcendental_but_not_hypertranscendental_functions}

- The [exponential function](wikipedia://en/9678), [logarithm](wikipedia://en/17860), and the [trigonometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonometric_function) and [hyperbolic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_function) functions.
- The [generalized hypergeometric functions](wikipedia://en/361184), including special cases such as [Bessel functions](wikipedia://en/4700) (except some special cases which are algebraic).

### Non-transcendental (algebraic) functions {#Non-transcendental_(algebraic)_functions}

- All [algebraic functions](wikipedia://en/974169), in particular [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000).
