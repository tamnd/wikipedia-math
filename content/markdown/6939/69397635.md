# Fabienne Comte

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fabienne Comte"}

**Fabienne Comte** is a French statistician known for her research on topics including [statistical finance](wikipedia://en/7458786), [stochastic volatility](wikipedia://en/5740025), [autoregressive conditional heteroskedasticity](wikipedia://en/610752), and [deconvolution](wikipedia://en/275626). She is a professor in the unit for mathematics and computer science at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_%282019%29).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Comte studied mathematics at [ENS Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENS_Cachan) and [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University), earning a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in 1988, a master's degree in 1989, and an [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in 1990, with a specialty in probability. She earned a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in 1991, through a cooperative program with [Paris 1 Panthéon-Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_1_Panthéon-Sorbonne_University), the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique), and [ENSAE ParisTech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENSAE_ParisTech), and completed her doctorate in applied mathematics in 1994 through Paris 1 with the thesis *Causalité, Cointégration, Mémoire Longue : Modélisation Stochastique en temps continu, estimation et simulation*, supervised by mathematical economist Eric Renault.

She worked as [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) from 1995 until 2001, earning a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 2000. In 2001 became professor at [Paris Descartes University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Descartes_University), which merged into the University of Paris in 2019.

## Book {#Book}

Comte is the author of the book *Estimation non-paramétrique* \[*Nonparametric estimation*\], published in 2015.
