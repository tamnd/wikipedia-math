# Order (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order"}

**Order** in mathematics may refer to:

:::

## Set theory {#Set_theory}

- [Total order](wikipedia://en/30330) and [partial order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_order), a binary relation generalizing the usual ordering of numbers and of words in a dictionary
- [Ordered set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordered_set)
- Order in [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175), uniform structures in consequence to critical set cardinality

## Algebra {#Algebra}

- [Order (group theory)](wikipedia://en/493527), the cardinality of a group or period of an element
- [Order of a polynomial (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_a_polynomial_%28disambiguation%29)
- [Order of a square matrix](wikipedia://en/166022), its dimension
- [Order (ring theory)](wikipedia://en/1114171), an algebraic structure
- [Ordered group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordered_group)
- [Ordered field](wikipedia://en/22430)

## Analysis {#Analysis}

- [Order (differential equation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_%28differential_equation%29) or [order of highest derivative](wikipedia://en/7921#Higher_derivatives), of a differential equation
- [Leading-order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leading-order) terms
- NURBS order, a number one greater than the degree of the polynomial representation of a [non-uniform rational B-spline](wikipedia://en/308474)
- [Order of convergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_convergence), a measurement of convergence
- [Order of derivation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_derivation)
- [Order of an entire function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_an_entire_function)
- [Order of a power series](wikipedia://en/59958#Order_of_a_power_series), the lowest degree of its terms
- Ordered list, a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) or [tuple](wikipedia://en/132729)
- [Orders of approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders_of_approximation) in Big O notation
- [Z-order (curve)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-order_%28curve%29), a space-filling curve

## Arithmetic {#Arithmetic}

- [Multiplicative order](wikipedia://en/344542) in modular arithmetic
- [Order of operations](wikipedia://en/212980)
- [Orders of magnitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orders_of_magnitude), a class of scale or magnitude of any amount

## Combinatorics {#Combinatorics}

- Order in the [Josephus permutation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josephus_permutation)
- [Ordered selections and partitions](wikipedia://en/3290705#Ordered_selections_and_partitions) of the twelvefold way in combinatorics
- Ordered set, a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942), [cyclic order](wikipedia://en/434673), or [permutation](wikipedia://en/44027)
- [Weak order of permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_order_of_permutations)

## Fractals {#Fractals}

- [Complexor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexor), or complex order in fractals
- Order of extension in [Lakes of Wada](wikipedia://en/5774599)
- Order of [fractal dimension](wikipedia://en/285907) (Rényi dimensions)
- Orders of construction in the [Pythagoras tree](wikipedia://en/2508505)

## Geometry {#Geometry}

- Long-range aperiodic order, in [pinwheel tiling](wikipedia://en/9402045), for instance

## Graphs {#Graphs}

- [Graph order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_graph_theory_terms#order), the number of nodes in a graph
- First order and second order [logic of graphs](wikipedia://en/47857242)
- [Topological ordering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_ordering) of directed acyclic graphs
- [Degeneracy ordering](wikipedia://en/27545816) of undirected graphs
- [Elimination ordering](wikipedia://en/744165#Perfect_elimination_and_efficient_recognition) of chordal graphs
- Order, the complexity of a structure within a graph: see [haven (graph theory)](wikipedia://en/5133456) and [bramble (graph theory)](wikipedia://en/36601188)

## Logic {#Logic}

In logic, model theory and type theory:

- [Zeroth-order logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeroth-order_logic)
- [First-order logic](wikipedia://en/10983)
- [Second-order logic](wikipedia://en/341428)
- [Higher-order logic](wikipedia://en/562883)

## Order theory {#Order_theory}

- [*Order* (journal)](wikipedia://en/28780245), an academic journal on order theory
- [Dense order](wikipedia://en/6320997), a total order wherein between any unequal pair of elements there is always an intervening element in the order
- [Glossary of order theory](wikipedia://en/453204)
- [Lexicographical order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lexicographical_order), an ordering method on sequences analogous to alphabetical order on words
- [List of order topics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_order_topics), list of order theory topics
- [Order theory](wikipedia://en/361924), study of various binary relations known as orders
- [Order topology](wikipedia://en/213650), a topology of total order for totally ordered sets
- [Ordinal numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_numbers), numbers assigned to sets based on their set-theoretic order
- [Partial order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_order), often called just "order" in order theory texts, a transitive antisymmetric relation
- [Total order](wikipedia://en/30330), a partial order that is also total, in that either the relation or its inverse holds between any unequal elements

- [Order statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_statistics)

- [Second-order statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second-order_statistics), e.g., [correlation](wikipedia://en/157057), power spectrum, variance
- [Higher-order statistics](wikipedia://en/9588430), e.g., bispectrum, kurtosis, skewness
