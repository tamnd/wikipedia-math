In 7-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **2<sub>31</sub>** is a [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392), constructed from the [E7](wikipedia://en/649115) group.

:::

Its [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) is **2<sub>31</sub>**, describing its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 2-node branch.

The **rectified 2<sub>31</sub>** is constructed by points at the mid-edges of the **2<sub>31</sub>**.

These polytopes are part of a family of 127 (or 2<sup>7</sup>−1) convex [uniform polytopes in seven dimensions](wikipedia://en/6821719), made of [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) facets and [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719), defined by all combinations of rings in this [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram): .

The **2<sub>31</sub>** is composed of 126 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 2016 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), 10080 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) (triangles), 20160 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) ([tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606)), 16128 4-faces ([4-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-simplex)), 4788 5-faces (756 [pentacrosses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentacross), and 4032 [5-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex)), 632 6-faces (576 [6-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex) and 56 [**2<sub>21</sub>**](wikipedia://en/19232473)). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [6-demicube](wikipedia://en/10275945). Its 126 vertices represent the root vectors of the [simple Lie group](wikipedia://en/292831) [E<sub>7</sub>](wikipedia://en/649115).

This polytope is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) for a [uniform tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_honeycomb) of 7-dimensional space, **[3<sub>31</sub>](wikipedia://en/19593724)**.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- [E. L. Elte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._L._Elte) named it V<sub>126</sub> (for its 126 vertices) in his 1912 listing of semiregular polytopes.[^o14]
- It was called **2<sub>31</sub>** by [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) for its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 2-node sequence.
- Pentacontahexa-pentacosiheptacontahexa-exon (Acronym: laq) - 56-576 facetted polyexon (Jonathan Bowers)[^o15]

### Construction {#Construction}

It is created by a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) upon a set of 7 [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) mirrors in 7-dimensional space.

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), .

Removing the node on the short branch leaves the [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex). There are 576 of these facets. These facets are centered on the locations of the vertices of the [3<sub>21</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_3_21_polytope) polytope, .

Removing the node on the end of the 3-length branch leaves the [2<sub>21</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_2_21_polytope). There are 56 of these facets. These facets are centered on the locations of the vertices of the [1<sub>32</sub>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gosset_1_32_polytope) polytope, .

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes the [6-demicube](wikipedia://en/10275945), 1<sub>31</sub>, .

Seen in a [configuration matrix](wikipedia://en/56419724), the element counts can be derived by mirror removal and ratios of [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004#Properties) orders.[^o18]
