In 7-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **1<sub>32</sub>** is a [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392), constructed from the [E7](wikipedia://en/649115) group.

:::

Its [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) is **1<sub>32</sub>**, describing its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of one of the 1-node sequences.

The **rectified 1<sub>32</sub>** is constructed by points at the mid-edges of the **1<sub>32</sub>**.

These polytopes are part of a family of 127 (2<sup>7</sup>−1) convex [uniform polytopes in 7 dimensions](wikipedia://en/6821719), made of [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) facets and [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719), defined by all permutations of rings in this [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram): .

### Construction {#Construction}

It is created by a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) upon a set of 7 [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) mirrors in 7-dimensional space.

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram),

Removing the node on the end of the 2-length branch leaves the [6-demicube](wikipedia://en/10275945), 1<sub>31</sub>,

Removing the node on the end of the 3-length branch leaves the [1<sub>22</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070548),

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes the [birectified 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_6-simplex), 0<sub>32</sub>,

Seen in a [configuration matrix](wikipedia://en/56419724), the element counts can be derived by mirror removal and ratios of [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004#Properties) orders.[^o21]

The **rectified 1<sub>32</sub>** (also called **0<sub>321</sub>**) is a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the 1<sub>32</sub> polytope, creating new vertices on the center of edge of the 1<sub>32</sub>.
