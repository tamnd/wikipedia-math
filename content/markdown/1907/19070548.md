::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="1 22 polytope"}

In 6-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **1<sub>22</sub>** polytope is a [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392), constructed from the [E<sub>6</sub>](wikipedia://en/293725) group. It was first published in [E. L. Elte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E._L._Elte)'s 1912 listing of semiregular polytopes, named as V<sub>72</sub> (for its 72 vertices).[^o4]

:::

Its [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) is **1<sub>22</sub>**, describing its bifurcating [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), with a single ring on the end of the 1-node sequence. There are two rectifications of the 1<sub>22</sub>, constructed by positions points on the elements of 1<sub>22</sub>. The **rectified 1<sub>22</sub>** is constructed by points at the mid-edges of the **1<sub>22</sub>**. The **birectified 1<sub>22</sub>** is constructed by points at the triangle face centers of the **1<sub>22</sub>**.

These polytopes are from a family of 39 convex [uniform polytopes in 6-dimensions](wikipedia://en/6821728), made of [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338) facets and [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719), defined by all permutations of rings in this [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram): .

The 1<sub>22</sub> polytope contains 72 vertices, and 54 [5-demicubic](wikipedia://en/7186232) facets. It has a [birectified 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_5-simplex) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719). Its 72 vertices represent the root vectors of the [simple Lie group](wikipedia://en/292831) [E<sub>6</sub>](wikipedia://en/293725).

#### Geometric folding {#Geometric_folding}

The **1<sub>22</sub>** is related to the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) by a geometric [folding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Folding_%28Dynkin_diagram%29) E6 → F4 of [Coxeter-Dynkin diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram), E6 corresponding to **1<sub>22</sub>** in 6 dimensions, F4 to the 24-cell in 4 dimensions. This can be seen in the [Coxeter plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) projections. The 24 vertices of the 24-cell are projected in the same two rings as seen in the 1<sub>22</sub>.
