# Sichere Inter-Netzwerk Architektur

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sichere Inter-Netzwerk Architektur"}

**Sichere Inter-Netzwerk Architektur** (SINA) is a [cryptographic system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographic_system) developed by and a product of the [Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundesamt_für_Sicherheit_in_der_Informationstechnik), an arm of the [German government](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_government).[^o2] It also has the anglicized name **Secure Inter-Network Architecture**.[^o3] As of April 2024 the cryptosystem was employed by "many European governments for transmitting classified information".[^o13]

:::

## History {#History}

SINA came to prominence in March 2023 when it was divulged in a court warrant for the arrest of the Austrian intelligence officer [Egisto Ott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egisto_Ott), who has a relationship with [Wirecard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirecard) fraudster [Jan Marsalek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Marsalek), that a SINA laptop was handed to the Russian secret services,[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11][^o12] specifically the [Lubyanka Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lubyanka_Building) office of the [FSB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Security_Service).[^o13]

The Austrian authorities report that Ott received €20,000 for the laptop from Marsalek.[^o14]
