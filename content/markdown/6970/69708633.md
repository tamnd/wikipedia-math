# Wayne Velicer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wayne Velicer"}

**Wayne Velicer** (March 4, 1944 – October 15, 2017) was an American psychologist known for his research in [quantitative](wikipedia://en/3067624) and [health psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_psychology). He taught at the [University of Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rhode_Island) from 1973 until his death in 2017. He worked with [James O. Prochaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_O._Prochaska) to help to found the University of Rhode Island's Cancer Prevention Research Center, of which he subsequently served as co-director.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 2004, Velicer was one of six University of Rhode Island faculty to be named an [ISI Highly Cited Researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_Highly_Cited_Researcher).[^o6] In 2013, he received the [Samuel J. Messick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samuel_J._Messick) Distinguished Scientific Contributions Award from Division 5 of the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association).[^o7] In 2018, he was posthumously inducted into the University of Rhode Island's Lifetime Service Society.[^o8]
