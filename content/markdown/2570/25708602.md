# Noncommutative residue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noncommutative residue"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **noncommutative residue**, defined independently by M. (Wodzicki 1984) and (Guillemin 1985), is a certain trace on the algebra of [pseudodifferential operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudodifferential_operator) on a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [differentiable manifold](wikipedia://en/2119219) that is expressed via a local density. In the case of the [circle](wikipedia://en/6220), the noncommutative residue had been studied earlier by M. (Adler 1978) and Y. (Manin 1978) in the context of one-dimensional [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213).

:::
