# Heine–Stieltjes polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heine–Stieltjes polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Heine–Stieltjes polynomials** or **Stieltjes polynomials**, introduced by T. J. Stieltjes,[^o2] are polynomial solutions of a second-order [Fuchsian equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuchsian_equation), a [differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309) all of whose singularities are [regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_singularity). The Fuchsian equation has the form

:::

$$\frac{d^2 S}{dz^2}+\left(\sum _{j=1}^N \frac{\gamma _j}{z - a_j} \right) \frac{dS}{dz} + \frac{V(z)}{\prod _{j=1}^N (z - a_j)}S = 0$$

for some polynomial *V*(*z*) of degree at most *N* − 2, and if this has a polynomial solution *S* then *V* is called a Van Vleck polynomial (after [Edward Burr Van Vleck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Burr_Van_Vleck)) and *S* is called a Heine–Stieltjes polynomial.

[Heun polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heun_polynomial) are the special cases of Stieltjes polynomials when the differential equation has four singular points.
