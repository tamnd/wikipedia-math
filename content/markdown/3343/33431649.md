# Fred Van Oystaeyen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fred Van Oystaeyen"}

**Fred Van Oystaeyen** (born 1947), also *Freddy van Oystaeyen*, is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics at the [University of Antwerp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Antwerp).[^o2] He has pioneered work on [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917), in particular [noncommutative algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/25493347).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1972, Fred Van Oystaeyen obtained his Ph.D. from the [Vrije Universiteit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vrije_Universiteit) of [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam). In 1975 he became professor at the University of Antwerp, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science.[^o4]

Van Oystaeyen has well over 200 scientific papers and several books. One of his recent books, *Virtual Topology and [Functor](wikipedia://en/10987) Geometry*, provides an introduction to [noncommutative topology](wikipedia://en/662624).

At the occasion of his 60th birthday, a conference in his honour was held in [Almería](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Almería), September 18 to 22, 2007;[^o5] on March 25, 2011, he received his first honorary doctorate from that same university, Universidad de Almería. At the campus of Universidad de Almería the street "Calle Fred Van Oystaeyen" (previously "Calle los Gallardos") is named after him.[^o6] In 2019, he received another honorary doctorate from the Vrije Universiteit Brussel. [^o7]
