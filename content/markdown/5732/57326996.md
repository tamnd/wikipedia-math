# List of graphs by edges and vertices

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of graphs by edges and vertices"}

This sortable list points to the articles describing various individual (finite) [graphs](wikipedia://en/325806).[^o2] The columns 'vertices', 'edges', '[radius](wikipedia://en/1020021#Related_concepts)', '[diameter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_diameter)', '[girth](wikipedia://en/12013)', 'P' (whether the graph is [planar](wikipedia://en/24314)), χ ([chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number)) and χ' ([chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index)) are also sortable, allowing searching for a parameter. In the planarity column, T indicates that the graph is planar and F indicates that the graph is not planar.

:::

See also [Graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) for the general theory, as well as [Gallery of named graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallery_of_named_graphs) for a list with illustrations.
