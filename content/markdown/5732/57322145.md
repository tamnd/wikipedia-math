# Alexandru Zaharescu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexandru Zaharescu"}

**Alexandru Zaharescu** (born June 4, 1961) is a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a professor in the Department of Mathematics,[^o3] [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign), and an Honorary Member[^o4] at the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230). He has two PhDs in mathematics, one from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) in 1991 under the direction of [Nicolae Popescu](wikipedia://en/1358791), the other from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1995 under the direction of [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak). Zaharescu made numerous contributions to number theory. In total, he has more than 350 publications.[^o5][^o6]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Zaharescu was born on June 4, 1961, and grew up in [Codlea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codlea), Romania. He graduated from high school in Codlea in 1980, and from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) in 1986. He joined the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230) in 1989, and in 1991 he received a PhD from the University of Bucharest.

## Career {#Career}

Zaharescu received his second PhD from Princeton University in 1995. After that, he held temporary positions at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), before joining the faculty at the University of Illinois in 2000.

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Zaharescu was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2017, for contributions to analytic number theory.[^o10] A conference in honor of his 60th birthday was held in June 2021 at the Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy.[^o11] In March 2024, he was elected Honorary Member of the Romanian Academy.[^o12]
