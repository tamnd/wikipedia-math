# Harris Hancock

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Harris Hancock"}

**Harris Hancock** (May 14, 1867 – March 19, 1944) was a mathematics professor at the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati) who worked on [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) and related areas. He was the brother of the horse breeder [Arthur B. Hancock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_B._Hancock).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Harris Hancock was born at his family's estate, Ellerslie, in [Albemarle County, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albemarle_County,_Virginia) on May 14, 1867. He graduated from the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia)'s school of mathematics in 1886. He received an [AB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) in 1888, an [AM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) and [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) in 1894, and an [ScD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1901.[^o4]

He married Belle Lyman Clay on September 30, 1907, and they had two children.[^o4]

Harris Hancock died at Ellerslie on March 19, 1944.[^o5]
