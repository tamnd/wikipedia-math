# Carlo Miranda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Carlo Miranda"}

**Carlo Miranda** (15 August 1912 – 28 May 1982) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working on [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [theory of elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759): he is known for giving the first proof of the [Poincaré–Miranda theorem](wikipedia://en/46736435),[^o3] for [Miranda's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miranda's_theorem) in complex analysis,[^o4] and for writing an influential monograph in the theory of elliptic partial differential equations.[^o5]

:::
