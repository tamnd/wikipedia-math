# Frobenius splitting

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frobenius splitting"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Frobenius splitting**, introduced by [Mehta](wikipedia://en/32577513) and [Ramanathan](wikipedia://en/32574686),[^n1] is a splitting of the [injective](wikipedia://en/45196) [morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891) $\mathcal{O}_X\to F_*\mathcal{O}_X$ from a [structure sheaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_sheaf) $\mathcal{O}_X$ of a [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) $p>0$ variety $X$ to its image $F_*\mathcal{O}_X$ under the [Frobenius endomorphism](wikipedia://en/1088270) $F_*$.

:::

Brion and [Kumar](wikipedia://en/26780647) give a detailed discussion of Frobenius splittings.[^n2]

A fundamental property of Frobenius-split [projective schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_scheme) $X$ is that the [higher cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_cohomology) $H^i(X,L)$ ($i>0$) of [ample line bundles](wikipedia://en/1186896) $L$ vanishes.
