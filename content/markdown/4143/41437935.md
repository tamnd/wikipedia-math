# Fundamental theorem of algebraic K-theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fundamental theorem of algebraic K-theory"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the **fundamental theorem of [algebraic *K*-theory](wikipedia://en/598500)** describes the effects of [changing the ring](wikipedia://en/41410139) of *K*-groups from a ring *R* to $R[t]$ or $R[t, t^{-1}]$. The theorem was first proved by [Hyman Bass](wikipedia://en/7085910) for $K_0, K_1$ and was later extended to higher *K*-groups by [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519).

:::

## Description {#Description}

Let $G_i(R)$ be the algebraic *K*-theory of the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of [finitely generated modules](wikipedia://en/326430) over a [noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) *R*; explicitly, we can take $G_i(R) = \pi_i(B^+\text{f-gen-Mod}_R)$, where $B^+ = \Omega BQ$ is given by Quillen's [Q-construction](wikipedia://en/41403415). If *R* is a [regular ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_ring) (i.e., has finite [global dimension](wikipedia://en/2291739)), then $G_i(R) = K_i(R),$ the *i*-th *K*-group of *R*.[^o3] This is an immediate consequence of the [resolution theorem](wikipedia://en/82883841), which compares the *K*-theories of two different categories (with inclusion relation).

For a noetherian ring *R*, the fundamental theorem states:[^o4]

- (i) $G_i(R[t]) = G_i(R), \, i \ge 0$.
- (ii) $G_i(R[t, t^{-1}]) = G_i(R) \oplus G_{i-1}(R), \, i \ge 0, \, G_{-1}(R) = 0$.

The proof of the theorem uses the [Q-construction](wikipedia://en/41403415). There is also a version of the theorem for the singular case (for $K_i$); this is the version proved in Grayson's paper.
