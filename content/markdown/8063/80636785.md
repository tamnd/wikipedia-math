# Johanna N. Y. Franklin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johanna N. Y. Franklin"}

**Johanna N. Y. Franklin** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics working at [Hofstra University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hofstra_University).[^o3] She is known for her work on [algorithmic randomness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithmic_randomness).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Franklin completed her BS and MS at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), and her PhD, on *Aspects of Schnorr Randomness* under the supervision of [Theodore Slaman](wikipedia://en/5693320) at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), in 2007.[^o5]

She has held postdoctoral research positions at the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880), [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768), [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College), and [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut), and has worked at [Hofstra University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hofstra_University) since 2014, where she was promoted to professor in 2023.[^o6]

Franklin is an editor of the *Archive for Mathematical Logic*[^o7] and a reviews editor for the *Bulletin of Symbolic Logic*.[^o8]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

In 2024, Franklin received the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) Service Award, which recognizes "individuals who have demonstrated exceptional service to the AWM by promoting and supporting women in mathematics."[^o10][^o11]
