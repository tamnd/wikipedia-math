# Alexander Doniphan Wallace

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Doniphan Wallace"}

**Alexander Doniphan Wallace** (21 August 1905, in [Hampton, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampton,_Virginia), USA – 16 October 1985, in [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans), USA) was an American mathematician who introduced [proximity spaces](wikipedia://en/1536920).[^o2]

:::

Wallace received from the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) B.A. in 1935, M.A. in 1936 and Ph.D. in 1940. He was an instructor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1940–1941 and became an assistant professor in mathematics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1941 and remained there until 1947. He was a professor and chair of the mathematics department at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) in 1947–1963. From 1963 until his retirement in 1973 he was a mathematics professor at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida).[^o4]

His doctoral students include [Chung Tao Yang](wikipedia://en/15315411).
