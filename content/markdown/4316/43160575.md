# Posner's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Posner's theorem"}

In algebra, **Posner's theorem** states that given a [prime](wikipedia://en/487540) [polynomial identity algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_identity_algebra) *A* with center *Z*, the ring $A \otimes_Z Z_{(0)}$ is a [central simple algebra](wikipedia://en/404082) over $Z_{(0)}$, the [field of fractions](wikipedia://en/25271) of *Z*.[^o2] It is named after [Ed Posner](wikipedia://en/47674352).

:::
