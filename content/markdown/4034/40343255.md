# Carl B. Allendoerfer Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl B. Allendoerfer Award"}

The **Carl B. Allendoerfer Award** is presented annually by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) (MAA) for "expository excellence published in *[Mathematics Magazine](wikipedia://en/4230329)*."[^o2] it is named after mathematician [Carl B. Allendoerfer](wikipedia://en/3466784) who was president of the MAA 1959–60.[^o3]

:::
