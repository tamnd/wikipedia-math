# Euler integral

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Euler integral"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), there are two types of **Euler integral**:[^o2]

:::

1. The *Euler [integral](wikipedia://en/15532) of the first kind* is the [beta function](wikipedia://en/245660) $$\mathrm{\mathrm{B}}(z_1,z_2) = \int_0^1t^{z_1-1}(1-t)^{z_2-1}\,dt = \frac{\Gamma(z_1)\Gamma(z_2)}{\Gamma(z_1+z_2)}$$
1. The *Euler integral of the second kind* is the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316)[^o3] $$\Gamma(z) = \int_0^\infty t^{z-1}\,\mathrm e^{-t}\,dt$$

For [positive integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_integer) $m$ and $n$, the two integrals can be expressed in terms of [factorials](wikipedia://en/10606) and [binomial coefficients](wikipedia://en/4668):

$$\mathrm{B}(n,m) = \frac{(n-1)!(m-1)!}{(n+m-1)! } = \frac{n+m}{nm \binom{n+m}{n}} = \left( \frac{1}{n} + \frac{1}{m} \right) \frac{1}{\binom{n+m}{n}}$$

$$\Gamma(n) = (n-1)!$$
