# System of equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="System of equations"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **system of equations**, also known as a set of **simultaneous equations** or an **equation system**, is a [finite set](wikipedia://en/11742) of [equations](wikipedia://en/9284) for which common solutions are sought.[^o2] An equation system is usually [classified in the same manner as single equations](wikipedia://en/9284#Types_of_equations), namely as one of the following:

:::

- [System of linear equations](wikipedia://en/113087)
- [System of nonlinear equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_of_nonlinear_equations)
- [System of bilinear equations](wikipedia://en/36499681)
- [System of polynomial equations](wikipedia://en/27420015)
- [System of differential equations](wikipedia://en/63871781)
- [System of difference equations](wikipedia://en/27567102)
