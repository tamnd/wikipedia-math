# Ambulatory assessment

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ambulatory assessment"}

**Ambulatory assessment** (**AA**) is [computer-assisted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer-assisted) methodology for self-reports, behavior records or psychological measurements, while the subject undergoes normal activities in daily life.[^o2][^o3]

:::

AA is used in clinical psychology to investigate symptoms, predict the recurrence or onset of new symptoms, monitor treatment effects, prevent relapse, and indicate necessary interventions.[^o4]

In the early ambulatory assessment, personal digital assistants and [interactive voice response systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interactive_voice_response) were used. They have now been replaced by [smartphones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smartphone), and this has made [data collection](wikipedia://en/11501746) more feasible.[^o5]

## Characteristics {#Characteristics}

1. The recording takes place in real-life situations
1. A computer-assisted methodology is widely used
1. The evaluator attempts to minimise the method dependent reactivity and strives to achieve a high degree of [ecological balance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_balance)[^o3]
1. The assessment can be continuous, event-based, interactive, time-based, or randomly prompted
1. It is idiographic in focus and allows for the examination of multiple individual processes[^o5]

The Ambulatory assessment can be applied in various areas of [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) and other [behavioral sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioural_sciences).

## Modes of Data Collection {#Modes_of_Data_Collection}

### Event Controlled/Self-Report {#Event_Controlled/Self-Report}

The subject self-monitors the happenings of events and assess them whenever it occurs.[^o3] Varieties of devices are used to collect the data from the self reports.

The main concern with the self-report assessment is whether biases may influence the credibility of the results.[^o5]

### Time Controlled/Observational {#Time_Controlled/Observational}

In this mode, the subject follows the time-sampling schedule.[^o3] This mode provides the data through the observer’s point of view. Forms of Ambulatory assessment observational method are Electronically Activated Recorder (EAR), [Global Positioning System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Positioning_System) (GPS) in phones or devices on the person. Light sensors to infer context, using video or sensors to detect interactions with others.[^o5]
