# Jeffrey Brock

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeffrey Brock"}

*Image: JFB-Profile4.jpg.* A recent picture of Jeffrey Brock

**Jeffrey Farlowe Brock** (born June 14, 1970, in [Bronxville, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronxville,_New_York)) is an American mathematician, working in low-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He is known for his contributions to the understanding of [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700) and the geometry of [Teichmüller spaces](wikipedia://en/1835661).

:::

Since July 2018, Brock has been a professor of mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University),[^o2] and in January 2019 he became the first FAS (Faculty of Arts and Sciences) dean of science at Yale.[^o2] In July 2019, he was additionally appointed Dean of the [Yale School of Engineering & Applied Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_School_of_Engineering_&_Applied_Science).[^o3]

Before joining [Yale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), he was a professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University),[^o4] and also founding director of the Data Science Initiative[^o5] at Brown University.

## Biography {#Biography}

Brock obtained a BA (with distinction in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831)) from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1992. He completed a Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1997, under the supervision of [Curtis T. McMullen](wikipedia://en/680168).[^o7]

Brock then held positions as (NSF-funded) Szego Assistant Professor [^o4] at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (1997–2000), assistant professor [^o4] at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) (2000–2003), and Donald D. Harrington Faculty Fellow [^o4] at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) (2003–2004). He became associate professor with tenure at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 2004, and a full professor in 2007.[^o8] He was chair of the Mathematics Department from 2013 to 2017.

Brock has been associate director of [ICERM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICERM) since 2013. Previously, he had been deputy director between 2010 and 2013.[^o9]

Since July 2018, Brock has been a professor of mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University),[^o2] and in January 2019 he became the first FAS (Faculty of Arts and Sciences) dean of science at Yale.[^o2] In July 2019, he was additionally appointed Dean of the [Yale School of Engineering & Applied Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_School_of_Engineering_&_Applied_Science).[^o3]

Brock is also an accomplished [jazz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jazz) musician. He was the founding bassist of the Vijay Iyer Trio, led by the acclaimed jazz pianist [Vijay Iyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vijay_Iyer).

He is married and has three children.

## Research {#Research}

Jeffrey Brock's research focuses on [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774) and geometry, particularly on spaces with [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291) or negative curvature. His joint work with [Richard Canary](wikipedia://en/46914909) and [Yair Minsky](wikipedia://en/46905726) resulted in a solution [^o11] to the "Ending Lamination Conjecture" of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190), culminating in the geometric [classification theorem](wikipedia://en/4576710) for (topologically finite) hyperbolic 3-manifolds in terms of their fundamental group and the structure of their ends.

More recently, he has worked to understand applications of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) to the structure of massive and complex data sets and the risks and implications of the increasing use of 'black box' algorithms in science and society.
