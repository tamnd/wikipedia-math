# Levy–Mises equations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Levy–Mises equations"}

The **Levi–Mises equations** (also called **flow rules**) describe the relationship between [stress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shear_stress) and [strain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strain_%28materials_science%29) for an ideal [plastic](wikipedia://en/240123) [solid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid) where the [elastic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29) strains are negligible.

:::

The generalized Levy–Mises equation can be written as:

$$\frac{\mathbf{d}\varepsilon_1}{\sigma'_1}=\frac{\mathbf{d}\varepsilon_2} {\sigma'_2}=\frac{\mathbf{d}\varepsilon_3}{\sigma'_3}=\mathbf{d}\lambda$$
