# Jacobsthal sum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacobsthal sum"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Jacobsthal sums** are finite sums of [Legendre symbols](wikipedia://en/18567) related to [Gauss sums](wikipedia://en/6378204). They were introduced by (txt).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The Jacobsthal sum is given by

$$\phi_n(a)=\sum_{m\bmod p}\left(\dfrac{m(m^n+a)}{p}\right)$$

where *p* is a prime and () is the [Legendre symbol](wikipedia://en/18567).

:::
