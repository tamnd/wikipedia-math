# Brewer sum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brewer sum"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Brewer sums** are finite [character sum](wikipedia://en/2986034) introduced by (txt) related to [Jacobsthal sums](wikipedia://en/37185041).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The Brewer sum is given by

$$\Lambda_n(a) = \sum_{x\bmod p}\binom{D_{n+1}(x,a)}{p}$$

where *D*<sub>*n*</sub> is the [Dickson polynomial](wikipedia://en/15640469) (or "Brewer polynomial") given by

$$D_{0}(x,a)=2,\quad D_1(x,a)=x, \quad D_{n+1}(x,a)=xD_n(x,a)-aD_{n-1}(x,a)$$

and () is the [Legendre symbol](wikipedia://en/18567).

The Brewer sum is zero when *n* is [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) to *q*<sup>2</sup>−1.

:::
