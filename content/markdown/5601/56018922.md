# André Gérardin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="André Gérardin"}

**André Gérardin** (1879, [Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France), [Meurthe-et-Moselle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meurthe-et-Moselle) – 1953, Nancy, Meurthe-et-Moselle) was a French mathematician, specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and calculating machines used in [factoring large positive integers](wikipedia://en/15491), finding primes, and calculating [quadratic residues](wikipedia://en/200091) modulo a given positive integer.[^o2]

:::

Gérardin became a member of the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France) in 1906.[^o3] He wrote many articles for *[L'Enseignement Mathématique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Enseignement_Mathématique)*, *[Nouvelles Annales de Mathématiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nouvelles_Annales_de_Mathématiques)*, and other French mathematical journals, as well as a few articles for foreign mathematical journals.[^o2] He was one of nine mathematicians who read the initial page proofs and suggested improvements for the first volume of [Leonard E. Dickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_E._Dickson)'s *[History of the Theory of Numbers](wikipedia://en/37174589)*.[^o4] In that volume, Dickson and Gérardin announced for the first time that the [Mersenne number](wikipedia://en/18908) M<sub>173</sub> has the factor 730753.[^o5]

Gérardin was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1912 at Cambridge UK,[^o6][^o7][^o8] in 1920 at Strasbourg,[^o9][^o10] in 1928 at Bologna, and in 1932 at Zürich. He was the founder and editor for many years of the mathematical review *Sphinx-Oedipe*, started in 1906. In 1944 he, along with Paul Belgodère, started the journal *Intermédiarie des Recherches Mathématiques*. In 1948 Gérardin started publishing the journal *Diophante* and continued with it until his death.[^o2]

In 1949, Paul Belgodère, the director (from 1949 to 1986) of the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595), purchased the important mathematical library that Gérardin had accumulated at Nancy.[^o2]
