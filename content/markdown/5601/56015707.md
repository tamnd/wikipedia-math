# Fabio Conforto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fabio Conforto"}

**Fabio Conforto** (13 August 1909 – 24 February 1954) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] His contributed to the fields of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849) and [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202).
