# Ugo Amaldi (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ugo Amaldi"}

**Ugo Amaldi** (18 April 1875 – 11 November 1957) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] He contributed to the field of [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202) and worked on [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945). His son [Edoardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edoardo_Amaldi) was a physicist.[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) (21 November 1898) at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna) under the guidance of [S. Pincherle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._Pincherle). He taught at the [University of Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cagliari) (1903-1905), [Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Modena) (1905-1919), [Padova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) (1919-1924), [Roma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) (1924-1950).
