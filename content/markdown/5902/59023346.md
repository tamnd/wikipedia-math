# Julie Bergner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Julie Bergner"}

**Julia Elizabeth Bergner** is a mathematician specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), and [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bergner graduated from [Gonzaga University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonzaga_University) in 2000. She completed her Ph.D. at the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) in 2005. Her dissertation, *Three Models for the Homotopy Theory of Homotopy Theories*, was supervised by [William Gerard Dwyer](wikipedia://en/57424279).

After postdoctoral research at [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University), she joined the mathematics faculty at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside) in 2008. She moved from there to the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) in 2016.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Bergner is the author of the book *The homotopy theory of* ($\infty$,1)*\-categories* (London Mathematical Society Student Texts 90, Cambridge University Press, 2018).

Her other publications include:

- Bergner, Julia E. (2007). "A model category structure on the category of simplicial categories". *Transactions of the American Mathematical Society*. 359 (5). p. 2043–2058. doi:10.1090/S0002-9947-06-03987-0. mr:2276611
- Bergner, Julia E. (2007). "Three models for the homotopy theory of homotopy theories". *Topology*. 46 (4). p. 397–436. doi:10.1016/j.top.2007.03.002. arxiv:math/0504334. mr:2321038
- Bergner, Julia E. (2010). "Towards Higher Categories". 152. Springer. p. 69–83. doi:10.1007/978-1-4419-1524-5\_2. isbn:978-1-4419-1523-8. mr:2664620

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2018, the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) gave Bergner the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) for her research on [algebraic $K$-theory](wikipedia://en/598500).
