# Alpha Data

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alpha Data"}

**Alpha Data** (formerly, but commonly known as **BuzzAngle Music**)[^o3] was a [music analytics](wikipedia://en/487132) firm which provided statistics for the music industry, including record sales and music streaming.[^o4] BuzzAngle partnered with *[Rolling Stone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone)* to provide information for the magazine's [music charts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolling_Stone_charts).[^o5]

:::

BuzzAngle was founded in 2013 by Border City Media.[^o6] It used [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151) collected from platforms used by people to listen to music. Its website showed total music consumption, including album sales, song sales, streaming history, and social media analytics.[^o6] The data it collected came from retailers, record stores, radio stations, and music venues. In 2018, BuzzAngle received an investment from [Penske Media Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penske_Media_Corporation), the parent company of *Rolling Stone*.[^o7] The following year it announced a partnership with *Rolling Stone* to provide data for the magazine's music charts, which would openly compete with those of the long-established music charts in *[Billboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Billboard_%28magazine%29)*.[^o8]

In 2021, Penske Media purchased *Billboard*, and discontinued music charts in its *Rolling Stone* holding.[^o9] As of July 2024, Alpha Data/BuzzAngle has no web presence nor functioning website.
