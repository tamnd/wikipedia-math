# Timothy Chambers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timothy Chambers"}

**Timothy Chambers** is a [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) who has written a number of articles which have appeared in the journals *Mind,* the *Monist* (1998), *Philosophy* (2001), the *Aristotelian Society Proceedings* (2000), and *Ratio* (1999). His research interests include [Hilary Putnam](wikipedia://en/75233), [time travel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_travel), the [Ontological Argument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontological_Argument), the [Doomsday Argument](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doomsday_Argument) and the [semantics](wikipedia://en/29107) of [vagueness](wikipedia://en/32369). His work on Putnam included a logical formula—the Chambers Conditional[^o2]—which figures in [Crispin Wright](wikipedia://en/302883)'s recent work on vagueness.[^o3][^o4]

:::
