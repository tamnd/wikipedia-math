# Philip Franklin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Philip Franklin"}

**Philip Franklin** (October 5, 1898 – January 27, 1965) was an American mathematician and professor whose work was primarily focused in analysis.

:::

Dr. Franklin received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) in 1918 from [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York) (who later awarded him its Townsend Harris Medal for the alumnus who achieved notable postgraduate distinction). He received his [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in 1920 and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1921 both from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). His dissertation, *The Four Color Problem*, was supervised by [Oswald Veblen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oswald_Veblen). After teaching for one year at Princeton and two years at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (as the [Benjamin Peirce](wikipedia://en/364270) Instructor), Franklin joined the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) Department of Mathematics, where he stayed until his 1964 retirement.

In 1922, Franklin gave the first proof that all planar graphs with at most 25 vertices can be four-colored.[^o3]

In 1928, Franklin gave the first description of an [orthonormal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthonormal) basis for *L*²(\[0,1\]) consisting of [continuous functions](wikipedia://en/6122) (now known as "[Franklin's system](wikipedia://en/50905#The_Franklin_system)").[^o4]

In 1934, Franklin disproved the [Heawood conjecture](wikipedia://en/1585274) for the [Klein bottle](wikipedia://en/17412) by showing that any map drawn on the Klein bottle can be coloured with at most six colours. An example which shows that six colours may be needed is the 12-vertex [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) now known as the [Franklin graph](wikipedia://en/23717056).[^o7]

Franklin also worked with [Jay W. Forrester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jay_Wright_Forrester) on [Project Whirlwind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Whirlwind) at the [Office of Naval Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Naval_Research) (ONR).

Franklin was editor of the [MIT Journal of Mathematics and Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Journal_of_Mathematics_and_Physics) from 1929.

In 1940, his comprehensive [textbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) [*A Treatise on Advanced Calculus*](https://archive.org/details/a_treatise-on-advanced-calculus_-_philip_franklin) was first published.

Franklin was married to [Norbert Wiener](wikipedia://en/63185)'s sister Constance. Their son-in-law is [Václav E. Beneš](wikipedia://en/12676927).[^o8]
