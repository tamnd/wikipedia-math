# Chen Greif

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chen Greif"}

**Chen Greif** (חן גרייף) is a professor and former department head of computer science at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia). In March 2022 he was elected a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) for "contributions to scientific computing, especially in numerical linear algebra and its applications."[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Greif attended [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), earning a bachelor's degree 1991 and a master's degree in 1994 in mathematics. He continued his education at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia), where he was awarded a PhD in Applied Mathematics in 1998. He was also a postdoctoral fellow at Stanford University from 1998 to 2000.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

His research is primarily concerning scientific computing and more specifically:

- Matrix theory and analysis
- Iterative solvers and preconditioning techniques for sparse linear systems
- Saddle-point linear systems
- Numerical solution of elliptic partial differential equations
- Linear algebra aspects of constrained optimization problems
