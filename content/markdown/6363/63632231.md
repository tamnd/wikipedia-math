# Lori Thombs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lori Thombs"}

**Lori A. Thombs** is an American statistician whose interests include [social statistics](wikipedia://en/285943), [time series](wikipedia://en/406624), and [resampling](wikipedia://en/3763850). She is an associate professor of statistics at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri), where she directs the Social Science Statistics Center, and president of the Southern Regional Council On Statistics.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Thombs is a 1981 graduate of [Baylor University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baylor_University), and completed a Ph.D. in statistics at [Southern Methodist University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Methodist_University) in 1985. Her dissertation, *Bootstrap Prediction Intervals for Autoregression*, was supervised by William R. Schucany.

She joined the statistics department at the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina), in [Columbia, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia,_South_Carolina), in 1985, and moved to the University of Missouri, in [Columbia, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia,_Missouri), in 2003.

She was elected president of the Southern Regional Council On Statistics in 2018.

## Books {#Books}

Thombs is a coauthor, with Don Edwards and John D. Spurrier, of the books *Elementary Statistics Laboratory Manual* (in two versions, Wadsworth, 1995) and *Statistics: Learning by Doing* (Whittier, 1999). She is a coeditor, with Stanislav Kolenikov and Douglas Steinle, of *Statistics in the Social Sciences: Current Methodological Developments* (Wiley, 2010).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Thombs was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2011.
