# Jan S. Hesthaven

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan S. Hesthaven"}

**Jan Sickmann Hesthaven** (born 10 December 1965) is a Danish mathematician,[^o5] currently president of the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology).[^o6] He was previously Vice President for Academic Affairs (starting in 2021)[^o7] at [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPFL) ([École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique_fédérale_de_Lausanne)) and held the Chair of Computational Mathematics and Simulation Science (MCSS).[^o8] He is particularly known for contributions to the development, analysis and application of high-order accurate computational methods for time-dependent partial differential equations.[^o4] He has also contributed substantially to the development of reduced order models and the application of neural networks and machine learning techniques to problems in science and engineering.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Prof. Hesthaven[^o5] obtained a Master of Science degree in computational physics from the [Technical University of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Denmark) (DTU) in 1991. In 1995, he received a Ph.D. in Numerical Analysis from the Institute of Mathematical Modelling ([DTU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Denmark)) and in 2009 he was awarded the degree of Dr.Techn. from [DTU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Denmark) for substantial and lasting contributions that has helped to move his research area forward and penetrated into applications.

After graduation, he was appointed in 1995 as Visiting Assistant Professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), then in 1999 as assistant professor and in 2003 he was promoted associate professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) with tenure at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) where in July 2005 he was promoted to professor of applied mathematics.

In 2006, he founded the [Center for Computation and Visualization (CCV)](https://ccv.brown.edu/) (9 August 2020). <https://web.archive.org/web/20200809024633/https://ccv.brown.edu/> at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) and was its director until 2013.[^o10]

He served from Aug 2010 to June 2013 as founding deputy director of the Institute of Computational and Experimental Research in Mathematics [ICERM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICERM), an [NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Mathematical Sciences Research Institute.

In 2013, he joined [EPFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPFL) ([École polytechnique fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique_fédérale_de_Lausanne)) where he was appointed full professor of computational mathematics and simulation science,[^o11] and shortly later, in February 2014, he founded the new unit of Scientific IT and Application Support (SCITAS)[^o12]

From 2016 to 2021 he served as Editor in Chief of [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Journal of Scientific Computing.

In February 2017, he became dean of the School of Basic Sciences (SB).[^o13] In September 2020, he was appointed as Vice President for Academic Affairs at EPFL for a term starting in 2021.[^o14]

In January 2024, he was elected as the future president of the German [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology) by the supervisory board.[^o15] The election was confirmed by the Senate on 19 February 2024.[^o16] He has assumed the presidency as of the 1st of October 2024.[^o17]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Brown University gave him their Philip J. Bray Award for Teaching Excellence in 2004.[^o19] He was elected as a [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Fellow in 2014.[^o4] He was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to computational methods for PDEs, high-order accurate methods, and the reduced order method".[^o20]

In 2022 he was inducted as member of the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters).[^o21] In 2023, he was elected as a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o22] and the [European Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o23]

In 2024, the [Technical University of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Denmark) awarded him an honorary doctorate in recognition of his scientific contributions [^o24]
