# Robert E. Valett

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert E. Valett"}

**Robert E. Valett** (November 22, 1927 – November 14, 2008) was an American [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology) professor who wrote more than 20 books primarily focused on [educational psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_psychology). He earned the distinguished psychologist award from the [San Joaquin Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Joaquin_Psychological_Association)[^o3] and was a president of the [California Association of School Psychologists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Association_of_School_Psychologists).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Robert Edward Valett was born in [Clinton, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton,_Iowa) on November 22, 1927. His father, Edward John Valett, worked for the railroad as a pipe fitter and his mother, Myrtle (née Peterson), was a saleswoman. Valett attended [Clinton High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinton_High_School_%28Iowa%29) while also achieving the rank of [Eagle Scout](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eagle_Scout_%28Boy_Scouts_of_America%29) in the Boy Scouts of America.[^o5] During [World War 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), he served in the [U.S. Navy Medical Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Navy_Medical_Corps). He then did his undergraduate work at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) and [George Williams College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Williams_College,_Chicago). Valett went on to earn an [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) (1951[^o6] ) and an ([Ed.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Education)) in educational psychology from the [University of California in Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Valett was a professor of psychology at [Orange Coast College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orange_Coast_College) in [Costa Mesa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa_Mesa,_California), Ca., and the [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury) in New Zealand and taught psychology from 1970 to 1992 at [California State University, Fresno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_Fresno) where he was named Professor Emeritus. He authored several books on [learning disabilities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_disability), [child development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_development), [dyslexia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dyslexia) and attention disorders/[hyperactivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Attention_deficit_hyperactivity_disorder). He received the distinguished psychologist award from the San Joaquin Psychological Association in 1982 and served as president of the California Association of School Psychologists from 1971 to 1972.[^o3][^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1950, Valett married Shirley Bellman with whom he had five children. He died on November 14, 2008, in [Fresno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fresno,_California), California.
