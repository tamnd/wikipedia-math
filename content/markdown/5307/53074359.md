::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 truncated pentagonal hexecontahedron"}

The **order-5 truncated pentagonal hexecontahedron** is a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with 72 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926): 60 hexagons and 12 pentagons triangular, with 210 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 140 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417). Its dual is the [pentakis snub dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/31224085).

:::

It is [Goldberg polyhedron](wikipedia://en/39781140) {5+,3}<sub>2,1</sub> in the icosahedral family, with chiral symmetry. The relationship between pentagons steps into 2 hexagons away, and then a turn with one more step.

It is a [Fullerene](wikipedia://en/10628) C140.[^o9]

::: {#o11 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It is explicitly called a *pentatruncated pentagonal hexecontahedron* since only the valence-5 vertices of the [pentagonal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/727271) are truncated.[^o12]

*Image: Pentagonal hexecontahedron.png.*

Its topology can be constructed in [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705) as *t5gD* and more simply *wD* as a *whirled dodecahedron*, reducing original pentagonal faces and adding 5 distorted hexagons around each, in clockwise or counter-clockwise forms. This picture shows its flat construction before the geometry is adjusted into a more spherical form. The snub can create a (5,3) geodesic polyhedron by k5k6.

*Image: Conway\_polyhedron\_wD-flat.png.*

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

The whirled dodecahedron creates more polyhedra by basic [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705). The zip whirled dodecahedron makes a chamfered truncated icosahedron, and Goldberg (4,1). Whirl applied twice produces Goldberg (5,3), and applied twice with reverse orientations produces goldberg (7,0).
