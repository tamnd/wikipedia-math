# M5-brane

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M5-brane"}

In [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028), an **M5-brane** is a fundamental [brane](wikipedia://en/26010282) of [M-theory](wikipedia://en/20406). As such, it can be described explicitly as a black brane solution[^o2] to [eleven-dimensional supergravity](wikipedia://en/77139118), the low-energy limit of M-theory. In particular, it carries a [magnetic charge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_charge) under the 3-form gauge field of the 11-dimensional [supergravity multiplet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermultiplet). The M5-brane is the [electric-magnetic dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montonen–Olive_duality) of the [M2-brane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M2-brane).

:::

Upon [compactification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactification_%28physics%29), the M5-brane becomes either the [D4-brane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D4-brane) or the [NS5-brane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NS5-brane) of [type IIA supergravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_IIA_supergravity), depending on whether the 11-dimensional theory is reduced along a direction parallel or orthogonal to the M5-brane, respectively.

The worldvolume theory on a stack of M5-branes at low energies is the [six-dimensional N=(2,0) superconformal field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6D_%282,0%29_superconformal_field_theory) (SCFT) with [gauge algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_group) of the [A-type](wikipedia://en/648042).[^o3] Other gauge algebras can be realised by placing the stack of branes on a singularity.[^o4]

The PST model provides a Green-Schwarz description of the worldvolume physics of a single M5-brane.[^o5] On the other hand, direct knowledge of the non-Abelian case, for instance via a [Lagrangian](wikipedia://en/23371726) description, is not presently available, due to various technical difficulties. Since superconformal symmetry is only available in 6 or lower spacetime dimensions, this maximally [supersymmetric](wikipedia://en/224636) theory may be used to derive an infinite number of other theories. Furthermore, via the [AdS/CFT correspondence](wikipedia://en/644443), the M5-brane worldvolume theory can be used to study [M-theory](wikipedia://en/20406) on asymptotically [Anti-de Sitter (AdS) spacetimes](wikipedia://en/301670).

Despite the lack of a Lagrangian formulation, brane constructions provide evidence that the M5-brane worldvolume theory can support four- and two-codimensional solitonic excitations, namely self-dual strings and three-branes.
