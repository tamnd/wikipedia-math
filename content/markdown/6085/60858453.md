# Julia Gordon (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julia Gordon"}

**Julia Gordon** is a Canadian mathematician at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) whose research concerns [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), including [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [$p$-adic groups](wikipedia://en/51423), [motivic integration](wikipedia://en/6838270), and the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

Gordon earned her PhD at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 2003 under the supervision of [Thomas C. Hales](wikipedia://en/2388356) and [Robert Griess](wikipedia://en/5034215). Her dissertation was *Some Applications of Motivic Integration to the Representation Theory of P-adic Groups*. After postdoctoral research at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) and the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768), she joined the University of British Columbia faculty in 2006. , she is an associate professor there. In 2026, Gordon is listed as an associate professor.[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2017, Gordon won the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).She served her Michler Fellowship at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o5] She is the 2019 winner of the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).
