# Don Blasius

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Don Blasius"}

**Don Malcolm Blasius** (born 5 September 1950 in [Paterson, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paterson,_New_Jersey))[^o2] is an American mathematician.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Blasius graduated from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1972 with a bachelor's degree and from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 1977 with a bachelor's degree. He received his PhD in 1981 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) with thesis *Arithmetic of Monomial Relations between the Periods of Abelian Varieties* under the supervision of [Gorō Shimura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gorō_Shimura). Blasius was an assistant professor from 1981 to 1985 at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and from 1985 to 1987 at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). He became in 1987 an associate professor at [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York) and in 1989 a full professor at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).

His research deals with number theory, arithmetic geometry, and automorphic forms, in particular, Hilbert modular forms and zeta functions of Shimura varieties.[^o5]

He was a visiting professor at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in 1989, at the Max Planck Institute in Bonn, and at the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837).

He is on the editorial staff of the *[Pacific Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Journal_of_Mathematics)*. In 1981 and 1989/90 he was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He was elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (Class of 2018).
