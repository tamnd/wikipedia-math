# László Lempert

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="László Lempert"}

**László Lempert** (4 June 1952, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest)) is a [Hungarian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_American) mathematician, working in several complex variables and complex geometry.[^o3] He proved that the Carathéodory and [Kobayashi distances](wikipedia://en/31025902) agree on convex domains.[^o4] He further proved that a compact, strictly pseudoconvex real analytic hypersurface can be embedded into the unit sphere of a Hilbert space.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Lempert graduated from the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) in 1975. He was at the Analysis Department of the same university (1977–1988) and is a professor of [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) since 1988. He was a visiting research fellow at the [Université de Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Paris_VII) (1979–1980), visiting lecturer at the [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (1984–1985), and visiting professor at the [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) (1994–1995).

## Degrees, awards {#Degrees,_awards}

Lempert received the Candidate of the mathematical sciences degree from the Hungarian Academy of Sciences in 1984. He was an invited session speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in Berkeley, California, 1986. He won the [Stefan Bergman](wikipedia://en/9395687#The_Bergman_Prize) Prize in 2001. He was elected an external member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) (2004). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
