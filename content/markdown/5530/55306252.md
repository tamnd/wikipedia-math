::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 octahedral honeycomb"}

In the [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-5 octahedral honeycomb** is a regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,4,5}. It has five [octahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octahedra) {3,4} around each edge. All vertices are ultra-ideal (existing beyond the ideal boundary) with infinitely many octahedra existing around each vertex in an [order-5 square tiling](wikipedia://en/9421082) [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644).
