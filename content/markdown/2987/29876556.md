# Group of symplectic type

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Group of symplectic type"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), a **p-group of symplectic type** is a [*p*-group](wikipedia://en/24664) such that all [characteristic](wikipedia://en/7198) [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974) [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011) are [cyclic](wikipedia://en/52327).

:::

According to (Thompson 1968, p.386), the *p*-groups of symplectic type were classified by P. Hall in unpublished lecture notes, who showed that they are all a central product of an [extraspecial group](wikipedia://en/6990619) with a group that is cyclic, [dihedral](wikipedia://en/100267), quasidihedral, or quaternion. (Gorenstein 1980, 5.4.9) gives a [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) of this result.

The **width** *n* of a group *G* of symplectic type is the largest integer *n* such that the group contains an extraspecial subgroup *H* of [order](wikipedia://en/493527) *p*<sup>1+2*n*</sup> such that *G* = *H*.*C*<sub>*G*</sub>(*H*), or 0 if *G* contains no such subgroup.

Groups of symplectic type appear in [centralizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) of involutions of [groups of GF(2)-type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groups_of_GF%282%29-type).
