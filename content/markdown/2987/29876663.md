# Group of GF(2)-type

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Group of GF(2)-type"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), a **group of GF(2)-type** is a group with an involution [centralizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) whose [generalized Fitting subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_Fitting_subgroup) is a [group of symplectic type](wikipedia://en/29876556) (Gorenstein 1982, definition 1.45).

:::

As the name suggests, many of the [groups of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) over the [field with 2 elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_with_2_elements) are groups of GF(2)-type. Also 16 of the 26 [sporadic groups](wikipedia://en/324806) are of GF(2)-type, suggesting that in some sense sporadic groups are somehow related to special properties of the field with 2 elements.

(Timmesfeld 1978) showed roughly that groups of GF(2)-type can be subdivided into 8 types. The groups of each of these 8 types were classified by various authors. They consist mainly of groups of Lie type with all roots of the same length over the field with 2 elements, but also include many exceptional cases, including the majority of the sporadic groups. (Smith 1980) gave a survey of this work.

(Smith 1979, p.279) gives a table of [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600) containing a large [extraspecial](wikipedia://en/6990619) [2-group](wikipedia://en/24664).
