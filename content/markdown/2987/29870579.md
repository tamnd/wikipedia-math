# Libgcrypt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Libgcrypt"}

**Libgcrypt** is a cryptography library developed as a separated module of [GnuPG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GnuPG).[^o5] It can also be used independently of GnuPG, but depends on its error-reporting library Libgpg-error.[^o6]

:::

It provides functions for all fundamental cryptographic building blocks:

| Primitive or Operation | Algorithms or Implementation[^o8] |

Libgcrypt features its own [*multiple precision arithmetic*](wikipedia://en/600892) implementation, with assembler implementations for a variety of processors, including Alpha, AMD64, HP PA-RISC, i386, i586, M68K, MIPS 3, PowerPC, and SPARC. It also features an *[entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropy_%28computing%29) gathering* utility, coming in different versions for Unix-like and Windows machines.

Usually multiple, stable branches of Libgcrypt are maintained in parallel; as of May 2026 this is the Libgrypt 1.12 branch as [LTS ("long-term support")](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long-term_support) branch, the 1.11 branch as previous [stable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_release_life_cycle#Stable_release) branch, which will be maintained at least until 2028-06-30[^o24] , and the 1.8 branch as "old-LTS" branch, which is still maintained as long a customer with an "extended Long Term Support contract" exists, despite its original [end-of-life](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_release_life_cycle#End-of-life) date being 2024-12-31.
