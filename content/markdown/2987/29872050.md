# Richard Balam

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Balam"}

**Richard Balam** ([fl.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floruit) 1653), was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Balam was the author of *Algebra, or the Doctrine of composing, inferring, and resolving an Equation* (1653).[^o2] It is a possible source of developments in [John Wallis](wikipedia://en/239290), *Mathesis Universalis* (1657), relating to [geometric progressions](wikipedia://en/23474670) treated as an [axiomatic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiomatic_theory).[^o3]
