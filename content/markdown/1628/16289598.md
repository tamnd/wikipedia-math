# SCORUS

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SCORUS"}

An acronym for "Standing Committee of Regional and Urban Statistics", **SCORUS** is a sub-committee of the [International Association for Official Statistics](wikipedia://en/38407748) (IAOS) which is a section of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). The sub-committee has specific responsibility for regional and urban [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and research. Its members form a dedicated international network of persons interested in regional and urban statistical issues.

:::
