# Bjorn Poonen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bjorn Poonen"}

**Bjorn Mikhail Poonen** (born July 27, 1968, in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston), Massachusetts) is a mathematician, four-time [Putnam Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition) winner, and a Distinguished Professor in Science in the Department of Mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3] His research is primarily in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), but he has occasionally published in other subjects such as [probability](wikipedia://en/22934)[^o4] and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).[^o5] He has edited two books.[^o6][^o7]

:::

He is the founding managing editor of the journal *[Algebra & Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_&_Number_Theory)*,[^o8] and serves also on the editorial boards of *[Involve: A Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Involve:_A_Journal_of_Mathematics)*[^o9] and the *[A K Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_K_Peters) Research Notes in Mathematics* book series.[^o10]

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Poonen was born on July 27, 1968 in Boston to an Indian father and Norwegian-American mother.[^o12] He attended the [Hampshire College Summer Studies in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/242549) while a student at [Winchester High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winchester_High_School_%28Massachusetts%29) in [Winchester, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winchester,_Massachusetts), where he graduated in 1985.[^o13] In 1989, Poonen graduated from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) with an [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.B.) in Mathematics and Physics, summa cum laude. He then studied under [Kenneth Alan Ribet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Alan_Ribet) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), completing a PhD there in 1994.

## Academic positions {#Academic_positions}

Poonen held postdoctoral positions at [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and served on the faculty of the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 1997 to 2008, before moving to [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o10] He has also held visiting positions at the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) (1998 and 2005), the [Université Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University) (2001), Harvard (2007), and MIT (2007).[^o10]

## Major honors and awards {#Major_honors_and_awards}

- [Joseph L. Doob Prize](wikipedia://en/70444084), 2023[^o17]
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2012.[^o18]
- [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences): elected in 2012[^o19]
- [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize): the 2011 winner, for his article "Undecidability in number theory"[^o20][^o21]
- [Miller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Institute) Research Professorship – University of California Berkeley.
- [David and Lucile Packard Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_and_Lucile_Packard_Foundation)[^o22]
- [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship)[^o23]
- [William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition): winner in 1985, 1986, 1987, and 1988 (the only other four-time winners since 1938 are [Don Coppersmith](wikipedia://en/540090), [Arthur Rubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Rubin), [Ravi D. Vakil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravi_D._Vakil), [Gabriel Carroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Carroll), [Reid W. Barton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reid_W._Barton), [Daniel Kane](wikipedia://en/12013216) and [Brian R. Lawrence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brian_R._Lawrence)).[^o24]
- [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208): silver medalist in 1985.[^o25]
- [American High School Mathematics Examination](wikipedia://en/665435): only participant (out of 380,000) to receive a perfect score in 1985.[^o26]

## Trivia {#Trivia}

- Poonen co-authored a paper entitled "How to spread rumors fast".[^o28]
