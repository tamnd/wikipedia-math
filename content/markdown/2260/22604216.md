# Professor of Mathematical Statistics (Cambridge)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Professor of Mathematical Statistics"}

The **Professorship of Mathematical Statistics** at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) was established in 1961[^o2] with the support of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) and the aid of donations from various companies and banks.[^o3][^o4] It was the first professorship in the [Statistical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge), and the first in [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) explicitly intended for the study of statistics. Until 1973 the professor was *[ex officio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ex_officio)* Director of the Statistical Laboratory.

:::
