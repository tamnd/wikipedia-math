# Ruled join

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ruled join"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), given irreducible subvarieties *V*, *W* of a [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) **P**<sup>*n*</sup>, the **ruled join** of *V* and *W* is the union of all lines from *V* to *W* in **P**<sup>2*n*+1</sup>, where *V*, *W* are embedded into **P**<sup>2*n*+1</sup> so that the last (resp. first) *n* + 1 coordinates on *V* (resp. *W*) vanish.[^o2] It is denoted by *J*(*V*, *W*). For example, if *V* and *W* are linear subspaces, then their join is the [linear span](wikipedia://en/56353) of them, the smallest linear subcontaining them.

:::

The join of several subvarieties is defined in a similar way.
