# Formal holomorphic function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Formal holomorphic function"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **formal holomorphic function** along a subvariety *V* of an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) *W* is an algebraic analog of a [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110) defined in a neighborhood of *V*. They are sometimes just called holomorphic functions when no confusion can arise. They were introduced by (txt).

:::

The theory of formal holomorphic functions has largely been replaced by the theory of [formal schemes](wikipedia://en/18829483) which generalizes it: a formal holomorphic function on a variety is essentially just a section of the structure sheaf of a related formal scheme.

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

If *V* is an affine subvariety of the affine variety *W* defined by an ideal *I* of the coordinate ring *R* of *W*, then a formal holomorphic function along *V* is just an element of the [completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completion_%28algebra%29) of *R* at the ideal *I*.

In general holomorphic functions along a subvariety *V* of *W* are defined by gluing together holomorphic functions on affine subvarieties.

:::
