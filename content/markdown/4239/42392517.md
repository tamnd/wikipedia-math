# Chasles' theorem (geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chasles' theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Chasles' theorem** says that if two pencils of curves have no curves in common, then the intersections of those curves form another [pencil of curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil_of_curves) the degree of which can be calculated from the degrees of the initial two pencils.

:::

The result is attributed to [Michel Chasles](wikipedia://en/2252716) (1793–1880).
