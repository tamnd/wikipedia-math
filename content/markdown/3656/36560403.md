# V. J. Havel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="V. J. Havel"}

**Václav Jaromír Havel** is a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechs) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is known for characterizing the [degree sequences](wikipedia://en/1374448) of [undirected graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) and the [Havel–Hakimi algorithm](wikipedia://en/43304405). It is an important contribution to graph theory. [^o2]

:::
