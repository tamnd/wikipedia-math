# Asclepius of Tralles

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Asclepius of Tralles"}

*Image: Asclepio di tralle, commentario al libro alpha meizon della metafisica di aristotele (in greco), corfù 1564 (pluteo 86.11).jpg.* Comentarius of *Alpha Meizon* from Aristotle's Metaphysics

**Asclepius of [Tralles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tralles)** (Ἀσκληπιός; died c. 560–570) was a [Neoplatonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoplatonic) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was a student of [Ammonius Hermiae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonius_Hermiae). Two works of his survive:

:::

- Commentary on [Aristotle](wikipedia://en/308)'s [*Metaphysics*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics_%28Aristotle%29), books I-VII (*In Aristotelis metaphysicorum libros Α - Ζ (1 - 7) commentaria*, ed. Michael Hayduck, Commentaria in Aristotelem Graeca, VI.2, Berin: Reiner, 1888).
- Commentary on [Nicomachus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicomachus)' *[Introduction to Arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Introduction_to_Arithmetic)* (Leonardo Tarán, *Asclepius of Tralles, Commentary to Nicomachus' Introduction to Arithmetic*, Transactions of the American Philosophical Society (n.s.), 59: 4. Philadelphia, 1969.

Both works seem to be notes on the lectures conducted by [Ammonius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ammonius_Hermiae).
