# Bracket algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bracket algebra"}

In mathematics, a **bracket algebra** is an algebraic system that connects the notion of a [supersymmetry algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersymmetry_algebra) with a symbolic representation of [projective invariants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_invariant).

:::

Given that *L* is a proper signed alphabet and Super\[*L*\] is the supersymmetric algebra, the bracket algebra Bracket\[*L*\] of dimension *n* over the field *K* is the quotient of the algebra Brace{*L*} obtained by imposing the congruence relations below, where *w*, *w**, ...,*****w*" are any monomials in Super\[*L*\]:***

1. {*w*} = 0 if length(*w*) ≠ *n*
1. {*w*}{*w*'}...{*w*"} = 0 whenever any positive letter *a* of *L* occurs more than *n* times in the monomial {*w*}{*w**}...{*****w*"}.***
1. Let {*w*}{*w**}...{*****w*"} be a monomial in Brace{*L*} in which some positive letter*a*occurs more than*n*times, and let*b*,*c*,*d*,*e*, ...,*f*,*g*be any letters in*L*.***
