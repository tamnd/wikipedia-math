# Joseph Gillis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Gillis"}

**Joseph E. Gillis** (יוסף גיליס; 3 August 1911 – 18 November 1993) was a British-Israeli mathematician and one of the founders of the Faculty of Mathematics at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), where he served as a professor of Applied Mathematics. He made notable contributions to fractal sets, fluid dynamics, random walks, and pioneered the combinatorial theory of special functions of mathematical physics.[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Gillis was born on 3 August 1911 in [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland), in the north east of England. He studied at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), completing his doctoral thesis on "Some Geometrical Properties of Linearly Measurable Plane Sets of Points" under A.S. Besicovitch in 1935.[^o6] During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) he worked in [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037) as a cryptographer. He taught maths at [Queen's University Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_Belfast)[^o7] between 1937 and 1947.[^o7]

In 1948 he immigrated to Israel and joined the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science) (then the Ziv Institute), where he became a founding member of the Department of Applied Mathematics. During the Academic Year 1954-1955 he visited the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) as part of the Electronic Computer Project headed by [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942). He was very active in advancing mathematics education, and chaired the department of Science Teaching at the Weizmann Institute. He also started the Israel [Mathematics Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_Olympiad) and coached the Israeli team for many years, as well as edited mathematics periodicals for high school students and amateurs.[^o8]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He was married to [Olga Kirsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Kirsch) and had two daughters. He died on 18 November 1993.[^o10]
