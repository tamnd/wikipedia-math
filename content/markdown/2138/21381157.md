# Heinrich Kleisli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Kleisli"}

*Image: Heinrich-Kleisli-1987.jpeg.* Heinrich Kleisli in 1987

**Heinrich Kleisli** (; October 19, 1930 – April 5, 2011) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_%28people%29) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is the namesake of several constructions in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), including the [Kleisli category](wikipedia://en/3957200) and [Kleisli triples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleisli_triple). He is also the namesake of the [Kleisli Query System](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kleisli_Query_System), a tool for integration of heterogeneous databases developed at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).

:::

Kleisli earned his Ph.D. at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in 1960, having been supervised by [Beno Eckmann](wikipedia://en/19613365) and [Ernst Specker](wikipedia://en/22209158). His dissertation was on [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) and [abelian categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_categories). He served as an associate professor at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa) before relocating to the [University of Fribourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Fribourg) in 1966. He became a full professor at Fribourg in 1967.
