# Yeshimabeit Milner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yeshimabeit Milner"}

**Yeshimabeit "Yeshi" Milner** is an American technologist and activist.[^o3][^o4] She is the executive director and co-founder of *[Data for Black Lives](wikipedia://en/69290079)*.[^o5][^o6]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Yeshimabeit Milner grew up in [Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami), [Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida).[^o8][^o13][^o9] Starting in her late teens, Milner became involved in activism and data science.[^o10][^o11][^o12] She worked with the *Power U Center for Social Change* as a high school senior.[^o3][^o13] Milner attended [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), graduating in 2012 with a BA degree in [Africana Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Africana_studies).[^o14][^o9]

## Career {#Career}

In 2013 at age 22, after returning to Miami after college, Milner started working with the *Power U Center for Social Change* and looking at Black infant mortality rates locally in trying to understand why they were disproportionately so high.[^o16][^o13] They were able to retrieve data from 300 mothers, and as a result changed local policy.[^o16]

One of her classmates at Brown University was mathematician Lucas Mason-Brown, together they founded Data for Black Lives in November 2017.[^o17][^o18] The Data for Black Lives (D4BL) annual conference was started in 2018 by Yeshimabeit Milner and Lucas Mason- Brown.[^o19] They use the slogan, "Abolish Big Data!" with hopes to redesign big data and to "put data into the hands of those who need it most".[^o20][^o21] In 2020, the group was able to compile state-level data about the impact of COVID-19 on Black people and are working on compiling a nationwide database of technologies used by police departments.[^o22] In 2021, Milner co-wrote a research piece for [Demos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demos_%28U.S._think_tank%29) on [algorithmic racism](wikipedia://en/55817338) from [Big Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Tech) companies.[^o23][^o24]

## Awards and accolades {#Awards_and_accolades}

Milner served on the board of the [Highlander Research and Education Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Highlander_Research_and_Education_Center) in Tennessee.[^o3] In 2018, she was awarded a Roddenberry Foundation Fellowship, which honors and invests in extraordinary people who can change the world.[^o26] In 2020, Data for Black Lives and its founders were awarded the [Forbes 30 Under 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbes_30_Under_30) and the New York Times 2020 Good Tech Awards.[^o5][^o27]
