# J. N. Srivastava

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="J. N. Srivastava"}

**Jagdish Narain Srivastava** (1933–2010) was an [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India)-born [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University).[^o2] Srivastava is known for the research in the area of [Design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541),[^o3] [Multivariate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis) and [Combinatorial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_mathematics). Srivastava was a Fellow of [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

:::

J. N. Srivastava received a Ph.D. in 1962 [^o4] from the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill). Prof. [R. C. Bose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._C._Bose) was Srivastava's advisor. He joined [Colorado State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorado_State_University) in 1966.[^o5]

He was known for his contributions in design of experiments as well as in multivariate analysis, survey sampling, reliability, [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869), combinatorial theory, and other areas of statistics and mathematics.[^o6] [Srivastava code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Srivastava_code) was invented by him.

[Gödel's incompleteness theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel's_incompleteness_theorem) inspired him to recognize the limitations of science. He slowly turned toward spirituality and studied all the major religions of the world. This led him to obtain his 1991 joint appointment in the philosophy department of CSU.

After his retirement, he moved to California. He died on 18 November 2010.[^o7] He is survived by his wife of sixty years Usha, son Arvind, daughter Gita and son-in-law Roy; son [Ashok](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashok_Srivastava), daughter-in-law Lynn and granddaughter Leela.
