# Paranormal operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paranormal operator"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675), a **paranormal operator** is a generalization of a [normal operator](wikipedia://en/190167). More precisely, a [bounded](wikipedia://en/455961) [linear operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator) *T* on a complex [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H* is said to be paranormal if:

:::

$$\|T^2x\| \ge \|Tx\|^2$$

for every unit vector *x* in *H*.

The class of paranormal operators was introduced by V. Istratescu in 1960s, though the term "paranormal" is probably due to Furuta.[^o2][^o3]

Every [hyponormal operator](wikipedia://en/21498423) (in particular, a [subnormal operator](wikipedia://en/7930037), a [quasinormal operator](wikipedia://en/7771798) and a normal operator) is paranormal. If *T* is a paranormal, then *T*<sup>*n*</sup> is paranormal.[^o3] On the other hand, [Halmos](wikipedia://en/318598) gave an example of a hyponormal operator *T* such that *T*<sup>2</sup> isn't hyponormal. Consequently, not every paranormal operator is hyponormal.[^o4]

A [compact](wikipedia://en/577441) paranormal operator is normal.[^o5]
