The **compound of four octahedra** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809). It's composed of a symmetric arrangement of 4 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635), considered as triangular [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms). It can be constructed by superimposing four identical octahedra, and then rotating each by 60 degrees about a separate axis (that passes through the centres of two opposite octahedral faces).

:::

Its dual is the [compound of four cubes](wikipedia://en/61587481).
