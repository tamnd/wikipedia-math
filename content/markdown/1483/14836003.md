This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 10 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668), aligned with the axes of three-fold [rotational symmetry](wikipedia://en/701096) of an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

(±$\sqrt{3}$, ±(τ<sup>−1</sup>−τ$\sqrt{3}$), ±(τ+τ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{3}$))

(±2$\sqrt{3}$, ±τ<sup>−1</sup>, ±τ)

(±(1+$\sqrt{3}$), ±(1−τ$\sqrt{3}$), ±(1+τ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{3}$))

(±(τ−τ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{3}$), ±$\sqrt{3}$, ±(τ<sup>−1</sup>+τ$\sqrt{3}$))

(±(1−τ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{3}$), ±(1−$\sqrt{3}$), ±(1+τ$\sqrt{3}$))

where τ = (1+$\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) (sometimes written φ).
