# List of presidents of the American Statistical Association

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of presidents of the American Statistical Association"}

The **president of the American Statistical Association** is the head of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA). According to the association's [bylaws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bylaws), the president is an [officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_title), and a member of the [board of directors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_directors) and of the [executive committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_committee). Elections for the position are held annually, in which all full members are eligible to vote. The term in office is typically three years, as president-elect and past president are also official positions.

:::
