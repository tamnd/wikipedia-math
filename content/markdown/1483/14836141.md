::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of eight triangular prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 8 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), aligned in pairs with the axes of three-fold rotational symmetry of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635). It results from composing the two [enantiomorphs](wikipedia://en/1170160) of the [compound of 4 triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/14836112).
