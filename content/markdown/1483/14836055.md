::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of four hexagonal prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 4 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668), aligned with the axes of threefold rotational symmetry of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635). It shares the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as a nonuniform [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the permutations of

(±1, ±(1−$\sqrt{6}$), ±(1+$\sqrt{6}$))
