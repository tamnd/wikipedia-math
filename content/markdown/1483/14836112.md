::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of four triangular prisms"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a [chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160) symmetric arrangement of 4 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), aligned with the axes of three-fold [rotational symmetry](wikipedia://en/701096) of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the [even permutations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Even_permutation) of

(±1, ±(1+$\sqrt{2}$), ±(1−$\sqrt{2}$))

with an even number of minuses in the '±' choices, together with all the odd permutations with an odd number of minuses in the '±' choices.
