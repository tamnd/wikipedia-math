# Journal of Statistical Computation and Simulation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Statistical Computation and Simulation"}

The ***Journal of Statistical Computation and Simulation*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that covers [computational statistics](wikipedia://en/15832717). It is published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) and was established in 1972. The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Richard Krutchkoff ([Virginia Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Polytechnic_Institute) and [State University, Blacksburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University,_Blacksburg)) and Andrei Volodin ([University of Regina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regina)).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [Current Index to Statistics](wikipedia://en/13958910)
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)
- [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2018 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.767.[^o4]
