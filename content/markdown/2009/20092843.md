# Statistical Applications in Genetics and Molecular Biology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Statistical Applications in Genetics and Molecular Biology"}

***Statistical Applications in Genetics and Molecular Biology*** is a bimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering the application of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) to problems in [computational biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology). It was established in 2002 and is published by [de Gruyter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_de_Gruyter). The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Guido Sanguinetti. According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2012 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.717.[^o3]

:::
