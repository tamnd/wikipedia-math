# Alfred W. Hales

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred W. Hales"}

**Alfred Washington Hales** (born November 30, 1938) is an American mathematician, a professor emeritus of mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), and one of the namesakes of the [Hales–Jewett theorem](wikipedia://en/640714). He was born in [Pasadena, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pasadena,_California), and is the older brother of [R. Stanton Hales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Stanton_Hales).[^o3]

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

As an undergraduate, Hales was a two-time [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition) for the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), in 1958 and 1959.[^o5] Hales stayed at Caltech for his graduate studies, earning his Ph.D. in 1962 under the supervision of [Robert P. Dilworth](wikipedia://en/9581646). He is the former chair of the mathematics department at UCLA, and in 2010 became chair of the board of trustees of the Institute for Pure and Applied Mathematics at UCLA.[^o7] From 1992 to 2003, he was director of the [IDA Center for Communications Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Defense_Analyses#Center_for_Communications_and_Computing) in La Jolla, California.[^o8]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In 1963, Hales and [Jewett](wikipedia://en/74543296) published the [Hales–Jewett theorem](wikipedia://en/640714), which is a standard part of [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175) now. They motivated their theorem as a form of [game theory](wikipedia://en/11924): it shows that certain high-dimensional generalizations of [tic tac toe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tic_tac_toe) cannot have any tied positions.

Hales also contributed to [Solomon W. Golomb](wikipedia://en/1860707)'s highly cited work on [shift registers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift_register),[^o10] and he has been noted for his work using [Ulm invariants](wikipedia://en/27972322) to characterize infinite [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974).[^o13]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1971, Hales shared the [George Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya_Prize) with [Ronald Graham](wikipedia://en/240694), Klaus Leeb, [Bruce Lee Rothschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Lee_Rothschild), and [Robert I. Jewett](wikipedia://en/74543296), for their work in [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175).[^o12] In 2009, Hales was elected a [Fellow of the AAAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_AAAS),[^o13] and in 2013 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]
