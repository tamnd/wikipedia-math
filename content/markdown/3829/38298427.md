# Wave maps equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wave maps equation"}

In mathematical physics, the **wave maps equation** is a geometric [wave equation](wikipedia://en/33691) that solves

:::

$$D^\alpha \partial_\alpha u = 0$$

where $D$ is a [connection](wikipedia://en/308815).[^o2][^o3]

It can be considered a natural extension of the [wave equation](wikipedia://en/33691) for [Riemannian manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_manifolds).[^o4]
