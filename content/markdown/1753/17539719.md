# Luxembourg Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luxembourg Mathematical Society"}

The **Luxembourg Mathematical Society** (French: Société mathématique du Luxembourg, **SML**) was founded in January 1989 on the initiative of Professor [Jean-Paul Pier](wikipedia://en/63040345).

:::

Its mission is the promotion of Pure and Applied Mathematics. The scope of action of the SML includes

- the organization of conferences, workshops, seminars, round table discussions etc.
- the edition of books, mathematical journals, newsletters etc.
- the representation of Mathematics and the mathematics profession in Luxembourg inside and outside the Grand-Duchy of [Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg)

The SML is a member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) (EMS). It has reciprocity agreements with the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and the Royal Spanish Mathematical Society.
