# Milo Sumner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Milo Sumner"}

**Milo Sumner,** ( – 21 March 1686) [D.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Divinity) also known as **Miles Symner**, **Miles Symmes** or **Myles Symner**, was an [Anglican](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anglican) priest and academic in [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland) in the second half of the seventeenth century.[^o2]

:::

Sumner was educated at [Trinity College, Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Dublin), where he was a scholar in 1626.[^o3] He was a major in the Parliamentary Army in the [Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Civil_War).[^o4][^o5] He was appointed Fellow and Professor of Mathematics by the Parliamentary Commissioners in 1652, a position which became the [Donegall Lectureship](wikipedia://en/60213290). He became [Archdeacon of Clogher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdeacon_of_Clogher) in 1661 and then [Archdeacon of Kildare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdeacon_of_Kildare) from 1668.[^o6]
