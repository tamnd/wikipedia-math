::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Steric 5-cubes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **steric 5-cube**, **steric 5-demicube** or **sterihalf 5-cube**, is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338). There are unique 4 steric forms of the 5-cube. Steric 5-cubes have half the vertices of [stericated 5-cubes](wikipedia://en/31325772).

:::

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the 80 vertices of a steric 5-cube centered at the origin are the permutations of

(±1,±1,±1,±1,±3)

with an odd number of plus signs.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

These polytopes are based on the [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232), a member of a dimensional family of [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6842392) called [demihypercubes](wikipedia://en/6833695) for being [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the [hypercube](wikipedia://en/39783) family.

There are 23 [uniform polytera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_polyteron) (uniform 5-polytopes) that can be constructed from the D${}_{5}$ symmetry of the 5-demicube, 8 of which are unique to this family, and 15 are shared within the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) family.
