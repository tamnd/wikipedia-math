# Spherical shell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spherical shell"}

*Image: Kugelschale.svg.* spherical shell, right: two halves

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **spherical shell** (a **[ball shell](wikipedia://en/160556#Regions)**) is a generalization of an [annulus](wikipedia://en/356158) to three dimensions. It is the region of a [ball](wikipedia://en/160556) between two [concentric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentric) [spheres](wikipedia://en/27859) of differing radii.[^o6]

:::

## Volume {#Volume}

The [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) of a spherical shell is the difference between the enclosed volume of the outer sphere and the [enclosed volume of the inner sphere](wikipedia://en/27859#Enclosed_volume):

$$\begin{aligned} V &= \tfrac43\pi R^3 - \tfrac43\pi r^3 \\[3mu] &= \tfrac43\pi \bigl(R^3 - r^3\bigr) \\[3mu] &= \tfrac43\pi (R-r)\bigl(R^2 + Rr + r^2\bigr) \end{aligned}$$

where $r$ is the radius of the inner sphere and $R$ is the radius of the outer sphere.

## Approximation {#Approximation}

An approximation for the volume of a thin spherical shell is the surface area of the inner sphere multiplied by the thickness $t$ of the shell:[^o7]

$$V \approx 4 \pi r^2 t,$$

when $t$ is very small compared to $r$ ($t \ll r$).

The total surface area of the spherical shell is $4 \pi r^2$.
