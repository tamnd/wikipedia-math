# Sylvie Méléard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvie Méléard"}

**Sylvie Méléard** is a French mathematician specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), [particle systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_system), and [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454). She is editor-in-chief of *[Stochastic Processes and Their Applications](wikipedia://en/41907213)*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Méléard grew up in [Picardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Picardy) as the daughter of two high school biology teachers, and was already aiming for a career as a mathematician by the time she was ten years old. She studied at the [Lycée Janson-de-Sailly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Janson-de-Sailly) and, from 1977 to 1981, at the École normale supérieure de Fontenay-aux-Roses, where she became known for [knitting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knitting) during lectures. After earning her [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in 1981, she completed her doctorate in 1984 at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) under the supervision of [Nicole El Karoui](wikipedia://en/26412227).

She took a position at the [University of Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maine_%28France%29), and then moved to Pierre and Marie Curie University as [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) in 1989. At Pierre and Marie Curie, she earned her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1991. She became a professor at [Paris Nanterre University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Nanterre_University) in 1992, and moved again to the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in 2006.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

With Vincent Bansaye, Méléard is the author of *Stochastic Models for Structured Populations: Scaling Limits and Long Time Behavior* (Springer, 2015). She is also the author of *Modèles aléatoires en ecologie et evolution* \[*Random models in ecology and evolution*\] (Springer, 2016).

As of 2018, she is the editor-in-chief of the journal *[Stochastic Processes and Their Applications](wikipedia://en/41907213)* and, as editor, an executive member in the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In September 2018, a conference on [population dynamics](wikipedia://en/501466) was held at the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) in honor of Méléard's 60th birthday. She was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2021.
