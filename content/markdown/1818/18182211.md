# Ralph Grimaldi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ralph Grimaldi"}

**Ralph Peter Grimaldi** (born January 1943) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) who is a full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose-Hulman_Institute_of_Technology).[^o2] He is known for his textbook *Discrete and Combinatorial Mathematics: An Applied Introduction* <http://www.pearsonhighered.com/educator/academic/product/0,,0201726343,00%2Ben-USS_01DBC.html>, first published in 1985 and now in its fifth edition, and his numerous research papers.

:::

He was born and raised in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) and graduated from what is now the [State University of New York at Albany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Albany) in 1964 (B.S.) and 1965 (M.S.), then earned his Ph.D. in Mathematical Sciences in 1972 at [New Mexico State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_State_University) under Ray Mines. He previously taught at the [State University of New York at Oswego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Oswego) and has held sabbatical appointments at [Clemson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemson_University) and [New Mexico State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_State_University), as well as in industry.
