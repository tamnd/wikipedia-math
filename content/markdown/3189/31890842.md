# Bost–Connes system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bost–Connes system"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Bost–Connes system** is a [quantum statistical dynamical system](wikipedia://en/712450) related to an [algebraic number field](wikipedia://en/28730822), whose [partition function](wikipedia://en/312252) is related to the [Dedekind zeta function](wikipedia://en/1131243) of the number field. (Bost Connes 1995) introduced Bost–Connes systems by constructing one for the [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024). (Connes Marcolli Ramachandran 2005) extended the construction to imaginary [quadratic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_fields).

:::

Such systems have been studied for their connection with [Hilbert's Twelfth Problem](wikipedia://en/2164726). In the case of a Bost–Connes system over **Q**, the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) acts on the ground states of the system.
