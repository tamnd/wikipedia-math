# Cun-Quan Zhang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cun-Quan Zhang"}

**C.Q. Zhang** (full name: Cun-Quan Zhang) was Eberly Distinguished Professor[^o3] at [West Virginia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_University), is a mathematician working mainly in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He received his Ph.D. (mathematics) in 1986 from [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University), under the supervision of [Brian Alspach](wikipedia://en/42451632).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Cun-Quan Zhang does not have undergraduate education due to [Cultural Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cultural_Revolution) in China. In 1978, the first year of the return of the Nationwide Entrance Examination for Admissions to Chinese Universities and Colleges ([Gaokao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaokao) in Chinese), he took Graduate Program Admission Exam, and was admitted by Qufu Normal University. In 1981, he earned his M.Sc. ([Operational Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_Research)) with his dissertation under the supervision of Prof. Yongjin Zhu in [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences). After a brief employment in [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences), he was admitted by [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) in 1982, earned his Ph.D. (mathematics) in 1986 under the supervision of Prof. [Brian Alspach](wikipedia://en/42451632).

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1987, Zhang joined the faculty of [West Virginia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_University), where he was promoted to Associate Professor in 1989 and Professor in 1996 and Eberly Distinguished Professor in 2009.

## Research achievements and recognitions {#Research_achievements_and_recognitions}

His research accomplishments in mathematics were recognized with his appointment as an Eberly Distinguished Professor at [West Virginia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia_University).[^o3]

C.Q. Zhang is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who has contributed to [nowhere-zero flow](wikipedia://en/9909979), [cycle double cover](wikipedia://en/21486302), and [Hamiltonian cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_cycle) in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

Some major results in Zhang's research include the following:

- [Hamilton cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_cycle) problems for graphs, especially his method of vertex inserting.
- [Cycle double cover](wikipedia://en/21486302) conjecture. He (collaborated with [Brian Alspach](wikipedia://en/42451632))[^o8] proved that every cubic graph without the Petersen minor has a cycle double cover.
- Integer 3-flow problems. He (collaborated with [L.M. Lovász](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L.M._Lovász), [C. Thomassen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._Thomassen) and [Y. Wu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y._Wu))[^o9] proved that every 6-connected graph admits a nowhere-zero-3-flow, which is the best partial result to Tutte’s 3-flow conjecture.
