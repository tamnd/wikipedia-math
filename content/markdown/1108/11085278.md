# Langlands decomposition

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Langlands decomposition"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Langlands decomposition** writes a [parabolic subgroup](wikipedia://en/588356) *P* of a [semisimple Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_Lie_group) as a [product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_of_subgroups) $P=MAN$ of a reductive subgroup *M*, an [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974) subgroup *A*, and a [nilpotent subgroup](wikipedia://en/144569) *N*.

:::

::: {#o3 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

A key application is in [parabolic induction](wikipedia://en/11118768), which leads to the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905): if $G$ is a reductive algebraic group and $P=MAN$ is the Langlands decomposition of a parabolic subgroup *P*, then parabolic induction consists of taking a representation of $MA$, extending it to $P$ by letting $N$ act trivially, and [inducing](wikipedia://en/357452) the result from $P$ to $G$.

:::
