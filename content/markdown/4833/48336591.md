# Arno Kuijlaars

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arno Kuijlaars"}

*Image: Arno Kuijlaars.jpg.* Kuijlaars at KU Leuven

**Arnoldus Bernardus Jacobus Kuijlaars** (born 1963) is a Dutch mathematician, specializing in [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713).[^o2]

:::

Kuijlaars completed his undergraduate studies at the [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology) and received in 1991 his Ph.D. from [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) with thesis *Approximation of Metric Spaces with Applications in Potential Theory*. Currently he is a professor at the [Katholieke Universiteit Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Universiteit_Leuven).

In 1998 Kuijlaars won the triennially awarded Popov prize.[^o4] In 2010 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at Hyderabad. In 2011 he was elected a corresponding member of the [KNAW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koninklijke_Nederlandse_Akademie_van_Wetenschappen).[^o5][^o6] In 2013 he was elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
