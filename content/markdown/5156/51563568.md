::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="List of countries by population in 1000"}

This is a **list of countries by population in 1000**. The bulk of these numbers are sourced from Alexander V. Avakov's *Two Thousand Years of Economic Statistics*, Volume 1, pages 12 to 14, which cover population figures from the year 1000 divided into modern borders. Avakov, in turn, cites a variety of sources, mostly [Angus Maddison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angus_Maddison).
