# Superiority and inferiority ranking method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Superiority and inferiority ranking method"}

The **superiority and inferiority ranking method** (or **SIR method**) is a [multi-criteria decision making](wikipedia://en/1050551) [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_modelling) (**MCDA**) which can handle real [data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data) and provides six different preference structures for the [system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System) user. MCDM is a sub-discipline of [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) that explicitly evaluates multiple conflicting criteria in [decision making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_making), both in daily life and in settings such as business, government and medicine.

:::

## Description {#Description}

It also incorporates outranking rationale to deal with the 'poor' true-criteria preference structure which appears in selecting proper equipment. The superiority and inferiority scores are produced through the generalized criteria. The SIR method can also analyze different criteria without compiling them into a small scale as GAs.
