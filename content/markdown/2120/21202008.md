# Jami (software)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jami"}

**Jami** is a telecommunications platform for [peer-to-peer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-to-peer) and [distributed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distributed_computing) [videotelephony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Videotelephony), videoconferencing, and [voice calls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_over_IP). Jami is [free and open-source software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software) released under the [GNU GPL-3.0-or-later](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License).[^o10]

:::

## History {#History}

In December 2004, Savoir-faire Linux launched the SFLPhone project.

In November 2009, *[CIO magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CIO_magazine)* listed SFLphone among the top five [open-source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_source) [VoIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_over_IP) [softphones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Softphone) to watch.[^o6]

In May 2015, SFLphone was renamed to Ring.[^o7]

In December 2018, Ring was renamed to Jami to avoid confusion with commercial products also using the English term *Ring*.[^o8][^o9]

In 2025, *[TechRadar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TechRadar)* listed it as one alternative to [Skype](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skype) following its discontinuation.[^o10]

## Features {#Features}

Available features depend on both the Jami client and the platform used.[^o12]

- Operates on a [peer-to-peer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-to-peer) basis without a central server (apart from the centralised name server). Jami can be used on local networks without an [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) connection.[^o8]
- [End-to-end encryption](wikipedia://en/1065362) used for [chat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Online_chat), [video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Videotelephony), and [voice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voice_over_IP).[^o12]
- [SIP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Session_Initiation_Protocol)-compatible with OpenDHT support.[^o13][^o12]
- Instant messaging.[^o12]
- Call recording.[^o13][^o8]
- Audio and video calls with multi-party audio[^o13] and video conferencing.[^o14][^o12]

Usernames are stored on a private [Ethereum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethereum) blockchain administered centrally by Jami.[^o15][^o16]
