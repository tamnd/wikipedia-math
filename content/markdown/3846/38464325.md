# Thought: A Journal of Philosophy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thought: A Journal of Philosophy"}

***Thought: A Journal of Philosophy*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by the [Philosophy Documentation Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_Documentation_Center) in cooperation with the Thought Trust. The journal covers [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [philosophy of mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_mind), [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology), [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language), and [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics). The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) are [Crispin Wright](wikipedia://en/302883) (Universities of Stirling and New York), John Divers ([University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds)), and [Kathrin Glüer-Pagin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kathrin_Glüer-Pagin) ([Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University)). From 2012 to 2021 this journal was published by [Wiley-Blackwell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiley-Blackwell).

:::
