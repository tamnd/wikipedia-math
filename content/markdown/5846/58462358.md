# Hitoshi Ishii

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hitoshi Ishii"}

**Hitoshi Ishii** (石井 仁司 *Ishii Hitoshi*, born on 12 October 1947), a Japanese mathematician, who is specialized in [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).

:::

He first studied physics and then mathematics at [Waseda University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waseda_University) with a master's degree in 1972 and a doctorate in 1975 with dissertation "偏微分方程式の初期値問題のLP可解性及び一意性" (translation: "L<sub>p</sub> solvability and uniqueness of the initial value problem for partial differential equations").[^o2] He became an assistant professor at [Chūō University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chūō_University) in 1976 and in 1989 a full professor. In 1996, he became a professor at [Tokyo Metropolitan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_Metropolitan_University) and in 2001, he became a professor at Waseda University. He studies nonlinear partial differential equations (PDEs) such as [Hamilton–Jacobi equation](wikipedia://en/897539), viscosity solutions of PDEs, optimal control theory, differential games, and evolution of surfaces.

From 1987 to 1988, he was a visiting professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), in 2011 at [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France), and in 2010 at [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). From 2011 to 2014 he was an adjunct professor at [King Abdulaziz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdulaziz_University).

In 1994, He received the Autumn Prize from the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786).[^o3] In 2002, he was named to the [Thomson ISI list of highly cited researchers in mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Scientific_Information). He was an invited speaker with talk *Asymptotic solutions for large time of Hamilton-Jacobi equations* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2006 in Madrid.[^o4] He was an invited speaker at the 7th International Congress on Industrial and Applied Mathematics (ICIAM) 2007 in Zurich. In 2012 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
