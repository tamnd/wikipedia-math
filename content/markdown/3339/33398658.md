# Roger Steare

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Roger Steare"}

**Roger Steare** is a British ethicist and corporate philosopher. He has been an Honorary Visiting Professor and Corporate Philosopher in Residence at the [Cass Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_Business_School), and Visiting Professor of European Studies at the [College of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Europe). He is the author of books on corporate ethics and a columnist for the [Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times), also working at the FT's Headspring Executive Development.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Steare's early career included roles as a [social worker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_work) and banker, and ten years as chief executive of a recruitment company.[^o4]

He was Honorary Visiting Professor of Organisational Ethics, and Corporate Philosopher in Residence at the [Cass Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cass_Business_School) in London. He taught on the Executive MBA Programs as well as consulting with external corporate clients through Cass Executive Education.[^o4][^o5]

He is also a fellow of the cross-party policy think tank, ResPublica[^o6][^o7] founded by [Phillip Blond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillip_Blond) in 2009. Steare's essay on *The Power of Love in Business* was included in ResPublica's "Changing The Debate: The Ideas Redefining Britain".[^o8]

Steare was a member of the Expert Drafting Committee for [Rights and Humanity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rights_and_Humanity), invited by the British Government to prepare recommendations for the G20 London Summit in April 2009.[^o9][^o10][^o11]

In collaboration with Athens-based chartered psychologist [Pavlos Stamboulides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavlos_Stamboulides), Steare conducts empirical research on moral character, judgement and behaviour. Some of this research was published in [The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times)[^o12] in October 2010, in a speech by [Hector Sants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hector_Sants) of the [Financial Services Authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Services_Authority) in 2010,[^o13][^o14] by [PwC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PricewaterhouseCoopers) UK in 2010,[^o15] and in *The challenges of risk, culture, behaviour and corporate integrity in financial services*[^o16] for [EY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_&_Young) in 2017. He is co-designer of the [psychometric profile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychometric_profile) *MoralDNA*, used to measure moral values.[^o4]

Steare is the author of *Ethicability*, first published in 2006 and now in its 5th edition,[^o17] and of *Thinking Outside the Inbox*, published in 2019.[^o17] He is a regular contributor to Chartered Banker[^o18] magazine in which he challenges the "dysfunctional totalitarian construct" of modern corporations,[^o19] In July 2020, he became a columnist for the [Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times).[^o20]

On 22 July 2012, he was a guest on the [BBC World Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_World_Service)'s "In the Balance" programme, in an episode entitled *Holding Companies to Account*.[^o21]

In 2014, Steare was appointed Visiting Professor of European Studies at the [College of Europe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Europe), Bruges.[^o22] He is a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Arts).[^o9][^o10][^o23][^o24]

Steare has developed programs in leadership, ethics and corporate responsibility for organisations including [BP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BP),[^o13] [Citigroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citigroup),[^o25] [HSBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HSBC),[^o13] PwC,[^o25][^o26] the Financial Services Authority[^o4] and the [Serious Fraud Office](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serious_Fraud_Office_%28United_Kingdom%29).[^o27]

Following the [Deepwater Horizon oil spill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deepwater_Horizon_oil_spill) in 2010, BP incorporated Steare's ethicability RIGHT framework for ethical decision-making into the BP 2011 Code of Conduct.[^o28]

The [Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times) profiled Steare in 2012[^o29] and has more than once cited his definition of corporate culture as "the way human beings behave together — what they value and what they celebrate".[^o13][^o30] In March 2018, Steare was featured in *[The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian)* in an article profiling the role of philosophers in business.[^o31] As of 2024, he is working at the *[Financial Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_Times)'* Headspring Executive Development.[^o32]
