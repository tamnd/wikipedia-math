# Blichfeldt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Blichfeldt"}

**Blichfeldt** is a Scandinavian [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname). Notable people with this name include:

:::

- Alberte Blichfeldt, Danish actress in *[The Woman That Dreamed About a Man](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Woman_That_Dreamed_About_a_Man)* (2010) and *[The Great Bear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Great_Bear_%28film%29)* (2011)
- Anders Blichfeldt, Danish pop/rock musician in [Big Fat Snake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Fat_Snake)
- Bror Blichfeldt, Norwegian footballer, scored in [Football at the 1997 Island Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Football_at_the_1997_Island_Games)
- [Ebbe Blichfeldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebbe_Blichfeldt), Danish wheelchair racer
- [Emil Blichfeldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Blichfeldt) (1849–1908), Danish architect
- [Emilie Blichfeldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilie_Blichfeldt) (born 1991), Norwegian filmmaker
- [Hans Frederick Blichfeldt](wikipedia://en/33390154) (1873–1945), Danish-American mathematician
- [Jon Frode Blichfeldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Frode_Blichfeldt) (born 1944), Norwegian psychologist
- Kathinka Blichfeldt, Norwegian textbook author, winner of 2006 [Brage Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brage_Prize)
- [Mia Blichfeldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mia_Blichfeldt) (born 1997), Danish badminton player
