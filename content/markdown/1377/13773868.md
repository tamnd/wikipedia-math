# Beautiful Young Minds

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Beautiful Young Minds"}

***Beautiful Young Minds*** was a documentary first shown at the [BRITDOC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BRITDOC) Festival on 26 July 2007[^o3][^o4] and first broadcast on [BBC 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_2) on 14 October 2007.[^o5] The documentary follows the [selection process](wikipedia://en/7020311#United_Kingdom) and training for the U.K. team to compete in the 2006 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (IMO), as well as the actual event in Slovenia. Many of the young mathematicians featured in the film had a form of [autism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autism), which the documentary links to mathematical ability. The team goes on to win numerous medals at the IMO, including four silver and one bronze. It was directed by [Morgan Matthews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morgan_Matthews_%28filmmaker%29), edited by Joby Gee and featured music by Sam Hooper. It was also screened at the [Bath Film Festival](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bath_Film_Festival) in October 2007. The documentary inspired the 2014 film *[X+Y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X+Y)*, which was also directed by Morgan Matthews, based on IMO participant [Daniel Lightwing](wikipedia://en/50955704).[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11]

:::
