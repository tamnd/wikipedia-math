# Ernest S. Croot III

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ernest S. Croot III"}

**Ernest S. Croot III** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the School of Mathematics, [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology). He is known for his solution of the [Erdős–Graham conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Graham_conjecture),[^o3][^o4] and for contributing to the solution of the [cap set](wikipedia://en/51223601) problem.[^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ernest Croot attended [Centre College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_College) at [Danville, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danville,_Kentucky), where he received a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and a B.S. in [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science) in 1994. In 2000, he completed a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in Mathematics at the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia) under the supervision of [Andrew Granville](wikipedia://en/2910434).
