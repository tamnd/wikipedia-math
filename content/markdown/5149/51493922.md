# Charles-René Reynaud

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles-René Reynaud"}

*Image: Reyneau - Analyse demontrée, 1739 - 4604591.tif.* *Analyse demontrée*, 1739

**Charles-René Reynaud** (or **Reyneau**), (1656, [Brissac](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brissac-Quincé), Province of Anjou – 24 February 1728, Paris) was a French mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

A priest of the [Oratory of Saint Philip Neri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oratory_of_Saint_Philip_Neri), father Reyneau was successively professor of philosophy at [Toulon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulon) and [Pézenas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pézenas), and then of mathematics at the college of [Angers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angers). He was a member of the Académie des sciences, belles-lettres et arts d'Angers and free associate of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).

His *Analyse démontrée* is a collection of the main theories prevalent in works of (?) etc.; Reyneau added demonstrations or offered better ones.
