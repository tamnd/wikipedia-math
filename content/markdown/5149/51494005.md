# Zhiwei Yun

Yun Zhiwei

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Zhiwei Yun"}

**Zhiwei Yun** (; born September 1982) is a Chinese-born American mathematician and writer. He is a Professor of Mathematics at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), with a particular focus on the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

He was previously a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 2010 to 2012, assistant professor then associate professor at Stanford University from 2012 to 2016, and professor at Yale University from 2016 to 2017.

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Yun was born in [Changzhou, China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changzhou).[^o4] As a high schooler, he participated in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 2000; he received a gold medal with a perfect score.[^o5] Yun received his bachelor's degree from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2004. In 2009, he received his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), under the direction of [Robert MacPherson](wikipedia://en/10238121).[^o6][^o7]

## Work {#Work}

His collaborations with [Wei Zhang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Wei_%28mathematician%29), [Xinyi Yuan](wikipedia://en/52461029) and [Xinwen Zhu](wikipedia://en/52460702) have received attention in publications such as [Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976) and [Business Insider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_Insider).[^o9][^o10] In particular, his work with [Wei Zhang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhang_Wei_%28mathematician%29) on the [Taylor expansion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_expansion) of L-functions is "already being hailed as one of the most exciting breakthroughs in an important area of number theory in the last 30 years."

Yun also made substantial contributions towards the global [Gan–Gross–Prasad conjecture](wikipedia://en/58884863).

## Awards {#Awards}

Yun was awarded the [SASTRA Ramanujan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_Ramanujan_Prize) in 2012 for his "fundamental contributions to several areas that lie at the interface of representation theory, algebraic geometry and number theory."[^o12]

In December 2017, he was awarded the [2018 New Horizons In Mathematics Prize](wikipedia://en/43125933) together with Wei Zhang, Aaron Naber and [Maryna Viazovska](wikipedia://en/50004967).

He was included in the 2019 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to geometry, number theory, and representation theory, including his construction of motives with exceptional Galois groups".[^o13] In 2019 he received the [Morningside Medal](wikipedia://en/39615068) jointly with [Xinwen Zhu](wikipedia://en/52460702).[^o14]
