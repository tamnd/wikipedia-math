# 71 knot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="71 knot"}

In [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), the **7<sub>1</sub> knot**, also known as the **septoil knot**, the **septafoil knot**, or the **(7, 2)-torus knot**, is one of seven [prime knots](wikipedia://en/315030) with [crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298598) seven. It is the simplest [torus knot](wikipedia://en/1165182) after the [trefoil](wikipedia://en/404773) and [cinquefoil](wikipedia://en/16856240). This knot is used to construct the simplest known counterexample to the conjecture that the [unknotting number](wikipedia://en/26711383) is additive under [connected sum](wikipedia://en/490134).[^o3][^o4]

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The 7<sub>1</sub> knot is [invertible](wikipedia://en/17351839) but not [amphichiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphichiral_knot). Its [Alexander polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107647) is

$$\Delta(t) = t^3 - t^2 + t - 1 + t^{-1} - t^{-2} + t^{-3}, \,$$

its [Conway polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander–Conway_polynomial) is

$$\nabla(z) = z^6 + 5z^4 + 6z^2 + 1, \,$$

and its [Jones polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107642) is

$V(q) = q^{-3} + q^{-5} - q^{-6} + q^{-7} - q^{-8} + q^{-9} - q^{-10}. \,$[^o7]
