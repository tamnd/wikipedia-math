# List of geodesic polyhedra and Goldberg polyhedra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of geodesic polyhedra and Goldberg polyhedra"}

This is a list of selected [geodesic polyhedra](wikipedia://en/53024913) and [Goldberg polyhedra](wikipedia://en/39781140), two infinite classes of [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470). Geodesic polyhedra and Goldberg polyhedra are [duals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_dual) of each other. The geodesic and Goldberg polyhedra are parameterized by integers *m* and *n*, with $m > 0$ and $n \ge 0$. *T* is the triangulation number, which is equal to $T = m^2 + mn + n^2$.

:::
