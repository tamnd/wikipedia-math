# Ludwig Danzer

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ludwig Danzer"}

**Ludwig Danzer** (15 November 1927 – 3 December 2011) was a German [geometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometer) working in [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468). He was a student of [Hanfried Lenz](wikipedia://en/14052690), starting his career in 1960 with a thesis about "Lagerungsprobleme".

:::

Danzer's name is popularized in the concepts of a [Danzer set](wikipedia://en/59958932), a set of points that touches all large convex sets, and the Danzer cube, an example of a non-shellable triangulation of the cube. It is an example of a power complex, studied by Danzer in the 1980s.[^o5]

Danzer also found many new [tilings](wikipedia://en/868145).

Ludwig Danzer worked at the [Technical University of Dortmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Dortmund) and died on December 3, 2011,[^o6] after a long illness.

Danzer had at least ten students, the most prominent one being [Egon Schulte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egon_Schulte).[^o4]
