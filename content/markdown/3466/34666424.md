# Trombi–Varadarajan theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Trombi–Varadarajan theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Trombi–Varadarajan theorem**, introduced by (txt), gives an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) between a certain space of [spherical functions](wikipedia://en/17708915) on a [semisimple Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_Lie_group), and a certain space of [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) defined on a [tubular neighborhood](wikipedia://en/3192042) of the [dual](wikipedia://en/5637655) of a [Cartan subalgebra](wikipedia://en/1336000).

:::
