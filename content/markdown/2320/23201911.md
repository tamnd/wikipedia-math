# Anioł Dowgird

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anioł Dowgird"}

**Anioł Dowgird** (Angelas Daugirdas; 1776–1835) was a [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) of [Polish Enlightenment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlightenment_in_Poland) and Lithuanian Enlightenment.

:::

Born into a noble family in the Mahiliou province, Dowgird studied in [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit) and [Piarist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piarist) schools in [Mscislau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mscislau), [Mahiliou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahiliou) and [Dubrouna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubrowna), then joined the [Piarist Order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piarist_Order) and took [holy orders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holy_orders). Subsequently, he taught at Piarist schools and for a time was a professor of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [ethics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethics) at [Vilnius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University).[^o2]

Dowgird derived his views from [John Locke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Locke)'s [empiricism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empiricism), the [Scottish School of Common Sense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_School_of_Common_Sense),[^o3] and [Immanuel Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant)'s *[Critique of Pure Reason](wikipedia://en/347543)*. But, unlike Kant, he ascribed to time and space a real existence independent of man.[^o2]

## Works {#Works}

- *O logice, metafizyce i filozofji moralnej* (On Logic, Metaphysics and Moral Philosophy)
- *Wykład przyrodzonych myślenia prawideł, czyli logika teoretyczna i praktyczna* (A Treatise on the Natural Laws of Thought, or Theoretical and Practical Logic)
- *Rezczywistość poznań ludzkich* (The Reality of Human Experience)[^o2]

He also wrote several sermons and left a manuscript treatise on [Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant)'s philosophy.[^o2]
