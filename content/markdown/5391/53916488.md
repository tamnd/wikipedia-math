# Amelia Haviland

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amelia Haviland"}

**Amelia M. Haviland** is an American statistician currently the Anna Loomis McCandless Professor of Statistics and Public Policy at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University).[^o2][^o3] She was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2021.[^o4]

:::
