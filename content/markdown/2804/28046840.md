# John C. Gittins

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John C. Gittins"}

**John Charles Gittins** (born 1938) is a researcher in [applied probability](wikipedia://en/366719) and [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476), who is a professor and Emeritus Fellow at [Keble College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keble_College), [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

:::

He is renowned as the developer of the "[Gittins index](wikipedia://en/9267447)", which is used for [sequential](wikipedia://en/3509530) [decision-making](wikipedia://en/16414027), especially in research and development in the pharmaceutical industry.[^o3][^o4] He has research interests in applied [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [decision analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_analysis) and [optimal decisions](wikipedia://en/16414027), including [optimal stopping](wikipedia://en/7189886) and [stochastic optimization](wikipedia://en/7325543).

Gittins was an Assistant Director of Research at the Department of Engineering, [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) from 1967 to 1974. Then he was a lecturer at the University of Oxford from 1975 to 2005 and head of the Department of Statistics there for 6 years. In 1992, the University of Oxford awarded him the honorary degree [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science#Commonwealth_of_Nations_and_Ireland) (DSc). In 1996, he became a Professor of Statistics at the University of Oxford.

He has been awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) (1982) for early-career [probabilists](wikipedia://en/23542), and the [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in Silver (1984).
