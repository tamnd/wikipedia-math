# Nicolas Vilant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicolas Vilant"}

*Image: Nicolas Vilant.png.* Front page of *Elements* (1798 edition)

**Nicolas Vilant** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (1737–1807) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) from Scotland in the 18th century, known for his textbooks. He was a joint founder of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1783.[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was baptised in Ferryport-on-Tay (now called [Tayport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tayport)) on 12 June 1737, the son of Rev William Vilant, the local minister (but of French descent), and his second wife, Jean Wilson. He studied mathematics at [St Andrews University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Andrews_University) from 1752 under Prof [David Gregory](wikipedia://en/703683).[^o5]

Vilant was [Regius Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regius_Professor_of_Mathematics) in the [University of Saint Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Saint_Andrews) from 1765 to his death in 1807. Often ill, he was unable to teach most of this time, and lectures were given by assistants, among them [John West](wikipedia://en/47662943).[^o6] Under Newtonian tradition, he was unable to follow the continental developments in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), like most of his British contemporaries.

He died in [St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Andrews) on 25 May 1807.[^o7]

## Family {#Family}

He married Elizabeth Brand around 1770. Two of their sons attended St Andrews University.

## Publications {#Publications}

He was a good mathematician, and his textbooks were very popular until the first years of the 19th century. The most renowned was *The Elements of Mathematical Analysis, Abridged for the Use of Students*, first printed in 1777 and used as a university textbook from 1783, reprinted for student use.

There are many manuscripts conserved in the archives of the [University of Saint Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Saint_Andrews).[^o10]
