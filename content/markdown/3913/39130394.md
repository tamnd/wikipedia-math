# William Makeham

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Makeham"}

**William Matthew Makeham** (11 September 1826 – 17 November 1891) was an English [actuary](wikipedia://en/43405) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Makeham was responsible for proposing the age-independent Makeham term in the [Gompertz–Makeham law of mortality](wikipedia://en/4185921) that, together with the [exponentially](wikipedia://en/191933) age-dependent [Gompertz term](wikipedia://en/501523), was one of the most effective theories to describe [human mortality](wikipedia://en/191305).[^o2]

Makeham was responsible for two important studies on human mortality:[^o3]

- Makeham. (1860). "On the Law of Mortality and the Construction of Annuity Tables". *The Assurance Magazine, and Journal of the Institute of Actuaries*. 8 (6). p. 301–310. doi:10.1017/S204616580000126X
- Makeham. (1874). "On an Application of the Theory of the Composition of Decremental Forces". *Journal of the Institute of Actuaries*. 18. p. 317–322. <https://babel.hathitrust.org/cgi/pt?id=uc1.b3018971&view=1up&seq=361>

He had one wife, Hepzibah Reed, and seven children, William, Amy, Elizabeth, Thomas, Frederick, Emily, and George.
