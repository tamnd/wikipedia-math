# Patrice Ossona de Mendez

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Patrice Ossona de Mendez"}

**Patrice Ossona de Mendez** is a French mathematician specializing in [topological graph theory](wikipedia://en/1631772) who works as a researcher at the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) in Paris.[^o3] He is editor-in-chief of the *[European Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Journal_of_Combinatorics)*, a position he has held since 2009.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ossona de Mendez was born on 13 December 1966 in Paris.[^o3] He represented France in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 1985, earning a bronze medal there.[^o6] He studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) from 1986 until 1990, and completed his Ph.D. in 1994 from the [School for Advanced Studies in the Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_for_Advanced_Studies_in_the_Social_Sciences).[^o3] His dissertation, jointly supervised by Rosenstiehl and Hubert de Fraysseix, concerned [bipolar orientations](wikipedia://en/40147217) of graphs.

He has worked at CNRS since 1995, and earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2009 from the [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1).[^o3]

Together with [Jaroslav Nešetřil](wikipedia://en/6948446), he received the 2025 [Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerode_Prize) for his work on Sparsity for the papers *Grad and classes with bounded expansion I. Decompositions*, *Grad and classes with bounded expansion II. Algorithmic aspects*, *First order properties on nowhere dense structures*, and *On nowhere dense graphs*.[^o8]

## Book {#Book}

With [Jaroslav Nešetřil](wikipedia://en/6948446), he is the author of the book *Sparsity: Graphs, Structures, and Algorithms* ([Algorithms and Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/57504451) 28, Springer, 2012), concerning the properties and applications of different types of [sparse graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparse_graph).[^o10][^o11] This book was included in [ACM Computing Reviews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Computing_Reviews) list of *Notable Books and Articles of 2012*.[^o12]
