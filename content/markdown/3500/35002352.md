# Mike Steel (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mike Steel"}

*Image: RSNZ honours Christchurch 2025 251028 Research-124 Mike Steel (cropped).jpg.* Steel in 2025

**Michael Anthony Steel** (born May 1960) is a New Zealand mathematician and statistician, a distinguished professor of mathematics and statistics [^o2] and the director of the Biomathematics Research Centre at the [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury) in [Christchurch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christchurch), New Zealand.[^o3] He is known for his research on modelling and reconstructing [evolutionary trees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolutionary_tree).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Steel studied at the University of Canterbury, earning a bachelor's degree in 1982, a masters in 1983, and a degree in journalism in 1985. He then moved to [Massey University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massey_University), where he received his Ph.D. in 1989, supervised by [Michael Hendy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Hendy) and [David Penny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Penny).[^o5] He joined the Canterbury faculty in 1994.[^o3]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Steel won the Hamilton Memorial Prize of the [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_New_Zealand) in 1994; this prize is given annually to a New Zealand mathematician for work done within five years of a Ph.D.[^o7]

In 1999 he won the research award of the [New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society) "for his fundamental contributions to the mathematical understanding of phylogeny, demonstrating a capacity for hard creative work in combinatorics and statistics and an excellent understanding of the biological implications of his results."[^o8]

He became a fellow of the Royal Society of New Zealand in 2003.[^o9]

In 2018, Steel was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISCB_Fellow) of the [International Society for Computational Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_for_Computational_Biology), for his outstanding contributions to the fields of [computational biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_biology) and [bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214).[^o10]
