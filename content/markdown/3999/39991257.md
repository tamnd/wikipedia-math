# G. Phillips Bevan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="G. Phillips Bevan"}

**George Phillips Bevan** (1829–1889) FSS FGS was a Welsh statistician, geographer and author, and the brother of [William Latham Bevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Latham_Bevan).[^o3]

:::

His father was William Hibbs Bevan (1788–1846), who was high sheriff for Breconshire in 1841, and his brother, [William Bevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Bevan_%28priest%29), was archdeacon of Brecon from 1875. His mother Margaret, daughter of Joseph Latham, was also of Beaufort. He was educated at [Rugby School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_School), going on to graduate from [St Andrew's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Andrew's_University) with M.D. in 1850, having also studied at King's College, London, and Edinburgh University.[^o5] He was Surgeon for the Beaufort Iron Works at the time of the 1851 census. By 1858 he was a Fellow of the [Geological Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geological_Society) and contributed a paper (in two parts) to the journal *The Geologist*. He was a partner in Bevan & Brewer, Colliery Proprietors until that company was dissolved in 1864.[^o6] By 1871 he had moved to [Kensington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kensington) and is listed in the census as a *Scientific author*.

## Bevan's *Statistical Atlas* (1882) {#Bevan's_Statistical_Atlas_(1882)}

His *Statistical Atlas* was a massive tome with 45 plates, each 20×28 inches, and many statistical tables.

It provides a useful reference list of schools of the period.

### *The Educational Condition of the United Kingdom* {#The_Educational_Condition_of_the_United_Kingdom}

These tables and map provide a useful reference to educational institutions of the 1880s, including statistical information about the following:

- [Primitive Methodist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primitive_Methodist)
  - *York Jubilee School* ([Elmfield College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elmfield_College))
  - *Birmingham [Bourne College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bourne_College)*
- [Moravian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moravian_Church)
  - [Fulneck School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulneck_School)
  - [Gomersal School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomersal_School)
  - [Mirfield School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirfield_School)
  - [Wyke School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyke_School)

## Publications {#Publications}

- 1858 *On the Geology of the Beaufort and Ebbw Vale District of the South Wales Coal-field*. The Geologist 1(2), Feb, pp. 49–54;1(4), Apr, pp. 124–129
- 1880 *The strikes of the past ten years*. Journal of the Statistical Society of London, 43(1), 35–64.
- 1880 *Tourists' Guide to the West Riding of Yorkshire ... With ... Maps.*
- 1882 *The Statistical Atlas of England, Scotland and Ireland*. Edinburgh & London: [W. & A. K. Johnston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._&_A._K._Johnston)
