# Ehrenpreis's fundamental principle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ehrenpreis's fundamental principle"}

In mathematical analysis, **Ehrenpreis's fundamental principle**, introduced by [Leon Ehrenpreis](wikipedia://en/14162704), states:[^o2]

:::

Every solution of a system (in general, overdetermined) of homogeneous [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) with constant [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203) can be represented as the integral with respect to an appropriate Radon measure over the complex “[characteristic variety](wikipedia://en/40030306)” of the system.[^o3]
