# Robert Russell (Irish mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Russell"}

**Robert Russell** (c. 1858–18 May 1938) was an Irish mathematician and academic at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin) (TCD), who served as [Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_Smith's_Professor_of_Mathematics) (1917–1921).

:::

Robert Russell was born in [Portadown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portadown), [County Armagh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/County_Armagh), and was educated at Santry School, [Portarlington, County Laois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portarlington,_County_Laois). He attended TCD, became a Scholar in 1877, and won the Brooke Prize, Bishop Law's Prize, McCullagh Prize, and Madden Prize.[^o2] He was awarded BA in mathematics (1880), became a Fellow a few years later, and got his MA (1888). In 1887, he was elected a member of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o3] He spent his whole career at TCD, at various times serving as Junior Bursar, Junior Dean, Registrar of Chambers,[^o4] and from the early 1920s on, Senior Bursar.[^o5]

He was [Donegall Lecturer in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donegall_Lecturer_in_Mathematics) (1904–1907), Erasmus Smith's Professor of Mathematics (1917–1921), and became Senior Fellow in 1920.
