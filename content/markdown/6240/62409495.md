# Rinat Kedem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rinat Kedem"}

**Rinat Kedem** (born 5 December 1965) is an American mathematician and mathematical physicist.

:::

Kedem graduated in 1988 with BA in physics from [Macalester College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macalester_College).[^o2] She received her PhD in physics in 1993 from Stony Brook University (the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook)) with thesis advisor [Barry M. McCoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_M._McCoy). She was a postdoc from 1993 to 1995 at Kyoto University's [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1978962) (RIMS), from 1995 to 1996 at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne), and from 1996 to 1997 at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). At the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst), she was an assistant professor of mathematics from 1997 to 2001. In the mathematics department of the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign), she was from 2001 to 2006 an assistant professor and from 2006 to 2012 an associate professor and is since 2012 a full professor.[^o2]

Kedem's research deals with mathematical physics, [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944), [integrable models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable_model), and [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423).[^o2] In 2014 she was an invited speaker with talk *Fermionic spectra in integrable systems* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul.[^o4] She was a plenary speaker in 2012 at the 24th [International Conference on Formal Power Series and Algebraic Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/75145047) in Nagoya and in 2019 at the 11th International Symposium on Quantum Theory and Symmetries (QTS2019) in Montreal.[^o2] For the academic year 2019–2020 she was awarded a [Simons Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Fellow).[^o5]
