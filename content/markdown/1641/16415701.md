# Fast Library for Number Theory

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fast Library for Number Theory"}

The **Fast Library for Number Theory (FLINT)** is a [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_%28programming_language%29) [library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Library_%28computing%29) for [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) applications. It implements efficient versions of various [ring arithmetics](wikipedia://en/48404) as well as derived functionality such as integer factorization using a [quadratic sieve](wikipedia://en/582340). The library is designed to be compiled with the [GNU Multi-Precision Library (GMP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Multi-Precision_Library) and is released under the [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License). It is developed by [William Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Hart_%28mathematician%29) of the [University of Kaiserslautern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kaiserslautern) (formerly [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick)) and [David Harvey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Harvey_%28mathematician%29) of [University of New South Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_South_Wales) (formerly [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University)) to address the speed limitations of the [PARI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PARI/GP) and [NTL](wikipedia://en/26907870) libraries.[^o3]

:::

FLINT along with a Cython wrapper for it is distributed with [SageMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SageMath). The development of FLINT has led to significant contributions in the areas of [integer factorization](wikipedia://en/15491) and [polynomial arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_arithmetic). For example as of May, 2007 on certain platforms FLINT factors integers in the [quadratic sieve](wikipedia://en/582340) range faster than any other general implementation[^o4], and as of February, 2008 it does arithmetic in $\mathbb{Z}[x]$ faster than any other package.[^o5][^o6]

## Functionality {#Functionality}

Core:

- Ring arithmetic (integers, rationals, algebraic, real, complex and p-adic numbers, finite fields, and number fields)
  - Exact real numbers (formerly the separate Calcium library)
  - Arbitrary-precision approximate real numbers with [ball arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ball_arithmetic) (formerly the separate Arb library)[^o8]
- Polynomials (univariate and multivariate), power series, and matrices building on top of ring arithmetics.

Based on core:

- Primality testing ([APRCL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/APRCL), Miller-Rabin, BPSW, etc.)
- Integer factorization (trial factor, [quadratic sieve](wikipedia://en/582340), Pollard's rho, Lenstra ECM)
- Multivariate polynomial GCD and factorisation
- FFTs
- Multimodular reconstruction
- Special functions
- Exact and approximate linear algebra
- LLL
- Finite field embeddings
- ... and more.
