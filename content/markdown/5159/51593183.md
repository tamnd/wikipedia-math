# Olivier Guéant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olivier Guéant"}

**Olivier Guéant** is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), focusing on [mean field game theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_field_game_theory) and [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics). He is currently a Full Professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [Université Paris Cité](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris_Cité).

:::

## Background {#Background}

Guéant studied mathematics and economics at [Ecole Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Normale_Supérieure)[^o3] and also studied at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard). He defended the first PhD thesis on [mean field games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_field_games) under the supervision of [Pierre-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/1270068). Guéant's PhD was awarded the Rosemont Demassieux prize.[^o4]

In 2010, along with [Jean-Michel Lasry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Michel_Lasry), [Pierre-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/1270068), and [Henri Verdier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Verdier), Guéant founded a start-up called MFG-Labs,[^o5] pioneering [Big Data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_Data). The company was acquired by [Havas Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Havas_Media) in 2013.[^o6]

From 2010 to 2016, Guéant served as Associate professor in applied mathematics at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University), and then as full Professor of applied mathematics at the Sorbonne from 2016 to 2025 and he currently holds a Full Professorship at [Université Paris Cité](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris_Cité).

His research on financial mathematics focuses on optimal execution and market making.[^o7] He also worked with [Roger Guesnerie](wikipedia://en/30431281), [Jean-Michel Lasry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Michel_Lasry) and [Olivier David Zerbib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_David_Zerbib) on environmental interest rates.

Between 2014 and 2017, Guéant was sitting on the Scientific Advisory Board of Havas Media.

## Mathematical modelling in finance {#Mathematical_modelling_in_finance}

As an expert of financial mathematics and stochastic control, Guéant has published several books and articles on liquidity management, optimal orders execution and multi-asset market making.[^o9] He is also an expert on pricing and asset management. Along with Charles-Albert Lehalle and Joaquin Fernandez-Tapia, he notably solved the Avellaneda-Stoikov equations, which are key to dealing with [inventory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventory) risk in market making.[^o10]

## Books {#Books}

- *Paris-Princeton Lectures on Mathematical Finance 2010*, 2011
- *The Financial Mathematics of Market Liquidity: From Optimal Execution to Market Making*, 2016

## Awards {#Awards}

- AAENSAE Prize for his research on environmental interest rates, 2008 (with Olivier David Zerbib)
- Rosemont-Demassieux Prize for his PhD on mean field game theory, 2010
- IEF-FBF Prize of the best paper in Finance, 2016 (with [Charles-Albert Lehalle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles-Albert_Lehalle))[^o13]
- Best Young Researcher in Finance and Insurance 2023 (IEF/Scor Foundation for Science)[^o14]
