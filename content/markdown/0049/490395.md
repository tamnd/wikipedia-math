# 2000 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="2000"}

**2000** (**two thousand**) is a [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 1999 and preceding 2001.

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

2000 is:

- the highest number expressible using only two unmodified characters in [Roman numerals](wikipedia://en/25657) (MM)
- an [Achilles number](wikipedia://en/7894744)
- smallest four digit [eban number](wikipedia://en/8282811)
- the sum of all the [nban numbers](wikipedia://en/8282811)

It is also the 6th and largest highly composite number that is a divisor of every higher highly composite number .
