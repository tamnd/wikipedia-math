# 10,000

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="10,000"}

**10,000** (**ten thousand**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [9,999](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9999_%28number%29) and preceding 10,001.

:::

## Name {#Name}

Many languages have a specific word for this number; in [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek) it is μύριοι (the etymological root of the word [myriad](wikipedia://en/20088) in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language)), in [Aramaic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aramaic_language) ܪܒܘܬܐ, in [Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language) רבבה \[he\], in [Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_language) 萬/万 (Mandarin cmn, [Cantonese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantonese) yue, Hokkien *bān*), in [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language) 万/萬 \[ja\], in [Khmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khmer_language) ម៉ឺន \[km\], in [Korean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_language) 만/萬 \[ko\], in [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) тьма \[ru\], in [Vietnamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_language) vạn, in [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit) अयुत \[*ayuta*\], in [Thai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thai_language) หมื่น \[th\], in [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam) പതിനായിരം \[ml\], and in [Malagasy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malagasy_language) *alina*.[^o4] In many of these languages, it often denotes a [very large but indefinite number](wikipedia://en/538913).[^o5]

The classical [Greeks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greeks) used letters of the [Greek alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_alphabet) to represent [Greek numerals](wikipedia://en/166697): they used a capital letter [mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_%28letter%29) (Μ) to represent ten thousand. This Greek root was used in early versions of the [metric system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metric_system) in the form of the decimal prefix [myria-](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-SI_unit_prefix).[^o6]

Depending on the country, the number ten thousand is usually written as 10,000 (including in the UK and US), 10.000, or 10 000.[^o7]

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

In [scientific notation](wikipedia://en/59715), it is written as **10<sup>4</sup>** or **1 E+4** (equivalently **1 E4**) in [E notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E_notation). It is the [square](wikipedia://en/659942) of [100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_%28number%29) and the [square root](wikipedia://en/29208) of [100,000,000](wikipedia://en/2847945).

The value of a [myriad](wikipedia://en/20088) to the [power](wikipedia://en/99491) of itself, 10000<sup>10000</sup> = 10<sup>40000</sup>.

It has a [reduced totient](wikipedia://en/1181756) of [500](wikipedia://en/442472), and a [totient](wikipedia://en/53452) of [4,000](wikipedia://en/562999), with a total of 16 [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) having a totient value of 10,000.

There are a total of [1,229](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1229_%28number%29) prime numbers less than ten thousand, a count that is itself prime.[^o12]

A [myriagon](wikipedia://en/523703) is a [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) with **ten thousand edges** and a total of 25 [dihedral](wikipedia://en/100267) symmetry groups when including the myriagon itself, alongside 25 [cyclic groups](wikipedia://en/52327) as [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011).[^o13]
