# Formal sum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Formal sum"}

In mathematics, a **formal sum**, **formal series**, or **formal linear combination** may be:

:::

- In group theory, an element of a [free abelian group](wikipedia://en/247296), a sum of finitely many elements from a given basis set multiplied by integer coefficients.
- In linear algebra, an element of a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370), a sum of finitely many elements from a given basis set multiplied by real, complex, or other numerical coefficients.
- In the study of [series (mathematics)](wikipedia://en/15287), a sum of an infinite sequence of numbers or other quantities, considered as an abstract mathematical object regardless of whether the sum converges.
- In the study of [power series](wikipedia://en/59958), a sum of infinitely many monomials with distinct positive integer exponents, again considered as an abstract object regardless of convergence (see [formal power series](wikipedia://en/60012)).
