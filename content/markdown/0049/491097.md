# Variational principle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Variational principle"}

A **variational principle** is a mathematical procedure that renders a physical problem solvable by the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), which concerns finding functions that optimize the values of quantities that depend on those functions. For example, the problem of determining the shape of a hanging chain suspended at both ends—a [catenary](wikipedia://en/7163)—can be solved using [variational calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_calculus), and in this case, the variational principle is the following: The solution is a function that minimizes the [gravitational potential energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_energy) of the chain.

:::

## History {#History}

### Physics {#Physics}

The concept of a variational principle emerged from earlier work like [Fermat's principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_principle) for [optics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optics) in 1662. The first application of the variational technique, albeit as a special-case rather than a general principle, was [James Bernoulli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Bernoulli)'s solution of the [brachistochrone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brachistochrone) problem in 1718.[^o4] [Pierre Louis Maupertuis](wikipedia://en/196221) generalized Fermat's concept to mechanics,[^o5] in the form of a [principle of least action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stationary-action_principle). These principles were linked to [a more general principle of least action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton's_optico-mechanical_analogy) by [William Rowan Hamilton](wikipedia://en/39817) in 1831, showing that the motion of matter particles and the motion of light waves could be described in the same way. Hamilton's work was an important influence on the early 20th century research into [wave-particle duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave-particle_duality), culminating in the 1926 discovery of [Schrodinger's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schrodinger's_equation).[^o6]

### Math {#Math}

[Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887)'s 1872 [Erlangen program](wikipedia://en/243382) attempted to identify invariants under a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of transformations.

## Examples {#Examples}

### In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

- [Ekeland's variational principle](wikipedia://en/21341034) in mathematical optimization
- The [finite element method](wikipedia://en/18233581)
- The variation principle relating [topological entropy](wikipedia://en/5463978) and [Kolmogorov-Sinai entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov-Sinai_entropy).

### In physics {#In_physics}

*Image: Classical\_mechanics\_roadmap.svg.* Variational principles for classical mechanics was developed in parallel with Newtonian mechanics.

- The [Rayleigh–Ritz method](wikipedia://en/2111994) for solving [boundary-value problems](wikipedia://en/436779) in elasticity and wave propagation
- [Fermat's principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat's_principle) in [geometrical optics](wikipedia://en/731780)
- [Hamilton's principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton's_principle) in [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586)
- [Maupertuis' principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maupertuis'_principle) in [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586)
- The [principle of least action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principle_of_least_action) in [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics), [electromagnetic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_theory), and [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202)
- The [variational method](wikipedia://en/37487265) in quantum mechanics
- [Hellmann–Feynman theorem](wikipedia://en/2028282)
- [Gauss's principle of least constraint](wikipedia://en/5534333) and [Hertz's principle of least curvature](wikipedia://en/5534333#Hertz's_principle_of_least_curvature)
- [Hilbert's action principle](wikipedia://en/855776) in general relativity, leading to the [Einstein field equations](wikipedia://en/424540).
- [Palatini variation](wikipedia://en/607686)
- [Hartree–Fock method](wikipedia://en/475393)
- [Density functional theory](wikipedia://en/209874)
- [Gibbons–Hawking–York boundary term](wikipedia://en/2531983)
- [Variational quantum eigensolver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_quantum_eigensolver)
