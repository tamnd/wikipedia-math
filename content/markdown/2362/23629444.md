# Mixed linear complementarity problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mixed linear complementarity problem"}

In mathematical [optimization theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29), the **mixed linear complementarity problem**, often abbreviated as **MLCP** or **LMCP**, is a generalization of the [linear complementarity problem](wikipedia://en/1470767) to include [free variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_variables).

:::
