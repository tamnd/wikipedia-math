# Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies"}

The **Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies** (**COPSS**) comprises the presidents, past presidents and presidents[-elect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/-elect) of the following, primarily [Northern American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_America), [professional societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_societies) of [statisticians](wikipedia://en/48661):[^o2]

:::

- [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)
- [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)
- Eastern North American Region of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society)
- Western North American Region of the International Biometric Society
- [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327)

It also includes the president-elect-elect of Institute of Mathematical Statistics and the past-past-president of the Statistical Society of Canada.[^o2]

COPSS is responsible for granting the following awards:[^o3]

- The [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) for "an outstanding contribution to the profession of statistics" by a member of one of the constituent societies aged under 41
- The [COPSS Distinguished Achievement Award and Lectureship](wikipedia://en/15967717) for "achievement and scholarship in statistical science" that has made a "highly significant impact ... on scientific investigations"
- The [Snedecor Award](wikipedia://en/47945620) for "a noteworthy publication in [biometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometry) within three years of the data of the award"
- The [Elizabeth L. Scott Award](wikipedia://en/60371939) for "fostering opportunities in statistics for women"
- The [Florence Nightingale David Award](wikipedia://en/58964720) recognizes "a female statistician who serves as a role model to other women"
