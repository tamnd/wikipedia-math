# Joseph J. Rotman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph J. Rotman"}

**Joseph Jonah Rotman** (May 26, 1934 – October 16, 2016)[^o2] was a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign)[^o3] and also a published author of 10 textbooks.

:::

Rotman was born in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago). He did his undergraduate and graduate work at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he received his doctorate in 1959 with a thesis in [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974) written under the direction of [Irving Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Kaplansky). In 1959 he moved to the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign, where he spent the rest of his mathematical career. Rotman retired from UIUC in 2004.[^o5] His research interests lay in the area of [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), involving [abelian groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_groups), [modules](wikipedia://en/276410), [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o3]

Rotman was the managing editor of the *[Proceedings of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society)* in 1972–1973.[^o5] In 1985 he was the annual visiting lecturer of the [South African Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/52070946).[^o6]

A partial list of Rotman's publications includes:

- *An Introduction to Homological Algebra* (1979), Pure and Applied Mathematics, vol. 85, Academic Press; [^o7]
- *An Introduction to Algebraic Topology* (1988), Springer-Verlag;
- *An Introduction to the Theory of Groups* (1995), Springer-Verlag;
- *A First Course in Abstract Algebra* (2000), Prentice Hall;
- *Advanced Modern Algebra* (2002), Prentice Hall;
- *Journey into Mathematics: an introduction to proofs* (2006), [Dover Publications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dover_Publications);
