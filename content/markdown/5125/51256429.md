# David Arnold (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Arnold"}

**David M. Arnold** (August 9, 1939 – January 3, 2021) was an American mathematician, formerly the Ralph and Jean Storm Professor of Mathematics at [Baylor University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baylor_University),[^o3] and also a published author of 10 books, currently held in 1,886 libraries.[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He received his master's degree from [Western Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Washington_University) and his Ph.D. at [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign), the latter had [Joseph J. Rotman](wikipedia://en/51256431) as his advisor. Other professorships he has held are Professor of Mathematics at [New Mexico State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico_State_University) and visiting professor at [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut), [University of Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Essen) and [Florida Atlantic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Atlantic_University).[^o3]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He was "well-loved" by his wife Betty, his 5 children, his 13 grandchildren, and his 6 great-grandchildren.[^o3] He enjoyed his family, reading, and [birding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birding).[^o3]
