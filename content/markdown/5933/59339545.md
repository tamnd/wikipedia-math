# Elena Mantovan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elena Mantovan"}

**Elena Mantovan** is a mathematician specializing in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). Educated in Italy and the US, she works in the US as Taussky-Todd–Lonergan Professor of Mathematics at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) (Caltech).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mantovan earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in mathematics at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) in 1995. She completed her Ph.D. in 2002 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). Her dissertation, *On Certain Unitary Group Shimura Varieties*, was supervised by [Richard Taylor](wikipedia://en/646931). She later published it as part of the monograph *Variétés de Shimura, espaces de Rapoport-Zink et correspondances de Langlands locales*, co-authored with [Laurent Fargues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurent_Fargues) (Astérisque 291, [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205), 2004).

She was a [Miller Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Research_Fellows) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), with [Ken Ribet](wikipedia://en/30876850) as a mentor, from 2002 until 2005. In 2005, she joined the Caltech faculty. From August 2010 through March 2011, she was a von Neumann Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). She was promoted to full professor at Caltech in 2010, and was the executive officer of the mathematics department from 2016 to 2019.

## Mentorship {#Mentorship}

Mantovan is faculty advisor for the Caltech chapter of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698). She has been cited as a mentor for undergraduate mathematicians including Ila Varma, 2009 honorable mention for the [Alice T. Schafer Prize](wikipedia://en/57181252), and Laura Lewis, 2021 winner of the National Center for Women and Information Technology (NCWIT) 2021 Collegiate Award.
