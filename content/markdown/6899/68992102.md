# Tim Hesterberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tim Hesterberg"}

**Tim Hesterberg** is an American statistician. He is a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) and currently works as a staff data scientist at [Instacart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instacart).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tim Hesterberg graduated with a B.A in mathematics from [St. Olaf College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Olaf_College) and received his Ph.D. in statistics from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o3] He is a member of the National Institute of Statistical Sciences(NISS) and was previously on the NISS Board of Trustees. He is currently on the board of the Canadian Statistical Sciences Institute.[^o3] Hesterberg previously worked as a senior statistician at [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google) as well as at [Franklin and Marshall College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_&_Marshall_College), [Pacific Gas & Electric Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Gas_and_Electric_Company), and Insightful/MathSoft.[^o3] He is currently a senior statistician at Instacart.

## Research {#Research}

Hesterberg has contributed to statistical research by writing a textbook, articles, and a software package:

- A 2011 undergraduate student textbook titled *Mathematical Statistics with Resampling and R*.[^o5]
- A 2015 article titled "What Teachers Should Know About the Bootstrap: Resampling in the Undergraduate Statistics Curriculum".[^o6]
- A 2015 [R (programming language)](wikipedia://en/376707) software package titled 'resample: Resampling Functions'.[^o7] It performs bootstraps, permutation tests, and other sampling refunctions, and is intended to be easy to use.[^o7]
- Scientific articles including "Bootstrap",[^o8] "Least-Angle Regression and LASSO for Large Datasets",[^o9] and "Least Angle and L1 Penalized Regression: A Review".[^o10]
