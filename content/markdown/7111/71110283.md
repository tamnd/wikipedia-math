# Khaleed S. Mekheimer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Khaleed S. Mekheimer"}

**Khaled S. Mekheimer** is an Egyptian professor of [Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the Department of Mathematics Faculty of Science (Men), [Al-Azhar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_University), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt). He is the Vice Dean of Research and Post-graduate Studies of the Faculty. He is an elected member of [African Academy of sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Academy_of_Sciences) and Egyptian Mathematical Society.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Mekheimer was born on March 22, 1963, in [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt). He obtained his BSc from the department of mathematics, Faculty of Science, [Ain-Shams University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ain_Shams_University) in 1984. He moved to [Al-Azhar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_University) for his MSc and PhD and he graduated in 1990 and 1994 respectively.[^o6][^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Mekheimer started his career in 1986 as a demonstrator of Mathematics at [Al-Azhar, University, Cairo, Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_University). He became an assistant lecturer immediately after his MSc in 1990 and assumed the role of a lecturer after his PhD in 1994. In 1997, he moved to [King Abdu Al-Aziz University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdulaziz_University) branch, [Madinah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medina) Munawwara, [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia) where he became an assistant Professor. He returned to [Al-Azhar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Azhar_University) in Cairo as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) and in 2010 he became a [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the Faculty of Science in the same institution.[^o4][^o6]
