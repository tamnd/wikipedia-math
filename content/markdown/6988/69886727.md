# Bas Edixhoven

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bas Edixhoven"}

**Sebastiaan Johan Edixhoven** (12 March 1962[^o14][^o10] – 16 January 2022[^o14][^o3]) was a Dutch mathematician who worked in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). He was a professor at [University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes_1) and [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University).[^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Bas Edixhoven was born on 12 March 1962 in [Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden), Netherlands.[^o6] Edixhoven graduated from Erasmus College in [Zoetermeer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoetermeer) in 1980.[^o6] He then studied at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) where he graduated with a master's degree in pure mathematics *[cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors#Netherlands)* in 1985 and a PhD in mathematics in 1989, both under the direction of [Frans Oort](wikipedia://en/58171100).[^o10][^o6] His thesis was about [modular curves](wikipedia://en/669552).[^o14]

## Career {#Career}

Edixhoven was a Morrey assistant professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) from 1989 to 1991, after which he returned to Utrecht University.[^o14] From 1992 to 2002, he was a professor at the [University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes_1).[^o14] He moved to [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) as a professor of geometry in 2002.[^o14]

In 2004, Edixhoven and [Peter Stevenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Stevenhagen) established Leiden's participation in the Algebra, Geometry and Number Theory (ALGANT) collaborative program for master's and PhD students across French, Italian, German, Canadian, and Dutch universities.[^o14]

Edixhoven was a co-managing editor of *[Compositio Mathematica](wikipedia://en/31882322)* from 2003 to 2012.[^o14] He was an editor for *Compositio Mathematica* from 2000 to 2012,[^o14] for the *[Journal de Théorie des Nombres de Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_de_Théorie_des_Nombres_de_Bordeaux)* from 1998 to 2004, for the *[Journal of Number Theory](wikipedia://en/8422003)* from 2004 to 2012, for *[Expositiones Mathematicae](wikipedia://en/63969183)* from 2003 to 2022, and for *[Indagationes Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indagationes_Mathematicae)* from 2010 to 2022.[^o10]

Edixhoven was involved in the Bèta-lerarenkamer think tank, which produced a mobile app for refugee teachers to improve their Dutch and mathematics abilities.[^o14]

## Research {#Research}

Edixhoven's early work was on modular curves.[^o14] He worked on the techniques used in [Wiles's proof of Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/21950759) and the proof of [Serre's modularity conjecture](wikipedia://en/2908546).[^o14]

He later made important contributions regarding the [André–Oort conjecture](wikipedia://en/32262980), as well as making [modular forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_forms) and associated [Galois representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_representations) more computationally accessible.[^o14] His latest works had focused on geometric interpretations of the quadratic [Chabauty method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chabauty_method).[^o14]

Outside of academia, he worked on [correction codes](wikipedia://en/10375) for the [French Ministry of Defense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Armed_Forces_%28France%29) and [Canon Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canon_Inc.)[^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

From 1989 to 1992, Edixhoven was a recipient of the Huygens scholarship. From 1995 to 2002, he was a member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France). In 2009, he became a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o14][^o12] In 2008, he gave an invited lecture at the 5th [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846).[^o10]

## Death {#Death}

Edixhoven died on 16 January 2022, at the age of 59, from a [tumor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tumor).[^o14][^o3]
