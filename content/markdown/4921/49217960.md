# Thomas Willwacher

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Willwacher"}

**Thomas Hans Willwacher** (born 12 April 1983) is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist) working as a Professor at the Institute of Mathematics, [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Willwacher completed his PhD at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) in 2009 with a thesis on "Cyclic Formality", under the supervision of [Giovanni Felder](wikipedia://en/68426150), [Alberto Cattaneo](wikipedia://en/68432169), and [Anton Alekseev](wikipedia://en/62391241). He was a Junior member of the [Harvard Society of Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Society_of_Fellows) from 2010 to 2013.[^o6]

He joined the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) as an assistant professor in 2013, and was promoted to associate professor in 2015.[^o9]

## Research and recognition {#Research_and_recognition}

In July 2016 Willwacher was awarded a prize from the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) for "his striking and important research in a variety of mathematical fields: homotopical algebra, geometry, topology and mathematical physics, including deep results related to Kontsevich's formality theorem and the relation between Kontsevich's graph complex and the Grothendieck-Teichmüller Lie algebra".[^o8][^o9]

Notable results of Willwacher include the proof of [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509)'s cyclic formality conjecture and the proof that the [Grothendieck–Teichmüller](wikipedia://en/37761264) [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) is isomorphic to the degree zero cohomology of [Kontsevich's graph complex](wikipedia://en/26049975#Kontsevich_graphs).[^o9]
