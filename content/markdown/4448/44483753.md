# Ikeda lift

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ikeda lift"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Ikeda lift** is a [lifting](wikipedia://en/10504570) of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) to [Siegel modular forms](wikipedia://en/7721927). The existence of the lifting was conjectured by W. Duke and Ö. Imamoḡlu and also by T. Ibukiyama, and the lifting was constructed by (Ikeda 2001). It generalized the [Saito–Kurokawa lift](wikipedia://en/44536593) from modular forms of weight 2*k* to genus 2 Siegel modular forms of weight *k* + 1.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that *k* and *n* are positive integers of the same parity. The Ikeda lift takes a [Hecke eigenform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hecke_eigenform) of weight 2*k* for SL<sub>2</sub>(**Z**) to a Hecke eigenform in the space of Siegel modular forms of weight *k*+*n*, degree 2*n*.

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

The Ikeda lift takes the Delta function (the weight 12 cusp form for SL<sub>2</sub>(**Z**)) to the [Schottky form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schottky_form), a weight 8 Siegel cusp form of degree 4. Here *k*=6 and *n*=2.

:::
