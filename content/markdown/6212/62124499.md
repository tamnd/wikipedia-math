# Ulrich Pinkall

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrich Pinkall"}

**Ulrich Pinkall** (born 1955) is a German mathematician, specializing in differential geometry and computer graphics.[^o3]

:::

Pinkall studied mathematics at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) with a [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) in 1979 and a doctorate in 1982 with thesis *Dupin'sche Hyperflächen* ([Dupin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Dupin)'s hypersurfaces)[^o4] under the supervision of [Martin Barner](wikipedia://en/42687455). Pinkall was then a research assistant in Freiburg until 1984 and from 1984 to 1986 at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn. In 1985 he completed his habilitation in Bonn with thesis *Totale Absolutkrümmung immersierter Flächen* (Total absolute curvature of immersed surfaces). Since 1986 he is professor at [TU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Berlin).[^o3]

In 1985 he received the [Otto Hahn Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Hahn_Medal) of the [Max Planck Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Society). In 1986 he received a *Heisenberg-Stipendium* from the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) (DFG). From 1992 to 2003 he was a speaker of the [Sonderforschungsbereich (SFB)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_Research_Centers) 288 (differential geometry and quantum physics).

In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker with talk *Quaternionic analysis of [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) and differential geometry* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o6]
