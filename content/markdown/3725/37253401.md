# All-Russian Mathematical Portal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="All-Russian Mathematical Portal"}

The **All-Russian Mathematical Portal** (better known as **Math-Net.Ru**) is a [web portal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_portal) that provides extensive access to all aspects of [Russian mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_mathematics), including journals, organizations, conferences, articles, videos, libraries, software, and people.[^o3][^o4] The portal is a joint project of the [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute) and the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). Access to information in the portal is generally free, except for the full-text sources of certain publications which have elected to make their content available on a fee basis.[^o5]

:::

The website can be read in either Russian or English. As a standard default, it renders on-screen mathematics using [MathJax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MathJax).[^o5]
