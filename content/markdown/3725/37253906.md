# Bruce Reed (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bruce Reed"}

*Image: Bruce Reed, Bellairs 2015.jpg.* Bruce Reed at the [Bellairs Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bellairs_Research_Institute), 2015

**Bruce Alan Reed** [FRSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), a former [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in Graph Theory at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).[^o2][^o3] His research is primarily in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).[^o3] He is a distinguished research fellow of the Institute of Mathematics in the [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica), Taiwan,[^o4] and an adjunct professor at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria) in Canada.[^o5]

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Reed earned his Ph.D. in 1986 from McGill, under the supervision of [Vašek Chvátal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vašek_Chvátal). Before returning to McGill as a Canada Research Chair, Reed held positions at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), and the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research).[^o8]

Reed was elected as a fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 2009,[^o9] and is the recipient of the 2013 [CRM-Fields-PIMS Prize](wikipedia://en/19284033).[^o10]

In 2021 he left McGill, and subsequently became a researcher at the Academia Sinica and an adjunct professor at the University of Victoria.[^o2][^o4][^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Reed's thesis research concerned [perfect graphs](wikipedia://en/670531).[^o7] With Michael Molloy, he is the author of a book on [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743) and the [probabilistic method](wikipedia://en/173525).[^o12] Reed has also published highly cited papers on the [giant component](wikipedia://en/2379792) in [random graphs](wikipedia://en/358477) with a given [degree sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degree_sequence),MR95MR98a random [satisfiability problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satisfiability_problem),CR92 [acyclic coloring](wikipedia://en/690769),AMR91 [tree decomposition](wikipedia://en/159023),R92R97 and constructive versions of the [Lovász local lemma](wikipedia://en/2211763).MR98b

He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2002.[^o13] His talk there concerned a proof by Reed and [Benny Sudakov](wikipedia://en/35862803), using the [probabilistic method](wikipedia://en/173525), of a conjecture by Kyoji Ohba that graphs whose number of vertices and [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) are (asymptotically) within a factor of two of each other have equal chromatic number and [list chromatic number](wikipedia://en/690669).RS02
