# Ross Honsberger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ross Honsberger"}

**Ross Honsberger** (1929–2016[^o3]) was a Canadian mathematician and author on [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Honsberger studied mathematics at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto), with a bachelor's degree, and then worked for ten years as a teacher in Toronto, before continuing his studies at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) (master's degree). Since 1964 he had been on the faculty of mathematics, where he later became a professor emeritus. He dealt with [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization), especially with [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471). He developed education courses, for example, on [combinatorial geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_geometry), frequently held lectures for students and math teachers, and was editor of the *Ontario Secondary School Mathematics Bulletin*. He wrote numerous books on elementary mathematics ([geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542)), and [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446) (often at the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772), MAA), with him in his own words using the book by [Hans Rademacher](wikipedia://en/1864916) and [Otto Toeplitz](wikipedia://en/547682) of numbers and figures as a model. Frequent were his expositions of problems at the [International Mathematical Olympiads](wikipedia://en/82208) and other competitions.

[Edsger W. Dijkstra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edsger_W._Dijkstra) called his *Mathematical Gems* "delightful".[^o5]

- *Mathematical Gems*, MAA 1973, 2003 (Mathematical Expositions [Dolciani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dolciani) Vol.1), German Mathematical gems of elementary combinatorics, number theory and geometry, Wiley, 1990, , Chapter "The Story of [Louis Posa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Posa)".
- *Mathematical Gems 2*, MAA 1975 (Vol.2 Dolciani Mathematical Expositions)
- *Mathematical Gems 3*, MAA 1985, 1991 (vol.9 Dolciani Mathematical Expositions)

- *More Mathematical Morsels*, MAA 1991 (Dolciani Bd.10 Mathematical Expositions)
- *Mathematical Plums*, MAA 1979 (vol.4 Dolciani Mathematical Expositions)
- *Mathematical Chestnuts from around the world*, MAA 2001 (Dolciani Bd.24 Mathematical Expositions)
- *Mathematical Diamonds*, MAA 2003[^o8]
- *In Pólya's Footsteps*, MAA 1997 (Dolciani Bd.19 Mathematical Expositions)
- *Episodes in nineteenth and twentieth century euclidean geometry*, MAA 1995
- *From Erdos to Kiev – Problems of Olympiad Caliber*, MAA 1997[^o9]
