# Stephen Milne (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Milne"}

**Stephen Carl Milne** is an American mathematician who works in the fields of [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Milne received a bachelor's degree from [San Diego State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Diego_State_University) in 1972 and a Ph.D. from the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) (UCSD) in 1976. His thesis, *Peano curves and smoothness of functions*, was written under [Adriano M. Garsia](wikipedia://en/18857510). From 1976 to 1978 he was a Gibbs Instructor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). Milne taught at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), UCSD, the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky), and [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), where he became in 1982 an associate professor and in 1985 a full professor.

Milne works on algebraic combinatorics, classical analysis, special functions, analytic number theory, and [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) (generalizations of the [Macdonald identities](wikipedia://en/5027268)).

### Awards {#Awards}

From 1981 to 1983 he was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship). In 2007 he was the joint recipient with [Heiko Harborth](wikipedia://en/22801588) of the [Euler Medal](wikipedia://en/17545919). In 2012 Milne was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
