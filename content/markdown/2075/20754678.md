# Operator space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Operator space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a discipline within mathematics, an **operator space** is a [normed vector space](wikipedia://en/21538) (not necessarily a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989))[^o2] "given together with an [isometric](wikipedia://en/254777) [embedding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective_map) into the space *B(H)* of all [bounded operators](wikipedia://en/455961) on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H*.".[^o3][^o4] The appropriate morphisms between operator spaces are [completely bounded maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completely_bounded_map).

:::

## Equivalent formulations {#Equivalent_formulations}

Equivalently, an operator space is a [subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) of a [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184).

## Category of operator spaces {#Category_of_operator_spaces}

The [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of operator spaces includes [operator systems](wikipedia://en/10698414) and [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987). For operator systems, in addition to an induced matrix norm of an operator space, one also has an induced matrix order. For operator algebras, there is still the additional [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) structure.
