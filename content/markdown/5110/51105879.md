# 2010 Dominican Republic census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2010 Dominican Republic census"}

The **2010 Dominican Republic census** is the ninth Dominican Republic national census, raised from 1–7 December 2010, during the presidency of [Leonel Fernández](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonel_Fernández). This census collected information respect on sex, occupation, age, fertility, marital status, nationality, literacy, ability to vote, and housing. The Dominican Republic's next census took place in [2022](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_Dominican_Republic_Census).

:::
