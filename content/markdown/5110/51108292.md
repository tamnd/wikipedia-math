# Sophia Rabe-Hesketh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sophia Rabe-Hesketh"}

**Sophia Rabe-Hesketh** is a [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who works as a professor in the Department of Educational Statistics and Biostatistics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o2] Her research involves the development of [generalized linear mixed models](wikipedia://en/15233551) of data that incorporate [latent variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_variable) to handle hidden data.

:::

Rabe-Hesketh earned a bachelor's degree in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) in 1988. She completed her doctorate at King's College, also in theoretical physics, in 1992. Her dissertation concerned [image analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_analysis). After postdoctoral studies at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds), she returned to King's College as a reader in statistics in the Department of Biostatistics and Computing, part of the Institute of Psychiatry there. She moved to Berkeley in 2003, with an additional part-time professorship at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) from 2006 to 2012. She became president of the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500) in 2014.[^o2]

She is the author or co-author of multiple books, including *The Analysis of Proximity Data* (1997, with B. S. Everitt), *Analyzing Medical Data using S-PLUS* (2001, with B. S. Everitt), *Generalized Latent Variable Modeling: Multilevel, Longitudinal and Structural Equation Models* (2004, with A. Skrondal), *A Handbook of Statistical Analyses Using Stata* (4th ed., 2006, with B. S. Everitt), and *Multilevel and Longitudinal Modeling Using Stata* (3rd ed., in two vols., with A. Skrondal).[^o3]

In 2014 she was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) "for groundbreaking contributions to the field of generalized linear latent and mixed models for analysis of complex data in medicine, education, and the social sciences; for development of computational software; and for service to the profession."[^o4] In 2015 she was elected to the [National Academy of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Education).[^o2]
