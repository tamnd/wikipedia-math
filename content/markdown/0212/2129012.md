# Kurt Gödel Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kurt Gödel Society"}

The **Kurt Gödel Society** (**KGS**) is a [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) which was founded in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), Austria in 1987. It is an international organization aimed at promoting research primarily on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), with special attention to subjects that are connected with the Austrian logician and mathematician [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736), in whose honour it was named.

:::

The group also organizes an ongoing lecture series called *Collegium Logicum*. Former speakers include [Henk Barendregt](wikipedia://en/1651213), [George Boolos](wikipedia://en/340621), [Jaakko Hintikka](wikipedia://en/1710903) and [Wilfrid Hodges](wikipedia://en/1789771).

In April 2006, the Gödel society organized *Horizons of Truth*, an international symposium celebrating the 100th birthday of Kurt Gödel.[^o2] The Gödel society, with support from the [John Templeton Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Templeton_Foundation), awards "Kurt Gödel Research Prize Fellowships".[^o3] In 2008, a total amount of US\$ 680,000 was awarded to the recipients David Fernández-Duque, Pavel Hrubeš, Andrey Bovykin, [Peter Koellner](wikipedia://en/27901050), and [Thierry Coquand](wikipedia://en/31842262). In 2011, a total amount of EUR 450,000, was awarded to the recipients Danko Ilik, Sean Walsh, [Maryanthe Malliaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryanthe_Malliaris), Matteo Viale, and [Ulrich Kohlenbach](wikipedia://en/18204005).[^o4] In 2015, [SIGLOG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_SIGLOG), [EATCS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Association_for_Theoretical_Computer_Science), [EACSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EACSL) and the Kurt Gödel Society established the "[Alonzo Church Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alonzo_Church_Award) for Outstanding Contributions to Logic and Computation".[^o5]
