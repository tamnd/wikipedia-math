# Polish Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polish Mathematical Society"}

The **Polish Mathematical Society** (Polskie Towarzystwo Matematyczne) is the main professional society of Polish mathematicians and represents Polish mathematics within the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) (EMS) and the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) (IMU).

:::

::: {#o4 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Antiquitates Mathematicae"}

## History {#History}

The society was established in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) on 2 April 1919 . It was originally called the Mathematical Society in Kraków, the name was changed to the Polish Mathematical Society on 21 April 1920. It was founded by 16 mathematicians, [Stanisław Zaremba](wikipedia://en/1028345), [Franciszek Leja](wikipedia://en/1983106), [Jan Śleszyński](wikipedia://en/3033762), Alfred Rosenblatt, [Stefan Banach](wikipedia://en/29646), [Otto Nikodym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Nikodym), [Kazimierz Żorawski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazimierz_Żorawski) and [Leon Chwistek](wikipedia://en/1029740) were among them.[^o5][^o6]

Ever since its foundation, the society's main activity was to bring mathematicians together by means of organizing conferences and lectures. The second main activity is the publication of its annals *Annales Societatis Mathematicae Polonae*, consisting of:

- Series 1: [*Commentationes Mathematicae*](https://web.archive.org/web/20100807105055/http://www.ptm.org.pl/wydawnictwa/2/)
- Series 2: [Wiadomości Matematyczne](https://web.archive.org/web/20100807105050/http://www.ptm.org.pl/wydawnictwa/1/) ("Mathematical News"), in Polish
- Series 3: *[Mathematica Applicanda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematica_Applicanda)* (formerly [*Matematyka Stosowana*](https://wydawnictwa.ptm.org.pl/index.php/matematyka-stosowana) until 2012)
- Series 4: *[Fundamenta Informaticae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundamenta_Informaticae)*
- Series 5: [*Didactica Mathematicae*](https://wydawnictwa.ptm.org.pl/index.php/didactica-mathematicae)
- Series 6: [*Antiquitates Mathematicae*](https://wydawnictwa.ptm.org.pl/index.php/antiquitates-mathematicae)
- Series 7: [Delta](https://web.archive.org/web/20100807040140/http://www.ptm.org.pl/wydawnictwa/7/), in Polish

The annals are also known under the Polish name *Roczniki Polskiego Towarzystwa Matematycznego* and under the English name *Polish Mathematical Society Annals*.

## International Stefan Banach Prize {#International_Stefan_Banach_Prize}

The International Stefan Banach Prize (Międzynarodowa Nagroda im. Stefana Banacha) is an annual award presented by the Mathematical Society to mathematicians for best doctoral dissertations in the [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436).[^o11] Its aim is to "promote and financially support the most promising young researchers" in the field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It was founded in 2009 and is named in honour of a renowned Polish mathematician [Stefan Banach](wikipedia://en/29646) (1892-1945). The laureates of the award also receive a cash prize of [zl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_zloty) 25,000 (c.\$6,500). List of laureates:

- 2009: [Tomasz Elsner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomasz_Elsner)
- 2010: Jakub Gismatullin
- 2011: Łukasz Pańkowski
- 2012: Andras Mathe
- 2013: [Marcin Pilipczuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcin_Pilipczuk)
- 2014: [Dan Petersen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Petersen_%28mathematician%29)
- 2015: [Joonas Ilmavirta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joonas_Ilmavirta)
- 2016: [Adam Kanigowski](wikipedia://en/77389197)
- 2017: [Anna Szymusiak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Szymusiak)
- 2018-2020: *No Award*
- 2021: [Michał Miśkiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michał_Miśkiewicz_%28mathematician%29)
- 2022: [Marcin Sroka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcin_Sroka)
- 2024: [Dalimil Peša](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalimil_Peša)[^o12]
