# Anthony Bartholomay

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anthony Bartholomay"}

**Anthony Francis Bartholomay** (1919–1975) was an American mathematician who introduced [molecular set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_set_theory), a topic on which he wrote books.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Bartholomay was born on August 11, 1919. He would receive degrees from [Hamilton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_College), [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University), and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). Bartholomay would work at [Harvard Medical School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Medical_School), [Medical School of Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toledo), [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), [Keuka College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keuka_College), [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute), and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).[^o3] He died on March 21, 1975, at 55 years old. A resident of the [Somerset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerset,_New_Jersey) section of [Franklin Township, Somerset County, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franklin_Township,_Somerset_County,_New_Jersey), he died at a [New Brunswick, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Brunswick,_New_Jersey) hospital from a [stroke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stroke) he had three weeks prior to his death.[^o4]

## Molecular set theory {#Molecular_set_theory}

Molecular set theory (MST) is a mathematical formulation of the wide-sense chemical kinetics of biomolecular reactions in terms of sets of molecules and their chemical transformations represented by set-theoretical mappings between molecular sets.

## Career {#Career}

Bartholomay was a professor and chairman of the Department of Mathematical Medicine at the [Medical College of Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_College_of_Ohio).[^o7] He was only part of the Department of Mathematical Medicine from 1969 until 1972, when he left. The department subsequently collapsed the following year.

[Howard Hughes Medical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Hughes_Medical_Institute) website had Anthony Bartholomay listed as an investigator but was hosted at [Harvard Medical School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Medical_School) in 1957.[^o8]
