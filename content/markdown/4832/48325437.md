# Harald Garcke

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harald Garcke"}

*Image: Garcke.jpeg.* Garcke in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2015

**Harald Garcke** (born June 5, 1963 in [Bremerhaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremerhaven))[^o2] is a German mathematician and professor at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg).

:::

## Career and important results {#Career_and_important_results}

Garcke studied Mathematics and Computer Science at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and finished his PhD 1993 as a student of [Hans Wilhelm Alt](wikipedia://en/56765498) (*Travelling-Wave-Lösungen als Realisierung von Phasenübergängen bei Gedächtnismetallen*).[^o4] 1993/94 he was post-doc with [Charles M. Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_M._Elliott) at the [University of Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sussex) and from 1994 he was scientific assistant in Bonn where he finished his habilitation in 2000 (with the habilitation thesis *On mathematical models for phase separation in elastically stressed solids*).[^o5] In the year 2001 he got offers for professur-positions at the Universities Regensburg and Duisburg. Since 2002 he is full professor at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) where he was dean of the Mathematics department from 2005 to 2007.

Garcke works on nonlinear [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), free boundary problems, phase field equations, numerical analysis and geometric evolution equations. Together with [Christof Eck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christof_Eck) and [Peter Knabner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Knabner) he is the author of a book on mathematical modelling.[^o6]

His most important works are fundamental results on the [Cahn-Hilliard equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cahn-Hilliard_equation),[^o5][^o7][^o8] results on the thin film equation [^o9] and work with [Britta Nestler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Britta_Nestler) on phase field models.[^o10] Work with [J.W. Barrett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.W._Barrett) and [R. Nürnberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Nürnberg) on the mathematics of snow crystals was also well received by the popular media.[^o11]
