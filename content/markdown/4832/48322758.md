# Daniel Huybrechts

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Huybrechts"}

**Daniel Huybrechts** (9 November 1966) is a German mathematician, specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Huybrechts studied mathematics from 1985 at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), where in 1989 he earned his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) with Diplom thesis supervisor Herbert Kurke. In 1990–1992 Huybrechts studied at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), where he earned his PhD in 1992 under Herbert Kurke with thesis *Stabile Vektorbündel auf algebraischen Flächen. Tjurins Methode zum Studium der Geometrie der Modulräume*. In the academic year 1994–1995 he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and in 1996 at [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES). In 1996 he was a research assistant at the [University of Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Essen), where in 1998 he earned his Habilitation. In 1997–1998 he was at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure). He was a professor in 1998–2002 at the University of Cologne and in 2002–2005 at the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique) (Chargé de Cours) and, simultaneously, at the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII). Since 2005 he has been a professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o3]

Huybrechts does research on [K3 surfaces](wikipedia://en/646116) and their higher-dimensional analogues (compact [hyperkähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/646120)) and [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609) of [sheaves](wikipedia://en/44887605) on [varieties](wikipedia://en/248808).

In 2010 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad) and gave a talk *Hyperkähler Manifolds and Sheaves*.
