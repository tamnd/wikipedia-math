# Sijue Wu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sijue Wu"}

Wu Sijue

**Sijue Wu** (; born May 15, 1964) is a [Chinese American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_American) mathematician who works as the Robert W. and Lynne H. Browne Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). Her research involves the mathematics of [water waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_wave).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wu earned bachelor's and master's degrees in 1983 and 1986 from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).[^o3][^o4] She completed her doctorate in 1990 from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), under the supervision of [Ronald Coifman](wikipedia://en/1558980).[^o6] After a temporary instructorship at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), she became an assistant professor at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). She moved in 1996 to the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) and again to the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) in 1998. She became the Browne Professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 2008.[^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

A 1997 paper by Wu in *[Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae)*, "Well-posedness in Sobolev spaces of the full water wave problem in 2-D", was the subject of a featured review in *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)*.[^o8]

Wu was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2002, speaking on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o9]

She won the [Ruth Lyttle Satter Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/37554140)[^o10] and the silver [Morningside Medal](wikipedia://en/39615068) in 2001, and the gold Morningside Medal in 2010, becoming the first female mathematician to win the gold medal.[^o4] She was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2022.[^o11]
