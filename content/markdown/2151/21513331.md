# Sanjeev Arora (computer scientist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sanjeev Arora"}

**Sanjeev Arora** (born January 1968) is an [Indian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_American) [theoretical computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_computer_scientist) who works in AI and Machine learning. He is a Charles C. Fitzmorris Professor in Computer Science at Princeton University.

:::

## Career {#Career}

He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 2002–03.[^o5]

In 2008 he was inducted as a Fellow of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery).[^o6] In 2011 he was awarded the [ACM Infosys Foundation Award](http://www.acm.org/news/featured/acm-infosys-award-2011) (now renamed [ACM Prize in Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Prize_in_Computing)), given to mid-career researchers in Computer Science. He is a two-time recipient of the [Gödel Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel_Prize) (2001 & 2010). Arora has been awarded the [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916) for 2012 for his work on improving the [approximation ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximation_ratio) for [graph separators](wikipedia://en/1499165) and related problems from $O(\log n)$ to $O(\sqrt{\log n})$ (jointly with [Satish Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satish_Rao) and [Umesh Vazirani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umesh_Vazirani)).[^o7] In 2012 he became a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Investigator).[^o8] Arora was elected in 2015 to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) and in 2018 to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o9] He was a [plenary speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plenary_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o10]

He is a coauthor (with [Boaz Barak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boaz_Barak)) of the book *Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach.* He was a founder of Princeton's Center for [Computational Intractability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_intractability).[^o11] He and his coauthors have argued that certain [financial products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_products) are associated with computational [asymmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymmetry), which under certain conditions may lead to [market instability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Market_instability).[^o12]

Since September 2023, he is the founding Director of [Princeton Language and Intelligence](https://pli.princeton.edu/), a new unit at Princeton University devoted to study of large AI models and their applications.
