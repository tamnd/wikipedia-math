# Bernhard Korte

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bernhard Korte"}

**Bernhard H. Korte** (3 November 1938 – 26 April 2025) was a German computer scientist, a professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and an expert in [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Korte earned his doctorate ([Doctor rerum naturalium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate)) from the University of Bonn in 1967. His thesis was entitled "Beiträge zur Theorie der Hardy'schen Funktionenklassen" (translated, "*Contributions to the theory of [Hardy function classes](wikipedia://en/314780)*"), and was supervised by [Ernst Peschl](wikipedia://en/40394737) and Walter Thimm.[^o4] He earned his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1971, and briefly held faculty positions at [Regensburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regensburg_University) and [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) before joining the University of Bonn as a faculty member in 1972.[^o5] At the University of Bonn, Korte was the director of the Research Institute for [Discrete Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_Mathematics).[^o6]

Korte was a guest professor at [Stanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), [Cornell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), [Yale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), along with institutions in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome), [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona) and [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).

Korte died in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), [North Rhine-Westphalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Rhine-Westphalia) on 26 April 2025, at the age of 86.[^o7]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 1997, Korte received the State Prize of [Nordrhein-Westfalen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordrhein-Westfalen),[^o12] and in 2002 he was awarded the Grand Cross of the [Order of Merit of the Federal Republic of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Merit_of_the_Federal_Republic_of_Germany). He was also a winner of the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) and a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o5]
