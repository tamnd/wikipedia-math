# David Wagner (mathematics education)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Wagner"}

**David Wagner** (born 1965) is a Canadian mathematics educator and full professor at the [University of New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Brunswick), [Fredericton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredericton), Canada. He is an adjunct professor (professor II) at the [University of South-Eastern Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South-Eastern_Norway). Wagner wrote for [CBC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_News) and EdCan.

:::

## Service and functions {#Service_and_functions}

He is co-editor-in-chief of *[Educational Studies in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Studies_in_Mathematics)*.[^o4] Previously, he was an associate editor of *[Educational Studies in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Studies_in_Mathematics)* since 2016. He was managing editor of *[For the Learning of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/For_the_Learning_of_Mathematics)*[^o5] from 2011 to 2018. Wagner also published articles for [CBC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBC_News) and EdCan in 2014 and 2015.[^o6][^o7]

His main area of expertise is [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471).
