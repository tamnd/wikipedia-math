# Philipp Habegger

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Philipp Habegger"}

**Philipp Habegger** (born 23 July 1978) is a Swiss[^o6] mathematician and a professor of mathematics at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel) who works in [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Habegger was born on 23 July 1978.[^o6] He received his Ph.D. under the supervision of [David Masser](wikipedia://en/2172777) at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel) in 2007.

## Career {#Career}

From 2008 to 2010, Habegger was a ETH Fellow at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o6] He moved to the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) for a lectureship position in 2010.[^o6] In 2013, he was a von Neumann Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o7] As of 2021, Habegger is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel).[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Habegger's research focuses on [height functions](wikipedia://en/3141855) and their applications to [unlikely intersections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unlikely_intersections).[^o7]
