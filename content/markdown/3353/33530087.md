# Tetsuji Shioda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tetsuji Shioda"}

*Image: Shioda tetsuji.jpg.* Tetsuji Shioda, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2005

**Tetsuji Shioda** (塩田 徹治) was a Japanese mathematician who introduced [Shioda modular surfaces](wikipedia://en/33530042) and who used [Mordell–Weil lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell–Weil_lattice) to give examples of dense [sphere packings](wikipedia://en/368621). He was an invited speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1990.

:::
