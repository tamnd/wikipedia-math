# Fbsp wavelet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fbsp wavelet"}

*Image: Complex fbsp wavelet.PNG.* Frequency B-spline wavelets: cubic spline fbsp 3-1-2 complex wavelet.

In applied mathematics, **fbsp wavelets** are **frequency [B-spline wavelets](wikipedia://en/45226844)**.

:::

These frequency B-spline wavelets are complex wavelets whose spectrum are [spline](wikipedia://en/457680).

$$\operatorname{fbsp}^{(m-\mathrm{fb}-f_c) }(t) := {\sqrt {\mathrm{fb}}} \operatorname{sinc}^m \left( \frac {t} {\mathrm{fb}^m} \right) e^{j2 \pi f_c t}$$

where [sinc function](wikipedia://en/610583) that appears in [Shannon sampling theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shannon_sampling_theorem).

- *m* > 1 is the order of the spline
- fb is a bandwidth parameter
- *f<sub>c</sub>* is the wavelet center frequency

The [Shannon wavelet](wikipedia://en/11800092) (sinc wavelet) is then clearly a special case of fbsp.
