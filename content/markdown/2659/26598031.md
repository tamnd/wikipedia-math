# Pairwise Stone space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pairwise Stone space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and particularly in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a **pairwise Stone space** is a [bitopological space](wikipedia://en/5089733) $\scriptstyle (X,\tau_1,\tau_2)$ that is [pairwise compact](wikipedia://en/5089733#Bitopological_variants_of_topological_properties), [pairwise Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/5089733#Bitopological_variants_of_topological_properties), and [pairwise zero-dimensional](wikipedia://en/5089733#Bitopological_variants_of_topological_properties).

:::

Pairwise Stone spaces are a bitopological version of [Stone spaces](wikipedia://en/380219).

Pairwise Stone spaces are closely related to [spectral spaces](wikipedia://en/544273).

Theorem:[^o2] If $\scriptstyle (X,\tau)$ is a spectral space, then $\scriptstyle (X,\tau,\tau^*)$ is a pairwise Stone space, where $\scriptstyle \tau^*$ is the [de Groot dual](wikipedia://en/26592825) topology of $\scriptstyle \tau$ . Conversely, if $\scriptstyle (X,\tau_1,\tau_2)$ is a pairwise Stone space, then both $\scriptstyle (X,\tau_1)$ and $\scriptstyle (X,\tau_2)$ are spectral spaces.
