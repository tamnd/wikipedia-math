# De Groot dual

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="De Groot dual"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in particular in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), the **de Groot dual** (after [Johannes de Groot](wikipedia://en/6022862)) of a topology *τ* on a [set](wikipedia://en/26691) *X* is the topology *τ*\* whose [closed sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_sets) are generated by [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [saturated](wikipedia://en/26592789) [subsets](wikipedia://en/27631) of (*X*, *τ*).

:::
