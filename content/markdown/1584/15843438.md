# Amy Cohen-Corwin

*Image: Amy Cohen-Corwin 1979.jpg.* Cohen-Corwin in 1979

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amy Cohen-Corwin"}

**Amy Cohen-Corwin** (formerly known as **Amy C. Murray**) is a professor emerita of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University),[^o2] and former Dean of University College at Rutgers University. In 2006, she was named [Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o3]

:::

Cohen-Corwin is especially interested in the [Korteweg–de Vries equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korteweg–de_Vries_equation), cubic [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) on the line, and improving undergraduate education, especially for future teachers.[^o4] She worked on [Project SEED](wikipedia://en/33639818) whilst at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1970 which fueled her interest in [Mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471).[^o5]

Cohen-Corwin has held numerous organizational positions, including Co-organizer for the AIM ([American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005)) and NSF ([National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation))-sponsored workshop "Finding and Keeping Graduate Students in the Mathematical Sciences."[^o6]

## Awards {#Awards}

- [Louise Hay Award](https://awm-math.org/awards/hay-award/hay-award-2013/) for Contributions to Mathematics Education, Joint Mathematics Meeting, [Association for Women in Mathematics](https://awm-math.org/awards/awm-fellows/)[^o8] - 2013
- Fellow, [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://www.aaas.org/fellows), elected 2006[^o9]
- Fellow, [Association for Women in Mathematics](https://awm-math.org/awards/awm-fellows/2019-awm-fellows/), 2019[^o10]

## Education {#Education}

- Ph.D., 1970, Mathematics, [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley), under the supervision of [Murray H. Protter](wikipedia://en/18876634)
- M.S., 1966, Mathematics, University of California at Berkeley
- A.B., 1964, Mathematics, [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) ([Radcliffe College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliffe_College))[^o13]
