# A∞-operad

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="A∞-operad"}

In mathematics, an **A<sub>∞</sub>-operad** is a type of [operad](wikipedia://en/3126358) used in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) to describe algebraic structures where the property of [associativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associativity) is loosened. In a simple associative operation, such as the multiplication of numbers, the order of operations does not matter: $(a \times b) \times c = a \times (b \times c)$. An algebraic structure governed by an A<sub>∞</sub>-operad is one where this equality is not strict, but the two sides are connected by a [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) (a continuous path or transformation). The operad itself provides the formal structure for these paths and for higher-level paths that ensure all possible ways of regrouping are compatible with each other.

:::

More formally, an A<sub>∞</sub>-operad is a parameter space for a multiplication map that is [homotopy coherently](wikipedia://en/212250) [associative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associative). The "A" stands for "associative", and the infinity symbol "∞" indicates that the associativity holds up to an infinite hierarchy of higher homotopies. This concept is fundamental to the study of [loop spaces](wikipedia://en/2174332) and is a key tool in fields like [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) (through the [Fukaya category](wikipedia://en/31598730)). (An operad that describes a multiplication that is both homotopy coherently associative and homotopy coherently commutative is called an [E<sub>∞</sub>-operad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-infinity_operad).)

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

In the (usual) setting of operads with an action of the symmetric group on topological spaces, an operad *A* is said to be an *A*<sub>∞</sub>-operad if all of its spaces *A*(*n*) are Σ<sub>*n*</sub>-[equivariantly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant) [homotopy equivalent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_equivalent) to the discrete spaces Σ<sub>*n*</sub> (the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901)) with its multiplication action (where *n* ∈ **N**). In the setting of non-Σ operads (also termed nonsymmetric operads, operads without permutation), an operad *A* is *A*<sub>∞</sub> if all of its spaces *A*(*n*) are contractible. In other [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) than topological spaces, the notions of *homotopy* and *contractibility* have to be replaced by suitable analogs, such as [homology equivalences](wikipedia://en/3653039) in the category of [chain complexes](wikipedia://en/138404).

:::

## *A*<sub>*n*</sub>-operads {#An-operads}

The letter *A* in the terminology stands for "associative", and the infinity symbols says that associativity is required up to "all" higher homotopies. More generally, there is a weaker notion of ***A*<sub>*n*</sub>-operad** (*n* ∈ **N**), parametrizing multiplications that are associative only up to a certain level of homotopies. In particular,

- *A*<sub>1</sub>-spaces are [pointed spaces](wikipedia://en/1236666);
- *A*<sub>2</sub>-spaces are [H-spaces](wikipedia://en/600570) with no associativity conditions; and
- *A*<sub>3</sub>-spaces are homotopy associative H-spaces.

## *A*<sub>∞</sub>-operads and single loop spaces {#A∞-operads_and_single_loop_spaces}

A space *X* is the [loop space](wikipedia://en/2174332) of some other space, denoted by *BX*, if and only if *X* is an [algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_over_an_operad) over an $A_{\infty}$-operad and the [monoid](wikipedia://en/19652) *π*<sub>0</sub>(*X*) of its connected components is a group. An algebra over an $A_{\infty}$-operad is referred to as an **$\mathbf{A}_{\infty}$-space**. There are three consequences of this characterization of loop spaces. First, a loop space is an $A_{\infty}$-space. Second, a connected $A_{\infty}$-space *X* is a loop space. Third, the [group completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_completion) of a possibly disconnected $A_{\infty}$-space is a loop space.

The importance of $A_{\infty}$-operads in [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) stems from this relationship between algebras over $A_{\infty}$-operads and loop spaces.

## *A*<sub>∞</sub>-algebras {#A∞-algebras}

An algebra over the $A_{\infty}$-operad is called an $A_{\infty}$-algebra. Examples feature the [Fukaya category](wikipedia://en/31598730) of a symplectic manifold, when it can be defined (see also [pseudoholomorphic curve](wikipedia://en/2294542)).

::: {#o8 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The most obvious, if not particularly useful, example of an $A_{\infty}$-operad is the *associative operad* *a* given by $a(n) = \Sigma_n$. This operad describes strictly associative multiplications. By definition, any other $A_{\infty}$-operad has a map to *a* which is a homotopy equivalence.

A geometric example of an A<sub>∞</sub>-operad is given by the Stasheff polytopes or [associahedra](wikipedia://en/19698446).

A less combinatorial example is the **operad of little intervals**: The space $A(n)$ consists of all embeddings of *n* disjoint [intervals](wikipedia://en/49172) into the unit interval.

:::
