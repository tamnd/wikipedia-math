# Adams filtration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adams filtration"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially in the area of [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) known as [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655), the **Adams filtration** and the **Adams–Novikov filtration** allow a stable homotopy group to be understood as built from layers, the *n*th layer containing just those maps which require at most *n* auxiliary spaces in order to be a composition of homologically trivial maps. These filtrations, named after [Frank Adams](wikipedia://en/1223959) and [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515), are of particular interest because the [Adams (–Novikov) spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/4720689) converges to them.[^o2][^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of stable homotopy classes $[X,Y]$ between two [spectra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum_%28homotopy_theory%29) *X* and *Y* can be given a [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) by saying that a map $f\colon X\to Y$ has filtration *n* if it can be written as a composite of maps

$$X=X_0 \to X_1 \to \cdots \to X_n = Y$$

such that each individual map $X_i\to X_{i+1}$ induces the zero map in some fixed [homology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_theory) *E*. If *E* is ordinary mod-*p* [homology](wikipedia://en/431041), this filtration is called the **Adams filtration**, otherwise the **Adams–Novikov filtration**.

:::
