# Marcinkiewicz–Zygmund inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marcinkiewicz–Zygmund inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Marcinkiewicz–Zygmund inequality**, named after [Józef Marcinkiewicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Józef_Marcinkiewicz) and [Antoni Zygmund](wikipedia://en/362477), gives relations between [moments](wikipedia://en/368684) of a collection of [independent random variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independent_random_variables). It is a generalization of the rule for the sum of [variances](wikipedia://en/32344) of independent random variables to moments of arbitrary order. It is a special case of the [Burkholder-Davis-Gundy inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkholder-Davis-Gundy_inequality) in the case of discrete-time martingales.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=named-heading name="Statement of the inequality"}

## Statement of the inequality {#Statement_of_the_inequality}

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem** [^o5][^o6] If $\textstyle X_{i}$, $\textstyle i=1,\ldots,n$, are independent random variables such that $\textstyle E\left( X_{i}\right) =0$ and $\textstyle E\left( \left\vert X_{i}\right\vert ^{p}\right) <+\infty$, $\textstyle 1\leq p<+\infty$, then

$$A_{p}E\left( \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\vert X_{i}\right\vert ^{2}\right) _{{}}^{p/2}\right) \leq E\left( \left\vert \sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}\right\vert ^{p}\right) \leq B_{p}E\left( \left( \sum_{i=1}^{n}\left\vert X_{i}\right\vert ^{2}\right) _{{}}^{p/2}\right)$$

where $\textstyle A_{p}$ and $\textstyle B_{p}$ are positive constants, which depend only on $\textstyle p$ and not on the underlying distribution of the random variables involved.

:::

:::

## The second-order case {#The_second-order_case}

In the case $\textstyle p=2$, the inequality holds with $\textstyle A_{2}=B_{2}=1$, and it reduces to the rule for the sum of variances of independent random variables with zero mean, known from elementary statistics: If $\textstyle E\left( X_{i}\right) =0$ and $\textstyle E\left( \left\vert X_{i}\right\vert ^{2}\right) <+\infty$, then

$$\mathrm{Var}\left(\sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}\right)=E\left( \left\vert \sum_{i=1}^{n}X_{i}\right\vert ^{2}\right) =\sum_{i=1}^{n}\sum_{j=1}^{n}E\left( X_{i}\overline{X}_{j}\right) =\sum_{i=1}^{n}E\left( \left\vert X_{i}\right\vert ^{2}\right) =\sum_{i=1}^{n}\mathrm{Var}\left(X_{i}\right).$$

## See also {#See_also}

Several similar moment inequalities are known as [Khintchine inequality](wikipedia://en/10002357) and [Rosenthal inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosenthal_inequalities), and there are also extensions to more general symmetric [statistics](wikipedia://en/26703) of independent random variables.[^o9]
