# HP 9g

::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="HP 9g"}

The **HP 9g** (F2222A) is a [graphing calculator](wikipedia://en/335004) designed by [Kinpo Electronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinpo_Electronics), Inc and produced by [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard).[^o6] It has basic graphing, scientific and [programming features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming) designed for use by students.

:::

Despite resembling a typical [scientific calculator](wikipedia://en/439711) in appearance, such as those by competitors [Casio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casio) and [Sharp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharp_Corporation), the 9g departs from HP calculator tradition because it does not have an [RPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_Polish_Notation) mode. It is also particular unusual for its display, which includes a compact [dot-matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dot-matrix_display) grid for displaying graphs, a dot-matrix character line which displays expressions being input (acting as a continuation of the dot-matrix grid), and a [seven-segment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven-segment_display) line to display answers to expressions.

The HP 9g is similar to Citizen SRP-325G, also designed by Kinpo Electronics, Inc.
