# Y-homeomorphism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Y-homeomorphism"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **y-homeomorphism**, or **crosscap slide**, is a special type of auto-[homeomorphism](wikipedia://en/13660) in [non-orientable](wikipedia://en/187446) [surfaces](wikipedia://en/27865).

:::

It can be constructed by sliding a [Möbius strip](wikipedia://en/37817) included on the surface around an essential [1-sided](wikipedia://en/4785316) [closed curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_curve) until the original position; thus it is necessary that the surfaces have [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) greater than one. The projective plane ${\mathbb RP}^2$ has no y-homeomorphism.
