# Logic of information

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Logic of information"}

The **logic of information**, or the **logical theory of information**, considers the information content of logical [signs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sign_%28semiotics%29) and expressions along the lines initially developed by [Charles Sanders Peirce](wikipedia://en/6117). In this line of work, the concept of information serves to integrate the aspects of signs and expressions that are separately covered, on the one hand, by the concepts of [denotation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denotation) and [extension](wikipedia://en/39095), and on the other hand, by the concepts of [connotation](wikipedia://en/55671) and [comprehension](wikipedia://en/3885297).

:::

Peirce began to develop these ideas in his lectures "On the Logic of Science" at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1865) and the [Lowell Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lowell_Institute) (1866).
