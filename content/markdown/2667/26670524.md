# Herglotz–Zagier function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Herglotz–Zagier function"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Herglotz–Zagier function**,[^n1] named after [Gustav Herglotz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Herglotz) and [Don Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557),[^n2] is the function

:::

$$F(x)= \sum^{\infty}_{n=1} \left\{\frac{\Gamma^{\prime}(nx)}{\Gamma (nx)} -\log (nx)\right\} \frac{1}{n}.$$

It was introduced by Zagier who used it to obtain a [Kronecker limit formula](wikipedia://en/5512894) for [real quadratic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_quadratic_field).[^n3]
