# Klaus Fischer (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Klaus Fischer"}

**Klaus Gunter Fischer** (November 12, 1943 – July 2, 2009) was a German-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) of [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) origin. He worked on a wide range of problems in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

Fischer was chair of the Mathematics Department at [George Mason University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Mason_University) at the time of his death.
