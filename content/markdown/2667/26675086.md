# Gregory R. Hancock

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gregory R. Hancock"}

**Gregory Robert Hancock** (born April 18, 1963 in [Seattle, Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle,_Washington), [USA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States)) is a Professor of Measurement, Statistics and Evaluation. He is the current [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) head of the Educational Department of Measurement and Statistics (EDMS) program.[^o2] Hancock also co-hosts the podcast *Quantitude*, which focuses on quantitative methodology, with fellow statistician [Patrick J. Curran](wikipedia://en/53551359).[^o3]

:::
