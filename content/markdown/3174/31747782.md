::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="6-simplex honeycomb"}

In [six-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_dimension) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **6-simplex honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). The tessellation fills space by [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex), [rectified 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_6-simplex), and [birectified 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_6-simplex) facets. These facet types occur in proportions of 1:1:1 respectively in the whole honeycomb.

:::

::: {#o7 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="A6 lattice"}

## A6 lattice {#A6_lattice}

This [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is called the A6 lattice or **6-simplex lattice**. The 42 vertices of the [expanded 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expanded_6-simplex) vertex figure represent the 42 roots of the ${\tilde{A}}_6$ [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004).[^o8] It is the 6-dimensional case of a [simplectic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplectic_honeycomb). Around each vertex figure are 126 facets: 7+7 [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex), 21+21 [rectified 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_6-simplex), 35+35 [birectified 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_6-simplex), with the count distribution from the 8th row of [Pascal's triangle](wikipedia://en/49497).

The A\* lattice (also called A7) is the union of seven A<sub>6</sub> lattices, and has the [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the dual to the [omnitruncated 6-simplex honeycomb](wikipedia://en/36580989), and therefore the [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell) of this lattice is the [omnitruncated 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_6-simplex).
