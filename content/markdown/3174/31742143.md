# Lorentz surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lorentz surface"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Lorentz surface** is a [two-dimensional](wikipedia://en/27865) [oriented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oriented_manifold) [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) with a [conformal equivalence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_equivalence) class of [Lorentzian metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lorentzian_metric). It is the analogue of a [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) in [indefinite signature](wikipedia://en/463225).

:::
