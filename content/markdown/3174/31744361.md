# Motivic L-function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Motivic L-function"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **motivic *L*-functions** are a generalization of [Hasse–Weil *L*-functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse–Weil_L-function) to general [motives](wikipedia://en/430680) over [global fields](wikipedia://en/288358). The local *L*-factor at a [finite place](wikipedia://en/174705#Places) *v* is similarly given by the [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of a [Frobenius element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frobenius_element) at *v* acting on the [*v*-inertial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inertia_group) invariants of the *v*-adic realization of the motive. For [infinite places](wikipedia://en/174705#Places), [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483) gave a recipe in (Serre 1970) for the so-called Gamma factors in terms of the Hodge realization of the motive. It is conjectured that, like other [*L*-functions](wikipedia://en/245283), that each motivic *L*-function can be [analytically continued](wikipedia://en/80842) to a [meromorphic function](wikipedia://en/80857) on the entire [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628) and satisfies a [functional equation](wikipedia://en/364377) relating the *L*-function *L*(*s*, *M*) of a motive *M* to *L*(1 − *s*, *M*<sup>∨</sup>), where *M*<sup>∨</sup> is the *dual* of the motive *M*.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Basic examples include [Artin *L*-functions](wikipedia://en/2774171) and Hasse–Weil *L*-functions. It is also known (Scholl 1990), for example, that a motive can be attached to a [newform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newform) (i.e. a primitive [cusp form](wikipedia://en/610165)), hence their *L*-functions are motivic.

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=heading name="Conjectures"}

## Conjectures {#Conjectures}

Several conjectures exist concerning motivic *L*-functions. It is believed that motivic *L*-functions should all arise as [automorphic *L*-functions](wikipedia://en/32328300),[^o9] and hence should be part of the [Selberg class](wikipedia://en/2200454). There are also conjectures concerning the values of these *L*-functions at integers generalizing those known for the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809), such as [Deligne's conjecture on special values of *L*-functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deligne's_conjecture_%28L-functions%29), the [Beilinson conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beilinson_conjecture), and the [Bloch–Kato conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloch–Kato_conjecture_%28L-functions%29) (on special values of *L*-functions).

:::
