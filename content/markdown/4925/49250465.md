# Wong graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wong graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Wong graph** is a 5-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821) [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 30 vertices and 75 edges.[^o4] It is one of the four [(5,5)-cage graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cage_graph), the others being the [Foster cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_cage), the [Meringer graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meringer_graph), and the [Robertson–Wegner graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robertson–Wegner_graph).

:::

Like the unrelated [Harries–Wong graph](wikipedia://en/23702758), it is named after Pak-Ken Wong.[^o5]

It has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 4, diameter 3, and is 5-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph).

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Wong graph is

$$(x-5)(x+1)^2(x^2-5)^3(x-1)^5(x^2+x-5)^8.$$

:::
