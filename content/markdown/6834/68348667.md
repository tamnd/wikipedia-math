# Fang Liu (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fang Liu"}

**Fang Liu** is a Chinese-American statistician and data scientist whose research topics include [differential privacy](wikipedia://en/23473696), data synthesis, trustworthy [statistical learning](wikipedia://en/1053303), [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412), [regularization](wikipedia://en/2009061), [missing data](wikipedia://en/7859676), and applications in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878). She is a Notre Dame Collegiate professor in the Department of Applied and Computational Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Liu was talented in mathematics as a child, competed in mathematics competitions, and wanted to become a mathematician, but was discouraged from doing so by her parents, who wanted her to become a physician. As a compromise, she studied biology at [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University), where she earned a bachelor's degree in 1997.

She began her graduate studies at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) intending to study genetics, but quickly switched to a program in statistics, and earned a master's degree there in 1999, and a Ph.D. from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 2003. Her dissertation, *Bayesian Methods for Statistical Disclosure Control in Microdata*, involved both [data privacy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_privacy) and [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412), and was supervised by [Roderick J. A. Little](wikipedia://en/64771099).

After completing her doctorate, she became a researcher at the [Merck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merck_&_Co.) Research Laboratories. She returned to academia, joining the Notre Dame faculty, in 2011. Her doctoral students at Notre Dame have included [Claire McKay Bowen](wikipedia://en/77056038).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Liu was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2021, "for novel contributions to differentially private synthetic data and Bayesian modeling; for outstanding interdisciplinary research in clinical and public health studies; for leadership in education and training; and for service to the profession". Liu became an [Elected Member of the International Statistical Institute](https://www.isi-web.org/elected-membership) in 2024.
