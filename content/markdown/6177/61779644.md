# Hajnal Andréka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hajnal Andréka"}

**Hajnal Ilona Andréka** (also known as Hajnalka Andréka, born November 17, 1947) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians) mathematician specializing in [algebraic logic](wikipedia://en/7870034). She is a research professor emeritus at the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Andréka was born on November 17, 1947, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest). She earned a diploma in mathematics in 1971 from [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), completed a Ph.D. there in 1975, and earned a [candidate's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_%28degree%29) in 1978. In 1992, she earned a [Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.) degree from the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).

She worked in the Hungarian Ministry of Heavy Industries from 1971 to 1977, and has been affiliated with the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1977.

## Books {#Books}

**Andréka's books include:**

- *Cylindric Set Algebras* (with [Leon Henkin](wikipedia://en/356165), Donald Monk, [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920), and István Németi, Lecture Notes in Mathematics 883, Springer, 1981, )
- *Universal Algebraic Logic: Dedicated to the Unity of Science* (with István Németi and Ildikó Sain, Studies in universal logic, Birkhäuser, 2008)
- *Cylindric-like Algebras and Algebraic Logic* (edited with Miklós Ferenczi and István Németi, Bolyai Society Mathematical Studies 22, Springer, 2013, )
- *Simple Relation Algebras* (with Steven Givant, Springer, 2017, )

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Andréka won the [Géza Grünwald](wikipedia://en/35261957) Commemorative Prize for young researchers of the [János Bolyai Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22777615) in 1975, and the [Gyula Farkas](wikipedia://en/6107158) Prize in applied mathematics of the János Bolyai Mathematical Society in 1978. In 1979, the [John von Neumann Computer Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann_Computer_Society) gave her their [László Kalmár](wikipedia://en/1988240) Prize, and in 1987, the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) gave her their [Alfréd Rényi Prize](wikipedia://en/26466805).
