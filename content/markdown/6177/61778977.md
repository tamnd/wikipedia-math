# Cabiria Andreian Cazacu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cabiria Andreian Cazacu"}

**Cabiria Andreian Cazacu** (February 19, 1928 – May 22, 2018) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) mathematician known for her work in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). She held the chair in mathematical analysis at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) from 1973 to 1975, and was dean of the faculty of mathematics at the University of Bucharest from 1976 to 1984.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Andreian Cazacu was born on February 19, 1928, in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași), the daughter of mathematics teacher Ioan T. Ardeleanu.[^o4] Towards the end of [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), her family became refugees in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest), where she completed her high school studies in 1945. She then enrolled in the Faculty of Mathematics at the University of Bucharest, graduating with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in 1949; her undergraduate thesis, on *Generalized nilpotent groups*, was written under the guidance of [Dan Barbilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_Barbu). She then continued at the university, first as a teaching assistant and then as a lecturer starting in 1950. She became a student of [Simion Stoilow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simion_Stoilow), completing a doctorate in 1955 under his supervision, with the dissertation *Normally exhaustible [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181)*. After being named associate professor in 1955, she completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1967, with the habilitation thesis *Classes of Riemann coverings*, and was promoted to full professor in 1968.

From 1951 to 1969 Andreian Cazacu held a research position at the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230), where she was a leading participant in Stoilow's seminar on complex analysis. She held visiting positions at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin), [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles), the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki), the [University of Łódź](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Łódź), and [Université de Moncton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Moncton).

Between 1976 and 2010 she supervised the Ph.D. theses of 15 students.[^o15] Andreian Cazacu was one of the main organizers of eleven editions of the Romanian-Finnish Seminar on complex analysis and potential theory, founded by [Rolf Nevanlinna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rolf_Nevanlinna) and Stoilow; the proceedings of four of these seminars, for which she was an editor, appeared in the [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) [Lecture Notes in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/47931235) series as four separate volumes.

She died on May 22, 2018, in Bucharest and was buried at [Ghencea Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghencea_Cemetery).

## Publications {#Publications}

Andreian Cazacu wrote "approximately 100 scientific papers and six books". The books include:

- Andreian Cazacu, Cabiria; Constantinescu, Corneliu; Jurchescu, Martin. (1965). "Probleme moderne de teoria funcțiilor". Editura Academiei Republicii Populare Române. mr:0200105
- Andreian Cazacu, Cabiria; Deleanu, Aristide; Jurchescu, Martin. (1966). "Topologie, categorii, suprafețe riemanniene". Editura Academiei Republicii Populare Române. mr:0215663
- Andreian Cazacu, Cabiria. (1975). "Theorie der Funktionen mehrerer komplexer Veränderlicher". 51. Birkhäuser. isbn:3-7643-0770-6. mr:0473209

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Andreian Cazacu won the [Simion Stoilow Prize](wikipedia://en/20268944) of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy) in 1966. In 1998 the [University of Craiova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Craiova) gave her an honorary doctorate. She was awarded in 2000 the [National Order of Faithful Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Faithful_Service), Officer rank by [President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Romania) [Ion Iliescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ion_Iliescu), while in 2011 she was awarded the National Order of Faithful Service, Commander rank by President [Traian Băsescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traian_Băsescu). She became an honorary member of the Romanian Academy in 2006. In 2010, the journal *Complex Variables and Elliptic Equations* published a special issue in honor of her 80th birthday.
