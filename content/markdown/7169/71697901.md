# Annals of Pure and Applied Logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annals of Pure and Applied Logic"}

The ***Annals of Pure and Applied Logic*** is a peer-reviewed scientific journal published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) that publishes papers on applications of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) in mathematics, in computer science, and in other related disciplines.[^o2] Until 1983, it was known as the ***Annals of Mathematical Logic***.

:::

The editors of *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic* include mathematicians [Ulrich Kohlenbach](wikipedia://en/18204005) at [TU Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Darmstadt) in Germany, [Thomas W. Scanlon](wikipedia://en/80080715) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), and [Philip Scott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philip_Scott) at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa).[^o2]
