# Beatrice Meini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beatrice Meini"}

**Beatrice Meini** (born 1968) is an Italian [computational mathematician](wikipedia://en/10433833) and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506) specializing in [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660) and its applications to [Markov chains](wikipedia://en/60876), [matrix equations](wikipedia://en/27567102), and [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578). She is Professor of Numerical Analysis in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Meini was born on 5 December 1968 in [Pontedera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontedera), in the [province of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Pisa). She earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in mathematics from the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) in 1993, and completed her Ph.D. there in 1998. Her dissertation, *Fast Algorithms For The Numerical Solution of Structured Markov Chains*, was supervised by Dario Bini.

After postdoctoral research with the [Italian National Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Council_%28Italy%29) (CNR) and at the University of Pisa, she became an associate professor of numerical analysis at the University of Pisa in 2005, and a full professor in 2016.

## Books {#Books}

Meini is the coauthor of books including:

- *Numerical Methods for Structured Markov Chains* (Oxford University Press, 2005, with Dario Bini and Guy Latouche)
- *Numerical Solution of Algebraic Riccati Equations* (Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 2011, with Dario Bini and Bruno Iannazzo)
