# Margaret Wu

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret Wu"}

**Margaret Wu** is an Australian statistician and [psychometrician](wikipedia://en/24982) who specialises in [educational measurement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_measurement). She is an honorary professor at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Wu studied [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) and graduated in 1972.[^o4] She worked at [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University) as a research assistant from 1973 to 1975, where she taught herself to program.[^o5] She worked with Watterson on the [Watterson estimator](wikipedia://en/9168589), a means to determine the [genetic diversity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genetic_diversity) of a population.[^o5]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Wu joined [CSIRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CSIRO) as a technical officer in 1977.[^o4] She earned a [graduate diploma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_diploma) in computer studies from the [Royal Melbourne Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Melbourne_Institute_of_Technology) in 1985. Wu became a high school teacher at [Ivanhoe Girls' Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivanhoe_Girls'_Grammar_School), teaching Chinese and mathematics.[^o4] She was appointed as a senior research fellow in the Australian Council of Educational Research Development (ACER) in 1992, where she was deputy director of [Programme for International Student Assessment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programme_for_International_Student_Assessment) (PISA). Working with ACER and the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne), Wu completed her [master's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree), which was awarded the Freda Cohen Award for the Best Masters Thesis in Education. Wu remained at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) for her PhD, modelling student assessment data with underlying abilities modelled as [latent variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_variable).[^o4]

She was appointed [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) in 2008. She studied whether collaborative teams of teachers using [evidence-based decisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evidence-based_design) influenced [student achievement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_achievement).[^o7] She was made a professor at [Victoria University, Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University,_Melbourne) in 2012. Wu is sceptical about the importance of the [National Assessment Program – Literacy and Numeracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Assessment_Program_–_Literacy_and_Numeracy) ([NAPLAN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAPLAN)) and [PISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programme_for_International_Student_Assessment) results due to [measurement errors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurement_error) acquired during collection of data.[^o8] She is also concerned about the [inferences](wikipedia://en/317465) that can be drawn from assessment data, such as using student performance to evaluate teacher performance.[^o9][^o10][^o11] She believes that whilst teachers contribute to education, other factors are likely to be more important.[^o12] After the data was published on the website *My School*, Wu began to speak up about the misuse of [standardised testing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardized_test).[^o12][^o13] Her efforts resulted into an enquiry into the effectiveness of [NAPLAN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAPLAN).[^o14] Her findings were confirmed in 2018, when [Les Perelman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Les_Perelman) and [Walt Haney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Haney) reported that [NAPLAN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAPLAN) results of one million students should "be discarded".[^o15][^o16]

In 1995, Wu began to concentrate on the development of [Item Response Theory](wikipedia://en/420159).[^o17] She developed two [item response](wikipedia://en/420159) software programs that analyse [PISA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programme_for_International_Student_Assessment) and [TIMSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TIMMS) data; *ACER ConQuest* (1998) and the [R](wikipedia://en/376707)-package *TAM* (2010).[^o18][^o19][^o20]

Wu's contributions to the [Watterson estimator](wikipedia://en/9168589) were uncovered by a team of undergraduate students led by [Emilia Huerta-Sánchez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilia_Huerta-Sánchez) ([Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University)) and [Rori Rohlfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rori_Rohlfs) ([San Francisco State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Francisco_State_University)).[^o4][^o5][^o21] The students searched every issue of [Theoretical Population Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_Population_Biology) published between 1970 and 1990. She was one of several "acknowledged programmers", who were disproportionately women, who contributed significantly to highly cited manuscripts.[^o21]
