# Gerlind Plonka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gerlind Plonka"}

**Gerlind Plonka-Hoch** is a German [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) specializing in [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324) and [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing), and known for her work on [refinable functions](wikipedia://en/4557519) and [curvelets](wikipedia://en/16137869). She is a professor at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), in the Institute for Numerical and Applied Mathematics.

:::

Plonka earned her Ph.D. from the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock) in 1993. Her dissertation, *Periodische Lagrange- und Hermite-Spline-Interpolation*, concerned [polynomial interpolation](wikipedia://en/203565) using [Lagrange polynomials](wikipedia://en/217523) and [Hermite splines](wikipedia://en/270977), and was supervised by Manfred Tasche.

She was the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2016.
