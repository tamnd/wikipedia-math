# Daniela De Angelis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Daniela De Angelis"}

**Daniela De Angelis MBE** is an Italian biostatistician specializing in infectious disease modelling. Her current roles are Professor of Statistical Science for Health at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in the Department of Primary Care and Public Health and Deputy Director and Programme Leader at the [Medical Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_Research_Council_%28United_Kingdom%29) [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) Unit.[^o3] She has published research on the development and application of statistical methods to monitor infectious diseases such as SARS COVID-19, influenza, HIV and hepatitis C. De Angelis is a member of scientific advisory groups such as [NICE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NICE), [WHO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WHO), and [UNAIDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNAIDS). She is a member of SPI-M (Scientific Pandemic Influenza Advisory Committee, subgroup on Modelling) and the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) Task Force for COVID-19.[^o3] In 2022, she was awarded an Honorary MBE for her services to Medical Research and Public Health.[^o4]

:::

## Career history {#Career_history}

In 2017, the [Infected Blood Inquiry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contaminated_blood_scandal_in_the_United_Kingdom#2017_–_Legal_action_and_UK_public_inquiry) was launched.[^o6] De Angelis is a member of the Statistics Expert Group as part of this inquiry.[^o7]

In 2019, De Angelis was appointed Professor of Statistical Science for Health at the University of Cambridge.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

### HIV {#HIV}

In HIV modelling, De Angelis was the senior author of a study that projected that England would diagnose 95% of people living with HIV by 2025.[^o11] This is one of three aspects of UNAIDS' 90-90-90 goal: to have 90% of people with HIV diagnosed, 90% of people diagnosed with HIV receiving antiretroviral therapy and 90% of people receiving this therapy having viral suppression.[^o12]

### COVID-19 {#COVID-19}

De Angelis was the senior statistician for the MRC Biostatistics Unit's "nowcasting" and forecasting of COVID-19 data: using statistical modelling to estimate current (and predict future) numbers of infections, [*R*<sub>t</sub>](wikipedia://en/917273#Effective_reproduction_number), numbers of hospital admissions and more, by age and region in England.[^o14][^o15] This information was provided directly to the SAGE sub-group, SPI-M and to regional teams at the [UK Health Security Agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Health_Security_Agency) (UKHSA).[^o14]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2018, De Angelis received the [Suffrage Science Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffrage_Science_award) in Maths & Computing.[^o17]

In 2021, she received the University of Cambridge Vice-Chancellor's Established Academic Award for her work on real-time monitoring of the SARS-COV2 pandemic.[^o18]

In 2022, De Angelis was awarded an honorary MBE for services to medical research and public health.[^o19] That same year, she also received the [Weldon Memorial Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weldon_Memorial_Prize) as a member of the Scientific Pandemic Influenza Group on Modelling, Operational sub-group (SPI-M-O).[^o20]
