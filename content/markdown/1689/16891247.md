# Flype

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Flype"}

*Image: Flype.svg.* A flype consists of turning a tangle, **T**, by 180 degrees.

In the [mathematical theory of knots](wikipedia://en/153008), a **flype** is a kind of manipulation of knot and [link diagrams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_diagram) used in the [Tait flyping conjecture](wikipedia://en/2749975). It consists of twisting a part of a knot, a [tangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangle_%28knot_theory%29) **T**, by 180 degrees. Flype comes from a [Scots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scots_language) word meaning *to fold* or *to turn back* ("as with a sock").[^o2] Two reduced alternating diagrams of an [alternating link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_link) can be transformed to each other using flypes. This is the [Tait flyping conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tait_flyping_conjecture), proven in 1991 by [Morwen Thistlethwaite](wikipedia://en/16705411) and [William Menasco](wikipedia://en/16854432).

:::
