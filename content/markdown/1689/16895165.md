# Photon diffusion equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Photon diffusion equation"}

**Photon diffusion equation** is a second order [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564) describing the time behavior of photon fluence rate distribution in a low-absorption high-scattering medium.

:::

Its mathematical form is as follows.

$$\nabla(D(r)\cdot\nabla)\Phi(\vec{r},t)-v\mu_a(\vec{r})\Phi(\vec{r},t)+vS(\vec{r},t)=\frac{\partial\Phi(\vec{r},t)}{\partial t}$$

where $\Phi$ is photon [fluence rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluence_rate) (W/cm<sup>2</sup>), $\nabla$ is [del](wikipedia://en/151925) operator, $\mu_a$ is absorption coefficient (cm<sup>−1</sup>), $D$ is [diffusion constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffusion_constant), $v$ is the speed of light in the medium (m/s), and $S$ is an isotropic source term (W/cm<sup>3</sup>).

Its main difference with [diffusion equation](wikipedia://en/645335) in physics is that photon diffusion equation has an absorption term in it.

::: {#o3 .application cue=heading name="Application"}

## Application {#Application}

### Medical Imaging {#Medical_Imaging}

The properties of photon diffusion as explained by the equation is used in [diffuse optical tomography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffuse_optical_tomography).
