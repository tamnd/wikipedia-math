# Henry C. Wente

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henry C. Wente"}

**Henry Christian Wente** (August 18, 1936 – January 20, 2020)[^o2] was an American mathematician, known for his 1997 discovery of the [Wente torus](wikipedia://en/28456892), an [immersed](wikipedia://en/6993953) [constant-mean-curvature surface](wikipedia://en/35544695) whose existence disproved a conjecture of [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Wente obtained both his bachelor's degree and his Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o2] He completed his doctorate in 1966, under the supervision of [Garrett Birkhoff](wikipedia://en/1180916). He was a distinguished professor emeritus of mathematics at the [University of Toledo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toledo), which he joined in 1971.[^o2] In 1988 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Berkeley, California.[^o6] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
