# Ursula Hamenstädt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ursula Hamenstädt"}

*Image: Ursula Hamenstädt, circa 1986 (re-scanned, headshot).jpg.* Ursula Hamenstädt, Berkeley 1986

**Ursula Hamenstädt** (born 15 January 1961) is a German mathematician who works as a professor at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).[^o2] Her primary research subject is [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hamenstädt earned her PhD from the University of Bonn in 1986, under the supervision of [Wilhelm Klingenberg](wikipedia://en/33387331). Her dissertation, *Zur Theorie der Carnot-Caratheodory Metriken und ihren Anwendungen* \[*The theory of Carnot–Caratheodory metrics and their applications*\], concerned the theory of [sub-Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/943637).

After completing her doctorate, she became a [Miller Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miller_Research_Fellows) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and then an assistant professor at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) before returning to Bonn as a faculty member in 1990.[^o2]

## Honors {#Honors}

Hamenstädt was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010.[^o6] In 2012 she was elected to the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina),[^o7] and in the same year she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] She was the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2017.[^o9]
