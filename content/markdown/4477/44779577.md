# Santiago López de Medrano

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Santiago López de Medrano"}

López de Medrano **Santiago López de Medrano Sánchez** (born October 15, 1942, in [Mexico City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico_City)) is a Mexican mathematician, who works as a researcher at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM).[^o2][^o3] His research has concerned [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), [biomathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomathematics), and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562).[^o3]

:::

López de Medrano did his undergraduate studies at UNAM,[^o3] and earned his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1969, under the supervision of [William Browder](wikipedia://en/6646431). He returned to UNAM as a researcher and professor in 1968, and was president of the Mexican Mathematical Society from 1969 to 1973.[^o3]

López de Medrano presented his work on [knot invariants](wikipedia://en/166898) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1970.[^o3] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
