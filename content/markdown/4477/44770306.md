# Tim Cochran

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tim Cochran"}

**Thomas** "**Tim**" **Daniel Cochran** (April 7, 1955 – December 16, 2014) was a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), especially [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), [the theory of knots and links](wikipedia://en/153008) and associated algebra.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

*Image: Tim Cochran 1986 (enlarged headshot).jpg.* Cochran in 1986

Tim Cochran was a valedictorian for the [Severna Park High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Severna_Park_High_School) Class of 1973. Later, he was an undergraduate at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1982 (*Embedding 4-manifolds in S<sup>5</sup>*). He then returned to MIT as a C.L.E. Moore Postdoctoral Instructor from 1982 to 1984. He was an [NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) postdoctoral fellow from 1985 to 1987. Following brief appointments at Berkeley and [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), he started at Rice University as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_Professor) in 1990. He became a [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) at Rice University in 1998. He died unexpectedly, aged 59, on December 16, 2014,[^o5] while on a year-long sabbatical leave supported by a fellowship from the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation).[^o6]

## Research contributions {#Research_contributions}

With his coauthors [Kent Orr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_Orr) and [Peter Teichner](wikipedia://en/63453744), Cochran defined the [solvable filtration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solvable_filtration) of the [knot concordance group](wikipedia://en/17519029), whose lower levels encapsulate many classical knot concordance invariants.

Cochran was also responsible for naming the [slam-dunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slam-dunk) move for [surgery diagrams](wikipedia://en/1240699) in low-dimensional topology.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

While at Rice, he was named an Outstanding Faculty Associate (1992–93), and received the Faculty Teaching and Mentoring Award from the Rice Graduate Student Association (2014)[^o9]

He was named a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o10] in 2014, for *contributions to low-dimensional topology, specifically knot and link concordance, and for mentoring numerous junior mathematicians*.
