# Susan Friedlander

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan Friedlander"}

**Susan Jean Friedlander** ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Née) **Poate**; born January 26, 1946) is an American mathematician. Her research concerns mathematical [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), the [Euler equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_equations) and the [Navier-Stokes equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navier-Stokes_equations).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Friedlander graduated from [University College, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) with a [BS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in Mathematics in 1967. She was awarded a [Kennedy Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kennedy_Scholarship) to study at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT), where she earned an [MS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in 1970. She completed her [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1972 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [Louis Norberg Howard](wikipedia://en/48284809).[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

From 1972–1974, Friedlander was a Visiting Member at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), followed by a year as an instructor at Princeton University. In 1975, she joined the faculty in the Mathematics department at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois-Chicago). In 2007, she moved to the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) where she is Professor of Mathematics and the Director of the Center for Applied Mathematical Sciences.

## Service {#Service}

From 1996–2010, Friedlander served as an officer of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the role of Associate Secretary. In 2005, she was appointed the first female Editor-in-Chief of the *[Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2668304)*.[^o7][^o8] Her other leadership activities include membership of the Scientific Advisory Committee of the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (2001–2006), the Board of Mathematical Sciences and their Applications (2008–2011), the Section A Steering Committee of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (2013–2015), and the MIT Mathematics Department Visiting Committee (2013–2021). She is currently the Chair of the Mathematical Council of the Americas.

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- 1967–1969 Kennedy Scholarship, MIT
- 1993 N.S.F Visiting Professorship for Women, [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University)
- 1995 Elected Honorary Member, [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251)
- 1998 Medal of the [Institute Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595)
- 2003 Senior Scholar Award, University of Illinois at Chicago
- 2012 Fellow, [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376)[^o10]
- 2012 Fellow, the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science)[^o11]
- 2012 Fellow, the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o12]

## Personal {#Personal}

Friedlander is married to mathematician [Eric Friedlander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Friedlander).[^o8]
