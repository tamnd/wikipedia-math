# Colin McLarty

*Image: Colin McLarty.jpg.* Colin McLarty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Colin McLarty"}

**Colin McLarty** (born July 12, 1951) is an American [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) whose publications have ranged widely in [philosophy](wikipedia://en/304939) and the [foundations of](wikipedia://en/169358) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/19636), as well as in the [history of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science) and of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).

:::

## Research {#Research}

### Category theory {#Category_theory}

He has written papers about [Saunders Mac Lane](wikipedia://en/336574), one of the founders of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).

McLarty's *Elementary Categories and Elementary Toposes* describes [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [topos theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topos_theory) at an elementary level.[^o4]

McLarty worked on establishing that [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953) can be proven in a setting with much weaker assumptions than the ones used in [Wiles's proof of Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/21950759), which makes use of involved category theoretical constructions.[^o5]

### History of Mathematics {#History_of_Mathematics}

He is a member of the Grothendieck Circle, which provides on-line and open access to many writings about the mathematician [Alexandre Grothendieck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Grothendieck), who revolutionized [Banach-space theory](wikipedia://en/3989) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and whose life has fascinated many biographers and mathematical scientists.[^o7]

McLarty has also written about the German [algebraist](wikipedia://en/18716923) [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) and her involvement with [German political history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_politics).[^o8]

## Positions {#Positions}

At [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University) (CWRU), Colin McLarty is the Truman P. Handy Professor of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and the current Chair of the Philosophy Department.[^o10]

At CWRU, he is also a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).
