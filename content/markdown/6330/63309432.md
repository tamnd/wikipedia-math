# International Journal of Geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Journal of Geometry"}

The ***International Journal of Geometry*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) that covers [Euclidean](wikipedia://en/9417), [Non-Euclidean](wikipedia://en/58610) and [Discrete](wikipedia://en/386468) [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).

:::

It was established in 2012 with two volumes per year, and as of 2021 is published quarterly by the Department of Mathematics of the [Vasile Alecsandri National College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasile_Alecsandri_National_College_%28Bacău%29) of [Bacău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacău). It is abstracted and indexed among others by [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH),[^o3] [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683),[^o4] the [Electronic Journals Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journals_Library)[^o5] and [Ebsco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBSCO_Information_Services). Its founding editor-in-chief is Cătălin Barbu, a professor of mathematics at the Vasile Alecsandri National College of Bacău.
