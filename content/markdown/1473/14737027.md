The **compound of twenty octahedra** is a [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809). It's composed of a symmetric arrangement of 20 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) (considered as triangular [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms)). It is a special case of the [compound of 20 octahedra with rotational freedom](wikipedia://en/14895644), in which pairs of octahedral vertices coincide.[^o5]

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

This compound shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [great dirhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/2910030), the [great disnub dirhombidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4145225), and the [compound of twenty tetrahemihexahedra](wikipedia://en/14736821).

It may be constructed as the [exclusive or](wikipedia://en/105979) of the two [enantiomorphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enantiomorphs) of the [great snub dodecicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157674).
