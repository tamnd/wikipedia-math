# Mark Pollicott

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mark Pollicott"}

**Mark Pollicott** (born 24 September 1959) is a British mathematician known for his contributions to [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). He has a particular interest in applications to other areas of mathematics, including [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and analysis.[^o3]

:::

Pollicott attended [High Pavement College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College_Nottingham#ncn_High_Pavement) in [Nottingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nottingham), where his teachers included the [Booker Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booker_Prize) winning author [Stanley Middleton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanley_Middleton). He gained a BSc in Mathematics and Physics in 1981 and a PhD in mathematics in 1984 both at the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick). His PhD supervisor was [Bill Parry](wikipedia://en/7618438) and his thesis title *The Ruelle Operator, [Zeta Functions](wikipedia://en/37041839) and the Asymptotic Distribution of Closed Orbits*.[^o4]

He held permanent positions at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), [University of Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Porto), and [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) before appointment to the [Fielden Chair of Pure Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fielden_Chair_of_Pure_Mathematics) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) (1996–2004). He then returned to a professorship at Warwick in 2005. In addition, he has held numerous visiting positions including ones at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) in Paris, the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech), and the [University of Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble_Alpes_University). He has been recipient of a Royal Society University Research Fellowship, two [Leverhulme Trust Senior Research Fellowships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverhulme_Trust_Senior_Research_Fellowship) and an E.U. Marie Curie Chair.
