# Andrea Braides

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrea Braides"}

*Image: Braides.jpg.* Braides at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2011

**Andrea Braides** (born 12 April 1961) is an Italian mathematician, specializing in the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882). He is a professor at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata) and at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) (SISSA) in Triest.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born in [Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Udine), Braides studied at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) and Scuola Normale Superiore obtaining the degree in Mathematics (Laurea) in 1983 (*Gamma-Limits of Functionals in the Calculus of Variations*) supervised by [Ennio De Giorgi](wikipedia://en/1752401) and then at the *corsi di perfezionamento* (course of higher specialization) at [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore). He taught at the [University of Udine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Udine) in 1985–86 and then served two years of *servizio civile*. At the [University of Brescia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brescia), he became in 1988 a research associate and in 1992 an associate professor. From 1995 to 2000 he was an associate professor at SISSA in Triest and from 2000 to the present a full professor at the University of Rome Tor Vergata.

He was a visiting professor at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (in 1994 and again in 2004), at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) in Leipzig (in 1998), at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech), at the Centre Émile Borel in Paris, at the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837), at the University of Paris VI and the University of Paris XIII, at [Carnegie-Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie-Mellon_University), at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) (Timoshenko scholar) and at the department of aerospace engineering at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). He was a one-year visiting fellow at Mansfied College and visiting professor at the Mathematical Institute in Oxford in 2013–14.

Braides has done research on the calculus of variations, [Gamma convergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma_convergence), [asymptotic homogenization](wikipedia://en/23852047), discrete variational problems, percolation, [fracture mechanics](wikipedia://en/843211), image processing, free-discontinuity problems, and [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694).

In 2014 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul with talk *Discrete-to-continuum variational methods for lattice systems*.[^o3]

On the occasion of his 60th birthday he was the dedicatee of the international conference "Calculus of Variations. Back to Carthage" held in Carthage, Tunisia from 16–20 May 2022.
