# Tadashi Tokieda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tadashi Tokieda"}

**Tadashi Tokieda** (Japanese: 時枝正; born 1968) is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939). He is a professor of mathematics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University); he formerly was a fellow and Director of Studies of Mathematics at [Trinity Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Hall,_Cambridge), [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). He also invents, collects, and studies toys that uniquely reveal and explore real-world surprises of mathematics and physics. In comparison with most mathematicians, he had an unusual path in life: he started as a painter, and then became a classical [philologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philology), before transitioning to mathematics. Tokieda is known for his public lectures where he demonstrates mathematical phenomena.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Tokieda was born in [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo) and initially intended to be a painter.[^o6] He then studied at [Lycée Sainte-Marie Grand Lebrun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Sainte-Marie_Grand_Lebrun)[^o3] in [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) as a classical [philologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philology). According to his personal homepage, he taught himself basic mathematics from Russian collections of problems.

He is a 1989 classics graduate from [Sophia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sophia_University)[^o3] in Tokyo and has a 1991 bachelor's degree from [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in mathematics (where he studied as a British Council Fellow). He obtained his PhD at [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1996 under the supervision of [William Browder](wikipedia://en/6646431).[^o7]

Tokieda joined the University of Illinois at Urbana Champaign as a J. L. Doob Research Assistant Professor for the 1997 academic year.[^o8]

He has been involved in the [African Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1575286) since its founding in 2003.

In 2004, he was elected a Fellow of Trinity Hall, where he became the Director of Studies in Mathematics and the Stephan and Thomas Körner Fellow.[^o9][^o10]

He was the William and Flora Hewlett Foundation Fellow in 2013–2014 at the [Radcliffe Institute for Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliffe_Institute_for_Advanced_Study) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o11]

In the 2015–2016 academic year he was the Poincaré Distinguished Visiting Professor at Stanford.[^o12]

Besides his native language Japanese, he is also fluent in French and English. In addition, he knows ancient Greek, Latin, classical Chinese, Finnish, Spanish, and Russian.[^o13] When asked how many languages he knows, he answered "I don't really know. It's like asking how many friends you have."[^o14] So far he has lived in eight countries.[^o15]

In March 2020, Tokieda was interviewed on *The Joy of X*, [Steven Strogatz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Strogatz)'s podcast for *[Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976)*.[^o16]
