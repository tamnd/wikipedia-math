::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of 5-cube and 5-orthoplex"}

In 5-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **5-cube 5-orthoplex compound** is a [polytope compound](wikipedia://en/23901) composed of a regular [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) and dual regular [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615).[^o7] A *compound polytope* is a figure that is composed of several polytopes sharing a common center. The outer vertices of a compound can be connected to form a [convex polytope](wikipedia://en/1817885) called the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634). The compound is a [facetting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facetting) of the convex hull.

:::

In 5-polytope compounds constructed as dual pairs, the hypercells and vertices swap positions and cells and edges swap positions. Because of this the number of hypercells and vertices are equal, as are cells and edges. Mid-edges of the 5-cube cross mid-cell in the 16-cell, and vice versa.

It can be seen as the 5-dimensional analogue of a [compound of cube and octahedron](wikipedia://en/8010874).

## Construction {#Construction}

The 42 [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of the compound are.

10: (±2, 0, 0, 0, 0), (0, ±2, 0, 0, 0), (0, 0, ±2, 0, 0), (0, 0, 0, ±2, 0), (0, 0, 0, 0, ±2)

32: (±1, ±1, ±1, ±1, ±1)

The [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of the vertices makes the dual of [rectified 5-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_5-orthoplex).
