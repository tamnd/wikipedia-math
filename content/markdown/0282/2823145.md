# Virtually fibered conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Virtually fibered conjecture"}

In the mathematical subfield of [3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1018257), the **virtually fibered conjecture**, formulated by [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190), states that every [closed](wikipedia://en/669475), [irreducible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreducible_manifold), [atoroidal](wikipedia://en/1241614) 3-manifold with infinite [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) has a finite [cover](wikipedia://en/250323) which is a [surface bundle over the circle](wikipedia://en/2011690).

:::

A 3-manifold which has such a finite cover is said to **virtually fiber**. If *M* is a [Seifert fiber space](wikipedia://en/1631931), then *M* virtually fibers if and only if the rational [Euler number](wikipedia://en/46895) of the Seifert fibration or the ([orbifold](wikipedia://en/293504)) Euler characteristic of the base space is zero.

The hypotheses of the conjecture are satisfied by [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700). In fact, given that the [geometrization conjecture](wikipedia://en/220642) is now settled, the only case needed to be proven for the virtually fibered conjecture is that of hyperbolic 3-manifolds.

The original interest in the virtually fibered conjecture (as well as its weaker cousins, such as the [virtually Haken conjecture](wikipedia://en/4692183)) stemmed from the fact that any of these conjectures, combined with Thurston's [hyperbolization theorem](wikipedia://en/9155837), would imply the geometrization conjecture. However, in practice all known attacks on the "virtual" conjecture take geometrization as a hypothesis, and rely on the geometric and group-theoretic properties of hyperbolic 3-manifolds.

The virtually fibered conjecture was not actually conjectured by Thurston. Rather, he posed it as a question, writing only that "\[t\]his dubious-sounding question seems to have a definite chance for a positive answer".[^n1]

The conjecture was finally settled in the affirmative in a series of papers from 2009 to 2012. In a posting on the [ArXiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArXiv) on 25 Aug 2009,[^o3] [Daniel Wise](wikipedia://en/38193678) implicitly implied (by referring to a then-unpublished longer manuscript) that he had proven the conjecture for the case where the 3-manifold is closed, hyperbolic, and Haken. This was followed by a survey article in Electronic Research Announcements in Mathematical Sciences.[^o4] Several other articles [^o5][^o6][^o7] have followed, including the aforementioned longer manuscript by Wise.[^o8] In March 2012, during a conference at [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) in Paris, [Ian Agol](wikipedia://en/32084718) announced he could prove the [virtually Haken conjecture](wikipedia://en/4692183) for closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds.[^o9] Taken together with Daniel Wise's results, this implies the virtually fibered conjecture for all closed hyperbolic 3-manifolds.
