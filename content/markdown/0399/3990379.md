# Joseph Ser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Ser"}

**Joseph Ser** (1875–1954) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), of whom little was known till now. He published 45 papers between 1900 and 1954, among which four monographs, edited in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) by [Henry Gauthier-Villars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Gauthier-Villars). In the main, he worked on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [infinite series](wikipedia://en/15287).

:::

He got important results in the domain of [factorial](wikipedia://en/10606) series. His representation of [Euler's constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler-Mascheroni_constant) as a series of [rational](wikipedia://en/19727024) terms is well known. It was used in 1926 by [Paul Appell](wikipedia://en/3359510) (1855–1930), in an unsuccessful attempt to prove the irrationality of Euler's constant.
