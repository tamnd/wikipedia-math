# Stewart Shapiro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stewart Shapiro"}

**Stewart Shapiro** (; born 1951) was O'Donnell [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) until his retirement, and is also distinguished visiting professor at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut). He is a leading figure in the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439) where he defends the [abstract variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_structuralism) of [structuralism](wikipedia://en/29391369).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Shapiro studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) as an undergraduate at [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University) in 1973. He earned his [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in mathematics at the [State University of New York at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo) in 1975. He transferred to the University at Buffalo Philosophy Department, where three years later he received a Ph.D. His doctoral supervisor was [John Corcoran](wikipedia://en/23686234).

He was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_&_Sciences) in 2021.[^o6]
