# Alasdair Urquhart

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alasdair Urquhart"}

**Alasdair Ian Fenton Urquhart** ( ; born 20 December 1945) is a Scottish–Canadian philosopher and [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).[^o3][^o4] He has made contributions to the field of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), especially [non-classical logic](wikipedia://en/3192556).[^o5] Amongst his most notable accomplishments is the proof of [undecidability](wikipedia://en/913118) of the [relevance logic](wikipedia://en/185076) **R**. He published numerous scientific papers in [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392) venues, mostly on [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) topics of [relevance to computer science](wikipedia://en/1151991).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Urquhart is a native of Scotland.[^o7] He received his [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MA_%28Scotland%29) in philosophy from the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 1967.[^o3][^o4] He then attended the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), receiving an MA and Ph.D. in 1973 under the supervision of [Alan Ross Anderson](wikipedia://en/3793208) and [Nuel Belnap](wikipedia://en/1251311).[^o3][^o4]

## Career {#Career}

From 1973 to 1975, Urquhart was an assistant professor at the [University of Toronto Mississauga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Mississauga) (then known as Erindale College).[^o4] He became an associate professor there in 1975.[^o4] Starting in 1986, Urquhart was a professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) in the [University of Toronto Faculty of Arts and Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Faculty_of_Arts_and_Science).[^o3][^o4]

From 1983 to 1989, Urquhart was a consulting editor for the *[Journal of Symbolic Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Symbolic_Logic)*.[^o4] He was also an editor of *Canadian Philosophical Monographs*.[^o4] In 2003, he became the managing editor of reviews for *[The Bulletin of Symbolic Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Bulletin_of_Symbolic_Logic)*.[^o9]

He is currently on the Council of the Division for Logic, Methodology and Philosophy of Science and Technology of the [International Union of History and Philosophy of Science and Technology](wikipedia://en/33072433) (2020–2023).[^o10]
