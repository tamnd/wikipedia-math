# Generalized Pochhammer symbol

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Generalized Pochhammer symbol"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **generalized Pochhammer symbol** of parameter $\alpha>0$ and [partition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_%28number_theory%29) $\kappa=(\kappa_1,\kappa_2,\ldots,\kappa_m)$ generalizes the classical [Pochhammer symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pochhammer_symbol), named after [Leo August Pochhammer](wikipedia://en/3363015), and is defined as

:::

$$(a)^{(\alpha )}_\kappa=\prod_{i=1}^m \prod_{j=1}^{\kappa_i} \left(a-\frac{i-1}{\alpha}+j-1\right).$$

It is used in multivariate [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).
