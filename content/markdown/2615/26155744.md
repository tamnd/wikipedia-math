# Hodge bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hodge bundle"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Hodge bundle**, named after [W. V. D. Hodge](wikipedia://en/384430), appears in the study of families of [curves](wikipedia://en/89246), where it provides an [invariant](wikipedia://en/1126638) in the [moduli theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_theory) of [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260). Furthermore, it has applications to the theory of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) on [reductive](wikipedia://en/1088386) [algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_groups)[^o2] and [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305).[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $\mathcal{M}_g$ be the [moduli space of algebraic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_space_of_algebraic_curves) of [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) *g* curves over some [scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme_%28algebraic_geometry%29). The **Hodge bundle** $\Lambda_g$ is a [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524)[^o6] on $\mathcal{M}_g$ whose [fiber](wikipedia://en/276524) at a point *C* in $\mathcal{M}_g$ is the space of [holomorphic differentials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_differential) on the curve *C*. To define the Hodge bundle, let $\pi\colon \mathcal{C}_g\rightarrow\mathcal{M}_g$ be the [universal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_object) algebraic curve of genus *g* and let $\omega_g$ be its [relative dualizing sheaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relative_dualizing_sheaf). The Hodge bundle is the [pushforward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Direct_image_sheaf) of this sheaf, i.e.,[^o7]

$$\Lambda_g=\pi_*\omega_g.$$
