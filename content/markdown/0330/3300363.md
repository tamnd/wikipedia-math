# Andreotti–Frankel theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andreotti–Frankel theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Andreotti–Frankel theorem**, introduced by (txt), states that if $V$ is a [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_algebraic_variety), complex [affine variety](wikipedia://en/320468) of [complex dimension](wikipedia://en/2783513) $n$ or, more generally, if $V$ is any [Stein manifold](wikipedia://en/1053909) of dimension $n$, then $V$ admits a [Morse function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse_function) with critical points of index at most *n*, and so $V$ is [homotopy equivalent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_equivalent) to a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022) of [real dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_dimension) at most *n*.

:::

Consequently, if $V \subseteq \mathbb{C}^r$ is a closed connected complex submanifold of complex dimension $n$, then $V$ has the homotopy type of a CW complex of real dimension $\le n$. Therefore

$$H^i(V; \mathbb{Z})=0,\text{ for }i>n$$

and

$$H_i(V; \mathbb{Z})=0,\text{ for }i>n.$$

This theorem applies in particular to any smooth, complex affine variety of dimension $n$.
