# George Gasper

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Gasper"}

**George Gasper** is a mathematician at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) working on [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), especially [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) and [basic hypergeometric series](wikipedia://en/2233526), who introduced the [Askey–Gasper inequality](wikipedia://en/19291343).

:::
