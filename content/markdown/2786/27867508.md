# Svante Janson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Svante Janson"}

**Carl Svante Janson**[^o4] (born 21 May 1955) is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). A member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) since 1994, Janson has been the chaired [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor#Main_positions_2) of mathematics at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) since 1987.

:::

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), Janson has publications in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) (especially [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147)) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). In [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711), Janson has made contributions to the theory of [U-statistics](wikipedia://en/16811057).[^o5][^o6] In [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), Janson has publications in [probabilistic combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_combinatorics), particularly [random graphs](wikipedia://en/358477) and in the [analysis of algorithms](wikipedia://en/2230): In the study of random graphs, Janson introduced U-statistics and the [Hoeffding](wikipedia://en/2547060) decomposition.[^o7]

## Biography {#Biography}

Svante Janson has already had a long career in mathematics, because he started research at a very young age.

*Image: Carleson cropped.jpg.* [Lennart Carleson](wikipedia://en/1669541) was the [doctoral supervisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) of Svante Janson, who received his Ph.D. on his 22nd birthday.[^o11] Carleson had himself received his Ph.D. when he was 22 years old.[^o9]

### From prodigy to docent {#From_prodigy_to_docent}

A [child prodigy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Child_prodigy) in mathematics, Janson took high-school and even university classes while in primary school. He was [admitted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_admission) in 1968 to [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg) at age 12. After his 1968 [matriculation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matriculation#United_States) at Uppsala University at age 13,[^o11] Janson obtained the following degrees in mathematics: a "candidate of philosophy" (roughly an "[honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honours_degree)" [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S._degree) with a thesis) at age 14 (in 1970) and a [doctor of philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at age 21–22 (in 1977). Janson's Ph.D. was awarded on his 22nd birthday.[^o11] Janson's [doctoral dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_dissertation) was [supervised](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) by [Lennart Carleson](wikipedia://en/1669541),[^o12] who had himself received his doctoral degree when he was 22 years old.[^o13]

After having earned his doctorate, Janson was a [postdoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoc) with the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) from 1978 to 1980. Thereafter he worked at Uppsala University. Janson's ongoing research earned him another PhD from Uppsala University in 1984 – this second doctoral degree being in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711);[^o14] the [supervisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) was [Carl-Gustav Esseen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl-Gustav_Esseen).[^o15]

In 1984, Janson was hired by [Stockholm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm_University) as [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent#Northern_Europe) (roughly [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) in the USA).[^o11]

### Professorships {#Professorships}

In 1985 Janson returned to Uppsala University, where he was named [the chaired professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor#Main_positions_2) in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711). In 1987 Janson became [the chaired professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor#Main_positions_2) of mathematics at Uppsala university.[^o11] Traditionally in Sweden, the *chaired professor* has had the role of a "*[professor ordinarius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor#Main_positions_2)*" in a [German university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_university) (roughly combining the roles of research professor and director of graduate studies at a [research university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_university) in the USA).

## Awards {#Awards}

Besides being a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) (KVA), Svante Janson is a member of the [Royal Society of Sciences in Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Sciences_in_Uppsala). His thesis received the 1978 Sparre Award from the KVA. He received the 1994 Swedish medal for the best young mathematical scientist, the [Göran Gustafsson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göran_Gustafsson_Prize). Janson's former [doctoral student](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor), Ola Hössjer, received the Göran Gustafsson prize in 2009, becoming the first [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) so honored.[^o18]

In December 2009, Janson received the [Eva & Lars Gårding](wikipedia://en/8000370) prize from the [Royal Physiographic Society in Lund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Physiographic_Society_in_Lund).[^o11] In 2021, Janson received the [Flajolet Lecture Prize](wikipedia://en/67160348). He delivered the fifth Flajolet Lecture at the 2022 [AofA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AofA—International_Meeting_on_Combinatorial,_Probabilistic,_and_Asymptotic_Methods_in_the_Analysis_of_Algorithms) conference.[^o19]
