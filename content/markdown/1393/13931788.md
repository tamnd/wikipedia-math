# Bob Hale (philosopher)

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Bob Hale"}

**Bob Hale**, [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) (1945 – 12 December 2017)[^o5] was a British philosopher, known for his contributions to the development of the [neo-Fregean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-logicism) (neo-logicist) [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439) in collaboration with [Crispin Wright](wikipedia://en/302883), and for his works in [modality](wikipedia://en/333365) and [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language).[^o6]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Hale obtained a BPhil in Philosophy in 1967 from [Linacre College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linacre_College), [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o8] From 2006 until his death, he was a professor of [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) in the department of philosophy at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield). Prior to that, he taught in the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow), the [University of St. Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St._Andrews) and the [University of Lancaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lancaster).

Hale produced the first published neo-Fregean [construction of the real numbers](wikipedia://en/296666).[^o6][^o9] In his book (*Necessary Beings*), he argues for an essentialist theory of necessity and possibility.[^o9]

## Notable positions {#Notable_positions}

- [British Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Academy) Research Reader (1997–99)
- Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) (from 2000)[^o9]
- President of the [Aristotelian Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotelian_Society) (2002–03)[^o11]
- [Leverhulme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverhulme) Senior Research Fellow (2009–11)

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

- (1987) *Abstract Objects*. Oxford: [Blackwell Publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackwell_Publishing).[^o13]
- (1997) Co-editor with Crispin Wright. *The Blackwell Companion to the Philosophy of Language*. Oxford: Blackwell Publishing.
- (2001) With Crispin Wright. *The Reason's Proper Study: Essays towards a Neo-Fregean Philosophy of Mathematics*. Oxford: [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press).[^o14]
- (2013) *Necessary Beings – An Essay on Ontology, Modality, and the Relations Between Them* Oxford: [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press).[^o15]
