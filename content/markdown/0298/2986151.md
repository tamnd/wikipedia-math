# Kodaira embedding theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kodaira embedding theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Kodaira embedding theorem** characterises [non-singular](wikipedia://en/253260#Singularities) [projective varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_varieties), over the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826), amongst [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Kähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/390538). In effect it says precisely which [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742) are defined by [homogeneous polynomials](wikipedia://en/1698977).

:::

[Kunihiko Kodaira](wikipedia://en/646326)'s result is that for a compact Kähler manifold *M*, with a **Hodge metric**, meaning that the [cohomology class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_class) in degree 2 defined by the [Kähler form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kähler_form) ω is an *integral* cohomology class, there is a complex-analytic embedding of *M* into [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977) of some high enough dimension *N*. The fact that *M* embeds as an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) follows from its compactness by [Chow's theorem](wikipedia://en/630505#Chow.27s_theorem). A Kähler manifold with a Hodge metric is occasionally called a **Hodge manifold** (named after [W. V. D. Hodge](wikipedia://en/384430)), so Kodaira's results states that Hodge manifolds are projective. The converse that projective manifolds are Hodge manifolds is more elementary and was already known.

Kodaira also proved (Kodaira 1963), by recourse to [the classification of compact complex surfaces](wikipedia://en/3029260), that every compact Kähler surface is a *deformation* of a projective Kähler surface. This was later simplified by Buchdahl to remove reliance on the classification (Buchdahl 2008).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Kodaira embedding theorem"}

## Kodaira embedding theorem {#Kodaira_embedding_theorem}

Let *X* be a compact Kähler manifold, and *L* a holomorphic line bundle on *X*. Then *L* is a [positive line bundle](wikipedia://en/10592733#Positive_line_bundles) if and only if there is a holomorphic embedding $\varphi:X\rightarrow \mathbb{P}^N$ of *X* into some projective space such that $\varphi^*\mathcal O_{\mathbb{P}^N}(1)=L^{\otimes m}$ for some *m* > 0.

:::
