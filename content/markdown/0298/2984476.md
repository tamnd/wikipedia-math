# Kaup–Kupershmidt equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kaup–Kupershmidt equation"}

The **Kaup–Kupershmidt equation** (named after David J. Kaup and Boris Abram Kupershmidt) is the [nonlinear](wikipedia://en/16678376) fifth-order [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564)

:::

$$u_t = u_{xxxxx}+10u_{xxx}u+25u_{xx}u_x+20u^2u_x = \frac16 (6u_{xxxx}+60uu_{xx}+45u_x^2+40u^3)_x.$$

It is the first equation in a hierarchy of [integrable equations](wikipedia://en/2573213) with the [Lax operator](wikipedia://en/5527029)

$\partial_x^3 + 2u\partial_x + u_x,$ .

It has properties similar (but not identical) to those of the better-known [KdV hierarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korteweg–de_Vries_equation) in which the Lax operator has order 2.
