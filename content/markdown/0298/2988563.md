# Composite measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Composite measure"}

**Composite measure** in statistics and research design refer to composite measures of [variables](wikipedia://en/30423282), i.e. measurements based on multiple data items.[^o2]

:::

An example of a composite measure is an [IQ test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IQ_test), which gives a single score based on a series of responses to various questions.

Three common composite measures include:

- [indexes](wikipedia://en/43558650) - measures that summarize and rank specific observations, usually on the [ordinal scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_scale);[^o2]
- [scales](wikipedia://en/268973) - advanced indexes whose observations are further transformed (scaled) due to their logical or empirical relationships;[^o2]
- [typologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typology_%28statistics%29) - measures that classify observations in terms of their attributes on multiple variables, usually on a [nominal scale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nominal_scale).[^o2]

## Indexes versus scales {#Indexes_versus_scales}

Indexes are often referred to as scales, but in fact not all indexes are scales.[^o4]

Whereas indexes are usually created by aggregating scores assigned to individual [attributes](wikipedia://en/30423282) of various variables, scales are more nuanced and take into account differences in intensity among the attribute of the same variable in question.[^o2] Indexes and scales should provide an [ordinal ranking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinal_scale) of cases on a given variable, though scales are usually more efficient at this.[^o5][^o6] While indexes are based on a simple aggregation of [indicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indicator_%28social_sciences%29) of a variable, scales are more advanced, and their calculations may be more complex, using for example scaling procedures such as [semantic differential](wikipedia://en/2208004).[^o6]

## Composite measure validation {#Composite_measure_validation}

A good composite measure will ensure that the indicators are independent of one another.[^o8] It should also successfully predict other indicators of the variable.[^o8]
