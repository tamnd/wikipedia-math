# Hugh Lowell Montgomery

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hugh Lowell Montgomery"}

**Hugh Lowell Montgomery** (born 1944) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) mathematician, working in the fields of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He is the namesake of [Montgomery's pair correlation conjecture](wikipedia://en/21627970) on the [zeros of the Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/19344125), is known for his development of [large sieve methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_sieve_method), and is the author of multiple books on number theory and analysis.[^o5] He is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Montgomery was born on August 26, 1944, in [Muncie, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muncie,_Indiana).[^o4] He was an undergraduate at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign). On graduating in 1966, he became a [Marshall scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_scholar) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in England. There, he became a Fellow of [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) in 1969, and completed his Ph.D. in 1972.[^o5] His dissertation, *Topics in Multiplicative Number Theory*, was supervised by [Harold Davenport](wikipedia://en/991111).

He became an assistant professor of mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1972. He was quickly promoted, to associate professor in 1973 and full professor in 1975.[^o5] At the University of Michigan, he advised 19 doctoral students, including [Sidney Graham](wikipedia://en/6716686) in 1977, [Brian Conrey](wikipedia://en/15375152) in 1980, and [Russell Lyons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Lyons) in 1983.[^o6] He retired as a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) in 2020.[^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Montgomery was a 1972 recipient of the [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110),[^o8] and the 1974 recipient of the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize).[^o9]

In 1974, Montgomery was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Vancouver.[^o10] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]
