# Ansgar Beckermann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ansgar Beckermann"}

**Ansgar Beckermann** (born 29 June 1945, in [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg)) is a German [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) who deals primarily with [philosophy of mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_mind) and [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology). He is professor for [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the University of [Bielefeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld) and was the president of the "Gesellschaft für analytische Philosophie" (German society for analytical philosophy). Beckermann is known for his arguments for [physicalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicalism) and his discussion of the concept of [emergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emergence).

:::
