# Victor Batyrev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Batyrev"}

*Image: Batyrev victor.jpg.* Victor Batyrev, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2006

**Victor Vadimovich Batyrev** (Виктор Вадимович Батырев, born 31 August 1961, Moscow)[^o2] is a Russian mathematician, specializing in [algebraic](wikipedia://en/1997) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177) and its applications to [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He is a professor at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Batyrev studied mathematics from 1978 to 1985 at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). From 1991 he was at the [University of Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Essen), where he earned his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1993. Since 1996 he has been a professor at the University of Tübingen.[^o4]

He received in 1994 the Gottschalk-Diederich-Baedeker Prize. In 1995 he received the Heinz Maier-Leibnitz Prize for his habilitation thesis *Hodge Theory of Hypersurfaces in Toric Varieties and Recent Developments in Quantum Physics*. In 1998 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Berlin and gave a talk *Mirror Symmetry and Toric Geometry*.[^o5] In 2003 he was elected a member of the [Heidelberger Akademie der Wissenschaften](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberger_Akademie_der_Wissenschaften).[^o6]
