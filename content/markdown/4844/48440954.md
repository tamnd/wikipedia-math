# Gauss Lectureship

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gauss Lectureship"}

The **Gauss Lectureship** (*Gauß-Vorlesung*) is an annually awarded mathematical distinction, named in honor of [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125). It was established in 2001 by the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) with a series of lectures for a broad audience.

:::

Each Gauss Lecture is paired with another presentation on the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).
