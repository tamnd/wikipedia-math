# 4104

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="4104"}

**4104** (**four thousand one hundred \[and\] four**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 4103 and preceding 4105.

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

4104 is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), as it has the [prime factorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_factorization) 2<sup>3</sup> × 3<sup>3</sup> × 19.

4104 can be expressed as the sum of two positive [cubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cubes) in two different ways: 4104 is the sum of 4096 + 8 (that is, 16<sup>3</sup> + 2<sup>3</sup>), and also the sum of 3375 + 729 (that is, 15<sup>3</sup> + 9<sup>3</sup>).[^o4] By the former property, it is also the sum of two powers of 8: 8<sup>4</sup> + 8<sup>1</sup>.

There are 4104 different [Cayley graphs](wikipedia://en/358277) on 26 [nodes](wikipedia://en/638899),[^o8] and 4104 ways of [4-coloring](wikipedia://en/10949) a [map](wikipedia://en/59823612) in which there is a central circle surrounded by a ring divided into 10 regions.
