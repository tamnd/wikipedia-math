# Next Nigerian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Next Nigerian census"}

The **next census of Nigeria** will be a detailed [enumeration](wikipedia://en/212115) of the [Nigerian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerians) [population](wikipedia://en/37641052) that will be the fifth national [census](wikipedia://en/6889) in the country since [Independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Independence).[^o3][^o4][^o5] [Censuses in Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_in_Nigeria) have a controversial history and the next census will be the first national census held since 2006. Over 190 Billion [Naira](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigerian_Naira) (₦) has been allocated for the exercise.[^o4] The help of the [Nigeria Security and Civil Defence Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria_Security_and_Civil_Defence_Corps) (NSCDC) has been requested by the Chairman of the [National Population Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Population_Commission) (NPC) in order to provide security.[^o5] The census will also be notable for being the first digital census in Nigeria's history.[^o5] The plan is to use GIS technologies such as [ArcGIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArcGIS) to aid with the planning process.[^o6]

:::

Originally scheduled for 2016, the census was repeatedly delayed.[^o7][^o8] In April 2023, the census was postponed indefinitely by the Buhari administration in order to allow the new administration to pick a date.[^o9] By April 2024, there was still no proposed date, although the 17 new commissioners were sworn in and produced a recommended date for President Tinubu.[^o10] In July, the National Population Commission proposed a date of November 2024, but are still awaiting [President Tinubu's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bola_Tinubu) approval.[^o11] There are concerns surrounding the use and management of the 200 billion Naira allocated for the next census, with the House of Representatives requesting an investigation.[^o12] Others, are calling for the Census to be delayed to more peaceful times due to the potential tension over population figures due to state revenue figures and representation in the [House of Representatives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Representatives_%28Nigeria%29) deriving from population numbers.[^o13]
