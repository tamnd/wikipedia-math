# Elliptic algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elliptic algebra"}

In algebra, an **elliptic algebra** is a certain [regular algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_algebra) of a [Gelfand–Kirillov dimension](wikipedia://en/35987396) three ([quantum polynomial ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_polynomial_ring) in three variables) that corresponds to a cubic divisor in the projective space **P**<sup>2</sup>. If the cubic divisor happens to be an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225), then the algebra is called a [Sklyanin algebra](wikipedia://en/67518897). The notion is studied in the context of [noncommutative projective geometry](wikipedia://en/43291919).

:::
