# Paul Seidel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Seidel"}

**Paul Seidel** (born 30 December 1970) is a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland)-[Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [homological mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/2164886). He is a faculty member at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Seidel attended [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), where he received his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) under supervision of [Albrecht Dold](wikipedia://en/31337135) in 1994. He then pursued his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) studies at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) under supervision of [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305) (Thesis: *Floer Homology and the Symplectic Isotopy Problem*) in 1998. He was a [chargé de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chargé_de_recherche) at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) from 1999 to 2002, a professor at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) from 2002 to 2003, a professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) from 2003 to 2007, and then a professor at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology from 2007 onwards.[^o4]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2000, Seidel was awarded the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize).[^o6] In 2010, he was awarded the [Oswald Veblen Prize in Geometry](wikipedia://en/7085075) "for his fundamental contributions to symplectic geometry and, in particular, for his development of advanced algebraic methods for computation of symplectic invariants."[^o7] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o8] and a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Investigator).[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Seidel is married to [Ju-Lee Kim](wikipedia://en/48611664), who is also a professor of mathematics at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o11]
