# Dinesh Thakur (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dinesh Thakur"}

**Dinesh S. Thakur** (born 1961) is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a professor of mathematics at [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester).[^o3] Before moving to Rochester, Thakur was a professor at [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona).[^o4] His main research interest is [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Thakur was born in Mumbai, India. He attended Balmohan Vidyamandir School in Bombay and completed his undergraduate education at Ruia College, [University of Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bombay). He got his Ph.D. in 1987 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University)[^o6] under the guidance of Professor [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933).

## Career {#Career}

Thakur has spent three and half years at [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), Princeton and three years at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research), Bombay. He held positions at [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) and [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). He moved to [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona) in 1993. He joined [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester) in July 2013. Thakur wrote a research monograph *Function Field Arithmetic*.[^o9][^o10] Thakur has been serving on the editorial boards of *[Journal of Number Theory](wikipedia://en/8422003)*, *[International Journal of Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Number_Theory)*, and *P-adic Numbers, Ultrametric Analysis and Applications*.[^o11] Thakur is a founding member of-and for 15 years a participant in-the NSF-funded Southwest Center for Arithmetic Geometry and the Arizona Winter School.[^o12]

He was elected as a member of the 2017 class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to the arithmetic of function fields, exposition, and service to the mathematical community".[^o13]

## Work {#Work}

His main work has been in number theory, where he has been instrumental in developing various aspects of function field arithmetic and arithmetic geometry.[^o15][^o16]
