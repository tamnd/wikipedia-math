# Synthetic measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Synthetic measure"}

A **synthetic measure** (or **synthetic indicator**) is a [value](wikipedia://en/4095147) that is the result of combining other [metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_of_measurement), which are measurements of various features.

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

### Quality of service {#Quality_of_service}

There is a method to measure quality of service in [hotels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel). In related study [^o5] authors aggregate tourist opinions, measured on a scale from 1 to 10. Synthetic measure (indicator) of service quality in each hotel is calculated with the help of the aggregation operator.

### Project performance {#Project_performance}

Other study[^o7] proposed to use classical parameters EV, PV and AC to carry out the synthetic measure of project performance.

### Rankings of countries {#Rankings_of_countries}

Different normalized stimulants and destimulants were used in research[^o9] to create synthetic measure that selects countries with the best and the worst levels of implementation of [Europe 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europe_2020) targets.

:::
