# Brenke–Chihara polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brenke–Chihara polynomials"}

In mathematics, **Brenke polynomials** are special cases of [generalized Appell polynomials](wikipedia://en/2462837), and **Brenke–Chihara polynomials** are the Brenke polynomials that are also [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718).

:::

(txt)[^o3] introduced sequences of **Brenke polynomials** *P*<sub>*n*</sub>, which are special cases of [generalized Appell polynomials](wikipedia://en/2462837) with generating function of the form

$$A(w)B(xw)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty P_n(x)w^n.$$

Brenke observed that [Hermite polynomials](wikipedia://en/195984) and [Laguerre polynomials](wikipedia://en/943917) are examples of Brenke polynomials, and asked if there are any other sequences of orthogonal polynomials of this form. Geronimus (1947)[^o4] found some further examples of orthogonal Brenke polynomials. (txt)[^o6][^o7] completely classified all Brenke polynomials that form orthogonal sequences, which are now called Brenke–Chihara polynomials, and found their orthogonality relations.
