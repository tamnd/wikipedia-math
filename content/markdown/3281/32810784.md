# James A. Wilson

bio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James A. Wilson"}

**James Arthur Wilson** is a mathematician working on [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110) and [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) who introduced [Wilson polynomials](wikipedia://en/19585496), [Askey–Wilson polynomials](wikipedia://en/19582010), and the [Askey–Wilson beta integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Askey–Wilson_beta_integral).

:::
