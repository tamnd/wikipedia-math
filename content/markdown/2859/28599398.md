# Frédéric Hélein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frédéric Hélein"}

**Frédéric Hélein** (born 22 April 1963) is a French mathematician. He is university professor at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University).

:::

Hélein earned his doctorate at [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique) under supervision of [Jean-Michel Coron](wikipedia://en/25110499). In 1998 Hélein was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o3] He won the 1999 [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195), jointly with [Fabrice Bethuel](wikipedia://en/28586899), for several important contributions to the theory of [variational calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_calculus).
