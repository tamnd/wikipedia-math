# Fabien Morel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fabien Morel"}

**Fabien Morel** (born 22 January 1965) is a French [algebraic geometer](wikipedia://en/1997) and key developer of [A¹ homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/17346948) with [Vladimir Voevodsky](wikipedia://en/104482).[^o3] Among his accomplishments is the proof of the Friedlander conjecture, and the proof of the complex case of the [Milnor conjecture](wikipedia://en/2914126) stated in [Milnor's](wikipedia://en/61632) 1983 paper 'On the homology of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) made discrete'. This result was presented at the Second Abel Conference, held in January–February 2012.

:::

In 2006, he was an invited speaker with a talk on *A<sup>1</sup>-algebraic topology* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid).[^o4]
