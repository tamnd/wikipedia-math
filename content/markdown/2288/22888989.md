# Kato surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kato surface"}

In mathematics, a **Kato surface** is a compact complex surface with positive first [Betti number](wikipedia://en/346262) that has a [global spherical shell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_spherical_shell). (Kato 1978) showed that Kato surfaces have small analytic deformations that are the blowups of [primary Hopf surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Primary_Hopf_surface) at a finite number of points. In particular they have an infinite cyclic [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004), and are never [Kähler manifolds](wikipedia://en/390538). Examples of Kato surfaces include [Inoue-Hirzebruch surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inoue-Hirzebruch_surface) and [Enoki surfaces](wikipedia://en/22889061). The [global spherical shell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_spherical_shell_conjecture) claims that all [class VII surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_VII_surface) with positive second Betti number are Kato surfaces.

:::
