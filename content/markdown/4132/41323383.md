# Hendecagrammic prism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hendecagrammic prism"}

*Image: HendecagramTypes.svg.* The four regular hendecagrams{11/2}, {11/3}, {11/4}, and {11/5}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **hendecagrammic [prism](wikipedia://en/283007)** is a [star polyhedron](wikipedia://en/7087334) made from two identical regular [hendecagrams](wikipedia://en/1173083) connected by [squares](wikipedia://en/659939). The related **hendecagrammic [antiprisms](wikipedia://en/1851)** are made from two identical regular hendecagrams connected by [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285).

:::

## Hendecagrammic prisms and bipyramids {#Hendecagrammic_prisms_and_bipyramids}

There are [4 hendecagrammic uniform prisms, and 6 hendecagrammic uniform antiprisms](wikipedia://en/11064788). The prisms are constructed by 4.4.11/q [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719), [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram). The hendecagrammic bipyramids, duals to the hendecagrammic prisms are also given.
