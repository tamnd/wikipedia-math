# Mitsuhiro Shishikura

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mitsuhiro Shishikura"}

*Image: Mitsuhiro Shishikura.jpg.* Mitsuhiro Shishikura

**Mitsuhiro Shishikura** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in the field of [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518). He is professor at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in Japan.

:::

Shishikura became internationally recognized[^o2] for two of his earliest contributions, both of which solved long-standing [open problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_problems).

- In his Master's thesis, he proved a conjecture of [Fatou](wikipedia://en/629100) from 1920[^o3] by showing that a [rational function](wikipedia://en/361210) of degree $d\,$ has at most $2d-2\,$ nonrepelling [periodic cycles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periodic_cycle).[^o4]
- He proved[^o5] that the boundary of the [Mandelbrot set](wikipedia://en/19562) has [Hausdorff dimension](wikipedia://en/14294) two, confirming a conjecture stated by [Mandelbrot](wikipedia://en/3999)[^o6] and [Milnor](wikipedia://en/61632).[^o7]

For his results, he was awarded the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) in 1992, and the Iyanaga Spring Prize of the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786) in 1995.

More recent results of Shishikura include

- *(in joint work with Kisaka[^o8])* the existence of a [transcendental entire function](wikipedia://en/10372) with a [doubly connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubly_connected) [wandering domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wandering_domain), answering a question of Baker from 1985;[^o9]
- *(in joint work with Inou[^o10])* a study of *near-parabolic renormalization* which is essential in [Buff](wikipedia://en/48291775) and [Chéritat](wikipedia://en/45468443)'s recent proof of the existence of polynomial [Julia sets](wikipedia://en/56434) of positive planar [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198).
- *(in joint work with Cheraghi)* A proof of the local connectivity of the [Mandelbrot set](wikipedia://en/19562) at some infinitely satellite renormalizable points.[^o11]
- *(in joint work with Yang)* A proof of the regularity of the boundaries of the high type [Siegel disks](wikipedia://en/12247909) of quadratic polynomials.[^o12]

One of the main tools pioneered by Shishikura and used throughout his work is that of [quasiconformal](wikipedia://en/2749226) surgery.

His doctoral students include [Weixiao Shen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weixiao_Shen).
