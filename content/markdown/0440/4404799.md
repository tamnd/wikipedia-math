# Kazuoki Azuma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kazuoki Azuma"}

**Kazuoki Azuma** (born 1939)[^o2] is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). [Azuma's inequality](wikipedia://en/450515) in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) is named after him.[^o3]

:::

## Publications {#Publications}

- Azuma, Kazuoki. (1967). "Weighted sums of certain dependent random variables". *Tohoku Mathematical Journal*. 19 (3). p. 357–367. doi:10.2748/tmj/1178243286
