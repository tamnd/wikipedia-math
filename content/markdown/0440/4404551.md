# 610 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="610"}

**610** is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 609 and preceding 611.

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

610 is a [deficient number](wikipedia://en/321843), a [Markov number](wikipedia://en/2965726), a [sphenic number](wikipedia://en/245203), a [generalized pentagonal number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_pentagonal_number), and the 15th number of the [Fibonacci sequence](wikipedia://en/10918), which is also the smallest Fibonacci number that begins with 6.[^o8]
