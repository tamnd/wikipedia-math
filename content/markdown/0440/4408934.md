# Palais–Smale compactness condition

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Palais–Smale compactness condition"}

The **Palais–Smale compactness condition**, named after [Richard Palais](wikipedia://en/34779708) and [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294), is a hypothesis for some theorems of the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882). It is useful for guaranteeing the existence of certain kinds of [critical points](wikipedia://en/1951424), in particular [saddle points](wikipedia://en/640249). The Palais-Smale condition is a condition on the [functional](wikipedia://en/1374948) that one is trying to extremize.

:::

In finite-dimensional spaces, the Palais–Smale condition for a continuously differentiable real-valued function is satisfied automatically for [proper maps](wikipedia://en/1190262): functions which do not take unbounded sets into bounded sets. In the calculus of variations, where one is typically interested in infinite-dimensional [function spaces](wikipedia://en/340630), the condition is necessary because some extra notion of [compactness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compactness) beyond simple boundedness is needed. See, for example, the proof of the [mountain pass theorem](wikipedia://en/4408952) in section 8.5 of Evans.

## Strong formulation {#Strong_formulation}

A continuously [Fréchet differentiable](wikipedia://en/2735727) [functional](wikipedia://en/1374948) $I\in C^1(H,\mathbb{R})$ from a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H* to the [reals](wikipedia://en/20646438) satisfies the Palais–Smale condition if every [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) $\{u_k\}_{k=1}^\infty\subset H$ such that:

- $\{I[u_k]\}_{k=1}^\infty$ is bounded, and
- $I'[u_k]\rightarrow 0$ in *H*

has a convergent subsequence in *H*.

## Weak formulation {#Weak_formulation}

Let *X* be a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) and $\Phi\colon X\to\mathbf R$ be a [Gateaux differentiable](wikipedia://en/2253139) functional. The functional $\Phi$ is said to satisfy the **weak Palais–Smale condition** if for each sequence $\{x_n\}\subset X$ such that

- $$\sup |\Phi(x_n)|<\infty,$$
- $\lim\Phi'(x_n)=0$ in $X^*$,
- $\Phi(x_n)\neq0$ for all $n\in\mathbf N$,

there exists a critical point $\overline x\in X$ of $\Phi$ with

$$\liminf\Phi(x_n)\le\Phi(\overline x)\le\limsup\Phi(x_n).$$
