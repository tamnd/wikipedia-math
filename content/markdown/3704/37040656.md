# Henneberg surface

*Image: Henneberg surface.jpg.* Henneberg surface.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henneberg surface"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), the **Henneberg surface** is a [non-orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-orientable) [minimal surface](wikipedia://en/276734)[^o2] named after [Lebrecht Henneberg](wikipedia://en/73848711).

:::

It has parametric equation

$$\begin{aligned} x(u,v) &= 2\cos(v)\sinh(u) - (2/3)\cos(3v)\sinh(3u)\\ y(u,v) &= 2\sin(v)\sinh(u) + (2/3)\sin(3v)\sinh(3u)\\ z(u,v) &= 2\cos(2v)\cosh(2u) \end{aligned}$$

and can be expressed as an order-15 algebraic surface.[^o3] It can be viewed as an [immersion](wikipedia://en/6993953) of a punctured [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350).[^o4] Up until 1981 it was the only known non-orientable minimal surface.[^o5]

The surface contains a [semicubical parabola](wikipedia://en/2669305) ("Neile's parabola") and can be derived from solving the corresponding [Björling problem](wikipedia://en/37039361).[^o6][^o7]
