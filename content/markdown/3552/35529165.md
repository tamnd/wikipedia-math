# Viktor Nemytskii

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Viktor Nemytskii"}

**Viktor Vladimirovich Nemytskii** (Виктор Владимирович Немыцкий; 22 November 1900 – 7 August 1967) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Nemytskii operators](wikipedia://en/12316009) and the Nemytskii plane ([Moore plane](wikipedia://en/5516188)). He was married to [Nina Bari](wikipedia://en/7097405), who was also a mathematician.

:::
