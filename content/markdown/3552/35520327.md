# Gibbons–Hawking space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gibbons–Hawking space"}

In [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), a **Gibbons–Hawking space**, named after [Gary Gibbons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary_Gibbons) and [Stephen Hawking](wikipedia://en/19376148), is essentially a [hyperkähler manifold](wikipedia://en/646120) with an extra [U(1)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U%281%29) symmetry.[^o2] (In general, Gibbons–Hawking metrics are a subclass of [hyperkähler metrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperkähler_metric).[^o3]) Gibbons–Hawking spaces, especially ambipolar ones,[^o4] find an application in the study of [black hole](wikipedia://en/4650) microstate geometries.[^o2][^o5]

:::
