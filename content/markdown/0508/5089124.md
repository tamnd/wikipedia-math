# Wolf Barth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolf Barth"}

*Image: Wolf Barth.jpeg.* Wolf Barth at Dortmund in 1980(photo from MFO)

**Wolf Paul Barth** (20 October 1942, in [Wernigerode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wernigerode) – 30 December 2016, in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who discovered [Barth surfaces](wikipedia://en/20746677) and whose work on [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) has been important for the [ADHM construction](wikipedia://en/5165589).[^o2][^o3] Until 2011, Barth was working in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg) in Germany.

:::

Barth received a PhD degree in 1967 from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). His dissertation, written under the direction of [Reinhold Remmert](wikipedia://en/856331) and [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031), was entitled *Einige Eigenschaften analytischer Mengen in kompakten komplexen Mannigfaltigkeiten* (*Some properties of analytic sets in compact, complex manifolds*).
