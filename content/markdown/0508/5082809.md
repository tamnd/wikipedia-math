# Mutual standardisation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mutual standardisation"}

**Mutual Standardisation** is a term used within [spatial epidemiology](wikipedia://en/4694241) to refer to when [ecological bias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_bias) results as a consequence of adjusting disease rates for [confounding](wikipedia://en/3105999) at the area level but leaving the exposure unadjusted and vice versa. This bias is prevented by adjusting in the same way both the exposure and disease rates. This adjustment is rarely possible as it requires data on within-area distribution of the exposure and confounder variables. (Elliot, 2001)

:::
