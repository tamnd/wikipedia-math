# Gaspare Mainardi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gaspare Mainardi"}

**Gaspare Mainardi** (June 1800 in [Abbiategrasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abbiategrasso), [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Milan) – 9 March 1879 in [Lecco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecco)) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) active in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He is remembered for the [Gauss–Codazzi–Mainardi](wikipedia://en/9633335) equations.

:::
