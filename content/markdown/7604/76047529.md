# Craig A. Hill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Craig A. Hill"}

**Craig A. Hill** was a research institute executive and a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association). His research focuses on [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151) and the application of new technologies to social science methods.[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He received his Ph.D. and M.A. in political science from the [University of New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Orleans)[^o2] and has published and presented more than 50 papers,[^o4] ranging from hospital ranking methodology, survey interviewer fraud, and new technology and data sources for social science research. He has published two books: *Social Media, Sociality, and Survey Research*[^o5] and *Big Data Meets Survey Science: A Collection of Innovative Methods*.[^o6]

Until July 2024, he was Senior Vice President at [RTI International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RTI_International) for more than 20 years.[^o7] Prior to RTI, he was a vice president at [NORC at the University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NORC_at_the_University_of_Chicago).[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2019, he was elected as a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o10] In 2021, he received the Warren J. Mitofsky Innovators Award from the [American Association for Public Opinion Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_Public_Opinion_Research) for founding the [Big Data Meets Survey Science](wikipedia://en/272134#The_role_of_big_data) (BigSurv) initiative.[^o11]
