# John Roe (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Roe"}

**John Roe** (6 October 1959 – 9 March 2018[^o3]) was a British mathematician.

:::

Roe grew up in the countryside in [Shropshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shropshire). He went to [Rugby School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_School), was an undergraduate at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University), and received his D.Phil. in 1985 from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) under the supervision of [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698). As a post-doctoral student, he was at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (MSRI) in Berkeley, and then a tutor at [Jesus College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesus_College,_Oxford). From 1998 until shortly before his death he was a professor at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).

His research interests center around [index theorems](wikipedia://en/324752), [coarse geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coarse_geometry), [operator algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operator_algebras), [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917), and the [Novikov conjecture](wikipedia://en/3035194) in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562). He was an editor of the *[Journal of Noncommutative Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Noncommutative_Geometry)* and the *[Journal of Topology](wikipedia://en/34784652)*.

In 1996 he was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
