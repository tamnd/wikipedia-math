::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Order-6 tetrahedral honeycomb"}

In [hyperbolic 3-space](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-6 tetrahedral honeycomb** is a paracompact regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)). It is *paracompact* because it has [vertex figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_figures) composed of an infinite number of faces, and has all vertices as [ideal points](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity. With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,6}, the order-6 tetrahedral honeycomb has six [ideal](wikipedia://en/64419430) [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) around each edge. All vertices are [ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ideal_vertex), with infinitely many tetrahedra existing around each vertex in a [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).[^o10]

:::

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

*Image: Hyperbolic subgroup tree 336-direct.png.* [Subgroup relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram#Subgroup_relations_of_paracompact_hyperbolic_groups)

The order-6 tetrahedral honeycomb has a second construction as a uniform honeycomb, with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3<sup>\[3\]</sup>}. This construction contains alternating types, or colors, of tetrahedral cells. In [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502), this half symmetry is represented as \[3,3,6,1<sup>\+</sup>\] ↔ \[3,((3,3,3))\], or \[3,3<sup>\[3\]</sup>\]: ↔ .

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The order-6 tetrahedral honeycomb is analogous to the two-dimensional [infinite-order triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/38758084), {3,∞}. Both tessellations are regular, and only contain triangles and ideal vertices.

*Image: Infinite-order triangular tiling.svg.*

The order-6 tetrahedral honeycomb is also a [regular hyperbolic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_hyperbolic_3-space) in 3-space, and one of 11 which are paracompact.

This honeycomb is [one of 15 uniform paracompact honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb#.5B6.2C3.2C3.5D_family) in the \[6,3,3\] Coxeter group, along with its dual, the [hexagonal tiling honeycomb](wikipedia://en/40281224).

The order-6 tetrahedral honeycomb is part of a sequence of [regular polychora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_polychora) and honeycombs with [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29).

It is also part of a sequence of honeycombs with [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) [vertex figures](wikipedia://en/1005719).
