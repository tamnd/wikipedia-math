::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Order-4 hexagonal tiling honeycomb"}

In the field of [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/318742), the **order-4 hexagonal tiling honeycomb** arises as one of 11 [regular paracompact honeycombs](wikipedia://en/39591384#Regular_paracompact_honeycombs) in 3-dimensional [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742). It is *paracompact* because it has [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) composed of an infinite number of faces. Each cell is a [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) whose vertices lie on a [horosphere](wikipedia://en/13487128): a flat plane in hyperbolic space that approaches a single [ideal point](wikipedia://en/4995922) at infinity.

:::

The [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of the order-4 hexagonal tiling honeycomb is {6,3,4}. Since that of the [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) is {6,3}, this honeycomb has four such hexagonal tilings meeting at each edge. Since the Schläfli symbol of the [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) is {3,4}, the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of this honeycomb is an octahedron. Thus, eight hexagonal tilings meet at each vertex of this honeycomb, and the six edges meeting at each vertex lie along three orthogonal axes.[^o10]

## Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The order-4 hexagonal tiling honeycomb is a [regular hyperbolic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/505253#Tessellations_of_hyperbolic_3-space) in 3-space, and one of 11 which are paracompact.

There are [fifteen uniform honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact_uniform_honeycomb#.5B6.2C3.2C4.5D_family) in the \[6,3,4\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) family, including this regular form, and its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope), the [order-6 cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order-6_cubic_honeycomb).

The order-4 hexagonal tiling honeycomb has a related [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) honeycomb, ↔ , with [triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/2863858) and [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) cells.

It is a part of sequence of regular honeycombs of the form {6,3,p}, all of which are composed of [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719) cells:

This honeycomb is also related to the [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422), [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318) and [order-4 dodecahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3754285), all of which have octahedral vertex figures.

The aforementioned honeycombs are also quasiregular:
