# Milan Kolibiar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Milan Kolibiar"}

**Milan Kolibiar** (born 14 February 1922 in [Detvianska Huta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detvianska_Huta), died 9 July 1994 in [Bratislava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bratislava)) was a [Slovak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia) mathematician.

:::

He worked mostly in [lattice theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_theory) and [universal algebra](wikipedia://en/45200).
