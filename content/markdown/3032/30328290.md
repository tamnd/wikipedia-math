# Dieudonné determinant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dieudonné determinant"}

In [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), the **Dieudonné determinant** is a generalization of the [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468) of a [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) to matrices over [division rings](wikipedia://en/9067) and [local rings](wikipedia://en/153391). It was introduced by (txt).

:::

If *K* is a division ring, then the Dieudonné determinant is a [group homomorphism](wikipedia://en/12396) from the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*K*) of [invertible](wikipedia://en/217122) *n*-by-*n* matrices over *K* onto the [abelianization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelianization) *K*<sup>×</sup>/\[*K*<sup>×</sup>, *K*<sup>×</sup>\] of the multiplicative group *K*<sup>×</sup> of *K*.

For example, the Dieudonné determinant for a 2-by-2 matrix is the residue class, in *K*<sup>×</sup>/\[*K*<sup>×</sup>, *K*<sup>×</sup>\], of

$$\det \left({\begin{array}{*{20}c} a & b \\ c & d \end{array}}\right) = \left\lbrace{\begin{array}{*{20}c} -cb & \text{if } a = 0 \\ ad - aca^{-1}b & \text{if } a \ne 0. \end{array}}\right.$$

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Let *R* be a local ring. There is a determinant map from the [matrix ring](wikipedia://en/487541) GL(*R*) to the abelianised [unit group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_group) *R*<sup>×</sup><sub>ab</sub> with the following properties:[^o5]

- The determinant is invariant under [elementary row operations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_row_operation)
- The determinant of the [identity matrix](wikipedia://en/59718) is 1
- If a row is left multiplied by *a* in *R*<sup>×</sup> then the determinant is left multiplied by *a*
- The determinant is multiplicative: det(*AB*) = det(*A*)det(*B*)
- If two rows are exchanged, the determinant is multiplied by −1
- If *R* is [commutative](wikipedia://en/61346), then the determinant is invariant under [transposition](wikipedia://en/173844)

:::

## Tannaka–Artin problem {#Tannaka–Artin_problem}

Assume that *K* is finite over its [center](wikipedia://en/53796787) *F*. The [reduced norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_norm) gives a homomorphism *N*<sub>*n*</sub> from GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*K*) to *F*<sup>×</sup>. We also have a homomorphism from GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*K*) to *F*<sup>×</sup> obtained by composing the Dieudonné determinant from GL<sub>*n*</sub>(*K*) to *K*<sup>×</sup>/\[*K*<sup>×</sup>, *K*<sup>×</sup>\] with the reduced norm *N*<sub>1</sub> from GL<sub>1</sub>(*K*) = *K*<sup>×</sup> to *F*<sup>×</sup> via the abelianization.

The **Tannaka–Artin problem** is whether these two maps have the same [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240) SL<sub>*n*</sub>(*K*). This is true when *F* is locally compact[^o8] but false in general.[^o9]
