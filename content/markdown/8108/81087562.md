# Zhiren Wang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zhiren Wang"}

**Zhiren Wang** (Chinese: 王之任) is a Chinese mathematician, specializing in [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). He is known for his contributions to the [rigidity theory](wikipedia://en/1458875) of [group actions](wikipedia://en/12781).[^o11]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Wang received his undergraduate education at [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University),[^o3] earning a B.S. in Mathematics (2004). He then pursued graduate studies in France, obtaining an [Ingénieur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingénieur) degree from [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) and a [DEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_education_in_France) (Diplôme d'études approfondies) in Mathematics from [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) ([Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University)) (both in 2006).[^o10] He completed his Ph.D. in Mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2011 under the supervision of [Elon Lindenstrauss](wikipedia://en/28371257).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

After completing his doctorate, Wang held a postdoctoral fellowship at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), followed by a position as Gibbs Assistant Professor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) from 2011 to 2014, and then faculty positions at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) from 2014[^o6] to 2025. In 2025, he moved to [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) as Professor.[^o7] He has also held a [Von Neumann Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_Fellowship) at the Institute for Advanced Study.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Wang's research focuses on several interconnected areas of mathematics: [Dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), classification of [group actions](wikipedia://en/12781) and [Diophantine approximation](wikipedia://en/251558) and [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153). His work has made contributions to [rigidity theory](wikipedia://en/1458875) in dynamical systems. In 2022, Wang received the [Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/47522601)[^o10][^o11] for his "fundamental contributions to the study of topological and measure rigidity of higher rank actions, and his proof of Möbius disjointness for several classes of dynamical systems."[^o12]
