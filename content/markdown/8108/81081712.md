# Sébastien Gouëzel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sébastien Gouëzel"}

**Sébastien Gouëzel** (born November 20, 1979) is a French mathematician and a research director at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) (French National Centre for Scientific Research). He is known for his contributions to [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and formal mathematics.[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gouëzel was educated at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) in Paris from 1998 to 2002. In 2000, he achieved first place in the [Agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in mathematics. He completed his DEA (master's degree) in pure mathematics at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) in 2001 with highest honors, ranking first in his class. His master's thesis, supervised by [Viviane Baladi](wikipedia://en/48221297), was titled "Spectrum of the transfer operator in dimension 1."[^o4]

He earned his PhD in mathematics in 2004 under the supervision of Viviane Baladi, with a dissertation titled "Decorrelation speed and limit theorems for non-uniformly expanding maps."[^o5]

Gouëzel's academic positions include:

- 2002–2005: *Agrégé préparateur* at [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) Paris
- 2005–2009: Research associate (second class) at CNRS, [University of Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes)
- 2009–2015: Research associate (first class) at CNRS, University of Rennes
- 2015–2020: Research director (second class) at CNRS, [University of Nantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nantes)
- 2020–2021: Research director (second class) at CNRS, University of Rennes
- 2021–present: Research director (first class) at CNRS, University of Rennes[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Gouëzel's research spans several areas of mathematics, with particular focus on:

### Dynamical Systems and Ergodic Theory {#Dynamical_Systems_and_Ergodic_Theory}

His work includes significant contributions to the study of [hyperbolic dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_dynamical_system), transfer operators, and [limit theorems](wikipedia://en/39406) for dynamical systems. Notable results include work on the [Teichmüller flow](wikipedia://en/1835661) and spectral properties of various dynamical systems.[^o9]

### Random Walks on Groups {#Random_Walks_on_Groups}

Gouëzel has made important contributions to the theory of [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451) on [hyperbolic groups](wikipedia://en/3172817), including work on [Martin boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_boundary) theory and local limit theorems.[^o11]

### Formal Mathematics {#Formal_Mathematics}

He is a major contributor to the [Lean theorem prover](wikipedia://en/62889984) and maintains the mathematical library mathlib. His formalization work includes differential geometry, the [Gromov-Hausdorff distance](wikipedia://en/482366), and various results in ergodic theory.[^o13]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 2018: Invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro[^o15]
- 2019: [Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/47522601) for his outstanding contributions to dynamical systems, ergodic theory, and geometric group theory[^o16]
- 2022: Grand Prix Madame Victor Noury from the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o17]
