# Lewis Bowen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lewis Bowen"}

**Lewis Bowen** is an American mathematician and professor at [The University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_University_of_Texas_at_Austin), where he holds the Jane and Roland Blumberg Centennial Professorship in Mathematics as of 2025. His research focuses on [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), with particular emphasis on the ergodic theory of non-amenable group actions and [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Bowen earned both his [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) (1997) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (2002) from [The University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_University_of_Texas_at_Austin). His Ph.D. was supervised by [Charles Radin](wikipedia://en/39630926).

## Career {#Career}

Before returning to UT Austin in 2012, Bowen held academic positions at several institutions including the [University of Hawaiʻi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hawaiʻi), [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), and [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University).

## Research {#Research}

Bowen's research interests include:

- [Ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986)
- [Probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542)
- [Dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems)
- Ergodic theory of non-amenable group actions
- [Geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164)

## Honors and recognition {#Honors_and_recognition}

- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (inaugural class, 2012)[^o7]
- [Michael Brin Prize in Dynamical Systems](wikipedia://en/47522601) (2017) for his "fundamental contributions to measurable and topological dynamics, particularly for non-amenable group actions, invariant random subgroups, and entropy theory"[^o8]
- Invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (2018, [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro))[^o9]
