# Christy Chuang-Stein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christy Chuang-Stein"}

**Christy Chuang-Stein** is a [Taiwanese-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_Americans) biostatistician who has worked in academia and in the [pharmaceutical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical_industry) industry. She was elected as a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA) in 1998.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Chuang-Stein was born and raised in [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan). Both of her parents were teachers; her mother taught her mathematics. After high school, she graduated from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) in mathematics in 1975. She earned her [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in statistics from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1980. Her doctoral dissertation, completed under Kinley Larntz and [Stephen Fienberg](wikipedia://en/2660488), was titled, "Analysis of Categorical Data with Ordered Categories".

## Career {#Career}

After obtaining her PhD, Chuang-Stein took a faculty position in the Cancer Center at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester). She stayed at Rochester for five years and then, in 1985, joined the [Upjohn Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upjohn_Company) in [Kalamazoo, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalamazoo,_Michigan). Upjohn was eventually absorbed into [Pfizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pfizer), where she spent the rest of her career. She retired from Pfizer in 2015 and formed her own consulting company. She is the author or co-author of more than 160 refereed papers, three textbooks on the application of biostatistics methods to pharmaceutical research and a number of chapters in books. She co-founded the journal [Pharmaceutical Statistics](wikipedia://en/20044338).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Chuang-Stein was elected as a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA) in 1998. She was awarded the [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224) (2012) and the Distinguished Achievement Award of the International Chinese Statistical Association (2014). She has earned the Drug Information Association's (DIA) [Donald E. Francke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_E._Francke) Award for Overall Excellence in Journal Publishing three times (2001, 2004, 2009) and DIA's Thomas Teal Award for Excellence in Statistics Publishing twice (2008, 2010). In 2004 she won the Recognition Award by the Clinical Leadership Committee of [PhRMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharmaceutical_Research_and_Manufacturers_of_America).
