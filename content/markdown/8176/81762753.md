# Fan graph

*Image: Fan graphs.svg.* The [path](wikipedia://en/4587190) $P_n$ and [empty graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empty_graph) $K_1$ in each *fan graph* $F_n$ are colored blue and orange respectively

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fan graph"}

In graph theory, a **fan graph** (also called a path-fan graph) is a graph formed by the [join](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_join) of a [path graph](wikipedia://en/4587190) and an [empty graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empty_graph) on a single vertex. The fan graph on $n + 1$ vertices, denoted $F_n$, is defined as $K_1 + P_n$, where $K_1$ is a single vertex and $P_n$ is a path on $n$ vertices.[^o2][^o3]

:::

The fan graph $F_n$ has $n + 1$ vertices and $2n - 1$ edges.[^o2]

## Saturation {#Saturation}

A graph $G$ is $H$-saturated if it does not contain $H$ as a subgraph, but the addition of any edge $e \notin E(G)$ results in at least one copy of $H$ as a subgraph. The [saturation number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saturation_%28graph_theory%29) $\text{sat}(n, H)$ is the minimum number of edges in an $H$-saturated graph on $n$ vertices, while the [extremal number](wikipedia://en/21682838) $\text{ex}(n, H)$ is the maximum number of edges possible in a graph $G$ on $n$ vertices that does not contain a copy of $H$.

The **$t$-fan** (sometimes called the [friendship graph](wikipedia://en/24363442)), $F_t(t \geq 2)$, is the graph consisting of $t$ edge-disjoint triangles that intersect at a single vertex $v$.[^o3]

The saturation number for $F_t$ is $\text{sat}(n, F_t) = n + 3t - 4$ for $t \geq 2$ and $n \geq 3t - 2$.[^o3]

## Graph coloring {#Graph_coloring}

The [packing chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packing_chromatic_number) $\chi_\rho(G)$ of a graph $G$ is the smallest integer $k$ for which there exists a mapping $\pi : V(G) \to {1, 2, \ldots, k}$ such that any two vertices of color $i$ are at distance at least $i + 1$. The packing chromatic number has been studied for various fan and wheel related graphs.[^o2]
