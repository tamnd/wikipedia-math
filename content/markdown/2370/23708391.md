# Formal manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Formal manifold"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a **formal manifold** can mean one of a number of related concepts:

:::

- In the sense of [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608), a formal manifold is one whose real [homotopy type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homotopy_type) is a formal consequence of its real [cohomology ring](wikipedia://en/1069723); algebro-topologically this means in particular that all [Massey products](wikipedia://en/2676948) vanish.[^o2]
- A stronger notion is a geometrically formal manifold, a manifold on which all [wedge products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedge_product) of [harmonic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_form) are harmonic.[^o3]
