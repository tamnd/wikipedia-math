# Decisional composite residuosity assumption

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Decisional composite residuosity assumption"}

The **decisional composite residuosity assumption (DCRA)** is a mathematical assumption used in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). In particular, the assumption is used in the proof of the [Paillier cryptosystem](wikipedia://en/480685).

:::

Informally, the DCRA states that given a [composite](wikipedia://en/82289) $n$ and an [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) $z$, it is hard to decide whether $z$ is an $n$-residue [modulo](wikipedia://en/20087) $n^2$. *I.e.* whether there exists a $y$ such that

$$z \equiv y^n \pmod{n^2}. \,$$
