# Nori motive

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nori motive"}

In algebraic geometry, a **Nori motive** is a [mixed motive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixed_motive_%28math%29) constructed by [Madhav Nori](wikipedia://en/71398482). Today, it is known that Nori's 1-motive coincides with that of Ayoub.[^o2]

:::

The construction is based on **Nori's basic lemma** and his [Tannakian theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tannakian_theorem).[^o3]
