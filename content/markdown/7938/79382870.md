# Keri Sather-Wagstaff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Keri Sather-Wagstaff"}

**Keri Ann Sather-Wagstaff** is an American mathematician and academic administrator, the associate provost of [Framingham State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framingham_State_University) in [Framingham, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Framingham,_Massachusetts). Educated as a [commutative algebraist](wikipedia://en/245990), she lists her current professional focus as "intersectional equity, justice, and inclusive excellence". She is known for her work to improve [diversity in STEM fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racial_diversity_and_discrimination_in_STEM_fields), particularly for members of the LGBTQ+ community.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Sather-Wagstaff majored in mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), graduating in 1993. She continued her studies in mathematics at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah), working there in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) with Paul Roberts. She received a master's degree in 1998, and completed her Ph.D. in 2000, with the dissertation *A Dimension Inequality for Excellent Cohen-Macaulay Rings Related to the Positivity of Serre's Intersection Multiplicity*.

After postdoctoral research in mathematics at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), and short-term assistant professorships at the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln), [California State University, Dominguez Hills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_Dominguez_Hills), and [Kent State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_State_University), Sather-Wagstaff became an assistant professor at [North Dakota State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Dakota_State_University) in 2007, and was tenured as an associate professor there in 2012. She moved to [Clemson University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clemson_University) in 2015, and became a full professor there in 2019. In 2022 she went on leave from Clemson to become a rotating program director at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation), in its directorate for STEM education. She moved to Framingham State University as its associate provost in early 2025.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Sather-Wagstaff was named by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) to the 2023 Class of AWM Fellows, "for her sustained advocacy, support and mentorship of women, girls, gender minorities, and other historically underrepresented groups in mathematics; and for spearheading local and national efforts targeting high-need areas to improve the working environment for all".

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Sather-Wagstaff is a transgender woman, and identifies as [neurodivergent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neurodivergent). She is part of the first generation of her family to obtain a college education.
