# Higher stack

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Higher stack"}

In mathematics, especially [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a **higher stack** is a [higher category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_category) generalization of a [stack](wikipedia://en/13185596) (a category-valued sheaf). The notion goes back to Grothendieck’s *[Pursuing Stacks](wikipedia://en/40783144)*.[^o2]

:::

Toën suggests the following principle:[^o3] As 1-stacks appear as soon as objects must be classified up to isomorphism, higher stacks appear as soon as objects must be classified up to a notion of equivalence which is weaker than the notion of isomorphism.

Sometimes a [derived stack](wikipedia://en/42074131) (or a [spectral stack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_stack)) is defined as a higher stack of some sort.
