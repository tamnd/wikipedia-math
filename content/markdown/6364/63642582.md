# Aline Gouget

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aline Gouget"}

**Aline Gouget Morin** (born 1977) is a French mathematician and cryptographer whose works include contributions to the design of the [SOSEMANUK](wikipedia://en/4546152) stream cipher and [Shabal](wikipedia://en/58888784) hash algorithm, and methods for anonymized [digital currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_currency). She is a researcher for [Gemalto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gemalto), an international digital security company.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Gouget completed a PhD in 2004 at the [University of Caen Normandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen_Normandy). Her dissertation, *Etude de propriétés cryptographiques des fonctions booléennes et algorithme de confusion pour le chiffrement symétrique*, was advised by Claude Carlet.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2017, Gouget was the winner of the [Irène Joliot-Curie Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irène_Joliot-Curie_Prize) in the category for women in business and technology.
