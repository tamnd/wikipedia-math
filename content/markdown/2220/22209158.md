# Ernst Specker

*Image: Ernst Specker.jpeg.* Ernst Specker, 1982

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ernst Specker"}

**Ernst Paul Specker** (11 February 1920, [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) – 10 December 2011, Zürich) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Much of his most influential work was on [Willard Van Orman Quine](wikipedia://en/33833)'s [New Foundations](wikipedia://en/945957), a [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) with a [universal set](wikipedia://en/303405), but he is most famous for the [Kochen–Specker theorem](wikipedia://en/3790812) in [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), showing that certain types of [hidden-variable theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden-variable_theories) are impossible. He also proved the [ordinal](wikipedia://en/26547932) [partition relation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partition_relation) ω<sup>2</sup> → (ω<sup>2</sup>, 3)<sup>2</sup>, thereby solving a problem of [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).

:::

Specker received his Ph.D. in 1949 from [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich),[^o2] where he remained throughout his professional career.
