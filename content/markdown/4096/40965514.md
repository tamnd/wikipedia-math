# Michel Las Vergnas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michel Las Vergnas"}

**Michel Las Vergnas** (11 January 1941 – 19 January 2013) was a French mathematician associated with [Pierre-and-Marie-Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre-and-Marie-Curie_University) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), and a research director emeritus at the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Las Vergnas earned his Ph.D. in 1972 from Pierre-and-Marie-Curie University, under the supervision of [Claude Berge](wikipedia://en/5893760). He was one of the founders of the *[European Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Journal_of_Combinatorics)*, which began publishing in 1980.[^o6]

A workshop on combinatorial geometry, held in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marseille) in April 2013, was dedicated to his memory.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Las Vergnas's early work was in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), on [matchings](wikipedia://en/581797) and [connectivity](wikipedia://en/1614492).[^o3] From 1975 he was one of the pioneers of the theory of [oriented matroids](wikipedia://en/24338041),[^o3][^o9][^o10] and with [Robert G. Bland](wikipedia://en/34886045) he wrote one of its founding papers;[^o11] his later work concerned connections between combinatorics and geometry.[^o3] In [matroid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid_theory) he introduced the [Tutte polynomial](wikipedia://en/3892303) of a morphism of matroids, studied in a series of papers from the 1970s to 2013;[^o12] its specialisation to graphs embedded in surfaces is known as the Las Vergnas polynomial.[^o13] He also showed that matroids in general admit no [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844).[^o14]

::: {#o16 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=heading name="Conjectures"}

## Conjectures {#Conjectures}

Several conjectures carry his name. The simplex conjecture, posed in 1980, states that every simple oriented matroid has a simplicial tope; it is known to hold for realizable oriented matroids and in rank three.[^o17][^o18]

The Cordovil–Las Vergnas conjecture, recorded by Roudneff and Sturmfels in 1988, states that any two uniform oriented matroids of the same rank on the same ground set are connected by a sequence of mutations; Roudneff and Sturmfels proved it for realizable oriented matroids.[^o19]

:::
