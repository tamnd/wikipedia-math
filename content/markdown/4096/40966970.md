# Stable model category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stable model category"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of mathematics, a **stable model category** is a [pointed model category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointed_model_category) in which the suspension functor is an equivalence of the homotopy category with itself.

:::

The prototypical examples are the category of [spectra](wikipedia://en/1033697) in the [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655) and the category of chain complex of *R*-modules. On the other hand, the category of pointed topological spaces and the category of pointed simplicial sets are not stable model categories.

Any stable model category is equivalent to a category of [presheaves of spectra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presheaf_of_spectra).
