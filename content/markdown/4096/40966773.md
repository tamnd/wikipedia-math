# Simplicial localization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simplicial localization"}

In category theory, a branch of mathematics, the **simplicial localization** of a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) *C* with respect to a class *W* of morphisms of *C* is a [simplicial category](wikipedia://en/29190294) *LC* whose $\pi_0$ is the [localization](wikipedia://en/611460) $C[W^{-1}]$ of *C* with respect to *W*; that is, $\pi_0 LC(x, y) = C[W^{-1}](x, y)$ for any objects *x*, *y* in *C*. The notion is due to Dwyer and [Kan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Kan).

:::
