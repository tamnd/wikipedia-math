# András Vargha

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="András Vargha"}

Vargha András

*Image: VarghaAndrás.JPG.*

**András Vargha** (born Budapest, 29 November 1949) is a Hungarian [psychologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychologist) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), head of the Institute of Psychology of the [Károli Gáspár University of Reformed Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Károli_Gáspár_University_of_Reformed_Church). His research field is [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982), on which he has held conferences.[^o2]

:::

As of May 2011, Vargha had 131 journal publications with an impact factor of 14050,[^o3] and has worked or continues to work as a [reviewer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reviewer) with [academic journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal).[^o4]
