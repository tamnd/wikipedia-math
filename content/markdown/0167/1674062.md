# CoCoA

::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="CoCoA"}

**CoCoA** (**Co**mputations in **Co**mmutative **A**lgebra)[^o9] is a free [computer algebra system](wikipedia://en/54427) developed by the [University of Genova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genova), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy), used to compute with numbers and [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000). The CoCoA Library (CoCoALib[^o10]) is available under [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License). CoCoA has been ported to many operating systems including [Macintosh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macintosh) on PPC and x86, [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux) on x86, [Unix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix) x86-64 & PPC, [Solaris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solaris_%28operating_system%29) on [SPARC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPARC) and [Windows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Windows) on x86. CoCoA is mainly used by researchers (see citations at[^o11] and[^o12]), but can be useful even for "simple" computations.

:::

CoCoA's features include:

- Very big [integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integers) and [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024) using the [GNU Multi-Precision Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Multi-Precision_Library)
- Multivariate [Polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000)
- [Gröbner basis](wikipedia://en/357328)
- User interfaces: text; [Emacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emacs)-based; [Qt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qt_%28framework%29)-based

It is able to perform simple and sophisticated operations on multivariate polynomials and on various data related to them (ideals, modules, matrices, rational functions). For example, it can readily compute [Gröbner basis](wikipedia://en/357328), [syzygies](wikipedia://en/1280843) and [minimal free resolutions](wikipedia://en/19745659#Graded_modules_and_algebras), intersection, division, the radical of an ideal, the ideal of zero-dimensional schemes, [Poincaré series](wikipedia://en/17006296) and [Hilbert functions](wikipedia://en/6612596), factorization of polynomials, and [toric ideals](wikipedia://en/43023424). The capabilities of CoCoA and the flexibility of its use are further enhanced by the dedicated high-level programming language.

Its mathematical core, CoCoALib, has been designed as an open source C++ library, focussing on ease of use and flexibility. It is available on GitHub: at https://github.com/cocoa-official/CoCoALib

CoCoALib is based on [GNU Multi-Precision Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Multi-Precision_Library).

CoCoALib is used by ApCoCoA[^o13] and NmzIntegrate[^o14]
