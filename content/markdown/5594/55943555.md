# Louis Crelier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Louis Crelier"}

**Louis Jacques Crelier** (3 November 1873, [Bure, Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bure,_Switzerland) – 28 November 1935) was a Swiss mathematician.[^o2]

:::

In 1886 he enrolled at l'Ecole normale in [Porrentruy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porrentruy) and then studied at the [University of Berne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berne), where he received his doctorate in 1895. He began his teaching career at the secondary school in [Saint-Imier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Imier) and then taught at the technical school (founded in 1873) in [Biel/Bienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biel/Bienne). He became in 1912 professor extraordinarius and in 1918 professor ordinarius at the University of Berne.[^o2]

Crelier served a two-year term from 1920 to 1921 as president of the [Swiss Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Mathematical_Society). He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1924 at Toronto[^o3] and in 1928 at Bologna.[^o4]
