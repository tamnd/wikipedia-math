# Sentient Information Systems

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sentient Information Systems"}

**Sentient Information Systems** BV is a [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people) [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software) provider specialized in [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253). The company was founded in 2001 out of the former Sentient Machine Research (SMR) and is located in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam).

:::

Sentient's flagship product is [DataDetective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DataDetective), a data mining platform capable of analyzing information from various domains. Users of this tool include several Dutch police departments, hospitals, insurance and media companies.
