# Mathematische Annalen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematische Annalen"}

*Image: Mathematische\_Annalen\_1869\_Titel.jpg.* Scan of Book "Mathematische Annalen" from 1869

***Mathematische Annalen*** (abbreviated as *Math. Ann.* or, formerly, *Math. Annal.*) is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) mathematical [research journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_journal) founded in 1868 by [Alfred Clebsch](wikipedia://en/1369957) and [Carl Neumann](wikipedia://en/915551). Subsequent managing editors were [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887), [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302), [Otto Blumenthal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Blumenthal), [Erich Hecke](wikipedia://en/1233990), [Heinrich Behnke](wikipedia://en/22922678), [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031), [Heinz Bauer](wikipedia://en/25512518), [Herbert Amann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Amann), [Jean-Pierre Bourguignon](wikipedia://en/1520814), [Wolfgang Lück](wikipedia://en/26197113), [Nigel Hitchin](wikipedia://en/1034164), and [Thomas Schick](wikipedia://en/59090155).[^o3] Currently, the managing editor of Mathematische Annalen is Yoshikazu Giga ([University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo)).

:::

Volumes 1–80 (1869–1919) were published by [Teubner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliotheca_Teubneriana). Since 1920 (vol. 81), the journal has been published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). In the late 1920s, under the editorship of Hilbert, the journal became embroiled in controversy over the participation of [L. E. J. Brouwer](wikipedia://en/172896) on its editorial board, a spillover from the foundational [Brouwer–Hilbert controversy](wikipedia://en/19245793).[^o4] Between 1945 and 1947, the journal briefly ceased publication.[^o3]
