# Spherical space form conjecture

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Spherical space form conjecture"}

**Spherical space form conjecture.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Spherical space form conjecture"}

In [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), the **spherical space form conjecture** (now a theorem) states that a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) acting on the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792) is conjugate to a [group of isometries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_of_isometries) of the 3-sphere.

:::

## History {#History}

The conjecture was posed by [Heinz Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082) in 1926 after determining the fundamental groups of three-dimensional spherical space forms as a generalization of the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) to the non-simply connected case.[^o4][^o5]

## Status {#Status}

The conjecture is implied by [Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190)'s [geometrization conjecture](wikipedia://en/220642), which was proven by [Grigori Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266) in 2003. The conjecture was independently proven for groups whose actions have [fixed points](wikipedia://en/449738)—this special case is known as the [Smith conjecture](wikipedia://en/4476828). It is also proven for various groups acting without fixed points, such as [cyclic groups](wikipedia://en/52327) whose orders are a power of two (George Livesay, Robert Myers) and cyclic groups of order 3 ([J. Hyam Rubinstein](wikipedia://en/4983922)).[^o7]
