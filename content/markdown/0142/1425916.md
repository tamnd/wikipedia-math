# Fulkerson Prize

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fulkerson Prize"}

The **Fulkerson Prize** for outstanding papers in the area of [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) is sponsored jointly by the [Mathematical Optimization Society](wikipedia://en/5751407) (MOS) and the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS). Up to three awards of \$1,500 each are presented at each (triennial) International Symposium of the MOS. Originally, the prizes were paid out of a memorial fund administered by the AMS that was established by friends of the late [Delbert Ray Fulkerson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delbert_Ray_Fulkerson) to encourage mathematical excellence in the fields of research exemplified by his work. The prizes are now funded by an endowment administered by MOS.

:::

## Winners {#Winners}

- 1979:
  - [Richard M. Karp](wikipedia://en/298763) for classifying many important [NP-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NP-complete) problems.[^o4]
  - [Kenneth Appel](wikipedia://en/311544) and [Wolfgang Haken](wikipedia://en/311546) for the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949).[^o5]
  - [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708) for generalizing the [max-flow min-cut theorem](wikipedia://en/78130) to [matroids](wikipedia://en/244321).[^o6]
- 1982:
  - D.B. Judin, [Arkadi Nemirovski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkadi_Nemirovski), [Leonid Khachiyan](wikipedia://en/565462), [Martin Grötschel](wikipedia://en/55085835), [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938) and [Alexander Schrijver](wikipedia://en/35280549) for the [ellipsoid method](wikipedia://en/4331203) in [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730) and [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555).[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10]
  - [G. P. Egorychev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgy_Petrovich_Egorychev) and D. I. Falikman for proving [Van der Waerden's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_der_Waerden's_conjecture) that the matrix with all entries equal has the smallest [permanent](wikipedia://en/215889) of any [doubly stochastic matrix](wikipedia://en/2906180).[^o11][^o12]
- 1985:
  - [Jozsef Beck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jozsef_Beck) for tight bounds on the [discrepancy](wikipedia://en/1116842) of [arithmetic progressions](wikipedia://en/168389).[^o13]
  - [H. W. Lenstra Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Lenstra) for using the [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153) to solve [integer programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integer_program) with few variables in time polynomial in the number of constraints.[^o14]
  - [Eugene M. Luks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_M._Luks) for a [polynomial time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_time) [graph isomorphism algorithm](wikipedia://en/1950766) for graphs of bounded [maximum degree](wikipedia://en/1374448).[^o15][^o16]
- 1988:
  - [Éva Tardos](wikipedia://en/2979149) for finding [minimum cost circulations](wikipedia://en/6808344) in [strongly polynomial time](wikipedia://en/405944).[^o17]
  - [Narendra Karmarkar](wikipedia://en/1162259) for [Karmarkar's algorithm](wikipedia://en/3736667) for [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730).[^o18]
- 1991:
  - [Martin E. Dyer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Dyer), [Alan M. Frieze](wikipedia://en/22466549) and [Ravindran Kannan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravindran_Kannan) for [random-walk](wikipedia://en/235451)-based [approximation algorithms](wikipedia://en/563105) for the volume of convex bodies.[^o19]
  - Alfred Lehman for [0,1-matrix](wikipedia://en/4109196) analogues of the theory of [perfect graphs](wikipedia://en/670531).[^o20]
  - Nikolai E. Mnev for [Mnev's universality theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mnev's_universality_theorem), that every semialgebraic set is equivalent to the space of realizations of an [oriented matroid](wikipedia://en/24338041).[^o21]
- 1994:
  - [Louis Billera](wikipedia://en/22444348) for finding bases of piecewise-polynomial function spaces over triangulations of space.[^o22]
  - [Gil Kalai](wikipedia://en/19709056) for making progress on the [Hirsch conjecture](wikipedia://en/10412371) by proving subexponential bounds on the diameter of *d*-dimensional polytopes with *n* facets.[^o23]
  - [Neil Robertson](wikipedia://en/1575704), [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708) and [Robin Thomas](wikipedia://en/24641679) for the six-color case of [Hadwiger's conjecture](wikipedia://en/1657860).[^o24]
- 1997:
  - [Jeong Han Kim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeong_Han_Kim) for finding the [asymptotic growth rate](wikipedia://en/641995) of the [Ramsey numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey_number) *R*(3,*t*).[^o25]
- 2000:
  - [Michel X. Goemans](wikipedia://en/34561422) and [David P. Williamson](wikipedia://en/46920695) for [approximation algorithms](wikipedia://en/563105) based on [semidefinite programming](wikipedia://en/4993539).[^o26]
  - Michele Conforti, [Gérard Cornuéjols](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gérard_Cornuéjols), and [M. R. Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendu_Rammohan_Rao) for recognizing [balanced 0-1 matrices](wikipedia://en/27803326) in [polynomial time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_time).[^o27][^o28]
- 2003:
  - [J. F. Geelen](wikipedia://en/24393682), A. M. H. Gerards and A. Kapoor for the [GF(4)](wikipedia://en/11615) case of [Rota's conjecture](wikipedia://en/36760081) on [matroid minors](wikipedia://en/36749660).[^o29][^o30]
  - Bertrand Guenin for a [forbidden minor characterization](wikipedia://en/13547826) of the weakly bipartite graphs (graphs whose bipartite subgraph polytope is 0-1).[^o31][^o30]
  - Satoru Iwata, Lisa Fleischer, Satoru Fujishige, and [Alexander Schrijver](wikipedia://en/35280549) for showing [submodular minimization](wikipedia://en/33273315#Optimization_problems) to be strongly polynomial.[^o32][^o33][^o30]
- 2006:
  - [Manindra Agrawal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manindra_Agrawal), [Neeraj Kayal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neeraj_Kayal) and [Nitin Saxena](wikipedia://en/2297722), for the [AKS primality test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AKS_primality_test).[^o34][^o35][^o36]
  - [Mark Jerrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Jerrum), [Alistair Sinclair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alistair_Sinclair) and Eric Vigoda, for [approximating the permanent](wikipedia://en/20749642#Approximate_computation).[^o37][^o36]
  - [Neil Robertson](wikipedia://en/1575704) and [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708), for the [Robertson–Seymour theorem](wikipedia://en/351769) showing that [graph minors](wikipedia://en/353042) form a [well-quasi-ordering](wikipedia://en/739775).[^o38][^o36]
- 2009:
  - [Maria Chudnovsky](wikipedia://en/19731900), Neil Robertson, Paul Seymour, and Robin Thomas, for the [strong perfect graph theorem](wikipedia://en/744171).[^o39][^o40]
  - [Daniel A. Spielman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_A._Spielman) and [Shang-Hua Teng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shang-Hua_Teng), for [smoothed analysis](wikipedia://en/18208194) of [linear programming](wikipedia://en/43730) algorithms.[^o41][^o40]
  - [Thomas C. Hales](wikipedia://en/2388356) and Samuel P. Ferguson, for proving the [Kepler conjecture](wikipedia://en/368311) on the densest possible [sphere packings](wikipedia://en/368621).[^o42][^o43][^o40]
- 2012:
  - [Sanjeev Arora](wikipedia://en/21513331), [Satish Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satish_Rao), and [Umesh Vazirani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umesh_Vazirani) for improving the [approximation ratio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approximation_ratio) for [graph separators](wikipedia://en/1499165) and related problems from $O(\log n)$ to $O(\sqrt{\log n})$.[^o44]
  - Anders Johansson, [Jeff Kahn](wikipedia://en/26919476), and [Van H. Vu](wikipedia://en/21419416) for determining the threshold of edge density above which a [random graph](wikipedia://en/358477) can be covered by disjoint copies of a given smaller graph.[^o45]
  - [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938) and [Balázs Szegedy](wikipedia://en/45719120) for characterizing subgraph multiplicity in sequences of [dense graphs](wikipedia://en/2777098).[^o46]
- 2015:
  - [Francisco Santos Leal](wikipedia://en/47275899) for *a counter-example of the [Hirsch conjecture](wikipedia://en/10412371)*.[^o47][^o48]
- 2018:
  - [Robert Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Morris_%28mathematician%29), [Yoshiharu Kohayakawa](wikipedia://en/46789502), Simon Griffiths, Peter Allen, and [Julia Böttcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julia_Böttcher) for *The chromatic thresholds of graphs*
  - [Thomas Rothvoss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Rothvoss) for his work on the [extension complexity](wikipedia://en/68641808) of the [matching polytope](wikipedia://en/64572404).[^o49]
- 2021:
  - [Béla Csaba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Béla_Csaba), [Daniela Kühn](wikipedia://en/37003499), [Allan Lo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Lo), [Deryk Osthus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deryk_Osthus), and [Andrew Treglown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Treglown) for *Proof of the 1-factorization and Hamilton decomposition conjectures*
  - [Jin-Yi Cai](wikipedia://en/68266753) and [Xi Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xi_Chen) for *Complexity of Counting CSP with Complex Weights*
  - [Ken-Ichi Kawarabayashi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken-ichi_Kawarabayashi) and [Mikkel Thorup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikkel_Thorup) for *Deterministic Edge Connectivity in Near-Linear Time*

Source: Mathematical Optimization Society official website.[^o50]

- 2024:
  - Ben Cousins and [Santosh Vempala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santosh_Vempala) for *Gaussian cooling and $O^*(n^3)$ algorithms for volume and Gaussian volume*
  - Zilin Jiang, Jonathan Tidor, Yuan Yao, Shengtong Zhang, and Yufei Zhao for *Equiangular lines with a fixed angle*
  - Nathan Keller and Noam Lifshitz for *The junta method for hypergraphs and the Erdős–Chvátal simplex conjecture*

Source: American Mathematical Society official website.[^o51]
