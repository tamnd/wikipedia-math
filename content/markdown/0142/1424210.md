# Annales de l'Institut Fourier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annales de l'Institut Fourier"}

The ***Annales de l'Institut Fourier*** () is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal) publishing papers in all fields of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It was established in 1949. The journal publishes one volume per year, consisting of six issues. The current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Hervé Pajot.[^o3] Articles are published either in English or in French.

:::

The journal is indexed in *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)*, *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)* and the [Web of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_of_Science). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal had a 2008 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.804.[^o4]
