# Yamamoto's reciprocity law

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yamamoto's reciprocity law"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Yamamoto's reciprocity law** is a [reciprocity law](wikipedia://en/247622) related to [class numbers](wikipedia://en/150907) of [quadratic number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_number_field), introduced by (Yamamoto 1986).

:::
