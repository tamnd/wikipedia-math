# Vicki Hertzberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vicki Hertzberg"}

**Vicki Stover Hertzberg** is an American [biostatistician](wikipedia://en/3878), who is currently professor in the [Nell Hodgson Woodruff School of Nursing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nell_Hodgson_Woodruff_School_of_Nursing) of Emory University, where she founded and continues to direct its Center for Data Science. Previously she worked as a professor of biostatistics and bioinformatics in the [Rollins School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollins_School_of_Public_Health) of [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University) between 1994 and 2015, serving as the department chair 1994-2001.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hertzberg studied mathematics and statistics at [Miami University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miami_University) of Ohio, graduating in 1976. She completed a PhD in biomathematics and health statistics in 1980 at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington). Her dissertation, *Inter-Viewer Variability in Assessing Coronary Arteriography: A Statistical Model*, was supervised by Lloyd Fisher. Next, she joined the faculty at the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati), where she remained until her 1995 move to Emory. At Emory, she chaired the Department of Biostatistics and Bioinformatics from 1994 to 2001.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Hertzberg won [American Statistical Association Founders Award](wikipedia://en/81174224) in 1994. She has been a [fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) since 1999,. She was one of the first American Statistical Association members to gain the P.Stat. certification designation.
