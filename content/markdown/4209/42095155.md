# Neukirch–Uchida theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Neukirch–Uchida theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Neukirch–Uchida theorem** shows that all problems about [algebraic number fields](wikipedia://en/28730822) can be reduced to problems about their [absolute Galois groups](wikipedia://en/2996781). [Jürgen Neukirch](wikipedia://en/17477195) showed that two algebraic number fields with the same absolute Galois group are [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic),[^n1][^n2] and Kôji Uchida strengthened this by proving Neukirch's conjecture that automorphisms of the algebraic number field correspond to [outer automorphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_automorphism) of its absolute Galois group.[^n3] [Florian Pop](wikipedia://en/24789740) extended the result to infinite fields that are [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/11985661) over [prime fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_field).[^n4][^n5]

:::

The Neukirch–Uchida theorem is one of the foundational results of [anabelian geometry](wikipedia://en/27119265), whose main theme is to reduce properties of geometric objects to properties of their [fundamental groups](wikipedia://en/11004), provided these fundamental groups are sufficiently non-abelian.

::: {#o8 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $K_1$, $K_2$ be two [algebraic number fields](wikipedia://en/28730822). The **Neukirch–Uchida theorem** says that, for every topological group [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828)

$$\phi:\operatorname{Gal}(\bar K_1/K_1)\to\operatorname{Gal}(\bar K_2/K_2)$$

of [absolute Galois groups](wikipedia://en/2996781), there exists a unique field isomorphism $\sigma:\bar K_1\to\bar K_2$ such that

$$\sigma(K_1)=K_2$$

and

$$\phi(g)=\sigma\circ g\circ\sigma^{-1}$$

for every $g\in\operatorname{Gal}(\bar K_1/K_1)$. The following diagram illustrates this condition.

$$\begin{matrix} K_1 & \hookrightarrow & \bar{K_1} & \xrightarrow[\cong]g & \bar{K_1} \\ {\scriptstyle\sigma|_{K_1}}\downarrow & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle\sigma} & & \downarrow{\scriptstyle\sigma} \\ K_2 & \hookrightarrow & \bar{K_2} & \xrightarrow[\phi(g)]{\cong} & \bar{K_2} \end{matrix}$$

In particular, for [algebraic number fields](wikipedia://en/28730822) $K_1$, $K_2$, the following two conditions are equivalent.

- $$\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{K_1}/K_1)\cong\operatorname{Gal}(\bar{K_2}/K_2)$$
- $$K_1\cong K_2$$

:::
