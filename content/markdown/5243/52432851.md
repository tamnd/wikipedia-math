# David Gauld (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Gauld"}

**David Barry Gauld** (born 28 June 1942) is a New Zealand mathematician. He is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Within mathematics, Gauld works in [set-theoretic topology](wikipedia://en/3665974) with emphasis on applications to [non-metrisable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrization_theorem) [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) and topological properties of manifolds close to metrisability. Gauld has authored two monographs[^o4][^o5] and over 70 research papers.[^o6]

Gauld was born on 28 June 1942 in [Inglewood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inglewood,_New_Zealand)[^o7] and grew up there. He was educated at [Wanganui Technical College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whanganui_City_College), [Inglewood High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inglewood_High_School_%28New_Zealand%29) and [New Plymouth Boys’ High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Plymouth_Boys'_High_School), and later obtained his BSc and MSc degrees with first-class honours in mathematics from the University of Auckland.

Awarded a [Fulbright Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program), he completed his PhD in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), in the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), supervised by [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424). He was Head of the Department of Mathematics for 15 years and Assistant Vice-Chancellor (Research) for two-and-a-half years at the University of Auckland.

Notable students of Gauld include [Sina Greenwood](wikipedia://en/70494973).[^o9]

## Honours {#Honours}

In the years 1981–1982, Gauld served as president of the [New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society). He was the founding secretary of the [New Zealand Mathematics Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematics_Research_Institute), and served in this position for 13 years, retiring in 2011. In 1997, he was awarded a New Zealand Science and Technology Medal by the [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_New_Zealand).[^o11] In 2015, he became an honorary life member of the New Zealand Mathematical Society.[^o12][^o13] In the [2016 New Year Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2016_New_Year_Honours_%28New_Zealand%29), Gauld was appointed an [Officer of the New Zealand Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit) for services to mathematics.[^o14][^o15]
