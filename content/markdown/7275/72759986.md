# Silvano Martello

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Silvano Martello"}

**Silvano Martello** (born in Bologna, Italy) is an Italian scientist and engineer, and an Emeritus Professor of [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research) at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna).[^o3] He is known for his research in [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research) and [Mathematical Programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Programming). In particular, he made significant contributions in the areas of [knapsack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knapsack_Problem) and [assignment](wikipedia://en/140592) problems, [packing problems](wikipedia://en/213003), and [vehicle routing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vehicle_Routing_Problem).[^o4] As of 2023, he published 160 peer-reviewed articles and was cited more than 7000 times.

:::

He was vice-president of the [Association of European Operational Research Societies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_of_European_Operational_Research_Societies) (EURO) from 2014 to 2017,[^o5] and has been chairman of the [European Chapter on Combinatorial Optimization](wikipedia://en/48855713) (ECCO) since 1997.[^o6]

He is editor-in-chief of [4OR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4OR), the joint official journal of the [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_Society_for_Operations_Research), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_française_de_Recherche_Opérationnelle_et_Aide_à_la_Décision), and [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Operations_Research_Society) Operations Research Societies.[^o7]

Among his PhD students are Andrew H. and Ann R. Tisch Professor [Andrea Lodi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Lodi)[^o8] ([Cornell Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_Tech)), and Professor [Mauro dell'Amico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mauro_dell'Amico)[^o9] ([University of Modena and Reggio Emilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Modena_and_Reggio_Emilia)).[^o10]

## Education and early career {#Education_and_early_career}

Martello graduated from the University of Bologna with a degree in [Electronic Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Engineering) in 1973. He was an assistant and then an associate professor at the University of Bologna from 1980 to 1990. From 1990 to 1994 he was a full professor of Operations Research and Management Science at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin). Since 1994 he is a full professor of Operations Research at the University of Bologna.

## Books {#Books}

He is the co-author, with [Paolo Toth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Toth), of the book *Knapsack problems: Algorithms and Computer implementations* (John Wiley & Sons, Inc., 1990).[^o17] He also co-authored, with [Rainer Burkard](wikipedia://en/27415322) and Mauro dell'Amico, the book *Assignment Problems* (SIAM, 2009).[^o18]
