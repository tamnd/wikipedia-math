# Prym differential

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Prym differential"}

In mathematics, a **Prym differential** of a [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) is a [differential form](wikipedia://en/221519) on the [universal covering space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_covering_space) that transforms according to some complex [character](wikipedia://en/293418) of the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004). Equivalently it is a section of a certain line bundle on the [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) in the same component as the [canonical bundle](wikipedia://en/734893). Prym differentials were introduced by (txt).

:::

The space of Prym differentials on a compact Riemann surface of genus *g* has dimension *g* – 1, unless the character of the fundamental group is trivial, in which case Prym differentials are the same as ordinary differentials and form a space of dimension *g*.
