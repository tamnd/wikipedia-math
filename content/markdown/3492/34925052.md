# Friedrich Prym

Friedrich Prym

*Image: ETH-BIB-Prym, Friedrich Emil (1841-1915)-Portrait-Portr 09682.tif (cropped).jpg.* Friedrich Prym in 1909

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Prym"}

**Friedrich Emil Fritz Prym** (28 September 1841, [Düren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Düren) – 15 December 1915, [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Prym varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prym_varieties) and [Prym differentials](wikipedia://en/34925017).

:::

Prym completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in 1863 with a thesis written under the direction of [Ernst Kummer](wikipedia://en/319117) and [Martin Ohm](wikipedia://en/4336770). In 1867 he started a Professor at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg), where he later became Dean, and then [Rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) in 1897–98.
