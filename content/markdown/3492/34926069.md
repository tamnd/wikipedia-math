# V. Kumar Murty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="V. Kumar Murty"}

*Image: Kumar Murty.jpg.* Kumar Murty

**Vijaya Kumar Murty** (born 20 May 1956) is an [Indo-Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Canadian) mathematician working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Murty obtained his BSc in 1977 from [Carleton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University) and his PhD in mathematics in 1982 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933).

He and his brother, [M. Ram Murty](wikipedia://en/14988649), have written more than 20 joint papers.[^o3] In a book edited by Alex Michalos,[^o4] there is a description of how the Murty brothers learned mathematics in their teens.

## Awards {#Awards}

Murty received the [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444) in 1991 from the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o6] He was elected to the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1995.[^o7] In 1996, he, along with his brother, M. Ram Murty, received the [Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Prize](wikipedia://en/57181134)[^o8] for the book "Non-vanishing of L-functions and their applications."[^o9]

In 2018, the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) listed him in their inaugural class of fellows.[^o10] He was named a [Member of the Order of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Order_of_Canada) in 2024.[^o11]

Murty is a professor at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto). In 2025, he was appointed as the founding director of the [Lodha Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/80884960).[^o12]
