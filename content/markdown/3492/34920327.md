# Helmut Hofer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Helmut Hofer"}

**Helmut Hermann W. Hofer** (born February 28, 1956)[^o3] is a German-American mathematician, one of the founders of the area of [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology).[^o4]

:::

He is a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o5] and the recipient of the 1999 [Ostrowski Prize](wikipedia://en/3159411)[^o6] and the 2013 [Heinz Hopf Prize](wikipedia://en/24560211). Since 2009, he has been a faculty member at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, New Jersey.[^o4] He currently works on [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298), [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems), and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). His contributions to the field include Hofer geometry. Hofer was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2020.[^o7]

He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in 1990 in Kyoto[^o8] and a plenary speaker at the ICM in 1998 in Berlin.[^o9]

He is currently an editor of *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*.[^o10]
