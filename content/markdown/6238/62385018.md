# Karin Baur

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Karin Baur"}

**Karin Baur** is a Swiss mathematician who is working in the mathematical fields [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [cluster algebras](wikipedia://en/27038423), [cluster categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluster_category), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944).[^o3] Currently she is a professor at [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds)[^o3] and she also a full professor at [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz).[^o4] From 2007–2012 she has been an assistant professor ([SNSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNSF) professor) at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o5] Moreover, she is one of the protagonists of the project *Women of Mathematics throughout Europe*.[^o6]

:::

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2018 Baur was awarded a [Royal Society Wolfson Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_Wolfson_Fellowship) for her work on *Surface categories and mutation*.[^o8]

For her project *Orbit Structures in Representation Spaces*, she won an [SNSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SNSF) Professorship in 2007.[^o5]
