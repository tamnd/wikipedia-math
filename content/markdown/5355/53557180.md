# Daniel J. Bauer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel J. Bauer"}

**Daniel John Bauer** (born June 29, 1973) is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), professor, and director of the [quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624) program at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), where he is also on the faculty at the Center for Developmental Science. He is known for rigorous methodological work on [latent variable models](wikipedia://en/4237572) and is a proponent of *integrative data analysis*, a [meta-analytic](wikipedia://en/62329) technique that pools [raw data](wikipedia://en/6895400) across multiple independent studies.[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Bauer graduated from [Trinity University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_University_%28Texas%29) in 1994 with a major in [psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychology), minors in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [history](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History), and a concentration in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science). He was a research coordinator at the [Baylor College of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baylor_College_of_Medicine) prior to graduate school.

He earned a PhD in [developmental psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Developmental_psychology) from the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 2000. He completed a postdoctoral fellowship in [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics) at UNC's Odum Institute for Research in Social Science, whereupon he joined the quantitative and developmental psychology faculty at [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University). He returned to UNC in 2004 as an assistant professor in the L. L. Thurstone Psychometric Laboratory. In addition, during that year, he was elected to the [Society of Multivariate Experimental Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Multivariate_Experimental_Psychology) and became a faculty associate at the [Statistical and Applied Mathematical Sciences Institute](wikipedia://en/41998171), leading a group for [multilevel](wikipedia://en/4521038) [latent variable models](wikipedia://en/4237572).

He was promoted to associate professor in 2008 and full professor in 2013.

### Extramural service {#Extramural_service}

Bauer serves on the editorial boards of *[Psychological Assessment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_Assessment)*, *[Psychological Methods](wikipedia://en/37384857)*, and the *[Journal of Educational and Behavioral Statistics](wikipedia://en/19685051)*. He is an editor of or reviewer for dozens of other journals.

In 2008, he cofounded the Curran–Bauer Analytics consulting firm with [Patrick Curran](wikipedia://en/53551359), a colleague in the Thurstone Lab, and has taught numerous doctoral-level workshops in [quantitative methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_methods) to social scientists worldwide. He was recognized by [UNC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) in 2016 "for exceptional teaching of post-baccalaureate students."[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Bauer has published widely in [factor analysis](wikipedia://en/253492), [multilevel modeling](wikipedia://en/4521038), [latent growth curves](wikipedia://en/6244696), [mixture models](wikipedia://en/871681), [latent class models](wikipedia://en/4094720), [structural equation modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structural_equation_model), and [item response theory](wikipedia://en/420159). He has presented his work at numerous conferences and has been cited in the scholarly literature more than 10,000 times.[^o8][^o9]

As the recipient of various grants from the [National Institute of Mental Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Mental_Health), the [National Institute on Drug Abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_on_Drug_Abuse), and the [Eunice Kennedy Shriver National Institute of Child Health and Human Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eunice_Kennedy_Shriver_National_Institute_of_Child_Health_and_Human_Development), he conducts [applied research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_research) in [psychopathology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychopathology) and negative [health behaviors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavior#Health) such as adolescent [substance use](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substance_abuse).
