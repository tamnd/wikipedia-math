# Olabisi Ugbebor

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Olabisi Ugbebor"}

**Olabisi Oreofe Ugbebor** (born Grace Olabisi Falode, 29 January 1951)[^o3] is the first female professor in mathematics in [Nigeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nigeria).[^o4][^o5] Born in [Lagos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagos), she studied mathematics at the [University of Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ibadan) and then at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), where she obtained a PhD in 1976.[^o4]

:::

## Education and academic career {#Education_and_academic_career}

Born in Lagos, Ugbebor had her secondary education at [Queen's College, Lagos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_College,_Lagos). She completed her first degree in mathematics from [University of Ibadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ibadan) in 1972. In 1973, she had a postgraduate diploma in statistics at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London), before completing her thesis on *Sample Path Properties of [Brownian Motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brownian_Motion)* (1976) at the age of 25. While at Unibadan, she was the only female student in her class. She is also the first Nigerian woman to get a PhD and become a professor in mathematics.[^o8] In 2017, she was made a Fellow of the Mathematics Association of Nigeria.[^o9]
