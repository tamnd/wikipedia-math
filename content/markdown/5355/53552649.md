# Juhani Karhumäki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Juhani Karhumäki"}

**Eero Urho Juhani Karhumäki**[^o3] (born 1949) is a [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) known for his contributions to [automata theory](wikipedia://en/103356). He is a professor at the [University of Turku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turku) in [southwestern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southwest_Finland) [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Karhumäki earned his doctorate from the [University of Turku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turku) in 1976. In 1980–1985, he was a junior researcher of [Academy of Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Finland). Since 1986, he has held teaching positions at the [University of Turku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turku), attaining full professorship in 1998. In 1998–2015, Karhumäki was the head of the mathematics department at the [University of Turku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turku). He has authored altogether around 200 research papers.

Karhumäki is a member of the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) since 2000 and of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) since 2006. A festschrift in his honour was published in 2009 as a special issue of [Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_Computer_Science_%28journal%29).[^o5]

## Research contributions {#Research_contributions}

Karhumäki has been a member of the [Lothaire](wikipedia://en/32232474) group of mathematicians that developed the foundations of combinatorics of words. In 1991, jointly with [Tero Harju](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tero_Harju), he solved the long-standing equivalence problem for multitape [finite automata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_automata) in automata theory.[^o7] Karhumäki contributed to different areas of [formal language theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_language_theory), such as [word equations](wikipedia://en/78148249),[^o8] [language equations](wikipedia://en/8031241)[^o9] and [descriptional complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descriptional_complexity) of finite automata.[^o10]
