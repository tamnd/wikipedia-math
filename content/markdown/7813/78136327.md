# Najiba Sbihi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Najiba Sbihi"}

**Najiba Sbihi** (born 1953) is a Moroccan mathematician and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476), known for her contributions to [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [graph algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_algorithm).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Sbihi earned a degree from the Faculty of Sciences of [Mohammed V University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammed_V_University) in [Rabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabat), Morocco in 1973. She continued her studies in France at [Joseph Fourier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University) in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble), first in computer science in which she earned a bachelor's degree in 1975. Continuing in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476), she earned a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in 1976, a [doctorat de troisième cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_de_troisième_cycle) in 1978 under the supervision of Michel Sakarovitch, and a [doctorat d'état](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_d'état) in 1987, supervised by Jean Fonlupt. Her doctoral study also included research in Canada with [Jack Edmonds](wikipedia://en/3625459) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) and with [Václav Chvátal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Václav_Chvátal) at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).

She worked with the Moroccan National Center for Scientific and Technical Research until, in 1992, becoming a professor of industrial engineering in the [Mohammadia School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohammadia_School_of_Engineering) in Rabat. She headed the Department of Industrial Engineering from 1995 to 1997.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Sbihi's contributions to graph theory and graph algorithms include the discovery that the [maximum independent set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_independent_set) problem can be solved in polynomial time on [claw-free graphs](wikipedia://en/12415907).A With Chvátal, she proved a special case of the [strong perfect graph theorem](wikipedia://en/744171), for the graphs that have no [bull graph](wikipedia://en/23396410) as an [induced subgraph](wikipedia://en/1655188).B Their work in this area introduced a type of graph decomposition that was central to the eventual proof of the full strong perfect graph theorem. She and Chvátal also devised efficient algorithms for recognizing the claw-free perfect graphs,C and later she and [Bruce Reed](wikipedia://en/37253906) showed how to recognize the Bull-free perfect graphs.D
