# Mieczysław Warmus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mieczysław Warmus"}

**Mieczysław Jan Warmus** (born 1 June 1918 in Dobrowlany, Poland (now Дабраўляны, Беларусь); died 20 September 2007 in [Wollongong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wollongong), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia)).[^o3][^o4] He was a [Polish mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_mathematician), a pioneer of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) in Poland,[^o5][^o6][^o7] professor, university lecturer, author of over a hundred scientific papers.[^o8][^o9] He defended his PhD at [Wrocław University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University) based on dissertation *Calculating Areas of Flat Surfaces Using Parallelogram Grids* under [Hugo Steinhaus](wikipedia://en/71735)' supervision.[^o10][^o11][^o12] The habilitation thesis titled *"Nomogrammable Functions"* was defended on February 22, 1958.[^o13] By a resolution dated May 29, 1958, the Central Qualification Committee for Scientific Workers awarded Associate Professor Dr. habil. Mieczysław Warmus the academic title of *Extraordinary Professor*.[^o3][^o6]

:::

Warmus died on September 20, 2007, in [Wollongong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wollongong), [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia). He was buried on October 2, 2007, at the [Powązki Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powązki_Cemetery) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw).
