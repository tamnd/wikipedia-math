# Mikhail Lyubich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Lyubich"}

**Mikhail (Misha) Lyubich** (born 25 February 1959 in [Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv), [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)) is a mathematician who has made important contributions to the fields of [holomorphic dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_dynamics) and [chaos theory](wikipedia://en/6295).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Lyubich graduated from [Kharkiv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv_University) with a master's degree in 1980, and obtained his PhD from [Tashkent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Uzbekistan) in 1984.

## Career {#Career}

Currently, he is a Professor of Mathematics at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) and the Director of the Institute of Mathematical Sciences at Stony Brook. From 2002-2008, he also held a position of Canada Research Chair at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).

He is credited with several important contributions to the study of [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems). In his 1984 Ph.D. thesis, he proved fundamental results on [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and the structural stability of [rational mapping](wikipedia://en/2783119).[^o5] Due to this work, the measure of maximal [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891) of a rational map (the [Mané-Lyubich measure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mané-Lyubich_measure)) bears his name.[^o6] In 1999, he published the first non-numerical proof of the [universality](wikipedia://en/32245) of the [Feigenbaum constants](wikipedia://en/79150) in chaos theory.[^o7]

He received the 2010 [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069) from the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o8] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9] He was selected as one of the plenary speakers for the 2014 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul).[^o10]
