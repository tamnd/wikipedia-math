# Peter Jagers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Jagers"}

**Peter Jagers** (12th March 1941) is a professor emeritus of mathematical statistics at [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg) and [Chalmers University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University_of_Technology) who made lasting contributions in [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [general branching processes](wikipedia://en/377202).[^o4] Jagers was first vice president (2007–2010) of [the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o4] and chair of the [Royal Society of Arts and Sciences in Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Arts_and_Sciences_in_Gothenburg) (2012).[^o4] He in an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute),[^o5] a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o6] and past president of the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society) (2005–2007).[^o7] He also served as a member of the Scientific Advisory Board of [Statistics Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Sweden).[^o8]

:::

Jagers has been coordinating editor of the *[Journal of Applied Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Applied_Probability)* and *[Advances in Applied Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advances_in_Applied_Probability)*. He is on the editorial committee for [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media)'s books on probability theory[^o9] and was editor of [Stochastic Processes and their Applications](wikipedia://en/41907213) 1989–1993.[^o4]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Jagers received an honorary doctorate from the [Bulgarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o4] and in 2012 was awarded the Carver Medal by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o11] In 2003 he received the Chalmers Medal[^o12] and in 2005 was awarded King [Carl XVI Gustaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_XVI_Gustaf) 's gold medal for "services to mathematics in Sweden and internationally".[^o13]

## Publications {#Publications}

He has published a large number of scientific works, notably in [branching process](wikipedia://en/377202) theory and its applications, and is the author of two books, Branching Processes with Biological Applications (J. Wiley 1975) and Branching Processes: Variation, Growth, and Extinction of Populations (Cambridge U. Press 2005). The Crump-Mode-Jagers (or generalized) branching process is named for Jagers.[^o15]
