# Takurō Mochizuki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takurō Mochizuki"}

*Image: Takuro Mochizuki cropped Takuro Mochizuki 20110412.jpg.* Takurō Mochizuki

**Takurō Mochizuki** (望月 拓郎, born 28 August 1972) is a Japanese mathematician at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University).

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

As a student at the University of Kyoto in 1994, Mochizuki left his undergraduate studies early to become a graduate student in mathematics at the same university. He completed his Ph.D. in 1999, and joined the faculty of [Osaka City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_City_University), returning to Kyoto in 2004.[^o4]

## Awards {#Awards}

*Image: Takuro Mochizuki cropped 1 Takurō Mochizuki.jpg.* Takurō Mochizuki in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) in 2015

He was awarded the [Japan Academy Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan_Academy_Prize_%28academics%29) in 2011 for his research on [D-modules](wikipedia://en/3440755) in [algebraic analysis](wikipedia://en/20846707).[^o4][^o5] In 2014 he was a [plenary speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065).[^o6]

Mochizuki was awarded the 2022 [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933) for his work on "the theory of bundles with flat connections over algebraic varieties".[^o7] The same year, he became a laureate of the [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) by the *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*.[^o8]
