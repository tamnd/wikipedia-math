# Tamar Ziegler

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Tamar Ziegler"}

**Tamar Debora Ziegler** (תמר ציגלר; born 1971) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [Ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [Additive combinatorics](wikipedia://en/18243392) and [Number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). She holds the Henry and Manya Noskwith Chair of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Einstein Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/59217494) at the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University). Ziegler is known for her contributions to the development of higher-order Fourier analysis and for applying methods from dynamical systems to problems in arithmetic combinatorics and number theory.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Ziegler received her Ph.D. in mathematics from the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) under the supervision of [Hillel Furstenberg](wikipedia://en/6450152). Her thesis title was “Non conventional ergodic averages”. She spent five years in the US as a postdoc at the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) at Princeton, and the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). She was a faculty member at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion) during the years 2007–2013, and joined the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) in the Fall of 2013 as a full professor.

Ziegler served as an editor of several journals. Among others she was an editor of the *[Journal of the European Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_European_Mathematical_Society)* (JEMS), an associate editor of the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)*, and the Editor in Chief of the *[Israel Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Journal_of_Mathematics)*.

## Research {#Research}

Ziegler's research lies in the interface of [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) with several mathematical fields including [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392).

Together with [Ben Green](wikipedia://en/1927952) and [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498), she developed the framework of [higher-order Fourier analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher-order_Fourier_analysis), which connects the [Gowers norms](wikipedia://en/38041703) with [nilsequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nilsequences), and is a powerful tool for analyzing arithmetic structures in sets of integers. These ideas led to major breakthroughs in understanding linear equations in primes.[^o8][^o9][^o10]

Other important contributions include the generalization of the [Green-Tao theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green-Tao_theorem) to polynomial patterns,[^o11][^o12] and the proof of the inverse conjecture for the [Gowers norms](wikipedia://en/38041703) in [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) geometry.[^o13][^o14][^o15]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Ziegler won the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize) of the [Israel Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/59341674) in 2011,\<ref>"Erdős Prize". *IMU*. <https://www.imu.org.il/erdos-prize>\<\\ref> the Bruno memorial award in 2015,[^o18] the [Alexanderson Award](wikipedia://en/76516158) in 2023, and the Rothschild Prize in 2024.[^o19] She received [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Consolidator grant in 2016 and [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Advanced grant in 2025.

Ziegler was the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) lecturer of the year in 2013, an invited sectional speaker at the 2014 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), an invited plenary speaker at the 2024 [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846), and an invited plenary speaker at the 2026 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o20]

Ziegler was named [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) Simons Professor for 2016–2017.[^o21] She was a Distinguished Visiting Professor at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 2022-23 leading the special year on Dynamics, Additive Number Theory and Algebraic Geometry\][^o22]

Ziegler was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2021.[^o23]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Ziegler is the daughter of Zvi Ziegler, a professor of mathematics at [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology).[^o25]
