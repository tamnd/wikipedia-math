# Annals of Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Annals of Statistics"}

The ***Annals of Statistics*** is a peer-reviewed statistics [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics). It was started in 1973 as a continuation in part of the *[Annals of Mathematical Statistics](wikipedia://en/23772805) (1930)*, which was split into the *Annals of Statistics* and the *[Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748)*.[^o3]

:::

The journal [CiteScore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CiteScore) is 5.8, and its [SCImago Journal Rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago_Journal_Rank) is 5.877, both from 2020.[^o4]

Articles older than 3 years are available on [JSTOR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JSTOR), and all articles since 2004 are freely available on the [arXiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArXiv).

## Editorial board {#Editorial_board}

The following persons have been editors of the journal:[^o6]

- [Ingram Olkin](wikipedia://en/26702356) (1972–1973)
- I. Richard Savage (1974–1976)
- [Rupert G. Miller](wikipedia://en/73622334) (1977–1979)
- [David V. Hinkley](wikipedia://en/9539349) (1980–1982)
- Michael D. Perlman (1983–1985)
- [Willem van Zwet](wikipedia://en/64492113) (1986–1988)
- Arthur Cohen (1988–1991)
- [Michael Woodroofe](wikipedia://en/55829743) (1992–1994)
- [Larry Brown](wikipedia://en/7886903) and John Rice (1995–1997)
- [Hans-Rudolf Künsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans-Rudolf_Künsch) and [James O. Berger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Berger_%28statistician%29) (1998–2000)
- John Marden and [Jon A. Wellner](wikipedia://en/73537857) (2001–2003)
- Morris Eaton and [Jianqing Fan](wikipedia://en/28282149) (2004–2006)
- [Susan Murphy](wikipedia://en/40783721) and [Bernard Silverman](wikipedia://en/7785395) (2007–2009)
- [Peter Bühlmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Bühlmann) and [T. Tony Cai](wikipedia://en/19317288) (2010–2012)
- [Peter Hall](wikipedia://en/13137979) and [Runze Li](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runze_Li) (2013–2015)
- Ed George and Tailen Hsing (2016–2018)
- [Richard J. Samworth](wikipedia://en/25180986) and Ming Yuan (2019–2021)
- Enno Mammen and Lan Wang (2022–2024)[^o7]
- Hans-Georg Müller and Harrison Zhou (2025–2027)
