# Eva Gallardo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eva Gallardo"}

**Eva Antonia Gallardo-Gutiérrez** (born 1973) is a Spanish mathematician specializing in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675). She is a professor of mathematics at the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complutense_University_of_Madrid), deputy director of the [Institute of Mathematical Sciences (Spain)](wikipedia://en/40134786), and past-president of the [Royal Spanish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/65501555) from 2022 until 2025.

:::

Gallardo completed her Ph.D. at the [University of Seville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Seville) in 2000. Her dissertation, *Ciclicidad de operadores: Teoría espectral*, was supervised by Alfonso Montes-Rodríguez. With Montes-Rodríguez, she is a coauthor of a research [monograph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monograph), *The Role of the Spectrum in the Cyclic Behavior of Composition Operators* (American Mathematical Society, 2004).
