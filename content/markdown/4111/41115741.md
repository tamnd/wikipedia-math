# Simple space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simple space"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **simple space** is a [connected](wikipedia://en/6233) [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) that has a homotopy type of a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022) and whose [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) is [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974) and [acts](wikipedia://en/12781) trivially on the homotopy and homology of the universal covering space, though not all authors include the assumption on the homotopy type.

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

### Topological groups {#Topological_groups}

For example, any [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) is a simple space (provided it satisfies the condition on the homotopy type).

### Eilenberg-Maclane spaces {#Eilenberg-Maclane_spaces}

Most [Eilenberg-Maclane spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eilenberg-Maclane_space) $K(A,n)$ are simple since the only nontrivial homotopy group is in degree $n$. This means the only non-simple spaces are $K(G,1)$ for $G$ [nonabelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonabelian_group).

### Universal covers {#Universal_covers}

Every connected topological space $X$ has an associated (universal) simple space from the universal cover $\pi:U_X \to X$; indeed, $\pi_1(U_X) = *$ and the universal cover is its own universal cover.

:::
