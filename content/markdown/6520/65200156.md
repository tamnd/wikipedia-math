# Christine De Mol

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christine De Mol"}

**Christine De Mol** (born 23 April 1954) is a Belgian applied mathematician and mathematical physicist interested in [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956), [regularization](wikipedia://en/2009061), [wavelets](wikipedia://en/50903), and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), and known for her work on [proximal gradient methods](wikipedia://en/39587805) and the application of [proximal gradient methods for learning](wikipedia://en/41200806). She is a professor of mathematics at the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles), and the former chair of the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Imaging Science.

:::

## Education {#Education}

De Mol was educated at the Université libre de Bruxelles, earning a [licence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in physics in 1975 and a Ph.D. in 1979, with a dissertation *Sur la régularisation des problèmes inverses linéaires* under the joint supervision of Jean Reignier and Mario Bertero.

## Career {#Career}

De Mol became a researcher for the Belgian [National Fund for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Fund_for_Scientific_Research) (FNRS), obtaining a permanent position there in 1981 and becoming a director of research in 1996. Meanwhile, she had obtained a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from the Université libre de Bruxelles; her habilitation thesis was *Super-résolution en microscopie confocale*. In 1998 she gave up her position with the FNRS, becoming an honorary researcher with them, to become a full professor at the Université libre de Bruxelles. She was head of the mathematics department at the university for 2009–2010, and chair of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) Activity Group on Imaging Science for 2012–2013.
