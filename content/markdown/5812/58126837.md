# Yael Tauman Kalai

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yael Tauman Kalai"}

**Yael Tauman Kalai** (יעל טאומן קלעי) is a [cryptographer](wikipedia://en/18934432) and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) and the Ellen Swallow Richards Professor at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in the [Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Computer_Science_and_Artificial_Intelligence_Laboratory). Previously, she worked as a Senior Principal Researcher at [Microsoft Research New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research_New_England).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kalai graduated from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1997. She earned a master's degree in 2001 at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science), working with [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314), and then completed a PhD advised by [Shafi Goldwasser](wikipedia://en/842178) at MIT in 2006. After postdoctoral study at Microsoft Research and the Weizmann Institute, she became a faculty member at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology). She took a permanent position at Microsoft Research in 2008 and became a professor at MIT in 2024.[^o4] She serves on the Scientific Advisory Board of the [Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Institute_for_the_Theory_of_Computing).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Kalai is known for co-inventing [ring signatures](wikipedia://en/5376829), which have become a key component of numerous systems such as [Cryptonote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptonote) and [Monero (cryptocurrency)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monero_%28cryptocurrency%29). Subsequently, together with her advisor [Shafi Goldwasser](wikipedia://en/842178), she demonstrated an insecurity in the widely used [Fiat–Shamir heuristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiat–Shamir_heuristic). Her work on [delegating computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verifiable_computing) has applications to cloud computing.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Kalai was elected a Fellow of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043) in 2022 for foundational contributions in delegated computation and leakage-resilient cryptography, and service to the cryptographic community.

Kalai was an invited speaker on mathematical aspects of computer science at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).

Her master's thesis introducing [ring signatures](wikipedia://en/5376829) won an outstanding master's thesis award and her MIT PhD dissertation was awarded the George M. Sprowls Award for Outstanding PhD Thesis in Computer Science.

She was co-chair of the [Theory of Cryptography Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Cryptography_Conference) in 2017.

She was awarded the 2022 [ACM Prize in Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Prize_in_Computing) "for breakthroughs in verifiable delegation of computation and fundamental contributions to cryptography".

## Personal {#Personal}

Kalai is the daughter of game theorist [Yair Tauman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yair_Tauman). Her husband, [Adam Tauman Kalai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Tauman_Kalai), works at OpenAI.
