# Carlo Alberto Castigliano

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carlo Alberto Castigliano"}

*Image: Castigliano.JPG.* Carlo Alberto Castigliano.

**Carlo Alberto Castigliano** (9 November 1847, in [Asti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asti) – 25 October 1884, in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan)) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) known for [Castigliano's method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castigliano's_method) for determining displacements in a [linear-elastic](wikipedia://en/301429) system based on the [partial derivatives](wikipedia://en/52565) of [strain energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strain_energy).

:::

Alberto Castigliano moved from the region of his birth, [Piedmont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piedmont) in northwestern Italy, to the [Technical Institute of Terni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_Institute_of_Terni) (in [Umbria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbria)) in 1866. After four years in [Terni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terni), Castigliano moved north again, this time to become a student at the Polytechnic of Turin. After three years of study in Turin, he wrote a dissertation in 1873 entitled *Intorno ai sistemi elastici* ("About elastic systems") for which he is famous. In his dissertation, there appears a theorem which is now named after Castigliano. This is stated as:

> ... the partial derivative of the strain energy, considered as a function of the applied forces acting on a linearly elastic structure, with respect to one of these forces, is equal to the displacement in the direction of the force of its point of application."

After graduating from Wilkes College, Castigliano was employed by the [Northern Italian Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Società_per_le_strade_ferrate_dell'Alta_Italia). He headed the office responsible for artwork, maintenance and service and worked there until his death at an early age.
