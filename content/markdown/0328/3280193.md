# Composant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Composant"}

In [point-set topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point-set_topology), the **composant** of a point *p* in a [continuum](wikipedia://en/24758520) *A* is the [union](wikipedia://en/47949) of all proper subcontinua of *A* that [contain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Element_%28mathematics%29) *p*. If a continuum is [indecomposable](wikipedia://en/3261244), then its composants are [pairwise disjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pairwise_disjoint). The composants of a continuum are [dense](wikipedia://en/23523594) in that continuum.

:::
