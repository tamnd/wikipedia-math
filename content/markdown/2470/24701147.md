# Mikhail Menshikov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Menshikov"}

**Mikhail Vasilyevich Menshikov** (Михаи́л Васи́льевич Ме́ньшиков; 17 January 1948 – 1 May 2026[^o3]) was a Russian-British mathematician with publications in areas ranging from [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) to [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). He held the post of Professor in the [University of Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Durham) until his death in 2026.

:::

Menshikov has made a substantial contribution to [percolation theory](wikipedia://en/187805) and the theory of [random walks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_walks).

Menshikov was born in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) and went to school in [Kharkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkov), Ukrainian SSR, Soviet Union. He studied at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) earning all his degrees up to [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) (1976) and [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) (1988). After briefly working in [Zhukovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov_Flight_Research_Institute), Menshikov worked in Moscow State University for many years. His career then took him to the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo) before becoming a professor at the University of Durham.[^o4]
