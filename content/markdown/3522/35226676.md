# Paul Dienes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Dienes"}

**Paul Dienes** ([Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people): *Dienes Pál*. November 24, 1882 [Tokaj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokaj), [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary) – March 23, 1952) was a Hungarian mathematician, philosopher, linguist and poet.

:::

Born in to a wealthy and aristocratic Protestant family, he married [Valéria Geiger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valéria_Geiger) (1879–1978) in December 1905. They had two sons, Gedeon Dienes (1914) and [Zoltan Paul Dienes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoltan_Paul_Dienes) (1916). Following their divorce, he married [Sari Dienes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sari_Dienes) in 1922. He was an active member of the [Galileo Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galileo_Circle) along with his brother [László Dienes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/László_Dienes).

Dienes joined the [Hungarian Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Communist_Party) during the establishment of the [Hungarian Soviet Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Soviet_Republic) and in 1919 was appointed the political commissar of the [University of Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University).[^o2] After the fall of the Soviet Republic he fled to Vienna and was later invited to the United Kingdom.

From 1921 to 1923 he lectured at [University College, Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Swansea), where his students included [Evan Tom Davies](wikipedia://en/47098303). From 1923 to 1948 he was Professor of Mathematics at [Birkbeck College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkbeck_College), London, where his students included [Ralph Henstock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Henstock) and [Abraham Robinson](wikipedia://en/46024).
