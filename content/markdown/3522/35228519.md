# Charles Napoleon Moore

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Napoleon Moore"}

**Charles Napoleon Moore** (1882–1967) was an American mathematician at [Bowling Green State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling_Green_State_University) who worked on [convergence factors](wikipedia://en/4576951).

:::

He was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1932 in Zürich.

His first doctoral student was [Bess Marie Eversull](wikipedia://en/77361797).[^o4]
