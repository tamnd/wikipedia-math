# Josef Anton Gmeiner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Josef Anton Gmeiner"}

**Josef Anton Gmeiner** (1862-1926) was an Austrian mathematician working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).[^o2]

:::

Gmeiner studied physics and mathematics at the [University of Innsbruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Innsbruck) from 1885. In 1890 he passed the examination qualifying him to teach at *[Gymnasien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasien)*. After two years as an assistant at the University of Innsbruck's physical institute, he worked as an auxiliary teacher at secondary schools in various locations, including Graz, [Fiume](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fiume), Klagenfurt and Vienna.[^o2] He earned his doctorate at the University of Innsbruck in 1895, under the joint supervision of [Leopold Gegenbauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopold_Gegenbauer) and [Otto Stolz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Stolz). He then found employment at the German-language *Gymnasium* in [Pula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pula). He became in 1899 a teacher at a *[Realschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Realschule)* in Vienna and in 1900 a [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) in mathematics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and at [TH Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TH_Wien). At the [German University in Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_University_in_Prague) he was appointed professor extraordinarius in 1901 and promoted to professor ordinarius in 1904. In 1906 he returned to the University of Innsbruck in the professorial chair vacated by the death of Stolz in 1905.[^o2]
