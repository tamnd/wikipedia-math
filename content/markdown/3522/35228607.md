# Aleksander Axer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aleksander Axer"}

**Aleksander Axer** (10 October 1880 – 4 October 1948) was a Polish mathematician from [Przemyśl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Przemyśl) who introduced [Axer's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axer's_theorem).[^o2]

:::

He died suddenly of complications from [pharyngitis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pharyngitis) in [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich), shortly before his 68th birthday.[^o3]
