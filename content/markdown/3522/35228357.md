# Eberhard Knobloch

*Image: Eberhard Knobloch (cropped).jpg.* Knobloch in 2009 at [MFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eberhard Knobloch"}

**Eberhard Knobloch** (born 6 November 1943) is a German [historian of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historian_of_science) and [mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historian_of_mathematics).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Knobloch was born in [Görlitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Görlitz). From 1962 to 1967 he studied classics and mathematics at [FU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FU_Berlin) and [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), after which he passed his state examination as a high school teacher. He taught ancient languages at Goethe-Gymnasium (Berlin-Wilmersdorf). Previous to 1970 he had been a research assistant in the history of science at TU Berlin. There he studied with [Christoph Scriba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christoph_Scriba), and with a thesis on Leibniz's [combinators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinators), Knobloch received his doctorate in 1972.

From 1973 he was professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the College of Education in Berlin. In 1976 he qualified as a professor in Berlin and was a visiting scholar at [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford), [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) and [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh). Since 1976 he is head of the math sections of the Academy edition of the works of [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz](wikipedia://en/12281) (and later the technical-scientific parts). In 1981 he became professor of history of science at Technische Universität Berlin (since 2002 academy professor); retiring in 2009. In 1984 he was a visiting professor at the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in Leningrad. Since 1999 he has been a regular guest professor at [Northwestern Polytechnical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_Polytechnical_University) in Xian, [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China). He also was a visiting professor at the [Ecole Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Normale_Supérieure) in Paris.

Besides the Leibniz Edition, he also oversaw the Tschirnhaus edition of the [Saxon Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxon_Academy_of_Sciences) and worked at Kepler with edition. He is also director of the Alexander von Humboldt Research Centre of the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences). He also dealt with Renaissance technology (such as military engineer [Mariano Taccola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taccola)), the notebooks[^o3] of [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) and Jesuit scholars like [Christopher Clavius](wikipedia://en/633645). Knobloch assisted the Dieter Lelgemann surveyors to decode and interpret the [Ptolemy](wikipedia://en/23979) chart with [Susudata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Susudata).

He is a member of the [International Academy of the History of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Academy_of_the_History_of_Science) in Paris (corresponding member since 1984, member since 1988, 2001 to 2005 as Vice President and later its president). Since 1996, a member of the Leopoldina, corresponding member of the [Saxon Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxon_Academy_of_Sciences), Member of [Academia Scientiarum et Artium Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Scientiarum_et_Artium_Europaea) since 1997 and the Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences . From 2001 to 2005 he was president of the German National Committee for the History of Science. In 2004 he was elected president of the European Society for the History of Science for 2006–2008.[^o4][^o5] Knobloch, along with [Roshdi Rashed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roshdi_Rashed), won the 2017 [Kenneth O. May Prize](wikipedia://en/25758309) of The [International Commission on the History of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/35011217).[^o6]
