# Rajchman measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rajchman measure"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Rajchman measure**, studied by (txt), is a regular [Borel measure](wikipedia://en/4322) on a [locally compact group](wikipedia://en/1651204) such as the circle, whose [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247) vanishes at infinity.

:::
