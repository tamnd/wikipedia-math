# Karl Rohn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Rohn"}

*Image: Karl Friedrich Wilhelm Rohn.jpeg.* Karl Rohn

**Karl Friedrich Wilhelm Rohn** (25 January 1855 in [Schwanheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bensheim) – 4 August 1920 in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who studied [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Rohn studied in [Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darmstadt), [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig) and [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), initially engineering but then mathematics by the influence of [Alexander von Brill](wikipedia://en/28565454), among the others. In 1878 he received a doctorate under the supervision of [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) in Munich, and in 1879 he habilitated at Leipzig. The subject of his doctoral thesis and habilitation was the [Kummer surfaces](wikipedia://en/647758) of order 4 and their relationship with [hyperelliptic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperelliptic_function) (with [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) of genus 2). In 1884 he became an associate professor at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig) and a year later at the [Dresden University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden_University_of_Technology), where in 1887 he was a professor of descriptive geometry. In 1904 he became a professor at Leipzig.

In addition to the Kummer surfaces, he studied algebraic space curves and completed the classification work of [Georges Halphen](wikipedia://en/1295437) and [Max Noether](wikipedia://en/2482337).

In 1913 he was the president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).
