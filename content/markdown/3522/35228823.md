# Aksel Frederik Andersen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aksel Frederik Andersen"}

**Aksel Frederik Andersen** (10 February 1891 Fodby, [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) – 1972 [Gentofte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gentofte), Denmark) was a Danish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [infinite series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_series) and in particular on [Cesàro summation](wikipedia://en/705600).

:::
