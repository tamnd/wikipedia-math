# Gustavo Sannia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gustavo Sannia"}

*Image: Gustavo Sannia.jpg.* Portrait of Gustavo Sannia

**Gustavo Sannia** (13 May 1875 – 21 December 1930)[^o2] was an Italian mathematician working in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849), and [summation of series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summation_of_series). He was the son of [Achille Sannia](wikipedia://en/59714572), mathematician and senator of the [Kingdom of Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Italy).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gustavo Sannia was born in [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples).[^o2]

Sannia lived in [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turin) from 1902 to 1915 and from 1919 to 1922, first as an assistant to [D'Ovidio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Ovidio) and Fubini and later as a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor).[^o2] From 1915 to 1919, he taught at the [University of Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cagliari).[^o2]

Sannia returned to Naples in 1924, where he would remain until his premature death.[^o2]
