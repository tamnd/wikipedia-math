# Walter Schnee

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Schnee"}

**Walter Schnee** (8 August 1885 – 10 June 1958) was a German mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Schnee was born on 8 August 1885 in [Rawicz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rawicz). From 1904 to 1908, he studied mathematics in Berlin. From 1909 to 1917, he worked at the [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau). He then went to the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig), where he stayed until 1954. He worked in the field of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He died on 10 June 1958 in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig).[^o3][^o4]
