# Lancelot Stephen Bosanquet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lancelot Stephen Bosanquet"}

**Lancelot Stephen Bosanquet** (26 December 1903 St. Stephen's-by-Saltash, Cornwall, England – 10 January 1984 Cambridge) was a British mathematician who worked in [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29), especially [Fourier series](wikipedia://en/59038).

:::

His daughter, [Rosamund Caroline Bosanquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caroline_Bosanquet) (1940–2013) was a British cellist, music teacher and composer.[^o2]
