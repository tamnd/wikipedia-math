# Horst Knörrer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horst Knörrer"}

*Image: Horst Knörrer.jpg.* Knörrer at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2010

**Horst Knörrer** (born 31 July 1953, in [Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayreuth)) is a German mathematician who studies [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

Knörrer studied from 1971 at [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) and [University of Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg) and received a doctorate in 1978 from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) under the supervision of [Egbert Brieskorn](wikipedia://en/34748690) (*Isolierte Singularitäten von Durchschnitten zweier Quadriken*). After that, he was a research assistant until 1985 in Bonn, interrupted by two years 1980 to 1982 at the [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University). In 1985 he completed his habilitation in Bonn and was a [Heisenberg fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heisenberg_fellow) the following two years. During 1986/87, he was a department representative at the [University of Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Düsseldorf). Since 1987, he is a full professor of mathematics at the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).

Knörrer studies algebraic geometry and its connection to mathematical physics, for example, for [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213), as well as mathematical theory of many-particle systems in [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [solid state physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_state_physics) ([Fermi liquids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_liquid)). Together with Brieskorn, he wrote an extensive and rich illustrated textbook on [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260), which also was translated into English.
