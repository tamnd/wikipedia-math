# Adolf Schepp

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adolf Schepp"}

**Adolf Schepp** (1837 – 9 March 1905)[^o2] was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who translated several mathematical works from [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) and [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_language) into [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language).[^o2][^o3]
