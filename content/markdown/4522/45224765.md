# Michael Loss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Loss"}

**Michael Loss** (born 1954)[^o2] is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) who works as a professor of mathematics at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology).[^o3]

:::

Loss obtained his Ph.D. in 1982 from the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), with a dissertation on the [three-body problem](wikipedia://en/1208420) jointly supervised by Walter Hunziker and [Israel Michael Sigal](wikipedia://en/33973309).[^o4]

He coauthors the graduate textbook *Analysis* ([Graduate Studies in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/8871519) 14. [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 1997; 2nd ed., 2001) with [Elliott H. Lieb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_H._Lieb).[^o5]

In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822),[^o6] and was elected as a Foreign Corresponding Member of the Chilean Academy of Sciences.[^o7] He is one of the 2015 winners of the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize).[^o8]
