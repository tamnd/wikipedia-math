# Ray Kunze

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ray Kunze"}

**Ray Alden Kunze** (March 7, 1928 – May 21, 2014) was an American mathematician who chaired the mathematics departments at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine) and the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia).[^o3][^o4] His mathematical research concerned the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447) and [noncommutative harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/24062542).[^o4]

:::

Kunze was born in [Des Moines, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Des_Moines,_Iowa) and grew up near [Milwaukee, Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee,_Wisconsin).[^o3][^o4] He began his undergraduate studies at [Denison University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denison_University) but transferred to the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) after two years, and earned bachelor's and master's degrees in mathematics.[^o4] After working as a military mathematical analyst,[^o3][^o4] he returned to the University of Chicago, and earned his Ph.D. in 1957 with a dissertation on [Fourier transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_transformation) supervised by [Irving Segal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Segal).[^o4][^o5] As well as his positions at UCI and Georgia, he also worked at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University), and [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis).[^o3][^o4] He has over 50 [academic descendants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_genealogy), many of them through his students [Paul Sally](wikipedia://en/2789475) at Brandeis and Edward N. Wilson at Washington University.[^o5]

With his advisor [Irving Segal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Segal), Kunze was the author of the textbook *Integrals and Operators* (McGraw-Hill, 1968; 2nd ed., Grundlehren der Mathematischen Wissenschaften 228, Springer, 1978).[^o6] With Kenneth M. Hoffman he was the author of *Linear Algebra* (Prentice-Hall, 1961; 2nd ed., Pearson, 1971).[^o4][^o7]

In 1994, a special session on [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) was held in honor of Kunze as part of the 889th meeting of the American Mathematical Society, and the papers from the session were published as a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift).[^o8] In 2012, Kunze was recognized as one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
