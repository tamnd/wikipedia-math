# Eduard Looijenga

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eduard Looijenga"}

*Image: Looijenga kurke.jpg.* Looijenga with Herbert Kurke, in 2007

**Eduard Jacob Neven Looijenga** (born 30 September 1948, [Zaandam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaandam))[^o2] is a Dutch mathematician who works in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and the theory of [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924).[^o3] He was a professor of mathematics at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) until his retirement in 2013.[^o4]

:::

Looijenga studied mathematics at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) beginning in 1965, and earned a master's degree there in 1971. He obtained a Dutch fellowship for two years of study at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) in France, and then returned to the University of Amsterdam, earning a Ph.D. in 1974 under the supervision of [Nicolaas Kuiper](wikipedia://en/3344929).[^o5] After postdoctoral research at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool), he took a faculty position at the [University of Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen) in 1975, returned as a professor to the University of Amsterdam in 1987, and moved again to Utrecht in 1991. Since his 2013 retirement, he has also held a professorship at [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University).[^o5]

In 1978, Looijenga was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065).[^o5] He became a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1995,[^o3] and in 2012 he became one of the inaugural fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] In 2013, a conference in honor of his retirement was held at Utrecht University.[^o4]
