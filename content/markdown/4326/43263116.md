# Ernst Kötter

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ernst Kötter"}

**Ernst Kötter** (1859-1922) was a German mathematician.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kötter graduated in 1884 from the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) under the supervision of [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) and [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450).

## Career {#Career}

Kötter's treatise *"Fundamentals of a purely geometrical theory of [algebraic plane curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_plane_curve)"* gained the 1886 prize of the [Berlin Royal Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin_Royal_Academy).[^o7]

In 1901, he published his report on *"The development of [synthetic geometry](wikipedia://en/267484) from [Monge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monge) to [Staudt](wikipedia://en/2186050) (1847)"*;[^o8] it had been sent to the press as early as 1897, but completion was deferred by Kötter's appointment to [Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aachen_University) and a subsequent persisting illness.[^o9] He constructed a mobile wood model to illustrate the theorems of [Dandelin spheres](wikipedia://en/683726).[^o10][^o11]

In a discussion with [Schoenflies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schoenflies) and Kötter, [Hilbert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert) reportedly uttered his famous quotation according to which [points](wikipedia://en/593693), [lines](wikipedia://en/946975), and [planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29) in geometry could be named as well *"tables, chairs, and beer mugs"*.[^o12]
