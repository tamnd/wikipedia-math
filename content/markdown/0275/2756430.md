# Tarski–Kuratowski algorithm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tarski–Kuratowski algorithm"}

In [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414) and [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) the **Tarski–Kuratowski algorithm** is a [non-deterministic algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-deterministic_algorithm) that produces an [upper bound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_bound) for the complexity of a given formula in the [arithmetical hierarchy](wikipedia://en/186475) and [analytical hierarchy](wikipedia://en/634233).

:::

The algorithm is named after [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920) and [Kazimierz Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075).

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Algorithm"}

## Algorithm {#Algorithm}

The Tarski–Kuratowski algorithm for the arithmetical hierarchy consists of the following steps:

1. Convert the formula to [prenex normal form](wikipedia://en/421068). (This is the non-deterministic part of the algorithm, as there may be more than one valid prenex normal form for the given formula.)
1. If the formula is quantifier-free, it is in $\Sigma^0_0$ and $\Pi^0_0$.
1. Otherwise, count the number of alternations of quantifiers; call this *k*.
1. If the first quantifier is [∃](wikipedia://en/91420), the formula is in $\Sigma^0_{k+1}$.
1. If the first quantifier is [∀](wikipedia://en/76174), the formula is in $\Pi^0_{k+1}$.

:::
