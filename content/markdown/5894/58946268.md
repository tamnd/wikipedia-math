# Zimmer's conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zimmer's conjecture"}

**Zimmer's conjecture** is a statement in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) "which has to do with the circumstances under which [geometric spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_space) exhibit certain kinds of [symmetries](wikipedia://en/53741)."[^o2] It was named after the mathematician [Robert Zimmer](wikipedia://en/4335903). The conjecture states that there can exist symmetries (specifically [higher-rank lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher-rank_lattice)) in a higher dimension that cannot exist in lower dimensions.

:::

A proof of the conjecture was announced in 2017 and published in 2020 by Aaron Brown (mathematician), [Sebastián Hurtado-Salazar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastián_Hurtado-Salazar), and David Fisher (mathematician).[^o2][^o3][^o4]
