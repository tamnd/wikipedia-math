# Stephen R. Doty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen R. Doty"}

**Stephen Richard Doty** (born April 16, 1953) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in algebraic [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) (especially [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685)). He earned a doctorate in mathematics from [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame) in 1982 under the supervision of [Warren J. Wong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_J._Wong) with dissertation *The Submodule Structure of Weyl Modules for Groups of Type A<sub>n</sub>*. After post-doctoral positions at [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) and [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame), he joined the faculty at [Loyola University Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_University_Chicago) in 1987.

:::

In 2007 Doty was named the Inaugural Yip Fellow of [Magdalene College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalene_College), [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University). In 2009 he was a Mercator Professor in Germany.
