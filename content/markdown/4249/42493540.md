# The Knot Atlas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Knot Atlas"}

***The Knot Atlas*** is a [website](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Website), an [encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encyclopedia) rather than atlas, dedicated to [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). It and its predecessor were created by mathematician [Dror Bar-Natan](wikipedia://en/10363723), who maintains the current site with Scott Morrison. According to Schiller, the site contains, "beautiful illustrations and detailed information about knots," as does *KnotPlot.com*.[^o2] According to the site itself, it is a knot [atlas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlas) (collection of maps), theory [database](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database), [knowledge base](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_base), and "a home for some [computer programs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_program)".

:::
