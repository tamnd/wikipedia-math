# Mariangiola Dezani-Ciancaglini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mariangiola Dezani-Ciancaglini"}

**Mariangiola Dezani-Ciancaglini** (born 22 December 1946) is an Italian [logician](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) whose research topics include [type theory](wikipedia://en/40282) and [intersection type disciplines](wikipedia://en/61408357), [lambda calculus](wikipedia://en/18203), and [programming language semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semantics_%28computer_science%29). She is a professor emerita at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

She was born on 22 December 1946 in Turin. After earning a master's degree in physics at the University of Turin in 1970, and working as a researcher in Turin as a [CNR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Council_%28Italy%29) fellow, she became an assistant professor of computer science at University of Turin in 1972, and full professor in 1981.

While continuing as a professor, she earned a Ph.D. from the [Catholic University of Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen) in the Netherlands in 1996. Her dissertation, *Logical Semantics for Concurrent Lambda-Calculus*, was jointly promoted by [Henk Barendregt](wikipedia://en/1651213) and [Corrado Böhm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrado_Böhm).

She served as dean of computer science at the University of Turin from 2005 to 2008, and retired to become a professor emerita in 2018.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 1993. In 2015 she was named a Fellow of the [European Association for Theoretical Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Association_for_Theoretical_Computer_Science) "for distinguished and seminal achievements in formal methods and foundations of programming languages, introducing or developing new type systems for the lambda-calculus as well as for the pi-calculus and related calculi".
