# Ionica Smeets

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ionica Smeets"}

**Ionica Smeets** (born 8 October 1979) is a [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people) mathematician, [science journalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_journalist), columnist, television presenter and professor in [science communication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_communication) at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ionica Smeets was born in [Delft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft), where she completed her [propedeuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propedeuse) in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology). She switched studies and graduated in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).

Smeets was working on her thesis in mathematics, on a subject within [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), at Leiden University when she started the blog *Wiskundemeisjes* (**Math girls*'), on 14 March 2006, together with Jeanine Daems. The website has been awarded in 2007 with two*Dutch Bloggies*.[^o4] Since 2010, the two 'math girls' ran a [column](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Column_%28periodical%29) in*[De Volkskrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Volkskrant)*for several years. Currently, Smeets writes a weekly piece titled "*Ionica zag een getal*" ("*Ionica saw a number*") in the periodical*Sir Edmund*(a magazine from*[De Volkskrant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Volkskrant)*).***

She completed her PhD at Leiden in 2010; her dissertation, *On Continued Fraction Algorithms*, was supervised by [Robert Tijdeman](wikipedia://en/9006087) and Cornelis Kraaikamp.[^o6] After this, Smeets became a science journalist. She performs research, together with [Bas Haring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas_Haring), on successful methods to explain science to the general audience. In this function, she appeared on Dutch television, such as in *[De Wereld Draait Door](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Wereld_Draait_Door)*. On 19 October 2011, the book *Ik was altijd heel slecht in wiskunde* (*I have always been bad at mathematics*) appeared, which she wrote together with Jeanine Daems. Begin 2012, Smeets co-presented eight editions of the [KRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KRO)-program *De Rekenkamer*, replacing [Sofie van den Enk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofie_van_den_Enk).[^o7] In 2013, Smeets presented the mathematics program *Eureka*, together with Van den Enk.[^o8] On 17 August 2014 she was guest in the Dutch television program *Zomergasten*. Furthermore, she participated in the [NCRV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCRV)'s [television quiz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quiz) *[De Slimste Mens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_slimste_mens)* in August 2014, almost reaching the final round.[^o9]

In July 2015, Smeets was appointed professor in Science Communication at the University of Leiden.[^o10] In December 2015, she started presenting the [VPRO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/VPRO) television program *de Nationale Wetenschapsquiz* (*the National Science Quiz*), which she has presented together with Pieter Hulst up until now.
