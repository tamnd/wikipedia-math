# Jan Hendrik Bruinier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Hendrik Bruinier"}

**Jan Hendrik Bruinier** (born 21 October 1971) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), whose work focuses on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Work {#Work}

In 2011, together with [Ken Ono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ken_Ono), he developed a finite algebraic formula for the values of the [partition function](wikipedia://en/312250).[^o4][^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

He was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to number theory, automorphic forms, and arithmetic geometry".[^o7]
