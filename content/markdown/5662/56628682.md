# Subfield of an algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Subfield of an algebra"}

In algebra, a **subfield of an [algebra](wikipedia://en/191788) *A*** over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) *F* is an *F*-[subalgebra](wikipedia://en/45239) that is also a field. A **maximal subfield** is a subfield that is not contained in a strictly larger subfield of *A*.

:::

If *A* is a finite-dimensional [central simple algebra](wikipedia://en/404082), then a subfield *E* of *A* is called a **strictly maximal subfield** if $[E : F] = (\dim_F A)^{1/2}$.
