# Association of Statisticians of American Religious Bodies

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Association of Statisticians of American Religious Bodies"}

The **Association of Statisticians of American Religious Bodies** (**ASARB**) is an American non-profit organization that brings together [statisticians](wikipedia://en/48661) from various [religious groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Religious_denomination) in the United States, with the aim of compiling accurate statistics regarding all such groups. It was established in 1935 and is based in [Lenexa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenexa), [Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas).[^o3][^o4] Since 1990, it has sponsored the US Religion Census, a national survey of Americans' religious beliefs conducted independently of, but at the same time as, the [United States Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Census). This survey had previously been conducted by the [National Council of Churches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_of_Churches).[^o5][^o6] It has become increasingly important since the US Census stopped asking Americans about their religion after [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).[^o7]
