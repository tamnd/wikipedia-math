# Eknath Prabhakar Ghate

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Eknath Prabhakar Ghate"}

**Eknath Prabhakar Ghate** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specialising in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and working at the School of Mathematics, [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research), Mumbai, India. He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize) for science and technology, the highest science award in India, for the year 2013 in the mathematical sciences category.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Ghate was schooled at [Mayo College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayo_College), Ajmer and at the [International School Manila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_Manila). He studied at [St. Stephen's College, Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Stephen's_College,_Delhi) and obtained his bachelor's degree from the College of Arts & Sciences, [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1991.[^o5] He earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), in 1996;[^o6] his doctoral advisor was [Haruzo Hida](wikipedia://en/46742133).

## Career {#Career}

Ghate is a professor at the [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research). In number theory, Ghate is mostly interested in problems connected to [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146), [Galois representations](wikipedia://en/486929), and the special values of [L-functions](wikipedia://en/245283).

## Awards {#Awards}

Ghate was awarded the [Bhatnagar Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swaroop_Bhatnagar_Award) in 2013. Ghate was elected a Fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2014. Ghate was awarded the [JTM Gibson Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Gibson_%28schoolmaster%29) for Excellence by Mayo College in 2019. He was elected Fellow of the Indian National Science Academy in 2021.
