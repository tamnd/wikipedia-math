# Tord Hall

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tord Hall"}

**Tord Hall** (7 January 1910 - 30 September 1987) was a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), university professor and bestselling author.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in 1910 in [Jönköping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jönköping), Sweden,[^o3] and died in 1987.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

He completed his Ph.D. in mathematics from [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University).[^o5] His PhD advisor was [Arne Beurling](wikipedia://en/286530).[^o5] The title of his PhD thesis was *On Polynomials bounded at an Infinity of Points*.

From 1959 to 1975, he was a professor of mathematics at Uppsala University.[^o3] He published several mathematical theorems.[^o6]

In addition to his academic career, he also worked as an employee for newspaper [Svenska Dagbladet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Svenska_Dagbladet) and [Sveriges Radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sveriges_Radio), the national Swedish radio.

## Bibliography {#Bibliography}

He is most well known as the biographer of [Carl Gauss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Gauss) (*Biography of Carl Gauss*).[^o8][^o9] It was translated into English by [Albert Froderberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Froderberg).[^o10]

The book generally received favorable reviews.[^o11]
