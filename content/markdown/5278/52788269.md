# Richard Ehrenborg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Ehrenborg"}

**Richard Ehrenborg** is a Swedish mathematician working in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582).[^o3] He is known for developing the [quasisymmetric function](wikipedia://en/29661763) of a poset.[^o4] He currently holds the Ralph E. and Norma L. Edwards Research Professorship at the University of Kentucky [^o5] and is the first recipient of the Royster Research Professor at [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky).[^o6]

:::

Ehrenborg earned his Ph.D. from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 1993 under the supervision of [Gian-Carlo Rota](wikipedia://en/101891). He is a descendant of another Richard Ehrenborg (jurist),[^o5] (born 1655) who was a professor and Rektor of [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University). He is also a juggler and magician.[^o8]
