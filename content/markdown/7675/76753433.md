# Almut Burchard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Almut Burchard"}

**Almut Burchard** is a mathematician whose research interests include [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and their applications in [network calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_calculus) for the [stochastic analysis](wikipedia://en/47895) of [communication networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communication_network). Educated in Germany and the US, she has worked in the US and Canada, where she is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Burchard earned a diploma in mathematics (the German equivalent of a master's degree) in 1989 from [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), with research supervised by [Willi Jäger](wikipedia://en/33490633) on mathematical modeling of chemical reactions. She shifted to pure mathematics for her doctoral work on the [Riesz rearrangement inequality](wikipedia://en/39228990) at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech), supervised by [Michael Loss](wikipedia://en/45224765), completing her Ph.D. in 1994.

She was a faculty member in the [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) department of mathematics from 1994 to 1998, and at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) from 1998 to 2005. She has held her present position at the University of Toronto since 2005.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2021, the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) listed Burchard as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fellows_of_the_Fields_Institute), recognizing her "energetic support" of the Fields Institute, in helping develop an online program on [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034), and in mentorship of undergraduates.
