# Carl-Erik Särndal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl-Erik Särndal"}

**Carl-Erik Särndal** (born 1937) is a Swedish-Canadian [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). specializing in survey statistics. He held professorial appointments at [Umeå University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umeå_University); [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia), [Université de Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Montréal), and [Statistics Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Sweden), Stockholm. He specialized in survey theory and methodology, especially with applications to official statistics production for a country. He worked, periodically, as researcher, expert and/or consultant, at national statistical agencies: Statistics Canada (Ottawa), Statistics Sweden (Stockholm) and Statistics Finland (Helsinki).

:::

## Education and professional years {#Education_and_professional_years}

Carl-Erik Särndal grew up in Sweden. He attended Lund University, where he earned a Bachelor of Science degree in 1957, and a PhD in statistics in 1962. He was a professor at the Department of Statistics, [Umeå University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umeå_University), Sweden, 1967–1970; Division of Management Science, Faculty of Commerce & Business Administration, [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia), Vancouver, 1970–1980; Département de mathématiques et de statistique, [Université de Montréal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Montréal), 1980–1997; Statistics Sweden, 1997–2002.

## Research {#Research}

Model assisted design-based inference; uses of auxiliary information in estimation; generalized regression (GREG) estimation; calibration weighting, in particular for survey nonresponse; critical examination of the probability sampling paradigm.

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- Fellow, [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), 1973.
- Honorary member, Finnish Statistical Society, 1995.
- Honorary doctor's degree (filosofie hedersdoktor), [Örebro University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Örebro_University), Sweden, 2000.
- Honorary Member, [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327), 2002.[^o5]
- Waksberg Award, by [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) and [Statistics Canada](wikipedia://en/186760), to prominent survey methodology statistician, 2007.[^o6]
- Jerzy Neyman Medal, [Polish Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/42302854), 2018.
- Honorary doctor's degree, [Université de Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Neuchâtel), Switzerland, 2022.[^o7]
