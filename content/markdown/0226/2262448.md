# John F. MacGregor

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John F. MacGregor"}

**John Frederick MacGregor** (born 1943 in [Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontario), Canada) is a statistician whose work in the field of [statistical process control](wikipedia://en/593680) has received significant recognition. His pioneering work was in the area of [latent variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_variable)/[multivariate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis) (MVA) methods ([principal components analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_components_analysis) and [partial least squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_least_squares)) applied to industrial processes.

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

- A [latent variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_variable) framework for [Model Predictive Control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Predictive_Control) (MPC).
- Latent variable modeling, monitoring and control of batch processes.
- [Differential Geometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_Geometric) control of polymer processes.
- Introduction of [Stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222) states in the [Extended Kalman Filter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extended_Kalman_Filter) for [integral action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_action).
- Multivariate image analysis
- Model-based product development

## Education {#Education}

He received his PhD degree in Statistics, and his M.S. degrees in both [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and in [Chemical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_Engineering) from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), and his Bachelor of Engineering degree from [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University) in Canada. At Wisconsin, he worked under the statistician [George EP Box](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_EP_Box).

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

MacGregor is a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), and has received many awards for his work in applied statistics and chemometrics, among them, the prestigious [Shewhart Medal](wikipedia://en/831964) and the [W.G. Hunter Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W.G._Hunter_Award) from the [American Society for Quality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_for_Quality), and the Herman Wold Medal[^o6] from the [Swedish Chemical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Chemical_Society). The Canadian Journal of Chemical Engineering dedicated the October 2008 edition to MacGregor in celebration of his 65th birthday.[^o7] Industrial & Engineering Chemistry Research published a Festschrift in his honour in September 2013, in honour of his 70th birthday.[^o8]

He holds the title of "Distinguished University Professor"[^o9] (the highest honor awarded to faculty at McMaster University)[^o10] as well as the [Dofasco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dofasco) Chair in Process Automation and Information Technology at McMaster University. He is a cofounder of the McMaster Advanced Control Consortium that is sponsored by many international companies.

## Industrial and Entrepreneurial Activities {#Industrial_and_Entrepreneurial_Activities}

In 2002, MacGregor established ProSensus Inc., a company that he spun off from the McMaster Advanced Control Consortium and incorporated in 2004. MacGregor continues to consult and teach courses through ProSensus.
