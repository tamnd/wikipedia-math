# Rudolf Lipschitz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Lipschitz"}

**Rudolf Otto Sigismund Lipschitz** (14 May 1832 – 7 October 1903) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made contributions to [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) (where he gave his name to the [Lipschitz continuity condition](wikipedia://en/51731)) and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), as well as [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [algebras](wikipedia://en/191788) with [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) and [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rudolf Lipschitz was born on 14 May 1832 in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg). He was the son of a landowner and was raised at his father's estate at Bönkein which was near Königsberg.[^o4] He entered the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg) when he was 15, but later moved to the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin) where he studied with [Gustav Dirichlet](wikipedia://en/240002). Despite having his studies delayed by illness, in 1853 Lipschitz graduated with a PhD in Berlin.[^o5]

After receiving his PhD, Lipschitz started teaching at local [Gymnasiums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28Germany%29). In 1857 he married Ida Pascha, the daughter of one of the landowners with an estate near to his father's,[^o4] and earned his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), where he remained as a [privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent). In 1862 Lipschitz became an extraordinary professor at the [University of Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Breslau) where he spent the following two years. In 1864 Lipschitz moved back to Bonn as a full professor. He was appointed Bonn's first chair of Mathematics in 1869.[^o6] He remained there for the rest of his career. Here he examined the dissertation of [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887). Lipschitz died on 7 October 1903 in Bonn.[^o7]
