# Additive group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Additive group"}

An **additive group** is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of which the group operation is to be thought of as *addition* in some sense. It is usually [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974), and typically written using the symbol **\+** for its binary operation.

:::

This terminology is widely used with structures equipped with several operations to specify structures obtained by forgetting the other operations. Examples include the *additive group*[^o2] of the [integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integers), of a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) and of a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404). This is particularly useful with rings and [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) to distinguish the additive underlying group from the [multiplicative group](wikipedia://en/1110742) of the [invertible elements](wikipedia://en/289666).

In older terminology, an additive subgroup of a ring has also been known as a *modul* or *module* (not to be confused with a [module](wikipedia://en/276410)).[^o3]
