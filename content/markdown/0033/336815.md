# List of conjectures by Paul Erdős

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of conjectures by Paul Erdős"}

The prolific [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and his various collaborators made many mathematical [conjectures](wikipedia://en/6138) across subjects, and in many cases Erdős offered monetary rewards for solving them.

:::

The unsolved problems are commonly known as [Erdős problems](wikipedia://en/601284#Erdős's_problems).

## Unsolved {#Unsolved}

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Gyárfás conjecture](wikipedia://en/336838) on cycles with lengths equal to a power of two in graphs with minimum degree 3.

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Hajnal conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Hajnal conjecture](wikipedia://en/42585591) that in a family of graphs defined by an excluded induced subgraph, every graph has either a large clique or a large independent set.[^o5]

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Mollin–Walsh conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Mollin–Walsh conjecture](wikipedia://en/462499) on consecutive triples of powerful numbers.

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Covering system"}

- The Erdős–Selfridge conjecture that a [covering system](wikipedia://en/2297820) with distinct moduli contains at least one even modulus.

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Straus conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Straus conjecture](wikipedia://en/2431002) on the Diophantine equation 4/*n* = 1/*x* + 1/*y* + 1/*z*.

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős conjecture on arithmetic progressions"}

- The [Erdős conjecture on arithmetic progressions](wikipedia://en/4312009) in sequences with divergent sums of reciprocals.

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Szekeres conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Szekeres conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Szekeres_conjecture) on the number of points needed to ensure that a point set contains a large convex polygon.

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Turán conjecture on additive bases"}

- The [Erdős–Turán conjecture on additive bases](wikipedia://en/31244492) of natural numbers.

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="quickly growing integer sequences with rational reciprocal series"}

- A conjecture on [quickly growing integer sequences with rational reciprocal series](wikipedia://en/4535485).

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Circle packing in an equilateral triangle"}

- A conjecture with Norman Oler[^o14] on [circle packing in an equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/31948928) with a number of circles one less than a [triangular number](wikipedia://en/213915).

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Minimum overlap problem"}

- The [minimum overlap problem](wikipedia://en/41375141) to estimate the limit of *M*(*n*).

:::

- A conjecture that the ternary expansion of $2^n$ contains at least one digit 2 for every $n > 8$.[^o16]

::: {#o17 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Moser equation"}

- The conjecture that the [Erdős–Moser equation](wikipedia://en/53551948), $1^{k} + 2^{k} + \cdots + (m - 1)^{k} = m^{k}$, has no solutions except $1^{1} + 2^{1} = 3^{1}$.

:::

## Solved {#Solved}

::: {#o19 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Faber–Lovász conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Faber–Lovász conjecture](wikipedia://en/691803) on coloring unions of cliques, proved (for all large n) by Dong Yeap Kang, Tom Kelly, [Daniela Kühn](wikipedia://en/37003499), Abhishek Methuku, and [Deryk Osthus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deryk_Osthus) in 2021.[^o20][^o21]

:::

::: {#o22 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős sumset conjecture"}

- The [Erdős sumset conjecture](wikipedia://en/64308146) on sets, proven by Joel Moreira, Florian Karl Richter and Donald Robertson in 2018. The proof appeared in the [Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785) journal in March 2019.[^o23]

:::

::: {#o24 .entry cue=list-entry name="Burr–Erdős conjecture"}

- The [Burr–Erdős conjecture](wikipedia://en/3086101) on Ramsey numbers of graphs, proved by Choongbum Lee in 2015.[^o25][^o26]

:::

::: {#o27 .entry cue=list-entry name="Equitable coloring"}

- A conjecture on [equitable colorings](wikipedia://en/21796230) proven in 1970 by [András Hajnal](wikipedia://en/21765779) and [Endre Szemerédi](wikipedia://en/663489) and now known as the [Hajnal–Szemerédi theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hajnal–Szemerédi_theorem).[^o28]

:::

::: {#o29 .entry cue=list-entry name="Furstenberg–Sárközy theorem"}

- A conjecture that would have strengthened the [Furstenberg–Sárközy theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Furstenberg–Sárközy_theorem) to state that the number of elements in a square-difference-free set of positive integers could only exceed the square root of its largest value by a polylogarithmic factor, disproved by [András Sárközy](wikipedia://en/22916190) in 1978.[^o30]

:::

::: {#o31 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Lovász conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Lovász conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Lovász_conjecture) on weak/strong [delta-systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_system), proved by [Michel Deza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Deza) in 1974.[^o32]

:::

::: {#o33 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Heilbronn conjecture](wikipedia://en/7960510) in combinatorial number theory on the number of sums of two sets of residues modulo a prime, proved by Dias da Silva and Hamidoune in 1994.[^o34]

:::

::: {#o35 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Graham conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Graham conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Graham_conjecture) in combinatorial number theory on monochromatic Egyptian fraction representations of unity, proved by [Ernie Croot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernie_Croot) in 2000.[^o36]

:::

::: {#o37 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Stewart conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Stewart conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Stewart_conjecture) on the [Diophantine equation](wikipedia://en/9109) *n*! + 1 = *p*<sub>*k*</sub><sup>*a*</sup> *p*<sub>*k*+1</sub><sup>*b*</sup>, solved by [Florian Luca](wikipedia://en/47516015) in 2001.[^o38]

:::

::: {#o39 .entry cue=list-entry name="Cameron–Erdős conjecture"}

- The [Cameron–Erdős conjecture](wikipedia://en/13587617) on sum-free sets of integers, proved by [Ben Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_J._Green) and Alexander Sapozhenko in 2003–2004.[^o40]

:::

::: {#o41 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Menger conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Menger conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Menger_conjecture) on disjoint paths in infinite graphs, proved by [Ron Aharoni](wikipedia://en/22744244) and Eli Berger in 2009.[^o42]

:::

::: {#o43 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős distinct distances problem"}

- The [Erdős distinct distances problem](wikipedia://en/30193394). The correct exponent was proved in 2010 by [Larry Guth](wikipedia://en/31875947) and [Nets Katz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nets_Katz), but the correct power of log *n* is still undetermined.[^o44]

:::

::: {#o45 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Rankin conjecture"}

- The [Erdős–Rankin conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cramer's_conjecture#Conditional_proven_results_on_prime_gaps) on prime gaps, proved by [Ford](wikipedia://en/55700462), [Green](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben_J._Green), [Konyagin](wikipedia://en/29007447), and [Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) in 2014.[^o46]

:::

::: {#o47 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős discrepancy problem"}

- The [Erdős discrepancy problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_discrepancy_problem) on partial sums of ±1-sequences. [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) announced a solution in September 2015; it was published in 2016.[^o48]

:::

::: {#o49 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős squarefree conjecture"}

- The [Erdős squarefree conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_squarefree_conjecture) that central binomial coefficients C(2*n*, *n*) are never squarefree for *n* > 4 was proved in 1996 by [Olivier Ramaré](wikipedia://en/2916107) and [Andrew Granville](wikipedia://en/2910434).[^o50][^o51]

:::

::: {#o52 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős primitive set conjecture"}

- The [Erdős primitive set conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_primitive_set_conjecture) that the sum $\sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{n\log n}$ for any primitive set A (a set where no member of the set divides another member) attains its maximum at the set of prime numbers, proved by Jared Duker Lichtman in 2022.[^o53][^o54][^o55]

:::

::: {#o56 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős–Sauer problem"}

- The [Erdős–Sauer problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős–Sauer_problem) about the maximum number of edges an n-vertex graph can have without containing a k-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821) subgraph, was solved by Oliver Janzer and [Benny Sudakov](wikipedia://en/35862803).[^o57][^o58]

:::

::: {#o59 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős problem 728"}

- Terence Tao reported that [Erdős problem 728](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_problem_728) was solved in 2026 using AI assistance.[^o60] The proof was constructed in the [Lean proof language](wikipedia://en/62889984) by Kevin Barreto and Liam Price with the assistance of [ChatGPT 5.2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GPT-5.2) and the [Aristotle Lean API](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle_Lean_API).[^o61][^o62]

:::

::: {#o63 .entry cue=list-entry name="Erdős problem 347"}

- [Erdős problem 347](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_problem_347) on the subset sums of sequences with ratio limit 2 was proved in January 2026 by Enrique Barschkis; the proof was formally verified in the [Lean](wikipedia://en/62889984) proof assistant.[^o64]

:::

- In March 2026, Erdős problem 369 was solved by 17-year-old Sky Yang (Yueer Yang), and the result was later proven correct after verification in Lean.[^o65]

::: {#o66 .entry cue=list-entry name="planar unit distance problem"}

- Erdős problem 90, the [planar unit distance problem](wikipedia://en/7158665), was solved by an internal [OpenAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI) model in May 2026.[^o67]

:::
