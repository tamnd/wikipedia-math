# Ferdinand von Lindemann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ferdinand von Lindemann"}

**Carl Louis Ferdinand von Lindemann** (12 April 1852 – 6 March 1939) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who proved in 1882 that [$\pi$](wikipedia://en/23601) (pi) is a [transcendental number](wikipedia://en/30325), meaning it is not a [root](wikipedia://en/23000#Solving_polynomial_equations) of any nonzero [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) with [rational](wikipedia://en/19727024) [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203).

:::

## Life and education {#Life_and_education}

Lindemann was born in [Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanover), the capital of the [Kingdom of Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Hanover). His father, Ferdinand Lindemann, taught modern languages at a [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29) in Hanover. His mother, Emilie Crusius, was the daughter of the gymnasium's headmaster. The family later moved to [Schwerin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwerin), where young Ferdinand attended school.

He [studied](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research) mathematics at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg), and the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München). At the University of Erlangen, he received a doctorate, supervised by [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887),[^o3] on [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610). Lindemann subsequently taught in [Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Würzburg) and at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg). During his time in Freiburg, Lindemann devised his proof that $\pi$ is a transcendental number (see the [Lindemann–Weierstrass theorem](wikipedia://en/348976)). After his time in Freiburg, Lindemann transferred to the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg). While a professor in [Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Königsberg), Lindemann acted as supervisor for the doctoral theses of the mathematicians [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302), [Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537), and [Arnold Sommerfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arnold_Sommerfeld).[^o5]

## Transcendence proof {#Transcendence_proof}

In 1882, Lindemann published the result for which he is best known, the [transcendence](wikipedia://en/30325) of [$\pi$](wikipedia://en/23601).[^o7] His methods were similar to those used nine years earlier by [Charles Hermite](wikipedia://en/22870422) to show that *[e](wikipedia://en/9633)*, the base of [natural logarithms](wikipedia://en/21476), is transcendental, and the works of [Joseph Liouville](wikipedia://en/341810) from the 1840s establishing the existence of transcendental numbers.[^o8] Before the publication of Lindemann's proof, [Johann Heinrich Lambert](wikipedia://en/93085) had already [shown that $\pi$ is irrational in 1768](wikipedia://en/12792547#Lambert's_proof).[^o8]
