# Cornelius Greither

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cornelius Greither"}

**Cornelius Greither** (born 1956)[^o3] is a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specialising in [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150) and the structure of [Galois modules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_module).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Greither completed his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1983 at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) under the supervision of Bodo Pareigis: his thesis bears the title *Zum Kürzungsproblem kommutativer Algebren*.[^o7] He [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1988 at same [university](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University), with thesis title *Cyclic Galois extensions and normal bases*.[^o7]

In 1992, Greither proved the [Iwasawa main conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwasawa_main_conjecture) for [abelian number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_number_field) in the $p=2$ case.[^o8][^o9] In 1999, together with D. R. Rapogle, [K. Rubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Rubin), and A. Srivastav, he proved a converse to the [Hilbert–Speiser theorem](wikipedia://en/1067670).[^o10]

Greither was a full professor at the [University of the Bundeswehr Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Bundeswehr_Munich). He retired in 2022,[^o11] and he is now an [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus).[^o12][^o13]

Greither is on the editorial boards of the journals *[Archivum Mathematicum (Brno)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archivum_Mathematicum_%28Brno%29)*,[^o4][^o14] *[New York Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Journal_of_Mathematics)*,[^o4][^o15] as well as the *[Journal de Théorie des Nombres de Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_de_Théorie_des_Nombres_de_Bordeaux)*.[^o4][^o16] Until 2014, he was an associate editor of *Annales mathématiques du Québec*.[^o4][^o17]
