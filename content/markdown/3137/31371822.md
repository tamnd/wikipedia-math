# Benjamin Osgood Peirce

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Benjamin Osgood Peirce"}

**Benjamin Osgood Peirce** (February 11, 1854 – January 14, 1914) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a holder of the [Hollis Chair of Mathematicks and Natural Philosophy](wikipedia://en/31306720) at Harvard from 1888 until his death in 1914.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Benjamin Osgood Peirce was born to M. (née Seccomb) and Benjamin Osgood Peirce on February 11, 1854, in [Beverly, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beverly,_Massachusetts). In 1876, he graduated from [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College). He then received a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from the [Leipzig University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig_University) in 1879.[^o6] He then studied in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) for another year.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Peirce taught at the [Boston Latin School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Latin_School) for one year. From 1881 to 1884, he taught mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He then taught mathematics and physics as an assistant professor until 1888. In 1888, he became the [Hollis Chair of Mathematics and Natural Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollis_Chair_of_Mathematics_and_Natural_Philosophy).[^o6]

Pierce was elected to the American Academy of Arts and Sciences in 1884.[^o8] Peirce was elected to the Council of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), serving from 1896 to 1898. He was a founder of the [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society) when it began in 1899 and was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) (United States) in 1906.[^o9][^o10] He was honored with election to foreign academies such as the [Mathematical Circle of Palermo](wikipedia://en/32146789) and the [French Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Physical_Society). In 1910 he was awarded an honorary degree by Harvard University.[^o9] That same year, he was elected to the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society).[^o11] In 1912 he represented Harvard University at the celebrations for the 250th anniversary of the founding of the [Royal Society of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_London).[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Peirce married Isabella Turnbull Landreth in 1882. Together, they had two daughters.[^o6]

Removed by several degrees, he was a cousin of [Charles Sanders Peirce](wikipedia://en/6117),[^o13] whose father, Benjamin Peirce, worked as the academic advisor to Joseph Lovering, Benjamin Osgood Peirce's predecessor as holder of the Hollis Chair of Mathematics and Natural Philosophy.

## Death {#Death}

Peirce died at his home in Cambridge from [angina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angina) on January 14, 1914.[^o6] He is buried at the Central Cemetery in Beverly.[^o15]
