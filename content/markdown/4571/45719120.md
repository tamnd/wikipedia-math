# Balázs Szegedy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Balázs Szegedy"}

*Image: Balázs Szegedy Oberwolfach 2007.jpg.* Szegedy at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2007

**Balázs Szegedy** is a Hungarian mathematician whose research concerns [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

Szegedy earned a master's degree in 1998 and a PhD in 2003 from [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University) in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest).[^o2] His dissertation, supervised by Péter Pál Pálfy, was about [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and was entitled "On the Sylow and Borel subgroups of classical groups".[^o3] After temporary positions at the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670), [Microsoft Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microsoft_Research), and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), he joined the faculty of the [University of Toronto Scarborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Scarborough) in 2006. He returned to the Rényi Institute in 2013.[^o2]

Szegedy won the [Géza Grünwald](wikipedia://en/35261957) Commemorative Prize for young researchers of the [János Bolyai Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22777615) in 2002.[^o2] He was one of two winners of the 2009 [European Prize in Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Prize_in_Combinatorics),[^o4] and in 2010 he became a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship).[^o5] With [László Lovász](wikipedia://en/1623938), he was one of the winners of the 2012 [Fulkerson Prize](wikipedia://en/1425916), for their joint work on [graph limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_graph).[^o6] He was the 2013 winner of the [Coxeter–James Prize](wikipedia://en/18739444) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055).[^o7]

In 2018, Szegedy was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#2018,_Rio_de_Janeiro) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).
