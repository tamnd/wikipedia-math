# Rhombitriheptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rhombitriheptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rhombitriheptagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/318742). At each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417) of the tiling there is one [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) and one [heptagon](wikipedia://en/365349), alternating between two [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29). The tiling has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) rr{7, 3}. It can be seen as constructed as a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) [triheptagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/8834492), r{7,3}, as well as an [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) [heptagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/8834198) or expanded [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365).

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Deltoidal triheptagonal tiling"}

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling is called a *deltoidal triheptagonal tiling*, and consists of congruent [kites](wikipedia://en/84840). It is formed by overlaying an [order-3 heptagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order-3_heptagonal_tiling) and an [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365).

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are eight hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular heptagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms.

### Symmetry mutations {#Symmetry_mutations}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of [cantellated](wikipedia://en/6317514) polyhedra with vertex figure (3.4.n.4), and continues as tilings of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/318742). These [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) figures have (\*n32) reflectional [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2915678).
