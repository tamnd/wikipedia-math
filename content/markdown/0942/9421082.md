# Order-5 square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 square tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-5 square tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {4,5}.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular polyhedra and tilings with vertex figure (4<sup>n</sup>).

This hyperbolic tiling is related to a [semiregular infinite skew polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_skew_polyhedron#Semiregular_infinite_skew_polyhedra) with the same vertex figure in Euclidean 3-space.
