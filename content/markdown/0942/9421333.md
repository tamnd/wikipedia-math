# Truncated heptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated heptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated heptagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There are one [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) and two [tetradecagons](wikipedia://en/6058229) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of *t*{7,3}. The tiling has a [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745) of 3.14.14.

:::

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling is called an *order-7 triakis triangular tiling*, seen as an [order-7 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/8834365) with each triangle divided into three by a center point.

*Image: Order-7 triakis triangular tiling.svg.*

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

This hyperbolic tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of uniform [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) polyhedra with [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) (3.2n.2n), and \[n,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) symmetry.

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are eight hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular heptagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are eight forms.
