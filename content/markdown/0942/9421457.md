# Truncated order-7 triangular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-7 triangular tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-7 truncated triangular tiling**, sometimes called the **hyperbolic soccerball**,[^o2] is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There are two [hexagons](wikipedia://en/59733) and one [heptagon](wikipedia://en/365349) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417), forming a pattern similar to a conventional [soccer ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football_ball) ([truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282)) with heptagons in place of [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t{3,7}.

:::

## Hyperbolic soccerball (football) {#Hyperbolic_soccerball_(football)}

This tiling is called a **hyperbolic soccerball** (football) for its similarity to the [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282) pattern used on [soccer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_football) balls. Small portions of it as a hyperbolic surface can be constructed in 3-space.

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling is called a *heptakis heptagonal tiling*, named for being constructible as a [heptagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/8834198) with every heptagon divided into seven triangles by the center point.

## Related tilings {#Related_tilings}

This hyperbolic tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of uniform [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) polyhedra with [vertex configurations](wikipedia://en/3603745) (n.6.6), and \[n,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) symmetry.

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are eight hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular heptagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms.
