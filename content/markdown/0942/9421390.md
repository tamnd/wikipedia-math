# Snub triheptagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub triheptagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-3 snub heptagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There are four [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and one [heptagon](wikipedia://en/365349) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of *sr{7,3}*. The [snub tetraheptagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snub_tetraheptagonal_tiling) is another related hyperbolic tiling with Schläfli symbol *sr{7,4}*.

:::

## Dual tiling {#Dual_tiling}

The dual tiling is called an *order-7-3 floret pentagonal tiling*, and is related to the [floret pentagonal tiling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floret_pentagonal_tiling).

*Image: 7-3 floret pentagonal tiling.svg.*

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

This semiregular tiling is a member of a sequence of [snubbed](wikipedia://en/9428917) polyhedra and tilings with vertex figure (3.3.3.3.*n*) and [Coxeter–Dynkin diagram](wikipedia://en/5358554) . These figures and their duals have (n32) rotational [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2915678), being in the Euclidean plane for n=6, and hyperbolic plane for any higher n. The series can be considered to begin with n=2, with one set of faces degenerated into [digons](wikipedia://en/5442728).

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are eight hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular heptagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are 8 forms.
