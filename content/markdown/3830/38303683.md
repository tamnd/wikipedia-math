# Divided domain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Divided domain"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), a **divided domain** is an [integral domain](wikipedia://en/15462) *R* in which every [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) $\mathfrak{p}$ satisfies $\mathfrak{p} = \mathfrak{p} R_\mathfrak{p}$. A **locally divided domain** is an integral domain that is a divided domain at every [maximal ideal](wikipedia://en/48164). A [Prüfer domain](wikipedia://en/12367835) is a basic example of a locally divided domain.[^o2] Divided domains were introduced by (Akiba 1967) who called them AV-domains.

:::
