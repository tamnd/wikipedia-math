# Congruence ideal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Congruence ideal"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the **congruence ideal** of a [surjective](wikipedia://en/27873) [ring homomorphism](wikipedia://en/26411) *f* : *B* → *C* of [commutative rings](wikipedia://en/61346) is the [image](wikipedia://en/579311) under *f* of the [annihilator](wikipedia://en/328684) of the [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240) of *f*.

:::

It is called a congruence ideal because when *B* is a [Hecke algebra](wikipedia://en/55907189) and *f* is a homomorphism corresponding to a [modular form](wikipedia://en/286000), the congruence ideal describes congruences between the modular form of *f* and other modular forms.

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

- Suppose *C* and *D* are rings with homomorphisms to a ring *E*, and let *B* = *C*×<sub>*E*</sub>*D* be the pullback, given by the subring of *C*×*D* of pairs (*c*,*d*) where *c* and *d* have the same image in *E*. If *f* is the natural projection from *B* to *C*, then the kernel is the ideal *J* of elements (0,*d*) where *d* has image 0 in *E*. If *J* has annihilator 0 in *D*, then its annihilator in *B* is just the kernel *I* of the map from *C* to *E*. So the congruence ideal of *f* is the ideal (*I*,0) of *B*.
- Suppose that *B* is the [Hecke algebra](wikipedia://en/55907189) generated by [Hecke operators](wikipedia://en/521837) *T*<sub>*n*</sub> acting on the 2-dimensional space of modular forms of level 1 and weight 12.This space is 2 dimensional, spanned by the Eigenforms given by the [Eisenstein series](wikipedia://en/1270458) *E*<sub>12</sub> and the [modular discriminant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_discriminant) Δ. The map taking a Hecke operator *T*<sub>*n*</sub> to its eigenvalues (σ<sub>11</sub>(*n*),τ(*n*)) gives a homomorphism from *B* into the ring **Z**×**Z** (where τ is the [Ramanujan tau function](wikipedia://en/1231708) and σ<sub>11</sub>(*n*) is the sum of the 11th powers of the divisors of *n*). The image is the set of pairs (*c*,*d*) with *c* and *d* congruent mod 619 because of Ramanujan's congruence σ<sub>11</sub>(*n*) ≡ τ(*n*) mod 691. If *f* is the homomorphism taking (*c*,*d*) to *c* in **Z**, then the congruence ideal is (691). So the congruence ideal describes the congruences between the forms *E*<sub>12</sub> and Δ.

:::
