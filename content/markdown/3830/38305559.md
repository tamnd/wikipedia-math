# Erica Flapan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erica Flapan"}

**Erica Flapan** (born August 14, 1956) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and the Lingurn H. Burkhead Professor of Mathematics at [Pomona College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona_College). She is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and former editor-in-chief of their monthly *[Notices](wikipedia://en/4977512)* journal.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Flapan did her undergraduate studies at [Hamilton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton_College_%28New_York%29), graduating in 1977,[^o7][^o13] and went on to graduate studies at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), earning a Ph.D. in 1983 under the supervision of Daniel McMillan.

After postdoctoral studies at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) and the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara) she joined the Pomona faculty in 1986.[^o7][^o13] Flapan's research is in [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774) and [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008).

## Books {#Books}

Flapan is the author or coauthor of books including:

- *[When Topology Meets Chemistry: A Topological Look at Molecular Chirality](wikipedia://en/64789278)* (Cambridge University Press and Mathematical Association of America, 2000)[^o9]
- *Number Theory: A Lively Introduction with Proofs, Applications, and Stories* (with James Pommersheim and Tim Marks, John Wiley & Sons, 2010)[^o10]
- *Knots, Molecules, and the Universe: An Introduction to Topology* (2015)[^o11]

Her [edited volumes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edited_volume) include *Applications of Knot Theory* (with Dorothy Buck, 2009), *Knots, Links, Spatial Graphs, and Algebraic Invariants* (with [Allison Henrich](wikipedia://en/63124039), Aaron Kaestner, and Sam Nelson, 2017), and *Topology and Geometry of Biopolymers* (with Helen Wong, 2018).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2011, Flapan was one of three winners of the [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics), from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o13][^o14] In 2012 she became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822),[^o15] and in the same year as part of the bicentennial of Hamilton College was honored with a Hamilton Alumni Achievement Medal.[^o16] In recognition of her devotion to mentoring, Flapan won the [M. Gweneth Humphreys Award](wikipedia://en/51370132) from the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) in 2018.[^o17] She delivered the Chan Stanek Lecture for Students at [MathFest](wikipedia://en/14984993) 2021.[^o18]
