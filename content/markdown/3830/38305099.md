# Marston Conder

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marston Conder"}

**Marston Donald Edward Conder** (born 9 September 1955) is a New Zealand mathematician, a distinguished professor of mathematics at [Auckland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland_University),[^o3] and the former co-director of the New Zealand Institute of Mathematics and its Applications. His main research interests are in [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), and their connections with each other.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Conder was born in [Hamilton, New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton,_New_Zealand), and studied at [Matamata College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matamata_College).[^o5] He earned a master's degree in social science from [Waikato University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waikato_University) in 1977, and a doctorate from [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) in 1980 under the supervision of [Graham Higman](wikipedia://en/2600152).[^o5][^o6] He served as president of the New Zealand Mathematical Society from 1993 to 1995,[^o6] and as president of the Academy of the Royal Society of New Zealand from 2006 to 2008. In 2011 he was selected as the inaugural Maclaurin Lecturer, as part of a reciprocal exchange between the [New Zealand Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Mathematical_Society) and the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Conder is a fellow of the New Zealand Mathematical Society and of the [Royal Society Te Apārangi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_Te_Apārangi),[^o6] and in 2012 became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] He was named a distinguished alumnus of Waikato University for 2013.[^o11] In 2011 he was awarded a [James Cook Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Cook_Research_Fellowship) for research on symmetry of discrete structures.[^o12][^o13]

In March 2021 it was announced that Conder has been awarded the 2020 Euler Medal by the [Institute of Combinatorics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/17545919)[^o14] for his "many distinguished contributions to combinatorics over the last 40 years."

In 2014 the Royal Society Te Apārangi awarded Conder the [Hector Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hector_Medal), and in 2018 the Jones Medal, named after [Vaughan Jones](wikipedia://en/1227628), "for his internationally renowned research on symmetry and chirality in discrete structures, and his exemplary leadership and service in the New Zealand mathematical sciences community".[^o15] In the [2020 Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2020_Birthday_Honours_%28New_Zealand%29), he was appointed an Officer of the [New Zealand Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit) (ONZM) for services to mathematics.[^o16]
