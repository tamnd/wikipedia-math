# Block reflector

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Block reflector"}

"A **block reflector** is an orthogonal, symmetric [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) that reverses a subspace whose dimension may be greater than one."[^o2]

:::

It is built out of many [elementary reflectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_reflector).

It is also referred to as a [triangular factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_factor), and is a [triangular matrix](wikipedia://en/374222) and they are used in the [Householder transformation](wikipedia://en/485424).

A reflector $Q$ belonging to $\mathcal M_n(\mathbb{R})$ can be written in the form : $Q = I -auu^T$ where $I$ is the [identity matrix](wikipedia://en/59718) for $\mathcal M_n(\mathbb{R})$, $a$ is a [scalar](wikipedia://en/3588331) and $u$ belongs to $\mathbb{R}^n$ .

## LAPACK routines {#LAPACK_routines}

Here are some of the [LAPACK](wikipedia://en/1013768) routines that apply to block reflectors

- "\*larft" forms the triangular vector T of a block reflector H=I-VTVH.
- "\*larzb" applies a block reflector or its [transpose](wikipedia://en/173844)/[conjugate transpose](wikipedia://en/217548) as returned by "\*tzrzf" to a general [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859).
- "\*larzt" forms the triangular vector T of a block reflector H=I-VTVH as returned by "\*tzrzf".
- "\*larfb" applies a block reflector or its [transpose](wikipedia://en/173844)/[conjugate transpose](wikipedia://en/217548) to a general rectangular matrix.
