# BEST theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="BEST theorem"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a part of [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), the **BEST theorem** gives a product formula for the number of [Eulerian circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eulerian_circuit) in [directed](wikipedia://en/19721986) (oriented) [graphs](wikipedia://en/325806). The name is an acronym of the names of people who discovered it: [N. G. de Bruijn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._G._de_Bruijn), [Tatyana van Aardenne-Ehrenfest](wikipedia://en/3969942), [Cedric Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedric_Smith_%28statistician%29) and [W. T. Tutte](wikipedia://en/52021).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Precise statement"}

## Precise statement {#Precise_statement}

Let *G* = (*V*, *E*) be a directed graph. An Eulerian circuit is a directed closed trail that visits each edge exactly once. In 1736, [Euler](wikipedia://en/17902) showed that *G* has an Eulerian circuit if and only if *G* is [connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connected_graph) and the [indegree](wikipedia://en/19721986#Indegree_and_outdegree) is equal to [outdegree](wikipedia://en/19721986#Indegree_and_outdegree) at every vertex. In this case *G* is called Eulerian. We denote the indegree of a vertex *v* by deg(*v*).

The BEST theorem states that the number ec(*G*) of Eulerian circuits in a connected Eulerian graph *G* is given by the formula

$$\operatorname{ec}(G) = t_w(G) \prod_{v\in V} \bigl(\deg(v)-1\bigr)!.$$

Here *t*<sub>*w*</sub>(*G*) is the number of [arborescences](wikipedia://en/13251077), which are [trees](wikipedia://en/48560) directed towards the root at a fixed vertex *w* in *G*. The number *t<sub>w</sub>(G)* can be computed as a [determinant](wikipedia://en/8468), by the version of the [matrix tree theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matrix_tree_theorem) for directed graphs. It is a property of Eulerian graphs that *t*<sub>*v*</sub>(*G*) = *t*<sub>*w*</sub>(*G*) for every two vertices *v* and *w* in a connected Eulerian graph *G*.

:::

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

The BEST theorem shows that the number of Eulerian circuits in directed graphs can be computed in [polynomial time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_time), a problem which is [\#P-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharp-P-complete) for undirected graphs.[^o6] It is also used in the asymptotic enumeration of Eulerian circuits of [complete](wikipedia://en/85816) and [complete bipartite graphs](wikipedia://en/525320).[^o7][^o8]

:::

## History {#History}

The BEST theorem is due to van Aardenne-Ehrenfest and de Bruijn (1951),[^o10] §6, Theorem 6. Their proof is [bijective](wikipedia://en/917006) and generalizes the [de Bruijn sequences](wikipedia://en/1565267). In a "note added in proof", they refer to an earlier result by Smith and Tutte (1941) which proves the formula for graphs with deg(v)=2 at every vertex.
