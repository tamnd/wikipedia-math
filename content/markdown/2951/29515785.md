# MAOL table book

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="MAOL table book"}

***MAOL tables*** (MAOL-taulukot, MAOLs tabeller) is a [*reference handbook*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reference_work) published by MAOL, the [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland) association for teachers of mathematical subjects, and distributed by [Otava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otava_%28publisher%29) in both printed and digital forms. It is a book of numeric tables to aid in studying [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at the [gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29) level. The book includes a list of mathematical notation and symbols, scientific units and constants, a diverse collection of formulae, and several numeric tables. The Finnish Matriculation Examination Board has accepted the book and allowed it to be used in the Finnish [matriculation examinations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matriculation_exam_%28Finland%29). From 2020 onwards, only the digital version has been allowed, and it is included for free in the digital examination environment, [Abitti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitti).

:::

The colour of the cover of the book is changed with each edition of the book.
