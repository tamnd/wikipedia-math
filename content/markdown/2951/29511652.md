# Semi-infinite

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Semi-infinite"}

In mathematics, **semi-infinite** objects are objects which are [infinite](wikipedia://en/20110824) or [unbounded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unbounded_set) in some but not all possible ways.

:::

## In ordered structures and Euclidean spaces {#In_ordered_structures_and_Euclidean_spaces}

Generally, a semi-infinite set is [bounded](wikipedia://en/48258) in one direction, and [unbounded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unbounded_set) in another. For instance, the [natural numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_numbers) are semi-infinite considered as a subset of the integers; similarly, the [intervals](wikipedia://en/49172) $(c,\infty)$ and $(-\infty,c)$ and their closed counterparts are semi-infinite subsets of $\mathbb{R}$ if $c$ is finite.[^o3] [Half-spaces](wikipedia://en/647297) and [half-lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Half-line_%28geometry%29) are sometimes described as semi-infinite regions.

Semi-infinite regions occur frequently in the study of [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations).[^o4][^o5] For instance, one might study solutions of the heat equation in an idealised semi-infinite metal bar.

A semi-infinite [integral](wikipedia://en/15532) is an [improper integral](wikipedia://en/346030) over a semi-infinite interval. More generally, objects indexed or parametrised by semi-infinite sets may be described as semi-infinite.[^o6]

Most forms of semi-infiniteness are [boundedness](wikipedia://en/48258) properties, not [cardinality](wikipedia://en/6174) or [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) properties: semi-infinite sets are typically infinite in cardinality and measure.

## In optimization {#In_optimization}

Many [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29) problems involve some set of variables and some set of constraints. A problem is called semi-infinite if one (but not both) of these sets is finite. The study of such problems is known as [semi-infinite programming](wikipedia://en/17597677).[^o8]
