# László Fuchs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="László Fuchs"}

**László Fuchs** (born June 24, 1924) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary)-born American mathematician, the Evelyn and John G. Phillips Distinguished Professor Emeritus in Mathematics at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University).[^o2] He is known for his research and textbooks in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384).[^o3][^o4]

*Image: Laszlo\_fuchs.jpg.* Fuchs in 1975

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fuchs was born on June 24, 1924, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest),[^o21] into an academic family: his father was a linguist and a member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o21] He earned a bachelor's degree in 1946 and a doctorate in 1947 from [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University).[^o21] After teaching high school mathematics for two years, and then holding positions at Eötvös Loránd, the [Mathematical Research Institute of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences](wikipedia://en/31714670), and the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami), he joined the Tulane faculty in 1968.[^o21][^o2] At Tulane, Fuchs chaired the mathematics department from 1977 to 1979.[^o21] He retired in 2004.[^o7]

In 2004, Fuchs was honored at the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) 80th anniversary as one of the "big five" most distinguished Hungarian mathematicians. The other honorees were [John Horvath](wikipedia://en/65971885), [János Aczél](wikipedia://en/22717925), [Ákos Császár](wikipedia://en/22603037) and [Steven Gaal](wikipedia://en/24774553).[^o8] Fuchs has nearly 100 [academic descendants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_genealogy), many of them through his student at Eötvös Loránd, [George Grätzer](wikipedia://en/42456445).[^o6] He was treasurer of the [János Bolyai Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22777615) from 1949 until 1963, and secretary-general of the society from 1963 to 1966.[^o21]

Fuchs [turned 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centenarian) on June 24, 2024.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Fuchs won the [Kossuth Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kossuth_Prize) in 1953.[^o21] He is a foreign member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o21] Two conferences were dedicated to him on the occasion of his 70th birthday, and another on his 75th.[^o21][^o3][^o4]

At Tulane University, Fuchs held the W. R. Irby Professorship from 1979 to 1992, and the Evelyn and John G. Phillips Distinguished Professorship from then until his retirement.[^o21]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o22]
