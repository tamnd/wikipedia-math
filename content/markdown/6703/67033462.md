# Ariane Mézard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ariane Mézard"}

**Ariane Mézard** is a French mathematician and a professor of mathematics at [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) who works in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Mézard studied at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon) from 1992 to 1996.[^o4][^o5] She received her Ph.D. under the supervision of Roland Gillard at [Joseph Fourier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University) in 1998.[^o5] She received her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2005 during her time at [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Mézard worked as a postdoc at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) and [Regensburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regensburg_University) in 1999 and 2000 respectively.[^o4][^o5] She was an assistant professor at Paris-Sud University from 2000 to 2006 and also served in the same role at [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) from 2005 to 2006.[^o4][^o5] From 2006 to 2012, she was a professor at [Versailles Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Versailles_Saint-Quentin-en-Yvelines_University).[^o4][^o5] Since 2012, Mézard has been a professor of mathematics at [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University).[^o4] Since 2016, she has worked as a part-time professor at the École normale supérieure.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

From 2010 to 2015, Mézard was a junior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) (IUF).[^o4][^o5] At the time of her appointment to the IUF, she was one of only two women out of its fifty mathematician members.[^o5] In 2018, she received the Fulbright for the Future Prize from the [Fulbright Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Association).[^o4][^o9]
