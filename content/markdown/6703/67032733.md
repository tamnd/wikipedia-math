# Giuseppe Arbia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giuseppe Arbia"}

**Giuseppe Arbia** (born July 3, 1958) is an Italian statistician. He is known for his contributions to the field of [spatial statistics](wikipedia://en/9467349) and [spatial econometrics](wikipedia://en/28544909). In 2006 together with [Jean Paelinck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Paelinck) he founded the Spatial Econometrics Association, which he has been chairing ever since.[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Giuseppe Arbia earned his bachelor's degree cum laude from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) in 1981, and the Doctor of Philosophy from [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) in 1987. In 1994 he become full professor. He currently holds the chair of Economic Statistics at the [Catholic University of the Sacred Heart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_the_Sacred_Heart) in Milan[^o4] and he is also Lecturer at the [University of Italian Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Italian_Switzerland) in Lugano.[^o5] He is the Leading Editor of the book series Spatial Statistics and Spatial Econometrics, by Elsevier, Editor-in-Chief of the Journal of Spatial Econometrics, by Springer-Verlag and Director of the Spatial Econometrics Advanced Institute. In his career he published 8 books and more than 200 articles, book chapters and reviews.[^o6] He is credited with coining [Arbia's law of geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arbia's_law_of_geography), also known as the second law of geography.[^o7][^o8][^o9]
