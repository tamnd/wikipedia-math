# Witold Roter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Witold Roter"}

**Witold Kazimierz Roter** (September 20, 1932 – June 19, 2015, in [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław).[^o2][^o3]) was a mathematician, of the [Polish School of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3008223),[^o4] expert in differential geometry.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Witold Kazimierz Roter was born on September 20, 1932, in Zabrze-Pawłów. He attended primary and then secondary school in Zabrze, and next, he studied mathematics at the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University) – 1st degree studies in 1950–1953 and 2nd degree studies (extramural) in 1955–1958. In 1958 he obtained a master's degree in mathematics. In 1953, after completing the first cycle of studies, he was ordered to work as a teacher at the Nowa Ruda Primary School No. 1 and in the Secondary School. He worked there until 1961.[^o7] In the same year, he was accepted as a senior assistant at the Faculty of Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry of the University of Wrocław.

Witold Kazimierz Roter died on June 19, 2015, in [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław). He is buried at [Osobowice Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osobowice_Cemetery) in [Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław)

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

In 1963 at the [Wrocław University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University) he defended his doctoral thesis "On certain properties of recursive and weak-recursive spaces" written under the supervision of [Władysław Ślebodziński](wikipedia://en/1983028), and in 1974 he obtained his habilitation. He was awarded the title of professor of mathematical sciences in 1992. He was an employee of the Mathematical Institute of the [University of Wrocław](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław_University), where he was deputy director of the Mathematical Institute, vice-dean of the Faculty of Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry, and for many years he headed the Department of Geometry,[^o10] and the Institute of Mathematics of the Wrocław University of Technology.

In the years 1989–1991 he was the president of the Wrocław Branch of [Polish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2121791), and in the years 1996–2004 the president of the Scholarship Foundation of Wrocław Mathematicians.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Andrzej Derdiński writes about his contribution to the development of differential geometry.[^o12][^o13][^o14] Since 1961 he published more than 36 scientific papers. A summary of his most important works can be found in Mathematical Reviews[^o15] and Zentralblatt fur Mathematik.[^o16]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

He was awarded the Gold Cross of Merit and the Knight's Cross of the Order of Polonia Restituta. In 1983 he was awarded the Tadeusz Ważewski Prize[^o18]

June 2, 2017, at the Secondary School im. Henryk Sienkiewicz in [Nowa Ruda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nowa_Ruda), a plaque dedicated to the professor was unveiled, and an obelisk with a commemorative plaque was unveiled in the square at the Market Square, which was named after Roter.
