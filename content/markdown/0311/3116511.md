# Vlastimil Pták

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vlastimil Pták"}

**Vlastimil Pták** (; 8 November 1925 in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague) – 5 May 1999) was a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechs) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He worked in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), theoretical [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), and [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422). Notable early work include generalizations of the [open mapping theorem](wikipedia://en/17395276).[^o2]

:::

During 1945–1949, Vlastimil Pták studied mathematics and physics at the [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in Prague. Later, he worked at the university and since 1952 in Mathematical Institute of [Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovak_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1965 he was named professor at the Charles University. He has published more than 160 mathematical research papers. He had three Ph.D. students: [Nicholas Young](wikipedia://en/58269490), Michal Zajac and Miroslav Engliš.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *On matrices with non-positive off-diagonal elements and positive principal minors.* (with Miroslav Fiedler) Czechoslovak Math. J. 12 (87) 1962 382–400.
