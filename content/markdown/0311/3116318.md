# Cartan formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cartan formula"}

The **Cartan formula** in mathematics may refer to two different formulae in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) or [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=named-heading name="Cartan formula in differential geometry"}

## Cartan formula in differential geometry {#Cartan_formula_in_differential_geometry}

The Cartan formula in differential geometry states:

$\mathcal L_X = \mathrm d \, \iota_X + \iota_X \mathrm d$, where $\mathcal L_X, \mathrm d$, and $\iota_X$ are [Lie derivative](wikipedia://en/294437), [exterior derivative](wikipedia://en/220782), and [interior product](wikipedia://en/2154963), respectively, acting on [differential forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_forms).

It is also called the **Cartan homotopy formula** or **Cartan magic formula**. This formula is named after [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=named-heading name="Cartan formula in algebraic topology"}

## Cartan formula in algebraic topology {#Cartan_formula_in_algebraic_topology}

The Cartan formula in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) is one of the five axioms of Steenrod algebra.[^o6] It reads:

$$\begin{aligned} Sq^n(x \smile y) & = \sum_{i+j=n} (Sq^i x) \smile (Sq^j y) \quad \text{or} \\ P^n(x \smile y) & = \sum_{i+j=n} (P^i x) \smile (P^j y) \end{aligned}.$$

The name derives from [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536), son of Élie.

:::
