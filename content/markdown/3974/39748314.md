# Surindar Kumar Trehan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Surindar Kumar Trehan"}

**Surindar Kumar Trehan** (**S.K. Trehan**) was an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specialised in [non-linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-linear) [stability](wikipedia://en/5493795) in [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310).

:::

He was awarded in 1976 the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in India, in the mathematical sciences category. Prof. Trehan has done significant work on stability of force-free magnetic fields, stability of jets and cylinders and stability of inhomogeneous plasmas. His work on the mathematical treatment of gaseous polytropes in the presence of a magnetic field is a breakthrough in this area. He has also done important work on hydromagnetic waves and rotating gaseous masses.[^o3]

|  | Surindar Kumar Trehan obtained [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (1958) from [University of Chicago, USA.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) He was [Dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29), Science Faculty, and CSIR Professor Emeritus, [Department of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/10387568), both at [Punjab University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panjab_University), [Chandigarh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandigarh) (1980–82, 1989). **Academic and Research Achievements:** Trehan made significant contributions to our understanding of the stability of force- free [magnetic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_fields), of [jets and cylinders](wikipedia://en/859283), and of [inhomogenous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inhomogeneous_cosmology) plasmas. His work on the mathematical treatment of [gaseous polytropes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas) in the presence of a magnetic field was noted with keen interest. He also made contributions to the study of hydromagnetic waves and rotating gaseous masses. He carried out nonlinear studies in [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310). He provided, for the first time, a consistent account of the [modulational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulatory_space) stability of bounded systems taking into account the [boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boundary_%28thermodynamic%29) conditions consistently. **Other Contributions:** Dr Trehan served on many expert committees of [CSIR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Council_of_Scientific_and_Industrial_Research), [UGC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_Grants_Commission_%28India%29), etc.; [Governing Council of Indian Statistical Institute and Indian Association for the Cultivation of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Governing_Council_of_Indian_Statistical_Institute_and_Indian_Association_for_the_Cultivation_of_Science) (The links indicated in red refers to " not created yet"); and Member, [INSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_and_National_Security_Alliance) Council (1977–79). He was also Editor, *[Bulletin of the Astronomical Society of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_of_the_Astronomical_Society_of_India)* (1980–91); Member, Editorial boards of: *Journal of [Astrophysics and Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysics),* and some other journals; and Editor of INSA Publications (1979–82). **Awards and Honours:** Dr Trehan received the [SS Bhatnagar Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) (1976); [BC Roy Award \`Eminent Person in Sciences’](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._B._C._Roy_Award) (1989); [UGC National Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UGC_National_Lecturer) (1977–78); and [UGC National Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UGC_National_Fellow) (1983). He was elected Fellow of the Indian Academy of Sciences, [Bangalore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangalore) and [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) (India), [Allahabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allahabad); Chairman, [National Committee for the International Astronomical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Committee_for_the_International_Astronomical_Union) (1978–81); Vice President, [Astronomical Society of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomical_Society_of_India) (1976–78); and President, Mathematics Section, [Indian Science Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Science_Congress_Association) (1984). <sup>[\[2\]](https://www.insaindia.res.in/detail.php?id=N76-0843)</sup> |
