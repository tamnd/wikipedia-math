# Elliptic cone

*Image: Elliptical Cone Quadric.Png.* An elliptical cone quadric surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elliptic cone"}

An **elliptical cone** is a [cone](wikipedia://en/782427) with an [elliptical](wikipedia://en/9277) base.[^o2] It is a generalization of the *[circular cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_cone)* and a special case of the *[generalized cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_cone)*.

:::

The term might refer to the [solid figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid_figure) bounded by the base or only to the lateral [conic surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conic_surface), a [quadric](wikipedia://en/145570) called *conical quadric* or *quadratic cone*.[^o3][^o4]

In a [three-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-dimensional) [Cartesian coordinate system](wikipedia://en/7706), an elliptic cone is the [locus](wikipedia://en/542465) of an equation of the form:[^o5]

$$\frac{x^2}{a^2} + \frac{y^2}{b^2} = z^2 .$$

It is an [affine image](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_map) of the unit [right circular cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_circular_cone) with equation $x^2+y^2=z^2\ .$ From the fact that the affine image of a [conic section](wikipedia://en/19008673) is a conic section of the same type (ellipse, [parabola](wikipedia://en/23231), etc.), any [plane section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_section) of an elliptic cone is a conic section (see [Circular section#Elliptic cone](wikipedia://en/54435709#Elliptic_cone)).

The intersection curve of an elliptic cone with a concentric sphere is a *[spherical conic](wikipedia://en/68189121)*.
