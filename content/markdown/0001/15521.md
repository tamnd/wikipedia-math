# Devanagari numerals

*Image: देवनागरी अंक.jpg.* Devanagari numerals with their Western Arabic counterparts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Devanagari numerals"}

The **Devanagari numerals** are the symbols used to write numbers in the [Devanagari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Devanagari) script, predominantly used for northern Indian languages. They are used to write [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214) numbers, instead of the [Western Arabic numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Arabic_numerals).

:::

In modern-era, languages like Hindi, Marathi and Nepali have adopted Devanagari as the standard script, before which they were respectively written using [Kaithi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaithi_script), [Modi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modi_script) and [Newari scripts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newari_scripts).

The word śūnya for zero was [calqued](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calque) into [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic_language) as صفر sifr, meaning 'nothing', which became the term "zero" in many European languages via [Medieval Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medieval_Latin) zephirum.[^o6] In [Hindustani language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindustani_language), it was borrowed from Arabic (via Persian) as सिफ़र (sifar).

## Variants {#Variants}

section

*Image: Sanskrit\_Arabic\_numerals.jpg.* A comparison of Sanskrit and [Eastern Arabic numerals](wikipedia://en/3655571)

Devanagari digits shapes may vary depending on geographical area or epoch. Some of the variants are also seen in older Sanskrit literature.[^o9][^o10]

::: {#o11 .table cue=table}

| १ | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 1 var 1.png.* Common | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 1 var 2.png.* Nepali | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| ५ | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 5 var 1.png.* "Bombay" Variant | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 5 var 2.png.* "Calcutta" Variant | 5 |
| ८ | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 8 var 1.png.* "Bombay" Variant | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 8 var 2.png.* "Calcutta" Variant | 8 |
| ९ | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 9 var 1.png.* Common | *Image: Devanagari Numeral 9 var 2.png.* Nepali Variant | 9 |

:::

In Nepali language **५, ८, ९** (5, 8, 9) - these numbers are slightly different from modern Devanagari numbers. The Nepali language uses the old Devanagari system for writing these numbers, like **५, ८, ९**

:::
