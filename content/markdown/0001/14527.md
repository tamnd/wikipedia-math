# Institut des hautes études scientifiques

*Image: IHES main building.jpg.* IHÉS main building

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institut des hautes études scientifiques"}

The **Institut des hautes études scientifiques** (**IHÉS**; English: Institute of Advanced Scientific Studies) is a French [research institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_institute) supporting advanced research in mathematics and [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028) (also with a small theoretical biology group). It is located in [Bures-sur-Yvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bures-sur-Yvette), just south of [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). It is an independently governed research institute and a founding member of the [University of Paris-Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Saclay).

:::

## History {#History}

The IHÉS was founded in 1958 by businessman and mathematical physicist [Léon Motchane](wikipedia://en/39434117) with the help of [Robert Oppenheimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Oppenheimer) and [Jean Dieudonné](wikipedia://en/1070084) as a research centre in France, modeled on the renowned [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, United States.[^o3]

IHÉS deeply draws inspiration from the model of the esteemed Institute for Advanced Study in Princeton (IAS). ... IHÉS adopted its basic characteristics – the intellectual ones – as a high level institute dedicated to basic research – and certain practical ones – such as the tradition of tea being served daily, conducive to exchanges of views. Of equal importance was the relationship between Oppenheimer and Léon Motchane (1900–1990), the founder and first director of IHÉS. As life member of the scientific committee, advisor to the director, frequent visitor and regular correspondent, Oppenheimer played a major role in the crucial early years at IHÉS. ... Oppenheimer and Motchane ... exchanged long letters and short telegrams on all aspects of life at IHÉS. ... Visits by Oppenheimer were cleverly orchestrated by Léon Motchane and reported in the press. Oppenheimer's presence was an event in itself and his coming to lHÉS gave the Institute important official recognition. Oppenheimer was to come to IHÉS three times: from 16 to 19 September 1959, on 17 and 18 October 1961 (in Bures, although the Institute had not moved there yet), and from 14 to 17 May 1963. His last visit, planned for 1965, was cancelled for health reasons.[^o4]

The strong personality of [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042) and the broad sweep of his revolutionizing theories were a dominating feature of the first ten years at the IHÉS.[^o5] [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633) received an invitation from IHÉS in 1963 and, after his appointment, remained there until his death in 2002.[^o6] [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608) is remembered as one who had a special talent for encouraging exchanges among visitors and provoking new and deeper insights into their ideas.

The IHÉS runs a highly regarded mathematical journal, *[Publications Mathématiques de l'IHÉS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publications_Mathématiques_de_l'IHÉS)*.

IHÉS celebrated its 40th anniversary in 1998[^o7] and its 50th in 2008.[^o3][^o8]

## Scientists associated with the IHES {#Scientists_associated_with_the_IHES}

[Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340) (Fields Medal 1982) has held the Léon Motchane Chair since 1979. Several [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) researchers are also based at the IHES: [Ahmed Abbes](wikipedia://en/66166683), [Thierry Baudineau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thierry_Baudineau), [Ofer Gabber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ofer_Gabber), [Fanny Kassel](wikipedia://en/58131166) and [Christophe Soulé](wikipedia://en/32705286) in mathematics and [Clément Delcamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clément_Delcamp) in physics. [Yilin Wang](wikipedia://en/78198484) is a junior professor.[^o36]
