# List of mathematics competitions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of mathematics competitions"}

**Mathematics competitions** or **mathematical olympiads** are competitive events where participants complete a [math](wikipedia://en/18831) test. These tests may require multiple choice or numeric answers, or a detailed written solution or proof.

:::

## International mathematics competitions {#International_mathematics_competitions}

::: {#o3 .entry cue=list-entry name="Championnat International de Jeux Mathématiques et Logiques"}

- [Championnat International de Jeux Mathématiques et Logiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Championnat_International_de_Jeux_Mathématiques_et_Logiques) — for all ages, mainly for French-speaking countries, but participation is not limited by language.

:::

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="China Girls Mathematical Olympiad"}

- [China Girls Mathematical Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China_Girls_Mathematical_Olympiad) (CGMO) — held annually for teams of girls representing different regions within China and a few other countries.

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad"}

- [European Girls' Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/36583162) (EGMO) — since April 2012.

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Integration Bee"}

- [Integration Bee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integration_Bee) — competition in integral calculus held in various institutions of higher learning in the United States and some other countries.

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="International Mathematical Modeling Challenge"}

- [International Mathematical Modeling Challenge](wikipedia://en/48157955) — team contest for high school students.

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="International Mathematical Olympiad"}

- [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (IMO) — the oldest international Olympiad, occurring annually since 1959.

:::

::: {#o9 .entry cue=list-entry name="International Mathematics Competition for University Students"}

- [International Mathematics Competition for University Students](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematics_Competition_for_University_Students) (IMC) — international competition for undergraduate students.

:::

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Mathematical Contest in Modeling"}

- [Mathematical Contest in Modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Contest_in_Modeling) (MCM) — team contest for undergraduates.

:::

::: {#o11 .entry cue=list-entry name="Mathematical Kangaroo"}

- [Mathematical Kangaroo](wikipedia://en/1640947) — worldwide competition.

:::

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Mental Calculation World Cup"}

- [Mental Calculation World Cup](wikipedia://en/4663849) — contest for the best mental calculators.

:::

::: {#o13 .entry cue=list-entry name="Primary Mathematics World Contest"}

- [Primary Mathematics World Contest](wikipedia://en/885310) (PMWC) — worldwide competition.

:::

::: {#o14 .entry cue=list-entry name="Rocket City Math League"}

- [Rocket City Math League](wikipedia://en/20228317) (RCML) — Competition run by students at Virgil I. Grissom High School with levels ranging from Explorer (Pre-Algebra) to Discovery (Comprehensive).

:::

::: {#o15 .entry cue=list-entry name="Romanian Master of Mathematics and Sciences"}

- [Romanian Master of Mathematics and Sciences](wikipedia://en/28088480) — Olympiad for the selection of the top 20 countries in the last IMO.

:::

::: {#o16 .entry cue=list-entry name="Tournament of the Towns"}

- [Tournament of the Towns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tournament_of_the_Towns) — worldwide competition based in Russia.

:::

- The [SUPERTMATIK](https://www.eudactica.com/en-gb/stk_en) Mental Math International Competition — worldwide competition, Grades 1 - 9.
- [World Mathematics Invitational](https://wminv.org) — for all grade levels K-12, Taiwanese-based international mathematics competition, with English and Chinese as the official languages of the papers.
