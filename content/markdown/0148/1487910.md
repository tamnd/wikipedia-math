# Schwarz–Ahlfors–Pick theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Schwarz–Ahlfors–Pick theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Schwarz–Ahlfors–Pick** theorem is an extension of the [Schwarz lemma](wikipedia://en/1432589) for [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), such as the [Poincaré half-plane model](wikipedia://en/461942).

:::

The [Schwarz–Pick lemma](wikipedia://en/1432589#Schwarz.E2.80.93Pick_theorem) states that every [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110) from the [unit disk](wikipedia://en/252738) *U* to itself, or from the [upper half-plane](wikipedia://en/287826) *H* to itself, will not increase the Poincaré distance between points. The unit disk *U* with the Poincaré metric has negative [Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623) −1. In 1938, [Lars Ahlfors](wikipedia://en/61636) generalised the lemma to maps from the unit disk to other negatively curved surfaces:

::: {#o2 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=inline-head name="Theorem"}

**Theorem** ([Schwarz](wikipedia://en/3165834)–[Ahlfors](wikipedia://en/61636)–[Pick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Alexander_Pick)). Let *U* be the unit disk with Poincaré metric $\rho$; let *S* be a [Riemann surface](wikipedia://en/173181) endowed with a [Hermitian metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermitian_metric) $\sigma$ whose [Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623) is ≤ −1; let $f:U\rightarrow S$ be a [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110). Then

$$\sigma(f(z_1),f(z_2)) \leq \rho(z_1,z_2)$$

for all $z_1,z_2 \in U.$

A generalization of this theorem was proved by [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137) in 1973.[^o3]

:::
