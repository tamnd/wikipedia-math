# Rolandas Pavilionis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rolandas Pavilionis"}

**Rolandas Pavilionis** (3 July 1944 – 10 May 2006) was a Lithuanian academic and politician who was a [Member of the European Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_European_Parliament) from 2004 to 2006 as a member of the [Liberal Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Democratic_Party_%28Lithuania%29); part of the [Union for a Europe of Nations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_for_a_Europe_of_Nations). He previously served as rector of [Vilnius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University) from 1990 to 2000, and as a member of the [Seimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seimas) from 2000 to 2004.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rolandas Pavilionis was born on 3 July 1944 in the western Lithuanian city of [Šiauliai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Šiauliai).[^o4] In 1962, he graduated from a night-school for workers in Šiauliai, attending the school at night after finishing his work at a bicycle factory. The same year he was admitted to [Vilnius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University) to study foreign languages. In 1968 he graduated the university with a degree in [philology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philology). From 1968 to 1971 he continued postgraduate studies at the [Academy of Sciences of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_Ukraine)[^o5] and earned a doctor's degree in logic. Pavilionis then returned to Lithuania and joined Vilnius University as member of the faculty (1971–1977 docent, 1982–1990 professor). For some time he was the head of the Department of History of Philosophy and Logic.

Pavilionis took courses at the [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) of Paris under [Algirdas Julius Greimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algirdas_Julius_Greimas). In 1981 Pavilionis defended his thesis in the field of language logic and philosophy of logic, earning degree of [Habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitated) Doctor of Logic.

In 1990 Pavilionis was elected as the rector of Vilnius University. He held the post until 2000, when was elected to [Seimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seimas) (parliament). In 2004, he was elected to the [European Parliament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Parliament).[^o6]

Pavilionis died at Santariškės hospital in Vilnius.[^o7]

## Works {#Works}

Pavilionis was the author of works in [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language), [Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_language) and [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language).

- „Язык и логика“, 1975,
- „Kalba. Logika. Filosofija“, Vilnius: Mintis, 1981.
- „Проблема смысла: современный логико-философский анализ языка“, Москва: Мысль, 1983.
- „Meaning and conceptual systems“, Moscow: Progress, 1990.
- „Prieš absurdą“, 2000.
- „Tarp šviesos ir tamsos“, 2000.
- „Prieš absurdą II“, 2004.
- „Prasmė ir tapatumas, arba kelionė į save“, Vilnius: Lietuvos mokslas, 2005.
