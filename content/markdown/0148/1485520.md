# Arnaud Denjoy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Arnaud Denjoy"}

**Arnaud Denjoy** (; 5 January 1884 – 21 January 1974) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Denjoy was born in [Auch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auch), [Gers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gers). His contributions include work in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). [His integral](wikipedia://en/412708) was the first to be able to integrate all [derivatives](wikipedia://en/7921). Among his students is [Gustave Choquet](wikipedia://en/17301193). He is also known for the more general [broad Denjoy integral, or Khinchin integral](wikipedia://en/37138377).

Denjoy was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) with talk *Sur une classe d'ensembles parfaits en relation avec les fonctions admettant une dérivée seconde généralisée* in 1920 at Strasbourg and with talk *Les equations differentielles periodiques* in 1950 at Cambridge, Massachusetts. In 1931 he was the president of the [Société Mathématique de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_Mathématique_de_France). In 1942 he was elected a member of the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences) and was its president in 1962.

Denjoy married in 1923 and was the father of three sons. He died in Paris in 1974. He was an atheist with a strong interest in philosophy, psychology, and social issues.[^o4]

The asteroid [(19349) Denjoy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%2819349%29_Denjoy) is named in his honor.
