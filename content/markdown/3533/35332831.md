# Klaus Kubinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Klaus Kubinger"}

*Image: Klaus Kubinger.JPG.* Klaus Kubinger in 2011

**Klaus D. Kubinger** (born May 25, 1949 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria)) is a psychologist as well as a statistician and has been until retirement professor for psychological assessment at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna), Faculty of Psychology. His main research work focuses on fundamental research of assessment processes and on application and advancement of [Item response theory models](wikipedia://en/420159) ([Rasch model](wikipedia://en/2161615)). He is also known as a textbook author of psychological assessment on the one hand and on statistics on the other hand.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Klaus D. Kubinger received 1973 the doctor's degree in psychology (PhD) in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria), and became assistant professor. In 1985 he achieved the postdoctoral lecture qualification of psychology. Since then he was head of the examination subject *psychological assessment* at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). In 1989 he graduated as a MSc in statistics. In 1998 he was promoted to a full professor of psychological assessment. From this time on, he has been head of the division of psychological assessment and applied [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982), which includes a *Center of Testing and Consulting*.

In 1997 he earned the qualification of a systemic [psychotherapist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychotherapy).

## Scientific Work {#Scientific_Work}

The beginning of Klaus D. Kubinger's career was characterized by applications of the [Rasch model](wikipedia://en/2161615) ([Item response theory](wikipedia://en/420159), IRT) on pertinent psychological tests.[^o4][^o5] Then he developed a non-parametric discriminant analysis[^o6] and an [analysis of variance](wikipedia://en/634) for a two-way layout for rank scaled data.[^o7] Later on, the intelligence test-battery AID (*Adaptives Intelligenz Diagnostikum*[^o8] has been established in German speaking countries and also exists in an English version (AID 3, *Adaptive Intelligence Diagnosticum*) since 2017.[^o9] This test-battery is based on (optionally either branched- or tailored) adaptive testing.[^o10] There have also been developed several objective personality tests sensu [R.B. Cattell](wikipedia://en/1145683).[^o11] Further on, his research work focused on fundamental research of assessment processes (fakeability of personality questionnaires,[^o12][^o13] guessing effects in multiple choice tests,[^o14][^o15] equivalency of computerized or in other cultures transposed tests[^o16][^o17]) and on the elaboration of self-assessments for university students’ counseling.[^o18] Besides this, he made, especially together with Dieter Rasch, contributions to statistics (for psychology),[^o19][^o20][^o21] in particular to sequential testing[^o22][^o23] and sample size planning.[^o24][^o25] Still he works on the advancement and application of Item response theory models, in particular on the Rasch model including the Linear logistic test model (LLTM): on problems of model testing[^o26][^o27][^o28][^o29][^o30] and problems of parameter estimation[^o31][^o32] as well as on specific generalizations[^o33][^o34][^o35] and on modelling the difficulty of various components of designing a task's item.[^o36][^o37][^o38][^o39]
