# Fatou conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fatou conjecture"}

In mathematics, the **Fatou conjecture**, named after [Pierre Fatou](wikipedia://en/629100), states that a quadratic family of maps from the complex plane to itself is hyperbolic for an open dense set of parameters.

:::
