# John Mueller Anderson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Mueller Anderson"}

**John Mueller Anderson** (29 July 1914 – 3 December 1999) was an American philosopher.[^o3] He was known for his expertise on [post-Kantian philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Post-Kantian_philosophy), [philosophy of art](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_art) and [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065).[^o4][^o5][^o6] Anderson was Evan Pugh Professor of Philosophy at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University).[^o7]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Anderson received a Bachelor of Arts (1935) and a Master of Arts (1936) from the University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign and a Doctor of Philosophy (1939) at the University of California, Berkeley.
