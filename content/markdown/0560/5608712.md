# Andrzej Białynicki-Birula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrzej Białynicki-Birula"}

**Andrzej Białynicki-Birula** (26 December 1935 – 19 April 2021) was a Polish mathematician, best known for his work on [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o2] He was considered one of the pioneers of [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587). He was a member of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences). Białynicki-Birula was married to a [paleontologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paleontologist) and [phylogeneticist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phylogeneticist) [Magdalena Borsuk-Białynicka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalena_Borsuk-Białynicka).[^o3]

:::

Białynicki-Birula was born in Nowogrodek, [Polish Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Polish_Republic), currently known as [Navahrudak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navahrudak), [West Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Belarus). His elder brother, [Iwo Białynicki-Birula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iwo_Białynicki-Birula), was born two years earlier and is a theoretical physicist and a fellow member of the Polish Academy of Sciences.[^o4][^o5]

Białynicki-Birula received his Ph.D. from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1960. His thesis was written under the direction of [Gerhard Hochschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerhard_Hochschild).[^o6] Since 1970, he was Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw), in 1977-1980 — Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics, Mechanics and Information; 1987-1990 — Vice Rector.[^o7]
