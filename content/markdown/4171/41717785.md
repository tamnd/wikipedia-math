# Webbed space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Webbed space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), particularly in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a **webbed space** is a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) designed with the goal of allowing the results of the [open mapping theorem](wikipedia://en/17395276) and the [closed graph theorem](wikipedia://en/64620077) to hold for a wider class of [linear maps](wikipedia://en/18102) whose codomains are webbed spaces. A space is called webbed if there exists a collection of [sets](wikipedia://en/26691), called a *web* that satisfies certain properties. Webs were first investigated by de Wilde.

:::

## Web {#Web}

Let $X$ be a [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [locally convex](wikipedia://en/518844) [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752). A **web** is a stratified collection of [disks](wikipedia://en/1734292) satisfying the following absorbency and convergence requirements.[^n1]

1. **Stratum 1**: The first stratum must consist of a sequence $D_{1}, D_{2}, D_{3}, \ldots$ of [disks](wikipedia://en/1734292) in $X$ such that their union $\bigcup_{i \in \mathbb{N}} D_i$ [absorbs](wikipedia://en/1728179) $X.$
1. **Stratum 2**: For each disk $D_i$ in the first stratum, there must exists a sequence $D_{i1}, D_{i2}, D_{i3}, \ldots$ of disks in $X$ such that for every $D_i$: $$D_{ij} \subseteq \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right) D_i \quad \text{ for every } j$$ and $\cup_{j \in \mathbb{N}} D_{ij}$ [absorbs](wikipedia://en/1728179) $D_i.$ The sets $\left(D_{ij}\right)_{i,j \in \mathbb{N}}$ will form the second stratum.
1. **Stratum 3**: To each disk $D_{ij}$ in the second stratum, assign another sequence $D_{ij1}, D_{ij2}, D_{ij3}, \ldots$ of disks in $X$ satisfying analogously defined properties; explicitly, this means that for every $D_{i,j}$: $$D_{ijk} \subseteq \left(\tfrac{1}{2}\right) D_{ij} \quad \text{ for every } k$$ and $\cup_{k \in \mathbb{N}} D_{ijk}$ [absorbs](wikipedia://en/1728179) $D_{ij}.$ The sets $\left(D_{ijk}\right)_{i,j,k \in \mathbb{N}}$ form the third stratum.

Continue this process to define strata $4, 5, \ldots.$ That is, use induction to define stratum $n + 1$ in terms of stratum $n.$

A **strand** is a sequence of disks, with the first disk being selected from the first stratum, say $D_i,$ and the second being selected from the sequence that was associated with $D_i,$ and so on. We also require that if a sequence of vectors $(x_n)$ is selected from a strand (with $x_1$ belonging to the first disk in the strand, $x_2$ belonging to the second, and so on) then the series $\sum_{n = 1}^{\infty} x_n$ converges.

A [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [locally convex topological vector space](wikipedia://en/518844) on which a web can be defined is called a **webbed space**.

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Theorem (de Wilde 1978)"}

**Theorem[^n2] (de Wilde 1978).**

A [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) $X$ is a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) if and only if it is both a webbed space and a [Baire space](wikipedia://en/200550).

:::

All of the following spaces are webbed:

- [Fréchet spaces](wikipedia://en/233801).[^n2]
- [Projective limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_limit) and [inductive limits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_limit) of sequences of webbed spaces.
- A sequentially closed vector subspace of a webbed space.[^n3]
- Countable [products](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_space) of webbed spaces.[^n3]
- A Hausdorff quotient of a webbed space.[^n3]
- The image of a webbed space under a [sequentially continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequentially_continuous) linear map if that image is [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637).[^n3]
- The [bornologification](wikipedia://en/1804457) of a webbed space.
- The continuous dual space of a metrizable locally convex space endowed with the [strong dual topology](wikipedia://en/63951372) is webbed.[^n2]
- If $X$ is the [strict inductive limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strict_inductive_limit) of a denumerable family of locally convex metrizable spaces, then the [continuous dual space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual_space) of $X$ with the [strong topology](wikipedia://en/63951372) is webbed.[^n4]
  - So in particular, the [strong duals](wikipedia://en/63951372) of locally convex [metrizable spaces](wikipedia://en/64278078) are webbed.[^n5]
- If $X$ is a webbed space, then any Hausdorff locally convex topology weaker than this (webbed) topology is also webbed.[^n3]

## Theorems {#Theorems}

::: {#o19 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Closed Graph Theorem"}

**Closed Graph Theorem[^n6].**

Let $A : X \to Y$ be a linear map between TVSs that is [sequentially closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequentially_closed) (meaning that its graph is a sequentially closed subset of $X \times Y$). If $Y$ is a webbed space and $X$ is an [ultrabornological space](wikipedia://en/63904912) (such as a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) or an inductive limit of Fréchet spaces), then $A$ is continuous.

:::

::: {#o20 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Closed Graph Theorem"}

**Closed Graph Theorem.**

Any closed linear map from the inductive limit of [Baire](wikipedia://en/200550) [locally convex spaces](wikipedia://en/518844) into a webbed locally convex space is continuous.

:::

::: {#o21 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Open Mapping Theorem"}

**Open Mapping Theorem.**

Any continuous surjective linear map from a webbed locally convex space onto an [inductive limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_limit) of Baire locally convex spaces is open.

:::

::: {#o23 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Open Mapping Theorem"}

**Open Mapping Theorem[^n6].**

Any continuous surjective linear map from a webbed locally convex space onto an [ultrabornological space](wikipedia://en/63904912) is open.

:::

::: {#o25 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Open Mapping Theorem"}

**Open Mapping Theorem[^n6].**

If the image of a closed linear operator $A : X \to Y$ from locally convex webbed space $X$ into Hausdorff locally convex space $Y$ is [nonmeager](wikipedia://en/226975) in $Y$ then $A : X \to Y$ is a surjective open map.

:::

If the spaces are not locally convex, then there is a notion of web where the requirement of being a disk is replaced by the requirement of being [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238). For such a notion of web we have the following results:

::: {#o26 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Closed Graph Theorem"}

**Closed Graph Theorem.**

Any closed linear map from the inductive limit of [Baire](wikipedia://en/200550) topological vector spaces into a webbed topological vector space is continuous.

:::
