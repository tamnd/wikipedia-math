# Mary Beisiegel

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mary Beisiegel"}

**Mary D. Beisiegel** is a Professor in the Department of Mathematics at [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University).[^o3] She is a Whiteley Faculty scholar for Teaching Excellence. Her research is in mathematics education.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Beisiegel received her PhD in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta) in 2009. Her dissertation, *Being (Almost) a Mathematician: Teacher Identity Formation in Post-Secondary Mathematics*, was supervised by Elaine Simmt, David Pimm, and Terrance Ronald Carson.[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Beisiegel received the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)'s [Henry L. Adler Award](wikipedia://en/65434512) in 2017.[^o7]
