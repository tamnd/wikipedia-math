# Jennifer Flegg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jennifer Flegg"}

**Jennifer A. Flegg** is an Australian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Flegg received her PhD in Applied Mathematics from [Queensland University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensland_University_of_Technology) in 2009. Her dissertation, "*Mathematical Modelling of Chronic Wound Healing",* was supervised by Dr. Sean McElwain.[^o5] From 2010 to 2013, she was a researcher at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) developing mathematical models for the spread of resistance to [antimalarial drugs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antimalarial_drug). From 2014 to early 2017, she was a mathematical lecturer in the School of Mathematical Sciences at [Monash University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monash_University). In May 2017, she joined the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Melbourne) as a senior lecturer in Applied Mathematics and was promoted to associate professor in 2020 and again to Professor in 2022.[^o6]

Flegg is an expert in the field of [mathematical biology](wikipedia://en/662088), with special focus in infectious disease [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997), [wound healing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wound_healing) and [tumor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neoplasm) growth.

As of 2020, Flegg also serves as an editorial board member for [PLOS Computational Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PLOS_Computational_Biology), [eLife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ELife) and the [Bulletin of Mathematical Biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_of_Mathematical_Biology).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2020, Flegg was awarded the JH Michell Medal for excellence in research by [ANZIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANZIAM) (Australian and New Zealand Industrial and Applied Mathematics),[^o8] as well as the Christopher Heyde Medal[^o9] from the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science) and the Society of Mathematical Biology. In 2025, Flegg was awarded the EO Tuck Medal by the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582) in recognition of her outstanding research and distinguished service to the field of applied mathematics.[^o10] She was elected a [Fellow of the Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fellows_of_the_Australian_Academy_of_Science) in 2026.[^o11]
