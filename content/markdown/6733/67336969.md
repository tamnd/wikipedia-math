# Tõnu Möls

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tõnu Möls"}

**Tõnu Möls** (12 June 1939 in [Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tartu) – 1 December 2019[^o2] ) was an [Estonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonia) mathematician and biologist.[^o3]

:::

In 1965 he described the moth *[Epirrhoe tartuensis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epirrhoe_tartuensis)*.[^o4]

From 1994 until 2004, he was the president of [Estonian Naturalists' Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonian_Naturalists'_Society).[^o3]

In 2001, he was awarded with [Order of the White Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_White_Star), V class.[^o5]
