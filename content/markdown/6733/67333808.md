# Anna Seigal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Seigal"}

**Anna Seigal** is a British mathematician who conducts research in [applied algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). She was awarded the 2020 [SIAM Richard C. DiPrima Prize](wikipedia://en/57164571)[^o3][^o4] and the Bernard Friedman Memorial Prize in Applied Mathematics.[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Seigal earned her bachelor's degree (BA honors) from [Emmanuel College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_College,_Cambridge), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 2013, scoring 1st class, top 10 in the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos). She earned her master's degree (MMath, 2014) there earning an honours pass with distinction on Part III of the Mathematical Tripos. Her Master's essay, *Iwasawa Theory of Elliptic Curves with Complex Multiplication,* was supervised by [John H. Coates](wikipedia://en/230705).

Seigal earned her PhD in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 2019.[^o7] Her dissertation, *Structured Tensors and the Geometry of Data*,[^o8] was supervised by [Bernd Sturmfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Sturmfels). It investigates the algebraic theory of tensors and algorithms for tensor data. At [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) she is a Junior Research Fellow in [The Queen's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen's_College,_Oxford) and Hooke Research Fellow in the Mathematical Institute.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

She was awarded the 2020 [SIAM Richard C. DiPrima Prize](wikipedia://en/57164571)[^o3][^o4] and the Bernard Friedman Memorial Prize in Applied Mathematics.[^o5]
