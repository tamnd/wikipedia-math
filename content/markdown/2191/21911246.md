# Cartan–Brauer–Hua theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cartan–Brauer–Hua theorem"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), the **Cartan–Brauer–Hua theorem** (named after [Richard Brauer](wikipedia://en/605134), [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274), and [Hua Luogeng](wikipedia://en/2337582)) is a theorem pertaining to [division rings](wikipedia://en/9067). It says that given two division rings *K* ⊆ *D* such that *xKx*<sup>−1</sup> is contained in *K* for every *x* not equal to 0 in *D*, either *K* is contained in the [center](wikipedia://en/53796787) of *D*, or *K* = *D*. In other words, if the [unit group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_group) of *K* is a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of the unit group of *D*, then either *K* = *D* or *K* is central (Lam 2001, 211).

:::
