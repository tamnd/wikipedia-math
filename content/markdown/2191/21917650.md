# John B. Garnett

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John B. Garnett"}

**John Brady Garnett** (born December 15, 1940) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), known for his work in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147). He received his Ph.D. at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1966, under the supervision of [Irving Glicksberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Glicksberg). He received the [Steele Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steele_Prize) for Mathematical Exposition in 2003 for his book, *Bounded Analytic Functions*.[^o2] As of February 2025, he has supervised the dissertations of 26 students[^o3] including [Peter Jones](wikipedia://en/1604461), [Jill Pipher](wikipedia://en/30887737), and Anthony Carbery.

:::

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
