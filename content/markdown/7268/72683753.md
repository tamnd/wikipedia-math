# Gianluigi Rozza

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gianluigi Rozza"}

**Gianluigi Rozza** is an [aerospace engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known for his work on [reduced-order modeling](wikipedia://en/42908722). He is currently full professor of Numerical Analysis at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) (SISSA) in Trieste,[^o3] where he serves as head of SISSA Mathematics Area and SISSA Director's Delegate for Research Valorisation, Innovation, and Industrial Cooperation.

:::

## Personal life and education {#Personal_life_and_education}

G. Rozza was born and raised in Italy where he earned a bachelor's and master's degree in Aerospace Engineering from the [Polytechnic University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Milan) in the year 2002. He subsequently moved to [Lausanne, Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne) to join the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) (EPFL) for postgraduate studies. At EPFL, he received a PhD in Numerical Analysis in 2005. His thesis, titled 'Shape Design by Optimal Flow Control and Reduced basis Techniques: Applications to Bypass Configurations in Haemodynamics', was completed under the supervision of [Alfio Quarteroni](wikipedia://en/53431016) and [Anthony Tyr Patera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_Tyr_Patera).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Following his doctoral studies, G. Rozza worked at [Ecole Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne) in professor Alfio Quarteroni's scientific group for a year. In 2006, he joined the Department of Mechanical Engineering and the Center for Computational Engineering at the MIT as a Postdoctoral Associate Researcher in the professor Anthony Tyr Patera's group. He stayed at the MIT until 2008. After a period as a Senior Researcher and Lecturer at the EPFL, in 2012, Rozza joined the Applied Mathematics group, SISSA mathLab, at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) (SISSA) where, in 2014, he became professor. In 2018, he became a Full Professor at SISSA, where he still works and also serves the role of the Director's Delegate for Innovation, Knowledge Valorization and Technology Transfer. At SISSA, Rozza also served as coordinator of the AMMA doctoral programs from 2018 to 2020 and as coordinator of the Mathematics Area from 2020 to 2024 (and concurrently as a member of the Academic Senate); he currently serves on the School’s board of directors[^o7].

In 2018 he received an ERC Consolidator Grant (CoG) from the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) (ERC) with the proposal «AROMA-CFD» (2016-2022),[^o8][^o9][^o10] and in 2022 he won an ERC Proof of Concept Grant PoC ([Proof of Concept](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_of_concept)).[^o11] for the project «ARGOS».[^o12][^o13]

In December 2021 he was ranked n.1152 in the Top Scientist-Mathematics from research.com, n.35 in Italy.[^o14] G. Rozza is currently n.20 in the Top Italian Scientists Mathematics ranking.[^o15] In September 2022 he was listed in the World's Top 2% Scientist ranking, made by [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in collaboration with [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus).[^o16] As of January 2023 Rozza co-authored 3 books and edited other 10 (12 volumes) on [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and [model order reduction](wikipedia://en/42908722).[^o17][^o18]

In 2019, Rozza joined the technical-scientific committee of the Maritime, Aerospace, Renewable Energies (MARE) Technology Cluster FVG, and was subsequently elected its president for the term 2022–2026.

In 2021, he joined the scientific council of [iNEST](https://www.consorzioinest.it/en/) (Interconnected Nord-Est Innovation Ecosystem, funded by the PNRR), and subsequently joined the board of directors in 2024[^o19].

In 2022, he was elected chairman of the supervisory board of SMACT (digital technologies—Social, Mobile, Analytics, Cloud, IoT), a highly specialized center of expertise and innovation strategy for MISE-affiliated companies focused on Industry 4.0 in the Triveneto region, following a term on the management board, to which he returned in 2025.

Also starting in 2022, he served as a member of the Executive Committee of ECCOMAS ([European Community on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences](https://eccomas.org/))[^o20]. That same year, he was one of the co-founders of [FAST Computing](https://fastcomputing.net/), a SISSA startup, where he serves as scientific director[^o21].

In September 2025, he was elected President of SIMAI, the Italian Society for Applied and Industrial Mathematics.[^o22] In the same year, he became President of the start-up [Simplifica](https://simplificabio.com/), joint venture of FAST Computing and [Bio4Dreams](https://bio4dreams.com/en/), and member of the Steering Committee of the [Data Science & Artificial Intelligence Foundation ETS](https://www.regione.fvg.it/enti-controllati/dettaglio?id=2618), an organization established by the [Friuli Venezia Giulia Autonomous Region](https://www.regione.fvg.it/rafvg/cms/RAFVG/) to promote scientific research, university education, and technology transfer in the fields of data science and artificial intelligence.

The end of 2025 and the beginning of 2026 mark two further milestones for Rozza, in two complementary roles: first, as Principal Investigator of the project ROSA – Reduced Order and Surrogate methods for advanced Applications, funded under the FIS 3 – Fondo Italiano per la Scienza programme; then, as co-founder of FAST Computing, with the SpeedAPP platform receiving funding from the [European Innovation Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Innovation_Council)[^o23].

In July 2026, Rozza was elected Vice President of ECCOMAS and was simultaneously named one of the ECCOMAS Fellows[^o24], an honor established that year for researchers in the field of mathematics who have made exceptional contributions to computational methods in the applied sciences.

## Recognitions {#Recognitions}

- In 2004 Rozza received the Bill Morton CFD Prize[^o26] at the [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford) ICFD conference.
- Rozza Received the *Riconoscenza Civica* from his birthplace ([Sant'Angelo Lodigiano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sant'Angelo_Lodigiano)).[^o27]
- In 2006 he received the *Phd Award*[^o28] from ECCOMAS Congress ([European Community on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Community_on_Computational_Methods_in_Applied_Sciences)) in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam).
- In 2009 Rozza received the *[Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Verlag) Computational Science and Engineering* (CSE) Award in [Monaco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monaco_di_Baviera) with Phuong Huynh and Cuong Nguyen[^o29] for the rbMIT software, developed at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston).
- Rozza received the [Dardo D'oro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dardo_D'oro) from his City of residence ([Castiraga Vidardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castiraga_Vidardo)) in 2010.[^o30]
- In 2014 he received the [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500) Award[^o31] at ECCOMAS Congress ([European Community on Computational Methods in Applied Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Community_on_Computational_Methods_in_Applied_Sciences)) in [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona).
- In 2022 Rozza gave the first *Solari Lecture*[^o32] at the [Polytechnic University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Milan), Italy.
- In 2025 Rozza has been nominated SIAM Fellow[^o33] for contributions to the development of reduced order models for CFD applications.
- In 2025 Rozza has been awarded the Gili Agostinelli Prize from Accademia delle Science di Torino[^o34] for his contributions in applied mathematics for physical and natural sciences, and engineering.
