# Rolf Rannacher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rolf Rannacher"}

*Image: Rannacher rolf.jpg.* Rannacher at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2007

**Rolf Rannacher** (born 10 June 1948 in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig)) is a German mathematician and a professor of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University).

:::

Rannacher studied mathematics and physics at the [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt). There he received his doctorate in 1974 with dissertation *Diskrete Störungstheorie für das Punktsystem linearer Operatoren und Sesquilinearformen mit Anwendungen auf Operatoren vom Schrödinger Typ* (Discrete perturbation theory for the point system of linear operators and sesquilinear forms with applications to operators of the Schrödinger type). From 1974 to 1980 he was an assistant to Jens Frehse at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), where he [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1978 and after habilitation spent a year at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). He was from 1980 to 1983 a professor at the [University of Erlangen–Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg) and from 1983 to 1988 a professor at [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University). Since 1988 he is a professor in Heidelberg.

His research focuses on the numerical analysis of the [finite element method](wikipedia://en/18233581) (FEM) in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) (PDEs) based on [functional analytic](wikipedia://en/11180) methods, for example, error estimation in the ${\mathcal L}^\infty$-norm for FEM approximation in [elliptic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_differential_equation) [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779). His research also deals with numerical [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), including high-performance computer software development with his long-time collaborator John Haywood. In the 1990s Rannacher dealt with [adaptive mesh refinement](wikipedia://en/1822182) in solving [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565) problems, often in collaboration with Claes Johnson and [Endre Süli](wikipedia://en/25003070). At Heidelberg's *Interdisziplinären Zentrum für Wissenschaftliches Rechnen* (acronym IWR, Interdisciplinary Center for Scientific Computing), Rannacher was, in the early 1990s, one of the pioneers in the development of parallel computer algorithms for [transputers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transputer).

He was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) in 1998 and at the ICM in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) in 2002. In 2009 he was made an honorary doctor of the University of Erlangen-Nuremberg.[^o3]
