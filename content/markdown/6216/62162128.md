# Friedrich Götze

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Götze"}

**Friedrich Götze** (born 6 August 1951 in [Hameln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hameln)) is a German mathematician, specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711), and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Götze studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) by means of a scholarship from the [Studienstiftung des deutschen Volkes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studienstiftung_des_deutschen_Volkes).[^o4] In 1978 he received his doctorate from the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne) with thesis *Asymptotic Expansions in the Central Limit Theorem in Banach Spaces* under the supervision of [Johann Pfanzagl](wikipedia://en/72137364). At the University of Cologne, Götze was an assistant, interrupted by a year as visiting professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). In 1983 he [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in Cologne with thesis *Asymptotic developments in central limit theorems*. In 1984 he became a professor of mathematics at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University). For the academic years 1990/91 and 2002/2003 he was Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics.[^o6]

Götze is a member of the scientific advisory board of the [Weierstrass Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute_for_Applied_Analysis_and_Stochastics) (of which he is a founding member) and of the board of the Gesellschaft für Mathematische Forschung, which supports and legally represents the [Mathematisches Forschungsinstitut Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach).[^o7] He is a Fellow of the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen)'s Institute for Mathematical Stochastics. He was in 2017/18 the vice-president and was elected for 2019/20 the president of the [Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung](wikipedia://en/30864973) (DMV).[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

His research deals with asymptotic methods, convergence rates and limit theorems in mathematical statistics, [Markov processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process), stochastic algorithms, probability theory, functional analysis, and spectral distribution in [random matrices](wikipedia://en/1648765).[^o10] He applied probabilistic methods to [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513) and the geometry of numbers, including the problem of distribution and density of lattice points in ellipses. With the introduction of fundamental new methods, he gave a new, effective proof of the [Oppenheim conjecture](wikipedia://en/5007342), which was first proved by [Grigory Margulis](wikipedia://en/1104948) in 1987.[^o11][^o12] Götze was the spokesperson for the [DFG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) Collaborative Research Center's *Spektrale Strukturen und Topologische Methoden in der Mathematik* (Spectral Structures and Topological Methods in Mathematics).[^o6]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Götze was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin in 1988.[^o14][^o15] In 2009 he became a member of the [Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o6] In 2012 he was the [Gauss Lecturer](wikipedia://en/48440954) with talk *Der mehrdimensionale zentrale Grenzwertsatz und die Geometrie der Zahlen* (The multidimensional central limit theorem and the geometry of numbers). For his contribution to the establishment of the [European Institute for Statistics, Probability, Stochastic Operations Research and its Applications](wikipedia://en/40639643) (Eurandom), he was awarded the [Order of Orange-Nassau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Orange-Nassau) in 2014.[^o16]
