# Sydney Goldstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sydney Goldstein"}

**Sydney Goldstein** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o3] (3 December 1903 – 22 January 1989) was a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Britain) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) noted for his contribution to [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034). He is described as: "... one of those who most influenced progress in fluid dynamics during the 20th century."[^o3]

:::

He was especially known for his work on steady-flow laminar boundary-layer equations and on the turbulent resistance to rotation of a disk in a fluid. Goldstein was extremely knowledgeable on [aerodynamics](wikipedia://en/2819) and his work had a significant impact in that area.[^o4]

## Early life {#Early_life}

Goldstein was born into the [Jewish community of Hull](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Kingston_upon_Hull), where his family ran a furniture store.[^o7] After his mother died he moved to live with an aunt and attended Bede Collegiate School in [Sunderland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunderland,_Tyne_and_Wear).[^o8] At the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) in 1921 he studied mathematics, but was to move to [St John's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge), [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), graduating from the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos) in 1925 and gaining the [Smith's Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith's_Prize) in 1927. He was awarded an Isaac Newton Studentship to continue research in applied mathematics under [Harold Jeffreys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Jeffreys). His 1928 PhD thesis was entitled *The Theory And Application Of [Mathieu Functions](wikipedia://en/1724802)*.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

He was appointed Rockefeller Research Fellow and spent a year working in [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).[^o4] In 1929 he became a fellow of St John's College but later the same year was appointed to a lectureship in Mathematics at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_of_Mathematics,_University_of_Manchester). At Manchester the influence of [Osborne Reynolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osborne_Reynolds) and [Horace Lamb](wikipedia://en/1192944) in fluid dynamics was still felt there and had a strong effect on Goldstein.[^o4] Moving to Cambridge in 1931 he took over the editorship of *Modern Developments in Fluid Dynamics* on Lamb's death.

During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) Goldstein worked on boundary layer theory at the [National Physical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Physical_Laboratory_%28United_Kingdom%29) and at the end of the war he was appointed to the [Beyer Chair of Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beyer_Chair_of_Applied_Mathematics) in Manchester. He was elected to the membership of [Manchester Literary and Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Literary_and_Philosophical_Society) in 1947.[^o10]

Goldstein strongly supported the [State of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_of_Israel) and in 1950 he accepted the chairmanship of the department of mathematics at [Technion – Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology). Having made a major contribution to the establishment of the Technion, he found the administrative load too heavy[^o4] and moved again, accepting the chair of Gordon McKay Professor of Applied Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1954.[^o8] He retired in 1968 but continued as an emeritus professor at Harvard.

## Honours {#Honours}

- [Mayhew Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayhew_Prize), 1925
- [Smith's Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith's_Prize), 1927
- [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110), 1935
- [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society), 1937[^o3]
- Worked at the Aerodynamics Division, [National Physical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Physical_Laboratory,_UK), 1939–45
- Chairman, [Aeronautical Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aeronautical_Research_Council), 1946–49
- Foreign Member, Royal Netherlands Academy of Sciences and Letters (Section for Sciences), 1950
- Plenary Speaker, [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), 1954[^o12]
- [Timoshenko Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoshenko_Medal), 1965
- Foreign Member, Finnish Scientific Society (Section for maths and physics), 1975
