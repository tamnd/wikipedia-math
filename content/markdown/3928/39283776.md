# Jeffery J. Leader

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeffery J. Leader"}

**Jeffery J. Leader** (born 1963) is an American academic [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) specializing in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). He is a member of the [Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose-Hulman_Institute_of_Technology).

:::

Leader received a B.S. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831); B.S.E.E. in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) (1985) from [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University); Sc.M. (1987); and Ph.D. (1989) from the Division of Applied Mathematics at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University)[^o2] under [Philip J. Davis](wikipedia://en/3753768). He has also taught at [Harvey Mudd College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College), the [Naval Postgraduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naval_Postgraduate_School), the [United States Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Military_Academy), and [Santa Clara University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Clara_University). He was a distinguished visiting professor of mathematical sciences at the [U.S. Air Force Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Air_Force_Academy).[^o3] He is the author of several journal articles and a textbook called *Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computation*.[^o4]
