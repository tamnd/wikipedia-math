# Frank Deutsch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frank Deutsch"}

**Frank Deutsch** (born December 18, 1936) is an American academic [applied mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_mathematician) specializing in [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713). He is an emeritus professor at [The Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pennsylvania_State_University),[^o2] and is the author of over 94 journal articles and two books,[^o3] and on the editorial board of five journals.

:::

He earned the B.S. in [Mechanical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_Engineering) from the [University of Hartford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hartford) (1960), the M.S. in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) (1961), and the Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics from Brown University (1966)[^o4] under [Philip J. Davis](wikipedia://en/3753768).
