# Tom Berson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tom Berson"}

**Thomas Alan Berson** (born 1946) is a [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security) researcher. His notable work includes, several [cryptanalytic attacks](wikipedia://en/5715), and research in the practical use of [cryptographic protocols](wikipedia://en/591994), particularly, [computer networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_network).

:::

A founding member of the [International Association for Cryptologic Research](wikipedia://en/87043), Tom Berson has been one of its officers since 1983, and was the first person selected as an IACR Fellow.[^o2] He served as editor of the *[Journal of Cryptology](wikipedia://en/87045)* from 1986 to 2001. At [ASIACRYPT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASIACRYPT) 2000, he delivered the IACR Distinguished Lecture, *Cryptography Everywhere*.

Berson was elected as a member of the [National Academy of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Engineering) in 2020, for contributions to cybersecurity in the commercial and intelligence communities. He serves on the advisory board of Salesforce.com.
