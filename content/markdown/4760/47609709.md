# Mean operation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mean operation"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a **mean** or **mean operation** on a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) *X* is a [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122), [commutative](wikipedia://en/294390), [idempotent](wikipedia://en/14972) [binary operation](wikipedia://en/3948) on *X*. If the operation is also [associative](wikipedia://en/1335), it defines a [semilattice](wikipedia://en/755647). A classic problem is to determine which spaces admit a mean. For example, [Euclidean spaces](wikipedia://en/9697) admit a mean -- the usual [average](wikipedia://en/612) of two vectors -- but [spheres of positive dimension](wikipedia://en/39782) do not, including the [circle](wikipedia://en/6220).

:::
