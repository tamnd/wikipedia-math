# Reification (statistics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reification"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), **reification** is the use of an idealized model of a statistical process. The model is then used to make [inferences](wikipedia://en/27577) connecting model results, which imperfectly represent the actual process, with experimental observations.

:::

Also,[^o2] a process whereby model-derived quantities such as [principal components](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_components), [factors](wikipedia://en/253492) and [latent variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latent_variable) are identified, named and treated as if they were directly measurable quantities.
