# Danijela Damjanović

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Danijela Damjanović"}

**Danijela Damjanović** is a Serbian mathematician whose research concerns [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), particularly focusing on the [smooth classification](wikipedia://en/4576710) and [rigidity](wikipedia://en/1458875) of [group actions](wikipedia://en/12781). She is a professor of mathematics in Sweden, at the [KTH Royal Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KTH_Royal_Institute_of_Technology) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Damjanović studied mathematics as an undergraduate at the [University of Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade) in Serbia, graduating in 1997. She continued her studies at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in the United States, where she completed her Ph.D. in 2004. Her doctoral dissertation, *Local Rigidity of Higher Rank Abelian Actions on the Torus via KAM*, was supervised by [Anatole Katok](wikipedia://en/43275327).

From 2004 to 2006 she was a European Post-Doctoral Institute Fellow, at the [Erwin Schrödinger International Institute for Mathematics and Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_International_Institute_for_Mathematics_and_Physics) in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), Austria, the [Institut des hautes études scientifiques](wikipedia://en/14527) in [Bures-sur-Yvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bures-sur-Yvette), France, the [Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences](wikipedia://en/67042302) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), Poland, and the [Institute for Mathematical Research](wikipedia://en/33084376) in [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich), Switzerland. She continued her postdoctoral research from 2006 to 2009 as a Benjamin Pierce Lecturer in the mathematics department at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in the United States.

In 2009 she joined [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in [Houston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston), Texas, and in 2013 she was tenured there as an associate professor. In 2015 she moved to her present position at KTH, again as an associate professor, and she was promoted to full professor in 2024.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Damjanović was an invited speaker at the 2026 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842). She was elected to the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2026.
