# Sun's curious identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sun's curious identity"}

In [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), **Sun's curious identity** is the following [identity](wikipedia://en/298428) involving [binomial coefficients](wikipedia://en/4668), first established by [Zhi-Wei Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhi-Wei_Sun) in 2002:

:::

$$(x+m+1)\sum_{i=0}^m(-1)^i\dbinom{x+y+i}{m-i}\dbinom{y+2i}{i} -\sum_{i=0}^{m}\dbinom{x+i}{m-i}(-4)^i=(x-m)\dbinom{x}{m}.$$

::: {#o3 .proof cue=heading name="Proofs"}

## Proofs {#Proofs}

After Sun's publication of this identity in 2002, five other proofs were obtained by various mathematicians:

- Panholzer and Prodinger's proof via [generating functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generating_functions);
- Merlini and Sprugnoli's proof using [Riordan arrays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riordan_arrays);
- Ekhad and Mohammed's proof by the [WZ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/WZ_theory) method;
- Chu and Claudio's proof with the help of [Jensen](wikipedia://en/1430666)'s formula;
- Callan's [combinatorial proof](wikipedia://en/741875) involving [dominos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dominos) and colorings.

:::
