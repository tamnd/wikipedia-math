# S. James Press

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="S. James Press"}

**Sheldon James Press** (February 4, 1931 – November 25, 2020) was an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Press was born in [Brooklyn, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn,_New_York). He moved to [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California) in 1951. He attended the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California), earning his [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). He also attended [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), earning his [PhD degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) in 1964.[^o4]

Press served as a professor in the department of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside) until 2005. During his years as a professor, he was named a [distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor),[^o5] and in 1981, he was named a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o6]

## Death {#Death}

Press died on November 25, 2020, at the age of 89.[^o8]
