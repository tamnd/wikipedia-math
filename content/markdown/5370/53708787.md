# Glissette

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Glissette"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **glissette** is a [curve](wikipedia://en/89246) determined by either the [locus](wikipedia://en/542465) of any point, or the [envelope](wikipedia://en/880235) of any line or curve, that is attached to a curve that slides against or along two other fixed curves.

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

### Ellipse {#Ellipse}

A basic example is that of a line segment of which the endpoints slide along two perpendicular lines. The glissette of any point on the line forms an [ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277).[^o5]

*Image: Glissette\_ellipse.gif.*

### Astroid {#Astroid}

Similarly, the envelope glissette of the line segment in the example above is an [astroid](wikipedia://en/769163).[^o7]

*Image: Astroid\_glissette.png.*

### Conchoid {#Conchoid}

Any [conchoid](wikipedia://en/898856) may be regarded as a glissette, with a line and one of its points sliding along a given line and fixed point.[^o9]

*Image: Nicomedes.gif.*
