# Hans J. Reiter

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hans J. Reiter"}

**Hans Jakob Reiter** (26 November 1921 – 13 August 1992) was an Austrian mathematician working in analysis.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Because of the [Anschluss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anschluss), Hans Reiter had to leave his hometown before his [Matura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matura). Via Italy he was able to emigrate to Brazil, where he studied under [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019). In 1953 he received his PhD from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) under [Szolem Mandelbrojt](wikipedia://en/1070245) with thesis *Investigations in harmonic analysis*.[^o5][^o6] In 1952 he became an assistant at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and in 1971 obtained a professorial chair there, after years in various foreign academic positions, including an interval from 1964 to 1971 as a professor at the [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utrecht). Reiter's doctoral students include [Hans Georg Feichtinger](wikipedia://en/31979049).

Reiter wrote the widely used textbook *Classical Harmonic Analysis and Locally Compact Groups*.[^o7]
