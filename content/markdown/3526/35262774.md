# Wendell Fleming

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wendell Fleming"}

**Wendell Helms Fleming** (March 7, 1928 – February 18, 2023) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in geometrical analysis and [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454).

:::

Fleming received his PhD in 1951 under [Laurence Chisholm Young](wikipedia://en/9620408) at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) with the thesis *Boundary and Related Notions for Generalized Parametric Surfaces*. Fleming was a professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), where he retired in 2009 as professor emeritus.

Together with [Herbert Federer](wikipedia://en/12185345), Fleming was a pioneer of [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694). Later in his career, he worked on [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), stochastic differential equations and their applications in control theory. From 1976 to 1977, he was a Guggenheim Fellow. In 1982 he gave a plenary address (*Optimal Control of Markov Processes*) at the [ICM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematical_Congress) in Warsaw.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1987 he received with Federer the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). In 1994 he won the Reid Prize from the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376). He was given an honorary doctorate at Purdue University in 1991. In 2006 he received the [Isaacs Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Society_of_Dynamic_Games). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5] In May 2012 his election to membership in the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) was announced.[^o6]
