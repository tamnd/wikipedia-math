# Géza Grünwald

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Géza Grünwald"}

**Géza Grünwald** (October 18, 1910, Budapest – September 7, 1943) was a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians)[^o2] mathematician of [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Hungary) heritage who worked on [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29). He died in the [Holocaust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holocaust).

:::
