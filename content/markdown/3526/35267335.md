# Lynn Harold Loomis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lynn Harold Loomis"}

**Lynn Harold Loomis** (April 25, 1915 – June 9, 1994) was an American mathematician working on analysis. Together with [Hassler Whitney](wikipedia://en/391794), he discovered the [Loomis–Whitney inequality](wikipedia://en/8233045).

:::

Loomis received his PhD in 1942 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under [Salomon Bochner](wikipedia://en/987847) with thesis *Some Studies on Simply-Connected Riemann Surfaces: I. The Problem of Imbedding II. Mapping on the Boundary for Two Classes of Surfaces*. After completing his PhD, Loomis was a professor at [Radcliffe College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radcliffe_College) and from 1949 at Harvard. From 1956, he was a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o2]
