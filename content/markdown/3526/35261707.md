# Alberto González Domínguez

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alberto González Domínguez"}

González

**Alberto González Domínguez** (11 April 1904 in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires) – 14 September 1982 in Buenos Aires) was an Argentine mathematician working on [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267).[^o2][^o3]

:::

González Domínguez received his Ph.D. from the [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires) in 1939 under the direction of [Julio Rey Pastor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julio_Rey_Pastor). That same year, González Domínguez received a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) and worked for two years with [Jacob Tamarkin](wikipedia://en/22870348) at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University).[^o2][^o4] González Domínguez spent most of his career as a professor at the University of Buenos Aires.[^o2]
