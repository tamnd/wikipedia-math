# Edward Schaumberg Quade

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Schaumberg Quade"}

**Edward Schaumberg Quade** (June 28, 1908 – June 4, 1988)[^o3] was an American mathematician at the [Rand Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rand_Corporation) who worked on trigonometric series and systems analysis.[^o4]

:::
