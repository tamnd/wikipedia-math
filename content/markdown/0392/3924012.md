# List of countries by English-speaking population

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of countries by English-speaking population"}

The following is a list of [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language)-speaking populations [by country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states), including information on both [native speakers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_language) and [second-language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_language) speakers.

:::

## List {#List}

This list may be sorted by column by clicking on the column header.

Some numbers have been calculated by combining data from different sources;

figures not attributed to sources and given with a date should be treated with caution. In most sources, the results shown are of people who **say** that they can speak English, while that was not verified, which means the actual number of English speakers could be higher or lower.

- EU The [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) is a supranational union composed of 27 member states. The total English-speaking population of the European Union and the United Kingdom combined (2012) is 256,876,220[^o128] (out of a total population of 500,000,000,[^o129] i.e. 51%) including 65,478,252 native speakers and 191,397,968 non-native speakers, and would be ranked 2nd if it were included. English native speakers amount to 13% of the whole population of the EU and the UK, while the percentage of people that speak English "well enough in order to be able to have a conversation", either as first (32%), second (11%) or third (3%) foreign language, was 38%.

:::

- When taken from this list and added together, the total number of English speakers in the world adds up to around 1,430,000,000. Likewise, the total number of native English speakers adds up to around 410,000,000. This implies that there are approximately 1,020,000,000 people who speak English as an additional language.
