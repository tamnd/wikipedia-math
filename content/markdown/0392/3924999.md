# Wykeham Professor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wykeham Professor"}

The [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) has three statutory professorships named after [William of Wykeham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_of_Wykeham), who founded [New College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford).

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Wykeham Professor of Logic"}

## Logic {#Logic}

The **Wykeham Professorship in Logic** was established in 1859, although it was not known as the Wykeham chair until later. Its first chair was Henry Wall.[^o4]

### List of holders of post {#List_of_holders_of_post}

- Henry Wall,[^o4] 1849?–1870
- [Thomas Fowler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Fowler_%28academic%29), 1873–1889
- [John Cook Wilson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Cook_Wilson), 1889–1915
- [Harold Henry Joachim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Henry_Joachim), 1919–1935
- [Henry Habberley Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Habberley_Price), 1935–1959
- [Alfred Jules Ayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Jules_Ayer), 1959[^o4]–1978
- [Michael Dummett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dummett), 1979–1992
- [David Wiggins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Wiggins), 1993–2000
- [Timothy Williamson](wikipedia://en/3055177), 2000–2023
- [Richard Pettigrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Pettigrew_%28philosopher%29), 2026–

:::

::: {#o7 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Wykeham Professor of Ancient History"}

## Ancient history {#Ancient_history}

The **Wykeham Professorship of Ancient History** was established in 1910. It concentrates on [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greece) history to avoid possible duplication with the far older [Camden Professorship of Ancient History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Camden_Professorship_of_Ancient_History), which focuses primarily on [Roman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Rome) history.

### List of holders of post {#List_of_holders_of_post}

- [J. L. Myres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._L._Myres), 1910–1939
- [Theodore Wade-Gery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_Wade-Gery), 1939–1953
- [Antony Andrewes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antony_Andrewes), 1953–1977
- [W. G. (George) Forrest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Forrest_%28Historian%29), 1977–1992
- [Robert Parker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Parker_%28historian%29), 1996–2016[^o9]
- [Nino Luraghi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nino_Luraghi), 2018–present

:::

::: {#o11 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Wykeham Professor of Physics"}

## Physics {#Physics}

The Wykeham Professorship of Physics is the only endowed chair in the [Rudolf Peierls Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Peierls_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).[^o12] This professorship is associated with [New College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford).[^o13]

### List of holders of post {#List_of_holders_of_post}

- [John Sealy Townsend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Sealy_Townsend), 1900–1941[^o15]
- [Maurice Pryce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice_Pryce), 1946–1955[^o15]
- [Willis Lamb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willis_Lamb), 1957–1962[^o15]
- Sir [Rudolf Peierls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Peierls), 1963–1974[^o15]
- Sir [Roger Elliott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Elliott_%28physicist%29), 1974–1988[^o16]
- [David Sherrington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Sherrington_%28physicist%29), 1989–2008[^o17]
- [Shivaji Sondhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shivaji_Sondhi), 2021–present.[^o18]

### Tencent Chair of Theoretical Physics {#Tencent_Chair_of_Theoretical_Physics}

In February 2021 [The Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Times) reported that the Wykeham Professorship of Physics was to be renamed "Tencent-Wykeham Professorship of Physics" following a donation,[^o20] but the University later said the Wykeham Professorship of Physics will not be renamed; instead the current holder will also be given a separate post called "Tencent Chair of Theoretical Physics" to run concurrently with his Wykeham post.[^o21]
