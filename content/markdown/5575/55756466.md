# Judy Green (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Judy Green"}

**Judith** (**Judy**) **Green** (born 1943)[^o3] is an American [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) and [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) who studies [women in mathematics](wikipedia://en/41400343). She is a founding member of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698); she has also served as its vice president, and as the vice president of the [American Association of University Professors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_of_University_Professors).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Green earned her bachelor's degree at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). She completed a master's degree at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), and a Ph.D. at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park). Her dissertation, supervised by [Carol Karp](wikipedia://en/11262047) and finished in 1972, was *Consistency Properties for Uncountable Finite-Quantifier Languages*.

Green was elected an [AMS](wikipedia://en/198822) Member at Large in 1975 and served for three years until 1977.[^o5] She belonged to the faculty of [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) before moving to [Marymount University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marymount_University) in 1989. After retiring from Marymount in 2007, she became a volunteer at the [National Museum of American History](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Museum_of_American_History).

## Book {#Book}

With [Jeanne LaDuke](wikipedia://en/55758349), she wrote *[Pioneering Women in American Mathematics: The Pre-1940 PhD’s](wikipedia://en/63513617)* ([American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), 2009). This was a biographical study of the first women in the U.S. to earn doctorates in mathematics.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She is part of the 2019 class of fellows of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o8]
