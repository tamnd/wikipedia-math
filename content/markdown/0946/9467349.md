# Spatial statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spatial statistics"}

*Image: USA\_Contiguous\_Poverty\_2020\_clusters.jpg.* Map of poverty clusters in the United States made using spatial statistics.

**Spatial statistics** is a field of [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics) dealing with [spatial data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_data). It involves [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) ([random fields](wikipedia://en/540838), [point processes](wikipedia://en/2147685)), [sampling](wikipedia://en/160361), [smoothing](wikipedia://en/4155662) and [interpolation](wikipedia://en/14569), [regional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regional) ([areal unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Areal_unit)) and [lattice](wikipedia://en/469246) ([gridded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gridded_data)) data, [point patterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_pattern), as well as [image analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_analysis) and [stereology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereology).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::
