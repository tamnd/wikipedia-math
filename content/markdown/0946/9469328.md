# Abhyankar–Moh theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abhyankar–Moh theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Abhyankar–Moh theorem** states that if $L$ is a [complex line](wikipedia://en/7975294) in the complex [affine plane](wikipedia://en/30764139) $\mathbb{C}^2$, then every embedding of $L$ into $\mathbb{C}^2$ extends to an [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) of the plane. It is named after [Shreeram Shankar Abhyankar](wikipedia://en/30876162) and Tzuong-Tsieng Moh, who published it in 1975. More generally, the same theorem applies to lines and planes over any [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) zero, and to certain well-behaved subsets of higher-dimensional complex affine spaces.

:::
