# Amicable triple

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amicable triple"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **amicable triple** is a [set](wikipedia://en/26691) of three different numbers so related that the [*restricted* sum of the divisors](wikipedia://en/12374274) of each is equal to the sum of the other two numbers.[^o2][^o3]

:::

In another equivalent characterization, an amicable triple is a set of three different numbers so related that the [sum of the divisors](wikipedia://en/407249) of each is equal to the sum of the three numbers.

So a triple (*a*, *b*, *c*) of [natural numbers](wikipedia://en/21474) is called amicable if *s*(*a*) = *b* + *c*, *s*(*b*) = *a* + *c* and *s*(*c*) = *a* + *b*, or equivalently if σ(*a*) = σ(*b*) = σ(*c*) = *a* + *b* + *c*. Here σ(*n*) is the sum of all positive divisors, and *s*(*n*) = σ(*n*) − *n* is the [aliquot sum](wikipedia://en/12374274). The smallest amicable triple is (1980, 2016, 2556). [^o4]
