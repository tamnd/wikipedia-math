# Quartan prime

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quartan prime"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quartan prime** is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) of the form *x*<sup>4</sup> + *y*<sup>4</sup> where *x* and *y* are positive [integers](wikipedia://en/14563). [Odd](wikipedia://en/143135) quartan primes are of the form 16*n* + 1.

:::

For example, 17 is the smallest odd quartan prime: 1<sup>4</sup> + 2<sup>4</sup> = 1 + 16 = 17.

With the exception of 2 (*x* = *y* = 1), one of *x* and *y* will be odd, and the other [even](wikipedia://en/143135). If both are odd or both are even, the result will be even, and 2 is the only even prime.

The first few quartan primes are:
