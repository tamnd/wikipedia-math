# Troels Jørgensen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Troels Jørgensen"}

**Troels Jørgensen** is a Danish mathematician at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) working on [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), who proved [Jørgensen's inequality](wikipedia://en/31074368). He wrote his thesis in 1970 at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) under the joint supervision of [Werner Fenchel](wikipedia://en/4784468) and [Bent Fuglede](wikipedia://en/3966927).

:::

## Work {#Work}

He is known for [Jørgensen's inequality](wikipedia://en/31074368),[^o4] and for his discovery of a [hyperbolic structure](wikipedia://en/1237700) on certain [fibered 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/2011690)[^o5] which were one of the inspirations for [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190)'s [Geometrisation Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometrisation_Conjecture).[^o6] He is also credited with being one of the co-discoverers of the ordered structure of the set of volumes of hyperbolic 3-manifolds.[^o7][^o8]
