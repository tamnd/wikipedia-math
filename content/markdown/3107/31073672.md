# Gerald J. Toomer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gerald J. Toomer"}

**Gerald James Toomer** (born 23 November 1934) is a [historian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historian) of [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) and [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) who has written numerous books and papers on ancient Greek and medieval Islamic astronomy. In particular, he translated [Ptolemy](wikipedia://en/23979)'s *[Almagest](wikipedia://en/148060)* into English.

:::

Formerly a fellow of [Corpus Christi College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpus_Christi_College,_Oxford), [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University), he moved to [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) as a [special student](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matriculation#Special_student) in 1959 to study "the history of mathematics in antiquity and the transmission of these systems through Arabic into medieval Europe."[^o3] He joined the History of Mathematics department in 1963, became an associate professor in 1965, and was the chairman from 1980 to 1986.[^o4][^o5]
