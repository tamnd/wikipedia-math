# Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics"}

The ***Journal of Computational and Graphical Statistics*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) on behalf of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o3] Established in 1992, the journal covers the use of computational and graphical methods in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [data analysis](wikipedia://en/2720954), including numerical methods, graphical displays and methods, and perception. It is published jointly with the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) and the [Interface Foundation of North America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interface_Foundation_of_North_America). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2021 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.884.[^o3]

:::
