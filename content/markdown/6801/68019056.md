# Peter Guttorp

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Guttorp"}

**Peter Guttorp** is a statistician, born in Sweden, with most of his career in the United States. He is a professor emeritus at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington)[^o2] and professor at the [Norwegian Computing Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Computing_Center).[^o3] Most of his work is in stochastic modeling of scientific data in hematology, geosciences, and climatology, with particular focus on spatial and spatio-temporal approaches. He has also worked in the history of statistics.

:::

Guttorp received a journalist exam from the Stockholm School of Journalism in 1969, and a PhD in Statistics from the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley) in 1980.[^o4] He was awarded an honorary doctorate from [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University) in 2009.[^o5]

Guttorp is a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988),[^o6] and the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).[^o7] He is an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute)[^o8] and served on its executive committee as vice president from 2017 to 2021.[^o9] He was president of the International Environmetric Society from 2002 to 2004.[^o10]
