# Marisa Fernández

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marisa Fernández"}

Fernández

**María Luisa** (**Marisa**) **Fernández Rodríguez** (11 June 1953 - 18 November 2025) was a Spanish mathematician specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298), and [$G_{2}$-structures](wikipedia://en/16933890). She was professor of geometry and topology in the department of mathematics at the [University of the Basque Country](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Basque_Country).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fernández was originally from [Ourense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ourense). She was a mathematics student at the [University of Santiago de Compostela](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Santiago_de_Compostela), earning a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) there in 1974 and completing her doctorate in 1976. Her dissertation, *Estructura y propiedades de las variedades G1*, was supervised by Enrique Vidal Abascal.

After postdoctoral research with [Alfred Gray](wikipedia://en/1465376) at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), she joined the faculty of science at the University of the Basque Country in 1986.

She died on 18 November 2025.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1988, the government of the [Province of Pontevedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Province_of_Pontevedra) gave Fernández the Antonio Odriozola Prize for basic research. She was a 2019 winner of the medal of the [Royal Spanish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/65501555).

## Research {#Research}

Fernández made influential contributions to differential and symplectic geometry, often working at the interface with topology.[^o7]

Together with Gray and Gotay, she provided the first example of a [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356) which does not admit a compatible complex structure, hence cannot be [Kähler](wikipedia://en/390538).[^o8]

Together with Vicente Muñoz, she resolved a question posed by Babenko and Taimanov by providing the first example of a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042), [simply connected](wikipedia://en/523879) symplectic manifold that is nonformal in the sense of [rational homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/13998981).[^o9] Their construction has become a landmark result in symplectic topology and significantly advanced the understanding of the homotopical properties of symplectic manifolds.[^o4][^o7]
