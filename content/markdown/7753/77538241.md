# Kosovar Mathematical Society

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kosovar Mathematical Society"}

The **Kosovar Mathematical Society** or **KMS** (Albanian: Shoqata e Matematikanëve të Kosovës, **SHMK**) is a [mathematical society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_society) founded in [Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo) in 2008. The KMS is based in [Pristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pristina), and has been a member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) since 2012.

:::

## History {#History}

The Kosovar Mathematical Society was founded on 22 August 2008 in [Pristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pristina), the same year that Kosovo [declared its independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2008_Kosovo_declaration_of_independence) from [Serbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbia). The society was founded by Qëndrim Gashi, who served as the first president. Gashi later worked as the Kosovan ambassador to France.

## Activities and publications {#Activities_and_publications}

Since 2011 the KMS has coordinated the Kosovan entry at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208). It sometimes faces financial difficulties in doing so. The society does not take part in the [Balkan Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/2461569) for political reasons.

The Kosovar Mathematical Society publishes the Bulletin of Mathematical Analysis and Applications jointly with the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science of the [University of Pristina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pristina). The Bulletin is published in English, has been purely electronic since its founding in 2009, and is indexed by [zbMATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZbMATH).

## Presidents {#Presidents}

The Kosovar Mathematical Society has had 5 presidents since its founding.

- Qëndrim Gashi (2008–2011)
- Qamil Haxhibeqiri (2012–2015)
- Muhib Lohaj (2016–2018)
- Armend Shabani (2019–2022)
- Valdete Rexhëbeqaj Hamiti (2022–present)
