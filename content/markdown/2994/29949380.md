# Classical involution theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Classical involution theorem"}

In mathematical [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **classical involution theorem** of (txt) classifies [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600) with a classical [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) and satisfying some other conditions, showing that they are mostly [groups of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of odd [characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_characteristic). (Berkman 2001) extended the classical involution theorem to [groups of finite Morley rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groups_of_finite_Morley_rank).

:::

A **classical involution** *t* of a finite group *G* is an involution whose centralizer has a [subnormal subgroup](wikipedia://en/2774415) containing *t* with quaternion [Sylow 2-subgroups](wikipedia://en/53993).
