# Gilman–Griess theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gilman–Griess theorem"}

In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), a [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) discipline, the **Gilman–Griess theorem**, [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by Robert H. Gilman and [Robert L. Griess](wikipedia://en/5034215), classifies the [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group) of [characteristic 2 type](wikipedia://en/29668519) with *e*(*G*) ≥ 4 that have a "standard component", which covers one of the three cases of the [trichotomy theorem](wikipedia://en/29598798).[^o2]

:::
