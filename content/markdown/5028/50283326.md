# International rankings of North Korea

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International rankings of North Korea"}

[North Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Korea) ranks as the third least democratic country in the world in the [Economist Intelligence Unit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economist_Intelligence_Unit)'s [Democracy Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Economist_Democracy_Index), while [The Heritage Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Heritage_Foundation) and *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal)*'s [Index of Economic Freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_of_Economic_Freedom) places the country as the one with least economic freedom. According to the [Press Freedom Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Press_Freedom_Index), North Korea has the second least free press in the world.

:::

According to the [Walk Free Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walk_Free_Foundation)'s [Global Slavery Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_Slavery_Index), North Korea has the highest proportion of people in [modern slavery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modern_slavery). The [Open Doors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_Doors) foundation's [World Watch List](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Watch_List) lists the country as the worst persecutor of Christians in the world.[^o49]
