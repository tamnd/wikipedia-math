# Will Frazer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Will Frazer"}

**William "Will" Frazer III** (born 1958) is an American educator.

:::

Frazer worked as a [bond trader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trader_%28finance%29) on [Wall Street](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_District,_Manhattan) before becoming a teacher at [Buchholz High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buchholz_High_School) in [Gainesville, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gainesville,_Florida), where he created and coached the nationally recognized [math team](wikipedia://en/19710#National_high_school_competitions_%28grade_9–12%29_and_lower), leading them to 16 of 17 consecutive [Mu Alpha Theta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_Alpha_Theta) national math championships.[^o3]

In 2024, Frazer opened The Frazer School, an independent K-12 school in Gainesville that focuses on competitive academics.

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Frazer was born and raised in Gainesville, Florida. His father, William Frazer II, was an economics professor at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida)'s [Warrington College of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warrington_College_of_Business).[^o5] His mother, Mary Ann, was the city clerk of Gainesville and worked as a realtor.[^o6]

Frazer attended Buchholz High School, where he was the first golfer to qualify for the state championship.[^o6] He graduated in 1976, then earned a bachelor's degree in accounting from the University of Florida's [Fisher School of Accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher_School_of_Accounting) in 1980.[^o5]

## Finance career {#Finance_career}

In the early 1980s, Frazer worked at [Lazard Frères](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazard) as a bond trader. Years later, he said he hated the [finance-industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_services) culture, in which he "just didn't feel healthy".[^o8]

He retired from the industry at age 27, bought two [Ferraris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferrari), and moved to Black Diamond Ranch, a luxurious [golf club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_club) in [Lecanto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecanto,_Florida), [Citrus County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citrus_County,_Florida).[^o9] He moved back to Gainesville a few years later, began coaching golf at [P.K. Yonge High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P._K._Yonge_Developmental_Research_School) and Buchholz High School, and served on the City of Gainesville's pension board.[^o5]

## Teaching career {#Teaching_career}

Frazer started teaching mathematics at Buchholz High School in spring 1997. He initially inquired about teaching a finance course at Buchholz's finance academy, but there were none available, so Buchholz offered Frazer a math course instead.[^o5] He founded the Buchholz math team in 1998, and under his leadership, the math team achieved success in state and national competitions. From 2007 to 2016, the team won ten consecutive [Mu Alpha Theta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_Alpha_Theta) national championships, then six of the next seven.[^o11][^o12][^o13]

In 2022, this success drew national media coverage. *[The Wall Street Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wall_Street_Journal)* described the team as "America's greatest math team" and a “dynasty built by one teacher.”[^o3] Frazer appeared on *[Fox & Friends](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_&_Friends)* to discuss the team's achievements.[^o14] The [Florida Department of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_Department_of_Education) recognized the Buchholz math program as a model for other schools.[^o3] Several Florida senators met with Frazer to discuss how the state could reproduce his success.[^o15]

The following year, Frazer said he would leave Buchholz to open an independent academic institution in Gainesville that would focus on competitive academics.[^o16] The Frazer School opened in August 2024.[^o17][^o18] It won the 2025 [PhysicsBowl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics_Bowl) World Championship and won the 2025 Mu Alpha Theta national championship. They won the Mu Alpha Theta national championship again in 2026.[^o19][^o20][^o21][^o22]
