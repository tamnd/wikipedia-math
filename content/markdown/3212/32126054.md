# Tomasz Łuczak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tomasz Łuczak"}

**Tomasz Łuczak** (born 13 March 1963 in [Poznań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poznań))[^o2] is a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Mickiewicz_University_in_Poznań) and [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University). His main field of research is [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), specifically [discrete](wikipedia://en/8492) [structures](wikipedia://en/1809181), such as [random graphs](wikipedia://en/358477), and their [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number).[^o3]

:::

Under supervision of [Michał Karoński](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michał_Karoński), Łuczak earned his doctorate at Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań in 1987. In 1991, he received the [Kuratowski Prize](wikipedia://en/78423454) and the following year, he was awarded the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize). In 1997, he won the prestigious [Prize of the Foundation for Polish Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize_of_the_Foundation_for_Polish_Science) for his work on the theory of [random discrete structures](wikipedia://en/8492).
