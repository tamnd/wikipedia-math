# Henri Villat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henri Villat"}

**Henri René Pierre Villat** (; 24 December 1879 – 19 March 1972) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was professor of [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988) at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) from 1927 until his death. Villat became a member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1932, and its president in 1948.[^o3]
