# Eva Bayer-Fluckiger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eva Bayer-Fluckiger"}

**Eva Bayer-Fluckiger** (born 25 June 1951) is a Hungarian and Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). She is a Professor Emeritus at [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne). She has worked on several topics in topology, algebra and number theory, e.g. on the theory of knots, on lattices, on quadratic forms and on [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315). Along with [Raman Parimala](wikipedia://en/11853200), she proved [Serre's conjecture II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre's_conjecture_II_%28algebra%29) regarding the Galois cohomology of a simply-connected semisimple algebraic group when such a group is of classical type.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Bayer-Fluckiger was born in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), Hungary.[^o5] She attended the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), where she obtained her [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) under supervision of [Michel Kervaire](wikipedia://en/13644049) in 1978. She was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 1983 to 1984. From 1990 to 1996, she was an executive committee member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470)[^o7] and since 2006 served on [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) of its *[Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commentarii_Mathematici_Helvetici)*.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2001, the Essen College of Gender Studies gave Bayer-Fluckiger their Maria Sibylla Merian Prize, "for her achievements in number theory".[^o10] She was Emmy Noether guest professor at the University of Göttingen in 2003. She was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to number theory, algebra, and topology, and for service to the profession".[^o11]
