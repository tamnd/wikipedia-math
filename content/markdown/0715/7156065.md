# Dagger symmetric monoidal category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dagger symmetric monoidal category"}

In the mathematical field of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a **dagger symmetric monoidal category** is a [monoidal category](wikipedia://en/330604) $\langle\mathbf{C},\otimes, I\rangle$ that also possesses a [dagger structure](wikipedia://en/7152070). That is, this [category](wikipedia://en/53759) comes equipped not only with a [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) in the category theoretic sense but also with a dagger structure, which is used to describe [unitary morphisms](wikipedia://en/225999) and [self-adjoint morphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-adjoint) in $\mathbf{C}$: abstract analogues of those found in **FdHilb**, the [category of finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/7185509). This type of category was introduced by Peter Selinger[^o2] as an intermediate structure between [dagger categories](wikipedia://en/7152070) and the [dagger compact categories](wikipedia://en/7155145) that are used in [categorical quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/28267626), an area that now also considers dagger symmetric monoidal categories when dealing with infinite-dimensional [quantum mechanical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_mechanical) concepts.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

A **dagger symmetric monoidal category** is a [symmetric monoidal category](wikipedia://en/3865249) $\mathbf{C}$ that also has a [dagger structure](wikipedia://en/7152070) such that for all $f:A\rightarrow B$, $g:C\rightarrow D$ and all $A,B,C$ and $D$ in $Ob(\mathbf{C})$,

- $$(f\otimes g)^\dagger=f^\dagger\otimes g^\dagger:B\otimes D\rightarrow A\otimes C;$$
- $$\alpha^\dagger_{A,B,C}=\alpha^{-1}_{A,B,C}:A\otimes (B\otimes C)\rightarrow (A\otimes B)\otimes C;$$
- $$\rho^\dagger_A=\rho^{-1}_A:A \rightarrow A \otimes I;$$
- $\lambda^\dagger_A=\lambda^{-1}_A: A \rightarrow I \otimes A$ and
- $$\sigma^\dagger_{A,B}=\sigma^{-1}_{A,B}:B \otimes A \rightarrow A \otimes B.$$

Here, $\alpha,\lambda,\rho$ and $\sigma$ are the [natural isomorphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_isomorphism) that form the [symmetric monoidal structure](wikipedia://en/3865249).

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The following [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) are examples of dagger symmetric monoidal categories:

- The [category](wikipedia://en/53759) **Rel** of [sets and relations](wikipedia://en/5930730) where the tensor is given by the [product](wikipedia://en/237868) and where the dagger of a relation is given by its relational converse.
- The [category](wikipedia://en/53759) **FdHilb** of [finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces](wikipedia://en/7185509) is a dagger symmetric monoidal category where the tensor is the usual [tensor product](wikipedia://en/30844) of Hilbert spaces and where the dagger of a [linear map](wikipedia://en/18102) is given by its [Hermitian adjoint](wikipedia://en/667175).

A dagger symmetric monoidal category that is also [compact closed](wikipedia://en/5557623) is a [dagger compact category](wikipedia://en/7155145); both of the above examples are in fact dagger compact.
