# Garlandus Compotista

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Garlandus Compotista"}

**Garlandus Compotista**, also known as **Garland the Computist**, was an early medieval logician, astronomer, and mathematician of the eleventh-century school of [Liège](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liège). Little is known of his life; the *Dialectica* published under his name by L. M. de Rijk is now commonly attributed to Gerlandus of Besançon (early 12th century).[^o2] Gerlandus was most known for his *Computus Gerlandi* a [computus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computus) that was more complex and accurate than those used in the [Early Middle Ages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Early_Middle_Ages). Using more exact mathematical and astronomical methods Garlandus changed the estimates for the [Date of birth of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Date_of_birth_of_Jesus).

:::
