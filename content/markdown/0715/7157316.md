# Attack rate

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Attack rate"}

In [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997), the **attack rate** is the proportion of an at-risk population that contracts the disease during a specified time interval.[^o2] It is used in hypothetical predictions and during actual [outbreaks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outbreaks) of disease. An at-risk population is defined as one that has no immunity to the attacking pathogen, which can be either a [novel pathogen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novel_pathogen) or an established pathogen. It is used to project the number of infections to expect during an [epidemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epidemic). This aids in marshalling resources for delivery of medical care as well as production of vaccines and/or anti-viral and anti-bacterial medicines.[^o3]

:::

The rate is arrived at by taking the number of new cases in the population at risk and dividing by the number of persons at risk in the population.[^o2]

$$\mbox{attack rate} = \frac{\mbox{number of new cases in the population at risk}}{\mbox{number of persons at risk in the population}}$$
