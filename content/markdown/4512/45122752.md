# Søren Galatius

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Søren Galatius"}

*Image: Søren Galatius Oberwolfach 2021.jpg.* Galatius at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 2021

**Søren Galatius** (born 1 August 1976) is a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danes) mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at the [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o2] He works in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), where one of his most important results concerns the [homology](wikipedia://en/142432) of the [automorphisms](wikipedia://en/1160) of [free groups](wikipedia://en/59735).[^o3] He is also known for his joint work with [Oscar Randal-Williams](wikipedia://en/61417035) on moduli spaces of manifolds, comprising several papers.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Galatius was born in [Randers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randers), Denmark. He earned his PhD from [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) in 2004 under the supervision of [Ib Madsen](wikipedia://en/38416618).[^o2] He then joined the [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) faculty, first with a temporary position as a [Szegő](wikipedia://en/46065) Assistant Professor and then two years later with a tenure-track position, eventually becoming full professor in 2011. He relocated to the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) in 2016. Since July 2025, he works as a professor at the [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o2]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2010, Galatius won the Silver Medal of the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters).[^o2] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] He was an invited speaker at the 2014 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), speaking about his joint work with [Oscar Randal-Williams](wikipedia://en/61417035).[^o2][^o8] In 2017, he won an *Elite Research Prize* from the Danish Government for his work.[^o9] In 2022 he was awarded the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) jointly with Oscar Randal-Williams.[^o10]
