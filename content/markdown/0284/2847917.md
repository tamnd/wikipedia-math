# 10,000,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="10,000,000"}

**10,000,000** (**ten million**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 9,999,999 and preceding 10,000,001.

:::

In [scientific notation](wikipedia://en/59715), it is written as 10<sup>7</sup>.

In [South Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Asia) except for [Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka), it is known as the [crore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crore).

In [Cyrillic numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyrillic_numerals), it is known as the vran (*вран* — [raven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Common_raven)).
