# 100,000,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="100,000,000"}

**100,000,000** (**one hundred million**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 99,999,999 and preceding 100,000,001.

:::

In [scientific notation](wikipedia://en/59715), it is written as 10<sup>8</sup>.

East Asian languages treat 100,000,000 as a counting unit, significant as the square of a [myriad](wikipedia://en/20088), also a counting unit. In Chinese, Korean, and Japanese respectively it is *yi* () (or in ancient texts), *eok* (억/億) and *oku* (億). These languages do not have single words for a thousand to the second, third, fifth powers, etc.

100,000,000 is also the [fourth power](wikipedia://en/665027) of [100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/100_%28number%29) and also the [square](wikipedia://en/659942) of [10,000](wikipedia://en/493491).
