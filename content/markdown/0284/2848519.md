# Algebraic semantics (mathematical logic)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algebraic semantics"}

In [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), **algebraic semantics** is a [formal semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_semantics_%28logic%29) based on algebras studied as part of [algebraic logic](wikipedia://en/7870034). For example, the [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365) S4 is characterized by the class of [topological boolean algebras](wikipedia://en/990454)—that is, boolean algebras with an [interior operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interior_operator). Other modal logics are characterized by various other algebras with operators. The class of [boolean algebras](wikipedia://en/3959) characterizes [classical propositional logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_propositional_logic), and the class of [Heyting algebras](wikipedia://en/309343) propositional [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262). [MV-algebras](wikipedia://en/2483815) are the algebraic semantics of [Łukasiewicz logic](wikipedia://en/11001950).

:::
