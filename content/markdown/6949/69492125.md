# Celia Greenwood

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Celia Greenwood"}

**Celia Margaret Theodora Greenwood** is a Canadian biostatistician specializing in [statistical genetics](wikipedia://en/1590736). She is a James McGill Professor of Oncology at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).

:::

Greenwood was born in [Victoria, British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria,_British_Columbia) and attended universities in Ontario and Quebec.[^o2] Greenwood earned a doctorate in biostatistics from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1998.[^o4] She was affiliated with [The Hospital for Sick Children](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hospital_for_Sick_Children_%28Toronto%29) and the [Dalla Lana School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalla_Lana_School_of_Public_Health) before joining the [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) faculty and the [Lady Davis Institute for Medical Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Davis_Institute_for_Medical_Research) in 2010.[^o2][^o3]

At McGill University, Greenwood became a full professor in 2017, and was named to a James McGill Professorship in 2019.[^o4] She is jointly appointed to Epidemiology, Biostatistics & Occupational Health, and is also the graduate program director of the new Quantitative Life Sciences PhD program.[^o5] In 2019, Greenwood and Karim Oualkacha together received over \$600,000 in funding from Génome Québec for their research into precision medicine in cellular epigenomics.[^o6]

Greenwood has published over 400 academic publications, which have been cited over 16,000 times, resulting in a [h-index](wikipedia://en/3604693) and [i10-index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I10-index) of 61 and 156 respectively.[^o7]
