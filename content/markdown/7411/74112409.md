# Edward Blades

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Blades"}

**Edward Blades** was a Scottish mathematician and teacher. He later became the [West Lothian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Lothian) Director of Education and was awarded an [OBE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire) for army education services during the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Edward Blades was the youngest child of Daniel and Janet Blades on 6 March 1875 in [Armadale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armadale,_West_Lothian) [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland). His father was a blacksmith. He attended Armadale Public School, where he later taught as a pupil teacher.[^o3]

He attended the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) from 1894 to 1900, graduating with a [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts_%28Scotland%29) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Natural Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_philosophy). He was later awarded a B.S.c (Pure) in 1902.[^o3]

After university, Blades attended the Training College and received a Diploma of Education at the Higher Grade as well as the Kelland memorial prize, the Ramsay memorial prize, and six additional medals.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Blades was the junior lecturer in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and Science and [Geography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geography) assistant at [Moray House Training College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moray_House_School_of_Education_and_Sport). He also lectured honours [Geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology) at [Heriot-Watt College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University). He then became the head of [George Watson's Ladies' College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Watson's_College) mathematics department. In 1905 he joined the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940).[^o3] From 1919, he was the Executive Officer to [West Lothian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Lothian) Education Authority until 1930 when he became the Director of Education for the West Lothian Education Committee (precursors to the current West Lothian Council.)[^o6] He retired from this post in 1939. He was then appointed the secretary of the Edinburgh University extra Mural Committee.[^o3]

During World War 2, although retired, Blades championed the *University Extra-Mural Committee* for civilian adult education.[^o3] For his service he was awarded an honorary [Bachelors of Law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Laws) from the University of Edinburgh as well as an [O.B.E. (Civil Division)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1948_New_Year_Honours) for Army Education services in 1948.[^o4][^o8]

Blades died in an Edinburgh nursing home on 21 November 1953.[^o8]
