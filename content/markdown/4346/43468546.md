*Image: Chamfered and truncated square tilings.svg.* The [truncated square tiling](wikipedia://en/2864244) is drawn over an equilateral chamfered square tiling.

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **chamfered square tiling** or **semitruncated square tiling** is a tiling of the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410). It is a [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) with each edge [chamfered](wikipedia://en/43468103) into new hexagonal faces.

:::

It can also be seen as the intersection of two [truncated square tilings](wikipedia://en/2864244) with offset positions. And its appearance is similar to a truncated square tiling, except only half of the vertices have been truncated, leading to its descriptive name *semitruncated square tiling*.

## Usage and Names in tiling patterns {#Usage_and_Names_in_tiling_patterns}

In floor tiling, this pattern with small squares has been labeled as *Metro Broadway Matte* and *alternate corner square tile*.[^o6]

With large squares it has been called a *Dijon tile pattern*.[^o6]

As 3 rows of rectangles, it has been called a *basketweave tiling* and *triple block tile pattern*.[^o7][^o6]

## Variations {#Variations}

Variations can be seen in different degrees of *truncation*. As well, geometric variations exist within a given symmetry. The second row shows the tilings with a 45 degree rotation which also look a little different.

Lower symmetry forms are related to the [cairo pentagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2957180) with axial edges expanded into [rectangles](wikipedia://en/70048).

## Semikis square tiling {#Semikis_square_tiling}

The dual tiling looks like a square tiling with half of the squares divided into central triangles. It can be called a semikis square tiling, as alternate squares with [kis operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kis_operator) applied. It can be seen as 4 sets of parallel lines.
