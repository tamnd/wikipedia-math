::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Expanded icosidodecahedron"}

The **expanded icosidodecahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed as an [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023). It has 122 faces: 20 triangles, 60 squares, 12 pentagons, and 30 rhombs. The 120 vertices exist at two sets of 60, with a slightly different distance from its center.

:::

It can also be constructed as a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486).

## Dissection {#Dissection}

This polyhedron can be dissected into a central [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025) surrounded by: 30 [rhombic prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombic_prism), 20 [tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra), 12 [pentagonal pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128461), 60 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584).
