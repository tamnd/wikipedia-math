# NUTS statistical regions of Belgium

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NUTS statistical regions of Belgium"}

The **Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics** (NUTS) is a [geocode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geocode) [standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standardization) for referencing the [subdivisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Country_subdivision) of [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) for statistical purposes.[^o2] The standard is developed and regulated by the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union).[^o3] The NUTS standard is instrumental in delivering the European Union's [Structural Funds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structural_Fund). The NUTS code for Belgium is *BE* and a hierarchy of three levels is established by [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat). Below these is a further levels of geographic organisation - the local administrative unit (LAU). In Belgium, the LAUs are [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Belgium).

:::

## Overall {#Overall}

### NUTS Levels {#NUTS_Levels}

::: {#o6 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| [NUTS 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_level_NUTS_of_the_European_Union#Belgium) | [Regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regions_of_Belgium) (*Gewesten/Régions*) | 3 |
| NUTS 2 | [Provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Provinces_of_Belgium) (*Provincies/Provinces*) (+ [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels)) | 11 |
| NUTS 3 | [Arrondissements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissements_of_Belgium) (*Arrondissementen/Arrondissements*) ([Verviers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrondissement_of_Verviers) split into two) | 44 |

:::

### Local administrative units {#Local_administrative_units}

Below the NUTS levels, the two LAU (Local Administrative Units) levels are:

::: {#o9 .table cue=table}

| Level | Subdivisions | \# |
|---|---|---|
| LAU 1 | — (same as NUTS 3) | 44 |
| LAU 2 | [Municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Belgium) (*Gemeenten/Communes*) | 589 |

:::

*The LAU codes of Belgium can be downloaded here:* <http://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/ramon/nuts/excel_files/BE_LAU_2007.xls>
