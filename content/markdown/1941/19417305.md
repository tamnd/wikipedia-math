# Burton Rodin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Burton Rodin"}

**Burton Rodin** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his research in [conformal mappings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_mapping) and [Riemann surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_surfaces). He is a professor emeritus at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Rodin received a Ph.D. at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1961. His thesis, titled *Reproducing Formulas on Riemann Surfaces*, was written under the supervision of [Leo Sario](wikipedia://en/40944268).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

He was a professor at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) from 1970 to 1994. He was chair of the Mathematics Department from 1977 to 1981, and became professor emeritus in June 1994.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Rodin's 1968 work on extremal length of Riemann surfaces, together with an observation of [Mikhail Katz](wikipedia://en/25830095), yielded the first [systolic geometry](wikipedia://en/2545455) inequality for surfaces independent of their genus.[^o8][^o9]

In 1980, Rodin and [Stefan E. Warschawski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_E._Warschawski) solved the [Visser–Ostrowski problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visser–Ostrowski_problem) for derivatives of conformal mappings at the boundary.[^o10] In 1987 he proved the [Thurston conjecture for circle packings](wikipedia://en/16648043#History), jointly with [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608).[^o11]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Rodin was elected fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13]
