# Multiplicative character

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Multiplicative character"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **multiplicative character** (or **linear character**, or simply **character**) on a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G* is a [group homomorphism](wikipedia://en/12396) from *G* to the [multiplicative group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_of_units) of a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) (Artin 1966), usually the field of [complex numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_numbers). If *G* is any group, then the [set](wikipedia://en/26691) Ch(*G*) of these morphisms forms an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) under pointwise multiplication.

:::

This group is referred to as the [character group](wikipedia://en/1384568) of *G*. Sometimes only *unitary* characters are considered (characters whose [image](wikipedia://en/579311) is in the [unit circle](wikipedia://en/27072071)); other such homomorphisms are then called *quasi-characters*. [Dirichlet characters](wikipedia://en/174515) can be seen as a special case of this definition.

Multiplicative characters are [linearly independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linearly_independent), i.e. if $\chi_1, \chi_2, \ldots, \chi_n$ are different characters on a group *G* then $a_1\chi_1 + a_2\chi_2 + \cdots + a_n\chi_n = 0$ only when $a_1 = a_2 = \cdots = a_n = 0.$

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- Consider the (*ax* + *b*)-group

$$G := \left\{ \left. \begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\ \right|\ a > 0,\ b \in \mathbf{R} \right\}.$$

Functions *f*<sub>*u*</sub> : *G* → **C** such that $f_u \left(\begin{pmatrix} a & b \\ 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}\right)=a^u,$ where *u* ranges over complex numbers **C** are multiplicative characters.

- Consider the multiplicative group of positive [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers) (**R**<sup>\+</sup>,·). Then functions *f*<sub>*u*</sub> : (**R**<sup>\+</sup>,·) → **C** such that *f*<sub>*u*</sub>(*a*) = *a*<sup>*u*</sup>, where *a* is an element of (**R**<sup>\+</sup>, ·) and *u* ranges over complex numbers **C**, are multiplicative characters.

:::
