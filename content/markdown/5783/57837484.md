# Minoru Tomita

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minoru Tomita"}

**Minoru Tomita** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who made significant contributions to the theory of [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987).

:::

He became deaf at the age of two. He was described as a "very singular" personality.[^o3]

Although he published relatively little, his 1967 manuscript on the theory of modular automorphisms of [von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185) was of major importance in the field. The manuscript was difficult to understand, but [Masamichi Takesaki](wikipedia://en/41007469) was able to revise it. In the summer of 1967 Takesaki communicated the results to [Jacques Dixmier](wikipedia://en/9169420),[^o4] and they became a major influence on the work of his student [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340) on the classification of type III factors.[^o5] The theory later became known as [Tomita-Takesaki theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomita-Takesaki_theory).
