# Sigekatu Kuroda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sigekatu Kuroda"}

**Sigekatu Kuroda** was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636).

:::

In 1942 he became a professor at the newly founded [Nagoya Imperial University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University#History), where he stayed for over twenty years. He was responsible for much of the effort in setting up its Department of Mathematics.[^o4]

He was married to the renowned number theorist [Teiji Takagi](wikipedia://en/174754)'s daughter Yakeo. The couple had three sons, all of whom became mathematicians, including [S.-Y. Kuroda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S.-Y._Kuroda), who was a professor of [linguistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics) at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).[^o5]

He published a text on the foundations of [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) with [Tomio Kubota](wikipedia://en/19487263) in 1963.
