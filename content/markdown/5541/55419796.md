# Geometry and the Imagination

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Geometry and the Imagination"}

***Geometry and the Imagination*** is the English translation of the 1932 book Anschauliche Geometrie by [David Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) and [Stefan Cohn-Vossen](wikipedia://en/3204307).[^o3]

:::

The book was based on a series of lectures Hilbert made in the winter of 1920–21. The book is an attempt to present some then-current mathematical thought to "contribute to a more just appreciation of mathematics by a wider range of people than just the specialists."[^o4] It differentiates between two tendencies in mathematics and any other scientific research: on the one hand, toward abstraction and logical relations, correlating the subject matter in a systematic and orderly manner, and on the other hand an intuitive approach, which moves toward a more immediate grasp of and a "live rapport" with the same material. Further he asserts that intuitive understanding actually plays a major role for the researcher as well as anyone who wishes to study and appreciate Geometry.[^o5]

## Contents {#Contents}

Topics covered by the chapters in the book include the [Leibniz formula for $\pi$](wikipedia://en/1301559), [configurations of points and lines](wikipedia://en/7200539) with equally many points on each line and equally many lines through each point, [curvature](wikipedia://en/60770) and [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610), [mechanical linkages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linkage_%28mechanical%29), the classification of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) by their [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420), and the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949).[^o8]

## Response {#Response}

The [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) said about the book, "this book is a masterpiece — a delightful classic that should never go out of print".[^o8] [Physics Today](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics_Today) called it "a readable exposition of modern geometry and its relation to other branches of mathematics".[^o9] [The Scientific Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Scientific_Monthly) said about it "has been a classic for twenty years . . . Although it deals with elementary topics, it reaches the fringe of our knowledge in many directions".[^o10]
