# Number: The Language of Science

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Number: The Language of Science"}

***Number: The Language of Science: A Critical Survey Written for the Cultured Non-Mathematician*** is a [popular mathematics](wikipedia://en/2069923) book by [Tobias Dantzig](wikipedia://en/1234390). The original U.S. publication was by Macmillan in 1930.[^o3] A second edition (third impression) was published in 1947 in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague) by Melantrich Company. The book recounts the [history of mathematical ideas](wikipedia://en/14220).[^o4]

:::

## Chapters {#Chapters}

The book has 12 chapters. There is an appendix of illustrations. The third edition contains a separate section for essays, at the book's end.

1. Fingerprints
1. The Empty Column
1. Number Lore
1. The Last Number
1. Symbols
1. The Unutterable
1. This Flowing World
1. The Act of Becoming
1. Filling the Gaps
1. The Domain of Number
1. The Anatomy of the Infinite
1. The Two Realities
