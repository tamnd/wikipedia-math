# Julio Ríos Gallego

Rios

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Julio Ríos Gallego"}

**Julio Alberto Ríos Gallego** ([Cali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cali), 22 March 1973) is a [Colombian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colombian_people) [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer), lecturer, mentor, [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of Mathematics and Physics.

:::

## Early life, education and family {#Early_life,_education_and_family}

Born in Cali, he attended elementary and high school at Colegio Lacordaire, being the best student of his promotion.[^o4][^o5] He studied civil engineering at Universidad del Valle. He is married and father of two daughters and currently lives in Cali.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

He has been professor in colleges and universities, such as "Colegio Hebreo Jorge Isaacs" the "*Corporación Universitaria Minuto de Dios*" and the Faculty of Medicine [Universidad San Martín](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_San_Martín) in Cali. Trying to motivate his students, on 6 April 2009 he went to the internet, creating virtual classes with the Julioprofe channel. His channel has 4.2 million subscribers in [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube), and more than 500 million views[^o8][^o9]

Known as **Julioprofe**, he appeared in [e-learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E-learning) videos of [free education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_to_education) in the areas of: [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [trigonometry](wikipedia://en/18717261), [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202), [computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) and higher mathematics. He uses a teaching technique where he is a professor [Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mentoring) and the student can learn from their [computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer), [smartphone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smartphone), [iPod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IPod), at home at any time.[^o10]

Since September 2011, he is part of the educational project *Academia Vasquez*, responsible for video production of algebra and representative of Colombia said educational project.[^o11]

In February 2013, he signed an agreement with the *"Fundación Transformemos"* (winner of the [UNESCO Confucius Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO_Confucius_Prize_for_Literacy) 2012), to produce videos of math[^o12]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

He was nominated in "Best Leaders of Colombia Awards in 2012"[^o14][^o15]

He was nominated the "Revolutionary Award" in 2013 in Latinos Innovation category, for his contribution to internet education, and U.S. recognition that use [social networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_networks) to generate positive changes.[^o16]
