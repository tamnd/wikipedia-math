# Communications in Mathematical Physics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Communications in Mathematical Physics"}

***Communications in Mathematical Physics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_%28publisher%29). The journal publishes papers in all fields of [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), but focuses particularly in [analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29) related to [condensed matter physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condensed_matter_physics), [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267), and in [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987), [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) and [relativity](wikipedia://en/30001).[^o3]

:::

## History {#History}

[Rudolf Haag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Haag) conceived this journal with [Res Jost](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Res_Jost), and Haag became the Founding Chief Editor. The first issue of *Communications in Mathematical Physics* appeared in 1965. Haag guided the journal for the next eight years. Then [Klaus Hepp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Hepp) succeeded him for three years, followed by [James Glimm](wikipedia://en/6901985), for another three years. [Arthur Jaffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Jaffe) began as chief editor in 1979 and served for 21 years. [Michael Aizenman](wikipedia://en/22411175) became the fifth chief editor in the year 2000 and served in this role until 2012. The current [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Horng-Tzer Yau](wikipedia://en/15917356).[^o5]

## Archives {#Archives}

Articles from 1965 to 1997 are available in electronic form free of charge, via [Project Euclid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Euclid), a [non-profit organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-profit_organization) initiated by [Cornell University Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University_Library).[^o7] This portion of the journal is provided through the Electronic Mathematical Archiving Network Initiative (EMANI)[^o8][^o9] to support the long-term electronic preservation of mathematical publications.
