# David W. Henderson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David W. Henderson"}

**David Wilson Henderson** (February 23, 1939 – December 20, 2018) was a professor of Mathematics in the Department of Mathematics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). His work ranges from the study of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) and [exploratory mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exploratory_mathematics) for teaching prospective mathematics teachers. His papers in the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439) place him with the [intuitionist school of philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/19513).[^o3][^o4] His practical geometry, which he put to work and discovered in his carpentry work, gives a perspective of geometry as the understanding of the infinite spaces through local properties.[^o5] [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417) is seen in his work as extendable to the spherical and hyperbolic spaces starting with the study and reformulation of the [5th postulate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5th_postulate).[^o5][^o6]

:::

He was struck by an automobile in a pedestrian crosswalk on December 19, 2018, and died the next day from his injuries.[^o7]
