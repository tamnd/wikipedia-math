# Total fertility rate in England by county / unitary authority

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Total fertility rate in England by county / unitary authority"}

This article lists the total fertility rate in England's counties or [unitary authorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unitary_authorities). In 2024, the [fertility rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_fertility_rate) in [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) and Wales fell to 1.41, the lowest on record since records began in 1938.

:::

The **total fertility rate (TFR)** is “the average number of live children a group of women would have if they experienced the age-specific fertility rates for the calendar year in question throughout their childbearing lifespan.”[^o2]

## Total fertility rate by county {#Total_fertility_rate_by_county}

Figures below are for **2024** and cover **England only** (Welsh local authorities are excluded). Data are drawn from the [ONS](wikipedia://en/324029) dataset *Births in England and Wales: birth registrations (2024 edition)* and correspond to the *Total fertility rate (TFR)* by local authority of usual residence of the mother.[^o4]
