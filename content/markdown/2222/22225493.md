# Arithmetic for Parents

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arithmetic for Parents"}

***Arithmetic for Parents*** (Sumizdat, 2007, ) is a book about [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) aimed at parents and teachers.[^o3][^o4]

:::

The author, [Ron Aharoni](wikipedia://en/22744244), is a professor of mathematics at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology); he wrote the book based on his experiences teaching [elementary mathematics](wikipedia://en/1775329) to Israeli schoolchildren.[^o4] The book was originally written in Hebrew and was translated to English, Portuguese and Dutch.
