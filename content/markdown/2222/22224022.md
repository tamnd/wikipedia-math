# Kefeng Liu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kefeng Liu"}

**Kefeng Liu** (; born 12 December 1965), is a Chinese-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is known for his contributions to [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), particularly the geometry, topology and analysis of [moduli spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_spaces) of [Riemann surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_surfaces) and [Calabi–Yau manifolds](wikipedia://en/188932). He is a professor of mathematics at [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), as well as the [executive director](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Executive_director) of the [Center of Mathematical Sciences at Zhejiang University](wikipedia://en/17806764). He is best known for his collaboration with Bong Lian and [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137) in which they establish some [enumerative geometry](wikipedia://en/3069092) conjectures motivated by [mirror symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_symmetry).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Liu was born in [Kaifeng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaifeng), [Henan province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henan_Province), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China). In 1985, Liu received his [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in mathematics from the Department of Mathematics of [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing). In 1988, Liu obtained his [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) from the Institute of Mathematics of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in Beijing.[^o5] Liu then went to study in the United States, obtaining a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1993 under [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137).[^o4]

From 1993 to 1996, Liu was [C. L. E. Moore Instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). From 1996 to 2000, Liu was an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). Liu joined the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) faculty in 2000, where he was promoted to full professor in 2002.[^o5] In September 2003, Liu was appointed as the head of [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University)'s mathematics department.[^o6] Liu is currently the executive director of the Center of Mathematical Sciences at Zhejiang University.
