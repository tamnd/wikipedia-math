# Cemal Yıldırım

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cemal Yıldırım"}

**Cemal Yıldırım** (1925–2009) was a Turkish [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), author, educator, and researcher. He wrote 15 books (4 in English and 11 in Turkish) and numerous academic papers.

:::

He wrote extensively on the [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science) and the [history of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science).[^o2] His popular books *[Bilimin Öncüleri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneers_of_Science)* ("Pioneers of Science") and *Evrim Kuramı ve Bagnazlık* ("[Theory of Evolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evolution) and Dogmatism") were widely read and discussed in [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey). His book *Matematiksel Düşünme* ("Mathematical Thinking") was also very influential.

He was the father of the mathematician [Cem Yıldırım](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cem_Yıldırım).
