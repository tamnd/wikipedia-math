# Multiscale geometric analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Multiscale geometric analysis"}

**Multiscale geometric analysis** or **geometric multiscale analysis** is an emerging area of high-dimensional [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324) and [data analysis](wikipedia://en/2720954).
