# Carlos Gustavo Moreira

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carlos Gustavo Moreira"}

**Carlos Gustavo Tamm de Araújo Moreira** (born 8 February 1973) is a Brazilian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). Moreira is currently a researcher at the [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708) (IMPA), where he goes by the nickname "Gugu".[^o3][^o4] He is also a member of the Brazilian Mathematical Olympiad Commission, a fanatic fan of the Brazilian football team [Flamengo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clube_de_Regatas_do_Flamengo) and a member of the [Brazilian Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Communist_Party) (PCB).

:::

Moreira obtained his Ph.D. from [IMPA](wikipedia://en/2334708) under the supervision of [Jacob Palis](wikipedia://en/1540070) in 1993, at the age of 20.[^o4][^o5] He is a member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences) since 2008.[^o3] In 2009 he was awarded the UMALCA Award for his contributions to mathematics.[^o4]

Moreira is the recipient of the [TWAS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS_Prize) in Mathematics in 2010.[^o6] He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) of 2014 in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul), [South Korea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Korea),[^o7][^o8] and a plenary speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil).[^o9]
