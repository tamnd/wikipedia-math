# Artur Oscar Lopes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Artur Oscar Lopes"}

**Artur Oscar Lopes** (born 17 October 1950 in the city of [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro)) is a [Brazilian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilians) [writer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writer)[^o3] and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986).[^o4] He is a professor at [UFRGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidade_Federal_do_Rio_Grande_do_Sul), [Porto Alegre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porto_Alegre).[^o5]

:::

He earned his Ph.D. from the [IMPA](wikipedia://en/2334708) in 1977 under the supervision of [Jacob Palis](wikipedia://en/1540070).

He is a recipient of [Brazil's National Order of Scientific Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Scientific_Merit_%28Brazil%29) in mathematics.[^o7] Since 2007 he has been a member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]

He is the author of the textbooks *Tópicos de Mecânica Clássica*, *Cálculo Tensorial*, *Teoria dos Corpos*, and *Equações Diferenciais Ordinárias*.
