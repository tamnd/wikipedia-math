# Free product of associative algebras

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Free product of associative algebras"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), the **free product** (**[coproduct](wikipedia://en/310889)**) of a family of [associative algebras](wikipedia://en/2112) $A_i, i \in I$ over a [commutative](wikipedia://en/61346) [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* is the associative algebra over *R* that is, roughly, defined by the generators and the relations of the $A_i$'s. The free product of two algebras *A*, *B* is denoted by *A* ∗ *B*. The notion is a [ring-theoretic](wikipedia://en/250424) analog of a [free product](wikipedia://en/360036) of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447).

:::

In the [category of commutative *R*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category_of_commutative_algebras), the free product of two algebras (in that [category](wikipedia://en/53759)) is their [tensor product](wikipedia://en/372395).

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

We first define a free product of two algebras. Let *A* and *B* be algebras over a commutative ring *R*. Consider their [tensor algebra](wikipedia://en/363628), the direct sum of all possible finite tensor products of *A*, *B*; explicitly, $T = \bigoplus_{n=1}^{\infty} T_n$ where

$$T_1 = A \oplus B, \,\, T_2 = (A \otimes A) \oplus (A \otimes B) \oplus (B \otimes A) \oplus (B \otimes B), \, \dots$$

We then set

$$A * B = T/I$$

where *I* is the two-sided [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) generated by elements of the form

$$a \otimes a' - a a', \, b \otimes b' - bb', \, 1_A - 1_B.$$

It is then straightforward to verify that the above construction possesses the [universal property](wikipedia://en/32245) of a coproduct.

A finite free product is defined similarly.

:::
