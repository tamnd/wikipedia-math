# Eleonor Harboure

Harboure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eleonor Harboure"}

**Eleonor** "**Pola**" **Ofelia Harboure de Aguilera** (15 June 1948 – 15 January 2022), who published professionally as **Eleonor Harboure**, was a mathematician from [Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina) who was the first woman president of [Unión Matemática Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unión_Matemática_Argentina) (UMA), the Argentinian mathematical professional society. Harboure also served as the UMA's secretary.[^o2]

:::

Harboure earned a PhD from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 1978. Her PhD advisor was Nestor Marcelo Riviere and her dissertation work was in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). She had 8 PhD students of her own.
