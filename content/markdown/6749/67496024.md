# Rudolf Rothe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Rothe"}

*Image: Rudolf Ernst Rothe.jpg.* Rudolf Ernst Rothe

**Rudolf Ernst Rothe** (15 October 1873 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) – 26 October 1942 in Berlin)[^o2] was a German applied mathematician.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

After graduating from the Sophien-Gymnasium in Berlin, Rothe studied from 1892 under [Hermann Amandus Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Amandus_Schwarz), [Johannes Knoblauch](wikipedia://en/67471826), [Lazarus Immanuel Fuchs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazarus_Immanuel_Fuchs), and [Ferdinand Georg Frobenius](wikipedia://en/341048)[^o5] at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin). There in 1897 he received his [*Promotion*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) (Ph.D.) with dissertation *Untersuchungen über die Theorie der isothermal Flächen* (Investigations on the theory of isothermal surfaces) supervised by Schwarz. In 1905 Rothe completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the *Technische Hochschule Berlin-Charlottenburg* (now [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin)). He was a professor from 1908 to 1914 at the [Clausthal University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clausthal_University_of_Technology) (TU Claustral) and from 1914 to 1915 at the *Technische Hochschule Hanover* ([University of Hanover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hanover)), where he founded the Institute for Applied Mathematics. From 1915 he was a professor at *Technische Hochschule Berlin*, where he was rector in 1921. After retiring in 1939, he was an honorary senator of Technische Universität Berlin.

His research deals with differential geometry and function theory. During WW I, he did research on [ballistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ballistics) and on determining the trajectories for the projectiles fired from the "[Paris gun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_gun)" (which in 1918 was fired at Paris from 120 kilometers away).[^o6][^o7]

In 1926 he was elected a member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o8]

In 1930 he was president of the [*Deutsche Mathematiker-Vereinigung*](wikipedia://en/30864973). From 1916 to 1918 and from 1939 to 1941 he was chair of the Berlin Mathematical Society, of which he was a founding member.

After the death of [Johannes Knoblauch](wikipedia://en/67471826), Rothe completed the editing and publishing of the collected works of [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459). Rothe was co-editor with [Felix Auerbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Auerbach) of the *Taschenbuch für Mathematiker und Physiker* (Pocket-sized book for mathematicians and physicists), published by *[B. G. Teubner Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._G._Teubner_Verlag)* in 1911[^o9] and 1913[^o10][^o11] (and first published in 1909 by Auerbach alone).
