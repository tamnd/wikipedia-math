# Chester Ittner Bliss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chester Ittner Bliss"}

**Chester Ittner Bliss** (February 1, 1899 – March 14, 1979) was an American [biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biologist), who is best known for his contributions to [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). He was born in [Springfield, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springfield,_Ohio) in 1899 and died in 1979. He was the first secretary of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society).

:::

## Academic qualifications {#Academic_qualifications}

- [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in [Entomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entomology) from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), 1921
- [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), 1922
- [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), 1926

Remarkably, his statistical knowledge was largely self-taught and developed according to the problems he wanted to solve (Cochran & Finney 1979). Nevertheless, in 1942 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o4]

## Major contributions {#Major_contributions}

The idea of the [probit](wikipedia://en/1689101) function was published by Bliss in a 1934 article in *[Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_%28journal%29)* on how to treat data such as the percentage of a pest killed by a [pesticide](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pesticide).[^o6] Bliss proposed transforming the percentage killed into a "**prob**ability un**it**" (or "probit").

Arguably his most important contribution was the development, with [Ronald Fisher](wikipedia://en/140824), of an iterative approach to finding [maximum likelihood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_likelihood) estimates in the [probit](wikipedia://en/1689101) method of [bioassay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioassay). Additional contributions in [biological assay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_assay) were work on the analysis of time-mortality data and of slope-ratio assays (Cochran & Finney 1979).

Bliss introduced the word [rankit](wikipedia://en/543116), meaning an [expected](wikipedia://en/9653) [normal](wikipedia://en/21462) [order statistic](wikipedia://en/160986).
