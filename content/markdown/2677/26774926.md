# Fritz John conditions

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fritz John conditions"}

The **Fritz John conditions** (abbr. **FJ conditions**), in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), are a [necessary condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Necessary_and_sufficient_conditions) for a solution in [nonlinear programming](wikipedia://en/679709) to be [optimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29).[^o2] They are used as lemma in the proof of the [Karush–Kuhn–Tucker conditions](wikipedia://en/2397362), but they are relevant on their own.

:::

We consider the following [optimization problem](wikipedia://en/1126536):

$$\begin{aligned} \text{minimize } & f(x) \, \\ \text{subject to: } & g_i(x) \le 0,\ i \in \left \{1,\dots,m \right \}\\ & h_j(x) = 0, \ j \in \left \{m+1,\dots,n \right \} \end{aligned}$$

where *ƒ* is the [function](wikipedia://en/185427) to be minimized, $g_i$ the inequality [constraints](wikipedia://en/7418540) and $h_j$ the equality constraints, and where, respectively, $\mathcal{I}$, $\mathcal{A}$ and $\mathcal{E}$ are the [indices](wikipedia://en/23624339) [sets](wikipedia://en/26691) of inactive, active and equality constraints and $x^*$ is an optimal solution of $f$, then there exists a non-zero vector $\lambda=[\lambda_0, \lambda _1, \lambda _2,\dots,\lambda _n]$ such that:

$$\begin{cases} \lambda_0 \nabla f(x^*) + \sum\limits_{i\in \mathcal{A}} \lambda_i \nabla g_i(x^*) + \sum\limits_{i\in \mathcal{E}} \lambda_i \nabla h_i (x^*) =0\\[10pt] \lambda_i \ge 0,\ i\in \mathcal{A}\cup\{0\} \\[10pt] \exists i\in \left( \{0,1,\ldots ,n\} \backslash \mathcal{I} \right) \left( \lambda_i \ne 0 \right) \end{cases}$$

$\lambda_0>0$ [if](wikipedia://en/14922) the $\nabla g_i (i\in\mathcal{A})$ and $\nabla h_i (i\in\mathcal{E})$ are [linearly independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linearly_independent) or, more generally, when a [constraint qualification](wikipedia://en/2397362#Regularity_conditions_.28or_constraint_qualifications.29) holds.

Named after [Fritz John](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fritz_John), these conditions are equivalent to the [Karush–Kuhn–Tucker conditions](wikipedia://en/2397362) in the case $\lambda_0 > 0$. When $\lambda_0=0$, the condition is equivalent to the violation of [Mangasarian–Fromovitz constraint qualification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mangasarian–Fromovitz_constraint_qualification) (MFCQ). In other words, the Fritz John condition is equivalent to the optimality condition KKT or not-MFCQ.
