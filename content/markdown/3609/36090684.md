# Ulrike Sattler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ulrike Sattler"}

**Ulrike M. Sattler** is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) in the [information management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_management) group of the [Department of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Computer_Science,_University_of_Manchester) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester)[^o3][^o4] and a visiting professor at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo).[^o18]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Sattler completed her [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1998 under the supervision of [Franz Baader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Baader) at [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University) in Germany.

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Sattler moved to the University of Manchester as [senior lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_lecturer) in 2003, was promoted to [reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29) in 2006 and then to professor in 2007.[^o22] Sattler's research focuses on logic to support [knowledge representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_representation) including [description logic](wikipedia://en/183503),[^o11][^o12] [Dynamic logic](wikipedia://en/31631871) and [Modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365). She is also investigating the [inference](wikipedia://en/317465) problems, [complexity theory](wikipedia://en/7543) and [decision procedures](wikipedia://en/8336) associated with knowledge representation. This research has been important in the development of the [Web Ontology Language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_Ontology_Language) (OWL)[^o13] and its use in [bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214) and [molecular biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_biology).[^o14][^o15][^o16]

Sattler's research has been funded by the [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council) (EPSRC).[^o17] She is the co-author of the textbook *An Introduction to Description Logic* with [Ian Horrocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Horrocks), [Franz Baader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Baader) and Carsten Lutz.[^o18]

### Academic service {#Academic_service}

Sattler serves as Editor-in-Chief of the *[Transactions on Graph Data and Knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transactions_on_Graph_Data_and_Knowledge)*, as editor of the *[Journal of Logic and Computation](wikipedia://en/27311945)*, and on the [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) of the *[Journal of Automated Reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Automated_Reasoning)*. Moreover, she has been the [chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman) for various conferences including the [International Joint Conference on Automated Reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Joint_Conference_on_Automated_Reasoning) (IJCAR).[^o20]

### Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Sattler was elected a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) (MAE) in 2014.[^o22] She was co-recipient with Bijan Parsia and Matthew Horridge of the best paper prize at the [International Semantic Web Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Semantic_Web_Conference) (ISWC) in 2008[^o23][^o24] which subsequently won the SWSA Ten Year award in 2018 for the highest impact paper from the ISWC proceedings ten years prior.[^o25]
