# Fundamental theorem of ideal theory in number fields

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fundamental theorem of ideal theory in number fields"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the **fundamental theorem of ideal theory in number fields** states that every nonzero [proper ideal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_ideal) in the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) of a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) admits unique [factorization](wikipedia://en/82341) into a product of nonzero [prime ideals](wikipedia://en/24928). In other words, every ring of integers of a number field is a [Dedekind domain](wikipedia://en/145132).

:::
