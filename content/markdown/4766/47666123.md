# Journal of Integer Sequences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Integer Sequences"}

The ***Journal of Integer Sequences*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) in mathematics, specializing in research papers about [integer sequences](wikipedia://en/51421).

:::

It was founded in 1998 by [Neil Sloane](wikipedia://en/400062).[^o5] Sloane had previously published two books on integer sequences, and in 1996 he founded the [On-Line Encyclopedia of Integer Sequences](wikipedia://en/500004) (OEIS). Needing an outlet for research papers concerning the sequences he was collecting in the OEIS, he founded the journal.[^o3][^o4] Since 2002 the journal has been hosted by the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Cheriton_School_of_Computer_Science) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), with Waterloo professor [Jeffrey Shallit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffrey_Shallit) as its editor-in-chief. There are no page charges for authors, and all papers are free to all readers. The journal publishes approximately 50–75 papers annually.[^o5]

In most years from 1999 to 2014, [SCImago Journal Rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago_Journal_Rank) has ranked the *Journal of Integer Sequences* as a third-quartile journal in [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o6] It is indexed by *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)*[^o7] and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH).[^o8]
