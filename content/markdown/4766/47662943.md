# John West (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John West"}

**John West** (1756-1817) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priest) from Scotland.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Fourth child of Samuel West and Margaret Mein, his father died in 1766. West matriculated in the [University of Saint Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Saint_Andrews) in 1769 thanks to financial help of the presbytery clerk Dr. Adamson.[^o4] He, like his brothers, studied mathematics under professor [Nicolas Vilant](wikipedia://en/46738641).[^o5]

*Image: John West (mathematician).png.* Frontpage of *Treatises* (1838)

He was assistant of professor Vilant, who had poor health, but lacking of prospects in his native land, he emigrated to [Jamaica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jamaica),[^o6] in 1784, the same year that *Elements of Mathematics* and *A System of Shorthand* were published.[^o7]

In Jamaica, West was initially teacher in the Manning's Free School at [Savanna-la-Mar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savanna-la-Mar) in [Westmoreland Parish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westmoreland_Parish).[^o8]

In 1790 he was appointed rector of [Saint Thomas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Thomas_Parish,_Jamaica) in [Morant Bay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morant_Bay), where he died in 1817.

During his life in Jamaica he never left his mathematical studies, despite his academic isolation.[^o9]

Before his death, he sent the manuscript of *Mathematical Treatises* to his old student John Leslie, who published it in 1838.
