# Stephen Gelbart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen Gelbart"}

**Stephen Samuel Gelbart** (סטיבן סמואל גלברט; born June 12, 1946) is an American-Israeli mathematician who holds the Nicki and J. Ira Harris Professorial Chair in mathematics at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science) in Israel.[^o3] He was named a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013 "for contributions to the development and dissemination of the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905)."[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gelbart was born in [Syracuse, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse,_New_York), son of the mathematician [Abe Gelbart](wikipedia://en/38968041). He graduated from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1967,[^o3] and earned a Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1970, with a dissertation on [Fourier analysis](wikipedia://en/11659) supervised by [Elias M. Stein](wikipedia://en/418073). He returned to Cornell as an assistant professor in 1971, was promoted to full professor in 1980, moved to the Weizmann Institute in 1984, and was given his named chair in 1998. He was president of the [Israel Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/59341674) from 1994 to 1996.[^o3] His doctoral students include [Erez Lapid](wikipedia://en/48314214).
