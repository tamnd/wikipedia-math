# Andreas Thom (mathematician)

*Image: AndreasThom.jpg.* Thom at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2013

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andreas Thom"}

**Andreas Thom** is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working on [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164), [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) of group actions, and [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987).[^o2]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Thom received in 2000 his [Certificate of Advanced Study in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Certificate_of_Advanced_Study_in_Mathematics) from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). In 2003 he obtained his doctorate ([Promotion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29)) from the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) with thesis advisor [Joachim Cuntz](wikipedia://en/38543091) and thesis *Connective E-Theory and Bivariant Homology for C\*-Algebra*. He was a Postdoc 2003–2005 at the University of Münster, and 2005–2007 at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). From 2007 to 2009 he was a junior professor for *Geometrical Aspects of Pure Mathematics* at the University of Göttingen. After being promoted to assistant professor in Göttingen, he moved in 2009 to become a full professor for *Theoretical Mathematics* at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig). In 2014 he moved to a full professorship in geometry at [TU Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Dresden).[^o5]

His work with Gábor Kun laid the foundations for the discovery of a [non-sofic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-sofic_group). Prior to this discovery their approach was not considered as the main line of attack on the problem, and approaches involving [quantum games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_game_theory) were considered more promising.[^o6][^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- In 2011, Thom received an ERC Starting Grant No. 277728 *Geometry and Analysis of Group Rings*.[^o9]
- In 2016, he received an ERC Consolidator Grant No. 681207 *Groups, Dynamics, and Approximation*.[^o10]
- In 2018, Thom was an invited speaker for a talk *Finitary approximations of groups and their applications* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro.
