# Emmanuel Breuillard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Emmanuel Breuillard"}

**Emmanuel F. Breuillard** (born 25 June 1977) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] He was the [Sadleirian Professor of Pure Mathematics](wikipedia://en/291556)[^o4] in the [Department of Pure Mathematics and Mathematical Statistics](wikipedia://en/3254271#Pure_Mathematics_and_Mathematical_Statistics) (DPMMS) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and is now Professor of Pure Mathematics at the [Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford](wikipedia://en/7905850) as of January 1, 2022.[^o5] He had previously been professor at [Paris-Sud 11 University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_11_University).

:::

In 2012, he won an [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) for his contributions to [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and other fields. His area of research has been in group theoretic aspects of geometry, number theory and combinatorics. In 2014 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul). He was elected a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2021[^o6] and a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2024.[^o7] In 2024–25, he was the first holder of the [Jacques Tits Chair](https://bms.ulb.ac.be/bms-prizes/) founded by the [Belgian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/71437283).
