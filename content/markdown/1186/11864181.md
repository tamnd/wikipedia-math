# Shunpei Ueyama

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shunpei Ueyama"}

**Shunpei Ueyama** was a [Japanese philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_philosopher) associated with the [postwar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postwar) [Kyoto School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_School).[^o2] He graduated in [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) from [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) in 1943, and trained in a [kamikaze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamikaze) human torpedo (*gyorai*:魚雷) squad. His main professional interest in philosophy were in the fields of logic, and American [pragmatism](wikipedia://en/95170), especially with its founding fathers [Charles Sanders Peirce](wikipedia://en/6117), [William James](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_James) and [John Dewey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dewey). He was [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) at Kyoto University.[^o3]

:::
