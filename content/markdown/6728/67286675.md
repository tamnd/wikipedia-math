# Geodesic circle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geodesic circle"}

A **geodesic circle** is either "the [locus](wikipedia://en/542465) on a [surface](wikipedia://en/4342970) at a constant [geodesic](wikipedia://en/91096) [distance](wikipedia://en/39378) from a fixed [point](wikipedia://en/593693)" or a [curve](wikipedia://en/89246) of constant [geodesic curvature](wikipedia://en/483526).[^o2] A **geodesic disk** is the region on a surface bounded by a geodesic circle. In contrast with the ordinary [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) and [disk](wikipedia://en/329542), the geodesic circle is not necessarily a [plane curve](wikipedia://en/971691) and the geodesic disk is not necessarily a [planar surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planar_surface). They can be used to define *[Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623#Alternative_formulas)*.

:::
