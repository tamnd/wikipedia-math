# Institute of Mathematics (National Academy of Sciences of Belarus)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institute of Mathematics"}

*Image: !Уваход у Інстытут матэматыкі (Менск, 20 красавіка 2019 г.).jpg.* Institute of Mathematics, main entrance. Minsk, 2019

The **Institute of Mathematics** of the [National Academy of Sciences of Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences_of_Belarus) is headquartered in [Minsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minsk), with a division in [Gomel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gomel).

:::

## History {#History}

The institute was stablished in 1959 on the basis of mathematical laboratories and the laboratory of electronic computers of the Institute of Physics and Mathematics of the [BSSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BSSR_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3] Initially its name was the Institute of Mathematics and Computer Technics of the Academy of Sciences of the BSSR.[^o4]

## Departments {#Departments}

the Institute has the following departments:[^o3]

- Algebra
- Computational Mathematics and Mathematical Modelling
- Differential Equations
- Finite Croup Theory and Applications (in Gomel)
- Nonlinear and Stochastic Analysis

## Directors {#Directors}

- Nikolay Yerugin (1959-1977)[^o7]
- [Vladimir Platonov](wikipedia://en/905909) (1977-1992)
- Ivan Gaishun (1992-2018)[^o8]
