# Henk van der Vorst

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henk van der Vorst"}

van der Vorst **Hendrik "Henk" Albertus van der Vorst** (born 5 May 1944, [Venlo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venlo))[^o2] is a Dutch mathematician and [Emeritus Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_Professor) of [Numerical Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_Analysis) at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University). According to the [Institute for Scientific Information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Scientific_Information) (ISI), his paper[^o3] on the [BiCGSTAB](wikipedia://en/25694537) method was the [most cited](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher) paper in the field of mathematics in the 1990s.[^o4] He is a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (KNAW) since 2002[^o5] and the [Netherlands Academy of Technology and Innovation](http://www.acti-nl.org/).[^o6] In 2006 he was awarded a knighthood of the [Order of the Netherlands Lion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Netherlands_Lion).[^o7] Henk van der Vorst is a Fellow of [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM).[^o8]

:::

His major contributions include [preconditioned](wikipedia://en/5165246) iterative methods, in particular the ICCG (incomplete Cholesky conjugate gradient) method (developed together with Koos Meijerink), a version of [preconditioned conjugate gradient method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preconditioned_conjugate_gradient_method),[^o9][^o10] the [BiCGSTAB](wikipedia://en/25694537)[^o3] and (together with Kees Vuik) GMRESR[^o11] [Krylov subspace](wikipedia://en/2542615) methods and (together with Gerard Sleijpen) the Jacobi-Davidson method[^o12] for solving ordinary, generalized, and [nonlinear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_eigenproblem) [eigenproblems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue,_eigenvector_and_eigenspace). He has analyzed convergence behavior of the [conjugate gradient](wikipedia://en/1448821)[^o13] and [Lanczos](wikipedia://en/2593852) methods. He has also developed a number of [preconditioners](wikipedia://en/5165246) for parallel computers,[^o14] including truncated [Neumann series](wikipedia://en/2533545) preconditioner, incomplete twisted factorizations, and the incomplete factorization based on the so-called "vdv" ordering.

He is the author of the book *Iterative Krylov Methods for Large Linear systems*[^o15] and one of the authors of the Templates projects for linear problems[^o16] and eigenproblems.[^o17]
