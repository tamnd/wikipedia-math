# Aron Simis

*Image: Aron Simis.jpg.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aron Simis"}

**Aron Simis** (born 1942 in [Recife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recife), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil)) is a Brazilian mathematician. He is a full professor at the [Federal University of Pernambuco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Pernambuco) and a Class A research [scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholarship) recipient from the Brazilian Research Council.[^o2] He earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from [Queen's University, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University,_Canada).[^o5][^o7]

:::

He has previously held a full professorship at IMPA ([Instituto de Matemática Pura e Applicada](wikipedia://en/2334708)) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro), Brazil. He was president of the Brazilian Mathematical Society and member on several occasions of international commissions of the IMU ([International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868)) and TWAS ([Academy of Sciences for the Developing World](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_for_the_Developing_World)).[^o5]

He has been director of three workshops in his field at the ICTP ([Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics)). In Brazil he is a recipient of the National Medal for Scientific Merit at the order of *Grã-Cruz* and a member of the Brazilian Research Group in Commutative Algebra and Algebraic Geometry (1997–2007).[^o5]

At large he is a [John Simon Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship)[^o6] and has been awarded other fellowships from the [Max Planck Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute), Japan Society for Promotion of Science, and the Istituto Nazionale di Alta Matematica. He is a member both of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences) and the Academy of Sciences for the Developing World ([Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy)).[^o5]

His main research interests in mathematics include: main structures in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990); projective varieties in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997); aspects of [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582); special graded algebras; foundations of Rees algebras; cremona and [birational maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birational_map); algebraic vector fields; differential methods.[^o5]

Simis is of [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) origin, his parents immigrated to Brazil from Romania in the 1920s.[^o3]
