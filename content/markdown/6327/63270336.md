# Jacques Tilouine

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jacques Tilouine"}

**Jacques Tilouine** is a professor of mathematics at [Université Sorbonne Paris Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Sorbonne_Paris_Nord) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms), particularly [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Tilouine received his PhD in mathematics from [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in 1989 under the supervision of [John H. Coates](wikipedia://en/230705). He is a professor of mathematics at Université Sorbonne Paris Nord.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Tilouine has worked on the [anticyclotomic main conjecture of Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/22091332),[^o7][^o8] [special values of L-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882),[^o9] and [Serre-type conjectures](wikipedia://en/2908546) for [symplectic groups](wikipedia://en/173977).[^o10]
