# Eric Urban

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Eric Urban"}

**Eric Jean-Paul Urban** is a professor of mathematics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms), particularly [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150).[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Urban received his PhD in mathematics from [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in 1994 under the supervision of [Jacques Tilouine](wikipedia://en/63270336). He is a professor of mathematics at Columbia University.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Together with [Christopher Skinner](wikipedia://en/59264582), Urban proved many cases of [Iwasawa–Greenberg main conjectures](wikipedia://en/22091332) for a large class of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000).[^o8] As a consequence, for a [modular elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/4559850) over the [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024), they prove that the vanishing of the [Hasse–Weil *L*-function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse–Weil_L-function) *L*(*E*, *s*) of *E* at *s* = 1 implies that the p-adic [Selmer group](wikipedia://en/1970862) of *E* is infinite. Combined with theorems of [Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863)-[Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557) and [Kolyvagin](wikipedia://en/4559839), this gave a conditional proof (on the [Tate–Shafarevich conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate–Shafarevich_conjecture)) of the conjecture that *E* has infinitely many rational points if and only if *L*(*E*, 1) = 0, a (weak) form of the [Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer_conjecture). These results were used (in joint work with [Manjul Bhargava](wikipedia://en/5102088) and [Wei Zhang](wikipedia://en/46408911)) to prove that a positive proportion of elliptic curves satisfy the [Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer_conjecture).[^o9][^o10]

## Awards {#Awards}

Urban was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 2007.[^o12]
