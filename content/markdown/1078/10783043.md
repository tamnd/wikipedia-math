# Fagnano's problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fagnano's problem"}

*Image: Fagnano problem.svg.* orthic triangle: $\triangle DEF$ inscribed triangles: $\triangle DEF\,,\triangle GHI$ $|DE|+|EF|+|FD|\leq |GH|+|HI|+|IG|$

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **Fagnano's problem** is an [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29) problem that was first stated by [Giovanni Fagnano](wikipedia://en/42789700) in 1775: For a given [acute triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acute_triangle) determine the inscribed triangle of minimal [perimeter](wikipedia://en/23636). The solution is the [orthic triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthic_triangle), with vertices at the base points of the [altitudes](wikipedia://en/161241) of the given triangle.

:::

## Solution {#Solution}

The [orthic triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthic_triangle), with vertices at the base points of the [altitudes](wikipedia://en/161241) of the given triangle, has the smallest perimeter of all triangles inscribed into an acute triangle, hence it is the solution of Fagnano's problem. Fagnano's original proof used [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) methods and an intermediate result given by his father [Giulio Carlo de' Toschi di Fagnano](wikipedia://en/2415799). Later, however, several geometric proofs were discovered as well, among others by [Hermann Schwarz](wikipedia://en/3165834) and [Lipót Fejér](wikipedia://en/3934357). These proofs use the geometrical properties of reflections to determine some minimal path representing the perimeter.

::: {#o4 .statement cue=named-heading name="Physical principles"}

## Physical principles {#Physical_principles}

A solution from physics is found by imagining putting a rubber band that follows [Hooke's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hooke's_law) around the three sides of a triangular frame $ABC$, such that it could slide around smoothly. Then the rubber band would end up in a position that minimizes its elastic energy, and therefore minimize its total length. This position gives the minimal perimeter triangle. The tension inside the rubber band is the same everywhere in the rubber band, so in its resting position, we have, by [Lami's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lami's_theorem), $\angle bcA = \angle acB, \angle caB = \angle baC, \angle abC = \angle cbA$

*Image: Altitudes and orthic triangle SVG.svg.* Triangle *abc* is the orthic triangle of triangle *ABC*

Therefore, this minimal triangle is the orthic triangle.

Fagnano's problem also provides a path along which light can reflect within a mirrored triangle, returning to its original position and orientation after finitely many reflections. Thus, it solves for acute triangles the problem of finding a periodic path in [triangular billiards](wikipedia://en/82161958). The existence of such a path remains open for arbitrary triangles.[^o5]
