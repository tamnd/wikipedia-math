# Toufik Mansour

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Toufik Mansour"}

**Toufik Mansour** is an Israeli mathematician working in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582). He is a member of the [Druze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Druze) community and is the first Israeli Druze to become a professional mathematician.[^o3]

:::

Mansour obtained his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Haifa) in 2001 under Alek Vainshtein.[^o4] As of 2007, he is a professor of mathematics at the University of Haifa.[^o5] He served as chair of the department from 2015 to 2017. He has previously been a faculty member of the Center for Combinatorics at [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University) from 2004 to 2007, and at The John Knopfmacher Center for Applicable Analysis and Number Theory at the [University of the Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Witwatersrand).

Mansour is an expert on [Discrete Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_Mathematics) and its applications. In particular, he is interested in [permutation patterns](wikipedia://en/24360911), colored permutations, [set partitions](wikipedia://en/340240), [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490), and [compositions](wikipedia://en/550741). He has published extensively in these areas.

Mansour is a founding editor of the peer-reviewed journal *Enumerative Combinatorics and Applications* and has served as its editor-in-chief since the journal’s establishment.[^o6]

Since 2022, he has chaired and organized the annual International Conference on Enumerative Combinatorics and Applications.[^o7]
