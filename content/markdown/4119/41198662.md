# Pillai's arithmetical function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pillai's arithmetical function"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the **gcd-sum** function,[^o2] also called **Pillai's arithmetical function**,[^o2] is defined for every $n$ by

:::

$$P(n)=\sum_{k=1}^n\gcd(k,n)$$

or equivalently[^o2]

$$P(n) = \sum_{d\mid n} d \varphi(n/d)$$

where $d$ is a divisor of $n$ and $\varphi$ is [Euler's totient function](wikipedia://en/53452).

it also can be written as[^o3]

$$P(n) = \sum_{d \mid n} d \tau(d) \mu(n/d)$$

where, $\tau$ is the [divisor function](wikipedia://en/407249), and $\mu$ is the [Möbius function](wikipedia://en/20961).

This multiplicative [arithmetical function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetical_function) was introduced by the Indian mathematician [Subbayya Sivasankaranarayana Pillai](wikipedia://en/681150) in 1933.[^o4]
