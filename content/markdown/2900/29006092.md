# Michał Misiurewicz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michał Misiurewicz"}

**Michał Misiurewicz** (born 9 November 1948[^o3]) is a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is known for his contributions to [chaotic dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087#Nonlinear_dynamical_systems_and_chaos) and [fractal geometry](wikipedia://en/10913), notably the [Misiurewicz point](wikipedia://en/9612488).

:::

Misiurewicz participated in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) for Poland, winning a bronze medal in 1965 and a gold medal (with perfect score and special prize) in 1966.[^o4] He earned his [Doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) under supervision of Bogdan Bojarski.

In 1990, he moved to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), where he visited [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), eventually settling down at [Indiana University–Purdue University Indianapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University–Purdue_University_Indianapolis) at Indianapolis, Indiana, where he currently is a professor.[^o5][^o6]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
