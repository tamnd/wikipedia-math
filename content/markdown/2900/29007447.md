# Sergei Konyagin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Konyagin"}

**Sergei Vladimirovich Konyagin** (Серге́й Владимирович Конягин; born 25 April 1957)[^o3] is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a professor of mathematics at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). His primary research interest is in applying harmonic analysis to number theoretic settings.

:::

Konyagin participated in the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) for the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), winning two consecutive gold medals with perfect scores in 1972 and 1973. At the age of 15, he became one of the [youngest people to achieve a perfect score](wikipedia://en/15712699#Exceptionally_young_participants) at the IMO.

In 1990 Konyagin was awarded the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize).

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
