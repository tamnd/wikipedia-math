# Stan Ackermans

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stan Ackermans"}

**Stanislaus Thomas Maria (Stan) Ackermans** (1936 – 1995) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and the seventh [rector magnificus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_magnificus) of the [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology). He was also one of the founders, the namesake and the first director of the [Stan Ackermans Instituut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3TU#Stan_Ackermans_Instituut).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ackermans was the son of Rie A.G. Schonk and Anton J.J.M. Ackermans, a high school teacher in Amsterdam.[^o2] Following his secondary education, Ackermans attended the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), where he studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831); he graduated under professor [N.G. de Bruijn](wikipedia://en/3166399).[^o5] In 1961 he followed his professor to Eindhoven to work under him again, this time on his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (which he was granted in 1964).[^o5] In the period of 1967–68 he worked at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA).[^o5] He was appointed full [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics back in Eindhoven in 1972, for the chairs of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).[^o5]

Ackermans became dean of the mathematics department in 1978; he remained as the dean until 1981, when he became conrector of the university. In 1982 he succeeded [Professor Hans Erkelens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Erkelens) as [rector magnificus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_magnificus).[^o5] This was also the year of the two-phase structural reformation of higher education in The Netherlands (Dutch: *Tweefasenstructuur wetenschappelijk onderwijs*) and the financial cutback program *Division of labor and concentration* (Dutch: *Taakverdeling en concentratie*), which were mandated by the [Ministry of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education,_Culture_and_Science_%28Netherlands%29).[^o5]

Ackermans completed one term as rector. In 1986 he took the initiative in founding the *Institute for Continuing Education* (Dutch: *Instituut Vervolgopleidingen*), of which he became the scientific director. This institute offers two-year, post-graduate technical designer education to graduates of the two-phase programs; the institute was renamed the [Stan Ackermans Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stan_Ackermans_Institute) after Ackerman's death in 1995.[^o5]

## Notable publications {#Notable_publications}

- *Algebra en Analyse* (Academic Science, Den Haag, 1976; ) together with [Professor Jack Van Lint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_van_Lint) — textbook used for decades at the university.[^o5]
