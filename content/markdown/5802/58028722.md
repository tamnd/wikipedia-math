# Stefaan Vaes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefaan Vaes"}

**Stefaan Vaes** (born February 29, 1976, in [Herentals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herentals), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium)) is a Belgian mathematician.

*Image: Vaes stefaan.jpg.* Stefaan Vaes, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669), 2015

Vaes studied mathematics at [KU Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katholieke_Universiteit_Leuven) with a diploma in 1998 and a PhD in 2001 with thesis advisor Alfons Van Daele and thesis Locally Compact Quantum Groups. As a postdoc, he worked from 1998 to 2002 at KU Leuven and from 1998 to 2002 in Paris, where he did research for [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). In 2002, he began part-time teaching at KU Leuven, where he became an associate professor in 2006 and a full professor in 2009.[^o3]

:::

He was a visiting professor in 2009 at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris VI) and in 2011 at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) (Paris VII) (where he habilitated in 2004). In 2005, he held the *Peccot Chair* at the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France).[^o3]

His research deals with [Von Neumann algebras](wikipedia://en/294185) and [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678).

In 2010, Vaes was an invited speaker with a talk on *Rigidity for von Neumann algebras and their invariants* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad).[^o4] In 2012, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). In 2015, he received the [Francqui Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francqui_Prize).

His doctoral students include Cyril Houdayer.
