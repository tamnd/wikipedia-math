# Kaoru Ono

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kaoru Ono"}

**Kaoru Ono** (小野 薫, *Ono Kaoru*, born 1962) is a Japanese mathematician, specializing in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298). He is a professor at the [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1978962) (RIMS) at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University).[^o2]

:::

Ono received from the University of [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo) his undergraduate degree in 1984, his master's degree in 1987, and his Ph.D. in 1990.[^o3][^o4] Within symplectic geometry, his research has focused on [Floer theory](wikipedia://en/2672856) and holomorphic symplectic geometry involving [holomorphic curves](wikipedia://en/34371378) and [pseudoholomorphic curves](wikipedia://en/2294542) and their applications. He has collaborated extensively with [Kenji Fukaya](wikipedia://en/40788879), [Yong-Geun Oh](wikipedia://en/70007916), and Hiroshi Ohta (see [Fukaya category](wikipedia://en/31598730)).

In a joint work with K. Fukaya, he constructed Floer cohomology of Hamiltonian diffeomorphisms on arbitrary closed symplectic manifolds as well as Gromov-Witten invariants. Using Novikov-Floer cohomology, he proved the $C^1$-flux conjecture. In recent years, he has been collaborating with Fukaya, Oh and Ohta in Floer theory for Lagrangian submanifolds and its implications in symplectic geometry and [homological mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/2164886).[^o2]

Ono was awarded by the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786) in 1999 the [Geometry Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry_prize) and in 2005 the Autumn Prize. He was a 2006 recipient of the Inoue Prize of the Inoue Foundation for Science.[^o6] In 2006 he was an Invited Speaker with talk *Development in symplectic Floer theory* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid).

In 2022, he serves as Director of the Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences of [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University) (KURIMS).
