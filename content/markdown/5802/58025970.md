# Nicole Spillane

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicole Spillane"}

*Image: Nicole Spillane Oberwolfach 2014.jpg.* Spillane at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2014

**Nicole Spillane** (born 2 January 1988) is a French and Irish applied mathematician. She is a researcher with the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) (CNRS) in France, where she works in the center for applied mathematics of the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). Her research concerns [parallel algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_algorithm) for solving large [systems of linear equations](wikipedia://en/113087).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Spillane studied for an engineering diploma at the [École des ponts ParisTech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_des_ponts_ParisTech) from 2006 to 2010. Over the same period she visited [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and the [Saclay Nuclear Research Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saclay_Nuclear_Research_Centre), and earned a master's degree in mathematics from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University). She completed her doctorate in applied mathematics at Pierre and Marie Curie University in 2014. Her dissertation, *Méthodes de décomposition de domaine robustes pour les problèmes symétriques définis positifs*, was jointly supervised by Frédéric Nataf and Patrice Hauret. After postdoctoral research at the [University of Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chile), she joined CNRS and the École Polytechnique in 2015.

In 2017 the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) gave Spillane their [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214).
