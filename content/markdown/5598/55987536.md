# Onorato Nicoletti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Onorato Nicoletti"}

*Image: Nicoletti Onorato.jpg.* Onoratio Nicoletti

**Onorato Nicoletti** (21 June 1872 – 31 December 1929)[^o2] was an Italian mathematician.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Nicoletti received his *laurea* in 1894 from the [Scuola Normale di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_di_Pisa). In 1898, he became a professor of infinitesimal calculus at the [University of Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Modena). After two years, he returned to Pisa, where he was a teacher of algebra, and then, after the death of [Ulisse Dini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulisse_Dini), of infinitesimal calculus.[^o5]

He published works in various fields of mathematics, including numerical analysis, infinitesimal analysis, the equations related to [hermitian matrices](wikipedia://en/189682), and [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations). He made original contributions to [Max Dehn](wikipedia://en/570602)'s theory of the equivalence of polyhedra under polyhedral dissection and reassembly ([scissors-congruence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scissors-congruent)), extending and generalizing the theory with an entire class of new relations.

Nicoletti collaborated in the writing of *Enciclopedia Hoepli delle Matematiche elementari e complementi* (published from 1930 to 1951) with the contribution of two monographic articles: *Forme razionali di una o più variabili* (Rational forms of one or more variables) and *Proprietà generali delle funzioni algebriche* (General properties of algebraic functions).

A leading expert in mathematics education, he edited with [Roberto Marcolongo](wikipedia://en/30792193) a series of successful editions for secondary schools.

Nicoletti was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rome.[^o6]
