# Max-Albert Knus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max-Albert Knus"}

*Image: ETH-BIB-Knus,\_Max-Albert\_(1942-)-Portr\_17411.jpg.* Max-Albert Knus in 2014

**Max-Albert Knus** is a Swiss mathematician specializing in algebra.

:::

He was born April 14, 1942, in Peseux, [Neuchatel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuchatel_%28canton%29).[^o4] He studied mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) with [Beno Eckmann](wikipedia://en/19613365) and [K. Chandrasekharan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._Chandrasekharan), graduating in 1967 with a PhD thesis titled *On a class of filtered algebras*.

He was a Batelle-fellow at ETH and [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University). He was director of research at [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva). In 1969 he returned to ETH as a professor and became emeritus in 2007.[^o4][^o5]

Knus expanded [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) to include a wider variety of [structures](wikipedia://en/1809181). The classical study considers [algebras over a field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebras_over_a_field), whereas Knus considers [algebras over a ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_over_a_ring) as in his monograph *Quadratic and Hermitian Forms over Rings* (1991).[^o10] In the preface he acknowledges colleagues that contributed to the science: "Many results in this book were developed by (or in collaboration with) [Raman Parimala](wikipedia://en/11853200), [Ramaiyengar Sridharan](wikipedia://en/18795598) and Manuel Orjanguran.[^o6] I am deeply grateful to them for doing mathematics in [Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bombay), [Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lausanne) or [Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zurich)."

Then in 1998 Knus joined [Alexander Merkurjev](wikipedia://en/31902178), [Markus Rost](wikipedia://en/6842771), and [Jean-Pierre Tignol](wikipedia://en/76151496) to write *The Book of Involutions*[^o11] published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7] This book is about "[central simple algebras](wikipedia://en/404082) with [involution](wikipedia://en/293450), in relation to [linear algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/395885)."

His research interests include [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315).

Since 1997 he has been a fellow of the [Swiss National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Science_Foundation).[^o8]
