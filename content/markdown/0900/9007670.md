# Frances Kirwan

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Frances Kirwan"}

**Dame Frances Clare Kirwan** (born 21 August 1959[^o4]) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), currently [Savilian Professor of Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savilian_Professor_of_Geometry) at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). Her fields of specialisation are [algebraic](wikipedia://en/1997) and [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298).[^o5][^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kirwan was educated at [Oxford High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_High_School_%28Oxford%29), and studied maths as an undergraduate at [Clare College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_College,_Cambridge).[^o8] She took a [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree at Oxford in 1984, with the dissertation title *The Cohomology of Quotients in [Symplectic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_Geometry) and [Algebraic Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_Geometry)*, which was supervised by [Michael Atiyah](wikipedia://en/20698).

## Research {#Research}

Kirwan's research interests include [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609) in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [geometric invariant theory](wikipedia://en/1639003) (GIT), and in the link between GIT and [moment maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_map) in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298).[^o11] Her work endeavours to understand the structure of geometric objects by investigation of their algebraic and [topological](wikipedia://en/29954) properties.[^o12] She introduced the [Kirwan map](wikipedia://en/42485711).

From 1983 to 1985, she held a junior fellowship at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard). From 1983 to 1986, she held a Fellowship at [Magdalen College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalen_College,_Oxford), before becoming a Fellow of [Balliol College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford).[^o13] She is an honorary fellow of [Clare College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clare_College,_Cambridge)[^o14] and also at Magdalen College.[^o15]

In 1996, she was awarded the [Title of Distinction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Title_of_Distinction) of Professor of Mathematics. From 2004 to 2006, she was president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), the second-youngest president in the society's history and only the second woman to be president.[^o16][^o17] In 2005, she received a five-year [EPSRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council) Senior Research Fellowship, to support her research on the [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609) of complex [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260).[^o18]

In 2017, she was elected [Savilian Professor of Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savilian_Professor_of_Geometry), becoming the first woman to hold the post.[^o19] While this entailed a move to [New College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_College,_Oxford), she was elected an emeritus fellow at Balliol.[^o20] She was the convenor of the 2008–2009 meeting of [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372) and deputy convenor of the following meeting in 2010–2011.[^o21]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Kirwan is the mother of Geoff Penington, a physics professor at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley).[^o23] She was married to Michael Penington, an investment banker and the son of [David Penington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Penington).[^o24]

## Prizes, awards and scholarships {#Prizes,_awards_and_scholarships}

- London Mathematical Society [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913), 1989[^o26]
- Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society), 2001[^o27]
- President, [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), 2003–2005
- [EPSRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EPSRC) Senior Research Fellowship, 2005–2010, for her work in algebraic geometry[^o28]
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2012[^o29]
- London Mathematical Society Senior Whitehead Prize, 2013[^o26]
- [DBE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dame_Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_British_Empire) for services to mathematics, 2014
- Maths and Computing [Suffrage Science award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suffrage_Science_award), 2016[^o31]
- Member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o13]
- Chairman of the [United Kingdom Mathematics Trust](wikipedia://en/552624)
- [Sylvester Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Medal) of The Royal Society, 2021
- Honorary degree, [University of York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_York), 2020[^o32]
- Honorary degree, [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews), 2022[^o33]
- [L'Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Oréal-UNESCO_For_Women_in_Science_Awards) (Laureate for Europe – Mathematics), 2023[^o34]
- Honorary degree, [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), 2026[^o35]

Kirwan served on the medal-selection committee that awarded the [Fields medal](wikipedia://en/10859) to [Maryam Mirzakhani](wikipedia://en/6409655).[^o36]
