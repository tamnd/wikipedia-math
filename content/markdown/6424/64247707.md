# Krein–Smulian theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Krein–Smulian theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), particularly in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the **Krein-Smulian theorem** can refer to two theorems relating the closed [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) and [compactness](wikipedia://en/6042) in the [weak topology](wikipedia://en/33662). They are named after [Mark Krein](wikipedia://en/1190470) and [Vitold Shmulyan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitold_Shmulyan), who published them in 1940.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Both of the following theorems are referred to as the Krein-Smulian Theorem.

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Krein-Smulian Theorem:"}

**Krein-Smulian Theorem:[^n1].**

Let $X$ be a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) and $K$ a weakly compact subset of $X$ (that is, $K$ is compact when $X$ is endowed with the [weak topology](wikipedia://en/33662)). Then the closed convex hull of $K$ in $X$ is weakly compact.

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=math-theorem name="Krein-Smulian Theorem"}

**Krein-Smulian Theorem[^n1].**

Let $X$ be a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) and $A$ a convex subset of the continuous dual space $X^{\prime}$ of $X$. If for all $r > 0,$ $A \cap \left\{x^{\prime} \in X^{\prime} : \left\| x^{\prime} \right\| \leq r\right\}$ is [weak-\* closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-star_topology) in $X^{\prime}$ then $A$ is weak-\* closed.

:::
