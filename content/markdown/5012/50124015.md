# Adriaan Anthonisz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adriaan Anthonisz"}

*Image: Mr Adriaan Anthonisz John Bier Noorderkade Alkmaar.JPG.* Statue of Adriaen Anthonisz by John Bier

**Adriaan Anthonisz** (also known as **Adriaen Anthonisz of Alcmaer**) (1527–1607)[^o2][^o3][^o4] was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [surveyor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveying), [cartographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartographer), and [military engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_engineer) who specialized in the design of fortifications. As a mathematician Anthonisz calculated in 1585 the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, which would later be called [pi](wikipedia://en/23601).[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Anthonisz served as [burgomaster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgomaster) (mayor) of [Alkmaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alkmaar) in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands) from 1582.[^o6]

Adriaan fathered two sons, and named them both Metius (from the Dutch word *meten*, meaning 'measuring', 'measurer', or surveyor). They each became prominent members of society.[^o7] [Adriaan Metius](wikipedia://en/50123722) (9 Dec 1571 – 6 Sep 1635) was a Dutch geometer and astronomer. [Jacob Metius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Metius) worked as an instrument-maker and a specialist in grinding lenses and applied for patent rights for the [telescope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telescope) a few weeks after Middelburg spectacle-maker [Hans Lippershey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Lippershey) tried to patent the same device.[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

In 1585 Anthonisz discovered that the ratio of a circle's [circumference](wikipedia://en/5916) to its [diameter](wikipedia://en/8007), later called [pi](wikipedia://en/23601), approximated the fractional value of $\tfrac{355}{113}$. His son Adriaan Metius later published his father's results, and the value $\tfrac{355}{113}$ is traditionally referred to as [Metius's number](wikipedia://en/7315901).[^o10][^o11]

He is regarded as one of the first military engineers to apply the principles of the Dutch fortification system.[^o12]

Some of his professional accomplishments included mapping the Berger lake and expanding and fortifying [Naarden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naarden) and [Muiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muiden).[^o13]
