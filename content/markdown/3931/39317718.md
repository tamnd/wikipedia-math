# Josef Stoer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Josef Stoer"}

**Josef Stoer** (born 21 June 1934) is a German mathematician specializing in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of the Institut für Mathematik of [Universität Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Würzburg).[^o3]

:::

Stoer was born in [Meschede](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meschede), and earned his Ph.D. in 1961 at [Johannes Gutenberg-Universität Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Gutenberg-Universität_Mainz) under [Friedrich Ludwig Bauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Ludwig_Bauer) and [Klaus Samelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Samelson). He has advised over 20 doctoral students.

He is the author (with [Roland Bulirsch](wikipedia://en/39317783)) of *Introduction to Numerical Analysis*, a standard reference for the theory of [numerical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_methods).[^o5] He has an honorary doctorate from the University of Augsburg (2007) and the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) (1997)[^o6] and is a member of the Bavarian Academy of Sciences (1981).[^o7] The [Bulirsch–Stoer algorithm](wikipedia://en/20221609) is named after him and [Roland Bulirsch](wikipedia://en/39317783).[^o8]
