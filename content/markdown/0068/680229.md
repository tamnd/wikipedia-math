# List of national and international statistical services

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of national and international statistical services"}

The following is a **list of national and international statistical services**.

:::

## Central national statistical services {#Central_national_statistical_services}

Nearly every country in the world has set a central public sector unit entirely devoted to the production, harmonisation and dissemination of [official statistics](wikipedia://en/18646178) that the public sector and the national community need to run, monitor and evaluate their operations and policies. This central statistical organisation does not produce every official statistic as other public sector organisations, like the national [central bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_bank) or [ministries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_ministry) in charge of agriculture, education or health, may be charged with producing and disseminating sector policy oriented statistical data. The statistical [legislation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legislation) and regulation generally attribute responsibilities and authorities according to statistical domains or functions in addition to those of the central unit.

The table below lists these central statistical organisations by country. The [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) has no central producing unit, but several units (also listed below) have been given responsibility over various federal statistics domains (see also: [Federal Statistical System of the United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Statistical_System_of_the_United_States)).
