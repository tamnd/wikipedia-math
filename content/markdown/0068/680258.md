# List of academic statistical associations

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of academic statistical associations"}

**[Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685)** is the study of the collection, organization, analysis, and interpretation of data. It deals with all aspects of this, including the planning of data collection in terms of the design of surveys and experiments. This is a list of [academic statistical associations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_association).

:::
