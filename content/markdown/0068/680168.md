# Curtis T. McMullen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Curtis T. McMullen"}

**Curtis Tracy McMullen** (born May 21, 1958) is an American mathematician who is the Cabot Professor of Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He was awarded the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1998 for his work in [complex dynamics](wikipedia://en/650518), [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291) and [Teichmüller theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teichmüller_theory).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

McMullen graduated as [valedictorian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valedictorian) in 1980 from [Williams College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Williams_College) and obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1985 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), supervised by [Dennis Sullivan](wikipedia://en/7349608). He held post-doctoral positions at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), after which he was on the faculty at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (1987–1990) and the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) (1990–1997), before joining Harvard in 1997. McMullen was chair of the [Harvard Mathematics Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_Mathematics_Department) from 2017 to 2020. His doctoral student [Maryam Mirzakhani](wikipedia://en/6409655) was the first woman to win the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

McMullen received the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) in 1991 and won the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) in 1998[^o5][^o6] at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in Berlin.[^o7] At the 1990 ICM in Kyoto he was an Invited Speaker.[^o8] He was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 2004, elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2007, and received the [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Research_Award) in 2011.
