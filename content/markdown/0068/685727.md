# Germinal Pierre Dandelin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Germinal Pierre Dandelin"}

*Image: Dandelin.jpg.* Germinal Pierre Dandelin

**Germinal Pierre Dandelin** (; , 12 April 1794 – 15 February 1847) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [soldier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soldier), and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born near [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) to a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) father and [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) mother, studying first at [Ghent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent) then returning to Paris to study at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). He was wounded fighting under [Napoleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon_I_of_France). He worked for the Ministry of the Interior under [Lazare Carnot](wikipedia://en/26532764). Later he became a citizen of the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), a professor of [mining engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mining_engineering) in [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium), and then a member of the Belgian army.

## Work {#Work}

He is the eponym of the [Dandelin spheres](wikipedia://en/683726), of Dandelin's theorem in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) (for an account of that theorem, see [Dandelin spheres](wikipedia://en/683726)), and of the [Dandelin–Gräffe numerical method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dandelin&ndash;Gräffe_method) of solution of algebraic equations. He also published on the [stereographic projection](wikipedia://en/143431), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).
