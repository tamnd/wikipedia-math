# MMB (cipher)

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="MMB"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **MMB** (**Modular Multiplication-based Block cipher**) is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) designed by [Joan Daemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Daemen) as an improved replacement for the [IDEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDEA_%28cipher%29) cipher.[^o5] [Modular](wikipedia://en/20087) [multiplication](wikipedia://en/20845) is the central element in the design. Weaknesses in the [key schedule](wikipedia://en/609603) were identified by [Eli Biham](wikipedia://en/348920), and this, together with the cipher's not having been designed to resist [linear cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/59469), meant that other designs were pursued instead, such as [3-Way](wikipedia://en/294099).

:::

MMB has a [key size](wikipedia://en/5749) and [block size](wikipedia://en/294065) of 128 bits.
