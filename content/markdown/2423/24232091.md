# Octagrammic prism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Octagrammic prism"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **octagrammic prism** is one of an infinite set of nonconvex [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) formed by square sides and two regular [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) caps, in this case two [octagrams](wikipedia://en/2667613). An octagrammic prism has 16 vertices, 2 [octagrammic stars](wikipedia://en/2667613), 8 square faces, and 24 edges.[^o3]

:::
