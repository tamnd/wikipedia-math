# C. T. C. Wall

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="C. T. C. Wall"}

**Charles Terence Clegg** "**Terry**" **Wall** (born 14 December 1936) is a British mathematician, educated at [Marlborough](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marlborough_College) and [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge). He is an emeritus professor of the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool), where he was first appointed professor in 1965. From 1978 to 1980 he was the president of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).

:::

## Work {#Work}

His early work was in [cobordism theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cobordism_theory) in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801); this includes his 1959 Cambridge PhD thesis entitled "Algebraic aspects of cobordism", written under the direction of [Frank Adams](wikipedia://en/1223959) and [Christopher Zeeman](wikipedia://en/871415). His research was then mainly in the area of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470), particularly [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732) and related [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) included in [surgery theory](wikipedia://en/5961115), of which he was one of the founders. In 1964, this lead to the four [Wall theorems](wikipedia://en/84014430). Also in 1964 he introduced the [Brauer–Wall group](wikipedia://en/36842500) of a field. His 1970 research monograph "Surgery on Compact Manifolds" is a major reference work in geometric topology.

In 1971 he [conjectured](wikipedia://en/6138) that every [finitely generated group](wikipedia://en/963042) is [accessible](wikipedia://en/19196631). The conjecture motivated much progress in the understanding of splittings of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447). In 1985 [Martin Dunwoody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Dunwoody) proved the conjecture for the class of [finitely presented groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely_presented_group). The resolution of the full conjecture took until 1991 when, surprising to most mathematicians at the time, Dunwoody found a finitely generated group that is not accessible and hence the conjecture turned out to be not correct in its general formulation.

Wall's work since the mid-1970s has mostly been in [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763) as developed by [R. Thom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Thom), [J. Milnor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Milnor) and [V. Arnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._Arnold), and especially concerns the classification of [isolated singularities](wikipedia://en/334325) of [differentiable maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differentiable_map) and of [algebraic varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_varieties). He has written two research monographs on singularity theory, "The Geometry of Topological Stability" (1995) (containing a great deal of original work) with Andrew du Plessis, and "Singular Points of Plane Curves" (2004).

His notable students include [Michael Boardman](wikipedia://en/8770110), [Bill Bruce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._W._Bruce), [Andrew Casson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Casson), [Francis E. A. Johnson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_E._A._Johnson), [David Mond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Mond), Andrew du Plessis, and [David Trotman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Trotman).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Terry Wall has been married to Sandra Hearnshaw since 1959, and they have four children together.[^o8] He was the treasurer of the [Wirral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirral_Peninsula) area [SDP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Democratic_Party_%28UK%29) from 1985 until its merger with the then Liberal Party in 1988. Wall continued on as treasurer of the newly formed [Wirral West](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wirral_West_%28UK_Parliament_constituency%29) [Liberal Democrats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liberal_Democrats_%28UK%29) but, as of May 2020, is no longer holding this position.[^o8][^o9] Wall has been an [LEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_education_authority) appointed [governor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_governor) of [West Kirby Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Kirby_Grammar_School) since 1987 but has also given up this position.[^o8][^o10] He has also held the post of treasurer at [Hoylake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hoylake) Chamber Concert Society.[^o8][^o11] He has 7 grandchildren and 4 great grandchildren as of 2024, Rory, Felix, Valentina and Leo.
