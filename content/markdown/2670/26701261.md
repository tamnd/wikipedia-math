# Twist knot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Twist knot"}

*Image: Blue 8\_1 Knot.png.* A twist knot with six half-twists.

In [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **twist knot** is a knot obtained by repeatedly twisting a closed [loop](wikipedia://en/1189560) and then linking the ends together. (That is, a twist knot is any [Whitehead double](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitehead_double) of an [unknot](wikipedia://en/60075).) The twist knots are an infinite family of knots, and are considered the simplest type of knots after the [torus knots](wikipedia://en/1165182).

:::

## Properties {#Properties}

*Image: Twist knot Stevedore steps horizontal.svg.* The four half-twist stevedore knot is created by passing the one end of an unknot with four half-twists through the other.

All twist knots have [unknotting number](wikipedia://en/26711383) one, since the knot can be untied by unlinking the two ends. Every twist knot is also a [2-bridge knot](wikipedia://en/3396136).[^o6] Of the twist knots, only the [unknot](wikipedia://en/60075) and the [stevedore knot](wikipedia://en/26652059) are [slice knots](wikipedia://en/4158126). A twist knot with $n$ half-twists has [crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298598) $n+2$. All twist knots are [invertible](wikipedia://en/17351839), but the only [amphichiral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amphichiral_knot) twist knots are the unknot and the [figure-eight knot](wikipedia://en/59565).

:::

## Invariants {#Invariants}

The invariants of a twist knot depend on the number $n$ of half-twists. The [Alexander polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107647) of a twist knot is given by the formula

$$\Delta(t) = \begin{cases} \frac{n+1}{2}t - n + \frac{n+1}{2}t^{-1} & \text{if }n\text{ is odd} \\ -\frac{n}{2}t + (n+1) - \frac{n}{2}t^{-1} & \text{if }n\text{ is even,} \\ \end{cases}$$

and the [Conway polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107647#Alexander.E2.80.93Conway_polynomial) is

$$\nabla(z) = \begin{cases} \frac{n+1}{2}z^2 + 1 & \text{if }n\text{ is odd} \\ 1 - \frac{n}{2}z^2 & \text{if }n\text{ is even.} \\ \end{cases}$$

When $n$ is odd, the [Jones polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107642) is

$$V(q) = \frac{1 + q^{-2} + q^{-n} - q^{-n-3}}{q+1},$$

and when $n$ is even, it is

$$V(q) = \frac{q^3 + q - q^{3-n} + q^{-n}}{q+1}.$$
