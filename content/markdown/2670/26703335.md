# Eulerian poset

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eulerian poset"}

In [combinatorial](wikipedia://en/5170) mathematics, an **Eulerian poset** is a [graded poset](wikipedia://en/2514970) in which every nontrivial [interval](wikipedia://en/23572#Intervals) has the same number of elements of even rank as of odd rank. An Eulerian poset which is a [lattice](wikipedia://en/469304) is an **Eulerian lattice**. These objects are named after [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902). Eulerian lattices generalize [face lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_lattice) of [convex polytopes](wikipedia://en/1817885) and much recent research has been devoted to extending known results from [polyhedral combinatorics](wikipedia://en/19696519), such as various restrictions on *f*-vectors of convex [simplicial polytopes](wikipedia://en/8853373), to this more general setting.

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The [face lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_lattice) of a [convex polytope](wikipedia://en/1817885), consisting of its faces, together with the smallest element, the empty face, and the largest element, the polytope itself, is an Eulerian lattice. The odd–even condition follows from [Euler's formula](wikipedia://en/148420).
- Any [simplicial generalized homology sphere](wikipedia://en/22359636) is an Eulerian lattice.
- Let *L* be a regular [cell complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_complex) such that |*L*| is a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) with the same Euler characteristic as the [sphere](wikipedia://en/39782) of the same dimension (this condition is vacuous if the dimension is odd). Then the [poset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poset) of cells of *L*, ordered by the inclusion of their closures, is Eulerian.
- Let *W* be a [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) with [Bruhat order](wikipedia://en/10163003). Then (*W*,≤) is an Eulerian poset.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The defining condition of an Eulerian poset *P* can be equivalently stated in terms of its [Möbius function](wikipedia://en/166980):

$$\mu_P(x,y)=(-1)^{|y|-|x|} \text{ for all } x\leq y.$$

- The dual of an Eulerian poset with a top element, obtained by reversing the partial order, is Eulerian.
- [Richard Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397) defined the **toric *h*-vector** of a [ranked poset](wikipedia://en/10158881), which generalizes the [*h*-vector](wikipedia://en/11503563) of a simplicial polytope.[^o6] He proved that the [Dehn–Sommerville equations](wikipedia://en/8276156)

$$h_k = h_{d-k} \,$$

hold for an arbitrary Eulerian poset of rank *d* + 1.[^o7] However, for an Eulerian poset arising from a regular cell complex or a convex polytope, the toric *h*-vector neither determines, nor is neither determined by the numbers of the cells or faces of different dimension and the toric *h*-vector does not have a direct combinatorial interpretation.

:::
