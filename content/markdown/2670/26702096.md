# Matsumoto zeta function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matsumoto zeta function"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Matsumoto zeta functions** are a type of [zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zeta_function) introduced by [Kohji Matsumoto](wikipedia://en/38200218) in 1990. They are functions of the form

:::

$$\phi(s)=\prod_{p}\frac{1}{A_p(p^{-s})}$$

where *p* is a [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) and *A*<sub>*p*</sub> is a [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000).
