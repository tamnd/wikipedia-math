# Jean-Pierre Tignol

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Pierre Tignol"}

**Jean-Pierre Tignol** (b. 1954) is a Belgian mathematician and historian of mathematics specializing in the field of [quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478) and [linear algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/395885).[^o3] He is a full professor emeritus at [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tignol was awarded a PhD (1988) at the Université catholique de Louvain for his thesis *Involution bodies of finite rank at their center and with characteristic other than 21* written under the supervision of [Jacques Tits](wikipedia://en/219570).[^o6] He subsequently became a professor at the same university.[^o4] He is the author of numerous papers on algebra and is responsible for extending concepts and results from quadratic form theory to the study of [involution algebras](wikipedia://en/293426).[^o3] In 1996, he was invited by the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest) to speak on "Algebras with involution and classical groups".[^o7]

In his 2001 book, *Galois' Theory of Algebraic Equations*, he explored the evolution of algebra from ancient [Babylon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babylon) to the eras of [Galois](wikipedia://en/9815) and [Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042). A review by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) said, "Anybody with an interest in algebra or the history of mathematics should look at this book. And of course it goes without saying that it belongs in any good university library."[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 1994 he was awarded the [Eugene Catalan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Catalan_Prize) by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in Belgium.[^o13]

In the years 2010 to 2012, Jean-Pierre Tignol was awarded several Senior Fellowships of the Zukunftskolleg at the [University of Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz).[^o14]
