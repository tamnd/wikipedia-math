# Taylor circle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Taylor circle"}

*Image: Taylor-Kreis.svg.* The feet of the perpendiculars $P_{ab},P_{ac},P_{ba}, P_{bc},P_{ca},P_{cb}$ lie on a common circle

The **Taylor circle** is a special [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) associated with a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654). It is named after [Henry Martyn Taylor](wikipedia://en/28786158) (1842–1927), who discussed it in 1882.[^o2] However it was already considered by [Eugène Charles Catalan](wikipedia://en/639553) in 1879 and first proposed by the French mathematician [Eutaris](wikipedia://en/76418053) in 1877.[^o3]

:::

Consider the three feet of the three [altitudes of triangle](wikipedia://en/161241). For each foot of an altitude construct the [perpendiculars](wikipedia://en/76944) onto the two other triangles sides. The intersections of those perpendiculars with the triangles sides yield six points and those six points are located on a common circle, the Taylor circle.[^o4]

The radius of the Taylor circle can be computed by the following formula taking the three angles of a triangle and the radius of its [circumcircle](wikipedia://en/1926432):

$$r_T = R\sqrt{\sin^2\alpha\sin^2\beta\sin^2\gamma+\cos^2\alpha\cos^2\beta\cos^2\gamma}$$
