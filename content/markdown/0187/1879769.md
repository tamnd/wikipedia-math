# Lagrangian foliation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lagrangian foliation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Lagrangian foliation** or **polarization** is a [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227) of a [symplectic manifold](wikipedia://en/28356), whose leaves are [Lagrangian submanifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lagrangian_submanifold). It is one of the steps involved in the [geometric quantization](wikipedia://en/1146267) of a square-integrable functions on a symplectic manifold.

:::
