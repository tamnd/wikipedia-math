# Susan Tolman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan Tolman"}

**Susan Tolman** is an American mathematician known for her work in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign), and Lynn M. Martin Professorial Scholar at Illinois.

:::

Tolman earned her Ph.D. in 1993 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). Her dissertation, *Group Actions And Cohomology*, was supervised by [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457). She was awarded a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1998, and was named Lynn M. Martin Professorial Scholar in 2008–2009.
