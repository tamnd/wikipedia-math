# Luis Nunes Vicente

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luis Nunes Vicente"}

**Luis Nunes Vicente** (born 1967) is an applied mathematician and optimizer who is known for his research work in [Continuous Optimization](wikipedia://en/420577) and particularly in [Derivative-Free Optimization](wikipedia://en/41680875). He is the Timothy J. Wilmott '80 Endowed Chair Professor[^o3] and Department Chair of the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering[^o4] of [Lehigh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehigh_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Luis Nunes Vicente was born in [Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coimbra), Portugal in 1967. He obtained a B.S. in Mathematics and Operations Research at the [University of Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Coimbra) in 1990. He continued his studies at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), USA, earning a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics in 1996. His Ph.D. dissertation, titled *Trust-Region Interior-Point Algorithms for a Class of Nonlinear Programming Problems*, was supervised by John Dennis.

## Career {#Career}

From 1996 to 2018, Luis Nunes Vicente was a faculty member at the Department of Mathematics[^o7] of the [University of Coimbra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Coimbra), [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal), becoming full professor in 2009. He held several visiting positions, namely at the [IBM T.J. Watson Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_J._Watson_Research_Center) and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota)[^o8] in 2002–2003, at the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences, [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)[^o9] and the [Université Paul Verlaine of Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Verlaine_University_–_Metz) in 2009–2010, and at [Roma/Sapienza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) and [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in 2016–2017. He was visiting Chercheur Sénior of the Fondation de Coopération Sciences et Technologies pour l'Aéronautique et l'Espace at CERFACS and IPN Toulouse, during 2010–2015.

He has served on numerous editorial boards, including [SIAM Journal on Optimization](https://www.siam.org/publications/journals/siam-journal-on-optimization-siopt) (2009–2017), [EURO Journal on Computational Optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EURO_Journal_on_Computational_Optimization), and [Optimization Methods and Software](https://www.tandfonline.com/loi/goms20) (2010–2018). He was Editor-in-Chief of [Portugaliae Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugaliae_Mathematica) during 2013–2018.

He was elected chair of the SIAM Activity Group on Optimization for 2023–2025 and President of the Association of Chairs of Operations Research Departments ([ACORD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACORD)) at INFORMS for 2024–2025.[^o10]

## Book {#Book}

Luis Nunes Vicente co-authored the book *Introduction to Derivative-Free Optimization*, MPS-SIAM Series on Optimization, SIAM, Philadelphia, 2009, with [Katya Scheinberg](wikipedia://en/59394806) and Andrew R. Conn.[^o12]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Luis Nunes Vicente was awarded the Lagrange Prize[^o14] of [SIAM (Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics)](wikipedia://en/1883376) and [MOS (Mathematical Optimization Society)](wikipedia://en/5751407) for the co-authorship of the book *Introduction to Derivative-Free Optimization*.[^o15]

With his Ph.D. dissertation, he received the Ralph Budd Thesis Award from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) in 1996 and was one of the three finalists of the 94-96 A. W. [Tucker Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucker_Prize) of MOS.[^o3]

He gave several plenary and keynote talks at major conferences, including the International Symposium on Mathematical Programming 2018 and the International Conference on Continuous Optimization 2010.[^o16]

In 2024, Luis Nunes Vicente was elected a Fellow of SIAM for ground-breaking contributions to derivative-free and bilevel optimization, and exemplary leadership in editorial and organizational service to the SIAM community.[^o17]
