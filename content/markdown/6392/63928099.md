# Lori E. Dodd

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lori E. Dodd"}

**Lori Elizabeth Dodd** is an American [mathematical statistician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Statistician) specializing in [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717) methodology, statistical analysis of [genomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics) data, design of clinical trials using [biomarkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomarker) and imaging modalities, and statistical methods for analyzing biomarkers. She is a statistician in the biostatistics research branch at the [National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Allergy_and_Infectious_Diseases).

:::

## Education {#Education}

In 1995, Dodd completed a bachelor's degree in the department of anthropology at the [University of Utah Honors College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah_Honors_College). Her undergraduate thesis was titled, *The old people know different: Navajo aging and the aged in the context of change*. Dodd's undergraduate academic advisor was [Per Hage](wikipedia://en/63928998), and Charles C. Hughes served as her thesis supervisor.[^o4] In 2001, she earned a Ph.D. in the department of biostatistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington).[^o5] Her dissertation was titled *Regression methods for areas and partial areas under the receiver-operating characteristic curve*. Dodd's doctoral advisor was [Margaret Sullivan Pepe](wikipedia://en/73015882).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Dodd worked at the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute) in the biometric research branch to evaluate [imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imaging_biomarker) in [oncology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oncology) trials.[^o5]

Dodd is a [mathematical statistician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Statistician) in the division of clinical research's biostatistics research branch at the [National Institute of Allergy and Infectious Diseases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Allergy_and_Infectious_Diseases) (NIAID). She specializes in [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717) methodology, statistical analysis of [genomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics) data, design of clinical trials using [biomarkers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomarker) and imaging modalities, and statistical methods for analyzing biomarkers.[^o8] At NIAID, she researches the statistical assessment of [medical diagnostic tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_diagnosis) and clinical trial design.[^o5]

In 2016, Dodd conceived of and edited a special issue on [Ebola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ebola) for the journal *[Clinical Trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_Trials_%28journal%29)*, which highlighted different approaches to trial design and various viewpoints from the individuals and organizations who contributed to research efforts.[^o9]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Dodd was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2024.[^o11]
