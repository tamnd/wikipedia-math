# Monroe Epic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monroe Epic"}

The **Monroe EPIC** was a [programmable calculator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programmable_calculator) that came on the market in the 1964. It consisted of a large [desktop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desktop_computer) unit which attached to a floor-standing logic tower and was capable of being programmed to perform many computer-like functions. However, the only form of a [branching instruction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branch_%28computer_science%29) available was a [hard-coded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hard_coding) [unconditional branch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branch_%28computer_science%29) (GOTO) that always executed at [program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_program) completion (the end of the operation [stack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_%28abstract_data_type%29)) to return to the starting instruction of the program (looped back to the beginning of operation stack). This made the creation of [conditional branching logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditional_%28computer_programming%29), such as IF-THEN-ELSE, impossible.
