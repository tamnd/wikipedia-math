# Stephen M. Gersten

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stephen M. Gersten"}

**Stephen M. Gersten** (born 2 December 1940) is an American mathematician, specializing in finitely presented groups and their geometric properties.[^o2]

:::

Gersten graduated in 1961 with an AB from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University)[^o2] and in 1965 with a PhD from [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge). His doctoral thesis was *Class Groups of Supplemented Algebras* written under the supervision of [John R. Stallings](wikipedia://en/18729795). In the late 1960s and early 1970s he taught at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University). In 1972–1973 he was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o4] In 1973 he became a professor at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign).[^o2] In 1974 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Vancouver.[^o5] At the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) he became a professor in 1975 and is now semi-retired there.[^o2] His PhD students include [Roger C. Alperin](wikipedia://en/66176553), [R. Keith Dennis](wikipedia://en/77896980) and [Edward W. Formanek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_W._Formanek).[^o3]

Gersten's conjecture has motivated considerable research.[^o6]

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Gersten's theorem"}

## Gersten's theorem {#Gersten's_theorem}

If $\varphi$ is an automorphism of a [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/963042) [free group](wikipedia://en/59735) $\mathbf{F}$ then $\{ x : x \in \mathbf{F}\text{ and } \varphi(x) = x$} is finitely generated.[^o9][^o10]

:::
