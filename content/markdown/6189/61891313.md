# Roger D. Nussbaum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roger D. Nussbaum"}

**Roger David Nussbaum** (born 29 January 1944, in [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia))[^o2] is an American mathematician, specializing in nonlinear functional analysis and differential equations.

:::

Nussbaum graduated in 1965 with a bachelor's degree from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He received his Ph.D. in 1969 from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) with thesis *The Fixed Point Index and Fixed Point Theorems for K-Set Contractions* supervised by [Felix Browder](wikipedia://en/10201445). At [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) Nussbaum became in 1969 an assistant professor, in 1973 an associate professor, and in 1977 a full professor. He retired there as professor emeritus.[^o4] He was elected in 2012 a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
