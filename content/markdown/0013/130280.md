# Short five lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Short five lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) and other applications of [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063) theory, the **short five lemma** is a special case of the [five lemma](wikipedia://en/138214). It states that for the following [commutative diagram](wikipedia://en/138484) (in any abelian [category](wikipedia://en/53759), or in the [category of groups](wikipedia://en/800092)), if the rows are [short exact sequences](wikipedia://en/138215), and if *g* and *h* are [isomorphisms](wikipedia://en/14828), then *f* is an isomorphism as well.

:::

*Image: Short\_5\_lemma.svg.*

It follows immediately from the [five lemma](wikipedia://en/138214).

The essence of the [lemma](wikipedia://en/18634) can be summarized as follows: if you have a [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) *f* from an object *B* to an object *B*, and this homomorphism induces an isomorphism from a subobject *A* of *B* to a subobject *A* of *B* and also an isomorphism from the factor object *B*/*A* to *B*/*A*, then *f* itself is an isomorphism. Note however that the existence of *f* (such that the diagram commutes) has to be assumed from the start; two objects *B* and *B* that simply have isomorphic sub- and factor objects need not themselves be isomorphic (for example, in the [category of abelian groups](wikipedia://en/454748), *B* could be the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) of [order](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_a_group) four and *B* the [Klein four-group](wikipedia://en/41928)).
