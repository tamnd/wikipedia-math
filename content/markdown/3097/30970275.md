# No small subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="No small subgroup"}

In mathematics, especially in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) $G$ is said to have **no small subgroup** if there exists a [neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_%28topology%29) $U$ of the identity that contains no [nontrivial subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trivial_subgroup) of $G.$ An abbreviation '"**NSS**"' is sometimes used. A basic example of a topological group with no small subgroup is the [general linear group](wikipedia://en/113564) over the complex numbers.

:::

A [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact), [separable metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separable_metric_space), [locally connected](wikipedia://en/6959754) group with no small subgroup is a [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945). (cf. [Hilbert's fifth problem](wikipedia://en/152760).)
