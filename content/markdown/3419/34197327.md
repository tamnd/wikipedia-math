# Hodge–Tate module

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hodge–Tate module"}

In mathematics, a **Hodge–Tate module** is an analogue of a [Hodge structure](wikipedia://en/10297305) over [p-adic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_field). (txt) introduced and named Hodge–Tate structures using the results of (txt) on [p-divisible groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-divisible_group).

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *G* is the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of a *p*-adic field *K*. Then *G* has a canonical [cyclotomic character](wikipedia://en/18846136) χ given by its action on the *p*th power [roots of unity](wikipedia://en/171950). Let *C* be the completion of the [algebraic closure](wikipedia://en/3129) of *K*. Then a finite-dimensional [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) over *C* with a semi-linear action of the [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) *G* is said to be of **Hodge–Tate type** if it is generated by the eigenvectors of integral powers of χ.

:::
