# Marina Logares

Logares

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marina Logares"}

**Marina Logares Jiménez** (born 1976, in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid)) is a Spanish mathematician and LGBT+ activist. She was named one of the 50 most influential LGBT+ people in Spain in 2019.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Logares was awarded her PhD in mathematics in 2006 from the [Autonomous University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autonomous_University_of_Madrid).[^o4][^o5] She later worked at the [Max Planck Institute](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn, the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), the [University of Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Porto) and the [University of Plymouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Plymouth).[^o4][^o5][^o6] She is currently a visiting professor at the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complutense_University_of_Madrid).[^o5] Logares studies within the field of geometry. Her areas of interest in research focus on algebraic and complex geometry and mathematical physics. She particularly studies [Yang–Mills equations](wikipedia://en/35544664), and their derivatives, Hitchin equations.[^o7]

In 2018 she published the book *Las geometrías y otras revoluciones* (Geometry and other revolutions),[^o8] which describes the history of the field of geometry.[^o6]

## Activism {#Activism}

Logares is notable for her social engagement, particularly her participation in the struggle for the LGBT community's rights and inclusion in science. She is a [lesbian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lesbian).[^o10][^o6] At the university of Plymouth, Logares was the co-chair of the LGBT co-forum.[^o6][^o11] In 2019 she was included in a list of the 50 most influential LGBT people in Spain by newspaper *[El Mundo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Mundo_%28Spain%29)*.[^o11][^o12] In 2020 she was the keynote speaker the LGBT STEMinar in Birmingham, UK.[^o13]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Logares is a 3rd degree black belt in [Taekwondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taekwondo)[^o4][^o6] and black belt in [Hapkido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hapkido).

## Articles {#Articles}

Some of her publications are:

- Paper 'Moduli Spaces of Framed G–Higgs Bundles and Symplectic Geometry'. With Indranil Biswas and Ana Peón Nieto. July 2019.[^o16]
- Paper 'On Higgs Bundles on Nodal Curves'. March 2019.[^o16]
- Book 'Las matemáticas y otras revoluciones'. Colección ¿Qué sabemos de? del CSIC y Catarata, 2018.[^o17]
- Paper 'A lax monoidal Topological Quantum Field Theory for representation varieties'. With Ángel González-Prieto. September 2017.[^o16]
- Paper 'Bohr--Sommerfeld Lagrangians of moduli spaces of Higgs bundles'. With Indranil Biswas and Niels Leth Gammelgaard. September 2014.[^o16]
- Hodge-Deligne polynomials of SL(2,C)-character varieties for curves of small genus'. With Vicente Muñoz y Peter E. Newstead. June 2011.[^o16]
- Artículo 'A Torelli theorem for the moduli space of parabolic Higgs bundles'. Con Tomás L. Gómez. Mayo 2009.[^o16]
- Thesis 'Parabolic U(p;q)Higgs bundles'. May 2006.[^o16]
