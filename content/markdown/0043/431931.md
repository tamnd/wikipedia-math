# 200 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="200"}

**200** (**two hundred**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [199](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/199_%28number%29) and preceding [201](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/201_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

200 is an [abundant number](wikipedia://en/321831),[^o4] as 265, the sum of its [proper divisors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_divisor), is greater than itself.[^o5]

The number appears in the [Padovan sequence](wikipedia://en/2161709), preceded by [86](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/86_%28number%29), [114](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/114_%28number%29), and [151](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/151_%28number%29) (it is the sum of the first two of these).[^o6]

The sum of [Euler's totient function](wikipedia://en/53452) φ(*x*) over the first twenty-five integers is 200.[^o7]

200 is the smallest base 10 unprimeable number – it cannot be turned into a prime number by changing just one of its digits to any other digit. It is also a [Harshad number](wikipedia://en/641307).

200 is an [Achilles number](wikipedia://en/7894744).
