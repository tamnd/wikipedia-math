# Aisenstadt Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aisenstadt Prize"}

The **André Aisenstadt Prize** recognizes a young [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) mathematician's outstanding achievement in pure or applied mathematics.[^o2]

:::

It has been awarded annually since 1992 (except in 1994, when no prize was given) by the [Centre de Recherches Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/3505436) at the [University of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montreal). The prize consists of a \$3,000 award and a medal. It is named after André Aisenstadt.
