# Favard constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Favard constant"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Favard constant** (also called the **Akhiezer–Krein–Favard constant**) of order $r$ is defined as[^o2]

$$K_r = \frac{4}{\pi} \sum\limits_{k=0}^{\infty} \left[ \frac{(-1)^k}{2k+1} \right]^{r+1}.$$

The particular values of Favard constant are $K_0 = 1$, $K_1 = \frac{\pi}{2}$, $K_2 = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$.

:::

This constant is named after the French mathematician [Jean Favard](wikipedia://en/9995378), and after the Soviet mathematicians [Naum Akhiezer](wikipedia://en/9119648) and [Mark Krein](wikipedia://en/1190470).

::: {#o4 .application cue=heading name="Uses"}

## Uses {#Uses}

This constant is used in solutions of several extremal problems, for example

- Favard's constant is the sharp constant in [Jackson's inequality](wikipedia://en/9356047) for [trigonometric polynomials](wikipedia://en/591568)
- the sharp constants in the [Landau–Kolmogorov inequality](wikipedia://en/8564970) are expressed via Favard's constants
- Norms of periodic [perfect splines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_spline).
- The second Favard constant, $K_2 = \frac{\pi^2}{8}$ is the same as the value of the internal 4-dimensional equivalent of the angles in a [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112). The first Favard constant is equal to the value of the internal solid angles in cubes, and the internal angles in squares.

:::
