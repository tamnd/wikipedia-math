# Fred Oswald

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fred Oswald"}

**Fred Oswald** is an American industrial organizational psychologist who is known for his contributions to artificial intelligence ethics,[^o3][^o4] psychometrics, workforce readiness,[^o5] and the measurement of individual differences in organizational settings.[^o6] He is currently a professor at the [University of California, Irvine School of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine_School_of_Education).[^o7] Until January, 2026, he was the Herbert S. Autrey Professor of Social Sciences at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University)[^o8] in [Houston, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Houston). He is a national associate of the [National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academies_of_Sciences,_Engineering,_and_Medicine),[^o9] a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Organizational Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Industrial_and_Organizational_Psychology),[^o10] and the [Association for Psychological Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Psychological_Science).[^o11] He currently serves as the editor of the journal *[Psychological Methods](wikipedia://en/37384857)*.[^o12] In 2021, Oswald was appointed to the U.S. Department of Commerce's Artificial Intelligence Consortium.[^o13][^o14]

:::
