# Thin category

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thin category"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a **thin category**, or **posetal category**, is a [category](wikipedia://en/53759) whose [homsets](wikipedia://en/53759#Small_and_large_categories) each contain at most one morphism.[^o3] As such, a thin category amounts to a [preordered class](wikipedia://en/15366532) (or a [preordered set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preordered_set), if its objects form a [set](wikipedia://en/26691)).[^o4] A thin category that is [skeletal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skeletal_category) (i.e. whose isomorphisms are endomorphisms) amounts to a [partially ordered class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partially_ordered_class) (or a [poset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poset) if the category is [small](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_category)). A thin category is sometimes assumed skeletal.

:::

Equivalently, a thin category is a category [enriched](wikipedia://en/142622) over the initial boolean algebra $2=\{0,1\}$ regarded as a [cartesian monoidal category](wikipedia://en/43676277).

All [diagrams](wikipedia://en/7651625) [commute](wikipedia://en/138484) in a thin category. When the commutative diagrams of a category are interpreted as a typed equational theory whose objects are the types, a [codiscrete](wikipedia://en/46876400) thin category corresponds to an inconsistent theory understood as one satisfying the axiom *x* = *y* at all types.

Viewing a [2-category](wikipedia://en/2740708) as an [enriched category](wikipedia://en/142622) whose hom-objects are categories, the hom-objects of any extension of a thin category to a [2-category](wikipedia://en/2740708) having the same 1-cells are [monoids](wikipedia://en/19652).

Some [lattice-theoretic](wikipedia://en/469304) structures are definable as (usually skeletal) thin categories of a certain kind. For example, under this assumption, a poset may be defined as a small skeletal thin category, a [distributive lattice](wikipedia://en/310883) as a small skeletal thin [distributive category](wikipedia://en/6053993), a [Heyting algebra](wikipedia://en/309343) as a small skeletal thin finitely [cocomplete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cocomplete) [cartesian closed category](wikipedia://en/237037), and a [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/54476844) as a small skeletal thin finitely cocomplete [\*-autonomous category](wikipedia://en/3865506). Conversely, categories, distributive categories, finitely cocomplete cartesian closed categories, and finitely cocomplete \*-autonomous categories can be considered the respective [categorifications](wikipedia://en/1596969) of posets, distributive lattices, Heyting algebras, and Boolean algebras.
