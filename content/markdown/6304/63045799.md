# Equal detour point

*Image: Equal detour point.svg.* solid black solid black 1px dashed #2378e8 2px dashed orange 2px $\begin{aligned} & h_A + h_C - b \\ ={}& h_A + h_B - c \\ ={}& h_B + h_C - a \end{aligned}$ $I, Q, P$ and the [Gergonne point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergonne_point) $G$ are [collinear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collinear) and form a [harmonic range](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harmonic_range): $\frac{\overline{QI}}{\overline{PI}} = \frac{\overline{QG}}{\overline{PG}}$

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Equal detour point"}

In [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **equal detour point** is a [triangle center](wikipedia://en/12401488) denoted by *X*(176) in [Clark Kimberling](wikipedia://en/25536421)'s [Encyclopedia of Triangle Centers](wikipedia://en/15003593). It is characterized by the equal detour property: if one travels from any vertex of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) $\triangle ABC$ to another by taking a detour through some inner point $P$, then the additional distance traveled is constant. This means the following equation has to hold:[^o2]

:::

$$\begin{aligned} & \overline{AP} + \overline{PC} - \overline{AC} \\[3mu] ={}& \overline{AP} + \overline{PB} - \overline{AB} \\[3mu] ={}& \overline{BP} + \overline{PC} - \overline{BC}. \end{aligned}$$

The equal detour point is the only point with the equal detour property [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) the following inequality holds for the angles $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ of $\triangle ABC$:[^o3]

$$\tan\tfrac12\alpha + \tan\tfrac12\beta + \tan \tfrac12\gamma \leq 2$$

If the inequality does not hold, then the [isoperimetric point](wikipedia://en/35968513) possesses the equal detour property as well.

The equal detour point, isoperimetric point, the [incenter](wikipedia://en/316837) and the [Gergonne point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gergonne_point) of a triangle are [collinear](wikipedia://en/3189581), that is all four points lie on a common line. Furthermore, they form a [harmonic range](wikipedia://en/16094600) (see graphic on the right).[^o4]

The equal detour point is the center of the inner [Soddy circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soddy_circle) of a triangle and the additional distance travelled by the detour is equal to the diameter of the inner Soddy Circle.[^o4]

The [barycentric coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barycentric_coordinates) of the equal detour point are[^o4]

$$\left( a+\frac{\Delta}{s-a} : b+\frac{\Delta}{s-b} : c+\frac{\Delta}{s-c} \right).$$

and the [trilinear coordinates](wikipedia://en/3837790) are:[^o2]

$$1 + \frac{\cos\tfrac12\beta\,\cos\tfrac12\gamma}{\cos\tfrac12\alpha} \ :\ 1 + \frac{\cos\tfrac12\gamma\,\cos\tfrac12\alpha}{\cos\tfrac12\beta} \ :\ 1 + \frac{\cos\tfrac12\alpha\,\cos\tfrac12\beta}{\cos\tfrac12\gamma}$$
