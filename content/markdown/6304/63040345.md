# Jean-Paul Pier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Paul Pier"}

**Jean-Paul Pier** (July 5, 1933 – December 14, 2016) was a [Luxembourgish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg) mathematician, specializing in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), particularly mathematical analysis in the 20th century.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Jean-Paul Pier was a graduate student in Luxembourg and at the universities of Paris and Nancy. He earned a [University of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Luxembourg) doctorate in [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436) and a French doctorate in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210). He also spent six months at the [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble) Nuclear Research Center (1961) and a year at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) (1966-1967).

He taught mathematics at the [Lycée de Garçons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_de_Garçons_de_Luxembourg) in [Esch-sur-Alzette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Esch-sur-Alzette) from 1956 to 1980. In 1971 he created the *Séminaire de mathématiques*[^o6] at the Centre universitaire de Luxembourg (now the [University of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Luxembourg)). He was a professor at the Centre from its creation in 1974 until 1998, when he retired as [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus).[^o7]

Pier was primarily responsible for the creation in January 1989 of the [Luxembourg Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/17539719),[^o8] of which he was president from 1989 to 1993 and again from 1995 to 1998. He was during the academic year 1994–1995 a visiting professor at the [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain).

Pier was the editor of two scholarly anthologies, which are standard works on the history of 20th-century mathematics. He organized several [colloquia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seminar) and conferences in Luxembourg. He was active internationally in various scientific bodies, including [NATO Science for Peace and Security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NATO_Science_for_Peace_and_Security) and [UNESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UNESCO).

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *Amenable locally compact groups*, Wiley, 1984.
- *Amenable Banach algebras*, Longman, 1988.[^o10]
- *L'Analyse harmonique. Son développement historique*, Masson, 1990.
- *Histoire de l'intégration, vingt-cinq siècles de mathématiques*, Masson, 1996.
- *Mathematical Analysis during the 20th century*, Oxford University Press, 2001[^o11]
- *Mathématiques entre savoir et connaissance*, Vuibert, 2006.
- *Development of Mathematics 1900-1950*, edited by Jean-Paul Pier, Birkhäuser, 1994.[^o12]
- *[Development of Mathematics 1950-2000](https://books.google.com/books?id=rB7BjYsD2j4C)*, edited by Jean-Paul Pier, Birkhäuser, 2000.[^o13][^o14]
- *Gabriel Lippmann. Commémoration par la section des sciences naturelles, physiques et mathématiques de l’Institut grand-ducal de Luxembourg du 150<sup>e</sup> anniversaire du savant né au Luxembourg lauréat du prix Nobel en 1908*, J.-P. Pier et J. A. Massard, éditeurs, 1997.
- *Le Choix de la parole*, Lethielleux/DDB, 2009.
