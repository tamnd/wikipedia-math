# Victor Goryunov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Goryunov"}

**Victor Vladimirovich Goryunov** is a Russian mathematician born in 1956. He is a leading figure in [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), whose contributions to the subject are fundamental.[^o3] He has published several books and a variety of papers in [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), [finite type invariants](wikipedia://en/3014395), and [Legendrian knots](wikipedia://en/7404755). Many of his papers in [Lagrangian](wikipedia://en/28356) and [Legendrian geometry](wikipedia://en/476398) are now considered to be classical in the subject.[^o3]

:::

Goryunov completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) thesis, titled "*Surface projection singularities*", at [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)'s [Lomonosov State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1981, under the direction of [Vladimir Arnold](wikipedia://en/32490). He is currently a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool).[^o4] He was an editorial advisor of the journal [Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).

## Publications {#Publications}

He is the co-author of two published books.

- Arnold, V. I.; Goryunov, V. V.; Lyashko, O. V.; Vassiliev, V. A. (1993). "Dynamical Systems VI: Singularity Theory I, Local and global theory". Springer
- Arnold, V. I.; Goryunov, V. V.; Lyashko, O. V.; Vassiliev, V. A. (1993). "Dynamical Systems VIII: Singularity Theory II, Classification and Applications". Springer

Since 1978, he has published more than 50 peer reviewed articles. A list of recent publications and preprints, together with PDF files, can be found [here](https://pcwww.liv.ac.uk/~goryunov/); a list of earlier publications can be found [here](https://web.archive.org/web/20090324064519/http://www.liv.ac.uk/maths/PURE/MIN_SET/CONTENT/members/V_Goryunov.html).
