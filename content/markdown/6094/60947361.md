# Robert McCann (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert McCann"}

**Robert John McCann** is a Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [transportation theory](wikipedia://en/6631661). He has worked as a professor at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) since 1998, and as Canada Research Chair in Mathematics, Economics, and Physics since 2020.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

McCann was raised in [Windsor, Ontario](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Windsor,_Ontario). He studied engineering and physics at [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston) before graduating with a degree in math, and earned a PhD in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1994.[^o4] McCann was a Tamarkin Assistant Professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) from 1994, before joining the [University of Toronto Department of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto_Department_of_Mathematics) in the fall of 1998.[^o5] He served as editor-in-chief of the *[Canadian Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Journal_of_Mathematics)* from 2007 to 2016, and again since 2022.[^o6] He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul in 2014.[^o7] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012, of the Royal Society of Canada in 2014, of the Fields Institute in 2015 and of the Canadian Mathematical Society in 2020. In 2025 he received the [Norbert Wiener Prize in Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norbert_Wiener_Prize_in_Applied_Mathematics).[^o8]
