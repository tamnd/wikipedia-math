::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Tetrahedral cupola"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **tetrahedral cupola** is a [polychoron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polychoron) bounded by one [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606), a parallel [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280), connected by 10 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), and 4 [triangular pyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_pyramid).[^o6]

:::

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The *tetrahedral cupola* can be sliced off from a [runcinated 5-cell](wikipedia://en/2386577), on a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) parallel to a tetrahedral cell. The [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) base passes through the center of the runcinated 5-cell, so the Tetrahedral cupola contains half of the tetrahedron and triangular prism cells of the runcinated 5-cell. The cupola can be seen in A<sub>2</sub> and A<sub>3</sub> Coxeter plane orthogonal projection of the runcinated 5-cell:
