::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Cubic cupola"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **cubic cupola** is a [4-polytope](wikipedia://en/24979) bounded by a [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213), a parallel [cube](wikipedia://en/6285), connected by 6 [square prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_prism), 12 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), 8 [triangular pyramids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_pyramid).[^o6]

:::

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}
