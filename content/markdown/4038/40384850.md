::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Octahedral cupola"}

In 4-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **octahedral cupola** is a [4-polytope](wikipedia://en/24979) bounded by one [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) and a parallel [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213), connected by 20 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), and 6 [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455).[^o6]

:::

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The *octahedral cupola* can be sliced off from a [runcinated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcinated_24-cell), on a hyperplane parallel to an octahedral cell. The cupola can be seen in a B<sub>2</sub> and B<sub>3</sub> Coxeter plane orthogonal projection of the runcinated 24-cell:
