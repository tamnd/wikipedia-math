# Paul W. Holland

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul W. Holland"}

**Paul William Holland** (born 25 April 1940) is an American statistician. He has worked on a wide range of fields including: [categorical data analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_data_analysis), [social network analysis](wikipedia://en/325726) and [causal inference](wikipedia://en/37103476) in [program evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Program_evaluation).[^o3]

:::

Paul Holland was born in [Tulsa, Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulsa,_Oklahoma). He attended the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) as an undergraduate, and [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) for a master's and doctorate in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), supervised by [Patrick Suppes](wikipedia://en/4824664).[^o2] [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University) and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) were his first teaching posts. He started at [Educational Testing Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Testing_Service) in 1975. From 1993 to 2000 he taught at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), before returning to Educational Testing Service.

He held the [Frederic M. Lord](wikipedia://en/16006063) Chair in Measurement and Statistics at the Educational Testing Service.[^o3][^o4]
