# Bifolium

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bifolium"}

*Image: Bifolium01.svg.* Bifolium with $a = 1$

A **bifolium** is a [quartic plane curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartic_plane_curve) with equation in [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate):

:::

$$(x^2 + y^2)^2 = ax^2y.$$

## Construction and equations {#Construction_and_equations}

*Image: Bifolium.gif.* Construction of the bifolium

Given a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) C through a [point](wikipedia://en/593693) O, and [line](wikipedia://en/946975) L [tangent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangent_line) to the circle at point O: for each point Q on C, define the point P such that PQ is [parallel](wikipedia://en/664497) to the tangent line L, and PQ = OQ. The collection of points P forms the bifolium.[^o3]

In [polar coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_coordinates), the bifolium's equation is

$$\rho=a\sin\theta\cdot\cos^2\theta,$$

while (first eqn.)

$$\rho^{2\cdot2}=a\cdot x^2y,\,\,\rho^2=\pm x\cdot(ay)^{1/2}.$$

For *a* = 1, the total included [area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Area_%28geometry%29) is approximately 0.10.
