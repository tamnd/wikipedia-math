# Sergey Kitaev

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sergey Kitaev"}

**Sergey Kitaev** (Russian: Сергей Владимирович Китаев; born 1 January 1975 in [Ulan-Ude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulan-Ude)) is a Professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde), [Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow), Scotland.[^o3][^o4][^o5] He obtained his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Gothenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Gothenburg) in 2003 under the supervision of [Einar Steingrímsson](wikipedia://en/80234085). Kitaev's research interests concern aspects of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Kitaev is best known for his book *Patterns in permutations and words* (2011), an introduction to the field of [permutation patterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutation_patterns).[^o8] He is also the author (with Vadim Lozin) of *Words and graphs* (2015) on the theory of [word-representable graphs](wikipedia://en/60141647) which he pioneered.

Kitaev has written over 120 research articles in mathematics.[^o9][^o10] Of particular note is his work generalizing [vincular patterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincular_pattern) to having partially ordered entries, a classification (with Anders Claesson) of bijections between 321- and 132-avoiding permutations, and a solution (with Steve Seif) of the word problem for the Perkins semigroup, as well as his work on word-representable graphs.[^o11]
