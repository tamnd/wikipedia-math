# Johannes Frischauf

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Frischauf"}

**Johannes Frischauf**(17 September 1837 in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) – 7 January 1924 in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz)) was an Austrian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist), [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer), [geodesist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesist) and [alpinist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpinist).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

*Image: Frischaufweg Graz.jpg.* Graz: Street sign "Frischaufweg"

Frischauf passed the [matura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matura) at the [Academic Gymnasium in Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akademisches_Gymnasium_%28Wien%29) and in 1857 studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna), as well as geodesy, [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) at the [Technischen Hochschule Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien). He obtained the [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1864, and became [Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent) for mathematics at the University of Vienna and assistant at the observatory of the university. In 1863 he was [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in mathematics. Starting in 1863, he was professor at the [University of Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Graz) for pure and applied mathematics. He worked together with [Ludwig Boltzmann](wikipedia://en/544255). Frischauf developed a new method of [map design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map_design) and wrote textbooks on [arithmetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetics) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) – for instance in 1872 and 1876 he wrote summaries of the then current knowledge about [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610) (which he called "absolute geometry"). In 1885 he was elected as a member of the [Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8]

*Image: Frischaufova koča 1903 Alois Beer.png.* The [Frischauf Lodge at Okrešelj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frischauf_Lodge_at_Okrešelj) ([Alois Beer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alois_Beer), 1903)

Starting with 1868, Frischauf pioneered the touristic development of the [Sannthaler and Steiner Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steiner_Alpen) by opening ways and huts. The streets over the [Paulitschsattel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulitschsattel) and the connection [Sulzbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solčava)-[Leutsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luče) were built because of his initiatives. Together with Franz von Juraschek and Mathias Spreiz he was the first to climb the [Admonter Reichenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Admonter_Reichenstein). Frischauf defended the view at a time of violent national conflicts, that alpinism should not be subordinated under nationalism or religion or political views. He participated in the foundation of the [Croatian Mountaineering Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatian_Mountaineering_Association). Frischauf's [Funerary urn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Funerary_urn) was placed on the Scheichenspitze in the [Dachstein Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dachstein_Mountains). His estate is located at the University of Graz.
