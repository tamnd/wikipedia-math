# Klaus Schmidt (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Klaus Schmidt"}

**Klaus D. Schmidt** (born 25 September 1943) is an Austrian mathematician and retired professor at the Faculty of Mathematics, [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna).

:::

After studying mathematics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) he received his doctorate in 1968 under [Edmund Hlawka](wikipedia://en/5442007).[^o3] He held visiting professorships in [Technical University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Vienna), [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) in 1969, [Bedford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_College_%28London%29) (1969–1974) and the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) from 1974 to 1994 after which he came back to the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). He retired in 2009. In 1975/76 [K. R. Parthasarathy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._R._Parthasarathy_%28probabilist%29) invited Klaus Schmidt to spend 7 months at the new [Delhi Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Statistical_Institute,_Delhi_Centre) of [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418) (Parthasarathy was then working at the [Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology,_Delhi)).

In 1994 he was awarded the [Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Prize](wikipedia://en/57181134) for the monograph *Dynamical systems of algebraic origin*. He is member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences).

He has researched among other things, [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) and its connections with [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118), [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).
