# Volodymyr Mazorchuk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Volodymyr Mazorchuk"}

**Volodymyr Mazorchuk** (born 1972) is a Ukrainian-Swedish mathematician at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) and was awarded the [Göran Gustafsson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göran_Gustafsson_Prize) in 2016.[^o2][^o3]

:::

He received his PhD in mathematics from [Taras Shevchenko National University of Kyiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taras_Shevchenko_National_University_of_Kyiv) in 1996 with advisor [Yuriy Drozd](wikipedia://en/65355197).
