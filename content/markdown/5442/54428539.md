# Teofilo Bruni

*Image: Bruni - Armonia astronomica e geometrica, 1622 - 1230097.jpg.* *Armonia astronomica e geometrica*, 1622

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Teofilo Bruni"}

**Teofilo Bruni** ([Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verona), 1569 - [Vicenza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vicenza), 1638 ) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Born in [Verona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Verona), he was a [capuchin friar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Friars_Minor_Capuchin) known by the name of Raffaele.[^o4] He wrote mainly about [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), but he also published a book about clocks and other tools based on mathematical concepts.[^o5]
