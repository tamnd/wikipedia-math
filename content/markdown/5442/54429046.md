# Jean Du Breuil

*Image: Breuil - Diverses methodes universelles et nouvelles, en tout ou en partie pour faire des perspectives, 1642 - 177384.jpg.* *Diverses methodes universelles et nouvelles, en tout ou en partie pour faire des perspectives*, 1642

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Du Breuil"}

**Jean Dubreuil**, also known as **Jean Du Breuil** (22 July 1602 – 27 April 1670), was a French mathematician, music theorist, writer and essayist.[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Son of the [bookseller](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bookselling) Claude Du Breuil, he continued his father's profession until he joined the [Society of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus). He lived for a long time in Rome where he studied architecture. He is known for his work about the theory and practice of [perspective](wikipedia://en/23912960).[^o4]
