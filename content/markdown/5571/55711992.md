# Hélène Barcelo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hélène Barcelo"}

**Hélène Barcelo** (born 11 November 1954)[^o3] is a Canadian[^o4][^o5] mathematician specializing in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582). Within that field, her interests include combinatorial [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), and [arrangements of hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/864438). She is Professor Emerita of Mathematics at [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University), and Deputy Director Emerita of the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (MSRI). She was editor-in-chief of the *[Journal of Combinatorial Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Combinatorial_Theory)*, Series A, from 2001 to 2009.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Barcelo completed her Ph.D. from the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego), in 1988. Her dissertation, *On the Action of the Symmetric Group on the Free Lie Algebra and on the Homology and Cohomology of the Partition Lattice*, was supervised by [Adriano Garsia](wikipedia://en/18857510).

She joined the Arizona State faculty after postdoctoral studies at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan). She retired from Arizona State, becoming a professor emerita there, and became deputy director at MSRI in 2008.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

From 2012-2014, Barcelo served as a Council Member at Large for the American Mathematical Society.[^o8] She was elected to the 2018 class of [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), to the 2019 class of fellows of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), and to the 2021 class of Fellows of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).
