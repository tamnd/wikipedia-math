# Sarah Witherspoon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sarah Witherspoon"}

**Sarah Jane Witherspoon** is an American mathematician interested in topics in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), including [Hochschild cohomology](wikipedia://en/10499629)SW99 and [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678).W96BW04 She is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Witherspoon graduated from [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University) in 1988, where she earned the Charles Wexler Mathematics Prize as the best mathematics student at ASU that year. She went on to graduate study in mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), and completed her Ph.D. in 1994. Her dissertation, supervised by [Jonathan Lazare Alperin](wikipedia://en/25403965), was *The Representation Ring of the Quantum Double of a Finite Group*.

## Career {#Career}

Witherspoon taught at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) from 1994 to 1998. After holding visiting assistant professorships at [Mills College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mills_College), the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), [Mount Holyoke College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Holyoke_College), the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst), and [Amherst College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amherst_College), she joined the Texas A&M faculty in 2004.

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

She was elected to the 2018 class of [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and cohomology of [Hopf algebras](wikipedia://en/325714), quantum groups, and related objects, and for service to the profession and mentoring".
