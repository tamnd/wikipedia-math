# Witold Nowacki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Witold Nowacki"}

Prof **Witold Nowacki** H[FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) PPAS (1911–1986) was a Polish mathematician and expert on the mechanics of elasticity and thermoelasticity. He served as President of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) from 1978 to 1980 and was the first President of the Society of the Interaction of Mathematics and Mechanics.

:::

## Life {#Life}

*Image: Witold Nowacki grób.JPG.* The grave of Witold Nowacki in Warsaw

Nowacki was born in [Zakrzewo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zakrzewo,_Gniezno_County) in Poland on 20 July 1911, the son of Ludwik Nowacki and his wife, Bronislawa Czyzewska.[^o3]

He studied at [Gdańsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gdańsk) Polytechnic graduating in 1934.

In the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War) he served as an officer in the Polish Army but was captured in 1939 and spent the entire war as a German [prisoner of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner_of_war). In 1945 he was accepted as a Professor at [Gdańsk University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gdańsk_University_of_Technology) lecturing in the Strength of Materials. In 1952 he transferred to Warsaw Polytechnic lecturing in Building Mechanics and from 1955 taught Elasticity and Plasticity at [Warsaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_University). In 1979 he was elected an Honorary Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o4]

Nowacki retired in 1980.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1936, Nowacki married Janina Sztabianka.[^o6] On 23 August 1986, Nowacki died in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), Poland.

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Thermoelasticity* (1962)
- *Dynamics of Elastic Systems* (1963)
- *Theory of Micropolar Elasticity* (1970)
- *Trends in Elasticity and Thermoelasticity* (1971)
- *Autobiographical Notes* (1985)
- *Theory of Asymmetric Elasticity* (1986)
