# Estelle Basor

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Estelle Basor"}

**Estelle Lucille Basor** (born 1947) is an American mathematician interested in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and the theory of [random matrices](wikipedia://en/1648765). She is professor emeritus of mathematics at the [California Polytechnic State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Polytechnic_State_University) (Cal Poly), and deputy director of the [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Basor earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) in 1969, and completed a Ph.D. there in 1975. Her dissertation, supervised by [Harold Widom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harold_Widom), was *Asymptotic Formulas for Toeplitz Determinants*.

She joined the Cal Poly faculty in 1976, and taught there until retiring in 2008.

She served as an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) Council member at large from 2011 to 2013.[^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

At Cal Poly, she was the 2005 winner of the Distinguished Research, Creative Activity and Professional Development Award, and a colloquium in her and professor Rami Shani’s honor was held in 2006. She was elected to the 2018 class of [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Basor's husband, Kent E. Morrison, is also a mathematician who went to school with her at Santa Cruz, worked with her at Cal Poly, and is now associated with the American Institute of Mathematics.
