# Michael Fourman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Fourman"}

**Michael Paul Fourman** (born 12 September 1950) is an Emeritus Professor of Computer Systems at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh)[^o3] in Scotland, UK, and was Head of the School of [Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Informatics_%28academic_field%29) from 2001 to 2009.

:::

Fourman has worked in applications of [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence), and [cognitive science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cognitive_science) – more specifically, [formal models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_model) of [digital systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_system), [system design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_design) tools, [proof assistants](wikipedia://en/1258607), [categorical semantics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_semantics) and [propositional planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositional_planning).

## Qualifications {#Qualifications}

Fourman received a BSc in Mathematics and Philosophy from the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) in 1971, then his MSc in [Mathematical Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Logic) from the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) in 1972. He wrote his [DPhil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPhil) thesis *Connections between Category Theory and Logic* under the supervision of [Dana Scott](wikipedia://en/161875) at Oxford, defending his thesis in 1974.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

He continued to work with Scott as an [SRC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_and_Engineering_Research_Council) postdoctoral Research Fellow and Junior Research Fellow of [Wolfson College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfson_College,_Oxford), in Oxford, until 1976, when he moved to the US, first as a Visiting Assistant Professor of Mathematics at [Clark University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_University) in [Worcester, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts), then, from 1977 to 1982, as [JF Ritt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Ritt) Assistant Professor of Mathematics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in New York.

In 1983 he moved, with a [Science and Engineering Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_and_Engineering_Research_Council) Fellowship, to the Department of Electronic and Electrical Engineering at [Brunel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University). He was appointed to a Readership, and then to the Chair of Formal Systems, at Brunel in 1986.

Fourman was co-founder and Technical Director of Abstract Hardware Limited (AHL), a company formed in 1986. He was central in the development of the [LAMBDA system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LAMBDA_system) (Logic And Mathematics Behind Design Automation) to aid [hardware design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hardware_design), a tool implemented in the [SML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_ML) programming language and marketed by AHL. He left the company in 1997.

In 1988 he joined the [Laboratory for Foundations of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laboratory_for_Foundations_of_Computer_Science) at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), and was appointed to the Chair of Computer Systems in the Department of Computer Science. In 1998 he was founding Head of the Division of Informatics, which became the current [School of Informatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh_School_of_Informatics), incorporating the former Department of Artificial Intelligence, the [Artificial Intelligence Applications Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_Intelligence_Applications_Institute), the Centre for Cognitive Science, the [Human Communication Research Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Communication_Research_Centre), and the Department of Computer Science.

He has again been Head of the School of Informatics since August 2002.

In 2010, Fourman was elected Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh.[^o7]

He has held visiting positions at universities in [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) (1975), [Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) (1977, 1980), [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (1979–80), [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney) (1982), [Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) (1983), and [Perth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Australia) (1994).
