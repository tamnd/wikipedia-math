# Eckart Viehweg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Eckart Viehweg"}

**Eckart Viehweg** (born 30 December 1948 in [Zwickau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zwickau), died 29 January 2010)[^o3] was a German mathematician. He was a professor of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) at the [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen).

:::

In 2003 he won the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize) with his wife, [Hélène Esnault](wikipedia://en/5358788).
