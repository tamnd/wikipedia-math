# Hélène Esnault

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hélène Esnault"}

*Image: Helene Esnault.jpg.* Esnault at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 2006

**Hélène Esnault** (born 17 July 1953) is a French and German mathematician, specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Born in Paris, Esnault earned her PhD in 1976 from the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University). She wrote her dissertation on *Singularites rationnelles et groupes algebriques* (Rational singularities and algebraic groups) under the direction of [Lê Dũng Tráng](wikipedia://en/42622216).[^o4]

She did her habilitation at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1985, and pursued her studies at the [University of Duisburg-Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen). Afterwards, she was a Heisenberg scholar of the Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft (DFG) at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn.[^o5]

She became the first Einstein Professor at [Freie Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freie_Universität_Berlin) in 2012, as head of the algebra and number theory research group, after working previously at the University of Duisburg-Essen, the Max-Planck-Institut für Mathematik in Bonn, and at the University of Paris VII.[^o6]

In 2007 Esnault was a founding editor of the journal [Algebra & Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_&_Number_Theory). From 1998 to 2010 she co-edited [Mathematische Annalen](wikipedia://en/1423381); she has also served as editor of Acta Mathematica Vietnamica, [Astérisque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astérisque), [Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal), Mathematical Research Letters, [Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2001 she won the [Prix Paul Doistau-Émile Blutet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Paul_Doistau-Émile_Blutet) of the [Académie des sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_sciences_%28France%29)[^o8]. In 2003, she received together with [Eckart Viehweg](wikipedia://en/5358736) the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize).[^o9]. In 2019, she won the [Cantor medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantor_medal).[^o10].

In 2014 she was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o11] and is a member of the [Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina), the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities)[^o12], the Europäische Akademie Nordrhein-Westfalen, the [Danish Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Academy)[^o13], the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o14].

She received a Dr. Honoris Causa of the Vietnam Academy of Science and Technology in 2009, of the l'University of Rennes [Université de Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Rennes) in 2013, of the l'[University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) en 2023 [^o15].

In 2026 she was awarded the [Legion of Honour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour).[^o16].
