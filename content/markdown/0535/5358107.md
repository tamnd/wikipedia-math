# Donald John Lewis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Donald John Lewis"}

**Donald John Lewis** (25 January 1926 – 25 February 2015), better known as **D.J. Lewis**, was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

Lewis received his PhD in 1950 at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) under supervision of [Richard Dagobert Brauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Dagobert_Brauer), and subsequently was an NSF fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton (1952–1953), an NSF senior fellow (1959–1961), a senior visiting fellow at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (1965, 1969), a visiting fellow at [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) (1976), and Humboldt Awardee (1980, 1983).

He chaired the Department of Mathematics at the University of Michigan (1984-1994), and served as director of the Division of Mathematical Sciences at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) (NSF). He was long active in the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS), and in 1995 received its [Distinguished Public Service Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMS_Distinguished_Public_Service_Award).
