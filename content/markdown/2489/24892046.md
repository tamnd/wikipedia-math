# Erik Alfsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erik Alfsen"}

**Erik Magnus Alfsen** (13 May 1930 – 20 November 2019) was a Norwegian mathematician. He is the author of *Compact Convex Sets and Boundary Integrals*, published in 1971. He was a board member of the [Norwegian Research Council for Science and the Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Research_Council_for_Science_and_the_Humanities) (NAVF) for two years, and has also been involved in [Nei til Atomvåpen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nei_til_Atomvåpen) and the [Pugwash Conferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pugwash_Conferences). He was a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters), the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters) and the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters).[^o4][^o2]

:::

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He was born in [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo) as the son of school headmaster Knut Alfsen (1897–1978) and his wife Harriet Helander Nilsen (1901–1991). He was the grandson of [Magnus Alfsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnus_Alfsen), and a first cousin of [Lars Walløe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Walløe). Both his father and grandfather were headmasters and writers of a well-known textbook in mathematics. In July 1955 he married Ellen Platou; his father-in-law was then [Carl Platou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Platou).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Erik Alfsen enrolled in the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 1949, studied at [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) from 1954 to 1955, and back in Oslo, he took the [cand.real.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cand.real.) degree in 1955. After two years as a [research fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_fellow) in [NAVF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAVF), he was appointed as lecturer at the University of Oslo. After a time as visiting assistant professor in [Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seattle) from 1961 to 1962, he was promoted to [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) in Oslo in 1963 and was a professor from 1975 to 2000.
