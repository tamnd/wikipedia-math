# Behavior Genetics (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Behavior Genetics"}

***Behavior Genetics*** is a bimonthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). It covers "research in the inheritance of behavior" and is the official journal of the [Behavior Genetics Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavior_Genetics_Association). The journal was established in 1971 with [Steven G. Vandenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_G._Vandenberg) as its founding [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief). He was succeeded by [Jan Bruell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Bruell) (1978–1986).[^o3] Each year, the [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) chooses a particularly meritorious paper in the previous year's volume of the journal for the Editors' Choice Award, acknowledged by "\$1000 and a good bottle of wine" as well as a citation made in the journal. This award was created in the honor of [David Fulker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Fulker), a past president of the Behavior Genetics Association (1982) and former editor-in-chief (1987–1998).[^o4] From 2001–2025, [John K. Hewitt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_K._Hewitt) was editor-in-chief. He was succeeded by [Valerie Knopik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerie_Knopik) (2025–present).[^o5] The abstracts of the annual meetings are printed in the journal.

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in *[Biological Abstracts/BIOSIS Previews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biological_Abstracts), [CAB Abstracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAB_Abstracts), [Current Awareness in Biological Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Awareness_in_Biological_Sciences), [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Social & Behavioral Sciences* and *Life Sciences, [Embase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Embase), [PsycINFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PsycINFO), [MEDLINE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MEDLINE)/[PubMed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PubMed), [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded), [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus), [Social Sciences Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Sciences_Citation_Index)*, and *[The Zoological Record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Zoological_Record)*. According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2023 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 2.6.[^o7]
