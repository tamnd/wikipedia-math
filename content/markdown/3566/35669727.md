# Amit Garg

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Amit Garg"}

Dr **Amit Garg** is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [mental calculator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_calculator).[^o3][^o4][^o5] He currently works as founder and CEO at ORMAE,[^o6] a firm registered in USA, UAE & India which engages in building innovative products, consulting and training in [Operations Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operations_Research) and [Data Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_Science).

:::

On 15 March 2012, he broke the [mental calculation](wikipedia://en/567292) [world record](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_record) of completing ten tasks to "divide a 10-digit number by a 5-digit number" in a record time of 5:45 minutes without any errors.[^o4] These tasks were constructed by a program provided by Dr Ralf Laue, author of the Book of Alternative Records and Chairman of [Mental Calculation World Cup](wikipedia://en/4663849), such that there are no remainders. The previous record holder was Willem Bouman from the Netherlands with a time of 6:07 minutes. This world record was accepted at [Guinness World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guinness_World_Records), [Limca Book of World Records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limca_Book_of_World_Records) and UK's Book of Alternative Records. As a world record holder in [mental calculation](wikipedia://en/567292), Ralf Laue confirmed him as participant in the 5th [Mental Calculation World Cup](wikipedia://en/4663849) 2012 to be conducted at Germany.[^o3]

On 22 August 2012, he won the silver medal in the Mental Calculation competition at the annually conducted [Mind Sports Olympiad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_Sports_Olympiad) in London (UK). He was the first Indian to receive any of the medals in this category since the inception of this event in 1997.[^o7] On 1 October 2012, he received a sixth rank in the "most versatile calculator" category based on his performance in surprise tasks at the fifth [Mental Calculation World Cup](wikipedia://en/4663849) in Germany. He secured a 10th place in the overall ranking based on combination of standard and surprise tasks.

On 5 December 2017, he astonished students at [Middlesex University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middlesex_University), Dubai using his mathematical feats and spoke on impact of applied mathematics in different industries.[^o8][^o9] On 26 February 2018 – 2 March 2018, he exhibited Math Show and business impact of Optimization & Analytics to 500+ students, 100+ educators from 100+ schools as key honored speaker at 5 day science show at Mauritius organized by [University of Mauritius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mauritius) and [Unesco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unesco).[^o10] On 24 May 2018, he displayed his mathematical feat at [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), [Auckland University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland_University_of_Technology), [University of Waikato](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waikato) and few companies in New Zealand.[^o11] [^o12]
