# David Heron (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Heron"}

**David Heron** (28 April 1881 - 4 November 1969) was a [Scottish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_people) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661)[^o2] who was president of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) from 1947 to 1949.

:::

He was born in Perth and studied Mathematics and Natural Philosophy at the University of St Andrews.[^o2]

He was [Karl Pearson](wikipedia://en/164695)'s research assistant.[^o3] Later he became a fellow at the Eugenics Laboratory of University College London.[^o4]

In 1906 he published "On the relation of fertility in man to social status".

In 1915 he became chief statistician for the London Guarantee & Accident Company, an insurance company. During the Second World War, he was Director of Statistics for the Ministry of Food.[^o2]

He was married to Ethel Medwin from 1916 until her death in 1959.[^o2]
