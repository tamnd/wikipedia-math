# Hector Leak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hector Leak"}

**Hector Leak** [CBE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBE) (23 July 1887 – 5 April 1976) was a British statistician. He was appointed a CBE in 1942.[^o2] He was also the president of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) during a short period in 1941. Awarded the silver [Guy Medal](wikipedia://en/6752269) in 1940.
