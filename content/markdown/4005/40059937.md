# Mean field annealing

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mean field annealing"}

**Mean field annealing** is a [deterministic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deterministic) [approximation](wikipedia://en/336271) to the [simulated annealing](wikipedia://en/172244) technique of solving [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization) problems.[^o2] This method uses [mean field theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mean_field_theory) and is based on [Peierls' inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_inequalities#Peierls.E2.80.93Bogoliubov_inequality).

:::
