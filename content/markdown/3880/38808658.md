# 1895 Mexican census

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="1895 Mexican census"}

The **1895 Mexico Census** was the first census that took place in [Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexico) (excluding the 1793 Census which has been lost). It took place on October 20, 1895, and the total population was 12,700,294. The results also showed that most [Mexicans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_people) were [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church).

:::

## Languages in Mexico {#Languages_in_Mexico}

\83.3% of the resident population spoke [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Spanish) with the remainder of the population mostly speaking Indigenous languages. The most widely spoken indigenous languages were [Nahuatl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nahuatl) and [Mayan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mayan_languages). The most widely spoken European language besides Spanish was [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language).
