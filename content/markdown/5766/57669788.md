# Philip Gressman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Philip Gressman"}

**Philip Thaxton Gressman** (born November 22, 1978) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania). His research is in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), geometry, and partial differential equations, including work on geometric averaging operators, oscillatory integral operators, Fourier restriction, and the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522). He was named a in the 2025 class.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Gressman grew up in [Ava, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ava,_Missouri), and graduated from Ava High School in 1997.[^o5] He received a Bachelor of Arts degree from [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) in 2001, where he double majored in mathematics and physics, and was advised by [Guido Weiss](wikipedia://en/36535208) and Edward N. Wilson.[^o6][^o7] He completed a PhD in mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2005 under the supervision of [Elias Stein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias_Stein); his dissertation was titled *Lp-Lq estimates for radon-like operators*.[^o8]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

After completing his doctorate, Gressman was a J. W. Gibbs Assistant Professor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) from 2005 to 2008.[^o10] He joined the faculty of the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 2008, where he serves as professor of mathematics and undergraduate chair.[^o11]

Gressman's research investigates the intersection of harmonic analysis and geometry. His areas of interest include geometric averaging operators, oscillatory integral operators, sublevel set estimates, Fourier restriction, and other problems in analysis and partial differential equations.[^o12]

He is known for his joint work with Robert M. Strain on the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522). Their work established existence, uniqueness, and rapid convergence to equilibrium for perturbative classical solutions of the Boltzmann equation with long-range interactions.[^o13][^o14]
