# Toponogov's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Toponogov's theorem"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), **Toponogov's theorem** (named after [Victor Andreevich Toponogov](wikipedia://en/12957669)) is a triangle comparison theorem. It is one of a family of [comparison theorems](wikipedia://en/6626644) that quantify the assertion that a pair of geodesics emanating from a point *p* spread apart more slowly in a region of high curvature than they would in a region of low curvature.

:::

Let *M* be an *m*-dimensional [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) with [sectional curvature](wikipedia://en/285618) *K* satisfying $K\ge \delta\,.$ Let *pqr* be a [geodesic triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_triangle), i.e. a triangle whose sides are geodesics, in *M*, such that the geodesic *pq* is minimal and if δ > *0*, the length of the side *pr* is less than $\pi / \sqrt \delta$. Let *p*′*q*′*r*′ be a geodesic triangle in the model space *M*<sub>δ</sub>, i.e. the [simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) space of [constant curvature](wikipedia://en/3638803) δ, such that the lengths of sides *p′q′* and *p′r′* are equal to that of *pq* and *pr* respectively and the angle at *p′* is equal to that at *p*. Then

$$d(q,r) \le d(q',r').\,$$

When the sectional curvature is bounded from above, a corollary to the [Rauch comparison theorem](wikipedia://en/16221737) yields an analogous statement, but with the reverse inequality .
