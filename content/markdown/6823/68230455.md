# Margaret C. Wu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Margaret C. Wu"}

**Margaret C. Wu** is a Chinese-American biostatistician who worked at the [National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Heart,_Lung,_and_Blood_Institute) on topics including the analysis of [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717), [longitudinal studies](wikipedia://en/1557623), and [censored data](wikipedia://en/11548952).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wu earned a Ph.D. in 1973 from [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), with the dissertation *Asymptotic Behavior of Posterior Distributions and Bayes's Estimators for the Independent Not Identically Distributed Case*, supervised by Charles A. Rohde.

She worked in the Office of Biostatistics Research at the [National Heart, Lung, and Blood Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Heart,_Lung,_and_Blood_Institute), part of the [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) (NIH), from 1973 until her retirement in 2001.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Wu won the [National Institutes of Health MERIT Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health_MERIT_Award) in 1989. She was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 1994.
