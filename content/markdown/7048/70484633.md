# Lancaster University School of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lancaster University School of Mathematics"}

**Lancaster University School of Mathematics**, also known as **LUSoM**, is a [maths school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maths_school) located in [Preston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preston,_Lancashire), [Lancashire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancashire), England.[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o12][^o8] As a maths school, it is a specialist [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) [free school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_school_%28England%29) [sixth form college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sixth_form_college).

:::

The school was set up by the Rigby Education Trust,[^o9] a single-academy trust set up in partnership between the [Lancaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster_University) and [Cardinal Newman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinal_Newman_College) for the purpose of opening and operating the school.[^o10] It opened to students in September 2022[^o11] and is located in a £8.5 million school building on London Road, Preston,[^o12][^o5] and is the first purpose-built specialist Maths School in the UK.[^o13]

The school is highly selective, with prospective students expected to have [GCSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Certificate_of_Secondary_Education) mathematics qualification at grade 8 or 9 and required to sit an admissions assessment.[^o14] The course structure at LUSoM requires all students to study [A-level](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-level) Mathematics and Further Mathematics and a third A-level from either Physics, Chemistry or Computer Science. In addition, students may select a fourth subject from those three, or choose any other A-level subject to be taught at [Cardinal Newman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardinal_Newman_College), which is located less than half a mile from the school site.[^o14]
