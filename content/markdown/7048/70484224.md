# Mike Hill (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mike Hill"}

**Michael Anthony "Mike" Hill** is an American mathematician known for his research in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He is a professor at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). Together with [Michael J. Hopkins](wikipedia://en/20652569) and [Douglas Ravenel](wikipedia://en/27877012), he received the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)'s [Oswald Veblen Prize in Geometry](wikipedia://en/7085075) in 2022 for the paper "On the nonexistence of elements of Kervaire invariant one."[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Hill's undergraduate degree is from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o5] He earned a PhD in mathematics from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 2006. His doctoral advisor was [Michael J. Hopkins](wikipedia://en/20652569). His dissertation, in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), was titled "Computational Methods for Higher Real [K-Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-Theory) with Applications to Tmf."[^o6]

## Career and personal life {#Career_and_personal_life}

Hill earned tenure at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) before moving to the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 2015.[^o5] He moved to the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) in 2024.

Hill is a co-founder of [Spectra](wikipedia://en/61935783), an association for LGBTQ+ mathematicians.[^o8] He is also a member of the Spectra board.[^o9]
