# Pliny Chase

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pliny Chase"}

**Pliny Earle Chase** (August 18, 1820, in [Worcester, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts) – December 17, 1886, in [Haverford, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverford,_Pennsylvania)) was an American [scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientist), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [educator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educator) who contributed to the fields of [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy), [electromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetism), and [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), among others.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1839, then taught in [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia) and engaged in business for many years, but employed his leisure in physical and philological studies. In 1863, he was elected a member of the [American Antiquarian Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Antiquarian_Society) and the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society).[^o5][^o6] In 1864, the [Magellanic gold medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magellanic_Premium) of the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society) was awarded him for his *Numerical Relations of Gravity and Magnetism*. The results of other mathematical and physical researches were published from time to time in the *Proceedings* of the American Philosophical Society, and brought him a worldwide reputation as a man of unusual scientific powers and attainments. In 1871, he became a member of the faculty of [Haverford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverford_College), [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania), and for a long time was professor of philosophy and logic.[^o7] He published *Elements of Meteorology* (1884).

## Family {#Family}

His brother [Thomas Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Chase_%28educator%29) was a noted Latin scholar. His mother, Lydia Earle Chase, was the daughter of the famous inventor [Pliny Earle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pliny_Earle_I). Pliny Chase had two brothers and three sisters. His sister [Lucy Chase](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucy_Chase) (1822–1909) was a noted [abolitionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abolitionist), supporter of [women's suffrage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_suffrage) and the [temperance movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temperance_movement), and teacher in contraband camps and freedman schools in the American South.[^o9] Upon his death, Pliny Chase left a widow, two sons and three daughters.[^o10]
