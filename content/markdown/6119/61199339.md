# List of uniform tilings on the sphere, plane, and hyperbolic plane

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of uniform tilings on the sphere, plane, and hyperbolic plane"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), many uniform tilings on sphere, euclidean plane, and hyperbolic plane can be made by [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) within a fundamental triangle, (p q r), defined by internal angles as π/p, π/q, and π/r. Special cases are right triangles (p q 2). Uniform solutions are constructed by a single generator point with 7 positions within the fundamental triangle, the 3 corners, along the 3 edges, and the triangle interior. All vertices exist at the generator, or a reflected copy of it. Edges exist between a generator point and its image across a mirror. Up to 3 face types exist centered on the fundamental triangle corners. Right triangle domains can have as few as 1 face type, making regular forms, while general triangles have at least 2 triangle types, leading at best to a quasiregular tiling.

:::

There are different notations for expressing these uniform solutions, [Wythoff symbol](wikipedia://en/3691548), [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram), and Coxeter's t-notation.

Simple tiles are generated by [Möbius triangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Möbius_triangle) with whole numbers p,q,r, while [Schwarz triangles](wikipedia://en/2069325) allow rational numbers p,q,r and allow [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303) faces, and have overlapping elements.

- $p\ q\ (r\ s)\ |$ – This is a mixture of $p\ q\ r\ |$ and $p\ q\ s\ |$, containing only the faces shared by both.
