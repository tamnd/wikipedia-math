# Arthur Edward Murphy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur Edward Murphy"}

**Arthur Edward Murphy** (September 1, 1901 – May 11, 1962) was an [American philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States).[^o2]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Murphy was born in [Ithaca, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ithaca,_New_York). He earned a bachelor's degree in philosophy from [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1923, then went on to earn a doctorate there. He took an appointment at [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1927, then went to [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1928 before returning to Chicago in 1929. He took a position as full professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1931.

In 1939 he became department chair at [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois), and then served in the same position at Cornell University from 1946 to 1953. That year, he took the same position at [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington). In 1957, he came to [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas) as a visiting professor before being appointed chair in 1958. Murphy had a long-term serious [cardiac condition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiac_condition), and he died in [Austin, Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austin,_Texas) at age 60.
