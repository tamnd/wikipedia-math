# Vladimir Turaev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Turaev"}

**Vladimir Georgievich Turaev** (Владимир Георгиевич Тураев, born in 1954) is a Russian mathematician, specializing in topology.

:::

Turaev received in 1979 from the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) his Candidate of Sciences degree (PhD) under [Oleg Viro](wikipedia://en/14465687). Turaev was a professor at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg) and then became a professor at [Indiana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University). In 2016, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

Turaev's research deals with low-dimensional topology, quantum topology, and knot theory and their interconnections with quantum field theory. In 1991, [Reshetikhin](wikipedia://en/7487626) and Turaev published a mathematical construction of new topological invariants of compact oriented 3-manifolds and framed links in these manifolds, corresponding to a mathematical implementation of ideas in quantum field theory published by [Witten](wikipedia://en/54894);[^o3] the invariants are now called Witten-Reshetikhin-Turaev (or Reshetikhin-Turaev) invariants. In 1992, Turaev and Viro introduced a new family of invariants for 3-manifolds by using state sums computed on triangulations of manifolds;[^o4] these invariants are now called Turaev-Viro invariants.

In 1990, Turaev was an Invited Speaker with talk *State sum models in low dimensional topology* at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Kyōto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyōto).[^o5] In 2016, he shared, with Alexis Virelizier, the Ferran Sunyer i Balaguer Prize for their monograph *Monoidal categories and topological field theory*.
