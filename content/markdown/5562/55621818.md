# Jane Ye

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jane Ye"}

**Jane Ye** ([Chinese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplified_Chinese_characters): 叶娟娟) is a Chinese-[Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works as a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria). Her interests include [variational analysis](wikipedia://en/40237498) and [optimization constraint problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_constraint_problems). She is the 2015 winner of the [Krieger–Nelson Prize](wikipedia://en/11851829), given annually by the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) to an outstanding [female researcher in mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_women_in_mathematics).

:::

Ye was born in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China) and received her B.Sc. from [Xiamen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiamen_University) in 1982. She completed her doctorate in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) in 1990 at [Dalhousie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalhousie_University), under the supervision of [Michael Dempster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dempster). From 1990 to 1992, she was a postdoctoral researcher at the [Centre de Recherches Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/3505436) in Montreal, under the supervision of [Francis Clarke](wikipedia://en/61726385), before joining the faculty of [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria) in 1992 as an [NSERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NSERC) Women's Faculty Award Holder. She was promoted to associate professor in 1997 and to full professor in 2002.
