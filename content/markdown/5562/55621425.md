# Heinrich Liebmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Liebmann"}

**Karl Otto Heinrich Liebmann** (; 22 October 1874 – 12 June 1939) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Liebmann was the son of [Otto Liebmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Liebmann) (1840–1912), a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) [neo-Kantian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Kantianism) [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) professor at the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena).[^o4] Heinrich studied from 1895 to 1897 at the universities [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig_University), Jena and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). In 1895 he was awarded the [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) under [Carl Johannes Thomae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Johannes_Thomae) with the subject *Die einzweideutigen projektiven Punktverwandtschaften der Ebene* and passed the [Lehramtsprüfung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Staatsexamen) in 1896. In 1897 he was an assistant in Göttingen and in 1898 in Leipzig, where he was [habilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) on the subject *Über die Verbiegung der geschlossenen Flächen positiver Krümmung*. In this work, among other things, he stated [Liebmann's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liebmann's_theorem) in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

In 1905, he became extraordinary professor in Leipzig, in 1910 extraordinary professor at the [Technischen Hochschule München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_München), where he became professor in 1915. In 1920 he followed [Paul Stäckel](wikipedia://en/9075676) as professor at the [Universität Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruprecht-Karls-Universität_Heidelberg), where he became [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) in 1926 and [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the faculty of mathematics and natural science in 1923/1924 as well as in 1928/1929. In 1935 he asked for retirement due to political pressure of the [national socialists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_socialist) because of Liebmann's [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish) ancestry.[^o5] He and his colleague [Arthur Rosenthal](wikipedia://en/33183847) were boycotted in his faculty. He spent his last years in Munich.

In 1913 he married his first wife Natalie Liebmann, née Kraus († 1924), who was the daughter of Karl Kraus, professor of agricultural science in Munich. After the death of his first wife he married Helene Ehlers. He had four children.

Liebmann was concerned, among other things, with [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610). He discovered the construction of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) from its three angles by circle and ruler within [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291). In his habilitation, he showed that a convex closed surface cannot be bent (theorem of Minding and [Liebmann's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liebmann's_theorem)). He translated the works of [Nikolai Lobachevsky](wikipedia://en/648527) into German.

Liebmann was a member of the [Saxon Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saxon_Academy_of_Sciences), the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) and the [Heidelberg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o6]
