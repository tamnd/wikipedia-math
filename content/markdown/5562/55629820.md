# Marie Hušková

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marie Hušková"}

**Marie Hušková** (born 1942) is a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [theoretical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_statistics) statistics and [change-point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change-point) problem. She was a doctoral student of [Jaroslav Hájek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jaroslav_Hájek).

:::

In 2012 she was awarded by title Officer of the [Order of Orange-Nassau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Orange-Nassau) for long term cooperation with Dutch mathematical statisticians.[^o3] She is also a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics),[^o4] and an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o5]
