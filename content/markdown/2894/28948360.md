# Geometry Expert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geometry Expert"}

**Geometry Expert** (**GEX**) is a Chinese software package for dynamic diagram drawing and automated geometry theorem proving and discovering.

:::

There's a new Chinese version of Geometry Expert, called [MMP/Geometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics-Mechanization_Platform).

**Java Geometry Expert** is free under [GNU General Public License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License).
