# Snub hexahexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub hexahexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub hexahexagonal tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of sr{6,6}.

:::

## Images {#Images}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

A higher symmetry coloring can be constructed from \[6,4\] symmetry as s{6,4}, . In this construction there is only one color of hexagon.
