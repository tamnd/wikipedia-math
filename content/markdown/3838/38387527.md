# Order-6 hexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-6 hexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-6 hexagonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {6,6} and is [self-dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Self-dual).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

This tiling represents a hyperbolic [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope) of 6 mirrors defining a regular hexagon fundamental domain. This symmetry by [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678) is called \*333333 with 6 order-3 mirror intersections. In [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502) can be represented as \[6<sup>\*</sup>,6\], removing two of three mirrors (passing through the hexagon center) in the \[6,6\] symmetry.

The even/odd fundamental domains of this [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope) can be seen in the alternating colorings of the tiling:

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular tilings with order-6 vertices with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {n,6}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) , progressing to infinity.

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular tilings with [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) faces, starting with the [hexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/2863719), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {6,n}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) , progressing to infinity.
