# Raghavan Narasimhan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raghavan Narasimhan"}

Raghavan Narasimhan

**Raghavan Narasimhan** (August 31, 1937–October 3, 2015) was an [Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) who worked on [real](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_manifold) and [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742) and who solved the [Levi problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_problem) for complex manifolds.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

He attended Loyola College in Madras, where, like many other well-known Indian mathematicians, he was taught by the French Jesuit priest Racine, and received his doctorate in 1963 from K. Chandrasekharan in Bombay. In 1966 he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) at Princeton. Narasimhan was a professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).
