# Gorenstein–Harada theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gorenstein–Harada theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), the **Gorenstein–Harada theorem**, [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by [Daniel Gorenstein](wikipedia://en/1569478) and [Koichiro Harada](wikipedia://en/33107805), classifies the [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group) of sectional 2-rank at most 4.[^o2][^o3] It is part of the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445).[^o4]

:::

Finite simple groups of section 2 with rank at least 5 have [Sylow 2-subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_2-subgroup) with a self-centralizing [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of rank at least 3, which implies that they have to be of either [component type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Component_type) or of [characteristic 2 type](wikipedia://en/29668519). Therefore, the Gorenstein–Harada theorem splits the problem of classifying finite simple groups into these two sub-cases.
