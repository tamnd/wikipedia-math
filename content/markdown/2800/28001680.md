# Paul Erdős Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Erdős Award"}

The **Paul Erdős Award**, named after [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), is given by the [World Federation of National Mathematics Competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Federation_of_National_Mathematics_Competitions) (WFNMC) for those who "have played a significant role in the development of mathematical challenges at the national or international level and which have been a stimulus for the enrichment of mathematics learning". The awards have been given in two-year periods since 1992.

:::
