# Hendrik Kloosterman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hendrik Kloosterman"}

**Hendrik Douwe Kloosterman** (9 April 1900 – 6 May 1968) was a Dutch mathematician, known for his work in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) (in particular, for introducing [Kloosterman sums](wikipedia://en/2993504)) and in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).[^o4]

:::

After completing his master's degree at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) from 1918–1922 he studied at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) with [Harald Bohr](wikipedia://en/638305) and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) with [G. H. Hardy](wikipedia://en/87599). In 1924, he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in Leiden under the supervision of [J. C. Kluyver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._C._Kluyver). From 1926 to 1928 he studied at the Universities of [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg), and he was an assistant at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) from 1928-1930. Kloosterman was appointed *lector* (associate professorship) at Leiden University in 1930 and full professor in 1947. In 1950, he was elected a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o4][^o5]
