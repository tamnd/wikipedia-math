# Jenő Hunyady

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jenő Hunyady"}

Hunyady Jenő

**Jenő Hunyady** (28 April 1838 in [Pest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pest,_Hungary) – 26 December 1889 in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest)) was a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) noted for his work on [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673) and [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), specifically on [determinants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Determinants). He received his Ph.D. in [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) (1864). He worked at the [University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest_University_of_Technology_and_Economics) of Budapest. He was elected a corresponding member (1867), member (1883) of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences). From 1885 he actively participated in the informal meetings of what became later the [Mathematical and Physical Society](wikipedia://en/22777615) of Hungary.
