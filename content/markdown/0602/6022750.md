# Pieter Hendrik Schoute

*Image: Pieter Hendrik Schoute, 1911.jpg.* Pieter Hendrik Schoute

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pieter Hendrik Schoute"}

**Pieter Hendrik Schoute** (21 January 1846, [Wormerveer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wormerveer) – 18 April 1913, [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen_%28city%29)) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work on [regular polytopes](wikipedia://en/518693) and [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417).

:::

He started his career as a civil engineer, but became a professor of mathematics at Groningen and published some thirty papers on [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) between 1878 and his death in 1913.[^n1] He collaborated with [Alicia Boole Stott](wikipedia://en/3325459) on describing the sections of the regular 4-polytopes.[^n2]

In 1886, he became member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o4]
