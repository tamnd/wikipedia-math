# Zoárd Geőcze

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zoárd Geőcze"}

*Image: Geöcze Zoárd.jpeg.* Zoárd Geőcze

**Zoárd Geőcze de Szendrő** (1873–1916) was a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people) mathematician famous for his *theory of surfaces* (Horváth 2005:219ff). He was born on 23 August 1873 in [Budapest, Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest,_Hungary) and died on 26 November 1916 in Budapest.

:::
