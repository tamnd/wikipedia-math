# August Adler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="August Adler"}

**August Adler** (24 January 1863, [Opava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opava), [Austrian Silesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Silesia) – 17 October 1923, [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866)) was a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechs) and [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) noted for using the theory of [inversion](wikipedia://en/295844) to provide an alternate proof of [Mascheroni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mascheroni)'s [compass and straightedge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass_and_straightedge) construction theorem.

:::
