# Bayesian Analysis (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bayesian Analysis"}

***Bayesian Analysis*** is an [open-access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-access) [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering theoretical and applied aspects of [Bayesian methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bayesian_methods).[^o3] It is published by the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) and is hosted at the [Project Euclid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Euclid) web site.[^o4]

:::

*Bayesian Analysis* is abstracted and indexed by [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2011 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.650.[^o5]
