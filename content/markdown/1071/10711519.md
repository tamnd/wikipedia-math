# Torkel Franzén

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Torkel Franzén"}

**Torkel Franzén** (1 April 1950, [Norrbotten County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norrbotten_County) – 19 April 2006, [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm)) was a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) academic.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Franzén worked at the Department of Computer Science and Electrical Engineering at [Luleå University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luleå_University_of_Technology), Sweden, in the fields of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science). He was known for his work on [Gödel's incompleteness theorems](wikipedia://en/58863) and for his contributions to [Usenet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usenet).[^o4] He was active in the online [science fiction fan community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_fandom), and even issued his own electronic [fanzine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_fiction_fanzine) *Frotz* on his fiftieth birthday.[^o5] He died of [bone cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bone_cancer) at age 56.[^o6]
