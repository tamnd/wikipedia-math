# Joseph Kampé de Fériet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Kampé de Fériet"}

*Image: Kampé de Fériet.jpg.* Joseph Kampé de Fériet

**Marie-Joseph Kampé de Fériet** (14 May 1893 – 6 April 1982) was a French mathematician at [Université Lille Nord de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Lille_Nord_de_France) from 1919 to 1969. Besides his works on mathematics and fluid mechanics, he directed the *Institut de mécanique des fluides de Lille* ([ONERA Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_National_d'Etudes_et_de_Recherches_Aerospatiales)) and taught fluid dynamics and [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773) at [École centrale de Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_centrale_de_Lille) from 1930 to 1969.

:::

He devised the [Kampé de Fériet functions](wikipedia://en/32787803), which further generalize the [generalized hypergeometric functions](wikipedia://en/361184).

He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1928 at Bologna,[^o2] in 1932 at Zurich, and in 1954 at Amsterdam.
