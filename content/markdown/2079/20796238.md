# Diamond graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Diamond graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **diamond graph** is a [planar](wikipedia://en/24314), [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 4 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 5 edges.[^o4] It consists of a [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) $K_4$ minus one edge.

:::

The diamond graph has radius 1, [diameter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_diameter) 2, [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 3, [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3 and [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3. It is also a 2-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 2-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph), [graceful](wikipedia://en/8978968),[^o5] [Hamiltonian graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph).

## Diamond-free graphs and forbidden minor {#Diamond-free_graphs_and_forbidden_minor}

A graph is diamond-free if it has no diamond as an [induced subgraph](wikipedia://en/1655188). The [triangle-free graphs](wikipedia://en/7667996) are diamond-free graphs, since every diamond contains a triangle. The diamond-free graphs are locally clustered: that is, they are the graphs in which every [neighborhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighborhood_%28graph_theory%29) is a [cluster graph](wikipedia://en/50931136). Alternatively, a graph is diamond-free if and only if every pair of maximal cliques in the graph shares at most one vertex.

The family of graphs in which each [connected component](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connected_component_%28graph_theory%29) is a [cactus graph](wikipedia://en/9944209) is [downwardly closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Downwardly_closed) under [graph minor](wikipedia://en/353042) operations. This graph family may be characterized by a single [forbidden minor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_minor). This minor is the diamond graph.[^o7]

If both the [butterfly graph](wikipedia://en/4372111) and the diamond graph are forbidden minors, the family of graphs obtained is the family of [pseudoforests](wikipedia://en/13511542).

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The full automorphism group of the diamond graph is a group of order 4 isomorphic to the [Klein four-group](wikipedia://en/41928), the [direct product](wikipedia://en/3706015) of the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) $\mathbb{Z} / 2\mathbb{Z}$ with itself.

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the diamond graph is $x(x+1)(x^2-x-4)$. It is the only graph with this characteristic polynomial, making it a graph determined by its spectrum.

:::
