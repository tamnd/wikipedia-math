# Manin conjecture

*Image: Rational points of bounded height outside the 27 lines on Clebsch's diagonal cubic surface.png.* Rational points of bounded height outside the 27 lines on [Clebsch's](wikipedia://en/23390462) diagonal [cubic surface](wikipedia://en/526126).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Manin conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Manin conjecture** describes the conjectural distribution of rational points on an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) relative to a suitable [height function](wikipedia://en/3141855). It was proposed by [Yuri I. Manin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_I._Manin) and his collaborators[^o2] in 1989 when they initiated a program with the aim of describing the distribution of rational points on suitable algebraic varieties.

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=heading name="Conjecture"}

## Conjecture {#Conjecture}

Their main conjecture is as follows. Let $V$ be a [Fano variety](wikipedia://en/3095929) defined over a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) $K$, let $H$ be a height function relative to the [anticanonical divisor](wikipedia://en/734893) and assume that $V(K)$ is [Zariski dense](wikipedia://en/241744) in $V$. Then there exists a non-empty [Zariski open subset](wikipedia://en/241744) $U \subset V$ such that the counting function of $K$-rational points of bounded height, defined by

$$N_{U,H}(B)=\#\{x \in U(K):H(x)\leq B\}$$

for $B \geq 1$, satisfies

$$N_{U,H}(B) \sim c B (\log B)^{\rho-1},$$

as $B \to \infty.$ Here $\rho$ is the rank of the [Picard group](wikipedia://en/1850134) of $V$ and $c$ is a positive constant which later received a conjectural interpretation by Peyre.[^o5]

Manin's conjecture has been proved for special families of varieties,[^o6] but is still open in general.
