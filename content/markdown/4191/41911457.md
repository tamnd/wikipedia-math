# Gilbert Hunt

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Gilbert Hunt"}

**Gilbert Agnew Hunt, Jr.** (March 4, 1916 – May 30, 2008)[^o2] was an American mathematician and amateur [tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennis) player active in the 1930s and 1940s.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hunt was born in Washington, D.C. and attended [Eastern High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_High_School_%28Washington,_D.C.%29).[^o6]

## Tennis career {#Tennis_career}

Hunt reached the quarterfinals of the [U.S. National Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Open_%28tennis%29) in [1938](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1938_U.S._National_Championships_–_Men's_singles) and [1939](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1939_U.S._National_Championships_–_Men's_singles).

## Scientific career {#Scientific_career}

Hunt received his bachelor's degree from [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University) in 1938 and his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1948 under [Salomon Bochner](wikipedia://en/987847). Hunt became a mathematics professor at Princeton University specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542),[^o3] [Markov processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_process), and [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342).[^o2]

The [Hunt process](wikipedia://en/4888399) is named after him. He was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1962 in Stockholm. His doctoral students include [Robert McCallum Blumenthal](wikipedia://en/56586935) and [Richard M. Dudley](wikipedia://en/3824977).

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Hunt's theorem"}

### Hunt's theorem {#Hunt's_theorem}

Hunt's theorem states that for a large class of positive [kernels](wikipedia://en/21180014) $V$ satisfying "the complete maximum principle" of [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), there corresponds a [contraction](wikipedia://en/10907456) [resolvent](wikipedia://en/8721698) and associated sub-Markovian [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) $P_t$ with

$V f= \int_0^\infty P_t f dt~.$ ($V$ is called the "potential kernel" of the semigroup.)[^o11]

:::
