# Christiane Tretter

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Christiane Tretter"}

**Christiane Tretter** is a German mathematician and mathematical physicist who works as a professor in the Mathematical Institute (MAI) of the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern) in Switzerland, and as co-director of the institute. Her research area in applied analysis includes, in particular, [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) and [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tretter studied mathematics, with a minor in physics, at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg), earning a [diploma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) in 1989, a Ph.D. in 1992, and a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1998. Her doctoral dissertation, *Asymptotische Randbedingungen für Entwicklungssätze bei Randeigenwertproblemen zu $N(y) = \lambda P(y)$ mit $\lambda$-abhängigen Randbedingungen*, was supervised by Reinhard Mennicken.

She became a lecturer at the [University of Leicester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leicester) in 2000, moved to the [University of Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bremen) as a professor in 2002, and took her present position as full professor at the [University of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern) in 2006.

Since 2008 she has been editor-in-chief of the journal *[Integral Equations and Operator Theory](wikipedia://en/1044194)*.. Having served as vice-president of the [Swiss Mathematical Society, SMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Mathematical_Society,_SMS), in 2024/25, she was elected president of SMS in 2026.

## Books {#Books}

Tretter is the author of two mathematical monographs, *Spectral Theory of Block Operator Matrices and Applications* (2008) and *On Lambda-Nonlinear-Boundary-Eigenvalue-Problems* (1993), and of two textbooks in analysis.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Tretter was awarded the [Richard von Mises Prize](wikipedia://en/56348140) of the [Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik, GAMM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik,_GAMM), in 1995. She became honorary member of the [Swedish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/77611922) in 2025.
