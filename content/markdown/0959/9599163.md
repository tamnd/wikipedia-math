# Adrien Pouliot Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adrien Pouliot Award"}

The **Adrien Pouliot Award** is presented annually by the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055). The award is presented to individuals or teams in recognition of significant contributions to [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada). The inaugural award was presented in 1995. Persons and teams that are nominated for the award will have their applications considered for a period of three years.[^o2] The award is named in honor of Canadian mathematician [Adrien Pouliot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adrien_Pouliot). It should be distinguished with a different but similarly-named award, the Adrien Pouliot Prize of the Mathematical Association of Québec.

:::
