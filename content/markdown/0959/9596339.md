# Abdusalam Abubakar

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Abdusalam Abubakar"}

**Abdusalam Abubakar** (born 1989/1990)[^o4] is a [Somali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalia)-born Irish scientist from [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin). He was the winner of the 43rd [Young Scientist and Technology Exhibition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Young_Scientist_and_Technology_Exhibition) in 2007 at the age of seventeen. He went on to be named [EU Young Scientist of the Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union_Contest_for_Young_Scientists) in September 2007.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Abubakar was born in [Somalia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somalia) to an Irish father[^o6] of Somali descent. He is an only child.[^o6] He moved to Ireland in May 2005,[^o6] joining [Synge Street CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synge_Street_CBS) in central [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin).[^o4] He first entered the Young Scientist and Technology Exhibition alongside two fellow students who invited him along and taught him to research and solve properly.[^o6] They won an award for [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the event.[^o6] He was mentored by [Jim Cooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Cooke).

Abubakar then re-entered the Young Scientist and Technology Exhibition for the 2007 event as a third-year student at Synge Street CBS.[^o4] His project at the exhibition was titled "An Extension of Wiener's Attack on [RSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_%28algorithm%29)".[^o5] His project was based on the topic of [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432).[^o4]

Abubakar won the Young Scientist and Technology Exhibition at the [RDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Dublin_Society), Dublin, on 12 January 2007.[^o4] He defeated runner-up [Beara Community School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beara_Community_School) in County Cork's Ciara Murphy and her study on hearing loss in teenagers.[^o5] He admitted afterwards that he had never used a computer before coming to Ireland twenty months earlier.[^o6]

An interview with Abubakar in *[Xclusive Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xclusive_Magazine)* called him "the hottest name in Ireland right now" and said the achievement was "obviously a landmark in science" after his win.[^o6] He appeared on the front cover of that edition of the magazine, under the headline "GENIUS! How Abdusalam Abubakar, a sixteen-year-old Somali, broke a 13-year-old Irish record".[^o6] Abubakar appeared on *[Dustin's Daily News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dustin's_Daily_News)* on 19 January 2007.[^o7]

He went on to represent Ireland at the 19th [European Union Contest for Young Scientists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union_Contest_for_Young_Scientists) in [Valencia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valencia,_Spain), Spain, in September 2007, claiming first prize in the field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) for Ireland.[^o8]

Abubakar studied financial mathematics at [Dublin City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_City_University).[^o9]
