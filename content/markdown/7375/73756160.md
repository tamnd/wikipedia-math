# Makoto Matsumoto (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Makoto Matsumoto"}

**Makoto Matsumoto** (松本眞, born February 18, 1965) is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) principally known as the inventor of the [Mersenne Twister](wikipedia://en/45538),[^o2][^o3] a widely used [pseudorandom number generator](wikipedia://en/45524). He is also the author of the [CryptMT](wikipedia://en/6338441) [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

In Jan 1998, while an associate professor at Keio University,[^o2] he invented the [Mersenne Twister](wikipedia://en/45538) along with [Takuji Nishimura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takuji_Nishimura). Two years later, he completed his Ph.D.[^o6] on random number generators.

Until his retirement in Aug 2023, he was a professor at the Department of Mathematics, Graduate School of Science, [Hiroshima University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hiroshima_University).[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

- Kirkman Medal - 1997[^o9]
- IBM Japan Science Prize - 1999[^o10]
- Japan Society for the Promotion of Science - 2008[^o11]
