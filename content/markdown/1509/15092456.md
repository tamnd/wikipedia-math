# ASReml

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ASReml"}

**ASReml** is a statistical software package for fitting linear [mixed models](wikipedia://en/5431921) using [restricted maximum likelihood](wikipedia://en/15211981), a technique commonly used in [plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_breeding) and [animal breeding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Animal_breeding) and [quantitative genetics](wikipedia://en/297382) as well as other fields. It is notable for its ability to fit very large and complex [data sets](wikipedia://en/8495) efficiently, due to its use of the average information algorithm[^o2] and [sparse matrix](wikipedia://en/341015) methods.

:::

It was originally developed by Arthur Gilmour.[^o3]

ASREML can be used in Windows, Linux, and as an add-on to [S-PLUS](wikipedia://en/3830007) and [R](wikipedia://en/376707).
