# Gradient method

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gradient method"}

In [optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29), a **gradient method** is an [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) to solve problems of the form

:::

$$\min_{x\in\mathbb R^n}\; f(x)$$

with the search directions defined by the [gradient](wikipedia://en/12461) of the function at the current point. Examples of gradient methods are the [gradient descent](wikipedia://en/201489) and the [conjugate gradient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_gradient).
