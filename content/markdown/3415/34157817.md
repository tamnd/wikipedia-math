# Tommaso Boggio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tommaso Boggio"}

**Tommaso Boggio** (22 December 1877 – 25 May 1963) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Boggio worked in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), and [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics). He was an invited speaker in [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) 1908 in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome).[^o3] He wrote, with [Burali-Forti](wikipedia://en/2452025), *Meccanica Razionale*, published in 1921 by S. Lattes & Compagnia.[^o4]

:::
