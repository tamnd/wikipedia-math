# Truncated order-5 hexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-5 hexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-5 hexagonal tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,1</sub>{6,5}.

:::
