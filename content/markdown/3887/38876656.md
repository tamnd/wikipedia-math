# Truncated order-6 pentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated order-6 pentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated order-6 pentagonal tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>1,2</sub>{6,5}.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual of this tiling represents the fundamental domains of the \*553 symmetry. There are no mirror removal subgroups of \[(5,5,3)\], but this symmetry group can be doubled to [652 symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/652_symmetry) by adding a bisecting mirror to the fundamental domains.
