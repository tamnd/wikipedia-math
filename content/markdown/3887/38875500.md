# Truncated pentahexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated pentahexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated tetrahexagonal tiling** is a semiregular tiling of the hyperbolic plane. There are one [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29), one [decagon](wikipedia://en/327393), and one [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486) on each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of *t<sub>0,1,2</sub>{6,5}*. Its name is somewhat misleading: literal geometric truncation of [pentahexagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/38876652) produces rectangles instead of squares.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

There are four small index subgroup from \[6,5\] by mirror removal and alternation. In these images fundamental domains are alternately colored black and white, and mirrors exist on the boundaries between colors.

## Related polyhedra and tilings {#Related_polyhedra_and_tilings}

From a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) there are fourteen hyperbolic [uniform tilings](wikipedia://en/18640974) that can be based from the regular order-5 hexagonal tiling.

Drawing the tiles colored as red on the original faces, yellow at the original vertices, and blue along the original edges, there are seven forms with full \[6,5\] symmetry, and three with subsymmetry.
