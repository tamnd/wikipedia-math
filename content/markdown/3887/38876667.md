# Snub pentahexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub pentahexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub pentahexagonal tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of sr{6,5}.

:::
