# Order-6 pentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-6 pentagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-6 pentagonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {5,6}.

:::

## Uniform coloring {#Uniform_coloring}

This regular tiling can also be constructed from \[(5,5,3)\] symmetry alternating two colors of pentagons, represented by t<sub>1</sub>(5,5,3).

*Image: H2 tiling 355-2.png.*

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

This tiling represents a hyperbolic [kaleidoscope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaleidoscope) of 6 mirrors defining a regular hexagon fundamental domain, and 5 mirrors meeting at a point. This symmetry by [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678) is called \*33333 with 5 order-3 mirror intersections.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This tiling is topologically related as a part of sequence of regular tilings with order-6 vertices with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {n,6}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) , progressing to infinity.
