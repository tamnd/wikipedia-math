# Truncated tetrahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated tetrahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated tetrahedron** is an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847). It has 4 regular [hexagonal](wikipedia://en/59733) faces, 4 [equilateral triangle](wikipedia://en/173285) faces, 12 vertices and 18 edges (of two types). It can be constructed by [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) all 4 vertices of a regular [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The truncated tetrahedron can be constructed from a [regular tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/5242521) by cutting all of its vertices off, a process known as [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429). The resulting polyhedron has 4 equilateral triangles and 4 regular hexagons, 18 edges, and 12 vertices. With edge length 1, the [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) of the 12 vertices are the permutations of

$$\bigl( {\pm\tfrac{3\sqrt{2}}{4} }, \pm\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4}, \pm\tfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4} \bigr)$$

that have an even number of minus signs.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Given the edge length $a$. The surface area of a truncated tetrahedron $A$ is the sum of 4 regular hexagons and 4 equilateral triangles' area, and its [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) $V$ is:

$$\begin{aligned} A &= 7\sqrt{3}a^2 &&\approx 12.124a^2, \\ V &= \tfrac{23}{12}\sqrt{2}a^3 &&\approx 2.711a^3. \end{aligned}$$

The dihedral angle of a truncated tetrahedron between triangle-to-hexagon is approximately 109.47°, and that between adjacent hexagonal faces is approximately 70.53°.

The densest packing of the truncated tetrahedron is believed to be $\Phi = \frac{207}{208}$, as reported by two independent groups using [Monte Carlo methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monte_Carlo_methods) by (Damasceno Engel Glotzer 2012) and (Jiao Torquato 2011). Although no mathematical proof exists that this is the best possible packing for the truncated tetrahedron, the high proximity to the unity and independence of the findings make it unlikely that an even denser packing is to be found. If the truncation of the corners is slightly smaller than that of a truncated tetrahedron, this new shape can be used to fill space completely.

*Image: Truncated tetrahedron.stl.* 3D model of a truncated tetrahedron

The truncated tetrahedron is an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847), meaning it is a vertex-transitive and semi-regular polyhedron, and two or more different [regular polygonal](wikipedia://en/333306) faces meet in a vertex. The truncated tetrahedron has the same [three-dimensional group symmetry](wikipedia://en/2694525) as the regular tetrahedron, the [tetrahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2904472) $\mathrm{T}_\mathrm{h}$. The polygonal faces that meet for every vertex are one equilateral triangle and two regular hexagons, and the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is denoted as $3 \cdot 6^2$. Its [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) is [triakis tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/722548), a [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393), shares the same symmetry as the truncated tetrahedron.

:::

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

*Image: Triakis truncated tetrahedron.png.* [Triakis](wikipedia://en/37100405) variant with triangles replaced by pyramids

The truncated tetrahedron can be found in the construction of polyhedra. For example, the [augmented truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/1200120) is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034) constructed from a truncated tetrahedron by attaching a [triangular cupola](wikipedia://en/1128465) onto one hexagonal face. The [triakis truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/37100405) is a polyhedron constructed from a truncated tetrahedron by adding three tetrahedra onto its triangular faces, as implied by the name "[triakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conway_kis_operator)". It is classified as [plesiohedron](wikipedia://en/53396858), meaning it can [tessellate](wikipedia://en/321671) in three-dimensional space known as [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654); an example is [triakis truncated tetrahedral honeycomb](wikipedia://en/37070120).

The **Friauf polyhedron** is named after [J. B. Friauf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._B._Friauf), who described it as an [intermetallic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermetallic) structure formed by a compound of metallic elements. It can be found in crystals such as complex metallic alloys, an example is dizinc magnesium MgZn<sub>2</sub>. It is a lower symmetry version of the truncated tetrahedron, interpreted as a truncated [tetragonal disphenoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetragonal_disphenoid) with its three-dimensional symmetry group as the [dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) $D_{2\mathrm{d}}$ of order 8.

Truncating a truncated tetrahedron gives a polyhedron with 54 edges, 32 vertices, and 20 faces—4 hexagons, 4 [nonagons](wikipedia://en/414824), and 12 [trapeziums](wikipedia://en/201826). This polyhedron was used by [Adidas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adidas) as the underlying geometry of the [Jabulani ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adidas_Jabulani) designed for the [2010 World Cup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FIFA_World_Cup_2010).

## Truncated tetrahedral graph {#Truncated_tetrahedral_graph}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a **truncated tetrahedral graph** is an [Archimedean graph](wikipedia://en/44969442), the [graph of vertices and edges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1-skeleton) of the truncated tetrahedron, one of the [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847). It has 12 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 18 edges.[^o12] It is a connected cubic graph,[^o13] and connected cubic transitive graph.[^o14]

As a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777), it is vertex transitive, and has 2 edge orbits of 6 (between triangles) and 12 (on triangle cycles). As a [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508), it can be represented by [LCF notation](wikipedia://en/24186720) as \[2, 6, -2\]<sup>4</sup> with 2 vertex orbits, and 4 edge orbits in a regular 12-gon.
