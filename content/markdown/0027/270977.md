# Hermite spline

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermite spline"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) subfield of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), a **Hermite spline** is a [spline curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spline_curve) where each [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) of the spline is in [Hermite form](wikipedia://en/195984).[^o2]

:::
