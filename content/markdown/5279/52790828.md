# William Jack (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Jack"}

*Image: William Jack c.1880.png.* William Jack c. 1880

**William Jack** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (29 May 1834 – 20 March 1924) was a Scottish mathematician and journalist. He was Editor of the [Glasgow Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_Herald) newspaper from 1870 to 1876, and Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow) from 1879 until 1909.[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on 29 May 1834 in [Stewarton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stewarton) in [Ayrshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ayrshire) the son of Robert Jack of Irvine. He was educated at [Irvine Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvine_Academy), going on to study mathematics at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow), graduating with an MA in 1853. He then continued his studies at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), graduating with a second MA in 1859.

From 1860 to 1866 he was HM Inspector of Schools for Scotland. In 1866 he accepted the post of Professor of Natural Philosophy (Physics) at [Owens College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owens_College) in [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester) and held this position until 1870 when he moved to Glasgow as Editor of the [Glasgow Herald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Herald_%28Glasgow%29) newspaper. He left in 1876 to run [Macmillan & Co](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macmillan_Publishers), a London publisher,[^o4] and in 1879 joined the staff of Glasgow University as Professor of Mathematics. In 1875, he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were, [William Thomson, Lord Kelvin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Thomson,_Lord_Kelvin), [James Thomson Bottomley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Thomson_Bottomley), [Allen Thomson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Thomson) and [Peter Guthrie Tait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Guthrie_Tait).[^o5]

In 1875 the University of Glasgow awarded him an honorary LLD and in 1902 the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) awarded him an honorary doctorate (DSc).

In his final working years he lived on the campus of the University of Glasgow, at 10 The College.[^o6]

He died on 20 March 1924.[^o7]

## Family {#Family}

He was married to Agnes Jane Nichol (1837–1901), daughter of [John Pringle Nichol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Pringle_Nichol) and sister of [Professor John Nichol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Nichol_%28biographer%29). Their children included sons William Tullis Jack (b.1862), William Robert Jack (b.1866), Adolphus Alfred Jack (b.1868), and a daughter Agnes Elizabeth Jack (b.1871).[^o9]
