# Comodule over a Hopf algebroid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Comodule over a Hopf algebroid"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), at the intersection of [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), there is the notion of a [Hopf algebroid](wikipedia://en/31530123) which encodes the information of a [presheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) of [groupoids](wikipedia://en/12543) whose object sheaf and arrow sheaf are represented by algebras. Because any such presheaf will have an associated site, we can consider [quasi-coherent sheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-coherent_sheaves) on the site, giving a [topos](wikipedia://en/21391464)-theoretic notion of [modules](wikipedia://en/276410). Dually[^o2]<sup>pg 2</sup>, comodules over a Hopf algebroid are the purely algebraic analogue of this construction, giving a purely algebraic description of quasi-coherent sheaves on a stack: this is one of the first motivations behind the theory.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Given a commutative Hopf-algebroid $(A,\Gamma)$ a **left** **comodule** $M$[^o5]<sup>pg 302</sup> is a left $A$-module $M$ together with an $A$-linear map

> $$\psi: M \to \Gamma\otimes_AM$$

which satisfies the following two properties

1. (**counitary**) $(\varepsilon\otimes Id_M)\circ \psi = Id_M$
1. (**coassociative**) $(\Delta\otimes Id_M) \circ \psi = (Id_\Gamma \otimes \psi) \circ \psi$

A right comodule is defined similarly, but instead there is a map

> $$\phi: M \to M \otimes_A \Gamma$$

satisfying analogous axioms.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Structure theorems"}

## Structure theorems {#Structure_theorems}

### Flatness of Γ gives an abelian category {#Flatness_of_Γ_gives_an_abelian_category}

One of the main structure theorems for comodules[^o5]<sup>pg 303</sup> is if $\Gamma$ is a flat $A$-module, then the category of comodules $\text{Comod}(A,\Gamma)$ of the Hopf-algebroid is an [abelian category](wikipedia://en/45063).

### Relation to stacks {#Relation_to_stacks}

There is a structure theorem[^o2] <sup>pg 7</sup> relating comodules of Hopf-algebroids and modules of presheaves of groupoids. If $(A,\Gamma)$ is a Hopf-algebroid, there is an equivalence between the category of comodules $\text{Comod}(A,\Gamma)$ and the category of quasi-coherent sheaves $\text{QCoh}(\text{Spec}(A),\text{Spec}(\Gamma))$ for the associated presheaf of groupoids

> $$\text{Spec}(\Gamma)\rightrightarrows \text{Spec}(A)$$

to this Hopf-algebroid.

:::

## Examples {#Examples}

### From BP-homology {#From_BP-homology}

Associated to the [Brown-Peterson](wikipedia://en/13698492) [spectrum](wikipedia://en/1033697) is the Hopf-algebroid $(BP_*,BP_*(BP))$ classifying p-typical [formal group laws](wikipedia://en/480335). Note

> $$\begin{aligned} BP_* &= \mathbb{Z}_{(p)}[v_1,v_2,\ldots] \\ BP_*(BP) &= BP_*[t_1,t_2,\ldots] \end{aligned}$$

where $\mathbb{Z}_{(p)}$ is the [localization](wikipedia://en/252216) of $\mathbb{Z}$ by the prime ideal $(p)$. If we let $I_n$ denote the ideal

> $$I_n = (p,v_1,\ldots, v_{n-1})$$

Since $v_n$ is a primitive in $BP_*/I_n$, there is an associated Hopf-algebroid $(A,\Gamma)$

> $$(v_n^{-1}BP_*/I_n, v_n^{-1}BP_*(BP)/I_n)$$

There is a structure theorem on the [Adams-Novikov spectral sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adams-Novikov_spectral_sequence) relating the Ext-groups of comodules on $(BP_*,BP_*(BP))$ to Johnson-Wilson homology, giving a more tractable spectral sequence. This happens through an equivalence of categories of comodules of $(A,\Gamma)$ to the category of comodules of

> $$(v_n^{-1}E(m)_*/I_n, v_n^{-1}E(m)_*(E(m)/I_n)$$

giving the isomorphism

> $$\text{Ext}^{*,*}_{BP_*BP}(M,N) \cong \text{Ext}^{*,*}_{E(m)_*E(m)}(E(m)_*\otimes_{BP_*} M,E(m)_*\otimes_{BP_*}N)$$

assuming $M$ and $N$ satisfy some technical hypotheses[^o2] <sup>pg 24</sup>.
