# Random algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Random algebra"}

In [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), the **random algebra** or **random real algebra** is the [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/54476844) of [Borel sets](wikipedia://en/43327) of the unit interval modulo the [ideal](wikipedia://en/12633368) of measure zero sets. It is used in **random forcing** to add **random reals** to a [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28model_theory%29) of set theory. The random algebra was studied by [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942) in 1935 (in work later published as (Neumann 1998, p. 253)) who showed that it is not isomorphic to the [Cantor algebra](wikipedia://en/43215632) of Borel sets modulo [meager sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meager_set). Random forcing was introduced by (Solovay 1970).

:::
