# Jónsson–Tarski algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jónsson–Tarski algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Jónsson–Tarski algebra** or **Cantor algebra** is an [algebraic structure](wikipedia://en/106364) encoding a [bijection](wikipedia://en/3942) from an [infinite set](wikipedia://en/142679) $X$ onto the [product](wikipedia://en/24104095) $X\times X$. They were introduced by (txt, Theorem 5). (txt), named them after [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216) because of Cantor's [pairing function](wikipedia://en/1145848) and Cantor's theorem that an infinite set $X$ has the same number of elements as $X\times X$. The term *Cantor algebra* is also occasionally used to mean the [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959) of all [clopen subsets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clopen_subset) of the [Cantor set](wikipedia://en/6172), or the Boolean algebra of [Borel subsets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_subset) of the [reals](wikipedia://en/20646438) modulo [meager sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meager_set) (sometimes called the [Cohen algebra](wikipedia://en/37966737)).

:::

The group of [order-preserving automorphisms](wikipedia://en/23916899) of the [free](wikipedia://en/333835) Jónsson–Tarski algebra on one [generator](wikipedia://en/3171371) is the [Thompson group](wikipedia://en/612245) $F$.

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A Jónsson–Tarski algebra of type 2 is a set $A$ with a product $w$ from $A\times A$ to $A$ and two 'projection' maps $p_{1}$ and $p_{2}$ from $A$ to $A$, satisfying $p_{1}(w(a_{1},a_{2})) = a_{1}$, $p_{2}(w(a_{1},a_{2})) = a_{2}$, and $w(p_{1}(a),p_{2}(a)) = a$. The definition for type > 2 is similar but with $n$ projection operators.

:::

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

If $w$ is any bijection from $A\times A$ to $A$ then it can be extended to a unique Jónsson–Tarski algebra by letting $p_{i}(a)$ be the projection of $w^{-1}(a)$ onto the $i$th factor.

:::
