# Fulkerson–Chen–Anstee theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fulkerson–Chen–Anstee theorem"}

The **Fulkerson–Chen–Anstee theorem** is a result in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a branch of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). It provides one of two known approaches solving the [digraph realization problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digraph_realization_problem), i.e. it gives a necessary and sufficient condition for pairs of nonnegative [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) $((a_1,b_1),\ldots,(a_n,b_n))$ to be the [indegree](wikipedia://en/19721986)-[outdegree](wikipedia://en/19721986) pairs of a [simple directed graph](wikipedia://en/19721986); a sequence obeying these conditions is called "digraphic". [D. R. Fulkerson](wikipedia://en/239238)[^o2] (1960) obtained a characterization analogous to the classical [Erdős–Gallai theorem](wikipedia://en/30208106) for graphs, but in contrast to this solution with exponentially many inequalities. In 1966 Chen [^o3] improved this result in demanding the additional constraint that the integer pairs must be sorted in non-increasing [lexicographical order](wikipedia://en/567667) leading to n inequalities. Anstee [^o4] (1982) observed in a different context that it is sufficient to have $a_1\geq\cdots\geq a_n$. Berger [^o5] reinvented this result and gives a direct proof.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

A sequence $((a_1,b_1 ),\ldots,(a_n,b_n))$ of nonnegative integer pairs with $a_1\geq\cdots\geq a_n$ is digraphic if and only if $\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_i=\sum_{i=1}^{n}b_i$ and the following inequality holds for *k* such that $1 \leq k \leq n$:

$$\sum^k_{i=1} a_i\leq \sum^k_{i=1} \min(b_i,k-1)+ \sum^n_{i=k+1} \min(b_i,k)$$

:::

## Stronger versions {#Stronger_versions}

Berger proved[^o5] that it suffices to consider the $k$th inequality such that $1 \leq k < n$ with $a_k > a_{k+1}$ and for $k = n$.

## Other notations {#Other_notations}

The theorem can also be stated in terms of zero-one [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859). The connection can be seen if one realizes that each [directed graph](wikipedia://en/19721986) has an [adjacency matrix](wikipedia://en/244463) where the column sums and row sums correspond to $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$ and $(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$. Note that the diagonal of the matrix only contains zeros. There is a connection to the relation [majorization](wikipedia://en/2864495). We define a sequence $(a^*_1,\ldots,a^*_n)$ with $a^*_k:=|\{b_i|i>k, b_i \geq k\}| + |\{b_i|i \leq k, b_i \geq k-1\}|$. Sequence $(a^*_1,\ldots,a^*_n)$ can also be determined by a [corrected Ferrers diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corrected_Ferrers_diagram). Consider sequences $(a_1,\ldots,a_n)$, $(b_1,\ldots,b_n)$ and $(a^*_1,\ldots,a^*_n)$ as $n$-dimensional vectors $a$, $b$ and $a^*$. Since $\sum_{i=1}^k a^*_i = \sum^k_{i=1} \min(b_i,k-1)+ \sum^n_{i=k+1} \min(b_i,k)$ by applying the principle of [double counting](wikipedia://en/296942), the theorem above states that a pair of nonnegative integer sequences $(a,b)$ with nonincreasing $a$ is digraphic [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) vector $a^*$ majorizes $a$.

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalization"}

## Generalization {#Generalization}

A sequence $((a_1,b_1 ),\ldots,(a_n,b_n))$ of nonnegative integer pairs with $a_1\geq\cdots\geq a_n$ is digraphic if and only if $\sum_{i=1}^{n}a_i=\sum_{i=1}^{n}b_i$ and there exists a sequence $c$ such that the pair $(c,b)$ is digraphic and $c$ majorizes $a$.[^o12]

:::

## Characterizations for similar problems {#Characterizations_for_similar_problems}

Similar theorems describe the degree sequences of simple graphs, simple directed graphs with loops, and simple bipartite graphs. The first problem is characterized by the [Erdős–Gallai theorem](wikipedia://en/30208106). The latter two cases, which are equivalent see Berger,[^o5] are characterized by the [Gale–Ryser theorem](wikipedia://en/42442683).
