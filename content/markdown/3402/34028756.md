# List of continents by GDP

*Image: Countries by GDP (nominal).svg.* }

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of continents by GDP"}

This article includes a **list of [continents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continent) of the world sorted by their [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP)**, the market value of all final goods and services from a continent in a given year. The GDP dollar estimates presented here are calculated at market or government official [exchange rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exchange_rate). The figures presented here do not take into account differences in the [cost of living](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cost_of_living) in different countries, and the results can vary greatly from one year to another based on fluctuations in the [exchange rates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exchange_rate) of the country's [currency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Currency). Such fluctuations may change a continents ranking from one year to the next, even though they often make little or no difference to the [standard of living](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Standard_of_living) of its population. Therefore, these figures should be used with caution. Comparisons of national wealth are also frequently made on the basis of [purchasing power parity](wikipedia://en/64489) (PPP), to adjust for differences in the cost of living in different countries. (*See* [List of countries by GDP (PPP)](wikipedia://en/485118).) PPP largely removes the exchange rate problem, but has its own drawbacks; it does not reflect the value of economic output in [international trade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_trade), and it also requires more estimation than GDP per capita. On the whole, PPP per capita figures are more narrowly spread than nominal GDP per capita figures.

:::

The following lists summarise the GDP (nominal) of each continent by adding GDP (nominal) of each nation as per the seven-continent model, sorted by USD. The first list includes 2024 data estimates for members of the [International Monetary Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund). The second list shows the [World Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Bank)'s estimates, and the third list includes mostly 2024 estimates from the [CIA World Factbook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Factbook).

In these lists, the sovereign states and dependent territories are classified according to the geoscheme created by the [United Nations Statistics Division](wikipedia://en/2996969).[^o2] According to the UN, the assignment of countries or areas to specific groupings is for statistical convenience and does not imply any assumption regarding political or other affiliation of countries or territories.[^o3]

For more information, see [list of sovereign states and dependent territories by continent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_sovereign_states_and_dependent_territories_by_continent).

## Notes {#Notes}

\1. 1 Estimated by the [International Monetary Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund) (IMF) staff.

\2. 2 Figures exclude [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) and the [special administrative regions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_administrative_regions_of_China) of [Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong) and [Macao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macau).

\3. 3 The [Eurozone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurozone) in 2010 was 16 of 27 [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) countries: [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium), [Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyprus), [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece), [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy), [Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luxembourg), [Malta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malta), the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands), [Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portugal), [Slovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovakia), [Slovenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slovenia), and [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain).

\4. 4 Data include the French overseas departments of [French Guiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Guiana), [Guadeloupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadeloupe), [Martinique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martinique), and [Réunion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Réunion).

\5. 5 Excludes [Kosovo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kosovo).

\6. 6 Hyperinflation and the plunging value of the Zimbabwean dollar makes [Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe)'s nominal GDP a highly inaccurate statistic.

\7. 7 Excludes data for [Transnistria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnistria).

\8. 8 Includes former [Spanish Sahara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Sahara).

\9. 9 Data are for the area controlled by the [Government of the Republic of Cyprus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Cyprus).

\10. 10 Covers [Mainland Tanzania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainland_Tanzania) only.

\11. 11 Excludes [Abkhazia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abkhazia) and [South Ossetia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Ossetia).

\12. 12 Based on the best available GDP figure for each country at the time of creation (16 June 2024). Best available GDP figure was defined as the latest available figure from the [International Monetary Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Monetary_Fund) (including forecasts for the current year), otherwise the latest available figure from the [World Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Bank), otherwise the latest available figure from the [United Nations Statistics Division](wikipedia://en/2996969); however, countries that didn't have any figure for a year more recent than 2019 were not included at all. For almost all countries, the latest IMF forecast figure for 2024 was used.
