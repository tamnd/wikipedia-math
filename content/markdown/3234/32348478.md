# Curt Meyer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Curt Meyer"}

**Curt Meyer** (19 November 1919 – 18 April 2011) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He made notable contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

A native of [Bremerhaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bremerhaven), Meyer obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in 1950, under supervision of [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118). In 1966 he became professor of mathematics at the [University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne), a position he held until 1985.

Among his most important results is an alternative solution to the [class number 1 problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_number_1_problem), building on the original [Stark–Heegner theorem](wikipedia://en/391251).[^o3]
