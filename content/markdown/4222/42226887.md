# Michael O'Nan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael O'Nan"}

**Michael Ernest O'Nan** (August 9, 1943, [Fort Knox, Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Knox,_Kentucky) – July 31, 2017, [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey))[^o3] was an American mathematician, specializing in group theory.[^o4]

:::

O'Nan received his PhD in 1970 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under [Daniel Gorenstein](wikipedia://en/1569478) with thesis *A Characterization of the Three-Dimensional Projective Unitary Group over a Finite Field*. He was a professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). In 1976 he found strong evidence for the existence of a [sporadic group](wikipedia://en/324806),[^o6] which [Charles Sims](wikipedia://en/20022166) constructed. The group is commonly called the [O'Nan group](wikipedia://en/1196724) after O'Nan.[^o7]

The [O'Nan–Scott theorem](wikipedia://en/40086598) in group theory is also named after O'Nan, who discovered it independently from Leonard Scott. It describes the [maximal subgroups](wikipedia://en/1472649) of the [symmetric groups](wikipedia://en/28901).
