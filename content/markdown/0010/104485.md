# Laurent Lafforgue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Laurent Lafforgue"}

**Laurent Lafforgue** (; born 6 November 1966) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He has made outstanding contributions to [Langlands' program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langlands'_program) in the fields of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396),[^o3] and in particular proved the Langlands conjectures for the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/1160#Automorphism_group) of a function field. The crucial contribution by Lafforgue to solve this question is the construction of compactifications of certain [moduli stacks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_stack) of [shtukas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shtuka). The [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) was the result of more than six years of concentrated efforts.[^o4]

:::

In 2002 at the 24th [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing), [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China), he received the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859) together with [Vladimir Voevodsky](wikipedia://en/104482).[^o5]

## Biography {#Biography}

Laurent Lafforgue has two brothers, Thomas and [Vincent](wikipedia://en/27229287), both mathematicians. Thomas is now a teacher in a *[classe préparatoire aux grandes écoles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classe_préparatoire_aux_grandes_écoles)* at Lycée Louis le Grand in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) and [Vincent](wikipedia://en/27229287) a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) directeur de recherches at the Institut Fourier in Grenoble.

He won 2 silver medals at [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (IMO) in 1984 and 1985. He entered the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in 1986. In 1994 he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) under the direction of [Gérard Laumon](wikipedia://en/22565856) in the [Arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) and [Algebraic Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_Geometry) team at the [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University).

Currently he is a research director of [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). He was detached as permanent professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) (IHÉS) in [Bures-sur-Yvette](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bures-sur-Yvette), France, in 2000–2021. In 2021, he left his IHÉS position and moved to [Huawei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huawei).[^o7] His goal there is to apply [topos theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topos_theory) to the area of [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

He received the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) in 2000, and the Grand Prix Jacques Herbrand of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences#Medals,_awards_and_prizes) in 2001 and was awarded the Fields Medal in 2002. His younger brother [Vincent Lafforgue](wikipedia://en/27229287) is also a notable mathematician. On 22 May 2011 Lafforgue was awarded an honorary Doctor of Science from the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame).[^o10]

## Views {#Views}

Lafforgue is a critic of what he calls the "pedagogically correct" in France's educational system. In 2005, he was forced to resign from the [Haut conseil de l'éducation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haut_conseil_de_l'éducation) after he expressed these views in a private letter that he sent to [Bruno Racine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Racine), president of the HCE, that later was made public.[^o12]

Laurent is a devout Catholic and never married.[^o13] He opposed [same-sex marriage in France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Same-sex_marriage_in_France), and supported the Les Veilleurs (mouvement).[^o14]

In 2024, he declared that he was “full of admiration for Huawei”, which would supposedly be suffering a [campaign of criticism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism_of_Huawei) from the USA.[^o15]
