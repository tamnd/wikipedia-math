# Snub order-6 square tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub order-6 square tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub order-6 square tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of s{(4,4,3)} or s{4,6}.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The symmetry is doubled as a *snub order-6 square tiling*, with only one color of square. It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of s{4,6}.

*Image: Uniform tiling 443-snub2.png.*

:::
