# Alternated order-4 hexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alternated order-4 hexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **alternated order-4 hexagonal tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of (3,4,4), h{6,4}, and hr{6,6}.

:::
