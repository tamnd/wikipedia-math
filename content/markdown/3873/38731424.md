# Cantic order-4 hexagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cantic order-4 hexagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **cantic order-4 hexagonal tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>0,1</sub>{(4,4,3)} or h<sub>2</sub>{6,4}.

:::
