# Cantic octagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cantic octagonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **tritetratrigonal tiling** or **shieldotritetragonal tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of t<sub>1,2</sub>(4,3,3). It can also be named as a **cantic [octagonal tiling](wikipedia://en/27990286)**, h<sub>2</sub>{8,3}.

:::
