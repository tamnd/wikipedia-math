# Snub order-8 triangular tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snub order-8 triangular tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **snub tritetratrigonal tiling** or **snub order-8 triangular tiling** is a [uniform](wikipedia://en/18640974) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbols](wikipedia://en/489394) of s{(3,4,3)} and s{3,8}.

:::

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) construction from the [truncated order-8 triangular tiling](wikipedia://en/38353605) has 2 colors of triangles and achiral symmetry. It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of s{3,8}.
