::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="3 31 honeycomb"}

In 7-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **3<sub>31</sub> honeycomb** is a uniform honeycomb, also given by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3,3,3<sup>3,1</sup>} and is composed of [3<sub>21</sub>](wikipedia://en/19236353) and [7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex) [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647), with 56 and 576 of them respectively around each vertex.

:::

::: {#o8 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It is created by a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) upon a set of 8 [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) mirrors in 7-dimensional space.

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram).

Removing the node on the short branch leaves the [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex) facet:

Removing the node on the end of the 3-length branch leaves the [3<sub>21</sub>](wikipedia://en/19236353) facet:

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes [2<sub>31</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070163) polytope.

The [edge figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_figure) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes [6-demicube](wikipedia://en/10275945) (1<sub>31</sub>).

The [face figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face_figure) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes [rectified 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_5-simplex) (0<sub>31</sub>).

The cell figure is determined by removing the ringed node of the face figure and ringing the neighboring nodes. This makes [tetrahedral prism](wikipedia://en/5977290) {}×{3,3}.

:::

## Kissing number {#Kissing_number}

Each vertex of this tessellation is the center of a 6-sphere in the densest known [packing](wikipedia://en/368621) in 7 dimensions; its [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) is 126, represented by the vertices of its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) [2<sub>31</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070163).

## E7 lattice {#E7_lattice}

The 3<sub>31</sub> honeycomb's [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is called the **E<sub>7</sub> lattice**.[^o12]

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

It is in a dimensional series of uniform polytopes and honeycombs, expressed by [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) as 3<sub>k1</sub> series. A degenerate 4-dimensional case exists as 3-sphere tiling, a tetrahedral [hosohedron](wikipedia://en/2316192).
