::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="1 33 honeycomb"}

In 7-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **1<sub>33</sub>** is a uniform honeycomb, also given by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {3,3<sup>3,3</sup>}, and is composed of [1<sub>32</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070862) [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647). It is also named pentacontahexa-hecatonicosihexa-exic heptacomb and Jonathan Bowers gives it acronym linoh[^n1]

:::

::: {#o9 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

It is created by a [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777) upon a set of 8 [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) mirrors in 7-dimensional space.

The facet information can be extracted from its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram).

Removing a node on the end of one of the 3-length branch leaves the [1<sub>32</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070862), its only [facet](wikipedia://en/149926) type.

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is determined by removing the ringed node and ringing the neighboring node. This makes the [trirectified 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_7-simplex), 0<sub>33</sub>.

The [edge figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_figure) is determined by removing the ringed nodes of the vertex figure and ringing the neighboring node. This makes the [tetrahedral duoprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedral_duoprism), {3,3}×{3,3}.

:::

## Kissing number {#Kissing_number}

Each vertex of this polytope corresponds to the center of a 6-sphere in a moderately dense [sphere packing](wikipedia://en/368621), in which each sphere is tangent to 70 others; the best known for 7 dimensions (the [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555)) is 126.

## Geometric folding {#Geometric_folding}

The ${\tilde{E}}_7$ group is related to the ${\tilde{F}}_4$ by a geometric [folding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram#Geometric_folding), so this honeycomb can be projected into the 4-dimensional [demitesseractic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demitesseractic_honeycomb).

## E<sub>7</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice {#E7*_lattice}

${\tilde{E}}_7$ contains ${\tilde{A}}_7$ as a subgroup of index 144.[^o14] Both ${\tilde{E}}_7$ and ${\tilde{A}}_7$ can be seen as affine extension from $A_7$ from different nodes:

*Image: Affine\_A7\_E7\_relations.png.*

The **E<sub>7</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice** (also called E<sub>7</sub><sup>2</sup>)[^o15] has double the symmetry, represented by \[\[3,3<sup>3,3</sup>\]\]. The [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell) of the E<sub>7</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice is the [1<sub>32</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070862) polytope, and [voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) the **1<sub>33</sub> honeycomb**.[^o16] The **E<sub>7</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice** is constructed by 2 copies of the E<sub>7</sub> lattice vertices, one from each long branch of the Coxeter diagram, and can be constructed as the union of four A<sub>7</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattices, also called A<sub>7</sub><sup>4</sup>:

### Related polytopes and honeycombs {#Related_polytopes_and_honeycombs}

The 1<sub>33</sub> is fourth in a dimensional series of uniform polytopes and honeycombs, expressed by [Coxeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter) as 1<sub>3k</sub> series. The final is a noncompact hyperbolic honeycomb, 1<sub>34</sub>.
