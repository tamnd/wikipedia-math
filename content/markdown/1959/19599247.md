# Koornwinder polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Koornwinder polynomials"}

In mathematics, **Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials** (also called **Koornwinder polynomials**) are a family of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in several variables, introduced by [Koornwinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_H._Koornwinder)[^n1] and [I. G. Macdonald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._G._Macdonald),[^o3] that generalize the [Askey–Wilson polynomials](wikipedia://en/19582010). They are the [Macdonald polynomials](wikipedia://en/10354285) attached to the non-reduced [affine root system](wikipedia://en/32702474) of type (*C*${}^{\lor}_{n}$, *C*<sub>*n*</sub>), and in particular satisfy analogues of [Macdonald's conjectures](wikipedia://en/10354285). In addition [Jan Felipe van Diejen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jan_Felipe_van_Diejen) showed that the Macdonald polynomials associated to any classical root system can be expressed as limits or special cases of Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials and found complete sets of concrete commuting difference operators diagonalized by them.[^n2] Furthermore, there is a large class of interesting families of multivariable orthogonal polynomials associated with classical root systems which are degenerate cases of the Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials.[^n3] The Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomials have also been studied with the aid of [affine Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/10361630).

:::

The Macdonald-Koornwinder polynomial in *n* variables associated to the partition λ is the unique [Laurent polynomial](wikipedia://en/61351) invariant under permutation and inversion of variables, with **leading monomial** *x*<sup>λ</sup>, and orthogonal with respect to the density

$$\prod_{1\le i<j\le n} \frac{(x_i x_j,x_i/x_j,x_j/x_i,1/x_ix_j;q)_\infty}{(t x_ix_j,t x_i/x_j,t x_j/x_i,t/x_ix_j;q)_\infty} \prod_{1\le i\le n} \frac{(x_i^2,1/x_i^2;q)_\infty}{(a x_i,a/x_i,b x_i,b/x_i,c x_i,c/x_i,d x_i,d/x_i;q)_\infty}$$

on the unit torus

$$|x_1|=|x_2|=\cdots|x_n|=1,$$

where the parameters satisfy the constraints

$$|a|,|b|,|c|,|d|,|q|,|t|<1,$$

and (*x*;*q*)<sub>∞</sub> denotes the infinite [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717). Here leading monomial *x*<sup>λ</sup> means that μ≤λ for all terms *x*<sup>μ</sup> with nonzero coefficient, where μ≤λ [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) μ<sub>1</sub>≤λ<sub>1</sub>, μ<sub>1</sub>+μ<sub>2</sub>≤λ<sub>1</sub>+λ<sub>2</sub>, …, μ<sub>1</sub>+…+μ<sub>*n*</sub>≤λ<sub>1</sub>+…+λ<sub>*n*</sub>. Under further constraints that *q* and *t* are real and that *a*, *b*, *c*, *d* are real or, if complex, occur in conjugate pairs, the given density is positive.
