# Racah polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Racah polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Racah polynomials** are [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) named after [Giulio Racah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giulio_Racah), as their orthogonality relations are equivalent to his orthogonality relations for [Racah coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racah_coefficient).

:::

The Racah polynomials were first defined by (Wilson 1978)[^o3] and are given by

$$p_n(x(x+\gamma+\delta+1)) = {}_4F_3\left[\begin{matrix} -n &n+\alpha+\beta+1&-x&x+\gamma+\delta+1\\ \alpha+1&\gamma+1&\beta+\delta+1\\ \end{matrix};1\right].$$

## Orthogonality {#Orthogonality}

$$\sum_{y=0}^N\operatorname{R}_n(x;\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta) \operatorname{R}_m(x;\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)\frac{\gamma+\delta+1+2y}{\gamma+\delta+1+y} \omega_y=h_n\operatorname{\delta}_{n,m},$$

when $\alpha+1=-N$,

where $\operatorname{R}$ is the Racah polynomial,

$$x=y(y+\gamma+\delta+1),$$

$\operatorname{\delta}_{n,m}$ is the [Kronecker delta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kronecker_delta_function) and the weight functions are

$$\omega_y=\frac{(\alpha+1)_y(\beta+\delta+1)_y(\gamma+1)_y(\gamma+\delta+2)_y}{(-\alpha+\gamma+\delta+1)_y(-\beta+\gamma+1)_y(\delta+1)_yy!},$$

and

$$h_n=\frac{(-\beta)_N(\gamma+\delta+1)_N}{(-\beta+\gamma+1)_N(\delta+1)_N}\frac{(n+\alpha+\beta+1)_nn!}{(\alpha+\beta+2)_{2n}}\frac{(\alpha+\delta-\gamma+1)_n(\alpha-\delta+1)_n(\beta+1)_n}{(\alpha+1)_n(\beta+\delta+1)_n(\gamma+1)_n},$$

$(\cdot)_n$ is the [Pochhammer symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pochhammer_symbol).

::: {#o7 .statement cue=named-heading name="Rodrigues-type formula"}

## Rodrigues-type formula {#Rodrigues-type_formula}

$\omega(x;\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)\operatorname{R}_n(\lambda(x);\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)=(\gamma+\delta+1)_n\frac{\nabla^n}{\nabla\lambda(x)^n}\omega(x;\alpha+n,\beta+n,\gamma+n,\delta),$[^o8]

where $\nabla$ is the [backward difference operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Backward_difference_operator),

$$\lambda(x)=x(x+\gamma+\delta+1).$$

:::

## Generating functions {#Generating_functions}

There are three generating functions for $x\in\{0,1,2,...,N\}$

when $\beta+\delta+1=-N\quad$or$\quad\gamma+1=-N,$

$${}_2F_1(-x,-x+\alpha-\gamma-\delta;\alpha+1;t){}_2F_1(x+\beta+\delta+1,x+\gamma+1;\beta+1;t)$$

$$\quad=\sum_{n=0}^N\frac{(\beta+\delta+1)_n(\gamma+1)_n}{(\beta+1)_nn!}\operatorname{R}_n(\lambda(x);\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)t^n,$$

when $\alpha+1=-N\quad$or$\quad\gamma+1=-N,$

$${}_2F_1(-x,-x+\beta-\gamma;\beta+\delta+1;t){}_2F_1(x+\alpha+1,x+\gamma+1;\alpha-\delta+1;t)$$

$$\quad=\sum_{n=0}^N\frac{(\alpha+1)_n(\gamma+1)_n}{(\alpha-\delta+1)_nn!}\operatorname{R}_n(\lambda(x);\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)t^n,$$

when $\alpha+1=-N\quad$or$\quad\beta+\delta+1=-N,$

$${}_2F_1(-x,-x-\delta;\gamma+1;t){}_2F_1(x+\alpha+1;x+\beta+\gamma+1;\alpha+\beta-\gamma+1;t)$$

$$\quad=\sum_{n=0}^N\frac{(\alpha+1)_n(\beta+\delta+1)_n}{(\alpha+\beta-\gamma+1)_nn!}\operatorname{R}_n(\lambda(x);\alpha,\beta,\gamma,\delta)t^n.$$

::: {#o11 .statement cue=named-heading name="Connection formula for Wilson polynomials"}

## Connection formula for Wilson polynomials {#Connection_formula_for_Wilson_polynomials}

When $\alpha=a+b-1,\beta=c+d-1,\gamma=a+d-1,\delta=a-d,x\rightarrow-a+ix,$

$$\operatorname{R}_n(\lambda(-a+ix);a+b-1,c+d-1,a+d-1,a-d)=\frac{\operatorname{W}_n(x^2;a,b,c,d)}{(a+b)_n(a+c)_n(a+d)_n},$$

where $\operatorname{W}$ are Wilson polynomials.

:::

## q-analog {#q-analog}

(Askey Wilson 1979) introduced the *q*-Racah polynomials defined in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by[^o14]

$$p_n(q^{-x}+q^{x+1}cd;a,b,c,d;q) = {}_4\phi_3\left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n} &abq^{n+1}&q^{-x}&q^{x+1}cd\\ aq&bdq&cq\\ \end{matrix};q;q\right].$$

They are sometimes given with changes of variables as

$$W_n(x;a,b,c,N;q) = {}_4\phi_3\left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n} &abq^{n+1}&q^{-x}&cq^{x-n}\\ aq&bcq&q^{-N}\\ \end{matrix};q;q\right].$$
