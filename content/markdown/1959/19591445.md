# Oleg Marichev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Oleg Marichev"}

**Oleg Igorevich Marichev** (Олег Игоревич Маричев; born 7 September 1945 in [Velikiye Luki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velikiye_Luki), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)) is a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). In 1949 he moved to Minsk with his parents. He graduated from the [University of Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusian_State_University), where he continued to study for the Ph.D. degree. His scientific supervisor was Fedor Gakhov. He is the co-author of a comprehensive five volume series of *Integrals and Series* (Gordon and Breach Science Publishers, 1986–1992) together with [Yury Brychkov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yury_Aleksandrovich_Brychkov) and [A. P. Prudnikov](wikipedia://en/36714052). Around 1990 he received the [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Sc.) degree ([Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)) in mathematics from the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena), Germany. In 1992, Marichev started working with [Stephen Wolfram](wikipedia://en/52469) on [Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematica). His wife Anna helps him in his job.

:::
