# Zygmunt Zawirski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zygmunt Zawirski"}

*Image: Zygmunt Zawirski.jpg.* Zygmunt Zawirski

**Zygmunt Zawirski** (29 July 1882 – 2 April 1948) was a Polish [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician).

:::

His main field of study was [philosophy of physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_physics), [history of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_science), [multi-valued logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-valued_logic) and relation of multi-valued logic to calculus of probability.

## Biography {#Biography}

Zawirski was born on 29 July 1882 in the village of Berezowica Mała ([Mala Berezovytsia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mala_Berezovytsia)) near [Zbarazh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zbarazh) (now [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)). In 1928 he became a professor of the [Adam Mickiewicz University in Poznań](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adam_Mickiewicz_University_in_Poznań) and in 1937 professor of the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków). In 1936 he became an editor of *Kwartalnik Filozoficzny* ("Philosophical Quarterly"). After 1945, he was president of the [Krakowskie Towarzystwo Filozoficzne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krakowskie_Towarzystwo_Filozoficzne) ("Kraków Philosophical Society").

He died on 2 April 1948 in [Końskie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Końskie), Poland.
