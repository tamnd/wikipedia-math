# Snaith's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snaith's theorem"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Snaith's theorem**, introduced by [Victor Snaith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Snaith), identifies the [complex K-theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_K-theory) [spectrum](wikipedia://en/1033697) with the [localization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Localization_of_a_spectrum) of the suspension spectrum of $\mathbb{C}P^\infty$ away from the [Bott element](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bott_element).

:::
