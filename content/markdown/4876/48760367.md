# George Logemann

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="George Logemann"}

**George Wahl Logemann** (31 January 1938, [Milwaukee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milwaukee), – 5 June 2012, [Hartford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartford))[^o4] was an American mathematician and computer scientist. He became well known for the [Davis–Putnam–Logemann–Loveland algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Davis–Putnam–Logemann–Loveland_algorithm) to solve [Boolean satisfiability problems](wikipedia://en/4715).[^o5] He also contributed to the field of computer music.[^o4][^o6]

:::
