# Popescu's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Popescu's theorem"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **Popescu's theorem**, introduced by Dorin Popescu,[^o2][^o3] states:[^o4]

:::

Let *A* be a [Noetherian ring](wikipedia://en/56129) and *B* a Noetherian [algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_over_a_ring) over it. Then, the structure map *A* → *B* is a [regular homomorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_homomorphism) [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) *B* is a [direct limit](wikipedia://en/211566) of [smooth *A*-algebras](wikipedia://en/36088541).

For example, if *A* is a [local](wikipedia://en/153391) [G-ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-ring) (e.g., a local [excellent ring](wikipedia://en/10016360)) and *B* its [completion](wikipedia://en/9477975), then the map *A* → *B* is regular by definition and the theorem applies.

Another [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) of Popescu's theorem was given by Tetsushi Ogoma,[^o5] while an exposition of the result was provided by [Richard Swan](wikipedia://en/18384954).[^o6]

The usual proof of the [Artin approximation theorem](wikipedia://en/2388724) relies crucially on Popescu's theorem. Popescu's result was proved by an alternate method, and somewhat strengthened, by Mark Spivakovsky.[^o7][^o8]
