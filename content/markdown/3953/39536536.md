# Michael Krivelevich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Krivelevich"}

**Michael Krivelevich** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): 'מיכאל קריבלביץ; born January 30, 1966) is a professor with the School of Mathematical Sciences of [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel).

:::

Krivelevich received his PhD from Tel Aviv University in 1997 under the supervision of [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651). He has published extensively in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and adjacent fields and specializes in [extremal](wikipedia://en/2675917) and [probabilistic combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_combinatorics).[^o3]

He serves as an editor-in-chief of the [Journal of Combinatorial Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Combinatorial_Theory) (Series B) and is on the editorial board of several other journals in the field.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2007, Krivelevich and [Alan Frieze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Frieze) won the Pazy Memorial Award for research into probabilistic reasoning in combinatorics.[^o5]

In 2014, Krivelevich gave an invited address in the Combinatorics section at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065).[^o6]

He was elected as a member of the 2017 class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to extremal and probabilistic combinatorics".[^o7]
