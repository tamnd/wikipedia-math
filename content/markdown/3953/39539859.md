# Richard Arratia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Arratia"}

**Richard Alejandro Arratia** is a mathematician noted for his work in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Arratia developed the ideas of [interlace polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interlace_polynomial) with [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956) and Gregory Sorkin,[^o3] found an equivalent formulation of the [Stanley–Wilf conjecture](wikipedia://en/7892663) as the convergence of a limit,[^o4] and was the first to investigate the lengths of [superpatterns](wikipedia://en/7371807) of permutations.[^o4]

He has also written highly cited papers on the [Chen–Stein method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen-Stein_method) on distances between [probability distributions](wikipedia://en/23543),[^o5][^o6] on [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451) with exclusion,[^o7] and on [sequence alignment](wikipedia://en/149289).[^o8][^o9]

He is a coauthor of the book *Logarithmic Combinatorial Structures: A Probabilistic Approach*.[^o10][^o11][^o12]

## Education and employment {#Education_and_employment}

Arratia earned his B.S. from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) and his Ph.D. in 1979 from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) under the supervision of David Griffeath. He is currently a professor of mathematics at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California).[^o15]
