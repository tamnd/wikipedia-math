# Miklós Bóna

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Miklós Bóna"}

**Miklós Bóna** (born in [Székesfehérvár](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Székesfehérvár)) is an American mathematician of [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) origin.

:::

Bóna completed his undergraduate studies in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest) and [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), then obtained his Ph.D. at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 1997 as a student of [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397). Since 1999, he has taught at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida), where in 2010 he was inducted to the Academy of Distinguished Teaching Scholars.[^o4]

Bóna's main fields of research include the [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) of [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027), as well as [enumerative](wikipedia://en/3925533) and [analytic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/75060288). Since 2010, he has been one of the [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) of the [Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics).[^o5]
