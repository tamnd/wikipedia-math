# John Steggall

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Steggall"}

*Image: The grave of John Steggall, Western Cemetery, Dundee.jpg.* The grave of John Steggall, Western Cemetery, Dundee

**John Edward Aloysius Steggall** ARIBA [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) LLD (19 November 1855 – 26 November 1935) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at the University College, Dundee (now [University of Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dundee)).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

He was born on 19 November 1855 in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), the son of Dr J W B Steggall of Charing Cross Hospital, a physician and surgeon, living at Queen Square in [Bloomsbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomsbury).[^o4]

He attended the [City of London School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_London_School) before entering [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he graduated second [wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrangler_%28University_of_Cambridge%29) in the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos) of 1878 and winning the [Smith's Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith's_Prize) of that year.

He taught as Assistant Master at [Clifton College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton_College), [Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bristol) from 1878 to 1879, before becoming [Fielden Lecturer](wikipedia://en/6694084) at [Owens College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Owens_College), [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester), from 1880 to 1882. In 1883 he was appointed as the Chair of Mathematics and Natural Philosophy (Physics) in the newly instituted University College at Dundee. He lived at "Woodend" in [Perth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perth,_Scotland).

In 1885 he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were [Balfour Stewart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balfour_Stewart), [William Evans Hoyle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Evans_Hoyle), [Patrick Geddes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Geddes) and [Peter Guthrie Tait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Guthrie_Tait). He resigned temporarily and was re-elected in 1914, his proposers being [Cargill Gilston Knott](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cargill_Gilston_Knott), Sir [Edmund Taylor Whittaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Taylor_Whittaker), Sir [Ernest Wedderburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernest_Wedderburn) and [Ellice Horsburgh](wikipedia://en/52249022).[^o5]

He remained at Dundee University until 1933 when he retired after completing fifty years of professional work.[^n1] St Andrews University (which at that time controlled Dundee University) awarded him an honorary doctorate (LLD) on his retiral.

Steggall was an active member of the [Association for the Improvement of Geometrical Teaching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_the_Improvement_of_Geometrical_Teaching) and member of the Dundee School Board.[^n2] As an enthusiastic traveler and skilled amateur photographer, he traveled widely in Europe; his photographic collection is preserved in the library of the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews).

He died in Dundee on 26 November 1935 a few days after his 80th birthday.[^o8] He is buried in the [Western Cemetery, Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Cemetery,_Dundee). The grave lies on the western wall on the lower section.

A keen photographer he left a collection of over 2000 photographs to [St Andrews University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Andrews_University).[^o9]

## Family {#Family}

In 1878 he married Isabella Katherine Frazer (d.1945), sister of [James George Frazer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_George_Frazer).[^o11]

Their only son, John William Abbot Steggall, was killed on 31 May 1916 serving on [HMS Invincible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMS_Invincible_%281907%29) on the first day of the [Battle of Jutland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Jutland).[^o12]
