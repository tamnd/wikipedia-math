# David Gottlieb (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David Gottlieb"}

**David Gottlieb** ([Hebrew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_language): דוד גוטליב; November 14, 1944 – December 6, 2008) was an [Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israelis) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

David Gottlieb was born in [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv). He received his PhD in 1972 from the Department of Mathematics at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) under the guidance of Saul Abarbanel. He was a professor of applied mathematics at [Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) from 1985 until his death.[^o4]

His research focused on numerical analysis, especially as applied to nonlinear partial differential equations.

He was a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) and the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).
