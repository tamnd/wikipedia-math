# Matthias Aschenbrenner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matthias Aschenbrenner"}

**Matthias Aschenbrenner** (born 1972 in [Bad Kötzting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Kötzting)) is a German-American mathematician. He is a [professor of mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_of_Mathematics) and director of the logic group at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna).[^o3] His research interests include [differential algebra](wikipedia://en/1816587) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Aschenbrenner earned his "Vordiplom" at the [University of Passau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Passau) in 1996.[^o6] In 2001, he received his Ph.D. from the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign), where he was a student of [Lou van den Dries](wikipedia://en/55143215). For his dissertation, he was awarded the 2001 Sacks Prize by the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438).[^o8] After a visiting position at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), Aschenbrenner joined the faculty at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) in 2003, moving to the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 2007.[^o6] In 2012, Aschenbrenner became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9] He was jointly awarded the 2018 [Karp Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karp_Prize) with [Lou van den Dries](wikipedia://en/55143215) and [Joris van der Hoeven](wikipedia://en/62070387) "for their work in model theory, especially on asymptotic differential algebra and the model theory of transseries".[^o10][^o11] In 2018, Aschenbrenner was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).[^o12] Aschenbrenner moved to the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 2020, where he is also director of the logic group.[^o3][^o6]
