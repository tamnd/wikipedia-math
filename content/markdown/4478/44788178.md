# Stuart S. Antman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stuart S. Antman"}

**Stuart Sheldon Antman** is an American mathematician. He is [Distinguished University Research Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_University_Research_Professor) at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland).[^o2] His research involves [continuum mechanics](wikipedia://en/5918), [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29), and [nonlinear partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/16678376).

:::

Antman did his undergraduate studies at the [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute), graduating in 1961.[^o2] He earned a Ph.D. in 1965 from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), under the supervision of William H. Warner.[^o3] He joined the [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) faculty in 1967, and moved to Maryland in 1972. He became Distinguished University Professor at Maryland in 2001, and Distinguished University Research Professor in 2014.[^o2]

Antman became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2009,[^o2] and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.[^o4] He was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1978, and with [John M. Ball](wikipedia://en/3578468) he won the [Theodore von Kármán Prize](wikipedia://en/24419775) in 1999.[^o2] In 1987 Antman won a [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award).[^o5] and in 2015 the Lyapunov Award from the American Society of Mechanical Engineers.

Antman is the author of the book *Nonlinear Problems of Elasticity* (Springer, 1995; 2nd ed., 2005).[^o6][^o7]
