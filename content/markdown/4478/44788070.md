# Michael T. Anderson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael T. Anderson"}

**Michael T. Anderson** (born November 18, 1950, in [Boulder, Colorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boulder,_Colorado))[^o2] is an American mathematician. He is a professor of mathematics at the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook).[^o3] His research concerns [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) including [Ricci curvature](wikipedia://en/276582) and [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734).

:::

After doing his undergraduate studies at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara),[^o2] Anderson received his Ph.D. from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1981 under the supervision of [H. Blaine Lawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._Blaine_Lawson).

In 2012, Anderson became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

## Major publications {#Major_publications}

- Anderson, Michael T.; [Schoen, Richard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Schoen). *Positive harmonic functions on complete manifolds of negative curvature.* Ann. of Math. (2) 121 (1985), no. 3, 429–461.
- Anderson, Michael T. *Ricci curvature bounds and Einstein metrics on compact manifolds.* J. Amer. Math. Soc. 2 (1989), no. 3, 455–490.
- Anderson, Michael T. *Convergence and rigidity of manifolds under Ricci curvature bounds.* Invent. Math. 102 (1990), no. 2, 429–445.
