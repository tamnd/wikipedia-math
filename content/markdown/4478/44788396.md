# Todd Arbogast

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Todd Arbogast"}

**Todd Arbogast** is an American mathematician. He is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), where he is a W. A. "Tex" Moncrief, Jr. Simulation-Based Engineering and Sciences Professor and Frank E. Gerth III Faculty Fellow. His research concerns the [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o2]

:::

Arbogast did his undergraduate studies at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), in mathematics and physics, graduating in 1981.[^o2] He received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1987 under the supervision of [Jim Douglas, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jim_Douglas,_Jr.)

In 2012, Arbogast became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
