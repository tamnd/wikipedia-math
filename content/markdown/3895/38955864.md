# Birmingham Statistical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Birmingham Statistical Society"}

The **Birmingham Statistical Society** (or **Birmingham Statistical Society for the Improvement of Education**) was a [society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Club_%28organization%29) founded in [Birmingham, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birmingham,_England), in the 1830s. In 1839, it produced "a very accurate enquiry into the state of education in that town".[^o3][^o4] It was still active as of 1887.[^o5]

:::
