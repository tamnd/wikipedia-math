::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 7-orthoplexes"}

In seven-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 7-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 7-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821719), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-orthoplex).

:::

There are unique 7 degrees of rectifications, the zeroth being the [7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-orthoplex), and the 6th and last being the [7-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube). Vertices of the rectified 7-orthoplex are located at the edge-centers of the 7-orthoplex. Vertices of the birectified 7-orthoplex are located in the triangular face centers of the 7-orthoplex. Vertices of the trirectified 7-orthoplex are located in the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cell centers of the 7-orthoplex.

The *rectified 7-orthoplex* is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) for the [demihepteractic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demihepteractic_honeycomb). The rectified 7-orthoplex's 84 vertices represent the [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) of a sphere-packing constructed from this honeycomb.

### Construction {#Construction}

There are two [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) associated with the *rectified heptacross*, one with the C<sub>7</sub> or \[4,3,3,3,3,3\] Coxeter group, and a lower symmetry with two copies of pentacross facets, alternating, with the D<sub>7</sub> or \[3<sup>4,1,1</sup>\] Coxeter group.

:::

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a rectified heptacross, centered at the origin, edge length $\sqrt{2}\$ are all permutations of:

(±1,±1,0,0,0,0,0)

#### Root vectors {#Root_vectors}

Its 84 vertices represent the root vectors of the [simple Lie group](wikipedia://en/292831) D<sub>7</sub>. The vertices can be seen in 3 [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862), with the 21 vertices [rectified 6-simplexs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_6-simplex) cells on opposite sides, and 42 vertices of an [expanded 6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expanded_6-simplex) passing through the center. When combined with the 14 vertices of the 7-orthoplex, these vertices represent the 98 root vectors of the B<sub>7</sub> and C<sub>7</sub> simple [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).
