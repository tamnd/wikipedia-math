::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 8-orthoplexes"}

In eight-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 8-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 8-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821715), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-orthoplex).

:::

There are unique 8 degrees of rectifications, the zeroth being the [8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-orthoplex), and the 7th and last being the [8-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cube). Vertices of the rectified 8-orthoplex are located at the edge-centers of the 8-orthoplex. Vertices of the birectified 8-orthoplex are located in the triangular face centers of the 8-orthoplex. Vertices of the trirectified 8-orthoplex are located in the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cell centers of the 8-orthoplex.

The rectified 8-orthoplex has 112 vertices. These represent the root vectors of the [simple Lie group](wikipedia://en/292831) D<sub>8</sub>. The vertices can be seen in 3 [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862), with the 28 vertices [rectified 7-simplexs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_7-simplex) cells on opposite sides, and 56 vertices of an [expanded 7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expanded_7-simplex) passing through the center. When combined with the 16 vertices of the 8-orthoplex, these vertices represent the 128 root vectors of the B<sub>8</sub> and C<sub>8</sub> simple [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).

### Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The *rectified 8-orthoplex* is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) for the [demiocteractic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demiocteractic_honeycomb).

- Rectified octacross

::: {#o16 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

### Construction {#Construction}

There are two [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) associated with the *rectified 8-orthoplex*, one with the C<sub>8</sub> or \[4,3<sup>6</sup>\] Coxeter group, and a lower symmetry with two copies of heptcross facets, alternating, with the D<sub>8</sub> or \[3<sup>5,1,1</sup>\] Coxeter group.

:::

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a rectified 8-orthoplex, centered at the origin, edge length $\sqrt{2}$ are all permutations of:

(±1,±1,0,0,0,0,0,0)
