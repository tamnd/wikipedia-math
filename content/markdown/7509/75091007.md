# Nelly Litvak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nelly Litvak"}

**Nelly Vladimirovna Litvak** (Нелли Владимировна Литвак, born January 27, 1972) is a Russian and Dutch [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) whose research includes the study of [complex networks](wikipedia://en/1704711), [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895), and their applications in [medical logistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_logistics). Formerly a professor at the [University of Twente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Twente), she moved to the [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology) in 2023.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nelly Litvak was born in Gorky (now [Nizhny Novgorod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizhny_Novgorod)), Russia to the journalist Nina Zvereva (journalist) and the researcher Vladimir Antonets.[^o3][^o4] In 1995 Litvak graduated from the [N. I. Lobachevsky State University of Nizhny Novgorod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._I._Lobachevsky_State_University_of_Nizhny_Novgorod),[^o5] and in 1998 she earned the [candidate's degree in physical and mathematical sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_%28degree%29) from the latter university with the dissertation *Adaptive Control of Conflicting Flows* supervised by Mikhail Andreevich Fedotkin.[^o6] In 1999 she moved to the Netherlands. She completed another doctoral degree in 2002, a Ph.D. from the Eindhoven University of Technology, with the dissertation *Collecting $n$ Items Randomly Located on a Circle*, completed in [EURANDOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EURANDOM),[^o7] jointly [promoted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ius_promovendi) by Ivo Adan, [Jaap Wessels](wikipedia://en/40568668), and [Henk Zijm](wikipedia://en/40570155).

She became a lecturer at the University of Twente in 2002, and was promoted to associate professor in 2011 and full professor in 2018. In 2017, she took on a second part-time affiliation with the Eindhoven University of Technology, and in 2023 she moved from Twente to a full-time position at the Eindhoven University of Technology, as professor of algorithms for complex networks.

## Books {#Books}

Litvak is the author of several [popular science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_science) books, including:

- *Наши хорошие подростки* (*Our good teenagers*, in Russian, Alpina, 2010)
- *Формула призвания – 7 правил выбора вуза* (*Vocation formula – 7 rules for choosing university*, Alpina, 2012)
- *IQ to Love: What makes highly intelligent men attractive to women* (self-published, 2014)
- *Кому нужна математика? Понятная книга о том, как устроен цифровой мир* (*Who Needs Mathematics? A Clear Book about how the Digital World Works*, with Andrey Raygorodsky, in Russian, Mann-Ivanov-Ferber («Манн, Иванов и Фербер»), 2017)
  - The book was shortlisted for the Russian 2017 [Enlightener Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlightener_Prize).[^o9]
- *Математика для безнадежных гуманитариев* (*Mathematics for hopeless humanities geeks*, with Alla Kechedzhan, in Russian, AST, 2019)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Litvak was awarded the 2002 [Stieltjes](wikipedia://en/40589) Prize for her Ph.D. dissertation.[^o11] She was the 2011 recipient of the Professor De Winter award of the University of Twente, given annually to recognize the research of a female faculty member. She was named the university's teacher of the year in 2022.

## Personal {#Personal}

While in the Netherlands, Nelly Litvak married to Pranab Mandal.[^o13] She has two daughters: Natalia (born in 1993 from the first marriage[^o3]) and Piyali (born in 2005 to Nelly and Pranab). She has younger sister Yekaterina and brother Pyotr.[^o14][^o3]
