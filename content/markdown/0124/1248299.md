# Hyperbolic link

*Image: Blue Figure-Eight Knot.png.* [4<sub>1</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/59565)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hyperbolic link"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **hyperbolic link** is a [link](wikipedia://en/1161784) in the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792) with [complement](wikipedia://en/1350120) that has a complete [Riemannian metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemannian_metric) of constant negative [curvature](wikipedia://en/60770), i.e. has a [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291). A **hyperbolic knot** is a hyperbolic link with one [component](wikipedia://en/6233).

:::

As a consequence of the work of [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190), it is known that every knot is precisely one of the following: hyperbolic, a [torus knot](wikipedia://en/1165182), or a [satellite knot](wikipedia://en/9100897). As a consequence, hyperbolic knots can be considered plentiful. A similar heuristic applies to hyperbolic links.

As a consequence of Thurston's [hyperbolic Dehn surgery](wikipedia://en/4450009) theorem, performing [Dehn surgeries](wikipedia://en/1240699) on a hyperbolic link enables one to obtain many more [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700).

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

*Image: BorromeanRings.svg.* [Borromean rings](wikipedia://en/873118) are a hyperbolic link.

- [Borromean rings](wikipedia://en/873118) are hyperbolic.
- Every [non-split](wikipedia://en/3687789), [prime](wikipedia://en/315030), [alternating](wikipedia://en/1236143) link that is not a [torus link](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torus_link) is hyperbolic by a result of [William Menasco](wikipedia://en/16854432).
- [4<sub>1</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/59565) (the figure-eight knot)
- [5<sub>2</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/26643955) (the three-twist knot)
- [6<sub>1</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/26652059) (the stevedore knot)
- [6<sub>2</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/26677990)
- [6<sub>3</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/26684180)
- [7<sub>4</sub> knot](wikipedia://en/38989391)
- [10 161 knot](wikipedia://en/16618607) (the "Perko pair" knot)
- [12n242 knot](wikipedia://en/9911144)
