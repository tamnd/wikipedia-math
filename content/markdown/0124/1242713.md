# Dynamic Bayesian network

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dynamic Bayesian network"}

*Image: Réseau bayésien dynamique.svg.* Dynamic Bayesian Network composed by 3 variables.

*Image: Réseau bayésien 3t.svg.* Bayesian Network developed on 3 time steps.

*Image: Réseau bayésien simplifié.svg.* Simplified Dynamic Bayesian Network. All the variables do not need to be duplicated in the graphical model, but they are dynamic, too.

A **dynamic Bayesian network** (DBN) is a [Bayesian network](wikipedia://en/203996) (BN) which relates variables to each other over adjacent time steps.

:::

## History {#History}

A dynamic Bayesian network (DBN) is often called a "two-timeslice" BN (2TBN) because it says that at any point in time T, the value of a variable can be calculated from the internal regressors and the immediate prior value (time T-1). DBNs were developed by [Paul Dagum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Dagum) in the early 1990s at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University)'s Section on Medical Informatics.[^o3][^o4] Dagum developed DBNs to unify and extend traditional linear [state-space models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Space_Model) such as [Kalman filters](wikipedia://en/180855), linear and normal forecasting models such as [ARMA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ARMA_model) and simple dependency models such as [hidden Markov models](wikipedia://en/98770) into a general probabilistic representation and inference mechanism for arbitrary nonlinear and non-normal time-dependent domains.[^o5][^o6]

Today, DBNs are common in [robotics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robotics), and have shown potential for a wide range of [data mining](wikipedia://en/42253) applications. For example, they have been used in [speech recognition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_recognition), [digital forensics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_forensics), [protein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protein) [sequencing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequencing), and [bioinformatics](wikipedia://en/4214). DBN is a generalization of [hidden Markov models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Markov_models) and [Kalman filters](wikipedia://en/180855).[^o7]

DBNs are conceptually related to probabilistic Boolean networks[^o8] and can, similarly, be used to model dynamical systems at steady-state.

## Software {#Software}

- bayesnet/bnt: the Bayes Net Toolbox for Matlab, by Kevin Murphy, (released under a [GPL license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License))
- [Graphical Models Toolkit](https://web.archive.org/web/20150228005644/http://melodi.ee.washington.edu/gmtk/) (GMTK): an open-source, publicly available toolkit for rapidly prototyping statistical models using dynamic graphical models (DGMs) and dynamic Bayesian networks (DBNs). GMTK can be used for applications and research in speech and language processing, bioinformatics, activity recognition, and any time-series application.
- "DBmcmc". <http://www.bioss.ac.uk/~dirk/software/DBmcmc/> : Inferring Dynamic Bayesian Networks with MCMC, for Matlab (free software)
- globalmit: Modeling gene regulatory network via global optimization of dynamic bayesian network (released under a [GPL license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_General_Public_License))
- [libDAI](http://staff.science.uva.nl/~jmooij1/libDAI/): C++ library that provides implementations of various (approximate) inference methods for discrete graphical models; supports arbitrary factor graphs with discrete variables, including discrete Markov Random Fields and Bayesian Networks (released under the [FreeBSD license](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FreeBSD_license))
- [aGrUM](http://agrum.gitlab.io): C++ library (with Python bindings) for different types of PGMs including Bayesian Networks and Dynamic Bayesian Networks (released under the GPLv3)
- [FALCON](https://github.com/sysbiolux/FALCON/): Matlab toolbox for contextualization of DBNs models of regulatory networks with biological quantitative data, including various regularization schemes to model prior biological knowledge (released under the GPLv3)
