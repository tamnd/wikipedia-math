# Boundary parallel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boundary parallel"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a connected [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095) of a compact [manifold with boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_with_boundary) is said to be **boundary parallel**, **∂-parallel**, or **peripheral** if it can be continuously deformed into a boundary component. This notion is important for [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) topology.

:::

## Boundary-parallel embedded surfaces in 3-manifolds {#Boundary-parallel_embedded_surfaces_in_3-manifolds}

If $F$ is an orientable closed surface smoothly embedded in the interior of an manifold with boundary $M$ then it is said to be boundary parallel if a connected component of $M \smallsetminus F$ is homeomorphic to $F \smallsetminus [0, 1[$.[^o3]

In general, if $(F, \partial F)$ is a topologically embedded compact surface in a compact 3-manifold $(M, \partial M)$ some more care is needed:[^n1] one needs to assume that $F$ admits a bicollar,[^o5] and then $F$ is boundary parallel if there exists a subset $P \subset M$ such that $F$ is the [frontier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frontier_%28mathematics%29) of $P$ in $M$ and $P$ is homeomorphic to $F \times [0, 1]$.
