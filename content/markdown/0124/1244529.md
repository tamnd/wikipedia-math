# Algebra bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Algebra bundle"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **algebra bundle** is a [fiber bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) whose [fibers](wikipedia://en/6603892) are [algebras](wikipedia://en/191788) and [local trivializations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_trivialization) respect the algebra structure. It follows that the [transition functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transition_map) are [algebra isomorphisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_isomorphism). Since algebras are also [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370), every algebra bundle is a [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524).

:::

Examples include the [tensor-algebra bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tensor-algebra_bundle), [exterior bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_bundle), and [symmetric bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_bundle) associated to a given [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524), as well as the [Clifford bundle](wikipedia://en/8687388) associated to any Riemannian vector bundle.
