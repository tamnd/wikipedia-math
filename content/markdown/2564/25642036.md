# Alexei Skorobogatov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexei Skorobogatov"}

**Alexei Nikolaievich Skorobogatov** (Алексе́й Никола́евич Скоробога́тов) is a [British-Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians_in_the_United_Kingdom) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Professor in Pure Mathematics at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) specialising in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). His work has focused on [rational points](wikipedia://en/2960200), the [Hasse principle](wikipedia://en/489707), the [Manin obstruction](wikipedia://en/18960214), [exponential sums](wikipedia://en/468937), and [error-correcting codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error-correcting_code).

:::

## Education {#Education}

He completed his [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) under the supervision of [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086), for which he was awarded a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree.

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2001 he was awarded a [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) by the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o6]

He was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2020 Class, for "contributions to the Diophantine geometry of surfaces and higher dimensional varieties".[^o7]
