# Viacheslav V. Nikulin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Viacheslav V. Nikulin"}

**Viacheslav Valentinovich Nikulin (Slava)** is a Russian mathematician working in the [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) of [K3 surfaces](wikipedia://en/646116) and [Calabi–Yau threefolds](wikipedia://en/188932), [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/644671), the arithmetic of [quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478), and hyperbolic [Kac–Moody algebras](wikipedia://en/1146292).[^o3] He is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool). A third chair of mathematics was established for Nikulin in 1999, the second chair having been established in 1964 for [C. T. C. Wall](wikipedia://en/2613478) and the first having been established in 1882.[^o4] Nikulin has made contributions towards the solution of [Hilbert's 16th problem](wikipedia://en/381750).[^o5]
