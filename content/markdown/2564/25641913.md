# Askold Vinogradov

Ivanovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Askold Vinogradov"}

**Askold Ivanovich Vinogradov** (Аско́льд Ива́нович Виногра́дов; 1929 – 31 December 2005) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513). The [Bombieri–Vinogradov theorem](wikipedia://en/4632596) is partially named after him.[^o2]

:::
