# Winning Ways for Your Mathematical Plays

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Winning Ways for Your Mathematical Plays"}

***Winning Ways for Your Mathematical Plays*** (Academic Press, 1982) by [Elwyn R. Berlekamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elwyn_R._Berlekamp), [John H. Conway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_H._Conway), and [Richard K. Guy](wikipedia://en/816308) is a compendium of information on [mathematical games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_games). It was first published in 1982 in two volumes.

:::

The first volume introduces [combinatorial game theory](wikipedia://en/292231) and its foundation in the [surreal numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surreal_numbers); [partizan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partizan_game) and [impartial games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impartial_game); [Sprague–Grundy theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprague–Grundy_theory) and [misère games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Misère_game). The second volume applies the theorems of the first volume to many games, including [nim](wikipedia://en/21885), [sprouts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sprouts_%28game%29), [dots and boxes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dots_and_boxes), [Sylver coinage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylver_coinage), [phutball (philosopher's football)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phutball), [fox and geese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fox_and_geese). A final section on puzzles analyzes the [Soma cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soma_cube), [Rubik's Cube](wikipedia://en/25971), [peg solitaire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peg_solitaire), and [Conway's Game of Life](wikipedia://en/37035).

A republication of the work by [A K Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_K_Peters) split the content into four volumes.
