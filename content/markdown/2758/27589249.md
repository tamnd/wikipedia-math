# Significance (magazine)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Significance"}

***Significance***, established in 2004, is a bimonthly print and digital [magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magazine) published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) on behalf of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) (RSS), the [Statistical Society of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Society_of_Australia) (SSA) and the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA).[^o9] It publishes articles on topics of statistical interest presented at a level suited for a general audience. Articles are reviewed by an editorial board of statistics experts drawn from the three societies.[^o3] The founding [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) was [Helen Joyce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helen_Joyce).[^o4] The current editor is Anna Britten. *Significance* replaced the RSS's journal, [*The Statistician*](wikipedia://en/13327143#Journal_of_the_Royal_Statistical_Society,_Series_D_%28The_Statistician%29).[^o5] The magazine also has a [website](https://significancemagazine.com).

:::

In addition to ordinary articles in the magazine, additional "virtual issues" (collections of articles on a particular subject area) are made available online.[^o6] In November 2010 the magazine launched its website.[^o7] Having been launched as a quarterly magazine, *Significance* changed to a bimonthly frequency in 2011. In 2020, the regular column 'Dr Fisher's Casebook' was renamed 'the secret statistician', a review prompted by concern about [Fisher's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_Aylmer_Fisher) views on eugenics (and supported by the lack of salience of the homage to *[Dr. Finlay's Casebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Finlay's_Casebook)*).[^o8]

Members of either the RSS or the ASA receive the magazine as part of their membership.[^o9] In January 2015, the RSS and ASA decided to make the magazine issues available to the public free of charge a year after their publication.[^o10]
