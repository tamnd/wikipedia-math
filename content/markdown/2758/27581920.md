# O\*-algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="O*-algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **O\*-algebra** is an [algebra](wikipedia://en/455987) of possibly [unbounded operators](wikipedia://en/1422584) defined on a dense subspace of a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932). The original examples were described by Borchers (1962)[^o2] and (Uhlmann 1962),[^o4] who studied some examples of O\*-algebras, called [Borchers algebras](wikipedia://en/27591226), arising from the [Wightman axioms](wikipedia://en/293622) of [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267). Powers (1971)[^o5] and Lassner (1972)[^o6] began the systematic study of algebras of unbounded operators.

:::
