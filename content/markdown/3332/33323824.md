# Thomas Zink

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Zink"}

**Thomas Zink** (born 14 April 1949 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin)) is a German mathematician. He currently holds a chair for [arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetics) [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University). He has been doing research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) and at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) among others.

:::

In 1992, he was awarded the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize) of the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) joint with [Christopher Deninger](wikipedia://en/32349981) ([Westfälische Wilhelms-Universität](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfälische_Wilhelms-Universität) of [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster_%28Westfalen%29)), [Michael Rapoport](wikipedia://en/26629548) ([University of Wuppertal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wuppertal)) and [Peter Schneider](wikipedia://en/64772763) ([University of Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne)). The four researchers succeeded to apply modern methods of algebraic geometry to the solution of [diophantine equations](wikipedia://en/9109). Furthermore, he is a member of the [German Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) ([Halle an der Saale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_an_der_Saale)).
