# Joseph Dennis (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Dennis"}

**Joseph James Dennis** (11 April 1905 in [Gainesville, Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gainesville,_Florida) – April 1977)[^o2] was an African-American mathematician. He served as the chairman of the [Clark College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clark_College_%28Washington%29) mathematics department from 1930 to 1974.

:::

Dennis gained his B.A. from Clark College in 1929, and his M.A. from Northwestern University in 1935.[^o3] He earned his Ph.D. at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 1944. This is the same year as two other African-American men earned a Ph.D in mathematics, [Wade Ellis Sr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wade_Ellis_Sr.) and [Warren Hill Brothers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warren_Hill_Brothers) (both from the University of North Western University). His thesis was "Some Points in the Theory of Positive Definite J-Fractions" (related to [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199)), supervised by [H. S. Wall](wikipedia://en/19463459). He was one of the first [African Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Americans) to earn a PhD.

A building, and a scholarship fund for junior and senior mathematics majors at Clark University, are named in J.J. Dennis' honor.[^o6][^o4]
