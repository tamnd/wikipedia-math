# Elisabeth M. Werner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elisabeth M. Werner"}

**Elisabeth M. Werner** is a mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University), as associate director of the [Institute for Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/10324088), and as [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) at the [Lille University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille_University_of_Science_and_Technology). Her research interests include [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), and their applications.[^o3]

:::

Werner earned a diploma in mathematics from the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen), in Germany, in 1985.[^o3] She moved to France for her graduate studies, finishing her doctorate in 1989 at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), under the supervision of Gilles Godefroy.[^o3] On completing her doctorate she took a faculty position at Case, and two years later added her affiliation with Lille. At Case, she was promoted to full professor in 2002.[^o3]

In 2012, she became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
