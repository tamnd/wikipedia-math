# Robert Feys

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Feys"}

**Robert Feys** (19 December 1889 – 13 April 1961) was a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgians) [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) and [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher), who worked at the [University of Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Leuven_%281834–1968%29) ([Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium)).[^o2]

:::

Feys was born in [Mechelen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechelen),[^o2] and received his PhD in 1909 from the [Institute of Philosophy, University of Leuven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Philosophy,_University_of_Leuven). In 1913 he was appointed Professor at the [Université Saint-Louis, Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Louis_University,_Brussels). But due to the War he enlisted in the Army. In 1919 he was appointed Professor at the Institute St. Gertrude in [Nivelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nivelles). In 1929 he returned to the Université Saint-Louis, Brussels, and in 1944 he was appointed Professor at the University of Leuven.[^o2]

In 1958 Feys and [Haskell B. Curry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haskell_B._Curry) devised the [type inference](wikipedia://en/279690) algorithm for the [simply typed lambda calculus](wikipedia://en/1986011) (*Combinatory Logic*).
