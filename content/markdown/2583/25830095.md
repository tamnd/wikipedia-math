# Mikhail Katz

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Katz"}

**Mikhail** "**Mischa**" **Gershevich Katz** (, מיכאיל כץ; born 1958)[^o3] is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University). His main interests are [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), [nonstandard analysis](wikipedia://en/21708), and [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471); he is the author of the book *Systolic Geometry and Topology*, which is mainly about [systolic geometry](wikipedia://en/2545455). The [Katz–Sabourau inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katz–Sabourau_inequality) is named after him and Stéphane Sabourau.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Mikhail Katz was born in [Chișinău](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chișinău) in 1958. His mother was Clara Katz ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Given_name#Name_at_birth) Landman). In 1976, he moved with his mother to the United States.[^o7][^o8]

Katz earned a bachelor's degree in 1980 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o3] He did his graduate studies at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), receiving his Ph.D. in 1984 under the joint supervision of [Troels Jørgensen](wikipedia://en/31074567) and [Mikhael Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Leonidovich_Gromov). His thesis title is *Jung's Theorem in Complex Projective Geometry.*

He moved to [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) in 1999, after previously holding positions at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University), [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington), the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques), the [University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes_1), [Henri Poincaré University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Poincaré_University), and [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University).[^o3]

## Work {#Work}

Katz has performed research in systolic geometry in collaboration with [Luigi Ambrosio](wikipedia://en/25337146), [Victor Bangert](wikipedia://en/25081608), [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29), [Steve Shnider](wikipedia://en/25430349), [Shmuel Weinberger](wikipedia://en/19159322), and others. He has authored research publications appearing in journals including *[Communications on Pure and Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communications_on_Pure_and_Applied_Mathematics), [Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal), [Geometric and Functional Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_and_Functional_Analysis_%28journal%29)*, and *[Journal of Differential Geometry](wikipedia://en/25823636)*. Along with these papers, Katz was a contributor to the book "[Metric Structures for Riemannian and Non-Riemannian Spaces](wikipedia://en/31859069)".[^o11] [Marcel Berger](wikipedia://en/12149363) in his article "What is... a Systole?"[^o12] lists Katz's 2007 book *Systolic Geometry and Topology* as one of two books he cites in systolic geometry.

More recently Katz also contributed to the study of [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), including work that provides an alternative interpretation of the number [0.999...](wikipedia://en/1849799).[^o13]
