# Louis Boutet de Monvel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Louis Boutet de Monvel"}

*Image: Louis Boutet de Monvel Oberwolfach 2011.jpg.* Boutet de Monvel at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2011

**Louis Boutet de Monvel** (22 June 1941 – 25 December 2014) was a French mathematician who worked on [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).[^o2]

:::

He was a student of [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) and was professor at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University). He was married to [Anne Boutet de Monvel](wikipedia://en/69201444), also a mathematician.[^o3]

In 2007, he was awarded the [Émile Picard Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Émile_Picard_Medal) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) and in 2003 the [Prix de l'État](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_de_l'État).

According to the [Mathematics Genealogy Project](wikipedia://en/1152126) [^o4] his Ph.D. students are AbdelAli Attioui (1994), Jean-Marc Delort, [Bernard Helffer](wikipedia://en/56549449) (1976), [Gilles Lebeau](wikipedia://en/61741078) (1984), [George Marinescu](wikipedia://en/61683094) (1994), [Philibert Nang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philibert_Nang) (1996), Serge Lukasiewicz (1997), Alexander Rezounenko (1997).
