# Giulio Vivanti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giulio Vivanti"}

**Giulio Benedetto Isacco Vivanti** (24 May 1859 – 19 November 1949) was an [Italian Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_Jews) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a mentor of [Bruno de Finetti](wikipedia://en/180825) and he spent most of his academic career at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) and [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan).

:::
