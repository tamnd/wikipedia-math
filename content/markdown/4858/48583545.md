# Patricia Hersh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Patricia Hersh"}

**Patricia Lynn Hersh** (born 1973)[^o3] is an American mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon).[^o4] Her research concerns [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), [topological combinatorics](wikipedia://en/17633579), and the connections between combinatorics and other fields of mathematics.[^o10]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hersh graduated *magna cum laude* with an A.B. in mathematics and computer science from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1995,[^o6] with a senior thesis supervised by [Persi Diaconis](wikipedia://en/65467).[^o10] She completed her Ph.D. in 1999 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), under the supervision of [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397); her dissertation was *Decomposition and Enumeration in Partially Ordered Sets*.[^o6]

After postdoctoral positions at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington), [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), and [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), she joined the faculty at [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) in 2004, moved to North Carolina State University in 2008, and then to the University of Oregon in 2019.[^o6] She served as an [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) Council member at large from 2011 to 2013.[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2010, Hersh won the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), funding a visiting position for her at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o10] In 2015 she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to algebraic and topological combinatorics, and for service to the mathematical community".[^o11]
