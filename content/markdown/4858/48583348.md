# Hernando Burgos-Soto

Burgos

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hernando Burgos-Soto"}

**Hernando Burgos Soto** is a Canadian (Colombian born) educator and mathematician, professor of mathematics at [George Brown College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Brown_College).[^o3] He is the author of several math papers in which he introduced some mathematics concepts and extended to [tangles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangle_theory) some celebrated results of [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008) about the Khovanov homology and the Jones polynomial.[^o4][^o5] During his career as a mathematician, his interests have included [Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Statistics), [Knot Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_Theory), [Algebraic Topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_Topology) and more recently [Mathematical Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Finance). He is comfortable writing in English and Spanish. When writing in Spanish, he works in the area of [prose fiction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prose_fiction) writing short stories. Some of his short stories were published at the website Cuentos y Cuentos.[^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Professor Burgos Soto holds a BSc.Ed in mathematics at the [University of Atlántico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Atlántico) and a MSc in Mathematics from the [University of Valle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Valle). He earned a PhD in mathematics from [National University of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Colombia) in 2009, and completed his dissertation at [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) under the guidance of professor [Dror Bar-Natan](wikipedia://en/10363723).[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

His works include [regression diagnostic](wikipedia://en/30641121) analysis for [General Linear Models](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Linear_Model),[^o10] extension to tangles of [Morwen Thistlethwaite](wikipedia://en/16705411)'s result on the alternation of the [Jones polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107642) for alternating links,[^o11] and a Lee's result on [Khovanov homology](wikipedia://en/2639906) for [links](wikipedia://en/1161784), that states that the Khovanov homology for [alternating links](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternating_link) is supported in two lines. During his works Professor Burgos has introduced some Mathematical concepts such as: Gravity Information in a tangle diagram, [Alternating planar algebras](wikipedia://en/48274833), and Rotation Number of Smoothings.[^o12]
