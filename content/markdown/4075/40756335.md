# William Davidson Niven

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Davidson Niven"}

**Sir William Davidson Niven** (24 March 1842 – 29 May 1917) was a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [electrical engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering).

:::

After an early teaching career at [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), Niven was Director of Studies at the [Royal Naval College, Greenwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Naval_College,_Greenwich), for thirty years.

## Life {#Life}

*Image: Naval College.JPG.* Royal Naval College, Greenwich

Niven was born at [Peterhead](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterhead) in [Aberdeenshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeenshire), one of five notable mathematician brothers: [Charles](wikipedia://en/55913930) and James the best known. He graduated first from the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen),[^o4] then from [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he was 3rd [wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrangler_%28University_of_Cambridge%29) in 1866. He was elected a Fellow of his college.

In 1882 Niven became Director of Studies at the [Royal Naval College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Naval_College,_Greenwich), [Greenwich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greenwich), succeeding [Thomas Archer Hirst](wikipedia://en/14718360).[^o6] He was appointed a [Companion of the Order of the Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Companion_of_the_Order_of_the_Bath) (Civil division) in [Queen Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Victoria)'s [Diamond Jubilee Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1897_Diamond_Jubilee_Honours) of 1897. He retired in 1903, when he was [knighted](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight) by being appointed a [Knight Commander of the Order of the Bath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knight_Commander_of_the_Order_of_the_Bath).[^o6]

Niven was a colleague of [James Clerk Maxwell](wikipedia://en/28989696) (1831–1879), whose scientific papers he edited after his death. Among Niven's students was [Alfred North Whitehead](wikipedia://en/43395), to whom he taught mathematics, by instructing him in the physics of Maxwell.[^o7]

In retirement Niven lived at Eastburn, [Sidcup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sidcup), [Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent),[^o6] where he died in 1917.[^o4]

## Major publications {#Major_publications}

Niven edited works by J. C. Maxwell:

- 1881: [A Treatise on Electricity and Magnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Treatise_on_Electricity_and_Magnetism), 2nd edition
- 1890: [The Scientific Papers of James Clerk Maxwell](https://www.biodiversitylibrary.org/bibliography/19718#/summary) from [Biodiversity Heritage Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biodiversity_Heritage_Library)
