# Stanislas Ouaro

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stanislas Ouaro"}

**Stanislas Ouaro** (born 19 January 1975[^o4]) is a [Burkinabé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkina_Faso) politician and mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Stanislas Ouaro was born on 19 January 1975. He graduated with a doctor's degree from [University of Ouagadougou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ouagadougou) in 2001 with his thesis titled *Etude de problèmes elliptiques-paraboliques nonlinéaires en une dimension d'espace*.[^o4] Before he joined government, he was the president of University of Ouaga II since 2012.[^o5]

On 31 January 2018, he was appointed the Minister of National Education and Literacy, replacing [Jean-Martin Coulibaly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Martin_Coulibaly).[^o6] On 19 January 2019, he [resigned](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2019_Burkina_Faso_government_resignation) together with other members of [Thieba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Kaba_Thieba) cabinet.[^o7] On 24 January, he was appointed the Minister of National Education, Literacy and Promotion of National Languages.[^o8]

## Health {#Health}

During the [2020 coronavirus outbreak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Burkina_Faso), on 21 March, Ouaro contracted the [coronavirus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SARS-CoV-2).[^o10]
