# Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency"}

**Zimbabwe National Statistics Agency** (**ZIMSTAT**) is the [statistics agency](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_agency) of [Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe). It is headquartered in the Kaguvi Building in [Harare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harare).[^o3]

:::

Census and Statistics Act of 2007 created the agency.[^o4] It replaced the **Central Statistical Office** (**CSO**).[^o5] It was headquartered in the Kaguvi Building in Harare.[^o6]
