# Otto Grün

Otto Grün

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Otto Grün"}

**Otto Grün** was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was known primarily for his work in the theory of [finite groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_groups), which have often been praised for being foundational to further developments in the field.[^o3] He was born on June 26, 1888, in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o4] He obtained his Ph.D. from [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) in 1948 under the advisory of Hermann Ludwig Schmid.[^o5] He died in October 1974 on or before the 10th.[^o6]

:::
