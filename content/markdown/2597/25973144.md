::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 6-orthoplexes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 6-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [6-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/10083518).

:::

There are unique 6 degrees of rectifications, the zeroth being the [6-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/10083518), and the 6th and last being the [6-cube](wikipedia://en/10083462). Vertices of the rectified 6-orthoplex are located at the edge-centers of the 6-orthoplex. Vertices of the birectified 6-orthoplex are located in the triangular face centers of the 6-orthoplex.

### Construction {#Construction}

There are two [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) associated with the *rectified hexacross*, one with the C<sub>6</sub> or \[4,3,3,3,3\] Coxeter group, and a lower symmetry with two copies of pentacross facets, alternating, with the D<sub>6</sub> or \[3<sup>3,1,1</sup>\] Coxeter group.

:::

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a rectified hexacross, centered at the origin, edge length $\sqrt{2}\$ are all permutations of:

(±1,±1,0,0,0,0)

### Root vectors {#Root_vectors}

The 60 vertices represent the root vectors of the [simple Lie group](wikipedia://en/292831) D<sub>6</sub>. The vertices can be seen in 3 [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862), with the 15 vertices [rectified 5-simplices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_5-simplex) cells on opposite sides, and 30 vertices of an [expanded 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expanded_5-simplex) passing through the center. When combined with the 12 vertices of the 6-orthoplex, these vertices represent the 72 root vectors of the B<sub>6</sub> and C<sub>6</sub> simple [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).
