# Itai Benjamini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Itai Benjamini"}

**Itai Benjamini** (איתי בנימין) is an Israeli mathematician who holds the Renee and Jay Weiss Chair in the Department of Mathematics at the [Weizmann Institute of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weizmann_Institute_of_Science).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Benjamini completed his Ph.D. in 1992 at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), under the supervision of [Benjamin Weiss](wikipedia://en/40423267). His dissertation was entitled "[Random Walks](wikipedia://en/235451) on Graphs and Manifolds". In 2004 he won the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) for young [probability theorists](wikipedia://en/23542) "for his work across probability, including the analytic and geometric, particularly in the study of random processes associated with graphs". In the same year he also won the Morris L. Levinson Prize of the Weizmann Institute. He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010, speaking about "random planar metrics".B

## Career {#Career}

Benjamini was a long time collaborator of [Oded Schramm](wikipedia://en/9605580). Their joint works included papers on limits of [planar graphs](wikipedia://en/24314),BS noise sensitivity of [Boolean functions](wikipedia://en/753349)BKS1 and [first passage percolation](wikipedia://en/44370787)BKS2. With Olle Häggström, Benjamini edited the selected works of [Oded Schramm](wikipedia://en/9605580).S

Benjamini has also made contributions to the study of the [Biham–Middleton–Levine traffic model](wikipedia://en/30054770) AB and [isoperimetric inequalities](wikipedia://en/326182) on [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652)BC.
