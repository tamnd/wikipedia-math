# Herbert Clemens

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Herbert Clemens"}

**Charles Herbert Clemens** **Jr.** (born 15 August 1939)[^o3] is an American mathematician specializing in complex [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Clemens graduated from the [College of the Holy Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_the_Holy_Cross) in [Worcester, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester,_Massachusetts), with a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in mathematics in 1961. He then earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), under [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414). His doctoral dissertation was titled, *Picard–Lefschetz Theorem for Families of Algebraic Varieties Acquiring Certain Singularities*. In 1970, he became an assistant professor at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and went on to become an associate professor before leaving in 1975 to become an associate professor at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah), where he became a full professor in 1976 and a Distinguished Professor in 2001. In 2002, he left Utah to become a professor of mathematics and mathematics education at [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).[^o7]

Clemens was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from September 1968 to March 1970 and from September 2001 to June 2003.[^o8] He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in 1976 at Vancouver and in 1986 at Berkeley and gave a talk *Curves on higher dimensional complex projective manifolds*. For the academic year 1974–1975 he was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellow).

In 1972 Clemens and Griffiths proved that a cubic [three-fold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three-fold) is in general not a [rational variety](wikipedia://en/865939), providing an example for three dimensions that unirationality does not imply rationality. In 1986 Clemens was an editor of the [Pacific Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pacific_Journal_of_Mathematics).

He married in 1983 and has three children.
