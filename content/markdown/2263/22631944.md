# Peng Shige

[Peng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peng_%28surname%29)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peng Shige"}

**Peng Shige** (, born December 8, 1947, in [Binzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binzhou), [Shandong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong)) is a Chinese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) noted for his contributions in [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis) and [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Peng Shige was born in Binzhou and raised in Shandong, while his parents' hometown is [Haifeng County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifeng_County) in south-eastern [Guangdong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guangdong), he is a grandnephew of the famous revolutionary [Peng Pai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peng_Pai), and his grandfather (Peng Pai's brother) is also recognized a "revolutionary martyr" by [the nation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China).[^o4][^o5][^o6] He [went to a countryside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_to_the_countryside) working with farmers as an "[Educated youth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educated_youth)" from 1968 to 1971, and studied in the Department of Physics, [Shandong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University) from 1971 to 1974 and went to work at the Institute of Mathematics, Shandong University in 1978. In 1983 he took an opportunity to enter [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France), under the supervision of Alain Bensoussan, who was a student of [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500). He obtained his PhDs from Paris Dauphine University in 1985[^o7] and from [University of Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Provence) in 1986. Then he returned to China and did postdoctoral research at [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University) before becoming a professor at [Shandong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University) in 1990. In 1992 he was awarded the [Habilitation à Diriger des Recherches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate#Higher_doctorates) by the [University of Provence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Provence). He was promoted to Distinguished Professor of the Ministry of Education of China (Cheung Kong Scholarship Programme) in 1999.[^o5][^o6]

## Academic contributions {#Academic_contributions}

Professor Peng generalized the stochastic maximum principle in stochastic [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565). In a paper published in 1990 with [Étienne Pardoux](wikipedia://en/47085794), Peng founded the general theory (including nonlinear expectation) of [backward stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/72408357) (BSDEs), though linear BSDEs had been introduced by [Jean-Michel Bismut](wikipedia://en/18887796) in 1973.[^o9] Soon [Feynman–Kac](wikipedia://en/630017) type connections of BSDEs and certain kinds of elliptic and parabolic [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) (PDEs), e.g., [Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman equation](wikipedia://en/730585), were obtained, where the solutions of these PDEs can be interpreted in the classical or [viscosity](wikipedia://en/2300687) senses. As a particular case the solution of the [Black–Scholes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black–Scholes) equation can be represented as the solution of a simple linear BSDE, which can be regarded as a starting point of the BSDEs' applications in mathematical finance. A type of [nonlinear expectation](wikipedia://en/36678615), called the [g-expectation](wikipedia://en/36678177), was also derived from the theory of BSDEs. General theories of nonlinear expectations were developed later. These have various applications in [utility](wikipedia://en/45479) theory, and the theory of dynamic [risk measures](wikipedia://en/12498127).

## Honours {#Honours}

In 2005, Peng was elected as an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).

On August 24, 2010, as one of the invited speakers, he gave a one-hour plenary lecture[^o11] at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) at [Hyderabad, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad,_India).[^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15]

In 2011, he was appointed as "Global Scholars" for academic years 2011–2014 by [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), hosted by the university's departments of mathematics, operations research and financial engineering, and the Program in Applied and Computational Mathematics, as he "is a global leader in the field of probability theory and financial mathematics."[^o16][^o17][^o18]

In March 2015, as one of six or seven nominees, Peng was nominated for [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) by Norwegian mathematician [Bernt Øksendal](wikipedia://en/6782528).[^o9]

In 2017 and again in 2021, he was a laureate of the [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) by the *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*.[^o19]

In September 2020, he was awarded [Future Science Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_Science_Prize) in mathematics and computer science.[^o20]

In 2023, he was elected as a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o21][^o22]
