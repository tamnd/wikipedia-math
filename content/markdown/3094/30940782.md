# Karoubi conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karoubi conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Karoubi conjecture** was a conjecture by [Max Karoubi](wikipedia://en/30942178) that the algebraic and topological [K-theories](wikipedia://en/246748) coincide on [C\* algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*_algebra) spatially tensored with the algebra of compact operators.[^n1] It was proved by [Andrei Suslin](wikipedia://en/7111820) and [Mariusz Wodzicki](wikipedia://en/57858922).[^n2][^n3]

:::
