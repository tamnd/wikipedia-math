# Max Karoubi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max Karoubi"}

*Image: Karoubi Werner.jpg.* Max Karoubi (right) with [Wendelin Werner](wikipedia://en/6607797) (left) at [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) 2006 in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid)

**Max Karoubi** () is a French mathematician, [topologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologist), who works on [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), [cyclic homology](wikipedia://en/1460172) and [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917) and who founded the first [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846).

:::

In 1967, he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics (Doctorat d'État) from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), under the supervision of [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536) and [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042).

In 1973, he was nominated full professor at the [University of Paris 7-Denis Diderot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) until 2007. He is now an [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) there. In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]

Karoubi has supervised 12 Ph.D. students, including [Jean-Louis Loday](wikipedia://en/36183327) and [Christophe Soulé](wikipedia://en/32705286).
