# Francesco Galigai

*Image: Galigai, Francesco – Pratica d'arithmetica, 1552 – BEIC IE7264584.jpg.* *Pratica d'arithmetica* (1552)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francesco Galigai"}

**Francesco Galigai**, or **Ghaligai** ([Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence), 1498 – 1537), was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2][^o3]

:::

In 1521, he published *Summa de arithmetica*, a summary of contemporary knowledge on [equations](wikipedia://en/9284) of the first and second degree, including problems in [indeterminate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indeterminate_analysis), along with notes on the history and development of mathematics in Italy.[^o4]
