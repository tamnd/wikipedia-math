## Religion {#Religion}

An estimated 90.0% of South Americans are [Christians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christians)[^o17] (82% [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church), 8% other Christian denominations mainly traditional [Protestants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestantism) and [Evangelicals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evangelicalism) but also [Orthodoxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthodoxy)), accounting for ca. 19% of Christians worldwide.

[Crypto-Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crypto-Jews) or [Marranos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrano), [conversos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Converso), and [Anusim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anusim) were an important part of colonial life in Latin America.

Both Buenos Aires, Argentina and São Paulo, Brazil figure among the largest [Jewish populations by urban area](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_population_by_city).

Japanese Buddhism and [Shinto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinto)-derived [Japanese new religions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_new_religions) are common in Brazil and Peru. [Korean Confucianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Confucianism) is especially found in Brazil while Chinese Buddhism and Chinese Confucianism is spread throughout the continent.

[Kardecist Spiritism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kardecist_spiritism) can be found in several countries.

## Ethnicities {#Ethnicities}

The demographics of South America is composed of ancestries from [Indigenous peoples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples_of_the_Americas), [Africans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_people), [Europeans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Europeans), [Anusim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anusim) or [Marranos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marrano), and to a lesser extent [Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabs), [Romanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanis), and [East Asians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asians). A mixture of Amerindian and European ancestry is often referred to as [mestizo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mestizo) or caboclo/mameluco. [Castizos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Castizo) are people whose DNA is mostly European with Indigenous markers assimilation. A mixture of Amerindian and African ancestry is referred to in many South American countries as [zambo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambo) or cafuzo. A mixture of European and African ancestry is referred to as [mulatto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulatto). A mix of European and Japanese is referred as ainoko or [hafu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hafu). Chile administers [Easter Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_Island) in the South Pacific, which is home to 2,500 Polynesians, the Rapa Nui people.

South America is home to 400 million people, of which 180 million is composed of [Whites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/White_Latin_American) with several different European extractions, as well as people with other lineages including 17 million [Levantine Arabs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arab_diaspora), mostly Christians from the [Levant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levant) region, (found in Brazil, Argentina, Venezuela, Colombia and Chile), 440 thousand [Ashkenazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi) and [Sephardi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sephardi) Jews (mostly Argentina and Brazil, with both figuring among the top 10 largest [Jewish diasporas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish_diaspora)) and 1.3 million [Crypto-Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crypto-Jews) or [Anusim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anusim).

Brazil has 31 million Italians and Argentina 20, Uruguay, Venezuela, Peru, Chile, Colombia and Ecuador also have considerable Italian diasporas. Brazil has 15.7 million Germans, Austrians, Luxembourgers and Swiss (second largest German diaspora after US) and Argentina 3.5 million consisting mostly of Russian Volga Germans. Other German groups can be found in Chile, Bolivia (mostly [Mennonites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mennonites_in_Bolivia)), Paraguay (mostly German-Brazilians but including [Mennonites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mennonites_in_Paraguay)), Peru, Uruguay and colonies in Venezuela.

Brazil has the largest Slavic population with the number of [Poles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people)[^o25] reaching 3 million people, followed by [Ukrainians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians) and [Russians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians) but comprising many other nationalities. This is followed by Argentina, then Chile.

Brazil has the largest Dutch and Scandinavian (mostly [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegians)) populations as well as the largest [Lithuanian diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_diaspora) outside [Baltic states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltic_states). A small number of [Boers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boer) or White [Afrikaners](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrikaners) migrated to South America during the 20th century, heading especially to Argentina and Brazil. And Lithuanian refugees fleeing the Nazi invasion and Soviet annexation in WW2 also formed communities in Colombia and Venezuela. Brazil also comprises the largest [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians), Estonian and [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_people) diasporas, being followed by Argentina in terms of nationalities of [Uralic languages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uralic_languages). Argentina has the largest [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) and Irish populations, respectively 6 million and 1 million, while [Argentine Patagonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentine_Patagonia) is home to [Welsh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patagonian_Welsh) and [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_Argentine) colonies. Brazil and Chile are also home to considerable [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) ancestry populations. A great part of the French immigrants in Chile were of [Basque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basque_Chileans) descent, with the number of Basques estimated at 4 million. Chile has 800 thousand [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Chilean) and Irish people. Brazil and Argentina also have sizable [British Latin American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Latin_American) populations, being the responsible for the introduction of football in the 19th century.

The number of [Romani people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romani_people) revolves around 1.120 million, with a possibility of being much higher, being spread all over the continent. Most of the Romanis in Brazil are of Eastern European and Baltic background, while most of the Romani people in Argentina, Chile and Colombia came from Spain.

Most [East Indian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indo-Caribbeans) Hindus and Muslims live in Guyana and Suriname which is also home to a large Muslim [Javanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_people) community. East Indians are the vast majority in Guyana, followed by Blacks and also the majority in Suriname where they are followed by Maroons and Creoles. Indonesians from the island of Java make 13.7% of Suriname's population which figures as 275 thousand [Javanese people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Javanese_people). [Dutch Surinamese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Surinamese) or [Boeroes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boeroes) make the minority of the country due to the fact that most of the Dutch emigrated to the Netherlands after Suriname's independence.

South America is home to over 5 million [Asians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asians), mostly [East Asians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Asians), with Brazil and Peru having the largest [Asian-Latin American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian-Latin_American) populations. Peru and Venezuela have greatly numerous 19th century Chinese [coolie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coolie) populations with almost 1.9 million Chinese people combined. Peru itself is home to over 1.4 million [Chinese Peruvians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Peruvian), mostly arriving as [indentured servants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indentured_servant). The Asian-Brazilian population is mostly composed of Japanese descendants and Japanese nationals, but it also comprises over 250 thousand Chinese and 50 thousand [Taiwanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwanese_people) recent immigrants. Colombia, Argentina and Ecuador also follow in numbers of Chinese immigrants. Brazil is home to the largest [Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_Brazilian) and Korean populations in the continent with respectively 1.8 million and 140 thousand people. The Nipponic or Nipponese population in Brazil is the largest [Japanese diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_diaspora) outside Japan. Koreans can also be found in Argentina, Colombia, Paraguay and Chile. Peru is home to 160 thousand Japanese descendants, and Nipponese populations are also found in Argentina, Bolivia, Paraguay, Chile and Venezuela. Argentina is home to the largest [Filipino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filipino_people) population with 15 thousand people. Other Asian nationalities can be found in smaller numbers, such as South Asians or East Indians, Southeast Asians and Central Asians. Brazil has 5 thousand Vietnamese people, 10 thousand East Indians, as well as 5 thousand Afghans, and a small number of Iranians. Argentina also have small numbers of Southern Asians or East Indians, while being home to most Southeast Asians, mostly Filipino.

Brazil is home to the largest [Euro-Latin Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro-Latin_Americans) population in absolute numbers constituting 98 million people. Uruguay has the largest percentage with 90.7% being Caucasian or over 3 million people. Argentina equally corresponds to the second largest population and percentage with 39 million people, followed by Colombia with 18m, Venezuela 13.1m, Chile 9.5m, Peru 5.8m, Bolivia 2m, Paraguay 1.3m and Ecuador with 980 thousand.

Roughly 14% of the population in French Guiana is of European ancestry, numbering at 35 thousand people. The vast majority of these are of French heritage, though there are also people of Dutch, British, Spanish and Portuguese ancestry. Countries that make part of South America but do not have Romance languages as their official national languages are [Guyana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guyana), [Suriname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suriname) and the [Falkland Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falkland_Islands). Suriname has 2% of Europeans and Levantines or 12 thousand people, including Lebanese and Jews. Most Boeroes or Dutch Surinamese left after independence in 1975. There are less than 3 thousand Whites in Guyana where 0.5% of the population is counted as "others". The [Falkland Islands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falkland_Islands) have a low population density. According to the 2012 census, the average daily population of the Falklands was 2,932, excluding military personnel serving in the archipelago and their dependents. The Falklands are a homogeneous society, with the majority of inhabitants descended from Scottish and Welsh immigrants who settled the territory in 1833. Other groups are English, French, Gibraltarian, Scandinavian, Saint Helenian and Argentine.

[Afro-Latin Americans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afro-Latin_Americans) and Mulatto population are respectively 18 and 48 million, combined 66 million.

### Indigenous people {#Indigenous_people}

*Image: SouthAmerican families.png.* Main indigenous [language families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_languages_of_South_America) of South America (except Quechua, Aymaran, and Mapuche).

*Image: Map-Most Widely Spoken Native Languages in Latin America.png.* }

Indigenous people make up about half of the population of [Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivia). In many places indigenous people still practice a traditional lifestyle based on [subsistence agriculture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subsistence_agriculture) or as hunter-gatherers. There are still some uncontacted tribes residing in the [Amazon rainforest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_rainforest).

#### Argentina {#Argentina}

[Argentina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argentina)'s indigenous population in 2010 was about 900,329 (2.38% of total population); this figure includes 457,363 people who self-identified as belonging to an indigenous ethnic group, and the remaining 142,966 who recognized themselves as first-generation descendants of an Amerindian people.[^o28] The ten most populous indigenous people are the [Mapuche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapuche) (113,680 people), the [Kolla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolla_people) (70,505), the [Toba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toba_people) (69,452) and the [Guaraní](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guaraní_people) (68,454).

#### Bolivia {#Bolivia}

In [Bolivia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolivia), a 62% majority of residents over the age of 15 self-identify as belonging to an indigenous people, while another 3.7% grew up with an indigenous mother tongue yet do not self-identify as indigenous.[^o30] Including both of these categories, and children under 15, some 66.4% of Bolivia's population was registered as indigenous in the 2001 Census.[^o31] The largest indigenous ethnic groups are: [Quechua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quechuas) (about 2,500,000 people), [Aymara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aymara_people) (2,000,000), [Chiquitano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiquitano) (181.000), [Guaraní](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guarani_people) (126.000) and [Mojeño](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mojeño) (69,000).

#### Brazil {#Brazil}

The Amerindians make up 0.4% of [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil)'s population, or about 700,000 people.[^o33][^o34] Indigenous people are found in the entire territory of Brazil, although the majority of them live in [indigenous territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_territory_%28Brazil%29) in the North and Centre-Western part of the country. On 18 January 2007, [FUNAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundação_Nacional_do_Índio) reported that it had confirmed the presence of 67 different [uncontacted tribes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uncontacted_peoples) in Brazil, up from 40 in 2005. With this addition Brazil has now overtaken the island of [New Guinea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Guinea) as the country having the largest number of uncontacted tribes.[^o35]

#### Chile {#Chile}

According to the 2002 Census, 4.6% of the Chilean population (including the [Rapanui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rapanui) of [Easter Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Easter_Island)) 692,000 people were self-identified of indigenous origins.[^o37] Many are descendants of the [Mapuche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapuche), and live in [Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago,_Chile), [Araucanía](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Araucanía_Region) and the lake district. Other groups include the [Aimara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aymara_people) who live mainly in [Arica-Parinacota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arica-Parinacota_Region) and [Tarapacá Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tarapacá_Region) and has the mayority of their alikes living in Bolivia and Peru and the [Alacalufe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alacalufe) survivors who now reside mainly in [Puerto Edén](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puerto_Edén).

#### Colombia {#Colombia}

Colombia's indigenous people nonetheless encompass at least 85 distinct cultures and more than 1,378,884 people.[^o39][^o40] A variety of [collective rights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_rights) for indigenous people are recognized in the 1991 Constitution. One of these is the [Muisca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muisca_people) culture, a subset of the larger [Chibcha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chibcha) [ethnic group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnic_group), famous for their use of [gold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold), which led to the legend of *[El Dorado](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/El_Dorado)*.

#### Ecuador {#Ecuador}

At the present the 25% of Ecuador's population is of indigenous heritage. Approximately 96.4% of Ecuador's Indigenous population are Highland Quichuas living in the valleys of the Sierra region. Primarily consisting of the descendants of Incans, they are [Kichwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kichwa) speakers and include the Caranqui, the [Otavaleños](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otavaleño), the Cayambi, the Quitu-Caras, the [Panzaleo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panzaleo), the Chimbuelo, the Salasacan, the Tugua, the Puruhá, the [Cañari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cañari), and the Saraguro.

#### Peru {#Peru}

Indigenous population in Peru make up around 25%. Native Peruvian traditions and customs have shaped the way Peruvians live and see themselves today. Cultural citizenship—or what Renato Rosaldo has called, "the right to be different and to belong, in a democratic, participatory sense" (1996:243)—is not yet very well developed in Peru. This is perhaps no more apparent than in the country's Amazonian regions where indigenous societies continue to struggle against state-sponsored economic abuses, cultural discrimination, and pervasive violence.[^o43]

#### Venezuela {#Venezuela}

Indigenous population in Venezuela form about 2% of the total population,[^o45] although many Venezuelans share some indigenous ancestry. Indigenous people are concentrated in the Southern Amazon rainforest state of Amazonas, where they make up nearly 50% of the population and in the Andes of the western state of Zulia. The most numerous indigenous people, at about 200,000, is the Venezuelan part of the Wayuu (or Guajiro) people who primarily live in Zulia between Lake Maracaibo and the Colombian border. Another 100,000 or so indigenous people live in the sparsely populated southeastern states of Amazonas, Bolívar, and Delta Amacuro.
