# Hideki Imai

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hideki Imai"}

**Hideki Imai** is a Japanese [information theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_theorist) and cryptographer. His research has included information theory, [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869), cryptography, and information security.[^o3][^o4]

:::

In 1977, together with S. Hirakawa, he proposed a coded multilevel signal modulation scheme using several classes of binary error-correcting codes, whose symbols are combined to set up the transmission signal. This scheme is known as the Imai–Hirakawa code.[^o5]

Imai received B.E., M.E., and Ph.D. degrees in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in 1966, 1968, and 1971, respectively. From 1971 to 1992 he was on the faculty of [Yokohama National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokohama_National_University), and from 1992 to 2006 he was a professor at the Institute of Industrial Science, University of Tokyo. In 2006 he was appointed emeritus professor of the University of Tokyo and professor at [Chuo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chuo_University). He also served as director of the Research Center for Information Security at the [National Institute of Advanced Industrial Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Advanced_Industrial_Science_and_Technology).[^o3]

He became an [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE) Fellow in 1992 for contributions to the theory of coded modulation and two-dimensional codes,[^o6] and an [IACR](wikipedia://en/87043) Fellow in 2007.[^o7]
