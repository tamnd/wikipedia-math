# Wilfried Imrich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilfried Imrich"}

**Wilfried Imrich** (born 25 May 1941) is an Austrian mathematician working mainly in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). He is known for his work on [graph products](wikipedia://en/11492935), and authored the books *Product Graphs: Structure and Recognition* (Wiley, 2000, with [Sandi Klavžar](wikipedia://en/40076007)),[^o2] *Topics in graph theory: Graphs and their Cartesian Products* (AK Peters, 2008, with Klavžar and Douglas F. Rall),[^o3] and *Handbook of Product Graphs* (2nd ed., CRC, 2011, with Klavžar and Richard Hammack).[^o4]

:::

Imrich earned his doctorate from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 1965, under the joint supervision of [Nikolaus Hofreiter](wikipedia://en/22209837) and [Edmund Hlawka](wikipedia://en/5442007). He has worked as a researcher for IBM in Vienna, as an assistant professor at [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) and the [University at Albany, SUNY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Albany,_SUNY), as a postdoctoral researcher at [Lomonosov University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_University), and, since 1973, as a full professor at the [University of Leoben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leoben) in Austria.[^o7] He retired in 2009, becoming a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at Leoben.[^o6] He is on board of advisors of the journal *[Ars Mathematica Contemporanea](wikipedia://en/19165561)*. Since 2012 he has been a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o7]
