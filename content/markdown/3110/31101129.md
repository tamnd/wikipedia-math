# Benktander type II distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Benktander type II distribution"}

The **Benktander type II distribution**, also called the Benktander distribution of the second kind, is one of two distributions introduced by Gunnar (txt) to model heavy-tailed losses commonly found in non-life/casualty [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373), using various forms of mean excess functions (Benktander Segerdahl 1960). This distribution is "close" to the [Weibull distribution](wikipedia://en/206948) (Kleiber Kotz 2003).

:::
