# Fatiha Alabau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fatiha Alabau"}

**Fatiha Alabau-Boussouira** (born 1961) is a French applied mathematician specializing in the [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). She is affiliated with the Laboratoire Jacques-Louis Lions of [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University) as an external member, a professor at the [University of Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lorraine) in the mathematics department of its Metz campus, and a former president of the [Société de Mathématiques Appliquées et Industrielles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Société_de_Mathématiques_Appliquées_et_Industrielles), a French society for [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Alabau was born 27 August 1961 in [Montmorency, Val-d'Oise](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montmorency,_Val-d'Oise). She earned a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) in 1984 at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), where she defended her doctoral thesis in 1987 under the supervision of [Roland Glowinski](wikipedia://en/20952046).

After postdoctoral research as a visiting assistant professor at [Arizona State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona_State_University), she became [maître de conferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conferences) at the [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1) in 1988, and earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 1996. She became a professor at [Louis Pasteur University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Pasteur_University) in 1997, and moved to [Paul Verlaine University – Metz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Verlaine_University_–_Metz) (which later became part of the University of Lorraine) in 1999.

## Service and recognition {#Service_and_recognition}

Alabau was president of the Société de Mathématiques Appliquées et Industrielles from 2014 to 2017.
