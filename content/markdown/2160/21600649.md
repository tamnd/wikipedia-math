# Dudley's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dudley's theorem"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Dudley's theorem** is a result relating the [expected](wikipedia://en/9653) [upper bound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_bound) and [regularity properties](wikipedia://en/12442391) of a [Gaussian process](wikipedia://en/302944) to its [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891) and [covariance](wikipedia://en/157059) structure.

:::

## History {#History}

The result was first stated and proved by V. N. Sudakov, as pointed out in a paper by [Richard M. Dudley](wikipedia://en/3824977).[^o3] Dudley had earlier credited [Volker Strassen](wikipedia://en/771960) with making the connection between entropy and regularity.

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let (*X*<sub>*t*</sub>)<sub>*t*∈*T*</sub> be a Gaussian process centered (with mean zero) and let *d*<sub>*X*</sub> be the [pseudometric](wikipedia://en/68503) on *T* defined by

$$d_{X}(s, t) = \sqrt{\mathbf{E} \big[ | X_{s} - X_{t} |^{2} ]}. \,$$

For *ε* > 0, denote by *N*(*T*, *d*<sub>*X*</sub>; *ε*) the [entropy number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropy_number), i.e. the minimal number of (open) *d*<sub>*X*</sub>-balls of radius *ε* required to cover *T*. Then

$$\mathbf{E} \left[ \sup_{t \in T} X_{t} \right] \leq 24 \int_0^{+\infty} \sqrt{\log N(T, d_{X}; \varepsilon)} \, \mathrm{d} \varepsilon.$$

Furthermore, if the entropy integral on the right-hand side converges, then *X* has a version with almost all sample path bounded and (uniformly) continuous on (*T*, *d*<sub>*X*</sub>).

:::
