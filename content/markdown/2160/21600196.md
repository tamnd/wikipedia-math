# B-convex space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="B-convex space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the class of ***B*-convex spaces** is a class of [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989). The concept of *B*-convexity was defined and used to characterize Banach spaces that have the [strong law of large numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_law_of_large_numbers) by Anatole Beck in 1962; accordingly, "B-convexity" is understood as an abbreviation of **Beck convexity**. Beck proved the following theorem: A Banach space is *B*-convex [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) every sequence of [independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_independence), symmetric, uniformly bounded and [Radon random variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon_random_variable) in that space satisfies the strong law of large numbers.

:::

Let *X* be a Banach space with [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) || ||. *X* is said to be ***B*-convex** if for some *ε* > 0 and some [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) *n*, it holds true that whenever *x*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *x*<sub>*n*</sub> are elements of the [closed unit ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_unit_ball) of *X*, there is a choice of signs *α*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *α*<sub>*n*</sub> ∈ {−1, +1} such that

$$\left\| \sum_{i = 1}^{n} \alpha_{i} x_{i} \right\| \leq (1 - \varepsilon) n.$$

Later authors have shown that B-convexity is equivalent to a number of other important properties in the theory of Banach spaces. Being **B-convex** and having **Rademacher type** $p>1$ were shown to be equivalent Banach-space properties by [Gilles Pisier](wikipedia://en/26438577).
