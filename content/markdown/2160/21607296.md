# Discretization of continuous features

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discretization of continuous features"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), **discretization** refers to the process of converting or partitioning continuous [attributes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable_%28statistics%29#Applied_statistics), [features](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Features_%28pattern_recognition%29) or [variables](wikipedia://en/437701) to discretized or [nominal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nominal_data) attributes/features/variables/[intervals](wikipedia://en/49172). This can be useful when creating probability mass functions – formally, in [density estimation](wikipedia://en/554671). It is a form of [discretization](wikipedia://en/330017) in general and also of [binning](wikipedia://en/18614570), as in making a [histogram](wikipedia://en/13266). Whenever [continuous](wikipedia://en/6122) data is discretized, there is always some amount of [discretization error](wikipedia://en/516070). The goal is to reduce the amount to a level considered negligible for the [modeling](wikipedia://en/2381958) purposes at hand.

:::

Typically data is discretized into partitions of *K* equal lengths/width (equal intervals) or K% of the total data (equal frequencies).[^o2]

Mechanisms for discretizing continuous data include [Fayyad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usama_Fayyad) & Irani's MDL method,[^o3] which uses [mutual information](wikipedia://en/427282) to recursively define the best bins, CAIM, CACC, Ameva, and many others[^o4]

Many machine learning algorithms are known to produce better models by discretizing continuous attributes.[^o5]

## Software {#Software}

This is a partial list of software that implement MDL algorithm.

- [discretize4crf](https://gforge.inria.fr/projects/discretize4crf) tool designed to work with popular [CRF](wikipedia://en/4118276) implementations ([C++](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C++))
- [mdlp](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/discretization/discretization.pdf) in the R package discretization
- [Discretize](https://cran.r-project.org/web/packages/RWeka/RWeka.pdf) in the R package RWeka
