# Jared Wunsch

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jared Wunsch"}

**Jared Wunsch** is an American mathematician working in the areas of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [microlocal analysis](wikipedia://en/6108841), [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He is currently a professor of mathematics at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wunsch attended [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), obtaining his A.B. in mathematics with a senior thesis under the supervision of [Elias M. Stein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias_Stein) in 1993. He received his PhD from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) under the supervision of [Richard B. Melrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burt_Melrose) in 1998.[^o5] Following his postdoctoral appointment at the [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), he was an assistant professor at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) from 2000 to 2002. After this appointment, he joined [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) and became tenured in 2009. He served as department chair from 2012 to 2015.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Wunsch was elected a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013,[^o7] and, along with [Bryna Kra](wikipedia://en/39522217), he was awarded the Simons Fellowship in mathematics by the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) in 2021.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

The main feature of Wunsch's work is the application of tools from microlocal analysis to problems in Schrödinger and wave equations, as well as numerical analysis. He analyzed the propagation of singularities for solutions of wave equation on conic manifolds with [Richard B. Melrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burt_Melrose)[^o10] and later on edge manifolds with [Richard B. Melrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Burt_Melrose) and [András Vasy](wikipedia://en/52321031).[^o11] His most cited results include his work on resolvent estimates on hyperbolic trapped sets with [Maciej Zworski](wikipedia://en/34110829)[^o12] and his work on sharp Strichartz estimates on conic manifolds with Andrew Hassell and [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498).[^o13]
