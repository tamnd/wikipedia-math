# Tarski monster group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tarski monster group"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **Tarski monster group**, named for [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920), is an infinite [group](wikipedia://en/19447) such that every proper subgroup, other than the identity subgroup, is a [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) of order a fixed [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) *p*. A Tarski monster group is necessarily [simple](wikipedia://en/47600). It was shown by [Alexander Yu. Olshanskii](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Olshansky) in 1979 that Tarski groups exist, and that there is a Tarski [*p*-group](wikipedia://en/24664) for every prime *p* > 10<sup>75</sup>. They are a source of [counterexamples](wikipedia://en/143151) to conjectures in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), most importantly to [Burnside's problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burnside's_problem) and the [von Neumann conjecture](wikipedia://en/662256).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A Tarski group is an [infinite group](wikipedia://en/4587030) such that all [proper subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_subgroup) have prime power order. Such a group is then a Tarski monster group if there is a [prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime) $p$ such that every non-trivial proper subgroup has [order](wikipedia://en/493527) $p$.[^o4]

An extended Tarski group is a group $G$ that has a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) $N$ whose [quotient group](wikipedia://en/11526) $G/N$ is a Tarski group, and any subgroup $H$ is either contained in or contains $N$.[^o4]

A Tarski Super Monster (or TSM) is an infinite simple group such that all proper subgroups are [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974), and is more generally called a Perfect Tarski Super Monster when the group is [perfect](wikipedia://en/585271) instead of simple. There are TSM groups which are not Tarski monsters.[^o5]

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

As every group of prime order is [cyclic](wikipedia://en/52327), every proper subgroup of a Tarski monster group is cyclic.[^o4] As a consequence, the intersection of any two different proper subgroups of a Tarski monster group must be the [trivial group](wikipedia://en/193059).[^o4]

- Every Tarski monster group is generated by any two elements such that neither is a power of the other, and two such elements do not commute.
- If $G$ is a Tarski monster group, then $G$ is simple. If $N\trianglelefteq G$ and $U\leq G$ is any subgroup distinct from $N$ the subgroup $NU$ would have $p^2$ elements.
- The construction of Olshanskii shows in fact that there are [continuum-many](wikipedia://en/24758677) non-isomorphic Tarski Monster groups for each prime $p>10^{75}$.
- Tarski monster groups are examples of non-[amenable groups](wikipedia://en/657430) not containing any [free subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_subgroup).
