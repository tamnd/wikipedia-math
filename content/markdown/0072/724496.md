# Stéphane Mallat

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stéphane Mallat"}

**Stéphane Georges Mallat** (born 24 October 1962) is a French applied mathematician, concurrently appointed as Professor at [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France) and [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure). He made fundamental contributions to the development of [wavelet](wikipedia://en/50903) theory in the late 1980s and early 1990s. He has additionally done work in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324), [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music) synthesis and [image segmentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_segmentation).

:::

With [Yves Meyer](wikipedia://en/2096465), he developed the [multiresolution analysis](wikipedia://en/1963076) (MRA) construction for compactly supported wavelets. His MRA wavelet construction made the implementation of wavelets practical for [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering) applications by demonstrating the equivalence of wavelet [bases](wikipedia://en/18420) and conjugate mirror [filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filter_%28signal_processing%29) used in [discrete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_signal), multirate [filter banks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filter_bank) in [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324). He also developed (with Sifen Zhong) the [wavelet transform modulus maxima method](wikipedia://en/31477634) for image characterization, a method that uses the local maxima of the wavelet coefficients at various scales to reconstruct images.

He introduced the scattering transform that constructs invariance for object recognition purposes. Mallat is the author of *A Wavelet Tour of Signal Processing* (1999; ), a text widely used in applied mathematics and engineering courses.

He has held teaching positions at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique) and at the [Ecole normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_normale_supérieure).[^o3] He is currently Professor of Data Science at [College de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_de_France).[^o4]

In 1998 he was a plenary speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o5]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2025 he received the [CNRS Gold Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Gold_Medal).[^o7] In 2025, he was considered a candidate for a [Nobel Prize in Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Physics) by the [Clarivate Citation Laureates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clarivate_Citation_Laureates).[^o8]
