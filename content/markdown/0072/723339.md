::: {#o9 .subject cue=lead name="Deltoidal icositetrahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **deltoidal icositetrahedron** (or **trapezoidal icositetrahedron**, **tetragonal icosikaitetrahedron**,[^o10] **tetragonal trisoctahedron**,[^o11] **strombic icositetrahedron**) is a [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393).

:::

## Description {#Description}

*Image: Deltoidal icositetrahedron.stl.* 3D model of a deltoidal icositetrahedron

A deltoidal icositetrahedron is a [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393) with 24 sides that are [kites](wikipedia://en/84840). All of its faces are [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330), each has three interior angles approximately 81.6 degrees and one angle 115.3 degrees. The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) between every two kites is 138.1 degrees. The deltoidal icositetrahedron has 48 edges, and 26 vertices – eight vertices surrounded by three kites and eighteen vertices by four kites. Its [dual polyhedron](wikipedia://en/8815) is the [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213), an [Archimedean solid](wikipedia://en/1847).[^o13] Deltoidal icositetrahedron and [deltoidal hexecontahedron](wikipedia://en/724753) are two Catalan solids with kite faces only.[^n1]

## Dimensions and angles {#Dimensions_and_angles}

### Dimensions {#Dimensions}

The deltoidal icositetrahedron with long body diagonal length *D* = 2 has:

- short body diagonal length:

$$d = \frac{2 \sqrt{3} \left( 2 \sqrt{2} + 1 \right) }{7} \approx 1.894\,580 ;$$

- long edge length:[^o17]

$$S = \sqrt{2 - \sqrt{2}} \approx 0.765\,367 ;$$

- short edge length:[^o17]

$$s = \frac{ \sqrt{20 - 2 \sqrt{2}} }{7} \approx 0.591\,980 ;$$

- inradius:[^o17]

$$r = \sqrt{ \frac{7 + 4 \sqrt{2}}{17} } \approx 0.862\,856 .$$

$r$ is the distance from the center to any face plane; it may be calculated by [normalizing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29#Operations##Distance_from_a_point_to_a_plane) the equation of plane [above](wikipedia://en/723339#Cartesian_coordinates), replacing (*x*, *y*, *z*) with (0, 0, 0), and taking the [absolute value](wikipedia://en/991) of the result.

A deltoidal icositetrahedron has its long and short edges in the ratio:

$$\frac{ S }{ s } = 2 - \frac{1}{ \sqrt{2} } \approx 1.292\,893 .$$

The deltoidal icositetrahedron with short edge length $s$ has:

- area:[^o17]

$$A = 6 \sqrt{29 - 2 \sqrt{2}}\,s ^{2} ;$$

- volume:[^o17]

$$V = \sqrt{122 + 71 \sqrt{2}}\,s ^{3} .$$

### Side Lengths {#Side_Lengths}

In a deltoidal icositetrahedron, each face is a kite-shaped quadrilateral. The side lengths of these kites can be expressed in the ratio 0.7731900694928638:1. Specifically, the side adjacent to the obtuse angle has a length of approximately 0.707106785, while the side adjacent to the acute angle has a length of approximately 0.914213565.

## Occurrences in nature {#Occurrences_in_nature}

The deltoidal icositetrahedron is a [crystal habit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_habit) often formed by the mineral [analcime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analcime) and occasionally [garnet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Garnet). The shape is often called a trapezohedron in mineral contexts, although in [solid geometry](wikipedia://en/507960) the name [trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/719845) has another meaning.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

The deltoidal icositetrahedron's projection onto a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) divides its squares into quadrants. The projection onto a regular [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) divides its equilateral triangles into kite faces. In [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705) this represents an *ortho* operation to a cube or octahedron.

The deltoidal icositetrahedron (dual of the [small rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213)) is tightly related to the [disdyakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/724752) (dual of the [great rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/285879)). The main difference is that the latter also has edges between the vertices on 3- and 4-fold symmetry axes (between yellow and red vertices in the images below).

### Dyakis dodecahedron {#Dyakis_dodecahedron}

A variant with [pyritohedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyritohedral_symmetry) is called a **[dyakis dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/75464679)**[^o24][^o25] or **diploid**.[^o26] It is common in [crystallography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystallography). A dyakis dodecahedron can be created by enlarging 24 of the 48 faces of a disdyakis dodecahedron. A [tetartoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetartoid) can be created by enlarging 12 of the 24 faces of a dyakis dodecahedron.
