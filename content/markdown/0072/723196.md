# Pentakis dodecahedron

Semireg dual polyhedron stat table

*Image: Pentakis dodecahedron.stl.* 3D model of a pentakis dodecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentakis dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **pentakis dodecahedron** or **kisdodecahedron** is a polyhedron created by attaching a [pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128461) to each face of a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361); that is, it is the [Kleetope](wikipedia://en/31165912) of the dodecahedron. Specifically, the term typically refers to a particular [Catalan solid](wikipedia://en/564393), namely the [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of a [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

Let $\phi$ be the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386). The 12 points given by $(0, \pm 1, \pm \phi)$ and cyclic permutations of these coordinates are the vertices of a [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968). Its dual [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361), whose edges intersect those of the icosahedron at right angles, has as vertices the points $(\pm 1, \pm 1, \pm 1)$ together with the points $(\pm\phi, \pm 1/\phi, 0)$ and cyclic permutations of these coordinates. Multiplying all coordinates of the icosahedron by a factor of $(3\phi+12)/19\approx 0.887\,057\,998\,22$ gives a slightly smaller icosahedron. The 12 vertices of this icosahedron, together with the vertices of the dodecahedron, are the vertices of a pentakis dodecahedron centered at the origin. The length of its long edges equals $2/\phi$. Its faces are acute isosceles triangles with one angle of $\arccos((-8+9\phi)/18)\approx 68.618\,720\,931\,19^{\circ}$ and two of $\arccos((5-\phi)/6)\approx 55.690\,639\,534\,41^{\circ}$. The length ratio between the long and short edges of these triangles equals $(5-\phi)/3\approx 1.127\,322\,003\,75$.

## Chemistry {#Chemistry}

*Image: C60-cpk.png.*

The *pentakis dodecahedron* in a model of [buckminsterfullerene](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckminsterfullerene): each (spherical) surface segment represents a [carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon) [atom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atom), and if all are replaced with planar faces, a pentakis dodecahedron is produced. Equivalently, a truncated icosahedron is a model of buckminsterfullerene, with each vertex representing a carbon atom.

## Concave pentakis dodecahedron {#Concave_pentakis_dodecahedron}

A **concave pentakis dodecahedron** replaces the pentagonal faces of a dodecahedron with *inverted* pyramids.

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

The faces of a regular dodecahedron may be replaced (or augmented with) any regular pentagonal pyramid to produce what is in general referred to as an **elevated dodecahedron**. For example, if pentagonal pyramids with equilateral triangles are used, the result is a non-[convex](wikipedia://en/1817885) [deltahedron](wikipedia://en/493995). Any such elevated dodecahedron has the same combinatorial structure as a pentakis dodecahedron, i.e., the same [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595).

- In [Doctor Atomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Atomic), the shape of the first atomic bomb detonated in [New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Mexico) was a pentakis dodecahedron.<https://www.scribd.com/doc/7817182/Doctor-Atomic-Libretto>

- Some Geodomes in which people play on are Pentakis Dodecahedra, or at least elevated dodecahedra.
