# Gregory G. Rose

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gregory G. Rose"}

**Gregory G. "Greg" Rose** (born July 15, 1955, in [Sydney, Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney)) was a senior vice president of technology for [Qualcomm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qualcomm).[^o2][^o3]

:::

Rose is noted for designing the [SOBER](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SOBER) family of [stream ciphers](wikipedia://en/49007) for wireless telephony.[^o4] Together with Philip Hawkes, he also designed [Turing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_%28cipher%29), a cipher system based on the SOBER-t32.[^o5] It was developed to address encryption issues, particularly the limitations to processing power, program space, and memory present in software encryption algorithms.[^o6]
