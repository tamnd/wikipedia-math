# Sergio Campanato

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sergio Campanato"}

**Sergio Campanato** (17 February 1930 – 1 March 2005) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who studied the theory of regularity for elliptic and parabolic [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).

:::

## Career {#Career}

He graduated in mathematics and physics at the University of Modena in the academic year 1952/54 with a thesis relating to the [heat equation](wikipedia://en/166415).[^o4] In 1956, he became an assistant to [Enrico Magenes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enrico_Magenes), with whom he worked on a problem of [Picone](wikipedia://en/10162009) relating to the equilibrium state of an elastic body, and on other differential equations related to [electrostatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrostatics).

In 1964, he moved to the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) at the invitation of [Alessandro Faedo](wikipedia://en/41893091), joining a group of mathematicians which included [Aldo Andreotti](wikipedia://en/34935909), [Jacopo Barsotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacopo_Barsotti), [Enrico Bombieri](wikipedia://en/10475), [Gianfranco Capriz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gianfranco_Capriz), [Ennio De Giorgi](wikipedia://en/1752401), [Giovanni Prodi](wikipedia://en/62908347), [Edoardo Vesentini](wikipedia://en/34938661), and [Guido Stampacchia](wikipedia://en/4872667), with whom Campanato collaborated fruitfully.[^o5]

From 1975 until 2000 he taught Nonlinear Analysis at the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa). He died in Pisa on 1 March 2005.

## Honors {#Honors}

- In 1985, the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei) awarded him the "Premio Linceo" prize for his work on the regularity of nonlinear problems as relating to his eponymous [Morrey–Campanato spaces](wikipedia://en/34070699).[^o7][^o8]
- In 2000, a conference was held in honor of his 70th birthday at SNS Pisa.
- In 2006, there was a conference held to commemorate his work at [Erice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erice), [Sicily](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sicily).[^o9]
