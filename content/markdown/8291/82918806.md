# Ian F. Putnam

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ian F. Putnam"}

**Ian Fraser Putnam** is a mathematician whose research focus on the intersection between [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), including [C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184) and [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748). He is a professor at the [University of Victoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Victoria).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He obtained his PhD at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1985, with [Marc Aristide Rieffel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marc_Rieffel) as advisor (thesis *The C\*-algebras Associated with Denjoy Homeomorphisms*).

## Honours {#Honours}

- [Israel Halperin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Halperin_Prize) in 1990.[^o8]
- [Aisenstadt Prize](wikipedia://en/8534576) in 1993.[^o9]
- Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada).[^o10]
- Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) since 2013 (inaugural class).[^o11]
