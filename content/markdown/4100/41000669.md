# Dionigi Galletto

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dionigi Galletto"}

**Dionigi Galletto** (26 January 1932 – 25 September 2011) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [academician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academician).

:::

He is known for his work on [rigid body mechanics](wikipedia://en/481856), on the mathematical theory of elasticity (including both [linear elasticity](wikipedia://en/301429) and [finite strain theory](wikipedia://en/2210759)), on the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) and on [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology) and extragalactic [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182): in particular he is considered one of the founders of the latter branch of cosmology.[^o5]

He was professor of mathematical physics at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin):[^o6] as such, he is considered to be the founder–reorganiser of the [Mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) school of Turin in the Post–[Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War) period.[^o7] Among his students was [Mauro Francaviglia](wikipedia://en/39983909).

## Biography {#Biography}

He started his university studies in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome) as a student of [Severi](wikipedia://en/1565716): however his studies were interrupted due to the military service, which led him to [Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padua).[^o9] There he graduated with honours in 1960 under [Giuseppe Grioli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Grioli)'s guidance, with a thesis on the [continuum theory with asymmetric stress](wikipedia://en/5918): from 1961 to 1968 he worked in Padua as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor),[^o9][^o10] holding also courses on [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) as a [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer).[^o11]

In 1968, having won a competitive examination for a chair in [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586), he was appointed [extraordinary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraordinary_professor) of [rational mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586) at the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo):[^o12] there, Galletto held also courses of mathematical methods for physicists as a lecturer.[^o11] In 1970 he moved to the University of Turin, working at the Faculty of Mathematics, Physics and Natural Sciences again as extraordinary professor of [higher mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_mechanics):[^o13] in 1971 he became [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) of mathematical physics, a position he held up to 2007, when he retired. Besides the course of mathematical physics, Galletto held various courses relating to the area of his scientific interest: for example he held courses on astronomy as a lecturer.[^o11] In 2008 he was appointed [Emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor).

### Honors {#Honors}

In 1965 he was awarded the [Ottorino Pomini prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottorino_Pomini_prize) by the [Unione Matematica Italiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unione_Matematica_Italiana), jointly with [Giuseppe Geymonat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Geymonat) and [Mario Miranda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mario_Miranda_%28mathematician%29): the judging commission was composed by [Dario Graffi](wikipedia://en/43118683) (as the president), [Giuseppe Grioli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Grioli), [Ennio De Giorgi](wikipedia://en/1752401) and [Enzo Martinelli](wikipedia://en/30265224) (as the secretary).[^o3]

He was [corresponding member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corresponding_member) of the [Accademia delle Scienze di Torino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_delle_Scienze_di_Torino) since 1974, and became national resident member in 1980.[^o15][^o10][^o16]

In 1979 he was elected corresponding member of the [Accademia Nazionale di Scienze, Lettere e Arti di Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Nazionale_di_Scienze,_Lettere_e_Arti_di_Modena):[^o15] later on, he became effective member in 1984 and emeritus member in 2002.[^o11] He was also corresponding member of the [Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Lombardo_Accademia_di_Scienze_e_Lettere).[^o15]

Since 1980 he was corresponding member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei), and was elected national member in 1990.[^o17][^o15][^o10] In 1989, the same academy awarded him the "*[Prize of the Minister of Heritage and Cultural Activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Cultural_Heritage_and_Activities_%28Italy%29)*" for his work in mathematics and mechanics.[^o18][^o15]

On November 2, 2006 he was elected member of the [Serbian Academy of Sciences and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serbian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Arts),[^o15][^o19] and few months later, on 7 March 2007, he was elected corresponding non resident member to the class of mathematical sciences of the [Società Nazionale di Scienze Lettere e Arti in Napoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Società_Nazionale_di_Scienze_Lettere_e_Arti_in_Napoli).[^o15][^o20]

## Work {#Work}

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

During his career, he published more than 150 papers in Italian and international journals.[^o9] The beginning of his scientific production was under Grioli's scientific influence: Galletto worked on the topic of his [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) thesis, i.e. on the theory of continua having asymmetric stress characteristics. However, soon he followed his independent research path, with pioneering works that forerun the so called generalised continuum theory. In this theory, among other things, the stress tensor considered is no longer the [Cauchy tensor](wikipedia://en/3323565) or any similar, double [symmetric tensor](wikipedia://en/23087738), but an asymmetric tensor that generalizes it: and this gives to the theory many further important structural peculiarities.
