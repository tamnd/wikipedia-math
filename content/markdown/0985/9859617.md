# Carl Jockusch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Jockusch"}

**Carl Groos Jockusch Jr.** (born July 13, 1941, in [San Antonio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Antonio), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas)) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] He graduated from [Alamo Heights High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alamo_Heights_High_School) in 1959, attended [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University) in [Nashville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nashville), [Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee), and transferred to [Swarthmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarthmore_College) in [Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania) in 1960, where he received his B.A. in 1963 with highest honors.[^o4] He then enrolled at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). He is a member of [Phi Beta Kappa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phi_Beta_Kappa) and [Sigma Xi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_Xi).[^o5] In 2014, he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] He is a professor emeritus at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign).

:::

In 1972 Jockusch and [Robert I. Soare](wikipedia://en/2660900) proved the [low basis theorem](wikipedia://en/9767227), an important result in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) with applications to [recursion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory) and [reverse mathematics](wikipedia://en/326365).
