# Ragsdale conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ragsdale conjecture"}

The **Ragsdale conjecture** is a [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) that concerns the possible arrangements of real [algebraic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_curves) embedded in the [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350). It was proposed by [Virginia Ragsdale](wikipedia://en/9728405) in her dissertation in 1906 and was disproved in 1979. It has been called "the oldest and most famous conjecture on the topology of real algebraic curves".[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Formulation of the conjecture"}

## Formulation of the conjecture {#Formulation_of_the_conjecture}

Ragsdale's dissertation, "On the Arrangement of the Real Branches of Plane Algebraic Curves," was published by the [American Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Journal_of_Mathematics) in 1906. The dissertation was a treatment of [Hilbert's sixteenth problem](wikipedia://en/381750), which had been proposed by [Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302) in 1900, along with [22 other unsolved problems of the 19th century](wikipedia://en/154584); it is one of the handful of Hilbert's problems that remains wholly unresolved. Ragsdale formulated a conjecture that provided an upper bound on the number of topological circles of a certain type,[^o5] along with the basis of evidence.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=heading name="Conjecture"}

## Conjecture {#Conjecture}

Ragsdale's main conjecture is as follows.

Assume that an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) of degree 2*k* contains *p* even and *n* odd ovals. Ragsdale conjectured that

$$p \le \tfrac32 k(k-1) + 1 \quad\text{and}\quad n \le \tfrac32 k(k-1).$$

She also posed the inequality

$$| 2(p-n)-1 | \le 3k^2 - 3k + 1,$$

and showed that the inequality could not be further improved. This inequality was later proved by [Petrovsky](wikipedia://en/4598657).

:::

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Disproving the conjecture"}

## Disproving the conjecture {#Disproving_the_conjecture}

The conjecture was held of very high importance in the field of real [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) for most of the twentieth century. Later, in 1980, [Oleg Viro](wikipedia://en/14465687)[^o10] introduced a technique known as "patchworking algebraic curves"[^o2] and used to generate a [counterexample](wikipedia://en/143151) to the conjecture.

In 1993, Ilia Itenberg[^o11] produced additional counterexamples to the Ragsdale conjecture, so Viro and Itenberg wrote a paper in 1996 discussing their work on disproving the conjecture using the "patchworking" technique.[^o2]

The problem of finding a sharp upper bound remains unsolved.

:::
