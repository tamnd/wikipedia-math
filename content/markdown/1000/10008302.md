# Brigitte Vallée

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brigitte Vallée"}

**Brigitte Vallée** (née Salesse) (born 6 June 1950, in Courbevoie, Hauts-de-Seine, France) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). She entered the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) de Jeunes Filles in 1970, and received her PhD in 1986 at the University of Caen (Lattice reduction algorithms in small dimensions).[^o3][^o4][^o5] Her doctoral advisor was [Jacques Stern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Stern_%28cryptographer%29).[^o5]

:::

Vallée has been Director of Research at the French [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at [Université de Caen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Caen),[^o6] since 2001 and specialized in computational [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [analysis of algorithms](wikipedia://en/2230).[^o7] Amongst the algorithms she studied are the celebrated [LLL](wikipedia://en/1735228) algorithm used for basis reductions in [Euclidean lattice](wikipedia://en/469246) and the different [Euclidean algorithms](wikipedia://en/10377) to determine [GCD](wikipedia://en/12354). The main tool used to achieve her results is the so-called *dynamical analysis*. Loosely speaking, it is a mix between analysis of algorithms and dynamical systems. Brigitte Vallée greatly contributed to the development of this method.

In the early 90s, Brigitte Vallée's work on small modular squares allowed her to hold the fastest [factorisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factorisation) algorithm with a proved probabilistic complexity bound. Nowadays, other factorisation algorithms are faster.

She was appointed a knight of the [Legion of Honor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legion_of_Honour) by the Ministry of Higher Education and Research on 12 July 2013.[^o3]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

According to [zbMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH), Vallée has authored 88 publications since 1986, including 3 books.[^o9]

- Brigitte Vallée, *Generation of Elements with Small Modular Squares and Provably Fast Integer Factoring Algorithms*, Mathematics of Computation, Vol. 56, No. 194 (Apr., 1991), pp. 823–849.
- Brigitte Vallée, *Algorithmique en géométrie des nombres. Applications à la cryptographie et à la factorisation des entiers* (*A geometric approach to the reduction of small-scale networks)*, 1986 \[University thesis\][^o4][^o5]
