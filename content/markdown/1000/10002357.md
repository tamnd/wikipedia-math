# Khintchine inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Khintchine inequality"}

*Image: Khintchine inequality (svg).svg.*

The **Khintchine inequality**, is a result in [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) also frequently used in [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) bounding the expectation a weighted sum of [Rademacher random variables](wikipedia://en/1856890) with [square-summable](wikipedia://en/1701075#ℓp_spaces) weights. It is named after [Aleksandr Khinchin](wikipedia://en/1029748) and spelled in multiple ways in the Latin alphabet.

:::

It states that for each $p\in (0,\infty)$ there exist constants $A_p,B_p>0$ depending only on $p$ such that for every sequence $x = (x_1, x_2, \dots)\in\ell^2$, and [i.i.d.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I.i.d.) Rademacher random variables $\epsilon_1, \epsilon_2, \dots$,

$$A_p\leq \frac{\mathbb E\left[\left|\sum_{n=1}^\infty \epsilon_n x_n\right|^p\right]^{1/p}}{\|x\|_2}\leq B_p.$$

As a particular case, consider $N$ [complex numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_numbers) $x_1,\dots,x_N \in\mathbb{C}$, which can be pictured as vectors in a plane. Now sample $N$ random signs $\epsilon_1, \dots, \epsilon_N \in \{-1, +1\}$, with equal independent probability. The inequality states that

$$\Big|\sum_i \epsilon_i x_i \Big| \approx \sqrt{|x_1|^{2}+\cdots + |x_N|^{2}}$$

with a bounded error.

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $\{\varepsilon_n\}_{n=1}^N$ be [i.i.d.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I.i.d.) [random variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_variables) with $P(\varepsilon_n=\pm1)=\frac12$ for $n=1,\ldots, N$, i.e., a sequence with [Rademacher distribution](wikipedia://en/1856890). Let $0<p<\infty$ and let $x_1,\ldots,x_N\in \mathbb{C}$. Then

$$A_p \left( \sum_{n=1}^N |x_n|^2 \right)^{1/2} \leq \left(\operatorname{E} \left|\sum_{n=1}^N \varepsilon_n x_n\right|^p \right)^{1/p} \leq B_p \left(\sum_{n=1}^N |x_n|^2\right)^{1/2}$$

for some constants $A_p,B_p>0$ depending only on $p$ (see [Expected value](wikipedia://en/9653) for notation). More succinctly,

$$\left(\operatorname{E} \left|\sum_{n=1}^N \varepsilon_n x_n\right|^p \right)^{1/p} \in [A_p, B_p]$$

for any sequence $x$ with unit $\ell^2$ norm.

The sharp values of the constants $A_p,B_p$ were found by Haagerup (Ref. 2; see Ref. 3 for a simpler proof). It is a simple matter to see that $A_p = 1$ when $p \ge 2$, and $B_p = 1$ when $0 < p \le 2$.

Haagerup found that

$$\begin{aligned} A_p &= \begin{cases} 2^{1/2-1/p} & 0<p\le p_0, \\ 2^{1/2}(\Gamma((p+1)/2)/\sqrt{\pi})^{1/p} & p_0 < p < 2\\ 1 & 2 \le p < \infty \end{cases} \\ &\text{and} \\ B_p &= \begin{cases} 1 & 0 < p \le 2 \\ 2^{1/2}(\Gamma((p+1)/2)/\sqrt\pi)^{1/p} & 2 < p < \infty \end{cases}, \end{aligned}$$

where $p_0\approx 1.847$ and $\Gamma$ is the [Gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316). One may note in particular that $B_p$ matches exactly [the moments of a normal distribution](wikipedia://en/21462#Moments).

:::

## Uses in analysis {#Uses_in_analysis}

The uses of this inequality are not limited to applications in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). One example of its use in [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) is the following: if we let $T$ be a [linear operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_operator) between two [L<sup>*p*</sup> spaces](wikipedia://en/45194) $L^p(X,\mu)$ and $L^p(Y,\nu)$, $1 < p < \infty$, with bounded [norm](wikipedia://en/246722) $\|T\|<\infty$, then one can use Khintchine's inequality to show that

$$\left\|\left(\sum_{n=1}^N |Tf_n|^2 \right)^{1/2} \right\|_{L^p(Y,\nu)}\leq C_p \left\|\left(\sum_{n=1}^N |f_n|^2\right)^{1/2} \right\|_{L^p(X,\mu)}$$

for some constant $C_p>0$ depending only on $p$ and $\|T\|$.[^o5]

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalizations"}

## Generalizations {#Generalizations}

For the case of [Rademacher](wikipedia://en/1856890) random variables, Pawel Hitczenko showed[^o8] that the sharpest version is:

$$A \left(\sqrt{p}\left(\sum_{n=b+1}^N x_n^2\right)^{1/2} + \sum_{n=1}^b x_n\right) \leq \left(\operatorname{E} \left|\sum_{n=1}^N \varepsilon_n x_n\right|^p \right)^{1/p} \leq B \left(\sqrt{p}\left(\sum_{n=b+1}^N x_n^2\right)^{1/2} + \sum_{n=1}^b x_n\right)$$

where $b = \lfloor p\rfloor$, and $A$ and $B$ are universal constants independent of $p$.

Here we assume that the $x_i$ are non-negative and non-increasing.

:::
