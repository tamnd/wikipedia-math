# Joseph-Émile Barbier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph-Émile Barbier"}

**Joseph-Émile Barbier** (1839–1889) was a French astronomer and mathematician, known for [Barbier's theorem](wikipedia://en/580264) on the perimeter of [curves of constant width](wikipedia://en/580238).[^o4]

:::

Barbier was born on 18 March 1839 in [Saint-Hilaire-Cottes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Hilaire-Cottes), [Pas-de-Calais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pas-de-Calais), in the north of [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France). He studied at the [College of Saint-Omer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colleges_of_St_Omer,_Bruges_and_Liège), also in Pas-de-Calais, and then at the [Lycée Henri-IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Henri-IV) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). He entered the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in 1857, and finished his studies there in 1860,[^o3] the same year in which he published the paper containing his theorem on constant-width curves.[^o5] In this paper he also presented a solution to [Buffon's needle problem](wikipedia://en/667479), known as [Buffon's noodle](wikipedia://en/12784536), that avoided the use of integrals.

He began teaching at a lycée in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice), but it was not a success, and he soon moved to a position as an assistant astronomer at the [Paris Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Observatory). He left there in 1865, and in 1880 [Joseph Louis François Bertrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Louis_François_Bertrand) found him in the [Charenton asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charenton_%28asylum%29). Bertrand arranged for Barbier's support and encouraged him to return to mathematical publication.[^o3]

In this later period of his work, he published ten more papers.[^o3] He contributed to Bertrand's studies of [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170),[^o6] and announced a generalization of [Bertrand's ballot theorem](wikipedia://en/1043036).[^o7] He was given the Francoeur Prize for his mathematical research by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in multiple years.[^o8][^o9]

Barbier died on 28 January 1889 in [Saint-Genest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Genest-Lerpt), [Loire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loire).[^o3]
