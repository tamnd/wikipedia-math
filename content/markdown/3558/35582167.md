# Rotunda (geometry)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rotunda** is any member of a family of [cyclic-symmetric](wikipedia://en/52327) [polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedra). They are similar to a [cupola](wikipedia://en/689743) but, instead of alternating squares and triangles, they alternate pentagons and triangles around an axis. The [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491) is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034).

:::

Other forms can be generated with dihedral symmetry and distorted equilateral pentagons.
