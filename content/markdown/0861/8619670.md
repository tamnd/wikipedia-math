# Polytetrahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polytetrahedron"}

**Polytetrahedron** is a term used for three distinct types of objects, all based on the [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606):

:::

- A [regular convex 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/18801624) made up of 600 [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29). It is more commonly known as a **[600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447)** or **hexacosichoron**.
- A [polyform](wikipedia://en/691765) of [regular](wikipedia://en/333299) tetrahedra.
- In [origami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origami), a **polypolyhedron** is "a compound of multiple linked [polyhedral](wikipedia://en/23470) skeletons with [uniform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-uniform) nonintersecting edges".[^o2] There exist two topologically distinct polytetrahedra, each made up of four intersecting [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654).
