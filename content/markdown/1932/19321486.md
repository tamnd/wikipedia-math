# Abstract and Applied Analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abstract and Applied Analysis"}

***Abstract and Applied Analysis*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) covering the fields of [abstract and applied analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analysis_%28mathematics%29) and traditional forms of analysis such as [linear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear) and [nonlinear](wikipedia://en/146103) ordinary and partial [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309), [optimization theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29), and [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039).

:::

It is published by [Hindawi Publishing Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindawi_Publishing_Corporation). It was established by Athanassios G. Kartsatos ([University of South Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Florida)) in 1996, who was [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) until 2005. Martin Bohner (Missouri S&T) was editor-in-chief from 2006 until 2011 when the journal converted to a model shared by all Hindawi journals of not having an editor-in-chief, with editorial decisions made by editorial board members.

The journal has faced delisting from the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)* (thus not receive an [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor)), for anomalous citation patterns.[^o3]
