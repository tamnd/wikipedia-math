# Bry and Boschan routine

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bry and Boschan routine"}

In [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390), the **Bry and Boschan routine** finds statistical [maxima and minima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxima_and_minima) while using certain [censoring](wikipedia://en/11548952) rules and phase and cycle length constraints.[^o2][^o3]

:::

The [OECD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OECD) has composite [leading indicators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_indicator#Classification_by_timing) and [turning points](wikipedia://en/885651) that are [recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recession) indicators with peak through the trough turning points chronologies determined by the rules in a computer routine (Bry and Boschan)[^o4]

The U.S. [National Bureau of Economic Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Bureau_of_Economic_Research) (NBER) uses a computer routine (Bry and Boschan) and includes a Phase-Average Trend (PAT) [detrending](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detrending) procedure. The turning point detection [algorithm](wikipedia://en/775) is decoupled from the de-trending procedure.[^o5]
