# Matthias Kohl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matthias Kohl"}

**Matthias Kohl** (born August 14, 1973 in [Vilseck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilseck), Germany) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who is known for his contributions to the asymptotic theory of robustness and [robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691). Kohl studied mathematics at the [University of Bayreuth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bayreuth) and earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in mathematics there, in 2005. His dissertation was entitled 'Numerical Contributions to the Asymptotic Theory of Robustness'.[^o3]

:::

He spent the first half of his career at the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena) as a biostatistician. At Jena, he worked on problems in the analysis of high dimensional biological data,[^o4] the development and validation of [microarray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microarray)-based predictive models[^o5] and [study design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_design). He then became professor at [Furtwangen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hochschule_Furtwangen_University). There, his research area are statistical methods for biomarker development and molecular diagnostics.
