# Virginie Bonnaillie-Noël

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Virginie Bonnaillie-Noël"}

**Virginie Bonnaillie-Noël** (born 3 October 1976 in [Calais](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calais)) is a French mathematician and research director specializing in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). Her research topics concern partial differential equations, asymptotic, spectral and numerical analysis of problems arising from physics or mechanics.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

After completing her preparatory classes at Faidherbe high school in [Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lille) between 1994 and 1997, Bonnaillie-Noël entered [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) where she obtained a bachelor's degree in 1998 and a master's degree in 1999. The same year, she was admitted to the [Ecole Normale Supérieure Paris-Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_Paris-Saclay) (ENS) where she obtained the aggregation in 2000 with the option of numerical analysis. In 2001, she obtained a Diploma of Advanced Studies (DEA) in Numerical Analysis and Partial Differential Equations.[^o4][^o9]

Between 2001 and 2003 she completed a thesis (as Virginie Bonnaillie) under the supervision of François Alouges and [Bernard Helffer](wikipedia://en/56549449) titled *Mathematical analysis of superconductivity in a corner domain: semi-classical and numerical methods,* which explored [interdisciplinarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdisciplinarity) between physics and mathematics, at the border of numerical analysis, partial differential equations and [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713).[^o4][^o5]

In 2004, she joined the Mathematical Research Institute of Rennes (IRMAR) as a research fellow. In 2011, she obtained authorization to direct research at the [University of Rennes I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes_1). In 2014, she left IRMAR to direct research in the Mathematics and Applications Department of ENS.[^o4]

In addition to her official work, Bonnaillie-Noël has frequently spoken about gender parity in science and research to encourage more young people to participate in the sciences.[^o4]

## Distinctions {#Distinctions}

- 2008: [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) bronze medal[^o4][^o7]
- 2009: [Irène-Joliot-Curie Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irène_Joliot-Curie_Prize) in the young female scientist category[^o4][^o9][^o8]
- 2011: Chevalier of the National Order of Merit by [Cédric Villani](wikipedia://en/27172341)[^o9]
- 2021: Officer of the National Order of Merit[^o10]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Bonnaillie-Noël, V., & Dauge, M. (2006, August). Asymptotics for the low-lying eigenstates of the Schrödinger operator with magnetic field near corners. In *Annales Henri Poincaré* (Vol. 7, No. 5, pp. 899–931). Birkhäuser-Verlag.
- Bonnaillie-Noël, V., & Fournais, S. (2007). Superconductivity in domains with corners. *Reviews in Mathematical Physics*, *19*(06), 607-637.
- Bonnaillie-Noël, V., Dambrine, M., Tordeux, S., & Vial, G. (2009). Interactions between moderately close inclusions for the Laplace equation. *Mathematical Models and Methods in Applied Sciences*, *19*(10), 1853-1882.
- Bonnaillie-Noël, V., Dambrine, M., Hérau, F., & Vial, G. (2010). On generalized Ventcel's type boundary conditions for [Laplace operator](wikipedia://en/174706) in a bounded domain. *SIAM journal on mathematical analysis*, *42*(2), 931-945.
- Bonnaillie-Noël, V., Helffer, B., & Vial, G. (2010). Numerical simulations for nodal domains and spectral minimal partitions. *ESAIM: Control, Optimisation and Calculus of Variations*, *16*(1), 221-246.
