# Finitely generated object

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Finitely generated object"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a **finitely generated object** is the quotient of a [free object](wikipedia://en/333835) over a [finite set](wikipedia://en/11742), in the sense that it is the target of a [regular epimorphism](wikipedia://en/59539) from a free object that is free on a finite set.[^o2]

:::

For instance, one way of defining a [finitely generated group](wikipedia://en/963042) is that it is the image of a [group homomorphism](wikipedia://en/12396) from a finitely generated [free group](wikipedia://en/59735).
