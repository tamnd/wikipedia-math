# Amrik Singh Nimbran

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Amrik Singh Nimbran"}

**Amrik Singh Nimbran**, retired [Indian Police Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Police_Service) officer and amateur mathematician.

:::

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Amrik Singh Nimran was born into a [Dalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalit) family at Gurgaon, Haryana.[^o5]

In 1992, he wrote *Poverty, Land, and Violence: An Analytical Study of Naxalism in Bihar*.

## Civil services {#Civil_services}

Nimbran is a 1979-batch Indian Police Service officer of Bihar cadre.

He is the only IPS officer who has held every position in [Patna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patna_district). He was SP, Senior SP, DIG Central Range, and I.G. (Patna Zone) and later appointed as DGP of Bihar (Fire Service and Home Guard).[^o7][^o8]

He retired in 2014, after giving 35 years of service to the state.[^o9]

He is the first State Security Advisor of Bihar and included under [Jitan Ram Manjhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jitan_Ram_Manjhi)'s government. Being a [Dalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalit) and senior officer, Nimbran was considered Majhi's comrade.[^o10]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

He is an amateur mathematician and his papers have been published in the *[Indian Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Journal_of_Pure_and_Applied_Mathematics)* and Indian Math Society in 2016 and *[The Ramanujan Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ramanujan_Journal)* in 2018 regarding Generalized Wallis-Euler Products and New Infinite Products for π.[^o12][^o13]

Published "Euler Sums and Integral Connections" in MDPI.[^o14] He has 64 publications on mathematics and he is mainly contributing in arc tangent identities, infinite series and infinite products for computing Pi.[^o15]
