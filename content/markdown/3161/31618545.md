# Algimantas Adolfas Jucys

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Algimantas Adolfas Jucys"}

**Algimantas Adolfas Jucys** (14 November 1936 – 29 July 1997) was a [Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania) theoretical physicist more prominent as a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), a son of [Lithuanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuania) physicist [Adolfas Jucys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolfas_Jucys). Since 1967 Algis Jucys was researcher at the Institute of Physics and Mathematics of [Lithuanian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Academy_of_Sciences), in 1977-1990 at the Institute of Physics and in 1990-1996 at the Institute of Theoretical Physics and Astronomy, Lithuania.[^o3]

:::

Algimantas (Algis) Adolfas (A.-A.A.) Jucys was member of Lithuanian Physical Society, [Lithuanian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/77767036), American Mathematical Society. [Jucys–Murphy elements](wikipedia://en/24153642) in the [group algebra](wikipedia://en/349223) $\mathbb{C} [S_n]$ of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) are named after him.
