# Spencer cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spencer cohomology"}

In mathematics, **Spencer cohomology** is [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) with coefficients in the [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) of solutions of a linear partial [differential operator](wikipedia://en/263886). It was introduced by [Donald C. Spencer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_C._Spencer) in 1969.

:::

With origin in the geometry of partial differential equations, Spencer cohomology is best characterized in certain algebraic and categorial framework [^o2]
