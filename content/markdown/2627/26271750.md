# Judgment sample

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Judgment sample"}

A **judgment sample**, also known as an **expert** or **purposive** sample, is a type of non-random [sample](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sample_%28statistics%29), where a researcher or expert selects the sample based on who they believe would be most useful or appropriate for the study.[^o2][^o3]

:::

Results obtained from a judgment sample are subject to some degree of [bias](wikipedia://en/17692) and may be hard to [generalize](wikipedia://en/1900609#In_experiments), due to the chosen sample not representing the larger population.[^o2]

A random sample would provide less bias, but potentially less raw information.[^o4] The pitfalls of this system are significant because of bias, limited statistical methods, and limits to an expert's ability to choose a good sample.
