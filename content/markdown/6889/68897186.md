# Yang-Hui He

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yang-Hui He"}

**Yang-Hui He** (; born 29 September 1975[^o3]) is a [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist), who is a Fellow at the [London Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Institute_for_Mathematical_Sciences),[^o4] which is based at the [Royal Institution of Great Britain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institution_of_Great_Britain), as well as lecturer and former Fellow at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford).[^o5][^o6] He holds honorary positions as visiting [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics at [City, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City,_University_of_London),[^o7] [Chang-Jiang Chair professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changjiang_Scholars) at [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University),[^o8] and President of STEMM Global scientific society.[^o9]

:::

Yang works on the interface between [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267), [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305), [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), as well as how [AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI) and [machine-learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine-learning) help with these problems.[^o10][^o11] He is one of the pioneers of the field of using AI for pure mathematics.

Yang is author of over 200 scientific publications[^o12] and is also a keen communicator of science, giving regular public lectures[^o13][^o14][^o15][^o16][^o17][^o18] including the Royal Institution Friday Evening Discourse,[^o19] as well as podcasts.[^o20][^o21][^o22] [^o23][^o24] His other outreach activities include acting as an advisor to BMUCO[^o25] and being a fellow of the One Garden.[^o26]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Yang received his [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in [Physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1996, with Highest Honours (*[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude)*, [Allen Shenstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allen_Shenstone) Prize and Kusaka Memorial Prize), joint with certificates in applied mathematics and in engineering physics. He received his [Masters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_III_of_the_Mathematical_Tripos) from [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1997 with Distinction and then obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 2002 in the [Center for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Center_for_Theoretical_Physics) ([NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Scholarship and MIT Presidential Award) under the supervision of Amihay Hanany.[^o28]

After postdoctoral work at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), in the group of [Burt Ovrut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burt_Ovrut), Yang joined the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) as [FitzJames Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_FitzJames) and Advanced Fellow of the [STFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/STFC), UK, working closely with [Philip Candelas](wikipedia://en/38529128).[^o29] He remains a tutor at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College) when taking up his professorships at the University of London and Nankai University, and more recently, when he joined the [London Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_Institute_for_Mathematical_Sciences).

## Works {#Works}

Yang has authored over 200 journal papers,[^o31] as well as several books, notably:

- *Topology and Physics*,[^o32] co-edited with [C. N. Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._N._Yang) and Mo-Lin Ge, with contributions from [Sir Michael Atiyah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Michael_Atiyah), [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894), [Sir Roger Penrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Roger_Penrose), [Robbert Dijkgraaf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robbert_Dijkgraaf) et al., recommended by Book Authority as one of the 20 most influential books in quantum field theory of all time.[^o33]
- *The Calabi-Yau Landscape: from geometry, to physics, to machine-learning*,[^o34] textbook aimed at early PhD students, introducing mathematics to physicists, physics to mathematicians and machine-learning to both, the first textbook on the AI mathematician.
- *Dialogues Between Physics and Mathematics: C. N. Yang at 100*,[^o35] co-edited with Mo-Lin Ge, with contributions from [Edward Witten](wikipedia://en/54894), [Sir Roger Penrose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Roger_Penrose), [Sir Anthony James Leggett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_Anthony_James_Leggett), [Alexander Polyakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Markovich_Polyakov), [Vladimir Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747) et al., celebrating the 100th birthday of [C. N. Yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._N._Yang).
- *Machine Learning in Pure Mathematics and Theoretical Physics*,[^o36] the first of its kind, as a collection of essays on the interactions between AI and pure mathematics/fundamental physics.
