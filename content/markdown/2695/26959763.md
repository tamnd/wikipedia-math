# Øystein Linnebo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Øystein Linnebo"}

**Øystein Linnebo** (born 1971) is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy). As of 2020 he is currently employed in the Department of Philosophy at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo), having earlier held a position as Professor of Philosophy at [Birkbeck College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkbeck_College), [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London). He is a fellow of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Linnebo earned his [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in Mathematics from the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) in 1995 and his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in Philosophy at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in June 2002.

Linnebo's primary areas of concentration are [philosophy of logic](wikipedia://en/21368888), [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), as well as [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language) and [philosophy of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_science). He is known for his numerous publications in many top international journals in his field including: *The Review of Symbolic Logic*, *[Dialectica](wikipedia://en/26204033)*, *[The Journal of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Journal_of_Philosophy)*, *[Notre Dame Journal of Formal Logic](wikipedia://en/40764984)* as well as editing a special edition of *[Synthese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Synthese)*.[^o5] Additionally, he is the author of the articles "Plural Quantification" and "Platonism in the Philosophy of Mathematics" in the *[Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Encyclopedia_of_Philosophy)*.[^o6][^o7] He is also an "Area Editor" for philosophy of mathematics on *[PhilPapers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhilPapers)*.[^o8]

In addition to being a Professorial fellow at the Northern Institute of Philosophy, [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen) he has also been awarded many grants. Most recently he led a research project as part of a [European Research Council Starting Grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) entitled "Plurals, Predicates, and Paradox: Towards a Type-Free Account" which ran from January 2010 until December 2013.[^o9]

In 2018, he published *Thin Objects: An Abstractionist Account*, an [abstractionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstractionism_%28philosophy_of_mathematics%29) approach to [thin objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_object_theory).

Since 2022, he is visiting professor at the [University of Italian Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Università_della_Svizzera_italiana).
