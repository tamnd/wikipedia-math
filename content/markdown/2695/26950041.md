# Yevgeny Dyakonov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yevgeny Dyakonov"}

**Evgenii Georgievich Dyakonov** (Евгений Георгиевич Дьяконов) (July 2, 1935 – August 11, 2006) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Dyakonov was a Ph.D. student of [Sergei Sobolev](wikipedia://en/380798). He worked at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). He authored over hundred papers and several books. Dyakonov was recognized for his pioneering work in the 60s–80s on efficient [spectrally equivalent preconditioning](wikipedia://en/5165246) for [linear systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_systems) and [eigenvalue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) problems. In the last decade, strengthened [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964) became Dyakonov's main topic of research, e.g., (Dyakonov, 2004).
