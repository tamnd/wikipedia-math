# Walter Knödel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Walter Knödel"}

**Walter Knödel** (May 20, 1926 – October 19, 2018) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). He was a computer science professor at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart).

:::

Born in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), Walter Knödel studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). Also in Vienna, Knödel received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1948 for his work on [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) under the direction of [Edmund Hlawka](wikipedia://en/5442007) and got habilitated in 1953. In 1961, Walter Knödel became professor for mathematics at the University of Stuttgart.[^o2]

Walter Knödel authored a number of books and scientific publications. He wrote the first [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language) textbook on [computer programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_programming) in 1961.[^o3] He was the founding dean of the faculty for computer science at the University of Stuttgart and founding member of the German Computer Society the [Gesellschaft für Informatik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Informatik).[^o4]

The [Knödel numbers](wikipedia://en/11754068) became named after Walter Knödel. He died in [Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart) on October 19, 2018.[^o5]
