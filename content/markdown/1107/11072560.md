# Anthony W. Knapp

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anthony W. Knapp"}

**Anthony William Knapp** (born 2 December 1941, [Morristown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morristown,_New_Jersey), [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey))[^o2] is an American mathematician and professor emeritus at the [State University of New York, Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York,_Stony_Brook) working in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). For much of his career, Knapp was a professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Knapp lived in [Baltimore, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore,_Maryland),[^o5] and graduated from the preparatory [McDonogh School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McDonogh_School) nearby.[^o4] He attended [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College),[^o9] graduating from there in 1962 and receiving a [National Science Foundation Graduate Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_Graduate_Fellowship) to continue his studies.[^o5] Knapp received his Ph.D. in 1965 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under the supervision of [Salomon Bochner](wikipedia://en/987847).[^o9]

## Teaching {#Teaching}

Knapp began his career as a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) for two years at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology),[^o9] before gaining a position as an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1967.[^o7] He was promoted to [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) there in 1970 and [full professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_professor) in 1975.[^o8] Knapp began spending some of his time at [SUNY Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SUNY_Stony_Brook) in 1986 and took a full-time position there in 1990.[^o9]

## Research {#Research}

In a series of papers from 1976 to 1984, Knapp and [Gregg Zuckerman](wikipedia://en/10822168) gave the classification of [tempered representations](wikipedia://en/10995628) of [semisimple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple) [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).[^o11][^o12][^o13][^o14]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He won the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition in 1997.[^o16] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o17]
