# Rose Whelan Sedgewick

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rose Whelan Sedgewick"}

**Rose Whelan Sedgewick** (c. 1904 – 2000) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). She was the first person to earn a PhD in mathematics from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), in 1929.[^o3] Her subsequent career in mathematics included assistant professorships at the University of Rochester, the University of Connecticut, Hillyer College, and the University of Maryland.[^o4]

:::

Sedgewick is the namesake of the Rose Whelan Society at Brown, an organization for women and gender minorities who are graduate students, post-doctoral fellows and faculty in pure and applied in mathematics.[^o5] She was married to fellow mathematician Charles H.W. Sedgewick and had four children.[^o6] She died on June 7, 2000, at the age of 96.[^o4]
