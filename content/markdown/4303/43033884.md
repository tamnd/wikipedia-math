# Carl Ludvig Godske

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Ludvig Godske"}

**Carl Ludvig Schreiner Godske** (20 May 1906 – 3 July 1970) was a Norwegian mathematician and meteorologist. He was born in [Bindal Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bindal_Municipality). He was a member of the [Bergen School of Meteorology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen_School_of_Meteorology), working as meteorologist in [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen_%28city%29) from 1938, and appointed professor at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen) from 1946. Among his publications is *Dynamic Meteorology and Weather Forecasting* from 1957. He was also a pioneer in the application of electronic computers in Norway. He chaired the [Norwegian Geophysical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Geophysical_Society) from 1956 to 1957 and was a fellow of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) from 1937. He was decorated Knight, First Class of the [Order of St. Olav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_St._Olav) in 1967.[^o2][^o3]
