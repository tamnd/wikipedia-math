# Thaine's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thaine's theorem"}

In mathematics, **Thaine's theorem** is an analogue of [Stickelberger's theorem](wikipedia://en/2202290) for real abelian fields, introduced by Francisco (txt). Thaine's method has been used to shorten the proof of the [Mazur–Wiles theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazur–Wiles_theorem) (Washington 1997), to prove that some [Tate–Shafarevich groups](wikipedia://en/25711736) are finite, and in the proof of [Mihăilescu's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mihăilescu's_theorem) (Schoof 2008).

:::

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Formulation"}

## Formulation {#Formulation}

Let $p$ and $q$ be distinct odd primes with $q$ not dividing $p-1$. Let $G^+$ be the Galois group of $F=\mathbb Q(\zeta_p^+)$ over $\mathbb{Q}$, let $E$ be its group of units, let $C$ be the subgroup of cyclotomic units, and let $Cl^+$ be its class group. If $\theta\in\mathbb Z[G^+]$ annihilates $E/CE^q$ then it annihilates $Cl^+/Cl^{+q}$.

:::
