# Henri Poincaré Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henri Poincaré Prize"}

The **Henri Poincaré Prize** is awarded every three years since 1997 for exceptional achievements in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416) and foundational contributions leading to new developments in the field. It is named after the French mathematician [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740). The prize is sponsored by the Daniel Iagolnitzer Foundation and is awarded to approximately three scientists at the [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905). The prize was also established to support promising young researchers that already made outstanding contributions in mathematical physics.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::
