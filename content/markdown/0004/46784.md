# Daniel Bernoulli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Bernoulli"}

**Daniel Bernoulli** (8 February 1700 – 27 March 1782) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and was one of the many prominent mathematicians in the [Bernoulli family](wikipedia://en/199226) from Basel. He is particularly remembered for his applications of mathematics to mechanics, especially [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), and for his pioneering work in [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685).[^o3] His name is commemorated in [Bernoulli's principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli's_principle), a particular example of the [conservation of energy](wikipedia://en/67088), which describes the mathematics of the mechanism underlying the operation of two important technologies of the 20th century: the [carburetor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carburetor) and the aeroplane [wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wing).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

*Image: HYDRODYNAMICA, Danielis Bernoulli.png.* Frontpage of *[Hydrodynamica](wikipedia://en/41265739)* (1738)

Daniel Bernoulli was born in [Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groningen_%28city%29), in the Netherlands, into a [family](wikipedia://en/199226) of distinguished mathematicians.[^o7] The Bernoulli family came originally from Antwerp, at that time in the [Spanish Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Netherlands), but emigrated to escape the Spanish persecution of the [Protestants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protestant). After a brief period in Frankfurt the family moved to Basel, in Switzerland.

Daniel was the son of [Johann Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/268886) (one of the early developers of [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176)) and a nephew of [Jacob Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/64740) (an early researcher in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and the discoverer of the mathematical constant [e](wikipedia://en/9633)).[^o7] He had two brothers, [Niklaus](wikipedia://en/1150998) and [Johann II](wikipedia://en/21044122). Daniel Bernoulli was described by [W. W. Rouse Ball](wikipedia://en/308290) as "by far the ablest of the younger Bernoullis".[^o8]

He is said to have had a bad relationship with his father. Both of them entered and tied for first place in a scientific contest at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). Johann banned Daniel from his house, allegedly being unable to bear the "shame" of Daniel being considered his equal. Johann allegedly plagiarized key ideas from Daniel's book *[Hydrodynamica](wikipedia://en/41265739)* in his book *Hydraulica* and backdated them to before *Hydrodynamica*.[^o9] Daniel's attempts at reconciliation with his father were unsuccessful.[^o10]

When he was in school, Johann encouraged Daniel to study business citing poor financial compensation for mathematicians. Daniel initially refused but later relented and studied both business and [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine) at his father's behest under the condition that his father would teach him mathematics privately.[^o10] Daniel studied medicine at [Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), and [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg), and earned a PhD in anatomy and botany in 1721.

He was a contemporary and close friend of [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902).[^o11][^o12] He went to [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg) in 1724 as professor of mathematics, but was very unhappy there. A temporary illness[^o10] together with the censorship by the [Russian Orthodox Church](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Orthodox_Church)[^o13] and disagreements over his salary gave him an excuse for leaving St. Petersburg in 1733.[^o14] He returned to the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), where he successively held the chairs of [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), and [natural philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_philosophy) until his death.[^o15]

In May 1750 he was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society).[^o16]

## Mathematical work {#Mathematical_work}

*Image: ETH-BIB-Bernoulli, Daniel (1700-1782)-Portrait-Portr 10971.tif (cropped).jpg.* Daniel Bernoulli

His earliest mathematical work was the *Exercitationes* (*Mathematical Exercises*), published in 1724 with the help of [Goldbach](wikipedia://en/6290). Two years later he pointed out for the first time the frequent desirability of resolving a compound motion into motions of translation and motion of rotation. In 1729, he published a [polynomial root-finding](wikipedia://en/72719782) algorithm which became known as [Bernoulli's method](wikipedia://en/79735921).[^o18] His chief work is *[Hydrodynamica](wikipedia://en/41265739)*, published in 1738. It resembles [Joseph Louis Lagrange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Louis_Lagrange)'s *Mécanique Analytique* in being arranged so that all the results are consequences of a single principle, namely, the conservation of [vis viva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vis_viva), an early version of the [conservation of energy](wikipedia://en/67088). This was followed by a memoir on the theory of the tides, to which, conjointly with the memoirs by Euler and [Colin Maclaurin](wikipedia://en/7824), a prize was awarded by the [French Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy): these three memoirs contain all that was done on this subject between the publication of [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627)'s *[Philosophiae Naturalis Principia Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophiae_Naturalis_Principia_Mathematica)* and the investigations of [Pierre-Simon Laplace](wikipedia://en/344783). Bernoulli also wrote a large number of papers on various mechanical questions, especially on problems connected with [vibrating strings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vibrating_string), and the solutions given by [Brook Taylor](wikipedia://en/210429) and by [Jean le Rond d'Alembert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_le_Rond_d'Alembert).[^o8]

## Economics and statistics {#Economics_and_statistics}

In his 1738 book *Specimen theoriae novae de mensura sortis (Exposition of a New Theory on the Measurement of Risk)*,[^o20] Bernoulli offered a solution to the [St. Petersburg paradox](wikipedia://en/466277) as the basis of the economic theory of [risk aversion](wikipedia://en/177700), [risk premium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Risk_premium), and [utility](wikipedia://en/45479).[^o21] Bernoulli often noticed that when making decisions that involved some uncertainty, people did not always try to maximize their possible monetary gain, but rather tried to maximize "[utility](wikipedia://en/45479)", an economic term encompassing their personal satisfaction and benefit. Bernoulli realized that for humans, there is a direct relationship between money gained and utility, but that it diminishes as the money gained increases. For example, to a person whose income is \$10,000 per year, an additional \$100 in income will provide more utility than it would to a person whose income is \$50,000 per year.[^n1]

One of the earliest attempts to analyze a statistical problem involving [censored data](wikipedia://en/11548952) was Bernoulli's 1766 analysis of [smallpox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smallpox) [morbidity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morbidity) and [mortality](wikipedia://en/191305) data to demonstrate the efficacy of [inoculation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inoculation).[^o23]

## Physics {#Physics}

In *Hydrodynamica* (1738) he laid the basis for the [kinetic theory of gases](wikipedia://en/64204), and applied the idea to explain [Boyle's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyle's_law).[^o8]

He worked with Euler on [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29) and the development of the [Euler–Bernoulli beam equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler–Bernoulli_beam_equation).[^o25] [Bernoulli's principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli's_principle) is of critical use in [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics).

According to [Léon Brillouin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Léon_Brillouin), the [principle of superposition](wikipedia://en/1201321) was first stated by Daniel Bernoulli in 1753: "The general motion of a vibrating system is given by a superposition of its proper vibrations."[^o26]
