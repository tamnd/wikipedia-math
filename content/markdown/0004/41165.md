# Foreign instrumentation signals intelligence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Foreign instrumentation signals intelligence"}

**Foreign instrumentation signals intelligence**, **FISINT** (*F*oreign *I*nstrumentation *S*ignature *INT*elligence) is [intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence_%28information_gathering%29) from the interception of foreign electromagnetic emissions associated with the testing and operational deployment of foreign aerospace, surface, and subsurface systems.[^o2] Since it deals with signals that have communicational content, it is a subset of [Communications Intelligence (COMINT)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence#Communications_Intelligence), which, in turn, is a subset of [SIGINT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIGINT). Unlike general COMINT signals, the content of FISINT signals is not in regular human language, but rather in [machine to machine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Machine_to_machine) (instrumentation) language or in a combination of regular human language and instrumentation language. FISINT is also considered as a subset of [MASINT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MASINT) (measurement and signature intelligence).

:::

Typical examples of such communication include:

- Telemetry data ([TELINT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TELINT)). Missiles, satellites and other remotely monitored devices often transmit streams of data concerning their location, speed, engine status and other metrics.
- Video data links. These may be from [UAVs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unmanned_aerial_vehicle) or from satellites used for reconnaissance.
- Remote access and control transmissions, such as from [remote keyless systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Remote_keyless_system) and wireless [traffic light control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traffic_light_control_and_coordination) systems.
- Command signals used in [teleoperation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teleoperation), such as the control of aerial vehicles, missiles and [remotely-controlled robots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telerobotics).

## Telecommunications {#Telecommunications}

In [telecommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunications), the term FISINT has the following meanings:

1. Intelligence [information](wikipedia://en/18985062) derived from electromagnetic emissions associated with the testing and operational deployment of foreign aerospace, surface, and subsurface systems.
1. Technical information and intelligence information derived from the [intercept](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence) of foreign instrumentation signals by other than the intended recipients. Foreign instrumentation [signals intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signals_intelligence) is a category of signals intelligence.

Foreign instrumentation signals include but are not limited to signals from [telemetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telemetry), beaconry, electronic interrogators, tracking/fusing/arming/firing [command](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Command_%28computing%29) systems, and [video](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video) [data](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data) links.
