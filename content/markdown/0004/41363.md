# Mean time between outages

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mean time between outages"}

In a [system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System) the **mean time between outages** (**MTBO**) is the mean [time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time) between equipment failures that result in loss of system continuity or unacceptable [degradation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Degradation_%28telecommunications%29).

:::

The MTBO is calculated by the equation,

$$MTBO = \frac{MTBF}{1-FFAS}$$

where MTBF is the nonredundant [mean time between failures](wikipedia://en/63397) and FFAS is the fraction of failures for which the failed equipment is automatically bypassed.
