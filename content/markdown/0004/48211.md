# Golomb ruler

*Image: Golomb Ruler-4.svg.* Golomb ruler of order 4 and length 6. This ruler is both *optimal* and *perfect*.

*Image: Perfect circular Golomb rulers.svg.* The perfect circular Golomb rulers (also called [difference sets](wikipedia://en/2992310)) with the specified order. (This preview should show multiple concentric circles. If not, click to view a larger version.)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Golomb ruler"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Golomb ruler** is a [set](wikipedia://en/26691) of marks at [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) positions along a [ruler](wikipedia://en/65593) such that no two pairs of marks are the same distance apart. The number of marks on the ruler is its *order*, and the largest distance between two of its marks is its *length*. Translation and reflection of a Golomb ruler are considered trivial, so the smallest mark is customarily put at 0 and the next mark at the smaller of its two possible values. Golomb rulers can be viewed as a one-dimensional special case of [Costas arrays](wikipedia://en/2068445).

:::

The Golomb ruler was named for [Solomon W. Golomb](wikipedia://en/1860707) and discovered independently by (Sidon 1932)[^o3] and (Babcock 1953). [Sophie Piccard](wikipedia://en/48027009) also published early research on these sets, in 1939, stating as a theorem the claim that two Golomb rulers with the same [distance set](wikipedia://en/58786399) must be [congruent](wikipedia://en/39330). This turned out to be false for six-point rulers, but true otherwise.[^o5]

There is no requirement that a Golomb ruler be able to measure *all* distances up to its length, but if it does, it is called a *[perfect](wikipedia://en/2515712)* Golomb ruler. It has been proved that no perfect Golomb ruler exists for five or more marks.[^o11] A Golomb ruler is *optimal* if no shorter Golomb ruler of the same order exists. Creating Golomb rulers is easy, but proving the optimal Golomb ruler (or rulers) for a specified order is computationally very challenging.

[Distributed.net](wikipedia://en/337011) has completed distributed massively [parallel searches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing) for optimal order-24 through order-28 Golomb rulers, each time confirming the suspected candidate ruler.[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10]

Currently, the [complexity](wikipedia://en/502426) of finding optimal Golomb rulers (OGRs) of arbitrary order *n* (where *n* is given in unary) is unknown. In the past there was some speculation that it is an [NP-hard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NP-hard) problem.[^o11] Problems related to the construction of Golomb rulers are provably shown to be NP-hard, where it is also noted that no known NP-complete problem has a similar flavor to finding Golomb rulers.[^o12]

Golomb rulers have practical applications in information theory and error correction, radio frequency selection and antenna placement, as well as current transformers.

## Definitions {#Definitions}

### Golomb rulers as sets {#Golomb_rulers_as_sets}

A set of integers $A = \{a_1,a_2,...,a_m\}$ where $a_1 < a_2 < ... < a_m$ is a Golomb ruler [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922)

$\text{for all } i,j,k,l \in \left\{1,2,...,m\right\} \text{such that } i \neq j \text{ and } k \neq l,\ a_i - a_j = a_k - a_l \iff i=k \text{ and } j=l.$[^o16]

The *order* of such a Golomb ruler is $m$ and its *length* is $a_m - a_1$. The [canonical form](wikipedia://en/515096) has $a_1 = 0$ and, if $m>2$, $a_2 - a_1 < a_m - a_{m-1}$. Such a form can be achieved through translation and reflection.

### Golomb rulers as functions {#Golomb_rulers_as_functions}

An [injective function](wikipedia://en/45196) $f:\left\{1,2,...,m\right\} \to \left\{0,1,...,n\right\}$ with $f(1) = 0$ and $f(m) = n$ is a Golomb ruler if and only if

$\text{for all } i,j,k,l \in \left\{1,2,...,m\right\} \text{such that } i \neq j \text{ and } k \neq l, f(i)-f(j) = f(k)-f(l) \iff i=k \text{ and } j=l.$[^o18]

The *order* of such a Golomb ruler is $m$ and its *length* is $n$. The canonical form has

$f(2)<f(m)-f(m-1)$ if $m>2$.

### Optimality {#Optimality}

A Golomb ruler of order m with length n may be [optimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimization_%28mathematics%29) in either of two respects:[^o18]

- It may be *optimally dense*, exhibiting maximal m for the specific value of n,
- It may be *optimally short*, exhibiting minimal n for the specific value of m.

The general term *optimal Golomb ruler* is used to refer to the second type of optimality.

:::

## Mathematical formulation {#Mathematical_formulation}

An optimization-based approach to find an optimal Golomb ruler of order *n* can be formulated as the following mixed-integer nonlinear programming (MINLP) problem. Let *x*<sub>i</sub> ∈ {0,1} be binary variables indicating the presence of a mark at position *i*, for *i* = 1, ..., *L*<sub>u</sub>, where *L*<sub>u</sub> is an upper bound on the length of the ruler. Let *t* be a continuous variable representing the total length of the ruler. The problem is formulated as:

$$\begin{aligned} \min_{t \geq 0,\ x_i \in \{0,1\}} \quad & t \\ \text{s.t.} \quad & i \cdot x_i \leq t, \quad \text{for } i = 1, \ldots, L_u, \\ & \sum_{i=1}^{L_u} x_i = n - 1, \\ & x_j + \sum_{i=1}^{L_u - j} x_i x_{i+j} \leq 1, \quad \text{for } j = 1, \ldots, L_u - 1. \end{aligned}$$

In this model, the variables $x_i$ define the ruler marks, and the constraint involving the bilinear terms $x_i x_{i+j}$ ensures that all pairwise distances are distinct. The objective is to minimize the largest marked position, which corresponds to the ruler's length.[^o21]

## Practical applications {#Practical_applications}

*Image: Golomb ruler conference room.svg.* Example of a conference room with proportions of a \[0, 2, 7, 8, 11\] Golomb ruler, making it configurable to 10 different sizes.[^o34]

### Information theory and error correction {#Information_theory_and_error_correction}

Golomb rulers are used within [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773) related to [error correcting codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error_correcting_code).[^o24]

### Radio frequency selection {#Radio_frequency_selection}

Golomb rulers are used in the selection of radio frequencies to reduce the effects of [intermodulation interference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intermodulation_interference) with both [terrestrial](wikipedia://en/9228)[^o26] and [extraterrestrial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_space)[^o27] applications.

### Radio antenna placement {#Radio_antenna_placement}

Golomb rulers are used in the design of phased arrays of radio antennas. In radio astronomy one-dimensional synthesis arrays can have the antennas in a Golomb ruler configuration in order to obtain minimum redundancy of the Fourier component sampling.[^o29][^o30]

### Current transformers {#Current_transformers}

Multi-ratio [current transformers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_transformer) use Golomb rulers to place transformer tap points.

## Methods of construction {#Methods_of_construction}

A number of construction methods produce [asymptotically optimal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotically_optimal) Golomb rulers.

### Erdős–Turán construction {#Erdős–Turán_construction}

The following construction, due to [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Pál Turán](wikipedia://en/614998), produces a Golomb ruler for every odd prime p.[^o34]

$$2pk+(k^2\,\bmod\,p),k\in[0,p-1]$$
