# Closed-loop transfer function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Closed-loop transfer function"}

In [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), a **closed-loop transfer function** is a [mathematical function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_function) describing the net result of the effects of a [feedback control loop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feedback_control_loop) on the input [signal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_%28information_theory%29) to the [plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_%28control_theory%29) under control.

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

The closed-loop [transfer function](wikipedia://en/31146) is measured at the output. The output signal can be calculated from the closed-loop transfer function and the input signal. Signals may be [waveforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Waveform), [images](wikipedia://en/71925), or other types of [data streams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_stream).

An example of a closed-loop block diagram, from which a transfer function may be computed, is shown below:

*Image: Closed Loop Block Deriv.png.*

The summing node and the *G*(*s*) and *H*(*s*) blocks can all be combined into one block, which would have the following transfer function:

$$\dfrac{Y(s)}{X(s)} = \dfrac{G(s)}{1 + G(s) H(s)}$$

$G(s)$ is called the [feed forward](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feed_forward_%28control%29) transfer function, $H(s)$ is called the [feedback](wikipedia://en/11545#Control_theory) transfer function, and their product $G(s)H(s)$ is called the **open-loop transfer function**.

## Derivation {#Derivation}

We define an intermediate signal Z (also known as [error signal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error_signal)) shown as follows:

Using this figure we write:

$$Y(s) = G(s)Z(s)$$

$$Z(s) =X(s)-H(s)Y(s)$$

Now, plug the second equation into the first to eliminate Z(s):

$$Y(s) = G(s)[X(s)-H(s)Y(s)]$$

Move all the terms with Y(s) to the left hand side, and keep the term with X(s) on the right hand side:

$$Y(s)+G(s)H(s)Y(s) = G(s)X(s)$$

Therefore,

$$Y(s)(1+G(s)H(s)) = G(s)X(s)$$

$$\Rightarrow \dfrac{Y(s)}{X(s)} = \dfrac{G(s)}{1+G(s)H(s)}$$
