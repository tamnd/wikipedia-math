# Cryptochannel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cryptochannel"}

In [telecommunications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunications), a **cryptochannel** is a complete [system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System) of [crypto-communications](wikipedia://en/18934432) between two or more holders or parties. It includes: (a) the cryptographic aids prescribed; (b) the holders thereof; (c) the indicators or other means of identification; (d) the area or areas in which effective; (e) the special purpose, if any, for which provided; and (f) pertinent notes as to distribution, usage, *etc.* A cryptochannel is analogous to a [radio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radio) [circuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Telecommunication_circuit).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::
