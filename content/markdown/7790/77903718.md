# John Sarli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Sarli"}

**John Sarli** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [academic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_staff). He is a [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor) of mathematics at [California State University at San Bernardino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_San_Bernardino).[^o3]

:::

Sarli's research focuses on the geometry of [groups of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) and the applications of [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291)[^o5] with his work published in *[Geometriae Dedicata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometriae_Dedicata)*, *[Journal of Geometry](wikipedia://en/73947765)*, *[Advances in Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Advances_in_Geometry)*, and the *[Journal of Elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Elasticity)*.

## Education {#Education}

In 1974, Sarli earned an [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University). He then pursued advanced studies and received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Mathematics from the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) in 1984.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Sarli was Chair of the Department of Mathematics at California State University, [San Bernardino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Bernardino,_California) from 1988 to 1994. In 1999, he joined the Mathematics Diagnostic Testing Project (MDTP) Workgroup. The following year, he took on the role of site director at CSU San Bernardino when an MDTP site was set up there. He assumed the position of Chair of the MDTP Workgroup in 2002 and held the role until 2020.[^o7] He holds the title of professor emeritus of Mathematics at California State University, San Bernardino.[^o8]

## Research {#Research}

Sarli, through his research, described an incidence structure for twisted groups 𝐺, where points are [elementary abelian root subgroups](wikipedia://en/4843202) establishing a correspondence between certain lines and planes in this structure, demonstrating that it induces a polarity on an embedded [metasymplectic space](wikipedia://en/35102687). He showed that [biharmonic functions](wikipedia://en/3010562), crucial for understanding equilibrium equations for elastic bodies, can be derived from a power series using [matrix representations](wikipedia://en/1523927) of 𝐶 and applied to describe solutions to planar equilibrium equations within [Möbius plane](wikipedia://en/38245519) geometry.[^o10] His alignment of the geometry of root subgroups in 𝐺=PSp<sub>4</sub>(𝑞) with a [system of conics](wikipedia://en/19008673) in the associated [generalized quadrangle](wikipedia://en/3947006) provided an interpretation of symplectic 2-transvections.[^o11] Classifying the intrinsic conics in the hyperbolic plane, using [collineation](wikipedia://en/4072976) invariants, he offered [metric characterizations](wikipedia://en/20018) and highlighted a natural duality among these classes, induced by an involution related to complementary angles of [parallelism](wikipedia://en/664497).[^o12]
