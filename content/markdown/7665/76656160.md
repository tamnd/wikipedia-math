# Thought (essay)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thought"}

"**Thought: A Logical Inquiry**" is an essay by [Gottlob Frege](wikipedia://en/48416).[^o2] It was published as "Der Gedanke. Eine logische Untersuchung" in the philosophy journal *Beiträge zur Philosophie des deutschen Idealismus* (English: Contributions to the philosophy of [German idealism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_idealism)) in 1918. It was republished in *[Mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_%28journal%29)* in English in 1956. In it, Frege argues against idealism and for [platonism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platonism) about thoughts, or [propositions](wikipedia://en/81094).[^o3][^o4] Frege says ideas are private, but thoughts are public. Frege said that such [abstract objects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_and_concrete) were members of a **third realm**.[^o5] Frege also argued for a [redundancy theory of truth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redundancy_theory_of_truth).

:::

Quoting Frege, "The thought, in itself immaterial, clothes itself in the material garment of a sentence and thereby becomes comprehensible to us. We say a sentence expresses a thought."
