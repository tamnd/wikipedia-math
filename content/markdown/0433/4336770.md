# Martin Ohm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Ohm"}

*Image: Martin Ohm2.gif.* Martin Ohm

**Martin Ohm** (May 6, 1792 in [Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlangen) – April 1, 1872 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a younger brother of [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) [Georg Ohm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Ohm).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He earned his doctorate in 1811 at [Friedrich-Alexander-University, Erlangen-Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich-Alexander-University,_Erlangen-Nuremberg) where his advisor was [Karl Christian von Langsdorf](wikipedia://en/15083433).[^o5] In 1817, he was appointed professor of mathematics and physics in the gymnasium at [Thorn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toruń). In 1821 he moved to Berlin, and in 1839 became a full professor in the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin). He delivered courses of lectures at the academy of architecture from 1824 to 1831, and at the schools of artillery and engineering from 1833 to 1852; and he also taught in the military school from 1826 to 1849.

## Work {#Work}

Ohm was the first to fully develop the theory of the [exponential](wikipedia://en/9678) *a*<sup>*b*</sup> when both *a* and *b* are [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) in 1823.[^o5]

The 1835 second edition of Ohm's textbook, *Die reine Elementar Mathematik* was the first time that Euclid's 'extreme and mean ratio' was given the name of the "[golden section](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_section)" (*goldener Schnitt*).[^o6][^o7] It was via sources relying on Ohm that the psychologist [Adolf Zeising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adolf_Zeising) adopted and popularised the term.

## Students {#Students}

Ohm's students included Friedrich August, Friedrich Bachmann, [Elwin Bruno Christoffel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elwin_Bruno_Christoffel), [Paul Bachmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Bachmann), Joseph Brutkowski, [Heinrich Eduard Heine](wikipedia://en/608289), [Rudolf Lipschitz](wikipedia://en/339965), [Leo Pochhammer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Pochhammer), [Friedrich Prym](wikipedia://en/34925052), Wilhelm Wagner, Hermann Waldaestel, Wilhelm Wernicke, Elena Gerz, Valentien Gerz, and Johanna Gerz.[^o9]

- *Aufsätze aus dem Gebiete der höhern Mathematik*
- *Die analytische und höhere Geometrie in ihren Elementen mit vorzüglicher Berücksichtigung der Theorie der Kegelschnitte,* Berlin Riemann 1826
- *Versuch eines vollkommen consequenten Systems der Mathematik*. Nürnberg, F. Korn, 1853-55.
- Lehrbuch der Mechanik zugleich mit den dazu nöthigen Lehren der höhern Analysis und der höhern Geometrie 1 Mechanik des Atoms. Berlin Enslin 1836
