# Lowell Schoenfeld

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lowell Schoenfeld"}

**Lowell Schoenfeld** (April 1, 1920 – February 6, 2002) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Schoenfeld received his Ph.D. in 1944 from [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) under the direction of [Hans Rademacher](wikipedia://en/1864916).

In 1953, as an assistant professor at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), he married (as his second wife) associate professor [Josephine M. Mitchell](wikipedia://en/67446236), causing the university to fire her from her [tenured](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_tenure) position under its anti-nepotism rules while allowing him to keep his more junior tenure-track job. They both resigned in protest, and after several short-term positions they were both able to obtain faculty positions at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 1958.[^o4] They were both promoted to full professor in 1961, and moved to the [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo) in 1968.[^o5]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Schoenfeld is known for obtaining the following results in 1976, assuming the [Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/19344125):

$$|\pi(x)-{\rm li}(x)|\le\frac{\sqrt x\,\ln x}{8\pi}$$

for all *x* ≥ 2657, based on the [prime-counting function](wikipedia://en/331826) *π*(*x*) and the [logarithmic integral function](wikipedia://en/47137) li(*x*), and

$$|\psi(x)-x|\le\frac{\sqrt x\,\ln^2 x}{8\pi}$$

for all *x* ≥ 73.2, based on the [second Chebyshev function](wikipedia://en/5793598) *ψ*(*x*).[^o7]
