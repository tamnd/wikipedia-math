# Joseph Ludwig Raabe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Ludwig Raabe"}

*Image: Joseph Ludwig Raabe (1801–1859) by Carl Friedrich Irminger (1813–1863) ZBZ (A).jpg.* Portrait by [Carl Friedrich Irminger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Friedrich_Irminger), c. 1850

**Joseph Ludwig Raabe** (15 May 1801 – 22 January 1859) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) best known for [Raabe's ratio test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raabe's_ratio_test).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Raabe was born on 15 May 1801 into a Jewish family in [Brody](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brody), [Austrian Galicia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Galicia) (now in [Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukraine)).[^o4] He was the son of Haja and Wolf Raabe, a writer and small businessman.[^o4] As his parents were quite poor, Raabe was forced to earn his living from a very early age by giving private lessons.[^o4] He studied mathematics at the [Imperial-Royal Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial-Royal_Polytechnic_Institute) in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866) from 1821 until 1827, the year he converted to Catholicism.[^o4]

Encouraged by Swiss mathematician Johannes Eschmann, Raabe moved to [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) in the autumn of 1831, where he became professor of mathematics at the *[gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29)* and *[Privatdozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Privatdozent)* at the [University of Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zürich) in 1833.[^o4] He was appointed associate professor at the university in 1843 and full professor in 1855.[^o4] Rabbe became professor at the newly founded Swiss Polytechnicum (now [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich)) in 1855.[^o2]

Raabe acquired the [bourgeoisie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_bourgeoisie) of [Schwamendingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schwamendingen)-[Oerlikon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oerlikon_%28Zurich%29) in 1836 and received a *doctor honoris causa* from the University of Zürich in 1846.[^o4] He died on 12 January 1859 in Zürich, aged 57.[^o4]

## Work {#Work}

Raabe published works on [definite integrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Definite_integrals), [Bernoulli numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_numbers) and [series theory](wikipedia://en/15287).[^o4] He is best known for [Raabe's ratio test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raabe's_ratio_test), an extension of [d'Alembert's ratio test](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D'Alembert's_ratio_test). Raabe's test serves to determine the [convergence](wikipedia://en/1743431) or [divergence](wikipedia://en/876428) of an infinite [series](wikipedia://en/15287), in some cases.[^o2] He is also known for the Raabe integral of the [gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316):[^o6]

$$\int_a^{a+1}\log\Gamma(t)\, dt = \tfrac{1}{2}\log 2\pi + a\log a - a,\quad a\ge0.$$

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Differential- und Integralrechnung* (3 volumes) (Zürich, 1839–1847)
- *Mathematische Mitteilungen* (2 volumes) (1857-1858)
