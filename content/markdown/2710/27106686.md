# Cornelis Simon Meijer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cornelis Simon Meijer"}

**Cornelis Simon Meijer** (17 August 1904, [Pieterburen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pieterburen) – 12 April 1974) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) who introduced the [Meijer G-function](wikipedia://en/6453527), a very general function that includes most of the elementary and higher [mathematical functions](wikipedia://en/185427) as special cases; he also introduced generalizations of the [Laplace transform](wikipedia://en/18610) that are referred to as [Meijer transforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meijer_transform).

:::
