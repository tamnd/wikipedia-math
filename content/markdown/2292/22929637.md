# Richard A. Brualdi

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard A. Brualdi"}

**Richard Anthony Brualdi** is a professor emeritus of [combinatorial mathematics](wikipedia://en/5170) at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).

:::

Brualdi received his Ph.D. from [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) in 1964; his advisor was [H. J. Ryser](wikipedia://en/20825197). Brualdi is an Editor-in-Chief of the [Electronic Journal of Combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Journal_of_Combinatorics). He has over 200 publications in several mathematical journals. According to current on-line database of Mathematics Genealogy Project, Richard Brualdi has 37 Ph.D. students and 48 academic descendants. The concept of [incidence coloring](wikipedia://en/47290509) was introduced in 1993 by Brualdi and Massey.

He received the [Euler medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_medal) from the [Institute of Combinatorics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/17545919) in 2000. In 2012, he was elected a fellow of the Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics. That same year, he became an inaugural fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
