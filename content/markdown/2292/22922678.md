# Heinrich Behnke

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Behnke"}

**Heinrich Adolph Louis Behnke** (9 October 1898 in [Horn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horn,_Germany) – 10 October 1979 in [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and rector at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

He was born into a [Lutheran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutheran) family in [Horn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horn,_Germany), a suburb of [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg). He attended the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and submitted his doctoral thesis to the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg).[^o4] He was noted for work on [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) with [Henri Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536) and [Peter Thullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Thullen). His first wife, Aenne Albersheim, was [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish), but she died soon after the birth of their son. He was concerned about his son's [ethnicity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ethnicity) during the [Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi) period. In 1936 he was elected a member of the *[Deutsche Akademie der Naturforscher Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Akademie_der_Naturforscher_Leopoldina)*.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- with [Peter Thullen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Thullen): Theorie der Funktionen mehrerer komplexer Veränderlicher, Springer Verlag, Ergebnisse der Mathematik und ihrer Grenzgebiete, 1934,[^o7] 2nd edn. with collaboration by [Reinhold Remmert](wikipedia://en/856331) 1970
- with Friedrich Sommer: Theorie der Funktionen einer komplexen Veränderlichen, Springer Verlag, 3rd edn. 1965
- Vorlesung über Differentialgeometrie, Münster, Aschendorff, 7th edn. 1966
- Vorlesung über gewöhnliche Differentialgleichungen, Münster, Aschendorff, 4th edn. 1963
- Vorlesungen über Algebra, Münster, Aschendorff, 3rd edn. 1958
- Vorlesungen über Zahlentheorie, Münster, Aschendorff, 5th edn. 1961
- Vorlesung über klassische Funktionentheorie, Aschendorff
- Vorlesung über Infinitesimalrechnung, Aschendorff
