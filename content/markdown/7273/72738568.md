# Bianca Falcidieno

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bianca Falcidieno"}

**Bianca Falcidieno** is an Italian applied mathematician whose research interests include [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics), [geometric modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_modeling), [shape analysis](wikipedia://en/16576796), and [mesh generation](wikipedia://en/3050716); she has been called a pioneer of semantics-driven shape representation. She is retired as a research director for the [Italian National Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Council_%28Italy%29) (CNR), where she led the Shape Modeling Group of the Institute for Applied Mathematics and Information Technologies (IMATI).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

As a master's student, after earning a degree in mathematics in the 1970s, Falcidieno became interested in computer graphics and geometric modeling through a project to visualize mathematical functions for use in teaching [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) to engineering students. By 1981, she was a permanent researcher for CNR, and founded its first research center for computer graphics.

In the 1990s, with Japanese researcher Tosiyasu Kunii, she helped found both the [Shape Modeling International](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shape_Modeling_International) conference and the associated journal, the [International Journal of Shape Modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Journal_of_Shape_Modeling), for which she was editor-in-chief.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2021, Falcidieno became the inaugural winner of the Shape Modeling International Tosiyasu Kunii Achievement Award, given "for her outstanding career achievements in shape modelling research". She is also a [Eurographics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurographics) Fellow and 2019 Eurographics Gold Medalist, and a Pioneer of the Solid Modeling Association.
