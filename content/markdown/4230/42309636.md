# Order-5 apeirogonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order-5 apeirogonal tiling"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **order-5 apeirogonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {∞,5}.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The dual to this tiling represents the fundamental domains of \[∞,5\*\] symmetry, [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678) \*∞∞∞∞∞ symmetry, a pentagonal domain with five ideal vertices.

*Image: H2chess 25ib.png.*

The *order-5 apeirogonal tiling* can be uniformly colored with 5 colored apeirogons around each vertex, and coxeter diagram: , except ultraparallel branches on the diagonals.

:::

## Related polyhedra and tiling {#Related_polyhedra_and_tiling}

This tiling is also topologically related as a part of sequence of regular polyhedra and tilings with five faces per vertex, starting with the [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {n,5}, and [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) , with n progressing to [infinity](wikipedia://en/20110824).
