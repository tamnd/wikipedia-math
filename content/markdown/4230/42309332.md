# Infinite-order pentagonal tiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infinite-order pentagonal tiling"}

In 2-dimensional [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), the **infinite-order pentagonal tiling** is a [regular](wikipedia://en/505253#Hyperbolic_tilings) tiling. It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of {5,∞}. All vertices are *ideal*, located at "infinity", seen on the boundary of the [Poincaré hyperbolic disk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poincaré_hyperbolic_disk) projection.

:::
