# Haïm Brezis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Haïm Brezis"}

**Haïm Brezis** (1 June 1944 – 7 July 2024) was a French mathematician, who mainly worked in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Riom-ès-Montagnes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riom-ès-Montagnes), [Cantal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantal), France. Brezis was the son of a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanians) immigrant father, who had come to France in the 1930s, and a Jewish mother who had fled from the Netherlands. His wife, [Michal Govrin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michal_Govrin), is an Israeli novelist, poet, and theater director.[^o4] Brezis received his Ph.D. from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1972 under the supervision of [Gustave Choquet](wikipedia://en/17301193). He was a professor at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) and a visiting distinguished professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). He was a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) (1988) and a foreign associate of the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) (2003). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5] He held honorary doctorates from several universities including [National Technical University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technical_University_of_Athens).[^o6] Brezis is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher).[^o7] He also served on the Mathematical Sciences jury for the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) in 2013 and 2014. In 2024 he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Lifetime Achievement of the AMS.

Brezis died in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem) on 7 July 2024, at the age of 80.[^o8]
