# Azriel Lévy

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Azriel Lévy"}

**Azriel Lévy**[^o3] (עזריאל לוי; born c. 1934) is an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician), and a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Lévy obtained his Ph.D. at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1958, under the supervision of [Abraham Fraenkel](wikipedia://en/528451) and [Abraham Robinson](wikipedia://en/46024). Later, using [Cohen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Cohen_%28mathematician%29)'s method of [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205), he proved several results on the [consistency](wikipedia://en/3435716) of various statements contradicting the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840). For example, with [J. D. Halpern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Halpern) he proved that the [Boolean prime ideal theorem](wikipedia://en/314919) does not imply the axiom of choice. He discovered the models *L*\[*x*\] used in [inner model theory](wikipedia://en/3796621). He also introduced the notions of [Lévy hierarchy](wikipedia://en/25129404) of the formulas of [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [Levy collapse](wikipedia://en/15550368#Levy_collapsing) and the [Feferman–Levy model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feferman–Levy_model).

His students include [Dov Gabbay](wikipedia://en/3662032), [Moti Gitik](wikipedia://en/25533079), and [Menachem Magidor](wikipedia://en/17115328).
