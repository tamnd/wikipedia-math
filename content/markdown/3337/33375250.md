# Marie Georges Humbert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marie Georges Humbert"}

**Marie Georges Humbert** (7 January 1859 Paris, France – 22 January 1921 Paris, France) was a French mathematician who worked on [Kummer surfaces](wikipedia://en/647758) and the [Appell–Humbert theorem](wikipedia://en/23379150) and introduced [Humbert surfaces](wikipedia://en/20711751). His son was the mathematician [Pierre Humbert](wikipedia://en/26005059). He won the [Poncelet Prize](wikipedia://en/20842828) of the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences) in 1891.

:::

He studied at the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique). He was the [brother-in-law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brother-in-law) of [Charles Mangin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Mangin).
