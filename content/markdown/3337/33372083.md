# Heinrich Maschke

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Maschke"}

*Image: Heinrich Maschke.jpg.* Heinrich Maschke.

**Heinrich Maschke** (24 October 1853 in [Breslau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breslau), Germany (now Wrocław, Poland) – 1 March 1908 [Chicago, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago,_Illinois), USA) was a German mathematician who proved [Maschke's theorem](wikipedia://en/30876007).

:::

Maschke earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in 1880. He came to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) in 1891, and took up an Assistant Professor position at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1892.
