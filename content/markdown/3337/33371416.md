# Frank Smithies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frank Smithies"}

**Frank Smithies** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) (10 March 1912 – 16 November 2002) was a British mathematician who worked on integral equations, [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), and the history of mathematics. He was elected as a fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 1961. He was an [alumnus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alumni) and an academic of [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University).

:::
