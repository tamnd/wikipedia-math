# Byeong Park

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Byeong Park"}

**Byeong Uk Park**is a South Korean statistician working in structured nonparametric regression, semiparametric inference and non-Euclidean data analysis. He is Distinguished Professor at the [Seoul National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul_National_University).

:::

Park received his PhD from the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1987 working with [Peter Bickel](wikipedia://en/26697127). He is a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988),[^o2] the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o3] and the [Korean Academy of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_Academy_of_Science_and_Technology), and an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o4] He has received the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) Carver Medal[^o5] in 2018 and, as the first statistician, the Inchon Memorial Foundation Inchon Award in 2019.[^o6] Park was the Laplace Lecturer[^o7] at the 9th World Congress in Probability and Statistics in 2016 in Toronto. He was also an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#2018,_Rio_de_Janeiro) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2018 in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).

Park was the President of the Korean Statistical Society 2021–2022, a Vice-President of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) 2019–2023, and served as Scientific Secretary of the [Bernoulli Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability) 2015-19. He was a Co-Editor of Computational Statistics and Data Analysis 2016-2025. He has published over 170 scientific articles in refereed journals.
