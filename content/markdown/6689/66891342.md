# Alberto Dou Mas de Xaxàs

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alberto Dou Mas de Xaxàs"}

Dou Mas

**Alberto Dou Mas de Xaxàs** (21 December 1915, [Olot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olot)–10 April 2009, [San Cugat del Vallés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Cugat_del_Vallés)) was a Spanish [Jesuit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesuit) and mathematician.[^o3]

:::

He fought on the Nationalist side in the [Spanish Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_Civil_War).[^o3]

In 1960, he was elected president of the [Royal Spanish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/65501555).[^o3]
