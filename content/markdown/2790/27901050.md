# Peter Koellner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Koellner"}

*Image: Peter Koellner 2006 (headshot).jpg.* Koellner in 2006

**Peter Koellner** is professor of philosophy at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 2003. His main areas of research are [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), specifically [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), and [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [philosophy of physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_physics), [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211), and [philosophy of language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_language).[^o2]

:::

In 2008 Koellner was awarded a Kurt Gödel Centenary Research Prize Fellowship. Currently, Koellner serves on the [American Philosophical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Association)'s Advisory Committee to the Eastern Division Program Committee in the area of Logic.[^o3]

According to a review by Pierre Matet on [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH), his joint paper with [Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071) *Incompatible Ω-Complete Theories* contains an illuminating discussion of the issues involved, which makes it recommended reading for anyone interested in modern set theory.[^o4]
