# Zariski ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zariski ring"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), a **Zariski ring** is a commutative [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/56129) [topological ring](wikipedia://en/60097) *A* whose topology is defined by an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) $\mathfrak a$ contained in the [Jacobson radical](wikipedia://en/57656), the intersection of all maximal ideals. They were introduced by (txt) under the name "semi-local ring" which now means [something different](wikipedia://en/977725), and named "Zariski rings" by (txt). Examples of Zariski rings are noetherian local rings with the topology induced by the maximal ideal, and [$\mathfrak a$-adic completions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completion_%28ring_theory%29) of Noetherian rings.

:::

Let *A* be a Noetherian topological ring with the topology defined by an ideal $\mathfrak a$. Then the following are equivalent.

- *A* is a Zariski ring.
- The completion $\widehat{A}$ is [faithfully flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faithfully_flat_module) over *A* (in general, it is only flat over *A*).
- Every maximal ideal is closed.
