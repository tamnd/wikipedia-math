## Calculus {#Calculus}

::: {#o47 .entry cue=list-entry name="Critical point"}

- [Critical point](wikipedia://en/1951424) ( [stationary point](wikipedia://en/885651)), any value *v* in the domain of a differentiable function of any real or complex variable, such that the derivative of *v* is 0 or undefined

:::

## Geometry {#Geometry}

::: {#o49 .entry cue=list-entry name="Antipodal point"}

- [Antipodal point](wikipedia://en/367577), the point diametrically opposite to another point on a sphere, such that a line drawn between them passes through the centre of the sphere and forms a true diameter

:::

::: {#o50 .entry cue=list-entry name="Conjugate point"}

- [Conjugate point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_point), any point that can almost be joined to another by a 1-parameter family of geodesics (e.g., the antipodes of a sphere, which are linkable by any meridian

:::

::: {#o51 .entry cue=list-entry name="Vertex (geometry)"}

- [Vertex (geometry)](wikipedia://en/11308417), a point that describes a corner or intersection of a geometric shape

:::

  - [Apex (geometry)](wikipedia://en/6022079), the vertex that is in some sense the highest of the figure to which it belongs

## Topology {#Topology}

::: {#o53 .entry cue=list-entry name="Adherent point"}

- [Adherent point](wikipedia://en/8140539), a point *x* in topological space *X* such that every open set containing *x* contains at least one point of a subset *A*

:::

::: {#o54 .entry cue=list-entry name="Condensation point"}

- [Condensation point](wikipedia://en/19472545), any point *p* of a subset *S* of a topological space, such that every open neighbourhood of *p* contains uncountably many points of *S*

:::

::: {#o55 .entry cue=list-entry name="Limit point"}

- [Limit point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_point), a set *S* in a topological space *X* is a point *x* (which is in X, but not necessarily in S) that can be approximated by points of *S*, since every neighbourhood of *x* with respect to the topology on *X* also contains a point of *S* other than *x* itself

:::

  - [Accumulation point](wikipedia://en/226505) (or [cluster point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluster_point)), a point *x* ∈ *X* of a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) (*x*<sub>*n*</sub>)<sub>*n* ∈ **N**</sub> for which there are, for every neighbourhood *V* of *x*, infinitely many natural numbers *n* such that *x<sub>n</sub>* ∈ *V*
