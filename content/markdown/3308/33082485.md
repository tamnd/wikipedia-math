# Suspension (dynamical systems)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suspension"}

**Suspension** is a construction passing from a map to a [flow](wikipedia://en/2619735). Namely, let $X$ be a [metric space](wikipedia://en/20018), $f:X\to X$ be a [continuous map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_map) and $r:X\to\mathbb{R}^+$ be a function (roof function or ceiling function) bounded away from 0. Consider the [quotient space](wikipedia://en/237213):

:::

$$X_r=\{(x,t):0\le t\le r(x),x\in X\}/(x,r(x))\sim(f(x),0).$$

The suspension of $(X,f)$ with roof function $r$ is the semiflow[^o2] $f_t:X_r\to X_r$ induced by the [time translation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_translation) $T_t:X\times\mathbb{R}\to X\times\mathbb{R}, (x,s)\mapsto (x,s+t)$.

If $r(x)\equiv 1$, then the quotient space is also called the [mapping torus](wikipedia://en/21034384) of $(X,f)$.
