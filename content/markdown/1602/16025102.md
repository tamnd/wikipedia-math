# Dataplot

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dataplot"}

**Dataplot** is a [public domain software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_domain_software) system for [scientific visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_visualization) and [statistical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_analysis). It was developed and is being maintained at the [National Institute of Standards and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institute_of_Standards_and_Technology).[^o3] Dataplot's [source code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_code) is available and in public domain.[^o4]

:::
