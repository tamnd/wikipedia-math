# Salomon Eduard Gubler

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Salomon Eduard Gubler"}

**Salomon Eduard Gubler** (7 July 1845 – 6 November 1921) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). With [Johann Heinrich Graf](wikipedia://en/56956105) he published *[Einleitung in Die Theorie Der Bessel'schen Funktionen](https://books.google.com/books?id=ueXASQAACAAJ) (A Treatise on the Theory of Bessel Functions)* in two volumes (1898–1900). He was the author of very appreciated textbooks on mathematics and numerous reports about the methodology and organization on mathematics teaching, and he was a member of the Swiss commission for the teaching of mathematics and founder of the Swiss association of teachers of mathematics.[^n1] His main research interest was the [Bessel functions](wikipedia://en/4700).[^n2]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Gubler graduated in the [university of Bern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bern) in 1870 as [Ludwig Schläfli](wikipedia://en/1452408)'s student. There are no records in the university about his doctoral thesis published in 1894.[^n3] He spent his academic career in secondary schools,[^n1] but it seems that he also taught in the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich).[^n2] He retired in 1914.
