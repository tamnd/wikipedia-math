# Herman Valentiner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Herman Valentiner"}

**Herman Valentiner** (8 May 1850 – 17 September 1913) was a Danish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced the [Valentiner group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valentiner_group) in 1889.[^o3]

:::

Valentiner earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1881 from the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) with a thesis on [space curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_curves), and took a teaching position. However, soon afterwards he moved to a Danish [life insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Life_insurance) company.[^o4]
