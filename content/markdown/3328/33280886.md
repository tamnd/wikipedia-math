# Wilhelm Grunwald

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Grunwald"}

**Wilhelm Grunwald** (15 July 1909 – 7 June 1989) was a German mathematician who introduced the [Grunwald–Wang theorem](wikipedia://en/20901695), though his original statement and proof of this contained a small error that was corrected by [Shianghao Wang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shianghao_Wang). He later left mathematics to become a science librarian, and was director of the [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) university library (Roquette 2005, p.29).

:::
