# Eugenio Bertini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eugenio Bertini"}

*Image: Eugenio Bertini.jpg.* Eugenio Bertini

**Eugenio Bertini** (8 November 1846 – 24 February 1933) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who introduced [Bertini's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertini's_theorem). He was born at [Forlì](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forlì) and died at [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa), [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy).

:::
