# Graded-symmetric algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Graded-symmetric algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), given a [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) *R*, the **graded-symmetric algebra** of a [graded *R*-module](wikipedia://en/103094#Graded_module) *M* is the [quotient](wikipedia://en/184635#For_algebras_over_a_ring) of the [tensor algebra](wikipedia://en/363628) of *M* by the [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) *I* generated by elements of the form:

:::

- $$xy - (-1)^{|x||y|}yx$$
- $x^2$ when |*x*| is [odd](wikipedia://en/143135)

for [homogeneous elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_element) *x*, *y* in *M* of degree |*x*|, |*y*|. By construction, a graded-symmetric algebra is [graded-commutative](wikipedia://en/52405825); i.e., $xy = (-1)^{|x||y|} yx$ and is universal for this.

In spite of the name, the notion is a common generalization of a [symmetric algebra](wikipedia://en/654098) and an [exterior algebra](wikipedia://en/221537): indeed, if *V* is a (non-graded) *R*-[module](wikipedia://en/276410), then the graded-symmetric algebra of *V* with trivial grading is the usual symmetric algebra of *V*. Similarly, the graded-symmetric algebra of the graded module with *V* in degree one and zero elsewhere is the exterior algebra of *V*.
