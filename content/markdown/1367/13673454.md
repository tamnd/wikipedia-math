# Conference Board of the Mathematical Sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conference Board of the Mathematical Sciences"}

The **Conference Board of the Mathematical Sciences** (**CBMS**) is an [umbrella organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umbrella_organization) of seventeen professional societies in the mathematical sciences in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States). It and its member societies are recognized by the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) as the national mathematical societies for their country.[^o2]

:::

The CBMS was founded in 1960 as the successor organization to the six-organization Policy Committee for Mathematics (founded by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) as the War Policy Committee in 1942) and the 1958 Conference Organization of the Mathematical Sciences.[^o3][^o4] As well as representing US mathematics at the IMU, it acts as a communication channel between its member societies and the US Government, and coordinates joint projects of its member societies.[^o5]
