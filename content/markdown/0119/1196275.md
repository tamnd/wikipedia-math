# Pentagonal orthocupolarotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal orthocupolarotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagonal orthocupolarotunda** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{32}$). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by joining a [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) ($J_{5}$) and a [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491) ($J_{6}$) along their [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) bases, matching the pentagonal faces. A 36-degree rotation of one of the halves before the joining yields a [pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1196281) ($J_{33}$).

:::

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faces_%28geometry%29) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\frac{5}{12}\left(11+5\sqrt{5}\right)a^3\approx9.24181...a^3$$

$$A=\left(5+\frac{1}{4}\sqrt{1900+490\sqrt{5}+210\sqrt{75+30\sqrt{5}}}\right)a^2\approx23.5385...a^2$$
