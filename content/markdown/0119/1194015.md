# Decision analysis cycle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Decision analysis cycle"}

The **decision analysis** (**DA**) **cycle** is the top-level procedure for carrying out a [decision analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_analysis). Decision analysis (**DA**) is the [discipline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discipline) comprising the [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), [methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodology), and [professional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional) practice necessary to address important [decisions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_making) in a formal manner.

:::

The traditional decision analysis cycle consists of four phases:

- basis development
- deterministic sensitivity analysis
- [probabilistic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_analysis)
- basis appraisal.

The diagram below depicts the decision analysis cycle:[^o2]

*Image: Dacycle.png.* The decision analysis cycle

A revised form of the cycle consists of an attention-focusing method followed by a [decision method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_method), each of which is composed of three stages:

- [formulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formulation_%28decision_analysis%29)
- [evaluation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evaluation_%28decision_analysis%29)
- [appraisal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appraisal_%28decision_analysis%29).
