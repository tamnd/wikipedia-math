# Rudvalis group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudvalis group"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Rudvalis group** *Ru* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

145,926,144,000 = 2<sup>14</sup>3<sup>3</sup>5<sup>3</sup>71329

≈ 1.

## History {#History}

*Ru* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was found by and constructed by . Its [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) has order 2, and its [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) is trivial.

In 1982 [Robert Griess](wikipedia://en/5034215) showed that *Ru* cannot be a [subquotient](wikipedia://en/1639294) of the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422).[^o3] Thus it is one of the 6 sporadic groups called the [pariahs](wikipedia://en/16569533).

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The Rudvalis group acts as a rank 3 permutation group on 4060 points, with one point stabilizer being the [Ree group](wikipedia://en/1739637) <sup>2</sup>*F*<sub>4</sub>(2), the automorphism group of the [Tits group](wikipedia://en/606719). This representation implies a [strongly regular graph](wikipedia://en/1399856) srg(4060, 2304, 1328, 1280). That is, each vertex has 2304 neighbors and 1755 non-neighbors, any two adjacent vertices have 1328 common neighbors, while any two non-adjacent ones have 1280 .

Its [double cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_covering_group) acts on a 28-dimensional lattice over the [Gaussian integers](wikipedia://en/48628). The lattice has 4×4060 minimal vectors; if minimal vectors are identified whenever one is 1, *i*, –1, or –*i* times another, then the 4060 equivalence classes can be identified with the points of the rank 3 permutation representation. Reducing this lattice modulo the [principal ideal](wikipedia://en/145126)

$$(1 + i)\$$

gives an action of the Rudvalis group on a 28-dimensional vector space over the field $\mathbb F_2$ with 2 elements. Duncan (2006) used the 28-dimensional lattice to construct a [vertex operator algebra](wikipedia://en/1287577) acted on by the double cover.

Alternatively, the double cover can be defined abstractly, by starting with the graph and lifting Ru to 2Ru in the double cover 2A<sub>4060</sub>. This is because 1 of the conjugacy classes of [involutions](wikipedia://en/293450#Group_theory) does not fix any points. Such an involution partitions the 4060 points of the graph into 2030 pairs, which can be regarded as 1015 double transpositions in the [alternating group](wikipedia://en/49726) A<sub>4060</sub>. Since 1015 is odd, these involutions are lifted to order 4 elements in the double cover 2A<sub>4060</sub>. For more information, see [Covering groups of the alternating and symmetric groups](wikipedia://en/25118486).

(Parrott 1976) characterized the Rudvalis group by the centralizer of a central involution. (Aschbacher Smith 2004) gave another characterization as part of their identification of the Rudvalis group as one of the [quasithin groups](wikipedia://en/12939181).

The degrees of [irreducible representations](wikipedia://en/385982) of the Rudvalis group *Ru* are 1, 378, 378, 406, 783, 3276, 3654, 20475, ... .

:::
