# Pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{33}$). Like the [pentagonal orthocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1196275) ($J_{32}$), it can be constructed by joining a [pentagonal cupola](wikipedia://en/1131073) ($J_{5}$) and a [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491) ($J_{6}$) along their [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) bases. The difference is that in this solid, the two halves are rotated 36 degrees with respect to one another.

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faces_%28geometry%29) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\frac{5}{12}\left(11+5\sqrt{5}\right)a^3\approx9.24181...a^3$$

$$A= \left(5+\frac{15}{4}\sqrt{3}+\frac{7}{4}\sqrt{25+10\sqrt{5}}\right) a^2\approx23.5385...a^2$$
