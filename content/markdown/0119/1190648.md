# Abraham–Minkowski controversy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abraham–Minkowski controversy"}

The **Abraham–Minkowski controversy** is a [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) debate concerning [electromagnetic momentum](wikipedia://en/20431#In_electromagnetics) within [dielectric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dielectric) media.[^o2][^o3] Two equations were first suggested by [Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537) (1908)[^o4] and [Max Abraham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Abraham) (1909)[^o5][^o6] for this momentum. They predict different values, from which the name of the controversy derives.[^o7] Experimental support has been claimed for both.[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11] Physicist [Vitaly Ginzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vitaly_Ginzburg) called it a perpetual problem.[^o3]

:::

The two points of view have different physical interpretations and thus neither need be more correct than the other.[^o12] [David J. Griffiths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_J._Griffiths) argues that, in the presence of matter, only the total [stress–energy tensor](wikipedia://en/70671) carries unambiguous physical significance; how one apportions it between an "electromagnetic" part and a "matter" part depends on context and convenience.[^o13]

Several papers have claimed to have resolved this controversy.[^o14][^o15][^o16][^o17][^o18][^o19][^o20]

The controversy is still claimed to have importance in [physics beyond the Standard Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physics_beyond_the_Standard_Model) where electrodynamics gets modifications, like in the presence of [axions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axion).[^o21]

## Equations {#Equations}

In SI units, the equations describe electromagnetic momentum and are given by[^o3]

::: {#o23 .table cue=table}

| Minkoskwi | Abraham |
|---|---|
| $\textbf{P} = \textbf{D} \times \textbf{B}$ | $\textbf{P} = \frac{\textbf{E} \times \textbf{H}}{c^2}$ |

:::

where:

- $\textbf{P}$ is the density of electromagnetic momentum
- $\textbf{D}$ is the [electric displacement field](wikipedia://en/1191101)
- $\textbf{B}$ is the [magnetic flux density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_flux_density)
- $\textbf{E}$ is the [electric field](wikipedia://en/41092)
- $\textbf{H}$ is the [magnetizing field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetizing_field)
- $c^2$ is the [speed of light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_light) squared

### Consequences {#Consequences}

According to Abraham, the momentum of an [electromagnetic wave](wikipedia://en/9426) in a medium should be slower. Minkowski predicts that the momentum should be larger.[^o25]

For a finite-lenght [optical fiber](wikipedia://en/3372377) emitting light at one of its ends, it is unclear if the fiber recoils backs in the direction of the light emission (as predicted by Abraham) or if the fiber is pulled in the direction of emission (as predicted by Minkowski).[^o26]
