# Mark Krein

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mark Krein"}

**Mark Grigorievich Krein** (Марк Григо́рьевич Крейн; Марко́ Григо́рович Крейн; 3 April 1907 – 17 October 1989) was a Soviet mathematician, one of the major figures of the Soviet school of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). He is known for works in [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) (in close connection with concrete problems coming from [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)), [the problem of moments](wikipedia://en/1244992), [classical analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_analysis) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

He was born in [Kiev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiev), in the Russian Empire, leaving home at age 17 to go to [Odessa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odessa). He had a difficult academic career, not completing his first degree and constantly being troubled by [antisemitic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antisemitic) discrimination. His supervisor was [Nikolai Chebotaryov](wikipedia://en/3033696).

He was awarded the [Wolf Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/714278) in 1982 (jointly with [Hassler Whitney](wikipedia://en/391794)), but was not allowed to attend the ceremony.

[David Milman](wikipedia://en/5210923), [Mark Naimark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Naimark), [Israel Gohberg](wikipedia://en/19685973), [Vadym Adamyan](wikipedia://en/25037059), [Mikhail Livsic](wikipedia://en/58614552) and other known mathematicians were his students.

He died in Odessa.

## Legacy {#Legacy}

On 14 January 2008, the memorial plaque of Mark Krein was unveiled on the main administration building of [Odesa University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odesa_University) in Ukraine.
