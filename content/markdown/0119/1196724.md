# O'Nan group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="O'Nan group"}

In the area of [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **O'Nan group** *O'N* or **O'Nan–Sims group** is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

460,815,505,920 = 2<sup>9</sup>3<sup>4</sup>57<sup>3</sup>111931 ≈ 5.

## History {#History}

*O'N* is one of the 26 [sporadic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_groups) and was found by (txt) in a study of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447) with a [Sylow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Ludwig_Mejdell_Sylow) 2-[subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of "[Alperin](wikipedia://en/25403965) type", meaning [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/12397) to a Sylow 2-Subgroup of a group of type (Z/2<sup>*n*</sup>Z ×Z/2<sup>*n*</sup>Z ×Z/2<sup>*n*</sup>Z).PSL<sub>3</sub>(**F**<sub>2</sub>). In O'Nan's original paper, the definition of Alperin type required that *n* ≥ 2, because the *n* = 1 case had already been classified. If this restriction is removed, then the following simple groups have Sylow 2-subgroups of Alperin type:

- For the [Chevalley group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevalley_group) *G*<sub>2</sub>(q), if q is congruent to 3 or 5 mod 8, *n* = 1 and the extension does not [split](wikipedia://en/138378).
- For the [Steinberg group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinberg_group_%28Lie_theory%29) <sup>3</sup>*D*<sub>4</sub>(q), if q is congruent to 3 or 5 mod 8, *n* = 1 and the extension does not split.
- For the [alternating group](wikipedia://en/49726) A<sub>8</sub>, *n* = 1 and the extension splits.
- For the O'Nan group, *n* = 2 and the extension does not split.
- For the [Higman-Sims group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higman-Sims_group), *n* = 2 and the extension splits.

The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) has order 3, and its [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2. Griess showed that *O'N* cannot be a [subquotient](wikipedia://en/1639294) of the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422). Thus it is one of the 6 sporadic groups called the [pariahs](wikipedia://en/16569533).

::: {#o5 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

(Ryba 1988) showed that its triple cover has two 45-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) representations over the field with 7 elements, exchanged by an outer automorphism.

The degrees of [irreducible representations](wikipedia://en/385982) of the O'Nan group are 1, 10944, 13376, 13376, 25916, ... .

:::

## O'Nan moonshine {#O'Nan_moonshine}

In 2017 John F. R. Duncan, Michael H. Mertens, and Ken Ono proved theorems that establish an analogue of [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) for the O'Nan group. Their results "reveal a role for the O'Nan pariah group as a provider of hidden [symmetry](wikipedia://en/2714149) to [quadratic forms](wikipedia://en/251478) and [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225)." The O'Nan moonshine results "also represent the intersection of moonshine theory with the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905), which, since its inception in the 1960s, has become a driving force for research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)." (Duncan Mertens Ono 2017, article 670).

An informal description of these developments was written by (txt) in *[Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976)*.
