# Harada–Norton group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harada–Norton group"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Harada–Norton group** *HN* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

273,030,912,000,000

\= 2<sup>14</sup>3<sup>6</sup>5<sup>6</sup>71119

≈ 3.

## History and properties {#History_and_properties}

*HN* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was found by (txt) and (txt).

Its [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) is trivial and its [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) has order 2.

*HN* has an involution whose [centralizer](wikipedia://en/145661) is of the form 2.HS.2, where HS is the [Higman-Sims group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higman-Sims_group) (which is how Harada found it).

The prime 5 plays a special role in the group. For example, it centralizes an element of order 5 in the [Monster group](wikipedia://en/47422) (which is how Norton found it), and as a result acts naturally on a [vertex operator algebra](wikipedia://en/1287577) over the field with 5 elements (Lux Noeske Ryba 2008). This implies that it acts on a 133 dimensional algebra over **F**<sub>5</sub> with a commutative but nonassociative product, analogous to the [Griess algebra](wikipedia://en/728596) (Ryba 1996).

The full normalizer of a 5A element in the Monster group is (D<sub>10</sub> × HN).2, so HN centralizes 5 involutions alongside the 5-cycle. These involutions are centralized by the [Baby monster group](wikipedia://en/324799), which therefore contains HN as a subgroup.

## Generalized monstrous moonshine {#Generalized_monstrous_moonshine}

Conway and Norton suggested in their 1979 paper that [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) is not limited to the monster, but that similar phenomena may be found for other groups. Larissa Queen and others subsequently found that one can construct the expansions of many Hauptmoduln from simple combinations of dimensions of sporadic groups. To recall, the prime number 5 plays a special role in the group and for *HN*, the relevant McKay-Thompson series is $T_{5A}(\tau)$ where one can set the constant term a(0) = −6 (),

$$\begin{aligned} j_{5A}(\tau) &= T_{5A}(\tau)-6\\ &= \left(\frac{\eta(\tau)}{\eta(5\tau)}\right)^{6}+5^3 \left(\frac{\eta(5\tau)}{\eta(\tau)}\right)^6\\ &= \frac{1}{q} - 6 + 134q + 760q^2 + 3345q^3 + 12256q^4 + 39350q^5 + \dots \end{aligned}$$

and *η*(*τ*) is the [Dedekind eta function](wikipedia://en/447020).
