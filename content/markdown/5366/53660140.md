# Jean-Éric Pin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Éric Pin"}

**Jean-Éric Pin** is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) known for his contributions to the algebraic [automata theory](wikipedia://en/103356) and [semigroup theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semigroup_theory). He is a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) research director.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Pin earned his undergraduate degree from [ENS Cachan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ENS_Cachan) in 1976 and his doctorate (Doctorat d'état) from the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1981. Since 1988 he has been a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) research director at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University). In the years 1992–2006 he was a professor at [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique).

Pin is a member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) (2011)[^o4] and an [EATCS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EATCS) fellow (2014).[^o5] In 2018, Pin became the first recipient of the [Salomaa](wikipedia://en/11157003) Prize in Automata Theory, Formal Languages, and Related Topics.[^o6]

## Notable Work {#Notable_Work}

Pin is the author of the prominent textbook *Varieties of Formal Languages* on [automata theory](wikipedia://en/103356) and [formal language theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_language_theory).
