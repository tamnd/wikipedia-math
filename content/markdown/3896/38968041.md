# Abe Gelbart

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Abe Gelbart"}

**Abraham Markham Gelbart** (December 2, 1911 – September 7, 1994) was an American mathematician, the founding dean of the Belfer Graduate School of Science at [Yeshiva University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yeshiva_University) and the namesake of the International Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University) in [Ramat Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat_Gan), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gelbart was born to a Polish immigrant family in [Paterson, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paterson,_New_Jersey).[^o6] He dropped out of high school at age 14, but studied mathematics at the [New York Public Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Public_Library), where he came under the mentorship of Yeshiva mathematician [Jekuthiel Ginsburg](wikipedia://en/21074637). Despite not having a high school diploma, he was accepted to [Dalhousie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalhousie_University) at age 23, and earned a bachelor's degree there in 1938. He did his graduate studies at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), earning a doctorate in 1940 under the supervision of [Norbert Wiener](wikipedia://en/63185).[^o3][^o4]

After taking non-tenure-track positions at [North Carolina State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University), [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), and [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA)'s [Langley Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langley_Research_Center), Gelbart took a faculty position at [Syracuse University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Syracuse_University) in 1943. He remained there until 1958, when he moved to Yeshiva, taking the position and the editorship of *[Scripta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/13550249)* both formerly held by his mentor Ginsburg. He retired from Yeshiva in 1979, and took a position as Distinguished Professor at [Bard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bard_College), where he remained until 1992. He was also a trustee of Bar-Ilan university.[^o4] His doctoral students include [Robert Finn](wikipedia://en/46487145). Gelbart died from complications following [cardiovascular surgery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiac_surgery).

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

With [Lipman Bers](wikipedia://en/9335972), Gelbart founded the theory of [pseudoanalytic functions](wikipedia://en/30411190) in [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034).[^o3][^o4][^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Gelbart was given an honorary doctorate by Dalhousie University in 1972, and by Bar-Ilan University in 1985. In 1981, Bard College gave him the Bard Medal.[^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Gelbart is the father of [Stephen Gelbart](wikipedia://en/45235502), also a noted mathematician.[^o8] He is also the uncle of mathematician [Charles Fefferman](wikipedia://en/502831).[^o13]
