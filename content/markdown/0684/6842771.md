# Markus Rost

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Markus Rost"}

**Markus Rost** is a German mathematician who works at the intersection of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384). He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2002 in Beijing, China.[^o2] He is a professor at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University).

:::

He is known for his work on [norm varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_varieties) (a key part in the proof of the [Bloch–Kato conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloch–Kato_conjecture)) and for the [Rost invariant](wikipedia://en/39486121) (a cohomological invariant with values in [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) of degree 3). Together with [J.-P. Serre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.-P._Serre) he is one of the cofounders of the theory of cohomological invariants of [linear algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/395885). He has also made numerous contributions to the theory of [torsors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torsor), [quadratic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadratic_forms), [central simple algebras](wikipedia://en/404082), [Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371) (the Rost-Serre invariant), [exceptional groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exceptional_group), and [essential dimension](wikipedia://en/16950168).

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
