# Zoltán Füredi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zoltán Füredi"}

**Zoltán Füredi** ([Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary), 21 May 1954) is a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_people) mathematician, working in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), mainly in [discrete geometry](wikipedia://en/386468) and [extremal combinatorics](wikipedia://en/2675917). He was a student of [Gyula O. H. Katona](wikipedia://en/9211532). He is a corresponding member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) (2004). He is a research professor of the [Rényi Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the Hungarian Academy of Sciences, and a professor at the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign) (UIUC).

:::

Füredi received his [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) degree in mathematics in 1981 from the Hungarian Academy of Sciences.

## Some results {#Some_results}

- In infinitely many cases he determined the maximum number of edges in a [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) with no [*C*<sub>4</sub>](wikipedia://en/878962).[^o4]
- With [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) he proved that for some *c*>1, there are *c*<sup>*d*</sup> points in *d*-dimensional space such that all triangles formed from those points are [acute](wikipedia://en/30654#By_internal_angles).
- With [Imre Bárány](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imre_Bárány) he proved that no [polynomial time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_time) algorithm determines the volume of [convex bodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_bodies) in dimension *d* within a multiplicative error *d*<sup>*d*</sup>.
- He proved that there are at most $O(n\log n)$ unit distances in a convex *n*-gon.[^o5]
- In a paper written with coauthors he solved the Hungarian [lottery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lottery) problem.[^o6]
- With [Ilona Palásti](wikipedia://en/58479429) he found the best known lower bounds on the [orchard-planting problem](wikipedia://en/29600081) of finding sets of points with many 3-point lines.[^o7]
- He proved an upper bound on the ratio between the fractional matching number and the [matching number in a hypergraph](wikipedia://en/63030703).[^o8]
