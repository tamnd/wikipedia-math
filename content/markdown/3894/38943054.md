# Restricted root system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Restricted root system"}

Homogeneous spaces In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **restricted root systems**, sometimes called **relative root systems**, are the [root systems](wikipedia://en/277087) associated with a [symmetric space](wikipedia://en/1642877). The associated finite reflection group is called the **restricted Weyl group**. The restricted root system of a symmetric space and its dual can be identified. For symmetric spaces of noncompact type arising as homogeneous spaces of a semisimple [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945), the restricted root system and its Weyl group are related to the [Iwasawa decomposition](wikipedia://en/1139148) of the Lie group.

:::
