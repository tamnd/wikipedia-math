# Dennis Barden

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dennis Barden"}

**Dennis Barden** is a mathematician at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) working in the fields of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).[^o3] He is known for his classification of the simply connected compact [5-manifolds](wikipedia://en/7763362)[^o4] and, together with [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679) and [John R. Stallings](wikipedia://en/18729795), for having proved the [s-cobordism theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-cobordism_theorem).[^o5] Barden received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from Cambridge in 1964 under the supervision of [C. T. C. Wall](wikipedia://en/2613478).

:::

## Academic Positions {#Academic_Positions}

Barden is a Life Fellow of [Girton College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girton_College,_Cambridge)[^o8] and emeritus fellow of [Pembroke College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembroke_College,_Cambridge). In 1991, he became Director of Studies for mathematics at Pembroke College, succeeding [Raymond Lickorish](wikipedia://en/11177096). He held the position until Michaelmas 2003, and in his time saw a great increase in the number of applicants for mathematics, with consistently high performances in [Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tripos) exams.[^o9] He remains an active [supervisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutorial_system) at Pembroke and Girton College.
