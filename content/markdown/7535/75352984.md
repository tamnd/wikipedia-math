# Dawid Kielak

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dawid Kielak"}

**Dawid Kielak** (Polish: ') is a Polish mathematician and Professor of Pure Mathematics at the [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850) of the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) as well as a Tutorial Fellow of [Hertford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertford_College,_Oxford). His research interests include [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and the theory of [group rings](wikipedia://en/349223).[^o3]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

He completed his undergraduate degree in [St. Peter's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Peter's_College,_Oxford) and his PhD degree in [Magdalen College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdalen_College,_Oxford). He subsequently continued his career in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) (2012), [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn) (2012–2015), and [Bielefeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld) (2015–2020) before returning to Oxford where he joined the university's Mathematical Institute.[^o5]

He is a member of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).[^o3]

He is known for formulating a mathematical theorem allowing for the recognition of spaces, which after minor modifications, behave like [spacetime](wikipedia://en/28758) in [Einstein](wikipedia://en/736)'s [theory of relativity](wikipedia://en/30001).[^o6]

In 2022, he was awarded the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for "his striking, original and fundamental contributions to the fields of [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), and in particular for his work on [automorphism groups](wikipedia://en/56661772) of [discrete groups](wikipedia://en/468536) and fibrings of manifolds and groups".[^o7]

In 2023, he became the co-recipient of the Frontiers of Science Award together with Marek Kaluba ([Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology)) and Piotr W. Nowak ([Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences)) for their research in the field of [algebraic](wikipedia://en/38801) and [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732). The award was presented at the inaugural International Congress of Basic Science (ICBS) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing).[^o8]

In September 2023 Kielak was awarded a [Title of Distinction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Titles_of_distinction_awarded_by_the_University_of_Oxford) of Professor of Pure Mathematics by the University of Oxford.[^o9]
