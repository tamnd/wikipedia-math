# Boué–Dupuis formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boué–Dupuis formula"}

In [stochastic calculus](wikipedia://en/228001), the **Boué–Dupuis formula** is [variational](wikipedia://en/171882) representation for [Wiener functionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiener_functional). The representation has application in finding [large deviation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_deviation_theory) asymptotics.

:::

The theorem was proven in 1998 by [Michelle Boué](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michelle_Boué) and [Paul Dupuis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Dupuis_%28Mathematician%29).[^o2] In 2000[^o3] the result was generalized to infinite-dimensional [Brownian motions](wikipedia://en/4436) and in 2009[^o4] extended to [abstract Wiener spaces](wikipedia://en/6592746).

::: {#o6 .statement cue=named-heading name="Boué–Dupuis formula"}

## Boué–Dupuis formula {#Boué–Dupuis_formula}

Let $C([0,1],\mathbb{R}^d)$ be the [classical Wiener space](wikipedia://en/6810363) and $B$ be a $d$-dimensional standard Brownian motion. Then for all [bounded](wikipedia://en/311509) and [measurable](wikipedia://en/44055) functions $f:C([0,1],\mathbb{R}^d)\to\mathbb{R}$ we have the following variational representation

$$-\log \mathbb{E}\left[e^{-f(B)}\right]=\inf\limits_{V}\mathbb{E}\left[\frac{1}{2}\int_0^1\|V_t\|^2\mathrm{d}t + f\left(B+\int_0^{\cdot}V_t\mathrm{d}t\right)\right],$$

where:

- The [expectation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expectation_value) is with respect to the [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325) of $B$.
- The [infimum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infimum) runs over all [processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_Process) which are [progressively measurable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Progressively_measurable) with respect to the [augmented filtration](wikipedia://en/57854817#Augmented_filtration) $\mathcal{F}^B$ generated by $B$
- $\|\cdot \|$ denotes the $d$-dimensional [Euclidean norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_norm).

:::
