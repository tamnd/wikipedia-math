# Repopulation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Repopulation"}

**Repopulation** is the phenomenon of increasing the numerical size of human inhabitants or organisms of a particular species after they had almost gone extinct.

:::

## Organisms {#Organisms}

An example of an organism that has repopulated after being on the brink of extinction is the [Dryococelus australis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dryococelus_australis).[^o3]

## Humans {#Humans}

The repopulation of humans after a catastrophic event is a [hypothetical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypothetical) concept that sometimes features in fictional as well as traditional literature.[^o5] In this scenario, only two members of the opposite sex are survivors after some sort of calamity that has happened.[^o6] This couple, the last two humans on Earth, ends up reproducing and their offspring reproduce with one another, until after a certain amount of time, the planet [Earth](wikipedia://en/9228) has a sizeable number of people.[^o7]
