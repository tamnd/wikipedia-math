# Jerzy Browkin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jerzy Browkin"}

**Jerzy Browkin** (5 November 1934 – 23 November 2015) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), studying mainly [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705). He was a professor at the Institute of Mathematics of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1994, together with Juliusz Brzeziński, he formulated the *n*-conjecture—a version of the [*abc* conjecture](wikipedia://en/174108) involving *n* > 2 integers.[^o3]
