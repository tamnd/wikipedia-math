# Monad (nonstandard analysis)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monad"}

In [nonstandard analysis](wikipedia://en/21708), a **monad** or also a **halo** is the set of points infinitesimally close to a given point.[^o2][^o3]

:::

Given a [hyperreal number](wikipedia://en/51429) *x* in **R**<sup>∗</sup>, the monad of *x* is the set

$$\text{monad}(x)=\{y\in \mathbb{R}^* \mid x-y \text{ is infinitesimal}\}.$$

If *x* is finite (limited), the unique real number in the monad of *x* is called the [standard part](wikipedia://en/19145800) of *x*.[^o4]
