# Papyrus Oxyrhynchus 90

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Papyrus Oxyrhynchus 90"}

**Papyrus Oxyrhynchus 90** (P. Oxy. 90) is a receipt for the payment of wheat, written in [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language). The manuscript was written on [papyrus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papyrus) in the form of a sheet. It was discovered in the Egyptian city of [Oxyrhynchus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxyrhynchus). The document was written between 179–180. Currently it is housed in the [British Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Library) (761) in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London).[^o2] It is known also as **P. Lond. 3 p. XXXII no. 761**.[^o3]

:::

## Description {#Description}

*Image: P.oxy.90.possible.cryptogram.png.* The possible cryptogram from P. Oxy. 90

The fragment contains a receipt, and is similar to [Papyrus Oxyrhynchus 89](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Papyrus_Oxyrhynchus_89). It was written by an unknown author. It states "that Clarus, ex-*[agoranomus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agoranomus),* had deposited 8 *artabae* 4 *[choenices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek_units_of_measurement#Volume)* in the public granary". According to [Grenfell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard_Grenfell) and [Hunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Surridge_Hunt), the last two lines are "written in Greek characters, but cannot be construed as Greek. Since they do not appear to be Graecized [demotic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demotic_Egyptian_script), they are possibly a [cryptogram](wikipedia://en/69794) of some kind." The measurements of the fragment are 103 by 80 mm.[^o5]

It was discovered by Grenfell and Hunt in 1897 in Oxyrhynchus. The text was published by Grenfell and Hunt in 1898.[^o5] The fragment was examined by [Frederic G. Kenyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederic_G._Kenyon) (1907), who attempted to decipher it to no avail.[^o3]

## Text {#Text}

μεμέ(τρηκεν)(\*) ἰς̣(\*) τὸ δη(μόσιον) (πυροῦ) γενή(ματος) τοῦ διελ(θόντος) ιθ (ἔτους) Αὐρηλίων Ἀντωνίνου καὶ Κομμόδου Κ\[α\]ισάρων τῶν κυρίων (διὰ) σι(τολόγων)(\*) λιβὸ(ς) τοπ(αρχίας) \[Σ\]ερύ(φεως)(\*) τόπ(ων) Κλάρος Διδύμου ἀγορανομή(σας) \[θ\]έμ(α) ἀρταβαι(\*) ὀκτο(\*) χ(οίνικας) δ, (γίνονται) (πυροῦ) (ἀρτάβαι) η \\χ(οίνικες) δ./ Διογ(ένης) σι(τολόγος) σεσημ(είωμαι).
