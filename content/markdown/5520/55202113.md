# Stanisław Krystyn Zaremba

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stanisław Krystyn Zaremba"}

**Stanisław Krystyn Zaremba** (August 15, 1903, in Cracow – January 14, 1990, in Aberystwyth, Wales) was a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), climber, and mountaineer. He was the son of [Stanisław Zaremba](wikipedia://en/1028345) (1863–1942), also a mathematician.

:::

Zaremba is known for his contributions to [low-discrepancy sequences](wikipedia://en/478601), low-discrepancy sets of points, and their application to [Quasi-Monte Carlo methods](wikipedia://en/487599) for numerical integration. He is one of the namesakes of the [Hlawka–Zaremba formula](wikipedia://en/478601#The_formula_of_Hlawka–Zaremba) in the theory of low-discrepancy sequences.

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Zaremba studied mathematics at the [Jagiellonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jagiellonian_University) (1921–1924, 1926–1929) and in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) (1924–26). After his studies he was an assistant at [Vilnius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University) and since 1936 he was an associate professor at Jagiellonian University. He then worked as a lecturer in Stalinabad (nowadays [Dushanbe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dushanbe) in [Tajikistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tajikistan)), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England), [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales), [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada), eventually settling in Wales, where he died in 1990. He visited Poland well into his old age, lecturing at the Jagiellonian University in 1981.

## Mountaineering {#Mountaineering}

Zaremba was an active climber in the years 1925–1927, especially during the winter. He is the author of many first ascents in the [Tatra Mountains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatra_Mountains). The new routes in the summer were, among others, on Little Kozi Wierch, Wieliczka Peak, Frog Turtle Muster, Mięguszowiecki Peak, Durny Peak. In winter he made his way to Hruby Wierch, Zadni Kościelec, Jaworowy Peak. Apart from the Tatras, he also climbed in the [Alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alps), the [Pyrenees](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyrenees) and the mountains of [Lebanon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebanon), [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran), Wales and Tajikistan.

He was the editor of the magazine *Taternik* and an honorary member of the Polish Alpine Association.

One of the [Wrocław's dwarfs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrocław's_dwarfs) (Alpinki group) is named Zarembek in his honor.
