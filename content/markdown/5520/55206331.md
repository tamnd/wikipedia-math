# James Moffat (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Moffat"}

**James Moffat** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was a researcher for the Ministry of Defence during the 1982 [Falklands War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falklands_War).[^o4] He wrote *Complexity Theory and Network Centric Warfare*,[^o5] which has 275 [scholarly citations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citation_count).[^o6]

:::

Moffat is currently Professor of Physics at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen), where he studies [quantum gravity](wikipedia://en/25312). He has published 135 articles. He is a recipient of the Napier Medal in Mathematics and the President’s Medal of the ORS; the 'nobel medal in analytics'. He is also a Fellow of OR, a [Fellow of the Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Institute_of_Mathematics_and_its_Applications), and a [Chartered Mathematician](wikipedia://en/1159333). His contributions to the literature cited 560 times include new theories for [Loop Quantum Gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loop_Quantum_Gravity) based on the Mathematics of [Operator Algebras](wikipedia://en/455987).[^o7]

Moffat was an early writer on the topic of the *Agile Organization*.[^o8] [Business agility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Business_agility), generally, had been discussed before, but agility, specifically in the context of military organizations, was a new field in 2005.
