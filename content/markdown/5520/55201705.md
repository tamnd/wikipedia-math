# Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics"}

***Mathematical Methods of Classical Mechanics*** (title of the original in Russian: ***Математические методы классической механики***) is a 1974 textbook by mathematician [Vladimir I. Arnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_I._Arnold). Originally written in Russian, an English translation was produced in 1978 by [A. Weinstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._Weinstein) and [K. Vogtmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vogtmann).[^o3] It is aimed at [graduate students](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_student).

:::

## Russian original and translations {#Russian_original_and_translations}

The original Russian first edition *Математические методы классической механики* was published in 1974 by [Наука](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nauka_%28publisher%29). A second edition was published in 1979, and a third in 1989. The book has since been translated into a number of other languages, including French, German, Japanese and Mandarin.

## Reviews {#Reviews}

The *[Bulletin of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/2668304)* said, "The \[book\] under review \[...\] written by a distinguished mathematician \[...is one of\] the first textbooks \[to\] successfully to present to students of mathematics and physics, \[sic\] classical mechanics in a modern setting."[^o7]

A book review in the journal *Celestial Mechanics* said, "In summary, the author has succeeded in producing a mathematical synthesis of the science of dynamics. The book is well presented and beautifully translated \[...\] Arnold's book is pure poetry; one does not simply read it, one enjoys it."[^o8]
