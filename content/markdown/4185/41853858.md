# Generalized iterative scaling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Generalized iterative scaling"}

In [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), **generalized iterative scaling** (**GIS**) and **improved iterative scaling** (**IIS**) are two early [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) used to fit [log-linear models](wikipedia://en/15689191),[^o2] notably [multinomial logistic regression](wikipedia://en/4433814) (MaxEnt) [classifiers](wikipedia://en/1579244) and extensions of it such as [MaxEnt Markov models](wikipedia://en/27900233)[^o3] and [conditional random fields](wikipedia://en/4118276). These algorithms have been largely surpassed by gradient-based methods such as [L-BFGS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-BFGS)[^o4] and [coordinate descent](wikipedia://en/33837524) algorithms.[^o5]

:::
