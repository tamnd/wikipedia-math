# Joint Statistical Meetings

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joint Statistical Meetings"}

The **Joint Statistical Meetings** (**JSM**) is a [professional conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_conference)/[academic conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_conference) for [statisticians](wikipedia://en/48661) and [data scientists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_scientist) held annually every year since 1840 (usually in August). Billed as "the largest gathering of statisticians held in North America", JSM has attracted over 5,000 participants in recent years. The following statistical societies are designated as official JSM partners:

:::

- the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA)
- the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (IMS)
- two regions of the [International Biometric Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Biometric_Society) (IBS)
  - the Eastern North American Region (ENAR)
  - the Western North American Region (WNAR)
- the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327) (SSC)
- [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) (ISBA)
- [International Chinese Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Chinese_Statistical_Association) (ICSA)
- [International Indian Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Indian_Statistical_Association) (IISA)
- [Korean International Statistical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korean_International_Statistical_Society) (KISS)

The founding members of JSM were the ASA, IMS, IBS, and SSC.[^o2]

In addition to committee meetings, JSM activities include

- career placement services
- [continuing education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuing_education) courses
- [oral presentations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oral_presentation)
- [panel sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panel_discussion)
- [plenary sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plenary_session)
- [poster sessions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poster_session)
