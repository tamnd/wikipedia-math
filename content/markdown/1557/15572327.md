# Statistical Society of Canada

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Statistical Society of Canada"}

The **Statistical Society of Canada** (abbreviated as **SSC**; Société statistique du Canada) is a [professional organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_organization) and [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) whose mission is to promote the use and development of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934).

:::

Its objectives are

- to make the general public aware of the value of statistical thought, the importance of this science and the contribution of [statisticians](wikipedia://en/48661) to Canadian society;
- to ensure that decisions that could have a major impact on Canadian society are based on relevant data, interpreted properly using statistics;
- to promote the pursuit of excellence in training and statistical practice in Canada;
- to encourage improvements in statistical methodology;
- to maintain a sense of belonging within the profession, and to promote dialogue among theoreticians and practitioners of statistics.

Each year the SSC awards the CRM-SSC Prize,[^o3] in collaboration with the [Centre de Recherches Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/3505436), to an exceptional young Canadian statistician.
