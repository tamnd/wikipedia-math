# Ahmed Abbes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ahmed Abbes"}

**Ahmed Abbes** (born 24 May 1970) is a Tunisian-French mathematician and a [Director of research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directeur_de_recherche) at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) at the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) (IHÉS). He is known for his work in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Abbes was born on 24 May 1970 in [Sfax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sfax), Tunisia.[^o4] Abbes received a bronze medal in 1988 and a silver medal in 1989 at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) while representing Tunisia.[^o5] Abbes has both French and Tunisian citizenship.[^o4]

Abbes studied at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) from 1990 to 1994 and then received his doctorate from [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in 1995 under the supervision of [Lucien Szpiro](wikipedia://en/26992384), with the thesis *Théorie d'Arakelov et courbes modulaires* on [Arakelov theory](wikipedia://en/16364229) and [modular curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modular_curves).[^o13] At Paris-Sud, [Michel Raynaud](wikipedia://en/4760928) was one of his mentors.[^o13] Abbes received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation#France) in 2003.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Abbes was a post-doctoral researcher at the Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques (IHÉS) from 1995 to 1996 and was also a post-doctoral researcher at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in 1996.[^o4] From 1996 to 2007, he was a [Chargé de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chargé_de_recherche) at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) at Paris-Sud University.[^o4] From 2007 to 2011, he was a CNRS Director of Research (2nd class) at the [University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes_1). In 2011, he moved to the IHÉS where he was a CNRS Director of Research (2nd class) until 2013 and where he has been a CNRS Director of Research (1st class) since 2013.[^o4][^o8]

Abbes was an editor for *[Astérisque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astérisque)* from 2010 to 2018 and is the co-editor-in-chief of the *Tunisian Journal of Mathematics*.[^o4]

Abbes is a Coordinator of the Tunisian Campaign for the Academic and Cultural Boycott of Israel (TACBI).[^o9][^o10] He is also a Secretary of the French Association of Academics for Respect for International Law in Palestine (AURDIP).[^o9][^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Abbes's research concerns the geometric and [cohomological](wikipedia://en/194060) properties of [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) on [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) over [perfect fields](wikipedia://en/505088) of positive characteristic and [*p*-adic fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_field).[^o8] He has worked on a *p*-adic [Simpson correspondence](wikipedia://en/46202375) and other topics in [*p*-adic Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/26086853) with Michel Gros.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2005, Abbes was awarded the [CNRS Bronze Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Bronze_Medal).[^o13] He is a corresponding member of the [Tunisian Academy of Sciences, Letters, and Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tunisian_Academy_of_Sciences,_Letters,_and_Arts).[^o8]
