# Anna Zdunik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anna Zdunik"}

**Anna Maria Zdunik** is a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). She specializes in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), and is a professor at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Zdunik earned her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2002, on the basis of an evaluation of her achievements and her [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation).[^o2]

## Career {#Career}

She became a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics in 2010.[^o2][^o7]

She was invited as a speaker for the 2015 [Fields Medal Symposium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fields_Medal_Symposium).[^o8]

She is the Chair of the Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Mechanics of the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) (MIMUW).[^o9] The Warsaw Center of Mathematics and Computer Science (Warszawskie Centrum Nauk Matematycznych) is a project of both MIMUW and the [Institute of Mathematics of the Polish Academy of Sciences](wikipedia://en/67042302) (IMPAN); the center is run by an executive committee that includes Zdunik.[^o9]
