# List of Jewish mathematicians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Jewish mathematicians"}

This **list of Jewish mathematicians** includes mathematicians and statisticians who are or were verifiably [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) or of Jewish descent. In 1933, when the [Nazis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazis) rose to power in Germany, one-third of all mathematics professors in the country were Jewish, while Jews constituted less than one percent of the population.[^o2] Jewish mathematicians made major contributions throughout the 20th century and into the 21st, as is evidenced by their high representation among the winners of major [mathematics awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_mathematics_awards): 27% for the [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859), 30% for the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285), and 40% for the [Wolf Prize](wikipedia://en/714278).[^o3]p.
