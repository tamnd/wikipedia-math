# Chandrashekhar Khare

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chandrashekhar Khare"}

**Chandrashekhar B. Khare**, (born 1968) is a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of California Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_Los_Angeles). In 2005, he made a major advance in the field of [Galois representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_representations) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) by proving the level 1 [Serre conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serre_conjecture_%28number_theory%29),[^o3] and later a proof of the full conjecture with [Jean-Pierre Wintenberger](wikipedia://en/30759845). He has been on the Mathematical Sciences jury for the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) from 2015, serving as Jury Chair from 2020.[^o4]

:::

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Resident of [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai), India and completed his undergraduate education at [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge). He finished his thesis in 1995 under the supervision of [Haruzo Hida](wikipedia://en/46742133) at [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology). His Ph.D. thesis was published in the *[Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal)*. He proved [Serre's modularity conjecture](wikipedia://en/2908546) with [Jean-Pierre Wintenberger](wikipedia://en/30759845), published in *[Inventiones Mathematicae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inventiones_Mathematicae)*.[^o6]

He started his career as a Fellow at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research). He was appointed as an associate professor of mathematics at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) until he moved to [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 2007. As of the year 2025, he is Professor and Chair of the [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) Department of Mathematics.[^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Khare is the winner of the INSA Young Scientist Award (1999),[^o9] [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195) (2007), the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) (2010),[^o10] and the [Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) (2011).

He gave an [invited talk at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2010, on the topic of "Number Theory".[^o11]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o12] and was elected as a Fellow of the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society).[^o13]
