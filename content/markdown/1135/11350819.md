# Birotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Birotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **birotunda** is any member of a family of [dihedral-symmetric](wikipedia://en/100267) [polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedra), formed from two [rotunda](wikipedia://en/35582167) adjoined through the largest face. They are similar to a [bicupola](wikipedia://en/3514224) but instead of alternating squares and triangles, it alternates [pentagons](wikipedia://en/20741014) and triangles around an axis. There are two forms, *ortho-* and *gyro-*: an *orthobirotunda* has one of the two rotundas is placed as the [mirror reflection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_reflection) of the other, while in a *gyrobirotunda* one rotunda is twisted relative to the other.

:::

The pentagonal birotundas can be formed with regular faces, one a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), the other a [semiregular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/1548894):

- [pentagonal orthobirotunda](wikipedia://en/1128619),
- pentagonal gyrobirotunda, which is also called an [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023).

Other forms can be generated with [dihedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_symmetry) and distorted equilateral pentagons.
