# Nicholas Hanges

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nicholas Hanges"}

**Nicholas William Hanges** (October 5, 1949 – July 20, 2019) was a mathematician and professor at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College) of the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York), known for his work in [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations) and the theory of [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Hanges earned his B.S degree in mathematics from the [New York City College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_College) in 1970. He received his M.A. (1975) and Ph.D. (1976) from [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University),[^o8][^o7] under the supervision of [M. Salah Baouendi](wikipedia://en/44998696). He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1977–1978, 1980–1981, 1991-1992 and again in 1994.[^o8] In 1983, Hanges joined the faculty of Lehman College of the City University of New York as a professor of mathematics from the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh). From 2017 he served as Chairman of the Mathematics Department.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- In 1982, Hanges received an [Alfred P. Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship).[^o8][^o11]
