# Gregory Eskin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gregory Eskin"}

**Gregory Eskin** (גרגורי אסקין, Григорий Ильич Эскин; born 5 December 1936) is a Russian-Israeli-American mathematician, specializing in partial differential equations.[^o3]

:::

Eskin received in 1963 his Ph.D. (Russian [candidate's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandidat_nauk)) from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) with thesis advisor [Georgiy Shilov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgiy_Shilov). In 1974 Eskin immigrated with his family to Israel and became a professor at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem). In 1983 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw). In 1982 he with his family emigrated from Israel to the USA and he became a professor at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2014.[^o5]

He is married to Marina Eskin, also a mathematician.[^o6] Their son [Alex](wikipedia://en/31883666) is a professor of mathematics at the University of Chicago, their other son Eleazar is a professor of computer science and human genetics at UCLA, and their daughter Ascia is a researcher in the Department of [Human Genetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_Genetics), David Geffen UCLA School of Medicine.[^o7][^o8]
