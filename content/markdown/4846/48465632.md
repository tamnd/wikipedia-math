# Yves Benoist

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yves Benoist"}

*Image: Yves Benoist.jpg.* Benoist at the *Rigidity of Stationary Measure* Workshop, [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) 2018

**Yves Benoist** is a French mathematician, known for his work on group dynamics on [homogeneous spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_spaces). He is currently a Senior Researcher (Directeur de Recherche) of [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud).

:::

In 1990 Benoist proved a longstanding open conjecture with [Patrick Foulon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrick_Foulon) and [François Labourie](wikipedia://en/26993580) about Anosov flows on compact, negatively curved manifolds.[^o2] In the 2000s he wrote a series of papers on the divisible convex sets in projective space and periodic tilings by such sets.

In 2011 he was awarded the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) along with his former doctoral student, [Jean-François Quint](wikipedia://en/54575530).[^o3] The prize citation highlighted their work on stationary measures and closed orbits for non-abelian group actions on homogeneous spaces, solving a long-standing conjecture of [Hillel Furstenberg](wikipedia://en/6450152). They showed that in homogeneous spaces of finite volume, orbits of a Zariski dense subgroup of a semisimple group equidistributed towards algebraic measures.

He gave the 2012 Takagi Lectures[^o4] in Kyoto at the [Research Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1978962) (RIMS). In 2014 he was an Invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Seoul.
