# Ritabrata Munshi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ritabrata Munshi"}

*Image: RM-at-Oberwolfach.jpg.* Munshi at [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669)

**Ritabrata Munshi** (born 14 September 1976 in Calcutta ([Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata)), West Bengal) is an Indian mathematician specialising in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in India, for the year 2015 in mathematical science category.[^o2]

:::

He is known for his contributions to the sub-convexity problem for automorphic L-functions. In a series of papers published in 2015[^o3][^o4][^o5] he introduced a new approach, based on the circle method, and was able to establish sub-convex bounds for genuine degree three L-functions. Later he extended his method to the case of GL(3)xGL(2) Rankin-Selberg convolutions.[^o6] These cases were earlier considered beyond the reach of known techniques.

He is affiliated to [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research), [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai), and the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata). Munshi obtained PhD degree from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2006 under the guidance of [Andrew John Wiles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_John_Wiles). After that he was a Hill Assistant Professor at the Rutgers University and worked with Henryk Iwaniec. During 2009–2010 he was a member at the Institute for Advanced Study, Princeton.

Munshi was awarded the Swarna-Jayanti fellowship by the Department of Science and Technology, Government of India in 2012. He also received the B.M. Birla Science prize in 2013, and was elected a fellow of the [Indian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2016. For his outstanding contributions to analytic aspects of number theory, he was awarded the [Infosys Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infosys_Prize) 2017 in Mathematical Sciences.[^o8] On 8 November 2018 he was awarded the [ICTP Ramanujan Prize](wikipedia://en/13595525) in a ceremony held at the Budinich Lecture Hall, ICTP.[^o9] In 2018 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM). In both 2018 and 2019, he was named a laureate of the [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100) by the *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*.[^o10] He was elected a fellow of the [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy) in 2020.

He serves in the editorial board of The Journal of the [Ramanujan Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramanujan_Mathematical_Society) and the Hardy-Ramanujan journal.
