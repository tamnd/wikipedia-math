# Leon Takhtajan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Leon Takhtajan"}

**Leon Armenovich Takhtajan** (Լևոն Թախտաջյան; Леон Арменович Тахтаджян, born 1 October 1950, [Yerevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan)) is a Russian (and formerly Soviet) mathematical physicist of Armenian descent, currently a professor of mathematics at the [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University), Stony Brook, NY, and a leading researcher at the Euler International Mathematical Institute, [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Leon Armenovich Takhtajan was born in [Yerevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan), [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union), in 1950, son of the Armenian Russian [botanist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany) [Armen Takhtajan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armen_Takhtajan).

### Education {#Education}

Takhtajan received in 1975 his Ph.D. (Russian candidate degree) from the [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute) (Leningrad Department) under [Ludvig Faddeev](wikipedia://en/4854639) with thesis *Complete Integrability of the Equation $u_{tt}-u_{xx}+\sin (u)=0$*. He was then employed at the Steklov Institute (Leningrad Department) and in 1982 received his D.S. degree ([doctor of science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science), 2nd degree in Russia) with thesis *Completely integrable models of field theory and statistical mechanics*.

### Career {#Career}

Since 1992 he has been a professor at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) where he was the chair of the mathematics department in 2009–2013.

## Research {#Research}

His research is on integrable systems of mathematical physics (such as the theory of solitons) and applications of [quantum field theories](wikipedia://en/25267) and models of string theory to [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759). It includes quantum field theories on algebraic curves and associated reciprocity laws, two-dimensional [quantum gravity](wikipedia://en/25312) and Weil–Petersson geometry of [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609), the [Kähler geometry](wikipedia://en/390538) of universal [Teichmüller space](wikipedia://en/1835661), and trace formulas. His major contributions are in the theory of classical and quantum integrable systems, quantum groups and Weil–Petersson geometry of moduli spaces. Together with Ludvig Faddeev and [Evgeny Sklyanin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgeny_Sklyanin) he formulated the algebraic [Bethe ansatz](wikipedia://en/4327884) and [quantum inverse scattering method](wikipedia://en/25672752). Together with Ludvig Faddeev and [Nicolai Reshetikhin](wikipedia://en/7487626) he proposed a method of quantization of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) and algebras called the FRT construction.[^o3] In 1983 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), Poland and gave a talk titled *Integrable models in classical and quantum field theory*.[^o9]
