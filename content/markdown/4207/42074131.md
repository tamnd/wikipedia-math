# Derived stack

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Derived stack"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **derived stack** is, roughly, a [stack](wikipedia://en/13185596) together with a [sheaf of commutative ring spectra](wikipedia://en/40784278).[^o2] It generalizes a [derived scheme](wikipedia://en/40776948). Derived stacks are the "spaces" studied in [derived algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/40778281).[^o3]

:::
