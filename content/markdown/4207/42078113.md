# European Mathematical Psychology Group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="European Mathematical Psychology Group"}

The **European Mathematical Psychology Group** (**EMPG**) is an informal association of scientists in [mathematical psychology](wikipedia://en/2081516). The group was founded in 1971 in Paris; it has not been formally organized as a society, although has been described as the "European branch" of the [Society for Mathematical Psychology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Mathematical_Psychology).[^o2] It holds a meeting each year in a European city, and beginning at the 23rd meeting, has published a [proceedings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proceedings) from that meeting as an edited volume.[^o3] As of 2001, there were approximately 100 attendees.[^o4]

:::
