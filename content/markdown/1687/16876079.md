# The American Statistician

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The American Statistician"}

***The American Statistician*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) on behalf of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). It was established in 1947. The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Minge Xie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minge_Xie), a professor at the [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).

:::
