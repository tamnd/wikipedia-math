# Schoch line

*Image: Schoch Line.svg.* The Schoch line (cyan) passes through the point *A*<sub>1</sub>.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Schoch line"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Schoch line** is a [line](wikipedia://en/946975) defined from an [arbelos](wikipedia://en/887197) and named by Peter Woo after Thomas Schoch, who had studied it in conjunction with the [Schoch circles](wikipedia://en/19107031).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

An [arbelos](wikipedia://en/887197) is a shape bounded by three mutually-tangent semicircular arcs with collinear endpoints, with the two smaller arcs nested inside the larger one; let the endpoints of these three arcs be (in order along the line containing them) *A*, *B*, and *C*. Let *K*<sub>1</sub> and *K*<sub>2</sub> be two more arcs, centered at *A* and *C*, respectively, with radii *AB* and *CB*, so that these two arcs are tangent at *B*; let *K*<sub>3</sub> be the largest of the three arcs of the arbelos. A circle, with the center *A*<sub>1</sub>, is then created [tangent](wikipedia://en/31482) to the arcs *K*<sub>1</sub>, *K*<sub>2</sub>, and *K*<sub>3</sub>. This circle is congruent with [Archimedes' twin circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedes'_circles), making it an [Archimedean circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_circle); it is one of the [Schoch circles](wikipedia://en/19107031). The Schoch line is [perpendicular](wikipedia://en/76944) to the line *AC* and passes through the point *A*<sub>1</sub>. It is also the location of the centers of [infinitely many](wikipedia://en/20110824) Archimedean circles, e.g. the [Woo circles](wikipedia://en/17794292).[^o4]

:::

## Radius and center of *A*<sub>1</sub> {#Radius_and_center_of_A1}

If *r* = *AB*/*AC*, and *AC* = 1, then the radius of A<sub>1</sub> is

$$\rho=\frac{1}{2}r\left(1-r\right)$$

and the center is

$\left(\frac{1}{2}r\left(-1+3r-2r^2\right)~,~r\left(1-r\right)\sqrt{\left(1+r\right)\left(2-r\right)}\right).$[^o4]
