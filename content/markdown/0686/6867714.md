# Zahorski theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zahorski theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Zahorski's theorem** is a theorem of [real analysis](wikipedia://en/26478). It states that a necessary and sufficient condition for a subset of the [real line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_line) to be the set of points of non-differentiability of a continuous real-valued function, is that it be the union of a [G<sub>δ</sub> set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G-delta_set) and a ${G_\delta}_\sigma$ set of [zero measure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zero_measure).

:::

This result was proved by Zygmunt Zahorski in 1939 and first published in 1941.
