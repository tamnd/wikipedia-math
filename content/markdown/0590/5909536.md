# Poset topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Poset topology"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **poset topology** associated to a [poset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poset) (*S*, ≤) is the [Alexandrov topology](wikipedia://en/542198) (open sets are [upper sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_set)) on the poset of finite [chains](wikipedia://en/30330#Chains) of (*S*, ≤), ordered by inclusion.

:::

Let *V* be a set of vertices. An [abstract simplicial complex](wikipedia://en/723105) Δ is a set of finite sets of vertices, known as faces $\sigma \subseteq V$, such that

$$\forall \rho \, \forall \sigma \!: \ \rho \subseteq \sigma \in \Delta \Rightarrow \rho \in \Delta.$$

Given a simplicial complex Δ as above, we define a (point set) [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) on Δ by declaring a subset $\Gamma \subseteq \Delta$ be **closed** if and only if Γ is a simplicial complex, i.e.

$$\forall \rho \, \forall \sigma \!: \ \rho \subseteq \sigma \in \Gamma \Rightarrow \rho \in \Gamma.$$

This is the [Alexandrov topology](wikipedia://en/542198) on the poset of faces of Δ.

The **order complex** associated to a poset (*S*, ≤) has the set *S* as vertices, and the finite chains of (*S*, ≤) as faces. The poset topology associated to a poset (*S*, ≤) is then the Alexandrov topology on the order complex associated to (*S*, ≤).
