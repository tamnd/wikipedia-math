# Léon-François-Antoine Aurifeuille

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Léon-François-Antoine Aurifeuille"}

**Léon-François-Antoine Aurifeuille** (1822–1882) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) after whom [Aurifeuillean factorizations](wikipedia://en/23683488) are named.[^o3]

:::

He was the author of three books: *Cours de géométrie élémentaire* (with C. Richaud, Paris: Bachelier, 1847), *Traité de géométrie élémentaire* (with C. Dumont, 2nd ed., Toulouse: Bonnal & Gibrac, 1860), and *Traité d'arithmétique* (with C. Dumont, 2nd ed., Toulouse: Bonnal & Gibrac, 1859).[^o4]

Under the [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) **Alfred de Caston**,[^o5] he also wrote several other books, including *Les Tricheurs, scènes de jeu* (Paris: E. Dentu, 1863) and *Les marchands de miracles; histoire de la superstition humaine* (Paris: E. Dentu, 1864).
