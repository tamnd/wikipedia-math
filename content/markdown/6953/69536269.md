# Rémi Abgrall

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rémi Abgrall"}

**Rémi Abgrall** (born 1961) is a French applied mathematician. He is known for his contributions in [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924), numerical analysis of [conservation laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_laws), [multiphase flow](wikipedia://en/5587769) and [Hamilton–Jacobi equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamilton–Jacobi_equations).[^o3][^o4] He has been editor in chief of the [Journal of Computational Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Computational_Physics)[^o5] since 2015 and is part of the editorial board of several international scientific journals. In 2014 he was invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematics)[^o6] in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul). He is author of more than 100 scientific papers published in international scientific journals.[^o7] He is editor of 4 books[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11] and author of one book[^o12] on advanced topics concerning [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924), [high-resolution schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High-resolution_scheme) and [conservation laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conservation_laws).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Abgrall received his degree in mathematics at [École normale supérieure de Saint-Cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Saint-Cloud) in 1985 and his PhD at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in 1987 with a thesis entitled *Conception d’un modèle semi-lagrangien de turbulence bidimensionnelle*.[^o14] He worked at [ONERA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ONERA) and then at [Inria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inria)[^o15] as a research scientist. From 1996 until 2013 he was professor at [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1) and then at [Institut polytechnique de Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_polytechnique_de_Bordeaux). Since 2014, he has been a professor of [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich).[^o16]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

In 2001 he received the [Prix Blaise-Pascal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Blaise-Pascal)[^o18] from the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences). He is honorary member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).[^o19] In 2008 he got an advanced [CORDIS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CORDIS) grant[^o20] from the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council). He was elected as a Fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376), in the 2022 Class of SIAM Fellows, "for fundamental contributions to the development of numerical methods for conservation laws, in particular for multi-fluid flows and residual distribution schemes".[^o21]
