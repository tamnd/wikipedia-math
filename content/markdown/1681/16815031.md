# Calogero–Degasperis–Fokas equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Calogero–Degasperis–Fokas equation"}

In mathematics, the **Calogero–Degasperis–Fokas equation** is the nonlinear [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564)

:::

$$\displaystyle u_t=u_{xxx}-\frac{1}{8}u_x^3 + u_x\left(Ae^u+Be^{-u}\right).$$

This equation was named after [F. Calogero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F._Calogero), [A. Degasperis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._Degasperis), and [A. Fokas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._Fokas).
