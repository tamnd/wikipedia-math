# Geoffrey Thomas Bennett

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geoffrey Thomas Bennett"}

*Image: Geoffrey Thomas Bennett.jpg.* Image of Geoffrey Thomas Bennett

**Geoffrey Thomas Bennett** [OBE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OBE) (1868–1943) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), professor at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Born in London,[^o3][^o4] he began his secondary studies at the [University College School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_School), under [Robert Tucker](wikipedia://en/54097762). After one year at [University College of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_of_London), Bennett obtained a scholarship at [St. John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._John's_College,_Cambridge), where he graduated in 1890 as [Senior Wrangler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Wrangler_%28University_of_Cambridge%29). However, the best grade in the [Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Tripos) of that year was for [Philippa Fawcett](wikipedia://en/639426), but she was not included in the list for her gender.[^n1]

Upon completion of his studies he was appointed college lecturer of mathematics at [Emmanuel College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_College,_Cambridge). He held a fellowship at the college from 1893 until his death in 1943.[^n2] He had also great interest in music and athletics. He was a keen bicyclist and a good pianist.[^n3]

During the [First World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_World_War) he was member of the Anti-Aircraft Experimental Section (AAES) for his versatility and for his ability solving geometrical problems by mechanical means.[^n4]
