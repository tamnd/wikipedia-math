# Moufang plane

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moufang plane"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **Moufang plane**, named for [Ruth Moufang](wikipedia://en/2003831), is a type of [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350), more specifically a special type of [translation plane](wikipedia://en/2628762). A translation plane is a projective plane that has a *translation line*, that is, a line with the property that the group of automorphisms that fixes every point of the line [acts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) transitively on the points of the plane not on the line.[^o2] A translation plane is Moufang if every line of the plane is a translation line.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Characterizations"}

## Characterizations {#Characterizations}

A Moufang plane can also be described as a projective plane in which the *[little Desargues theorem](wikipedia://en/358488#The_little_Desargues_theorem)* holds.[^o6] This theorem states that a restricted form of [Desargues' theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desargues'_theorem) holds for every line in the plane.[^o7] For example, every [Desarguesian plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Desarguesian_plane) is a Moufang plane.[^o8]

In algebraic terms, a projective plane over any [alternative division ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_division_ring) is a Moufang plane,[^o9] and this gives a 1:1 correspondence between isomorphism classes of alternative division rings and of Moufang planes.

As a consequence of the algebraic [Artin–Zorn theorem](wikipedia://en/14818089), that every finite alternative division ring is a field, every finite Moufang plane is Desarguesian, but some infinite Moufang planes are [non-Desarguesian planes](wikipedia://en/21092112). In particular, the [Cayley plane](wikipedia://en/9410951), an infinite Moufang projective plane over the [octonions](wikipedia://en/51436), is one of these because the octonions do not form a division ring.[^o10]

:::

::: {#o12 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The following conditions on a projective plane *P* are equivalent:[^o13]

- *P* is a Moufang plane.
- The group of automorphisms fixing all points of any given line acts transitively on the points not on the line.
- Some ternary ring of the plane is an alternative division ring.
- *P* is isomorphic to the projective plane over an alternative division ring.

Also, in a Moufang plane:

- The group of automorphisms acts transitively on quadrangles.[^o14][^o15]
- Any two [ternary rings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ternary_ring) of the plane are isomorphic.

:::
