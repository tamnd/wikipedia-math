# Milnor's theorem on Kan complexes

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Milnor's theorem on Kan complexes"}

In mathematics, especially [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a **theorem of Milnor** says that the geometric realization functor from the [homotopy category](wikipedia://en/2040756) of the category Kan of [Kan complexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan_complex) to the homotopy category of the category Top of (reasonable) topological spaces is [fully faithful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fully_faithful). The theorem in particular implies Kan and Top have the same homotopy category.[^o2]

:::

In today’s language, Kan is typically identified as ∞-Grpd, the category of [∞-groupoids](wikipedia://en/40965675). Thus, the theorem can be viewed as an instance of Grothendieck's [homotopy hypothesis](wikipedia://en/40965653) which says ∞-groupoids are spaces (or that they can model spaces from the [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831) point of view).

The pointed version of the theorem is also true.[^o3]

::: {#o5 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

## Proof {#Proof}

A key step in the proof of the theorem is the following result (which is also sometimes called Milnor's theorem):

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/proposition cue=math-theorem name="Proposition"}

**Proposition.**

[^o7][^o8] For a Kan complex $X$, let $\eta_X : X \to \operatorname{Sing}(|X|)$ be a [unit of the adjunction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_of_adjunction) between the geometric realization and the singular complex functor. Then $\eta_X$ is a homotopy equivalence; i.e., $\pi_n \eta_X : \pi_n X \to \pi_n \operatorname{Sing}(|X|)$ is bijective for each $n$.

:::

Indeed, the above says that $\eta : \operatorname{id} \to \operatorname{Sing}(| -|)$ is invertible on the homotopy category or, equivalently, $| - |$ is fully faithful there.

:::
