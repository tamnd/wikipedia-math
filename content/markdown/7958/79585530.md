# Steve Decent

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve Decent"}

**Stephen Paul Decent** is a British applied mathematician and university administrator who has served as the Vice-Chancellor of [Lancaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster_University) since January 2026, having previously served as principal and vice-chancellor of [Glasgow Caledonian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow_Caledonian_University).[^o4] He has previously held a number of senior leadership positions across different universities and holds research interests in [free-surface flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open-channel_flow) and [hydrogen fuel cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen_fuel_cells).[^o5]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

The first member of his own family to go to university, Decent holds a BSc in mathematics from [Brunel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunel_University) and a PhD from the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews).[^o7] His doctoral thesis was on the theory of wave motion and was funded by the [Engineering and Physical Sciences Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_and_Physical_Sciences_Research_Council).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

After completing his PhD, Decent joined the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham) as a research fellow and was eventually promoted to the head of the School of Mathematics. He then moved to the [University of Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dundee) to take up the position of vice-principal and head of the College of Art, Science and Engineering. Afterwards, he joined [Lancaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lancaster_University) as pro-vice-chancellor of research and enterprise and later pro-vice-chancellor of academic development before moving again to take up the position of provost and deputy vice-chancellor at [Manchester Metropolitan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester_Metropolitan_University).[^o10]

He was appointed as the vice-chancellor of Glasgow Caledonian University in January 2023. He returned to Lancaster in January 2026 to take up his next appointment as the university's vice-chancellor.[^o5]
