# Kharitonov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kharitonov"}

**Kharitonov** (Харитонов), feminine: **Kharitonova** (Харитонова) is a Russian [patronymic surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic_surname) derived from the male given name [Khariton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khariton) and literally means *Khariton's*. Ukrainian form: [Kharytonov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharytonov). Notable people with the surname include:

:::
