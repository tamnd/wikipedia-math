# Yum-Tong Siu

[Siu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiao_%28surname%29)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Yum-Tong Siu"}

**Yum-Tong Siu** (; born May 6, 1943) is a Chinese mathematician. He is the William Elwood Byerly Professor of Mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).

:::

Siu is a prominent figure in the study of [functions of several complex variables](wikipedia://en/360243). His research interests involve the intersection of complex variables, [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He has resolved various conjectures by applying estimates of the complex [Neumann problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neumann_problem) and the theory of [multiplier ideal](wikipedia://en/28300822) [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) to algebraic geometry.[^o4][^o5]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Siu obtained his B.A. in mathematics from the [University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hong_Kong) in 1963, his M.A. from the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), and his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1966.[^o7] Siu completed his doctoral dissertation, titled "Coherent Noether-Lasker decomposition of subsheaves and sheaf cohomology", under the supervision of [Robert C. Gunning](wikipedia://en/22647108).[^o8] Before joining Harvard, he taught at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame), [Yale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale), and [Stanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford). In 1982 he joined [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard) as a professor, of Mathematics. He previously served as the Chairman of the Harvard Math Department.[^o9]

In 2006, Siu published a proof of the finite generation of the [pluricanonical ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pluricanonical_ring).[^o10]

## Awards, honors and professional memberships {#Awards,_honors_and_professional_memberships}

In 1993, Siu received the [Stefan Bergman Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Bergman_Prize)[^o12] of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). He has holds honorary doctorates from the University of Hong Kong, University of Bochum, Germany, and University of Macau. He is a Corresponding Member of the Goettingen Academy of Sciences (elected 1993); a Foreign member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences (elected 2004); and a member of the [American Academy of Arts & Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_&_Sciences) (elected 1998),[^o13] the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) (elected 2002),[^o14] and [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica), [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) (elected 2004).[^o15] He has been an [invited speaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) (1978), [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) (1983) and [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) (2002).

Currently, Siu is a member of the Scientific Advisory Board of the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655) (since 2003); the Advisory Committee for the Shaw Prize In Mathematical Sciences (since 2010);[^o16] the Advisory Committee for the [Millennium Prize Problems](wikipedia://en/27599234) under the sponsorship of the Clay Mathematics Institute; the Scientific Advisory Board for the [Institute for Mathematics Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Mathematics_Sciences), [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880)[^o17] (since 2009) and the Institute of Advanced Studies, [Nanyang Technological University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanyang_Technological_University), [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore) (since 2006).[^o18]
