# Advances in Applied Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Advances in Applied Mathematics"}

***Advances in Applied Mathematics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal) publishing research on [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969). Its founding editor was [Gian-Carlo Rota](wikipedia://en/101891) ([Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology)); from 1980 to 1999, [Joseph P. S. Kung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_P._S._Kung) ([University of North Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Texas)) served as managing editor. It is currently published by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) with eight issues per year and edited by [Hal Schenck](wikipedia://en/67786208) ([Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University)) and [Catherine Yan](wikipedia://en/55706943) ([Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University)).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed by:[^o4]

- [ACM Guide to Computing Literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Guide_to_Computing_Literature)
- [CompuMath Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CompuMath_Citation_Index)
- [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physics, Chemical, & Earth Sciences
- *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)*
- [Science Citation Index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index)
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.848.[^o5]
