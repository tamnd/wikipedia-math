# Ronald M. Foster

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald M. Foster"}

**Ronald Martin Foster** (3 October 1896 – 2 February 1998), was an American mathematician at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs) whose work was of significance regarding [electronic filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_filter) for use on telephone lines. He published an important paper, *A Reactance Theorem*,[^o2] (see [Foster's reactance theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster's_reactance_theorem)) which quickly inspired [Wilhelm Cauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Cauer) to begin his program of [network synthesis filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Network_synthesis_filters) which put the design of filters on a firm mathematical footing.[^o3] He is also known for the [Foster census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foster_census) of [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508), [symmetric graphs](wikipedia://en/1577896)[^o5] and the 90-vertex cubic symmetric [Foster graph](wikipedia://en/11197065).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Foster was a [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College) graduate S.B. (Mathematics), summa cum laude, Class of 1917. He also received two honorary Sc.D.s.[^o5]

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

- 1917 – 1943 Research & Development Department (later [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs)), [American Telephone & Telegraph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Telephone_&_Telegraph), as a Research Engineer (Applied Mathematician), New York City, New York.
- 1943 – 1963 Professor and Head of Department of Mathematics, [Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_Institute_of_New_York_University), Brooklyn, New York City, New York.

## Publications {#Publications}

- Campbell, GA, Foster, RM, *Fourier Integrals for Practical Applications*, "Bell System Technical Journal", pp. 639–707, 1928.[^o8]
- Pierce, BO, Foster. RM. "A Short Table of Integrals", Fourth Edition, Ginn and Company, pp. 1–189, 1956.
