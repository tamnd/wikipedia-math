# Fitting ideal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fitting ideal"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), the **Fitting ideals** of a [finitely generated module](wikipedia://en/326430) over a [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) describe the obstructions to generating the module by a given number of elements. They were introduced by (txt).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

If *M* is a finitely generated module over a commutative ring *R* generated by elements *m*<sub>1</sub>,...,*m*<sub>*n*</sub> with relations

$$a_{j1}m_1+\cdots + a_{jn}m_n=0\ (\text{for }j = 1, 2, \dots)$$

then the *i*th Fitting ideal $\operatorname{Fitt}_i(M)$ of *M* is generated by the minors (determinants of submatrices) of order $n-i$ of the matrix $a_{jk}$. The Fitting ideals do not depend on the choice of generators and relations of *M*.

Some authors defined the Fitting ideal $I(M)$ to be the first nonzero Fitting ideal $\operatorname{Fitt}_i(M)$.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The Fitting ideals are increasing

$$\operatorname{Fitt}_0(M) \subseteq \operatorname{Fitt}_1(M) \subseteq \operatorname{Fitt}_2(M) \subseteq \cdots$$

If *M* can be generated by *n* elements then Fitt<sub>*n*</sub>(*M*) = *R*, and if *R* is local the converse holds. We have Fitt<sub>0</sub>(*M*) ⊆ Ann(*M*) (the annihilator of *M*), and Ann(*M*)Fitt<sub>*i*</sub>(*M*) ⊆ Fitt<sub>*i*−1</sub>(*M*), so in particular if *M* can be generated by *n* elements then Ann(*M*)<sup>*n*</sup> ⊆ Fitt<sub>0</sub>(*M*).

:::

::: {#o8 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

If *M* is free of rank *n* then the Fitting ideals $\operatorname{Fitt}_i(M)$ are zero for *i*\<*n* and *R* for *i* ≥ *n*.

If *M* is a finite [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) of order $|M|$ (considered as a module over the integers) then the Fitting ideal $\operatorname{Fitt}_0(M)$ is the ideal $(|M|)$.

The [Alexander polynomial](wikipedia://en/1107647) of a knot is a generator of the Fitting ideal of the first homology of the infinite abelian cover of the [knot complement](wikipedia://en/1350120).

:::

## Fitting image {#Fitting_image}

The zeroth Fitting ideal can be used also to give a variant of the notion of [scheme-theoretic image](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme-theoretic_image) of a morphism, a variant that behaves well in families. Specifically, given a [finite morphism](wikipedia://en/2139357) of [noetherian schemes](wikipedia://en/2783891) $f \colon X \rightarrow Y$, the $\mathcal{O}_Y$-module $f_* \mathcal{O}_X$ is [coherent](wikipedia://en/603780), so we may define $\operatorname{Fitt}_0(f_* \mathcal{O}_X)$ as a coherent sheaf of $\mathcal{O}_Y$-ideals; the corresponding [closed subscheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_subscheme) of $Y$ is called the **Fitting image** of *f*.[^o10]
