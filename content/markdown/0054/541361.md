# Stoneham number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stoneham number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Stoneham numbers** are a certain class of [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438), named after [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Richard G. Stoneham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_G._Stoneham) (1920–1996).[^o2] For [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime) numbers *b*, *c* > 1, the Stoneham number α<sub>*b*,*c*</sub> is defined as

:::

$$\alpha_{b,c} = \sum_{n=c^k>1} \frac{1}{b^nn} = \sum_{k=1}^\infty \frac{1}{b^{c^k}c^k}$$

It was shown by Stoneham in 1973 that α<sub>*b*,*c*</sub> is *b*-[normal](wikipedia://en/204277) whenever *c* is an odd [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) and *b* is a [primitive root](wikipedia://en/186864) of *c*<sup>2</sup>. In 2002, Bailey & Crandall showed that coprimality of *b*, *c* > 1 is sufficient for *b*-normality of α<sub>*b*,*c*</sub>.[^o3]
