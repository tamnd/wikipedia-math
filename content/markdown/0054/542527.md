# Cryptologia

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cryptologia"}

***Cryptologia*** is a journal in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) published six times per year since January 1977. Its remit is all aspects of cryptography, with a special emphasis on [historical aspects of the subject](wikipedia://en/520066). The founding editors were Brian J. Winkel, [David Kahn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Kahn_%28writer%29), Louis Kruh, Cipher A. Deavours and Greg Mellen.[^o3] The current Editor-in-Chief is Craig Bauer.

:::

The journal was initially published at the [Rose-Hulman Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rose-Hulman_Institute_of_Technology). In July 1995, it moved to the [United States Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Military_Academy), and was then published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis) since the January 2006 issue (Volume 30, Number 1).[^o4]
