# Central Statistics Office (Ireland)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Central Statistics Office"}

The **Central Statistics Office** (**CSO**; An Phríomh-Oifig Staidrimh) is the [statistical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical) agency responsible for the gathering of "information relating to economic, social and general activities and conditions" in the [Republic of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland) (it does not operate in [Northern Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Ireland)), in particular the [census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_of_Ireland) which is held every five years. The office is answerable to the [Taoiseach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taoiseach) and has its main offices in [Cork](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cork_%28city%29). The Director General of the CSO is Jennifer Banim.

:::

## History {#History}

The CSO was established on a statutory basis in 1994 to reduce the number of separate offices responsible for collecting statistics for the state.[^o5]

The CSO had existed, as an independent [ad hoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ad_hoc) office within the [Department of the Taoiseach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_the_Taoiseach), from June 1949, and its work greatly increased in the following decades, particularly from 1973 with the [Republic of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republic_of_Ireland) joining the [European Economic Community](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Economic_Community) (EEC). Previous to the 1949 reforms, statistics were collected by the Statistics Branch of the [Department of Industry and Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Department_of_Enterprise,_Tourism_and_Employment) on the creation of the [Irish Free State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irish_Free_State) in 1922. The Statistics Branch amalgamated a number of statistics gathering organisations that had existed in [Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ireland) since 1841, when the first comprehensive census was undertaken by the Irish Constabulary (later renamed as the [Royal Irish Constabulary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Irish_Constabulary)).

On 15 September 2020, on the advice of the Central Statistics Office, the Irish Government postponed the quinquennial population [census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Census_of_Ireland), originally scheduled for 18 April 2021, until 3 April 2022 because of health and logistical obstacles caused by the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic).[^o6][^o7]

## Head of the Office {#Head_of_the_Office}

The current Director-General of the Central Statistics Office is Jennifer Banim.[^o9]

## Household Finance and Consumption Survey {#Household_Finance_and_Consumption_Survey}

In 2013, the first ever [Household Finance and Consumption Survey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Household_Finance_and_Consumption_Survey_%28Ireland%29) (HFCS) was conducted in the Republic of Ireland by the CSO on behalf of the [Central Bank of Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Bank_of_Ireland) as part of the [European Central Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Central_Bank) (ECB) HFCS scheme/network.[^o11]
