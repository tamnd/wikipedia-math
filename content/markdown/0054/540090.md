# Don Coppersmith

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Don Coppersmith"}

**Don Coppersmith** (born c. 1950) is a [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was involved in the design of the [Data Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/7978) [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) at [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM), particularly the design of the [S-boxes](wikipedia://en/240991), strengthening them against [differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/8536).[^o3] He also improved the [quantum Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/30872292) discovered by [Peter Shor](wikipedia://en/68338) in the same year (1994).[^o4] He has also worked on [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) for computing [discrete logarithms](wikipedia://en/181334), the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of [RSA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_%28algorithm%29), methods for rapid [matrix multiplication](wikipedia://en/125280) (see [Coppersmith–Winograd algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppersmith–Winograd_algorithm)) and IBM's [MARS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MARS_%28cryptography%29) cipher. He is also a co-designer of the [SEAL](wikipedia://en/294108) and [Scream](wikipedia://en/4159011) ciphers.

:::

In 1972, Coppersmith obtained a bachelor's degree in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and a Masters and Ph.D. in mathematics from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1975 and 1977 respectively.[^o3] He was a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition#Putnam_Fellows) each year from 1968–1971, becoming the first four-time Putnam Fellow in history.[^o5] In 1998, he started *Ponder This*, an online monthly column on [mathematical puzzles](wikipedia://en/366445) and problems. In October 2005, the column was taken over by James Shearer.[^o6] Around that same time, he left [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM) and began working at the [IDA Center for Communications Research, Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Defense_Analyses#Center_for_Communications_and_Computing).[^o7]

In 2002, Coppersmith won the [RSA Award for Excellence in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RSA_Award_for_Excellence_in_Mathematics).[^o8][^o9]

In 2022, Coppersmith was awarded the [Levchin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levchin_Prize) for “foundational innovations in cryptanalysis”[^o10] .
