# Minkowski's first inequality for convex bodies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Minkowski's first inequality for convex bodies"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Minkowski's first inequality for convex bodies** is a [geometrical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometrical) result due to the [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537). The inequality is closely related to the [Brunn–Minkowski inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brunn–Minkowski_inequality) and the [isoperimetric inequality](wikipedia://en/326182).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=named-heading name="Statement of the inequality"}

## Statement of the inequality {#Statement_of_the_inequality}

Let *K* and *L* be two *n*-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [convex bodies](wikipedia://en/8652020) in *n*-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) **R**<sup>*n*</sup>. Define a quantity *V*<sub>1</sub>(*K*, *L*) by

$$n V_{1} (K, L) = \lim_{\varepsilon \downarrow 0} \frac{V (K + \varepsilon L) - V(K)}{\varepsilon},$$

where *V* denotes the *n*-dimensional [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198) and + denotes the [Minkowski sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_sum). Then

$$V_{1} (K, L) \geq V(K)^{(n - 1) / n} V(L)^{1 / n},$$

with equality [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) *K* and *L* are [homothetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homothetic_transformation), i.e. are equal up to [translation](wikipedia://en/204682) and [dilation](wikipedia://en/1591770).

:::

## Remarks {#Remarks}

- *V*<sub>1</sub> is just one example of a class of quantities known as *[mixed volumes](wikipedia://en/32953045)*.
- If *L* is the *n*-dimensional [unit ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_ball) *B*, then *n* *V*<sub>1</sub>(*K*, *B*) is the (*n* − 1)-dimensional surface measure of *K*, denoted *S*(*K*).

## Connection to other inequalities {#Connection_to_other_inequalities}

::: {#o7 .statement cue=named-heading name="The Brunn–Minkowski inequality"}

### The Brunn–Minkowski inequality {#The_Brunn–Minkowski_inequality}

One can show that the Brunn–Minkowski inequality for convex bodies in **R**<sup>*n*</sup> implies Minkowski's first inequality for convex bodies in **R**<sup>*n*</sup>, and that equality in the Brunn–Minkowski inequality implies equality in Minkowski's first inequality.

:::

::: {#o9 .statement cue=named-heading name="The isoperimetric inequality"}

### The isoperimetric inequality {#The_isoperimetric_inequality}

By taking *L* = *B*, the *n*-dimensional unit ball, in Minkowski's first inequality for convex bodies, one obtains the isoperimetric inequality for convex bodies in **R**<sup>*n*</sup>: if *K* is a convex body in **R**<sup>*n*</sup>, then

$$\left( \frac{V(K)}{V(B)} \right)^{1 / n} \leq \left( \frac{S(K)}{S(B)} \right)^{1 / (n - 1)},$$

with equality if and only if *K* is a ball of some radius.

:::
