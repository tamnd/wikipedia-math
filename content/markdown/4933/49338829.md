# Wolfgang Leinberer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Leinberer"}

**Wolfgang Leinberer** ([Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuttgart), 1635-10-19 – [Altötting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altötting), 1693) was a priest in the [Society of Jesus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Jesus). He was German astronomer, philosopher, mathematician and professor, considered as "the most enthusiastic, even ingenious disciple in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome) of the famous mathematician Father [Athanasius Kircher](wikipedia://en/93815)".[^o3]

:::

He taught [grammar](wikipedia://en/12569), [humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanities), [rhetoric](wikipedia://en/25447), [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at the [University of Ingolstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ingolstadt). At the same institution, he was also a master of novices and rector. He worked as a *socius* of the Provincial for five years and an instructor of third-year priests in [Altötting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altötting), a position which he kept until his death.[^o4]
