# Quasimartingale

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasimartingale"}

A **quasimartingale** is a concept from [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes) and refers to a [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) that has finite [mean](wikipedia://en/9653) [variation](wikipedia://en/683561). Quasimartingales are generalizing [semimartingales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semimartingales) in the sense as they do not have to be [càdlàg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Càdlàg_function), and they are exactly semimartingales if they are càdlàg. Quasimartingales were introduced by the American mathematician [Donald Fisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Fisk) in 1965.[^o2]

:::

Some authors use the term as a synonym for semimartingale and assume the process is càdlàg.

## Quasimartingale {#Quasimartingale}

Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, (\mathcal{F}_t)_{t \geq 0}, P)$ be a [filtred](wikipedia://en/57854817) [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325) and let $\tau$ be a [partition](wikipedia://en/471332) of the interval $[0, \infty]$. Further, let $X = (X_t){t \geq 0}$ be an [adapted](wikipedia://en/6583420) stochastic process. The *(mean) variation of $X$* is defined as

$$\operatorname{Var}(X) := \sup\limits_{\tau} \mathbb{E}\left[\sum\limits_{i=0}^n \left|\mathbb{E}[X_{t_i} - X_{t_{i+1}} | \mathcal{F}_{t_i}]\right|\right]$$

The process $X$ is a **quasimartingale** if $\mathbb{E}[|X_t|] < \infty$ for all $t$ and the process has finite variation:

$\operatorname{Var}(X) < \infty.$[^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

### Properties {#Properties}

- Every semimartingale is a quasimartingale.
- A quasimartingale is a semimartingale if and only if it is [càdlàg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Càdlàg_function).[^o7]
- [Rao's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rao's_theorem) is formulated for quasimartingales.

:::
