# The Math(s) Fix

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="The Math(s) Fix"}

***The Math(s) Fix: An Education Blueprint for the AI Age*** is a 2020 book by British technologist and entrepreneur [Conrad Wolfram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conrad_Wolfram). It argues for a fundamental shift in the way [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) is taught in schools, advocating for a curriculum that emphasizes a [computational education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_thinking#Computational_education) and [computer-based mathematics education](wikipedia://en/7824950) for 21st-century problem-solving skills with [computers in the classroom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computers_in_the_classroom), rather than manual [handwritten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handwriting) calculation techniques.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::
