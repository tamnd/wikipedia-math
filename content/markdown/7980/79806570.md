# Ajtai–Komlós–Tusnády theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ajtai–Komlós–Tusnády theorem"}

The **Ajtai–Komlós–Tusnády theorem** (also known as the **AKT optimal matching theorem**) is a result in [probabilistic combinatorics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_combinatorics). Given two random, distinct sets of points $X=(X_1,\dots,X_k)$ and $Y=(Y_1,\dots,Y_k)$ in the [unit square](wikipedia://en/285301) $[0,1]^2$, the theorem gives [upper and lower bounds](wikipedia://en/42693) for the minimal total distance needed to match the points in one set to those in the other.

:::

The theorem was proven in 1984 by the [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians) mathematicians [Miklós Ajtai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miklós_Ajtai), [János Komlós](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/János_Komlós_%28mathematician%29), and [Gábor Tusnády](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gábor_Tusnády).[^o2][^o3]

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $(X_1,\dots,X_k)$ and $(Y_1,\dots,Y_k)$ be two independent random vectors, uniformly distributed over $[0,1]^2$ (i.e., $X_i \in [0,1]^2$). Let $S_n$ denote the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901), and $|\cdot|$ the [Euclidean norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_norm) on $\mathbb{R}^2$.

Then,

$$\mathbb{P}\left(C_1\sqrt{n \log n}<\inf\limits_{\sigma \in S_n}\sum\limits_{k=1}^n|X_k-Y_{\sigma(k)}| <C_2\sqrt{n \log n}\right)=1-o(1),$$

where $C_1, C_2$ are real constants.

### Remarks {#Remarks}

- The notation $o(1)$ means

$f(n)\in o(1)\iff \lim\limits_{n\to\infty}f(n)= 0.$ see [Landau notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Landau_notation).

- The theorem implies that

$$\inf\limits_{\sigma \in S_n}\frac{1}{n}\sum\limits_{k=1}^n|X_k-Y_{\sigma(k)}| \sim \sqrt{\frac{\log n}{n}}$$

with high probability.

:::
