# HP 30 series

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="HP 30 series"}

The **HP-30** or **Spice series** are [RPN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reverse_Polish_Notation) scientific hand-held calculators introduced by [Hewlett-Packard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hewlett-Packard) in 1978. Some models are programmable.

*Image: HP 32E calculator - pi in display.jpg.* The HP-32E

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

Perhaps the HP-30 series, Spice, was to be released as a replacement for the aging HP-20 series.[^o9][^o18] It has no expandability. The display provided better readability by increasing the digit size and adding commas.

The entry-level models were the HP-31E and 32E, which were not programmable, but even the 31E provided a self-check function.[^o11]

The HP-37E and 38E/C were the financial models of the Spice series.

The battery of these calculators can be changed without using tools. The housing is closed by screws.[^o13]

### Programming {#Programming}

The HP-33E/C (49 steps plus subroutines),[^o14] HP-34C (70 steps)[^o15] and 38E/C (99 steps)[^o18] are programmable. The C-suffixed models have a [continuous memory](wikipedia://en/6053018).

The production of the 31E ended in 1980,[^o12] production of the other Spice models ended in 1983.[^o15] This calculator is well regarded in terms of quality, key stroke feel and daily usability for engineers.

- Programmability (keystroke programming with [branching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branch_%28computer_science%29), [loops](wikipedia://en/45459#Loops), tests and flags)
