# Shaher Momani

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Shaher Momani"}

**Shaher Momani** is a Jordanian distinguished professor of [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and one of the top ten scientists in the world in the field of [fractional calculus](wikipedia://en/229939) according to [Web of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_of_Science) several times between 2009 and 2013. He was selected as one of the world's most influential scientific minds according to [Thomson Reuters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomson_Reuters) in 2014 and 2015.[^o4][^o5] Momani was also selected by Thomson Reuters as one of the highly cited researchers between 2014 and 2018.[^o6][^o7] Al Momani was honored by King [Abdullah II Bin Al Hussein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdullah_II_Bin_Al_Hussein)[^o8] among the Jordanian Stars of Science for his contributions to his field of specialization during the [World Science Forum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Science_Forum) in 2017.[^o9]

:::

## Qualifications and positions {#Qualifications_and_positions}

- [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) in mathematics in 1984 from [Yarmouk University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yarmouk_University).
- [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics in 1991 from [University of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wales).[^o11]
- [Islamic World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamic_World_Academy_of_Sciences) fellowship.[^o12]
- Dean of the Faculty of Science at university of Jordan between 2014-2016.
- Dean of Academic Research at university of Jordan between 2016-2018.
- Member of [Princess Sumaya University for Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princess_Sumaya_University_for_Technology) board of trustees.[^o2]
- [Jordan Scientific Research Support Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_Scientific_Research_Support_Fund) member between 2010-2012.
- Member at the [editorial board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editorial_board) of *[The Jordanian Journal of Mathematics and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Jordanian_Journal_of_Mathematics_and_Statistics)*.[^o13]
- Editor in chief of University of Jordan *Dirasat* journal.
- Chairman of the Department of Mathematics at the University of Jordan between 2012 and 2014.
- Chairman of the Department of Mathematics at [Mutah University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mutah_University) between 1994 and 1995.

## Awards {#Awards}

- The Order of [King Abdullah II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Abdullah_II) for Excellence of the Second Class for scientific research.
- [ISESCO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISESCO) Science Prize in 2008.[^o15]
- Ali Mango Distinguished Researcher Prize in 2016.
- [TWAS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TWAS_Prize) for the Young Scientists in 2000.
- Researcher Prize in Jordan in 2012.
- Distinguished Researcher Prize at [The University of Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_University_of_Jordan).
