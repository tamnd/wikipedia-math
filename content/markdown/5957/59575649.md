# Matthias Schonlau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matthias Schonlau"}

**Matthias Schonlau** (born 9 November 1967)[^o2] is a German Professor of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [actuarial science](wikipedia://en/189373) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Matthias Schonlau was born in [Höxter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Höxter), Germany. He attended [University of Ulm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ulm) for an undergrad degree but never obtained it because he became enrolled into a master's program at the [Queen's University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston)[^o4] from which he graduated in 1993.[^o5] In 1997 Matthias got his Ph.D. from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).[^o5]

From 1997 to 1999 he was a researcher for the [National Institute of Statistical Sciences](wikipedia://en/56043115) under mentorship of Alan Karr and during the same time worked as a [security hacker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Security_hacker) at the [AT&T Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT&T_Labs).[^o4]

From 1999 to 2011 he worked as [survey methodologist](wikipedia://en/272134) for [RAND Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation) under mentorship of Sally Morton[^o4] at its [Santa Monica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Monica) headquarters and eventually moved to their Pittsburgh office.[^o6] From 2009 to 2010 he attended a sabbatical at the [German Institute for Economic Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Institute_for_Economic_Research) which was hosted in cooperation with the [Max Planck Institute for Human Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Institute_for_Human_Development)[^o5] and from 2015 to 2016 he was a [visiting scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar) at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland).[^o7] From 2015 to 2018 he was a member of the [European Survey Research Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Survey_Research_Association) and in the end of 2018 became the president of the survey methods section of the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327). He is also an associate editor of the *[Survey Research Methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Survey_Research_Methods)*.[^o6]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Schonlau is an *NISS Distinguished Alumni Award* recipient.[^o9] He was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2020.[^o10] In 2022 he was the recipient of a Humboldt Prize.[^o11]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Matthias Schonlau is married to Dr. Karla Kaphengst, a physician from the University Health and Counselling Centre. They have two children.[^o13]
