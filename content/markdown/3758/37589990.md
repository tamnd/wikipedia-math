# Matsushima's formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Matsushima's formula"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Matsushima's formula**, introduced by (txt), is a formula for the [Betti numbers](wikipedia://en/346262) of a quotient of a [symmetric space](wikipedia://en/1642877) *G*/*H* by a [discrete group](wikipedia://en/468536), in terms of [unitary](wikipedia://en/292788) [representations](wikipedia://en/12695) of the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G*. [^o3] The **Matsushima–Murakami formula** is a generalization giving dimensions of spaces of [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146), introduced by (Matsushima Murakami 1968).[^o5]

:::
