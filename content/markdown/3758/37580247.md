# Wonderful compactification

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wonderful compactification"}

In [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924) theory, a **wonderful compactification** of a variety acted on by an algebraic group $G$ is a $G$-equivariant [compactification](wikipedia://en/6617) such that the [closure](wikipedia://en/42309) of each [orbit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29#Orbits_and_stabilizers) is smooth. (txt, 1–44) constructed a wonderful compactification of any [symmetric variety](wikipedia://en/37635994) given by a [quotient](wikipedia://en/11526) $G/G^{\sigma}$ of an algebraic group $G$ by the subgroup $G^{\sigma}$ fixed by some involution $\sigma$ of $G$ over the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826), sometimes called the **De Concini–Procesi compactification**. (txt) generalized this construction to arbitrary characteristic. In particular, by writing a group $G$ itself as a symmetric homogeneous space, $G=(G \times G)/G$ (modulo the diagonal subgroup), this gives a wonderful compactification of the group $G$ itself.

:::
