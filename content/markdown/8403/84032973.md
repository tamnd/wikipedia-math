# Tai Melcher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tai Melcher"}

**Tai Alexis Melcher** is an American mathematician and an associate professor of mathematics at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia). Her research combines [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), especially focusing on [heat kernels](wikipedia://en/3904336) in [degenerate](wikipedia://en/232952) geometric spaces.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Melcher completed her Ph.D. in 2004 at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego), with the dissertation *Hypoelliptic Heat Kernel Inequalities on Lie Groups* supervised by Bruce Driver.

After postdoctoral research at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), she joined the University of Virginia mathematics department, and was tenured there in 2015.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Melcher was one of two 2014 recipients of the [AWM Service Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM_Service_Award) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698). She was named as a Fellow of the Association for Women in Mathematics, in the 2027 class of fellows, "for her creation of the Women in Probability network; co-organizing various activities thereof; professional mentoring of early career probabilists; and involvement in numerous AWM events".
