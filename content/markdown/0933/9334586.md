# Vladimir Markov (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Markov"}

**Vladimir Andreyevich Markov** (Влади́мир Андре́евич Ма́рков; May 8, 1871 – January 18, 1897) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for proving the [Markov brothers' inequality](wikipedia://en/9318403) with his older brother [Andrey Markov](wikipedia://en/915). He died of tuberculosis at the age of 25.[^o3]

:::
