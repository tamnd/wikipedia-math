# Journal of Industrial and Management Optimization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Industrial and Management Optimization"}

The ***Journal of Industrial and Management Optimization*** (JIMO) is an international journal published by American Institute of Mathematical Sciences and sponsored by Department of Mathematics and Statistics, [Curtin University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curtin_University_of_Technology), and Department of Mathematics, [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University). This journal illustrates original research papers on the non-trivial interplay between numerical optimization methods and problems in industry or management. The objective of this journal is to develop new optimization ideas so as to solve industrial and management problems by the use of appropriate, advanced optimization techniques.

:::

Its [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) has been frequently ranked by [SCImago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCImago) as in the top quartile of business and international management journals.[^o2]
