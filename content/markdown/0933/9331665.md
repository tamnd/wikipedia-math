# Visibility (geometry)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Visibility"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **visibility** is a mathematical [abstraction](wikipedia://en/2972) of the real-life notion of visibility.

:::

Given a set of obstacles in the [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697), two points in the space are said to be visible to each other, if the [line segment](wikipedia://en/22634860) that joins them does not intersect any obstacles. (In the [Earth's atmosphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth's_atmosphere) light follows a slightly curved path that is not perfectly predictable, complicating the calculation of actual visibility.)

Computation of visibility is among the basic problems in [computational geometry](wikipedia://en/176927) and has applications in [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics), [motion planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Motion_planning), and other areas.
