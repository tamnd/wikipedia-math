# Xin Zhou

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xin Zhou"}

**Xin Zhou** is a mathematician known for his contributions in [scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory), [integrable systems](wikipedia://en/2573213), [random matrices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_matrices) and [Riemann–Hilbert problems](wikipedia://en/9710396).

:::

He is Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University). Zhou had obtained M.Sc. from the [University of the Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1982 and then got his Ph.D. in 1988 from the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester).[^o2] He received the [Pólya prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28SIAM%29) in 1998[^o3] and was awarded with the Guggenheim Fellowship in 1999.[^o4] He is most well known for his work with [Percy Deift](wikipedia://en/25799769) on the steepest descent method for oscillatory Riemann–Hilbert problems.[^o5]
