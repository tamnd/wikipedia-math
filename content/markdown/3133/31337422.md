# Dold manifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dold manifold"}

In mathematics, a **Dold manifold** is one of the [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) $P(m,n) = (S^m \times \mathbb{CP}^n)/\tau$, where $\tau$ is the involution that acts as −1 on the [*m*-sphere](wikipedia://en/39782) $S^m$ and as [complex conjugation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_conjugation) on the [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977) $\mathbb{CP}^n$. These manifolds were constructed by (txt),[^o3] who used them to give explicit generators for [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633)'s [unoriented cobordism ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unoriented_cobordism_ring).[^o4] Note that $P(m,0)=\mathbb{RP}^m$, the [real projective space](wikipedia://en/1014694) of dimension *m*, and $P(0,n)=\mathbb{CP}^n$.[^o5]

:::
