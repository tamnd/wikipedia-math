In 5-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 31 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/14415338) with B<sub>5</sub> symmetry. There are two regular forms, the [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615), and [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) with 10 and 32 vertices respectively. The [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232) is added as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the 5-cube.

:::

They can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the B<sub>5</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 32 polytopes can be made in the B<sub>5</sub>, B<sub>4</sub>, B<sub>3</sub>, B<sub>2</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>k</sub> has *\[k+1\]* symmetry, and B<sub>k</sub> has *\[2k\]* symmetry.

These 32 polytopes are each shown in these 5 symmetry planes, with vertices and edges drawn, and vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position.
