# James reduced product

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James reduced product"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **James reduced product** or **James construction** *J*(*X*) of a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) *X* with given basepoint *e* is the [quotient](wikipedia://en/237213) of the [disjoint union](wikipedia://en/188946) of all powers *X*, *X*<sup>2</sup>, *X*<sup>3</sup>, ... obtained by identifying points (*x*<sub>1</sub>,...,*x*<sub>*k*−1</sub>,*e*,*x*<sub>*k*+1</sub>,...,*x*<sub>*n*</sub>) with (*x*<sub>1</sub>,...,*x*<sub>*k*−1</sub>, *x*<sub>*k*+1</sub>,...,*x*<sub>*n*</sub>). In other words, its underlying set is the free monoid generated by *X* (with unit *e*). It was introduced by (txt).

:::

For a connected [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022) *X*, the James reduced product *J*(*X*) has the same homotopy type as ΩΣ*X*, the [loop space](wikipedia://en/2174332) of the [suspension](wikipedia://en/1251702) of *X*.

The commutative analogue of the James reduced product is called the [infinite symmetric product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_symmetric_product).
