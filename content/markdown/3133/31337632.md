In 5-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 19 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/14415338) with A<sub>5</sub> symmetry. There is one self-dual regular form, the [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex) with 6 vertices.

:::

Each can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in the [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the A<sub>5</sub> Coxeter group and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 19 polytopes can be made in the A<sub>5</sub>, A<sub>4</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, A<sub>2</sub> [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>k</sub> graphs have *\[k+1\]* symmetry. For even k and symmetrically nodea\_1ed-diagrams, symmetry doubles to *\[2(k+1)\]*.

These 19 polytopes are each shown in these 4 symmetry planes, with vertices and edges drawn and vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position.
