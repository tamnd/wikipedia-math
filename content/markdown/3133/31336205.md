::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Cantellated 5-orthoplexes"}

In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantellated 5-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [cantellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation) of the regular [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615).

:::

There are 6 cantellation for the 5-orthoplex, including truncations. Some of them are more easily constructed from the dual [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215).

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a cantitruncated 5-orthoplex, centered at the origin, are all sign and coordinate [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of

(±3,±2,±1,0,0)
