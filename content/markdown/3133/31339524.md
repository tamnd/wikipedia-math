# Ramin Takloo-Bighash

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ramin Takloo-Bighash"}

**Ramin Takloo-Bighash** (born 1974) is a mathematician who works in the field of [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146) and [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205) and is a professor at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago).

:::

## Mathematical career {#Mathematical_career}

Takloo-Bighash graduated from the [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_University_of_Technology), where he enrolled after winning a Silver medal at the 1992 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208). In 2001, Takloo-Bighash graduated under [Joseph Shalika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Shalika) from [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University). He spent 2001-2007 at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), first as an instructor and then as an assistant professor. He is a professor at the University of Illinois at Chicago.

## Research {#Research}

Takloo-Bighash computed the local factors of [spinor](wikipedia://en/29276) [L-function](wikipedia://en/245283) attached to generic automorphic forms on the [symplectic group](wikipedia://en/173977) GSp(4). He has joint works with Joseph Shalika and [Yuri Tschinkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yuri_Tschinkel) on the distribution of [rational points](wikipedia://en/2960200) on certain group compactifications. He is a co-author, with [Steven J. Miller](wikipedia://en/46929926), of *An Invitation To Modern Number Theory* (Princeton University Press, 2006).
