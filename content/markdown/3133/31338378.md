# Boolean-valued

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boolean-valued"}

**Boolean-valued** usually refers to:

:::

- in most applied fields: something taking one of two values (example: True or False, On or Off, 1 or 0) referring to [two-element Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/2584449) (the [Boolean domain](wikipedia://en/3954077)), e.g. [Boolean-valued function](wikipedia://en/2757237) or [Boolean data type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_data_type)
- in mathematics: something taking values over an arbitrary, [abstract Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959), for example [Boolean-valued model](wikipedia://en/2732301)
