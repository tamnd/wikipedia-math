# Albrecht Dold

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Albrecht Dold"}

**Albrecht Dold** (5 August 1928 – 26 September 2011) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) who proved the [Dold–Thom theorem](wikipedia://en/31336432), the [Dold–Kan correspondence](wikipedia://en/39431504), and introduced [Dold manifolds](wikipedia://en/31337422), [Dold–Puppe stabilization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dold–Puppe_stabilization), and [Dold fibrations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dold_fibration).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Albrecht Dold was born in Triberg, and studied mathematics and physics at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University), earning a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree in 1954 under the direction of [Herbert Seifert](wikipedia://en/637024). He visited the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton in 1956–58, and taught at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1960–62 and at the [University of Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zürich) in 1962–63. In 1963 he returned to Heidelberg, where he stayed most of his career, till his retirement in 1996.

Dold's work in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), in particular, his work on [fixed-point theory](wikipedia://en/1011848) has made him known in economics as well as mathematics. His book "Lectures on Algebraic Topology" is a standard reference among economists as well as mathematicians. He had 19 doctoral students, including [Mónica Clapp](wikipedia://en/45285384), [Eberhard Freitag](wikipedia://en/50890271), Volker Puppe, and Carl-Friedrich Bödigheimer, as well as 58 descendants.

Dold was married to [Yvonne Dold-Samplonius](wikipedia://en/56493375), a distinguished historian of mathematics.
