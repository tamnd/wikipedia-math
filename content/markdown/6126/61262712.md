# Phokion G. Kolaitis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Phokion G. Kolaitis"}

**Phokion Gerasimos Kolaitis** <sup>[ACM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Fellow)</sup> (born 4 July 1950) is a [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) who is currently a Distinguished Research Professor at [UC Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Santa_Cruz) and a Principal Research Staff Member at the [IBM Almaden Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Almaden_Research_Center). He is known for his work on principles of [database systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database_systems), [logic in computer science](wikipedia://en/1151991), [computational complexity](wikipedia://en/6511), and other related fields.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Kolaitis obtained a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the [University of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Athens) in 1973 and a master's degree and [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Mathematics from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1974 and 1978, respectively.[^o5]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Kolaitis is currently a Distinguished Research Professor at the Computer Science and Engineering Department of [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz). He is also a Principal Research Staff Member in the theory group at the [IBM Almaden Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Almaden_Research_Center).
