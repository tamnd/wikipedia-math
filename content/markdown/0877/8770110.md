# Michael Boardman

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Boardman"}

**John Michael Boardman** (13 February 1938 – 18 March 2021) was a mathematician whose speciality was [algebraic](wikipedia://en/38801) and [differential](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_geometry_and_topology) topology. He was affiliated with the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), England and the [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) in [Baltimore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baltimore), Maryland. Boardman was most widely known for his construction of the first rigorously correct model of the [homotopy category](wikipedia://en/2040756) of [spectra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectrum_%28homotopy_theory%29).

:::

He received his PhD from the University of Cambridge in 1964. His thesis advisor was [C. T. C. Wall](wikipedia://en/2613478).[^o2] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4] He died on 18 March 2021.[^o5]
