*Image: Small-icosiicosahedron-in-icosidodecahedron.png.* It is also a [faceting](wikipedia://en/8613537) of the icosidodecahedron.

The **compound of five octahedra** is one of the five regular [polyhedron compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound), and can also be seen as a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253). It was first described by [Edmund Hess](wikipedia://en/9191575) in 1876. It is unique among the regular compounds for not having a regular convex hull. It can also be called a small icosicosahedron.

:::

## As a stellation {#As_a_stellation}

It is the second [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084), and given as [Wenninger model index 23](wikipedia://en/2915428#Stellations_of_icosahedron).

It can be constructed by a [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025) with rhombic-based [pyramids](wikipedia://en/3307045) added to all the faces, as shown by the five colored model image. (This construction does not generate the *regular* compound of five octahedra, but shares the same topology and can be smoothly deformed into the regular compound.)

It has a density of greater than 1.

## As a compound {#As_a_compound}

It can also be seen as a [polyhedral compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_compound) of five [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) arranged in [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844) (**I**<sub>h</sub>).

The [spherical](wikipedia://en/10393861) and [stereographic](wikipedia://en/143431) projections of this compound look the same as those of the [disdyakis triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/725111). But the convex solid's vertices on 3- and 5-fold symmetry axes (gray in the images below) correspond only to edge crossings in the compound.

Replacing the octahedra by [tetrahemihexahedra](wikipedia://en/2851834) leads to the [compound of five tetrahemihexahedra](wikipedia://en/14729346).

## Other 5-octahedra compounds {#Other_5-octahedra_compounds}

A second 5-octahedra compound, with octahedral symmetry, also exists. It can be generated by adding a fifth octahedron to the [standard 4-octahedra compound](wikipedia://en/14836193).
