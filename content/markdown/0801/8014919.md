# Lindelöf's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lindelöf's theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Lindelöf's theorem** is a result in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) named after the [Finnish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Ernst Leonard Lindelöf](wikipedia://en/960668). It states that a [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110) on a half-strip in the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628) that is [bounded](wikipedia://en/311509) on the [boundary](wikipedia://en/72824) of the strip and does not grow "too fast" in the unbounded direction of the strip must remain bounded on the whole strip. The result is useful in the study of the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809), and is a special case of the [Phragmén&ndash;Lindelöf principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phragmén&ndash;Lindelöf_principle). Also, see [Hadamard three-lines theorem](wikipedia://en/30418364).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let $\Omega$ be a half-strip in the complex plane:

$$\Omega = \{ z \in \mathbb{C} | x_1 \leq \mathrm{Re} (z) \leq x_2\ \text{and}\ \mathrm{Im} (z) \geq y_0 \} \subsetneq \mathbb{C}.$$

Suppose that $f$ is [holomorphic](wikipedia://en/14110) (i.e. [analytic](wikipedia://en/61478)) on $\Omega$ and that there are constants $M$, $A$, and $B$ such that

$$| f(z) | \leq M \ \text{for all}\ z \in \partial \Omega$$

and

$$| f (x + i y) | \leq B y^A\ \text{for all}\ x + i y \in \Omega.$$

Then $f$ is bounded by $M$ on all of $\Omega$:

$$| f(z) | \leq M\ \text{for all}\ z \in \Omega.$$

:::

::: {#o5 .proof cue=heading name="Proof"}

## Proof {#Proof}

Fix a point $\xi=\sigma+i\tau$ inside $\Omega$. Choose $\lambda>-y_0$, an integer $N>A$ and $y_1>\tau$ large enough such that $\frac{By_1^A}{(y_1 + \lambda)^N}\le \frac {M}{(y_0+\lambda)^N}$. Applying [maximum modulus principle](wikipedia://en/880710) to the function $g(z)=\frac {f(z)}{(z+i\lambda)^N}$ and the rectangular area $\{z \in \mathbb{C} \mid x_1 \leq \mathrm{Re} (z) \leq x_2\ \text{and}\ y_0 \leq \mathrm{Im} (z) \leq y_1 \}$ we obtain $|g(\xi)|\le \frac{M}{(y_0+\lambda)^N}$, that is, $|f(\xi)|\le M\left(\frac{|\xi + \lambda|}{y_0+\lambda}\right)^N$. Letting $\lambda \to +\infty$ yields $|f(\xi)| \le M$ as required.

:::
