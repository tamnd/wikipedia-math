The **[compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_compound) of five [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285)** is one of the five [regular polyhedral compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_compound#Regular_compounds). It was first described by [Edmund Hess](wikipedia://en/9191575) in 1876.

:::

Its vertices are those of a regular [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407). Its edges form [pentagrams](wikipedia://en/197544#Geometry), which are the [stellations](wikipedia://en/180253) of the pentagonal faces of the dodecahedron.

It is one of the stellations of the [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025). Its dual is the [compound of five octahedra](wikipedia://en/8010546). It has [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844) (**I**<sub>h</sub>).

The compound of five cubes can also be known as a rhombihedron.

## Geometry {#Geometry}

The compound is a [faceting](wikipedia://en/8613537) of the [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407). Each cube represents a selection of 8 of the 20 vertices of the dodecahedron.

If the shape is considered as a union of five cubes yielding a simple nonconvex solid without self-intersecting surfaces, then it has 360 faces (all [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654)), 182 vertices (60 with degree 3, 30 with degree 4, 12 with degree 5, 60 with degree 8, and 20 with degree 12), and 540 edges, yielding an [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) of 182 − 540 + 360 = 2.

## Edge arrangement {#Edge_arrangement}

Its [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) is a regular [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).[^n3] It additionally shares its [edge arrangement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_arrangement) with the [small ditrigonal icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157350), the [great ditrigonal icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4156959), and the [ditrigonal dodecadodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157586). With these, it can form polyhedral compounds that can also be considered as degenerate [uniform star polyhedra](wikipedia://en/8464397); respectively, the [small complex rhombicosidodecahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_complex_rhombicosidodecahedron), [great complex rhombicosidodecahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_complex_rhombicosidodecahedron) and [complex rhombidodecadodecahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_rhombidodecadodecahedron).

The [compound of ten tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/8010465) can be formed by taking each of these five [cubes](wikipedia://en/6285) and replacing them with the two [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) of the [stella octangula](wikipedia://en/2601866) (which share the same vertex arrangement of a cube).

## As a stellation {#As_a_stellation}

This compound can be formed as a stellation of the [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025). The 30 rhombic faces exist in the planes of the 5 cubes.
