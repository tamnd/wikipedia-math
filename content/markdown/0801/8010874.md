The **compound of cube and octahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) which can be seen as either a polyhedral [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) or a [compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound).

## Construction {#Construction}

The 14 [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of the compound are.

6: (±2, 0, 0), ( 0, ±2, 0), ( 0, 0, ±2)

8: ( ±1, ±1, ±1)

:::

## As a compound {#As_a_compound}

It can be seen as the [compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound) of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) and a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285). It is one of four compounds constructed from a [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905) or [Kepler-Poinsot polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-Poinsot_polyhedron) and its dual.

It has [octahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2900805) (**O**<sub>*h*</sub>) and shares the same vertices as a [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631).

This can be seen as the three-dimensional equivalent of the compound of two squares ({8/2} "[octagram](wikipedia://en/2667613)"); this series continues on to infinity, with the four-dimensional equivalent being the compound of tesseract and 16-cell.

## As a stellation {#As_a_stellation}

It is also the first [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) and given as [Wenninger model index 43](wikipedia://en/2915428#Stellations_of_cuboctahedron).

It can be seen as a [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280) with [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) and [triangular](wikipedia://en/30654) [pyramids](wikipedia://en/3307045) added to each face.
