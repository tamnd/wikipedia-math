## As a compound {#As_a_compound}

It can be seen as the [compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound) of an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) and [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407). It is one of four compounds constructed from a [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905) or [Kepler-Poinsot solid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-Poinsot_solid), and its [dual](wikipedia://en/8815).

It has [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844) (**I**<sub>*h*</sub>) and the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as a [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025).

This can be seen as the three-dimensional equivalent of the compound of two pentagons ({10/2} "[decagram](wikipedia://en/19546742)"); this series continues into the fourth dimension as the [compound of 120-cell and 600-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_120-cell_and_600-cell) and into higher dimensions as compounds of hyperbolic tilings.

## As a stellation {#As_a_stellation}

## As a Faceting {#As_a_Faceting}

The compound of a Dodecahedron and an Icosahedron shares the same vertices as a list of other polyhedra, including the [Rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025) and the [Small triambic icosahedron](wikipedia://en/18437022).

## In popular culture {#In_popular_culture}

In the film *[Tron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tron)* (1982), the character [Bit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Tron_%28franchise%29_characters#Bit) took this shape when not speaking.

In the cartoon series *[Steven Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Universe)* (2013-2019), [Steven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Steven_Universe_characters#Steven_Universe)'s shield bubble, briefly used in the episode [Change Your Mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change_Your_Mind_%28Steven_Universe%29), had this shape.
