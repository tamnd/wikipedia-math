::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of great icosahedron and great stellated dodecahedron"}

There are two different **compounds of great icosahedron and great stellated dodecahedron**: one is a [dual compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_compound) and a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [great icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157391), the other is a stellation of the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023).

:::

## Dual compound {#Dual_compound}

It can be seen as a [polyhedron compound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedron_compound) of a [great icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3029898) and [great stellated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/3029878). It is one of five compounds constructed from a [Platonic solid](wikipedia://en/23905) or [Kepler-Poinsot solid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kepler-Poinsot_solid), and its dual. It is a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [great icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/4157391).

It has [icosahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2877844) (**I**<sub>*h*</sub>) and it has the same [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) as a [great rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/20823563).

This can be seen as one of the two three-dimensional equivalents of the compound of two pentagrams ({10/4} "[decagram](wikipedia://en/19546742)"); this series continues into the fourth dimension as [compounds of star 4-polytopes](wikipedia://en/23901#Compounds_with_regular_star_4-polytopes).

## Stellation of the icosidodecahedron {#Stellation_of_the_icosidodecahedron}

This [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) is a [stellation](wikipedia://en/180253) of the [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023), and given as [Wenninger model index 61](wikipedia://en/2915428#Stellations_of_icosidodecahedron). It has the same vertex arrangement as a [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025), its convex hull.
