# FGLM algorithm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FGLM algorithm"}

**FGLM** is one of the main [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) in [computer algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbolic_computation), named after its designers, [Faugère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Charles_Faugère), [Gianni](wikipedia://en/70319019), [Lazard](wikipedia://en/34227839) and [Mora](wikipedia://en/53596978). They introduced their algorithm in 1993. The input of the algorithm is a [Gröbner basis](wikipedia://en/357328) of a zero-dimensional [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) in the ring of [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) with respect to a [monomial order](wikipedia://en/696408) and a second [monomial order](wikipedia://en/696408). As its output, it returns a Gröbner basis of the ideal with respect to the second ordering. The algorithm is a fundamental tool in computer algebra and has been implemented in most of the [computer algebra systems](wikipedia://en/54427). The [complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity_of_algorithms) of FGLM is *O*(*nD*<sup>3</sup>), where *n* is the number of variables of the polynomials and D is the degree of the ideal.[^o2] There are several generalization and various applications for FGLM.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::
