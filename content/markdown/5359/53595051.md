# Jean Berstel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean Berstel"}

**Jean Berstel** (born 1941) is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical computer scientist](wikipedia://en/323392) known for his contributions to [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490) and [formal language theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_language_theory). He is currently a professor emeritus at the [University of Marne-la-Vallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marne-la-Vallée).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Berstel earned his doctorate (doctorat d'État) at [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) in 1973. In 1973–1995 he was a professor at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University), and in 1995–2005 a professor at the [University of Marne-la-Vallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marne-la-Vallée), where he has been a professor emeritus since 2005.

In 2006, Berstel was awarded an honorary doctorate from the [University of Turku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turku), [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland). A festschrift in his honour was published in 2003 as a special issue of [*Theoretical Computer Science*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_Computer_Science_%28journal%29).[^o4]

## Research contributions {#Research_contributions}

Berstel has been a member of the [Lothaire](wikipedia://en/32232474) group of mathematicians that developed the foundations of combinatorics of words. He has published several scientific monographs, including *Transductions and Context-free Languages* (1979),[^o6] *Theory of Codes* (1985, jointly with [Dominique Perrin](wikipedia://en/53543152)),[^o7] and *Codes and Automata* (2009; jointly with [Dominique Perrin](wikipedia://en/53543152) and Christophe Reutenauer)[^o8] as well as the three [Lothaire books](wikipedia://en/32232474).[^o9][^o10][^o11]
