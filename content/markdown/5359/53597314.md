# Jacqueline Naze Tjøtta

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jacqueline Naze Tjøtta"}

**Jacqueline Andrée Naze Tjøtta** (1 June 1935 – 9 March 2017) was a Norwegian mathematician who became the first female mathematical sciences professor in Norway.[^o4]

:::

Jacqueline Naze was born in [Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montpellier), France, to musician Maurize André Naze and Renée Marie Courbet. She graduated in mathematics from the [Aix-Marseille University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-Marseille_University) and the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). She married Norwegian mathematician [Sigve Tjøtta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigve_Tjøtta) in 1964. She was appointed professor in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen) from 1966. Her research interests focused on kinetic theory, [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310) and theoretical [acoustics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustics).[^o3]

She died in Oslo on 9 March 2017 at the age of 81.[^o4]
