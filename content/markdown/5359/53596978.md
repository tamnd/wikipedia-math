# Teo Mora

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Teo Mora"}

**Ferdinando 'Teo' Mora** is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and since 1990 until 2019 a professor of [algebra](wikipedia://en/1997#Computational_algebraic_geometry) at the [University of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genoa).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Mora's degree is in mathematics from the [University of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genoa) in 1974. Mora's publications span forty years; his notable contributions in [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805) are the [tangent cone](wikipedia://en/1416915) algorithm[^o3][^o4] and its extension of [Buchberger](wikipedia://en/1924233) theory of [Gröbner bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gröbner_bases) and related [algorithm](wikipedia://en/696317) earlier[^o5] to non-commutative [polynomial rings](wikipedia://en/373065)[^o6] and more recently[^o7] to effective rings; less significant[^o8] the notion of [Gröbner fan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gröbner_fan); marginal, with respect to the other authors, his contribution to the [FGLM algorithm](wikipedia://en/53596792).

Mora is on the [managing-editorial-board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_peer_review) of the journal *Applicable Algebra in Engineering, Communication and Computing* published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media),[^o14] and was also formerly an editor of the *Bulletin of the [Iranian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/46332302)*.

He is the author of the tetralogy *Solving Polynomial Equation Systems*:

- *Solving Polynomial Equation Systems I: The [Kronecker](wikipedia://en/385253)-[Duval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duval) Philosophy*, on equations [in one variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Univariate_polynomial#Equations)[^o16]
- *Solving Polynomial Equation Systems II: [Macaulay](wikipedia://en/3592252)'s paradigm and [Gröbner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gröbner_bases) technology*, on [equations in several variables](wikipedia://en/27420015)[^o15][^o16]
- *Solving Polynomial Equation Systems III: Algebraic Solving*,
- *Solving Polynomial Equation Systems IV: [Buchberger](wikipedia://en/1924233) Theory and Beyond*, on the [Buchberger algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buchberger_algorithm)

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Mora lives in [Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genoa).[^o13] Mora published a book trilogy in 1977-1978 (reprinted 2001-2003) called *Storia del cinema dell'orrore* on the [history of horror films](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horror_film#History).[^o13] [Italian television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiotelevisione_Italiana) said in 2014 that the books are an "authoritative guide with in-depth detailed descriptions and analysis."[^o12]
