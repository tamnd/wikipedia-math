# Hyperspecial subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hyperspecial subgroup"}

In the theory of [reductive groups](wikipedia://en/1088386) over [local fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_fields), a **hyperspecial subgroup** of a reductive group *G* is a certain type of compact subgroup of *G*.

:::

In particular, let *F* be a nonarchimedean [local field](wikipedia://en/145375), *O* its ring of integers, *k* its residue field and *G* a reductive group over *F*. A subgroup *K* of *G(F)* is called **hyperspecial** if there exists a smooth [group scheme](wikipedia://en/510523) Γ over *O* such that

- Γ<sub>F</sub>=*G*,
- Γ<sub>k</sub> is a connected reductive group, and
- Γ(*O*)=*K*.

The original definition of a hyperspecial subgroup (appearing in section 1.10.2 of [^o2]) was in terms of *hyperspecial points* in the [Bruhat–Tits building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruhat–Tits_building) of *G*. The equivalent definition above is given in the same paper of Tits, section 3.8.1.

Hyperspecial subgroups of *G(F)* exist if, and only if, *G* is unramified over *F*.[^o3]

An interesting property of hyperspecial subgroups, is that among all compact subgroups of *G(F)*, the hyperspecial subgroups have maximum measure.
