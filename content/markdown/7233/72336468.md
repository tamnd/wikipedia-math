# Martha Guzmán Partida

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martha Guzmán Partida"}

Guzmán **Martha Dolores Guzmán Partida** is a Mexican mathematician specializing in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), including [Fourier analysis](wikipedia://en/11659), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), and the theory of [distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distribution_%28mathematics%29). She is a professor of mathematics at the [Universidad de Sonora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_de_Sonora).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Guzmán Partida was an undergraduate at the [Meritorious Autonomous University of Puebla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meritorious_Autonomous_University_of_Puebla). She completed her doctorate in 1995 at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico). Her dissertation, *Hardy Spaces of Conjugates Temperatures*, was supervised by Salvador Pérez Esteva.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Guzmán was elected to the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2013.
