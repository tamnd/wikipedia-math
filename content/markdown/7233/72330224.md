# Hortensia Galeana Sánchez

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hortensia Galeana Sánchez"}

Galeana **Hortensia Galeana Sánchez** (born 6 November 1955)[^o2] is a Mexican mathematician specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), including [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743) and the [independent dominating sets](wikipedia://en/1747972) ("kernels") of [directed graphs](wikipedia://en/19721986). She is the director of the Institute of Mathematics at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Galeana is originally from Mexico City. She was educated at UNAM, earning her bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees there in 1978, 1981, and 1985 respectively. Her 1985 doctoral dissertation, *Algunos resultados en la teoría de núcleos en digráficas*, was supervised by [Víctor Neumann-Lara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Víctor_Neumann-Lara).

She has taught at UNAM since 1977 and was named director of the UNAM Institute of Mathematics in 2022.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Galeana is a member of the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1995 and again in 2015 UNAM gave her their National University Prize.
