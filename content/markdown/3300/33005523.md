# Denisyuk polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denisyuk polynomials"}

In mathematics, **Denisyuk polynomials** De<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) or *M*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*) are generalizations of the [Laguerre polynomials](wikipedia://en/943917) introduced by (Denisyuk 1954) given by the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)[^n1]

:::

$$\displaystyle \sum_{n=0}^\infty t^nM_n(x)=\frac 1{1+t}\exp\left(-\frac{xt}{1-t}\right).$$
