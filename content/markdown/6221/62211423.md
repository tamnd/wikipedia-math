# Helmut Schwichtenberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Helmut Schwichtenberg"}

*Image: Moschovakis Schwichtenberg Troelstra.jpg.* *From left:* [Yiannis Moschovakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yiannis_Moschovakis), Helmut Schwichtenberg, [Anne Sjerp Troelstra](wikipedia://en/15067195), 2002 at the [MFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach)

**Helmut Schwichtenberg** (born 5 April 1942[^o4]) is a German [mathematical logician](wikipedia://en/19636).

:::

Schwichtenberg studied mathematics from 1961 at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin) and from 1964 at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster), where he received his doctorate in 1968 from [Dieter Rödding](wikipedia://en/66658943).[^o4][^o5] He then worked as an assistant and then as a professor in Münster,[^o7] and since 1978 has been professor of mathematical logic at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich)[^o4] (successor of [Kurt Schütte](wikipedia://en/16184365)).

Schwichtenberg deals with, among other things, [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478), [theory of computability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_computability), [lambda calculus](wikipedia://en/18203) and applications of logic in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science). He is a member of the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o7]
