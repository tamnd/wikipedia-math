# Hall–Petresco identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hall–Petresco identity"}

In mathematics, the **Hall–Petresco identity** (sometimes misspelled **Hall–Petrescu identity**) is an identity holding in any group. It was introduced by (txt) and (txt). It can be proved using the [commutator collecting process](wikipedia://en/46214749), and implies that *p*-groups of small class are [regular](wikipedia://en/15442661).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The Hall–Petresco identity states that if *x* and *y* are elements of a group *G* and *m* is a positive integer then

$$x^my^m=(xy)^mc_2^{\binom{m}{2}}c_3^{\binom{m}{3}}\cdots c_{m-1}^{\binom{m}{m-1}}c_m$$

where each *c*<sub>*i*</sub> is in the subgroup *K*<sub>*i*</sub> of the descending central series of *G*.

:::
