# Balaban 10-cage

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Balaban 10-cage"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Balaban 10-cage** or **Balaban $(3,10)$-cage** is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 70 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 105 edges named after [Alexandru T. Balaban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Balaban). Published in 1972,[^o4] It was the first 10-[cage](wikipedia://en/7735022) discovered but it is not unique.[^o5]

:::

The proof of minimality of the number of vertices was given by Mary R. O'Keefe and Pak Ken Wong.[^o6] There are 2 other distinct $(3,10)$-cages, the [Harries graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harries_graph) and the [Harries–Wong graph](wikipedia://en/23702758).[^o7] The Harries–Wong graph and Harries graph are also [cospectral](wikipedia://en/534914).

The Balaban 10-cage has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, [diameter](wikipedia://en/1020021#Related_concepts) 6, [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 10 and is [hamiltonian](wikipedia://en/244437). It is also a 3-[vertex-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph). The [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) is 3 and the [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) is 2.[^o8]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Balaban 10-cage is

$$(x-3) (x-2) (x-1)^8 x^2 (x+1)^8 (x+2) (x+3) \cdot$$

$$\cdot(x^2-6)^2 (x^2-5)^4 (x^2-2)^2 (x^4-6 x^2+3)^8.$$
