# McGee graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="McGee graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **McGee graph** or the **(3-7)-cage** is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 24 vertices and 36 edges.

:::

The McGee graph is the unique (3,7)-[cage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cage_graph) (the smallest [cubic graph](wikipedia://en/980508) of girth 7). It is also the smallest cubic cage that is not a [Moore graph](wikipedia://en/4112719).

First discovered by Sachs but unpublished,[^o4] the graph is named after McGee who published the result in 1960.[^o5] Then, the McGee graph was proven the unique (3,7)-cage by [Tutte](wikipedia://en/52021) in 1966.[^o6][^o7][^o8]

The McGee graph requires at least eight crossings in any drawing of it in the plane. It is one of three non-isomorphic graphs tied for being the smallest cubic graph that requires eight crossings. Another of these three graphs is the [generalized Petersen graph](wikipedia://en/23716451) *G*(12,5), also known as the [Nauru graph](wikipedia://en/24052345).[^o10]

The McGee graph has radius 4, diameter 4, [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3 and [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3. It is also a 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph) graph. It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o11] The graph is [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o12]

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the McGee graph is

$$x^3(x-3)(x-2)^3(x+1)^2(x+2)(x^2+x-4)(x^3+x^2-4x-2)^4.$$

The automorphism group of the McGee graph is of order 32 and doesn't act transitively upon its vertices: there are two vertex orbits, of lengths 8 and 16. The McGee graph is the smallest cubic cage that is not a [vertex-transitive graph](wikipedia://en/581175).[^o15]

The [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the McGee graph, meaning its group of symmetries, has 32 elements. This group is isomorphic to the group of all [affine transformations](wikipedia://en/38449) of $\mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$, i.e., transformations of the form

$$x \mapsto ax + b$$

where $a,b \in \mathbb{Z}/8\mathbb{Z}$ and $a$ is invertible, so $a = 1, 3, 5, 7$.[^o16] This is one of the two smallest possible group $G$ with an [outer automorphism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_automorphism) that maps every element $g \in G$ to an element conjugate to $g$.[^o17]
