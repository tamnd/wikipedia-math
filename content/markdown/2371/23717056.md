# Franklin graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Franklin graph"}

*Image: Klein\_bottle\_colouring.svg.* A 6-colored Klein bottle, the only exception to the Heawood conjecture

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Franklin graph** is a 3-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821) [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) with 12 vertices and 18 edges.

:::

The Franklin graph is named after [Philip Franklin](wikipedia://en/11458078), who disproved the [Heawood conjecture](wikipedia://en/1585274) on the number of colors needed when a two-dimensional surface is partitioned into cells by a [graph embedding](wikipedia://en/8149170).[^o3] The Heawood conjecture implied that the maximum chromatic number of a map on the [Klein bottle](wikipedia://en/17412) should be seven, but Franklin proved that in this case six colors always suffice. (The Klein bottle is the only surface for which the Heawood conjecture fails.) The Franklin graph can be embedded in the Klein bottle so that it forms a map requiring six colors, showing that six colors are sometimes necessary in this case. This embedding is the [Petrie dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrie_dual) of its embedding in the [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350) shown below.

It is [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3, [radius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radius_%28graph_theory%29) 3, [diameter](wikipedia://en/2979341) 3 and [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 4. It is also a 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph) [perfect graph](wikipedia://en/670531).

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of the Franklin graph is of [order](wikipedia://en/493527) 48 and is [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/12397) to **Z**/2**Z**×*S*<sub>4</sub>, the [direct product](wikipedia://en/3706015) of the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) **Z**/2**Z** and the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) *S*<sub>4</sub>. It [acts](wikipedia://en/12781) transitively on the vertices of the graph, making it [vertex-transitive](wikipedia://en/581175).

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Franklin graph is

$$(x-3) (x-1)^3 (x+1)^3 (x+3) (x^2-3)^2.\$$
