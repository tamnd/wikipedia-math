# Alexanderson Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexanderson Award"}

*Image: AlexandersonAwardMedal.jpg.* The Alexanderson Award Medal

The **Alexanderson Award**[^o2] is given annually by the [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005).

:::

The award was instituted in 2018. It honors outstanding research articles arising from AIM research activities (workshop, research community, or SQuaRE) which have been published within the past three years.[^o3]

## Motivation {#Motivation}

The award was conceived of by John Fry in order to honor [Gerald L. Alexanderson](wikipedia://en/43156665),[^o3] Professor at Santa Clara University and founding chair of AIM's board of trustees.

## Prize {#Prize}

Winners of the Alexanderson Award are given a medal, a cash prize and a trip to Bock Cay in the Bahamas.[^o3]
