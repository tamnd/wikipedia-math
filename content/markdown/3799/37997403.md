# Spaltenstein variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spaltenstein variety"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Spaltenstein variety** is a variety given by the [fixed point](wikipedia://en/449738) set of a nilpotent transformation on a [flag variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_variety). They were introduced by (txt). In the special case of full flag varieties the Spaltenstein varieties are [Springer varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_variety).

:::
