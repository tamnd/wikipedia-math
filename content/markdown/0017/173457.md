# 9

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="9"}

**9** (**nine**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following 8 and preceding 10.

:::

## Evolution of the Hindu–Arabic digit {#Evolution_of_the_Hindu–Arabic_digit}

*Image: Evo9glyph.svg.*

Circa 300 BC, as part of the [Brahmi numerals](wikipedia://en/1903587), various Indians wrote a digit 9 similar in shape to the modern closing [question mark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Question_mark) without the bottom dot. The Kshatrapa, Andhra and Gupta started curving the bottom vertical line coming up with a 3-look-alike.[^o4] How the numbers got to their Gupta form is open to considerable debate. The Nagari continued the bottom stroke to make a circle and enclose the 3-look-alike, in much the same way that the sign @ encircles a lowercase *a*. As time went on, the enclosing circle became bigger and its line continued beyond the circle downwards, as the 3-look-alike became smaller. Soon, all that was left of the 3-look-alike was a squiggle. The Arabs simply connected that squiggle to the downward stroke at the middle and subsequent European change was purely cosmetic.

While the shape of the glyph for the digit 9 has an [ascender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ascender_%28typography%29) in most modern [typefaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typeface), in typefaces with [text figures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_figures) the character usually has a [descender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Descender), as, for example, in

The form of could possibly derive from the Arabic letter *[waw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/و)*, which in its isolated form (و) resembles the number 9.

*Image: Seven-segment 9.svg.*

The modern digit resembles an inverted *6*. To disambiguate the two on objects and labels that can be inverted, they are often underlined. It is sometimes handwritten with two strokes and a straight stem, resembling a raised lower-case letter **q**, which distinguishes it from the 6. Similarly, in [seven-segment display](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seven-segment_display), the number 9 can be constructed either with a hook at the end of its stem or without one. Most [LCD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liquid-crystal_display) calculators use the former, but some [VFD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vacuum_fluorescent_display) models use the latter.

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

9 is the fourth [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), the first odd [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289), a [square](wikipedia://en/659939), a [Motzkin number](wikipedia://en/853141), and the number of [derangements](wikipedia://en/162132) of 4 items.

[Casting out nines](wikipedia://en/575641) is a quick way of testing the calculations of sums, differences, products, and [quotients](wikipedia://en/332640) of [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) in [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214), a method known as long ago as the 12th century.[^o6] A decimal integer is divisible by 9 if and only if the sum of its digits is divisible by 9.

9 is the only square number that is the sum of two consecutive, positive cubes: $3^2 = 9 = 1^3 + 2^3$[^o7]

If an [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) [perfect number](wikipedia://en/23670) exists, it will have at least nine distinct [prime factors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_factor).[^o8]

*Image: MotzkinChords4.svg.* 9 is a [Motzkin number](wikipedia://en/853141): it counts the ways of drawing non-intersecting chords among four points on a circle, allowing some points to remain unmatched.

9 is the sum of the [cubes](wikipedia://en/451445) of the first two non-zero positive integers $1^{3} + 2^{3}$ which makes it the first cube-sum number greater than [one](wikipedia://en/77591639). A number that is 4 or 5 [modulo](wikipedia://en/20087) 9 cannot be represented as the [sum of three cubes](wikipedia://en/60187666).[^o10]

*Image: Yang Hui magic circle.svg.* Four concentric [magic circles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_circle_%28mathematics%29) with **9** in the center (by [Yang Hui](wikipedia://en/1716898)), where numbers on each circle and [diameter](wikipedia://en/8007) around the center generate a [magic sum](wikipedia://en/546969) of [138](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/138_%28number%29).

There are nine [Heegner numbers](wikipedia://en/522147), or [square-free positive integers](wikipedia://en/29525) $n$ that yield an imaginary [quadratic field](wikipedia://en/454781) $\mathbb{Q}\left[\sqrt{-n}\right]$ whose [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) has a [unique factorization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unique_factorization), or [class number](wikipedia://en/150907) of 1.[^o11] 9 is the largest single-digit number in the [decimal system](wikipedia://en/8214).

*Image: 9dots.svg.* The "nine dots" puzzle. The puzzle asks to link all nine dots using four straight lines or fewer, without lifting the pen.

The **[nine dots puzzle](wikipedia://en/6231735)** is a [mathematical puzzle](wikipedia://en/366445) whose task is to connect nine squarely arranged points with a pen by four (or fewer) straight lines without lifting the pen or retracing any lines.

### Geometry {#Geometry}

A [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) with nine sides is called an [nonagon](wikipedia://en/414824) or occasionally, an enneagon.[^o13] A regular nonagon can be [constructed](wikipedia://en/523430) with a regular [compass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compass), [straightedge](wikipedia://en/147854), and [angle trisector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angle_trisector).[^o14]

The lowest number of [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) needed for a [perfect tiling](wikipedia://en/70048#Squared,_perfect,_and_other_tiled_rectangles) of a [rectangle](wikipedia://en/70048) is 9.

## Culture and mythology {#Culture_and_mythology}

[Ne Win](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ne_Win), the leader of Myanmar from 1962 to 1988, was a very superstitious man.[^o21] In 1987, he reissued banknotes of Myanmar's currency, the [kyat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myanmar_kyat), in denominations of 45 and 90 kyats on advice from an astrologer who informed him that 9 was his lucky number.[^o22][^o23]

### Indian culture {#Indian_culture}

Nine is a number that appears often in [Indian culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Culture_of_India) and [mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_mythology).[^o25] For example, there are nine [influencers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navagraha) attested to in [Indian astrology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_astrology). In the [Vaisheshika](wikipedia://en/307366) branch of [Hindu philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hindu_philosophy), there are nine universal substances or elements: [Earth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prithvi), [Water](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ap_%28water%29), [Air](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vayu), [Fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agni), [Ether](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akasha), [Time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kāla_%28time%29), [Space](wikipedia://en/307366#The_Categories_or_Padārtha), [Soul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ātman_%28Hinduism%29), and [Mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manas_%28early_Buddhism%29).[^o26] [Navaratri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navaratri) is a nine-day festival dedicated to the [nine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navadurga) forms of [Durga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durga).[^o27][^o28]

### Chinese culture {#Chinese_culture}

- Nine (九; ) is considered a good [number in Chinese culture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numbers_in_Chinese_culture) because it sounds the same as the word "long-lasting" (久; ).[^o30]
- Nine is strongly associated with the [Chinese dragon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_dragon), a symbol of magic and power. There are nine forms of the dragon, it is described in terms of nine attributes, and it has nine children. It has 117 scales – 81 [yang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yin_and_yang) (masculine, heavenly) and 36 [yin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yin_and_yang) (feminine, earthly). All three numbers are multiples of 9.[^o31]

## Anthropology {#Anthropology}

### Idioms {#Idioms}

- To "go the whole nine yards" is to do something fully, despite any difficulty
- To be "dressed to the nines" is to be as well dressed as possible
- To be "on cloud nine" is to be in a state of complete bliss
- Cats are said to have "[nine lives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cat#Superstitions_and_rituals)", describing their ability to avoid or survive dangers
- "A stitch in time saves nine", describing the greater ease of preparation and prevention as compared with repair or recovery
- "K-9" is pronounced nearly identically to *canine* and is used in many US police departments to denote the [police dog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Police_dog) unit. Despite the terms not being [homophonous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homophone) in other languages, many police and military units around the world [borrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistic_borrowing) the same designation from English.

## Religion and philosophy {#Religion_and_philosophy}

*Image: Bahai star.svg.* A nine-pointed star

- Nine, as the largest single-digit number (in [base ten](wikipedia://en/8214)), symbolizes completeness in the [Baháʼí Faith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baháʼí_Faith). In addition, the word Baháʼ in the [Abjad notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abjad_numerals) has a value of 9, and a 9-pointed star is used to [symbolize the religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baháʼí_symbols).
- The number 9 is revered in Hinduism and considered a complete, perfected and divine number because it represents the end of a cycle in the [decimal](wikipedia://en/8214) system, which originated from the Indian subcontinent as early as [3000 BC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/30th_century_BC).
- In [Norse mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norse_mythology), the number nine is associated with [Odin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Odin), as that is how many days he hung from the [world tree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_tree) [Yggdrasil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yggdrasil) before attaining knowledge of the [runes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runes).
- Nine is the number associated with Satan in [LaVeyan Satanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LaVeyan_Satanism). [Anton LaVey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anton_LaVey) wrote in The Satanic Rituals that this is because nine is the number of the [ego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ego_%28Freudian%29) since it "always returns to itself" even after being multiplied by any number.

## Science {#Science}

### Chemistry {#Chemistry}

*Image: Four nines fine gold.jpeg.* [Gold bullion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_bullion) with [four nines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Significant_digits) denoting 99.99% purity'; a [decimal comma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decimal_comma) is used here

[Nines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_%28purity%29) are a notation for expressing the [purity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purity_%28chemistry%29) of a chemical.

In [chemical nomenclature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_nomenclature), the [prefix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefix) "non-" stands for the number nine, for example in molecular chains with nine atoms such as "[nonane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonane)" for an [alkane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alkane) with nine [carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon) atoms. The prefixes play a role in the naming of molecules, especially in [organic chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organic_chemistry) to indicate the number of similar atoms or groups in the molecule (e.g. "nonan" for 9 carbon atoms, "nonavalent" for nine-valent. [Nonapeptides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonapeptide) are peptides that consist of nine [amino acids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amino_acid).

### Psychology {#Psychology}

9 is a common terminal digit in [psychological pricing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_pricing).
