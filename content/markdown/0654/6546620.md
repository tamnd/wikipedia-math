# T. Edward Damer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="T. Edward Damer"}

**T. Edward "Ed" Damer** (August 16, 1937 - March 21, 2024) was an American philosopher, author, father, and grandfather.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Damer was a professor of philosophy and chair of the Division Visual and Performing Arts at [Emory and Henry College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_and_Henry_College) in [Emory, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory,_Virginia).[^o3] He started on the Emory faculty in 1967 and retired in 2012.[^o4] In 1991, he won the James A. David Faculty Recognition Award from the Emory alumni in recognition of his teaching.[^o5]

## Works {#Works}

He is the author of *[Attacking Faulty Reasoning](wikipedia://en/2175085)*, a textbook on logical [fallacies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fallacies). The book defines and explains 60 of the most commonly committed fallacies. It also gives specific suggestions about how to address or to "attack" each fallacy when it is encountered.[^o7] The organization of the fallacies comes from the author's own theories that have accumulated through years of research and study, which defines a fallacy as a violation of one of the five criteria of a good argument.
