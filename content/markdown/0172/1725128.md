# FK-space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FK-space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) a **FK-space** or **Fréchet coordinate space** is a [sequence space](wikipedia://en/1701075) equipped with a [topological structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_structure) such that it becomes a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801).[^o2] FK-spaces with a [normable topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normable_topology) are called [BK-spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BK-spaces).

:::

There only exists one topology to turn a sequence space into a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801), namely the [topology of pointwise convergence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topology_of_pointwise_convergence). Thus the name *coordinate space* because a sequence in an FK-space converges if and only if it converges for each coordinate.

FK-spaces are examples of [topological vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_vector_spaces). They are important in [summability theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summability_theory).

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A **FK-space** is a [sequence space](wikipedia://en/1701075) of $X$, that is a [linear subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) of vector space of all complex valued sequences, equipped with the topology of [pointwise convergence](wikipedia://en/340198).

We write the elements of $X$ as

$$\left(x_n\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$$

with $x_n \in \mathbb{C}$.

Then sequence $\left(a_n\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}^{(k)}$ in $X$ converges to some point $\left(x_n\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$ if it converges pointwise for each $n.$ That is

$$\lim_{k \to \infty} \left(a_n\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}^{(k)} = \left(x_n\right)_{n \in \mathbb{N}}$$

if for all $n \in \mathbb{N},$

$$\lim_{k \to \infty} a_n^{(k)} = x_n$$

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The sequence space $\omega$ of all [complex valued sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_of_complex_valued_sequences) is trivially an FK-space.

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Given an FK-space of $X$ and $\omega$ with the topology of pointwise convergence the [inclusion map](wikipedia://en/492516)

$$\iota : X \to \omega$$

is a [continuous function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_function_%28topology%29).

:::

::: {#o10 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="FK-space constructions"}

## FK-space constructions {#FK-space_constructions}

Given a countable family of FK-spaces $\left(X_n, P_n\right)$ with $P_n$ a countable family of [seminorms](wikipedia://en/292067), we define

$$X := \bigcap_{n=1}^{\infty} X_n$$

and

$$P := \left\{p_{\vert X} : p \in P_n\right\}.$$

Then $(X,P)$ is again an FK-space.

:::
