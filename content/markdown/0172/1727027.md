# Mathematical software

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical software"}

**Mathematical software** is [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software) used to [model](wikipedia://en/20590), analyze or calculate numeric, symbolic or geometric data.[^o2]

:::

## Evolution of mathematical software {#Evolution_of_mathematical_software}

Mathematical knowledge of techniques such as [algorisms](wikipedia://en/417534) which existed before the [invention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invention) of the [electronic computer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer), helped to develop mathematical software. On the other hand, due to the growth of [computing power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing_power) (such as [Moore's law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moore's_law)), the new treatment (for example, a new kind of technique such as [data assimilation](wikipedia://en/2458875) which combined numerical analysis and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685)) needed the progress of the [mathematical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_science) or [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969). The progress of mathematical information presentation such as [TeX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TeX) or [MathML](wikipedia://en/52313)[^o4] will demand to evolution form *formula manipulation language* to true *mathematics manipulation language* (not withstanding the problem that whether [mathematical theory is inconsistent](wikipedia://en/58863) or not). And popularisation of general purpose mathematical software, special purpose mathematical software[^o5] so called *one purpose software* which use special subjects will adapt for environmental progress at the normalisation of the platform, keeping the diversity of mathematical software.

## Numerical analysis {#Numerical_analysis}

The [Netlib](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netlib) repository contains various collections of software routines for numerical problems, mostly in [Fortran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortran) and [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C_%28programming_language%29). Commercial products implementing many different numerical algorithms include the [IMSL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMSL_Numerical_Libraries), [NMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NMath) and [NAG libraries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NAG_Numerical_Libraries); a free alternative is the [GNU Scientific Library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GNU_Scientific_Library). A different approach is taken by the [Numerical Recipes](wikipedia://en/632539) library, where emphasis is placed on clear understanding of algorithms.

Many [computer algebra systems](wikipedia://en/54427) (listed above) can also be used for numerical computations.
