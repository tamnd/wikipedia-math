# Torrence Parsons

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Torrence Parsons"}

**Torrence Douglas Parsons** (March 7, 1941 – April 2, 1987) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his study of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Parsons worked mainly in graph theory, and is known for introducing a graph-theoretic view of [pursuit–evasion](wikipedia://en/3857398) problems.[^o6][^o7] He obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1966 under the supervision of [Albert W. Tucker](wikipedia://en/935841).[^o4] Parsons traveled often for his work, especially to [Former Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialist_Federal_Republic_of_Yugoslavia). In 1981, Parsons traveled to [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana) to be the thesis advisor for [Tomaž Pisanski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomaž_Pisanski)'s thesis defense. In 1985, he gave a lecture in [Dubrovnik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubrovnik).[^o8]

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

Parsons died on April 2, 1987, at the age of 46 in Chico, California. He had been set to host a lecture at the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582) in May 1987. The cause of death was [ventricular fibrillation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ventricular_fibrillation), following Parsons running a marathon. Parsons regularly ran marathons. Following Parsons' death, the [University of Chico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_Chico) established a memorial fund in his name.[^o3]

Parsons was married and had two children, a pair of twins named Craig and Russell.[^o3]
