# Zassenhaus group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zassenhaus group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Zassenhaus group**, named after [Hans Zassenhaus](wikipedia://en/1376825), is a certain sort of [doubly transitive permutation group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doubly_transitive_permutation_group) very closely related to rank-1 [groups of Lie type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groups_of_Lie_type).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A **Zassenhaus group** is a permutation group *G* on a finite set *X* with the following three properties:

- *G* is doubly transitive.
- Non-trivial elements of *G* fix at most two points.
- *G* has no regular [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918). ("Regular" means that non-trivial elements do not fix any points of *X*; compare [free action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_action).)

The **degree** of a Zassenhaus group is the number of elements of *X*.

Some authors omit the third condition that *G* has no regular normal subgroup. This condition is put in to eliminate some "degenerate" cases. The extra examples one gets by omitting it are either [Frobenius groups](wikipedia://en/1716123) or certain groups of degree 2<sup>*p*</sup> and order 2<sup>*p*</sup>(2<sup>*p*</sup> − 1)*p* for a prime *p*, that are generated by all [semilinear mappings](wikipedia://en/24918060) and Galois automorphisms of a field of order 2<sup>*p*</sup>.

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

We let *q* = *p<sup>f</sup>* be a power of a prime *p*, and write *F<sub>q</sub>* for the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) of order *q*. [Michio Suzuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michio_Suzuki_%28mathematician%29) proved that any Zassenhaus group is of one of the following four types:

- The [projective special linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_linear_group) PSL<sub>2</sub>(*F*<sub>*q*</sub>) for *q* > 3 odd, acting on the *q* + 1 points of the projective line. It has order (*q* + 1)*q*(*q* − 1)/2.
- The [projective general linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_general_linear_group) PGL<sub>2</sub>(*F*<sub>*q*</sub>) for *q* > 3. It has order (*q* + 1)*q*(*q* − 1).
- A certain group containing PSL<sub>2</sub>(*F*<sub>*q*</sub>) with [index](wikipedia://en/230581) 2, for *q* an odd square. It has order (*q* + 1)*q*(*q* − 1).
- The [Suzuki group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suzuki–Ree_group) Suz(*F*<sub>*q*</sub>) for *q* a power of 2 that is at least 8 and not a square. The order is (*q*<sup>2</sup> + 1)*q*<sup>2</sup>(*q* − 1)

The degree of these groups is *q* + 1 in the first three cases, *q*<sup>2</sup> + 1 in the last case.

:::
