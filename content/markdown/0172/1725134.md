# BK-space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="BK-space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **BK-space** or **Banach coordinate space** is a [sequence space](wikipedia://en/1701075) endowed with a suitable [norm](wikipedia://en/990534) to turn it into a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989). All BK-spaces are normable [FK-spaces](wikipedia://en/1725128).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The [space of convergent sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_of_convergent_sequences) $c,$ the [space of vanishing sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_of_vanishing_sequences) $c_0,$ and the [space of bounded sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_of_bounded_sequences) $\ell^\infty$ under the [supremum norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supremum_norm) $\|\cdot\|_{\infty}.$[^o2]

The space of [absolutely p-summable sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolutely_p-summable_sequences) $\ell^p$ with $p \geq 1$ and the norm $\|\cdot\|_p.$[^o2]

:::
