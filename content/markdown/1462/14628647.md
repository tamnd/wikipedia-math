# Günter M. Ziegler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Günter M. Ziegler"}

**Günter Matthias Ziegler** (born 19 May 1963) is a German mathematician who has been working as president of the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin) since 2018.[^o3] Ziegler is known for his research in [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), and particularly on the [combinatorics of polytopes](wikipedia://en/19696519).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ziegler studied at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) from 1981 to 1984, and went on to receive his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in [Cambridge, Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge,_Massachusetts), in 1987, under the supervision of [Anders Björner](wikipedia://en/24753353). After postdoctoral positions at the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg) and the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452), he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1992 from [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), which he joined as a professor in 1995.[^o6][^o8] Ziegler has since joined the faculty of the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Ziegler was awarded the one million [Deutschmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutschmark) Gerhard Hess Prize by the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) (DFG) in 1994 and the 1.5 million Deutschmark [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize), Germany's highest research honor, by the DFG in 2001.[^o6][^o7] He was awarded the 2005 [Gauss Lectureship](wikipedia://en/48440954) by the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973). In 2006, the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) awarded Ziegler and Florian Pfender its highest honor for mathematical exposition, the [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize), for their paper on [kissing numbers](wikipedia://en/408555).[^o8][^o9]

In 2006 Ziegler became president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) for a two-year term.[^o8] In 2009, the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) (ERC) awarded Ziegler one of the ERC Advanced Grants in the amount of 1.85 million [Euros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro).[^o10] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11] In 2013, Ziegler was granted the [Hector Science Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hector_Science_Award)[^o12] and became a member of the [Hector Fellow Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hector_Fellow_Academy).[^o13] Since 2016, Ziegler has been chair of the [Berlin Mathematical School](wikipedia://en/14614258). In 2018, he received the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition (jointly with [Martin Aigner](wikipedia://en/24448177)) for *Proofs from THE BOOK*.[^o14]
