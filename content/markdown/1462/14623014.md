# Weierstrass ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weierstrass ring"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Weierstrass ring**, named by Nagata[^o2] after [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459), is a [commutative](wikipedia://en/61346) [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) that is [Henselian](wikipedia://en/14623383), [pseudo-geometric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudo-geometric), and such that any [quotient ring](wikipedia://en/184635) by a [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) is a [finite extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_extension) of a [regular local ring](wikipedia://en/506959).

:::

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- The [Weierstrass preparation theorem](wikipedia://en/372198) can be used to show that the ring of convergent [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) over the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) in a finite number of variables is a Weierstrass ring. The same is true if the complex numbers are replaced by a [perfect field](wikipedia://en/505088) with a [valuation](wikipedia://en/347049).
- Every ring that is a [finitely-generated module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely-generated_module) over a Weierstrass ring is also a Weierstrass ring.

:::
