# Peter Ozsváth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Ozsváth"}

**Peter Steven Ozsváth** (born October 20, 1967) is a professor of mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). He created, along with [Zoltán Szabó](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoltán_Szabó_%28mathematician%29), [Heegaard Floer homology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heegaard_Floer_homology), a [homology theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_theory) for [3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1018257).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ozsváth received his PhD from Princeton in 1994 under the supervision of [John Morgan](wikipedia://en/8487966); his dissertation was entitled *On Blowup Formulas For SU(2) [Donaldson Polynomials](wikipedia://en/7684340)*.

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2007, Ozsváth was one of the recipients of the [Oswald Veblen Prize in Geometry](wikipedia://en/7085075).[^o5] In 2008 he was named a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow).[^o6] In July 2017, he was a plenary lecturer in the Mathematical Congress of the Americas.[^o7] He was elected a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2018.[^o8] He was elected to the 2026 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
