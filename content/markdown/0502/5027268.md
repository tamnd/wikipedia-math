# Macdonald identities

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Macdonald identities"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Macdonald identities** are some infinite product identities associated to [affine root systems](wikipedia://en/32702474), introduced by (txt). They include as special cases the [Jacobi triple product identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_triple_product_identity), Watson's [quintuple product identity](wikipedia://en/32747510), several identities found by (Dyson 1972), and a 10-fold product identity found by (Winquist 1969).

:::

(Kac 1974) and (Moody 1975) pointed out that the Macdonald identities are the analogs of the [Weyl denominator formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_denominator_formula) for [affine Kac–Moody algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Affine_Kac–Moody_algebra) and superalgebras.
