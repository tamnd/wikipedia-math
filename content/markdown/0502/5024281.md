# 65537-gon

Regular polygon stat table

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="65537-gon"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **65537-gon** is a [polygon](wikipedia://en/23621) with [65,537](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/65,537) (i.e., 2<sup>16</sup> + 1) sides. The sum of the interior angles of any non-[self-intersecting](wikipedia://en/50055439) 65537-gon is 11,796,300°.

:::

## Regular 65537-gon {#Regular_65537-gon}

The area of a *regular 65537-gon* is (with *t* = edge length)

$$A = \frac{65537}{4} t^2 \cot \frac{\pi}{65537}$$

The [perimeter](wikipedia://en/23636) of a [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) 65537-gon differs from that of the [circumscribed circle](wikipedia://en/161262) by about 15 [parts per billion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parts_per_billion).

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The regular 65537-gon (one with all sides equal and all angles equal) is of interest for being a [constructible polygon](wikipedia://en/523430): that is, it can be constructed using a compass and an unmarked straightedge. This is because 65,537 is a [Fermat prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermat_prime), being of the form 2<sup>2<sup>*n*</sup></sup> + 1 (in this case *n* = 4). Thus, the values $\cos \frac{\pi}{65537}$ and $\cos \frac{2\pi}{65537}$ are 32768-[degree](wikipedia://en/5930652) [algebraic numbers](wikipedia://en/1158), and like any [constructible numbers](wikipedia://en/7439), they can be written in terms of [square roots](wikipedia://en/29208) and no higher-order roots.

Although it was known to [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) by 1801 that the regular 65537-gon was constructible, the first explicit construction of a regular 65537-gon was given by [Johann Gustav Hermes](wikipedia://en/10448726) (1894). The construction is very complex; Hermes spent 10 years completing the 200-page manuscript.[^o5] Another method involves the use of at most 1332 [Carlyle circles](wikipedia://en/39453637), and the first stages of this method are pictured below. This method faces practical problems, as one of these Carlyle circles solves the [quadratic equation](wikipedia://en/25175) *x*<sup>2</sup> + *x* − 16384 = 0 (16384 being 2<sup>14</sup>).[^o6]

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The *regular 65537-gon* has [D<sub>65537</sub> symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_symmetry), order 131074. Since 65537 is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666), D<sub>65537</sub> has 2 proper [cyclic subgroups](wikipedia://en/52327): Z<sub>65537</sub>, and Z<sub>2</sub>, which correspond to the subgroup generated by a [rotation](wikipedia://en/39789) and a [reflection](wikipedia://en/295829) respectively.[^o9]

:::

## 65537-gram {#65537-gram}

A 65537-gram is a 65,537-sided [star polygon](wikipedia://en/313303). As 65,537 is prime, there are 32,767 regular forms generated by [Schläfli symbols](wikipedia://en/489394) $\{{65537}/{n}\}$ for all [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) 2 ≤ *n* ≤ 32768 as $\left\lfloor \frac{65537}{2} \right\rfloor = 32768$.
