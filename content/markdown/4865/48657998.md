# Littlewood's 4/3 inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Littlewood's 4/3 inequality"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), **Littlewood's 4/3 inequality**, named after [John Edensor Littlewood](wikipedia://en/158371),[^o2] is an inequality that holds for every complex-valued [bilinear form](wikipedia://en/449477) defined on [$c_0$](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C0_space), the [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) of scalar sequences that converge to zero.

:::

Precisely, let $B:c_0\times c_0 \to \mathbb{C}$ or $\mathbb{R}$ be a bilinear form. Then the following holds:

$$\left( \sum_{i,j=1}^\infty |B(e_i,e_j)|^{4/3} \right)^{3/4} \le \sqrt{2} \| B \|,$$

where

$$\| B \| = \sup \{|B(x_1,x_2)|: \|x_i\|_\infty \le 1 \}.$$

The exponent 4/3 is optimal, i.e., cannot be improved by a smaller exponent.[^o3] It is also known that for real scalars the aforementioned constant is sharp.[^o4]

### Bohnenblust–Hille inequality {#Bohnenblust–Hille_inequality}

Bohnenblust–Hille inequality[^o9] is a [multilinear](wikipedia://en/832212) extension of Littlewood's inequality that states that for all $m$-linear mapping $M : c_0\times \cdots \times c_0 \to \mathbb{C}$ the following holds:

$$\left( \sum_{i_1,\ldots,i_m=1}^\infty |M(e_{i_1},\ldots,e_{i_m})|^{2m/(m+1)} \right)^{(m+1)/(2m)} \le 2^{(m-1)/2} \| M \|,$$

:::
