In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **quarter order-6 square tiling** is a uniform tiling of the [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/241291). It has [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) of q{4,6}. It is constructed from \*3232 [orbifold notation](wikipedia://en/2915678), and can be seen as a half symmetry of \*443 and \*662, and quarter symmetry of \*642.
