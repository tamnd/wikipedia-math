# Regular scheme

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regular scheme"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **regular scheme** is a [locally Noetherian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_Noetherian_scheme) [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) whose [local rings](wikipedia://en/153391) are [regular](wikipedia://en/506959) everywhere.[^o2][^o3] Every [smooth scheme](wikipedia://en/35033819) is regular, and every regular [scheme of finite type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scheme_of_finite_type) over a [perfect field](wikipedia://en/505088) is smooth.[^o4]

:::

For an example of a regular scheme that is not smooth, see Geometrically regular ring#Examples.
