# Goldberg–Seymour conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Goldberg–Seymour conjecture"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Goldberg–Seymour conjecture** states that, for a [multigraph](wikipedia://en/1774970) $G$[^o2][^o3]

:::

$$\operatorname{\chi'}(G) \leq \max(1 + \operatorname{\Delta}(G),\, \operatorname{\Gamma}(G))$$

where $\operatorname{\chi'}(G)$ is the [edge chromatic number](wikipedia://en/690647) of *G*, $\operatorname{\Delta}(G)$ is its maximum [degree](wikipedia://en/1374448), and

$$\operatorname{\Gamma} G = \max_{H \subset G} \frac{|E(H)|}{\lfloor\frac{1}{2} |V(H)| \rfloor}.$$

This above quantity is twice the [arboricity](wikipedia://en/2476311) of *G*. It is sometimes called the **density** of *G*.[^o3]

Here, *G* can be a [multigraph](wikipedia://en/1774970) and can have [loops](wikipedia://en/1449083). For simple graphs, this result follows from [Vizing's theorem](wikipedia://en/5449464).

## Background {#Background}

It is already known that for [loopless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simple_graph) *G* (but can have parallel edges):[^o3][^o7]

$$\operatorname{\chi'} G \geq \max(\operatorname{\Delta} G, \lceil \operatorname{\Gamma} G \rceil).$$

When does equality not hold? It does not hold for the [Petersen graph](wikipedia://en/194926). It is hard to find other examples. It is currently unknown whether there are any [planar graphs](wikipedia://en/24314) for which equality does not hold.

This [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) is named after Mark K. Goldberg of [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute)[^o5] and [Paul Seymour](wikipedia://en/1575708) of [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), who arrived to it independently of Goldberg.[^o7]

## Announced proof {#Announced_proof}

In 2019, an alleged [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) was announced by Chen, Jing, and Zang in the paper.[^o7] Part of their proof was to find a suitable generalization of [Vizing's theorem](wikipedia://en/5449464) (which says that for simple graphs $\operatorname{\chi'} G \leq 1 + \operatorname{\Delta} G$) to multigraphs. In 2023, Jing[^o8] announced a new proof with a [polynomial-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial-time) edge coloring algorithm achieving the conjectured [bound](wikipedia://en/42693).
