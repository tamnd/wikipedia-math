# Ray Turner (computer scientist)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ray Turner"}

Professor **Raymond Turner** (born 28 April 1947) is an English logician, philosopher, and theoretical [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) based at the [University of Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Essex). He is best known for his work on [logic in computer science](wikipedia://en/1151991) and for his pioneering work in the [philosophy of computer science](wikipedia://en/22458313). He is on the editorial boards for the [Journal of Logic and Computation](wikipedia://en/27311945)[^o3] and the [Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Encyclopedia_of_Philosophy), for Logic, Computation, and Agency.[^o4]

:::
