# Stochastic grammar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stochastic grammar"}

Grammar A **stochastic grammar** (**statistical grammar**) is a [grammar framework](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammar_framework) with a [probabilistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic) notion of [grammaticality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grammaticality):

:::

- [Stochastic context-free grammar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_context-free_grammar)
- Statistical parsing
- [Data-oriented parsing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data-oriented_parsing)
- [Hidden Markov model](wikipedia://en/98770) (or stochastic [regular grammar](wikipedia://en/25855)[^o2])
- [Estimation theory](wikipedia://en/1565926)

The grammar is realized as a [language model](wikipedia://en/1911810). Allowed sentences are stored in a database together with the frequency how common a sentence is.[^o3] Statistical [natural language processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_language_processing) uses [stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222), [probabilistic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic) and [statistical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical) methods, especially to resolve difficulties that arise because longer sentences are highly [ambiguous](wikipedia://en/154040) when processed with realistic grammars, yielding thousands or millions of possible analyses. Methods for disambiguation often involve the use of [corpora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpus_linguistics) and [Markov models](wikipedia://en/22022581). "A probabilistic model consists of a non-probabilistic model plus some numerical quantities; it is not true that probabilistic models are inherently simpler or less structural than non-probabilistic models."[^o4]

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

A probabilistic method for rhyme detection is implemented by Hirjee & Brown[^o7] in their study in 2013 to find internal and imperfect rhyme pairs in rap lyrics. The concept is adapted from a [sequence alignment](wikipedia://en/149289) technique using [BLOSUM](wikipedia://en/5558617) (**BLO**cks **SU**bstitution **M**atrix). They were able to detect rhymes undetectable by non-probabilistic models.
