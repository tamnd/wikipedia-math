# Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra"}

The ***Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [platinum open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platinum_open_access) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [matrix analysis](wikipedia://en/40956141) and [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422), together with their applications. It is published by the [International Linear Algebra Society](wikipedia://en/63337149) and its [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Vanni Noferini ([Aalto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalto_University)).

:::

## Editors-in-chief {#Editors-in-chief}

The first editors-in-chief were [Volker Mehrmann](wikipedia://en/61754880) ([Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin); 1996–1999) and [Daniel Hershkowitz](wikipedia://en/20713156) ([Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University); 1996–2010). Other former editors-in-chief are Ludwig Elsner ([Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University); 2010–2011), [Bryan Shader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryan_Shader) ([University of Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wyoming); 2010–2019), Michael Tsatsomeros ([Washington State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_State_University); 2016–2022), and Froilán M. Dopico ([Universidad Carlos III de Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Carlos_III_de_Madrid); 2019-2025). The current editor-in-chief is Vanni Noferini ([Aalto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aalto_University); since 2025).[^o4]

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents)/Physical, Chemical & Earth Sciences[^o6]
- [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)[^o7]
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)[^o6]
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus)[^o8]
- [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)[^o7]

According to the [Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports), the journal has a 2024 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.8.[^o9]
