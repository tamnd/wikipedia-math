# Micha Perles

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Micha Perles"}

**Micha Asher Perles** (מיכה פרלס) is an Israeli mathematician working in geometry, a professor emeritus at the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University).[^o3] He earned his Ph.D. in 1964 from the Hebrew University, under the supervision of [Branko Grünbaum](wikipedia://en/1731976). His contributions include:

:::

- The [Perles configuration](wikipedia://en/35289232), a set of nine points in the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410) whose collinearities can be realized only by using irrational numbers as coordinates. Perles used this configuration to prove the existence of irrational [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) in higher dimensions.[^o5]
- The [Perles–Sauer–Shelah lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perles–Sauer–Shelah_lemma), a result in [extremal set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extremal_set_theory) whose proof was credited to Perles by [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211).[^o6][^o7]
- The [pumping lemma for context-free languages](wikipedia://en/3193758), a widely used method for proving that a language is not [context-free](wikipedia://en/6867) that Perles discovered with [Yehoshua Bar-Hillel](wikipedia://en/739314) and [Eli Shamir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Shamir).[^o8]

Notable students of Perles include [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651), [Gil Kalai](wikipedia://en/19709056), and [Nati Linial](wikipedia://en/28755276).[^o4]
