# Cayley's sextic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cayley's sextic"}

*Image: Cayley sextic 001.svg.*

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **Cayley's sextic** (**sextic of Cayley**, **Cayley's sextet**) is a [plane curve](wikipedia://en/971691), a member of the [sinusoidal spiral](wikipedia://en/18633469) family, first discussed by [Colin Maclaurin](wikipedia://en/7824) in 1718. [Arthur Cayley](wikipedia://en/311034) was the first to study the curve in detail and [Raymond Clare Archibald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Clare_Archibald) named the curve after him.

:::

The curve is symmetric about the *x*-axis (*y* = 0) and self-intersects at *y* = 0, *x* = −*a*/8. Other intercepts are at the origin, at (*a*, 0) and [with the *y*-axis](wikipedia://en/464331) at ±$\tfrac{3}{8}$$\sqrt{3}$*a*

The curve is the [pedal curve](wikipedia://en/845864) (or *roulette*) of a [cardioid](wikipedia://en/642101) with respect to its cusp.[^o2]

## Equations of the curve {#Equations_of_the_curve}

The equation of the curve in [polar coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_coordinates) is[^o2][^o4]

*r* = *4a* cos<sup>3</sup>(*θ*/3)

In [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) the equation is[^o2][^o5]

4(*x*<sup>2</sup> + *y*<sup>2</sup> − (*a*/4)*x*)<sup>3</sup> = 27(*a*/4)<sup>2</sup>(*x*<sup>2</sup> + *y*<sup>2</sup>)<sup>2</sup> .

Cayley's sextic may be [parametrised](wikipedia://en/576108) (as a [periodic function](wikipedia://en/76848), period $\pi$, $\mathbb{R} \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^2$) by the equations:

- *x* = cos<sup>3</sup>*t* cos 3*t*
- *y* = cos<sup>3</sup>*t* sin 3*t*

The node is at *t* = ±$\pi$/3.[^o6]
