# Martin Gardner bibliography

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Gardner bibliography"}

In a publishing career spanning 80 years (1930–2010),[^o2] [popular mathematics](wikipedia://en/2069923) and [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_science) writer [Martin Gardner](wikipedia://en/20435) (1914–2010) authored or edited over 100 books and countless articles, columns and reviews.

:::

All Gardner's works were non-fiction except for two novels – *The Flight of Peter Fromm* (1973) and *[Visitors from Oz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visitors_from_Oz)* (1998) – and two collections of short pieces – *[The Magic Numbers of Dr. Matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Joshua_Matrix)* (1967, 1985) and *The No-Sided Professor* (1987).
