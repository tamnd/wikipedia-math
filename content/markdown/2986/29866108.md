# B-theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="B-theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **B-theorem** is a result in [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory) formerly known as the **B-conjecture**.

:::

The [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) states that if $C$ is the [centralizer](wikipedia://en/145661) of an [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707), then every component of $C/O(C)$ is the image of a component of $C$.[^o2]
