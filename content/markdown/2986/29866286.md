# Balance theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Balance theorem"}

In **mathematical** group theory, the **balance theorem** states that if *G* is a group with no [core](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Core_%28group%29), then *G* either has disconnected [Sylow 2-subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup), or it is of [characteristic 2 type](wikipedia://en/29668519), or it is of component type (Gorenstein 1983, 7).

:::

The significance of this theorem is that it splits the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445) into three major subcases.
