# James Haglund

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Haglund"}

**James Haglund** is an American mathematician who specializes in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) and [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533),[^o2] and works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Haglund received his Ph.D. in 1993 from the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia), with the dissertation *Compositions, Rook Placements, and Permutations of Vectors* supervised by Earl Rodney Canfield.

## Research contributions {#Research_contributions}

In 2005, together with [M. Haiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Haiman) and N. Loehr gave the first proof of a combinatorial interpretation of the Macdonald polynomials. In 2007, Haglund, Haiman and Loehr gave a combinatorial formula for the non-symmetric [Macdonald polynomials](wikipedia://en/10354285).

Haglund is the author of *The $q,t$-Catalan Numbers and the Space of Diagonal Harmonics: With an Appendix on the Combinatorics of Macdonald Polynomials*.[^o7]

## Academic talks {#Academic_talks}

In 2024, Haglund gave a talk at [KAIST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KAIST) on Superization of Symmetric Functions.[^o9]

In 2015, together with [Alexandre Kirillov](wikipedia://en/3772515) and [Ching-Li Chai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ching-Li_Chai), Haglund gave a talk at [Penn Wharton China Center](https://pwcc.upenn.edu/) on Penn Math Day, sponsored by the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) and [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University).[^o10]

In 2006, he gave a Plenary Address at the 18th [International Conference on Formal Power Series and Algebraic Combinatorics](wikipedia://en/75145047) ([FPSAC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FPSAC) '06), San Diego (USA).[^o11]

## Editorial {#Editorial}

Haglund was on the editorial boards of [Transactions of the AMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transactions_of_the_AMS), Journal of Combinatorics, and a few other academic journals.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2013, Haglund became an inaugural Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o16]
