# Armenian Mathematical Union

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Armenian Mathematical Union"}

The **Armenian Mathematical Union** or **AMU** (Armenian: Հայկական Մաթեմատիկական Միություն, **ՀՄՄ**) is a [mathematical society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_society) founded in [Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenia) in 1991. The AMU is based in [Yerevan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan), and has been a member of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) since 2016. It is recognised by the [International Mathematics Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematics_Union) as the official mathematical society of Armenia.

:::

## History {#History}

The first association of Armenian mathematicians was formed in 1920, during the [First Armenian Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Armenian_Republic); its president was Ervand Kogbetlianz, a professor at [Yerevan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yerevan_State_University). The modern Armenian Mathematical Union was founded in 1991, the same year that Armenia gained independence due to the [dissolution of the USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissolution_of_the_USSR). In 2020 the AMU dedicated its annual session to the 100th anniversary of the first association.

## Activities and awards {#Activities_and_awards}

The AMU has organised several international conferences. In May–June 2018 it hosted a conference to celebrate the 120th anniversary of [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351)'s birth.

Since 2001 the AMU has annually awarded the [Emil Artin Junior Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/28842092) to a mathematician under the age of thirty-five who studied in Armenia, for "outstanding contributions in algebra, geometry, topology, and number theory." The prize is named after Emil Artin. The winner is announced in the *[Notices of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4977512)*, and as of 2024 receives a cash prize of US\$1400.

## Presidents {#Presidents}

The Armenian Mathematical Union has had 7 presidents since its founding.

- Alexander Talalyan (1991–1994)
- Norayr Arakelian (1994–1997)
- [Ruben Ambartzumian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rouben_V._Ambartzumian) (1997–2001)
- Norayr Yengibaryan (2001–2004)
- Hrachik Hayrapetyan (2004–2011)
- Tigran Harutunyan (2011–2017)
- Yuri Movsisyan (2017–present)
