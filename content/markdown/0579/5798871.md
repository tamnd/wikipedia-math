# 1989 Transnistrian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1989 Transnistrian census"}

The **1989 Transnistrian census** was organized by the authorities of the [MSSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldavian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) in the final days of its existence as a [Soviet republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Republics_of_the_Soviet_Union). It took place as part of the [Soviet Census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Census) of 1989.

:::

Results from the 1989 census showed that among the population of Transnistria, approximately 40% were ethnic [Moldovans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldovans), 28% [Ukrainians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians) and 25% [Russians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians).

## Census results {#Census_results}

- Total population on the left bank of the Dniester river (minus Tighina): 546,400
- Total population in [raions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raion) mostly on the left bank of the Dniester river (minus Tighina): 601,660[^o4]
- (note that percentages below are given from the second figure)
- [Moldovans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldovans): 39.9%
- [Ukrainians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians): 28.3%
- [Russians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians): 25.5%
- Others: 6.4%
