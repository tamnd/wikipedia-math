# Grosshans subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grosshans subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [algebraic](wikipedia://en/247924) [groups](wikipedia://en/19447), a **Grosshans subgroup**, named after [Frank Grosshans](wikipedia://en/22184091), is an algebraic [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of an algebraic group that is an [observable subgroup](wikipedia://en/5784200) for which the [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) of functions on the quotient [variety](wikipedia://en/248808) is [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/11985661).[^o2]

:::
