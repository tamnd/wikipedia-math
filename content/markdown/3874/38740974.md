# Risk inclination formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Risk inclination formula"}

The **risk inclination formula** uses the principle of moments, or [Varignon's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varignon's_theorem_%28mechanics%29),[^o2][^o3] to calculate the first [factorial moment](wikipedia://en/790823) of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) in order to define this center point of balance among all [confidence weights](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confidence_weighting) (i.e., the point of risk equilibration).

:::

The formal derivation of the RIF is divided into three separate calculations: (1) calculation of 1st factorial moment, (2) calculation of inclination, and (3) calculation of the risk inclination score.

The RIF [^o4] is a component of the [risk inclination model](wikipedia://en/19269621).
