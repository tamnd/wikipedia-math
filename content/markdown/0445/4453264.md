# Kenneth Davidson (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kenneth Davidson"}

*Image: Kenneth Davidson.jpg.* Kenneth Davidson

**Kenneth Ralph Davidson** (born 1951 in [Edmonton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmonton), [Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta))[^o2] is [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Pure Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pure_Mathematics) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo). He did his undergraduate work at Waterloo and received his Ph.D. under the supervision of [William Arveson](wikipedia://en/659727) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1976. Davidson was Director of the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) from 2001 to 2004. His areas of research include [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and [C\*-algebras](wikipedia://en/7184). Since 2007 he has been appointed University Professor at the University of Waterloo.[^o3]

:::

He is a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada). He was appointed Fellow of the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768) in 2006. In 2018 the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) listed him in their inaugural class of fellows.[^o4]

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Real Analysis and Applications*, with Allan Donsig, [Undergraduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/8871375), Springer, 2009.
- *$C^\ast$-Algebras by Example*, Fields Institute Monograph 6, AMS, 1996.
- *Nest Algebras*, Pitman Research Notes in Math. 191, Longman, 1988.
