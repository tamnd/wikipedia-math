# De Furtivis Literarum Notis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="De Furtivis Literarum Notis"}

***De Furtivis Literarum Notis*** (*On the Secret Symbols of Letters*) is a 1563 book on [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) written by [Giambattista della Porta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giambattista_della_Porta).[^o2]

:::

The book includes three sets of cypher discs for coding and decoding messages,[^o3][^o4] a [substitution cipher](wikipedia://en/48356) improving on the work of [Al-Qalqashandi](wikipedia://en/5647376),[^o5] and one of the earliest known [music substitution ciphers](wikipedia://en/76087628#porta).
