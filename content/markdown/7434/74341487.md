# Nadel vanishing theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nadel vanishing theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Nadel vanishing theorem** is a global [vanishing theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanishing_theorem) for [multiplier ideals](wikipedia://en/28300822), introduced by A. M. Nadel in 1989.[^o2] It generalizes the [Kodaira vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/2988093) using singular metrics with (strictly) positive curvature, and also it can be seen as an analytical analogue of the [Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/22999259).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The theorem can be stated as follows.[^o5][^o6][^o7] Let X be a smooth [complex projective variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_projective_variety), D an effective $\mathbb{Q}$-[divisor](wikipedia://en/1693055#Q-divisors) and L a [line bundle](wikipedia://en/361366) on X, and $\mathcal{J}(D)$ is a multiplier ideal sheaves. Assume that $L - D$ is [big](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_line_bundle) and [nef](wikipedia://en/3404309). Then

$$H^{i} \left(X, \mathcal{O}_{X}(K_X + L) \otimes \mathcal{J}(D) \right) = 0 \;\; \text{for} \;\; i > 0.$$

Nadel vanishing theorem in the analytic setting:[^o8][^o9] Let $(X, \omega)$ be a [Kähler manifold](wikipedia://en/390538) (X be a reduced complex space ([complex analytic variety](wikipedia://en/18748850)) with a Kähler metric) such that weakly [pseudoconvex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoconvex), and let F be a [holomorphic line bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_line_bundle) over X equipped with a singular [hermitian metric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermitian_metric) of weight $\varphi$. Assume that $\sqrt{-1} \cdot \theta(F) > \varepsilon \cdot \omega$ for some continuous positive function $\varepsilon$ on X. Then

$$H^{i} \left(X, \mathcal{O}_{X}(K_X + F) \otimes \mathcal{J}(\varphi) \right) = 0 \;\; \text{for} \;\; i > 0.$$

Let arbitrary [plurisubharmonic function](wikipedia://en/2519085) $\phi$ on $\Omega \subset X$, then a multiplier ideal sheaf $\mathcal{J}(\phi)$ is a coherent on $\Omega$, and therefore its zero variety is an analytic set.

:::
