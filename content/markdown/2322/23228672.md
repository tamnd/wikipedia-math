# Sigurður Helgason (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sigurður Helgason"}

**Sigurdur Helgason** (Sigurður Helgason; 30 September 1927 – 3 December 2023) was an Icelandic [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research has been devoted to the geometry and analysis on [symmetric spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_spaces). In particular, he used new integral geometric methods to establish fundamental existence theorems for differential equations on symmetric spaces as well as some new results on the representations of their isometry groups. He also introduced a [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247) on these spaces and proved the principal theorems for this transform, the inversion formula, the [Plancherel theorem](wikipedia://en/454315) and the analog of the [Paley–Wiener theorem](wikipedia://en/632992).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Sigurdur Helgason was born in [Akureyri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akureyri), Iceland on 30 September 1927. In 1954, he earned a PhD from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under [Salomon Bochner](wikipedia://en/987847). Helgason became a professor of mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1965, and he retired from the faculty in 2014.

Helgason received the [Børge Jessen Diploma Award](wikipedia://en/20754801) of the [Danish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/22351308) in 1982, and the Grand Knight's Cross (Stórriddarakross) of the Icelandic [Order of the Falcon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Falcon) in 1991. He was winner of the 1988 [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Seminal Contributions for his books *Groups and Geometric Analysis* and *Differential Geometry, Lie Groups and Symmetric Spaces*. This was followed by the 2008 book *Geometric Analysis on Symmetric Spaces*. On 31 May 1996, Helgason received an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) from the Faculty of Science and Technology at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), Sweden.[^o4]

Helgason was elected a member of the Icelandic Academy of Sciences in 1960, the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 1970, and the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters) in 1972. In 2013, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). He was made an honorary member of the [Icelandic Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/74367512) when he turned 70, and a symposium was held in his honor.

Helgason died on 3 December 2023, at the age of 96.[^o5]
