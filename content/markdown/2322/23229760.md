# Mikhail Shubin (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mikhail Shubin"}

**Mikhail Aleksandrovich Shubin** (19 December 1944 – 13 May 2020) was an American and Soviet mathematician and professor at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University), as well as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). He died in May 2020 at the age of 75.[^o3]

:::

## Work {#Work}

Shubin has written over 140 papers and books, and supervised almost twenty doctoral theses.

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
