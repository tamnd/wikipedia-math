# Carl Theodore Heisel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carl Theodore Heisel"}

**Carl Theodore Heisel** (1852–1937)[^o2] was a mathematical crank who wrote several books in the 1930s challenging accepted mathematical truths. Among his claims is that he found a way to [square the circle](wikipedia://en/201359). He is credited with 24 works in 62 publications.[^o3] Heisel did not charge money for his books; he gave thousands of them away for free. Because of this, they are available at many libraries and universities. Heisel's books have historic and monetary value.[^o4] [Paul Halmos](wikipedia://en/318598) referred to one of Heisel's works (a book claiming to have accomplished the impossible task of [squaring the circle](wikipedia://en/201359)) as a "classic crank book."[^o5]

:::
