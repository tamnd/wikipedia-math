# Coralia Cartis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coralia Cartis"}

**Coralia Cartis** is a Romanian mathematician at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) whose research interests include [compressed sensing](wikipedia://en/11403316), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), and [regularisation](wikipedia://en/2009061) methods in [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033). At Oxford, she is a Professor in Numerical Optimization in the [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850), and a tutorial fellow of [Balliol College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Balliol_College,_Oxford).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born in [Cluj-Napoca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca), [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania), Cartis earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Babeș-Bolyai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Babeș-Bolyai_University), and completed her PhD in 2005 at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). Her dissertation, *On Interior Point Methods for Linear Programming*, was supervised by [Michael J. D. Powell](wikipedia://en/16082618). In the same year, she was one of the Second Prize winners of the [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214).

After working as a researcher at the [Rutherford Appleton Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutherford_Appleton_Laboratory) and a postdoctoral researcher at Oxford, she became a lecturer at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 2007. She took her present position at Oxford in 2013.

In 2018 she became a member of the scientific board of the Smith Institute for Industrial Mathematics and System Engineering, and was a plenary speaker at the 16th EUROPT Workshop on Advances in Continuous Optimization in Spain.

Cartis was elected to the 2023 Class of [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Fellows.[^o4]
