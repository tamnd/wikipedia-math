# Göran Dillner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Göran Dillner"}

*Image: Göran Dillner SPA3 (cropped).jpg.* Göran Dillner SPA3

**Göran Dillner** (April 26, 1832 – March 28, 1906) was a Swedish mathematician.

:::

He founded the Swedish *Tidskrift för matematik och fysik* (Journal of Mathematics and Physics), and was its editor-in-chief in 1868–1871 and 1874. He was a city councilor in [Uppsala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala) from 1875 to 1882.
