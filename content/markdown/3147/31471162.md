::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Runcic 5-cubes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcic 5-cube**, **runcic 5-demicube** or **runcihalf 5-cube**, is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338). There are 2 runcic forms for the 5-cube. Runcic 5-cubes have half the vertices of [runcinated 5-cubes](wikipedia://en/31324900).

:::

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Cantellated 5-demicube/demipenteract
- Small rhombated hemipenteract (sirhin) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the 960 vertices of a runcic 5-cubes centered at the origin are coordinate permutations:

(±1,±1,±1,±3,±3)

with an odd number of plus signs.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

These polytopes are based on the [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232), a member of a dimensional family of [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6842392) called [demihypercubes](wikipedia://en/6833695) for being [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the [hypercube](wikipedia://en/39783) family.

There are 23 [uniform 5-polytopes](wikipedia://en/14415338) that can be constructed from the D<sub>5</sub> symmetry of the 5-demicube, of which are unique to this family, and 15 are shared within the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) family.
