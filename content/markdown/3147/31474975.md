# Abstract model theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abstract model theory"}

In [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), **abstract model theory** is a generalization of [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858) that studies the general properties of extensions of [first-order logic](wikipedia://en/10983) and their models.[^o2]

:::

Abstract model theory provides an approach that allows us to step back and study a wide range of logics and their relationships.[^o3] The starting point for the study of abstract models, which resulted in good examples was [Lindström's theorem](wikipedia://en/7510462).[^o4]

In 1974 [Jon Barwise](wikipedia://en/912858) provided an [axiomatization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Axiomatization) of abstract model theory.[^o5]
