# Mainarizumu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mainarizumu"}

*Image: Mainarizumu.png.* A 5x5 Mainarizumu puzzle

**Mainarizumu** (マイナリズム), also known as **Minarism**, is a type of [logic puzzle](wikipedia://en/101595) published by [Nikoli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikoli_%28publisher%29).

:::

## Rules {#Rules}

*Mainarizumu* is played on a square grid, typically 7x7 or smaller, with two kinds of clues: a greater-than or less-than sign connecting two adjacent squares, or a circled digit connecting two adjacent squares. The solver is to enter a digit into every square so that:

- No digit is repeated in any row or column (much like in [Sudoku](wikipedia://en/1365807) puzzles).[^o3]
- If two squares are connected by a greater-than or less-than sign, then the digits in those squares must obey that inequality.[^o3]
- If two squares are connected by a circled digit, then the digits in those squares must differ by exactly that amount.[^o3]
