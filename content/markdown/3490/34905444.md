# Johann Georg Rosenhain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Georg Rosenhain"}

*Image: George Rosenhain.JPG.*

**Johann Georg Rosenhain** (10 June 1816 in Königsberg – 14 March 1887 Berlin) was a German mathematician who introduced [theta characteristics](wikipedia://en/7193470).

:::

Rosenhain was born to a Jewish family, to Nathan Rosenhain and Röschen Joseph.[^o3]
