# Jean-Claude Sikorav

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Claude Sikorav"}

*Image: Sikoravphoto.png.* Jean-Claude Sikorav

**Jean-Claude Sikorav** (born 21 June 1957) is a French mathematician. He is professor at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon). He is specialized in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298).[^o2]

:::

## Main contributions {#Main_contributions}

Sikorav is known for his proof, joint with [François Laudenbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/François_Laudenbach), of the [Arnold conjecture](wikipedia://en/72203783) for Lagrangian intersections in cotangent bundles,[^o4] as well as for introducing [generating families](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generating_families) in symplectic topology.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Sikorav is one of fifteen members of a group of mathematicians who published the book *Uniformisation des surfaces de Riemann* under the [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) of Henri Paul de Saint-Gervais.[^o6]

He has written the survey

- Sikorav, Jean-Claude. (1994). "Holomorphic curves in symplectic geometry". 117. Birkhäuser. p. 165–189. mr:1274929.

and research papers

- Hofer, Helmut; Lizan, Véronique; Sikorav, Jean-Claude. (1997). "On genericity for holomorphic curves in four-dimensional almost-complex manifolds". *Journal of Geometric Analysis*. 7 (1). p. 149–159. doi:10.1007/BF02921708. mr:1630789.
- Laudenbach, François; Sikorav, Jean-Claude. (1985). "Persistance d'intersection avec la section nulle au cours d'une isotopie hamiltonienne dans un fibré cotangent". *Inventiones Mathematicae*. 82 (2). p. 349–357. doi:10.1007/BF01388807. mr:809719.

## Honors {#Honors}

Sikorav is a Knight of the [Ordre des Palmes Académiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_des_Palmes_Académiques).
