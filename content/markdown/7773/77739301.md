# Cipher device

*Image: Wheel cipher.png.* [M-94](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M-94_%28cipher_machine%29) cipher device showing possible ciphertexts for the ATTACK AT DAWN plaintext

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cipher device"}

A **cipher device** was a term used by the US military in the first half of the 20th century to describe a manually operated **cipher equipment** that converted the [plaintext](wikipedia://en/157935) into [ciphertext](wikipedia://en/175560) or vice versa. A similar term, **cipher machine**, was used to describe the cipher equipment that required external power for operation.[^n1] **Cipher box** or **crypto box** is a physical [cryptographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographic) device used to [encrypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Encrypt) and [decrypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decrypt) messages between plaintext (unencrypted) and ciphertext (encrypted or secret) forms. The ciphertext is suitable for transmission over a channel, such as radio, that might be observed by an adversary the communicating parties wish to conceal the plaintext from.

:::
