# General hypergeometric function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="General hypergeometric function"}

In mathematics, a **general hypergeometric function** or **Aomoto–Gelfand hypergeometric function** is a generalization of the [hypergeometric function](wikipedia://en/2055223) that was introduced by (Gelfand 1986). The general hypergeometric function is a function that is (more or less) defined on a [Grassmannian](wikipedia://en/373810), and depends on a choice of some complex numbers and signs.

:::
