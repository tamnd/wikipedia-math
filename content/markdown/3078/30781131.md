# David Buchsbaum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Buchsbaum"}

**David Alvin Buchsbaum** (November 6, 1929[^o2] – January 8, 2021[^o3]) was a mathematician at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University) who worked on [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He proved the [Auslander–Buchsbaum formula](wikipedia://en/30774458) and the [Auslander–Buchsbaum theorem](wikipedia://en/30774055).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Buchsbaum earned his Ph.D. under [Samuel Eilenberg](wikipedia://en/362501) in 1954 from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) with thesis *Exact Categories and Duality*. Among his doctoral students are [Peter J. Freyd](wikipedia://en/4593361) and [Hema Srinivasan](wikipedia://en/55841154). In 1995, he was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences). In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
