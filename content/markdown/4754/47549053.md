# Peter W. Bates

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter W. Bates"}

**Peter W. Bates** is a professor of mathematics at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University).[^o2]

:::

Bates received his Ph.D. from the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) in 1976.

In 2012, Bates became an inaugural fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4][^o5]
