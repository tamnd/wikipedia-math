# Sigurd Angenent

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sigurd Angenent"}

**Sigurd Bernardus Angenent** (born 1960)[^o2] is a Dutch-born mathematician and was a professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) from 1987 until 2023.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Angenent was raised in [Haarlem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haarlem), the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands).[^o4] He obtained his PhD in Mathematics from [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) in 1986.[^o5] He became professor at the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 1987.[^o6] In 1996 Angenent became a correspondent of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o8] At the University of Wisconsin–Madison, he was the director of the Undergraduate Mathematics Program. After becoming frustrated with textbooks' high prices and poor quality, he wrote and made available his own notes for all classes.[^o7] Angenent retired in 2023.[^o6]

Angenent works on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), with his recent research focusing on [heat equation](wikipedia://en/166415) and [diffusion equation](wikipedia://en/645335).[^o8][^o9] The [Angenent torus](wikipedia://en/48546702) and [Angenent ovals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angenent_oval) are special solutions to the [mean curvature flow](wikipedia://en/13110176) published by Angenent in 1992;[^o10] The Angenent torus remains self-similar as it collapses to a point under the flow, and the Angenent ovals are the only compact convex [ancient solutions](wikipedia://en/48528005) other than [circles](wikipedia://en/6220) for the [curve-shortening flow](wikipedia://en/44089758).[^o11]
