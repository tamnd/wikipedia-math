# Daniel Allcock

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Allcock"}

**Daniel Allcock** is a mathematician specializing in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He is an emeritus professor of mathematics at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).[^o2]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Allcock graduated from the University of Texas in 1991 with a [double major](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_major) in mathematics and physics.[^o2] He received his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1996 under the supervision of [Richard Borcherds](wikipedia://en/292169) and [Andrew Casson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Casson). After temporary positions at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) and [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), he returned to the University of Texas as a faculty member in 2002.[^o2]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2012, Allcock became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
