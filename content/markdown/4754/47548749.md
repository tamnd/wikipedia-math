# Paul Balmer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Balmer"}

**Paul Balmer** (born 1970) is a Swiss mathematician working in tensor triangular geometry, algebraic geometry, modular representation theory, and homotopy theory. He is a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o3]

:::

Balmer received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lausanne) in 1998, under the supervision of [Manuel Ojanguren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manuel_Ojanguren), with the thesis *Groupes de Witt dérivés des Schémas* (in French).

His research centers around [triangulated categories](wikipedia://en/1679700). More specifically, he is a proponent of tensor-triangular geometry, an umbrella topic that covers geometric aspects of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685), [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655), and other areas, by means of relevant tensor-triangulated categories.

Balmer was an [Invited Speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#2010,_Hyderabad) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad) in 2010, with a talk on *Tensor Triangular Geometry*.[^o5] In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] He was awarded the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in 2015.[^o7]
