# Henning Haahr Andersen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henning Haahr Andersen"}

*Image: Henning Haahr Andersen 2012.jpg.* Andersen in 2012

**Henning Haahr Andersen** is a mathematician specializing in [Algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944), [Quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678) and [Representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).[^o2]

:::

Andersen received his Ph.D. from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1977 under the supervision of [Steven Lawrence Kleiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Lawrence_Kleiman).

In 2012, Andersen became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
