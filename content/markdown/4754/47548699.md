# Avner Ash

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Avner Ash"}

**Avner Ash** is a professor of mathematics at [Boston College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_College).[^o2] Prior to that, he held faculty positions at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Ash received his Ph.D. from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1975 under the supervision of [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594).[^o4]

In 2012, Ash became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
