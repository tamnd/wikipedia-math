# David E. Barrett

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David E. Barrett"}

**David Eugene Barrett** is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).[^o2]

:::

Barrett received his Ph.D. from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1982 under the supervision of [Raghavan Narasimhan](wikipedia://en/33109671).

In 2012, Barrett became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
