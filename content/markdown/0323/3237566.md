# Yegorov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yegorov"}

**Yegorov**, also **Egorov** (Егоров), or **Yegorova** (feminine; Егорова), is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) last name that is derived from the male given name [Yegor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yegor) and literally means *Yegor's*. It may refer to:

:::

- [Alexander Ilyich Yegorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Ilyich_Yegorov) (1883–1939), Soviet military commander and Marshal of the Soviet Union
- [Alexander Valentinovich Yegorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Valentinovich_Yegorov), Russian diplomat and ambassador

- [Anatoly G. Yegorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoly_G._Yegorov) (1920–1997), Soviet philosopher
- [Boris Yegorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Yegorov) (1937–1994), Soviet cosmonaut
- [Daniil Yegorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniil_Yegorov) (b. 1975), Russian economist
- [Dimitri Egorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimitri_Egorov) (1869–1931), Russian mathematician

- [Pavel Yegorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Yegorov) (1889/1895–1965), Soviet military commander
