# Deligne's conjecture on Hochschild cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Deligne's conjecture on Hochschild cohomology"}

In [deformation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_theory), a branch of mathematics, **Deligne's conjecture** is about the [operadic](wikipedia://en/3126358) structure on [Hochschild cochain complex](wikipedia://en/10499629). Various proofs have been suggested by [Dmitry Tamarkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Tamarkin),[^o2][^o3] [Alexander A. Voronov](wikipedia://en/68168204),[^o4] [James E. McClure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_E._McClure) and [Jeffrey H. Smith](wikipedia://en/2350459),[^o5] [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509) and [Yan Soibelman](wikipedia://en/26985688),[^o6] and others, after an initial input of construction of [homotopy algebraic structures](wikipedia://en/1321050) on the Hochschild complex.[^o7][^o8] It is of importance in relation with [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305).
