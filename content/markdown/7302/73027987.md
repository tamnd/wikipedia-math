# Piecewise algebraic space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Piecewise algebraic space"}

In mathematics, a **piecewise algebraic space** is a generalization of a [semialgebraic set](wikipedia://en/13329722), introduced by [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509) and [Yan Soibelman](wikipedia://en/26985688). The motivation was for the proof of [Deligne's conjecture on Hochschild cohomology](wikipedia://en/73028060). Robert Hardt, Pascal Lambrechts, Victor Turchin, and [Ismar Volić](wikipedia://en/64105841) later developed the theory.

:::
