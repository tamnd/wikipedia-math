# Journal of Number Theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Number Theory"}

The ***Journal of Number Theory*** (***JNT***) is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering all aspects of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). The journal was established in 1969 by R.P. Bambah, [P. Roquette](wikipedia://en/41199245), [A. Ross](wikipedia://en/2410144), A. Woods, and [H. Zassenhaus](wikipedia://en/1376825) ([Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University)). It is currently published monthly by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) and the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Dorian Goldfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorian_Goldfeld) ([Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University)). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2022 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.7.[^o3]

:::

## David Goss prize {#David_Goss_prize}

The **David Goss Prize** in Number theory, founded by the Journal of Number Theory, is awarded every two years, to mathematicians under the age of 35 for outstanding contributions to number theory. The prize is dedicated to the memory of [David Goss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Goss) who was the former editor in chief of the Journal of Number Theory. The current award is US\$10,000.

The winners are selected and chosen by the scientific organizing committee of the JNT Biennial Conference and announced during the JNT Biennial Conference.
