# Fantasia Mathematica

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fantasia Mathematica"}

***Fantasia Mathematica*** [^o3] is an [anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology) published in 1958 containing stories, humor, poems, etc., all on mathematical topics, compiled by [Clifton Fadiman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifton_Fadiman). A companion volume was published as *[The Mathematical Magpie](wikipedia://en/31886113)* (1962). The volume contains writing by authors including [Robert Heinlein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Heinlein), [Aldous Huxley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldous_Huxley), [H. G. Wells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._G._Wells), and [Martin Gardner](wikipedia://en/20435).

:::
