# Chihara–Ismail polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chihara–Ismail polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Chihara–Ismail polynomials** are a family of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) introduced by (txt),[^o3] generalizing the **van Doorn polynomials** introduced by van Doorn (1981)[^o4] and the [Karlin–McGregor polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlin–McGregor_polynomials). They have a rather unusual [measure](wikipedia://en/19873), which is [discrete](wikipedia://en/8912350) except for a single [limit point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_point) at 0 with jump 0, and is non-symmetric, but whose support has an infinite number of both positive and negative points.

:::
