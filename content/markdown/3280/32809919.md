# Theodore Seio Chihara

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodore Seio Chihara"}

**Theodore Seio Chihara** (March 14, 1929 – February 11, 2026) was an American mathematician working on [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) who introduced [Al-Salam–Chihara polynomials](wikipedia://en/32680308), [Brenke–Chihara polynomials](wikipedia://en/32814670), and [Chihara–Ismail polynomials](wikipedia://en/32809583). His brother was composer [Paul Chihara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Chihara). He earned his PhD from [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University), where his thesis advisor was [Arthur Rosenthal](wikipedia://en/33183847).[^o2] Chihara died on February 11, 2026, at the age of 96.[^o3]

:::
