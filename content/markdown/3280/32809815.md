# Mourad Ismail

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mourad Ismail"}

**Mourad E. H. Ismail** (مُراد اسماعيل; born April 27, 1944, in [Cairo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo), [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt)) is an [Egyptian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Egyptian_Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) and [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110).

:::

Ismail received his bachelor's degree from [Cairo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo_University). He holds Masters and doctorate degrees from the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta). He worked at and visited several universities. Currently he is a visiting professor at the [University of Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Louisiana). Before he held a research professorship at the [University of Central Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Florida) and a Distinguished Scientist Fellowship at [King Saud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Saud_University) in [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia).

Ismail is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)[^o3] and the [Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Physics). He is among the ISI highly cited scientists. He served on the editorial boards of several journals including *Constructive Approximation*, *Journal of Approximation Theory*, *Journal of Physics A*, and *The Ramanujan Journal*. He published over 290 research articles, one book and edited several books.

Mourad Ismail works in the area of [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function), [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) and their applications. His research also touches upon [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713) and [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199). Most of Ismail's work is joint with other mathematicians and physicists and some of his papers are interdisciplinary. He worked on [infinite divisibility](wikipedia://en/13588803) problems in [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) that led to questions about monotonicity properties of special functions. He also worked on monotonicity and inequalities for zeros of orthogonal polynomials. He made several contributions to the [asymptotic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic_theory) of orthogonal polynomials. He also studied moment problems and found the orthogonality measure for several orthogonal polynomials. This includes the *q*-ultraspherical polynomials (also known as the Askey–Ismail or Rogers–Askey–Ismail polynomials), the random walk polynomials (also known as the Askey–Ismail polynomials), the Al-Salam–Ismail polynomials, and the Chihara–Ismail polynomials. Ismail also worked on [*q*-series](wikipedia://en/2233526) and [Rogers–Ramanujan identities](wikipedia://en/2324711). Ismail is also interested in the combinatorial theory of orthogonal polynomials and their linearization coefficients.
