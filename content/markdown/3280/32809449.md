# Al-Salam–Ismail polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Al-Salam–Ismail polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **Al-Salam–Ismail polynomials** are a family of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) introduced by [Waleed Al-Salam](wikipedia://en/32680535) and [Mourad Ismail](wikipedia://en/32809815).[^n1]

:::
