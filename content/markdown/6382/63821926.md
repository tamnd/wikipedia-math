# Simone Gutt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Simone Gutt"}

**Simone Gutt** (born 1956) is a Belgian mathematician specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). She is a professor of mathematics at the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gutt was born on 13 July 1956 in [Uccle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uccle), near [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels). She completed her doctorate in 1980 at the Université libre de Bruxelles; her dissertation, *Déformations formelles de l'algèbre des fonctions différentiables sur une variété symplectique*, was jointly supervised by Michel Cahen and Moshé Flato.

She was a researcher for the [National Fund for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Fund_for_Scientific_Research) from 1981 until 1991, and became a professor at the Université libre de Bruxelles in 1992.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Gutt was the 1998 winner of the quadrennial [Francois Deruyts Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francois_Deruyts_Prize) in geometry of the [Royal Academies for Science and the Arts of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academies_for_Science_and_the_Arts_of_Belgium). She was elected to the [Royal Academy of Science, Letters and Fine Arts of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Science,_Letters_and_Fine_Arts_of_Belgium) in 2004.
