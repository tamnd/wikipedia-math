# U. K. Anandavardhanan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="U. K. Anandavardhanan"}

**U. K. Anandavardhanan** (born 25 May 1976) is an Indian mathematician specialising in [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He was awarded the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize for Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology), the highest science award in India, for the year 2020 in mathematical science category.[^o3] He is affiliated to [Indian Institute of Technology, Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Technology,_Bombay).[^o4][^o5]

:::

After completing undergraduate studies in [University of Calicut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calicut), Anandavardhanan joined [University of Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hyderabad) for further studies. He secured M Sc in mathematics from there in 1998 and Ph D in 2003. He was with Tata Institute of Fundamental Research during February 2003 - July 2005 and spent the spring of 2004 in University of Iowa. He has been a faculty member of Indian Institute of Technology Bombay since July 2005.

## Awards {#Awards}

In addition to the Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize, he has been conferred the following awards also:[^o7]

- [Asian Scientist 100](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist_100), *[Asian Scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asian_Scientist)*, 2021[^o8]
- Young Scientist Platinum Jubilee Award, [National Academy of Sciences, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences,_India), Allahabad, 2009
- INSA Medal for Young Scientist, [Indian National Science Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_National_Science_Academy), New Delhi, 2008
