# Miquel configuration

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Miquel configuration"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **Miquel configuration** is a [configuration](wikipedia://en/7200539) of eight [points](wikipedia://en/593693) and six [circles](wikipedia://en/6220) in the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410), (8<sub>3</sub> 6<sub>4</sub>), with four points per circle and three circles through each point.[^o2]

:::

## In two dimensions {#In_two_dimensions}

Its [Levi graph](wikipedia://en/1396888) is the [rhombic dodecahedral graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombic_dodecahedral_graph), the skeleton of the [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631). The configuration is related to [Miquel's theorem](wikipedia://en/19335221).

## In three dimensions {#In_three_dimensions}

The configuration has maximal symmetry in 3-dimension, and can be seen as 6 circles circumscribe the square faces of a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285). It has 12 sets of pairwise circle intersections, corresponding to the edges of the cube and octahedron. Structurally it has 48 automorphisms of [octahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2900805).

If two opposite circles are removed the configuration becomes (8<sub>2</sub> 4<sub>2</sub>), with 128 automorphisms (4 rotations by 2<sup>3</sup> pair interchanges)

A different (8<sub>3</sub> 6<sub>4</sub>) can be found as with 6 central circles on a cube. The circles are on the 6 mirror planes of [tetrahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2904472). In full it has 384 automorphisms of [hyperoctahedral symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperoctahedral_symmetry) as the maximal geometric symmetry can be seen in 6, [C(4,2)](wikipedia://en/5308), orthogonal circles as central squares in a [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422).

## Dual configuration {#Dual_configuration}

The dual configuration (6<sub>4</sub> 8<sub>3</sub>) can be drawn with the 6 vertices of an [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) and the 8 circles circumscribe the 8 triangular faces.

Taking half of the circles makes (6<sub>2</sub> 4<sub>3</sub>) with [tetrahedral symmetry](wikipedia://en/2904472) and 24 automorphisms. This is isomorphic to the point-line configuration [complete quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/2605272).

Three central circles can also go through the same 6 vertices, and can be seen as square faces in the [tetrahemihexahedron](wikipedia://en/2851834).
