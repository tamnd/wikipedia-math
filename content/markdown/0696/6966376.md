# Associação Brasileira de Estatística

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Associação Brasileira de Estatística"}

The **Brazilian Statistical Association** (Associação Brasileira de Estatística (*ABE*)) is a [non-profit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-profit) [organization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organization) with the purpose of promoting the development and application of [statistical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_science). It also acts as a debating place for any [professional body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_body) that has an interest in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). Its headquarters is at the [Institute of Mathematics and Statistics, University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematics_and_Statistics,_University_of_São_Paulo).

:::

ABE organizes the *[Simpósio Nacional de Probabilidade e Estatística](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simpósio_Nacional_de_Probabilidade_e_Estatística)* (*SINAPE*), a symposium where statisticians from all over the country can debate the latest advances in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685).

ABE publishes the *[Brazilian Journal of Probability and Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Journal_of_Probability_and_Statistics)*. The journal is published four times per year.
