# Jeremy Rickard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeremy Rickard"}

*Image: Rickard jeremy.jpg.* Jeremy Rickard, [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669) 2006

**Jeremy Rickard**, also known as **J. C. Rickard** or **J. Rickard**, is a British mathematician who deals with [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801). He researches [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685) of [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) and related questions of algebraic topology, representation theory of finite algebras and [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410). Rickard or derived equivalences as a generalization of [Morita equivalences](wikipedia://en/3296134) of [rings](wikipedia://en/48404) and algebras are named after him.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Rickard received his PhD in 1988 from [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London) under [Aidan Schofield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aidan_Schofield). He is a professor at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Rickard was a winner of the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 1995. In 2002, he received the [Senior Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Berwick_Prize). In 1998, he was an Invited Speaker with talk *The abelian defect group conjecture* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o5]
