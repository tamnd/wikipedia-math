# National Institute of Statistics (Guatemala)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="National Institute of Statistics"}

The **National Statistics Institute of Guatemala** (Instituto Nacional de Estadística de Guatemala, INE) is the statistics agency of the [Government of Guatemala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Guatemala). It has responsibility to collect, prepare, and publish official statistics. INE runs the population census and issues statistics on employment, price levels, poverty rates, and other standard national statistics.[^o4]

:::

INE will conduct a population census of Guatemala in July and August 2018, the twelfth such census.[^o5]

INE publishes monthly consumer price index (CPI) statistics. Annual consumer price inflation was estimated at 4-5% in 2016.[^o6]

INE publishes general agriculture, health, and environmental statistics.[^o7]
