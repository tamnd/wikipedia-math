# Richard Teng

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Teng"}

**Richard Teng** is a Singaporean business executive who is the [co-chief executive officer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Co-chief_executive_officer) (CEO) of [Binance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binance), a [cryptocurrency exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptocurrency_exchange). Previously, he worked for the [Monetary Authority of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monetary_Authority_of_Singapore) and was chief regulatory officer of the [Singapore Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Exchange) and CEO of the [Abu Dhabi Global Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Dhabi_Global_Market).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Teng was born in [Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore).[^o5] He earned an accounting degree from [Nanyang Technological University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nanyang_Technological_University) and a [master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in applied finance from the [University of Western Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Western_Australia).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Teng was a [civil servant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_servant) and business executive in the financial and regulatory sectors for three decades.[^o8][^o9] He was the director of [corporate finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corporate_finance) at the [Monetary Authority of Singapore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monetary_Authority_of_Singapore), where he worked for thirteen years. He later held multiple positions at the [Singapore Exchange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singapore_Exchange) (SGX), including: senior vice president, head of issuer regulation, chief of staff for risk management, and chief regulatory officer.[^o5] He was CEO of the [Abu Dhabi Global Market](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abu_Dhabi_Global_Market) for six years starting in 2015.[^o10]

Teng joined [Binance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binance) in August 2021 as CEO of the company's Singapore business.[^o5] He became regional head of Asia, Europe, and the [Middle East and North Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_and_North_Africa), in April 2023,[^o10] and was later the global head of regional markets.[^o6][^o11] He was named CEO of Binance in November 2023, replacing founder [Changpeng Zhao](wikipedia://en/56786814) who stepped down after pleading guilty to criminal charges related to his management of Binance.[^o12][^o13] Previously, Teng was a board member of Abu Dhabi-based Lulu Financial Group in 2021–2022.[^o5]
