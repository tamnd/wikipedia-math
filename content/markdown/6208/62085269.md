# Maurizio Cornalba

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maurizio Cornalba"}

*Image: Cornalba maurizio.jpg.* Maurizio Cornalba, Berkeley 1975

**Maurizio Cornalba** (born 17 January 1947) is an Italian mathematician, specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

Cornalba completed his undergraduate studies at [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) in 1969 and his graduate studies at the [Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore_di_Pisa) in 1970. He was a postdoc at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) from 1970 to 1971 and an assistant professor at the University of Pisa from 1971 to 1976; he was on leave of absence for the academic year 1971–1972 at Princeton University, for the academic year 1974–1975 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), and for the academic year 1975–1976 at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). At the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) he was a full professor from 1976 to 2017, when he retired as professor emeritus.[^o2]

Cornalba's research deals with the geometry of [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260) and the geometry and topology of [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609).

He was in 1993/94, 2005 and 2007 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), in 1995 at the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595), in 1998 at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam), and in 1984/85 at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University).[^o2]

In 1975 he received the [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize). He is a member of the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei) (elected in 1990 a corresponding member and in 2005 a full member), the [Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Lombardo_Accademia_di_Scienze_e_Lettere) (elected in 2000), and the Accademia delle Scienze di Torino (elected in 2011).[^o2]

In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker with talk *Cohomology of Moduli Spaces of Stable Curves* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o3]

He was one of the co-authors for [Guido Castelnuovo](wikipedia://en/1558891)'s *Opere matematiche: memorie e note*, published in 4 volumes by the [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_Nazionale_dei_Lincei) from 2002 to 2007, and for [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414)'s *Selected works*, published in 4 volumes by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2003.
