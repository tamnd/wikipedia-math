# Zeuthen–Segre invariant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Zeuthen–Segre invariant"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Zeuthen–Segre invariant** *I* is an [invariant](wikipedia://en/1126638) of a [projective surface](wikipedia://en/4342970#Projective_surface) found in a [complex projective space](wikipedia://en/762977) which was introduced by (txt) and rediscovered by (txt).

:::

The invariant *I* is defined to be *d* – 4*g* – *b* if the surface has a [pencil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pencil_%28mathematics%29) of [curves](wikipedia://en/253260), non-singular of [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) *g* except for *d* curves with 1 ordinary [node](wikipedia://en/3090324), and with *b* base points where the curves are non-singular and transverse.

(txt) showed that the Zeuthen–Segre invariant *I* is χ–4, where χ is the topological [Euler–Poincaré characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler–Poincaré_characteristic) introduced by (txt), which is equal to the [Chern number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_number) *c*<sub>2</sub> of the surface.
