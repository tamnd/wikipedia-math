# Peter M. Gruber

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter M. Gruber"}

*Image: Peter M Gruber.JPG.* Peter M. Gruber

**Peter Manfred Gruber** (28 August 1941, [Klagenfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klagenfurt) – 7 March 2017, [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866)) was an Austrian mathematician working in [geometric number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_number_theory)[^o2] as well as in convex and discrete geometry.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gruber obtained his PhD at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 1966, under the supervision of [Nikolaus Hofreiter](wikipedia://en/22209837). From 1971, he was Professor at the [University of Linz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Linz), and from 1976, at the [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien).[^o2] He was a member of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o2][^o7] a foreign member of the Russian Academy of Sciences,[^o2][^o8] and a corresponding member of the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o2][^o9]

His past doctoral students include [Monika Ludwig](wikipedia://en/38459575).[^o6]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Gruber, P.M. (2007). "Convex and Discrete Geometry". Springer-Verlag
- Gruber, P.M.; Lekkerkerker, C.G. (1987). "Geometry of Numbers". North-Holland

## Decorations and awards {#Decorations_and_awards}

- 1967: Prize of the [Austrian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Mathematical_Society)
- 1978, 1980 and 1982: Chairman of the Austrian Mathematical Society
- 1991: Full member of the Austrian Academy of Sciences (Corresponding member since 1988)
- 1996: Medal of the Union of Czech Mathematicians and Physicists
- 2001: [Austrian Cross of Honour for Science and Art, 1st class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Cross_of_Honour_for_Science_and_Art,_1st_class)[^o14]
- 2001: Medal of the mathematical and physical faculty of [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in Prague
- 2003: Foreign member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences)
- 2008: [Grand Silver Medal for Services to the Republic of Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Silver_Medal_for_Services_to_the_Republic_of_Austria)[^o15]
- 2013: [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), for "contributions to the geometry of numbers and to convex and discrete geometry".[^o3]
- Honorary doctorates from the Universities of [Siegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Siegen), [Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin) and [Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salzburg)
- Member of the Academies of Sciences in [Messina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Messina) and [Modena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modena)
- Corresponding member of the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences)
