# Timeline of information theory

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Timeline of information theory"}

A timeline of events related to

*Image: Red pog.svg.*

[information theory](wikipedia://en/14773),

*Image: Orange pog.svg.*

[quantum information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_theory) and [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics),

*Image: Green pog.svg.*

[data compression](wikipedia://en/8013),

*Image: Purple pog.svg.*

[error correcting codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Error_correcting_code) and related subjects.

:::

- 1872 *Image: Orange pog.svg.* – [Ludwig Boltzmann](wikipedia://en/544255) presents his [H-theorem](wikipedia://en/424440), and with it the formula Σ*p*<sub>i</sub> log *p*<sub>i</sub> for the entropy of a single gas particle
- 1878 *Image: Orange pog.svg.* – [J. Willard Gibbs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Willard_Gibbs) defines the [Gibbs entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gibbs_entropy): the probabilities in the entropy formula are now taken as probabilities of the state of the *whole* system
- 1924 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Harry Nyquist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harry_Nyquist) discusses quantifying "intelligence" and the speed at which it can be transmitted by a communication system
- 1927 *Image: Orange pog.svg.* – [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942) defines the [von Neumann entropy](wikipedia://en/2515425), extending the Gibbs entropy to quantum mechanics
- 1928 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Ralph Hartley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Hartley) introduces [Hartley information](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hartley_information) as the logarithm of the number of possible messages, with information being communicated when the receiver can distinguish one sequence of symbols from any other (regardless of any associated meaning)
- 1929 *Image: Orange pog.svg.* – [Leó Szilárd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leó_Szilárd) analyses [Maxwell's demon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell's_demon), showing how a [Szilard engine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szilard_engine) can sometimes transform information into the extraction of useful work
- 1940 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Alan Turing](wikipedia://en/1208) introduces the [deciban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deciban) as a measure of information inferred about the German [Enigma machine](wikipedia://en/9256) cypher settings by the [Banburismus](wikipedia://en/1530481) process
- 1944 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Claude Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693)'s theory of information is substantially complete
- 1947 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Richard W. Hamming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_W._Hamming) invents [Hamming codes](wikipedia://en/41226) for error detection and correction (to protect patent rights, the result is not published until 1950)
- 1948 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Claude E. Shannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_E._Shannon) publishes *[A Mathematical Theory of Communication](wikipedia://en/828061)*
- 1949 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Claude E. Shannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_E._Shannon) publishes *Communication in the Presence of Noise* – [Nyquist–Shannon sampling theorem](wikipedia://en/37864) and [Shannon–Hartley law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shannon–Hartley_law)
- 1949 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Claude E. Shannon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_E._Shannon)'s *[Communication Theory of Secrecy Systems](wikipedia://en/452085)* is declassified
- 1949 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [Robert M. Fano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_M._Fano) publishes *Transmission of Information*. M.I.T. Press, Cambridge, Massachusetts – [Shannon–Fano coding](wikipedia://en/62544)
- 1949 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – Leon G. Kraft discovers [Kraft's inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraft's_inequality), which shows the limits of [prefix codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefix_codes)
- 1949 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Marcel J. E. Golay](wikipedia://en/1988182) introduces [Golay codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golay_code_%28disambiguation%29) for [forward error correction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forward_error_correction)
- 1950 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Richard Hamming](wikipedia://en/99326) publishes [Hamming code](wikipedia://en/41226) paper, creates a new field of study [Coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869)
- 1951 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Solomon Kullback](wikipedia://en/506420) and [Richard Leibler](wikipedia://en/4869853) introduce the [Kullback–Leibler divergence](wikipedia://en/1115052)
- 1951 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [David A. Huffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_A._Huffman) invents [Huffman encoding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huffman_encoding), a method of finding optimal [prefix codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefix_codes) for [lossless](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lossless) [data compression](wikipedia://en/8013)
- 1953 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [August Albert Sardinas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Albert_Sardinas) and George W. Patterson devise the [Sardinas–Patterson algorithm](wikipedia://en/23632960), a procedure to decide whether a given [variable-length code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable-length_code) is uniquely decodable
- 1954 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Irving S. Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_S._Reed) and [David E. Muller](wikipedia://en/35667779) propose [Reed–Muller codes](wikipedia://en/2032752)
- 1955 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Peter Elias](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Elias) introduces [convolutional codes](wikipedia://en/40962)
- 1957 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Eugene Prange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eugene_Prange) first discusses [cyclic codes](wikipedia://en/4706825)
- 1959 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Alexis Hocquenghem](wikipedia://en/23349325), and independently the next year [Raj Chandra Bose](wikipedia://en/1162266) and [Dwijendra Kumar Ray-Chaudhuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwijendra_Kumar_Ray-Chaudhuri), discover [BCH codes](wikipedia://en/40779)
- 1960 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Irving S. Reed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_S._Reed) and [Gustave Solomon](wikipedia://en/2151199) propose [Reed–Solomon codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reed–Solomon_code)
- 1962 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Robert G. Gallager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_G._Gallager) proposes [low-density parity-check codes](wikipedia://en/516393); they are unused for 30 years due to technical limitations
- 1965 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Dave Forney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_Forney) discusses [concatenated codes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concatenated_code)
- 1966 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [Fumitada Itakura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fumitada_Itakura) ([Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University)) and Shuzo Saito ([Nippon Telegraph and Telephone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nippon_Telegraph_and_Telephone)) develop [linear predictive coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_predictive_coding) (LPC), a form of [speech coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speech_coding)[^o2]
- 1967 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Andrew Viterbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Viterbi) reveals the [Viterbi algorithm](wikipedia://en/228015), making decoding of convolutional codes practicable
- 1968 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Elwyn Berlekamp](wikipedia://en/655893) invents the [Berlekamp–Massey algorithm](wikipedia://en/514832); its application to decoding BCH and Reed–Solomon codes is pointed out by [James L. Massey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Lee_Massey) the following year
- 1968 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [Chris Wallace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chris_Wallace_%28computer_scientist%29) and David M. Boulton publish the first of many papers on [Minimum Message Length](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_Message_Length) ([MML](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimum_Message_Length)) statistical and inductive inference
- 1970 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Valerii Denisovich Goppa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerii_Denisovich_Goppa) introduces [Goppa codes](wikipedia://en/37834267)
- 1972 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Jørn Justesen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jørn_Justesen) proposes [Justesen codes](wikipedia://en/18557138), an improvement of Reed–Solomon codes
- 1972 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [Nasir Ahmed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N._Ahmed) proposes the [discrete cosine transform](wikipedia://en/59962) (DCT), which he develops with T. Natarajan and [K. R. Rao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._R._Rao) in 1973;[^o3] the DCT later became the most widely used [lossy compression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lossy_compression) algorithm, the basis for multimedia formats such as [JPEG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JPEG), [MPEG](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MPEG) and [MP3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MP3)
- 1973 *Image: Red pog.svg.* – [David Slepian](wikipedia://en/1706809) and [Jack Wolf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Keil_Wolf) discover and prove the [Slepian–Wolf coding](wikipedia://en/30474423) limits for distributed [source coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_coding)[^o4]
- 1976 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Gottfried Ungerboeck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Ungerboeck) gives the first paper on [trellis modulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trellis_modulation); a more detailed exposition in 1982 leads to a raising of analogue modem [POTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plain_old_telephone_service) speeds from 9.6 kbit/s to 33.6 kbit/s
- 1976 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – Richard Pasco and [Jorma J. Rissanen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorma_J._Rissanen) develop effective [arithmetic coding](wikipedia://en/62545) techniques
- 1977 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [Abraham Lempel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Lempel) and [Jacob Ziv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_Ziv) develop Lempel–Ziv compression ([LZ77](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LZ77))
- 1982 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Valerii Denisovich Goppa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valerii_Denisovich_Goppa) introduces [algebraic geometry codes](wikipedia://en/1882683)
- 1989 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [Phil Katz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phil_Katz) publishes [the `.zip` format](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZIP_%28file_format%29) including [DEFLATE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DEFLATE) (LZ77 + Huffman coding); later to become the most widely used archive container
- 1993 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Claude Berrou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Berrou), [Alain Glavieux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alain_Glavieux) and [Punya Thitimajshima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Punya_Thitimajshima) introduce [Turbo codes](wikipedia://en/497535)
- 1994 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [Michael Burrows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Burrows_%28computer_scientist%29) and [David Wheeler](wikipedia://en/532495) publish the [Burrows–Wheeler transform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burrows–Wheeler_transform), later to find use in [bzip2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bzip2)
- 1995 *Image: Orange pog.svg.* – [Benjamin Schumacher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Schumacher) coins the term [qubit](wikipedia://en/25284) and proves the quantum noiseless coding theorem
- 2003 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [David J. C. MacKay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_J._C._MacKay) shows the connection between information theory, inference and machine learning in his book.
- 2006 *Image: Green pog.svg.* – [Jarosław Duda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jarosław_Duda_%28computer_scientist%29) introduces first [Asymmetric numeral systems](wikipedia://en/51250465) entropy coding: since 2014 popular replacement of [Huffman](wikipedia://en/13883) and [arithmetic coding](wikipedia://en/62545) in compressors like [Facebook Zstandard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zstandard), [Apple LZFSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LZFSE), [CRAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRAM_%28file_format%29) or [JPEG XL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JPEG_XL)
- 2008 *Image: Purple pog.svg.* – [Erdal Arıkan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdal_Arıkan) introduces [polar codes](wikipedia://en/38104403), the first practical construction of codes that achieves capacity for a wide array of channels
