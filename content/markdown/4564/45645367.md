# Lynne H. Walling

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lynne H. Walling"}

**Lynne Heather Walling** (October 9, 1958 – May 28, 2021) was an American mathematician specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527),[^o3] who became a reader in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol).[^o4][^o5] She was known for her research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). She died on 28 May 2021.[^o6]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Walling was born on October 9, 1958, and grew up in northern California. She began her undergraduate studies at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego), and after a two-year gap continued at [Sonoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonoma_State_University), studying accounting at first but then finishing a bachelor's degree in mathematics.[^o3] She received her Ph.D. from [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) in 1987 under Thomas Richard Shemanske.

## Career and later life {#Career_and_later_life}

She then taught at [St. Olaf College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Olaf_College), [Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minnesota). She lived in an old farmhouse, which had no indoor plumbing. She built a hand-operated pump, enabling her to install a bathtub in the kitchen, beside a wood-burning stove.[^o5]

She became a [tenure-track](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenure-track) [Assistant Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_Professor) at the [University of Colorado, Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado,_Boulder) in 1990. In 1995 she received tenure, and in 2000 became a [full Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_Professor). She came to Bristol as a reader in mathematics in 2007.[^o5] From 2011 to 2015 she was Head of Pure Mathematics, and from 2018 was Director of the Institute of Pure Mathematics.[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Walling became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]
