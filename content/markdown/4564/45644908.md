# Nancy K. Stanton

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nancy K. Stanton"}

**Nancy Kahn Stanton** is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of mathematics at [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame).[^o3] She is known for her research in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Stanton received her Ph.D. from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1973 under [Isadore Singer](wikipedia://en/324564). Stanton now works at [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame).[^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1981, Stanton became a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellow).[^o8]

In 2012, Stanton became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9][^o4]
