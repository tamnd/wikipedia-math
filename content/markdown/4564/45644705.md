# Christel Rotthaus

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Christel Rotthaus"}

**Christel Rotthaus** is a professor of mathematics at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University).[^o3] She is known for her research in [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Rotthaus received her Ph.D. from [Westfälische Wilhelms-Universität Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westfälische_Wilhelms-Universität_Münster) in 1975 under [Hans-Joachim Nastold](wikipedia://en/36982769). Rotthaus now works at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University).[^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012, Rotthaus became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
