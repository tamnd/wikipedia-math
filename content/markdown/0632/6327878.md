# Eric Harshbarger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eric Harshbarger"}

**Eric C. Harshbarger** (born c. 1971)[^o4] is a Senior Lecturer of mathematics at [Auburn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_University).[^o5] He previously was an [Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabama)-based builder of large-scale [Lego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lego) brick mosaics and sculptures.[^o6][^o7] From around 2000 to 2006 he was commissioned to build for [television shows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Television_show) and [magazines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magazine) for promotional purposes.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Harshbarger grew up in [Auburn, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn,_Alabama) where he went to [Auburn High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn_High_School_%28Alabama%29). In 1992, he obtained a Bachelor of Science from Auburn University in the Mathematics Department . In 1994, he also received a Master of Science from the Mathematics department at Auburn. In the late 1990s, he moved to California to pursue a job in the computer industry. In 1999, he returned to his home town of Auburn .[^o9]

## Puzzles and games {#Puzzles_and_games}

Harshbarger is also well known for his work in puzzle and game design. His *Digits in a Box*[^o11] toy has been produced by Popular Playthings since 2007. *[Wired](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wired_%28magazine%29)* magazine[^o12] featured an optical illusion puzzle of his design in 2009. Collaborating with [Mike Selinker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_Selinker), Harshbarger co-authored an optimization puzzle for the *Maze of Games* in 2015.[^o13] From 2004-2017 he also has hosted one or more [puzzle parties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puzzlehunt) in and around his hometown of [Auburn, Alabama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auburn,_Alabama). [^o14] His puzzle TicTac's Tactics won Jury Honorable Mention at the 2018 International [Nob Yoshigahara Puzzle Design Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nob_Yoshigahara_Puzzle_Design_Competition).[^o15]

In the state of Alabama, Eric Harshbarger is ranked number one and is in the top 100 players in the United States for the game *[Scrabble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrabble)*.[^o16]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Harshbarger is a senior lecturer in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at Auburn University.[^o18] Harshbarger has been credited for significant research, development, and production of [Go First Dice](wikipedia://en/62247552).[^o19]
