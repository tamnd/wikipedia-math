# Ibrahim ibn Sinan

Ibn Sina

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ibrahim ibn Sinan"}

**Ibrahim ibn Sinan** ([Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic): *Ibrāhīm ibn Sinān ibn Thābit ibn Qurra*, ابراهيم بن سنان بن ثابت بن قرة; born 295 – 296 AH/c. 908 in [Baghdad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baghdad), died: 334-335 AH/946 in Baghdad, aged 38) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) who belonged to a family of scholars originally from [Harran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harran) in northern [Mesopotamia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesopotamia). He was the son of [Sinan ibn Thabit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinan_ibn_Thabit) (c. 880 – 943) and the grandson of [Thābit ibn Qurra](wikipedia://en/207547) (c. 830 – 901).[^n1] Like his grandfather, he belonged to a religious sect of [star worshippers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_worship) known as the [Sabians of Harran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabians_of_Harran).[^o4]

:::

Ibrahim ibn Sinan studied [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), in particular [tangents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangents) to circles. He made advances in the [quadrature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrature_%28geometry%29) of the [parabola](wikipedia://en/23231) and the theory of [integration](wikipedia://en/15532), generalizing the work of [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844), which was unavailable at the time.[^n1][^o6] Ibrahim ibn Sinan is often considered to be one of the most important mathematicians of his time.
