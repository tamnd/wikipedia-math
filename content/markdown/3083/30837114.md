# Subbaramiah Minakshisundaram

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Subbaramiah Minakshisundaram"}

**Subbaramiah Minakshisundaram** (12 October 1913 - 13 August 1968), also known as Minakshi or SMS, was an Indian mathematician who worked on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [heat kernels](wikipedia://en/3904336). In 1946, he worked at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, America, where he met [Åke Pleijel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Åke_Pleijel). In 1949, the two wrote a paper together called, *Some properties of the eigenfunctions of the Laplace-operator on [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652)*, in which they introduced the [Minakshisundaram-Pleijel zeta function](wikipedia://en/4744575).[^o3][^o4][^o9]

:::

On the 13th of August 1968, Subbaramiah suffered a heart attack and died at the age of 55.

## Biography {#Biography}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Subbaramiah Minakshisundaram was the first of three children, born in 1913 and called "Jeja" by his parents, meaning God. In his early years he learned [Malayalam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malayalam), but when his family moved to Chennai because of his father's job as a sanitary engineer they spoke [Tamil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tamil_language).[^o3]

His family were Hindus, and as a boy Minakshisundaram would chant the [Gayatri Japa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gayatri_Mantra) and perform the [Sandhyavandanam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sandhyavandanam) with his grandfather every morning and night.[^o7]

In 1919, Minakshi attended the [Calavala Ramanujam Chetty High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calavala_Ramanujam_Chetty_High_School), where he displayed a "marked aptitude" for mathematics, and graduated in 1929 with a Secondary School Leaving Certificate. After graduating, he studied at Pachaiyappa's College and went to Loyola College in 1931 to pursue a B.A. in mathematics, which he attained in 1934.

### Career {#Career}

After graduating from Loyola College, Subbaramiah joined the [University of Madras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Madras) as a research scholar and worked in the library.[^o9]

During his time at the University of Madras, Minakshi was influenced by [K Ananda Rau](wikipedia://en/35226529), and began studying the summability of [series](wikipedia://en/15287). His first paper, *Tauberian theorems on Dirichlet's series*, was published in 1936 and expanded upon Rau's theorem. He followed this with two papers, both published in 1937: *On the extension of a theorem of Caratheodory in the theory of Fourier series*, and, *The Fourier series of a sequence of functions*.[^o3]

### Marriage {#Marriage}

On the 10th of May, 1937, Minakshisundaram married M Parvathi. They had three children together:

- K Ramu (born 1943)
- K Girija (born 1945)
- K Radha (birthdate unknown)[^o3]
