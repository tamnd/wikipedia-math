# Mary P. Dolciani Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mary P. Dolciani Award"}

The **Mary P. Dolciani Award** is an award established in 2012 by the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o3] The award recognizes a pure or applied mathematician with a record of distinguished contributions to K-16 mathematics education in the United States or Canada and comes with a \$5,000 award.[^o3] Examples of significant contributions include, but are not limited to, the development of K-16 mathematics curriculum, educational technology, or programs to improve teaching or teacher preparation.[^o4] The prize is funded by a grant established by mathematician, educator, and author [Mary P. Dolciani](wikipedia://en/11916611), who dedicated her career to improving mathematics education and is the author of several secondary- and college-level mathematics textbooks.

:::

It should be distinguished from the Mary P. Dolciani Prize for Excellence in Research, awarded beginning in 2019 by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

## Award recipients {#Award_recipients}

- **2024**: Darryl Yong, Harvey Mudd College[^o7][^o8]
- **2023**: Stan Yoshinobu, University of Toronto
- **2022**: [Roger Howe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Evans_Howe), Texas A&M University
- **2021**: John Ewing, Math for America
- **2020**: [Henry O. Pollak](wikipedia://en/19662367), Columbia University
- **2019**: [Joseph Gallian](wikipedia://en/18351855), University of Minnesota Duluth
- **2018**: [Al Cuoco](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al_Cuoco), Distinguished Scholar, Education Development Center[^o9]
- **2017**: [Tatiana Shubin](wikipedia://en/65705932), San Jose State University[^o10]
- **2015**: [Sybilla Beckmann](wikipedia://en/52200311), University of Georgia
- **2014**: [Alan H. Schoenfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_H._Schoenfeld), University of California at Berkeley[^o11]
- **2013**: [Hyman Bass](wikipedia://en/7085910), University of Michigan
- **2012**: [William G. McCallum](wikipedia://en/39953348), University of Arizona
