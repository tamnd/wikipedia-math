# Jacques Touchard

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Jacques Touchard"}

**Jacques André Charles Touchard** (1885–1968) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). In 1953, he proved that an odd [perfect number](wikipedia://en/23670) must be of the form $12k + 1$ or $36k + 9$. In [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) and [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), he introduced the [Touchard polynomials](wikipedia://en/342592). He is also known for his solution to the [ménage problem](wikipedia://en/21068755) of counting seating arrangements in which men and women alternate and are not seated next to their spouses.

:::

## Touchard's Catalan identity {#Touchard's_Catalan_identity}

The following algebraic identity involving the [Catalan numbers](wikipedia://en/216488)

$$C_k ={ 1\over{k+1}}{{2k}\choose {k}},\quad k \ge 0$$

is apparently due to Touchard (according to [Richard P. Stanley](wikipedia://en/1633397), who mentions it in his panorama article "[Exercises on Catalan and Related Numbers](http://www-math.mit.edu/~rstan/ec/catalan.pdf)" giving an overwhelming plenitude of different definitions for the Catalan numbers). For $n \geq 0$ one has

$$C_{n+1} = \sum_{k \,\le\, n/2} 2^{n-2k} {n \choose 2k} C_k. \,$$

Using the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993)

$$C(t)=\sum_{n \ge 0} C_n t^n ={{1-\sqrt{1-4t}}\over {2t}}$$

it can be proved by algebraic manipulations of generating [series](wikipedia://en/15287) that Touchard's identity is equivalent to the [functional equation](wikipedia://en/364377)

$${t \over {1-2t}} C\left({t^2\over (1-2t)^2}\right) = C(t)-1$$

satisfied by the Catalan generating series $C(t)$.
