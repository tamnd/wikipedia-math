# W. Hugh Woodin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="W. Hugh Woodin"}

**William Hugh Woodin** (born April 23, 1955) is an American mathematician at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) specializing in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553). He has made many notable contributions to the theory of [inner models](wikipedia://en/1118686) and [determinacy](wikipedia://en/2596030). A type of [large cardinals](wikipedia://en/383644), the [Woodin cardinals](wikipedia://en/248105), bears his name. In 2023, he was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

William Hugh Woodin was born on April 23, 1955, in [Tucson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson), [Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arizona) to Ann (nee Snow) and William Woodin III, the grandson of [United States Secretary of the Treasury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_the_Treasury) [William Hartman Woodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Hartman_Woodin). Hugh's father was the executive director of the Arizona-Sonora Desert Museum.[^o5]

Woodin earned a BS from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1977 followed by a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the University of California, Berkeley, in 1984 under [Robert M. Solovay](wikipedia://en/3160110). His dissertation title was *Discontinuous Homomorphisms of C(Ω) and Set Theory*.[^o6]

In 1985, Woodin joined the Caltech faculty as a full professor[^o7] and remained there until 1988[^o8] when he left for UC Berkeley.[^o9]

He served as [chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chairman) of the Berkeley mathematics department for the 2002–2003 academic year. Woodin is a managing editor of the *[Journal of Mathematical Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Mathematical_Logic)*. In 2015, Woodin retires from Berkeley[^o10] and moves to Harvard.

He was elected a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2000.[^o11]

He is the great-grandson of [William Hartman Woodin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Hartman_Woodin), former [Secretary of the Treasury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_the_Treasury).

## Work {#Work}

Woodin's earliest mathematical work concerned a connection between set theory and the theory of [Banach algebras](wikipedia://en/4665). In the 1980s he made major contributions in the study of the [Axiom of Determinacy](wikipedia://en/1002300) (AD) via [inner model theory](wikipedia://en/3796621), culminating in determining the precise [consistency strength](wikipedia://en/3435716#Consistency_strength) of AD relative to the standard [large cardinal](wikipedia://en/383644) hierarchy.[^o13]

Woodin has done work on the theory of generic multiverses and the related concept of [Ω-logic](wikipedia://en/13772472), which suggested an argument that the [continuum hypothesis](wikipedia://en/5705) is either undecidable or false in the sense of [mathematical platonism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_platonism). Woodin criticizes this view arguing that it leads to a counterintuitive reduction in which all truths in the set theoretical universe can be decided from a small part of it. He claims that these and related mathematical results lead (intuitively) to the conclusion that the continuum hypothesis has a [truth value](wikipedia://en/161711) and the Platonistic approach is reasonable.

Woodin now predicts that there should be a way of constructing an inner model for almost all known large cardinals, which he calls the Ultimate L and which would have similar properties as [Gödel's constructible universe](wikipedia://en/343335). In particular, the continuum hypothesis would be true in this universe.[^o14]

## Honors {#Honors}

In 2008, Woodin gave the [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431) titled *The Continuum Hypothesis, the Conjecture, and the inner model problem of one supercompact cardinal.* In 2010, he was a plenary speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad).[^o16] In 2018, he was the [Tarski lecturer](wikipedia://en/54104355).
