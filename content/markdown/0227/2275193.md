# Cabal (set theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cabal"}

The **Cabal** was, or perhaps is, a set of [set theorists](wikipedia://en/27553) in [Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_California), particularly at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) and [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech), but also at [UC Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Irvine). Organization and procedures range from informal to nonexistent, so it is difficult to say whether it still exists or exactly who has been a member, but it has included such notable figures as [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985), [Yiannis N. Moschovakis](wikipedia://en/2291002), [John R. Steel](wikipedia://en/2290967), and [Alexander S. Kechris](wikipedia://en/2291355). Others who have published in the proceedings of the Cabal seminar include [Robert M. Solovay](wikipedia://en/3160110), [W. Hugh Woodin](wikipedia://en/2275071), [Matthew Foreman](wikipedia://en/3332703), and [Steve Jackson](wikipedia://en/3265875).

:::

The work of the group is characterized by free use of [large cardinal](wikipedia://en/383644) axioms, and research into the [descriptive set theoretic](wikipedia://en/396619) behavior of [sets](wikipedia://en/26691) of [reals](wikipedia://en/20646438) if such assumptions hold.

Some of the philosophical views of the Cabal seminar were described by [Penelope Maddy](wikipedia://en/4023241).[^n1][^n2]
