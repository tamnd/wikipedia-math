# Dialgebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dialgebra"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), a **dialgebra** is the generalization of both [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [coalgebra](wikipedia://en/310886). The notion was originally introduced by Lambek as "subequalizers",[^o2][^o4] and named as dialgebras by Tatsuya Hagino.[^o3][^o4] Many algebraic notions have previously been generalized to dialgebras.[^o5] Dialgebra also attempts to obtain [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) from associated algebras.[^o6]

:::
