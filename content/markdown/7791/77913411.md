# Hans Hefer

Biographies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Hefer"}

**Hans Hefer** (born on 27 February 1907[^o2] and died on 22 November 1941, [Eastern Front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Front_%28World_War_II%29)[^o6]) was a German mathematician who worked on [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables). [Hefer's lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hefer's_lemma) or [Hefer's theorem](wikipedia://en/78178811) bear his name.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Life {#Life}

There is not sufficient documentation regarding the life of Hans Hefer. Hefer is known to have earned a Ph.D. at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) in 1941, supervised by [Heinrich Behnke](wikipedia://en/22922678).[^o6]

In a posthumous publication made by [Karl Stein](wikipedia://en/7218529) and [Heinrich Benke](wikipedia://en/22922678) with Hefer's name in *Mathematische Annalen* in 1950/51, it was written in a footnote that the author had fallen in the [East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Front_%28World_War_II%29) and the article is an excerpt from his dissertation which he defended in 1940.[^o8] It was also added there that *Since 1941, papers by [K. Oka](wikipedia://en/381978) and [H. Cartan](wikipedia://en/362536) have appeared that contain Hefer's results but use different proofs.*
