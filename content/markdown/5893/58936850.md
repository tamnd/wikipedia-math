# Serge Cantat

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Serge Cantat"}

**Serge Marc Cantat** (born 3 June 1973, in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989))[^o2] is a French mathematician, specializing in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).[^o3]

:::

Cantat received his PhD under the supervision of [Étienne Ghys](wikipedia://en/7561937) in 1999 at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon). Cantat is a [directeur de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France) of [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) at the Institut de recherches mathématiques de Rennes ([University of Rennes 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes_1)). He was previously [directeur de recherche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_France) of [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) at [ENS Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29).

His research deals with complex dynamics and dynamics of automorphisms of [algebraic surfaces](wikipedia://en/648311). He examined the algebraic structure of [Cremona groups](wikipedia://en/7252026) (*i.e.* groups of [birational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birational) automorphisms of $n$-dimensional [projective spaces](wikipedia://en/242135) over a field $k$) and showed with Stéphane Lamy that for an algebraically closed field $k$ and for dimension $n=2$ the Cremona group $\mathrm{Cr}(\mathbb{P}^n(k))$ is not a [simple group](wikipedia://en/47600). In particular, if $k$ is the field of complex numbers and $n=2$, the Cremona group contains an infinite non-countable family of different [normal subgroups](wikipedia://en/21918).

In 2018, Cantat was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Rio de Janeiro. In 2012 he received the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602) for his work on dynamic systems (and especially [holomorphic dynamic systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_dynamics)). In 2012 he was an invited speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków). In 2012 he was awarded the Prix La Recherche. In 2025, he received the [CNRS Silver Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Silver_Medal).[^o5]
