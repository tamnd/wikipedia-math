# Angelika Bunse-Gerstner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Angelika Bunse-Gerstner"}

**Angelika Bunse-Gerstner** (born 1951) is a German mathematician specializing in [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660) and [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bunse-Gerstner earned her Ph.D. from [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University) in 1978. Her dissertation, *Der HR-Algorithmus zur numerischen Bestimmung der Eigenwerte einer Matrix*, was jointly supervised by Ludwig Elsner and Hans Johnen.

Until 2017, Bunse-Gerstner was head of the Numerics group in the Zentrum für Technomathematik (ZeTeM) at the [University of Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bremen).

## Book {#Book}

Bunse-Gerstner is the author of a German-language textbook on [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660), *Numerische lineare Algebra* (with Wolfgang Bunse, Teubner Mathematical Textbooks, 1985).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2017, the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) listed Bunse-Gerstner as a Fellow, "for contributions in numerical linear algebra, control theory, and model reduction".
