# List of largest video game employers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of largest video game employers"}

This is a listing of top 30 largest video game publishers and developers by number of employees. [Ubisoft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_%28division%29) is the largest video game employer in the industry, followed by [Electronic Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Arts) and [Xbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xbox_%28division%29).

:::

Some companies, such as [Tencent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tencent), [Valve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valve_Corporation), [Warner Bros. Games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_Bros._Games), and others, do not publicly disclose how many employees work specifically in their interactive entertainment or gaming divisions.
