# Wolfgang Viechtbauer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Viechtbauer"}

**Wolfgang Viechtbauer** is a statistician. He is an associate professor of methodology and statistics at the [Maastricht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maastricht_University) in the Faculty of Health, Medicine and Life Sciences and Faculty of Psychology and Neuroscience. His most influential work has been focused on the field of [meta-analysis](wikipedia://en/62329) and evidence synthesis.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Viechtbauer completed a M.A. (2002) and a Ph.D. (2004) at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign).[^o4][^o5] His master's thesis was titled *Bias of certain variance estimators in meta-analysis* and his dissertation was titled *Choosing between the fixed-, random-, and mixed-effects model in meta-analysis: an analysis of existing and new model selection methods*.[^o4][^o5] His doctoral advisor was [David Budescu](wikipedia://en/21647430).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Viechtbauer is an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) of methodology and statistics at the [Maastricht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maastricht_University) in the Faculty of Health, Medicine and Life Sciences and Faculty of Psychology and [Neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscience).[^o7]

## Research and software {#Research_and_software}

Viechtbauer’s research centres on statistical methods for meta-analysis, including heterogeneity estimation, publication-bias diagnostics and multilevel or multivariate models.[^o9] In 2009 he released metafor, an [R package](wikipedia://en/65740838) that provides a comprehensive suite of functions for computing effect sizes, fitting fixed-, random- and mixed-effects models, and producing diagnostic graphics.[^o10] The JSS article introducing metafor has received more than 18,000 citations in Google Scholar as of May 2025,[^o11] and the software is referenced in methodological tutorials, and domain-specific guides across health, ecology, education and psychology.[^o12]
