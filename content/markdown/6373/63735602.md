# Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet"}

The **Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet** is a biennial prize awarded by the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in the fields of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physical sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_sciences) since 1954. Each recipient receives 3000 euros.[^o2][^o3] The prize is also awarded quadrennially in [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology).[^o4] The award is also occasionally awarded in other disciplines.[^o14]

:::
