# Belgian Mathematical Society

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Belgian Mathematical Society"}

The **Belgian Mathematical Society** (abbreviated as **BMS**), founded in 1921 by [Théophile de Donder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Théophile_de_Donder) at the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles),[^o3] is the national mathematical society of [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) and a member society of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470). Its mission is to assemble all Belgian mathematicians and defend their interests. Foreign members are also welcome.[^o4]

:::

The society publishes the journal *[Bulletin of the Belgian Mathematical Society - Simon Stevin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_of_the_Belgian_Mathematical_Society_-_Simon_Stevin)* and distributes a newsletter to members, both at a rate of five issues per year.
