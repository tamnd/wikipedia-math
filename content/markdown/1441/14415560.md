# Christian Pommerenke

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Pommerenke"}

**Christian Pommerenke** (17 December 1933 – 18 August 2024) was a German mathematician known for his work in complex analysis.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Pommerenke studied at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) (1954–1958), achieving diploma in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) (1957), [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (1959) on the dissertation *Über die Gleichverteilung von Gitterpunkten auf m-dimensionalen Ellipsoiden* (1959) and [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) (1963). Pommerenke subsequently joined the faculty as Assistant (1958–1964) and Privatdozent (1964–1966). Around the same time he served as [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in [Ann Arbor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ann_Arbor) (1961–1962), was at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1962–1963) and was guest lecturer and reader at [Imperial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College) in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) (1965–1967). Since 1967 he was [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in complex analysis at the mathematics department of [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin).[^o4] He was later an [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus). His doctoral students include [Herbert Robert Stahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Robert_Stahl), known for proving the [Bessis-Moussa-Villani (BMV) conjecture](wikipedia://en/62052484).

Pommerenke died on 18 August 2024, at the age of 90.[^o5]
