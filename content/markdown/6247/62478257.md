# Christoph Schwab

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christoph Schwab"}

**Christoph Schwab** (born 14 October 1962 in [Flörsheim am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flörsheim_am_Main), Germany) is a German applied mathematician, specializing in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and boundary integral equations.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He studied mathematics from 1982 to 1985 at the [Technische Universität Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt). By means of a Fulbright Scholarship, from 1985 he studied at the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park), where he received his PhD in 1989.[^o3] His thesis *Dimensional Reduction for Elliptic Boundary Value Problems*[^o5] was written under the supervision of [Ivo Babuška](wikipedia://en/13503456). Schwab was a postdoc for the academic year 1989–1990 at London's [University of Westminster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Westminster). At the [University of Maryland, Baltimore County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_Baltimore_County) he was an assistant professor from 1990 to 1995 and was appointed in 1995 an associate professor. At [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), Schwab was from 1995 to 1998 an associate professor and is since 1998 a full professor. For the academic year 1993–1994 he was a visiting scientist at the *IBM Deutschland Wissenschaftliches Zentrum* (IBM German Scientific Center) in [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg).[^o3]

In his research the central issues are the finite and boundary element methods (BEM) for numerical solutions of partial differential equations in technology, as well as the mathematical analysis of the modeling of physical processes.[^o3]

In 2002 Schwab was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing.[^o7]
