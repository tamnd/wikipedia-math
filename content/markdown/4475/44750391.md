# Griffiths group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Griffiths group"}

In mathematics, more specifically in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Griffiths group** of a [projective](wikipedia://en/320469) [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) *X* measures the difference between homological equivalence and algebraic equivalence, which are two important [equivalence relations](wikipedia://en/25384599) of [algebraic cycles](wikipedia://en/9520954).

:::

More precisely, it is defined as

$$\operatorname{Griff}^k(X) := Z^k(X)_\mathrm{hom} / Z^k(X)_\mathrm{alg}$$

where $Z^k(X)$ denotes the group of algebraic cycles of some fixed [codimension](wikipedia://en/525149) *k* and the subscripts indicate the groups that are homologically trivial, respectively algebraically equivalent to zero.[^o2]

This group was introduced by [Phillip Griffiths](wikipedia://en/3660414) who showed that for a general [quintic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quintic) in $\mathbf P^4$ (projective 4-space), the group $\operatorname{Griff}^2(X)$ is not a torsion group.
