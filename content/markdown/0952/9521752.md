# Thévenin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thévenin"}

**Thévenin** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Charles Thévenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Thévenin) (1764–1838), neoclassical French painter
- [Denis Thévenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Duhamel_%28author%29), birth name of French author Georges Duhamel
- [Léon Charles Thévenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Léon_Charles_Thévenin) (1857–1926), French engineer
  - [Thévenin's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thévenin's_theorem), electrical engineering theorem developed by him
- [Nicolas Thévenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolas_Thévenin) (born 1958), bishop and Vatican diplomat
- [Olivier Thévenin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olivier_Thévenin) (born 1968), French racing driver
