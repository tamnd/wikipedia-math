# Kinoshita–Lee–Nauenberg theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kinoshita–Lee–Nauenberg theorem"}

The **Kinoshita–Lee–Nauenberg theorem** or **KLN theorem** states that perturbatively the [Standard Model](wikipedia://en/47641) as a whole is infrared (IR) finite. That is, the [infrared divergences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infrared_divergences) coming from loop integrals are canceled by IR divergences coming from [phase space](wikipedia://en/191101) integrals. It was introduced independently by (txt) and (txt).

:::

An analogous result for [quantum electrodynamics](wikipedia://en/25268) alone is known as Bloch–Nordsieck theorem.

Ultraviolet divergences in perturbative [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) are dealt with in [renormalization](wikipedia://en/291453).
