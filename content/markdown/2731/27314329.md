# Ronald DeVore

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald DeVore"}

**Ronald Alvin DeVore** (born May 14, 1941) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and academic. He is the Walter E. Koss Professor and a Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University). DeVore is a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) and the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

:::

## Academic biography {#Academic_biography}

DeVore received a B.S. from [Eastern Michigan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_Michigan_University) in 1964 and a Ph.D. in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1967 under the supervision of Ranko Bojanic.[^o4] From 1968 to 1977 he was at [Oakland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oakland_University). In 1977 he became a professor at the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina), where he served as the Robert L. Sumwalt Professor of Mathematics from 1986 to 2005. From 1999 to 2005 he also served as the director of the Industrial Mathematics Institutes, which he founded. In 2005 he retired from the [University of South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Carolina). Since 2008 he has been the Walter E. Koss Professor at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) and will be named Distinguished Professor in Fall 2010.[^o5]

DeVore has been a visiting professor at a number of universities around the world, including: [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) (1967–1968), the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta) (1971–1972), [University of Erlangen–Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg) (1975–1976), [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) (1977, 1978, 1979), [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) (1983), [Scuola Normale di Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_di_Pisa) (1984), the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) (1983–1984, 1985, 1991), [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) (1990), the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI) (1996, 2000, 2002, 2004, 2005), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (1997–1998), [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University) (2002), the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) (2004–2005), [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) (2005–2006), the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) (2006–2007), and the Fondation Sciences Mathématiques de Paris (2009–2010).[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

DeVore has been active in the development of many areas of applied mathematics such as [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) algorithms, [approximation](wikipedia://en/1092713) of functions, [wavelet transforms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelets), and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). He has also made significant contributions to the theory of [compressive sensing](wikipedia://en/11403316).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

DeVore has received numerous awards, including an [Alexander von Humboldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt) Fellowship from 1975 to 1976, the Journal of Complexity Outstanding Paper Award in 2000, the Bulgarian Gold Medal of Science in 2001, the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in 2002, the ICS Hot Paper Award in 2003, an honorary doctorate from [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University) in 2004,[^o8] and the [SPIE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SPIE) Wavelet Pioneer Award in 2007. He was also a plenary lecturer at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2006.[^o9]

In 2001 he became a member of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences),[^o10] and in 2007 he became a member of the [Bulgarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarian_Academy_of_Sciences). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11] From 2000 to 2002 he was the Chair of the [Society for the Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics) In 2017 he was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o12] He was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) in 2018.[^o13]
