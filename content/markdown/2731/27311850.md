# Philosophia Mathematica

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Philosophia Mathematica"}

***Philosophia Mathematica*** is a [philosophical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) devoted to the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press). The journal publishes three issues per year.
