# Journal of Logic and Computation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Logic and Computation"}

The ***Journal of Logic and Computation*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) focused on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065) and [computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computing). It was established in 1990 and is published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) under licence from Professor [Dov Gabbay](wikipedia://en/3662032) as owner of the journal.

:::
