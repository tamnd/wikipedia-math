# Paul Dedecker

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Dedecker"}

**Paul Dedecker** ([Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_of_Brussels), 1921 – [Caracas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caracas), 2007) was a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked primarily in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) on the subjects of [nonabelian cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060), [general category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), [variational calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_calculus) and its relations to [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410), [exterior calculus](wikipedia://en/220782) on [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2119219) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

He graduated in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1948 at the [Free University of Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles), where he was a student of [van den Dungen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frans-H._van_den_Dungen).
