# Xhezair Teliti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xhezair Teliti"}

**Xhezair Teliti** (born 17 February 1948, in [Kavajë](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavajë)) is a professor of mathematics and has served as chief of the Department of Mathematics at [Tirana University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirana_University) since 2008. He was Albania's Minister of Education from 1993–1996.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

His field of study is Functional Analysis and Theory of Mass and Integration. Teliti is author of many text-books:

1. "Teoria e Funksioneve te Variablit Real, I, II(Theory of Functions of Real Variable)", 1980, Tirana;
1. "Përgjithësimi i Konceptit të Integrali(Generalization of the Concept of Integral)", 1981;
1. "Teoria Konstruktive e Funksioneve(The Constructive Theory of Functions)", P. Pilika, Xh. Teliti – 1984;
1. "Përmbledhje Problemash në Analizën Funksional(Summary of Problems for Functional Analysis)", 1989, Tirana;
1. "Probleme dhe Ushtrime të Analizës Matematike (Problems and Exercises of Mathematical Analysis", 1997, Tirana;
1. "Teoria e Masës dhe e Integrimit(Theory of Mass and Integration)", 1997, Tirana;
1. "Problema në Teorinë e Masës e të Integrimit(Problems for the Theory of Mass and Integration)", 1998, Tirana;
1. "Topologjia e Përgjithshme dhe Analiza Funksionale(General Topology and Funbctional Analysis)", 2002, Tirana;
1. "Elemente Strukturorë dhe Topologjikë në Hapësirat R dhe R (n) (Topological and Structural Elements in R and R (n) spaces", 2008, Tirana.

Prof. Teliti has also written many articles in the Bulletin of Natural Sciences at the University.[^o6]
