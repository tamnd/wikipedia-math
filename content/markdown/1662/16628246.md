# EqWorld

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="EqWorld"}

**EqWorld** ("The World of Mathematical Equations") is a free online [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) reference site that lists information about mathematical [equations](wikipedia://en/9284).[^o2]

:::

The site[^o3] is part of the [Institute for Problems in Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Problems_in_Mechanics), which is part of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).

EqWorld covers [ordinary differential](wikipedia://en/32742753), [partial differential](wikipedia://en/52564), [integral](wikipedia://en/474234), [functional](wikipedia://en/364377), and other mathematical equations. It also outlines some methods for solving equations, and lists many resources for solving equations, and has an equation archive which users can add to.

## Organization {#Organization}

The Editor-in-Chief is the Russian mathematician [A. D. Polyanin](wikipedia://en/40362148). The editorial board includes members form other countries, e.g., [William E. Schiesser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_E._Schiesser), [Daniel I. Zwillinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_I._Zwillinger), etc.[^o5]

## Publications {#Publications}
