# Pataraia's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pataraia's theorem"}

In mathematics, **Pataraia's theorem** states each monotone map $f : P \to P$ for an [inductive poset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_poset) $P$ admits a least [fixed point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fixed_point), where an inductive poset means a [dcpo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directed_complete_partial_order) with a least element.[^o2][^o3] It was introduced by Dito Pataraia in 1997.[^o4] The theorem is a variant of the [Bourbaki–Witt theorem](wikipedia://en/1011270).

:::
