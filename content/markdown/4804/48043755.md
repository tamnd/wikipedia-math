# Kenji Ueno

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kenji Ueno"}

*Image: Ueno kenji.jpg.* Kenji Ueno, Madrid 2006

**Kenji Ueno** (上野 健爾, Ueno Kenji, 1945, [Kumamoto Prefecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumamoto_Prefecture)) is a Japanese mathematician, specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o2]

:::

He was in the 1970s at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) and was from 1987 to 2009 a professor at the [University of Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kyoto) and is now the director of [Yokkaichi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yokkaichi_University)'s [Seki Kōwa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seki_Kōwa) Institute for Mathematics.[^o2] In 1978 he was an Invited Speaker (*Classification of algebraic manifolds*) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki).[^o3]

Ueno was a visiting professor at several universities, including the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim) in the 1970s.

He is the author and editor of several books on algebraic geometry.[^o4]
