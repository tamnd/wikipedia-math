# Indranil Biswas

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Indranil Biswas"}

**Indranil Biswas** (born 19 October 1964)[^o3] is an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Currently, He is a [senior professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Shiv Nadar University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiv_Nadar_University), [Delhi-NCR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delhi-NCR). He is known for his work in the areas of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), and [deformation quantization](wikipedia://en/4278245).

:::

In 2006, the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) awarded him the [Shanti Swarup Bhatnagar Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanti_Swarup_Bhatnagar_Prize_for_Science_and_Technology) in [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436) for his contributions to "algebraic geometry, centering around [moduli problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_problem) of [vector bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_bundles)."[^o4]

## Biography {#Biography}

Biswas is an Indian citizen. He received a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mumbai).
