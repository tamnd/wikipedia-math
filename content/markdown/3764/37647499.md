# Diamond operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Diamond operator"}

⋄ In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the **diamond operators** 〈*d*〉 are operators acting on the space of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000) for the [congruence subgroup](wikipedia://en/382694) Γ<sub>1</sub>(*N*), given by the action of a matrix (${}^{a b}_{c \delta}$) in Γ<sub>0</sub>(*N*) where δ ≈ d mod N. The diamond operators form an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) and commute with the [Hecke operators](wikipedia://en/521837).

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="⋄"}

## Unicode {#Unicode}

In [Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode), the diamond operator is represented by the character 22C4.[^o4]
