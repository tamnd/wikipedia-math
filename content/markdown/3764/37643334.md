# Parshin chain

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parshin chain"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **Parshin chain** is a higher-dimensional analogue of a [place](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Place_%28mathematics%29) of an [algebraic number field](wikipedia://en/28730822). They were introduced by (Parshin 1978) in order to define an analogue of the [idele class group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idele_class_group) for 2-dimensional [schemes](wikipedia://en/364754).

:::

A Parshin chain of dimension *s* on a scheme is a finite sequence of [points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glossary_of_scheme_theory#Points) *p*<sub>0</sub>, *p*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *p*<sub>*s*</sub> such that *p*<sub>*i*</sub> has dimension *i* and each point is contained in the closure of the next one.
