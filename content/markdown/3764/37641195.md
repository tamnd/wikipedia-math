# SBI ring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SBI ring"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), an **SBI ring** is a type of [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) *R* (with identity) such that every [idempotent](wikipedia://en/361940) of *R* [modulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Modulo_%28jargon%29) the [Jacobson radical](wikipedia://en/57656) can be [lifted](wikipedia://en/10504570) to *R*. The abbreviation SBI was introduced by [Irving Kaplansky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irving_Kaplansky) and stands for "suitable for building idempotent elements".[^n1]

## Examples {#Examples}

- Any ring with [nil](wikipedia://en/3100140) radical is SBI.
- Any [Banach algebra](wikipedia://en/4665) is SBI: more generally, so is any [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [topological ring](wikipedia://en/60097).
- The ring of [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024) with [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) denominator is not SBI.
