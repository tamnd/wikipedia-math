::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="E6 polytope"}

In 6-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 39 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821728) with E<sub>6</sub> symmetry. The two simplest forms are the [2<sub>21</sub>](wikipedia://en/19232473) and [1<sub>22</sub>](wikipedia://en/19070548) polytopes, composed of 27 and 72 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417) respectively.

:::

They can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the E<sub>6</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 39 polytopes can be made in the E<sub>6</sub>, D<sub>5</sub>, D<sub>4</sub>, D<sub>2</sub>, A<sub>5</sub>, A<sub>4</sub>, A<sub>3</sub> [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>k</sub> has *k+1* symmetry, D<sub>k</sub> has *2(k-1)* symmetry, and E<sub>6</sub> has *12* symmetry.

Six symmetry planes graphs are shown for 9 of the 39 polytopes in the E<sub>6</sub> symmetry. The vertices and edges drawn with vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position in progressive order: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, magenta, red-violet.
