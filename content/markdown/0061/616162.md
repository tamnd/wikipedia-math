# Tikhonov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tikhonov"}

**Tikhonov** (Ти́хонов; masculine) and **Tikhonova** (Ти́хонова; feminine) are [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_language) surnames derived from the male given name [Tikhon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tikhon), the Russian form of the Greek name Τύχων (Latin form: Tycho), and literally means 'Tikhon's'. Notable people with the name include:

:::
