# Thompson sporadic group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thompson sporadic group"}

In the area of modern algebra known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Thompson group** *Th* is a [sporadic simple group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sporadic_simple_group) of [order](wikipedia://en/493527)

:::

90,745,943,887,872,000

\= 2<sup>15</sup>3<sup>10</sup>5<sup>3</sup>7<sup>2</sup>131931

≈ 9.

## History {#History}

*Th* is one of the 26 sporadic groups and was found by (txt) and constructed by (Smith 1976). They constructed it as the [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) of a certain lattice in the 248-dimensional Lie algebra of E<sub>8</sub>. It does not preserve the Lie bracket of this lattice, but does preserve the Lie bracket mod 3, so is a subgroup of the [Chevalley group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevalley_group) E<sub>8</sub>(3). The subgroup preserving the Lie bracket (over the integers) is a maximal subgroup of the Thompson group called the [Dempwolff group](wikipedia://en/29678093) (which unlike the Thompson group is a subgroup of the compact Lie group E<sub>8</sub>).

::: {#o6 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Representations"}

## Representations {#Representations}

The centralizer of an element of order 3 of type 3C in the [Monster group](wikipedia://en/47422) is a product of the Thompson group and a group of order 3, as a result of which the Thompson group acts on a [vertex operator algebra](wikipedia://en/1287577) over the field with 3 elements. This vertex operator algebra contains the E<sub>8</sub> Lie algebra over **F**<sub>3</sub>, giving the embedding of *Th* into E<sub>8</sub>(3).

The full normalizer of a 3C element in the Monster group is S<sub>3</sub> × Th, so Th centralizes 3 involutions alongside the 3-cycle. These involutions are centralized by the [Baby monster group](wikipedia://en/324799), which therefore contains Th as a subgroup.

The [Schur multiplier](wikipedia://en/1121587) and the [outer automorphism group](wikipedia://en/628466) of the Thompson group are both trivial.

:::

## Generalized monstrous moonshine {#Generalized_monstrous_moonshine}

Conway and Norton suggested in their 1979 paper that [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) is not limited to the monster, but that similar phenomena may be found for other groups. Larissa Queen and others subsequently found that one can construct the expansions of many Hauptmoduln from simple combinations of dimensions of sporadic groups. For *Th*, the relevant McKay-Thompson series is $T_{3C}(\tau)$ (),

$$T_{3C}(\tau) = \Big(j(3\tau)\Big)^{1/3} = \frac{1}{q}\,+\,248q^2\,+\,4124q^5\,+\,34752q^8\,+\,213126q^{11}\,+\,1057504q^{14}+\cdots\,$$

and *j*(*τ*) is the [j-invariant](wikipedia://en/451988).
