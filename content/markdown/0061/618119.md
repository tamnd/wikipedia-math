# Provability logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Provability logic"}

**Provability logic** is a branch of [proof theory](wikipedia://en/183478) and a [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365), in which the box (or "necessity") operator is interpreted as 'it is provable that'. The point is to capture the notion of a proof predicate of a reasonably rich [formal theory](wikipedia://en/3512103), such as [Peano arithmetic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peano_arithmetic).

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

There are a number of provability logics, some of which are covered in the literature mentioned in . The basic system is generally referred to as **GL** (for [Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736)–[Löb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Hugo_Löb)) or **L** or **K4W** (**W** stands for [well-foundedness](wikipedia://en/319712)). It can be obtained by adding the modal version of [Löb's theorem](wikipedia://en/693002) to the [logic **K**](wikipedia://en/911820) (or **K4**).

Namely, the **axioms** of **GL** are all [tautologies](wikipedia://en/4495335) of classical [propositional logic](wikipedia://en/18154) plus all formulas of one of the following forms:

- **Distribution axiom**: $\square(p \to q) \to (\square p \to \square q);$
- **Löb's axiom**: $\square(\square p \to p) \to \square p.$

And the **rules of inference** are:

- ***Modus ponens***: From *p* → *q* and *p* conclude *q*;
- **Necessitation**: From $\vdash$ *p* conclude $\vdash$ $\square p$.

:::

## History {#History}

[Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736) wrote the first paper on provability logic in 1933.[^o5] In this paper, he introduced translations from intuitionistic propositional logic into modal logic and briefly mentioned that provability could be viewed as a modal operator.[^o6]

The **GL** model was pioneered by [Robert M. Solovay](wikipedia://en/3160110) in 1976. Since then, until his death in 1996, the prime inspirer of the field was [George Boolos](wikipedia://en/340621). Significant contributions to the field have been made by [Sergei N. Artemov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_N._Artemov), Lev Beklemishev, [Giorgi Japaridze](wikipedia://en/1866981), [Dick de Jongh](wikipedia://en/3888928), Franco Montagna, Giovanni Sambin, Vladimir Shavrukov, Albert Visser and others.

## Generalizations {#Generalizations}

[Interpretability logics](wikipedia://en/618086) and [Japaridze's polymodal logic](wikipedia://en/46851461) present natural extensions of provability logic.
