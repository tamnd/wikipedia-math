# KHAZAD

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="KHAZAD"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **KHAZAD** is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) designed by [Paulo S. L. M. Barreto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paulo_S._L._M._Barreto) together with [Vincent Rijmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Rijmen), one of the designers of the [Advanced Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/1260) ([Rijndael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rijndael)). KHAZAD is named after [Khazad-dûm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moria_%28Middle-earth%29), the fictional [dwarven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwarf_%28Middle-earth%29) realm in the writings of [J. R. R. Tolkien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._R._R._Tolkien) (see also [Khazad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khazad)). KHAZAD was presented at the first [NESSIE](wikipedia://en/497430) workshop in 2000, and, after some small changes, was selected as a finalist in the project.

:::

KHAZAD has an eight-round [substitution–permutation network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution–permutation_network) structure similar to that of [SHARK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SHARK_%28cipher%29), a forerunner to Rijndael. The design is classed as a "*legacy-level*" algorithm, with a 64-bit [block size](wikipedia://en/294065) (in common with older [ciphers](wikipedia://en/5244) such as [DES](wikipedia://en/7978) and [IDEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDEA_%28cipher%29)) and a 128-bit [key](wikipedia://en/53039). KHAZAD makes heavy use of [involutions](wikipedia://en/293450) as subcomponents; this minimises the difference between the algorithms for [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) and [decryption](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decryption).

The authors have stated that, *"KHAZAD is not (and will never be) [patented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patent). It may be used free of charge for any purpose."*

Frédéric Muller has discovered an attack which can break five of KHAZAD's eight rounds. No attacks better than this are known as of August 2009.
