# Qin Jiushao

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Qin Jiushao"}

[Qin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Qin_%28surname%29)

*Image: Third order equation-Shu-shu-jiu-zhang.jpg.* Third order equation Yi Jia Tang "Six Book Nine Chapters" positive and negative opening power map

**Qin Jiushao** (, ca. 1202–1261), [courtesy name](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courtesy_name) **Daogu** (道古), was a Chinese mathematician, meteorologist, inventor, politician, and writer of the [Southern Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Song_dynasty#Southern_Song,_1127–1279). He is credited for discovering [Horner's method](wikipedia://en/14263) as well as inventing [Tianchi basins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianchi_basin), a type of rain gauge instrument used to gather meteorological data.[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Although Qin Jiushao was born in [Ziyang, Sichuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ziyang,_Sichuan), his family came from [Shandong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong) province. He is regarded as one of the greatest mathematicians in Chinese history. This is especially remarkable because Qin did not devote his life to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). He was accomplished in many other fields and held a series of bureaucratic positions in several [Chinese provinces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_provinces).

Qin wrote *Shùshū Jiǔzhāng* (*[Mathematical Treatise in Nine Sections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Treatise_in_Nine_Sections)*) in 1247 CE. This treatise covered a variety of topics including indeterminate equations, and the numerical solution of certain polynomial equations up to the tenth [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652). The treatise addressed [surveying](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveying) and military matters. Qin included a general form of the [Chinese remainder theorem](wikipedia://en/7713) that used *Da yan shu* (大衍术) or [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) to solve it. In geometry, he discovered "Qin Jiushao's formula" for finding the area of a triangle from the given lengths of three sides. This formula is the same as [Heron's formula](wikipedia://en/194123), proved by [Heron of Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heron_of_Alexandria) about 60 BCE, though knowledge of the formula may go back to [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844).

As precipitation was important agriculture and food production, Qin developed [rain gauges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rain_gauge) that were widely used in 1247 during the [Mongol Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mongol_Empire)/[Southern Song dynasty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_Song_dynasty) to gather meteorological data. Qin Jiushao later records the application of rainfall measurements in the mathematical treatise. The book also discusses the use of large [snow gauges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snow_gauge) made from bamboo situated in mountain passes and uplands, which are speculated to be first referenced to snow measurement.[^o4][^o5]

Qin recorded the earliest explanation of how [Chinese calendar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_calendar) experts calculated [astronomical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) data according to the timing of the [winter solstice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dongzhi_%28solar_term%29). He also introduced the use of the [zero symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/0_%28number%29) into written [Chinese mathematics](wikipedia://en/1524543). He developed summation of [arithmetic series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_series), in particular the interpretation of a [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) as a nested sequence of arbitrary sums and multiples of a given number. This method of polynomial evaluation is now referred to as [Horner's method](wikipedia://en/14263).

After he completed his work on mathematics, he ventured into politics. As a government official he was boastful, corrupt, and was accused of bribery and of poisoning his enemies. As a result, he was relieved of his duties multiple times. Yet in spite of these problems he managed to become very wealthy.[^o6]
