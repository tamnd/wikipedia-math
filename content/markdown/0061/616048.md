# Andrey Tikhonov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrey Tikhonov"}

**Andrey Nikolayevich Tikhonov** (Андре́й Никола́евич Ти́хонов; 17 October 1906 – 7 October 1993) was a leading [Soviet Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [geophysicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysicist) known for important contributions to [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), and [ill-posed problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ill-posed_problem). He was also one of the inventors of the [magnetotellurics](wikipedia://en/3178175) method in geophysics. Other transliterations of his surname include "Tychonoff", "Tychonov", "Tihonov", "Tichonov".

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Gzhatsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gzhatsk), he studied at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) where he received a Ph.D. in 1927 under the direction of [Pavel Sergeevich Alexandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavel_Sergeevich_Alexandrov). In 1933 he was appointed as a professor at Moscow State University. He became a corresponding member of the [USSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_Academy_of_Sciences) on 29 January 1939 and a [full member](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academician) of the USSR Academy of Sciences on 1 July 1966.

## Research work {#Research_work}

Tikhonov worked in a number of different fields in mathematics. He made important contributions to [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), and certain classes of [ill-posed problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ill-posed_problem). [Tikhonov regularization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tikhonov_regularization), one of the most widely used methods to solve ill-posed [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956), is named in his honor. He is best known for his work on topology, including the [metrization theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrization_theorems) he proved in 1926, and the [Tychonoff's theorem](wikipedia://en/82739), which states that every product of arbitrarily many [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) is again [compact](wikipedia://en/6042). In his honor, [completely regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Completely_regular) topological spaces are also named *[Tychonoff spaces](wikipedia://en/30554)*.

In mathematical physics, he proved the fundamental [uniqueness theorems](wikipedia://en/29759120) for the [heat equation](wikipedia://en/166415)[^o6] and studied [Volterra integral equations](wikipedia://en/2091691).

He founded the theory of [asymptotic analysis](wikipedia://en/641995) for differential equations with small parameter in the leading derivative.[^o7]

## Organizer work {#Organizer_work}

Tikhonov played the leading role in founding the [Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Computational_Mathematics_and_Cybernetics) of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and served as its first dean during the period of 1970–1990.

## Awards {#Awards}

Tikhonov received numerous honors and awards for his work, including the [Lenin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize) (1966) and the [Hero of Socialist Labor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Socialist_Labor) (1954, 1986).
