# Square (cipher)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Square"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **Square** (sometimes written **SQUARE**) is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) invented by [Joan Daemen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Daemen) and [Vincent Rijmen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincent_Rijmen). The design, published in 1997, is a forerunner to [Rijndael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rijndael), which has been adopted as the [Advanced Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/1260). Square was introduced together with a new form of [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) discovered by [Lars Knudsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Ramkilde_Knudsen), called the "[Square attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_attack)".

:::

The structure of Square is a [substitution–permutation network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution–permutation_network) with eight rounds, operating on 128-bit blocks and using a 128-bit [key](wikipedia://en/53039).

Square is not patented.
