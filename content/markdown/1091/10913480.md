*Image: Healthy life expectancy bar chart -world -sex.png.* Life expectancy and healthy life expectancy for males and females

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Brazilian states by life expectancy"}

There are 26 **States of Brazil**, or *Estados* in Portuguese, which are the [federal states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_%28national%29) of [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil), plus the Federal District which holds the capital city, Brasília. The second number in bold corresponds to the map. This and the figures below are based on 2022 estimate data, the life expectancy at birth for women being 81.23 years and for men 74.30, with an average of 77.76 years expected at birth, both sexes combined.

:::
