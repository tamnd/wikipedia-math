# Fermat Prize

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fermat Prize"}

*Image: France\_Toulouse\_Capitole\_Fermat\_072007.jpg.* France, Haute-Garonne (31), Toulouse, Salle des Illustres at the Capitole, Pierre de Fermat.

The **Fermat Prize** of [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research) biennially rewards research works in fields where the contributions of [Pierre de Fermat](wikipedia://en/7576966) have been decisive:

:::

- Statements of [variational principles](wikipedia://en/491097)
- Foundations of [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202)
- [Number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

The spirit of the prize is focused on rewarding the results of research accessible to the greatest number of professional [mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians) within these fields. The Fermat Prize was created in 1989 and is awarded once every two years in [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse) by the [Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse](wikipedia://en/20908760). The amount of the Fermat Prize has been fixed at 20,000 Euros for the twelfth edition (2011).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

## Pierre Fermat medal {#Pierre_Fermat_medal}

There has also been a *Pierre Fermat medal*, which has been awarded for example to chemist [Linus Pauling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linus_Pauling) (1957),[^o27] mathematician [Ernst Peschl](wikipedia://en/40394737) (1965)[^o28] and botanist [Francis Raymond Fosberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Raymond_Fosberg).[^o29]

## Junior Fermat Prize {#Junior_Fermat_Prize}

The Junior Fermat Prize is a mathematical prize, awarded every two years to a student in the first four years of university for a contribution to mathematics. The amount of the prize is 2000 [Euros](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euro).
