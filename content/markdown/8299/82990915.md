# Piecewise isometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Piecewise isometry"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **piecewise isometry** is a [dynamical system](wikipedia://en/9087) that consists of finitely many [Euclidean isometries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_isometry) acting in different places, including [rotations](wikipedia://en/410009), [translations](wikipedia://en/204682), and [reflections](wikipedia://en/295829).[^o2] Piecewise isometries are higher-dimensional generalizations of [interval exchange transformations](wikipedia://en/2606680); the theory has applications in [outer billiards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Outer_billiards), [digital filters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_filter), and [granular mixing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Granular_mixing).[^o2][^o3]

:::
