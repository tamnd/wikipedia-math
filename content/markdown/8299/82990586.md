# 2017 Chilean census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2017 Chilean census"}

Censo chileno de 2017

The **2017 Chilean Population and Housing Census** (Censo Chileno de Población y Vivienda 2017), officially the **XIX National Population Census and VIII Housing Census** (XIX Censo Nacional de Población y VIII de Vivienda), took place on 19 April 2017, and was organized by the [National Statistics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Statistics_Institute_%28Chile%29).

:::

## Census {#Census}

The census took place on 19 April 2017, starting at 08:00 in the morning, and by 10:00 in the morning, according to a joint statement by [Minister of Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Economy,_Development,_and_Tourism_%28Chile%29) [Luis Felipe Céspedes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Luis_Felipe_Céspedes) and the director of the [National Statistics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Statistics_Institute_%28Chile%29) Ximena Clark, over 75% of local people were entered into the system.[^o4] People who were not censed by the end of the day could fill up the questionnaire either in physical form or via a digital platform in later dates.[^o5][^o6]

Multiple attacks against census workers were reported in cities like [Santiago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago) and [Coquimbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coquimbo).[^o7][^o8] The most severe of these was an attack against census workers in [Temucuicui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temucuicui), where the census was suspended due to the attack.[^o9][^o10][^o11] Due to heavy rain in Northern Chile, census data forms related to 72,000 homes in the [Atacama Region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atacama_Region), amounting to 60% of the region's total population and 72% of the region's housing, were damaged, which caused the National Statistics Institute to consider reconducting the census in the affected communes.[^o12][^o13][^o14][^o15]

## Questionnaire {#Questionnaire}

The census questionnaire consisted of 21 questions among four sections; the *A section*, which is filled in by the censist and consists of housing identification, the *B section*, which consists of the data of the housing itself, with the first two questions being filled by the censist, the *C section*, which consists of two questions identifying the people living in the housing, and the *D section*, which asks about the personal information of the residents of the housing.[^o17]

The census was translated into six languages aside from [Spanish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spanish_language): [Mapudungun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mapudungun), [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language), [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language), [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_language), [Portuguese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portuguese_language) and [Haitian Creole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haitian_Creole).[^o18]

## Results {#Results}

In late August 2017, the preliminary results of the census were published, showing a population of 17,574,003 people between 6,499,355 homes (or approximately 2.7 people per home), a 5.6% increase in population since the 2012 census.[^o21]
