# Michael Shulman (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Shulman"}

**Michael** "**Mike**" **Shulman** (; born 1980) is an American professor of mathematics at the [University of San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_San_Diego)[^o3] who works in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013), [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), [logic as applied to set theory](wikipedia://en/19636), and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Shulman did his undergraduate work at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and his postgraduate work at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he received his Ph.D. in 2009.

His doctoral thesis and subsequent work dealt with applications of category theory to homotopy theory.

In 2009, he received a [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Mathematical Sciences Postdoctoral Research Fellowship.[^o5]

In 2012–13, he was a visiting scholar at the Institute for Advanced Study,[^o6] where he was one of the official participants in the *Special Year on Univalent Foundations of Mathematics*.[^o7] Shulman was one of the principal authors of the book *Homotopy type theory: Univalent foundations of mathematics*,[^o8] an informal exposition on the basics of [univalent foundations](wikipedia://en/44554214) and [homotopy type theory](wikipedia://en/33974223).[^o9] In 2014, Shulman was part of a team headed by [Steve Awodey](wikipedia://en/26946267) that was awarded a \$7.5M grant from the [Air Force Research Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Air_Force_Research_Laboratory) for homotopy type theory.[^o10]

## Social views {#Social_views}

Shulman is a [social conservative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_conservatism), opposing the idea that [gender is socially constructed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_construction_of_gender) and the use of [singular they](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_they).[^o12] In April 2022, organizers of the Homotopy Type Theory Electronic Seminar Talks canceled a distinguished lecture series to be given by Shulman due to statements he made being in conflict with the organization's commitment to diversity in the mathematics community.[^o13]

## Blogs {#Blogs}

Shulman is a supporter of using web-based software systems, such as [GitHub](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GitHub), to promote collaborative work by mathematicians—the six-hundred-page *Homotopy type theory* book being a notable example. He is a prolific contributor to the [*n*Lab](wikipedia://en/27226892)[^o15] (and a member of its steering committee[^o16]), and a co-host of the homotopy type theory blog[^o17] and of the [*n*-Category Cafe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Baez#Blogs), a blog focusing on higher category theory,[^o18] as well as a blog about Narya proof assistant.[^o19]
