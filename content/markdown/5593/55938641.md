# Jonathan Rougier (statistician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jonathan Rougier"}

**Jonathan C. Rougier** is professor of [statistical science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_science) at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol).[^o2][^o3] Rougier is a specialist in the assessment of the risk from natural hazards.[^o4]

:::

He was an undergraduate at [University College, Durham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College,_Durham).[^o5] He completed a doctorate at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) in 1996, the thesis being titled *Price change and volume in a speculative market*.[^o6]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- J.C. Rougier, R.S.J. Sparks, and L.J. Hill (eds). *Risk and Uncertainty Assessment for Natural Hazards*. [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press), Cambridge, 2013.
