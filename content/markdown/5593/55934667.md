# Maria Eulália Vares

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maria Eulália Vares"}

**Maria Eulália Vares** is a Brazilian [mathematical statistician](wikipedia://en/888711) and [probability theorist](wikipedia://en/23542) who is known for her expertise in [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895) and [large deviations theory](wikipedia://en/3772909). She is a professor of statistics in the Institute of Mathematics of the [Federal University of Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro), from 2006 to 2009 was the editor-in-chief of the journal *[Stochastic Processes and their Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_Processes_and_their_Applications)*, publisher by [Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier) for the [Bernoulli Society for Mathematical Statistics and Probability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernoulli_Society_for_Mathematical_Statistics_and_Probability), and from 2015 to 2017 was the editor-in-chief of the [Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748),[^o2] published by the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

:::

Vares graduated in 1975 from the [Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul](wikipedia://en/889448) with a bachelor's degree in mathematics. After earning a master's degree in statistics in 1977 from the [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708), she went to the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) for doctoral study in statistics. She completed her Ph.D. in 1980; her dissertation, supervised by P. Warwick Millar, was *On Two Parameter Lévy Processes*.

With Enzo Olivieri, Vares is the author of the book *Large Deviations and Metastability* (Encyclopedia of Mathematics and its Applications 100, Cambridge University Press, 2005).

She is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), and an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).
