# Weak trace-class operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weak trace-class operator"}

In mathematics, a **weak trace class** operator is a [compact operator](wikipedia://en/577441) on a [separable](wikipedia://en/27855) [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H* with [singular values](wikipedia://en/709116) the same order as the [harmonic sequence](wikipedia://en/142488). When the dimension of *H* is infinite, the ideal of weak trace-class operators is strictly larger than the ideal of [trace class operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trace_class_operator), and has fundamentally different properties. The usual [operator trace](wikipedia://en/192266#Definition) on the trace-class operators does not extend to the weak trace class. Instead the ideal of weak trace-class operators admits an infinite number of linearly independent quasi-continuous traces, and it is the smallest two-sided ideal for which all traces on it are [singular traces](wikipedia://en/40083877).

:::

Weak trace-class operators feature in the [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917) of French mathematician [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340).

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A [compact operator](wikipedia://en/577441) *A* on an infinite dimensional [separable](wikipedia://en/27855) [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) *H* is *weak trace class* if μ(*n*,*A*) = O(*n*<sup>−1</sup>), where μ(*A*) is the sequence of [singular values](wikipedia://en/709116). In mathematical notation the two-sided [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of all weak trace-class operators is denoted,

$$L_{1,\infty} = \{ A \in K(H) : \mu(n,A) = O(n^{-1}) \}.$$

where $K(H)$ are the compact operators. The term weak trace-class, or weak-*L*<sub>1</sub>, is used because the operator ideal corresponds, in J. W. Calkin's [correspondence](wikipedia://en/40116557) between two-sided ideals of bounded linear operators and rearrangement invariant sequence spaces, to the [weak-*l*<sub>1</sub> sequence space](wikipedia://en/45194).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- the weak trace-class operators admit a [quasi-norm](wikipedia://en/6652599) defined by

$$\| A \|_{w} = \sup_{n \geq 0} (1+n)\mu(n,A),$$

making *L*<sub>1,∞</sub> a quasi-Banach operator ideal, that is an ideal that is also a [quasi-Banach space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-Banach_space).

:::
