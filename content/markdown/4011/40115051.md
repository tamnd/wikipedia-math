# Khalida Inayat Noor

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Khalida Inayat Noor"}

**Khalida Inayat Noor** is a Pakistani [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is known for work on topics such as [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [variational inequalities](wikipedia://en/1468817), and [integral operators](wikipedia://en/2649421). She was awarded the [Pride of Performance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pride_of_Performance_Awards_%282010-2019%29) by the [President of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_Pakistan) in 2011.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Khalida Noor got her [master's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) and [bachelor's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) from [University of the Punjab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Punjab) in [Lahore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lahore) in 1967 and 1965 respectively. She received her [Master of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Philosophy) in mathematics from [Islamabad University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Islamabad) in 1968 and a Ph.D. from [Wales University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wales) in 1972.[^o6] From 1980 to 1981, she was a student advisor and member of the [Board of Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_of_Studies) at [Islamia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Islamia_University), [Bahawalpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bahawalpur) and from 1982 to 1988 was an *Equivalence Committee* member in [Riyadh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riyadh), [Saudi Arabia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saudi_Arabia).[^o5]

In 2010, she was a member of the editorial board of many international journals of mathematics and engineering sciences, and had as many as 800 research papers published in scientific journals.[^o6] She is also a member of the [Higher Education Commission of Pakistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_Education_Commission_of_Pakistan) and a [Chartered Mathematician](wikipedia://en/1159333) in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).[^o6] Besides those qualifications, she also is a fellow of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139).[^o6] Noor is a professor of mathematics in the [United Arab Emirates University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Arab_Emirates_University).[^o7] In October 1996, one of her papers appeared in the *[Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Mathematics)*.[^o8]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She is a fellow and member of the following Scientific Societies:

- Member, [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), United States[^o5]
- Life Member, Punjab Mathematical Society, Pakistan
- Fellow, [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139), U.K.[^o5]
