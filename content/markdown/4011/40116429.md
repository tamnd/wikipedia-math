# Heptadecahedron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heptadecahedron"}

A **heptadecahedron** (or **heptakaidecahedron**) is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with 17 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926). No heptadecahedron is [regular](wikipedia://en/333299); hence, the name is ambiguous. There are heptadecahedra which are nearly spherical, like those seen in some chemical structures,[^o2][^o3] however their faces are not composed of regular polygons. There also exist heptadecahedra made up of regular polygons, such as the [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491) and [augmented sphenocorona](wikipedia://en/1170930), but their symmetry is low. In addition, there are numerous topologically distinct forms of a heptadecahedron; for example, the [hexadecagonal pyramid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hexadecagonal_pyramid) and [pentadecagonal prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentadecagonal_prism).

:::

In industry, heptadecahedra have many uses. For example, specially-designed heptadecahdra can help reduce empty space and make stacking more convenient.[^o4][^o5]

## Convex heptadecahedra {#Convex_heptadecahedra}

There are 6,415,851,530,241 [topologically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologically) distinct *convex* heptadecahedra, excluding [mirror images](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mirror_image), having at least 11 vertices.[^o7] (Two polyhedra are "topologically distinct" if they have intrinsically different arrangements of faces and vertices, such that it is impossible to distort one into the other simply by changing the lengths of edges or the angles between edges or faces.)

The infinite [Laves graph](wikipedia://en/44557148) has convex heptadecahedral [Voronoi cells](wikipedia://en/177668). Because of the symmetries of the graph, these heptadecahedra are [plesiohedra](wikipedia://en/53396858) and form an isohedral [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) of three-dimensional space.[^o8]

Other convex polyhedra with 17 faces are the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) [pentagonal rotunda](wikipedia://en/1128491), [triaugmented hexagonal prism](wikipedia://en/1177983), and [augmented sphenocorona](wikipedia://en/1170930).[^o9]

## Non-convex heptadecahedra {#Non-convex_heptadecahedra}

There are also many non-convex heptadecahedra, such as star-shaped prisms, cones, or truncated prisms.

## Common heptadecahedra {#Common_heptadecahedra}

### Pentadecagonal prism {#Pentadecagonal_prism}

*Image: Pentadecagonal prism.svg.* Pentadecagonal prism

A pentadecagonal prism is a [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) with a [pentadecagon](wikipedia://en/1496229) base. It consists of 17 faces, 45 edges, and 30 vertices. A regular pentadecagonal prism is one whose faces are all regular polygons. Each vertex is a common vertex of 2 squares and 1 pentadecagon, and its [vertex configuration](wikipedia://en/3603745) is $4{.}4{.}15$, and therefore has the property of being [isogonal](wikipedia://en/581560) and can be classified as a semi-regular heptadecahedron.

In [Schläfli notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli_notation), it can be denoted as {15}×{} or *t*{2,15}, and its [Coxeter-Dynkin diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter-Dynkin_diagram) is given by ; its [Wythoff symbol](wikipedia://en/3691548) is 2 15 $|$ 2, and in [Conway polyhedron notation](wikipedia://en/5021705) it is represented by P15.

If the pentadecagonal prism has base side length $s$ and height $h$, then its volume $V$ and surface area $S$ is given by:

$$V=\frac{15 h s^2 \cot{\frac{\pi}{15}}}{4}\approx 17.6424 h s^2$$

$$S=15s\left(h+\frac{s\cot{\frac{\pi}{15}}}{2}\right)\approx 15s\left(h+2.35232s\right)$$

### Hexadecagonal pyramid {#Hexadecagonal_pyramid}

*Image: Hexadecagonal pyramid.svg.* Hexadecagonal pyramid

A hexadecagonal pyramid is a [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045) with a [hexadecagon](wikipedia://en/6440317) base. It consists of 17 faces, 32 edges, and 17 vertices. Its dual polyhedron is itself. A regular hexadecagonal pyramid is one whose base is a regular hexadecagon. In [Schläfli notation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schläfli_notation), it can be denoted as {}∨{16}.

If the hexadecagonal pyramid has base side length $s$ and height $h$, then its volume $V$ and surface area $S$ is given by:[^o15]

$$V=\frac{4 h s^2 \cot{\frac{\pi}{16}}}{3}\approx 6.70312 h s^2$$

$$S=4s\left(\sqrt{4h^2+s^2\cot^{2}{\frac{\pi}{16}}}+s\cot{\frac{\pi}{16}}\right)\approx 4s\left(\sqrt{4h^2+25.2741s^2}+5.02734s\right)$$

### Elongated octagonal pyramid {#Elongated_octagonal_pyramid}

*Image: Elongated octagonal pyramid.svg.* Elongated octagonal pyramid

An elongated octagonal pyramid is formed by attaching an octagonal prism to an octagonal pyramid. It consists of 17 faces, 32 edges, and 17 vertices.

### Pentagonal rotunda {#Pentagonal_rotunda}

*Image: Green\_pentagonal\_rotunda.svg.* Pentagonal rotunda

A pentagonal rotunda is a [rotunda](wikipedia://en/35582167) with a [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014) as its base. It consists of 17 faces, 35 edges and 20 vertices. Its 17 faces consist of 1 [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014) top, 1 [decagon](wikipedia://en/327393) base, 5 [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014) sides, and 10 [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) sides.

A regular pentagon rotunda is one whose base is a regular pentagon. It has regular pentagons on both its top and side faces. All faces of this polyhedron are [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306). It is therefore a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), and is the only rotunda that belongs in the Johnson group.[^o18]

The symmetry group of the pentagonal rotunda is [C<sub>5v</sub>](wikipedia://en/2922089), and its order is 10.

### Triangular cupolarotunda {#Triangular_cupolarotunda}

A [triangular cupolarotunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_cupolarotunda) is formed by joining a [triangular cupola](wikipedia://en/1128465) and a [triangular rotunda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangular_rotunda) with the base having more edges. It consists of 17 faces, 30 edges, and 15 vertices. Its 17 faces consist of 2 [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) bases, 9 [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) sides, 3 [rectangle](wikipedia://en/70048) sides, and 3 [pentagon](wikipedia://en/20741014) sides.

The triangular cupolarotunda can be either ortho- or gyro- depending on how the cupola and rotunda are joined.
