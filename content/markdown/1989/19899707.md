# Journal of Official Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Official Statistics"}

The ***Journal of Official Statistics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes papers related to [official statistics](wikipedia://en/18646178). It is published by [Statistics Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Sweden), the national statistical office of [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden). The journal was established in 1985, when it replaced the [Swedish language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_language) journal ***Statistisk Tidskrift*** (Statistical Review), which had been published by [Statistics Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Sweden) since 1860 (with a hiatus between 1914 and 1952). It publishes four issues each year.[^o3]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

*Journal of Official Statistics* is indexed in the [Current Index to Statistics](wikipedia://en/13958910).
