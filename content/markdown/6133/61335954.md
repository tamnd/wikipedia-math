# Georg Glaeser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georg Glaeser"}

**Georg Glaeser** is an Austrian mathematician, a professor for [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) at the [University of Applied Arts Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Applied_Arts_Vienna). He has written books on computer graphics and biology in relation to mathematics and geometry.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He studied mathematics and geometry at the [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) from 1973 to 1978 from where he also received his doctor's degree in 1980, advised by Walter Wunderlich. After working as a teacher for mathematics and geometry at a higher technical school, he returned to university. He was visiting professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) from 1986 to 1987 where he worked with [Steve M. Slaby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steve_M._Slaby).[^o5] In 1998 Glaeser obtained his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in computer graphics at the TU Wien and became a full professor at the [University of Applied Arts Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Applied_Arts_Vienna).
