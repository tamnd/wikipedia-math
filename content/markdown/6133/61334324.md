# Emmett Keeler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emmett Keeler"}

**Emmett Brown Keeler** (born September 28, 1941)[^o3] is an American mathematician who works as a senior mathematician at the [RAND Corporation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation). He is also a professor at the [UCLA Fielding School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA_Fielding_School_of_Public_Health) and at the [Pardee RAND Graduate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pardee_RAND_Graduate_School). An elected member of the [National Academy of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Medicine), he is known for his work on the [RAND Health Insurance Experiment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Health_Insurance_Experiment).[^o4]
