# Austrian Statistical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Austrian Statistical Society"}

The **Austrian Statistical Society** (Österreichischen Statistischen Gesellschaft) is a national scientific organization. It publishes the *Austrian Journal of Statistics* (609923675), formerly known as the *Österreichische Zeitschrift für Statistik* (44588411).

:::

## Journal {#Journal}

The official journal of the society is the *Austrian Journal of Statistics* (1026-597X), a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) published under a [Creative Commons Attribution License](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_Attribution_License) (CC-BY).[^o3] It was established in 1972 and covers the use of [statistical methods](wikipedia://en/26685) in all kind of theoretical and applied disciplines. Special emphasis is put on methods and results in [official statistics](wikipedia://en/18646178).

### Editors {#Editors}

The following persons have been [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) of the journal:

- 2014–2016 Matthias Templ
- 2004–2013 Herwig Friedl
- 2002–2012 Rudolf Dutter
