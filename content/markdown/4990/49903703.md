# John Erik Fornæss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Erik Fornæss"}

*Image: John Erik Fornaess.jpg.* John Erik Fornæss

**John Erik Fornæss** (born 14 October 1946, [Hamar, Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamar,_Norway)) is a Norwegian-American mathematician and author. Fornæss earned his master's degree in 1970 from the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) with the thesis *Uniform approximation on manifolds* and earned his PhD in 1974 from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) under Edgar Lee Stout with thesis *Embedding [Strictly Pseudoconvex](wikipedia://en/360243#Strongly_%28Strictly%29_pseudoconvex) Domains in Convex Domains*. At [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1974, he became an instructor, assistant professor in 1976, associate professor in 1978, and a full professor in 1981. Since 1991, he has served as a professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).

:::

He concentrates on the geometry and dynamics of functions of various complex variables in his studies. He developed a Fatou-Julia theory in two complex variables with Nessim Sibony. Additionally, he is recognized for creating a counterexample in [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables) in 1976.[^o3][^o4]

Fornæss is a fellow of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o5] In 2015, he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] Fornæss has an Erdős number of 2.
