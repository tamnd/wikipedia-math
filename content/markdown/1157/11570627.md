# Hans-Volker Niemeier

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans-Volker Niemeier"}

**Hans-Volker Niemeier** is a German mathematician who in 1973 classified the [Niemeier lattices](wikipedia://en/1652072), the even positive definite [unimodular lattices](wikipedia://en/1648605) in 24 dimensions.

:::
