# Alexander Yule Fraser

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Yule Fraser"}

**Alexander (Alec) Yule Fraser** (1857–1890) was a Scottish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), one of the founders of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Fraser studied at the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen)[^n1] where he graduated in 1881. After that, he was appointed Mathematical Master at [George Watson's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Watson's_College) ([Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh)).[^n2] In 1889 he was appointed Headmaster at [Allan Glen's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allan_Glen's_School) ([Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow)) but he remained only two months because a severe [pleurisy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleurisy) forced him to leave Scotland going to [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa) in search of a better climate. Back to Scotland, his health deteriorated rapidly and died in Glasgow thirty three years old.

Alec married the singer [Marjory Kennedy-Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marjory_Kennedy-Fraser) in 1887 and they had a couple, David (1888–1962) and Helen Patuffa (1889–1967). [David Kennedy Fraser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Kennedy_Fraser) became a psychologist, educator and amateur mathematician. Patuffa was a singer and musician, as her mother.

Fraser is mainly remembered for being one of the founding fathers of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940).[^n3]
