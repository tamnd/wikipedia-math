# Monopole (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monopole"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **monopole** is a [connection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connection_%28fiber_bundle%29) over a [principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) *G* with a section of the associated [adjoint bundle](wikipedia://en/9943932).

:::

## Physical interpretation {#Physical_interpretation}

Physically, such a monopole can be interpreted in a [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155) coupled to a [scalar field](wikipedia://en/5173456) as a configuration of the scalar and gauge fields which satisfies the [Bogomolny equations](wikipedia://en/35175454) and has finite action. Due to the presence of a scalar field, this monopole is an example of an ['t Hooft–Polyakov monopole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/'t_Hooft–Polyakov_monopole)[^o3] and should not be confused with the singular [monopole solutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_monopoles) to Maxwell's equations which are mathematically described by nontrivial principal bundles.
