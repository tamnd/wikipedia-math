# Hurwitz scheme

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hurwitz scheme"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Hurwitz scheme** $\mathcal{H}_{d, g}$ is the [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) parametrizing pairs ($C, \pi: C \to \mathbf{P}^1$) where *C* is a smooth [curve](wikipedia://en/253260) of [genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus_of_an_algebraic_curve) *g* and $\pi$ has degree *d*.[^o2]

:::
