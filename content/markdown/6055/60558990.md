# Severi variety (Hilbert scheme)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Severi variety"}

In mathematics, a **Severi variety** is an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808) in a [Hilbert scheme](wikipedia://en/6612581) that parametrizes [curves in projective space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_curve) with given degree and [geometric genus](wikipedia://en/3115807) and at most [node singularities](wikipedia://en/4230456). Its dimension is 3*d* + *g* − 1.

:::

It is a theorem that Severi varieties are [algebraic varieties](wikipedia://en/248808), i.e. irreducible.
