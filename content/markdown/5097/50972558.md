# Fichera's existence principle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fichera's existence principle"}

In mathematics, and particularly in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), **Fichera's existence principle** is an existence and uniqueness theorem for solution of [functional equations](wikipedia://en/1374948), proved by [Gaetano Fichera](wikipedia://en/22175811) in 1954.[^o2] More precisely, given a general [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) $\mathbf{V}$ and two [linear maps](wikipedia://en/18102) from it [onto](wikipedia://en/27873) two [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989), the principle states necessary and sufficient conditions for a [linear transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_transformation) between the two [dual](wikipedia://en/7988#Continuous_dual_space) Banach spaces to be invertible for every vector in $\mathbf{V}$.[^o3]
