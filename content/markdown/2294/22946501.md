# S. L. Hakimi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="S. L. Hakimi"}

**Seifollah Louis Hakimi** (1932–June 23, 2006)[^o2] was an Iranian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in Iran, a professor emeritus at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), where he chaired the department of [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) from 1973 to 1978.[^o3] He was chair of the Department of Electrical Engineering at [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis), from 1986 to 1996.[^o2]

:::

Born in [Mashhad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mashhad), Iran, to an [Iranian Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_Jews) family, Hakimi moved to the United States in the early 1950s.[^o4] He received his Ph.D. from the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign) in 1959, under the supervision of [Mac Van Valkenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mac_Van_Valkenburg). He has over 100 academic descendants, most of them via his student [Narsingh Deo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narsingh_Deo).

He is known for characterizing the [degree sequences](wikipedia://en/1374448) of [undirected graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph),[^o6] for formulating the [Steiner tree problem](wikipedia://en/498304) on networks,[^o7] and for his work on [facility location](wikipedia://en/10469986) problems on networks.[^o8]
