::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stericated 5-simplexes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **stericated 5-simplex** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338) with fourth-order [truncations](wikipedia://en/4224429) ([sterication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterication)) of the regular [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex).

:::

There are six unique sterications of the 5-simplex, including permutations of truncations, cantellations, and runcinations. The simplest stericated 5-simplex is also called an **expanded 5-simplex**, with the first and last nodes ringed, for being constructible by an [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070) operation applied to the regular 5-simplex. The highest form, the *steriruncicantitruncated 5-simplex* is more simply called an omnitruncated 5-simplex with all of the nodes ringed.

A **stericated 5-simplex** can be constructed by an [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070) operation applied to the regular [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex), and thus is also sometimes called an **expanded 5-simplex**. It has 30 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 120 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), 210 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926) (120 [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 90 [squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29)), 180 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29) (60 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) and 120 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584)) and 62 [4-faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-face) (12 [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821), 30 [tetrahedral prisms](wikipedia://en/5977290) and 20 [3-3 duoprisms](wikipedia://en/41745711)).

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Expanded 5-simplex
- Stericated hexateron
- Small cellated dodecateron (Acronym: scad) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Cross-sections {#Cross-sections}

The maximal cross-section of the stericated hexateron with a 4-dimensional hyperplane is a [runcinated 5-cell](wikipedia://en/2386577). This cross-section divides the stericated hexateron into two [pentachoral hypercupolas](wikipedia://en/689743#Hypercupolas) consisting of 6 [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821), 15 [tetrahedral prisms](wikipedia://en/5977290) and 10 [3-3 duoprisms](wikipedia://en/41745711) each.

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *stericated 5-simplex* can be constructed on a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) in 6-space as permutations of (0,1,1,1,1,2). This represents the positive [orthant](wikipedia://en/1283725) [facet](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [stericated 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stericated_6-orthoplex).

A second construction in 6-space, from the center of a [rectified 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_6-orthoplex) is given by coordinate permutations of:

(1,-1,0,0,0,0)

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) in 5-space for the normalized vertices of an origin-centered **stericated hexateron** are:

$$\left(\pm1,\ 0,\ 0,\ 0,\ 0\right)$$

$$\left(0,\ \pm1,\ 0,\ 0,\ 0\right)$$

$$\left(0,\ 0,\ \pm1,\ 0,\ 0\right)$$

$$\left(\pm1/2,\ 0,\ \pm1/2,\ -\sqrt{1/8},\ -\sqrt{3/8}\right)$$

$$\left(\pm1/2,\ 0,\ \pm1/2,\ \sqrt{1/8},\ \sqrt{3/8}\right)$$

$$\left( 0,\ \pm1/2,\ \pm1/2,\ -\sqrt{1/8},\ \sqrt{3/8}\right)$$

$$\left( 0,\ \pm1/2,\ \pm1/2,\ \sqrt{1/8},\ -\sqrt{3/8}\right)$$

$$\left(\pm1/2,\ \pm1/2,\ 0,\ \pm\sqrt{1/2},\ 0\right)$$

### Root system {#Root_system}

Its 30 vertices represent the root vectors of the [simple Lie group](wikipedia://en/292831) A<sub>5</sub>. It is also the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the [5-simplex honeycomb](wikipedia://en/31202395).

### Full snub 5-simplex {#Full_snub_5-simplex}

The **full snub 5-simplex** or **omnisnub 5-simplex**, defined as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the omnitruncated 5-simplex is not uniform, but it can be given Coxeter diagram and [symmetry](wikipedia://en/32088502) 3,3,3,3<sup>\+</sup>, and constructed from 12 [snub 5-cells](wikipedia://en/2386577#Full_snub_5-cell), 30 [snub tetrahedral antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_octahedral_prism#Related_polytopes), 20 [3-3 duoantiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-6_duoprism#Related_polytopes), and 360 irregular [5-cells](wikipedia://en/513821) filling the gaps at the deleted vertices.
