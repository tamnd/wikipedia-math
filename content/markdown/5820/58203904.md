# Yitzchak Ratner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yitzchak Ratner"}

**Yitzchak ben Nechemia Ratner** (יצחק בן נחמיה ראטנער; 1857, [Shklov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shklow), [Russian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Empire) — ?) was a nineteenth-century [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) [maskilic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maskilic) mathematician.[^o2] He wrote mathematical and astronomical articles for various journals, and was the author of *Mishpat Emet* (1884), a criticism of [Lichtenfeld](wikipedia://en/6778745)'s pamphlets against [Slonimski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hayyim_Selig_Slonimski)'s works.[^o3] In 1898 he edited a second edition of Slonimski's *Yesodei Chokmat ha-Shi'ur* on the principles of algebra.

:::
