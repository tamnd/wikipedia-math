# QAMA Calculator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="QAMA Calculator"}

The **QAMA Calculator** is a [calculator](wikipedia://en/7593) that requires users to provide a reasonable estimate of the answer before the precise answer is delivered.[^o2][^o3] QAMA stands for Quick Approximate Mental [Arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118).

:::

Invented by Ilan Samson, it aims to get users to think first by estimating before they get the correct answer.[^o4] [Estimation](wikipedia://en/36687154) is seen by many as an essential part of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and some believe that the presence and popularity of calculators could inhibit the use of estimation skills.[^o5][^o6]

A physical version of the calculator was released for sale in 2014, with [apps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_app) for smartphones and tablets developed in 2016.
