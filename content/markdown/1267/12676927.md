# Václav E. Beneš

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Václav E. Beneš"}

**Václav Edvard "Vic" Beneš** (born July 24, 1930) is a [Czech-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech-American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his contributions to the theory of [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes), [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578) and [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), as well as the design of telecommunications [switches](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switch).

:::

He studied under [John Kemeny](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_George_Kemeny) and gained a doctorate in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (1953) on a treatise on *[Mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636)*.

He then worked for [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs) until 1986,[^o4] contributing to [Kalman filter](wikipedia://en/180855) theory[^o5] as well as the [Beneš network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clos_network#Beneš_network), a [permutation network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Permutation_network) of the [Clos network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clos_network) type. In the 1980s he held a position at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) as well. He has continued to publish independently since 1989.

He was elected [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) (1991) for "contributions to the structure of telephone connecting networks, [stochastic control](wikipedia://en/17058216), and [nonlinear filtering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_filtering)".[^o6] The *Benesfest* was celebrated at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) (2001) to honor his 70th birthday.[^o7] He resides in [Millburn, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Millburn,_New_Jersey) (since 1985) where he has been a long-time [mountain climber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountain_climber)[^o8][^o9] and member of the [American Alpine Club](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Alpine_Club),[^o10] and currently heads the local historical society.[^o11]

He is a relative of the former President of Czechoslovakia [Edvard Beneš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edvard_Beneš) and politician [Vojta Beneš](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vojta_Beneš). [Emilie Benes Brzezinski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emilie_Benes_Brzezinski), a sculptor, was his sister.[^o12]

His first wife Janet was the daughter of [Philip Franklin](wikipedia://en/11458078) and niece of [Norbert Wiener](wikipedia://en/63185).[^o13]
