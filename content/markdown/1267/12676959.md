# Pars destruens and pars construens

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pars destruens and pars construens"}

***Pars destruens*** and ***pars construens*** (Latin) are complementary parts of [argumentation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argumentation). The negative part of [criticizing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criticism) views is the *pars destruens*. And the positive part of stating one's own position and [arguments](wikipedia://en/13018310) is the *pars construens*.

:::

The distinction goes back to [Francis Bacon](wikipedia://en/11319) and his work *[Novum Organum](wikipedia://en/1625306)* (1620). There he puts forth his inductive method that has two parts: a negative part, *pars destruens*, that removes all prejudices and errors; and the positive part, *pars construens*,[^o2] that is about gaining knowledge and truth.
