::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="8-demicubic honeycomb"}

The **8-demicubic honeycomb**, or **demiocteractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 8-space. It is constructed as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the regular [8-cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12677924).

:::

It is composed of two different types of [facets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet_%28mathematics%29). The [8-cubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cube) become alternated into [8-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube) h{4,3,3,3,3,3,3}

*Image: Demiocteract ortho petrie.svg.*

and the alternated vertices create [8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-orthoplex) {3,3,3,3,3,3,4} facets

*Image: Cross graph 8 Nodes highlighted.svg.*

.

## D8 lattice {#D8_lattice}

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the **8-demicubic honeycomb** is the **D<sub>8</sub> lattice**.[^o10] The 112 vertices of the [rectified 8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_8-orthoplex) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the *8-demicubic honeycomb* reflect the [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) 112 of this lattice.[^o11] The best known is 240, from the [E<sub>8</sub> lattice](wikipedia://en/1800359) and the [5<sub>21</sub> honeycomb](wikipedia://en/26842937).

${\tilde{E}}_8$ contains ${\tilde{D}}_8$ as a subgroup of index 270.[^o12] Both ${\tilde{E}}_8$ and ${\tilde{D}}_8$ can be seen as affine extensions of $D_8$ from different nodes:

*Image: Affine D8 E8 relations.png.*

The D+ lattice (also called D2) can be constructed by the union of two D8 lattices.[^o13] This packing is only a lattice for even dimensions. The kissing number is 240. (2<sup>n-1</sup> for n\<8, 240 for n=8, and 2n(n-1) for n>8).[^o14] It is identical to the [E8 lattice](wikipedia://en/1800359). At 8-dimensions, the 240 contacts contain both the 2<sup>7</sup>=128 from lower dimension contact progression (2<sup>n-1</sup>), and 16\*7=112 from higher dimensions (2n(n-1)).

The D\* lattice (also called D4 and C2) can be constructed by the union of all four *D8 lattices*:[^o15] It is also the 7-dimensional [body centered cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_centered_cubic), the union of two [7-cube honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube_honeycomb) in dual positions.

The [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) of the D\* lattice is 16 (*2n* for n≥5).[^o16] and its [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) is a [quadrirectified 8-cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrirectified_8-cubic_honeycomb), , containing all [trirectified 8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_8-orthoplex) [Voronoi cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell), .[^o17]

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are three uniform construction symmetries of this tessellation. Each symmetry can be represented by arrangements of different colors on the 256 [8-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube) facets around each vertex.
