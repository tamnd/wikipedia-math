::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="7-demicubic honeycomb"}

The **7-demicubic honeycomb**, or **demihepteractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 7-space. It is constructed as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the regular [7-cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12677802).

:::

It is composed of two different types of [facets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet_%28mathematics%29). The [7-cubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube) become alternated into [7-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube) h{4,3,3,3,3,3} and the alternated vertices create [7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-orthoplex) {3,3,3,3,3,4} facets.

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="D7 lattice"}

## D7 lattice {#D7_lattice}

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the **7-demicubic honeycomb** is the **D<sub>7</sub> lattice**.[^o10] The 84 vertices of the [rectified 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_7-orthoplex) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the *7-demicubic honeycomb* reflect the [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) 84 of this lattice.[^o11] The best known is 126, from the [E<sub>7</sub> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E7_lattice) and the [3<sub>31</sub> honeycomb](wikipedia://en/19593724).

The D+ packing (also called D2) can be constructed by the union of two *D<sub>7</sub> lattices*. The D+ packings form lattices only in even dimensions. The kissing number is 2<sup>6</sup>=64 (2<sup>n-1</sup> for n\<8, 240 for n=8, and 2n(n-1) for n>8).[^o12]

∪

The D\* lattice (also called D4 and C2) can be constructed by the union of all four 7-demicubic lattices:[^o13] It is also the 7-dimensional [body centered cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_centered_cubic), the union of two [7-cube honeycombs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube_honeycomb) in dual positions.

∪ ∪ ∪ = ∪ .

The [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) of the D\* lattice is 14 (*2n* for n≥5) and its [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) is a [quadritruncated 7-cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadritruncated_7-cubic_honeycomb), , containing all with [tritruncated 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tritruncated_7-orthoplex), [Voronoi cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell).[^o14]

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are three uniform construction symmetries of this tessellation. Each symmetry can be represented by arrangements of different colors on the 128 [7-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube) facets around each vertex.
