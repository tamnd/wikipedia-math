::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="7-cubic honeycomb"}

The **7-cubic honeycomb** or **hepteractic honeycomb** is the only regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 7-space.

:::

It is analogous to the [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) of the plane and to the [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318) of 3-space.

There are many different [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777) of this honeycomb. The most symmetric form is [regular](wikipedia://en/518693), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3<sup>5</sup>,4}. Another form has two alternating [7-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube) facets (like a checkerboard) with Schläfli symbol {4,3<sup>4</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>}. The lowest symmetry Wythoff construction has 128 types of facets around each vertex and a prismatic product Schläfli symbol {∞}<sup>(7)</sup>.

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

The \[4,3<sup>5</sup>,4\], , Coxeter group generates 255 permutations of uniform tessellations, 135 with unique symmetry and 134 with unique geometry. The [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) 7-cubic honeycomb is geometrically identical to the 7-cubic honeycomb.

The *7-cubic honeycomb* can be [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) into the [7-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12677782), replacing the 7-cubes with [7-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube), and the alternated gaps are filled by [7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-orthoplex) facets.

::: {#o8 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Quadritruncated 7-cubic honeycomb"}

### Quadritruncated 7-cubic honeycomb {#Quadritruncated_7-cubic_honeycomb}

A **quadritruncated 7-cubic honeycomb**, , contains all [tritruncated 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tritruncated_7-orthoplex) facets and is the [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) of the [D<sub>7</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube_honeycomb#D7_lattice). Facets can be identically colored from a doubled ${\tilde{C}}_7$×2, \[\[4,3<sup>5</sup>,4\] symmetry, alternately colored from ${\tilde{C}}_7$, \[4,3<sup>5</sup>,4\] symmetry, three colors from ${\tilde{B}}_7$, \[4,3<sup>4</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry, and 4 colors from ${\tilde{D}}_7$, \[3<sup>1,1</sup>,3<sup>3</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry.

:::
