# Paul Monsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Monsky"}

**Paul Monsky** (born June 17, 1936) is an American mathematician and [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of Mathematics at [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University).

:::

After earning a bachelor's degree from [Swarthmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarthmore_College), he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1962 from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) under the supervision of [Walter Lewis Baily, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walter_Lewis_Baily,_Jr.) He has introduced the [Monsky–Washnitzer cohomology](wikipedia://en/26005931) and he has worked intensively on [Hilbert–Kunz functions](wikipedia://en/43263796) and Hilbert–Kunz multiplicity. In 2007, Monsky and Holger Brenner gave an example showing that [tight closure](wikipedia://en/6921893) does not commute with localization.[^o4]

The first proof of [Monsky's theorem](wikipedia://en/35098575), published in 1970, which states that a square cannot be divided into an odd number of [equal-area triangles](wikipedia://en/36643448), is named after him.[^o5]

In the mid-1970s, Monsky stopped paying U.S. federal income tax in protest against military spending. He resisted income tax withholding by claiming extra exemptions, and this led to a criminal conviction on tax charges in 1980.[^o6]
