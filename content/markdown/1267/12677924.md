::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="8-cubic honeycomb"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **8-cubic honeycomb** or **octeractic honeycomb** is the only regular space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean](wikipedia://en/9697) 8-space.

:::

It is analogous to the [square tiling](wikipedia://en/2863809) of the plane and to the [cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3733318) of 3-space, and the [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139) of 4-space.

There are many different [Wythoff constructions](wikipedia://en/2950777) of this honeycomb. The most symmetric form is [regular](wikipedia://en/518693), with [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3<sup>6</sup>,4}. Another form has two alternating [hypercube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octeract) facets (like a [checkerboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Checkerboard)) with Schläfli symbol {4,3<sup>5</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>}. The lowest symmetry Wythoff construction has 256 types of [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) around each vertex and a prismatic product Schläfli symbol {∞}<sup>(8)</sup>.

## Related honeycombs {#Related_honeycombs}

The \[4,3<sup>6</sup>,4\], , Coxeter group generates 511 permutations of [uniform tessellations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_honeycomb), 271 with unique symmetry and 270 with unique geometry. The [expanded](wikipedia://en/6341070) 8-cubic honeycomb is geometrically identical to the 8-cubic honeycomb.

The *8-cubic honeycomb* can be [alternated](wikipedia://en/4349117) into the [8-demicubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/12677988), replacing the 8-cubes with [8-demicubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube), and the alternated gaps are filled by [8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-orthoplex) facets.

::: {#o8 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Quadrirectified 8-cubic honeycomb"}

### Quadrirectified 8-cubic honeycomb {#Quadrirectified_8-cubic_honeycomb}

A **quadrirectified 8-cubic honeycomb**, , contains all [trirectified 8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trirectified_8-orthoplex) facets and is the [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) of the [D<sub>8</sub><sup>\*</sup> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube_honeycomb#D8_lattice). Facets can be identically colored from a doubled ${\tilde{C}}_8$×2, \[\[4,3<sup>6</sup>,4\]\] symmetry, alternately colored from ${\tilde{C}}_8$, \[4,3<sup>6</sup>,4\] symmetry, three colors from ${\tilde{B}}_8$, \[4,3<sup>5</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry, and 4 colors from ${\tilde{D}}_8$, \[3<sup>1,1</sup>,3<sup>4</sup>,3<sup>1,1</sup>\] symmetry.

:::
