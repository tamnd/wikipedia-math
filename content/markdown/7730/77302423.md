# Maarten Solleveld

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Maarten Solleveld"}

**Maarten Solleveld** is a Dutch [chess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess) grandmaster and mathematician.

:::

## Chess career {#Chess_career}

He won the 2000 Gouda Open ahead of IM Johan van Mil.[^o5]

He achieved his GM norms at the:[^o2]

- Dutch Team Championship in June 2003
- North Sea Cup in July 2005
- Dutch Team Competition in April 2012

He has been inactive in chess since June 2017.

## Mathematics career {#Mathematics_career}

He obtained his PhD in mathematics from the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in 2007 under Eric Opdam.[^o7] He has held teaching positions at University of Amsterdam, [Hausdorff Center for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/23690221), and [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).[^o8] In 2011, he became a professor of mathematics at [Radboud University Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen).[^o9]
