# 1976 Canadian census

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="1976 Canadian census"}

The **1976 Canadian census** was a detailed enumeration of the [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) population. Census day was June 1, 1976. On that day, Statistics Canada attempted to count every person in Canada. The total population count of Canada was 22,992,604. This was a 6.6% increase over the 1971 census of 21,568,311.[^o3]

:::

The previous census was the [1971 census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1971_Canadian_census) and the following census was in [1981 census](wikipedia://en/75010843).
