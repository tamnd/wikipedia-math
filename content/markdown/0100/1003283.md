# Olinde Rodrigues

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olinde Rodrigues"}

**Benjamin Olinde Rodrigues** (6 October 1795 – 17 December 1851), more commonly known as **Olinde Rodrigues**, was a French banker, [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and [social reformer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_movement). In mathematics Rodrigues is remembered for [Rodrigues' rotation formula](wikipedia://en/1525933) for vectors, the [Rodrigues formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rodrigues_formula) for the [Legendre polynomials](wikipedia://en/100349), and the [Euler–Rodrigues parameters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler–Rodrigues_parameters).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Rodrigues was born into a well-to-do [Sephardi Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sephardi_Jew) family in [Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bordeaux). His family was of Portuguese-Jewish descent.[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9] He was awarded a [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in mathematics on 28 June 1815 by the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris).[^o10] His dissertation contains the result now called [Rodrigues' formula](wikipedia://en/1003242).[^o11]

After graduation, Rodrigues became a banker. A close associate of the [Comte de Saint-Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Henri_de_Rouvroy,_comte_de_Saint-Simon), Rodrigues continued, after Saint-Simon's death in 1825, to champion the older man's [socialist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socialism) ideals, a school of thought that came to be known as [Saint-Simonianism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Simonianism). During this period, Rodrigues published writings on politics, social reform, and banking.

Rodrigues' 1840 paper developed new results on [transformation groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_group).[^o12] It uses three numbers to parameterize the entries of a rotation matrix using only rational functions. When converted to four parameters, this representation is equivalent to a [unit quaternion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_quaternion), and describes the axis and angle of a rotation. In addition, he applied [spherical trigonometry](wikipedia://en/650405) to relate changes in rotation axis and angle due to the composition of two rotations. This formula is a precursor to the [quaternion](wikipedia://en/51440) product of [William Rowan Hamilton](wikipedia://en/39817).[^o13][^o14] In 1846, [Arthur Cayley](wikipedia://en/311034) acknowledged[^o15] Euler's and Rodrigues' priority describing [orthogonal transformations](wikipedia://en/1382381).

Rodrigues is credited as originating the idea of the artist as an [avant-garde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Avant-garde).[^o16]
