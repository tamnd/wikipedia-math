# Nikolai Brashman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolai Brashman"}

**Nikolai Dmitrievich Brashman** (Никола́й Дми́триевич Брáшман; Nikolaus Braschmann; June 14, 1796 – 25 May 1866) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) of [Jewish-Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish-Austrian) origin. He was a student of [Joseph Johann Littrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Johann_Littrow), and the advisor of [Pafnuty Chebyshev](wikipedia://en/149568) and [August Davidov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Davidov).

:::

He was born in Neu-Raußnitz (today [Rousínov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rousínov) in [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic), then in [Austrian Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Empire)) and studied at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) and [Vienna Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology). In 1824 he moved to [Saint Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg) and then accepted a position at the [Kazan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan_State_University). In 1834 he became a professor of applied mathematics at the [Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). There he is best remembered as a founder of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251) and its journal *[Matematicheskii Sbornik](wikipedia://en/11555472)*.[^o5]

For his mechanics textbook, in 1836 Brashman was awarded the [Demidov Prize](wikipedia://en/6905037) by the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences). The academy elected him a corresponding member in 1855. He died in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) in 1866.
