# Laura Monk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Laura Monk"}

**Laura Monk** is a French mathematician whose research in [spectral geometry](wikipedia://en/20635289), on the [expansion](wikipedia://en/9313) and [spectral](wikipedia://en/34878672) properties of random [hyperbolic surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_surface), continues the work of [Maryam Mirzakhani](wikipedia://en/6409655). She works in England at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) as a Royal Society Dorothy Hodgkin Research Fellow and proleptic (tenure-track) [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_the_United_Kingdom).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After preparatory studies at the [Lycée privé Sainte-Geneviève](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_privé_Sainte-Geneviève), Monk received bachelor's degrees in both mathematics and physics at [Paris-Sud University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Sud_University) in 2015, passed her [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in mathematics in 2017, and received a master's degree with honors in 2018, also including study at the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure). She completed her Ph.D. in 2021 at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg), with the dissertation *Geometry and spectrum of typical hyperbolic surfaces* supervised by [Nalini Anantharaman](wikipedia://en/41980659).

She was a postdoctoral fellow at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960), working there with [Ursula Hamenstädt](wikipedia://en/44771577), from 2021 to 2022. She joined the University of Bristol in 2022 as a research associate of [Jens Marklof](wikipedia://en/29345557). She became a research fellow and proleptic lecturer in 2024.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

As a doctoral student, Monk was named a French Young Talent in the 2021 [L'Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Oréal-UNESCO_For_Women_in_Science_Awards). She was a 2024 recipient of the [Maryam Mirzakhani New Frontiers Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryam_Mirzakhani_New_Frontiers_Prize), given to her "for advancing our understanding of random hyperbolic surfaces of large genus".

The [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) awarded Monk a Dorothy Hodgkin Fellowship in 2024.
