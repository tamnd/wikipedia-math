# Comparison theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Comparison theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **comparison theorems** are theorems whose statement involves comparisons between various mathematical objects of the same type, and often occur in fields such as [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176), [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243).

:::

## Differential equations {#Differential_equations}

In the theory of [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), **comparison theorems** assert particular properties of solutions of a differential equation (or of a system thereof), provided that an auxiliary equation/inequality (or a system thereof) possesses a certain property. Differential (or integral) inequalities, derived from differential (respectively, integral) equations by replacing the equality sign with an inequality sign, form a broad class of such auxiliary relations.[^o3][^o4]

One instance of such theorem was used by Aronson and Weinberger to characterize solutions of [Fisher's equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fisher's_equation), a reaction-diffusion equation.[^o5] Other examples of comparison theorems include:

- [Chaplygin's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chaplygin's_theorem)
- [Grönwall's inequality](wikipedia://en/1770447), and its various generalizations, provides a comparison principle for the solutions of first-order ordinary differential equations
- [Lyapunov comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lyapunov_comparison_theorem)
- [Sturm comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sturm_comparison_theorem)
- [Hille-Wintner comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hille-Wintner_comparison_theorem)

## Riemannian geometry {#Riemannian_geometry}

In [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/195243), it is a traditional name for a number of theorems that compare various metrics and provide various estimates in Riemannian geometry. [^o7]

- [Rauch comparison theorem](wikipedia://en/16221737) relates the [sectional curvature](wikipedia://en/285618) of a [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) to the rate at which its [geodesics](wikipedia://en/91096) spread apart
- [Toponogov's theorem](wikipedia://en/19848340)
- [Myers's theorem](wikipedia://en/932460)
- [Hessian comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hessian_comparison_theorem)
- [Laplacian comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laplacian_comparison_theorem)
- [Morse&ndash;Schoenberg comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Morse&ndash;Schoenberg_comparison_theorem)
- [Berger comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berger_comparison_theorem), [Rauch&ndash;Berger comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rauch&ndash;Berger_comparison_theorem)[^o8]
- [Berger&ndash;Kazdan comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berger&ndash;Kazdan_comparison_theorem)
- [Warner comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warner_comparison_theorem) for [lengths](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Length_%28vector_field%29) of [N-Jacobi fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N-Jacobi_field) (*N* being a submanifold of a complete Riemannian manifold)[^o10]
- [Bishop–Gromov inequality](wikipedia://en/929870), conditional on a lower bound for the [Ricci curvatures](wikipedia://en/276582)[^o11]
- [Lichnerowicz comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lichnerowicz_comparison_theorem)
- [Eigenvalue comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue_comparison_theorem)
  - [Cheng's eigenvalue comparison theorem](wikipedia://en/23636044)
- [Comparison triangle](wikipedia://en/5845706)

## Between algebraic and analytic geometry {#Between_algebraic_and_analytic_geometry}

There exist various comparisons between [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202) often known as [GAGA theorems](wikipedia://en/630505) due to the abbreviation of [Serre's](wikipedia://en/205483) foundational article *Géométrie Algébrique et Géométrie Analytique*[^o13].

- [Algebraic and analytic geometry over the complex numbers (GAGA)](wikipedia://en/630505)
- [Artin comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artin_comparison_theorem) - identifies the [Étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) of [finite type schemes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_type_scheme) and the [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of their analytic counterparts.
