# Locally finite measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Locally finite measure"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **locally finite measure** is a [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) for which every point of the [measure space](wikipedia://en/45270) has a [neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/1529485) of [finite](wikipedia://en/11742) measure.[^o2][^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $(X, T)$ be a [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) and let $\Sigma$ be a [$\sigma$-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma_algebra) on $X$ that contains the topology $T$ (so that every [open set](wikipedia://en/39358) is a [measurable set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measurable_set), and $\Sigma$ is at least as fine as the [Borel $\sigma$-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borel_sigma_algebra) on $X$). A measure/[signed measure](wikipedia://en/2985811)/[complex measure](wikipedia://en/3057532) $\mu$ defined on $\Sigma$ is called **locally finite** if, for every point $p$ of the space $X,$ there is an open [neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/1529485) $N_p$ of $p$ such that the $\mu$-measure of $N_p$ is finite.

In more condensed notation, $\mu$ is locally finite [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922)

$$\text{for all } p \in X, \text{ there exists } N_p \in T \mbox{ such that } p \in N_p \mbox{ and } \left|\mu\left(N_p\right)\right| < + \infty.$$

:::

::: {#o7 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

1. Any [probability measure](wikipedia://en/45437) on $X$ is locally finite, since it assigns unit measure to the whole space. Similarly, any measure that assigns finite measure to the whole space is locally finite.
1. [Lebesgue measure](wikipedia://en/18198) on [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) is locally finite.
1. By definition, any [Radon measure](wikipedia://en/521933) is locally finite.
1. The [counting measure](wikipedia://en/191786) is sometimes locally finite and sometimes not: the counting measure on the [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) with their usual [discrete topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_topology) is locally finite, but the counting measure on the [real line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_line) with its usual Borel [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) is not.

:::
