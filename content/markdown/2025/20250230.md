# Low and high hierarchies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Low and high hierarchies"}

In the [computational complexity theory](wikipedia://en/7543), the **low hierarchy** and **high hierarchy** of complexity levels were introduced in 1983 by [Uwe Schöning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uwe_Schöning) to describe the internal structure of the [complexity class NP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity_class_NP).[^o2] The low hierarchy starts from [complexity class P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complexity_class_P) and grows "upwards", while the high hierarchy starts from class NP and grows "downwards".[^o3]

:::

Later these hierarchies were extended to sets outside NP.

The framework of high/low hierarchies makes sense only under the assumption that [P is not NP](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P_is_not_NP). On the other hand, if the low hierarchy consists of at least two levels, then P is not NP.[^o4]

It is not known whether these hierarchies cover all NP.
