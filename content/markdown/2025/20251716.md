# Yuri Ofman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Ofman"}

**Yuri Petrovich Ofman** (Ю́рий Петро́вич Офман, born 1939) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in [computational complexity theory](wikipedia://en/7543).

:::

He obtained his [Doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), where he was advised by [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161).[^o3] He did important early work on [parallel algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_algorithm) for [prefix sums](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prefix_sum) and their application in the design of [Boolean circuits for addition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adder_%28electronics%29).

## Publications {#Publications}

- (1963). "О приближенной реализации непрерывных функций на автоматах". *Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR*. 152 (4). p. 823–826
- (1962). "Об алгоритмической сложности дискретных функций". *Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR*. 145 (1). p. 48–51 Translated in "none". *Soviet Physics Doklady*. 7. p. 589
- Anatolii A. Karatsuba and Yu. P. Ofman (1962), "Умножение многозначных чисел на автоматах" ("Multiplication of Many-Digital Numbers by Automatic Computers"), *Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR*, vol. 146, pages 293–294. (Published by A. N. Kolmogorov, with two separate results by the two authors.[^o8])
- Yu. P. Ofman (1965), "A universal automaton". *Transactions of the Moscow Mathemathematical Society*, volume 14, pages 200–215.
