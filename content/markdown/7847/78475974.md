# Michael Hallett

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Hallett"}

**Michael Frank Hallett** is a British philosopher and John Frothingham Professor of Logic and Metaphysics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University). He is known for his works on [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Books {#Books}

- Hallett, Michael. (1984). "Cantorian Set Theory and Limitation of Size". Clarendon Press. isbn:0-19-853179-6.
