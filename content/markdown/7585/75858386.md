# Sumudu transform

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sumudu transform"}

The **Sumudu transform** is an [integral transform](wikipedia://en/430912) introduced in 1990 by G K Watagala.[^o2][^o3][^o4] It is defined over the set of functions [^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::

$$A = \{f(t) : \ni M, p ,q> 0 , |f(t)|\leq M \exp(1/u) \}$$

where $p \leq u \leq q$, the Sumudu transform is defined as

$$S[f(t)]= \frac 1 u \int_0^\infty f(t) \exp\left(-\frac tu\right) \, dt$$

## Relationship with other transforms {#Relationship_with_other_transforms}

Sumudu transform is related to [Laplace transform](wikipedia://en/18610) by the following relation: $S[f(t)](u)=\frac{1}{u} L[f(t)](\frac{1}{u})$; and to Elzaki transform by the following relation: $u^2S[f(t)](u)= E[f(t)](u)$.
