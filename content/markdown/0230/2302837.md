# Lehmer sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lehmer sequence"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Lehmer sequence** $U_n(\sqrt R, Q)$ or $V_n(\sqrt R, Q)$ is a generalization of a [Lucas sequence](wikipedia://en/734635) $U_n(P, Q)$ or $V_n(P, Q)$, allowing the square root of an integer *R* in place of the integer *P*.[^o2]

:::

To ensure that the value is always an integer, every other term of a Lehmer sequence is divided by $\sqrt{R}$ compared to the corresponding Lucas sequence. That is, when *R* = *P*<sup>2</sup> the Lehmer and Lucas sequences are related as:

$$\begin{aligned} P\,U_{2n}(\sqrt{P^2},Q) &= U_{2n}(P,Q) & U_{2n+1}(\sqrt{P^2},Q) &= U_{2n+1}(P,Q) \\ V_{2n}(\sqrt{P^2},Q) &= V_{2n}(P,Q) & P\,V_{2n+1}(\sqrt{P^2},Q) &= V_{2n+1}(P,Q) \end{aligned}$$

## Algebraic relations {#Algebraic_relations}

If *a* and *b* are [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) with

$$a + b = \sqrt{R}$$

$$ab = Q$$

under the following conditions:

- *Q* and *R* are [relatively prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relatively_prime) nonzero [integers](wikipedia://en/14563)
- $a/b$ is not a [root of unity](wikipedia://en/171950).

Then, the corresponding Lehmer numbers are:

$$U_n(\sqrt{R},Q) = \frac{a^n-b^n}{a-b}$$

for *n* [odd](wikipedia://en/143135), and

$$U_n(\sqrt{R},Q) = \frac{a^n-b^n}{a^2-b^2}$$

for *n* [even](wikipedia://en/143135).

Their companion numbers are:

$$V_n(\sqrt{R},Q) = \frac{a^n+b^n}{a+b}$$

for *n* odd and

$$V_n(\sqrt{R},Q) = a^n+b^n$$

for *n* even.

## Recurrence {#Recurrence}

Lehmer numbers form a linear [recurrence relation](wikipedia://en/146806) with

$$U_n = (R-2Q)U_{n-2}-Q^2U_{n-4} = (a^2+b^2)U_{n-2}-a^2b^2U_{n-4}$$

with initial values $U_0=0,\, U_1=1,\, U_2=1,\, U_3=R-Q=a^2+ab+b^2$. Similarly the companion sequence satisfies

$$V_n = (R-2Q)V_{n-2}-Q^2V_{n-4} = (a^2+b^2)V_{n-2}-a^2b^2V_{n-4}$$

with initial values $V_0=2,\, V_1=1,\, V_2=R-2Q=a^2+b^2,\, V_3=R-3Q=a^2-ab+b^2.$

All Lucas sequence recurrences apply to Lehmer sequences if they are divided into cases for even and odd *n* and appropriate factors of $\sqrt{R}$ are incorporated. For example,

$$\begin{aligned} U_{2n}(\sqrt R,Q) &= \phantom{R\,} U_{2n-1}(\sqrt R,Q) - Q\, U_{2n-2}(\sqrt R,Q) & U_{2n+1}(\sqrt R,Q) &= R\, U_{2n}(\sqrt R,Q) - Q\, U_{2n-1}(\sqrt R,Q) \\ V_{2n}(\sqrt R,Q) &= R\, V_{2n-1}(\sqrt R,Q) - Q\, V_{2n-2}(\sqrt R,Q) & V_{2n+1}(\sqrt R,Q) &= \phantom{R\,} V_{2n}(\sqrt R,Q) - Q\, V_{2n-1}(\sqrt R,Q) \end{aligned}$$
