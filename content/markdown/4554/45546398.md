# Wallis Professor of Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wallis Professor of Mathematics"}

*Image: John Wallis by Sir Godfrey Kneller, Bt.jpg.* [John Wallis](wikipedia://en/239290)

The **Wallis Professorship of Mathematics** is a chair in the Mathematical Institute of the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). It was established in 1969 in honour of [John Wallis](wikipedia://en/239290), who was [Savilian Professor of Geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Savilian_Professor_of_Geometry) at Oxford from 1649 to 1703.

:::

## List of Wallis Professors of Mathematics {#List_of_Wallis_Professors_of_Mathematics}

- 1969 to 1985: [John Kingman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Kingman)[^o3]
- 1985 to 1997: [Simon Donaldson](wikipedia://en/976305)[^o4]
- 1999 to 2022: [Terence Lyons](wikipedia://en/31709329)[^o4]
- 2022 to date: Massimiliano Gubinelli[^o5]
