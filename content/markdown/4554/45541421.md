# PC1 cipher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="PC1 cipher"}

The **PC1 cipher**, also called the **Kindle cipher** or **Pukall cipher 1**, is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) introduced in 1991. It is most prominently used by [Amazon, Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon,_Inc.), for their [Kindle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_Kindle) e-book reader's [DRM](wikipedia://en/18938226) system.[^o2]

:::

## History {#History}

The PC1 cipher was designed by Alexander Pukall in 1991.[^o4][^o5]

## Successors {#Successors}

Caracachs Cipher formerly known as PC3 Cipher was released in 2000.[^o7] This algorithm was used by the North Korean hacker group [Lazarus Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lazarus_Group). [^o8]

PC4 was released in 2015. It's a block cipher specifically designed for DMR radio communication systems. It uses 253 rounds and the key size can vary from 8 bits to 2112 bits. The block size is 49 bits, the exact size of an AMBE+ DMR voiceframe.[^o9]
