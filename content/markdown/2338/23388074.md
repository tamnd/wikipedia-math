# Susskind–Glogower operator

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susskind–Glogower operator"}

The **Susskind–Glogower operator**, first proposed by [Leonard Susskind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Susskind) and Jonathan Glogower,[^o2] refers to the operator where the phase is introduced as an approximate polar decomposition of the [creation and annihilation operators](wikipedia://en/701991).

:::

It is defined as

$$V=\frac{1}{\sqrt{aa^{\dagger}}}a,$$

and its adjoint

$$V^{\dagger}=a^{\dagger}\frac{1}{\sqrt{aa^{\dagger}}}.$$

Their [commutation relation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutation_relation) is

$$[V,V^{\dagger}]=|0\rangle\langle 0|,$$

where $|0\rangle$ is the vacuum state of the [harmonic oscillator](wikipedia://en/13899).

They may be regarded as a (exponential of) [phase operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phase_operator) because

$$Va^{\dagger}a V^{\dagger}=a^{\dagger}a+1,$$

where $a^{\dagger}a$ is the number operator. So the exponential of the phase operator displaces the [number operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_operator) in the same fashion as the [momentum operator](wikipedia://en/2288549) acts as the generator of [translations in quantum mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Translation_operator_%28quantum_mechanics%29): $\exp\left(i\frac{\hat{p}x_o}{\hbar}\right)\hat{x}\exp\left(-i\frac{\hat{p}x_o}{\hbar}\right)=\hat{x}+x_0$.

They may be used to solve problems such as atom-field interactions,[^o3] level-crossings [^o4] or to define some class of [non-linear coherent states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-linear_coherent_states),[^o5] among others.
