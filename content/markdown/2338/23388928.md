# Cayley's ruled cubic surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cayley's ruled cubic surface"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), **Cayley's ruled cubic surface** is the [ruled](wikipedia://en/705158) [cubic surface](wikipedia://en/526126)

:::

$$z=xy-x^3/3 \ .$$

In projective coordinates it is $zw^2 = xyw -x^3/3\$.

It contains a (double) line of self-intersection $x=w=0\$and two [pinch points](wikipedia://en/19426773).[^o2]
