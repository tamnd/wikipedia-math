# Stefan Grigorievich Samko

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefan Grigorievich Samko"}

**Stefan Grigorievich Samko** (Стефан Григорьевич Самко; born March 28, 1941) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) active in the field of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [function spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Function_spaces) and [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675). He is a retired professor of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Algarve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algarve_University) and [Rostov State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostov_State_University).[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

### Research activity {#Research_activity}

S. Samko has more than 260 research papers spread throughout the areas of,[^o6][^o7]

- Harmonic Analysis and Operator Theory in Variable Exponent Function Spaces;
- Function spaces;
- Potential type operators;
- Hypersingualr integrals and the method of approximative inverse operators;
- Fractional calculus of one and many variables;
- Integral equations of the first kind (including multi-dimensional ones).
