# Theory of Lie groups

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theory of Lie groups"}

In mathematics, ***Theory of Lie groups*** is a series of books on [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) by (txt). The first in the series was one of the earliest books on Lie groups to treat them from the global point of view, and for many years was the standard text on Lie groups. The second and third volumes, on [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924) and [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944), were written in French, and later reprinted bound together as one volume. Apparently further volumes were planned but not published, though his lectures (Chevalley 2005) on the classification of [semisimple algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_algebraic_group) could be considered as a continuation of the series.

:::
