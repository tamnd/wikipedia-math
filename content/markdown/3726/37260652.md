# Mumford measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mumford measure"}

In mathematics, a **Mumford measure** is a [measure](wikipedia://en/19873) on a [supermanifold](wikipedia://en/891255) constructed from a bundle of relative dimension 1|1. It is named for [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594).

:::
