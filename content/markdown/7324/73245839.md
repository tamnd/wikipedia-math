# Bogomolov–Sommese vanishing theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bogomolov–Sommese vanishing theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Bogomolov–Sommese vanishing theorem** is a result related to the [Kodaira–Itaka dimension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kodaira–Itaka_dimension). It is named after [Fedor Bogomolov](wikipedia://en/8823369) and [Andrew Sommese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Sommese). Its statement has differing versions:

:::

**Bogomolov–Sommese vanishing theorem for snc pair**:[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5] Let *X* be a projective manifold (smooth [projective variety](wikipedia://en/320469)), *D* a simple [normal crossing divisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_crossing_divisor) (snc divisor) and $A \subseteq \Omega ^{p} _ {X} (\log D)$ an [invertible subsheaf](wikipedia://en/581859). Then the Kodaira–Itaka dimension $\kappa(A)$ is not greater than *p*.

This result is equivalent to the statement that:[^o6]

$$H^{0}\left(X,A^{- 1} \otimes \Omega ^{p}_{X} (\log D) \right) = 0$$

for every complex projective snc pair $(X, D)$ and every [invertible sheaf](wikipedia://en/581859#The_Picard_group) $A \in \mathrm{Pic}(X)$ with $\kappa(A) > p$.

Therefore, this theorem is called the vanishing theorem.

**Bogomolov–Sommese vanishing theorem for lc pair**:[^o7][^o8] Let (X,D) be a [log canonical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Log_canonical) pair, where X is projective. If $A \subseteq\Omega ^{[p]}_{X} (\log \lfloor D \rfloor)$ is a $\mathbb{Q}$-Cartier [reflexive subsheaf](wikipedia://en/45159116) of rank one,[^o9] then $\kappa(A) \leq p$.
