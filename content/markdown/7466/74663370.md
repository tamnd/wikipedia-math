# Ignacas Stasys Uždavinys

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ignacas Stasys Uždavinys"}

**Ignacas Stasys Uždavinys** (30 June 1935 – 2 August 2023) was a Lithuanian mathematician and politician. A member of the [Lithuanian Christian Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Christian_Democratic_Party), he served in the [Seimas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seimas) from 1992 to 2000.[^o3]

:::

Uždavinys died on 2 August 2023, at the age of 88.[^o4]
