# Spherics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spherics"}

**Spherics** (sometimes spelled **sphaerics** or **sphaerica**) is a term used in the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) for historical works on [spherical geometry](wikipedia://en/174026),[^o2][^o3] exemplified by the *[Spherics](wikipedia://en/37098504)* (τὰ σφαιρικά grc), a treatise by the [Hellenistic mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_mathematics) [Theodosius](wikipedia://en/2828050) (2nd or early 1st century BC),[^o4] and another treatise of the same title by [Menelaus of Alexandria](wikipedia://en/46301) (c. 100 AD).[^o5]

:::
