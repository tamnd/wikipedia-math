# Predual

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Predual"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **predual** of an object *D* is an object *P* whose [dual space](wikipedia://en/7988) is *D*.

:::

For example, the predual of the space of [bounded operators](wikipedia://en/455961) is the space of [trace class](wikipedia://en/192266) operators, and the predual of the space *L*<sup>∞</sup>(**R**) of essentially bounded functions on **R** is the Banach space *L*<sup>1</sup>(**R**) of integrable functions.

In [operator algebra](wikipedia://en/455987), if a dual Banach/operator space $A$ is realized as the dual of some [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) $A_*$, then $A_*$ is called the predual of $A$ (formally: $A \cong (A_* )^*$). The predual $A_*$ induces a [weak topology](wikipedia://en/33662) on $A$, under which [algebra operations](wikipedia://en/455987) are separately weak continuous.[^o2]
