# Waring

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Waring"}

**Waring** is an English surname with two derivation hypotheses: from the [Frankish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankish_language) *Warin*, meaning 'guard,' via [Norman French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_language) *Guarin,*[^o2] or from the Anglo-Saxon *Wæring*, meaning 'confederate' or, more literally, 'oath companion.'[^o3] Both hypotheses suggest that *Wareing* is a variant of this name. (*Ware,* as in the modern English *aware* and *beware,* is derived from the Anglo-Saxon *waer*.[^o4]) Notable people with the surname include:
