# Beatrice Pelloni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beatrice Pelloni"}

**Beatrice Pelloni** is an Italian mathematician specialising in applied [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). She is a professor of mathematics at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh), the former editor-in-chief of the *Proceedings of the Royal Society of Edinburgh, Section A: Mathematics*, and the former chair of the [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Activity Group on Nonlinear Waves and Coherent Structures.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pelloni was born on 28 June 1962 in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome). After earning a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) in 1985, she entered graduate study at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), but had to take several periods of time off from the program to raise three children. She completed her Ph.D. at Yale in 1996. Her dissertation, *Spectral Methods for the Numerical Solution of Nonlinear Dispersive Wave Equations*, was supervised by [Peter Jones](wikipedia://en/1604461).

While still a graduate student, Pelloni also worked as a researcher for the Institute of Applied Computational Mathematics of the [Foundation for Research & Technology – Hellas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundation_for_Research_&_Technology_–_Hellas) (IACM-FORTH). After completing her doctorate she was a research associate at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) and then joined the [University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Reading) as a lecturer in 2001. At Reading she became a professor in 2012. She moved to Heriot-Watt University in 2016.

She is the president of the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) for the 2025–2027 term.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Pelloni was the Olga Taussky-Todd Prize Lecturer at the 2011 [International Congress on Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13606431), speaking on "Boundary value problems and integrability", and the 2019 Mary Cartwright Lecturer of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), speaking on "Nonlinear transforms in the study of fluid dynamics". She was elected Fellow of the IMA in 2012, and Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh) in 2020.
