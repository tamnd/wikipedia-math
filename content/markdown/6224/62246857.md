# Sema Salur

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sema Salur"}

**Sema Salur** is a Turkish-American mathematician, currently serving as a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester).[^o2] She was awarded the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) for 2014–2015,[^o3] a prize intended to give a recently promoted associate professor a year-long fellowship at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University);[^o4] and has been the recipient of a [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) Research Award beginning in 2017.[^o5] She specialises in the "geometry and topology of the moduli spaces of calibrated submanifolds inside [Calabi–Yau](wikipedia://en/188932), [G<sub>2</sub>](wikipedia://en/292864) and [Spin(7)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spin%287%29) [manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifolds)",[^o3][^o6] which are important to certain aspects of [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) and [M-theory](wikipedia://en/20406) in physics, theories that attempt to unite [gravity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity), [electromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetism), and the [strong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strong_nuclear_force) and [weak nuclear forces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_nuclear_force) into one coherent [Theory of Everything](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theory_of_Everything).[^o6]

:::

## Education {#Education}

- 1993: B.S. in Mathematics, [Boğaziçi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boğaziçi_University), Turkey.[^o3]
- 2000: PhD in Mathematics, [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University)[^o3]
