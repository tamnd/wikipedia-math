# Miriam Gasko Donoho

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Miriam Gasko Donoho"}

**Miriam** (**Miki**) **Gasko Donoho** (also published as **Miriam Gasko-Green**) is an American statistician whose research topics have included [data visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization),A equivalences between [binary regression](wikipedia://en/60567021) and [survival analysis](wikipedia://en/419259),B and [robust regression](wikipedia://en/2713327).C

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Gasko completed her Ph.D. in statistics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1981. Her dissertation was *Testing Sequentially Selected Outliers from Linear Models*. She became a professor of marketing and quantitative studies in the College of Business at [San Jose State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San_Jose_State_University), director of the Silicon Valley Consumer Confidence Survey, and treasurer of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

She is a Fellow of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics. MacSpin, a program for three-dimensional data visualization that she developed with her husband [David Donoho](wikipedia://en/26137723) and brother-in-law Andrew Donoho, was named as the best scientific/engineering software of 1987 by *[MacUser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacUser)* magazine.
