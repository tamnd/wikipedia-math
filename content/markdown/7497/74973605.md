# Marie-Paule Malliavin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marie-Paule Malliavin"}

**Marie-Paule Malliavin**, née *Brameret*, (1935 in [Mahdia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahdia) – 25 September 2019 in Paris)[^o3] was a French mathematician who specialised in the field of algebra.[^o4]

:::

## Family {#Family}

She was married to the mathematician [Paul Malliavin](wikipedia://en/10459388) since 27 April 1965. They had two children (Thérèse and Marie-Joseph).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

She published her first mathematical article in 1960[^o8] and received her doctorate in 1965.[^o9]

After her doctorate, she first became maître de conférences at the [University of Caen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen_Normandy).[^o10] Later she became a professor at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (UPMC, Paris 6) in Paris, where she remained until her retirement.[^o9][^o10]

Her mathematical students include Jacques Alev and Youssef El From.[^o11] She wrote several textbooks; the books on commutative algebra and representation theory of finite groups are frequently cited.[^o10]

At the beginning of her career, she worked on [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), later on [non-commutative algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-commutative_algebra). This was at a time when [enveloping algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enveloping_algebra_%28disambiguation%29) and then [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678) were evolving. She collaborated with her husband Paul Malliavin in particular in the study of [measures](wikipedia://en/40645) on infinite-dimensional groups.[^o10][^o12]

Marie-Paule Malliavin was editor of top-ranked international research journals.[^o10] She also organised the Algebra Seminar at the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) for several decades, succeeding the deceased Professor [Paul Dubreil](wikipedia://en/1389591).[^o10]
