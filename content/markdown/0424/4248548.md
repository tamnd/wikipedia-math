# Felix Otto (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Felix Otto"}

**Felix Otto** (born 19 May 1966) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Otto was born on 19 May 1966 in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), [Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavaria). He studied mathematics at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), finishing his PhD thesis in 1993 under the supervision of [Stephan Luckhaus](wikipedia://en/31696418). After postdoctoral studies at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) and at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), in 1997 he became a professor at the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara). From 1999 to 2010 he was a professor for [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn), and he currently serves as one of the directors of the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics in the Sciences](wikipedia://en/2114506) in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig).

## Work {#Work}

Otto specialises in [materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science), and he has worked on the theory of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564).[^o6] He is especially known for his work on the [Otto–Villani theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto–Villani_theorem) and the invention of the [Otto calculus](wikipedia://en/75608576).

## Honours {#Honours}

In 2006, he was awarded the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_Prize) by the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation), which is the considered the most important research award in Germany.

In 2008, he became a member of the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina).[^o8]

In 2009, he was awarded a [Gauss Lectureship](wikipedia://en/48440954) by the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).

In 2024, he was awarded the [Cantor Medal](wikipedia://en/1691369) by the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).[^o9]
