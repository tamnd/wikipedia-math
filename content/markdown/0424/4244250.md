# Joel Spencer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joel Spencer"}

**Joel Harold Spencer** (born April 20, 1946) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is a [combinatorialist](wikipedia://en/5170) who has worked on [probabilistic](wikipedia://en/22934) methods in combinatorics and on [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175). He received his doctorate from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1970, under the supervision of [Andrew Gleason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Gleason). Spencer is currently () a professor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). His work was heavily influenced by [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), with whom Spencer co-authored many papers (giving him an [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) of 1).

:::

In 1963, while studying at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), Spencer became a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Fellow).[^o4] In 1984, he received a [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award).[^o5] He was an [Erdős Lecturer](wikipedia://en/56645465) at [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 2001. In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6] He was elected as a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2017, "for contributions to discrete mathematics and theory of computing, particularly random graphs and networks, Ramsey theory, logic, and randomized algorithms".[^o7] In 2021, he received the [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition with his coauthor [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651) for their book *The Probabilistic Method*.[^o8]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *Probabilistic methods in combinatorics*, with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), New York: Academic Press, 1974.
- *Ramsey theory*, with Bruce L. Rothschild and [Ronald L. Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ronald_L._Graham), New York: Wiley, 1980; 2nd ed., 1990.
- *Ten lectures on the probabilistic method*, Philadelphia: Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics, 1987; 2nd ed., 1994.
- *[The strange logic of random graphs](wikipedia://en/63598605)*, Berlin: Springer-Verlag, 2001.
- *The probabilistic method*, with [Noga Alon](wikipedia://en/4939651), New York: Wiley, 1992; 2nd ed., 2000; 3rd ed., 2008; 4th ed., 2016.
- *Deterministic random walks on regular trees*, American Mathematical Society, New York, 2008.
- *Asymptopia*, with Laura Florescu, American Mathematical Society, 2014.
