# Daniela di Serafino

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniela di Serafino"}

**Daniela di Serafino** (8 April 1966 – 22 August 2022) was an Italian applied mathematician and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506) whose research involved [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660), [gradient descent](wikipedia://en/201489) methods for [nonlinear optimization](wikipedia://en/679709), and applications in [scientific computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_computing). She was a professor of numerical analysis in the Department of Mathematics and Applications at the [University of Naples Federico II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Di Serafino was born in Naples, on 8 April 1966. She was an undergraduate at the University of Naples Federico II, where she earned a master's degree in mathematics in 1989. After two years working as a researcher in the Center of Research for [Parallel Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parallel_computing) and Supercomputers of the [National Research Council (Italy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Council_%28Italy%29) in Naples, she returned to the University of Naples for doctoral study in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), completing her Ph.D. in 1995.

## Career and later life {#Career_and_later_life}

After finishing her doctorate, di Serafino worked as an assistant professor at the [Università degli Studi della Campania Luigi Vanvitelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Università_degli_Studi_della_Campania_Luigi_Vanvitelli) (originally the "Second University of Naples") from 1995 to 2004, and as an associate professor there from 2005 to 2018. In this time frame she also held positions as a researcher at the Institute for High-Performance Computing and Networking of the National Research Council.

In 2014, she earned a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) as a professor of numerical analysis, and in 2018 she obtained a full professorship at the University of Campania. In 2020 she returned to the University of Naples Federico II as a full professor.

She died on 22 August 2022.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

A 2010 paper by di Serafino with Marco D'Apuzzo and Valentina De Simone, "On mutual impact of numerical linear algebra and large-scale optimization with focus on interior point methods", received the *Computational Optimization and Applications* Best Paper Award.
