# Quarter hypercubic honeycomb

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quarter hypercubic honeycomb"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **quarter hypercubic honeycomb** (or **quarter n-cubic honeycomb**) is a dimensional infinite series of [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654), based on the [hypercube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypercube_honeycomb). It is given a [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) q{4,3...3,4} or Coxeter symbol qδ<sub>4</sub> representing the regular form with three quarters of the vertices removed and containing the symmetry of [Coxeter group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram#Infinite_Coxeter_groups) ${\tilde{D}}_{n-1}$ for n ≥ 5, with ${\tilde{D}}_4$ = ${\tilde{A}}_4$ and for quarter n-cubic honeycombs ${\tilde{D}}_5$ = ${\tilde{B}}_5$.[^o2]
