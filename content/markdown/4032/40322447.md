# Lambda g conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lambda g conjecture"}

In algebraic geometry, the **$\lambda_g$-conjecture** gives a particularly simple formula for certain integrals on the Deligne–Mumford compactification $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g, n}$ of the [moduli space of curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_space_of_curves) with marked points. It was first found as a consequence of the [Virasoro conjecture](wikipedia://en/34694378) by (txt, 701–714). Later, it was proven by (txt, 97–124) using virtual localization in [Gromov–Witten theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gromov–Witten_theory). It is named after the factor of $\lambda_g$, the *g*th [Chern class](wikipedia://en/294349) of the [Hodge bundle](wikipedia://en/26155744), appearing in its integrand. The other factor is a monomial in the $\psi_i$, the first Chern classes of the *n* cotangent line bundles, as in [Witten's conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Witten's_conjecture).

:::

Let $a_1, \ldots, a_n$ be positive integers such that:

$$a_1 + \cdots + a_n = 2g-3+n.$$

Then the $\lambda_g$-formula can be stated as follows:

$$\int_{\overline{\mathcal M}_{g, n}} \psi_1^{a_1} \cdots \psi_n^{a_n}\lambda_g = \binom{2g + n - 3}{a_1, \ldots, a_n} \int_{\overline{\mathcal M}_{g, 1}} \psi_1^{2g - 2}\lambda_g.$$

The $\lambda_g$-formula in combination with

$$\int_{\overline{\mathcal M}_{g, 1}} \psi_1^{2g - 2}\lambda_g = \frac{2^{2g-1} - 1}{2^{2g - 1}} \frac{|B_{2g}|}{(2g)!},$$

where the *B*<sub>2*g*</sub> are [Bernoulli numbers](wikipedia://en/4964), gives a way to calculate all integrals on $\overline{\mathcal M}_{g, n}$ involving products in $\psi$-classes and a factor of $\lambda_g$.
