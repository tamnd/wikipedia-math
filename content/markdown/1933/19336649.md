# Geoff Sutcliffe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Geoff Sutcliffe"}

**Geoff Sutcliffe** is a US-based [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) working in the field of [automated reasoning](wikipedia://en/2884728). He was born in the former British colony of [Northern Rhodesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_Rhodesia) (now [Zambia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zambia)), grew up in [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa), and earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia). Sutcliffe currently works at the [University of Miami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Miami), and is of both [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) and [Australian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australians) nationality.[^o3]

:::

Geoff Sutcliffe is the developer of the [Thousands of Problems for Theorem Provers](wikipedia://en/64143095) (TPTP) problem library, and of the [TPTP language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TPTP_language) for formal specification of [Automated theorem proving](wikipedia://en/2546) problems and solutions. Since 1996 he has been organizing the annual [CADE ATP System Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CADE_ATP_System_Competition) (CASC), associated with the [Conference on Automated Deduction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conference_on_Automated_Deduction) and [International Joint Conference on Automated Reasoning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Joint_Conference_on_Automated_Reasoning). He has been a co-organizer of several [Automated reasoning](wikipedia://en/2884728) challenges, including the Modal Logic \$100 Challenge,[^o8] the MPTP \$100 Challenges,[^o9] and the SUMO \$100 Challenges.[^o4] Together with [Stephan Schulz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephan_Schulz), Sutcliffe founded and has been organizing the ES\* Workshop series,[^o5] a venue for presentation and publishing of practically oriented Automated Reasoning research.

In 2025 Sutcliffe went to [Federal University of Goiás](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Goiás) and gave a two-day lecture about TPTP.[^o6]
