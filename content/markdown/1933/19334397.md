# Five circles theorem

*Image: Five circles theorem.svg.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Five circles theorem"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **five circles theorem** states that, given five [circles](wikipedia://en/6220) centered on a common sixth circle and intersecting each other chainwise on the same circle, the lines joining their second intersection points forms a [pentagram](wikipedia://en/197544) whose points lie on the circles themselves.

:::
