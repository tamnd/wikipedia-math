# Seven circles theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Seven circles theorem"}

*Image: Teorema de les set circumferències.svg.* Seven circles theorem

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **seven circles theorem** is a theorem about a certain arrangement of seven [circles](wikipedia://en/6220) in the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410). Specifically, given a chain of six circles all [tangent](wikipedia://en/31482) to a seventh circle and each tangent to its two neighbors, the three [lines](wikipedia://en/946975) drawn between opposite pairs of the points of tangency on the seventh circle all pass through the same [point](wikipedia://en/593693). Though elementary in nature, this theorem was not discovered until 1974 (by Evelyn, Money-Coutts, and Tyrrell).

:::
