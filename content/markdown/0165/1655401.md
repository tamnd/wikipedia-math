# Heinz Prüfer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heinz Prüfer"}

*Image: Prüfer,Heinz 1930 Jena.jpg.* Heinz Prüfer (1930).

**Ernst Paul Heinz Prüfer** (10 November 1896 – 7 April 1934) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Wilhelmshaven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelmshaven). His major contributions were on [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), [algebraic numbers](wikipedia://en/1158), [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008) and [Sturm–Liouville theory](wikipedia://en/490990).

:::

In 1915 he began his university studies in mathematics, [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) and [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin). After that he started his doctorate degree with [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522) as his advisor at [Friedrich Wilhelm University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt-Universität_Berlin), Berlin. In 1921 he obtained his doctorate degree. His thesis was named *Unendliche Abelsche Gruppen von Elementen endlicher Ordnung* (Infinite abelian groups of elements of finite order). This thesis set the road for his contributions on abelian groups. In 1922 he worked with mathematician [Paul Koebe](wikipedia://en/4313643) in the [University of Jena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jena), and in 1923 he obtained tenure and was at this university until 1927. In that year he moved to [Münster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Münster_University) where he worked until the end of his life. His final work was about [projective geometry](wikipedia://en/243849), but it was posthumously completed by his students Gustav Fleddermann and [Gottfried Köthe](wikipedia://en/27641787).

Heinz Prüfer was married, but never had children. He died prematurely at 37 years of age in 1934 in Münster, Germany, due to lung cancer.

## Mathematical contributions {#Mathematical_contributions}

Heinz Prüfer created the following mathematical notions that were later named after him:

- [Prüfer sequence](wikipedia://en/1650168) (also known as a Prüfer code; it has broad applications in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [network theory](wikipedia://en/766409)).
- [Prüfer domain](wikipedia://en/12367835). Also see [Bézout domain](wikipedia://en/3131407), which is a Prüfer domain
- [Prüfer rank](wikipedia://en/1202488)
- [Prüfer manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prüfer_manifold) also known as Prüfer surface or Prüfer analytical manifold
- [Prüfer group](wikipedia://en/4885031)
- [Prüfer theorems](wikipedia://en/27970235)
