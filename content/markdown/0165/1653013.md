# Millionth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Millionth"}

One **millionth** is equal to 0.000 001, or 1 × 10<sup>−6</sup> in [scientific notation](wikipedia://en/59715). It is the [reciprocal](wikipedia://en/229940) of a [million](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Million), and can be also written as $\tfrac{1}{1,000,000}$.[^o2] Units using this fraction can be indicated using the prefix "micro-" from [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language), meaning "small".[^o3] Numbers of this quantity are expressed in terms of μ (the Greek letter [mu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mu_%28letter%29)).[^o4]

:::

"Millionth" can also mean the [ordinal number](wikipedia://en/26547932) that comes after the nine hundred, ninety-nine thousand, nine hundred, ninety-ninth and before the million and first.[^o5]
