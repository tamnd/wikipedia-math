# Philip Hall

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Philip Hall"}

**Philip Hall** [^o3] (11 April 1904 – 30 December 1982) was an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His major work was on [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), notably on [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) and [solvable groups](wikipedia://en/105012).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was educated first at [Christ's Hospital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ's_Hospital), where he won the Thompson Gold Medal for mathematics, and later at [King's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College,_Cambridge). He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 1951 and awarded its [Sylvester Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylvester_Medal) in 1961. He was President of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) from 1955–1957, and was awarded its [Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berwick_Prizes) in 1958 and [De Morgan Medal](wikipedia://en/1096532) in 1965.
