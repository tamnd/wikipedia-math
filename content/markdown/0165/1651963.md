# Biomedical cybernetics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Biomedical cybernetics"}

**Biomedical cybernetics** investigates [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324), [decision making](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decision_making) and control structures in living [organisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organism). Applications of this research field are in [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), [ecology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecology) and [health sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_sciences).

:::
