# Henk Barendregt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henk Barendregt"}

*Image: Henk Barendregt at the Old Jewish Cemetery in Prague.jpg.* Henk Barendregt during his visit in Prague in April 2012

**Hendrik Pieter (Henk) Barendregt** (born 18 December 1947, [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam))[^o2] is a Dutch [logician](wikipedia://en/19636), known for his work in [lambda calculus](wikipedia://en/18203) and [type theory](wikipedia://en/40282).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Barendregt studied [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University), obtaining his master's degree in 1968 and his PhD in 1971, both *[cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cum_laude)*, under [Dirk van Dalen](wikipedia://en/26828544) and [Georg Kreisel](wikipedia://en/11761785). After a postdoctoral position at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), he taught at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University).

Since 1986, Barendregt has taught at [Radboud University Nijmegen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radboud_University_Nijmegen), where he now holds the Chair of Foundations of Mathematics and Computer Science. His research group works on Constructive Interactive Mathematics. He is also adjunct professor at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University), Pittsburgh, US. He has been a visiting scholar at [Darmstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Darmstadt), [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich), Siena, and Kyoto.

Barendregt was elected a member of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 1992.[^o4] In 1997 Barendregt was elected member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o5] On 6 February 2003 Barendregt was awarded the [Spinozapremie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spinozapremie) for 2002, the highest scientific award in the Netherlands.[^o6] In 2002 he was knighted in the [Orde van de Nederlandse Leeuw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Netherlands_Lion).

Barendregt received an honorary doctorate from [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) in 2015.[^o7]
