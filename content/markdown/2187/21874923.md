# Magnus Pegel

*Image: Magnus Pegel.jpg.* Magnus Pegel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Magnus Pegel"}

**Magnus Pegel** (or **Pegelius** or **Pegelow**) (15 May 1547 – 1619) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) doctor and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2] Pegel was born in [Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rostock) in Pomerania/Germany and was one of the first authors to write (in 1604) about the theory of [blood transfusions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blood_transfusion).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] He died at [Stettin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stettin).

:::
