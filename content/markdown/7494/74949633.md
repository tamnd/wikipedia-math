# Ruixiang Zhang

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ruixiang Zhang"}

**Ruixiang Zhang** () is a Chinese mathematician specializing in Euclidean [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [additive combinatorics](wikipedia://en/18243392). He is an assistant professor in the Department of Mathematics at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o3] He and collaborator Shaoming Guo of the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) proved a multivariable generalization of the central conjecture in [Vinogradov's mean-value theorem](wikipedia://en/43822295). Zhang was awarded the 2023 [SASTRA Ramanujan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_Ramanujan_Prize) for his contributions to mathematics.[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Zhang was born and raised in [China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/China). Representing China, he earned a gold medal at the 2008 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) held in Madrid. He obtained a BS degree from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2012 and a PhD from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2017. At Princeton, Zhang worked under the supervision of [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak); his doctoral dissertation was *Perturbed Brascamp-Lieb inequalities and application to Parsell-Vinogradov systems*. After receiving his PhD, Zhang remained in Princeton for a post-doctoral year as a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and then spent three years as the Van Vleck Visiting Assistant Professor at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Madison). In 2020, he returned to Princeton and spent one more year as a member of the Institute for Advanced Study. He has been a member of the Berkeley mathematics faculty since July 2021.[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

Zhang's contributions to mathematics include a generalization of an important conjecture in Vinogradov's Mean-Value Theorem, using novel techniques to solve Carleson's problem on pointwise convergence of solutions to the [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) and solving the two-dimensional case of Sogge's conjecture for wave equations.[^o4][^o7]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

Two of Zhang's research papers were selected for the Frontier Science Award in two separate categories during the International Congress for Basic Science held in Beijing in July 2023.[^o9] He is a recipient of the [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) and a winner of the Silver Prize for his doctoral thesis in the 5th New World Mathematics Awards.[^o10] He is an Editor of Bulletin of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097), Journal of the London Mathematical Society[^o11] and Pacific Journal of Mathematics.[^o12]
