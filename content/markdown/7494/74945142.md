# Vladimir Balabanov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Balabanov"}

**Vladimir Anastasovich Balabanov** (Влади́мир Анаста́сович Балаба́нов, 2 December 1932, Avranlo, [Georgian SSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgian_Soviet_Socialist_Republic) — 28 August 2021, [Thessaloniki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thessaloniki), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece)) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet) and [Georgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_%28country%29) mathematician of Greek origin, a [Doctor of Physics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) and a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Vladimir Balabanov was born on 2 December 1932 in the Greek village called Avranlo in the Georgian SSR.

His father, Anastas Isidorovich Balabanov, worked in Soviet government organizations, while his mother, Sofia Stefanovna Karibova, originates from a family of educators.

In 1950, he graduated from the [Bolnisi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bolnisi) Secondary School with a gold medal. In 1954, he graduated with honors from the Faculty of Physics and Mathematics at the Tbilisi Pedagogical Sulkhan-Saba Orbeliani University.

In 1960, he defended his candidate thesis on the topic *Some Issues of Stability of Solutions of Nonlinear Functional Equations.*[^o4]

In 1990, he defended his doctoral thesis on the topic *Some Issues of Nonlinear Functional Analysis and Their Applications.*[^o5]

In 1991, he was conferred the academic degree of Doctor of Physical and Mathematical Sciences and the title of professor.[^o6]

Balabanov taught mathematics at several higher-education institutions. He lectured at the Faculty of Physics of [Tbilisi State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_State_University), the Tbilisi branch of the [Russian University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_University) of Cooperation, the [Tbilisi Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tbilisi_Institute) for Teacher Enhancement, the Tbilisi Institute of Economics and Pedagogy, the [Aristotle University of Thessaloniki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle_University_of_Thessaloniki), and the Tbilisi University of Economic Relations.

While living and working in Georgia, Vladimir Balabanov was a member of the Qualification Board of Professors for the Award of Academic Titles and a member of the Board of the Union of Mathematicians of Georgia. By the decree of the President of Georgia [Eduard Shevardnadze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eduard_Shevardnadze), he was awarded the *Order of Honor*.[^o7] He was also elected an academician of the Greek-Georgian International Humanitarian Academy.[^o8]

Since 2007, he lived with his family in [Thessaloniki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thessaloniki), [Greece](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greece).

He was married and had two children.

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

His primary works were in the field of nonlinear [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and its applications in [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), the theory of differential equations, and approximation theory. Some of his research focused on the stability of solutions and bifurcations of solutions of nonlinear operator equations.

He authored more than 70 scientific papers and monographs.
