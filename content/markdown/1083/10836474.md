# T. A. Springer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="T. A. Springer"}

**Tonny Albert Springer** (13 February 1926 – 7 December 2011) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University) who worked on linear [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), [Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/521808), [complex reflection groups](wikipedia://en/5381096), and who introduced [Springer representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_representations) and the [Springer resolution](wikipedia://en/32065220).

:::

Springer began his undergraduate studies in 1945 at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) and remained there for his graduate work in mathematics, earning his PhD in 1951 under [Hendrik Kloosterman](wikipedia://en/6022800) with thesis *Over symplectische Transformaties*. As a postdoc Springer spent the academic year 1951/1952 at the [University of Nancy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nancy) and then returned to Leiden University, where he was employed until 1955. In 1955 he accepted a lectureship at [Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University), where he became professor ordinarius in 1959 and continued in that position until 1991 when he retired as professor emeritus. Springer's visiting professorships included many institutions: the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) (1963), the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (1961/1962, 1969, 1983),[^o3] [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES) (1964, 1973, 1975, 1983), [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (1968, 1980), [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (1965/1966), the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata), the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel), the [Erwin Schrödinger Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_Institute) in Vienna, and the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI).

In 1964 Springer was elected to the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o4] In 2006 in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) with lecture on *Some results on compactifications of semisimple groups*. (At the 1962 ICM in Stockholm he made a short contribution *Twisted composition algebras*, but was not an invited speaker.)
