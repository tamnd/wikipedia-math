# Jürgen Gärtner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jürgen Gärtner"}

*Image: Newman molchanov gaertner.jpg.* From left: Charles Newman, Stanislav Molchanov, Jürgen Gärtner, Oberwolfach 2003

**Jürgen Gärtner** (born 1950) is a German mathematician, specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Gärtner was born in 1950 in [Reichenbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichenbach_%28Oberlausitz%29). He graduated in 1973 with *[Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom)* from [TU Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Dresden). He received in 1976 his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Lomonosov University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_University) under the supervision of [Mark Freidlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Freidlin). At the [Weierstrass Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute), Gärtner was from 1976 to 1985 a research associate; he habilitated there in 1984 with Dissertation B: *Zur Ausbreitung von Wellenfronten für Reaktions-Diffusions-Gleichungen* (The propagation of wave fronts for reaction-diffusion equations). At the Weierstrass Institute he was from 1985 to 1995 the head of the probability group. He was a professor of the Academy of Sciences of the GDR from 1988 until its disbandment in late 1991. At TU Berlin he was from 1992 to 2011 a professor, retiring as professor emeritus in 2011.[^o4]

In 1977 he proved a general form of [Cramér's Theorem](wikipedia://en/57800229) in the theory of [large deviations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_deviations) (LD);[^o5] the theorem is known as the Gärtner-Ellis Large Deviations Principle (LDP). ([Richard S. Ellis](wikipedia://en/57923205) proved the theorem in 1984 with weaker premises.) In 1982 Gärtner wrote an important paper on the famous [KPP equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/KPP_equation) (a semi-linear diffusion equation introduced in 1937).[^o6] In 1987 Gärtner, with [Donald A. Dawson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_A._Dawson), introduced the construction of a [projective limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_limit) in the LDP. From 1987 to 1989 Gärtner and Dawson wrote a series of important papers on the [McKean-Vlasov process](wikipedia://en/38309972). Their results were extended by other mathematicians in the 1990s to random mean-field interactions and to spin-glass mean-field interactions. In 1990 Gärtner and [Molchanov](wikipedia://en/61934211) wrote a seminal paper on [intermittency](wikipedia://en/4662960) in the parabolic Anderson model; the paper introduced a new approach to intermittency via the study of [Lyapunov coefficients](wikipedia://en/166441).[^o7]

Gärtner was a member from 1984 to 1992 of the editorial board of *[Probability Theory and Related Fields](wikipedia://en/30852593)* and from 1990 to 2000 of the editorial board of *[Mathematische Nachrichten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematische_Nachrichten)*.[^o4]

In 1992 Gärtner was an invited lecturer at the first [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in Paris. In 1994 he was an invited speaker with talk *Parabolic Systems in Random Media and Aspects of Intermittency* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich). A conference was held in honor of his 60th birthday.[^o8][^o9]
