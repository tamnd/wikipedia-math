# Wilhelm Fuhrmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Fuhrmann"}

**Wilhelm Ferdinand Fuhrmann** (28 February 1833 – 11 June 1904) was a German mathematician. The [Fuhrmann circle](wikipedia://en/14898988) and the [Fuhrmann triangle](wikipedia://en/62319195) are named after him.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Fuhrmann was born on 28 February 1833 in [Burg bei Magdeburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burg_bei_Magdeburg). Fuhrmann had shortly worked as sailor before he returned to school and attended the [Altstadt Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altstadt_Gymnasium) in Königsberg, where his teachers noticed his interest and talent in mathematics and geography. He graduated in 1853 and went on to study mathematics and physics at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg). One of his peers later remembered him as the most talented and diligent student of his class. Fuhrmann however despite his talent did not pursue a career at the university, instead he became a math and science teacher at the [Burgschule](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burgschule_%28Königsberg%29) in Königsberg after his graduation. He joined the school in 1860 and remained there until his death in 1904.[^o5]

Fuhrmann authored several books and a number of papers on different mathematical subjects. Today he is best remembered for his interest in and contribution to elementary geometry. With *Synthetische Beweise planimetrischer Sätze* he wrote an influential book on the subject and in 1890 he published an article entitled *Sur un nouveau cercle associé à un triangle* in the Belgian math journal [Mathesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathesis_%28journal%29). In this article Fuhrmann described the circle and the triangle that now carry his name.[^o5][^o6]

- *Aufgaben über Kegelschnitte* (1879)
- *Aufgaben aus der niederen Analysis* (1886)
- *Der Brocardsche Winkel* (1889)
- "Sur un nouveau cercle associé à un triangle". In: *Mathesis*, 1890 ([English translation](http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2011volume11/FG201103.pdf) (2019-12-31). <https://web.archive.org/web/20191231044131/http://forumgeom.fau.edu/FG2011volume11/FG201103.pdf>)
