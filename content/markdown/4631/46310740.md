# Kuratowski and Ryll-Nardzewski measurable selection theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kuratowski and Ryll-Nardzewski measurable selection theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Kuratowski–Ryll-Nardzewski measurable selection theorem** is a result from [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory) that gives a sufficient condition for a [set-valued function](wikipedia://en/12506030) to have a measurable [selection function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Selection_function).[^o2][^o3][^o4] It is named after the Polish mathematicians [Kazimierz Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075) and [Czesław Ryll-Nardzewski](wikipedia://en/1926283).[^o5]

:::

Many classical selection results follow from this theorem[^o6] and it is widely used in [mathematical economics](wikipedia://en/18967255) and [optimal control](wikipedia://en/362565).[^o7]

::: {#o9 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let $X$ be a [Polish space](wikipedia://en/396622), $\mathcal{B} (X)$ the [Borel](wikipedia://en/43327) [σ-algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma-algebra) of $X$, $(\Omega, \mathcal{F})$ a [measurable space](wikipedia://en/43333) and $\psi$ a multifunction on $\Omega$ taking values in the set of nonempty closed subsets of $X$.

Suppose that $\psi$ is $\mathcal{F}$-weakly measurable, that is, for every open subset $U$ of $X$, we have

$$\{\omega : \psi (\omega) \cap U \neq \emptyset \} \in \mathcal{F}.$$

Then $\psi$ has a [selection](wikipedia://en/1432664) that is $\mathcal{F}$-$\mathcal{B} (X)$-measurable.[^o10]

:::
