# Area chart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Area chart"}

*Image: De wanderung.svg.* Stacked area chart

*Image: US\_and\_USSR\_nuclear\_stockpiles.svg.* Layered area chart

An **area chart** or **area graph** displays graphically quantitative data. It is based on the [line chart](wikipedia://en/6879051). The area between axis and line are commonly emphasized with colors, textures and hatchings. Commonly one compares two or more quantities with an area chart.

:::

## History {#History}

[William Playfair](wikipedia://en/1411831) is usually credited with inventing the area charts as well as the [line](wikipedia://en/6879051), [bar](wikipedia://en/393311), and [pie charts](wikipedia://en/728487). His book *The Commercial and Political Atlas*, published in 1786, contained a number of [time-series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time-series) graphs, including *Interest of the National Debt from the Revolution* and *Chart of all the Imports and Exports to and from England from the Year 1700 to 1782* that are often described as the first area charts in history.[^o3][^o4]

## Common uses {#Common_uses}

Area charts are used to represent cumulated totals using numbers or percentages (stacked area charts in this case) over time. Use the area chart for showing trends over time among related attributes. The area chart is like the plot chart except that the area below the plotted line is filled in with color to indicate volume.

When multiple attributes are included, the first attribute is plotted as a line with color fill followed by the second attribute, and so on.

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Variations"}

## Variations {#Variations}

Area charts which use vertical and horizontal lines to connect the data points in a series forming a step-like progression are called *step-area charts*.

Area charts in which data points are connected by smooth curves instead of straight lines are called *spline-area charts*.[^o8]

Stacked area charts in which the area is displaced around the central axis are called [*streamgraphs*](wikipedia://en/36530691).

:::
