# Rose Peltesohn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rose Peltesohn"}

**Rose Pauline Peltesohn** (רוז פאולין פלטסון; 16 May 1913 – 21 March 1998) was an Israeli mathematician of German origin.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Rose Peltesohn was the daughter of the physician Ludwig Peltesohn (1882–1937) and of Cilly Caro.[^o4] After graduation (Abitur) in March 1931 she studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt-Universität_Berlin) and got her Ph.D. in Mathematics at 1936 with [Issai Schur](wikipedia://en/617522)[^o5] as supervisor (*Das Turnierproblem für Spiele zu je dreien*, The tournament problem for three person games). Her dissertation was valued *opus valde laudabile*<sup>(de)</sup>. Being Jewish she emigrated through Italy to Palestine, arriving 1938.[^o6] Between the years 1939–1942 she worked in a bank and later as a lawyer's secretary and translator in Tel Aviv. She married her cousin Gerhard Peltesohn, a lawyer (1909–1965), and they had two daughters, Ruth (born 1940) and Judith (born 1943).

## Solution of Heffter's Difference Problems {#Solution_of_Heffter's_Difference_Problems}

Peltesohn solved the Difference Problems of Lothar Heffter (1896) in combinatorics in 1939.[^o8] A Difference Triple (*a*, *b*, *c*) is defined as three different elements from the set $1, 2,\dots, v-1$, whose sum $\bmod v$ equals zero ($a+b+c=0 \bmod v$) or for which one element $\bmod v$ equals the sum of the other two ($a+b=c \bmod v$).

- First Difference Problem of Heffter: Let $v=6 m+1$. Is there a partition of the set $1, 2,\ldots, 3m$ in difference triples?
- Second Difference Problem of Heffter: Let $v=6m+3$. Is there a partition of the set $1,2,\ldots,2m, 2m+2,\ldots, 3m+1$ in difference triples ?

Following Peltesohn, such a partition exists with the exception of the case *v* = 9.

An example of the partition for $v=13$ is: $(1,3,4)$ (with $1+3=4 \bmod 13$) and $(2,5,6)$ (with $2+5+6=13=0 \bmod 13$).

The solution of the Difference Problem of Heffter also gives a construction of cyclic [Steiner triple systems](wikipedia://en/28016#Steiner_triple_systems).
