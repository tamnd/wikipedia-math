# Felix Leinen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Felix Leinen"}

**Felix Leinen** (born 15 May 1957) is a German [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and politician of the [Ecological Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_Democratic_Party) (ÖDP.)[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Leinen studied mathematics from 1976 to 1982 at the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz). He obtained a PhD (*Dr. rer. nat.*) in 1984 at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg) as a student of [Otto H. Kegel](wikipedia://en/80543894). From 1984 to 1985 he worked as a Visiting assistant professor at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University), East Lansing. Since 1985 Leinen has worked at the University of Mainz. Since 1997 Leinen has been [supernumerary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_Germany) at the Johannes Gutenberg-university.[^o5] In the meantime, he made several professional visits to England, Italy and the USA. He volunteered in 1974–1984 supporting the [Technisches Hilfswerk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technisches_Hilfswerk). Leinen is married and has four children.

## Political career {#Political_career}

Since 1995 he has been active in a citizens' initiative that fights against the conversion of the *Laubenheimer Höhe* area in [Mainz-Laubenheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainz-Laubenheim) into a [quarry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quarry). In 1999 he joined the [Ecological Democratic Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_Democratic_Party) and served as the deputy national chairman from 2008 to 2010. He heads the Federal Education Policy Working Group of the ÖDP. Leinen is treasurer of his party in Rhineland-Palatinate and ÖDP-chairman of the local chapter of [Mainz-Hechtsheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mainz-Hechtsheim). In the local elections of 2009, he succeeded in gaining a seat on Mainz City Council.
