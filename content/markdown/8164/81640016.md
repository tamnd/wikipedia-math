# Stuart Levenbach

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stuart Levenbach"}

**Stuart Levenbach** is a U.S. government official. He has been the [chief statistician of the United States](wikipedia://en/45199545) since January 2026, in charge of the Statistical Policy Branch of the Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs (OIRA) in the U.S. government's [Office of Management and Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Management_and_Budget) (OMB).

:::

## Background {#Background}

Levenbach earned a Ph.D. in marine ecology from the University of California, Santa Barbara. He served in the U.S. Peace Corps as a volunteer forester. He worked in staff positions in at OIRA, elsewhere in OMB, and for the [National Economic Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Economic_Council_%28United_States%29). In the first Trump Administration, he was the Chief of Staff for the [National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Oceanic_and_Atmospheric_Administration). Then he was director of public affairs for the Americas for the [Baker Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baker_Hughes) energy company.[^o4][^o5]
