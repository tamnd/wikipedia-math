# Alfreds Meders

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfreds Meders"}

**Alfreds Arnolds Adolfs Meders** was a German-Latvian mathematician, and a student of [Adolf Kneser](wikipedia://en/1368530) at the [University of Tartu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tartu). He was a professor at the [Riga Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riga_Technical_University) until his mandatory repatriation to Germany in 1939. In Riga, [Eizens Leimanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eizens_Leimanis) was one of his students.

:::
