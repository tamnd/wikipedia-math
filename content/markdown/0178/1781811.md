# Hans Bruun Nielsen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Bruun Nielsen"}

academics

**Hans Bruun Nielsen** (1943 – 2015) was a Danish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Associate Professor of [Technical University of Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Denmark) specializing in [Numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) and the application of [numerical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_methods).

:::

- Eldén, Wittmeyer-Koch, **Nielsen** *Introduction to Numerical Computation - analysis and MATLAB illustrations*, 2004 [Studentlitteratur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studentlitteratur) (<http://www.studentlitteratur.se/o.o.i.s/103> (2005-04-09). <https://web.archive.org/web/20050409163053/http://www.studentlitteratur.se/o.o.i.s/103>),
  - [The book's Contents page](http://www.studentlitteratur.se/o.o.i.s/?id=2362&artnr=31885-01&what=toc&csid=102) (2006-03-07). <https://web.archive.org/web/20060307151724/http://www.studentlitteratur.se/o.o.i.s/?id=2362&artnr=31885-01&what=toc&csid=102>
  - [Formulae from the book](https://web.archive.org/web/20040826151543/http://www.imm.dtu.dk/~hbn/IntroNumComp/formulas.pdf) (E.g. order of [Cholesky decomposition](wikipedia://en/134433) computations, maximum error in [Chebyshev interpolation](wikipedia://en/647470#Approximation), and maximum [stable step length](wikipedia://en/233807) using [Runge-Kutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge-Kutta).)
