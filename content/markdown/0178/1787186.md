# Peter C. Fishburn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter C. Fishburn"}

**Peter Clingerman Fishburn** (September 2, 1936 – June 10, 2021) was an American mathematician, known as a pioneer in the field of [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216). In collaboration with [Steven Brams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Brams), Fishburn published a paper about [approval voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approval_voting) in 1978.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

### Intellectual {#Intellectual}

Fishburn received his B.S. in [industrial engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_engineering) from [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 1958, his M.S. in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) in 1961, and a Ph.D. in operations research in 1962, the latter two from the [Case Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Institute_of_Technology).

In collaboration with [Steven Brams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Brams), Fishburn published a paper about [approval voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approval_voting) in 1978.[^o3] In 1996, he won the [John von Neumann Theory Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_von_Neumann_Theory_Prize). He also won the Decision Analysis Publication Award in 1991[^o6] and the Frank P. Ramsey Medal in 1987.[^o7]

He was elected to the 2002 class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).[^o8]

### Personal {#Personal}

Fishburn retired after many years of research at [AT&T Bell Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT&T_Bell_Laboratories) in the state of [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey), United States. He was married to the theologian Janet Forsythe Fishburn. He died on June 10, 2021, in Racine, Wisconsin.[^o10]
