# Lazar Stankov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lazar Stankov"}

**Lazar Stankov** is an Australian psychologist and emeritus professor of psychology at the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), where he taught for over thirty years. Currently, he is a professor at the Institute for Positive Psychology & Education at [Australian Catholic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Catholic_University). He received his BA from the [University of Belgrade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Belgrade) and his PhD from the [University of Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Denver).[^o2][^o3] He is known for his research on [intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intelligence),[^o4] including studies on the link between political ideology and intelligence,[^o5][^o6] the existence of [emotional intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emotional_intelligence),[^o7] and the psychology of [terrorism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terrorism).[^o8]

:::
