# Joachim Schwermer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joachim Schwermer"}

*Image: Joachim Schwermer.jpg.* Schwermer at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2010

**Joachim Schwermer** (26 May 1950, [Kulmbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kulmbach)[^o2]) is a German mathematician, specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Schwermer received his *[Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)* in 1969 at [Aloisiuskolleg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aloisiuskolleg) in [Bad Godesberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Godesberg) and then studied mathematics at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn). After graduating in 1974 with his *[Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom)*, he received in 1977 his [*Promotion*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Promotion_%28Germany%29) (Ph.D.) under [Günter Harder](wikipedia://en/50792386) with thesis *Eisensteinreihen und die Kohomologie von Kongruenzuntergruppen von $SL_n(\mathbb{Z})$*. In 1982 he received his *[Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation)* from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn).

## Career {#Career}

From 1986 he was a professor at the [Catholic University of Eichstätt-Ingolstadt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_University_of_Eichstätt-Ingolstadt), then at the [University of Düsseldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Düsseldorf),[^o6] and finally in the 2000s at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). During the academic year 1980–1981 Schwermer was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He is the scientific director at the [Erwin Schrödinger International Institute for Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_International_Institute_for_Mathematical_Physics).

## Research {#Research}

Schwermer's research deals with algebraic groups in number theory, [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), and [L-functions](wikipedia://en/245283). He has written essays on the history of mathematics, for example, about [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118), [Hermann Minkowski](wikipedia://en/193537), and [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 1987 he was awarded the [Gay-Lussac-Humboldt-Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gay-Lussac-Humboldt-Prize).
