# Ed Perkins

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ed Perkins"}

**Edwin Arend Perkins** (born 1953)[^o3] is a Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), including the analysis of [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) and the applications to probability of [non-standard analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-standard_analysis).[^o4] He is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of mathematics and statistics at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia).[^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Perkins received a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 1975 from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).[^o7] He obtained his PhD in 1979 under the supervision of Frank Bardsley Knight at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) with a dissertation titled 'A Nonstandard Approach to Brownian Local Time'.

He came to the University of British Columbia as a postdoctoral researcher in 1979, and became an assistant professor there in 1982. He was promoted to associate professor in 1985 and full professor in 1989.[^o7] He was given a tier 1 [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in Probability in 2001.[^o9]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Perkins was elected to the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) (FRSC) in 1988[^o12] and to the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society) (FRS) in 2007.[^o4]

He was the 1983 recipient of the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize),[^o11] the 2002 recipient of the [Jeffery–Williams Prize](wikipedia://en/18739069) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055)[^o4] and (with [John McKay](wikipedia://en/4583624)) one of two recipients of the 2003 [CRM-Fields-PIMS prize](wikipedia://en/19284033).[^o12]

In 2019 the University of Illinois gave him their Mathematics Alumni Award for Outstanding Professional Achievement.[^o13]
