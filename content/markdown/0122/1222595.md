# Tame group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tame group"}

In [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a **tame group** is a certain kind of [group](wikipedia://en/19447) defined in [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858).

:::

Formally, we define a **bad field** as a structure of the form (*K*, *T*), where *K* is an [algebraically closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraically_closed) [field](wikipedia://en/10603) and *T* is an [infinite](wikipedia://en/20110824), proper, distinguished [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of *K*, such that (*K*, *T*) is of finite [Morley rank](wikipedia://en/1401941) in its full language. A group *G* is then called a **tame group** if no bad field is [interpretable](wikipedia://en/621176) in *G*.
