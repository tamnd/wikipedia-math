# M-325

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="M-325"}

In the [history of cryptography](wikipedia://en/520066), **M-325**, also known as **SIGFOY**,[^o4] was an American [rotor machine](wikipedia://en/196223) designed by [William F. Friedman](wikipedia://en/175115) and built in 1944.[^o5] Between 1944 and 1946, more than 1,100 machines were deployed within the [United States Foreign Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Foreign_Service). Its use was discontinued in 1946 because of faults in operation. Friedman applied for a patent on the M-325 on 11 August 1944;[^o6] it was and was granted on 17 March 1959 (US patent #2,877,565).

:::

Like the [Enigma](wikipedia://en/9256), the M-325 contains three intermediate rotors and a reflecting rotor.
