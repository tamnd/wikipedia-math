# Professor of Statistical Science (Cambridge)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Professor of Statistical Science"}

The **Professorship of Statistical Science** is a [professorship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Professorships_at_the_University_of_Cambridge) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o2] It was established in 1994 as the third professorship within the Cambridge [Statistical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge).[^o3]

:::

## List of Professors of Statistical Science {#List_of_Professors_of_Statistical_Science}

- 1994–1996, [Richard L. Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_L._Smith)
- 2002–2015, [L. C. G. Rogers](wikipedia://en/22806119)
- 2017–present, [Richard Samworth](wikipedia://en/25180986)
