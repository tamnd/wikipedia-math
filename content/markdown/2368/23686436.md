# Jerzy Perzanowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jerzy Perzanowski"}

*Image: Jerzy Perzanowski (2009).jpg.* Jerzy Perzanowski

**Jerzy Perzanowski** (April 23, 1943 in [Aix-les-Bains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aix-les-Bains) – May 17, 2009 in [Bydgoszcz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bydgoszcz)), was a Polish [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logician) and [ontologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontologist), Professor of Logic to the [University of Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toruń) (Poland) from 1992 to 2004. Founder of the Polish review *Logic and Logical Philosophy*, his main contributions are to the development of [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365), [paraconsistent logic](wikipedia://en/421085) and ontology.

:::

- *Art of Philosophy. A Selection of Jerzy Perzanowski's Works* Frankfurt: Ontos Verlag, 2011.
