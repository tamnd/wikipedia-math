# Pseudo Jacobi polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pseudo Jacobi polynomials"}

In mathematics, the term **Pseudo Jacobi polynomials** was introduced by Lesky[^o2] for one of three finite sequences of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) y.[^o3] Since they form an orthogonal subset of Routh polynomials[^o4] it seems consistent to refer to them as **Romanovski-Routh** **polynomials**,[^o5] by analogy with the terms **Romanovski-Bessel** and **Romanovski-Jacobi** used by Lesky. As shown by Askey [^o6] for two other sequencesth is finite sequence [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) of can be expressed in terms of [Jacobi polynomials](wikipedia://en/30863587) of imaginary argument. In following Raposo et al.[^o7] they are often referred to simply as **Romanovski** **polynomials**.

:::
