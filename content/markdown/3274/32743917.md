# Paul Meier (statistician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Meier"}

**Paul Meier** (July 24, 1924 – August 7, 2011)[^o3] was a [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who promoted the use of [randomized trials](wikipedia://en/2940730) in medicine.[^o4][^o5]

:::

Meier is known for introducing, with [Edward L. Kaplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_L._Kaplan), the [Kaplan–Meier estimator](wikipedia://en/3168650),[^o6][^o7] a [nonparametric estimator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonparametric_estimator) of [survival functions](wikipedia://en/30876757) that accounts for changes in sample size over time.[^o8]

Meier's 1957 evaluation of polio vaccine practices published in *Science* has been described as influential, and the Kaplan–Meier method is thought to have indirectly extended tens of thousands of lives.[^o4]
