# Rogers polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rogers polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Rogers polynomials**, also called the **Rogers–Askey–Ismail polynomials** or **continuous *q*-ultraspherical polynomials**, are a family of [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) introduced by [Leonard James Rogers](wikipedia://en/9024921)[^n1][^n2][^n3] in the course of his work on the [Rogers–Ramanujan identities](wikipedia://en/2324711). They are [*q*-analogs](wikipedia://en/1619396) of [ultraspherical polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ultraspherical_polynomials), and are the [Macdonald polynomials](wikipedia://en/10354285) for the special case of the *A*<sub>1</sub> [affine root system](wikipedia://en/32702474).[^n4]

:::

The Rogers polynomials can be defined in terms of the [*q*-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by

$$C_n(x;\beta|q) = \frac{(\beta;q)_n}{(q;q)_n}e^{in\theta} {}_2\phi_1(q^{-n},\beta;\beta^{-1}q^{1-n};q,q\beta^{-1}e^{-2i\theta})$$

where ${}_2\phi_1$ is the [basic hypergeometric series](wikipedia://en/2233526) and $x=\cos\theta$.

(Askey Ismail 1983) and (Gasper Rahman 2004, section 7.4) discuss the properties of Rogers polynomials in detail.
