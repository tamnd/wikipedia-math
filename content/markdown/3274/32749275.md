# Krein–Rutman theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Krein–Rutman theorem"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the **Krein–Rutman theorem** is a generalisation of the [Perron–Frobenius theorem](wikipedia://en/1540333) to infinite-dimensional [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989).[^o2] It was proved by [Krein](wikipedia://en/1190470) and [Rutman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Aronovich_Rutman) in 1948.[^o3]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $X$ be a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989), and let $K\subset X$ be a [convex cone](wikipedia://en/3558266) such that $K\cap -K = \{0\}$, and $K-K$ is [dense](wikipedia://en/23523594) in $X$, i.e. the closure of the set $\{u - v : u,\,v\in K\}=X$. $K$ is also known as a **total cone**. Let $T:X\to X$ be a non-zero [compact operator](wikipedia://en/577441), and assume that it is *positive*, meaning that $T(K)\subset K$, and that its [spectral radius](wikipedia://en/386169) $r(T)$ is strictly positive.

Then $r(T)$ is an [eigenvalue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvalue) of $T$ with positive [eigenvector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eigenvector), meaning that there exists $u\in K\setminus {0}$ such that $T(u)=r(T)u$.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="De Pagter's theorem"}

## De Pagter's theorem {#De_Pagter's_theorem}

If the positive operator $T$ is assumed to be ideal *irreducible*, namely, there is no ideal $J\ne0$ of $X$ such that $TJ \subset J$, then de Pagter's theorem[^o8] asserts that $r(T)>0$.

Therefore, for ideal irreducible operators the assumption $r(T)>0$ is not needed.

:::
