# Larry A. Wasserman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Larry A. Wasserman"}

**Larry Alan Wasserman** (born 1959) is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians)-American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and a professor in the Department of Statistics & Data Science and the Machine Learning Department at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Wasserman received his Ph.D. from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 1988 under the supervision of [Robert Tibshirani](wikipedia://en/31404681).

He received the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) in 1999[^o4] and the [CRM](wikipedia://en/3505436)-[SSC](wikipedia://en/15572327) Prize in 2002.[^o5]

He was elected a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1996,[^o6] of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2004,[^o7] and of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 2011.[^o8] He was elected to [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in May, 2016.[^o9]

## Selected works {#Selected_works}

Wasserman has written many research papers about [nonparametric inference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonparametric_inference), asymptotic theory, causality, and applications of statistics to astrophysics, bioinformatics, and genetics. He has also written two advanced statistics textbooks, *All of Statistics*[^o11] and *All of Nonparametric Statistics*.[^o12]

- \2004. *All of Statistics: A Concise Course in Statistical Inference*. Springer-Verlag, New York.
