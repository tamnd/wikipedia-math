# Quintuple product identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quintuple product identity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) the Watson **quintuple product identity** is an infinite product identity introduced by (txt) and rediscovered by (Bailey 1951) and (Gordon 1961). It is analogous to the [Jacobi triple product identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobi_triple_product_identity), and is the [Macdonald identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald_identity) for a certain non-reduced [affine root system](wikipedia://en/32702474). It is related to Euler's [pentagonal number theorem](wikipedia://en/340294).

:::

::: {#o6 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

$$\prod_{n\ge 1} (1-s^n)(1-s^nt)(1-s^{n-1}t^{-1})(1-s^{2n-1}t^2)(1-s^{2n-1}t^{-2}) = \sum_{n\in \mathbf{Z}}s^{(3n^2+n)/2}(t^{3n}-t^{-3n-1})$$

:::
