# Rogers–Szegő polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rogers–Szegő polynomials"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Rogers–Szegő polynomials** are a family of [orthogonal polynomials on the unit circle](wikipedia://en/32704652) introduced by [Gábor Szegő](wikipedia://en/46065),[^n1] who was inspired by the [continuous *q*-Hermite polynomials](wikipedia://en/32848732) studied by [Leonard James Rogers](wikipedia://en/9024921). They are given by

:::

$$h_n(x;q) = \sum_{k=0}^n\frac{(q;q)_n}{(q;q)_k(q;q)_{n-k}}x^k,$$

where $(q;q)_n$ is the descending [*q*-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717). The Rogers-Szegő polynomials satisfy the recurrence relation[^n2]

$$h_{n+1}(x;q) = (1+x)h_n(x;q) + x(q^n-1)h_{n-1}(x;q)$$

with $h_0(x;q)=1$ and $h_1(x;q)=1+x$.
