# Xihong Lin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xihong Lin"}

Lin Xihong

*Image: Xihong Lin, Harvard University faculty.jpg.* Xihong Lin in 2024

**Xihong Lin** () is a [Chinese–American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese–American) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) known for her contributions to [mixed models](wikipedia://en/5431921), [nonparametric](wikipedia://en/4536068) and [semiparametric regression](wikipedia://en/4536125), and [statistical genetics](wikipedia://en/1590736) and genomics. , she is the Henry Pickering Walcott Professor and Chair of the Department of [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) at [Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_T.H._Chan_School_of_Public_Health) and Coordinating Director of the Program in Quantitative [Genomics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomics).

:::

Lin received the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483) in 2006,[^o2] the Spiegelman award of the outstanding health statistician from the [American Public Health Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Public_Health_Association) in 2002, and the [MERIT Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MERIT_Award)[^o3] from the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute) (2007-2016).

Lin was elected a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2000[^o4] and of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 2007, and an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) in 2006.[^o5][^o6] She won the [Florence Nightingale David Award](wikipedia://en/58964720) of the [Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies](wikipedia://en/44155344) in 2017 "for leadership and collaborative research in statistical genetics and bioinformatics; and for passion and dedication in mentoring students and young statisticians".[^o7] Lin was elected to the [National Academy of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Medicine) in 2018[^o8] and [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2023.[^o9]

Lin received her BSc from [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) in 1989 and her PhD in biostatistics from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1994, where her supervisor was [Norman Breslow](wikipedia://en/10816081). Her dissertation was *Bias Correction in Generalized Linear Mixed Models*.
