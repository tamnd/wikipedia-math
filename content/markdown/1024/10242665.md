# Chia-Chiao Lin

Lin Chia-Chiao

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Chia-Chiao Lin"}

**Chia-Chiao Lin** (; 7 July 1916 – 13 January 2013) was a Chinese-born American [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [Institute Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_institute_professors_at_the_Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o4]

:::

Lin made major contributions to the theory of [hydrodynamic stability](wikipedia://en/21757046), [turbulent flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turbulent_flow), [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and [astrophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysics).

## Biography {#Biography}

Lin was born in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) with ancestral roots in [Fuzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuzhou). In 1937 Lin graduated from the department of physics, [Tsinghua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsinghua_University) in Beijing.

After graduation he was a [teaching assistant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_assistant) in the Tsinghua University physics department. In 1939 Lin won a [Boxer Indemnity Scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boxer_Indemnity_Scholarship) and was initially supported to study in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom). However, due to [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Lin and several others were sent to [North America](wikipedia://en/21139) by ship. Unluckily, Lin's ship was stopped in [Kobe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kobe), [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire_of_Japan), and all students had to return to China.

In 1940, Lin finally reached [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) and studied at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) from which he earned his M.Sc. In 1941. Lin continued his studies in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and received his PhD from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) in 1944 under [Theodore von Kármán](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theodore_von_Kármán). His PhD thesis provided an analytic method to solve a problem in the stability of parallel shearing flows, which was the subject of [Werner Heisenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Heisenberg)'s PhD thesis.[^o6]

Lin also taught at Caltech between 1943 and 1945. He taught at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) between 1945 and 1947. Lin joined the faculty of the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1947. Lin was promoted to professor at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 1953 and became an [Institute Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_Professor) of [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 1963. He was President of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) from 1972 to 1974.[^o7] Lin retired from MIT in 1987.

In 2002, he moved back to China and helped found the Zhou Pei-Yuan Center for Applied Mathematics (ZCAM) at Tsinghua University. He died in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) in 2013, aged 96.[^o8]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

During his career Lin has received many prizes and awards, including:

- The first [Fluid Dynamics Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_Dynamics_Prize) (from the [American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Physical_Society), in 1979)[^o10]
- The 1976 NAS Award in Applied Mathematics and Numerical Analysis[^o11]
- The 1975 [Timoshenko Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoshenko_Medal)[^o12]
- The 1973 [Otto Laporte Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otto_Laporte_Award)
- Caltech's Distinguished Alumni Award[^o13]

Lin was a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences),[^o14] the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences),[^o15] and the [American Philosophical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Philosophical_Society),[^o16] cited in the [American Men and Women of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Men_and_Women_of_Science). and a Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science). Lin was elected Academician of [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica) in 1958, and became a Foreign Member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1994.
