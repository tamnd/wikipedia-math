# Kali S. Banerjee

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kali S. Banerjee"}

**Kali S. Banerjee** (September 17, 1914 – April 9, 2002) was a math and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) expert, and a professor of statistics at the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

He was born in [Dhaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dhaka), (now in [Bangladesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh)) in 1914. He earned his bachelor's degree in mathematics and his master's and doctoral degrees in statistics from the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta).

## Career {#Career}

In 1962, Kali S. Banerjee moved to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and he was naturalized in 1974, and joined as a faculty of statistics at University of Delaware. Before joining the University of Delaware, he taught at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and at [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University) Dr. Banerjee received the university's excellence in teaching award in 1972 and was named a fellow of [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) at [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) in 1975. He wrote many books such as *The Cost Of Learning Index*. He wrote about 15 study books on Statistics, about 13 books on Economics, in all writing about 40 books through his life.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He and his wife raised two children, a daughter, Swapna and a son, Deb.
