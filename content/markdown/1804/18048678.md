# Robert C. Elston

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert C. Elston"}

**Robert C. Elston** (born 4 February 1932) is a British born [statistical geneticist](wikipedia://en/1590736) and distinguished professor emeritus at [Case Western Reserve University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Case_Western_Reserve_University). He is one of the eponyms of the [Elston–Stewart algorithm](wikipedia://en/17440918) and [Haseman–Elston regression](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haseman–Elston_regression).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Elston was born in [London, England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London,_England) in 1932.

In the 1970s he worked with John Stewart to create the [Elston–Stewart algorithm](wikipedia://en/17440918) which enables researchers to estimate the likelihood of genotype data given a [pedigree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pedigree_chart).[^o4]

In 1980s Elston was working at [the Louisiana State University Medical School](https://www.medschool.lsuhsc.edu/) in [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans) working on statistical genetics. Elston left LSU for Case Western Reserve University in Cleveland in 1995 taking other staff with him.[^o5]
