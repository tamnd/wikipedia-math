# Peter M. Bentler

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter M. Bentler"}

**Peter M. Bentler** is an American psychologist, statistician, and distinguished professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).

:::

In [multivariate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis) and [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982), Bentler is the developer of the [structural equation modeling](wikipedia://en/2007748) software EQS.[^o3] Bentler received a doctorate in clinical psychology from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1964.[^o4] His publications have over 300,000 citations .[^o5] In 2014, he was awarded the Psychometric Society Career Award.[^o6] In 2015, he was elected [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o7]
