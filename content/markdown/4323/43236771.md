# Suslin algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suslin algebra"}

In mathematics, a **Suslin algebra** is a [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/3959) that is [complete](wikipedia://en/1839944), [atomless](wikipedia://en/998824), countably [distributive](wikipedia://en/103118), and satisfies the [countable chain condition](wikipedia://en/1550771). They are named after [Mikhail Yakovlevich Suslin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Yakovlevich_Suslin).[^o2]

:::

The existence of Suslin algebras is independent of the axioms of [ZFC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ZFC), and is equivalent to the existence of [Suslin trees](wikipedia://en/5028195) or [Suslin lines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suslin_line).[^o3]
