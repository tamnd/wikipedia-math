# Cyclic algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyclic algebra"}

In algebra, a **cyclic division algebra** is one of the basic examples of a [division algebra](wikipedia://en/26549625) over a field and plays a key role in the theory of [central simple algebras](wikipedia://en/404082).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *A* be a finite-dimensional [central simple algebra](wikipedia://en/404082) over a field *F*. Then *A* is said to be **cyclic** if it contains a [strictly maximal subfield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strictly_maximal_subfield) *E* such that *E*/*F* is a [cyclic field extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_field_extension) (i.e., the [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) is a [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327)).

:::
