# Monomial conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monomial conjecture"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), a field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **monomial conjecture** of [Melvin Hochster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melvin_Hochster) says the following:[^o2]

:::

Let *A* be a [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/56129) [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) of [Krull dimension](wikipedia://en/56079) *d* and let *x*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *x*<sub>*d*</sub> be a system of parameters for *A* (so that *A*/(*x*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *x*<sub>*d*</sub>) is an [Artinian ring](wikipedia://en/489273)). Then for all positive [integers](wikipedia://en/14563) *t*, we have

$$x_1^t \cdots x_d^t \not\in (x_1^{t+1},\dots,x_d^{t+1}). \,$$

The statement can be relatively easily shown in [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) zero.
