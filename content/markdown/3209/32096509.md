# Olaus Henrici

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Olaus Henrici"}

**Olaus Magnus Friedrich Erdmann Henrici**, [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) (9 March 1840, [Meldorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meldorf), [Duchy of Holstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duchy_of_Holstein) – 10 August 1918, [Chandler's Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandler's_Ford), [Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampshire), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England)) was a German mathematician who became a professor in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London).

:::

After three years as an apprentice in engineering, Henrici entered [Karlsruhe Polytechnium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology) where he came under the influence of [Alfred Clebsch](wikipedia://en/1369957) who encouraged him in mathematics. He then went to Heidelberg where he studied with [Otto Hesse](wikipedia://en/740995). Henrici attained his Dr. phil. degree on 6 June 1863 at [University of Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Heidelberg). He continued his studies in Berlin with [Karl Weierstrass](wikipedia://en/93459) and [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450). He was briefly [docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent) of mathematics and physics at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kiel), but ran into financial difficulties.[^o3]

Henrici moved to London in 1865 where he worked as a private [tutor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutor). In 1869 Hesse introduced him to [J. J. Sylvester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._J._Sylvester) who in turn brought him into contact with [Arthur Cayley](wikipedia://en/311034), [William Kingdon Clifford](wikipedia://en/250820), and [Thomas Archer Hirst](wikipedia://en/14718360). It was Hirst that gave him some work at [University College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_London). Henrici also became a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Bedford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bedford_College_%28London%29). When Hirst fell ill, Henrici filled his position at University College. He held the position until 1884, turning to [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) after 1880.

From 1882 to 1884 Henrici was President of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097). In 1884 he moved to [Central Technical College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Technical_College) where he directed a Laboratory of [Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) which included calculating machines, [planimeters](wikipedia://en/1103376), moment integrators, and a harmonic analyzer.[^o4]

Henrici was impressed by the work of [Robert Stawell Ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Stawell_Ball) in [screw theory](wikipedia://en/1318037) as presented in a German textbook by Gravelius.[^o5] In 1890 Henrici wrote a [book review](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_review) for [Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nature_%28journal%29) outlining the program of the theory.[^o6]

In 1911 he retired and took up [gardening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gardening) at [Chandler's Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandler's_Ford) in [Hampshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hampshire).
