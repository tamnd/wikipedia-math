# Jens Carsten Jantzen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jens Carsten Jantzen"}

*Image: Jens Carsten Jantzen.jpg.* Jantzen at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2007

**Jens Carsten Jantzen** (born 1948) is a German mathematician and professor emeritus at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) working on [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924). He introduced the [Jantzen filtration](wikipedia://en/32084423) and [translation functors](wikipedia://en/32176298).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Jantzen was born in 1948 in [Störtewerkerkoog](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stedesand), [Nordfriesland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nordfriesland).[^o3] He received his PhD from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1973 under the supervision of [Jacques Tits](wikipedia://en/219570).[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

In the late 1970s, Jantzen and [Gregg Zuckerman](wikipedia://en/10822168) independently introduced [translation functors](wikipedia://en/32176298).[^o5][^o6] In 1979, he introduced the [Jantzen filtration](wikipedia://en/32084423).[^o6]

His doctoral students include [Wolfgang Soergel](wikipedia://en/68408304).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
