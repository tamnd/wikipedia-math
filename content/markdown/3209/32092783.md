# Nagayoshi Iwahori

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nagayoshi Iwahori"}

**Nagayoshi Iwahori** was a Japanese mathematician who worked on [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924) over [local fields](wikipedia://en/145375) who introduced [Iwahori–Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/521808) and [Iwahori subgroups](wikipedia://en/32092579).

:::
