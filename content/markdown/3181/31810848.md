# Alexei Kostrikin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexei Kostrikin"}

**Alexei Ivanovich Kostrikin** (Алексей Иванович Кострикин) (12 February 1929 – 22 September 2000) was a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Kostrikin graduated from the [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1952. In 1960, he earned a [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) degree under [Igor Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815) at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) with a thesis on the [Burnside problem](wikipedia://en/151452). He became a faculty member at Moscow State University in 1963.[^o5]

## Prizes {#Prizes}

Kostrikin was awarded the [USSR State Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize) in 1968 for his research on [finite groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_groups) and [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) and was elected the corresponding member of the [USSR Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1976.

## Books {#Books}

Alexei Kostrikin published many scientific articles, books and textbooks, including a university textbook about [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) *Introduction to algebra*, translated into [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) and other languages.
