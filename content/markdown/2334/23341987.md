# Sarti surface

*Image: Sarti surface.png.*

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sarti surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Sarti surface** is a degree-12 [nodal surface](wikipedia://en/46573101) with 600 nodes, found by [Alessandra Sarti](wikipedia://en/76985673) in 1999 and published by her in 2001. The maximal possible number of nodes of a degree-12 surface is not known (as of 2015), though [Yoichi Miyaoka](wikipedia://en/22717073) showed that it is at most 645.

:::

Sarti has also found sextic, octic and dodectic nodal surfaces with high numbers of nodes and high degrees of symmetry.
