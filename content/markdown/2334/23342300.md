# Châtelet surface

*Image: Chatelet surface.png.* Some snapshots showing the real points of the Châtelet surface with *P*(*x*) = *x*<sup>3</sup> – 5*x*<sup>2</sup> – 6*x*. Axes: *x* = red, *y* = yellow, *z* = blue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Châtelet surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Châtelet surface** is a [rational surface](wikipedia://en/2380148) studied by (txt) given by an equation

:::

$$y^2-az^2=P(x), \,$$

where *P* has degree 3 or 4. They are [conic bundles](wikipedia://en/23214145).
