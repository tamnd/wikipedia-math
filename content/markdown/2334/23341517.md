# Segre surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Segre surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Segre surface**, studied by (txt) and (txt), is an intersection of two [quadrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadric_surface) in 4-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135). They are [rational surfaces](wikipedia://en/2380148) isomorphic to a [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350) [blown up](wikipedia://en/1817790) in 5 points with no 3 on a line, and are [del Pezzo surfaces](wikipedia://en/732323) of degree 4, and have 16 rational lines.

:::

The term "Segre surface" is also occasionally used for various other surfaces, such as a [quadric](wikipedia://en/145570) in 3-dimensional projective space (see [Segre embedding](wikipedia://en/1623980)), or the hypersurface

$$x_1 x_2 x_3 + x_2 x_3 x_4 + x_3 x_4 x_5 + x_4 x_5 x_1 + x_5 x_1 x_2 = 0. \,$$
