# Elliptic singularity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elliptic singularity"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), an **elliptic singularity** of a surface, introduced by Philip Wagreich in 1970, is a surface singularity such that the [arithmetic genus](wikipedia://en/1950390) of its [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) is 1.[^o2]

:::
