# Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem** is an extension of the [Kodaira vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/2988093), on the vanishing of coherent [cohomology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_group), to [logarithmic pairs](wikipedia://en/22991379), proved independently by [Viehweg](wikipedia://en/5358736)[^o2] and [Kawamata](wikipedia://en/47502077)[^o3] in 1982.

:::

The theorem states that if *L* is a [big](wikipedia://en/13024295#Big_line_bundles) [nef](wikipedia://en/3404309) [line bundle](wikipedia://en/361366) (for example, an [ample line bundle](wikipedia://en/1186896)) on a complex projective manifold with [canonical line bundle](wikipedia://en/734893) *K*, then the coherent cohomology groups *H*<sup>*i*</sup>(*L*⊗*K*) vanish for all positive *i*.
