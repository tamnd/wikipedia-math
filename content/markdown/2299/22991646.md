# Psychometrika

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Psychometrika"}

***Psychometrika*** is the official [journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) of the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500), a [professional body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professional_body) dedicated to [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982) and [quantitative psychology](wikipedia://en/3067624). The journal focuses on quantitative methods for the measurement and evaluation of [human behavior](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Human_behavior), including [statistical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_methods) and other mathematical techniques.[^o3] Past editors include [Marion Richardson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion_Webster_Richardson), [Dorothy Adkins](wikipedia://en/45295186), [Norman Cliff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Cliff), and [Willem J. Heiser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willem_J._Heiser). According to *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal had an [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 3.1 in 2024.[^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

In 1935, [LL Thurstone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Leon_Thurstone), [EL Thorndike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EL_Thorndike) and [JP Guilford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JP_Guilford) founded *Psychometrika* and also the [Psychometric Society](wikipedia://en/20788500).

## Editors-in-chief {#Editors-in-chief}
