# Ken Stroud

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ken Stroud"}

**Kenneth Arthur Stroud** (; [Richmond, Surrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richmond,_Surrey), 28 September 1908 – [Hertfordshire township](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hertfordshire_township), February 3, 2000)[^o2] was a mathematician and Principal Lecturer in Mathematics at [Lanchester Polytechnic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry_University) in [Coventry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coventry), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England).[^o3][^o4] He is most widely known as the author of several mathematics textbooks, especially the very popular *Engineering Mathematics*.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Stroud held a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) and a [DipEd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DipEd).[^o6]

## Work {#Work}

Stroud was an innovator in [programmed learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Programmed_learning) and the identification of precise [learning outcomes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learning_outcome),[^o8][^o9] and Nigel Steele calls his textbook *Engineering Mathematics*, based on the programmed learning approach, "one of the most successful mathematics textbooks ever published."[^o10]

He died in February 2000, aged 91.[^o10]
