# Ramanujam vanishing theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramanujam vanishing theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Ramanujam vanishing theorem** is an extension of the [Kodaira vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/2988093) due to (txt), that in particular gives conditions for the vanishing of first [cohomology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_group) of [coherent sheaves](wikipedia://en/603780) on a surface. The [Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/22999259) generalizes it.

:::
