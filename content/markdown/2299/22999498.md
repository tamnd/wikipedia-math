# Mumford vanishing theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mumford vanishing theorem"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Mumford vanishing theorem** proved by [Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594)[^o2] in 1967 states that if *L* is a semi-ample [invertible sheaf](wikipedia://en/581859) with [Iitaka dimension](wikipedia://en/13024295) at least 2 on a [complex projective manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_projective_manifold), then

:::

$$H^i(X,L^{-1})=0\text{ for }i = 0,1.\$$

The Mumford vanishing theorem is related to the [Ramanujam vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/22999216), and is generalized by the [Kawamata–Viehweg vanishing theorem](wikipedia://en/22999259).
