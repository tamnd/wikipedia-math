# Pappus graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Pappus graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Pappus graph** is a [bipartite](wikipedia://en/244431), 3-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821), [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 18 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 27 edges, formed as the [Levi graph](wikipedia://en/1396888) of the [Pappus configuration](wikipedia://en/2414085). It is named after [Pappus of Alexandria](wikipedia://en/509300), an ancient [Greek mathematician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_mathematics) who is believed to have discovered the "hexagon theorem" describing the Pappus configuration. All the [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508), [distance-regular graphs](wikipedia://en/7768943) are known; the Pappus graph is one of the 13 such graphs.[^o4]

:::

The Pappus graph has [rectilinear crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298594) 5, and is the smallest cubic graph with that crossing number . It has [girth](wikipedia://en/12013) 6, diameter 4, radius 4, [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3 and is both 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph). It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o5] The graph is [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o6]

The Pappus graph has a [chromatic polynomial](wikipedia://en/1460126) equal to: $\begin{aligned} (x-1)x(&x^{16} - 26x^{15} \\ &+ 325x^{14} - 2600x^{13} \\ &+ 14950x^{12}- 65762x^{11} \\ &+ 229852x^{10} - 653966x^9 \\ &+ 1537363x^8 - 3008720x^7 \\ &+ 4904386x^6 - 6609926x^5 \\ &+ 7238770x^4 - 6236975x^3 \\ &+ 3989074x^2 - 1690406x + 356509) \end{aligned}$

The name "Pappus graph" has also been used to refer to a related nine-vertex graph,[^o7] with a vertex for each point of the Pappus configuration and an edge for every pair of points on the same line; this nine-vertex graph is 6-regular, is the [complement graph](wikipedia://en/1175649) of the union of three disjoint [triangle graphs](wikipedia://en/27071812), and is the complete tripartite graph K<sub>3,3,3</sub>. The first Pappus graph can be embedded in the torus to form a self-[Petrie dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrie_dual) [regular map](wikipedia://en/21533914) with nine hexagonal faces; the second, to form a regular map with 18 triangular faces. The two regular toroidal maps are dual to each other.

::: {#o9 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The automorphism group of the Pappus graph is a group of order 216. It acts transitively on the vertices, on the edges and on the arcs of the graph. Therefore the Pappus graph is a [symmetric graph](wikipedia://en/1577896). It has automorphisms that take any vertex to any other vertex and any edge to any other edge. According to the *Foster census*, the Pappus graph, referenced as F018A, is the only cubic symmetric graph on 18 vertices.[^o10][^o11]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Pappus graph is $(x-3) x^4 (x+3) (x^2-3)^6$. It is the only graph with this characteristic polynomial, making it a graph determined by its spectrum.
