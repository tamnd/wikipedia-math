# Luigi Fantappiè

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Luigi Fantappiè"}

**Luigi Fantappiè** (15 September 1901 – 28 July 1956) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for work in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and for creating the theory of [analytic functionals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_functional): he was a student and follower of [Vito Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Volterra). Later in life, he proposed scientific theories of sweeping scope.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Luigi Fantappiè was born in [Viterbo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viterbo), and studied at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), graduating in mathematics in 1922. After time spent abroad, he was offered a chair by the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence) in 1926, and a year later by the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo). He spent the years 1934 to 1939 in the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil) collaborating with Benedito Castrucci notorious Italian-Brazilian mathematician. In 1939 he was offered a chair at the [University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_La_Sapienza).

In 1941 he discovered that negative entropy has qualities that are associated with life: The cause of processes driven by negative energy lies in the future, exactly such as living beings work for a better day tomorrow. A process that is driven by negative entropy will increase order with time, such as all forms of life tend to do. This was a very controversial view at the time and not at all accepted by his colleagues. His findings indicate that negative entropy is associated with life in the same way as consciousness is. Consciousness could be a process based on negative entropy. In 1942 he put forth a unified theory of physics and biology and the [syntropy](wikipedia://en/361356) concept. In 1952 he started to work on a unified physical theory called *projective relativity*, for which, he asserted, [special relativity](wikipedia://en/26962) was a limiting case. [Giuseppe Arcidiacono](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giuseppe_Arcidiacono) worked with him on this theory.[^o4]

He tried to combine his scientific research with his Catholic faith.[^o5]
