# Accidental necessity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Accidental necessity"}

In [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) and [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), **accidental necessity**, often stated in its [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) form, *necessitas per accidens*, refers to the necessity attributed to the past by certain views of time. It is a controversial concept: its supporters argue that it has intuitive validity while others contest it creates a contradiction in terms by positing such a thing as a "contingent necessity." It is especially important in contemporary discussions of logical and theological [fatalism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fatalism).

:::
