# Dubins–Schwarz theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dubins–Schwarz theorem"}

In the theory of [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536), the **Dubins–Schwarz theorem** (or **Dambis–Dubins–Schwarz theorem**) is a theorem that says all continuous [local martingales](wikipedia://en/7302795) and martingales are time-changed [Brownian motions](wikipedia://en/4436).

:::

The theorem was proven in 1965 by [Lester Dubins](wikipedia://en/36027272) and [Gideon E. Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gideon_E._Schwarz)[^o2] and independently in the same year by [K. E. Dambis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K._E._Dambis), a doctoral student of [Eugene Dynkin](wikipedia://en/1351540).[^o3][^o4]

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Dubins–Schwarz theorem"}

## Dubins–Schwarz theorem {#Dubins–Schwarz_theorem}

Let

- $\mathcal{M}_{0,\operatorname{loc}}^{c}$ be the space of $\mathcal{F}_t$-adapted continuous local martingales $M=(M_t)_{t\geq 0}$ with $M_0=0$.
- $\langle M\rangle$ be the [quadratic variation](wikipedia://en/2143889).

::: {#o8 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

### Statement {#Statement}

Let $M\in \mathcal{M}_{0,\operatorname{loc}}^{c}$ and $\langle M\rangle_{\infty}=\infty$ and define for all $t\geq 0$ the time-changes (i.e. [stopping times](wikipedia://en/1202193))[^o9]

$$T_t=\inf \{s:\langle M\rangle_s>t\}.$$

Then $B:=(B_t):=(M_{T_t})$ is a $\mathcal{F}_{T_t}$-Brownian motion and $(M_t)=(B_{\langle M\rangle_t})$.

:::

### Remarks {#Remarks}

- The condition $\langle M\rangle_{\infty}=\infty$ guarantees that the underlying probability space is rich enough so that the Brownian motion exists. If one removes this conditions one might have to use [enlargement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enlargement_%28probability_theory%29) of the filtered probability space.
- $B$ is not a $\mathcal{F}_{t}$-Brownian motion.
- $(T_t)$ are [almost surely](wikipedia://en/351908) finite since $\langle M\rangle_{\infty}=\infty$.

:::
