# Potential good reduction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Potential good reduction"}

In mathematics, **potential good reduction** is a property of the [reduction modulo a prime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduction_modulo_a_prime) or, more generally, [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928), of an [algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

"Good reduction" refers to the reduced variety having the same properties as the original, for example, an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) having the same [genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus_of_a_curve), or a [smooth variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_variety) remaining smooth. "*Potential* good reduction" refers to the situation over a sufficiently large finite extension of the field of definition.

:::

## Equivalent formulations {#Equivalent_formulations}

For [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225), potential good reduction is equivalent to the [j-invariant](wikipedia://en/451988) being an [algebraic integer](wikipedia://en/98981).
