# Octadecahedron

*Image: Closo-undecaborate(11)-dianion-from-xtal-3D-bs-17.png.* [Ball-and-stick model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ball-and-stick_model) of the octadecahedral *closo*-undecaborate ion, \[B<sub>11</sub>H<sub>11</sub>\]<sup>2−</sup>, as found in the [crystal structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_structure) of the benzyltriethylammonium salt.[^o1]

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Octadecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), an **octadecahedron** (or **octakaidecahedron**) is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) with 18 [faces](wikipedia://en/149926). No octadecahedron is [regular](wikipedia://en/333299); hence, the name does not commonly refer to one specific polyhedron.

:::

In [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), "*the* octadecahedron" commonly refers to a specific structure with C<sub>2v</sub> symmetry, the [edge-contracted icosahedron](wikipedia://en/40146883), formed from a regular [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) with one edge [contracted](wikipedia://en/3758115). It is the shape of the [closo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closo)-boranate ion \[[B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boron)<sub>11</sub>[H](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen)<sub>11</sub>\]<sup>2−</sup>.

## Convex {#Convex}

There are 107,854,282,197,058 topologically distinct *convex* octadecahedra, excluding mirror images, having at least 11 vertices.[^o4] (Two polyhedra are "topologically distinct" if they have intrinsically different arrangements of faces and vertices, such that it is impossible to distort one into the other simply by changing the lengths of edges or the angles between edges or faces.)

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

### Examples {#Examples}

The most familiar octadecahedra are the [heptadecagonal](wikipedia://en/196890) [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045), [hexadecagonal](wikipedia://en/6440317) [prism](wikipedia://en/283007), and the [octagonal antiprism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octagonal_antiprism). The hexadecagonal prism and the octagonal antiprism are [uniform polyhedra](wikipedia://en/2537223), with [regular](wikipedia://en/333306) bases and [square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square_%28geometry%29) or [equilateral triangular](wikipedia://en/173285) sides. Four more octadecahedra are also found among the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034): the [square gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1128615), the [square orthobicupola](wikipedia://en/1128614), the [elongated square cupola](wikipedia://en/1128506) (also known as the diminished rhombicuboctahedron), and the [sphenomegacorona](wikipedia://en/1142743). Four Johnson solids have octadecahedral duals: the [elongated triangular orthobicupola](wikipedia://en/1196304), the [elongated triangular gyrobicupola](wikipedia://en/1196309), the [gyroelongated triangular bicupola](wikipedia://en/1196315), and the [triangular hebesphenorotunda](wikipedia://en/1170312). Among [near-miss Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/3881930), the [chamfered cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chamfered_cube) is one notable non-uniform octadecahedron.
