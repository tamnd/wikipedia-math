# Lehrbuch der Topologie

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lehrbuch der Topologie"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), ***Lehrbuch der Topologie*** (German for "textbook of topology") is a book by [Herbert Seifert](wikipedia://en/637024) and [William Threlfall](wikipedia://en/37303142), first published in 1934 and published in an English translation in 1980. It was one of the earliest [textbooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Textbook) on [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), and was the standard reference on this topic for many years.

:::

[Albert W. Tucker](wikipedia://en/935841) wrote a review.[^o2]
