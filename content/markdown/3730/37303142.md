# William Threlfall

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Threlfall"}

*Image: William Threlfall.jpg.* William Threlfall in 1936

**William Richard Maximilian Hugo Threlfall** (25 June 1888, in [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden) – 4 April 1949, in [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach)) was a British-born [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked on [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801). He was a coauthor of the standard textbook [Lehrbuch der Topologie](wikipedia://en/37302995).

:::

In 1933 he signed the *[Vow of allegiance of the Professors of the German Universities and High-Schools to Adolf Hitler and the National Socialistic State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vow_of_allegiance_of_the_Professors_of_the_German_Universities_and_High-Schools_to_Adolf_Hitler_and_the_National_Socialistic_State)*.
