# Vanaja Iyengar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vanaja Iyengar"}

**Vanaja Iyengar** (died 2001) was an Indian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [educationist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educationist)[^o3] and the founder vice-chancellor of [Sri Padmavati Mahila Visvavidyalayam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Padmavati_Mahila_Visvavidyalayam), [Tirupati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tirupati), in the south Indian state of [Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh).[^o4] She was one of the founders of the Andhra Mahila Sabha School of Informatics.[^o5] The [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) awarded her the fourth highest civilian honour of [Padma Shri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Shri) in 1987.[^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in the undivided [Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Pradesh), she completed her early education at [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad)[^o8] and obtained higher education in Mathematics from [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) in 1950, after which she visited Yugoslavia, Czechoslovakia and Hungary as a part of student forums.[^o9] Her career started as a member of faculty at [Osmania University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osmania_University) and worked in two of the colleges affiliated to the university, [University College for Women, Koti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_for_Women,_Koti) (Osmania Women's College) and [Nizam College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nizam_College).[^o9]

During her tenure at Osmania, Iyengar secured a doctoral degree in mathematics from the [University of Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delhi) in 1958. She served Osmania University as a reader, professor, head of the department of Mathematics department and the principal of the [University College for Women, Koti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_for_Women,_Koti)[^o8] and held the post of the vice-chancellor for a while.[^o9] When [Sri Padmavati Mahila Visvavidyalayam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Padmavati_Mahila_Visvavidyalayam), an all women university, was established in 1983, she was appointed as its vice-chancellor and continued at the post till 1986.[^o10] She was also one of the founder members of the Osmania University Teachers Association.[^o9] She was a life trustee of [Andhra Mahila Sabha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andhra_Mahila_Sabha) and she served organisation as its vice-president and the president, a post she held since 1994 till her death.[^o9] She is also credited with articles on the topic of education.[^o4][^o11]

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

In 1987, the [Government of India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_India) awarded Iyengar the civilian honour of [Padma Shri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Shri).[^o6] She received the Best Teacher award from the [Government of Andhra Pradesh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_of_Andhra_Pradesh) and was a fellow of the [Rajiv Gandhi Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rajiv_Gandhi_Foundation).[^o9] She died in 2001, survived by her husband, [Mohit Sen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mohit_Sen), a known communist intellectual, who also died two years later.[^o13]
