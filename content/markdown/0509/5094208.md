# Fritz Carlson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Fritz Carlson"}

**Fritz David Carlson** (23 July 1888 – 28 November 1952) was a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose work on [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) left a lasting mark on twentieth-century mathematics. After the death of [Torsten Carleman](wikipedia://en/6063370), he headed the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Born in [Vimmerby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vimmerby) on 23 July 1888, Fritz David Carlson completed his secondary schooling at [Linköping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linköping) in 1907 and went on to earn his doctorate at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University) in 1914 with a thesis on a class of [Taylor series](wikipedia://en/30448) whose [coefficients](wikipedia://en/51203) vary analytically with the index. He was appointed professor of descriptive geometry at the Royal Institute of Technology in Stockholm in 1920 and in 1928 took up the chair of higher analysis at the Stockholm College of Advanced Studies.[^o6]

From 1930 he served on the editorial board of *[Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708)*, and after the death of [Torsten Carleman](wikipedia://en/6063370) in early 1949 he was entrusted with the administration of the [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452) at [Djursholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Djursholm). Carlson's research ranged from the arithmetic properties of [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) to [Dirichlet series](wikipedia://en/393258) (an [infinite series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_series) with applications in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527)) and the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) (a function closely tied to the distribution of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666)), yielding theorems that remain standard references. He also authored a three-volume Swedish textbook series on elementary and [spatial geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatial_geometry) (published 1943–48) and for thirty years acted as examiner for the Swedish secondary-school [baccalaureate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor's_degree) examination.[^o6] [Germund Dahlquist](wikipedia://en/8663731), [Tord Ganelius](wikipedia://en/37360717), [Olof Hanner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olof_Hanner) and [Hans Rådström](wikipedia://en/34034780) were among his students.[^o7]

Carlson's contributions to [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) include [Carlson's theorem](wikipedia://en/4065365), [Carlson's zero-density theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlson's_zero-density_theorem) for the zeros of the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809), the [Pólya–Carlson theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya–Carlson_theorem) on [rational functions](wikipedia://en/361210), and [Carlson's inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlson's_inequality):[^o6][^o4]

$$\left( \sum_{n=1}^\infty |a_n|\right)^4 \leq \pi^2 \sum_{n=1}^\infty |a_n|^2 \, \sum_{n=1}^\infty n^2 |a_n|^2~.$$
