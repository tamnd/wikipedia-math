# Maria Adelaide Sneider

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Maria Adelaide Sneider"}

**Maria Adelaide Sneider** (6 December 1937 – 1 May 1989)[^o2] (also known as **Maria Adelaide Sneider Ludovici**, her second [surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surname) being "Ludovici")[^o4] was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [numerical](wikipedia://en/21506) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). She is known for her work on the [theory of electrostatic capacities](wikipedia://en/14368398) of non-smooth [closed](wikipedia://en/47279) [hypersurfaces](wikipedia://en/525101):[^o5] Apart from the development of precise estimates for the [numerical approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_approximation) of the electrostatic [capacity](wikipedia://en/140711#Capacitance_of_simple_systems) of the unit [cube](wikipedia://en/6285),[^o6] this work also led her to give a rigorous proof of [Green's identities](wikipedia://en/805700) for large classes of hypersurfaces with singularities,[^o7] and later to develop an accurate mathematical analysis of the [points effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Points_effect).[^o8] She is also known for her contributions to the [Dirichlet problem](wikipedia://en/571109) for [pluriharmonic functions](wikipedia://en/2519049) on the unit [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) of $\mathbb{C}^n.$[^o9]

:::

Classe di Scienze Fisiche, Matematiche e Naturali*. LXXXII (3–4). p. 145–150. mr:0264472. zbl:0189.42101. <http://www.bdim.eu/item?id=RLINA_1969_8_47_3-4_145_0>.
- Sneider Ludovici, Maria Adelaide. (1970). "Sulla capacità elettrostatica di una superficie chiusa". *Atti della Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei. Memorie. Classe di Scienze Fisiche, Matematiche e Naturali*. X (2). p. 97–215. zbl:0232.31010. An accurate analysis of the problem of calculation of capacitances of surfaces with singularities.
- Sneider Ludovici, M. A. (14 April 1972). "Alcune osservazioni sulle formule di quadratura approssimata". *Pontificia Academia Scientiarum Commentarii*. 2 (52). p. 1–16. <http://www.casinapioiv.va/content/dam/accademia/pdf/commentarii/2_52.pdf>, is an analysis of the numerical performance of several [one–dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimension_%28mathematics%29) [quadrature formulas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrature_formula).
- Fichera, Gaetano; Sneider Ludovici, Maria Adelaide. (13 May 1974). "Distribution de la charge electrique dans le voisinage des sommets et des aretes d'un cube". *Comptes rendus hebdomadaires des séances de l'Académie des Sciences*. 278. p. 1303–1306. mr:0351281. zbl:0282.35038. <http://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/bpt6k6236784g/f81.image>.
- Fichera, Gaetano; Sneider Ludovici, Maria Adelaide. (1976). "Trends in Applications of Pure Mathematics to Mechanics. A collection of papers presented at a conference at the University of Lecce, Italy, in May 1975". 2. Pitman Publishing. p. 63–90. isbn:0-273-00129-9. mr:483901. zbl:0355.65049. <https://archive.org/details/trendsinapplicat0000symp/page/63>.
- Fichera, Gaetano; Sneider, Maria A.; Wyman, Jeffreys. (1977). "On the existence of a steady state in a biological system". *Atti della Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei. Memorie. Classe di Scienze Fisiche, Matematiche e Naturali*. XIV (1). p. 1–26. zbl:0414.92004. A work presenting a complete interdisciplinary analysis of the stability of a system of [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753) containing a large number of parameters, modeling a biological system.
- Fichera, Gaetano; Sneider, Maria Adelaide; Wyman, Jeffreys. (1977a). "On the existence of a steady state in a biological system". *PNAS*. 74 (10). p. 4182–4184. doi:10.1073/pnas.74.10.4182. A short research announcement reporting the results detailed in (Fichera Sneider Wyman 1977).
- Sneider, Maria Adelaide. (1983). "Sul problema pluriarmonico in un campo sferico di per". *Atti della Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei. Rendiconti. Classe di Scienze Fisiche, Matematiche e Naturali*. LXXIV (6). p. 351–356. mr:0756715. zbl:0577.32017. <http://www.bdim.eu/item?id=RLIN_1983_8_74_6_351_0>. is Maria Adelaide Sneider's conclusive work on the [Dirichlet problem](wikipedia://en/571109) for pluriharmonic functions on a ball in the [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) [euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697).
- Sneider, Maria Adelaide. (1988). "Steady state in a biological system: global asymptotic stability". *Atti della Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei. Rendiconti. Classe di Scienze Fisiche, Matematiche e Naturali*. LXXXII (2). p. 345–352. mr:1152650. zbl:0964.92502. <http://www.bdim.eu/item?id=RLIN_1988_8_82_2_345_0>. Maria Adelaide Sneider's last paper, completing the results first presented in (Fichera Sneider Wyman 1977).
