# Serafino Raffaele Minich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Serafino Raffaele Minich"}

*Image: Serafino Rafaele Minich.jpg.* Serafino Raffaele Minich

**Serafino Raffaele Minich** or **Serafin Rafael Minić** (8 December 1808 – 29 May 1883) was a [Croatian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croatia)-[Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy) mathematician.[^o2]

:::

Minić was born in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice). His father, a sea captain from [Prčanj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prčanj), settled in the early nineteenth century in Venice where Minić has spent his entire life. After receiving a degree in mathematics at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua), in 1830 he started working at the University as an assistant, and since 1842 as a lecturer.[^o2]

During his lifetime, he served as the [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) of the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua), [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the Faculty of Arts, dean of the Faculty of Science, and for several years he led the [Istituto di scienze, letere ed arti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_di_scienze,_letere_ed_arti) in Venice.[^o2]

He published more than 60 papers in the theory of [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics) and [hydraulics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydraulics). In 1875–76 he led the project of altering the port on [Lido](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lido_di_Venezia) in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice) and regulating the flow of the river [Brenta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brenta_%28river%29). He wrote several treatises on [Dante](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dante_Alighieri), [Petrarch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Petrarch) and [Tasso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torquato_Tasso). In the hall of the University of Padua a memorial is raised in his honor.[^o2]

He died in [Venice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venice).
