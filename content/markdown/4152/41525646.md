# Alexander Holevo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Holevo"}

**Alexander Semenovich Holevo** (Алекса́ндр Семéнович Хóлево,[^o3] also spelled as Kholevo and Cholewo) is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), one of the pioneers of [quantum information science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_science).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

[Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute), Moscow, since 1969. He graduated from [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology) in 1966, defended a PhD Thesis in 1969 and a Doctor of Science Thesis in 1975. Since 1986 A.S. Holevo is a Professor ([Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and [Moscow Institute of Physics and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Physics_and_Technology)).[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Holevo made substantial contributions in the mathematical foundations of [quantum theory](wikipedia://en/25202), [quantum statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_statistics) and [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) theory. In 1973 he obtained an upper bound for the amount of classical information that can be extracted from an ensemble of quantum states by quantum measurements (known as *[Holevo's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holevo's_theorem)*). Holevo developed the mathematical theory of [quantum communication channels](wikipedia://en/1013550), the noncommutative theory of statistical decisions, proved [coding theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_capacity) in quantum information theory and revealed the structure of [quantum Markov semigroups](wikipedia://en/67944523) and measurement processes. Holevo authored about one-hundred and seventy published works, including five monographs.[^o7]
