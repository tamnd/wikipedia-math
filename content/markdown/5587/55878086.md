# Jean-Marie Le Roux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Marie Le Roux"}

**Jean-Marie Le Roux** (4 April 1863, [Prat, Côtes-d'Armor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prat,_Côtes-d'Armor) – 1949, [Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rennes)) was a French applied mathematician.

:::

Le Roux, the son of farmers, studied at the [University of Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes) and, possibly, at the University of Bordeaux. He was an instructor at [Guingamp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guingamp) from 1882 to 1889, a professor at the *[lycée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée)* at [Brest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brest,_France) from 1889 to 1896, and a professor at the *lycée* at [Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montpellier) from 1896 to 1898. At the [University of Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes) he became in 1898 a *[maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences)* and in 1902 a professor of applied mathematics. He retired there in 1933 as professor emeritus with the title of *professeur honoraire*. He passed his *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* examination in mathematics in 1889. He received his doctorate in 1894 at the [Sorbonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), with [Gaston Darboux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaston_Darboux), [Gabriel Koenigs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Koenigs) and [Paul Appell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Appell) on his thesis committee. The title of Le Roux's doctoral dissertation is *Sur les intégrales des équations linéaires aux dérivées partielles du second ordre à deux variables indépendantes*.[^o3]

Le Roux did research on partial differential equations, integral equations, and differential geometry. For the French edition of [Klein's encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klein's_encyclopedia) he edited the article *Wahrscheinlichkeitstheorie* by [Emanuel Czuber](wikipedia://en/34422782). In 1924 Le Roux was a Plenary Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Toronto.[^o3][^o4]

Le Roux was a critic of [Einstein](wikipedia://en/736)'s [theory of relativity](wikipedia://en/30001). In 1923 in a newspaper article, he expressed a very negative opinion of relativity theory. He was one of the authors of the 1931 book *[Hundert Autoren gegen Einstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hundert_Autoren_gegen_Einstein)*.[^o3]

Le Roux married in 1897.
