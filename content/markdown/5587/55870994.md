# Denny Borsboom

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denny Borsboom"}

**Denny Borsboom** (born November 9, 1973) is a Dutch psychologist and psychometrician. He has been a professor of psychology at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) since 2013.[^o3] His work has included applying [network theory](wikipedia://en/766409) to the study of [mental disorders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_disorder) and their symptoms.[^o4] In 2018 he presented the [Paul B. Baltes Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_B._Baltes_Lecture) at the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities). He was the President of the Psychometric Society between 2024 and 2025.[^o5]

:::
