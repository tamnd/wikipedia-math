# Thomas Jerome Schaefer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Jerome Schaefer"}

**Thomas Jerome Schaefer** is an American mathematician.

:::

He obtained his Ph.D. in December 1978 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where he worked in the Department of Mathematics. His Ph.D. advisor was [Richard M. Karp](wikipedia://en/298763).[^o4][^o5][^o6]

He is well-known for his [dichotomy theorem](wikipedia://en/4638322), stating that any problem generalizing [Boolean satisfiability](wikipedia://en/4715) in a certain way is either in the [complexity class P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P_%28complexity_class%29) or is [NP-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NP-complete).[^o7]
