- [Felix Klein medal](wikipedia://en/8818410#Awards), named after the first president of the [ICMI](wikipedia://en/8818410) (1908–1920), honours a lifetime achievement in mathematics education research.
- The [Klein project](http://www.mathunion.org/icmi/other-activities/klein-project/introduction/) of the [IMU](wikipedia://en/14868) and [ICMI](wikipedia://en/8818410) aims to produce a book for upper secondary teachers that communicates the breadth and vitality of the research discipline of mathematics and connects it to the senior secondary school curriculum.
