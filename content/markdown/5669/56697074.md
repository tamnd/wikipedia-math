# Sonia Petrone

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sonia Petrone"}

**Sonia Petrone** is an Italian mathematical statistician, known for her work in Bayesian statistics, including use of [Bernstein polynomials](wikipedia://en/340136) for [nonparametric methods](wikipedia://en/223369) in [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412).RBPBDECPP With Patrizia Campagnoli and Giovanni Petris she is the author of the book *Dynamic Linear Models with R* (Springer, 2009).DLM

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Petrone earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in [economic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic) and [social sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_sciences) from [Bocconi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bocconi_University). She completed her Ph.D. in 1989 at the [University of Trento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Trento). After working at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) from 1991 to 1998, and at the [University of Insubria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Insubria) from 1998 to 2001, she became a full professor of statistics at Bocconi University.

Petrone was the president of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) for the 2014 term. She was a co-editor of the journal Bayesian Analysis (2010–2014) and the editor of the journal *[Statistical Science](wikipedia://en/15936018)* for 2020–2022.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

With Sara Wade and Silvia Mongelluzzo, Petrone won the Lindley Prize of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) in 2010. She was a Medallion Lecturer of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) for 2018. She is also a Fellow of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312), and an Elected Member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute). She was named to the 2022 class of Fellows of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), for "significant and impacting contributions to the foundations of Bayesian statistics and Bayesian nonparametric inference and prediction, as well as long-standing professional service and dedicated mentoring throughout her career".
