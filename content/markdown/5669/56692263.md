# Aurel Voss

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aurel Voss"}

**Aurel Voss** (7 December 1845, [Altona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altona,_Hamburg), Germany – 19 April 1931, [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), Germany) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), best known today for his contributions to [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanics).[^o3][^o4] He served as president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) for the 1898 term. He was a professor at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München) from 1902 until 1923. He became [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) in 1923.

:::

In 1880, Voss published a version of the [contracted Bianchi identities](wikipedia://en/57574496).[^o5][^o6]

## Career {#Career}

### Education {#Education}

Voss studied [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at the [University of Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hannover), the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), and [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University) between 1864 and 1868.

He received a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) the following year.

## Honors {#Honors}

He was awarded an [Honorary Doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_Doctorate) in Engineering Sciences by [TH Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TH_Munich) in 1915.
