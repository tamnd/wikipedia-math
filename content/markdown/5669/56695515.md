# Majda's model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Majda's model"}

**Majda's model** is a qualitative model (in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)) introduced by [Andrew Majda](wikipedia://en/23295125) in 1981 for the study of interactions in the combustion theory of shock waves and explosive chemical reactions.[^o2]

:::

The following definitions are with respect to a [Cartesian coordinate system](wikipedia://en/7706) with 2 variables. For [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) $u(x,t)$, $z(x,t)$ of one spatial variable $x$ representing the [Lagrangian specification of the fluid flow field](wikipedia://en/5289693) and the [time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time) variable $t$, functions $f(w)$, $\phi (w)$ of one variable $w$, and positive constants $k, q, B$, the **Majda model** is a pair of coupled [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564):[^o3]

$$\frac{\partial u(x,t)}{\partial t} + q \cdot \frac{\partial z(x,t)}{\partial t} + \frac{\partial f(u(x,t))}{\partial x} = B \cdot \frac{\partial^2u(x,t)}{\partial x^2}$$

$\frac{\partial z(x,t)}{\partial t} = - k \cdot \phi (u(x,t)) \cdot z(x,t)$[^o3]

the unknown function $u = u(x,t)$ is a [lumped variable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lumped_element_model), a scalar variable formed from a complicated nonlinear average of various aspects of density, velocity, and temperature in the exploding gas;

the unknown function $z = z(x,t) \in [0,1]$ is the [mass fraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass_fraction_%28chemistry%29) in a simple one-step chemical reaction scheme;

the given flux function $f = f(w)$ is a nonlinear convex function;

the given ignition function $\phi = \phi (w)$ is the starter for the chemical reaction scheme;

$k$ is the constant [reaction rate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reaction_rate);

$q$ is the constant heat release;

$B$ is the constant [diffusivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffusivity).[^o3]

Since its introduction in the early 1980s, Majda's simplified "qualitative" model for detonation ... has played an important role in the mathematical literature as test-bed for both the development of mathematical theory and computational techniques. Roughly, the model is a $2 \times 2$ system consisting of a [Burgers' equation](wikipedia://en/1023353) coupled to a [chemical kinetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_kinetics) equation. For example, Majda (with [Colella](wikipedia://en/24924923) & Roytburd) used the model as a key diagnostic tool in the development of fractional-step computational schemes for the Navier-Stokes equations of compressible reacting fluids ...[^o4]
