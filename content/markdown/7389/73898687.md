# Francis Brown (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Francis Brown"}

**Francis Brown** is a Franco-British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works on [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Brown studied at the University of Cambridge and the École normale supérieure (Paris) and University of Bordeaux,[^o4] with [Pierre Cartier](wikipedia://en/1470549), graduating in 2006 with a Ph.D. He then spent time at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) and [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452). In 2007 he moved to [Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche](wikipedia://en/57642956) where he won a [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) starter grant in 2010. In 2012, he moved to the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) and was awarded a [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) [Bronze Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Gold_Medal) and [Élie Cartan Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Élie_Cartan_Prize) for his proof of two conjectures related to [multiple zeta functions](wikipedia://en/18037845).[^o6][^o7] He had a Von Neumann Fellowship at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 2014 to 2015 and is currently a senior research fellow at All Souls College, at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

Brown's work is on the intersection of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). He has published on Tate Motives.[^o8] He also works on Zeta functions in quantum field theory.

He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2026.[^o9]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *Mixed Tate motives over $\mathbb{Z}$.* Ann. of Math. (2) 175 (2012), no. 2, 949–976. [ArXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/1102.1312)
- *Dedekind zeta motives for totally real number fields.* Invent. Math. 194 (2013), no. 2, 257–311. [ArXiv](http://arxiv.org/abs/0804.1654)
- *Motivic periods and $P^1\setminus\left\{0,1,\infty\right\}$*. Proceedings of the ICM 2014. [online](http://www.ihes.fr/%7Ebrown/MotivicPeriodsandP1minus3pointsv3.pdf)
