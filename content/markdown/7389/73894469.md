# Andrea Bréard

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrea Bréard"}

**Andrea Bréard** (also published as Andrea Eberhard and Andrea Eberhard-Bréard) is a German [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220) and [sinologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sinology) specializing in [Chinese mathematics](wikipedia://en/1524543). She is [Alexander von Humboldt Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Professorship) of Sinology in the Department of Classical World and Asian Cultures at the [University of Erlangen–Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bréard earned a diploma in mathematics at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) in 1991. After continuing study in China at [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University), she returned to Germany for a PhD at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin) in 1997, supervised by [Eberhard Knobloch](wikipedia://en/35228357). She earned a second doctorate in 1998 in France through [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University), and completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at Technische Universität Berlin in 2008.

She worked as a researcher at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) from 1992 to 2004, under the direction of Konrad Königsberger. From 2005 to 2017 she was [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) at the [University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille) in France, also holding various short-term positions in France, Germany, and the US. From 2017 to 2021 she held a professorship at [Paris-Saclay University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris-Saclay_University), and in 2021 she moved to the University of Erlangen–Nuremberg as Humboldt Professor.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Bréard was one of two 1998 recipients of the thesis prize of the Association française d'études chinoises. She was the 1999 recipient of the prize for young historians of the [International Academy of the History of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Academy_of_the_History_of_Science).

Bréard was elected as a corresponding member of the International Academy of the History of Science in 2015. She was elected to the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) in 2021.

## Books {#Books}

Bréard's books include:

- *Re-Kreation eines mathematischen Konzeptes im chinesischen Diskurs: "Reihen" vom 1. bis zum 19. Jahrhundert* \[*Re-creation of a mathematical concept in Chinese discourse: "Series" from the first to the nineteenth century*\] (dissertation, TU Berlin, 1997; Boethius: Texte und Abhandlungen zur Geschichte der Mathematik und der Naturwissenschaften, vol. 42, Franz Steiner Verlag, 1999)
- *Reform, Bureaucratic Expansion and Production of Numbers: Statistics in Early 20th Century China* (Habilitationsschrift, TU Berlin, 2008)
- *Nine Chapters on Mathematical Modernity: Essays on the Global Historical Entanglements of the Science of Numbers in China* (Transcultural Research—Heidelberg Studies on Asia and Europe in a Global Context, Springer, 2019)
- *Li Shanlan. Catégories analogues d'accumulations discrètes.* \[*Analogical categories of discrete accumulations*\] (Bibliothèque chinoise, vol. 40, Les Belles Lettres, 2023)
