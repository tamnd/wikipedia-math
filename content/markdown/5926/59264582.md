# Christopher Skinner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christopher Skinner"}

**Christopher McLean Skinner** (born 4 June 1972) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University). He works in [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) and arithmetic aspects of the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Skinner was born on 4 June 1972, in [Little Rock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Rock), Arkansas.[^o4] Skinner graduated with a B.A. from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1993.[^o4] He received a Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1997 under the supervision of [Andrew Wiles](wikipedia://en/2027).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Skinner was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 1997 to 2000.[^o4] He was then an associate professor of mathematics at the University of Michigan from 2000 to 2004, and then a full professor from 2004 to 2006.[^o4] He became a professor of mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2006.[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

Skinner and Wiles proved [modularity](wikipedia://en/174475#Generalizations) results for residually reducible [Galois representations](wikipedia://en/486929) in joint work.[^o7]

Skinner and [Eric Urban](wikipedia://en/63270274) proved many cases of [Iwasawa–Greenberg main conjectures](wikipedia://en/22091332) for a large class of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000).[^o8] As a consequence, for a [modular elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/4559850) over the [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024), they prove that the vanishing of the [Hasse–Weil *L*-function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse–Weil_L-function) *L*(*E*, *s*) of *E* at *s* = 1 implies that the p-adic [Selmer group](wikipedia://en/1970862) of *E* is infinite. Combined with theorems of [Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863)–[Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557) and [Kolyvagin](wikipedia://en/4559839), this gave a conditional proof (on the [Tate–Shafarevich conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate–Shafarevich_conjecture)) of the conjecture that *E* has infinitely many rational points if and only if *L*(*E*, 1) = 0, a (weak) form of the [Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer_conjecture). These results were used by [Manjul Bhargava](wikipedia://en/5102088), Skinner, and [Wei Zhang](wikipedia://en/46408911) to prove that a positive proportion of elliptic curves satisfy the [Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer_conjecture).[^o9][^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Skinner was a [Packard Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packard_Foundation) Fellow from 2001 to 2006[^o4][^o12] and a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellow) from 2001 to 2002.[^o4] He was named an inaugural [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in 2013.[^o13] In 2015, he was named a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Investigator) in Mathematics.[^o14][^o15]

He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Madrid in 2006.[^o16]
