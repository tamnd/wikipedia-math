# Ilse Fischer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ilse Fischer"}

*Image: Ilse Fischer Oberwolfach 2026.jpg.* Fischer at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2026

**Ilse Fischer** (born 29 June 1975) is an Austrian mathematician whose research concerns [enumerative combinatorics](wikipedia://en/3925533) and [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), connecting these topics to [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fischer was born in [Klagenfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klagenfurt). She studied at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) beginning in 1993, earning a master's degree (mag. rer. nat.), doctorate ([dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)), and [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there respectively in 1998, 2000, and 2006. Her doctoral dissertation, *Enumeration of perfect matchings: Rhombus tilings and Pfaffian graphs*, was jointly supervised by [Christian Krattenthaler](wikipedia://en/58451722) and Franz Rendl, and her habilitation thesis was *A polynomial method for the enumeration of plane partitions and alternating sign matrices*.

She worked as an assistant at [Alpen-Adria-Universität Klagenfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpen-Adria-Universität_Klagenfurt) from 1999 to 2004, with a year of postdoctoral research at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 2001. She moved to the University of Vienna in 2004, and at Vienna she was promoted to associate professor in 2011 and to full professor in 2017.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Fischer won the 2006 Dr. Maria Schaumayer Prize, and the 2009 [Start-Preis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Start-Preis) of the [Austrian Science Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund).

With Roger Behrend and Matjaž Konvalinka, Fischer is a winner of the 2019 [David P. Robbins Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_P._Robbins_Prize) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) and [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772), for their joint research on [alternating sign matrices](wikipedia://en/1740155).
