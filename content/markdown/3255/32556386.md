# Leonid Polterovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leonid Polterovich"}

**Leonid Polterovich** (לאוניד פולטרוביץ'; Леонид В. Полтерович; born 30 August 1963) is a [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)-[Israeli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University). His research field includes [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298) and [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems).

:::

A native of [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), Polterovich earned his undergraduate degree at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1984. He moved to Israel after the [collapse of communism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revolutions_of_1989), earning his doctorate from [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 1990. In 1996, he was awarded the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize),[^o3] in 1998 the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize), and in 2003 the Michael Bruno Memorial Award by [Yad Hanadiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yad_Hanadiv). In 1998, he was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o4] In 2016, he gave a plenary lecture at the 7th [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in Berlin.[^o5] He was a member of the faculty of the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).

He was elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2024.[^o6]

His brother, Iosif Polterovich, is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Montreal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Montreal).[^o7]
