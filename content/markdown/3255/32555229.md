# Alain Lascoux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alain Lascoux"}

**Alain Lascoux** (17 October 1944 – 20 October 2013) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Université de Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Paris_VII), [University of Marne la Vallée](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Marne_la_Vallée) and [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University). His research was primarily in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), particularly [Hecke algebras](wikipedia://en/10361630) and [Young tableaux](wikipedia://en/683368).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Lascoux earned his doctorate in 1977 from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris). He worked for twenty years with [Marcel-Paul Schützenberger](wikipedia://en/2817337) on properties of the symmetric group. They wrote many articles together and had a major impact on the development of [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582). They succeeded in giving a combinatorial understanding of various algebraic and geometric questions in representation theory. Thus they introduced many new objects related to both fields like [Schubert polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schubert_polynomials) and [Grothendieck polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck_polynomials), as well as novel terminology like the [plactic monoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plactic_monoid) and [vexillary permutations](wikipedia://en/31942906). They were also the first to define the [crystal graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crystal_bases) structure on Young tableaux (though not under this name).

Lascoux was an invited speaker at the 1998 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany).[^o5]
