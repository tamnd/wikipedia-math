# John Lomas (RAF officer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Lomas"}

**John Lomas** (1920–2019) was an RAF intelligence officer during the Second World War, when he decoded German communications at [Bletchley Park](wikipedia://en/4037). After the war, he became a senior officer in the [Immigration Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Immigration_Service).[^o3][^o4]

:::
