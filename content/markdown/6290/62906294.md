# Marc G. Genton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marc G. Genton"}

**Marc G. Genton**, (born September 1969 in Geneva, Switzerland) is currently a Distinguished Professor of Statistics with the King Abdullah University of Science and Technology, (KAUST), Thuwal, Saudi Arabia.[^o3] He is known as a specialist in [Spatio-Temporal Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spatio-Temporal_Statistics), [Data Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_Science) and their applications in [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics), [climate science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_science), and [marine science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marine_science). The [International Association for Mathematical Geosciences](wikipedia://en/43927898) awarded him the [Georges Matheron Lectureship](wikipedia://en/43969425) in 2020. He is a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (2007), Elected member to the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (2008), Fellow member of the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) (2009), Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (2010), and Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) (2012).

:::
