# Gelfand–Fuks cohomology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gelfand–Fuks cohomology"}

In mathematics, **Gelfand–Fuks cohomology**, introduced by [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) and [Dmitry Fuchs](wikipedia://en/56558134) in (Gel'fand Fuks 1969–70), is a [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) theory for [Lie algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_algebras) of smooth [vector fields](wikipedia://en/62641). It differs from the [Lie algebra cohomology](wikipedia://en/2096669) of Chevalley-Eilenberg in that its cochains are taken to be continuous $\mathbb{R}-$multilinear alternating forms on the Lie algebra of smooth vector fields where the latter is given the $C^{\infty}$ topology.

:::
