# Dixmier–Ng theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dixmier–Ng theorem"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), the **Dixmier–Ng theorem** is a characterization of when a [normed space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space) is in fact a [dual Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989#Dual_space). It was proven by Kung-fu Ng, who called it a variant of a theorem proven earlier by [Jacques Dixmier](wikipedia://en/9169420).[^o3][^o2]

:::

**Dixmier-Ng theorem.**[^o3] Let $X$ be a normed space. The following are equivalent:

1. There exists a [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [locally convex topology](wikipedia://en/518844) $\tau$ on $X$ so that the [closed unit ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Closed_unit_ball), $\mathbf{B}_X$, of $X$ is $\tau$-compact.
1. There exists a Banach space $Y$ so that $X$ is isometrically isomorphic to the dual of $Y$.

That 2. implies 1. is an application of the [Banach–Alaoglu theorem](wikipedia://en/577366), setting $\tau$ to the [Weak-\* topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak-*_topology). That 1. implies 2. is an application of the [Bipolar theorem](wikipedia://en/35128576).

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Let $M$ be a [pointed](wikipedia://en/1236666) [metric space](wikipedia://en/20018) with distinguished point denoted $0_M$. The Dixmier-Ng Theorem is applied to show that the [Lipschitz space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipschitz_space) $\text{Lip}_0(M)$ of all real-valued [Lipschitz functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipschitz_function) from $M$ to $\mathbb{R}$ that vanish at $0_M$ (endowed with the [Lipschitz constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lipschitz_function) as norm) is a dual Banach space.[^o6]
