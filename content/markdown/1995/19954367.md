# Ross Ihaka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ross Ihaka"}

**George Ross Ihaka** (born 1954)[^o3] is a New Zealand [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) who was an associate professor of statistics at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland) until his retirement in 2017.[^o4] Alongside [Robert Gentleman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Gentleman_%28statistician%29), he is one of the creators of the [R programming language](wikipedia://en/376707).[^o5][^o6][^o17] In 2008, Ihaka received the [Pickering Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pickering_Medal), awarded by the [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_New_Zealand), for his work on R.[^o7]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ihaka completed his undergraduate education at the [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), and obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1985 from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) supervised by [David R. Brillinger](wikipedia://en/70523091).[^o9] His thesis was on statistical modelling for [seismic interferometry](wikipedia://en/27341944) and was titled [Rūaumoko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rūaumoko), after the god of earthquakes, volcanoes and seasons in [Māori mythology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Māori_mythology).[^o10][^o11]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

As of 2010, he was working on a new statistical programming language based on [Lisp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lisp_%28programming_language%29).[^o13][^o14] The Department of Statistics at the University of Auckland started a public lecture series in his honour in 2017.[^o15]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Ihaka is of [Ngāti Kahungunu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ngāti_Kahungunu), [Rangitāne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rangitāne) and [Ngati Pākehā](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_New_Zealanders) (New Zealand European) descent.[^o17]
