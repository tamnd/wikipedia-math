# Kronecker limit formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kronecker limit formula"}

In mathematics, the classical **Kronecker limit formula** describes the constant term at *s* = 1 of a [real analytic Eisenstein series](wikipedia://en/5562599) (or [Epstein zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epstein_zeta_function)) in terms of the [Dedekind eta function](wikipedia://en/447020). There are many generalizations of it to more complicated Eisenstein series. It is named for [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=named-heading name="First Kronecker limit formula"}

## First Kronecker limit formula {#First_Kronecker_limit_formula}

The (first) Kronecker limit formula states that

$$E(\tau,s) = {\pi\over s-1} + 2\pi(\gamma-\log(2)-\log(\sqrt{y}|\eta(\tau)|^2)) +O(s-1),$$

where

- *E*(τ,*s*) is the real analytic Eisenstein series, given by

$$E(\tau,s) =\sum_{(m,n)\ne (0,0)}{y^s\over|m\tau+n|^{2s}}$$

for Re(*s*) > 1, and by analytic continuation for other values of the [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) *s*.

- γ is [Euler–Mascheroni constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler–Mascheroni_constant)
- τ = *x* + *iy* with *y* > 0.
- $\eta(\tau) = q^{1/24}\prod_{n\ge 1}(1-q^n)$, with *q* = e<sup>2π i τ</sup> is the [Dedekind eta function](wikipedia://en/447020).

So the Eisenstein series has a pole at *s* = 1 of residue π, and the (first) Kronecker limit formula gives the constant term of the [Laurent series](wikipedia://en/61213) at this pole.

This formula has an interpretation in terms of the [spectral geometry](wikipedia://en/1763356) of the elliptic curve $E_\tau$ associated to the lattice $\mathbb{Z} + \mathbb{Z} \tau$: it says that the [zeta-regularized determinant](wikipedia://en/2703380) of the Laplace operator $\Delta$ associated to the flat metric $\frac{1}{y} |dz|^2$ on $E_\tau$ is given by $4y |\eta(\tau)|^4$. This formula has been used in [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) for the one-loop computation in [Polyakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Markovich_Polyakov)'s perturbative approach.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=named-heading name="Second Kronecker limit formula"}

## Second Kronecker limit formula {#Second_Kronecker_limit_formula}

The second Kronecker limit formula states that

$$E_{u,v}(\tau,1) = -2\pi\log|f(u-v\tau;\tau)q^{v^2/2}|,$$

where

- *u* and *v* are real and not both integers.
- *q* = e<sup>2π i τ</sup> and *q<sup>a</sup>* = e<sup>2π i *a*τ</sup>
- *p* = e<sup>2π i *z*</sup> and *p<sup>a</sup>* = e<sup>2π i *az*</sup>
- $$E_{u,v}(\tau,s) =\sum_{(m,n)\ne (0,0)}e^{2\pi i (mu+nv)}{y^s\over|m\tau+n|^{2s}}$$

for Re(*s*) > 1, and is defined by analytic continuation for other values of the complex number *s*.

- $$f(z,\tau) = q^{1/12}(p^{1/2}-p^{-1/2})\prod_{n\ge1}(1-q^np)(1-q^n/p).$$

:::
