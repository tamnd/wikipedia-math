# Cohl Furey

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cohl Furey"}

**Cohl Furey**, also known as **Nichol Furey**,[^o3] is a [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Furey has a bachelor's degree in mathematics and physics from [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) (2005), Master's degree from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) (2006) and a Ph.D in theoretical physics from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo) (2015).[^o7] She was a research fellow at the University of Cambridge's [Trinity Hall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_Hall,_Cambridge) from 2016 to 2019[^o8] and spent a few months at the [African Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1575286) in [Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Town). Since 2020, she has been at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) on a Freigeist Fellowship by the [Volkswagen Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Foundation).[^o3][^o9]

Her main interests are [division algebras](wikipedia://en/26549625), [Clifford algebras](wikipedia://en/45305), and [Jordan algebras](wikipedia://en/936371), and their relation to [particle physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics). Her work focuses on finding an underlying mathematical structure to the [Standard Model](wikipedia://en/47641) of [particle physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics). She is most noted for her work on [octonions](wikipedia://en/51436).[^o11][^o10][^o12]

She has worked on attempting to obtain the Standard Model of particle physics from octonionic constructions.[^o4][^o11][^o12] In her 2018 paper "*SU*(3)${}_{C}$ × *SU*(2)${}_{L}$ × *U*(1)${}_{Y}$ (× *U*(1)${}_{X}$) as a symmetry of division algebraic ladder operators,"[^o13] according to *[Quanta Magazine](wikipedia://en/51804976)*, "she consolidated several findings to construct the full Standard Model symmetry group, [SU(3) × SU(2) × U(1)](wikipedia://en/2024795), for a single generation of particles, with the math producing the correct array of electric charges and other attributes for an [electron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electron), [neutrino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutrino), three [up quarks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Up_quark), three [down quarks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Down_quark) and their [anti-particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiparticle). The math also suggests a reason why [electric charge](wikipedia://en/9804) is quantized in discrete units — essentially, because whole numbers are."[^o4] In 2022 together with Mia Hughes, she linked the [symmetry breaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetry_breaking) in physics to division algebras including octonions.[^o14]

In 2019, *[Wired.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wired.com)* listed her in their article "10 Women in Science and Tech Who Should Be Household Names".[^o16]
