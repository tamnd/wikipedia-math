# Transactions of the American Mathematical Society

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Transactions of the American Mathematical Society"}

The ***Transactions of the American Mathematical Society*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of pure and applied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) published by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3] It was established in 1900. As a requirement, all articles must be more than 15 printed pages. Its ISSN number is 0002-9947.[^o4]

:::
