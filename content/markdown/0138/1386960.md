# Max Planck Institute for Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Max Planck Institute for Mathematics"}

The **Max Planck Institute for Mathematics** (Max-Planck-Institut für Mathematik, **MPIM**) is a research institute located in [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). It is named in honor of the [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) [Max Planck](wikipedia://en/19848) and forms part of the [Max Planck Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Planck_Society) (*Max-Planck-Gesellschaft*), an association of 84 institutes engaging in fundamental research in the arts and the sciences. The MPIM is the only Max Planck institute specializing in [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210).

:::

The Institute was founded by [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495) in 1980, having emerged from the collaborative research center "Theoretical Mathematics" ([Sonderforschungsbereich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sonderforschungsbereich) "Theoretische Mathematik"). Hirzebruch shaped the institute as its director until his retirement in 1995. Currently, the institute is managed by a board of three directors consisting of [Peter Teichner](wikipedia://en/63453744) (managing director), [Peter Scholze](wikipedia://en/39409306) and [Dennis Gaitsgory](wikipedia://en/6571030). [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495) and [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086) were, and [Günter Harder](wikipedia://en/50792386), [Werner Ballmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Werner_Ballmann), [Gerd Faltings](wikipedia://en/862226) and [Don Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557) are, acting as [emeriti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeriti).[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

The Max Planck Institute for Mathematics offers mathematicians from around the world the opportunity to visit Bonn and engage in [sabbatical](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sabbatical) work lasting from weeks to several months. This guest program distinguishes the MPIM from other Max Planck institutes, and results in only a limit number of permanent positions [^o3] and the absence of separate departments within the institute.

The research of the members and guests of the institute can be classified into the following areas:[^o5]

- [Algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101)
- [Algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924)
- [Algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801)
- [Arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177)
- [Differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562)
- [Dynamical system](wikipedia://en/9087)
- [Global analysis](wikipedia://en/42027498)
- [Mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416)
- [Noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917)
- [Number theory](wikipedia://en/21527)
- [Representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200)
