# Paul Dubreil

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Dubreil"}

**Paul Dubreil** (; 1 March 1904 – 9 March 1994) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was born in [Le Mans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Le_Mans), [Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maine_%28province%29), [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) and died in [Soisy-sur-École](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soisy-sur-École), France. Dubreil was married to fellow mathematician [Marie-Louise Dubreil-Jacotin](wikipedia://en/46727148).
