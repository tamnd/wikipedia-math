# Johannes Scheubel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Scheubel"}

Johannes Scheubel **Johannes Scheubel** (18 August 1494 – 20 February 1570) was a German mathematician. His books include *De Numeris et Diversis Rationibus* (1545)[^o2] and *Algebrae Compendiosa* (1551).[^o3]

:::
