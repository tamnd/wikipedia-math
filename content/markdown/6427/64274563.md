# Quasi-ultrabarrelled space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasi-ultrabarrelled space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quasi-ultrabarrelled space** is a [topological vector spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_vector_spaces) (TVS) for which every [bornivorous ultrabarrel](wikipedia://en/1733629) is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_%28topology%29) of the origin.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A subset *B*<sub>0</sub> of a TVS *X* is called a **bornivorous ultrabarrel** if it is a closed, [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238), and [bornivorous](wikipedia://en/64267682) subset of *X* and if there exists a sequence $\left( B_{i} \right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ of closed balanced and bornivorous subsets of *X* such that *B*<sub>*i*+1</sub> + *B*<sub>*i*+1</sub> ⊆ *B*<sub>*i*</sub> for all *i* = 0, 1, .... In this case, $\left( B_{i} \right)_{i=1}^{\infty}$ is called a **defining sequence** for *B*<sub>0</sub>. A TVS *X* is called **quasi-ultrabarrelled** if every bornivorous ultrabarrel in *X* is a [neighbourhood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neighbourhood_%28topology%29) of the origin.[^n1]

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

A [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) quasi-ultrabarrelled space is [quasi-barrelled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasibarrelled_space).[^n1]

:::

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

[Ultrabarrelled spaces](wikipedia://en/64268157) and [ultrabornological spaces](wikipedia://en/63904912) are quasi-ultrabarrelled. Complete and metrizable TVSs are quasi-ultrabarrelled.[^n1]
