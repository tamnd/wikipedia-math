# Haim Shapira

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Haim Shapira"}

**Haim Shapira** (חיים שפירא; born 1962) is an Israeli mathematician, pianist, speaker, philosopher and [game theorist](wikipedia://en/11924). He writes in Hebrew, and his books have been translated into English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Italian, Russian, Bengali and Korean. He is a lecturer in Israel and was a speaker at [TEDx](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TEDx)Jaffa on game theory and strategy.[^o3] His first two books in English are *Happiness and Other Small Things of Absolute Importance*[^o4][^o5] and *Gladiators, Pirates and Games of Trust*.[^o6] He also arranged and performed music on the soundtrack for *John Wick: Chapter 2*.[^o7]

:::

Shapira was born in Vilnius, Lithuania in 1962 and immigrated to Israel at the age of 14 in 1977.[^o8]

## Education {#Education}

Shapira's academic journey includes a Bachelor of Science degree in Theoretical Mathematics from the School of Mathematical and Computer Sciences at Tel Aviv University, which he completed in 1983 to 1987. Subsequently, he pursued a Master of Science degree in Probability and Statistics, spanning from 1987 to 1991. Shapira's educational pursuits culminated in two distinct PhDs: the first in Mathematical Genetics, obtained in 1995, with his dissertation titled "The 'Volunteer's Dilemma' as a Generalized War of Attrition," which was published in 1996. His second doctorate was in the field of Science Education, also from Tel Aviv University, where his research centered on the development of intuition related to the concept of infinity in various mathematical contexts. These academic achievements show his dual expertise in mathematics and science education.[^o10]

## Current position {#Current_position}

Shapira was a lecturer at Tel Aviv University from 1994 to 2000.

He is at present a senior lecturer at [The College of Management Academic Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_College_of_Management_Academic_Studies) in [Rishon LeZion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rishon_LeZion), Israel. He is Head of the Excellent Students Program and lecturer at the School of Economics and at the School of Behavioral Sciences.

His main research areas are game theory and philosophy. In 2016, he taught game theory and statistics.[^o12]
