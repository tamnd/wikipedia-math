# Susan Goldstine

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susan Goldstine"}

**Susan Goldstine** is an American mathematician active in [mathematics and fiber arts](wikipedia://en/13410380). She is a professor of mathematics at [St. Mary's College of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Mary's_College_of_Maryland), and (for 2019–2022) the Steven Muller Distinguished Professor in the Sciences at St. Mary's College.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Goldstine graduated *[summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_honors)* from [Amherst College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amherst_College) in 1993. She completed a Ph.D. in mathematics at Harvard University in 1998. Her dissertation, *Spin Representations and Lattices*, was supervised by [Benedict Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863).

After postdoctoral and visiting assistant professorships at [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University), [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), and Amherst College, she joined the St. Mary's College faculty in 2004.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Goldstine has made and exhibited many pieces of mathematical art, often involving textiles. A set of [bead crochet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bead_crochet) jewelry pieces by her visualizing the [map coloring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Map_coloring) problem on three different [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) won the prize for "best textile, sculpture, or other medium" in the art show of the 2015 [Joint Mathematics Meetings](wikipedia://en/14985143).

She is the coauthor of the book *Crafting Conundrums: Puzzles and Patterns for the Bead Crochet Artist* (with Ellie Baker, [A K Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_K_Peters) / [CRC Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRC_Press), 2014).

Combining her interests in mathematics and fiber arts she is one of 24 mathematicians and artists who make up the [Mathemalchemy](wikipedia://en/70985345) Team.[^o4]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Goldstine is the granddaughter of teacher and author [Bel Kaufman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bel_Kaufman) and the great-great-granddaughter of [Sholem Aleichem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sholem_Aleichem).
