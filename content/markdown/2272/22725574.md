# List of works by Nikolay Bogolyubov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of works by Nikolay Bogolyubov"}

List of some published works of [Nikolay Bogolyubov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikolay_Bogolyubov) in chronological order:

:::

## 1924 {#1924}

- N. N. Bogolyubov (1924). *On the behavior of solutions of linear differential equations at infinity* (О поведении решений линейных дифференциальных уравнений на бесконечности).

## 1934 {#1934}

- N. M. Krylov and N. N. Bogolyubov. (1934). "On various formal expansions of non-linear mechanics". Izdat. Zagal'noukr. Akad. Nauk

## 1937 {#1937}

- N. N. Bogoliubov and N. M. Krylov (1937). "La theorie generalie de la mesure dans son application a l'etude de systemes dynamiques de la mecanique non-lineaire" (in French). Ann. Math. II 38: 65–113. Zbl. 16.86.

## 1945 {#1945}

- Боголюбов Н. Н. (1945). Изд-во АН УССР
  - N. N. Bogoliubov. (1945). "On Some Statistical Methods in Mathematical Physics". Izdatelstvo AN USSR

## 1946 {#1946}

::: {#o10 .entry cue=list-entry name="Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Physics"}

- N. N. Bogoliubov (1946). "Kinetic Equations" (in Russian). [Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JETP) **16** (8): 691–702.

:::

  - N. N. Bogoliubov (1946). "Kinetic Equations" (in English). Journal of Physics **10** (3): 265–274.

## 1947 {#1947}

::: {#o12 .entry cue=list-entry name="Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Physics"}

- N. N. Bogoliubov, K. P. Gurov (1947). "Kinetic Equations in Quantum Mechanics" (in Russian). [Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JETP) **17** (7): 614–628.

:::

- N. N. Bogoliubov (1947). "К теории сверхтекучести" (in Russian). Известия АН СССР, физика, 1947, 11, № 1, 77.
  - N. N. Bogoliubov (1947). "On the Theory of Superfluidity" (in English). Journal of Physics **11** (1): 23–32.

## 1948 {#1948}

- N. N. Bogoliubov (1948). "Equations of Hydrodynamics in Statistical Mechanics" (in Ukrainian). Sbornik Trudov Instituta Matematiki AN USSR **10**: 41—59.

## 1949 {#1949}

- Боголюбов М. М. (1949)
  - N. N. Bogoliubov (1967—1970): *Lectures on Quantum Statistics. Problems of Statistical Mechanics of Quantum Systems*. New York, Gordon and Breach.

## 1955 {#1955}

- Bogoliubov, N. N.; Ostap S. Parasyuk. (1955). *Doklady Akademii Nauk SSSR*. 100. p. 25–28
- Bogoliubov, N. N.; Shirkov D. V. (1955). *JETP*. 30. p. 77–86

## 1957 {#1957}

- Боголюбов Н. Н.; Ширков Д. В. (1957). Наука
  - N. N. Bogoliubov; D. V. Shirkov. (1957). "Introduction to the Theory of Quantized Field". Nauka
  - N. N. Bogoliubov; D. V. Shirkov. (1959). "Introduction to the Theory of Quantized Field". John Wiley & Sons Inc. isbn:0-470-08613-0 (1st edition)
  - N. N. Bogoliubov; D. V. Shirkov. (1980). "Introduction to the Theory of Quantized Field". John Wiley & Sons Inc. isbn:0-471-04223-4 (3rd edition)

::: {#o24 .entry cue=list-entry name="Acta Mathematica"}

- N. N. Bogoliubov, O. S. Parasyuk (1957). "[Uber die Multiplikation der Kausalfunktionen in der Quantentheorie der Felder](https://doi.org/10.1007%2FBF02392399)" (in German). [Acta Mathematica](wikipedia://en/6860708) **97**: 227–266. .

:::

## 1958 {#1958}

::: {#o26 .entry cue=list-entry name="Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Physics"}

- N. N. Bogoliubov (1958). *On a New Method in the Theory of Superconductivity*. [Journal of Experimental and Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Experimental_and_Theoretical_Physics) **34** (1): 58.

:::

- Боголюбов Н. Н.; Медведев Б. В.; Поливанов М. К. (1958). Физматгиз
  - N. N. Bogoliubov; B. V. Medvedev; M. K. Polivanov. (1958). "Problems in the Theory of Dispersion Relations". Institute for Advanced Study

## 1965 {#1965}

::: {#o30 .entry cue=list-entry name="B. V. Struminsky"}

- N. N. Bogolubov, [B. V. Struminsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Struminsky), A. N. Tavkhelidze (1965). *On composite models in the theory of elementary particles.* [JINR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/JINR) Preprint D-1968, [Dubna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dubna).

:::
