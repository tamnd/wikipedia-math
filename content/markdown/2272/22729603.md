# Dolgachev surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dolgachev surface"}

In mathematics, **Dolgachev surfaces** are certain [simply connected](wikipedia://en/523879) [elliptic surfaces](wikipedia://en/3073462), introduced by (txt). They can be used to give examples of an infinite family of [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) simply connected [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [4-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1362795), no two of which are [diffeomorphic](wikipedia://en/8564).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The [blowup](wikipedia://en/1817790) $X_0$ of the [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350) in 9 points can be realized as an elliptic fibration all of whose fibers are irreducible. A Dolgachev surface $X_q$ is given by applying [logarithmic transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithmic_transformation) of orders 2 and *q* to two smooth fibers for some $q\ge 3$.

The Dolgachev surfaces are simply connected, and the bilinear form on the second [cohomology group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cohomology_group) is odd of [signature](wikipedia://en/1152311) $(1,9)$ (so it is the [unimodular lattice](wikipedia://en/1648605) $I_{1,9}$). The [geometric genus](wikipedia://en/3115807) $p_g$ is 0 and the [Kodaira dimension](wikipedia://en/1866873) is 1.

(txt) found the first examples of simply-connected homeomorphic but not diffeomorphic 4-manifolds $X_0$ and $X_3$. More generally the surfaces $X_q$ and $X_r$ are always homeomorphic, but are not diffeomorphic unless $q=r$.

(txt) showed that the Dolgachev surface $X_3$ has a [handlebody decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Handlebody_decomposition) without 1- and 3-handles.

:::

## Detection of exotic smooth structures {#Detection_of_exotic_smooth_structures}

According to [Freedman's classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freedman_classification), a simply connected oriented closed [topological](wikipedia://en/2119193) 4-manifold with [intersection form](wikipedia://en/19057150) $[+1]\oplus 9[-1]$ is homeomorphic to $\mathbb{C}P^2\# 9\overline{\mathbb{C}P^2}$. Any such smooth manifold with a non-vanishing [Seiberg–Witten invariant](wikipedia://en/14407762) has an exotic smooth structure since $\mathbb{C}P^2\# 9\overline{\mathbb{C}P^2}$ with the induced smooth structure allows a [Riemannian metric](wikipedia://en/144652) of positive [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622) (psc metric) and therefore has only vanishing Seiberg–Witten invariants.[^o8]
