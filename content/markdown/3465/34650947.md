# Orbot

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Orbot"}

**Orbot** is a [free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_software) [proxy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proxy_server) [app](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mobile_app) that provides [anonymity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anonymity) on the [Internet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) for users of the [Android](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Android_%28operating_system%29) and [iOS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IOS) [operating systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operating_system). It allows traffic from apps such as web browsers, [email](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Email) clients, map programs, and others to be routed via the Tor network.[^o5]

:::

This tool is used to keep the communications of users anonymous and hidden from governments and third parties that might be [monitoring their internet traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2010s_global_surveillance_disclosures).[^o6]

## Reception {#Reception}

In 2014 Orbot was discussed in detail in an article on "reporting securely from an Android device".[^o8] In January 2016, Lisa Vaas of NakedSecurity by Sophos described how to use Tor, including with Orbot on Android, to connect to [Facebook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facebook).[^o9]

In July 2021, Tech Radar named Orbot one of 8 "Best privacy apps for Android in 2021" but warned of slower speeds.[^o10] In July 2021 Android Authority discussed Tor Browser and Orbot in brief reviews of "15 best Android browsers".[^o11]

In November 2021, John Leyden of The Daily Swig described collaboration between the Tor Project and [the Guardian Project](wikipedia://en/39399882) to develop Orbot for censorship circumvention for any application on a device, but warned Orbot does not remove identifying information from app traffic.[^o12] In July 2022, Laiba Mohsin of PhoneWorld.com described Orbot as a simple way to access the [Dark Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dark_web) on mobile.[^o13]

In October 2022, Damir Mujezinovic of MakeUseOf described Orbot as a "flagship" product for both iOS and Android to use the Tor network, and said it "will not make you completely anonymous, but it can certainly help bypass certain geographical restrictions,"[^o14] In November 2022, Mujezinovic wrote a detailed guide to using Orbot on iOS or Android.[^o15]
