# Kumiko Nishioka

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kumiko Nishioka"}

**Kumiko Nishioka** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Keio University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keio_University). She specializes in [transcendental numbers](wikipedia://en/30325), and is known for her research related to the theory of [Mahler functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahler_function)[^o2][^o3][^o4] and [Painlevé transcendents](wikipedia://en/2033003).[^o5] In 1996 she published the first comprehensive text on transcendence of Mahler functions, *Mahler Functions and Transcendence*, extending and generalizing [Mahler's method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahler's_method).[^o6] Her husband Keiji Nishioka is also a mathematician, and a coauthor.

:::
