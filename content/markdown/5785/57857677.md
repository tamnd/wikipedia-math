# Euler measure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Euler measure"}

In [measure theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), the **Euler measure** of a [polyhedral set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedral_set) equals the [Euler integral](wikipedia://en/245658) of its [indicator function](wikipedia://en/240790).

:::

## The magnitude of an Euler measure {#The_magnitude_of_an_Euler_measure}

By induction, it is easy to show that independent of [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398), the Euler measure (sometimes called an "Euler Charactaristic"[^o3]) of a [closed](wikipedia://en/47279) [bounded](wikipedia://en/48258) [convex polyhedron](wikipedia://en/1817885) always equals 1, while the Euler measure of a *d*-D [relative](wikipedia://en/910926)-[open](wikipedia://en/39358) [bounded convex polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_convex_polyhedron) is $(-1)^d$.[^o4]
