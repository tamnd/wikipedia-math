# Michael Neale

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Neale"}

**Michael C. Neale** (born 10 March 1958)[^o3] is a British behavior geneticist and professor at the [Virginia Institute for Psychiatric and Behavioral Genetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Institute_for_Psychiatric_and_Behavioral_Genetics) at [Virginia Commonwealth University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Commonwealth_University). He is known for his research in the field of [psychiatric genetics](wikipedia://en/6828663), which aims to determine the roles of genetic and environmental factors in [psychiatric disorders](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychiatric_disorder) and [substance abuse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substance_abuse). He is estimated to have mentored over 2,000 students in his field.[^o4] He was president of the [Behavior Genetics Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavior_Genetics_Association) from 2009 to 2010.[^o3]
