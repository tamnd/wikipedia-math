# Mariusz Wodzicki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mariusz Wodzicki"}

**Mariusz Wodzicki** (Polish: '; born 1956) is a Polish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), whose works primarily focus on [analysis](wikipedia://en/1134), algebraic [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917), and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Wodzicki was born in [Bytom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bytom), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) in 1956. He received a [MSc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1980,[^o4] and he completed his doctoral degree in 1984 at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow) under the advisement of [Yuri Manin](wikipedia://en/1692086) (Spectral Asymmetry and Zeta-Functions).

In 1985–1986 he was a research assistant at the [Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford](wikipedia://en/7905850), after which he became an assistant professor at the Mathematical Institute of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4] He is currently a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o6]

In 1992, Wodzicki was an invited speaker of the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) (Algebraic K-theory and functional analysis). In 1994, he was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) (The algebra of functional analysis).[^o7]
