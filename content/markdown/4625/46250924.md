# Cubical set

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cubical set"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **cubical set** is a set-valued [contravariant functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contravariant_functor) on the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of (various) *n*-cubes.

:::

Cubical sets have been often considered as an alternative to simplicial sets in [combinatorial topology](wikipedia://en/363400), including in the early work of [Daniel Kan](wikipedia://en/2290155) and [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483). They have also been developed in computer science, in particular in [concurrency theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concurrency_%28computer_science%29) and in [homotopy type theory](wikipedia://en/33974223).[^o2]
