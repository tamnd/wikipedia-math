# Friedhelm Eicker

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Friedhelm Eicker"}

**Friedhelm Eicker** (5 April 1927 – 19 March 2022) was a German [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [former professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at the [University of Dortmund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dortmund). He is known for his contributions in the development of [heteroscedasticity-consistent standard errors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heteroscedasticity-consistent_standard_errors).[^o3]

:::

A native of [Radevormwald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radevormwald), Eicker earned his PhD from the [University of Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mainz) in 1956.
