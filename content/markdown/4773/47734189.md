# Jan Jaworowski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Jaworowski"}

**Jan W. Jaworowski** (March 2, 1928 in [Augustów](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augustów), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) – April 10, 2013[^o8] in [Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Indiana), [Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana)) was a Polish and American mathematician, [topologist](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

His father was Jan Leonard Jaworowski, and his mother—Helena (maiden name Heybowicz).

Jaworowski graduated with a master's degree from the mathematics department of the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw). He got his Ph.D. from the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1955, in [Algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), under [Karol Borsuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol_Borsuk).[^o5][^o7] He generalized the [Borsuk–Ulam theorem](wikipedia://en/4064) about [antipodes](wikipedia://en/367577).

He taught at University of Warsaw, [University of Ljubljana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ljubljana), and for years at The [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington). He published 64 papers during his 57-years official career (and another 12 after his retirement in 1995).[^o9] He was a promoter of at least 11 doctoral theses.

He was a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) during the 1960/61.[^o6]

Jaworowski specialized in the [transformation groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_groups) theory.
