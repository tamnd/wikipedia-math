# Hacettepe Journal of Mathematics and Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hacettepe Journal of Mathematics and Statistics"}

***Hacettepe Journal of Mathematics and Statistics*** is an international [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) open-access journal (with no article processing/publishing charge) devoted to the publication of original research papers and occasional surveys of a suitably high standard, written in English, on all aspects of Mathematics and [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). The journal is published by [Hacettepe University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hacettepe_University) Faculty of Science. It is currently hosted on [DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2023 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.7 .[^o4] The journal is indexed in SCI-EXP, [Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports), Mathematical Reviews, Zentralblatt MATH, Current Index to Statistics, Statistical Theory & Method Abstracts, [SCOPUS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SCOPUS), Tübitak-Ulakbim.[^o5]
