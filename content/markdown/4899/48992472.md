# Kerrie Mengersen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Kerrie Mengersen"}

**Kerrie Mengersen** (born 1962)[^o3] is an Australian [statistician](wikipedia://en/26685). She is a Professor of Statistics at [Queensland University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queensland_University_of_Technology), and 2024 winner of the [Ruby Payne-Scott Medal and Lecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruby_Payne-Scott_Medal_and_Lecture) from the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science).[^o20][^o21][^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mengersen earned BA (Hons I) and PhD degrees in mathematics, majoring in Statistics and Computing, from the [University of New England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_England_%28Australia%29) (Armidale, New South Wales, Australia) in 1985 and 1989, respectively.[^o20] She is the Director of the Bayesian Research and Applications Group (BRAG) within QUT's School of Mathematical Sciences[^o6], a member of the university's Centre for Data Science,[^o7] and was Deputy Director of the former Australian Research Council (ARC) Centre of Excellence for Mathematical and Statistical Frontiers (ACEMS) of Big Data, Big Models, New Insights.[^o23]

She has co-authored three books and edited two, and has written 27 book chapters and approximately 250 journal articles.[^o24][^o25] Her research covers a broad spectrum of statistical practice, from theory to its applications. She is primarily known for her work in [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412) and [meta-analysis](wikipedia://en/62329), and has worked in applications of statistics in [medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicine) and [environmental science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environmental_science).[^o22] In 2016, she was the first woman to be awarded the Statistical Society of Australia's [Pitman Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pitman_Medal), which recognises outstanding achievement in the statistics discipline.[^o21] She talks about new challenges for statisticians in a YouTube video.[^o10] She has contributed to Australian [biosecurity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosecurity) efforts.[^o11] In October 2015, her research in building virtual [habitats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habitat) was highlighted on the [ABC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Broadcasting_Corporation).[^o12]

Mengersen was the National President of the Statistical Society of Australia (SSAI) in 2013, and was the International President of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) (ISBA) in 2016.[^o28] As of 2022, she is a member of the Academy Council of the Queensland Academy of Arts and Sciences.[^o13]

- Elected Fellow of the [Society for Modelling and Simulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Modelling_and_Simulation)[^o26]
- 2015 Biennial Medalist of the Modelling and Simulation Society of Australia and New Zealand (MSSANZ)[^o27]

- 2016 [Statistical Society of Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Society_of_Australia) Pitman Medal[^o21]
- 2018 Elected Fellow of the [Australian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_Academy_of_Science) (FAA)[^o17]
- 2018 Elected Fellow of the [Academy of the Social Sciences in Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_the_Social_Sciences_in_Australia) (FASSA)[^o18]
