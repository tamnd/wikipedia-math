# Monika Ludwig

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monika Ludwig"}

**Monika Ludwig** (born 1966 in [Cologne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cologne))[^o2] is an Austrian mathematician, University Professor of Convex and Discrete Geometry at the [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology).[^o3]

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Ludwig earned a Dipl.-Ing. degree from the Vienna University of Technology in 1990, and a doctorate in 1994 under the supervision of [Peter M. Gruber](wikipedia://en/35098363).[^o3] She remained at the same university as an assistant and associate professor from 1994 until 2007, when she moved to the [Polytechnic Institute of New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_Institute_of_New_York_University). She returned to the Vienna University of Technology as a full professor in 2010.[^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Ludwig won the Edmund and Rosa Hlawka Prize of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences), given to an outstanding Austrian researcher in [geometry of numbers](wikipedia://en/250153) or [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) under the age of 30, in 1998.[^o7] She won the [Prize of the Austrian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30770384) in 2004.[^o8]

She became a corresponding member of the Austrian Academy of Sciences in 2011,[^o9] and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.[^o10] She became a full member of the Austrian Academy of Sciences in 2013.
