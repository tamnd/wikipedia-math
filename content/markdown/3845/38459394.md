# Joyce McLaughlin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joyce McLaughlin"}

**Joyce Rogers McLaughlin** (8 October 1939 – 23 October 2017)[^o2] was an American mathematician, the Ford Foundation Professor of Mathematics at [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute).[^o4] Her research interests were primarily in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), and in particular in [inverse problems](wikipedia://en/203956).

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

McLaughlin did her undergraduate studies at [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University). After earning a master's degree from the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland), she moved to the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside) for her doctoral studies, earning a Ph.D. in 1968 under the supervision of Joaquin Basilio Diaz.[^o4] She joined the Rensselaer faculty in 1978.[^o4]

She was chair of the board of trustees of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) from 1996 to 1998.[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

McLaughlin was an invited lecturer at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1994. She was chosen by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) and the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) as the [AWM-SIAM Sonia Kovalevsky Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM-SIAM_Sonia_Kovalevsky_Lecturer) in 2004.[^o4]

She became a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) in 2009[^o7] and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013 in the inaugural class.[^o8]
