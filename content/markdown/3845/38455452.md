# Stable process

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stable process"}

In [probability](wikipedia://en/22934) theory, a **stable process** is a type of [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895). It includes stochastic processes whose associated [probability distributions](wikipedia://en/23543) are [stable distributions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_distributions).[^o2]

:::

Examples of stable processes include the [Wiener process](wikipedia://en/149984), or [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436), whose associated probability distribution is the [normal distribution](wikipedia://en/21462). They also include the [Cauchy process](wikipedia://en/38454328). For the symmetric Cauchy process, the associated probability distribution is the [Cauchy distribution](wikipedia://en/7003).[^o2]

The degenerate case, where there is no random element, i.e., $X(t) = mt$, where $m$ is a constant, is also a stable process.[^o2]
