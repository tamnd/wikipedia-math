# Claiborne Latimer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Claiborne Latimer"}

**Claiborne Green Latimer** (1893–1960) was an American mathematician, known for the [Latimer–MacDuffee theorem](wikipedia://en/5647547).[^o2]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Latimer earned his PhD in 1924 from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) under [Leonard Dickson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Dickson) with thesis *Arithmetic of Generalized Quaternion Algebras*. He was an assistant professor at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) for 2 years,[^o5] before becoming a mathematics professor at the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky) in 1927. After 20 years at the University of Kentucky, he resigned in 1947 and became a professor at [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University).[^o6] Latimer was an amateur photographer; some of his photographs are preserved in the archives of the University of Kentucky and Emory University.[^o7]
