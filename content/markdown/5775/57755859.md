# Wilhelm Franz Meyer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Franz Meyer"}

**Friedrich Wilhelm Franz Meyer** (1856–1934) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and one of the main editors of the *[Encyclopädie der Mathematischen Wissenschaften](wikipedia://en/33686790)*.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Meyer studied at [Leipzig University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig_University) and the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München). In 1878, he was awarded a doctorate by Munich. He studied further in Berlin under [Weierstrass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass), [Kummer](wikipedia://en/319117) and [Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450). In 1880, he got the *venia legendi* at the [University of Tübingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tübingen). In 1888, he became a full professor at the Bergakademie of Clausthal (today [Clausthal University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clausthal_University_of_Technology)). From October 1897 until October 1924, when he retired, he taught at the [University of Königsberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Königsberg).[^n1]

The wide research work of Meyer (more than 130 papers) is centred basically on [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and, specifically, on [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762).[^n2]

Notwithstanding he is mainly known for he was one of the main editors[^n3] of the *[Encyklopädie der mathematischen Wissenschaften](wikipedia://en/33686790)* published from 1898 to 1933 in 23 separate books. Meyer was directly in charge to edit the geometry volumes.[^n4]
