# Takahiro Kawai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takahiro Kawai"}

Takahiro Kawai is a Japanese mathematician working on [algebraic analysis](wikipedia://en/20846707). He is a professor emeritus at [RIMS](wikipedia://en/1978962).[^o3] He was a student of [Mikio Sato](wikipedia://en/373336) at the same time as [Masaki Kashiwara](wikipedia://en/4376209)[^o4] with whom he later shared the [Asahi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asahi_Prize) in 1987.[^o5]

:::
