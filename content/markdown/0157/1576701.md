# Fundamenta Mathematicae

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fundamenta Mathematicae"}

***Fundamenta Mathematicae*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) with a special focus on the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358), concentrating on [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and its interactions with [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).

:::

The first specialized journal in the field of mathematics, originally it covered only [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), and [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] It is published by the Mathematics Institute of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).

## History {#History}

The journal was conceived by [Zygmunt Janiszewski](wikipedia://en/362471) as a means to foster mathematical research in Poland.[^o8] Janiszewski posited that, to achieve its goal, the journal should not compel Polish mathematicians to submit articles written exclusively in Polish, and should be devoted only to a specialized topic in mathematics;[^o9] *Fundamenta Mathematicae* thus became the first specialized journal in the field of mathematics.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

Despite Janiszewski having, in a 1918 article, given the initial impetus for the creation of the journal,[^o10] he did not live long enough to see the first issue published, in [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw), as he died on 3 January 1920. [Wacław Sierpiński](wikipedia://en/34122) and [Stefan Mazurkiewicz](wikipedia://en/629203) took over as [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief). Soon after its launch, the founding editors were joined by [Kazimierz Kuratowski](wikipedia://en/46075) and, later, by [Karol Borsuk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karol_Borsuk). Between 1920-1939, 32 volumes of the journal were published. They comprised 932 papers by 212 authors from 19 countries. Notable foreign contributors included [René Maurice Fréchet](wikipedia://en/290931), [Felix Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/10989), [Henri Lebesgue](wikipedia://en/49514) and [Hans Hahn](wikipedia://en/1075212).[^o11]

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in the [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded),[^o13] [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus),[^o14] and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH).[^o15] According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2016 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.609.[^o16]
