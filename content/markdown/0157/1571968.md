# Gerhard Ringel

*Image: Gerhard Ringel surfing.jpg.* Gerhard Ringel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gerhard Ringel"}

**Gerhard Ringel** (October 28, 1919 in [Kollnbrunn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bad_Pirawarth), [Austria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/First_Austrian_Republic) – June 24, 2008 in [Santa Cruz, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santa_Cruz,_California)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was one of the pioneers in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and contributed significantly to the proof of the [Heawood conjecture](wikipedia://en/1585274) (now the [Ringel–Youngs theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ringel–Youngs_theorem)), a mathematical problem closely linked with the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949).

:::

Although born in Austria, Ringel was raised in [Czechoslovakia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovakia) and attended [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) before being drafted into the [Wehrmacht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wehrmacht) in 1940 (after Germany had taken control of much of what had been Czechoslovakia). After the war Ringel served for over four years in a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) prisoner of war camp.[^o2]

He earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1951 with a thesis written under the supervision of [Emanuel Sperner](wikipedia://en/374404) and [Ernst Peschl](wikipedia://en/40394737). Ringel started his academic career as professor at the [Free University Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_Berlin). In 1970 he left Germany due to bureaucratic consequences of the [German student movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_student_movement), and continued his career at the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz), having been invited there by his coauthor, [John W. T. (Ted) Youngs](wikipedia://en/38288859). He was awarded honorary doctorate degrees from the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology) and the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin).

Besides his mathematical skills he was a widely acknowledged [entomologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entomologist). His main emphasis lay on collecting and breeding butterflies. Prior to his death, he gave his outstanding collection of butterflies to the [UCSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz) Museum of Natural History Collections.[^o4]
