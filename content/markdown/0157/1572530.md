# John Montroll

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Montroll"}

*Image: Montroll Origami Tyrannosaurus.jpg.* An origami Tyrannosaurus folded from Montroll's instructions

**John Montroll** (born January 1, 1960) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [origami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origami) artist, author, teacher, and mathematician. He has written many books on origami, promoting the single-square, no-cut, no glue approach. Montroll taught mathematics at [St. Anselm's Abbey School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Anselm's_Abbey_School) in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.) from 1990 to 2021.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

John Montroll was born in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.)[^o3] He is the son of [Elliott Waters Montroll](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliott_Waters_Montroll), an American scientist and mathematician. He has a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) degree in Mathematics from the [University of Rochester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rochester), a [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in [Electrical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_Engineering) from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), and a Master of Arts in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland).[^o4]

Montroll mastered his first origami book, Isao Honda's *How to make Origami*, at the age of six,[^o3] the same age he began creating his own origami animals.[^o5] He became a member of the [Origami Center of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origami_Center_of_America) at age twelve.[^o3] He attended his first origami convention at age 14. In 2021, Montroll retired from his job at [St. Anselm's Abbey School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Anselm's_Abbey_School) in Washington, D.C., where he taught [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), as well as an origami class. One of John Montroll's hobbies is whistling. He claims to be able to whistle in five octaves and to have shown this talent at two whistling conventions in [Louisburg, North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisburg,_North_Carolina).[^o6]

John Montroll pioneered modern origami with the publication of his first book, *Origami for the Enthusiast*; Dover Publications, 1979, which was the first origami book where each model is folded from single square sheet and no cuts.[^o7] In the same book he introduced the origami term "double rabbit ear fold."[^o8] He is also known for inventing the "dog base".[^o9] His work in the field of origami was briefly mentioned in the York series by author [Laura Ruby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laura_Ruby).
