# Bernd Siebert

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bernd Siebert"}

**Bernd Siebert** (born 5 March 1964 in [Berlin-Wilmersdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Wilmersdorf)) is a German mathematician who researches in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Siebert studied mathematics starting 1984 at the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen). In 1986, he changed to the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and in 1987 to the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) where he finished his Diplom in 1989 under the supervision of [Hans Grauert](wikipedia://en/2979031) with distinction. He became Grauert's PhD student and assistant in [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen). He received his PhD in 1992 (*Faserzykelräume, geometrische Plattifikation und meromorphe Äquivalenzrelationen*). A stay at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) followed in 1993–94 after which we went to Bochum. In 1997–98 he spent some time at the [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) as a visiting scholar before completing his habilitation in Bochum in 1998 (*Gromov–Witten invariants for general symplectic manifolds*). As a DFG-Heisenberg Fellow, he went to the [University of Paris VI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VI)/[University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_VII) from 2000 to 2002. From there, he was called to a professorship at the [Albert-Ludwigs-Universität Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert-Ludwigs-Universität_Freiburg) in 2002. He moved on to the [Universität Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universität_Hamburg) in 2008, and in 2011, he became the head of the Graduiertenkolleg *Mathematics Inspired by String Theory and QFT*. In 2018, Siebert joined the faculty of the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) as a professor of mathematics[^o4] and holds the Sid W. Richardson Foundation Regents Chair in Mathematics #4.[^o5]

In his research, Bernd Siebert contributed substantially to the theory of [Gromov–Witten invariants](wikipedia://en/2301291). Around 2002 by his insights in [logarithmic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logarithmic_geometry), he entered into an ongoing joint research program with [Mark Gross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Gross_%28mathematician%29). This generated a sequence of relevant papers that relate to [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/644671) and [tropical geometry](wikipedia://en/867041).

In 2014, jointly with Mark Gross, he became an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Congress_of_Mathematics) in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul) for the section [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101) (Local mirror symmetry in the tropics). Both of them were awarded the [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167) in 2016.
