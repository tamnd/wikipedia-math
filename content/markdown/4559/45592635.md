# Rudolf Berghammer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Berghammer"}

**Rudolf Berghammer** (Born in 1952, Oberndorf, Bavaria, Germany) is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Rudolf Berghammer worked as an [electrician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrician) at the [Farbwerke Hoechst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farbwerke_Hoechst), Kelheim, from 1966 until 1970. He began studying Mathematics and Computer Science in 1973 at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) (TUM). His academic teachers were [Friedrich L. Bauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_L._Bauer), [Klaus Samelson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Samelson), [Gottfried Tinhofer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Tinhofer), and [Gunther Schmidt](wikipedia://en/44939247). After obtaining his diploma in 1979, he started working as an assistant mainly to Gunther Schmidt and [Friedrich L. Bauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_L._Bauer) at TUM where he obtained his award-winning Ph.D. in 1984. From 1988 on, he worked as an assistant to Gunther Schmidt at the Faculty for Computer Science of the [University of the Bundeswehr Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Bundeswehr_Munich), where he finally got his habilitation in 1990. Since 1993, he is a professor for Computer-aided Program Development at the Department of Computer Science at [Kiel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kiel_University).

## Work {#Work}

For many years he has served as head of the steering committee of the international [RAMiCS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAMiCS) conference series (formerly termed RelMiCS).

Rudolf Berghammer is known for his work in [relational mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relational_mathematics), or Formal Methods of Programming, Semantics, Relational Methods in Computer Science. He developed the **RelView** system for the manipulation and visualisation of [relations](wikipedia://en/19509) and relational programming.

For instance, in 2019, he was coauthor of "Cryptomorphic topological structures: a computational relation algebraic approach".[^o4] This work relates the classical neighborhood system approach to [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) to closure operators, kernel operators, and [Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Aumann) contact relations. The formulation of one approach to another is done with [calculus of relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calculus_of_relations). The article notes the contributions of RelView experiments with finite topologies, for instance for a set with seven elements, 9,535,241 topologies are tested. (see § 9).

## Personal {#Personal}

One of Berghammer's hobbies is [mountaineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mountaineering). In his youth he climbed [Ortler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ortler) or [Piz Bernina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piz_Bernina) and other noted summits. He actively spends several days climbing in the [alps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alps) every year. Furthermore, he is a sailor who owns a sailing vessel in the Baltic Sea.
