# Percey F. Smith

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Percey F. Smith"}

**Percey Franklyn Smith** (August 21, 1867 – June 3, 1956) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics at [Sheffield Scientific School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_Scientific_School) of [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Smith was born in [Nyack, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nyack,_New_York). He studied mathematics at [Sheffield Scientific School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sheffield_Scientific_School) of Yale College, finishing the regular course in 1888 and receiving the [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in 1891.[^o3] Starting in 1888 he was instructor for mathematics in Yale until 1894, followed by academic studies in [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany) and [France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France)[^o3] at the universities of [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen), [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Berlin), [Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris).[^o5] After returning to Yale in 1896 he was assistant professor of mathematics until 1900, then professor until 1936.[^o6]

He wrote several papers related to [Lie sphere geometry](wikipedia://en/16646944),[^o7][^o8][^o9][^o10] and is the author of several textbooks such as *The elements of analytic geometry* (1904), *New analytic geometry* (1912), *Theoretical mechanics* (1910).

He married Julia C. Lum on December 23, 1890.[^o3]

He died at his home in [Hamden, Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamden,_Connecticut) on June 3, 1956.[^o12]

The title "Percey F. Smith Professor of Mathematics" is still used in Yale.[^o13] He was a member of the [Connecticut Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connecticut_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).
