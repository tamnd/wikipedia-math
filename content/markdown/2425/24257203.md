# Randić index

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Randić index"}

The **Randić index**, also known as the **connectivity index**, of a [graph](wikipedia://en/325806) is the sum of bond contributions $1/(d_i d_j)^{1/2}$ where $d_i$ and $d_j$ are the degrees of the vertices making bond *i* ~ *j*.

:::

## History {#History}

This [graph invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_invariant) was introduced by [Milan Randić](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan_Randić) in 1975.[^o3] It is often used in [chemoinformatics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemoinformatics) for investigations of [organic compounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Organic_compound).
