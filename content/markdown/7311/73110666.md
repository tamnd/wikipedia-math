# Émery topology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Émery topology"}

In [martingale theory](wikipedia://en/274536), **Émery topology** is a [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) on the space of [semimartingales](wikipedia://en/9653775). The topology is used in [financial mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_mathematics). The class of [stochastic integrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_integral) with general [predictable](wikipedia://en/33414015) integrands coincides with the [closure](wikipedia://en/42309) of the set of all simple integrals.[^o2]

:::

The topology was introduced in 1979 by the French mathematician [Michel Émery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_Émery).[^o3]

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $(\Omega,\mathcal{A},\{\mathcal{F_t}\},P)$ be a [filtered](wikipedia://en/57854817) probability space, where the filtration satisfies the [usual conditions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Usual_conditions) and $T\in (0,\infty)$. Let $\mathcal{S}(P)$ be the space of real semimartingales and $\mathcal{E}(1)$ the space of simple [predictable](wikipedia://en/33414015) processes $H$ with $|H|=1$.

We define

$$\|X\|_{\mathcal{S}(P)}:=\sup\limits_{H\in \mathcal{E}(1)}\mathbb{E}\left[1\wedge \left(\sup\limits_{t\in[0,T]}|(H\cdot X)_t|\right)\right].$$

Then $(\mathcal{S}(P),d)$ with the metric $d(X,Y):=\|X-Y\|_{\mathcal{S}(P)}$ is a [complete metric space](wikipedia://en/7832) and the induced topology is called **Émery topology**.[^o6][^o2]

:::
