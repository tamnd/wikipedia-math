# Baik–Deift–Johansson theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Baik–Deift–Johansson theorem"}

The **Baik–Deift–Johansson theorem** is a result from probabilistic [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). It deals with the [subsequences](wikipedia://en/236098) of a randomly [uniformly](wikipedia://en/1699214) drawn [permutation](wikipedia://en/44027) from the set $\{1,2,\dots,N\}$. The theorem makes a statement about the [distribution](wikipedia://en/23543) of the length of the longest increasing subsequence in the limit. The theorem was influential in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/22934) since it connected the [KPZ-universality](wikipedia://en/30724348) with the theory of [random matrices](wikipedia://en/1648765).

:::

The theorem was proven in 1999 by [Jinho Baik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jinho_Baik), [Percy Deift](wikipedia://en/25799769) and [Kurt Johansson](wikipedia://en/62288113).[^o2][^o3]

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

For each $N \geq 1$ let $\pi_N$ be a uniformly chosen permutation with length $N$. Let $l(\pi_N)$ be the length of the longest, increasing subsequence of $\pi_N$.

Then we have for every $x \in \mathbb{R}$ that

$$\mathbb{P}\left(\frac{l(\pi_N)-2\sqrt{N}}{N^{1/6}}\leq x \right)\to F_2(x),\quad N \to \infty$$

where $F_2(x)$ is the [Tracy-Widom distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tracy-Widom_distribution) of the [Gaussian unitary ensemble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaussian_unitary_ensemble).

:::
