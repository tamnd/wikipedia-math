# James Pickands

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Pickands"}

**James Pickands III** (September 4, 1931 – March 9, 2022) was an American mathematical statistician known for his contribution to [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732) and [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895).[^o2][^o3]

:::

Pickands was born in [Euclid, Ohio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclid,_Ohio) to James Pickands II and Sarah Cornelia Martin.[^o4][^o6] He studied at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), where he obtained his bachelor's degree. He then moved to [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), where he received his PhD under Simeon Berman in 1965. While at Columbia, Pickands also worked with [Emil Gumbel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Gumbel),[^o5] and developed interests in [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732).

Pickands served in the US Army in [Aberdeen, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aberdeen,_Maryland)[^o6] after graduating from Columbia university. He briefly taught at [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech) as an assistant professor before joining the [Wharton School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School_of_the_University_of_Pennsylvania) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in 1969 as an associate professor, where he stayed for the rest of his career.[^o2]

Pickands had three doctoral students, including [Bruce Cooil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Cooil).[^o7]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Pickands's great-grandfather James S. Pickands (1839–1896)[^o9] is a cofounder of the American shipping company [Pickands Mather Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pickands_Mather_Group).
