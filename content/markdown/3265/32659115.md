# Richard Johnsonbaugh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Johnsonbaugh"}

**Richard F. Johnsonbaugh** (born 1941)[^o2] is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist). His interests include [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492) and the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220). He is the author of several textbooks.

:::

Johnsonbaugh earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), and then moved to the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) for graduate study.[^o3] He completed his Ph.D. at Oregon in 1969. His dissertation, *I. Classical Fundamental Groups and Covering Space Theory in the Setting of Cartan and Chevalley; II. Spaces and Algebras of Vector-Valued Differentiable Functions*, was supervised by Bertram Yood. He also has a second master's degree in computer science from the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago).[^o3]

He is currently [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at [De Paul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Paul_University).[^o3]

## Books {#Books}

- *Discrete Mathematics* (MacMillan, 1984; 8th ed., Pearson, 2018)[^o6]
- *Foundations of Mathematical Analysis* (with W. E. Pfaffenberger, Marcel Dekker, 1981; Dover, 2010)[^o7]
- *Applications Programming in ANSI C* (with Martin Kalin, Prentice Hall, 1993; 3rd ed., 1996)
- *Object-oriented Programming in C++* (with Martin Kalin, Prentice Hall, 1995)
- *Algorithms* (with Marcus Schaefer, Prentice Hall, 2003)[^o8]
