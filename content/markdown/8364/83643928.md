# Michele Guindani

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Michele Guindani"}

**Michele Guindani** is an Italian statistician, specializing in [Bayesian](wikipedia://en/49571) nonparametrics and [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878). He is a professor of Biostatistics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles)[^o3]. He was president of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) in 2025.[^o4] He was the editor-in-chief on the academic journal *[Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/39096238)* from 2019 to 2021,[^o5] and became editor-in-chief of *[Computational Statistics & Data Analysis](wikipedia://en/34877794)* in 2026.[^o6]

:::

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- 2019: Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)[^o8]
- 2022: Fellow of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312)[^o9]
