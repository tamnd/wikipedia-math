# Henri Milloux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Henri Milloux"}

**Paul Henri Milloux** (13 April 1898, in [Crépy-en-Laonnois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crépy,_Aisne) – 28 June 1980, in [Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bordeaux)) was a French mathematician, specializing in [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) and [meromorphic functions](wikipedia://en/80857) in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).[^o2][^o3]

:::

Milloux did his secondary and undergraduate studies at the [University of Lille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lille) from 1916 to 1921, although his studies were seriously disrupted by WW I. In 1921 he passed the *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)*. He received his PhD from the University of Paris in 1924 with thesis *Le théorème de M. Picard. Suites de functions holomorphes. Fonctions méromorphes et functions entières*. After several teaching jobs, he was appointed in 1926 as lecturer at the Faculty of Sciences of the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg). At Strasbourg, he worked closely with [Georges Valiron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georges_Valiron), who left Strasbourg in 1931 for a position at the Sorbonne.[^o4] In 1933 Milloux was appointed to the chair of infinitésimal calculus and higher mathematical analysis at the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux), where he remained until his retirement in 1965 as professor emeritus.[^o2]

Milloux was an Invited Speaker at the [International Mathematical Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Mathematical_Congress) at [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) in 1932 and again at [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo) in 1936. He established precise estimates that sharpened the qualitative results of [Montel's theorem](wikipedia://en/1484228).[^o4] He was elected in 1956 a corresponding member and in 1959 a full member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o2]
