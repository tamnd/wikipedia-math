# Ola Rosling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ola Rosling"}

**Ola Rosling** (born 1 November 1975) is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swedish_people) statistician known for his work for the [Gapminder Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gapminder_Foundation) on changing the global quality of life. He is the chairman, director and co-founder of the foundation.[^o3]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Rosling co-founded the Gapminder Foundation together with his wife [Anna Rosling Rönnlund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Rosling_Rönnlund) and his father [Hans Rosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Rosling). Ola led the development of the [Trendalyzer](wikipedia://en/13720879) software that creates interactive graphics from statistical datasets.[^o5] The software was bought by [Google](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google) in 2007[^o6] and Rosling and his team worked for Google from then on. He became Google's Public Data product manager.[^o7]

*Image: Anna Rosling Rönnlund, Hans Rosling, and Ola Rosling on "Factfulness".jpg.* Left to right: Anna Rosling Rönnlund, Hans Rosling, and Ola Rosling discuss their book "Factfulness" in 2016.

Ola and Anna Rosling collaborated with [Hans Rosling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans_Rosling) and co-authored the book called *[Factfulness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Factfulness).*[^o8][^o9]
