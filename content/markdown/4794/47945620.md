# Snedecor Award

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Snedecor Award"}

The **Snedecor Award**, named after [George W. Snedecor](wikipedia://en/1134409), is given by the [Committee of Presidents of Statistical Societies](wikipedia://en/44155344) to a [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) for contribution to [biometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometry).

:::
