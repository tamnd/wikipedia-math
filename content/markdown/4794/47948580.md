# Sucharit Sarkar

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Sucharit Sarkar"}

**Sucharit Sarkar** (born 1983) is an Indian topologist and professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) who works in low-dimensional topology.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Sarkar attended secondary school at [South Point High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Point_School) in his hometown, [Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata), India. In the [International Mathematical Olympiads](wikipedia://en/82208) in 2001 and 2002, he received gold and silver medals respectively.[^o4][^o5] He completed his [Bachelor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Mathematics) degree from the [Indian Statistical Institute](wikipedia://en/2238418), Bangalore from 2002 to 2005.[^o6]

Sarkar received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2009 under the guidance of [Zoltán Szabó](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zoltán_Szabó_%28mathematician%29). He went on to postdoctoral fellowships at the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) and [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), before becoming an assistant professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2012. In 2016 he moved to the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o8]

Sarkar's research area is [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), with particular interests in [knot theory](wikipedia://en/366808), [Heegaard Floer homology](wikipedia://en/2672856#Heegaard_Floer_homology), and [Khovanov homology](wikipedia://en/2639906).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- Sarkar was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro) in 2018.[^o10]
- Sarkar was a [Clay Research Fellow](wikipedia://en/7655) from 2009 until 2013.[^o11]
