# Victor Kac

Victor Katz

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Kac"}

**Victor Gershevich** (**Grigorievich**) **Kac** (Виктор Гершевич (Григорьевич) Кац; born 19 December 1943) is a Soviet and American mathematician at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT), known for his work in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He co-discovered[^o4] [Kac–Moody algebras](wikipedia://en/1146292), and used the [Weyl–Kac character formula](wikipedia://en/5011916#Weyl.E2.80.93Kac_character_formula) for them to reprove the [Macdonald identities](wikipedia://en/5027268). He classified the finite-dimensional simple [Lie superalgebras](wikipedia://en/297501), and found the [Kac determinant formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kac_determinant_formula) for the [Virasoro algebra](wikipedia://en/348029). He is also known for the Kac–Weisfeiler conjectures with [Boris Weisfeiler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Weisfeiler).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kac studied mathematics at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), receiving his MS in 1965 and his PhD in 1968.[^o6] From 1968 to 1976, he held a teaching position at the [Moscow Institute of Electronic Machine Building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Institute_of_Electronic_Machine_Building) (MIEM). He left the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) in 1977, becoming an associate professor of mathematics at MIT. In 1981, he was promoted to full professor. Kac received a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) and the Medal of the [Collège de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collège_de_France), both in 1981, and a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1986. He received the [Wigner Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wigner_Medal) (1996) "in recognition of work on affine [Lie algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_algebras) that has had wide influence in [theoretical physics](wikipedia://en/19594028)". In 1978 he was an invited speaker (*Highest weight representations of infinite dimensional Lie algebras*) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (ICM) in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki). Kac was a plenary speaker at the 1988 [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) centennial conference. In 2002 he gave a plenary lecture, *Classification of Supersymmetries*, at the ICM in Beijing.

Kac is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822),[^o7] an honorary member of the [Moscow Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9098251), Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) and a Member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).

The research of Victor Kac primarily concerns [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). His work appears in mathematics and physics and in the development of [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267), [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) and the theory of [integrable systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable_systems).

Kac has published 13 books and over 200 articles in mathematics and physics journals and is listed as an [ISI highly cited researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_highly_cited_researcher).[^o8] Victor Kac was awarded the 2015 AMS [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Lifetime Achievement.[^o9][^o10]

He was married with [Michèle Vergne](wikipedia://en/40970117)[^o11] and they have a daughter, Marianne Kac-Vergne, who is a professor of American civilization at the university of Picardie. His brother [Boris Katz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boris_Katz) is a principal research scientist at MIT.[^o12]

## Kac–Moody algebra {#Kac–Moody_algebra}

"Almost simultaneously in 1967, Victor Kac in the USSR and [Robert Moody](wikipedia://en/4311313) in Canada developed what was to become [Kac–Moody algebra](wikipedia://en/1146292). Kac and Moody noticed that if [Wilhelm Killing](wikipedia://en/1531824)'s conditions were relaxed, it was still possible to associate to the [Cartan matrix](wikipedia://en/1146433) a Lie algebra which, necessarily, would be infinite dimensional." – A.J. Coleman[^o14]
