# Virtually Haken conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Virtually Haken conjecture"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), an area of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **virtually Haken conjecture** states that every [compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_manifold), [orientable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orientable_manifold), [irreducible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreducible_manifold) [three-dimensional manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) with infinite [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) is *virtually Haken*. That is, it has a finite cover (a [covering space](wikipedia://en/250323) with a finite-to-one covering map) that is a [Haken manifold](wikipedia://en/236567).

:::

After the proof of the [geometrization conjecture](wikipedia://en/220642) by [Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266), the conjecture was only open for [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700).

The conjecture is usually attributed to [Friedhelm Waldhausen](wikipedia://en/20163961) in a paper from 1968,[^o2] although he did not formally state it. This problem is formally stated as Problem 3.2 in [Kirby's problem list](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirby's_problem_list).

A proof of the conjecture was announced on March 12, 2012 by [Ian Agol](wikipedia://en/32084718) in a seminar lecture he gave at the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595). The proof appeared shortly thereafter in a preprint which was eventually published in [Documenta Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documenta_Mathematica).[^o3] The proof was obtained via a strategy by previous work of [Daniel Wise](wikipedia://en/38193678) and collaborators, relying on actions of the fundamental group on certain auxiliary spaces (CAT(0) cube complexes, also known as [median graphs](wikipedia://en/16970848))[^o4] It used as an essential ingredient the freshly-obtained solution to the [surface subgroup conjecture](wikipedia://en/4565128) by [Jeremy Kahn](wikipedia://en/48625747) and [Vladimir Markovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Markovic).[^o5][^o6] Other results which are directly used in Agol's proof include the Malnormal Special Quotient Theorem of Wise[^o7] and a criterion of [Nicolas Bergeron](wikipedia://en/69003741) and Wise for the cubulation of groups.[^o8]

In 2018 related results were obtained by Piotr Przytycki and Daniel Wise proving that mixed 3-manifolds are also virtually special, that is they can be cubulated into a cube complex with a finite cover where all the hyperplanes are embedded which by the previous mentioned work can be made virtually Haken.[^o9][^o10]
