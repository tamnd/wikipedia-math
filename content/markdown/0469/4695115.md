# Cameron Gordon (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cameron Gordon"}

*Image: Cameron Gordon 2010.jpg.* Gordon in 2010

**Cameron Gordon** (born 1945) is a [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and Sid W. Richardson Foundation Regents Chair[^o2] in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), known for his work in [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). Among his notable results is his work with [Marc Culler](wikipedia://en/29258614), [John Luecke](wikipedia://en/29631637), and [Peter Shalen](wikipedia://en/3520910) on the [cyclic surgery theorem](wikipedia://en/29580519). This was an important ingredient in his work with Luecke showing that [knots](wikipedia://en/366808) were determined by their [complement](wikipedia://en/1350120). Gordon was also involved in the resolution of the [Smith conjecture](wikipedia://en/4476828).

:::

[Andrew Casson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrew_Casson) and Gordon defined and proved basic theorems regarding strongly irreducible [Heegaard splittings](wikipedia://en/864418), an important concept in the modernization of Heegaard splitting theory. They also worked on the [slice](wikipedia://en/4158126)-[ribbon](wikipedia://en/9809292) conjecture, inventing the Casson-Gordon invariants in the process.

Gordon was a 1999 [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellow).[^o3] In 2005, Gordon was elected a Corresponding Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o4] In 2023, Gordon was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5]
