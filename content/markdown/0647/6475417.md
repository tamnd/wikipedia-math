# Matt Visser

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Matt Visser"}

**Matt Visser** () is a mathematics professor at [Victoria University of Wellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Wellington), in New Zealand.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Visser completed a PhD at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), supervised by [Mary K. Gaillard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mary_K._Gaillard).[^o4]

Visser's research interests include [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) and [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology).

Visser has produced a large number of research papers on the subject of [wormholes](wikipedia://en/34043), gravitational horizons and notably the emerging subject of [acoustic metrics](wikipedia://en/2497795).

He is the author of the reference book on the current state of wormhole theory, *Lorentzian Wormholes — from Einstein to Hawking* (1996) and co-editor of *Artificial Black Holes* (2002).

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2013 Visser was awarded the [Dan Walls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Walls) Medal by the New Zealand Institute of Physics.[^o6]
