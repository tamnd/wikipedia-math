## Algebraic equations {#Algebraic_equations}

An [algebraic equation](wikipedia://en/404001) is an equation involving only algebraic expressions in the unknowns. These are further classified by [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652).

- [Linear equation](wikipedia://en/17570) – algebraic equation of degree one.
- [Polynomial equation](wikipedia://en/23000#Solving_equations) – equation in which a polynomial is set equal to another polynomial.
- [Transcendental equation](wikipedia://en/6073963) – equation involving a [transcendental function](wikipedia://en/297021) of one of its variables.
- [Functional equation](wikipedia://en/364377) – equation in which the unknowns are [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) rather than simple quantities.
- [Differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309) – equation involving [derivatives](wikipedia://en/7921).
- [Integral equation](wikipedia://en/474234) – equation involving [integrals](wikipedia://en/15532).
- [Diophantine equation](wikipedia://en/9109) – equation where the only [solutions](wikipedia://en/537048) of interest of the unknowns are the [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) ones.

## General algebra concepts {#General_algebra_concepts}

- [Fundamental theorem of algebra](wikipedia://en/51414) – states that every non-constant single-variable polynomial with complex coefficients has at least one complex root. This includes polynomials with real coefficients, since every real number is a [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) with an imaginary part equal to zero.
- [Equations](wikipedia://en/9284) – equality of two mathematical expressions
- [Linear equation](wikipedia://en/17570) – an algebraic equation with a degree of one
- [Quadratic equation](wikipedia://en/25175) – an algebraic equation with a degree of two
- [Cubic equation](wikipedia://en/180787) – an algebraic equation with a degree of three
- [Quartic equation](wikipedia://en/251591) – an algebraic equation with a degree of four
- [Quintic equation](wikipedia://en/303368) – an algebraic equation with a degree of five
- [Polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) – an algebraic expression consisting of variables and coefficients
- [Inequalities](wikipedia://en/89489) – a comparison between values
- [Functions](wikipedia://en/185427) – mapping that associates a single output value with each input value
- [Sequences](wikipedia://en/27838) – ordered list of elements either finite or infinite
- [Systems of equations](wikipedia://en/249617) – finite set of equations
- [Vectors](wikipedia://en/23218956) – element of a vector space
- [Matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) – two dimensional array of numbers
- [Vector space](wikipedia://en/32370) – basic algebraic structure of linear algebra
- [Field](wikipedia://en/10603) – algebraic structure with addition, multiplication and division
- [Groups](wikipedia://en/19447) – algebraic structure with a single binary operation
- [Rings](wikipedia://en/48404) – algebraic structure with addition and multiplication
