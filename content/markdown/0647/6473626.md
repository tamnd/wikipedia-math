# Outline of geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Outline of geometry"}

[Geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) is a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) concerned with questions of shape, size, relative position of figures, and the properties of space. Geometry is one of the oldest mathematical sciences. Modern geometry also extends into non-Euclidean spaces, topology, and fractal dimensions, bridging pure mathematics with applications in physics, computer science, and data visualization.

:::
