# Martin Schottenloher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin Schottenloher"}

**Martin Schottenloher** is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on July 25, 1944, in Lindau, Germany.

He specializes in [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775), [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence) and complex analysis.[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

He completed his Dr. rer. nat. degree at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) in 1972. His doctoral supervisors were Walter Roelcke and [Karl Stein](wikipedia://en/7218529).[^o5] He obtained his habilitation in 1975 also from LMU Munich.[^o6]

At LMU Munich, he supervised the doctoral [dissertations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) of more than a dozen students.[^o5]
