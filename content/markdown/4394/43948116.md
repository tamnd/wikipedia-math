# Borel's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Borel's theorem"}

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Borel's theorem**, due to (txt), says the [cohomology ring](wikipedia://en/1069723) of a [classifying space](wikipedia://en/923556) or a [classifying stack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classifying_stack) is a [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065).[^o3]

:::
