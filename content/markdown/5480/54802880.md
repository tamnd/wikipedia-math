# Barbara Wohlmuth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Wohlmuth"}

*Image: Barbara Wohlmuth.jpg.* Wohlmuth at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2014

**Barbara I. Wohlmuth** is a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in the numerical solution of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). She holds the [chair of numerical mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TUM_Department_of_Mathematics) at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) (TUM).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wohlmuth earned a master's degree in mathematics in 1991 from [Joseph Fourier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Fourier_University) in France, and a diploma in 1992 from TUM. She completed a doctorate at TUM in 1995, under the supervision of Ronald Hoppe, and earned her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2000 at the [University of Augsburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Augsburg).

She worked as a full professor at the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart) from 2001 until 2010, when she returned to TUM.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2005, the [Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Lombardo_Accademia_di_Scienze_e_Lettere) in Milan gave her their [International Giovanni Sacchi Landriani Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Giovanni_Sacchi_Landriani_Prize). She won the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize) of the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft) in 2012. In 2013 she was elected to the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities). In 2020 she was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) "for sustained seminal contributions to the field of numerical mathematics and for exemplary leadership and service to the computational science community". She was elected to the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) in 2022.

She was the [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2014, and an invited speaker on numerical analysis and scientific computing at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).
