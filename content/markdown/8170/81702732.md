# Daniel W. Apley

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel W. Apley"}

**Daniel W. Apley** is an American statistician and engineer. He is a professor of [industrial engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industrial_engineering) and [management sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management_sciences) at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). He is known for contributing to the fields of [statistical modeling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_modeling), [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), and [quality engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quality_engineering), particularly in the diagnosis and control of complex manufacturing systems.

:::

Apley served as the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) of the *[Journal of Quality Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Quality_Technology)* from 2009 to 2012 and of *[Technometrics](wikipedia://en/5854084)* from 2017 to 2019. He was elected a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2019.[^o3]

## Education {#Education}

Apley attended the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan), where he earned a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) (B.Sc.) and [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) (M.S.) in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering) in 1990 and 1992 respectively. Then in 1995 he earned a second M.S. in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering).[^o5] He completed his [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (Ph.D.) in mechanical engineering in 1997 under the supervision of [Jianjun Shi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jianjun_Shi) and [Jun Ni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jun_Ni).[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

Following his doctoral studies, Apley served as an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) from 1998 to 2003. He joined the faculty of [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University) in 2003 as [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) and was subsequently promoted to full professor in the Department of Industrial Engineering and Management Sciences. He also served as the director of the Manufacturing and Design Engineering Program at Northwestern from 2004 to 2008.[^o6]

Apley has held significant leadership roles in major academic journals within the fields of statistics and quality engineering:

- 2002-2009: Associate Editor for *Technometrics*, a leading journal for statistics in the physical, chemical, and engineering sciences published by the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA) and the [American Society for Quality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_for_Quality) (ASQ).
- 2009-2012: Editor-in-Chief of the *[Journal of Quality Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Quality_Technology)*.[^o5]
- 2017-2019: Editor-in-Chief of *Technometrics*.

## Research {#Research}

Apley's research focuses on the interface of engineering modeling, statistical analysis, and data mining. His work addresses the challenges of data-rich manufacturing environments, specifically in the development of methods for [statistical process control](wikipedia://en/593680), fault diagnosis, and the analysis of simulation models.

He is also known for developing [accumulated local effects](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accumulated_local_effects) (ALE) plots, a method for visualizing the effects of predictor variables in [supervised learning](wikipedia://en/20926) ("black box") models. This method is considered an improvement over [partial dependence plots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_dependence_plots) when predictor variables are correlated.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 2001: [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) CAREER Award.[^o5]
- 2003: [IIE Transactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIE_Transactions) Best Paper Award.[^o6]
- 2008 (and again in 2023): [Frank Wilcoxon Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilcoxon_Award) from the ASA and ASQ.[^o6]
- 2019: [Lloyd S. Nelson Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lloyd_S._Nelson_Award) for the paper with the greatest immediate impact to practitioners appearing in the Journal of Quality Technology in the prior year.[^o5]
- 2019: Elected [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) for "leadership in statistics at the national level" and outstanding publications leadership.[^o3][^o11]
