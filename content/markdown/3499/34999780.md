# Abhyankar's inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abhyankar's inequality"}

**Abhyankar's inequality** is an inequality involving extensions of [valued fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valued_field) in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), introduced by (txt).

:::

Abhyankar's inequality states that for an extension *K*/*k* of [valued fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valued_field), the [transcendence degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcendence_degree) of *K*/*k* is at least the [transcendence degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcendence_degree) of the [residue field](wikipedia://en/10789680) extension plus the rank of the [quotient](wikipedia://en/332640) of the [valuation groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valuation_group); here the rank of an abelian group $A$ is defined as $\dim_{\mathbb{Q}}(A \otimes \mathbb{Q})$.
