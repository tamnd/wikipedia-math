# Atiyah–Jones conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Atiyah–Jones conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Atiyah–Jones conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) about the [homology](wikipedia://en/142432) of the [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609) of [instantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instantons). The original form of the conjecture considered instantons over a 4-dimensional [sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sphere_%28geometry%29). It was introduced by (txt, 97–118) and proved by (txt). The more general version of the Atiyah–Jones conjecture is a question about the [homology](wikipedia://en/142432) of the [moduli spaces](wikipedia://en/361609) of [instantons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instantons) on any 4-dimensional real manifold, or on a complex surface. The Atiyah–Jones conjecture has been proved for ruled surfaces by R. J. Milgram and J. Hurtubise, and for rational surfaces by Elizabeth Gasparim. The conjecture remains unproved for other types of 4 manifolds.
