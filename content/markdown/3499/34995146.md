# Dieudonné plank

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dieudonné plank"}

In mathematics, the **Dieudonné plank** is a specific [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) introduced by (txt). It is an example of a [metacompact space](wikipedia://en/5074872) that is not [paracompact](wikipedia://en/48631).

:::

The notion has since been generalized (by Barr et al.) to that of an absolute CR-epic space.[^o3]
