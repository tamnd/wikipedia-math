# Nagata's conjecture

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Nagata's conjecture"}

**Nagata's conjecture.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nagata's conjecture"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), **Nagata's conjecture** states that **Nagata's automorphism** of the [polynomial ring](wikipedia://en/373065) *k*\[*x*,*y*,*z*\] is [wild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wild_automorphism). The [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) was proposed by (txt) and [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by (txt, 197–227).

:::

Nagata's [automorphism](wikipedia://en/1160) is given by

$$\phi(x,y,z) = (x-2\Delta y-\Delta^2z, y+\Delta z, z),$$

where $\Delta = xz+y^2$.

For the [inverse](wikipedia://en/14907), let $(a,b,c)=\phi(x,y,z)$ Then $z=c$ and $\Delta= b^2+ac$. With this $y=b-\Delta c$ and $x=a+2\Delta y+\Delta^2 z$.
