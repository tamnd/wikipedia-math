# Divergent geometric series

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Divergent geometric series"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an [infinite geometric series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_geometric_series) of the form

:::

$$\sum_{n=1}^\infty ar^{n-1} = a + ar + ar^2 + ar^3 +\cdots$$

is [divergent](wikipedia://en/876428) if and only if $|r| >= 1.$ Methods for summation of divergent series are sometimes useful, and usually evaluate divergent geometric series to a sum that agrees with the formula for the convergent case

$$\sum_{n=1}^\infty ar^{n-1} = \frac{a}{1-r}.$$

This is true of any summation method that possesses the properties of [regularity, linearity, and stability](wikipedia://en/876428#Properties_of_summation_methods).

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

In increasing order of difficulty to sum:

- [1 − 1 + 1 − 1 + ⋯](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_−_1_+_1_−_1_+_⋯), whose common ratio is [−1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/−1_%28number%29)
- [1 − 2 + 4 − 8 + ⋯](wikipedia://en/9939158), whose common ratio is −2
- [1 + 2 + 4 + 8 + ⋯](wikipedia://en/9552145), whose common ratio is [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2_%28number%29)
- [1 + 1 + 1 + 1 + ⋯](wikipedia://en/9929142), whose common ratio is 1.

:::

## Motivation for study {#Motivation_for_study}

It is useful to figure out which summation methods produce the geometric series formula for which common ratios. One application for this information is the so-called **Borel-Okada principle**: If a [regular summation method](wikipedia://en/876428#Properties_of_summation_methods) assigns $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} z^n$ to $1 / (1 - z)$ for all $z$ in a subset $S$ of the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628), given certain restrictions on $S$, then the method also gives the [analytic continuation](wikipedia://en/80842) of any other function $f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a_n z^n$ on the intersection of $S$ with the [Mittag-Leffler star](wikipedia://en/10612837) for $f(z)$.[^o5]

## Summability by region {#Summability_by_region}

### Open unit disk {#Open_unit_disk}

Ordinary summation succeeds only for common ratios $|r| < 1.$

### Closed unit disk {#Closed_unit_disk}

- [Cesàro summation](wikipedia://en/705600)
- [Abel summation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abel_summation)

### Larger disks {#Larger_disks}

- [Euler summation](wikipedia://en/9780066)

### Half-plane {#Half-plane}

The series is [Borel summable](wikipedia://en/2357705) for every *z* with real part \< 1.

### Shadowed plane {#Shadowed_plane}

Certain [moment constant methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moment_constant_method) besides Borel summation can sum the geometric series on the entire Mittag-Leffler star of the function 1/(1 − *z*), that is, for all *z* except the ray *z* ≥ 1.[^o12]
