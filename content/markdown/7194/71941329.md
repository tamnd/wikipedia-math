# Robert D. Hough

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert D. Hough"}

**Robert D. Hough** is an American born mathematician specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), and [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492). He is currently an associate professor of mathematics at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hough holds BS in Math, MS in CS, and PhD in Math degrees from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). He completed his PhD under [Kannan Soundararajan](wikipedia://en/8053624) in 2012. Hough was a post-doctoral researcher at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) and [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University) in the United Kingdom working with [Ben Green](wikipedia://en/1927952) from 2013 to 2015, and was a post-doctoral member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) from 2015 to 2016.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Hough joined [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) as an assistant professor in 2016 and has been an associate professor of mathematics since 2022.[^o4]

## Achievements {#Achievements}

Hough won the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)'s [David P. Robbins Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_P._Robbins_Prize) at the [Joint Math Meetings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joint_Math_Meetings) in 2017.[^o7] The prize was given for finding the solution of a problem imposed by [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).[^o8]

In February 2020, Hough won the [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship).[^o9] He also won a Trustees Faculty Award from Stony Brook University.[^o10]
