# Luiz Velho

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Luiz Velho"}

**Luiz Carlos Pacheco Rodrigues Velho** (born 5 June 1956) is a Brazilian applied mathematician working primarily on [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics) and [computer vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision). He is a full researcher and professor at [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708) (IMPA) and leading scientist of VISGRAF, laboratory that conducts researches in the field of modeling, rendering, imaging, and animation.[^o3][^o4]

:::

In 2010, Luiz Velho received the [National Order of Scientific Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Scientific_Merit) for his scientific and technical contributions.[^o5] In 2005, he was keynote speaker in [Eurographics Symposium on Geometry Processing](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/events/SGP05/speakers.html), in Vienna.

His academic background includes a BE in industrial design from [ESDI-UERJ](https://www.cg.tuwien.ac.at/events/SGP05/speakers.html), a specialization in computer science from [PUC-Rio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PUC-Rio), a MS in computer graphics from the [MIT Media Lab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Media_Lab), a Ph.D. in computer science from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) and a postdoctoral fellowship in applied mathematics from IMPA.[^o6]
