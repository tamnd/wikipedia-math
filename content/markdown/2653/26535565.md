::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 7-orthoplexes"}

In seven-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 7-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 7-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821719), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-orthoplex).

:::

There are 6 truncations of the 7-orthoplex. Vertices of the truncation 7-orthoplex are located as pairs on the edge of the 7-orthoplex. Vertices of the bitruncated 7-orthoplex are located on the triangular faces of the 7-orthoplex. Vertices of the tritruncated 7-orthoplex are located inside the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells of the 7-orthoplex. The final three truncations are best expressed relative to the 7-cube.

There are two [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) associated with the *truncated 7-orthoplex*, one with the C<sub>7</sub> or \[4,3<sup>5</sup>\] Coxeter group, and a lower symmetry with the D<sub>7</sub> or \[3<sup>4,1,1</sup>\] Coxeter group.
