# Goddard problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Goddard problem"}

In [rocketry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rocket), the **Goddard problem** is to optimize the peak [altitude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altitude) of a rocket, ascending vertically, and taking into account [atmospheric drag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_drag) and the [gravitational field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_field). This was first posed by [Robert H. Goddard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_H._Goddard) in his 1919 publication, "A Method of Reaching Extreme Altitudes".[^o2][^o3]

:::
