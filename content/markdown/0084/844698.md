# Lindström

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lindström"}

**Lindström** or **Lindstrøm** is a [Scandinavian surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scandinavian_family_name_etymology) used in the Swedish, Danish and Norwegian languages. In English-speaking countries, the name is commonly spelled as **Lindstrom**. Notable people with the surname include:
