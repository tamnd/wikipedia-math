# Hundredth

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hundredth"}

In [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118), a **hundredth** is a single part of something that has been divided equally into a hundred parts.[^o2] For example, a hundredth of 675 is 6.75. In this manner it is used with the prefix "[centi-](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centi-)" such as in [centimeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centimeter). A hundredth is also one [percent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Percent).

:::

A hundredth is the [reciprocal](wikipedia://en/229940) of 100.

A hundredth is written as a [decimal fraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decimal_fraction) as 0.01, and as a [vulgar fraction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vulgar_fraction) as 1/100.[^o3]

“Hundredth” is also the [ordinal number](wikipedia://en/26547932) that follows “ninety-ninth” and precedes “hundred and first.” It is written as 100th.
