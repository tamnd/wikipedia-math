# Grothendieck's connectedness theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grothendieck's connectedness theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Grothendieck's connectedness theorem**,[^o2][^o3] states that if *A* is a complete [Noetherian](wikipedia://en/56129) [local ring](wikipedia://en/153391) whose spectrum is *k*-connected and *f* is in the [maximal ideal](wikipedia://en/48164), then Spec(*A*/*fA*) is (*k* − 1)-connected. Here a [Noetherian scheme](wikipedia://en/2783891) is called *k*-connected if its dimension is greater than *k* and the complement of every [closed subset](wikipedia://en/47279) of dimension less than *k* is connected.[^o2]

:::

It is a local analogue of [Bertini's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertini's_theorem).
