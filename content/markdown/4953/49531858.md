# Stefano da San Gregorio

*Image: Stefano da San Gregorio - Aritmetica pratica, 1642 - BEIC 4714882.tiff.* *Aritmetica pratica*, title page

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefano da San Gregorio"}

**Stefano da San Gregorio** was a [17th-century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17th-century) [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theologian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theologian) from the [Order of Discalced Augustinians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discalced_Augustinians).[^o2]

:::
