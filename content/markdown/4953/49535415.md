# Brian C. Moyer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Brian C. Moyer"}

**Brian C. Moyer** is an American economist who is the director of the U.S. [National Center for Health Statistics](wikipedia://en/1042734).[^o3] Moyer serves as senior advisor to the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention) and to the secretary of the [U.S. Department of Health and Human Services](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Department_of_Health_and_Human_Services); he also serves as the statistical official for the department.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Moyer received a bachelor's and master's degrees in economics from the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) and a Ph.D. in economics in 2002 from [American University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_University).[^o5][^o6][^o7][^o8]

## Career {#Career}

Moyer spent more than 25 years with the [U.S. Department of Commerce](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Department_of_Commerce). He served as Director of the [Bureau of Economic Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Economic_Analysis) (BEA), where he led modernization efforts to improve official economic statistics, including the measures of [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP).[^o10][^o5]
