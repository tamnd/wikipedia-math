# Vadim Yankov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vadim Yankov"}

**Vadim Anatolyevich Yankov** (Вади́м Анато́льевич Янко́в; 1 February 1935 – 11 March 2024) was a Russian mathematician, philosopher, and [political prisoner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_prisoner).

:::

In 1968, Yankov co-signed a Letter of 99 asking for the release of imprisoned mathematician [Alexander Esenin-Volpin](wikipedia://en/2638312).[^o3]

Shortly before the 1981 [martial law crisis in Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martial_law_in_Poland), Yankov sent out a seven-page [samizdat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samizdat) letter "A Letter to Russian Workers about the Events in Poland". In it, he encouraged the Soviet working class to follow the example of [Solidarity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solidarity_%28Polish_trade_union%29), stressing social participation and non-violent protest.[^o4]

In 1982, he was arrested and charged with [anti-Soviet agitation and propaganda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Soviet_agitation).[^o5] He was sentenced in January 1983 to four years of [imprisonment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gulag) and three years in [internal exile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exile). He served his time in Dubravlag labor camp in [Mordovia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordovia), near [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), and his exile in [Buryatia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buryatia) in south-central Siberia. He was released in January 1987 and [rehabilitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehabilitation_%28Soviet%29) in 1991.[^o6] Yankov is featured as a prominent character in [Levan Berdzenishvili](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levan_Berdzenishvili)'s novel *Sacred Darkness*, describing their joint time in prison.[^o7]

After 1991, he taught mathematics and philosophy at the Department of Intellectual Systems at [Russian State University for the Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_State_University_for_the_Humanities) in Moscow.

Yankov died on 11 March 2024, at the age of 89.[^o8]
