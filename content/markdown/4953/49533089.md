# Ottavio Scarlattini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ottavio Scarlattini"}

**Ottavio Scarlattini** (1623 – 1699) was an Italian [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theologian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theologian).[^o2]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ottavio Scarlattini became a [Canon Regular of the Lateran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canon_Regular_of_the_Lateran) at [Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bologna) in 1639, and then he was appointed [Archpriest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archpriest) of Villa Fontana in [Medicina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medicina).[^o2] Scarlattini's 1679 publication *Dell'Epicuro contro gli epicurei* was an 830 page treatise arguing for the [beatification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beatification) of [Epicurus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicurus) in which he defended [Epicureanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epicureanism) as a philosophy.[^o4] Translated into Latin, the work became well known across Europe.[^o5] His 1683 publication *L'huomo, e sue parti figurato, e simbolico, anatomico, rationale, morale, mistico, politico, e legale* was a discourse on the symbolism of the parts of the human body; giving each part an anatomical, rational, moral, mystic, political, and legal analysis.[^o6]
