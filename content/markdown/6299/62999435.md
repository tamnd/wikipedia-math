# Mihaela Ignatova

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mihaela Ignatova"}

**Mihaela Ignatova** is a Bulgarian mathematician who won the 2020 [Sadosky Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadosky_Prize) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) for her research in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), and in particular in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and [fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/11034).

:::

## Education {#Education}

In 2004, Ignatova earned both a bachelor's degree from [Sofia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia_University) and a master's degree from the [University of Nantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nantes). She earned a second master's degree from Sofia University in 2006, working under the supervision of mathematician [Emil Horozov](wikipedia://en/46252121). She then completed PhD studies in mathematics from [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) in 2011 under the supervision of Igor Kukavica.

## Career {#Career}

After working as a visiting assistant professor at the [University of California, Riverside](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Riverside), a postdoctoral researcher at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), and an instructor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), she moved to [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) as an assistant professor in 2018.
