# Aaron Galuten

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Aaron Galuten"}

**Aaron Galuten** (March 2, 1917–September 23, 1994)[^o2] was an American mathematician, known mainly as the founder and principal operator of the [Chelsea Publishing Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chelsea_Publishing_Company).[^o3]

:::
