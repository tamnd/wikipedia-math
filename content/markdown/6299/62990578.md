# Mireille Capitaine

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mireille Capitaine"}

**Mireille Capitaine** is a French mathematician whose research focuses on random matrices and free probability theory. In 2012 she was a recipient of the [G. de B. Robinson Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._de_B._Robinson_Award) for a paper she coauthored that introduced free Bessel laws, a two-parameter family of generalizations of the [free Poisson distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_Poisson_distribution).[^o2][^o3] She received her PhD in 1996 from [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sabatier_University), where she was advised by Michel Ledoux. She is currently a researcher for the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) (CNRS), associated with the Toulouse Institute of Mathematics.[^o5]
