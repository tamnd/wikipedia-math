# Leah M. Smith

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leah M. Smith"}

**Leah M. Smith** is a Canadian [biostatistician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biostatistician) affiliated with the [Canadian Cancer Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Cancer_Society) and known for her work on the effectiveness and safety of [HPV vaccines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HPV_vaccine). She is the 2020 winner of the Lise Manchester Award, given annually by the [Statistical Society of Canada](wikipedia://en/15572327) in recognition of "excellence in statistical research that helps guide public policy in Canada".

:::

After studying psychology at the [Memorial University of Newfoundland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memorial_University_of_Newfoundland), Smith earned a master's degree in [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997) in 2010 from [Queen's University at Kingston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_at_Kingston), and completed her PhD in 2014 at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University). She returned to Queen's University for postdoctoral research before joining the Canadian Cancer Society in 2015.

Beyond her work on the HPV vaccine, Smith has participated in studies on [screening](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer_screening) for [colorectal cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colorectal_cancer) and the prevalence of cancer more generally in the Canadian population,
