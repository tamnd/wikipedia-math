# Post-Minkowskian expansion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Post-Minkowskian expansion"}

*Image: PM vs PN expansion EN.svg.* Post-minkowskian vs Post-newtonian expansions

In [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), precisely in the [general theory of relativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_theory_of_relativity), **post-Minkowskian expansions** (**PM**) or **post-Minkowskian approximations** are mathematical methods used to find approximate solutions of [Einstein's equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein's_equations) by means of a [power series development](wikipedia://en/30448) of the [metric tensor](wikipedia://en/195795).

:::

Unlike [post-Newtonian expansions](wikipedia://en/4383472) (PN), in which the series development is based on a combination of powers of the velocity $v$ (which must be negligible compared to [that of light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_light) $c$) and the [gravitational constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_constant) $G$, in the post-Minkowskian case the developments are based only on the gravitational constant, allowing analysis even at velocities close to that of light (relativistic).[^o2]

One of the earliest works on this method of resolution is that of [Bruno Bertotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruno_Bertotti), published in *[Nuovo Cimento](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuovo_Cimento)* in 1956.[^o6]
