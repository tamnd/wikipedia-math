# Gary Grove Koch

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gary Grove Koch"}

**Gary Grove Koch** (born January 6, 1942)[^o3] is an American biostatistician who serves as professor of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) and director of the [Biometric Consulting Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biometric_Consulting_Laboratory) at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), where he has been a faculty member since 1968.[^o4] In 1972, he was elected a [fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association), and in 1974, he received the [Mortimer Spiegelman Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mortimer_Spiegelman_Award) from the [American Public Health Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Public_Health_Association). In 1996, he was awarded an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from [DeMontfort University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeMontfort_University) in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).[^o3]
