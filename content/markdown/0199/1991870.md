# Normed algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normed algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **normed algebra** *A* is an [algebra over a field](wikipedia://en/191788) which has a sub-multiplicative [norm](wikipedia://en/990534):

:::

$$\forall x,y\in A\qquad \|xy\|\le\|x\|\|y\|.$$

Some authors require it to have a [multiplicative identity](wikipedia://en/191788#Unital_algebras) such that ‖‖ = 1.
