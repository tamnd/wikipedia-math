# R. R. Rockingham Gill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="R. R. Rockingham Gill"}

**Richard Rowan Rockingham Gill** (born 1944) is a lecturer of philosophy, in particular logic, and is an author.

:::

Gill received a [MA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) from the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews) and has published variously in the [*Journal of Symbolic Logic*](wikipedia://en/3770438), *Archiv für mathematische Logik und Grundlagenforschung* (Archive for Mathematical Logic and Basic Research) and other journals.

He lectured at the [University of Wales, Lampeter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wales,_Lampeter) and is also the former head of the Department of Philosophy.
