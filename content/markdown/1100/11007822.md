# Harish-Chandra class

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harish-Chandra class"}

In mathematics, **Harish-Chandra's class** is a class of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945) used in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). Harish-Chandra's class contains all [semisimple](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semisimple_Lie_group) connected [linear](wikipedia://en/3983656) Lie groups and is closed under natural operations, most importantly, the passage to [Levi subgroups](wikipedia://en/2774198). This closure property is crucial for many inductive arguments in representation theory of Lie groups, whereas the classes of semisimple or connected semisimple Lie groups are not closed in this sense.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A Lie group *G* with the [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) *g* is said to be in Harish-Chandra's class if it satisfies the following conditions:

- *g* is a [reductive](wikipedia://en/1337587) Lie algebra (the product of a semisimple and abelian Lie algebra).
- The Lie group *G* has only a finite number of [connected components](wikipedia://en/6233).
- The [adjoint action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjoint_representation_of_a_Lie_group) of any element of *G* on *g* is given by an action of an element of the connected component of the Lie group of Lie algebra automorphisms of the [complexification](wikipedia://en/755604) *g*⊗**C**.
- The subgroup *G*<sub>ss</sub> of *G* generated by the image of the semisimple part *g*<sub>ss</sub>=\[*g*,*g*\] of the Lie algebra *g* under the [exponential map](wikipedia://en/44612290) has finite [center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_%28group%29).

:::
