# Dmitrii Abramovich Raikov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dmitrii Abramovich Raikov"}

**Dmitrii Abramovich Raikov** (Дмитрий Абрамович Райков, born 11 November 1905 in Odessa; died 1980 in Moscow) was a Russian mathematician who studied [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

Raikov studied in Odessa and Moscow, graduating in 1929. He was secretary of the [Komsomol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Komsomol) at Moscow State University and was active in the 1929–1930 campaign against the mathematician [Dmitri Fyodorovich Egorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Fyodorovich_Egorov). At that time he and his fellow campaigners also rejected non-applied research, but this soon changed. In 1933, he was dismissed from the Communist Party on charges of Trotskyism and exiled to [Voronezh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronezh), but was rehabilitated two years later and returned to Moscow. From 1938 to 1948, he was at the Mathematical Institute of the [Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_Academy_of_Sciences) and in the [Second World War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_World_War) in the militia. He was habilitated (Russian doctorate) in 1941 with [Aleksandr Yakovlevich Khinchin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Yakovlevich_Khinchin) at the [Lomonosov University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_University)[^o4] and in 1950 became professor. He taught at the Pedagogical Institute in [Kostroma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kostroma) and from 1952 in Shuysky, before he taught from 1957 at the State Pedagogical University in Moscow. He also supervised students and taught at Lomonosov University.

[Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) and Raikov's 1943 theorem[^o5] states that a [locally compact group](wikipedia://en/1651204) $G$ is completely determined by its (possibly infinite-dimensional) [irreducible](wikipedia://en/385982) [unitary representations](wikipedia://en/381010): for every two elements $g,h$ of $G$ there is an irreducible unitary representation $\rho$ with $\rho (g) \neq \rho (h)$.[^o7]

He also worked on [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), for example in 1938 he proved an equivalent of the [Cramér's theorem](wikipedia://en/13509534) for the [Poisson distribution](wikipedia://en/23009144).

He edited the Russian editions of [Nicolas Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394)'s "Topology and Integration Theory" and translated numerous other mathematical works from Italian, English and German, for example the lectures on the theory of [algebraic numbers](wikipedia://en/1158) by [Erich Hecke](wikipedia://en/1233990), the book *[Moderne Algebra](wikipedia://en/37252648)* by [Bartel Leendert van der Waerden](wikipedia://en/91390), the *[Problems and Theorems in Analysis](wikipedia://en/77356586)* by [George Pólya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Pólya) and [Gábor Szegő](wikipedia://en/46065), the introduction to the theory of [Fourier integrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourier_integral) by [Edward Charles Titchmarsh](wikipedia://en/1709459), the lectures on [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) by [Francesco Tricomi](wikipedia://en/19830487), the introduction to [differential](wikipedia://en/50416) and [integral calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_calculus) by [Edmund Landau](wikipedia://en/222230), the monograph on [divergent series](wikipedia://en/15287) by [Godfrey Harold Hardy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godfrey_Harold_Hardy) and the [finite dimensional vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370) by [Paul Halmos](wikipedia://en/318598).
