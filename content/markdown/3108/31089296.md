# The geometry and topology of three-manifolds

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The geometry and topology of three-manifolds"}

***The geometry and topology of three-manifolds*** is a set of widely circulated notes for a graduate course taught at Princeton University by [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190) from 1978 to 1980 describing his work on 3-manifolds. They were written by Thurston, assisted by students [William Floyd](wikipedia://en/18986817) and [Steven Kerchoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Kerckhoff).[^o2] The notes introduced several new ideas into geometric topology, including [orbifolds](wikipedia://en/293504), [pleated manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleated_manifold), and [train tracks](wikipedia://en/1277396).

:::

## Distribution {#Distribution}

Copies of the original 1980 notes were circulated by Princeton University. Later the Geometry Center at the University of Minnesota sold a loosely bound copy of the notes. In 2002, Sheila Newbery typed the notes in TeX and made a PDF file of the notes available, which can be downloaded from [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) using the links below. The book (Thurston 1997) is an expanded version of the first three chapters of the notes. In 2022 the American Mathematical Society published a typeset version of the notes as part of the collected works of Thurston.

## Contents {#Contents}

Chapters 1 to 3 mostly describe basic background material on hyperbolic geometry.

Chapter 4 covers [Dehn surgery](wikipedia://en/1240699) on hyperbolic manifolds

Chapter 5 covers results related to [Mostow's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mostow's_theorem) on rigidity

Chapter 6 describes Gromov's invariant and his proof of Mostow's theorem.

Chapter 7 (by Milnor) describes the [Lobachevsky function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lobachevsky_function) and its applications to computing volumes of hyperbolic 3-manifolds.

Chapter 8 on [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) introduces Thurston's work on [train track](wikipedia://en/1277396) and [pleated manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pleated_manifold)

Chapter 9 covers convergence of Kleinian groups and hyperbolic manifolds.

Chapter 10 does not exist.

Chapter 11 covers deformations of Kleinian groups.

Chapter 12 does not exist.

Chapter 13 introduces [orbifolds](wikipedia://en/293504).
