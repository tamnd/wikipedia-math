# Axioms (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Axioms"}

***Axioms*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that focuses on all aspects of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). It was established in 2012 and is published quarterly by [MDPI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MDPI),[^o3] a publisher whose practices have been frequently and thoroughly called into question.[^o4][^o5] The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Humberto Bustince ([Public University of Navarre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_University_of_Navarre)). Axioms was discontinued in Scopus.[^o6]

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in:

- [EBSCO databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EBSCO_databases)[^o8]
- [ProQuest databases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ProQuest_databases)[^o8]
- [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded)
- [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus) (2012–2023)[^o6][^o9]
- [zbMATH Open](wikipedia://en/1701961) (2012–2019)[^o10]

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2024 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 1.6.[^o11]
