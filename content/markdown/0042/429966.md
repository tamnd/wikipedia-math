# Cyclotomic identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyclotomic identity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **cyclotomic identity** states that

:::

$${1 \over 1-k z}=\prod_{j=1}^\infty\left({1 \over 1-z^j}\right)^{M_j(k)}$$

where *M* is [Moreau's necklace-counting function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moreau's_necklace-counting_function),

$$M_n(k)={1\over n}\sum_{d\,|\,n}\mu\!\left({n \over d}\right)k^d,$$

and *μ* is the classic [Möbius function](wikipedia://en/20961) of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).

The name comes from the denominator, 1 − *z*<sup>*j*</sup>, which is the product of [cyclotomic polynomials](wikipedia://en/171992).

The left hand side of the cyclotomic identity is the [generating function](wikipedia://en/160993) for the free associative algebra on *k* generators, and the right hand side is the generating function for the [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551) of the [free Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/3174646) on *k* generators. The cyclotomic identity witnesses the fact that these two algebras are isomorphic.

Another interpretation is as the [Hasse–Weil zeta function](wikipedia://en/1157842) of the [affine line](wikipedia://en/298834) over the [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) $\mathbb F_q$ with $k = q$ elements. The exponent $M_n(k)$ counts the number of number of maximal ideals of degree *n* in the coordinate ring $\mathbb F_q[x]$, i.e. the number of monic [irreducible polynomials](wikipedia://en/188725) of degree *n*.

There is also a symmetric generalization of the cyclotomic identity found by Strehl:

$$\prod_{j=1}^\infty\left({1 \over 1-k z^j}\right)^{M_j(\ell)} =\prod_{j=1}^\infty\left({1 \over 1-\ell z^j}\right)^{M_j(k)}$$
