# Yves André

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yves André"}

**Yves André** (born December 11, 1959) is a French mathematician, specializing in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

André received his doctorate in 1984 from [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (Paris VI) with thesis advisor Daniel Bertrand and thesis *Structure de Hodge, équations différentielles p-adiques, et indépendance algébrique de périodes d'intégrales abéliennes*. He became at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) in 1985 a Researcher, in 2000 a Research Director 2nd Class, and in 2009 a Research Director 1st Class (at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) and [Institut de mathématiques de Jussieu – Paris Rive Gauche](wikipedia://en/57642956)).[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

In 1989, he formulated the one-dimensional-subvariety case of what is now known as the [André-Oort conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/André-Oort_conjecture) on special subvarieties of Shimura varieties.[^o7] Only partial results have been proven so far; by André himself and by [Jonathan Pila](wikipedia://en/31960316) in 2009. In 2016, André used [Scholze](wikipedia://en/39409306)'s method of [perfectoid spaces](wikipedia://en/39197973) to prove [Melvin Hochster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melvin_Hochster)'s direct summand conjecture that any finite extension of a regular [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) splits as a module.[^o8][^o9]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2011, André received the [Prix Paul Doistau–Émile Blutet](wikipedia://en/63735602) of the [Académie des Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Académie_des_Sciences). In 2015, he was elected as a [Member of the Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Member_of_the_Academia_Europaea). He was an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro) and gave a talk titled *Perfectoid spaces and the homological conjectures*.[^o11]
