# Michel André (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michel André"}

**Michel André** (26 March 1936 – 9 July 2009) was a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in non-commutative [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and its applications to [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He is known for [André–Quillen cohomology](wikipedia://en/23974317).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

André received in 1958 his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) from [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and in 1962 his doctorate from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) with thesis advisor [Claude Chevalley](wikipedia://en/366040) and thesis *Cohomology of the algèbres différentielles où opère and algèbre de Lie*. André became a full professor in 1971 at the [École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique_Fédérale_de_Lausanne).

In 1967, he was one of the founders of the theory of non-abelian derived functors; the theory was developed simultaneously by [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519) and [Jonathan Mock Beck](wikipedia://en/37065609) — the three mathematicians worked independently. In 1970 André was an invited speaker with talk *Homologie des algèbres commutatives* at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice).[^o5]

On 9 July 2009, André died in a hiking accident.[^o6]
