# Principal geodesic analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Principal geodesic analysis"}

In [geometric data analysis](wikipedia://en/17812310) and [statistical shape analysis](wikipedia://en/8707155), **principal geodesic analysis** is a generalization of [principal component analysis](wikipedia://en/76340) to a [non-Euclidean](wikipedia://en/9417), non-linear setting of [manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifolds) suitable for use with [shape descriptors](wikipedia://en/16576796#Shape_descriptors) such as [medial representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medial_representation).

:::
