# Juhani Kakkuri

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Juhani Kakkuri"}

**Juhani Antero Kakkuri[^o2]** (September 12, 1933 – August 6, 2022) was a Finnish [geodesist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesist), writer, and former director of the [Finnish Geodetic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Geodetic_Institute).

:::

Kakkuri was born in [Kurikka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurikka), [Finland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finland) on September 12, 1933.[^o3] He lived his childhood there, but moved to [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki) for [upper secondary school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_secondary_school), continuing his education at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki), where he studied [geophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geophysics) and physics. He received a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree in 1960.[^o4]

In 2021, the autobiographical memoir *Muistelmia geodeettien maailmasta – Memoirs From the World of Geodesy* was published bilingually in Finnish and English.[^o5] In recognition of his career, he was awarded an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_doctorate) from the [University of Stuttgart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Stuttgart).[^o6] The minor planet [3597 Kakkuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/3597_Kakkuri) is also named in his honor.[^o7]
