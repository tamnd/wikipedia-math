# João Tilly

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="João Tilly"}

**João José Rodrigues Tilly** (born 30 May 1960)[^o3] is a [Portuguese politician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politics_of_Portugal), math teacher, and [YouTuber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTuber) who was elected as a member of the [Assembly of the Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assembly_of_the_Republic_%28Portugal%29) following the [2024 legislative election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2024_Portuguese_legislative_election).[^o4]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Tilly was born in 1960. He studied mechanical engineering and is currently a math teacher.[^o6] He lives in [Seia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seia), in the [Guarda District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guarda_District), since 1989.[^o7]

He gained popularity through his YouTube videos commenting on current politics. In his videos, João Tilly has spread [hesitancy about COVID-19 vaccines](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_vaccine_misinformation_and_hesitancy) and has strongly criticized Ukraine President [Volodymyr Zelensky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volodymyr_Zelenskyy) after the start of the [Russian invasion of Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_invasion_of_Ukraine).[^o8]
