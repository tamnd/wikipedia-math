# Coarse function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coarse function"}

In mathematics, **coarse functions** are [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) that may appear to be continuous at a distance, but in reality are not necessarily continuous.[^o2] Although [continuous functions](wikipedia://en/6122) are usually observed on a small scale, coarse functions are usually observed on a large scale.[^o2]

:::
