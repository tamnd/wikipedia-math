# Dimensions (animation)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dimensions"}

**Dimensions** is a French project that makes educational movies about [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), focusing on [spatial geometry](wikipedia://en/9697).[^o2] It uses [POV-Ray](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POV-Ray) to render some of the animations, and the films are released under a [Creative Commons licence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Creative_Commons_licence).

:::

*Image: Dimensions-math 4A-5.jpg.* The fourth chapter, showing the stereographic projection of a [polychoron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polychoron) on our three-dimensional space.

The film is separated in nine chapters, which follow this plot:

- **Chapter 1: Dimension two** explains [Earth](wikipedia://en/9228)'s coordinate system, and introduces the [stereographic projection](wikipedia://en/143431).
- **Chapter 2: Dimension three** discusses how two-dimensional beings would imagine three-dimensional objects.
- **Chapters 3 and 4: The fourth dimension** talks about [four-dimensional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-dimensional) [polytopes](wikipedia://en/23471) (*polychora*), projecting the regular ones stereographically on the three-dimensional space.
- **Chapters 5 and 6: Complex numbers** are about the [square root of negative numbers](wikipedia://en/5826), [transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_%28geometry%29), and [fractals](wikipedia://en/10913).
- **Chapters 7 and 8: Fibration** show what a [fibration](wikipedia://en/316826) is. Complex numbers are used again, and there are [circles](wikipedia://en/6220) and [tori](wikipedia://en/74800) rotating and being transformed.
- **Chapter 9: Proof** emphasizes the importance of [proofs](wikipedia://en/82285) in mathematics, and proves the circle-conservationess of the stereographic projection as an example.

They are available for download in several languages.[^o3]
