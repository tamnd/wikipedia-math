# Korteweg-de Vries-Burgers-Fisher equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Korteweg-de Vries-Burgers-Fisher equation"}

The **KdV-Burgers-Fisher equation** (or Koçak's equation) is a nonlinear [partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/52564),

:::

$$\displaystyle \partial_t u + \mu \partial_x^3 u + \epsilon u \partial_x u -\nu \partial_x^2 u = ru(1-u),$$

which combines different entities such as dispersion (from the [Korteweg–de Vries equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Korteweg–de_Vries_equation)), dissipation (from the [Burgers' equation](wikipedia://en/1023353)) and reaction (from the [KPP–Fisher equation](wikipedia://en/1822746)). The KdV-Burgers-Fisher equation is often abbreviated as the KBF equation or the KdVBF equation.[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]
