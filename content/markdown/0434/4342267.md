# Rambutan (cryptography)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rambutan"}

**Rambutan** is a family of [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) technologies designed by the [Communications-Electronics Security Group](wikipedia://en/12884#CESG) (CESG), the technical division of the [United Kingdom government's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Her_Majesty's_Government) secret communications agency, [GCHQ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Government_Communications_Headquarters).

:::

It includes a range of encryption products designed by CESG for use in handling confidential (not secret) communications between parts of the British government, government agencies, and related bodies such as [NHS Trusts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NHS_Trust). Unlike CESG's [Red Pike](wikipedia://en/537171) system, Rambutan is not available as software: it is distributed only as a self-contained electronic device (an [ASIC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Application-specific_integrated_circuit)) which implements the entire cryptosystem and handles the related key distribution and storage tasks.[^o2] Rambutan is not sold outside the government sector.[^o3]

Technical details of the Rambutan algorithm are secret. Security researcher [Bruce Schneier](wikipedia://en/36732) describes it as being a [stream cipher](wikipedia://en/49007) ([linear-feedback shift register](wikipedia://en/58992)) based cryptosystem with 5 [shift registers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shift_register) each of around 80 bits, and a [key size](wikipedia://en/5749) of 112 bits.[^o3] RAMBUTAN-I communications chips (which implement a secure [X.25](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X.25) based communications system) are made by approved contractors [Racal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Racal) and Baltimore Technologies/Zergo Ltd.[^o4][^o5] CESG later specified RAMBUTAN-II, an enhanced system with backward compatibility with existing RAMBUTAN-I infrastructure.[^o6] The RAMBUTAN-II chip is a 64-pin [quad ceramic pack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QFP) chip, which implements the electronic codebook, cipher block chaining, and output feedback [operating modes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_cipher_modes_of_operation) (each in 64 bits) and the cipher feedback mode in 1 or 8 bits.[^o7] Schneier suggests that these modes may indicate Rambutan is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) rather than a stream.[^o3] The three 64 bit modes operate at 88 megabits/second.[^o7] Rambutan operates in three modes: ECB, CBC, and 8 bit CFB.[^o8]
