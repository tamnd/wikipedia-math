# Karl Egil Aubert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Egil Aubert"}

**Karl Egil Aubert** (19 August 1924 – 21 October 1990) was a Norwegian mathematician.[^o2]

:::

Karl Aubert was born in [Christiania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo) (now Oslo), Norway. He was the brother of sociologist [Vilhelm Aubert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilhelm_Aubert).[^o3]

He studied at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo) and took his [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) degree at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1957. He stayed at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) from 1958 to 1960. From 1962 to 1990 he was a professor at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo). He was also a visiting professor at the [University of Washington in Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington_in_Seattle) and [Tufts University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tufts_University). He chaired the [Norwegian Mathematics Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Mathematics_Society) from 1960 to 1967. [^o4]
