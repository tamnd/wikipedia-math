# Christophe Gadbled

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christophe Gadbled"}

**Christophe Gadbled** (1732–1782) was professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [hydrography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrography) at the [University of Caen Normandy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Caen_Normandy).[^n1][^n2]

:::

Gadbled was born in [Saint-Martin-le-Bouillant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Martin-le-Bouillant) on 29 November 1732, and died in [Caen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caen) on 11 October 1782.[^n1] Robert Fox describes him as a "modern-minded mathematician",[^n3] who had a particular influence on [Pierre-Simon Laplace](wikipedia://en/344783).[^n1][^n3]

- *Exercice sur la théorie de la navigation*, Caen, 1779
