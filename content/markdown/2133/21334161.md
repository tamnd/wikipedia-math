# Jitendra Nath Mohanty

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jitendra Nath Mohanty"}

**Jitendra Nath Mohanty** (1928 – 7 March 2023) was an Indian philosopher. He served as emeritus professor of philosophy at [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Cuttack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuttack), in 1928 in [Orissa, India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orissa,_India), Mohanty had a distinguished career where he stood first in all public examinations and in [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) [Presidency College, Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presidency_College,_Kolkata) and [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) examination at the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta). Subsequently, he did a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) in 1954. In his long academic career, he taught at the [University of Burdwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Burdwan), [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta), [New School for Social Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_School_for_Social_Research), [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma), [Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University), and [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University) and held visiting professorships at many renowned universities.

Mohanty's area of expertise included both [Western](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_philosophy) (particularly [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_philosophy)) philosophy and [Eastern philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eastern_philosophy) (particularly [Indian philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_philosophy)). He wrote over twenty scholarly books and numerous journal articles on different areas of philosophy including [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology), [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), and [phenomenology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenomenology_%28philosophy%29). He wrote extensively on [Immanuel Kant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant), founded *Husserl Studies,* and published a major book on the development of [Edmund Husserl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edmund_Husserl)’s thought.[^o4]

Mohanty was also a president of the Indian Philosophical Congress, the Society for Asian and Comparative philosophy. His other honours included a gold medal from the [Asiatic Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asiatic_Society), [Kolkata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolkata), and the [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) from the German government in honor of his scholarly work. He received an honorary [D.Litt.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.Litt.) from the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta) in 2013 and an honorary doctorate from [Ravenshaw University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ravenshaw_University) in 2017.[^o5][^o6]

Mohanty died in [Philadelphia, Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia,_Pennsylvania) on 7 March 2023, at the age of 95.[^o7]
