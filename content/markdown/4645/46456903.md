# Friederich Ignaz Mautner

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Friederich Ignaz Mautner"}

**Friederich Ignaz Mautner** (14 May 1921–2002)[^o3] was an Austrian-American mathematician, known for his research on the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of groups, [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He is known for [Mautner's Lemma](wikipedia://en/3018887) and Mautner's Phenomenon in the representation theory of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).[^o4]

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Following the [Anschluss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anschluss) in 1938, Mautner, a Jew, emigrated from Austria to the UK where he became one of the thousands or refugees who were interred by the British and shipped off to [Hay Camp 7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hay_Internment_and_POW_camps) in Australia. While there he was fortunate in that he got to study mathematics under [Felix Behrend](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Behrend). When he got back to the UK, he garnered a BSc at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) and then went to Ireland in 1944 where he got an assistantship with Paul Ewald at [Queens University Belfast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen's_University_Belfast) (QUB).[^o6] He then became a scholar at the [Dublin Institute for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin_Institute_for_Advanced_Studies) in 1944–1946.[^o7]

He then moved to the USA, where he was a visiting scholar[^o8] at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (IAS) in Princeton (1946-47).[^o9]

He then attended [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and got a Ph.D. in 1948 with the thesis *Unitary Representations of Infinite Groups*.

He was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) in the academic year 1954-55.[^o11]

Working in the fields of [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986) of [geodesic flows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geodesic_flow), he published a paper in 1957 that established the lemma and the phenomenon that bear his name.[^o12]

He published a ground-breaking paper in 1958 that established him as a pioneer in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of reducible [p-adic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_group).[^o13]

The Mautner Group, a special five-dimensional [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945), is named after him.[^o14]

Frederich had one daughter, Jean Mautner.
