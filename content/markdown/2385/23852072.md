# Bocher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bocher"}

**Bocher** is a surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Christiane Bøcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christiane_Bøcher) (1798–1874), Norwegian stage actress who was engaged at the Christiania Offentlige Theater
- [Édouard Bocher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Édouard_Bocher) (1811–1900), French politician who was one of the founders of the Conférence Molé-Tocqueville
- [Herbert Böcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herbert_Böcher) (1903–1983), German middle-distance runner who competed in the 1928 Summer Olympics
- [Joan Bocher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Bocher) (died 1550), English Anabaptist burned at the stake for heresy
- [Main Bocher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Bocher) (1890–1976), American fashion designer who founded the fashion label Mainbocher
- [Maxime Bôcher](wikipedia://en/3180285) (1867–1918), American mathematician who published about 100 papers on differential equations, series, and algebra
- [Tyge W. Böcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tyge_W._Böcher) (1909–1983), Danish botanist, evolutionary biologist, plant ecologist and phytogeographer
