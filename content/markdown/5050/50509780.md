# Alfred Errera

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfred Errera"}

**Alfred Errera** (1886 – 1960) was a Belgian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2]

:::

Errera studied at the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles), where he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1921 with dissertation *Du coloriage des cartes et de quelques questions d'analysis situs*. In his dissertation he introduced what is now called the [Errera graph](wikipedia://en/24209919),[^o4] which is a counterexample to the validity of [Alfred Kempe](wikipedia://en/9092767)'s alleged proof of the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949). From 1928 to 1956 he was a professor at the Université libre de Bruxelles.

Errera did research on [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), especially the theory of [polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedra) and the [Jordan curve theorem](wikipedia://en/267444). With [Théophile Lepage](wikipedia://en/47203435) he conducted a seminar on analysis at the University of Brussels.

During [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I), he worked on the [acoustic source localization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustic_source_localization) of artillery fire and from 1921 to 1938 taught courses on this subject at the [Royal Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Military_Academy_%28Belgium%29).

Errera was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1924 in Toronto, in 1932 in Zürich, and in 1936 in Oslo.[^o5] In 1932 he participated in the Congresul Matematicienilor Români in [Drobeta-Turnu Severin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drobeta-Turnu_Severin). In 1935 he gave a talk at the inaugural Congrés International des Récréations Mathématiques in Brussels. His doctoral students included [Guy Hirsch](wikipedia://en/51033271).
