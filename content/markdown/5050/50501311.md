# Kengo Hirachi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kengo Hirachi"}

**Kengo Hirachi** (平地 健吾 *Hirachi Kengo*, born 30 November 1964) is a Japanese mathematician, specializing in [CR geometry](wikipedia://en/3131794) and mathematical analysis.

:::

Hirachi received from [Osaka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_University) his B.S. in 1987, his M.S. in 1989, and his Dr.Sci., advised by Gen Komatsu, in 1994 with dissertation *The second variation of the Bergman kernel for ellipsoids*. He was a research assistant from 1989 to 1996 and a lecturer from 1996 to 2000 at Osaka University. He was an associate professor from 2000 to 2010 and a full professor from 2010 to the present at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo). He was a visiting professor at the Mathematical Sciences Research Institute from October 1995 to September 1996, at the [Erwin Schrödinger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger) Institute for [Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/173416) from March 2004 to April 2004, at Princeton University from October 2004 to July 2005, and at the Institute for Advanced Study from January 2009 to April 2009.[^o3]

Hirachi’s work employs a wide range of tools in geometry and analysis, including several complex variables, the complex Monge-Ampère equation, microlocal analysis, parabolic invariant theory, explicit computations, and computer algebra packages. In a paper in the *Annals of Mathematics* (2000) Hirachi constructed CR invariants of strongly pseudoconvex boundaries via a deep study of the logarithmic singularity of the Bergman kernel. He has proved various results linking the Bergman and Szegő kernels, and he has made significant progress to a program in which the Bergman kernel function plays a role analogous to the heat kernel of Riemannian geometry.[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- Takebe Senior Prize (1999) of the Mathematical Society of Japan
- [Geometry Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometry_prize) (2003) of the Mathematical Society of Japan
- [Stefan Bergman](wikipedia://en/9395687) Prize (2006)[^o4]
- Inoue Prize for Science (2012)
- Invited lecture at ICM, Seoul 2014
