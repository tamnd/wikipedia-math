# Alexander Moiseevich Olevskii

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Moiseevich Olevskii"}

**Alexander Moiseevich Olevskii** (Александр Моисеевич Олевский;[^o3] born February 12, 1939, in Moscow) is a Russian-Israeli mathematician at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), specializing in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).[^o4] As of July 2021, he is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o5]

:::

He graduated in 1963 with a [Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) degree (PhD) from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University). There he received in 1966 a Russian [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) degree (habilitation).[^o4] At the [Moscow Institute of Electronics and Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9974195), he was from 1988 to 1992 head of the department of algebra and analysis. In the spring of 1996 he was at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o7] He has held visiting appointments at universities or institutes in several countries, including France, Australia, Germany, Italy, and the United States.[^o4]

In 1986 Olevskii was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California).[^o8] He was a member of the 2013 Class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (announced in 2012).[^o9] In 2014 he was an invited speaker at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków).[^o10]

His doctoral students include [Gady Kozma](wikipedia://en/53635128).[^o6]
