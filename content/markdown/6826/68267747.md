# Weiwen Miao

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Weiwen Miao"}

**Weiwen Miao** is a Chinese-American statistician, [statistics educator](wikipedia://en/24985094), and scholar of [legal statistics](wikipedia://en/45327439) and [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369). She is a professor of mathematics and statistics at [Haverford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverford_College).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Miao has a bachelor's degree in mathematics from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University). She went to [Tufts University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tufts_University) for graduate study in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and statistics, earning a master's degree and a Ph.D. there. Her 1995 doctoral dissertation, *Maximum Likelihood Estimation for Exponential Families*, was supervised by [Marjorie Hahn](wikipedia://en/55896755).

After teaching statistics at [Mount Holyoke College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Holyoke_College) and [Colby College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colby_College), and becoming an associate professor at [Macalester College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macalester_College), she moved to Haverford College in 2007.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Miao was named a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2021.
