# Wolfgang Dahmen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wolfgang Dahmen"}

*Image: Wolfgang Dahmen.jpg.* Dahmen at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2004

**Wolfgang Dahmen** (born 19 October 1949) is a German mathematician working in [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). In 2002, he was awarded the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize) and in 2011 the [Gauss Lectureship](wikipedia://en/48440954). He was also a [taekwondo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taekwondo) athlete. He has been the Chair of the [Society for the Foundations of Computational Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foundations_of_Computational_Mathematics) (2014–).

:::

In 2019, he was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) "for contributions to numerical methods for partial differential equations, signal processing, and learning".[^o2] He was elected as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society), in the 2025 class of fellows.[^o3]
