# Yutaka Yamamoto (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yutaka Yamamoto"}

**Yutaka Yamamoto**, is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [systems theory](wikipedia://en/29238), [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), and [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324).

:::
