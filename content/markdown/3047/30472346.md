# George Baron

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Baron"}

**George Baron** (died June 18, 1818) was a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who emigrated from [Northumberland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northumberland), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) to [Hallowell, Maine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hallowell,_Maine) in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States), thereafter moving to [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29). He was the first superintendent and mathematics professor at what would become the [United States Military Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Military_Academy) in 1801[^o2] and the founder and editor-in-chief of the *[Mathematical Correspondent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Correspondent)*, which was the first American "specialized scientific journal"[^o3] and the first American [mathematics journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_journal), first published May 1, 1804.[^o4]

:::

Baron was first offered the position at the fledgling academy at [West Point, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Point,_New_York) by the newly elected [United States President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_President) [Thomas Jefferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson)'s [Secretary of War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_War) [Henry Dearborn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henry_Dearborn), a friend of Baron's who had lived near him in Maine. After agreeing upon salary and perks, instruction began on September 21, 1801 employing the use of [Charles Hutton](wikipedia://en/938017)'s *A Course in Mathematics* and a [blackboard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blackboard), the first recorded use of the latter in America. In October, there was a disagreement between Baron and one of the cadets, [Joseph Gardner Swift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Gardner_Swift). Swift was called upon to apologize and was reprimanded for the language he employed against Baron, but went on to become the Military Academy's first graduate, and later a [Brigadier General](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigadier_General). For a variety of reasons, Baron was [court-martialled](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Court-martialled) in December, and [Major Jonathan Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jonathan_Williams_%28engineer%29) became the supervisor and Captain William Amherst Barron became the instructor of mathematics. [^o5]

Baron became a teacher of mathematics in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), there joining the Deistical Society of New York,[^o6] a [deist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deist) group led by [Elihu Palmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elihu_Palmer) that came to public attention in the course of a pamphlet war between supporters of [United States Vice President](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Vice_President) [Aaron Burr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aaron_Burr) and supporters of then [United States Senator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Senator) from New York [DeWitt Clinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DeWitt_Clinton).[^o7]
