# Collectanea Mathematica

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Collectanea Mathematica"}

***Collectanea Mathematica*** (Collect. Math.) is a [mathematical journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_journal) of the Institute of Mathematics of the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona) ([IMUB](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IMUB)), published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) since 2011, with a periodicity of three issues per year. It publishes original research papers in all fields of pure and applied mathematics.

:::

## History {#History}

Collectanea Mathematica was founded in 1948 by José M. Orts (it is the oldest mathematical journal in [Spain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spain)[^o4]). Thanks to the contribution of some relevant mathematicians in [Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia), like [Ferran Sunyer Balaguer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferran_Sunyer_Balaguer), and eminent international collaborators ([Wilhelm Blaschke](wikipedia://en/728998), [Hugo Hadwiger](wikipedia://en/630451), [Gaston Julia](wikipedia://en/249107) or [Ernst Witt](wikipedia://en/2619911)), the journal reached a central role in the Spanish scientific publications, under the direction of Enrique Linés (1969-1971), who was also president of the Real Sociedad Matemática Española, and specially with Josep Teixidor (1971-1986), president of the [Societat Catalana de Ciències Físiques, Químiques i Matemàtiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Societat_Catalana_de_Ciències_Físiques,_Químiques_i_Matemàtiques) (1968-1973). During the period 1987–2007, with [Joan Cerdà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joan_Cerdà) as the Main Editor, the journal took several steps forward to further improve its scientific quality. In 2003, the recently created Institute of Mathematics of the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona) started to be in charge of its publication, providing Collectanea Mathematica with a stable economic and scientific support. As a consequence, coverage of Collectanea Mathematica by the [Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports) (JCR) began with the 2005 volume and 2007 was its first impact factor in JCR. In 2008 [Rosa Maria Miró Roig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosa_Maria_Miró_Roig) became [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief). In this period the journal has changed its editorial policy and [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) is the new publisher since 2011. Since 2021 the current editor-in-chief is [Carlos D'Andrea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carlos_D'Andrea).

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

*Collectanea Mathematica* is indexed in databases such as [Current Contents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Current_Contents) (Physical Chemical and Earth Sciences) and [ISI Web of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_of_Science), [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/1485646),[^o6] [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH),[^o7] and [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus).
