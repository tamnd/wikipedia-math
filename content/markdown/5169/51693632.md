# George Bard Ermentrout

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Bard Ermentrout"}

**G. Bard Ermentrout** is an American mathematician and distinguished professor at [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh) and a member of the Odor2Action research network.[^o8] He uses nonlinear dynamics for the mathematical modeling of problems in [neuroscience](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neuroscience). He explores patterns of activation in neural systems as they relate to biological problems such as [olfaction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olfaction).[^o4]

:::

Bard Ermentrout is known for his contributions to computational and mathematical neuroscience. His joint work with Nancy Kopell derived the [Ermentrout and Kopell canonical model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_model),[^o5] He and David Terman wrote the book *Mathematical Foundations of Neuroscience*.[^o6] He helped to develop the dynamical systems software XPPAuto.[^o7]

One approach he uses in the study of olfaction is to program a virtual creature, implement various movement strategies for tracking scents, and examine their success rate under a different conditions. This enables researchers to better understand olfactory navigation strategies such as [tropotaxis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tropotaxis) and [klinotaxis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klinotaxis) and how they work in conditions such as high turbulence.[^o8][^o9]

Outside of work, he is fond of his many pets and has owned many pet parrots over the years. He most recently owns a galah and two corgis. He is also a lover of limericks.
