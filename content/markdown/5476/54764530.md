# Carlos Kenig

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carlos Kenig"}

**Carlos Eduardo Kenig** (born November 25, 1953) is an Argentine-American mathematician and Louis Block Distinguished Service Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o3] He is known for his work in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) and [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations). He was [President](wikipedia://en/14868#Presidents) of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) between 2019 and 2022.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Kenig obtained his PhD at the University of Chicago in 1978 under the supervision of [Alberto Calderón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Calderón). Since then, he has held positions at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) before returning to the University of Chicago in 1985. He has done extensive work in [elliptic](wikipedia://en/662787) and [dispersive](wikipedia://en/8477417) partial differential equations. He is a member of the National Academy of Sciences since 2014. His students include [Zhongwei Shen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhongwei_Shen), [Kin Ming Hui](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kin_Ming_Hui), [Gigliola Staffilani](wikipedia://en/44916025) and [Panagiota Daskalopoulos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Panagiota_Daskalopoulos).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 1984 - [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize)
- 1986 - [Invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065)[^o6] [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) (and 2002)
- 2002 - Elected Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences)
- 2008 - [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872)[^o7]
- 2010 - Plenary speaker, 2010 International Congress of Mathematicians
- 2014 - Elected Member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences)[^o8]
- 2024 - [ICMAM Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICMAM_Prize) Awarded by the research organization ICMAM Latin America.[^o9]
- 2026 - [King Faisal Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_Faisal_Prize) in the category of "Science".[^o10]

Kenig was elected President of the [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) in July 2018, a position that he holds until 2022.
