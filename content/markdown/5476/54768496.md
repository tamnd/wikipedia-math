# Emily Grundy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emily Grundy"}

**Emily Marjata Dorothea Grundy**, (born 24 July 1955) is a British [demographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Demographer) and academic, specialising in [ageing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerontology) and [health inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Health_inequalities). Since 2013, she has been Professor of Demography at the [London School of Economics and Political Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics_and_Political_Science) (LSE). She was previously Professor of Demographic Gerontology at the [London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Hygiene_and_Tropical_Medicine) (LSHTM) from 2003 to 2012, and Professor of Demography at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) from 2012 to 2013.[^o2][^o3] From October 2017, she will be Professor of Population Science and Director of the Institute for Social and Economic Research at the [University of Essex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Essex).[^o4]

:::

## Honours {#Honours}

In October 2016, Grundy was elected [Fellow of the Academy of Social Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Academy_of_Social_Sciences) (FAcSS).[^o6][^o7] In July 2017, she was elected a [Fellow of the British Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_British_Academy) (FBA), the United Kingdom's [national academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_academy) for the humanities and social sciences.[^o8]
