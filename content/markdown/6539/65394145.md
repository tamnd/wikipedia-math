# Division lattice

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Division lattice"}

*Image: Infinite lattice of divisors.svg.* The non-negative integers partially ordered by divisibility.

The **division lattice** is an infinite complete bounded [distributive lattice](wikipedia://en/310883) whose elements are the [natural numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Natural_numbers) ordered by [divisibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisibility). Its least element is 1, which divides all natural numbers, while its greatest element is 0, which is divisible by all positive natural numbers. The meet operation is [greatest common divisor](wikipedia://en/12354) while the join operation is [least common multiple](wikipedia://en/17961).[^o2]

:::

The [prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_numbers) are precisely the [atoms](wikipedia://en/998824) of the division lattice, namely those natural numbers divisible only by themselves and 1.[^o3]

For any [square-free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Square-free) number *n*, its divisors form a [Boolean algebra](wikipedia://en/54476844) that is a [sublattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sublattice) of the division lattice. The elements of this sublattice are representable as the subsets of the set of prime factors of *n*. [^o4]
