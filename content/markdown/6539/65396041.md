# Kryptographik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kryptographik"}

*Image: Kryptographik Lehrbuch Der Geheimschreibekunst, by Johann Ludwig Klueber, 1809 - National Cryptologic Museum - DSC07736.JPG.* A copy of *Kryptographik* at the [National Cryptologic Museum](wikipedia://en/734289)

***Kryptographik Lehrbuch der Geheimschreibekunst*** (*Cryptology: Instruction Book on the Art of Secret Writing*) is an 1809 book on [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) written by [Johann Ludwig Klüber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Ludwig_Klüber).[^o2]

:::

In 2011 the [National Security Agency](wikipedia://en/21939) included a copy of *Kryptographik*, used by a German cryptographer during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), as part of a 50,000 page release of classified documents.[^o3][^o4]
