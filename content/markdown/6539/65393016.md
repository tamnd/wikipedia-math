# Barbara Abraham-Shrauner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Barbara Abraham-Shrauner"}

**Barbara Wayne Abraham-Shrauner** is an American physicist, applied mathematician, and electrical engineer. She was known for her research on [magnetohydrodynamics](wikipedia://en/40310) and [plasma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_%28physics%29) phenomena, including mathematical modeling of shocks and [Alfvén waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfvén_waves), observation of the [solar wind](wikipedia://en/28538), and applications to the [plasma etching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plasma_etching) of [semiconductors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiconductor), as well as on hidden symmetries and nonlocal symmetries in [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309). She is retired as a professor of electrical and systems engineering at the [McKelvey School of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McKelvey_School_of_Engineering) at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Abraham-Shrauner graduated from Madison High School in 1952. She earned her bachelor's degree from the [University of Colorado Boulder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Boulder) in 1956.[^o4] She earned a master's degree from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1957 and completed her Ph.D. there in 1962.

She joined Washington University in St. Louis faculty in 1966, after postdoctoral research at the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles) and [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) [Ames Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ames_Research_Center), and retired from full-time service in 2003 at Washington University, where she continues to hold an affiliation as a part-time Senior Professor in the Department of Electrical and Systems Engineering.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Abraham-Shrauner was named a [Fellow of the American Physical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Physical_Society) (APS) in 1999, after a nomination from the APS Division of Plasma Physics, "for important theoretical contributions to a broad range of plasma topics, including: space plasmas, nonlinear dynamics, and plasma processing". She is a part of the Barbara Abraham Shrauner Endowed Scholarship in Physics to award undergraduate students that are involved in pursuing Physics or Engineering Physics.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Abraham-Shrauner was married to James Ely Shrauner (1933–2015), a professor of physics in [Arts and Sciences at Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arts_and_Sciences_at_Washington_University_in_St._Louis).[^o7]
