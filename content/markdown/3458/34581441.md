# Higman group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Higman group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Higman group**, introduced by (txt), was the first example of an [infinite](wikipedia://en/4587030) [finitely presented](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finitely_presented_group) [group](wikipedia://en/19447) with no [nontrivial](wikipedia://en/193059) [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [quotients](wikipedia://en/11526). The quotient by the maximal proper [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) is a [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/963042) infinite [simple group](wikipedia://en/47600). (Higman 1974) later found some finitely presented infinite groups $G_{n,r}$ that are simple if $n$ is even and have a simple [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of [index](wikipedia://en/230581) 2 if $n$ is odd, one of which is one of the [Thompson groups](wikipedia://en/612245).

:::

Higman's group is generated by 4 elements $a, b, c, d$ with the relations

$$a^{-1}ba = b^2,\quad b^{-1}cb = c^2,\quad c^{-1}dc = d^2,\quad d^{-1}ad = a^2.$$
