# 2010 Malaysian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2010 Malaysian census"}

The **Population and Housing Census of Malaysia, 2010**, was conducted by [Department of Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malaysian_Government) from 6 July to 22 August 2010. It was carried out in three phases; the first phase from 6 to 21 July, the second phase from 22 July to 6 August, and the third phase from 6 to 22 August. To ensure a complete coverage, mapping-out activities were undertaken at the end of each phase. All persons living in private living quarters, collective living quarters such as [college or university hostels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dormitory), charitable or [social welfare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welfare_spending) institutions, [prisons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prison_cell), and [shelters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeless_shelter) for [homeless persons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homelessness); were enumerated based on their usual place of residence in Malaysia on the Census Day that is 6 July 2010.
