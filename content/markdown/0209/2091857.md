# Pinsker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pinsker"}

**Pinsker** is an habitational surname for someone from [Pinsk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pinsk), in [Belarus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarus). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Leon Pinsker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Pinsker) (1821–1891), Russian physician and Zionist activist
- [Mark Semenovich Pinsker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Semenovich_Pinsker) (1925–2003), Russian mathematician
- [Sarah Pinsker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sarah_Pinsker), American science fiction and fantasy author
- [Scott Pinsker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scott_Pinsker), filmmaker, talk show host, and author
- [Simhah Pinsker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simhah_Pinsker) (1801–1864), Polish-Jewish scholar and archeologist
