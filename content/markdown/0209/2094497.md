# Abel polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abel polynomials"}

The **Abel polynomials** are a [sequence](wikipedia://en/195990) of polynomials named after [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573), defined by the following equation:

:::

$$p_n(x)=x(x-an)^{n-1}$$

They occur, for instance, in the enumeration of trees (specifically, rooted labeled forests) as well as in connection with geometric probability (the random placement of nonoverlapping arcs on a circle). This polynomial sequence is of [binomial type](wikipedia://en/200077): conversely, every polynomial sequence of binomial type may be obtained from the Abel sequence using [umbral calculus](wikipedia://en/214124).

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

For $a = 1$, the polynomials are

$$p_0(x)=1;$$

$$p_1(x)=x;$$

$$p_2(x)=-2x+x^2;$$

$$p_3(x)=9x-6x^2+x^3;$$

$$p_4(x)=-64x +48x^2-12x^3+x^4;$$

For $a = 2$, the polynomials are

$$p_0(x)=1;$$

$$p_1(x)=x;$$

$$p_2(x)=-4x+x^2;$$

$$p_3(x)=36x-12x^2+x^3;$$

$$p_4(x)=-512x +192x^2-24x^3+x^4;$$

$$p_5(x)=10000x-4000x^2+600x^3-40x^4+x^5;$$

$$p_6(x)=-248832x+103680x^2-17280x^3+1440x^4-60x^5+x^6;$$

:::
