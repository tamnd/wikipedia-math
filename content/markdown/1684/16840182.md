# Hans Jørgen G. Gundersen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans Jørgen G. Gundersen"}

**Hans Jørgen Gottlieb Gundersen** (2 May 1943 – 1 February 2021) was a Danish [stereology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stereology) researcher. He was employed as a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University).

:::

In January 2008 he received the [August Krogh Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Krogh_Prize) "for his ground-breaking research in stereology for the spatial description and understanding of biological tissue".[^o2] He was also a recipient of the [Novo Nordisk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novo_Nordisk) Prize.[^o3]
