# William Gerard Dwyer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="William Gerard Dwyer"}

**William Gerard Dwyer** (born 1947) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). For many years he was a professor at the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame), where he is the William J. Hank Family Professor Emeritus.

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born in 1947 in [Jersey City, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey_City,_New_Jersey).

## Career {#Career}

Dwyer completed his [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) at [Boston College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_College) in 1969.[^o5]

He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1973. His doctoral thesis was on *Strong Convergence of the Eilenberg-Moore Spectral Sequence* and his doctoral advisor was [Daniel Kan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Marinus_Kan). Afterwards he taught at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and visited the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) before joining the faculty at the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame).[^o7]

In 1998 Dwyer was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin).[^o8] In 2007 he was awarded a [Doctor Honoris Causa degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) by the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw).[^o7] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012. He is currently emeritus professor of mathematics at the University of Notre Dame.[^o5]
