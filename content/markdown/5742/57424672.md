# Leslie Colin Woods

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Leslie Colin Woods"}

**Leslie Colin Woods** (6 December 1922 – 15 April 2007) was a New Zealand mathematician.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Woods was born on 6 December 1922 in Reporoa, New Zealand.[^o4] Woods' father was a fisherman.[^o5] His surname was originally Woodhead.

His school education was completed in New Zealand, where he attended Seddon Memorial Technical College (where he was Head Boy).[^o6] In his autobiography *Against the Tide: An Autobiographical Account of a Professional Outsider*, he gives credit to his school teachers, including Colin Maloy and G J Park, for kindling his interest in science and encouraging him to take up a career in academia.[^o7]

In 1940 Woods went on to study at [Auckland University College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), but left to join the [Royal New Zealand Air Force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_New_Zealand_Air_Force) in December 1941. After completing his training as a pilot he was posted to the Pacific Area in 1943, serving three tours of duty in 1944 and 1945.[^o6]

After the war Woods returned to Auckland University College, taking his MSc in 1945 and a BE in 1947. The following year he matriculated at [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford) on a Rhodes Scholarship; he studied there until 1951, taking a DPhil in 1950, and then, unusually, a first-class BA in Mathematics in 1951.[^o6]

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Woods was the Nuffield Research Professor of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Technology at Sydney.[^o9] He was elected a Fellow of Balliol College, Oxford in 1961 where he researched the theory of magnetically-confined hot plasmas. Woods was professor of mathematics at the University of Oxford from 1970 until his retirement in 1990. [^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1943, Woods married Gladys Elizabeth (Betty) Bayley; they had five daughters.[^o5][^o6]

Woods died on 15 April 2007 in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford).[^o4]

## Bibliography {#Bibliography}

His notable books include:[^o13]

- *The Theory of Subsonic Plane Flow* (1961)
- *The Thermodynamics of Fluid Systems* (1975)
- *Principles of Magnetoplasma Dynamics* (1987)
- *Kinetic Theory of Gases and Magnetoplasmas* (1993)
- *Thermodynamic Inequalities with Applications to Gases and Magnetoplasmas* (1996)
- *Against the Tide: An Autobiographical Account of a Professional Outsider* (2000)
- *Physics of Plasmas* (2003)
- *Theory of Tokamak Transport: New Aspects for Nuclear Fusion Reactor Design* (2006)
