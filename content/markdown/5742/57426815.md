# Gabriel–Rosenberg reconstruction theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gabriel–Rosenberg reconstruction theorem"}

In algebraic geometry, the **Gabriel–Rosenberg reconstruction theorem**, introduced in (Gabriel 1962), states that a [quasi-separated](wikipedia://en/28281191) [scheme](wikipedia://en/364754) can be recovered from the category of [quasi-coherent sheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-coherent_sheaf) on it.[^o3] The theorem is taken as a starting point for [noncommutative algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/25493347) as the theorem says (in a sense) working with stuff on a space is equivalent to working with the space itself. It is named after [Pierre Gabriel](wikipedia://en/30716698) and [Alexander L. Rosenberg](wikipedia://en/57780441).

:::
