# James E. Humphreys

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="James E. Humphreys"}

**James Edward Humphreys** (December 10, 1939 – August 27, 2020) was an American mathematician who worked in [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), and [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) and applications of these mathematical structures. He is known as the author of several mathematical texts, such as *Introduction to Lie Algebras and Representation Theory*[^o3] and *Reflection Groups and Coxeter Groups*.[^o4]

:::

After contracting [COVID-19](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19) weeks earlier during the [COVID-19 pandemic in Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic_in_Massachusetts), Humphreys died on August 27, 2020, at the age of 80.[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

Humphreys attended elementary and secondary school in Erie, Pennsylvania and then studied at [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College) (bachelor's degree 1961) and from 1961 philosophy and mathematics at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). At [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) he earned his master's degree in 1964 and his PhD in 1966 under [George Seligman](wikipedia://en/46217412) with thesis *Algebraic Lie Algebras over fields of prime characteristic*.

## Career {#Career}

In 1966, he became an assistant professor at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) and in 1970, an associate professor at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University). At the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst) he became in 1974 an associate professor and in 1976 a full professor; he retired there in 2003 as professor emeritus. In 1968/69 and in 1977, he was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179)[^o9] and in 1969/70 at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of New York University. In 1985, he was a visiting professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).

## Awards {#Awards}

Humphreys received the [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award) for the publication *Representations of $\operatorname{SL}(2,p)$* in 1976.[^o15]
