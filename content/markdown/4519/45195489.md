# Amedeo Agostini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Amedeo Agostini"}

**Amedeo Agostini** (6 March 1892, in [Capugnano di Porretta Terme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porretta_Terme) — 27 June 1958, in [Livorno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livorno)) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born in [Capugnano di Porretta Terme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Porretta_Terme).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1919 he graduated at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna), where he later worked on a freelance basis as teaching assistant and professor of [History of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Mathematics). Since 1925 he taught [analytical geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytical_geometry) at the Naval Academy in Livorno. Agostini also held - on assignment - several lectures at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa). He dealt mainly with the history of mathematics following the principles of his own teacher, [Ettore Bortolotti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ettore_Bortolotti), emphasizing the assessment of the facts more than their synthesis.

His studies on [Pietro Mengoli](wikipedia://en/11335853) and the origins of the theory of limits are particularly worth mentioning.
