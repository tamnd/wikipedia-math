# Chief Statistician of the United States

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chief Statistician of the United States"}

The **chief statistician of the United States** is a position in the U.S. federal government's [Office of Management and Budget](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Management_and_Budget) (OMB). The chief statistician is charged with providing coordination, guidance, and oversight for [U.S. federal statistical agencies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Statistical_System_of_the_United_States) and activities.

:::

The chief statistician heads the Statistical Policy Branch of the OMB's [Office of Information and Regulatory Affairs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Information_and_Regulatory_Affairs) (OIRA). The chief statistician is appointed by the Administrator of the OIRA.[^o8]

[Katherine Wallman](wikipedia://en/41309488) was the chief statistician from 1992 to January 2017.[^o2] [Nancy Potok](wikipedia://en/52876266) held the position from January 2017 to December 2019.[^o3] After Potok left office, a job posting was listed on [USAJobs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USAJobs) to find a replacement.[^o4] Dominic (Dom) Mancini was the acting replacement. The position remained vacant until April 2022, when [Karin Orvis](wikipedia://en/70695141) was appointed.[^o5] In July 2025, [Mark Calabria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_A._Calabria) was appointed, replacing Karin Orvis.[^o6]

## History {#History}

The current position of chief statistician was created by the [Paperwork Reduction Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paperwork_Reduction_Act) in 1980.[^o8]

An earlier U.S. chief statistician, Stuart Rice, chaired the nuclear session of the [United Nations Statistical Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Statistical_Commission) in 1946.[^o9][^o10]
