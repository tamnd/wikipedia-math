# Alex Kontorovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alex Kontorovich"}

*Image: Kontorovich 2011.jpg.* Alex Kontorovich

**Alex V. Kontorovich** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who works in the areas of [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [L-functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-functions), [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), and homogeneous dynamics.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kontorovich earned a bachelor's degree from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 2002, and a PhD from Columbia University in 2007, where he studied under [Dorian Goldfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorian_Goldfeld) and [Peter Sarnak](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Sarnak). From 2007 to 2010 he was a Tamarkin Assistant Professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University). He was later an assistant professor at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) and then assistant professor and associate professor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). Since 2014 he has been at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), where he is currently a distinguished professor.[^o4]

Kontorovich has held visiting positions at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard), [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), and the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). He served as the editor-in-chief for the journal [*Experimental Mathematics*](wikipedia://en/18797498), and currently serves as managing editor for the *Journal of the Association for Mathematical Research* [^o5][^o6] and is an editor for the *Annals of Formalized Mathematics*.[^o7]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Kontorovich is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), elected in the 2017 class of fellows.[^o9] He was a Sloan Research Fellow from 2013 to 2015.[^o10]

In 2014 Kontorovich received the [Levi L. Conant Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_L._Conant_Prize) for the paper "From Apollonius to Zaremba: Local-global phenomena in thin orbits".[^o11]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Kontorovich is the son of Vladimir Kontorovich, an economics professor at [Haverford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverford_College), and Alla Gil, the former managing director of the investment banking division at [Goldman Sachs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldman_Sachs).[^o14][^o15] He is married to Amy Rosen Kontorovich, a professor at the [Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Icahn_School_of_Medicine_at_Mount_Sinai).[^o13][^o16]
