# Demographics of Tehran

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Tehran"}

**[Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran)** is one of the most ethnically diverse cities in [Asia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asia). The city of Tehran (not to be confused with the larger, Tehran Metropolitan) had a population of approximately 9,729,740 in 2025[^o3] and the Tehran Province had a 14,437,000 population in 2023.[^o4]

:::

## Ethnic groups {#Ethnic_groups}

### Persians {#Persians}

According to a 2010 census conducted by the Sociology Department of [Tehran University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran_University) in many districts of Tehran across various socio-economic classes in proportion to the population sizes of each district and socio-economic class, 63% of people in [Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran) were born in Tehran, 98% can speak the [Persian language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_language), 75% identify themselves as ethnic [Persians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Persian_people), and 13% have some degree of proficiency in a [European language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_language).[^o38]

### Azerbaijanis {#Azerbaijanis}

Azerbaijanis in Tehran also known as Turks in Tehran or Azeris in Tehran are [Iranians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran) of [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijanis) ethnicity. Azerbaijanis comprise 25% of [Tehran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran)'s population and 20.3%[^o40] – 25%[^o41] of [Tehran Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tehran_Province)'s population.

### Mazanderanis {#Mazanderanis}

Ethnic [Mazanderanis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mazanderani_people) are the third-largest, comprising about 8.2% of the total population.[^o43][^o44]

:::
