# PowerVu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="PowerVu"}

**PowerVu** is a [conditional access system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditional_access_system) for [digital television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_television) developed by [Scientific Atlanta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_Atlanta).[^o2] It is used for professional broadcasting, notably by [Retevision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retevision), [Bloomberg Television](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Television), [Discovery Channel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discovery_Channel), [AFRTS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AFRTS), [ABS-CBN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ABS-CBN), [GMA Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GMA_Network), and [American Forces Network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Forces_Network). It is also used by cable companies to prevent viewing by unauthorized viewers and non-cable subscribers.

:::

PowerVu has decoders that decode signals from certain satellites for cable distribution services. These decoders can also be used just like the [FTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FTA_receiver) (Free-To-Air) satellite receivers if properly configured.
