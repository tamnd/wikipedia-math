# Ostilio Ricci

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Ostilio Ricci"}

**Ostilio Ricci da Fermo** (1540–1603) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was a university professor in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence) at the [Accademia delle Arti del Disegno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_delle_Arti_del_Disegno), founded in 1560 by [Giorgio Vasari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giorgio_Vasari). Ricci is also known for being [Galileo Galilei](wikipedia://en/29688374)'s teacher.

Ricci was the Court Mathematician to the [Grand Duke Francesco de' Medici](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francesco_I_de'_Medici,_Grand_Duke_of_Tuscany) in Florence, in 1580, when Galileo attended his lectures in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa).

Galileo was enrolled at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), by his father [Vincenzo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincenzo_Galilei), in order to study medicine. Instead, Galilei became more interested in mathematics, after meeting Ostilio Ricci, a former student of [Niccolò Tartaglia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niccolò_Tartaglia).[^o5] Ricci taught Galileo the mathematics of [Euclid](wikipedia://en/9331) and [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844), who both deeply influenced Galileo's later work.[^o6] Ricci considered mathematics not to be a distinct science, but a practical tool for problems in mechanics and engineering. Ostilio Ricci is systematically cited in the various biographies of Galileo Galilei.

He died at age 62, in Florence. Italy. His cause of death remains unknown.
