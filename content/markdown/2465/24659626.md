# Karin Erdmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karin Erdmann"}

*Image: KarinErdmann.jpg.* Erdmann at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach) in 2009

**Karin Erdmann** (born 1948) is a German mathematician specializing in the areas of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) known as [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) (especially [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685)) and [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) (especially [Hochschild cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hochschild_cohomology)). She is notable for her work in modular representation theory which has been cited over 1500 times according to the [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646). Her nephew Martin Erdmann is professor for experimental [particle physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Particle_physics) at the [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

She attended the [Justus-Liebig-Universität Gießen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justus-Liebig-Universität_Gießen) and wrote her Ph.D. thesis on "*2-Hauptblöcke von Gruppen mit Dieder-Gruppen als 2-Sylow-Gruppen*" (Principal 2-blocks of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447) with [dihedral](wikipedia://en/100267) [Sylow 2-subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_subgroup)) in 1976 under the direction of [Gerhard O. Michler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gerhard_O._Michler).

## Professional career {#Professional_career}

Erdmann was a Fellow of [Somerville College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Somerville_College,_Oxford). Erdmann is a university lecturer emeritus at the [Mathematical Institute at the University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Mathematical_Institute,_University_of_Oxford) where she has had 25 doctoral students and 45 descendants.[^o3] She has published over 115 papers and her work has been cited over 2000 times.[^o5] She has contributed to the understanding of the [representation theory of the symmetric group](wikipedia://en/874641).

## Honors {#Honors}

Erdmann was the inaugural [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2008.[^o7]
