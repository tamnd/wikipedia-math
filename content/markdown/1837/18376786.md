# Mathematical Society of Japan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical Society of Japan"}

The **Mathematical Society of Japan** (**MSJ**, 日本数学会) is a [learned society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_society) for mathematics in [Japan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japan).

:::

In 1877, the organization was established as the *Tokyo Sugaku Kaisha* and was the first academic society in Japan. It was re-organized and re-established in its present form in 1946.

The MSJ has roughly 5,000 members.[^o2] They have the opportunity to participate in programs at MSJ meetings which take place in spring and autumn each year. They also have the opportunity to announce their own research at these meetings.

### Iyanaga Prize {#Iyanaga_Prize}

### Geometry Prize {#Geometry_Prize}

The **Geometry Prize** is a mathematics award granted by the Mathematical Society of Japan to recognise significant or long-time research work in the field of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), including [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). It was established in 1987[^o9] by [Morio Obata](wikipedia://en/80481508).[^o10]

:::

### Takebe Prize {#Takebe_Prize}

In the context of its 50th anniversary celebrations, the Mathematical Society of Japan established the Takebe Prize for the encouragement of those who show promise as mathematicians. The award is named after [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period) mathematician [Takebe Katahiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Takebe_Kenkō) (also known as Takebe Kenkō).[^o12]

## English Publications from MSJ {#English_Publications_from_MSJ}

MSJ publishes the following journals in English.

- Journal of the Mathematical Society of Japan (JMSJ)[^o18]
- Japanese Journal of Mathematics (JJM)[^o19][^o20]
- *Publications of the Mathematical Society of Japan*[^o21]
- *Advanced Studies in Pure Mathematics*[^o22]
- *MSJ Memoirs*[^o23][^o24]
