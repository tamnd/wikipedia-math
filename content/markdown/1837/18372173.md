# Information diagram

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Information diagram"}

An **information diagram** is a type of [Venn diagram](wikipedia://en/61701) used in [information theory](wikipedia://en/14773) to illustrate relationships among Shannon's basic [measures of information](wikipedia://en/6101309): [entropy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_entropy), [joint entropy](wikipedia://en/910967), [conditional entropy](wikipedia://en/908548) and [mutual information](wikipedia://en/427282).[^o2][^o3] Information diagrams are a useful pedagogical tool for teaching and learning about these basic measures of information. Information diagrams have also been applied to specific problems such as for displaying the [information theoretic similarity](wikipedia://en/41129889) between sets of [ontological terms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology_%28information_science%29).[^o4]

:::
