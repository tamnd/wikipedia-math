# Skip counting

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Skip counting"}

**Skip counting** is a mathematics technique taught as a kind of multiplication in [reform mathematics](wikipedia://en/9902256) textbooks such as [TERC](wikipedia://en/6877531). In older textbooks, this technique is called **counting by twos** (threes, fours, etc.).

:::

In skip counting by twos, a person can count to 10 by only naming every other even number: 2, 4, 6, 8, 10.[^o2] Combining the base (two, in this example) with the number of groups (five, in this example) produces the standard multiplication equation: two multiplied by five equals ten.
