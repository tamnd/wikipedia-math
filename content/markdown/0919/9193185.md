# Countryman line

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Countryman line"}

In mathematics, a **Countryman line** (named after Roger Simmons Countryman Jr.) is an [uncountable](wikipedia://en/32248) [linear ordering](wikipedia://en/30330) whose square is the union of [countably](wikipedia://en/6026) many [chains](wikipedia://en/30330#Chains). The existence of Countryman lines was first proven by [Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211). Shelah also conjectured that, assuming [PFA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proper_Forcing_Axiom), every [Aronszajn line](wikipedia://en/9211715) contains a Countryman line. This conjecture, which remained open for three decades, was proven by [Justin Moore](wikipedia://en/56288965).

:::
