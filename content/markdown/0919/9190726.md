# Néron model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Néron model"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Néron model** (or **Néron minimal model**, or **minimal model**) for an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) *A<sub>K</sub>* defined over the [field of fractions](wikipedia://en/25271) *K* of a [Dedekind domain](wikipedia://en/145132) *R* is the "push-forward" of *A<sub>K</sub>* from Spec(*K*) to Spec(*R*), in other words the "best possible" [group scheme](wikipedia://en/510523) *A<sub>R</sub>* defined over *R* corresponding to *A<sub>K</sub>*.

:::

They were introduced by (txt) for abelian varieties over the quotient field of a Dedekind domain *R* with [perfect](wikipedia://en/505088) residue fields, and (Raynaud 1966) extended this construction to semiabelian varieties over all Dedekind domains.

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Suppose that *R* is a [Dedekind domain](wikipedia://en/145132) with field of fractions *K*, and suppose that *A<sub>K</sub>* is a [smooth](wikipedia://en/35033819) [separated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Separated_morphism) scheme over *K* (such as an abelian variety). Then a **Néron model** of *A<sub>K</sub>* is defined to be a [smooth](wikipedia://en/7837041) separated scheme *A<sub>R</sub>* over *R* with fiber *A<sub>K</sub>* that is [universal](wikipedia://en/32245) in the following sense.

If *X* is a smooth separated scheme over *R* then any *K*-morphism from *X*<sub>*K*</sub> to *A<sub>K</sub>* can be extended to a unique *R*-morphism from *X* to *A<sub>R</sub>* **(Néron mapping property)**.

In particular, the canonical map $A_R(R)\to A_K(K)$ is an isomorphism. If a Néron model exists then it is unique up to unique isomorphism.

In terms of sheaves, any scheme *A* over Spec(*K*) represents a sheaf on the category of schemes smooth over Spec(*K*) with the smooth [Grothendieck topology](wikipedia://en/12910), and this has a pushforward by the injection map from Spec(*K*) to Spec(*R*), which is a sheaf over Spec(*R*). If this pushforward is representable by a scheme, then this scheme is the Néron model of *A*.

In general the scheme *A<sub>K</sub>* need not have any Néron model. For abelian varieties *A<sub>K</sub>* Néron models exist and are unique (up to unique isomorphism) and are commutative [quasi-projective](wikipedia://en/1321785) [group schemes](wikipedia://en/510523) over *R*. The fiber of a Néron model over a [closed point](wikipedia://en/33086408) of Spec(*R*) is a smooth commutative [algebraic group](wikipedia://en/247924), but need not be an abelian variety: for example, it may be disconnected or a torus. Néron models exist as well for certain commutative groups other than abelian varieties such as tori, but these are only locally of finite type. Néron models do not exist for the additive group.

:::

::: {#o7 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The formation of Néron models commutes with products.
- The formation of Néron models commutes with [étale](wikipedia://en/3022771) base change.
- An [Abelian scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_scheme) *A*<sub>*R*</sub> is the Néron model of its generic fibre.

:::

## The Néron model of an elliptic curve {#The_Néron_model_of_an_elliptic_curve}

The Néron model of an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225) *A*<sub>*K*</sub> over *K* can be constructed as follows. First form the minimal model over *R* in the sense of algebraic (or arithmetic) surfaces. This is a regular proper surface over *R* but is not in general smooth over *R* or a group scheme over *R*. Its subscheme of smooth points over *R* is the Néron model, which is a smooth group scheme over *R* but not necessarily proper over *R*. The fibers in general may have several irreducible components, and to form the Néron model one discards all multiple components, all points where two components intersect, and all singular points of the components.

[Tate's algorithm](wikipedia://en/14795246) calculates the [special fiber](wikipedia://en/3073462) of the Néron model of an elliptic curve, or more precisely the fibers of the minimal surface containing the Néron model.
