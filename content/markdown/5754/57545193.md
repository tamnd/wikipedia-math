# M22 graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M22 graph"}

The **M<sub>22</sub> graph**, also called the **Mesner graph** or **Witt graph**,[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] is the unique [strongly regular graph](wikipedia://en/1399856) with parameters (77, 16, 0, 4).[^o7] It is constructed from the [Steiner system](wikipedia://en/28016) (3, 6, 22) by representing its 77 blocks as vertices and joining two vertices [iff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iff) they have no terms in common, or by deleting a vertex and its neighbors from the [Higman–Sims graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higman–Sims_graph).[^o8][^o9]

:::

For any term, the family of blocks that contain that term forms an [independent set](wikipedia://en/524501) in this graph, with 21 vertices. In a result analogous to the [Erdős–Ko–Rado theorem](wikipedia://en/296188) (which can be formulated in terms of independent sets in [Kneser graphs](wikipedia://en/2724154)), these are the unique [maximum independent sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum_independent_set) in this graph.[^o6]

It is one of seven known [triangle-free](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Triangle-free) strongly regular graphs.[^o10] Its [graph spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_spectrum) is (−6)<sup>21</sup>2<sup>55</sup>16<sup>1</sup>,[^o8] and its [automorphism group](wikipedia://en/56661772) is the [Mathieu group M22](wikipedia://en/15200699).[^o7]
