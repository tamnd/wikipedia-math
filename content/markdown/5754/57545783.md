# Ping Zhang (graph theorist)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ping Zhang"}

**Ping Zhang** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401). She is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Western Michigan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Western_Michigan_University) and the author of many textbooks on graph theory and [mathematical proof](wikipedia://en/82285).

:::

Zhang earned a master's degree in 1989 from the [University of Jordan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Jordan), working there on [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424) with Hasan Al-Ezeh. She completed her Ph.D. in 1995 at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University). Her dissertation, in [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), was *Subposets of Boolean Algebras*, and was supervised by [Bruce Sagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bruce_Sagan).

After a short-term position at the [University of Texas at El Paso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_El_Paso), she joined the Western Michigan faculty in 1996.

## Books {#Books}

Zhang is the author of:

- *Mathematical Proofs: A Transition to Advanced Mathematics* (with [Gary Chartrand](wikipedia://en/22782409) and A. D. Polimeni, [Addison-Wesley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addison-Wesley), 2002; 2nd ed., 2007; 3rd ed., 2012)
- *Introduction to Graph Theory* (with Gary Chartrand, McGraw-Hill, 2004; Chinese ed., 2006); revised as *A First Course in Graph Theory* (Dover, 2012)
- *Chromatic Graph Theory* (with [Gary Chartrand](wikipedia://en/22782409), [CRC Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRC_Press), 2008)
- *Graphs & Digraphs* (by Gary Chartrand and Linda Lesniak, with Zhang added as a co-author on the 5th ed., CRC Press, 2010)
- *Discrete Mathematics* (with Gary Chartrand, Waveland Press, 2011)
- *Covering Walks in Graphs* (with Futaba Fujie, Springer, 2014)
- *Color-Induced Graph Colorings* (Springer, 2015)
- *The Fascinating World of Graph Theory* (with [Arthur T. Benjamin](wikipedia://en/12428320) and Gary Chartrand, [Princeton University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University_Press), 2015)
- *A Kaleidoscopic View of Graph Colorings* (Springer, 2016)
- *How to Label a Graph* (with Gary Chartrand and Cooroo Egan, Springer, 2019)
- *Irregularity in Graphs* (with Akbar Ali and Gary Chartrand, Springer, 2021)

She is also the co-editor of:

- *Handbook of Graph Theory* (originally edited by Jonathan L. Gross and Jay Yellen, with Zhang added as a co-editor on the 2nd ed., CRC Press, 2013)
