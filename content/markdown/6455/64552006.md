# Mathematically Gifted & Black

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematically Gifted & Black"}

**Mathematically Gifted & Black** (**MGB**) is a website that features the accomplishments of black scholars in [mathematical sciences](wikipedia://en/25750436). In addition to highlighting the work and lives of established [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) in the [African Diaspora](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_diaspora), the platform aims to support the next generation of these mathematicians as they pursue career goals in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and mathematical sciences fields.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Featured mathematicians must have a degree in mathematics that they use in their work and be recognized as a leader in research, education, industry, government, academia, or outreach.[^o5]

Founders of MGB received the [Association for Women in Mathematics’](wikipedia://en/9858698) 2022 AWM Presidential Recognition Award.[^o6][^o7]

## History {#History}

Mathematically Gifted & Black was founded in 2017 by Erica Graham ([Bryn Mawr College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryn_Mawr_College)), [Raegan Higgins](wikipedia://en/64235331) ([Texas Tech University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Tech_University)), [Candice Price](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candice_Renee_Price) ([Smith College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smith_College)), and Shelby Wilson ([University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park)). The professors created the website with the intent of highlighting the contributions and lives of black mathematicians. The name of the website was partially inspired by the song "[To Be Young, Gifted and Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/To_Be_Young,_Gifted_and_Black)" performed by [Nina Simone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nina_Simone) with lyrics by [Weldon Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weldon_Irvine).[^o9][^o10][^o11]

## Notable people recognized {#Notable_people_recognized}

Mathematically Gifted & Black features a collective archive called the "Circle of Excellence" that documents the website's [Black History Month](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black_History_Month) honorees that have been recognized since the site's inception. During the month of February, a prominent black mathematician is recognized each day, their accomplishments are highlighted, and a short Q&A with the recipient is often included. The mathematicians featured by MGB include university professors and a diverse variety of mathematical professionals.[^o13][^o14]

Several notable individuals are included among the extensive archive of honorees. Their names appear below.
