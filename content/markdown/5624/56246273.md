# Opinion polling for the 2018 Mexican general election

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Opinion polling for the 2018 Mexican general election"}

Opinion polling was carried out prior to the [2018 Mexican general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2018_Mexican_general_election).

:::

The 2018 Mexican election features nine registered political parties, including the [Institutional Revolutionary Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institutional_Revolutionary_Party) (PRI), [National Action Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Action_Party_%28Mexico%29) (PAN), [Party of the Democratic Revolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Party_of_the_Democratic_Revolution) (PRD), and the newly formed [National Regeneration Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Regeneration_Movement) (Morena).

## Coalition backgrounds {#Coalition_backgrounds}

### Todos por México {#Todos_por_México}

PRI, which held the presidency from 1929 to 2000 and is currently in power under President Peña Nieto, is primarily a [center-right](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre-right_politics) party. It will be contesting the elections in a coalition alongside the [Ecological Green Party of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecological_Green_Party_of_Mexico) and the [New Alliance Party (Mexico)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Alliance_Party_%28Mexico%29) under the coalition "*[Todos por México](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Todos_por_México)*" ("Everyone for Mexico").

### Por México al Frente {#Por_México_al_Frente}

PAN is a center-right, [Christian democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_democracy)[^o5] party which held the presidency in 2 [sexenios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sexenio_%28Mexico%29) from 2000-2012.

PRD grew out of the PRI in 1986 as the leftists who created the *Corriente Democrática* ([Democratic Current](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Democratic_Front_%28Mexico%29)) within PRI split off. It came quite close to claiming the presidency in [2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2006_Mexican_general_election). Surprisingly, the PRD decided to form a coalition with the right-wing PAN, and they will contest the elections alongside [Citizens' Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Citizens'_Movement_%28Mexico%29) in a coalition called "*[Por México al Frente](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Por_México_al_Frente)*" ("For Mexico, to the Front").[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9]

### Juntos Haremos Historia {#Juntos_Haremos_Historia}

MORENA, created in 2014 by [Andrés Manuel López Obrador](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrés_Manuel_López_Obrador), affectionately called AMLO, is a left-leaning party which split from the PRD.[^o11] It will contest the elections as a coalition with the [Labor Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_Party_%28Mexico%29) (PT) and the [Social Encounter Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Encounter_Party) (PES), called "*[Juntos Haremos Historia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juntos_Haremos_Historia)*" ("Together we'll make history").
