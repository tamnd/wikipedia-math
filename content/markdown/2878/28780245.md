# Order (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Order"}

***Order*** (subtitled *A Journal on the Theory of Ordered Sets and its Applications*) is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) on [order theory](wikipedia://en/361924) and its applications, published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media). It was established in 1984 by [Ivan Rival](wikipedia://en/28784080) ([University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary)).[^o3][^o4] From 2010 to 2018, its [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) was [Dwight Duffus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dwight_Duffus) ([Emory University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emory_University)). He was succeeded in 2019 by Ryan R. Martin ([Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University)).[^o5]

:::
