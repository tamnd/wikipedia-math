# Component theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Component theorem"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) [classification](wikipedia://en/4576710) of [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group), the **component theorem** of (txt) shows that if *G* is a [simple group](wikipedia://en/47600) of odd type, and various other assumptions are satisfied, then *G* has a [centralizer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) of an [involution](wikipedia://en/293450) with a "standard component" with small centralizer.

:::
