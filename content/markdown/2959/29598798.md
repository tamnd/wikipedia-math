# Trichotomy theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Trichotomy theorem"}

In [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **trichotomy theorem** divides the [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group) of [characteristic 2 type](wikipedia://en/29668519) and [rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rank_%28group_theory%29) at least 3 into three classes. It was [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by Michael Aschbacher[^o2][^o3] for rank 3, and by Gorenstein and Lyons[^o4] for rank at least 4. The three classes are [groups of GF(2) type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groups_of_GF%282%29_type) (classified by Timmesfeld and others), groups of "standard type" for some [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) (classified by the [Gilman–Griess theorem](wikipedia://en/29949637) and work by several others), and [groups of uniqueness type](wikipedia://en/29931063), where Aschbacher proved that there are no [simple groups](wikipedia://en/47600).

:::
