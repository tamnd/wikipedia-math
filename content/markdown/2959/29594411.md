# Brauer–Suzuki–Wall theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brauer–Suzuki–Wall theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Brauer–Suzuki–Wall theorem**, [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by (Brauer Suzuki Wall 1958), characterizes the one-dimensional unimodular [projective groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_group) over [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615).

:::
