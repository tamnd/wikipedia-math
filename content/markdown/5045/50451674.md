# Steve Simpson (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve Simpson"}

**Stephen George Simpson** (born September 8, 1945) is an American mathematician whose research concerns the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358), including work in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [recursion theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recursion_theory), and [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175). He is known for his extensive development of the field of [reverse mathematics](wikipedia://en/326365) founded by [Harvey Friedman](wikipedia://en/3038108), in which the goal is to determine which axioms are needed to prove certain mathematical theorems.[^o3] He has also argued for the benefits of [finitistic](wikipedia://en/152900) mathematical systems, such as [primitive recursive arithmetic](wikipedia://en/6637022), which do not include [actual infinity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Actual_infinity).[^o4]

:::

A conference in honor of Simpson's 70th birthday was organized in May 2016.[^o5]

## Education {#Education}

Simpson graduated in 1966 from [Lehigh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehigh_University) with a B.A. (summa cum laude) and M.A. in mathematics.[^o7] He earned a Ph.D. from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1971, with a dissertation entitled *Admissible Ordinals and Recursion Theory* and supervised by [Gerald Sacks](wikipedia://en/3050504).

## Career {#Career}

After short-term positions at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), Simpson became an assistant professor at the [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University) in 1975. At Penn State, he was Raymond N. Shibley professor from 1987 to 1992.[^o7]

In 2016, his wife, computer scientist [Padma Raghavan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Padma_Raghavan), moved from Penn State to [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University) to become vice provost for research,[^o10] and Simpson followed her, becoming a [research professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_professor) at Vanderbilt.[^o11]
