# Ancient solution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ancient solution"}

In mathematics, an **ancient solution** to a [differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309) is a solution that can be extrapolated backwards to all past times, without singularities. That is, it is a solution "that is defined on a time interval of the form $(-\infty, T)$."[^o2]

:::

The term was introduced by [Richard Hamilton](wikipedia://en/758911) in his work on the [Ricci flow](wikipedia://en/288291).[^o3] It has since been applied to other [geometric flows](wikipedia://en/14617515)[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7] as well as to other systems such as the [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395)[^o8][^o9] and [heat equation](wikipedia://en/166415).[^o10]
