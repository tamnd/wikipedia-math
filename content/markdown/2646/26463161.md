# Novikov's compact leaf theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Novikov's compact leaf theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Novikov's compact leaf theorem**, named after [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515), states that

:::

*A codimension-one [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227) of a compact 3-manifold whose [universal covering space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_covering_space) is not contractible must have a compact leaf.*

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Novikov's compact leaf theorem for S3"}

## Novikov's compact leaf theorem for *S*<sup>3</sup> {#Novikov's_compact_leaf_theorem_for_S3}

Theorem: *A smooth codimension-one foliation of the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792)* *S*<sup>3</sup> *has a compact leaf. The leaf is a torus* *T*<sup>2</sup> *bounding a [solid torus](wikipedia://en/1251473) with the [Reeb foliation](wikipedia://en/2604269).*

The theorem was proved by [Sergei Novikov](wikipedia://en/628515) in 1964. Earlier, [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108) had conjectured that every smooth codimension-one foliation on *S*<sup>3</sup> had a compact leaf, which was known to be true for all known examples; in particular, the [Reeb foliation](wikipedia://en/2604269) has a compact leaf that is *T*<sup>2</sup>.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/theorem cue=named-heading name="Novikov's compact leaf theorem for any M3"}

## Novikov's compact leaf theorem for any *M*<sup>3</sup> {#Novikov's_compact_leaf_theorem_for_any_M3}

In 1965, Novikov proved the compact leaf theorem for any *M*<sup>3</sup>:

Theorem: *Let* *M*<sup>3</sup> *be a closed 3-manifold with a smooth codimension-one foliation* *F*. *Suppose any of the following conditions is satisfied:*

1. the *[fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) $\pi_1(M^3)$ is finite,*
1. the *second [homotopy group](wikipedia://en/297506) $\pi_2(M^3)\ne 0$,*
1. *there exists a leaf $L\in F$ such that the map $\pi_1(L)\to\pi_1(M^3)$ induced by inclusion has a non-trivial [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240).*

*Then* *F* *has a compact leaf of [genus](wikipedia://en/61891)* *g* ≤ 1.

In terms of covering spaces:

*A codimension-one [foliation](wikipedia://en/371227) of a compact 3-manifold whose [universal covering space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_covering_space) is not contractible must have a compact leaf.*

:::
