# Alfréd Rényi Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfréd Rényi Prize"}

The **Alfréd Rényi Prize** is awarded biennially by the [Alfréd Rényi Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/31714670) of the [Hungarian Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Science) in honor of founder [Alfréd Rényi](wikipedia://en/614988). By the current rules it is given to one or two fellows of the Institute in recognition of their outstanding performance in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) research of the previous five-year period. Members of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) and the director are not eligible.

:::
