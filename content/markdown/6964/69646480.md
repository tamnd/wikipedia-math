# George Paxton Young

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="George Paxton Young"}

**George Paxton Young** (9 November 1818 – 26 February 1889) was a Canadian philosopher and professor of logic, metaphysics and ethics at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto).[^o3][^o4] He studied the quintic polynomial equation and in 1888 described how to solve a solvable quintic equation, without providing an explicit formula.[^o5]

:::

## George Paxton Young Memorial Prize {#George_Paxton_Young_Memorial_Prize}

The Department of Philosophy at the University of Toronto grants the George Paxton Young Memorial Prize annually to students who read a refereed philosophy paper at an international, national or regional philosophy conference.[^o7]
