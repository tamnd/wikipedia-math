# Restriction conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Restriction conjecture"}

In [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), the **restriction conjecture**, also known as the **Fourier restriction conjecture**, is a conjecture about the behaviour of the [Fourier transform](wikipedia://en/52247) on curved hypersurfaces.[^o2][^o3] It was first hypothesized by [Elias Stein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elias_Stein).[^o4] The conjecture states that two necessary conditions needed to solve a problem known as the *restriction problem* in that scenario are also sufficient.[^o3][^o4]

:::

The restriction conjecture is closely related to the [Kakeya conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kakeya_conjecture), [Bochner-Riesz conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochner-Riesz_conjecture) and the [local smoothing conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_smoothing_conjecture).[^o5][^o6]

::: {#o8 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

The *restriction conjecture* states that $\|\widehat{g\,d\sigma}\|_{L^q(\mathbb R^n)} \lesssim \|g\|_{L^p(S^{n-1})}$ for certain *q* and *n*, where $\|f\|_{L^p}$ represents the [L<sup>*p*</sup> norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lp_norm), or $\int_{-\infty}^\infty f(x)^p \, dx$ and $f \lesssim g$ means that $f \le Cg$ for some constant $C$.[^o9]

The requirements of *q* and *n* set by the conjecture are that $\frac{1}{q} < \frac{n-1}{2n}$ and $\frac{1}{q} \le \frac{n-1}{n+1}\frac{1}{p}$.[^o9]

The restriction conjecture has been proved for dimension $n = 2$ as of 2021.[^o9]

:::
