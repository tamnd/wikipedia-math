# Marcel Berger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marcel Berger"}

**Marcel Berger** (14 April 1927 – 15 October 2016) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), working in the field of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), and a former director of the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) (IHÉS), France.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

After studying from 1948 to 1951 at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29) in Paris, Berger obtained in 1954 his PhD from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris), with thesis written under the direction of [André Lichnerowicz](wikipedia://en/2389188). From 1958 to 1964 he taught at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg) and had visiting positions at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). From 1964 to 1966 he taught at the [University of Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Nice-Sophia-Antipolis), after which he joined the [University of Paris VII](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University). From 1985 to 1993 he served as director of the IHÉS. He was President of the [French Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/1691205) in 1979–1980.[^o5]

Formerly residing in Le Castera in [Lasseube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lasseube), Berger was instrumental in [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29)'s accepting positions both at the University of Paris and at the IHÉS.[^o6]
