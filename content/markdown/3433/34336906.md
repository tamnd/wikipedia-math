# Robert W. Brooks

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert W. Brooks"}

*Image: Robert W. Brooks.jpg.* Robert W. Brooks (1985)

**Robert Wolfe Brooks** (September 16, 1952 – September 5, 2002) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [spectral geometry](wikipedia://en/20635289), [Riemann surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_surfaces), [circle packings](wikipedia://en/16879665), and [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Brooks was born in 1952 in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.) and grew up in [Bethesda, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bethesda,_Maryland), where he graduated in 1970 from [Walt Whitman High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walt_Whitman_High_School_%28Maryland%29). In 1974 he completed his Masters thesis from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University);[^o4] his thesis "[Russell](wikipedia://en/4163), [Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740), and the foundations of geometry" won him the [Bowdoin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowdoin_Prizes) for Essays in the Natural Sciences in 1975.[^o5]

He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from Harvard University in 1977; his thesis, *The smooth cohomology of groups of diffeomorphisms*, was written under the supervision of [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457). He then undertook [postdoctoral studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postdoctoral_researcher) with J. Peter Matelski at the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University), where they created pictures of fractals, leading to [Benoit Mandelbrot](wikipedia://en/3999)'s creation of the [Mandelbrot set](wikipedia://en/19562) in 1980.[^o4]

He worked at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) (1979–1984), then at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California), and then, from 1995, at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion) in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa).[^o4][^o6]

Brooks died from [heart attack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myocardial_infarction) during a visit to [Montreal, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montreal,_Canada) and was buried in Sde Yehoshua cemetery in Haifa.[^o7] He was survived by his parents David and Harriet Brooks, his wife Sharon and four children.[^o4] His eldest son [Shimon Brooks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimon_Brooks) is a mathematics professor at [Bar-Ilan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bar-Ilan_University).

## Work {#Work}

In an influential paper (Brooks 1981), Brooks proved that the [bounded cohomology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_cohomology) of a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to the bounded cohomology of its [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004).[^o10]
