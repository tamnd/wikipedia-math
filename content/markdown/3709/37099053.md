# Johann Wilhelm Andreas Pfaff

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Wilhelm Andreas Pfaff"}

**Johann Wilhelm Andreas Pfaff** (5 December 1774 – 26 June 1835) was professor of pure and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) successively at [Dorpat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorpat_University), Nuremberg, Würzburg and [Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg). He was a brother of [Johann Friedrich Pfaff](wikipedia://en/1669324), a mathematician; and of [Christian Heinrich Pfaff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Heinrich_Pfaff), a physician and physicist.

:::
