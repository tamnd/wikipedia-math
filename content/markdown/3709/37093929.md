# Leopoldo Penna Franca

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Leopoldo Penna Franca"}

**Leopoldo Penna Franca** (April 7, 1959 – September 19, 2012, in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro), [Brazil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazil))[^o3] was a Brazilian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He received his PhD in 1987 from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in engineering under [Thomas J. R. Hughes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_J._R._Hughes). After graduation, he worked at the pt:Laboratório Nacional de Computação Científica - LNCC in Brazil. From 1993 to 2011, he was a full professor and researcher of mathematics at the [University of Colorado Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Colorado_Denver). From 2008 to 2010, he was a visiting professor and researcher of mathematics at the [Federal University of Rio de Janeiro - UFRJ / Coppe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro_-_UFRJ_/_Coppe) at the [Civil Engineering Department](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_Engineering_Department) in collaboration to [Alvaro Coutinho](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alvaro_Coutinho). From 2011 until 2012, he worked for [IBM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM) Research Brazil. He was known for his work on stabilized [finite element methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_element_methods). He was a recipient of the [United States Association for Computational Mechanics - USACM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Association_for_Computational_Mechanics_-_USACM). Received in 1999 the [Gallagher Young Investigator Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gallagher_Young_Investigator_Award) for "outstanding accomplishments in computational mechanics, particularly in the published literature, by a researcher 40 years old or younger".[^o4] He was listed[^o5] as an ISI Highly Cited Author in Engineering by the [ISI Web of Knowledge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISI_Web_of_Knowledge), Thomson Scientific Company.
