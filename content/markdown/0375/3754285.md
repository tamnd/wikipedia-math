::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Order-4 dodecahedral honeycomb"}

In [hyperbolic geometry](wikipedia://en/241291), the **order-4 dodecahedral honeycomb** is one of four compact [regular](wikipedia://en/518693) space-filling [tessellations](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycombs](wikipedia://en/6008654)) of [hyperbolic 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_3-space). With [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) $\{5,3,4\},$ it has four [dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/7149361) around each [edge](wikipedia://en/12158034), and 8 dodecahedra around each [vertex](wikipedia://en/11308417) in an [octahedral](wikipedia://en/80177635) arrangement. Its vertices are constructed from 3 orthogonal axes. Its [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) is the [order-5 cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3870617). The order-4 dodecahedral honeycomb has the lowest sectional curvature relative to cell edge length of the regular hyperbolic tessellations in 3-space.

:::

## Description {#Description}

The [dihedral angle](wikipedia://en/338046) of a [regular dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/7149361) is ~116.6°, so it is impossible to fit 4 of them on an edge in Euclidean 3-space. However in hyperbolic space a properly-scaled regular dodecahedron can be scaled so that its dihedral angles are reduced to 90 degrees, and then four fit exactly on every edge.

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Symmetry"}

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

It has a half symmetry construction, {5,3<sup>1,1</sup>}, with two types (colors) of dodecahedra in the [Wythoff construction](wikipedia://en/2950777). ↔ .

:::
