# Anonym.OS

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Anonym.OS"}

**Anonym.OS** was a [live CD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Live_CD) operating system based on [OpenBSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenBSD) 3.8 with strong [encryption](wikipedia://en/10294) and anonymization tools. The goal of the project was to provide secure, anonymous [web browsing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet) access to everyday users.[^o3] The operating system was [OpenBSD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenBSD) 3.8, although many [packages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_package_%28installation%29) have been added to facilitate its goal. It used [Fluxbox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluxbox) as its [window manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Window_manager).

:::

The project was discontinued after the release of Beta 4 (2006).

## Distributed {#Distributed}

Designed, created and distributed by [kaos.theory/security.research](https://kaos.to).

## Legacy {#Legacy}

Although this specific project is no longer updated, its successors are Incognito OS (discontinued in 2008) and [FreeSBIE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FreeSBIE).[^o6]
