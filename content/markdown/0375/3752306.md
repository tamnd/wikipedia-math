# Friedrich Wilhelm Levi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Friedrich Wilhelm Levi"}

*Image: Levi,Friedrich Wilhelm 1930 Jena.jpg.* Friedrich Wilhelm Levi (1930).

**Friedrich Wilhelm Daniel Levi** (February 6, 1888 – January 1, 1966) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his work in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), especially [torsion-free abelian groups](wikipedia://en/11630973). He also worked in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), and [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Levi was born to Georg Levi and Emma Blum in [Mulhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mulhouse) in [Alsace-Lorraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alsace-Lorraine), then part of the [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire). He received his Ph.D. in 1911 under [Heinrich Martin Weber](wikipedia://en/2643615) at the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg).[^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Levi served his mandatory military service in the German Army in 1906–1907, and was called up again serving in the artillery during [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I), 1914–18. Awarded the [Iron Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iron_Cross), he was discharged as a lieutenant. In 1917, he married Barbara Fitting, with whom he eventually had three children ([Paul Levi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Levi_%28Framer%29), Charlotte, and Suzanne). He taught at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig) from 1920 to 1935, when the [Nazi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazism) government dismissed him because of his Jewish ancestry. Friedrich and Barbara moved to [Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India).[^o3]

In 1935 he accepted an offer as head of the Mathematics Department at the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta).[^o3] He introduced the [Levi graph](wikipedia://en/1396888) in 1940 at a series of lectures on [finite geometry](wikipedia://en/267061).[^o5] He contributed to the understanding of [combinatorics on words](wikipedia://en/20913490) when he articulated the [Levi lemma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Levi_lemma) in an article for the [Calcutta Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calcutta_Mathematical_Society).[^o6] In 1948, Levi became professor of mathematics at [Tata Institute of Fundamental Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) in [Mumbai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mumbai), [India](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/India). According to [Raghavan Narasimhan](wikipedia://en/33109671), Levi had an important influence on the development of 20th century mathematics in India, especially by introducing modern algebra at the University of Calcutta.[^o7]

In 1952, he returned to Germany and was a professor at the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin) and later [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg). He died in [Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg_im_Breisgau) on the first day of 1966.[^o3] A bibliography of 70 works in mathematics by Levi is included in the 1991 tribute by [László Fuchs](wikipedia://en/34809998) and Rüdiger Göbel.

- *Geometrische Konfigurationen*. Hirzel, Leipzig 1929.[^o9]
- [Reinhold Baer](wikipedia://en/1612114) and Friedrich Levi: *Ränder topologischer Räume*. Hirzel, Leipzig 1930.
- *On the fundamentals of analysis. Six lectures delivered in February 1938 at the University of Calcutta*. University of Calcutta, Calcutta 1939.

- *Algebra*. University of Calcutta, Calcutta 1942.
