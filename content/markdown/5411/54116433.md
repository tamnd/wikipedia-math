# Fernanda Botelho (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fernanda Botelho"}

**Fernanda Maria Botelho** (born 1957)[^o3] is an American mathematician, a professor and the director of graduate studies and coordinator of mathematics at the [University of Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Memphis).[^o4] Botelho earned her Ph.D. in 1988 at the [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley), where [Jenny Harrison](wikipedia://en/4673481) was her doctoral advisor. Earlier she did her M.Sc. in 1985 and B.Sc. in 1981 in mathematics at the [Universidade do Porto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidade_do_Porto). Her research interests include [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087). From 2013 through 2016, she held the Dunavant Professorship at the University of Memphis.[^o6]
