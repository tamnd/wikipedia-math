# Evarist Giné

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Evarist Giné"}

**Evarist Giné-Masdéu** (July 31, 1944 – March 13, 2015), or simply **Evarist Giné**, was a Catalan mathematician and statistician. He is known for his pioneering works in probability in [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989), [empirical process theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical_process_theory), [U-statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-statistics) and processes, and [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Giné was born in [Falset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Falset,_Tarragona) in [Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia). He studied at the [University of Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Barcelona), obtaining a [Licenciatura](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) degree in 1967. He went to the United States and completed his PhD at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1973 under the supervision of [Richard M. Dudley](wikipedia://en/3824977). He was a lecturer in statistics at University of California, Berkeley from 1974 to 1975. He spent time afterwards at the [Venezuelan Institute for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Venezuelan_Institute_for_Scientific_Research), where he was the head of the mathematics department, before moving back to the United States.[^o7]

In 1983, Giné became a professor at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) and later moved to [College of Staten Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_Staten_Island) of the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York) in 1988.[^o8] He became a professor of mathematics at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut) in 1990, and was the head of the department of mathematics from 2012.[^o9] He stayed at the University of Connecticut until his death.[^o2]
