# Veronica Bowman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Veronica Bowman"}

**Veronica Elizabeth** (**Ronni**) **Bowman** is a British military statistician specialising in [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571) and [uncertainty](wikipedia://en/63778), and their applications in [knowledge management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_management) for chemical, biological and radiological threats. She is a professor and data science fellow at the [Defence Science and Technology Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defence_Science_and_Technology_Laboratory), and an associate member of the Southampton Statistical Sciences Research Institute at the [University of Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southampton).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Bowman was a mathematics student at the University of Southampton, where she earned a BSc with first class honours before completing a doctorate in statistical modelling.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Bowman is a [Fellow of the Royal Statistical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Statistical_Society) and of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139).

Bowman was the 2021 winner of the Innovation and Creativity Award at the Women in Defence UK Awards. In the [2022 New Year Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2022_New_Year_Honours), Bowman was named as an Officer of the [Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire), for her work developing CrystalCast, a system for uncertainty analysis and Bayesian model combination that was applied to combine academic and government [epidemiological](wikipedia://en/66997) predictions in the [COVID-19 pandemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/COVID-19_pandemic) in order to provide an estimate for the [basic reproduction number](wikipedia://en/917273) of the disease.
