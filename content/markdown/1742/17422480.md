# Neural Computation (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Neural Computation"}

***Neural Computation*** is a monthly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering all aspects of [neural computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neural_computation), including modeling the brain and the design and construction of neurally-inspired information processing systems. It was established in 1989 and is published by [MIT Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT_Press). The [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is [Terrence J. Sejnowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terry_Sejnowski) ([Salk Institute for Biological Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salk_Institute_for_Biological_Studies)).[^o3]

:::

According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2021 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 3.278.[^o4]
