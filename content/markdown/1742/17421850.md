# The Review of Economics and Statistics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Review of Economics and Statistics"}

***The Review of Economics and Statistics*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) that covers [applied economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_economics), with specific relevance to the scope of [econometrics](wikipedia://en/10390). The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Will Dobbie ([Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University)) and Raymond Fisman ([Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University)). The journal is over 100 years old.[^o3]

:::

## History {#History}

The journal, founded initially as *The Review of Economic Statistics* at Harvard University in 1917, published its official “inaugural volume” in 1919. The journal obtained its current title in 1948.

As the first editor-in-chief, [Charles J. Bullock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_J._Bullock) remarked in his *Prefatory Statement* to the first issue that "the purpose of the Review is to promote the collection, criticism, and interpretation of economic statistics, with a view to making them more accurate and valuable than they are at present for business and scientific purposes."
