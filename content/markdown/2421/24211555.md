# Hoffman graph

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Hoffman graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Hoffman graph** is a 4-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 16 vertices and 32 edges discovered by [Alan Hoffman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Hoffman_%28mathematician%29). Published in 1963, it is cospectral to the [hypercube graph](wikipedia://en/6706815) Q<sub>4</sub>.[^o5][^o6]

:::

The Hoffman graph has many common properties with the hypercube Q<sub>4</sub>—both are [Hamiltonian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and have [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 2, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 4, girth 4 and diameter 4. It is also a 4-[vertex-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 4-[edge-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph). However, it is not [distance-regular](wikipedia://en/7768943) and not [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o7] It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o8]

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The Hoffman graph is not a [vertex-transitive graph](wikipedia://en/581175) and its full automorphism group is a group of order 48 isomorphic to the [direct product](wikipedia://en/3706015) of the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) S<sub>4</sub> and the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) **Z**/2**Z**. Despite not being vertex- or edge-transitive, the Hoffmann graph is still [1-walk-regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1-walk-regular_graph) (but not [distance-regular](wikipedia://en/7768943)).

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Hoffman graph is equal to

$$(x-4) (x-2)^4 x^6 (x+2)^4 (x+4)$$

making it an [integral graph](wikipedia://en/24845923)—a graph whose [spectrum](wikipedia://en/534914) consists entirely of integers. It is the same spectrum as the hypercube Q<sub>4</sub>.
