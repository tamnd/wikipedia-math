# Jerry Kazdan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jerry Kazdan"}

*Image: Jerry Kazdan.jpg.* Jerry Kazdan in 1974

**Jerry Lawrence Kazdan** (born 31 October 1937 in [Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit), [Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan)) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) noted for his work in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and the study of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). His contributions include the [Berger&ndash;Kazdan comparison theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berger&ndash;Kazdan_comparison_theorem), which was a key step in the proof of the [Blaschke conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blaschke_conjecture) and the classification of [Wiedersehen manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wiedersehen_manifold). His best-known work, done in collaboration with [Frank Warner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Wilson_Warner), dealt with the problem of prescribing the [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622) of a [Riemannian metric](wikipedia://en/144652).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Kazdan received his bachelor's degree in 1959 from [Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rensselaer_Polytechnic_Institute) and his master's degree in 1961 from NYU. He obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1963 from the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University); his thesis was entitled *A Boundary Value Problem Arising in the Theory of Univalent Functions* and was supervised by [Paul Garabedian](wikipedia://en/22618676). He then took a position as a [Benjamin Peirce](wikipedia://en/364270) Instructor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). Since 1966, he has been a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).

[Dennis DeTurck](wikipedia://en/6642843) was a student of his.

## Honours {#Honours}

In 1999 he received the [Lester Randolph Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_Randolph_Ford_Award) for his expository article *Solving equations, an elegant legacy*.[^o6] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
