# Birol Emir

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Birol Emir"}

**Birol Emir** (born 1966) is a [Turkish-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkish_Americans) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He is an Executive Director and head of Real-World Evidence Statistics.[^o3] at [Pfizer Inc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pfizer). He has been serving as an adjunct professor of statistics[^o4] and lecturer of Applied Analytics at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in New York City. Additionally, he serves as an external committee member of the Graduate School of Arts and Science for the Ph.D. degree at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Birol grew up in [Turkiye](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey) and graduated from [Middle East Technical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Middle_East_Technical_University) with a B.S. with Honors. He earned his M.S. and Ph.D. in statistics from [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University).[^o6] He completed post-doctoral work for the National Surgical Adjuvant Breast and Bowel Project at the [University of Pittsburgh Medical Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh_Medical_Center).

## Research and Career {#Research_and_Career}

Birol's areas of research and professional expertise lie in [causal inference](wikipedia://en/37103476), [real-world evidence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_world_evidence) generation, [predictive modeling](wikipedia://en/2538775), [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151), and [clinical trials](wikipedia://en/241717).[^o8] He co-authored the book *Interface Between Regulation and Statistics in Drug Development*,[^o9] co-edited the book *Statistical Topics in Healthcare Economics and Outcomes Research*,[^o10] and authored a chapter in *A Picture is Worth a Thousand Tables: Graphics in Life Sciences*[^o11]

### Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Birol was elected as a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) (ASA) in 2024.[^o13]
