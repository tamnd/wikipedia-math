# Thompson factorization

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thompson factorization"}

In [finite group theory](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Thompson factorization**, introduced by (txt), is an expression of some [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) as a product of two [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011), usually [normalizers](wikipedia://en/145661) or [centralizers](wikipedia://en/145661) of [*p*-subgroups](wikipedia://en/24664) for some [prime](wikipedia://en/23666) *p*.

:::
