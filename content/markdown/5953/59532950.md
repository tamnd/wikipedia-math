# On Conoids and Spheroids

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="On Conoids and Spheroids"}

*Image: A page from Archimedes' On Conoids and Spheroids.jpg.* A page from Archimedes' On Conoids and Spheroids

***On Conoids and Spheroids*** (Περὶ κωνοειδέων καὶ σφαιροειδέων) is a surviving work by the Greek mathematician and engineer [Archimedes](wikipedia://en/1844) (c. 287 BC – c. 212 BC). Consisting of 32 [propositions](wikipedia://en/30977#Terminology), the work explores properties of and theorems related to the [solids](wikipedia://en/507960) generated by revolution of [conic sections](wikipedia://en/19008673) about their axes, including [paraboloids](wikipedia://en/145845), [hyperboloids](wikipedia://en/145844), and [spheroids](wikipedia://en/82365).[^o2] The principal result of the work is comparing the [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) of any segment cut off by a plane with the volume of a [cone](wikipedia://en/782427) with equal base and axis.[^o3]

:::

The work is addressed to Dositheus of Pelusium.
