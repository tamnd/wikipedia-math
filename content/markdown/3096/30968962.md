# Yadolah Dodge

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Yadolah Dodge"}

**Yadolah Dodge** (یدالله داج, also [Romanized](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanize) as "Yadollāh Dāj") is an [Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran) and [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). His major contributions are in the theory of [operational research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operational_research), [design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541), [simulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation), and [regression](wikipedia://en/826997).

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

He spent his early years in [Abadan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abadan), [Iran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran). He went then to the [Gundeshapur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gundeshapur) or Jundi Shapour University and obtained his Post Licentiate in Engineering in Agriculture in 1966 with distinction. He got his PhD at the [Oregon State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oregon_State_University) in 1974.

## Work {#Work}

In the 1980s he became professor of statistics at the [University of Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Neuchâtel), Switzerland, where he is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus). He is author of more than 60 papers and 25 books, many of which have appeared in multiple editions.[^o5] In 2015 Professor Dodge started the 'Iranian Film Festival Zurich' in order to have a cultural exchange between Iran and Switzerland.
