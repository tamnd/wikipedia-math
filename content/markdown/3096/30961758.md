# Johannes Hjelmslev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Hjelmslev"}

**Johannes Trolle Hjelmslev** (; 7 April 1873 – 16 February 1950) was a mathematician from [Hørning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hørning), [Denmark](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark). Hjelmslev worked in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) and [history of geometry](wikipedia://en/11953). He was the discoverer and eponym of the [Hjelmslev transformation](wikipedia://en/3535050), a method for [mapping](wikipedia://en/516931) an entire [hyperbolic plane](wikipedia://en/318742) into a [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) with a finite [radius](wikipedia://en/1780815). He was the father of [Louis Hjelmslev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Hjelmslev).

:::

Originally named Johannes Trolle Petersen, he changed his patronymic to the surname Hjelmslev to avoid confusion with [Julius Petersen](wikipedia://en/76861). Some of his results are known under his original name, including the [Petersen–Morley theorem](wikipedia://en/48136809).[^o2]
