# Monica Vișan

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Monica Vișan"}

**Monica Vișan** (born August 5, 1979, in [Drobeta-Turnu Severin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Drobeta-Turnu_Severin), [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania)) is a Romanian mathematician at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) who specializes in [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564) and is well known for her work on the [nonlinear Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/1422748).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Vișan earned a bachelor's degree at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) in 2002. She became a student of [Terence Tao](wikipedia://en/1118498) at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) (UCLA), where she completed her doctorate in 2006. Her dissertation was titled *The Defocusing Energy-Critical Nonlinear Schrödinger Equation in Dimensions Five and Higher*.

After postdoctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), Vișan became an assistant professor in the mathematics department at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 2008. She returned to UCLA as a faculty member in 2009 and (keeping her appointment at UCLA) spent 2010–2011 as Harrington Faculty Fellow at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Vișan won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2010. She was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2024 class of fellows.

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

With [Herbert Koch](wikipedia://en/27321694) and [Daniel Tătaru](wikipedia://en/31450291), Vișan is the author of the book *Dispersive Equations and Nonlinear Waves: Generalized Korteweg–de Vries, Nonlinear Schrödinger, Wave and Schrödinger Maps*.[^o6]
