# Chuck Easttom

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Chuck Easttom"}

**William** "**Chuck**" **Easttom II** (born October 5, 1968) is an American scientist working in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), [cyber security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyber_security), [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), [digital forensics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_forensics), [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220), [aerospace engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineering), [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence), [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), and [systems engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Easttom holds a B.A. from [Southeastern Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Oklahoma_State_University), a M.Ed. from [Southeastern Oklahoma State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southeastern_Oklahoma_State_University), a master's degree in Applied Computer Science from [Northcentral University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northcentral_University)[^o5] and a master's degree in Systems Engineering from the [University of Texas at El Paso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_El_Paso), a master's degree in Defense and Strategic Studies from [University of Texas at El Paso](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_El_Paso),[^o6] as well as a D.Sc. [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) in cyber security from [Capitol Technology University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capitol_Technology_University)[^o7][^o8][^o9] dissertation topic "A Comparative Study Of Lattice Based Algorithms For Post Quantum Computing", a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Technology focused on Nanotechnology[^o10] dissertation topic "The Effects of Complexity on Carbon Nanotube Failures", and a Ph.D.[Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in Computer Science from [University of Portsmouth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Portsmouth)[^o11] dissertation topic "A Systematic Framework for Network Forensics Using Graph Theory".

## Professional work {#Professional_work}

In addition to computer security, Easttom has done work in [software engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering),[^o13][^o14] [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), [aerospace engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aerospace_engineering), [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220), [artificial intelligence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artificial_intelligence), [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488),[^o15][^o16] and other areas. He has authored 44 books[^o17] on computer security, programming languages, [Linux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linux), cyber forensics, [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220), [computer networks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_networks), penetration testing, and [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432). His books are used as textbooks for both undergraduate and graduate programs at over 60 universities.[^o18][^o19][^o20][^o21][^o22][^o23][^o24][^o25][^o26][^o27][^o28] He is an inventor with 27 patented computer science inventions.[^o29] He is a frequent speaker at major cybersecurity conferences.[^o30][^o31][^o32][^o33]

Easttom was part of the team that created the original [CompTIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CompTIA) Security+ [^o34] and [CompTIA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CompTIA) Linux+ exams. He created the OSFCE (OSForensics Certified Examiner) course and test,[^o35] the EC Council Certified Encryption Specialist course and certification test,[^o36] and EC Council CAST Advanced Encryption course [^o37] as well as working on other [EC-Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EC-Council) certifications [^o38]

Easttom has been involved in numerous IEEE standards. He is currently the Chair of IEEE P3123 - Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning (AI/ML) Terminology and Data Formats Working Group,[^o39] a member of IEEE 2731 BCI unified terminology working group,[^o40] a former member (2017 to 2019) of IEEE Software & Systems Engineering Standards Committee. Working on the DevOps 2675,[^o41] a member of IEEE P2995 - Trial-Use Standard for a Quantum Algorithm Design and Development Standards Group,[^o42] and Vice Chair IEEE P23026 - Systems and Software Engineering—Engineering and Management of Websites for Systems, Software, and Services Information[^o43]

Chuck Easttom has been interviewed regarding cyber security topics by [CNN](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNN) Money[^o44] and [CBS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CBS) Smart Planet,[^o45] and other media outlets,[^o46][^o47][^o48][^o49][^o50][^o51][^o52] and He has been named a Distinguished Speaker of the [ACM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery)[^o53] and a Distinguished Visitor of the [IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers).[^o54] He is also a Senior member of the IEEE [^o55] and Senior member of the ACM.[^o56] He is also a member of the American Institute of Aeronautics and Astronautics (AIAA).

Easttom was Editor in Chief for the *American Journal of Science and Engineering* from 2018 to 2022.[^o57] He was also the director of the Quantum Computing and Cryptography Lab at Capitol Technology University,[^o58] from 2019 to 2022 he was an adjunct professor for the University of Dallas,[^o59] he is currently an adjunct lecturer for Georgetown University [^o60] and an adjunct professor for Vanderbilt University.[^o61]

Chuck Easttom lives in Plano Texas [^o62] with his wife Teresa.
