# Robert D. Gibbons

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert D. Gibbons"}

**Robert D. Gibbons** is a professor in the field of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), with expertise in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878), [environmental statistics](wikipedia://en/8919339), and [psychometrics](wikipedia://en/24982).[^o3] He holds the position of Blum-Riese Professor and Pritzker Scholar at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), with appointments in the Departments of Medicine,[^o4] Public Health Sciences (Biostatistics),[^o5] and Comparative Human Development.[^o6] Gibbons is a Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988), the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute), and the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664), and a member of the [National Academy of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Medicine) of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences). He has made contributions to statistical work spanning areas such as longitudinal data analysis, [item response theory](wikipedia://en/420159), environmental statistics, and drug safety, with over 350 [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) scientific papers[^o7] and several books[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11] to his name.

:::
