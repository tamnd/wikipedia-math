# Donald Solitar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Donald Solitar"}

**Donald Solitar** (September 5, 1932 in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29), United States – April 28, 2008 in [Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toronto), Canada) was an American and Canadian mathematician, known for his work in [combinatorial group theory](wikipedia://en/979834).[^o3] The [Baumslag–Solitar groups](wikipedia://en/11485892) are named after him and [Gilbert Baumslag](wikipedia://en/11449648), after their joint 1962 paper on these groups.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Solitar competed on the mathematics team of [Brooklyn Technical High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_Technical_High_School) with his future co-author Abe Karrass, one year ahead of him in school. He graduated from [Brooklyn College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn_College) in 1953 (with the assistance of tutoring from Karrass, who went to [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)) and went to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) for graduate study in mathematics. However, his intended mentor there, [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351), was no longer interested in [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), so he left with a master's degree and earned his doctorate from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) instead, in 1958, under the supervision of [Wilhelm Magnus](wikipedia://en/8337666).[^o2][^o3]

After finishing his studies, he joined the faculty of [Adelphi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelphi_University) in 1959, and Karrass soon joined him there as a doctoral student, earning a Ph.D. under Solitar's supervision in 1961; this was the first Ph.D. awarded at Adelphi. Karrass remained on the faculty with Solitar, where they founded a summer institute for high school mathematics teachers. Solitar moved to [Polytechnic University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_Polytechnic_School_of_Engineering) in 1967, and then (as department chair) to [York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/York_University) in 1968, along with Karrass.[^o2][^o3]

Solitar married J. Francien Hageman, a [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people) woman, in 1976. He died of a heart attack on April 28, 2008.[^o2][^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Solitar became a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1982.[^o3]
