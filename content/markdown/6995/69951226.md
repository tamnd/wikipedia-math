# Robert P. Lipton

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert P. Lipton"}

**Robert P. Lipton** is the Nicholson Professor of Mathematics in the Department of Mathematics at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University).[^o3] He is known for his research on Mathematics of [Materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science).

:::

## Academic biography {#Academic_biography}

Lipton obtained his PhD in Mathematics at [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) in 1986, as a student of [Robert V. Kohn](wikipedia://en/48477231).[^o5] He was a Postdoctoral Associate and Visiting Assistant Professor in the Mathematical Sciences Institute at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and a C. B. Morrey Assistant Professor in the Department of Mathematics at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley)[^o6]

## Honors {#Honors}

He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822),[^o8] a fellow of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376),[^o9] and a fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science)[^o10]
