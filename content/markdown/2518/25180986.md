# Richard Samworth

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Samworth"}

**Richard John Samworth** (born May 1978) is a British statistician who is the [Professor of Statistical Science](wikipedia://en/23689227) and the [Director](wikipedia://en/3254271#Directors_of_the_Statistical_Laboratory) of the [Statistical Laboratory, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge),[^o3] and a Teaching Fellow of [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge). He was educated at [St John's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Cambridge). His main research interests are in [nonparametric](wikipedia://en/223369) and [high-dimensional statistics](wikipedia://en/19330658). Particular topics include shape-constrained [density estimation](wikipedia://en/554671) and other nonparametric function estimation problems, nonparametric [classification](wikipedia://en/1579244), [clustering](wikipedia://en/669675) and [regression](wikipedia://en/4536068), the [bootstrap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bootstrap_%28statistics%29) and high-dimensional [variable selection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variable_selection) problems.

:::
