# Hartmann number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hartmann number"}

The **Hartmann number** (**Ha**) is the ratio of electromagnetic force to the viscous force, first introduced by Julius Hartmann (1881 – 1951) of Denmark.[^o2][^o3] It is frequently encountered in fluid flows through magnetic fields.[^o4] It is defined by:

:::

$$\mathrm{Ha} = B L\sqrt{\frac{\sigma} {\mu}}$$

where

- *B* is the [magnetic field](wikipedia://en/36563) intensity
- *L* is the characteristic length scale
- *σ* is the [electrical conductivity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_conductivity)
- *μ* is the [dynamic viscosity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamic_viscosity)
