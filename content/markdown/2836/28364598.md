::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated 5-simplexes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcinated 5-simplex** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338) with 3rd order [truncations](wikipedia://en/4224429) ([Runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination)) of the regular [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex).

:::

There are 4 unique runcinations of the 5-simplex with [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of truncations, and [cantellations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation).

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Runcinated hexateron
- Small prismated hexateron (Acronym: spix) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *runcinated 5-simplex* can be most simply constructed on a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) in 6-space as permutations of (0,0,1,1,1,2) *or* of (0,1,1,1,2,2), seen as facets of a [runcinated 6-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcinated_6-orthoplex), or a [biruncinated 6-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biruncinated_6-cube) respectively.
