# Opetope

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Opetope"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), a branch of mathematics, an **opetope**, a portmanteau of "[operation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operation_%28algebra%29)" and "[polytope](wikipedia://en/23471)", is a [shape](wikipedia://en/169191) that captures higher-dimensional [substitutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Substitution_%28algebra%29).[^o2] It was introduced by [John C. Baez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_C._Baez) and James Dolan so that they could define a [weak *n*-category](wikipedia://en/7349264) as a certain [presheaf](wikipedia://en/4840944) on the category of opetopes.[^o3]

:::
