# Marvin Greenberg

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Marvin Greenberg"}

*Image: Marvin Greenberg.jpg.* Marvin Greenberg at [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1975

**Marvin Jay Greenberg** (December 22, 1935 – December 12, 2017[^o2]) was an American mathematician.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Greenberg studied at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) where he received his bachelor's degree in 1955 (he was a Ford Scholar as an undergraduate) and received his doctorate 1959 from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) under [Serge Lang](wikipedia://en/872991) with the thesis *Pro-Algebraic Structure on the Rational Subgroup of a P-Adic Abelian Variety*.

## Career {#Career}

From 1955 Greenberg was an assistant at Princeton, from 1958 an assistant at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and in 1958 and 1959, an instructor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University). From 1959 to 1964 he was an assistant professor at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), two years of which time he spent on [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) postdoctoral fellowships at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and the [Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_des_Hautes_Études_Scientifiques) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989).[^o5]

From 1965 to 1967 he was an associate professor at [Northeastern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northeastern_University) and from 1967 he worked as an associate professor, and later full professor, at the [University of California, Santa Cruz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Cruz). He retired early in 1992 and moved back to Berkeley.

He was known for his book on [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610) (1st edition, 1974; 4th edition, 2008)[^o6][^o7] and his book on [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) (1st edition, 1967, published with the title *Lectures on Algebraic Topology*; revised edition published, with John R. Harper as co-author, in 1981 with the title *Algebraic Topology: A First Course*).[^o8][^o9][^o10]

Greenberg was also a passionate golfer and a founding member of the Shivas Irons Society.
