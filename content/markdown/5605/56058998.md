# Andrew Reid (writer)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew Reid"}

**Andrew Reid** (died c. 1767) was a Scottish writer.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Reid moved to [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), probably about 1720, and interested himself in literary and scientific subjects. In 1728, he launched *The Present State of the Republick of Letters*, a periodical which he edited until 1736, when it ceased; two volumes appeared each year.[^o3] An influence on the publication was the earlier editor [Michel de La Roche](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michel_de_La_Roche).[^o4]

Reid attended [James Thomson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Thomson_%28poet,_born_1700%29) in his last illness, in 1748.[^o5] It is presumed that he died in 1767.[^o3]

## Works {#Works}

In 1732, Reid published an abridgment of [Isaac Newton](wikipedia://en/14627)'s *[The Chronology of Ancient Kingdoms Amended](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Chronology_of_Ancient_Kingdoms_Amended)*, of which another edition appeared at Dublin in 1782. In 1733, in with [John Gray](wikipedia://en/56055774), he edited an *Abridgment* of the *[Philosophical Transactions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophical_Transactions)* from 1720 to 1732; it was published in two volumes.[^o3]

In 1747, Reid published a *Letter to Dr. Hales concerning the Nature of Tar*, and in 1767 an *Essay on Logarithms*, which he dedicated to his old friend John Gray. In the same year, he was employed by [George Lyttelton, 1st Baron Lyttelton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lyttelton,_1st_Baron_Lyttelton) to correct for the press the first two editions of his *History of the Life of Henry II*. The correction of the third edition, which appeared in 1768, was entrusted to another.[^o3] *Elements of the Theory and Practice of Chymistry* (appearing in 1775 as a third edition) was translation from a work by [Pierre Joseph Macquer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Joseph_Macquer).[^o7]
