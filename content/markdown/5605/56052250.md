# Max Noether's theorem on curves

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max Noether's theorem on curves"}

In algebraic geometry, **Max Noether's theorem** on curves is a theorem about curves lying on [algebraic surfaces](wikipedia://en/648311), which are [hypersurfaces](wikipedia://en/525101) in *P*<sup>3</sup>, or more generally [complete intersections](wikipedia://en/3116835). It states that, for degree at least four for hypersurfaces, the *[generic](wikipedia://en/12886758)* such surface has no curve on it apart from the [hyperplane section](wikipedia://en/3459767). In more modern language, the [Picard group](wikipedia://en/1850134) is [infinite cyclic](wikipedia://en/52327), other than for a short list of degrees. This is now often called the Noether-Lefschetz theorem.

:::
