# Avadhesh Narayan Singh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Avadhesh Narayan Singh"}

**Avadhesh Narayan Singh** (Benares, 1901 – July 10, 1954) was an Indian mathematician and [historian of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).

:::

Singh received a master's degree from [Banaras Hindu University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banaras_Hindu_University) in his hometown ([Varanasi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Varanasi) was then called Banaras or Benares) in 1924, where he was a student of [Ganesh Prasad](wikipedia://en/27759837). He received his DSc in mathematics from the [University of Calcutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calcutta) in 1929 for his dissertation titled "[Derivation](wikipedia://en/7921) and Non-Differentiable functions". After securing a DSc, Singh went to [Lucknow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucknow_University), where he became a Reader in 1940 and a professor in 1943. There he opened a Hindu Mathematics section and revived the nearly defunct Banaras Mathematical Society under the name of Bharata Ganita Parisad. In the 1930s he wrote a history of [Indian mathematics](wikipedia://en/1848052) with [Bibhutibhushan Datta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibhutibhushan_Datta), which became a standard work. As a mathematician, he dealt with non-differentiable functions (an example of an everywhere non-differentiable function is the [Weierstrass function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_function)).[^o2][^o3]

## Publications {#Publications}

Singh published about a dozen papers related to the history of Indian mathematics, and three dozen papers related to the non-differentiability of functions. He also published the following two books:[^o3]

- "The Theory and Construction of Non-Differentiable Functions", Lucknow University Studies No. I, 1935. <https://andrescaicedo.files.wordpress.com/2014/10/a-n-singh-the-theory-and-construction-of-non-differentiable-functions.pdf> (accessed on 2 August 2023).
- Bibhuti Bhushan Datta and Avadhesh Narayan Singh. (1935). "A History of Hindu Mathematics: A Source Book (Part I Numerical Notation and Arithmetic)". Motilal Banarsidass. <https://archive.org/details/in.gov.ignca.9325/mode/2up?view=theater>
- Bibhuti Bhushan Datta and Avadhesh Narayan Singh. (1938). "A History of Hindu Mathematics: A Source Book (Part II Algebra)". Motilal Banarsi Das. <https://archive.org/details/in.gov.ignca.7758/page/n5/mode/2up?view=theater>

Volume 3 of the "History of Hindu Mathematics" was edited by [Kripa Shankar Shukla](wikipedia://en/74505292) and published in several papers in the Indian Journal of History of Science (Vol. 5, 1980 to Vol. 28, 1993). These edited papers are available in the *Studies in Indian Mathematics and Astronomy (Selected Articles of Kripa Shankar Shukla)*.[^o7]
