# Modified Morlet wavelet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Modified Morlet wavelet"}

**Modified [Mexican hat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_hat_wavelet)**, **Modified Morlet** and **Dark soliton** or **Darklet wavelets** are derived from [hyperbolic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyperbolic_function) (sech) (bright soliton) and hyperbolic tangent (tanh) (dark soliton) pulses. These functions are derived intuitively from the solutions of the nonlinear [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) in the anomalous and normal dispersion regimes in a similar fashion to the way that the Morlet and the Mexican hat are derived. The modified Morlet is defined as: $\psi_2(t)=C_{\psi_2}\cos(\omega_0 t){\rm sech}(t)$

:::
