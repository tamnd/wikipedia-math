# Cocompact embedding

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cocompact embedding"}

In mathematics, **cocompact embeddings** are [embeddings](wikipedia://en/52119) of [normed vector spaces](wikipedia://en/21538) possessing a certain property similar to but weaker than [compactness](wikipedia://en/6746775). Cocompactness has been in use in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) since the 1980s, without being referred to by any name [^o2](Lemma 6),[^o3](Lemma 2.5),[^o4](Theorem 1), or by ad-hoc monikers such as *vanishing lemma* or *inverse embedding*.[^o5]

:::

Cocompactness property allows to verify convergence of sequences, based on translational or scaling invariance in the problem, and is usually considered in the context of [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964). The term *cocompact embedding* is inspired by the notion of [cocompact topological space](wikipedia://en/12746615).

::: {#o7 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let $G$ be a group of isometries on a normed vector space $X$. One says that a sequence $(x_k)\subset X$ converges to $x\in X$ $G$-weakly, if for every sequence $(g_k)\subset G$, the sequence $g_k(x_k-x)$ is weakly convergent to zero.

A [continuous embedding](wikipedia://en/7277255) of two normed vector spaces, $X\hookrightarrow Y$ is called *cocompact* relative to a group of isometries $G$ on $X$ if every $G$-weakly convergent sequence $(x_k)\subset X$ is convergent in $Y$.[^o8]

:::

## An elementary example: cocompactness for $\ell^\infty\hookrightarrow\ell^\infty$ {#An_elementary_example:_cocompactness_for_\ell^\infty\hookrightarrow\ell^\infty}

Embedding of the space $\ell^\infty(\mathbb Z)$ into itself is cocompact relative to the group $G$ of shifts $(x_n)\mapsto (x_{n-j}), j\in\mathbb Z$. Indeed, if $(x_n)^{(k)}$, $k=1,2,\dots$, is a sequence $G$-weakly convergent to zero, then $x_{n_k}^{(k)}\to 0$ for any choice of $n_k$. In particular one may choose $n_k$ such that $2|x_{n_k}^{(k)}|\ge \sup_n|x_n^{(k)}|=\|(x_n)^{(k)}\|_\infty$, which implies that $(x_{n})^{(k)}\to 0$ in $\ell^\infty$.

## Some known embeddings that are cocompact but not compact {#Some_known_embeddings_that_are_cocompact_but_not_compact}

- $\ell^p(\mathbb Z)\hookrightarrow \ell^q(\mathbb Z)$, $q< p$, relative to the action of translations on $\mathbb Z$:[^o11] $(x_n)\mapsto (x_{n-j}), j\in\mathbb Z$.
- $H^{1,p}(\mathbb R^N)\hookrightarrow L^q(\mathbb R^N)$, $p<q<\frac{pN}{N-p}$, $N>p$, relative to the actions of translations on $\mathbb R^N$.[^o2]
- $\dot H^{1,p}(\mathbb R^N)\hookrightarrow L^\frac{pN}{N-p}(\mathbb R^N)$, $N>p$, relative to the product group of actions of dilations and translations on $\mathbb R^N$.[^o3][^o4][^o11]
- Embeddings of Sobolev space in the [Moser–Trudinger case](wikipedia://en/2971303) into the corresponding [Orlicz space](wikipedia://en/17686010).[^o12]
- Embeddings of Besov and Triebel–Lizorkin spaces.[^o13]
- Embeddings of [Strichartz spaces](wikipedia://en/42682101).[^o5]
