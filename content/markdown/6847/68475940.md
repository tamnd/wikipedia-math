# Mahouton Norbert Hounkonnou

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mahouton Norbert Hounkonnou"}

**Mahouton Norbert Hounkonnou** is a Beninese [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor), [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist) and writer. He is professor of Mathematics and Physics at the [University of Abomey-Calavi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Abomey-Calavi), the president of the Benin [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences), Arts and Letters, and a member of the [African Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Academy_of_Sciences) and [the World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3]

:::
