# Leonard Blumenthal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leonard Blumenthal"}

**Leonard Mascot Blumenthal** (February 27, 1901 – August 1984) was an American mathematician.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Blumenthal was Jewish.[^o3] He received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1927 from [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University), under the supervision of [Frank Morley](wikipedia://en/589167); his [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) was titled *Lagrange Resolvents in Euclidean Geometry*.[^o6] He taught for the majority of his professional career at the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) and was the author of *A Modern View of Geometry*.[^o4]

He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 1933 to 1936.[^o5] According to the [Mathematics Genealogy Project](wikipedia://en/1152126), he had 18 Ph.D. students at Missouri, among them [Leroy Milton Kelly](wikipedia://en/25620406) and [William Arthur Kirk](wikipedia://en/11968842); he is the [academic ancestor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_genealogy) of over 80 mathematicians.[^o6]

## Legacy {#Legacy}

The Leonard M. Blumenthal Distinguished Professorship in Mathematics at the University of Missouri was established in 1992 in honor of Blumenthal. This [endowed chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Endowed_chair) is given on a five-year rotating basis to Missouri mathematics professors; the Blumenthal Professors at Missouri have included John Beem, Mark Ashbaugh, Alex Koldobsky, and Zhenbo Qin.[^o8] The American Mathematical Society also issued the [Blumenthal Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blumenthal_Award) in his honor.
