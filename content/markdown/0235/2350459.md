# Jeffrey H. Smith

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeffrey H. Smith"}

**Jeffrey Henderson Smith** is a former professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in [Lafayette, Indiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette,_Indiana). He received his Ph.D. from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1981, under the supervision of [Daniel Kan](wikipedia://en/2290155),[^o3] and was promoted to full professor at Purdue in 1999.[^o4] His primary research interest is [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801); his best-cited work[^o5] consists of two papers in the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)* on "nilpotence and stable homotopy".

:::
