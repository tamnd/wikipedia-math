# Dan Abramovich

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Abramovich"}

**Dan Abramovich** ('דן אברמוביץ; born March 12, 1963) is an Israeli-American mathematician working in the fields of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). As of 2019, he holds the title of L. Herbert Ballou University Professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), and he is an Elected Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Abramovich received a bachelor's degree at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in 1987 and completed a doctorate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1991 under [Joe Harris](wikipedia://en/3610251) (*Subvarieties of abelian varieties and of Jacobians of curves*). From 1991 to 1994 he was [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). Thereafter he held faculty positions at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) from 1994 to 1999 and since 2003 has been Professor at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University).[^o8]

## Career {#Career}

Among other topics, he has dealt with [birational geometry](wikipedia://en/382733), the [resolution of singularities](wikipedia://en/9914115), [subvarieties](wikipedia://en/1021753) of [abelian varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_varieties), limits for the [torsion](wikipedia://en/3039330) of [elliptic curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elliptic_curves), [rational](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_numbers) and [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) points on [algebraic varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_varieties) and [moduli spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moduli_spaces) of [vector bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_bundles) on [curves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_curves). Together with [Felipe Voloch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/José_Felipe_Voloch), in 1992 he succeeded in making progress toward proving the Mordell-Lang Conjecture in characteristic *p* (the full proof came later from [Ehud Hrushovski](wikipedia://en/3132981)). He has been a guest scholar at, among other institutions, the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn, the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) (MSRI), the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in Paris and at [IHES](wikipedia://en/14527). He was an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) ("Resolution of singularities of complex algebraic varieties and their families").[^o10] From 1996 to 1998 he was a [Sloan Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship). He is a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
