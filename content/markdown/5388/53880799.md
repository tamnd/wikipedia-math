# Nikolai Chernov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolai Chernov"}

**Nikolai Chernov** (November 10, 1956 – August 7, 2014) was a Ukrainian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Since 1994, he worked at [University of Alabama at Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alabama_at_Birmingham). In 2012, he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o2][^o3]
