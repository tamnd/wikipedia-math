# Valery Alexeev (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valery Alexeev"}

**Valery Alexeev** (born 1964)[^o2] is an American mathematician who is currently the David C. Barrow Professor at [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia), and an Elected Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3][^o4] He received his PhD from Lomonosov Moscow State University in 1990.[^o5]

:::
