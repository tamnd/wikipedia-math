# Ladder-DES

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ladder-DES"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **Ladder-DES** is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) designed in 1994 by Terry Ritter. It is a 4-round [Feistel cipher](wikipedia://en/352709) with a [block size](wikipedia://en/294065) of 128 bits, using [DES](wikipedia://en/7978) as the round function. It has no actual [key schedule](wikipedia://en/609603), so the total [key size](wikipedia://en/5749) is 4×56=224 bits.

:::

In 1997, [Eli Biham](wikipedia://en/348920) found two forms of [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) for Ladder-DES that depend on the [birthday paradox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birthday_paradox); the [key](wikipedia://en/53039) is deduced from the presence or absence of *collisions*, plaintexts that give equal intermediate values in the encryption process. He presented both a [chosen-plaintext attack](wikipedia://en/63852) and a [known-plaintext attack](wikipedia://en/517019); each uses about 2<sup>36</sup> plaintexts and 2<sup>90</sup> work, but the known-plaintext attack requires much more memory.
