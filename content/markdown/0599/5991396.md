# Cyclotomic unit

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cyclotomic unit"}

In mathematics, a **cyclotomic unit** (or **circular unit**) is a [unit](wikipedia://en/289666) of an [algebraic number field](wikipedia://en/28730822) which is the product of numbers of the form (ζ${}^{a}_{n}$ − 1) for ζ${}_{n}$ an *n*<sup>th</sup> [root of unity](wikipedia://en/171950) and 0 \< *a* \< *n*.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The cyclotomic units form a subgroup of finite [index](wikipedia://en/230581) in the [group of units](wikipedia://en/460227) of a [cyclotomic field](wikipedia://en/30872462). The index of this subgroup of *real* cyclotomic units (those cyclotomic units in the maximal real subfield) within the full real unit group is equal to the [class number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Class_number_%28number_theory%29) of the maximal real subfield of the [cyclotomic field](wikipedia://en/30872462).[^o4]

- If $n$ is the power of a prime, then $\zeta^{a}_{n} - 1$ is not a unit; however the numbers $(\zeta^{a}_{n} - 1)/(\zeta_{n} - 1)$ for $(a, n) = 1$, and ±ζ${}^{a}_{n}$ generate the group of cyclotomic units.
- If $n$ is a [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289) having two or more distinct prime factors, then $\zeta^{a}_{n} - 1$ is a unit. The subgroup of cyclotomic units generated by $(\zeta^{a}_{n} - 1)/(\zeta_{n} - 1)$ with $(a, n) = 1$ is not of finite index in general.[^o5]

The cyclotomic units satisfy *distribution relations*. Let $a$ be a rational number prime to $p$ and let $g_{a}$ denote $\exp(2\pi ia) - 1$. Then for $a \ne 0$ we have $\prod_{p b=a} g_b = g_a$.[^o6]

Using these distribution relations and the symmetry relation $\zeta^{a}_{n} - 1 = -\zeta^{a}_{n} (\zeta^{-a}_{n} - 1)$ a basis *B*<sub>*n*</sub> of the cyclotomic units can be constructed with the property that $B_{d} \subseteq B_{n}$ for $d | n$.[^o7]

:::
