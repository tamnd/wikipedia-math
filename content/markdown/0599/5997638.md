# Didier Dubois (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Didier Dubois"}

**Didier Dubois** (born 1952) is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Since 1999, he is a co-editor-in-chief of the journal *[Fuzzy Sets and Systems](wikipedia://en/20814025)*. In 1993–1997 he was vice-president and president of the International Fuzzy Systems Association. His research interests include [fuzzy set theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuzzy_set_theory), [possibility theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Possibility_theory), and [knowledge representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_representation). Most of his works are co-authored by [Henri Prade](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henri_Prade).

:::
