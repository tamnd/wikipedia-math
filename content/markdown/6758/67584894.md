# Constantin Corduneanu

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Constantin Corduneanu"}

**Constantin Corduneanu** (July 26, 1928 – December 26, 2018) was a Romanian-American mathematician and professor of mathematics at the [University of Texas at Arlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Arlington). In 2015, he was elected a titular member of the [Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Academy); he was a corresponding member of the Academy since 1974.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Corduneanu was born in [Potângeni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Potângeni), [Iași County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași_County), the son of Aglaea and Costache Corduneanu.[^o5] From 1940 to 1947, he attended military high school in [Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iași) and [Predeal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Predeal). In 1951, he graduated from the Faculty of Sciences of the [University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandru_Ioan_Cuza_University), Department of Mathematics.[^o8] His teachers there included [Alexander Myller](wikipedia://en/70713199), [Octav Mayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octav_Mayer), Mendel Haimovici, Dimitrie Mangeron, [Ilie Popa](wikipedia://en/70713128), Gheorghe Gheorghiev, Alexandru Climescu, Ion L. Creangă, and Adolf Haimovici.[^o9] Corduneanu received his Ph.D. in 1956 from the University of Iași with thesis *Probleme globale pentru ecuațiile diferențiale de ordin I și II* written under the supervision of Ilie Popa. The thesis defense the committee was composed of [Miron Nicolescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miron_Nicolescu), [Grigore Moisil](wikipedia://en/4000394), and Nicolae-Victor Teodorescu.[^o6]

From 1949 to 1977 he was a faculty member at the University of Iași, advancing in rank to full professor, and serving as Dean of the Faculty of Mathematics (1968–1972) and [pro-rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_academic_ranks) (1972–1973 and 1974–1977).[^o8] He also held faculty positions at the [Gheorghe Asachi Technical University of Iași](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gheorghe_Asachi_Technical_University_of_Iași) and at the [University of Suceava](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ștefan_cel_Mare_University_of_Suceava), where he served as [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) from 1966 to 1968.[^o8][^o4]

In 1977, Corduneanu decided to leave Romania. He first went to [Italy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italy), where he taught some courses at the [International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics) in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste).[^o4] In January 1978, he emigrated to the United States, and secured a teaching position at the [University of Rhode Island](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rhode_Island) (where he had been a visiting professor in 1967–1968 and 1973–1974). After spending a year as visiting professor at the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee) at [Knoxville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knoxville), he became a tenured professor in the fall of 1979 at the University of Texas at Arlington,[^o4] where he taught until his retirement in 1996.[^o8] He died at Medical City [Arlington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arlington,_Texas) in 2018, at age 90.[^o5]

A Festschrift in his honor, titled *Mathematical Analysis With Applications*, was published in 2018.
