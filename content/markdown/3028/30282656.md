# Siegfried Heinrich Aronhold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Siegfried Heinrich Aronhold"}

*Image: Siegfried Heinrich Aronhold.jpeg.* Siegfried Heinrich Aronhold

**Siegfried Heinrich Aronhold** (16 July 1819 – 13 March 1884) was a German mathematician who worked on invariant theory and introduced the [symbolic method](wikipedia://en/565742). He was born in [Angerburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angerburg), [East Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Prussia), and died, aged 64, in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin), Germany.
