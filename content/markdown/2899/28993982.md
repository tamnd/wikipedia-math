# Quantum algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quantum algebra"}

In mathematics, **quantum algebra** is the study of noncommutative analogues and generalizations of [commutative algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_algebra_%28structure%29), especially those arising in [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681).[^o2]. It is one of the top-level mathematics categories used by the [arXiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArXiv), and is a unification of algebraic deformations, [Hopf algebras](wikipedia://en/325714), [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917), and [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678) within [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

Subjects include:

- [Quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678)
- [Skein theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skein_theories)
- [Operadic algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Operadic_algebra)
- [Diagrammatic algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diagrammatic_algebra)
- [Quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267)
- [Racks and quandles](wikipedia://en/1140981)
