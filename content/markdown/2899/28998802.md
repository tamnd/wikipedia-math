In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rhombicuboctahedral prism** is a convex [uniform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_polychoron) [polychoron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polychoron) (four-dimensional [polytope](wikipedia://en/23471)).

:::

It is one of 18 convex [uniform polyhedral prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_polychoron#Polyhedral_hyperprisms) created by using uniform [prisms](wikipedia://en/283007) to connect pairs of [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905) or [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847) in parallel [hyperplanes](wikipedia://en/99862).

A related polychoron is the **runcic snub cubic hosochoron**, also known as a **parabidiminished [rectified tesseract](wikipedia://en/4023286)**, **truncated tetrahedral alterprism**, or **truncated tetrahedral cupoliprism**, s<sub>3</sub>{2,4,3}, . It is made from 2 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651), 6 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606), and 8 [triangular cupolae](wikipedia://en/1128465) in the gaps, for a total of 16 cells, 52 faces, 60 edges, and 24 vertices. It is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive), and equilateral, but not uniform, due to the cupolae. It has symmetry \[2<sup>\+</sup>,4,3\], order 48.[^o17][^o18]

It is related to the [16-cell](wikipedia://en/716422) in its s{2,4,3}, construction.

It can also be seen as a prismatic polytope with two parallel truncated tetrahedra in dual positions, as seen in the [compound of two truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/14788670). Triangular cupolae connect the triangular and hexagonal faces, and the tetrahedral connect edge-wise between.
