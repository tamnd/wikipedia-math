# Albert Schwarz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albert Schwarz"}

Solomonovich **Albert Solomonovich Schwarz**[^o2] ( ; Альберт Соломонович Шварц; born June 24, 1934) is a Soviet-American mathematician and a theoretical physicist educated in the Soviet Union and now a professor at the [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Schwarz was born in [Kazan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazan) to [Ashkenazi Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ashkenazi_Jewish) parents, [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union). His parents were arrested in the [Stalinist purges](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalinist_purges) in 1937.[^o4]

Schwarz studied under [Vadim Yefremovich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vadim_Yefremovich) at Ivanovo Pedagogical Institute, having been denied admittance to [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) on the grounds that he was the son of "[enemies of the people](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Enemies_of_the_people)."[^o5]

## Career and later life {#Career_and_later_life}

After defending his dissertation in 1958, he took a job at Voronezh University. In 1964 he was offered a job at [Moscow Engineering Physics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Engineering_Physics_Institute).[^o7] He immigrated to the United States in 1989.[^o8]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Schwarz is one of the pioneers of [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560) and brought up the first example of a [topological quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/443757).[^o10] The Schwarz genus, one of the fundamental notions of [topological complexity](wikipedia://en/36365888), is named after him.[^o11] Schwarz worked on some examples in [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917). He is the "S" in the [AKSZ model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AKSZ_theory) (named after Mikhail Alexandrov, [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509), Schwarz, and Oleg Zaboronski).[^o12]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1990, Schwarz was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Kyoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto). He was elected to the 2018 class of [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]
