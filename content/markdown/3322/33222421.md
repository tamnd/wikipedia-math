# Richard Schelp

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Schelp"}

**Richard H. Schelp** (April 21, 1936, [Kansas City, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri), United States – November 29, 2010, [Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memphis,_Tennessee), [Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tennessee), USA) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2][^o3]

:::

Schelp received his bachelor's degree in mathematics and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) from the [University of Central Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Missouri) and his master's degree and doctorate in mathematics from [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University). The adviser from his thesis was [Richard Joseph Greechie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Joseph_Greechie).[^o4] He was an associate mathematician and missile scientist at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) for five years. He then became an instructor of mathematics at [Kansas State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_State_University) for four years. Finally in 1970 he became a professor of mathematics in the Department of Mathematical Sciences at the [University of Memphis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Memphis). He retired in 2001.

Schelp, an [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) one mathematician, was the fourth most frequent scholarly collaborator with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284).[^o5] He also collaborated on research with another top ten most frequent Erdős collaborator, [Ralph Faudree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ralph_Faudree), who was based at the University of Memphis as well.[^o5][^o7]
