# Christoffer Dybvad

*Image: Christoffer Dybvad Decarithmia 01.jpeg.* "Decarithmia. Ded er Thinde-Regnskab" (1602)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christoffer Dybvad"}

**Christoffer Dybvad** (1578–1622) was a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in [Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen), the son of Professor [Jørgen Dybvad](wikipedia://en/33343462).[^o2]

:::

He adapted [Simon Stevin](wikipedia://en/72586)'s *De Thiende* into [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_language).[^o3]
