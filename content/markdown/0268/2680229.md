# Joseph Saurin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Saurin"}

**Joseph Saurin** (1 September 1659, in [Courthézon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courthézon) – 29 December 1737, in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989)) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a converted Protestant minister. He was the first to show how the [tangents](wikipedia://en/31482) at the multiple points of [curves](wikipedia://en/89246) could be determined by [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). He was accused in 1712 by [Jean-Baptiste Rousseau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Baptiste_Rousseau) of being the actual author of defamatory verses that gossip had attributed to Rousseau.

:::

He was the father of [Bernard-Joseph Saurin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernard-Joseph_Saurin).
