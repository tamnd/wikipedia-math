# Poincaré series (modular form)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Poincaré series"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **Poincaré series** is a [mathematical series](wikipedia://en/15287) generalizing the classical [theta series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theta_series) that is associated to any [discrete group](wikipedia://en/468536) of symmetries of a [complex domain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_domain), possibly of [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables). In particular, they generalize classical [Eisenstein series](wikipedia://en/1270458). They are named after [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740).

:::

If Γ is a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) acting on a domain *D* and *H*(*z*) is any [meromorphic function](wikipedia://en/80857) on *D*, then one obtains an [automorphic function](wikipedia://en/974148) by averaging over Γ:

$$\sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma} H(\gamma(z)).$$

However, if Γ is an infinite [discrete group](wikipedia://en/468536), then additional factors must be introduced in order to assure convergence of such a series. To this end, a **Poincaré series** is a series of the form

$$\theta_k(z) = \sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma^*} (J_\gamma(z))^k H(\gamma(z))$$

where *J*<sub>γ</sub> is the [Jacobian determinant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobian_determinant) of the group element γ,[^o2] and the asterisk denotes that the summation takes place only over coset representatives yielding distinct terms in the series.

The classical **Poincaré series** of weight 2*k* of a [Fuchsian group](wikipedia://en/471365) Γ is defined by the series

$$\theta_k(z) = \sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma^*} (cz+d)^{-2k}H\left(\frac{az+b}{cz+d}\right)$$

the summation extending over congruence classes of fractional linear transformations

$$\gamma=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$$

belonging to Γ. Choosing *H* to be a [character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Character_%28group_theory%29) of the [cyclic group](wikipedia://en/52327) of order *n*, one obtains the so-called Poincaré series of order *n*:

$$\theta_{k,n}(z) = \sum_{\gamma\in\Gamma^*} (cz+d)^{-2k}\exp\left(2\pi i n\frac{az+b}{cz+d}\right)$$

The latter Poincaré series converges absolutely and uniformly on compact sets (in the upper halfplane), and is a [modular form](wikipedia://en/286000) of weight 2*k* for Γ. Note that, when Γ is the full [modular group](wikipedia://en/325019) and *n* = 0, one obtains the Eisenstein series of weight 2*k*. In general, the Poincaré series is, for *n* ≥ 1, a [cusp form](wikipedia://en/610165).
