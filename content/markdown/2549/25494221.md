# Ronald Cramer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ronald Cramer"}

**Ronald John Fitzgerald Cramer** (born 3 February 1968 in [Haarlem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haarlem))[^o2] is a professor at the [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063) (CWI) in Amsterdam and the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leiden). He obtained his PhD from the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) in 1997. Prior to returning to the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands) he was at the [University of Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aarhus).

:::

He is best known for his work with [Victor Shoup](wikipedia://en/3978418) on chosen ciphertext secure encryption in the standard model, in particular the [Cramer–Shoup encryption scheme](wikipedia://en/1564406).[^o3]

Cramer became a member of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2013.[^o4] He is member of the advisory board of the German Center for Advanced Security Research Darmstadt [CASED](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CASED).
