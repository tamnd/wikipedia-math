# Parabolic line

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parabolic line"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), a smooth [surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_%28differential_geometry%29) in three dimensions has a **parabolic point** when the [Gaussian curvature](wikipedia://en/285623) is zero. Typically such points lie on a curve called the **parabolic line** which separates the surface into regions of positive and negative Gaussian curvature.

:::

Points on the parabolic line give rise to folds on the [Gauss map](wikipedia://en/378881): where a [ridge](wikipedia://en/18896093) crosses a parabolic line there is a cusp of the Gauss map.[^o2]
