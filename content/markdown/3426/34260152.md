# Neat submanifold

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Neat submanifold"}

In [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), an area of mathematics, a **neat submanifold** of a [manifold with boundary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manifold_with_boundary) is a kind of "well-behaved" [submanifold](wikipedia://en/525095).

:::

To define this more precisely, first let

$M$ be a manifold with boundary, and

$A$ be a submanifold of $M$.

Then $A$ is said to be a neat submanifold of $M$ if it meets the following two conditions:[^o2]

- The boundary of $A$ is a subset of the boundary of $M$. That is, $\partial A \subset \partial M$.
- Each point of $A$ has a neighborhood within which $A$'s embedding in $M$ is equivalent to the embedding of a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) in a higher-dimensional Euclidean space.

More formally, $A$ must be [covered](wikipedia://en/317552) by [charts](wikipedia://en/2073470#Charts) $(U, \phi)$ of $M$ such that $A \cap U = \phi^{-1}(\mathbb{R}^m)$ where $m$ is the dimension of $A$. For instance, in the category of [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold), this means that the embedding of $A$ must also be smooth.
