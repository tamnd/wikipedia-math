# Locally simply connected space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Locally simply connected space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **locally simply connected space** is a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) that admits a [basis](wikipedia://en/59220) of [simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) sets.

:::

*Image: Hawaiian earrings.svg.* The Hawaiian earring is not locally simply connected

The [circle](wikipedia://en/6220) is an example of a locally simply connected space which is not simply connected. The [Hawaiian earring](wikipedia://en/910263) is a space which is neither locally simply connected nor simply connected. The [cone](wikipedia://en/782162) on the Hawaiian earring is [contractible](wikipedia://en/1752414) and therefore simply connected, but still not locally simply connected.

All [topological manifolds](wikipedia://en/2119193) and [CW complexes](wikipedia://en/353022) are locally simply connected. In fact, these satisfy the much stronger property of being [locally contractible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_contractible_space).

A strictly weaker condition is that of being [semi-locally simply connected](wikipedia://en/518118). Both locally simply connected spaces and simply connected spaces are semi-locally simply connected, but neither converse holds.
