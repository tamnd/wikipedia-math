# Rowan Yeats

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rowan Yeats"}

**Rowan Yeats** (born 1980) is a Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) whose research connects [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) to [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267). He holds the [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in Combinatorics in Quantum Field Theory at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Yeats is from [Halifax, Nova Scotia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halifax,_Nova_Scotia). As an undergraduate at the University of Waterloo, he won an honourable mention for the 2003 [Morgan Prize](wikipedia://en/4180157) for his research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), the theory of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), and [non-standard models of arithmetic](wikipedia://en/6251420). He graduated in 2003, and went to [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University) for graduate school, where he completed his Ph.D. in 2008. His dissertation, *Growth Estimates for Dyson-Schwinger Equations*, was supervised by [Dirk Kreimer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dirk_Kreimer). In 2016, Yeats was awarded a [Humboldt Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation) to visit Kreimer at the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin).

Yeats is the author of the books *Rearranging Dyson–Schwinger Equations* (Memoirs of the American Mathematical Society, 2011) and *A Combinatorial Perspective on Quantum Field Theory* (Springer, 2017).
