# Susanna Terracini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Susanna Terracini"}

*Image: Susanna Terracini.jpg.* Terracini in 2020 or earlier

**Susanna Terracini** (; born 29 April 1963) is an Italian mathematician known for her research on [chaos](wikipedia://en/6295) in [Hamiltonian dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/1197531), including the [*n*-body problem](wikipedia://en/23859945), [reaction–diffusion systems](wikipedia://en/8635379), and the [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Terracini was born in [South London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_London) to a distant cousin of Italian [anti-fascist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-fascist) politician [Umberto Terracini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Umberto_Terracini). She earned a *[laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea)* in 1986 in mathematics at the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin), supervised by Fulvia Skof. She completed her Ph.D. at the [International School for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_School_for_Advanced_Studies) in 1990. Her dissertation, *Periodic Solutions to Singular Newtonian Systems*, was supervised by [Ivar Ekeland](wikipedia://en/31377247) and Sergio Solimini.

She was a researcher at [Paris Dauphine University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Dauphine_University) from 1988 to 1989, and became a faculty member at the [Polytechnic University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_University_of_Milan) in 1990. In 2001 she became a full professor at the [University of Milano-Bicocca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milano-Bicocca), and in 2012 she returned to Turin as a professor. Terracini is a member of the Scientific Advisory Board of the Barcelona [Centre de Recerca Matemàtica](wikipedia://en/21193525).

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

One of Terracini's papers on the [*n*-body problem](wikipedia://en/23859945) was selected for a featured review in *[Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646)*. She was the winner of the 2002 [Vinti Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinti_Prize), a prize of the [Italian Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/11969092) for young researchers in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). In 2007 she won the Bruno Finzi Prize of the [Istituto Lombardo Accademia di Scienze e Lettere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istituto_Lombardo_Accademia_di_Scienze_e_Lettere). In 2020 she was awarded the Schauder Medal from the Juliusz P. Schauder Center for Nonlinear Studies at the [Nicolaus Copernicus University in Toruń](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus_University_in_Toruń), Poland.

## Positions on other issues {#Positions_on_other_issues}

In early 2024, amid [Israel's war on Gaza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war), Terracini was the only member of the [academic senate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_senate) at Turin to vote against [severing ties with Israeli institutions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_boycott_of_Israel), declaring herself opposed to academic boycotts by principle and stating dialogue should always be favoured; she had previously opposed the suspension of agreements with Russian universities during the [war on Ukraine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russo-Ukrainian_war_%282022–present%29). She later criticized [student protesters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaza_war_protests_at_universities) for their vocal [anti-Zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-Zionist) stance which, according to her, was due to the blind adoption of a [Hamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamas) narrative.
