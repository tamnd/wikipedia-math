# Andrée Ehresmann

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrée Ehresmann"}

**Andrée Ehresmann** ([née](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birth_name) Bastiani) (9 July 1935 – 21 August 2026) was a French mathematician who specialised in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Ehresmann obtained her *[baccalauréat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baccalauréat)* in 1952; subsequently, she studied in Marseille and Paris.[^o5]

She was a researcher at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) from 1957 to 1963. She was awarded a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1962 at [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) under the supervision of [Gustave Choquet](wikipedia://en/17301193). Her thesis was entitled *Différentiabilité dans les espaces localement convexes. Distructures* \[Differentiability in locally convex spaces. Distructures\].[^o6]

In 1967 she became a professor at [IRCAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IRCAM), at the [University of Picardie Jules Verne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Picardie_Jules_Verne); after she retired, she became [emerita professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus_professor).[^o7]

Ehresmann died in [Amiens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amiens) on 21 August 2026, at the age of 91.[^o8][^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Ehresmann published over 100 works on Analysis (Differential Calculus and Infinite-dimensional Distributions, Guiding Systems and Optimization Problems), Category Theory (with her husband, [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108): sketches and internal categories, multiple categories, closed monoidal structures) and the modeling of complex autonomous systems (Memory Scalable Systems, including the MENS model for neuro-cognitive systems).

She developed, with Jean-Paul Vanbremeersch a model of Memory Evolutive Systems, which proposes a mathematical model for 'living' systems with a hierarchy of complex components with multiple temporalities, such as biological, neuro-cognitive, or social systems. Based on a theory of 'dynamic' categories, evolving memory systems can analyze complexity, emergence and self-organization.

Ehresmann was the director of the mathematical journal [Cahiers de Topologie et Géométrie Différentielle Catégoriques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cahiers_de_Topologie_et_Géométrie_Différentielle_Catégoriques).[^o10]
