# Yoshiro Mori (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yoshiro Mori"}

**Yoshiro Mori** is a Japanese mathematician working on commutative algebra who introduced the [Mori–Nagata theorem](wikipedia://en/35957110) and whose work led to [Mori domains](wikipedia://en/35957282) in the 1970s.

:::
