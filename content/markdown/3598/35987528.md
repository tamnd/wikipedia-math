# Distribution algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Distribution algebra"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the **distribution algebra** $D(G, K)$ of a [*p*-adic Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_Lie_group) *G* is the *K*-algebra of *K*-valued distributions on *G*. (See the reference for a more precise definition.)

:::
