# Takeshi Saito (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takeshi Saito"}

**Takeshi Saito** (斎藤 毅 Saitō Takeshi, born 11 September 1961) is a Japanese mathematician, specializing in some areas of [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).[^o2] His thesis advisor was [Kazuya Kato](wikipedia://en/4616818).

:::

Saito was an invited speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2010.[^o4]
