# Gustav Ferdinand Mehler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gustav Ferdinand Mehler"}

*Image: Das Fotoalbum für Weierstraß 024 (Gustav Ferdinand Mehler).jpg.* Gustav Ferdinand Mehler

**Gustav Ferdinand Mehler**, or **Ferdinand Gustav Mehler** (13 December 1835 in [Schönlanke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schönlanke), [Kingdom of Prussia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Prussia) – 13 July 1895 in [Elbing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elbing), [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire)) was a German mathematician.

:::

He is credited with introducing [Mehler's formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehler's_formula); the [Mehler–Fock transform](wikipedia://en/32798661); the [Mehler–Heine formula](wikipedia://en/32798823); and [Mehler functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mehler_function) ([conical functions](wikipedia://en/5842560)), in connection with his utilization of [Zonal spherical functions](wikipedia://en/17708915) in Electromagnetic theory.
