# Stefanie Petermichl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stefanie Petermichl"}

*Image: Stefanie Petermichl Oberwolfach 2025.jpg.* Petermichl at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2025

**Stefanie Petermichl** (born 1971) is a German [mathematical analyst](wikipedia://en/48396) who works as a professor at the [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse), in France.[^o2] Topics of her research include [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [several complex variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Several_complex_variables), [stochastic control](wikipedia://en/17058216), and [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Petermichl studied at the [Karlsruhe Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karlsruhe_Institute_of_Technology),[^o2] and then did her graduate studies at [Michigan State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_State_University), completing her Ph.D. in 2000 under the supervision of [Alexander Volberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Volberg).[^o2] After postdoctoral studies at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), she joined the faculty of the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin) in 2005. She moved to the [University of Bordeaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux) in 2007, and again to Toulouse in 2009.[^o2] Since 2019, she holds the Humboldt chair at the [University of Würzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Würzburg).[^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Petermichl won the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize) for 2006 "for her work on several crucial impacts to the theory of vector valued singular operators".[^o7] She was the first woman to win that prize.[^o8] In 2012, the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) gave her their Ernest Déchelle Prize.[^o9] She became a member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France) in 2013.[^o10] She is an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), speaking in the section on Analysis and Operator Algebras.[^o11] In 2016, she was awarded a [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) (ERC) grant.[^o12]
