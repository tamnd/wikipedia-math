# Svitlana Mayboroda

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Svitlana Mayboroda"}

**Svitlana Mayboroda** (Світлана Майборода; born 1981) is a Ukrainian mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota)[^o2] and [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich),[^o3] and is a Distinguished Visiting Professor at the [Institute for Advanced Study](https://www.ias.edu/).[^o4]

:::

## Research {#Research}

Mayboroda's research concerns [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) geometric measure theory and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), including [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779) for [elliptic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/662787).[^o11] In particular, her work has provided a new mathematical approach to [Anderson localization](wikipedia://en/2213942), a phenomenon in physics in which waves are confined to a local region rather than propagating throughout a medium, and has enjoyed wide ranging applications in condensed matter physics, cold atoms systems, research and engineering of organic and nonorganic semiconductor materials and devices.[^o6][^o7]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mayboroda was born on June 2, 1981, in [Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kharkiv). She earned the Ukrainian equivalent of two master's degrees, one in finance and one in applied mathematics, from the [University of Kharkiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kharkiv) in 2001, and completed her Ph.D. in 2005 from the [University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Missouri) under the supervision of Marius Mitrea.[^o2] After visiting positions at the [Australian National University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australian_National_University), [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), and [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), she joined the [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) faculty in 2008, and moved to the University of Minnesota in 2011.[^o2] In 2023, she joined the ETH Zurich faculty.[^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Mayboroda was a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) for 2010–2015.[^o2] In 2013, she became the inaugural winner of the [Sadosky Research Prize in Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sadosky_Prize) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o11] In 2015 she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12] In 2016, she was awarded the first Northrop Professorship at the University of Minnesota.[^o13] and in 2020 she became the McKnight Presidential Professor.[^o14] She was an invited speaker at the 2018 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), speaking in the section on Analysis and Operator Algebras.[^o15] She became the 2023 National Blavatnik Laureate in Physical Sciences & Engineering, given to an outstanding scientist under the age of 42.[^o16] That same year, she was also elected to the European Academy of Sciences,[^o17] and, together with Marcel Filoche, was awarded the Elias M. Stein Prize for New Perspectives in Analysis[^o18] by the [American Mathematical Society, AMS](https://www.ams.org/home/page). In 2025, Mayboroda was elected to the [German National Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_National_Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) for her contributions to mathematical analysis and partial differential equations.[^o19] She is also an elected member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea), recognizing her distinguished scholarship in mathematics .[^o20] In the same year, she received the [Blaise Pascal Medal in Mathematics](https://math.ethz.ch/news-and-events/news/d-math-news/2025/07/svitlana-mayboroda-honoured-with-blaise-pascal-medal.html) from the [European Academy of Sciences (EurASc)](https://www.eurasc.eu/), awarded for outstanding contributions to the theory of elliptic partial differential equations and spectral theory.[^o21][^o22] Svitlana Mayboroda is the Director of the Simons Collaboration on [Localisation of waves](https://cse.umn.edu/wave) and the [Simons Initiative on Geometry of Flows](https://www.ias.edu/simonsgeometry) .[^o23][^o24][^o25]
