# Bill Casselman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bill Casselman"}

**William Allen Casselman** (born November 27, 1941) is an [American Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Canadian) mathematician who works in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms). He is a professor emeritus at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia).[^o3] He is closely connected to the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905) and has been involved in posting all of the work of [Robert Langlands](wikipedia://en/25986) on the internet.[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Casselman did his undergraduate work at [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College) where his advisor was [Raoul Bott](wikipedia://en/337457) and received his PhD from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1966 where his advisor was [Goro Shimura](wikipedia://en/174724). He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in 1974, 1983, and 2001.[^o6] He emigrated to Canada in 1971 and is a professor emeritus in mathematics at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia).[^o3]

## Research {#Research}

Casselman specializes in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [automorphic forms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_forms), [geometric combinatorics](wikipedia://en/20898181), and the structure of [algebraic groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_groups). He has an interest in mathematical graphics[^o8] and has been the graphics editor of the *Notices of the American Mathematical Society* since January, 2001.[^o9]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]
