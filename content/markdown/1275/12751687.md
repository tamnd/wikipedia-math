# God Created the Integers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="God Created the Integers"}

***God Created the Integers: The Mathematical Breakthroughs That Changed History*** is a 2005 [anthology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthology), edited by [Stephen Hawking](wikipedia://en/19376148), of "excerpts from thirty-one of the most important works in the history of mathematics."[^o3]

:::

Each chapter of the work focuses on a different [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and begins with a biographical overview. Within each chapter, Hawking examines the mathematician's key discoveries, presents formal proofs of significant results, and explains their impact on the development of the mathematical field.

The title of the book is a reference to a quotation attributed to mathematician [Leopold Kronecker](wikipedia://en/318450), who once wrote that "God made the [integers](wikipedia://en/14563); all else is the work of man."[^o4]
