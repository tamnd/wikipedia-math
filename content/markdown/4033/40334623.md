# Grace–Walsh–Szegő theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grace–Walsh–Szegő theorem"}

In mathematics, the **Grace–Walsh–Szegő coincidence theorem**[^o2][^o3] is a result named after [John Hilton Grace](wikipedia://en/13051535), [Joseph L. Walsh](wikipedia://en/14002828), and [Gábor Szegő](wikipedia://en/46065).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose *ƒ*(*z*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *z*<sub>*n*</sub>) is a [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) with [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) coefficients, and that it is

- symmetric, i.e. invariant under [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of the variables, and
- multi-affine, i.e. [affine](wikipedia://en/38449) in each variable separately.

Let *A* be a circular region in the complex plane. If either *A* is [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) or the degree of *ƒ* is *n*, then for every $\zeta_1,\ldots,\zeta_n\in A$ there exists $\zeta\in A$ such that

$$f(\zeta_1,\ldots,\zeta_n) = f(\zeta,\ldots,\zeta).$$

:::
