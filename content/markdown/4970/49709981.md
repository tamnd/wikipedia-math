# Raymond Ogden

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Raymond Ogden"}

**Raymond William Ogden** (born 19 September 1943) is a British [applied](wikipedia://en/24295969) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is the [George Sinclair Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_of_Mathematics_%28Glasgow%29) at the Department of Mathematics and Statistics of the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow).[^o8]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Ogden earned his BA and PhD degrees from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1970, under the supervision of [Rodney Hill](wikipedia://en/20573612). His thesis was entitled *On Constitutive Relations for Elastic and Plastic Materials*.[^o3]

## Work {#Work}

Ogden's research has been focused on the [nonlinear](wikipedia://en/146103) theory of [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29) and its applications. His theoretical contributions include the derivation of exact solutions of nonlinear [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779), for both [compressible and incompressible](wikipedia://en/166084) materials, and an analysis of the linear and nonlinear [stability](wikipedia://en/5493795) of pre-stressed bodies and related studies of [elastic](wikipedia://en/301429) wave propagation.[^o8]

In the field of applications, Ogden worked on modelling the elastic and inelastic behaviour of rubber-like solids. He has also made contributions to the [biomechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomechanics) of [soft biological tissues](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soft_tissue), the [electroelasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electroelasticity) and [magnetoelasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetoelasticity) of electromechanically sensitive [elastomeric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elastomer) materials, and the effects on [residual stress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residual_stress) in materials that are capable of large elastic deformations.[^o8]

His book, *Non-Linear Elastic Deformations*, published in 1984 and reissued in 1999, has become a standard reference in this branch of [solid mechanics](wikipedia://en/458866).[^o8]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

Ogden was awarded the [ASME](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_of_Mechanical_Engineers) [Koiter Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Koiter_Medal) in 2005 and the [Prager Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prager_Medal) of the [Society of Engineering Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_of_Engineering_Science) in 2010. He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society (FRS) in 2006](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Fellows_of_the_Royal_Society_elected_in_2006).[^o8]

In 2016, the [American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ASME) awarded Ogden the [Timoshenko Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Timoshenko_Medal).[^o9]
