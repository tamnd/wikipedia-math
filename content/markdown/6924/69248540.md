# Michela Varagnolo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michela Varagnolo"}

**Michela Varagnolo** is a mathematician whose research topics have included [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [Hecke algebra](wikipedia://en/55907189), [Schur–Weyl duality](wikipedia://en/15291970), [Yangians](wikipedia://en/15272567), and [quantum affine algebras](wikipedia://en/25666636). She earned a doctorate in 1993 at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa), under the supervision of [Corrado de Concini](wikipedia://en/35219521), and is [maître de conférences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maître_de_conférences) in the department of mathematics at [CY Cergy Paris University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CY_Cergy_Paris_University), affiliated there with the research laboratory on analysis, geometry, and modeling.

:::

Varagnolo was an invited speaker at the 2014 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842). In 2019, with Éric Vasserot, she won the [Prix de l'État](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_de_l'État) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) for their work on the geometric representation theory of Hecke algebras and quantum groups.
