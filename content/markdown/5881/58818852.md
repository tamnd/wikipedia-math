# Yomtov Garti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yomtov Garti"}

**Yomtov Bonjour Garti** (2 September 1915 – 21 February 2011) was a Turkish mathematician and a teacher of mathematics, physics and cosmography in Istanbul, Turkey.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Yomtov Garti was born in [Kadıköy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kadıköy), at the Asian part of [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul) ([Ottoman Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ottoman_Empire), later to become [Turkey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turkey)). His father Maer Garti was a veterinarian doctor, who died in the typhus epidemics during the World War I, while serving in the Ottoman Army. Yomtov Garti was educated at the French high school [Lycée Saint-Joseph, Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Saint-Joseph,_Istanbul) . After graduating from the Department of Mathematics and Physics of [Istanbul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul_University), he was approached by the famous mathematician [Richard Edler von Mises](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Edler_von_Mises), who was then located in Istanbul[^o3] and proposed him a PhD position. Garti's PhD was on statistics and probability theory tutored by Richard Edler von Mises and [William Prager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Prager).[^o4]

Yomtov Garti received his PhD in 1939, as the first PhD student of von Mises and third PhD of Turkey. He later published his findings in an article (Garti, 1940),[^o5] which is a generalization of initial distributions to n dimensions published in[^o6] (cited in[^o7]). Garti served as an assistant for a summer to Harry Dember, a professor in the Institute of Applied Physics in Istanbul. After receiving his doctoral degree in 1939, he started to teach at famous high schools in Istanbul. In 1954, he presented an article to Richard von Mises in a book published in his honor.[^o8] Yomtov Garti continued teaching until age 92. He died, at the age of 96 in 2011. All main newspapers of Turkey announced the loss of “The teacher of teachers”. At his funeral, the several generations of students overfilled the [Hemdat Israel Synagogue at Kadıköy, Istanbul.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hemdat_Israel_Synagogue)

## Career {#Career}

Yomtov Garti became a renowned mathematics teacher in Istanbul. He thought mathematics and physics to thousands of students over six decades, in several famous schools, namely [Galatasary High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galatasaray_High_School) ([Galatasaray Lisesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galatasaray_Lisesi)), [Haydarpaşa High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haydarpaşa_High_School), [Lycee Saint-Joseph Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Saint-Joseph,_Istanbul), and [Notre Dame de Sion Lisesi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Notre_Dame_de_Sion_Istanbul). He also thought in Musevi Lisesi and [Boğaziçi Üniversity.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boğaziçi_University) Yomtov Garti was rewarded the title of “Chevalier” of “[Ordre des Palmes Academiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_des_Palmes_Academiques)” by the French Government in recognition of his educational work in French schools in Turkey.
