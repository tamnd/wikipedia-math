# List of Norwegian mathematicians

List of mathematicians

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of Norwegian mathematicians"}

A [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) is a scholar in the fields of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). They solve and research [mathematical problems](wikipedia://en/256700) which can be applied in real life or completely abstract (pure). This article covers notable **mathematicians from [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway)**.

:::

A pioneer of modern mathematics,[^o2] [Niels Henrik Abel](wikipedia://en/21573) contributed greatly towards various fields of mathematics[^o3] during his short life. He died in 1829, aged 26, from [tuberculosis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuberculosis).[^o3] German mathematician [Felix Klein](wikipedia://en/41887) spoke of his reluctance "to part from this ideal type of researcher".[^o4] In 2001, the [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285) was established in his honour.[^o5]

Other notable mathematicians include (in alphabetical order) [Carl Anton Bjerknes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Anton_Bjerknes), [Vilhelm Bjerknes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilhelm_Bjerknes), [Bernt Michael Holmboe](wikipedia://en/20142485), who is known for being Abel's teacher and tutor,[^o6] [Sophus Lie](wikipedia://en/54010), [Idun Reiten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idun_Reiten), [Atle Selberg](wikipedia://en/2021), [Thoralf Skolem](wikipedia://en/453765) and [Carl Størmer](wikipedia://en/1906523).
