# International Congress of Actuaries

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="International Congress of Actuaries"}

The **International Congress of Actuaries** (**ICA**) is a conference held under the auspices of the [International Actuarial Association](wikipedia://en/228150) every three or four years. The most recent conference was the 33rd Congress held in [Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydney), Australia in 2023. Future congresses are planned for [Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo), Japan in 2026, and [Nairobi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nairobi), [Kenya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenya) in 2029.

:::
