# Hirzebruch signature theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hirzebruch signature theorem"}

In [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), an area of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Hirzebruch signature theorem**[^o10] (sometimes called the Hirzebruch index theorem) is [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495)'s 1954 result expressing the [signature](wikipedia://en/1152311) of a smooth closed oriented manifold by a linear combination of [Pontryagin numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontryagin_number) called the [L-genus](wikipedia://en/5574857#L_genus). It was used in the proof of the [Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/2892412).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

The L-genus is the [genus for the multiplicative sequence of polynomials](wikipedia://en/5574857) associated to the characteristic power series

$${x\over \tanh(x)} = \sum_{k\ge 0} {{2^{2k}B_{2k}\over (2k)!}x^{2k}} = 1 + {x^2 \over 3} - {x^4 \over 45} +\cdots .$$

The first two of the resulting L-polynomials are:

- $$L_1 = \tfrac13 p_1$$
- $$L_2 = \tfrac1{45}(7p_2 - p_1^2)$$

(for further *L*-polynomials see [^o4] or ).

By taking for the $p_i$ the Pontryagin classes $p_i(M)$ of the tangent bundle of a 4*n* dimensional smooth closed oriented manifold M one obtains the L-classes of M. Hirzebruch showed that the n-th L-class of M evaluated on the [fundamental class](wikipedia://en/669440) of M, $[M]$, is equal to $\sigma(M)$, the signature of M (i.e. the signature of the intersection form on the 2*n*th cohomology group of M):

$$\sigma(M) = \langle L_n(p_1(M), \dots, p_n(M)), [M]\rangle.$$

:::

::: {#o6 .proof cue=named-heading name="Sketch of proof of the signature theorem"}

## Sketch of proof of the signature theorem {#Sketch_of_proof_of_the_signature_theorem}

[René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633) had earlier proved that the signature was given by some linear combination of [Pontryagin numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontryagin_number), and Hirzebruch found the exact formula for this linear combination by introducing the notion of the genus of a multiplicative sequence.

Since the rational [oriented cobordism ring](wikipedia://en/391832#Oriented_cobordism) $\Omega_*^{\text{SO}}\otimes \mathbb{Q}$ is equal to

$$\Omega_*^{\text{SO}}\otimes \mathbb{Q} =\mathbb{Q} [\mathbb{P}^{2}(\mathbb{C}), \mathbb{P}^{4}(\mathbb{C}), \ldots ],$$

the polynomial algebra generated by the oriented cobordism classes $[\mathbb{P}^{2i}(\mathbb{C})]$ of the even dimensional [complex projective spaces](wikipedia://en/762977), it is enough to verify that

$$\sigma(\mathbb{P}^{2i})= 1 = \langle L_i(p_1(\mathbb{P}^{2i}), \ldots, p_n(\mathbb{P}^{2i})), [\mathbb{P}^{2i}]\rangle$$

for all i.

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Generalizations"}

## Generalizations {#Generalizations}

The signature theorem is a special case of the [Atiyah–Singer index theorem](wikipedia://en/324752) for the [signature operator](wikipedia://en/19051228). The analytic index of the signature operator equals the signature of the manifold, and its topological index is the L-genus of the manifold. By the Atiyah–Singer index theorem these are equal.

:::
