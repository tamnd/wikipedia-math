# Theta characteristic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theta characteristic"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **theta characteristic** of a [non-singular](wikipedia://en/253260#Singularities) [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) *C* is a [divisor class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisor_class) Θ such that 2Θ is the [canonical class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canonical_class). In terms of [holomorphic line bundles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_line_bundle) *L* on a connected [compact Riemann surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Riemann_surface), it is therefore *L* such that *L*<sup>2</sup> is the [canonical bundle](wikipedia://en/734893), here also equivalently the [holomorphic cotangent bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_cotangent_bundle). In terms of [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the equivalent definition is as an [invertible sheaf](wikipedia://en/581859), which squares to the sheaf of [differentials of the first kind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differentials_of_the_first_kind). :::

## History and genus 1 {#History_and_genus_1}

The importance of this concept was realised first in the analytic theory of [theta functions](wikipedia://en/449745), and geometrically in the theory of [bitangents](wikipedia://en/2605212). In the analytic theory, there are four fundamental theta functions in the theory of [Jacobian elliptic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobian_elliptic_function). Their labels are in effect the theta characteristics of an [elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/10225). For that case, the canonical class is trivial (zero in the [divisor class group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Divisor_class_group)) and so the theta characteristics of an elliptic curve *E* over the [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) are seen to be in 1-1 correspondence with the four points *P* on *E* with 2*P* = 0; this is counting of the solutions is clear from the group structure, a product of two [circle groups](wikipedia://en/508177), when *E* is treated as a [complex torus](wikipedia://en/3055042).

## Higher genus {#Higher_genus}

For *C* of genus 0 there is one such divisor class, namely the class of *\-P*, where *P* is any point on the curve. In case of higher genus *g*, assuming the field over which *C* is defined does not have [characteristic 2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_2), the theta characteristics can be counted as

2<sup>2*g*</sup>

in number if the base field is algebraically closed.

This comes about because the solutions of the equation on the divisor class level will form a single [coset](wikipedia://en/98759) of the solutions of

2*D* = 0.

In other words, with *K* the canonical class and Θ any given solution of

2Θ = *K*,

any other solution will be of form

Θ + *D*.

This reduces counting the theta characteristics to finding the 2-rank of the [Jacobian variety](wikipedia://en/489734) *J*(*C*) of *C*. In the complex case, again, the result follows since *J*(*C*) is a complex torus of dimension 2*g*. Over a general field, see the theory explained at [Hasse-Witt matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse-Witt_matrix) for the counting of the [p-rank of an abelian variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-rank_of_an_abelian_variety). The answer is the same, provided the characteristic of the field is not 2.

A theta characteristic Θ will be called *even* or *odd* depending on the dimension of its space of global sections $H^0(C, \Theta)$. It turns out that on *C* there are $2^{g - 1} (2^g + 1)$ even and $2^{g-1}(2^g - 1)$ odd theta characteristics.

## Classical theory {#Classical_theory}

Classically the theta characteristics were divided into these two kinds, odd and even, according to the value of the [Arf invariant](wikipedia://en/6950659) of a certain [quadratic form](wikipedia://en/251478) *Q* with values mod 2. Thus in case of *g* = 3 and a plane [quartic curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quartic_curve), there were 28 of one type, and the remaining 36 of the other; this is basic in the question of counting bitangents, as it corresponds to the 28 [bitangents of a quartic](wikipedia://en/5204204). The geometric construction of *Q* as an [intersection form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersection_form_%284-manifold%29) is with modern tools possible algebraically. In fact the [Weil pairing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weil_pairing) applies, in its [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) form. Triples (θ<sub>1</sub>, θ<sub>2</sub>, θ<sub>3</sub>) of theta characteristics are called *syzygetic* and *asyzygetic* depending on whether Arf(θ<sub>1</sub>)+Arf(θ<sub>2</sub>)+Arf(θ<sub>3</sub>)+Arf(θ<sub>1</sub>+θ<sub>2</sub>+θ<sub>3</sub>) is 0 or 1.

## Spin structures {#Spin_structures}

(Atiyah 1971) showed that, for a compact complex manifold, choices of theta characteristics correspond bijectively to [spin structures](wikipedia://en/2844303).
