# Fujita Sadasuke

Fujita

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fujita Sadasuke"}

**Fujita Sadasuke**, also known as Honda Teiken,[^o2] was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) in the [Edo period](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edo_period).[^o3] He is the author of *Seiyō sampō* (*Essence of Mathematics*) which was published in 1781.[^o4]

:::

Sadasuke was the father of [Fujita Kagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fujita_Kagen) (1765–1821),[^o5] who is credited with publishing the first collection of *[sangaku](wikipedia://en/1055365)* problems.[^o3]
