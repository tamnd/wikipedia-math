# Revista Colombiana de Estadística

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Revista Colombiana de Estadística"}

The ***Revista Colombiana de Estadística*** (English: *Colombian Journal of Statistics*) is a biannual [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) on [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) published by the [National University of Colombia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Colombia). It covers research on statistics, including applications, statistics education, and the [history of statistics](wikipedia://en/14986442).

:::

## History {#History}

The *Revista Colombiana de Estadística* was established in 1968. During the first years, the journal only published papers in Spanish but since 1985 it also publishes papers in English.[^o4] The journal stopped publication between 1969 and 1979. In 1979, it was relaunched by Luis Thorin and since 1981 the publication has been continuous with two issues per year. Recently, since 2011 the Journal only publishes articles in English language.

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The *Revista Colombiana de Estadística* is abstracted and indexed in [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus), [SciELO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SciELO), [Current Index to Statistics](wikipedia://en/13958910), [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646), [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH), [Redalyc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Redalyc), [Latindex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latindex), and [Publindex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Publindex) (category A1). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2014 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.179.[^o6]
