# Alessandra Celletti (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alessandra Celletti"}

**Alessandra Celletti** (born 12 February 1962) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Celletti earned a master's degree in mathematics in 1984 at the [University of Rome La Sapienza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome), and a PhD in 1989 at the Swiss Federal Institute of Technology in Zurich ([ETH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH)) under the supervision of [Jürgen Moser](wikipedia://en/1752316) and Jörg Waldvogel.

She worked from 1990 to 1999 at [University of l'Aquila](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_L'Aquila). In 1999 she moved at the [University of Rome Tor Vergata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rome_Tor_Vergata), where she was appointed in 2016 full professor of [Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

Since 2001 she has worked with the [Mathematics Genology Project](wikipedia://en/1152126), an online project to highlight the accomplishments of those who complete a doctorate in Mathematics in association with the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).

## Research {#Research}

Celletti's research activity concerns [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), [Kolmogorov–Arnold–Moser (KAM) theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kolmogorov–Arnold–Moser_theory), and [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182).

Her book "Stability and Chaos in Celestial Mechanics" has been adapted as the staple for advanced physics and mathematics classes.

Since 2016 she has been editor-in-chief of the journal *[Celestial Mechanics and Dynamical Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celestial_Mechanics_and_Dynamical_Astronomy)*.[^o5]

## Other academic and institutional roles {#Other_academic_and_institutional_roles}

Since 1993 Celletti has coordinated the international CELMEC meetings.

She is a founding member of the Italian Society of Celestial Mechanics and Astrodynamics,[^o7] which she chaired from 2001 to 2013.

Since 2010 she has been an honorary "Celestial Mechanics Institute" member.

Since 2012 she has been a member of the Scientific Committee of the [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372). Since 2017 she has chaired the Women in Mathematics Committee[^o8] of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).

In 2015 she was elected vice-President and in 2018 became President of the Scientific Committee in Celestial Mechanics of the [International Astronomical Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Astronomical_Union) till 2021.

Since April 2020 she is member of the Governing Board and Vice-President of [ANVUR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANVUR) (the Italian National Agency for the Evaluation of Universities and Research Institutes).

## Prizes and awards {#Prizes_and_awards}

In 2007, her book *Ordine e caos nel sistema solare* in collaboration with Ettore Perozzi was a finalist for the Galileo prize for popular science.

The [asteroid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asteroid) 2005 DJ1 has been named in her honour ([117539 Celletti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/117539_Celletti)).

In 2012 she was an invited speaker at the 6th [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) as well as a key speaker at the 2012 European Women in Mathematics conference.[^o10]

In 2023, Alessandra Celletti won the [Brouwer Career Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brouwer_Award_%28Division_on_Dynamical_Astronomy%29) from the [Division on Dynamical Astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_on_Dynamical_Astronomy) of the [American Astronomical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Astronomical_Society).
