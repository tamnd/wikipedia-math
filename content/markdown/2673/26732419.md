# Triebel–Lizorkin space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Triebel–Lizorkin space"}

In the mathematical discipline known as [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a **Triebel–Lizorkin space** is a generalization of many standard function spaces such as [*L*<sup>*p*</sup> spaces](wikipedia://en/45194) and [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964). It is named after Hans Triebel (born February 7th 1936 in [Dessau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dessau)) and Petr Ivanovich Lizorkin.

:::
