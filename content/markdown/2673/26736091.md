# List of prime knots

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of prime knots"}

In [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), [prime knots](wikipedia://en/315030) are those knots that are [indecomposable](wikipedia://en/810549) under the operation of [knot sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knot_sum). The prime knots with ten or fewer crossings are listed here for quick comparison of their properties and varied naming schemes.

:::
