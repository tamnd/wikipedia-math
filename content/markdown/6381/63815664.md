# Dieter Puppe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dieter Puppe"}

*Image: Puppe Adams.jpg.* Dieter Puppe (middle) with [John Frank Adams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Frank_Adams) in 1962, in [Aarhus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus).

**Siegmund Dieter Puppe** (16 December 1930 – 13 August 2005[^o2]) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562) and [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410). He is known for the [Puppe sequence](wikipedia://en/4734210) in algebraic topology.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Dieter Puppe was born the son of the lawyer Siegmund Puppe. The mathematician Volker Puppe (born 1938) and the legal scholar Ingeborg Puppe (born 1941) were his siblings. From 1948 he studied physics and mathematics in [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) and from 1951 at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). In 1954 he received his doctorate under [Herbert Seifert](wikipedia://en/637024) (*On the homotopy of images of a polyhedron*. [Mathematische Zeitschrift](wikipedia://en/7457514) Bd. 61, 1954, S. 303). From 1951 he was an assistant in Heidelberg and after his habilitation in 1957, a lecturer. In 1960 he became a professor in [Saarbrücken](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarbrücken). In 1968 he returned to Heidelberg, where he stayed until his retirement in 1996, apart from guest stays at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) in 1957/58, in [Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago) in 1961, and in [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis) in 1966/67.

Puppe worked on [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008) (with [Martin Kneser](wikipedia://en/17620650) as early as the 1950s) and [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831).

From 1972 he was a member of the [Heidelberg Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_Academy_of_Sciences). In 1962 he gave a lecture at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Stockholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stockholm) (*Korrespondenzen in abelschen Kategorien*).

His students included [Tammo tom Dieck](wikipedia://en/56729165), [Hans-Werner Henn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hans-Werner_Henn), and [Rudolf Fritsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rudolf_Fritsch).
