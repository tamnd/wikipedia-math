# James Crowley (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Crowley"}

**James M. Crowley** (born 19 February 1949) is an American mathematician currently at [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) and an Elected Fellow to the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::

After graduating with a bachelor's degree in mathematics from the [College of the Holy Cross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_the_Holy_Cross), Crowley graduated in 1972 with an M.S. from [Virginia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Tech). From 1972 to 1977 he was a mathematician working for the U. S. Air Force Foreign Technology Division. From 1977 to 1986 he was an associate professor at the U.S. Air Force Academy. At [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) he studied from 1978 to 1981, graduating in 1982 with doctoral thesis *Numerical Methods Of Parameter Identification For Problems Arising In Elasticity* under the supervision of Harvey Thomas Banks. After another four years (from 1986 to 1990) of work for the U. S. Air Force, Crowley was a program manager at [DARPA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DARPA) from 1992 to 1994. He then became the SIAM executive director and has continued in that post until the present.[^o7][^o8] In 2012 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
