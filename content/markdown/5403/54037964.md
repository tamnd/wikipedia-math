# Philippe Di Francesco

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Philippe Di Francesco"}

**Philippe Di Francesco** is a French-American mathematician, focusing in mathematical physics, physical combinatorics and integrable systems. He is senior researcher (Directeur de Recherche) at the [Institute of Theoretical Physics, Saclay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Theoretical_Physics,_Saclay) in France, and is currently the Morris and Gertrude Fine Distinguished Professor of Mathematics at [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign).[^o2][^o3] He is also author of the book 'Conformal Field Theory'.[^o4] He received his PhD in 1989, under Jean-Claude Le Guillou and [Jean-Bernard Zuber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Bernard_Zuber), at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University).[^o5]

:::

Di Francesco was elected as member of [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2025.
