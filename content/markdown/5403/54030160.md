# Alexander Dranishnikov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Dranishnikov"}

**Alexander Nikolaevich Dranishnikov** (Александр Николаевич Дранишников; born 5 February 1958) is a Russian-American mathematician, focusing in geometry and topology, currently a distinguished professor at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida).[^o2][^o3][^o4] In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o5] In 2012 he became one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
