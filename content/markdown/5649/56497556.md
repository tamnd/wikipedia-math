# Sergei Stepanov (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Stepanov"}

**Sergei Aleksandrovich Stepanov** (Сергей Александрович Степанов; [^o2] 24 February 1941) is a Russian mathematician, specializing in number theory. He is known for his 1969 proof using elementary methods of the Riemann hypothesis for zeta-functions of hyperelliptic curves over finite fields, first proved by [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019) in 1940–1941 using sophisticated, deep methods in algebraic geometry.

:::

Stepanov received in 1977 his Russian doctorate (higher doctoral degree) from the [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute) under [Dmitry Konstantinovich Faddeev](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitry_Konstantinovich_Faddeev) with dissertation (translated title) *An elementary method in algebraic number theory*.[^o3] He was from 1987 to 2000 a professor at the Steklov Institute in Moscow.[^o4] In the 1990s he was also at [Bilkent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bilkent_University) in [Ankara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ankara). He is at the Institute for Problems of Information Transmission of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences).

Stepanov is best known for his work in arithmetic algebraic geometry, especially for the [Weil conjectures](wikipedia://en/244705) on algebraic curves. He gave in 1969 an "elementary" (*i.e.* using elementary methods) proof of a result first proved by André Weil using sophisticated methods, not readily understable by mathematicians who are not specialists in algebraic geometry. [Wolfgang M. Schmidt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolfgang_M._Schmidt) extended Stepanov's methods to prove the general result, and [Enrico Bombieri](wikipedia://en/10475) succeeded in using the work of Stepanov and Schmidt to give a substantially simplified, elementary proof of the Riemann hypothesis for zeta-functions of curves over finite fields.[^o5][^o6][^o7] Stepanov's research also deals with applications of algebraic geometry to [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869).

He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1974 in Vancouver.[^o8][^o9] He received in 1975 the [USSR State Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR_State_Prize).[^o4] He was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2012.
