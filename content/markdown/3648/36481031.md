# Jun-iti Nagata

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jun-iti Nagata"}

**Jun-iti Nagata** was a Japanese mathematician specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954).

:::

In 1956, Jun-iti Nagata earned his PhD from [Osaka University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_University) under the direction of [Kiiti Morita](wikipedia://en/12034472). He was the author of two standard graduate texts in topology: *Modern Dimension Theory*[^o3] and *Modern General Topology*.[^o4] His name is attached to the [Nagata–Smirnov metrization theorem](wikipedia://en/1236569), which was proved independently by Nagata in 1950 and by [Smirnov](wikipedia://en/68008035) in 1951, as well as the [Assouad–Nagata dimension](wikipedia://en/68496655) of a metric space, which he introduced in a 1958 article.[^o5]

Nagata became a professor emeritus at both [Osaka Kyoiku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_Kyoiku_University), where he taught for 10 years, and [Osaka Electro-Communication University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osaka_Electro-Communication_University), where he taught for 5 years.
