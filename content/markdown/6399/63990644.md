# Fréchet lattice

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fréchet lattice"}

In mathematics, specifically in [order theory](wikipedia://en/361924) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a **Fréchet lattice** is a [topological vector lattice](wikipedia://en/63989962) that is also a [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801).[^n1] Fréchet lattices are important in the theory of [topological vector lattices](wikipedia://en/63989962).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every Fréchet lattice is a [locally convex vector lattice](wikipedia://en/63990620).[^n1] The set of all [weak order units](wikipedia://en/63990033) of a [separable](wikipedia://en/27855) Fréchet lattice is a dense subset of its positive cone.[^n1]

:::

::: {#o8 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Every [Banach lattice](wikipedia://en/54391334) is a Fréchet lattice.

:::
