# Regularly ordered

Numbers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regularly ordered"}

In mathematics, specifically in [order theory](wikipedia://en/361924) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), an [ordered vector space](wikipedia://en/8099221) $X$ is said to be **regularly ordered** and its order is called **regular** if $X$ is [Archimedean ordered](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archimedean_ordered) and the [order dual](wikipedia://en/12259232) of $X$ distinguishes points in $X$.[^n1] Being a regularly ordered vector space is an important property in the theory of [topological vector lattices](wikipedia://en/63989962).

:::

::: {#o4 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Every ordered locally convex space is regularly ordered.[^n2] The canonical orderings of subspaces, products, and direct sums of regularly ordered vector spaces are again regularly ordered.[^n2]

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

If $X$ is a regularly ordered [vector lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_lattice) then the [order topology](wikipedia://en/63989892) on $X$ is the finest topology on $X$ making $X$ into a locally convex [topological vector lattice](wikipedia://en/63989962).[^n3]

:::
