# Normed vector lattice

Neologisms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Normed vector lattice"}

In mathematics, specifically in [order theory](wikipedia://en/361924) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a **normed lattice** is a [topological vector lattice](wikipedia://en/63989962) that is also a [normed space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space) whose unit ball is a [solid set](wikipedia://en/63989659).[^n1] Normed lattices are important in the theory of [topological vector lattices](wikipedia://en/63989962). They are closely related to [Banach vector lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_vector_lattice), which are normed vector lattices that are also [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every normed lattice is a [locally convex vector lattice](wikipedia://en/63990620).[^n1]

The strong dual of a normed lattice is a [Banach lattice](wikipedia://en/54391334) with respect to the dual norm and canonical order. If it is also a [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) then its continuous dual space is equal to its [order dual](wikipedia://en/12259232).[^n1]

:::

::: {#o8 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

Every [Banach lattice](wikipedia://en/54391334) is a normed lattice.
