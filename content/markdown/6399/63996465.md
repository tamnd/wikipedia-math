# Stony Brook Seawolves men's basketball statistical leaders

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stony Brook Seawolves men's basketball statistical leaders"}

*Image: Stony brook athletics wordmark.png.* Stony Brook Seawolves primary logo

The **Stony Brook Seawolves men's basketball statistical leaders** are individual statistical leaders of the [Stony Brook Seawolves men's basketball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_Seawolves_men's_basketball) program in various categories, including [points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_%28basketball%29), [assists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assist_%28basketball%29), [blocks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Block_%28basketball%29), [rebounds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rebound_%28basketball%29), and [steals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steal_%28basketball%29). Within those areas, the lists identify single-game, single-season, and career leaders.[^o2] The Seawolves represent [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University) in the [NCAA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Collegiate_Athletic_Association) [Division I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NCAA_Division_I) [Colonial Athletic Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonial_Athletic_Association).

:::

Stony Brook played its first Division I season in 1999.[^o2] These lists are updated through the end of the [2021–22](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021–22_Stony_Brook_Seawolves_men's_basketball_team) season.[^o3]
