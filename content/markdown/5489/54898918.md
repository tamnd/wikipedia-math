# Valeria Simoncini

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valeria Simoncini"}

**Valeria Simoncini** (born 1966) is an Italian researcher in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506) who works as a professor in the mathematics department at the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna). Her research involves the computational solution of equations involving large [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859), and their applications in scientific computing. She is the chair of the [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Activity Group on Linear Algebra.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Simoncini earned a degree from the University of Bologna in 1989, became a [visiting scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar) at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) from 1991 to 1993, and completed her PhD at the [University of Padua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padua) in 1994. After working at [CNR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Council_%28Italy%29) from 1995 to 2000, she returned to Bologna as an associate professor in 2000, and was promoted to full professor in 2010.

## Book {#Book}

With Antonio Navarra, she is the author of the book *A Guide to Empirical Orthogonal Functions for Climate Data Analysis* (Springer, 2010).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Simoncini was a second-place winner of the [Leslie Fox Prize for Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/15366214) in 1997. In 2014 she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) "for contributions to [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660)". She was named to the 2021 class of fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to computational mathematics, in particular to numerical linear algebra". In 2023, she was elected to serve on the [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) Council.[^o5]
