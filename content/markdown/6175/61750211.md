# Paul Deheuvels

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Paul Deheuvels"}

**Paul Deheuvels** (11 March 1948 – 30 January 2026) was a French [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and a specialist in [probability](wikipedia://en/22934).[^o3]

:::

From 1974 to 2013, Paul Deheuvels was a professor of mathematics at the university of [Pierre-et-Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) (now [Sorbonne University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sorbonne_University)) in Paris before being appointed [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o4] He taught probability and statistics.[^o3]

Deheuvels was elected Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o5] in 1985, and Member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2000.[^o6] He was also a member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) (ISI), and a foreign corresponding member of the Royal Academy of Exact, Physical and Natural Sciences of Spain.[^o7]

## Education {#Education}

Deheuvels completed his secondary education at [Lycée Lakanal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Lakanal) in [Sceaux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sceaux,_Hauts-de-Seine), France, at the age of 17. After preparatory classes in mathematics at [Lycée Louis-le-Grand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lycée_Louis-le-Grand), he was admitted in 1967, at the age of 19, to the [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure), rue d’Ulm.[^o9]

## Research {#Research}

Deheuvels' work was in the fields of [extreme value theory](wikipedia://en/50732), [empirical processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empirical_processes), [nonparametric estimation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonparametric_estimation) of tails and tail indices, extreme dependence and extreme [copula (statistics)](wikipedia://en/1793003).[^o3]

## Séralini affair {#Séralini_affair}

The [Séralini affair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Séralini_affair) was the controversy surrounding the publication, retraction, and republication of a [journal article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_article) by French [molecular biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Molecular_biology) [Gilles-Éric Séralini](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gilles-Éric_Séralini). The study was criticized by various regulatory authorities and scientists prior to retraction. With few exceptions, the scientific community dismissed the study and called for a more rigorous peer-review system in scientific journals.[^o12] Deheuvels was one scientist who supported the study.

## Death {#Death}

Deheuvels died on 30 January 2026, at the age of 77.[^o14]
