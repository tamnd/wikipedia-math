# Volker Mehrmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Volker Mehrmann"}

**Volker Ludwig Mehrmann** (born 1955) is a German mathematician known for research in [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660), [differential-algebraic equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential-algebraic_equation), and [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039). He is on the editorial staffs of several journals, including *[Linear Algebra and Its Applications](wikipedia://en/42068102)* and *[Numerische Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerische_Mathematik)*.[^o3] From 2019 to 2022 he was president of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470) (EMS).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mehrmann studied mathematics at [Bielefeld University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), where he earned his Diploma (equivalent to an undergraduate degree) in 1979. He completed his Ph.D. there in 1982 under the supervision of Ludwig F. Elsner, following a spell at [Kent State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kent_State_University), where he worked under [Richard S. Varga](wikipedia://en/25492358). His dissertation was "On classes of matrices containing M-matrices and hermitian positive semidefinite matrices". He also received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1987 from the same university.

From 1990 to 1992 Mehrmann was a [*Vertretungsprofessor*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_Germany#Other_positions) (interim professor) at the [RWTH Aachen University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RWTH_Aachen_University). From 1993 to 2000 he was a professor at the [Chemnitz University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemnitz_University_of_Technology). Since 2000 he has been a professor at the Institute for Mathematics at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin). From June 2008 to May 2016 he was the spokesperson for the [Deutsche Forschungsgemeinschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deutsche_Forschungsgemeinschaft)'s mathematical center called [Matheon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matheon). From January 2011 to December 2013 he was president of the [Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik) (GAMM). In 2011 he received from the [European Research Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) (ERC) an advanced grant for modeling, simulation, and control of [multiphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiphysics) systems.[^o7]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Mehrmann is a member of the [Deutschen Akademie der Technikwissenschaften (Acatech)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acatech) and of [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea). In 2015 he gave the [Gauss Lecture](wikipedia://en/48440954) and in 2018 was awarded the [W. T. and Idalia Reid Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._T._and_Idalia_Reid_Prize)[^o9] and in 2019 the [Hans Schneider Prize in Linear Algebra](wikipedia://en/58527033).[^o10] In 2022 he was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to scientific computing and numerical linear algebra, and service and leadership in the mathematical community".
