# Base period

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Base period"}

In [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), a **base period** or **reference period** is a point in time used as a reference point for comparison with other periods.[^o2][^o3] It is generally used as a benchmark for measuring financial or economic data.[^o4] Base periods typically provide a [point of reference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reference_point_%28disambiguation%29) for economic studies, consumer demand, and unemployment benefit claims.

:::

In [public transport scheduling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Public_transport_timetable), the base period is the period of reduced service on weekdays that generally exists between the morning and afternoon [rush hours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rush_hour).
