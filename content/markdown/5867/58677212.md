# Ranee Brylinski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ranee Brylinski"}

**Ranee Kathryn Brylinski** (née **Gupta**, January 28, 1957 – September 9, 2026) was an American mathematician known for her research in [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [quantum logic gates](wikipedia://en/888587). Originally a professor of mathematics at [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University), she left academia in 2003 to found the mathematical consulting company Brylinski Research with her husband, [Jean-Luc Brylinski](wikipedia://en/10306904).

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Brylinski was born in [Detroit, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit,_Michigan), on January 28, 1957. She graduated from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1977, and completed her Ph.D. at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (MIT) in 1981. Her dissertation, *Abelian Algebras and Adjoint Orbits*, was supervised by [Steven Kleiman](wikipedia://en/16169105).

After a year as an NSF Mathematical Sciences Postdoctoral Fellow at MIT, she joined the faculty at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) as Tamarkin Assistant Professor of Mathematics in 1982. She moved from Brown to Pennsylvania State University in 1988. At Pennsylvania State, she was co-director of the Center for Geometry and Mathematical Physics.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

The Brylinski–Kostant filtration of [weight spaces](wikipedia://en/277179) is named after her. She originally developed this filtration in 1989, motivated by earlier work of [Bertram Kostant](wikipedia://en/3520590).LWS She is also known for Brylinski's theorem, a theorem from her dissertation on the closures of orbits of [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924).

Another result, also called "Brylinski's theorem", comes from a paper written jointly by Brylinski and her husband, characterizing [universal quantum logic gates](wikipedia://en/888587#Universal_quantum_gates).UQG

## Death {#Death}

Brylinski died on September 9, 2026, at the age of 69.[^o5]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Brylinski won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1990. In 1998, she won the [G. de B. Robinson Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._de_B._Robinson_Award) of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) for her work on [quantization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantization_%28physics%29) of algebraic groups.Q4D
