# Steve Butler (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve Butler"}

**Steven Kay Butler** (born May 16, 1977) is an American mathematician specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170). He is a Morrill Professor and the Barbara J. Janson Professor in Mathematics at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Butler earned his master's degree at [Brigham Young University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brigham_Young_University) in 2003. His master's thesis was titled *Bounding the Number of Graphs Containing Very Long Induced Paths*.[^o4] He completed a doctorate at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) in 2008, authoring the dissertation *Eigenvalues and Structures of Graphs*, advised by [Fan Chung](wikipedia://en/246562).[^o6] Upon completing his postdoctoral studies at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles), Butler joined the [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) faculty in 2011.[^o9][^o7] In 2015, Butler became the 512th (and so far final) person to have an [Erdős number](wikipedia://en/9742) of 1, when he published a paper with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and [Ronald Graham](wikipedia://en/240694) on [Egyptian fractions](wikipedia://en/336349).[^o8] In 2017, Butler was named the Barbara J. Janson Professor in Mathematics,[^o9] and to a Morrill Professorship in 2022.[^o10]

## Work with undergraduates {#Work_with_undergraduates}

Butler has been a project lead for the Iowa State University Math [REU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research_Experiences_for_Undergraduates) in 2015, 2017, 2019, 2022, 2023, and 2024, and 2025.[^o12]
