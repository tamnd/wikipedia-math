# Douglas West (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Douglas West"}

**Douglas Brent West** is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) at [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign). He received his [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy). from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1978; his advisor was [Daniel Kleitman](wikipedia://en/5299894). He is the "W" in [G. W. Peck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G._W._Peck), a [pseudonym](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudonym) for a group of six [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902) that includes West.[^o4] He is the editor of the journal *[Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/21988115)*.

:::
