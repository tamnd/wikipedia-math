# Floyd Williams

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Floyd Williams"}

**Floyd Leroy Williams** (born September 20, 1939) is a **North American mathematician** well known for his work in [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681) and, most recently, [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o2] In addition to **Lie theory**, his research interests are in [homological algebra](wikipedia://en/139410) and the mathematics of [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202). He received his **B.S.(1962) in Mathematics** from [Lincoln University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_University_of_Missouri), and later his **M.S.(1965) and Ph.D.(1972)** from [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis).[^o2] Williams was appointed [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of mathematics at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst) in 1984, and has been professor [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) since 2005.[^o2] Williams' accomplishments earned him recognition by [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006) as a **Black History Month 2019 Honoree.[^o3]**

:::

## Biographical Sketch {#Biographical_Sketch}

Floyd Williams was born on September 20, 1939, and lived in [Kansas City, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kansas_City,_Missouri). He was raised in extreme [poverty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poverty). His mother told him not to complain about their situation, but rather to have faith in [God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/God) and work hard. Her advice was taken, and it worked. He eventually was [ordained](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordained) in addition to being a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

However, it was [music](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music), not [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), that appealed to him through [high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school). "In fact," he admits, "mathematics was the only course in which I did not do well." Williams had not thought of going to [college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College) until his last week in high school when he was offered a music [scholarship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholarship) at [Lincoln University of Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_University_of_Missouri) in [Jefferson City, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jefferson_City,_Missouri).

It was in his sophomore year that he became intrigued by the [theory of relativity](wikipedia://en/30001), which turned out to be his main motivation for studying [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). In 1972 he completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis) where his [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) was in the field of [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681). He was an [instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and [lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecturer) at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) from 1972 to 1975, before moving to the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Massachusetts_Amherst) as an assistant professor in 1975. In 1983 he received an [MRI grant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MRI_grant) to continuing researching in this field, ushering him into the mainstream of mathematics.

As an [African-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African-American) in a field that has had little [minority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minority_group) representation, Williams has felt the sting of [discrimination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrimination) during his career. However, he has been a motivation and role model for many young minorities, encouraging them to enter [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science) and [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering). Williams has helped to set up programs that allow pre-college students and [undergraduates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduates) to meet and talk with [mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematicians), [scientists](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientists) and [engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineers), most notably at a summer camp run at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT). "All that many of these youngsters see is different courses," he says, "but they want to know what mathematicians do from 8 am to 5 pm. Once [minorities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minorities) commit to [graduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postgraduate_education) work in [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science) or [engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering)," he continues, "they need extra help and support for what, for many, is the foreign environment of [graduate school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graduate_school). Such programs exist at few [universities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universities), but we need more of them."

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Williams' recent contribution to [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202) has been in the area of [Nikiforov-Uvarov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikiforov-Uvarov) theory of generalized [hypergeometric differential equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypergeometric_differential_equation), used to solve the [Schrödinger equation](wikipedia://en/59874) and to obtain the quantization of energies from a single unified point of view. This [theory](wikipedia://en/30746) is developed and is also used to give a uniform approach to the theory of [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110). This study furthers to connect the modern studies of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) with [physics](wikipedia://en/22939).
