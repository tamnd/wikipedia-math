# Extinction probability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Extinction probability"}

In [population genetics](wikipedia://en/219268), **extinction probability** is the chance of an [inherited trait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inherited_trait) becoming [extinct](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extinction) as a [function](wikipedia://en/185427) of [time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time) *t*.[^o2] If *t* = ∞ this may be the complement of the [chance](wikipedia://en/22934) of becoming a universal [trait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenotypic_trait). This opposing process is also known as proceeding to [fixation](wikipedia://en/9185794).

:::
