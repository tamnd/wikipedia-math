# Alberto Cattaneo

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alberto Cattaneo"}

**Alberto Sergio Cattaneo** (26 June 1967 in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan))[^o3] is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist), specializing in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) related to [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) and [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

After attending *[Liceo scientifico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liceo_scientifico)* *A. Volta* in Milan, Cattaneo studied physics at [University of Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Milan), graduating in 1991. In 1995, he obtained a PhD in theoretical physics at the same university; his thesis, entitled *Teorie topologiche di tipo BF ed invarianti dei nodi* (Topological [BF theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BF_theory) and [knot invariants](wikipedia://en/166898)), was supervised by Maurizio Martellini.[^o5]

Cattaneo worked as a postdoc in 1995-1997 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (with [Arthur Jaffe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Jaffe)) and in 1997-1998 at the University of Milan (with [Paolo Cotta-Ramusino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paolo_Cotta-Ramusino)). In 1998, he moved to [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich)'s mathematics department as an assistant professor, and he became a full professor in 2003.[^o3]

In 2006 he was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#2006,_Madrid), with the talk *From topological field theory to deformation quantization and reduction*, at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid).[^o6] Cattaneo was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Cattaneo's research interests include [deformation quantization](wikipedia://en/4278245), [symplectic](wikipedia://en/294298) and [Poisson geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poisson_geometry), [topological quantum field theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological_quantum_field_theories), and the mathematical aspects of perturbative quantization of [gauge theories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gauge_theories).[^o3]

With [Giovanni Felder](wikipedia://en/68426150) he developed a [path integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Path_integral_formulation) interpretation of the [deformation quantization of Poisson manifolds](wikipedia://en/26049975) (introduced in 2003 by [Maxim Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509)),[^o9] as well as a description of the [symplectic groupoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_groupoid) integrating a [Poisson manifold](wikipedia://en/882902) as an infinite-dimensional [symplectic quotient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_quotient).[^o10]

He supervised 14 PhD students as of 2022.[^o5]
