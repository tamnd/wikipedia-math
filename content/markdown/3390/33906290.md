# Yuli Rudyak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuli Rudyak"}

**Yuli B. Rudyak** (24 April 1948 - 24 August 2024) was a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida) in Gainesville, Florida. He obtained his doctorate from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) under the supervision of [M. M. Postnikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M._M._Postnikov). His main research interests are [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology).

:::

## Books {#Books}

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Rudyak is the father of Marina Rudyak, who is an Assistant Professor of Chinese Studies at the University of Heidelberg.
