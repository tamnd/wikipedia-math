::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 6-simplexes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 6-simplex** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex).

:::

There are three unique degrees of rectifications, including the zeroth, the 6-simplex itself. Vertices of the *rectified 6-simplex* are located at the edge-centers of the *6-simplex*. Vertices of the *birectified 6-simplex* are located in the triangular face centers of the *6-simplex*.

::: {#o5 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Rectified 6-simplex"}

## Rectified 6-simplex {#Rectified_6-simplex}

Uniform polypeton stat table [E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as S1. It is also called **0<sub>4,1</sub>** for its branching Coxeter-Dynkin diagram, shown as .

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Rectified heptapeton (Acronym: ril) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *rectified 6-simplex* can be most simply positioned in 7-space as permutations of (0,0,0,0,0,1,1). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [rectified 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_7-orthoplex).

## Related uniform 6-polytopes {#Related_uniform_6-polytopes}

The rectified 6-simplex polytope is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the [7-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-demicube), and the [edge figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_figure) of the uniform [2<sub>41</sub> polytope](wikipedia://en/19091447).

These polytopes are a part of 35 [uniform 6-polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821728#The_A6_.5B3.2C3.2C3.2C3.2C3.5D_family_.286-simplex.29) based on the \[3,3,3,3,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004), all shown here in A<sub>6</sub> [Coxeter plane](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052).
