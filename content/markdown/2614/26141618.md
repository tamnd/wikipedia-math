::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Cantic 5-cube"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446) of [five dimensions](wikipedia://en/2851410) or higher, a **cantic 5-cube**, **cantihalf 5-cube**, **truncated 5-demicube** is a [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232). It has half the vertices of a [cantellated 5-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellated_5-cube).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the 160 vertices of a cantic 5-cube centered at the origin and edge length 6$\sqrt{2}$ are coordinate permutations:

(±1,±1,±3,±3,±3)

with an odd number of plus signs.

## Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Cantic penteract, truncated demipenteract
- Truncated hemipenteract (thin) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It has half the vertices of the [cantellated 5-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellated_5-cube), as compared here in the B5 Coxeter plane projections:

::: {#o12 .table cue=table}

| *Image: 5-demicube t01 B5.svg.* Cantic 5-cube | *Image: 5-cube t02.svg.* Cantellated 5-cube |
|---|---|

:::

This polytope is based on the [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232), a part of a dimensional family of [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6842392) called [demihypercubes](wikipedia://en/6833695) for being [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the [hypercube](wikipedia://en/39783) family.

There are 23 [uniform 5-polytopes](wikipedia://en/14415338) that can be constructed from the D<sub>5</sub> symmetry of the 5-demicube, of which are unique to this family, and 15 are shared within the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) family.
