::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 5-cubes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 5-cube** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215).

:::

There are four unique truncations of the 5-cube. Vertices of the truncated 5-cube are located as pairs on the edge of the 5-cube. Vertices of the bitruncated 5-cube are located on the square faces of the 5-cube. The third and fourth truncations are more easily constructed as second and first truncations of the 5-orthoplex.

- Truncated penteract (Acronym: tan) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Construction and coordinates {#Construction_and_coordinates}

The *truncated 5-cube* may be constructed by [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) the vertices of the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) at $1/(\sqrt{2}+2)$ of the edge length. A regular [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821) is formed at each truncated vertex.

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *truncated 5-cube* having edge length 2 are all permutations of:

$$\left(\pm1,\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2})\right)$$

### Images {#Images}

The truncated 5-cube is constructed by a truncation applied to the 5-cube. All edges are shortened, and two new vertices are added on each original edge.

- Bitruncated penteract (Acronym: bittin) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Construction and coordinates {#Construction_and_coordinates}

The *bitruncated 5-cube* may be constructed by [bitruncating](wikipedia://en/6792316) the vertices of the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) at $\sqrt{2}$ of the edge length.

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *bitruncated 5-cube* having edge length 2 are all permutations of:

$$\left(0,\ \pm1,\ \pm2,\ \pm2,\ \pm2\right)$$
