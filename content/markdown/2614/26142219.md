::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 6-orthoplexes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 6-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [6-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/10083518).

:::

There are 5 degrees of truncation for the 6-orthoplex. Vertices of the truncated 6-orthoplex are located as pairs on the edge of the 6-orthoplex. Vertices of the bitruncated 6-orthoplex are located on the triangular faces of the 6-orthoplex. Vertices of the tritruncated 6-orthoplex are located inside the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells of the 6-orthoplex.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Truncated hexacross
- Truncated hexacontatetrapeton (Acronym: tag) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

::: {#o14 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

### Construction {#Construction}

There are two [Coxeter groups](wikipedia://en/297004) associated with the *truncated hexacross*, one with the C<sub>6</sub> or \[4,3,3,3,3\] Coxeter group, and a lower symmetry with the D<sub>6</sub> or \[3<sup>3,1,1</sup>\] Coxeter group.

:::

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a truncated 6-orthoplex, centered at the origin, are all 120 vertices are sign (4) and coordinate (30) [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of

(±2,±1,0,0,0,0)
