In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantic 6-cube** (or a truncated 6-demicube) is a [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728).

:::

## Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Truncated 6-demicube
- Truncaced demihexeract
- Truncated hemihexeract (Acronym: thax) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the 480 vertices of a cantic 6-cube centered at the origin and edge length 6$\sqrt{2}$ are coordinate permutations:

(±1,±1,±3,±3,±3,±3)

with an odd number of plus signs.
