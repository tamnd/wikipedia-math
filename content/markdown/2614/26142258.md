::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 6-simplexes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 6-simplex** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [6-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex).

:::

There are unique 3 degrees of truncation. Vertices of the truncation 6-simplex are located as pairs on the edge of the 6-simplex. Vertices of the bitruncated 6-simplex are located on the triangular faces of the 6-simplex. Vertices of the tritruncated 6-simplex are located inside the [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells of the 6-simplex.

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *truncated 6-simplex* can be most simply positioned in 7-space as permutations of (0,0,0,0,0,1,2). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [truncated 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_7-orthoplex).

The **tritruncated 6-simplex** is an [isotopic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet-transitive) uniform polytope, with 14 identical [bitruncated 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_5-simplex) facets.

The tritruncated 6-simplex is the [intersection](wikipedia://en/23476429) of two [6-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6-simplex) in dual configuration: and .

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Tetradecapeton (as a 14-facetted 6-polytope) (Acronym: fe) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The vertices of the *tritruncated 6-simplex* can be most simply positioned in 7-space as permutations of (0,0,0,1,2,2,2). This construction is based on [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647) of the [bitruncated 7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_7-orthoplex). Alternately it can be centered on the origin as permutations of (-1,-1,-1,0,1,1,1).
