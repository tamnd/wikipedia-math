::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 5-cubes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 5-cube** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215).

:::

There are 5 degrees of rectifications of a 5-polytope, the zeroth here being the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215), and the 4th and last being the [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615). Vertices of the rectified 5-cube are located at the edge-centers of the 5-cube. Vertices of the birectified 5-cube are located in the square face centers of the 5-cube.

- Rectified penteract (acronym: rin) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Construction {#Construction}

The rectified 5-cube may be constructed from the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) by [truncating](wikipedia://en/2192366) its vertices at the midpoints of its edges.

:::

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) of the vertices of the rectified 5-cube with edge length $\sqrt{2}$ is given by all permutations of:

$$(0,\ \pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm1)$$

## Birectified 5-cube {#Birectified_5-cube}

Uniform polyteron stat table

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, identifying it as Cr<sub>5</sub><sup>2</sup> as a second rectification of a 5-dimensional [cross polytope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_polytope).

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Birectified 5-cube/penteract
- Birectified pentacross/5-orthoplex/triacontaditeron
- Penteractitriacontaditeron (acronym: nit) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]
- Rectified 5-demicube/demipenteract

### Construction and coordinates {#Construction_and_coordinates}

The birectified 5-cube may be constructed by [birectifying](wikipedia://en/2192366) the vertices of the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) at $\sqrt{2}$ of the edge length.

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *birectified 5-cube* having edge length 2 are all permutations of:

$$\left(0,\ 0,\ \pm1,\ \pm1,\ \pm1\right)$$
