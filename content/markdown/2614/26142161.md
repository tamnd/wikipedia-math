::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 6-cubes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 6-cube** (or **truncated hexeract**) is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [6-cube](wikipedia://en/10083462).

:::

There are 5 truncations for the 6-cube. Vertices of the truncated 6-cube are located as pairs on the edge of the 6-cube. Vertices of the bitruncated 6-cube are located on the square faces of the 6-cube. Vertices of the tritruncated 6-cube are located inside the [cubic](wikipedia://en/6285) cells of the 6-cube.

### Construction and coordinates {#Construction_and_coordinates}

The truncated 6-cube may be constructed by [truncating](wikipedia://en/4224429) the vertices of the [6-cube](wikipedia://en/10083462) at $1/(\sqrt{2}+2)$ of the edge length. A regular [5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex) replaces each original vertex.

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of a *truncated 6-cube* having edge length 2 are the permutations of:

$$\left(\pm1,\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2}),\ \pm(1+\sqrt{2})\right)$$
