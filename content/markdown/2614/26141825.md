::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated 5-orthoplexes"}

In five-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 5-orthoplex** is a convex [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), being a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615).

:::

There are 4 unique truncations of the 5-orthoplex. Vertices of the truncation 5-orthoplex are located as pairs on the edge of the 5-orthoplex. Vertices of the [bitruncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated) 5-orthoplex are located on the triangular faces of the 5-orthoplex. The third and fourth truncations are more easily constructed as second and first truncations of the [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215).

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Truncated pentacross
- Truncated triacontaditeron (Acronym: tot) (Jonathan Bowers)[^n1]

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of a truncated 5-orthoplex, centered at the origin, are all 80 vertices are sign (4) and coordinate (20) [permutations](wikipedia://en/44027) of

(±2,±1,0,0,0)

### Images {#Images}

The truncated 5-orthoplex is constructed by a [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) operation applied to the [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615). All edges are shortened, and two new vertices are added on each original edge.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The truncated 5-orthoplex and bitruncated 5-orthoplex are from the family of 31 [uniform 5-polytopes](wikipedia://en/14415338) generated from the regular [5-cube](wikipedia://en/7184215) or [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615).
