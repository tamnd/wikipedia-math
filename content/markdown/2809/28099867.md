# New rhetoric

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="New rhetoric"}

**New rhetoric** is an [interdisciplinary field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdisciplinary_field)[^o2] approaching for the broadening of [classical rhetorical canon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_rhetorics).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

"New" rhetoric is the name for various efforts to reclaim the study and teaching of [rhetoric](wikipedia://en/25447) from the unsavory reputation[^o2] it developed from association with "political lies, corporate spin ... [purple prose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purple_prose), [boiler-plate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boilerplate_text) arrangement schemas, unimaginative reproductions of bullshit and so on."[^o6] The New Rhetoricians hoped to place rhetoric once again as a scholarly discipline [^o2] at least to the level of other contemporary fields in social, cultural and linguistic studies.

The field is often dated to [Chaïm Perelman](wikipedia://en/3784254)'s and [Lucie Olbrechts-Tyteca's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lucie_Olbrechts-Tyteca) *The New Rhetoric* (1969)[^o7] but both the notion and the idea for the need of "new" rhetoric, different from the "old" one, can be traced to the works of [Kenneth Burke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Burke) - *A Rhetoric of Motives* (1950) and *Rhetoric - Old and New* (1967).[^o8]

The emergence of New Rhetoric was helped by the idea that rhetoric could be [epistemic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemic).[^o2] An attempt to apply New Rhetoric as a social philosophy was made by the Polish philosopher [Mieczysław Maneli](wikipedia://en/63332374) in his 1994 book *Perelman's New Rhetoric as Philosophy and Methodology for the Next Century*.[^o9] Maneli wrote: "The New Rhetoric is modern [humanism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humanism). The struggle for humanism never ends. The most essential features to a humanistic approach to life are: individuals should be given the chance to develop their personal talents and energies, they should be able to be creative and become happy...Their essence and value are creativity and self-determination...Once new rhetoric took as its basic proposition that nothing is absolutely good or sacred except human dignity, one must constantly search for new values, for better forms, and ways of life. There are three specific areas that is especially important for modern humanism: social and individual justice, freedom from oppression with a genuine opportunity for a decent life; and tolerance and privacy."[^o10]

New Rhetoric has an ambivalent relationship with Classical Rhetoric.[^o2] For example, New Rhetoric attempts to break with the [formalistic](wikipedia://en/324863) and [logocentric](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logocentric) (i.e. [patriarchal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patriarchal)) [Neo-Aristotelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neo-Aristotelianism_%28literature%29) analysis in favour of interplay between [text](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Text_%28literary_theory%29) and [context](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Context_%28language_use%29), but according to DeGenaro it does not successfully place itself outside the "Western-patriarchal" tradition, as it is unable to depart from "elite backgrounds and scopes of study"[^o4] to a diversity of voices, topics, etc. This probably makes new rhetoric rather a ground for [postmodern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postmodernism) rhetoric[^o11] which "puts into question the identities of the speaker, the audience, and the messages that pass between them"[^o12] with evaluating the [intersubjective](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intersubjective) philosophy[^o13] because of the idea inherently accepted in [postmodernistic philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Postmodern_philosophy) that "differences cannot be overcome, in [Hegelian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hegel) fashion, by cancelling them under a higher-order [synthesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sublation), but must be eroded or defaced in the course of traversing them".[^o14]
