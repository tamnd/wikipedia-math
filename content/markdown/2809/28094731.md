# Enrico Arbarello

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Enrico Arbarello"}

**Enrico Arbarello** is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is an expert in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Career {#Career}

He earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in New York in 1973. He was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 1993 to 1994.[^o5] He is now a Mathematics Professor at [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
