# Robin Cockett

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robin Cockett"}

**James Robin Bernard Cockett** (born May 4, 1952)[^o3] is a British [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist) and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Cockett received his [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) from the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick) in 1974,[^o6] and earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) from the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds) in 1979 for his research in [injectives](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injective) and [localizations](wikipedia://en/611460) in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), studying under [Alfred Goldie](wikipedia://en/20962143).[^o7]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Cockett has been a professor of computer science at the University of Calgary since 1991, previously holding the title of professor at the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee).[^o9][^o10]

Cockett works primarily in the fields of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869), categorical programming, and [quantum programming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_programming).[^o6] Cockett is a member of the organizing committee for the *Foundational Methods in Computer Science* (FMCS) conference,[^o6] an annual workshop for researchers in [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392) and category theory supported by the [Pacific Institute for the Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/34978739) (PIMS).[^o11]

### Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

His most cited publications[^o4] include:

- *Weakly distributive categories*[^o13]
- *Restriction categories I: categories of partial maps*[^o14]
- *Differential categories*[^o15]
- *Cartesian differential categories*[^o16]
- *Differential structure, tangent structure, and SDG*[^o17]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Cockett has been married to fellow University of Calgary professor Polly Knowlton Cockett since 1984,[^o19] they have three children together: Audrey Lane, Rowan, and Grayson.[^o20] Cockett is an avid cross-country skier, taking part in the [Canadian Birkebeiner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Birkebeiner) on more than one occasion,[^o21][^o22] as well as volunteering as an instructor.[^o20]

Cockett and his wife have volunteered for numerous environmentally-focused initiatives, including as lecturers for the Calgary Parks Biodiversity Conservation program at [Nose Hill Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nose_Hill_Park),[^o20] and with the [Alberta Wilderness Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberta_Wilderness_Association),[^o20] where his family's work earned them an award from the city of Calgary.[^o20]
