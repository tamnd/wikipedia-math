# Giovanni Felder

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Felder"}

**Giovanni Felder** (18 November 1958 in [Aarau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarau)) is a Swiss mathematical physicist and mathematician, working at ETH Zurich. He specializes in algebraic and geometric properties of [integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable) models of [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Felder attended school in [Lugano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano) and [Willisau District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Willisau_District). He studied physics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), where he graduated with M.Sc. in 1982 and with Ph.D. in 1986.[^o5] His doctoral dissertation, entitled *Renormalization Group, Tree Expansion, and Non-renormalizable Quantum Field Theories*, was supervised by [Jürg Fröhlich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jürg_Fröhlich) (and [Konrad Osterwalder](wikipedia://en/19188374)).

Felder held postdoctoral positions from 1986 to 1988 at [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES), from 1988 to 1989 at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179),[^o5] and from 1989 to 1991 at the Institute of Theoretical Physics, ETH Zurich.

From 1991 to 1994 he became an assistant professor of mathematics at ETH Zurich. From 1994 to 1996 he worked as professor of mathematics at the [University of North Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina). In 1996 he returned at ETH Zurich as professor of mathematics.[^o7] From 2013 to 2019, he was the director of the Institute for Theoretical Studies at ETH Zurich.[^o8]

In 1994 Felder was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#1994,_Zürich) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Zurich.[^o9] He was elected member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2012[^o7] and fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013.[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Felder's research involves mathematical problems motivated by physical ideas.

In the late 1980s Felder did research with [Krzysztof Gawedzki](wikipedia://en/68471920) and [Antti Kupiainen](wikipedia://en/48334698) on the geometry of the [Wess-Zumino-Witten model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wess-Zumino-Witten_model) in [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774).[^o12][^o13] In 1989 he introduced a [BRST](wikipedia://en/6769980) approach to the "minimal two-dimensional conformal invariant models of [Belavin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Belavin), [Polyakov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Markovich_Polyakov) and [Zamolodchikov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Zamolodchikov)."[^o14]

With [Alexander Varchenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Varchenko) and Vitaly Tarasov, Felder did research on various [integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable) models[^o15] in [quantum field theory](wikipedia://en/25267) and [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and resulting [special functions](wikipedia://en/23709110) (such as the [elliptic gamma function](wikipedia://en/2070683),[^o16] elliptic [quantum groups](wikipedia://en/340678),[^o17] and elliptic [Macdonald polynomials](wikipedia://en/10354285)).[^o18]

With [Alberto Cattaneo](wikipedia://en/68432169) in 2000 he gave a [path integral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Path_integral_formulation) interpretation of [Kontsevich](wikipedia://en/104509)'s [deformation quantization of Poisson manifolds](wikipedia://en/26049975)[^o19] as well as a description of the [symplectic groupoid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_groupoid) integrating a [Poisson manifold](wikipedia://en/882902) as an infinite-dimensional [symplectic quotient](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_quotient).[^o20]

He supervised 22 doctoral students as of 2022, including [Thomas Willwacher](wikipedia://en/49217960).[^o6]
