# David Tweedie (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Tweedie"}

**David Tweedie** (1865, [Swinton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swinton,_Scottish_Borders) – 1934, [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh)) was a Scottish mathematician, who taught in [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland) and [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Tweedie was the son of George Tweedie (1837–1905, a schoolmaster, [inspector of the poor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_Poor_Laws) and [registrar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/General_Register_Office)) and Charlotte Lugton (1836–1909). He was the oldest of nine children: Annie (born 1866), [Charles](wikipedia://en/59536038) (born 1868), Euphemia (born 1870), George (born 1872), Andrew (born 1873), Charlotte (born 1875), William (born 1877), and Jessie (born 1879).[^o10][^o9] One of his brothers, [Charles Tweedie](wikipedia://en/59536038), also became a mathematician.[^o8]

He attended Swinton Parish School from 1869 to 1879 and [George Watson's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Watson's_School) in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh) from 1879 to 1881. He matriculated at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) in 1881 at the age of sixteen. He obtained an MA (First class) in Mathematics from University of Edinburgh in 1886 and a BSc (Mathematics) in 1887.[^o9]

He taught mathematics at George Watson's College and in 1892 joined the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940). He was appointed Honorary Treasurer of the society from 1895 to 1898. In 1898 Tweedie went to [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt) to work at the Ras-El-Tin Government School in [Alexandria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandria). Tweedie taught at the Government College in Cairo, and then in 1904 moved to the Ministry of Public Instruction in Cairo.[^o9]

From 1915 to 1923 Tweedie worked as Lecturer in Mathematics at the Royal School of Engineering, Cairo.[^o9]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

In 1905 Tweedie married Elizabeth Mary Inglis and they had two children: Rose Marie (born 1906) and George (born 1907). Elizabeth died in 1931.[^o10][^o9]

In 1932 Tweedie moved to [Llanwern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Llanwern), [Newport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newport,_Wales), [Monmouthshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monmouthshire), [Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wales) and in 1933 he married Gladys Emily Jones. Tweedie died on 22 March 1934 in Edinburgh.[^o9]
