::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Triakis truncated tetrahedral honeycomb"}

The **triakis truncated tetrahedral honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in [Euclidean 3-space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclidean_3-space) made up of [triakis truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/37100405). It was discovered in 1914.[^o5][^o6]

:::

## Voronoi tessellation {#Voronoi_tessellation}

It is the [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) of the [carbon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carbon) atoms in [diamond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond),[^o8][^o9] which lie in the [diamond cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diamond_cubic) crystal structure.

Being composed entirely of [triakis truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/37100405), it is [cell-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell-transitive).

## Relation to quarter cubic honeycomb {#Relation_to_quarter_cubic_honeycomb}

It can be seen as the uniform [quarter cubic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/3757507) where its [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells are subdivided by the center point into 4 shorter tetrahedra, and each adjoined to the adjacent truncated tetrahedral cells.
