# Stacks Project

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stacks Project"}

The **Stacks Project** is an [open source](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_source) [collaborative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collaborative_writing) mathematics textbook writing project with the aim to cover "[algebraic stacks](wikipedia://en/717377) and the [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) needed to define them".[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] , the book consists of 116 chapters[^o7] (excluding the license and index chapters) spreading over 7500 pages. The maintainer of the project, who reviews and accepts the changes, is [Aise Johan de Jong](wikipedia://en/29023548).[^o3][^o4]

:::
