# Mathematical sculpture

*Image: Bathsheba Grossman geometric art.jpg.* Mathematical sculpture by [Bathsheba Grossman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathsheba_Grossman), 2007

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical sculpture"}

A **mathematical sculpture** is a sculpture which uses [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) as an essential concept.[^o2][^o3] [Helaman Ferguson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helaman_Ferguson), [George W. Hart](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_W._Hart), [Bathsheba Grossman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathsheba_Grossman), [Peter Forakis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Forakis) and [Jacobus Verhoeff](wikipedia://en/42255840) are well-known [mathematical sculptors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_artist).

:::
