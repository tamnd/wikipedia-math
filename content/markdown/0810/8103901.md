# Roxana Moslehi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roxana Moslehi"}

**Roxana Moslehi** (رکسانا مصلحی) is an [Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iran)-born [genetic epidemiologist](wikipedia://en/16017459).

:::

Her research is on [cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer) and cancer precursors, including work on [radiation-induced cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiation-induced_cancer) of the eyes,[^o3] genetic and environmental causes of [ovarian cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ovarian_cancer), and ethnic differences in [breast cancer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breast_cancer) incidence.[^o4] She also conducts molecular epidemiologic and genomic studies of other diseases such as [myalgic encephalomyelitis/chronic fatigue syndrome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myalgic_encephalomyelitis/chronic_fatigue_syndrome) (ME/CFS)[^o5] and gestational complications. She is an associate professor in [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997) and [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in the School of Public Health at the [University at Albany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Albany).[^o6]

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Moslehi received her B.Sc. with honors, M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees from the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia);[^o6] her 2000 Ph.D. was supervised by J.M. Friedman and Steven Narod.[^o8] She was a postdoctoral fellow at the [National Institutes of Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Institutes_of_Health) Division of Cancer Epidemiology and Genetics, under the supervision of Mitchell Gail, and while there she received an adjunct assistant professor position at [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University). She is currently an assistant professor at the University of Albany.[^o6]
