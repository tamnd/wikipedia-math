# Lingua generalis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lingua generalis"}

***Lingua generalis*** was an essay written by [Gottfried Leibniz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Leibniz) in February, 1678 in which he presented a [philosophical language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophical_language) he created, which he named **lingua generalis** or **lingua universalis**.[^o2]

:::

Leibniz aimed for his **lingua universalis** to be adopted as a [universal language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universal_language) and be used for calculations.[^o2] As a result of this work, he developed [binary calculus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Binary_calculus).[^o2]
