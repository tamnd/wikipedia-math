# Denis Higgs

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Denis Higgs"}

**Denis A. Higgs** (1932-5-6 – 2011) was a British mathematician, Doctor of Mathematics, and professor of mathematics who specialised in [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [universal algebra](wikipedia://en/45200), and [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869). He wrote one of the most influential papers in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) entitled *A category approach to boolean valued set theory*, which introduced many students to [topos theory](wikipedia://en/21391464).[^o4] He was a member of the [National Committee of Liberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Resistance_Movement) and was an outspoken critic against the [apartheid in South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid_in_South_Africa).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He earned degrees from [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University), St John's College, in England, [University of the Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Witwatersrand) in South Africa, and [McMaster University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McMaster_University) in Canada.[^o2]

In 1962, he became a member of the [National Committee of Liberation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Resistance_Movement), a movement whose main objective was to dismantle the [apartheid in South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid_in_South_Africa).[^o6]

On 28 August 1964, he was kidnapped from his home in [Lusaka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lusaka), Zambia. Then South Africa's Justice Minister [John Vorster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Vorster), who later became Prime Minister, denied any involvement by either the South African government or the police, but accused Higgs of being an accessory to the [bombing that killed Ethel Rhys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_John_Harris) and wounded many others.[^o7]

On 1 September, an unidentified man who claimed to be part of British Protectorates called the [*Rand Daily Mail* newspaper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rand_Daily_Mail) and gave specific details of Denis Higgs's whereabouts. On 2 September, police authorities found him. He was blindfolded and bound in a van over by the Zoo Park area. Although Higgs was wanted for the [Johannesburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannesburg) railway bombing that killed Rhys, authorities declined to prosecute since Higgs had been returned to South Africa via an extrajudicial kidnapping rather than proper extradition channels.[^o7]

After first leaving South Africa to return to Zambia, on 6 September 1964, Higgs fled to London, accompanied by his family. He later stated that he feared for his safety and that of his family, since a day before his departure, the South African government had begun proceedings of extradition for his alleged participation in the explosion at the Johannesburg Railway Station that killed Rhys.[^o6]

## Career {#Career}

He emigrated to Canada in 1966, earning a doctorate from McMaster,[^o2] and held a position as a professor of Pure Mathematics at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), where he wrote one of the most influential papers in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) entitled *A category approach to boolean valued set theory*, which introduced many students to [topos theory](wikipedia://en/21391464).[^o4]

In 1973, he generalised the Rasiowa-Sikorski Boolean models to the case of [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869).[^o9]

His academic papers were published in *[Algebra Universalis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_Universalis)*, the *[Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Pure_and_Applied_Algebra)*, the *[Journal of the Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582#Society_journals)*, the *[Journal of the London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097#Publications)*, and *[Mathematics of Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_of_Computation)*, among other journals.

He died on 25 February 2011.[^o10]
