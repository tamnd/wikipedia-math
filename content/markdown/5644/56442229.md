# Vladimir Alekseev (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Alekseev"}

**Vladimir Mikhailovich Alekseev** (Владимир Михайлович Алексеев, sometimes transliterated as "Alexeyev" or "Alexeev", 17 June 1932, [Bykovo, Ramensky District, Moscow Oblast](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bykovo,_Ramensky_District,_Moscow_Oblast) – 1 December 1980) was a Russian mathematician who specialized in [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182) and [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).[^o2]

:::

He attended secondary school in Moscow at one of the special schools of mathematics affiliated with [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) and participated in several mathematical olympiads. From 1950 he studied at the Faculty of Mathematics and Mechanics at the Moscow State University, where he worked as a student of [Andrei Kolmogorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrei_Kolmogorov) on the asymptotic behavior in the three-body problem of celestial mechanics. Already as an undergraduate, Alekseev proved significant new results on quasi-random motion associated with the three-body problem. This was the subject of his dissertation for the Russian candidate degree (Ph.D.) and then his dissertation in 1969 for the Russian doctorate (higher doctoral degree). From 1957 he taught at Moscow State University.[^o2]

In 1970 Alekseev was an Invited Speaker with talk *Sur l´allure finale du mouvement dans le problème de trois corps* at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Nice.[^o3]

Over a 20-year period, he conducted 3 ongoing seminars: with [Yakov Sinai](wikipedia://en/1751626) on dynamical systems, with V. A. Egorov on celestial mechanics, and with M. Zelikin and [V. M. Tikhomirov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Mikhailovich_Tikhomirov) on variational problems and optimal control.[^o2]
