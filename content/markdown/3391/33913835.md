# Stochastic investment model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Stochastic investment model"}

A **stochastic investment model** tries to forecast how [returns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rate_of_return) and [prices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Price) on different assets or asset classes, (e. g. equities or bonds) vary over time. Stochastic models are not applied for making [point estimation](wikipedia://en/160379) rather [interval estimation](wikipedia://en/160381) and they use different [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895). Investment models can be classified into single-asset and multi-asset models. They are often used for [actuarial](wikipedia://en/43405) work and [financial planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Financial_plan) to allow optimization in [asset allocation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asset_allocation) or [asset-liability-management (ALM)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asset_liability_management).

:::
