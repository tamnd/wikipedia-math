# Otfrid Mittmann

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Otfrid Mittmann"}

**Otfrid Mittmann** (27 December 1908 in [Ruda Śląska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruda_Śląska) — 1998)[^o3][^o4] was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Starting in 1927, he studied mathematics and natural sciences in [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen) and [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig), and got his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in Apr 1935.[^o4] He joined the [Nazi movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_movement) in Oct 1929.[^o5] and published on statistical aspects of [Nazi eugenics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_eugenics). After the war, he published in Göttingen[^o6][^o7] and [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn).[^o8][^o9]

:::
