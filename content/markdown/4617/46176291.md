# Takao Nishizeki

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Takao Nishizeki"}

**Takao Nishizeki** was a Japanese mathematician and computer scientist who specialized in [graph algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_algorithm) and [graph drawing](wikipedia://en/325813).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nishizeki was born in 1947 in [Fukushima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fukushima,_Fukushima), and was a student at [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University), earning a bachelor's degree in 1969, a master's in 1971, and a doctorate in 1974. He continued at Tohoku as a faculty member, and became a full professor there in 1988.[^o4] He was the Dean of the Graduate School of Information Sciences, Tohoku University, from April 2008 to March 2010. He retired in 2010, becoming a professor emeritus at Tohoku University, but continued teaching as a professor at [Kwansei Gakuin University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kwansei_Gakuin_University) until March 2015.[^o5] He was an Auditor of Japan Advanced Institute of Science and Technology from April 2016 to October 2018.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Nishizeki made significant contributions to algorithms for [series–parallel graphs](wikipedia://en/9938559),[^n1] finding [cliques](wikipedia://en/524466) in [sparse graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sparse_graph),[^n2] [planarity testing](wikipedia://en/15972636)[^n3] and the secret sharing with any access structure. He is the co-author of two books on [planar graphs](wikipedia://en/24314) and [graph drawing](wikipedia://en/325813).[^o10]

In 1990, Nishizeki founded the annual [International Symposium on Algorithms and Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Symposium_on_Algorithms_and_Computation) (ISAAC).[^o12]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

At the 18th ISAAC symposium, in 2007, a workshop was held to celebrate his 60th birthday.[^o12]

In 1996, he became a [life fellow of the IEEE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) "for contributions to graph algorithms with applications to physical design of electronic systems."[^o13] In 1996 he was selected as a fellow of the [Association for Computing Machinery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Association_for_Computing_Machinery) "for contributions to the design and analysis of efficient algorithms for planar graphs, network flows and VLSI routing".[^o14] Nishizeki was also a foreign fellow of the [Bangladesh Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bangladesh_Academy_of_Sciences);[^o15] one of his students and frequent co-authors, [Md. Saidur Rahman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saidur_Rahman_%28professor,_Bangladesh_University_of_Engineering_and_Technology%29), is from Bangladesh.
