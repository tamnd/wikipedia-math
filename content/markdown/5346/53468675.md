# Rina Zazkis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rina Zazkis"}

**Rina Zazkis** is a Canadian scholar in education and mathematics. She is a professor in the Faculty of Education at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) and a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in STEM Teaching and Learning.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Zazkis began her academic career at [Simon Fraser University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon_Fraser_University) (SFU) in 1991 as an assistant professor. She was eventually promoted to full professor in the Faculty of Education by 2000.[^o4] In 2011, she was a recipient of the Dean of Graduate Studies Awards for Excellence.[^o5]

In 2017, she was appointed a [Canada Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada_Research_Chair) in STEM Teaching and Learning.[^o6] Two years later she received the 2019 Partners In Research National Award.[^o7]
