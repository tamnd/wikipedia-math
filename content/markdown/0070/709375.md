# Lars Knudsen (cryptographer)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lars Knudsen"}

**Lars Ramkilde Knudsen** (born 21 February 1962) is a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) professor in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432).

:::

## Academic {#Academic}

Knudsen enrolled at [Aarhus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aarhus_University) in 1984 studying [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), gaining an MSc in 1992 and a PhD in 1994. In 1999 he became a professor at the [University of Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bergen), [Norway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) and in 2001 he became a professor at the Technical University of Denmark (DTU). [Ivan Damgård](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Damgård) was Lars' mentor during his studies at Aarhus University.

## Publications {#Publications}

Knudsen has published papers on cryptanalysis of cryptographic primitives, including the R-MAC scheme, the [SHA-1](wikipedia://en/26672) and [MD2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MD2_%28cryptography%29) hash functions, and some block [ciphers](wikipedia://en/5244): [DES](wikipedia://en/7978), [DFC](wikipedia://en/7955867), [IDEA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IDEA_%28cipher%29), [ICE](wikipedia://en/1188540), [LOKI](wikipedia://en/647844), [MISTY](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MISTY), [RC2](wikipedia://en/632592), [RC5](wikipedia://en/102600), [RC6](wikipedia://en/160216), [SC2000](wikipedia://en/7942288), [Skipjack](wikipedia://en/474702), [Square](wikipedia://en/613945) and [SAFER](wikipedia://en/630497).

Knudsen was involved in designing ciphers: [Serpent](wikipedia://en/385154) and the lightweight cipher [PRESENT](wikipedia://en/34917449) included in ISO/IEC 29192-2:2019. He was involved in designing [Grøstl](wikipedia://en/20394803), a hash function which was one of the submissions to the [NIST SHA-3 competition](wikipedia://en/20051952) (it was not the winner).

He introduced the technique of [impossible differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/3329225)[^o5] and [integral cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/7210758).

## Industry {#Industry}

Knudsen is a co-founder of the communications companies Dencrypt A/S and PiiGuard ApS. Knudsen has been an editor for journals such as IEEE Transactions on Information Forensics and Security and Design, Codes and Cryptography. He has also chaired a number of international cryptography conferences, including FSE 1999, Eurocrypt 2002 and SAC 2012.

## Awards and recognitions {#Awards_and_recognitions}

In August 2021, Knudsen was knighted as Ridder af Dannebrog. He has been a Fellow of the International Association of Cryptologic Research (IACR) since 2013 and a member of the Danish Academy of Technical Sciences (ATV) since 2005.
