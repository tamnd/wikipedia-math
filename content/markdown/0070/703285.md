# Delfino Codazzi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Delfino Codazzi"}

**Delfino Codazzi** (7 March 1824 in [Lodi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodi,_Lombardy) – 21 July 1873 in [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia)) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2][^o3]

:::

He made some important contributions to the [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) of surfaces, such as the [Codazzi–Mainardi equations](wikipedia://en/9633335).

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated in mathematics at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia), where he was a pupil of [Antonio Bordoni](wikipedia://en/36553947). For a long period Codazzi taught first at the Ginnasio Liceale of [Lodi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lodi,_Lombardy), then at the liceo of [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia). Meanwhile, he devoted himself to research in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

In 1865, he was appointed professor of complementary algebra and [analytic geometry](wikipedia://en/2202) at University of Pavia. He remained in his position at Pavia until his death in 1873.

He also obtained results concerning isometric lines, [geodesic triangles](wikipedia://en/15513875), [equiareal mapping](wikipedia://en/8953468) and the stability of floating bodies.
