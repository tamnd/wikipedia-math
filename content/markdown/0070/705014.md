# List of surfaces

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of surfaces"}

This is a **list of [surfaces](wikipedia://en/4342970)** in mathematics. They are divided into [minimal surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_surfaces), [ruled surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruled_surfaces), [non-orientable surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-orientable_surfaces), [quadrics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quadrics), [pseudospherical surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudospherical_surfaces), [algebraic surfaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraic_surfaces), and other types of surfaces.

:::
