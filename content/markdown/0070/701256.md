# Thorvald N. Thiele

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thorvald N. Thiele"}

**Thorvald Nicolai Thiele** (24 December 1838 – 26 September 1910) was a [Danish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denmark) [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) and director of the [Copenhagen Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copenhagen_Observatory).[^o13] He was also an [actuary](wikipedia://en/43405) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), most notable for his work in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), [interpolation](wikipedia://en/14569) and the [three-body problem](wikipedia://en/23859945#Three-body_problem).

:::

Thiele made notable contributions to the statistical study of random time series and introduced the [cumulants](wikipedia://en/359684) and [likelihood functions](wikipedia://en/44968), and was considered to be one of the greatest statisticians of all time by [Ronald Fisher](wikipedia://en/140824).[^o3] In the early 1900s he also developed and proposed a generalisation of [approval voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Approval_voting) to multiple winner elections called [sequential proportional approval voting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequential_proportional_approval_voting),[^o4] which was briefly used for party lists in [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) when proportional representation was introduced in 1909.

Thiele also was a founder and Mathematical Director of the [Hafnia Insurance Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Codan_A/S) and led the founding of the [Danish Society of Actuaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danish_Society_of_Actuaries). It was through his [insurance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Insurance) work that he came into contact with fellow mathematician [Jørgen Pedersen Gram](wikipedia://en/851232).

Thiele was the father of astronomer [Holger Thiele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holger_Thiele).

The main-belt asteroids [843 Nicolaia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/843_Nicolaia) (discovered by his son Holger) and [1586 Thiele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1586_Thiele) are named in his honour.[^o13]
