# Marius Lacombe

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Marius Lacombe"}

**Marius Lacombe** (7 February 1862 – 19 March 1938) was a Swiss [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Lacombe studied mathematics at Engineers Department of the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^n1] From 1890 to 1894 he was teaching [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) in the [university of Lausanne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lausanne).[^n2] In 1894 he was appointed professor at ETH Zurich[^n3] to fill a newly created chair of descriptive geometry in French language.[^n4] After fourteen years in Zurich, in 1908 he returned to university of Lausane, where he retired in 1927.[^n5]

He was more a teacher than a researcher. His only known works deal on pedagogy of mathematics.[^n6][^n5] In 1896 he was one of the organizers of the first International Congress of Mathematicians.
