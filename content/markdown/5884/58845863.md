# Caterina Consani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Caterina Consani"}

*Image: Consani2.jpg.* [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach), 2011

**Caterina** (**Katia**) **Consani** (born 1963) is an Italian mathematician specializing in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). She is a professor of mathematics at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

*Image: Consani-Scholten.png.* A slice of the [Consani–Scholten quintic](wikipedia://en/58873681)

Consani is the namesake of the [Consani–Scholten quintic](wikipedia://en/58873681), a [quintic threefold](wikipedia://en/23562429) that she described with Jasper Scholten in 2001,Q3 and of the Connes–Consani plane connection, a relationship between the [field with one element](wikipedia://en/13798040) and certain [group actions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_action_%28mathematics%29) on [projective spaces](wikipedia://en/242135) investigated by Consani with [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340).AC She is also known for her work with [Matilde Marcolli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matilde_Marcolli) on [Arakelov theory](wikipedia://en/16364229) and [noncommutative geometry](wikipedia://en/295917).NG

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Consani was born January 9, 1963, in [Chiavari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiavari). She earned a [laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) in mathematics in 1986 at the [University of Genoa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Genoa), a doctorate (*dottorato di ricerca*) in 1992 from the University of Genoa and the [University of Turin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Turin), and a second doctorate in 1996 from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). Her first doctoral dissertation was *Teoria dell’ intersezione e K-teoria su varietà singolari*, supervised by Claudio Pedrini, and her second dissertation was *Double Complexes and Euler L-factors on Degenerations of Algebraic Varieties*, supervised by [Spencer Bloch](wikipedia://en/25794633).

She was a [C. L. E. Moore instructor](wikipedia://en/21957255) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) from 1996 to 1999, overlapping with a research visit in 1998 to the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). After additional postdoctoral research at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), she became an assistant professor at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) in 2000, and moved to Johns Hopkins in 2005.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Consani was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2024 class of fellows.
