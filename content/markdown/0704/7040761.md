# Arthur P. Dempster

::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Arthur P. Dempster"}

**Arthur Pentland Dempster** (October 8, 1929 – January 30, 2026) was an American mathematician who was professor emeritus in the [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) department of statistics. He was one of four faculty members composing the department when it was founded in 1957.

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Dempster received his B.A. in mathematics and physics (1952) and M.A. in mathematics (1953), both from the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto). He obtained his Ph.D. in mathematical statistics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1956. His thesis, titled *The two-sample multivariate problem in the degenerate case*, was written under the supervision of [John Tukey](wikipedia://en/415974).

Dempster died on January 30, 2026, at the age of 96.[^o7]

## Academic works {#Academic_works}

Among his contributions to statistics are the initial theory that was expanded into the [Dempster–Shafer theory](wikipedia://en/226660) with [Glenn Shafer](wikipedia://en/226650), and the [expectation-maximization (EM) algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expectation-maximization_algorithm).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Dempster was a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition) in 1951.[^o13] He was elected as an [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) Fellow in 1964,[^o2] an [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) Fellow in 1963,[^o3] and an [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) Fellow in 1997.[^o14] In 2009 he received a honorary doctorate from [Prague University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague_University_of_Economics_and_Business).[^o15]
