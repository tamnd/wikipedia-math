# Glossary of shapes with metaphorical names

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Glossary of shapes with metaphorical names"}

*Image: Gaussian 2d.svg.* Gaussian curve with a two-dimensional domain

Many **shapes have metaphorical names**, i.e., their names are [metaphors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphor): these [shapes](wikipedia://en/169191) are named after a most common object that has it. For example, "U-shape" is a shape that resembles the letter [U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U), a [bell-shaped curve](wikipedia://en/245552) has the shape of the vertical cross section of a [bell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_%28instrument%29), etc. These terms may variously refer to objects, their [cross sections](wikipedia://en/1337370) or [projections](wikipedia://en/3909097).

## Types of shapes {#Types_of_shapes}

Some of these names are "classical terms", i.e., words of [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin) or [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek) [etymology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Etymology). Others are [English language](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) constructs (although the base words may have non-English etymology). In some disciplines, where shapes of subjects in question are a very important consideration, the shape naming may be quite elaborate, see, e.g., [the taxonomy of shapes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaf_shape) of [plant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant) [leaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leaves) in [botany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany).

- **[Ampersand curve](wikipedia://en/79847126)**
- **[Arbelos](wikipedia://en/887197)**, from [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek) *árbylos*, meaning "shoemaker's knife"
- **[Astroid](wikipedia://en/769163)**
- **[Aquiline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aquiline_nose),** shaped like an eagle's beak (as in a Roman nose)
- [Bathtub curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bathtub_curve)
- [Basket-handle arch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basket-handle_arch)
- **[Bell-shaped curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_curve)**
- **Biconic shape**, a shape in a way opposite to the hourglass: it is based on two oppositely oriented [cones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cone_%28geometry%29) or [truncated cones](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_cone) with their bases joined; the cones are not necessarily the same
  - **Bowtie** shape, in two dimensions
  - [Atmospheric reentry apparatus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atmospheric_reentry#Biconic)
  - Centerbody of an [inlet cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inlet_cone) in [ramjets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramjet)
- **Bow shape**
  - [Bow curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bow_curve)
- **Bullet Nose**[^o4] an open-ended hourglass
- **Butterfly**
  - [Butterfly curve (algebraic)](wikipedia://en/5203165)[^o5]
  - [Butterfly curve (transcendental)](wikipedia://en/2748941)[^o5]
  - [Butterfly lemma](wikipedia://en/877209)
  - [Butterfly graph](wikipedia://en/4372111)
- **[Catenary](wikipedia://en/7163)**, derived from the Latin word *catēna*, which means "[chain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chain)".
- **[Cissoid](wikipedia://en/899085)** (from [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek_language) κισσοειδής *(kissoeidēs)* '[ivy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivy)-shaped')
- **[Cocked hat curve](wikipedia://en/2737646)**, also known as **Bicorn**[^o6]
- **[Cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cone_%28geometry%29)** (from the Greek word for « [pine cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pine_cone) »)
- **[Doughnut shape](wikipedia://en/74800)**
- **[Dragon curve](wikipedia://en/367064)**
- **[Egg-shaped](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg-shaped)**, see "Oval", below
  - [Superegg](wikipedia://en/10405346)
  - [Moss's Egg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moss's_egg)
- **Elephant**
  - [Elephant curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elephant_curve)
  - [Von Neumann's elephant](wikipedia://en/71711287)
- **[Fish curve](wikipedia://en/4066072)**
- **[Geoid](wikipedia://en/176244)** (From Greek Ge (γη) for "[Earth](wikipedia://en/9228)"), the term specifically introduced to denote the approximation of the shape of the [Earth](wikipedia://en/9228), which is approximately spherical, [but not exactly so](wikipedia://en/233668)
- [**Heart** shape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heart_shape), long been used for its varied symbolism
  - [Cardioid](wikipedia://en/642101)
- **Hedgehog**
  - [Hedgehog (geometry)](wikipedia://en/65764375)
  - [Hedgehog space](wikipedia://en/8016170)
- **Horseshoe-shaped** or **hippocrepiform**, resembling a [horseshoe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horseshoe), cf. [horseshoe (disambiguation)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horseshoe_%28disambiguation%29). In [botany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany), also called *lecotropal* (see below)
- **Hourglass shape** or **hourglass figure**, the one that resembles an [hourglass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hourglass); nearly symmetric shape wide at its ends and narrow in the middle; some flat shapes may be alternatively compared to the figure eight or hourglass
  - **Dog bone** shape, an hourglass with rounded ends[^o7]
    - [Dogbone space](wikipedia://en/25781757)
  - [Hourglass corset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hourglass_corset)
  - [Ntama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Talking_drum)
  - [Engraved Hourglass Nebula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engraved_Hourglass_Nebula)
- **[Inverted bell](wikipedia://en/7007010)**
- **[Kite](wikipedia://en/84840)**
- **Lecotropal**, in [botany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Botany), shaped like a horseshoe (see *horseshoe-shaped*, above). From Greek λέκος dish + -τροπος turning[^o9][^o10]
- **[Lens](wikipedia://en/2038396)** or **[Vesica](wikipedia://en/471830)** shape (the latter taking its name from the shape of the [lentil](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lentil) seed); see also [mandorla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandorla), almond-shaped
  - [Lens space](wikipedia://en/885725)
- **[Lune](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lune_%28mathematics%29)**, from the Latin word for the Moon
- **Maltese Cross curve**[^o11]
- **[Mandorla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mandorla)**, almond-shaped (Italian for "almond"), often used as a frame in mediaeval Christian iconography.
- **Mushroom shape**, which became infamous as a result of the [mushroom cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mushroom_cloud)[^o12]
- [Nephroid](wikipedia://en/843192) (from [Ancient Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Greek_language) ὁ νεφρός *(ho nephros)* '[kidney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kidney)-shaped')
- **[Oval](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oval_%28geometry%29)** (from the Latin "ovum" for [egg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_%28biology%29)), a descriptive term applied to several kinds of "rounded" shapes, including the [egg shape](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egg_shape)
- **Pear** shaped, in reference to the shape of a [pear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pear), i.e., a generally rounded shape, tapered towards the top and more spherical/circular at the bottom
- **Rod**, a 3-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398), [solid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Solid) (filled) [cylinder](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cylinder_%28geometry%29)
  - [Rod shaped bacteria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bacillus)
- **Rose**, like the petals of a flower
  - [Rose curve](wikipedia://en/727472)
  - [Rose (topology)](wikipedia://en/3526954)
- **[Salinon](wikipedia://en/16922787)**, meaning 'salt-cellar' in Greek
- **Scarabaeus curve**[^o13] resembling a scarab
- **[Serpentine](wikipedia://en/4687116)**, shaped like a snake
  - [Serpentine curve](wikipedia://en/2738036)
- **Snowflake**, fractal branching structure with rotational symmetry (often of order 6)
  - [Koch snowflake](wikipedia://en/46959)
  - [Flowsnake](wikipedia://en/3918047)
  - [Mosely snowflake](wikipedia://en/48794726)
- **[Stadium](wikipedia://en/38383047)**, two half-circles joined by straight sides[^o14]
- **Stirrup curve**[^o15]
- **[Star](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_%28polygon%29)**, a figure with multiple sharp points emanating from a common center
  - [Star domain](wikipedia://en/5983414)
  - [Astroid](wikipedia://en/769163)
- **[Sunburst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sunburst)**
  - [Farey sunburst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farey_sunburst)
- **[Tomahawk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tomahawk_%28geometric_shape%29)**
- **Tree**, a figure with branches that do not rejoin that may also have branches that do not rejoin and so on.
  - [Tree (graph theory)](wikipedia://en/48560)
- **[Ungula](wikipedia://en/23117441)**, shaped like a horse's hoof

## Numbers and letters {#Numbers_and_letters}

- **A-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A)
  - [A-frame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-frame), the shape of a common structure that resembles the capital letter [A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A)
  - [A-frame house](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-frame_house), a common style of house construction
  - [A-line skirt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A-line_skirt) or dress
- **B-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [B](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B)
- **C-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [C](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C)
- **D-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [D](wikipedia://en/8123)
  - [D-ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D-ring)
- **Deltoid**, the shape that resembles the Greek capital letter [Δ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Δ)
  - [Deltahedron](wikipedia://en/493995)
  - [Y-Δ transform](wikipedia://en/268145)
  - [Deltoid muscle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deltoid_muscle)
  - [River delta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/River_delta)
  - [Delta wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delta_wing)
- **E-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [E](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E)
  - [Magnetic cores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetic_core) of [transformers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformer) may be E-shaped
  - A number of notable buildings have an E-shaped [floorplan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Floorplan)
- **F-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [F](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/F)
- **Figure 0**, the shape that resembles the numeral [0](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/0_%28number%29), more commonly referred to as an O-shape, see below
- **Figure 1**, the shape that resembles the numeral [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_%28number%29)
- **Figure 2**, the shape that resembles the numeral [2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2_%28number%29)

- **Figure 5**, the shape that resembles the numeral [5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_%28number%29)
- **Figure 6**, the shape that resembles the numeral [6](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/6_%28number%29)
- **Figure 7**, the shape that resembles the numeral [7](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7_%28number%29)
- **Figure 8**, the shape that resembles the numeral [8](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8_%28number%29)
  - [Figure eight knot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Figure-eight_knot)
  - [Lemniscate](wikipedia://en/5210590)
- **Figure 9**, the shape that resembles the numeral [9](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/9_%28number%29)
- **G-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [G](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/G)
- **H-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [H](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H)
  - [H-beam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H-beam), a [beam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beam_%28structure%29) with H-shaped section
  - [Goals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goal_%28sport%29) in several sports ([gridiron football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gridiron_football) (old style), [Gaelic football](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gaelic_football), [rugby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rugby_football), [hurling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurling)) are described as "H-shaped"
  - [H topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_filter_topology) in electronic filter design
  - Also see [Balbis](wikipedia://en/28325493)
- **I-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I) in a [serif font](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serif_font), i.e., with horizontal strokes
  - [\-beam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I-beam), a beam with an -shaped section
  - The court in the [Mesoamerican ballgame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mesoamerican_ballgame) is I-shaped
- **J-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [J](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J)
  - [J curve](wikipedia://en/2839796)
- **K-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [K](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K)
  - [K-shaped recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recession_shapes)
  - [K turn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turning_in_the_road)
- **[Kappa curve](wikipedia://en/1037324)**, resembling the [Greek letter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_alphabet) [ϰ (kappa)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kappa_%28letter%29).
- **L-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [L](wikipedia://en/4860524)
  - [L-beam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-beam), a beam with an L-shaped section
  - [The L-Shaped Room](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_L-Shaped_Room)
  - [L game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L_game)
  - [L-shaped recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L-shaped_recession)
- **Lemniscate**, the shape that resembles the [infinity symbol](wikipedia://en/1605389)
- **M-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [M](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M) (interchangeable with the **W-shape**)
- **N-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [N](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/N) (interchangeable with the **Z-shape**)
- **O-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [O](wikipedia://en/22314)
  - [O-ring](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/O-ring)
- **P-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [P](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P)
  - [P-trap](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-trap), a P-shaped pipe under a sink or basin
- **Pi-shape**, the shape that resembles the Greek capital letter [Π](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi_%28letter%29)
  - [Π topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_filter_topology) in electronic filter design
- **Q-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [Q](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Q)
- **R-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [R](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R)
- **S-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [S](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S)
  - The [sigmoid colon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigmoid_colon), an S-shaped bend in the human intestine
  - [Sigmoid function](wikipedia://en/87210)
  - [S-twist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S-twist), contrasted with Z-twist for yarn
- **T-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T)
  - [T junction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T_junction)
  - [T topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_filter_topology#Bridged-T_topologies) in electronic filter design
  - [T-shaped (chemistry)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-shaped_%28chemistry%29)
  - [T-shaped skills](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-shaped_skills), a format for [résumés](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Résumés)
  - [T-shirt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-shirt)
  - [T-pose](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/T-pose), used in computer animation models
- **U-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [U](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U)
  - [U-shaped valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-shaped_valley)
  - [U-turn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-turn)
  - [U-shaped recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U-shaped_recession)
- **Hyoid**, the shape that resembles the Greek letter [υ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Υ)
  - [Hyoid bone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyoid_bone)
- **V-shape**, the shape that resembles the letter [V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V), also known as the [Chevron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevron_%28insignia%29) (which includes the inverted-V shape)
  - [V-shaped valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V-shaped_valley)
  - [V-shaped recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V-shaped_recession)
  - [V-shaped body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_shape) – male human body shape with broad shoulders
  - [V-shaped passage grave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V-shaped_passage_grave)
  - [V sign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V_sign)
  - [V-tail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V-tail)
- **W-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [W](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W) (interchangeable with the **M-shape**)
  - [W-shaped recession](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W-shaped_recession)
- **X-shape**, the shape that resembles the letter [X](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/X)
  - [Saltire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saltire)
  - [X topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_filter_topology) in electronic filter design
- **Chiasm**, crossings that resemble the Greek letter [χ](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Χ)
  - [Chiasmus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiasmus)
  - [Chiastic structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chiastic_structure)
  - [Optic chiasm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optic_chiasm)
- **Y-shape**, the shape that resembles the letter [Y](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y)
  - [Y-front](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Y-front) briefs
  - [Pall](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pall_%28heraldry%29)
  - [Y-Δ transform](wikipedia://en/268145)
- **Z-shape**, the shape that resembles the capital letter [Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z) (interchangeable with the **N-shape**)
  - [Z-twist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Z-twist), contrasted with S-twist for yarn
