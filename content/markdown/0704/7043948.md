# Fredholm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fredholm"}

**Fredholm** is a Swedish surname. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Erik Ivar Fredholm](wikipedia://en/669963) (1866–1927), Swedish mathematician
  - [Fredholm alternative](wikipedia://en/3050954), in mathematics
  - [Fredholm determinant](wikipedia://en/2685562), in mathematics
  - [Fredholm integral equation](wikipedia://en/363377), in mathematics
  - [Fredholm kernel](wikipedia://en/2702780), in mathematics
  - [Fredholm module](wikipedia://en/20986604), In noncommutative geometry
  - [Fredholm number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_number), in number theory, apparently not in fact studied by Fredholm
  - [Fredholm operator](wikipedia://en/279722), in mathematics
  - [Fredholm's theorem](wikipedia://en/1955708), in mathematics
  - [Analytic Fredholm theorem](wikipedia://en/12312576), in mathematics
  - [Fredholm theory](wikipedia://en/4355120), in mathematics
  - [Fredholm (crater)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredholm_%28crater%29), a small lunar impact crater
  - [21659 Fredholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/21659_Fredholm) (1999 PR3), main-belt asteroid discovered in 1999 by P. G. Comba
- Ludwig Fredholm (1830–1891), Swedish industrialist
- [Gert Fredholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gert_Fredholm) (born 1941), Danish film director and screenwriter
- [Patrik Fredholm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patrik_Fredholm) (born 1978), Swedish footballer
