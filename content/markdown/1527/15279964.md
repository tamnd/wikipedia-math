# Daoxing Xia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daoxing Xia"}

Xia Daoxing **Daoxing Xia** () is a [Chinese American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is professor emeritus at the Department of Mathematics, [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University) in the United States.[^o2] He was elected an academician of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1980.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Xia was born on October 20, 1930, in [Taizhou, Jiangsu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taizhou,_Jiangsu).[^o4] He pursued his undergraduate studies at the Department of Mathematics at [Shandong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University) and subsequently obtained his postgraduate degree from the Department of Mathematics at [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) in 1952.[^o5] His advisor was [Chen Jiangong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chen_Jiangong), a pioneer of modern Chinese mathematics who was then dean of the Department of Mathematics.

In 1952, Xia went to [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University) in [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai) as an assistant. In 1954 he became a lecturer and in 1956 he received a position as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor). In September 1957, he was sent to Moscow State University in the USSR, where he conducted one and a half years of research with the prominent mathematician [I.M. Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643).

In 1978, Xia obtained his professorship at Fudan University and rose to the position of vice director of the university's Mathematics Research Institute. In 1980 he was elected a member of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences). He was also an adjunct professor in the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) Mathematical Physics Research Institute and the Department of Mathematics at [Shandong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_University). Xia was a visiting professor of many universities and gave lectures. During the 1983–1984 academic year, Xia served as a visiting member at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton.

In 1984, Xia become a professor at the Department of Mathematics, [Vanderbilt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vanderbilt_University). He retired in 2017.

His son, Jingbo Xia, is a professor of mathematics at [SUNY Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo). His son-in-law, Bennett Chow, is a professor of mathematics at [UC San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego).
