# Demographics of Glasgow

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Demographics of Glasgow"}

*Image: Population Density Glasgow 2011 Census.png.* Population density in the 2011 census in Glasgow.

**[Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glasgow)** is the most populous city in [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland) and the fourth most populous city in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).

*Image: Glasgow's industry sectors over time.svg.* Industry sectors of Glasgow overtime

:::

## Population {#Population}

Glasgow's total population, according to the [2011 UK census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2011_UK_census), was 593,245. The [population density](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Population_density) was 593245.

## National identity {#National_identity}

The 2022 census found that of the 620,756 people residing in Glasgow, 62.3% identified with the [Scottish identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scottish_national_identity) only, 11.8% identified with the British identity only and 7.1% identified with both identities. 2.5% identified with other UK identities (including English identity), 12.9% identified with an other identity only and the remaining 0.5% identified with at least one UK identity and an other identity.[^o75]

:::
