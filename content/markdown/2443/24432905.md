# NUTS statistical regions of Liechtenstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="NUTS statistical regions of Liechtenstein"}

As a member of the [EFTA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Free_Trade_Association), **[Liechtenstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liechtenstein)** (LI) is included in the **[Nomenclature of Territorial Units for Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nomenclature_of_Territorial_Units_for_Statistics) (NUTS)**. The three NUTS levels all correspond to the country itself:

:::

- NUTS-1: LI0 Liechtenstein
- NUTS-2: LI00 Liechtenstein
- NUTS-3: LI000 Liechtenstein

Below the NUTS levels, there are [LAU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local_administrative_unit): [municipalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Municipalities_of_Liechtenstein).
