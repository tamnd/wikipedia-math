# Hsien Chung Wang

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hsien Chung Wang"}

**Hsien Chung** (or **Hsien-Chung** or **Hsien-chung**) **Wang** (王宪钟 *Wang Xian Zhong*; 18 April 1918 in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing) – 25 June 1978 in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City)) was a Chinese-American mathematician, specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945), and [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Hsien Chung Wang was part of a family from [Shandong Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shandong_Province) that had produced distinguished scholars for several generations, including the archeologist [Wang Yirong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wang_Yirong).[^o4]

He studied in [Tianjin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianjin) at [Nankai High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianjin_Nankai_High_School) and in 1936 he matriculated at [Tsing Hua University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsing_Hua_University) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing). Due to the outbreak of the [Second Sino-Japanese War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_Sino-Japanese_War) in 1937, the university had to be moved to Southwest China. Wang graduated in 1941 and began to study under [Chern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shing-Shen_Chern). He was awarded a master's degree in 1944 and began teaching in a school.[^o4][^o6]

However, after one year, he was awarded a [British Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Council) Scholarship to continue his studies in England. He studied first in [Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield) and then at [Manchester University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) under [Newman](wikipedia://en/19867), and received a Ph.D. in 1948.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

Returning to China, Wang took a research position at the [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica), which was soon moved to [Taiwan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taiwan) following the end of the [Chinese Civil War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Civil_War).[^o4][^o5]

In 1949 Wang moved to the United States, obtaining a lecturer position at [Louisiana State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louisiana_State_University). In the subsequent years, he hold a series of temporary positions at the [Institute for Advanced Study at Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Advanced_Study,_Princeton), the [Alabama Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alabama_Polytechnic_Institute), the [University of Washington in Seattle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington_in_Seattle) and [Columbia University in New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University_in_New_York).[^o5][^o6]

He obtained his first permanent position in 1957 at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University). In 1966 he accepted a professorship at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), which he hold until his sudden death from leukemia.[^o5][^o6]

## Research and awards {#Research_and_awards}

While he was a PhD student in Manchester,[^o8] Wang worked in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801). He discovered an [exact sequence](wikipedia://en/138215) (called nowadays the [Wang sequence](wikipedia://en/803894#Wang_sequence)) involving the [homology groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_groups) associated with [fiber bundles](wikipedia://en/276526) over spheres.[^o4][^o5]

At the same time,[^o9] he also solved an open problem arising from the work of [Hopf](wikipedia://en/543082) and [Samelson](wikipedia://en/17290837):[^o10] determine the closed subgroups of maximal [rank](wikipedia://en/18720088) in a [compact Lie group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Lie_group).[^o4][^o5] In 1952[^o11] he showed that any [connected](wikipedia://en/6233) [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [metric space](wikipedia://en/20018), such that any two pairs of points separated by the same distance are related by an [isometry](wikipedia://en/254777), is a [homogeneous space](wikipedia://en/363325) of a compact Lie group.[^o4][^o5]

Since 1956[^o12] and until the end of his career he worked mostly on [discrete subgroups](wikipedia://en/468536) of [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945).[^o4][^o5]

Among other topics in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625),[^o4][^o13][^o5] he worked on [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742),[^o14][^o15] he described a construction of [contact manifolds](wikipedia://en/476398) which is now called the [Boothby-Wang construction](wikipedia://en/84007440),[^o16] and he studied [transformation groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transformation_groups) of [n-spheres](wikipedia://en/39782).[^o17]

Wang was an Invited Speaker at the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1958 in Edinburgh.[^o18] He was a [Guggenheim Fellow for the academic year 1960–1961](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Guggenheim_Fellowships_awarded_in_1960).[^o19]

His doctoral students include [J. Stephen Halperin](wikipedia://en/58919732).[^o20]
