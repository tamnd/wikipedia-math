# Ralph Ernest Powers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ralph Ernest Powers"}

**Ralph Ernest Powers** (April 27, 1875 – January 31, 1952) was an American amateur mathematician who worked on [prime numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prime_numbers).

:::

He is credited with discovering the [Mersenne primes](wikipedia://en/18908) $M_{89}$ and $M_{107}$, in 1911 and 1914 respectively.[^o2][^o3] In 1934 he verified that the Mersenne number $M_{241}$ is composite.[^o4]

## Life {#Life}

Powers was born in [Fountain, Colorado Territory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fountain,_Colorado). Details of his life are little-known,[^o6] though he appears to have been an employee of the [Denver and Rio Grande Western Railroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Denver_and_Rio_Grande_Western_Railroad).[^o7]

Soon after Powers announced the discovery of $M_{107}$, the Frenchman E. Fauquembergue claimed that he had discovered it earlier, but many of Fauquembergue's other claims were later demonstrated as erroneous; thus, many prefer recognizing Powers as the discoverer, including the well-known Internet resource the [PrimePages](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PrimePages).

After his own discoveries of Mersenne primes in 1911 and 1914, no [Mersenne primes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mersenne_primes) were discovered until [Raphael M. Robinson](wikipedia://en/693495) used a computer to find the next two, on January 30, 1952, the night before Powers's death.[^o6]
