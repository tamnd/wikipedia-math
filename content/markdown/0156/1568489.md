# Todd class

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Todd class"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Todd class** is a certain construction now considered a part of the theory in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801) of [characteristic classes](wikipedia://en/363530). The Todd class of a [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524) can be defined by means of the theory of [Chern classes](wikipedia://en/294349), and is encountered where Chern classes exist — most notably in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), the theory of [complex manifolds](wikipedia://en/509742) and [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). In rough terms, a Todd class acts like a reciprocal of a Chern class, or stands in relation to it as a [conormal bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conormal_bundle) does to a [normal bundle](wikipedia://en/2370200).

:::

The Todd class plays a fundamental role in generalising the classical [Riemann–Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/247261) to higher dimensions, in the [Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/2892412) and the [Grothendieck–Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch theorem](wikipedia://en/1532606).

## History {#History}

It is named for [J. A. Todd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._A._Todd), who introduced a special case of the concept in algebraic geometry in 1937, before the Chern classes were defined. The geometric idea involved is sometimes called the **Todd-Eger class**. The general definition in higher dimensions is due to [Friedrich Hirzebruch](wikipedia://en/987495).

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

To define the Todd class $\operatorname{td}(E)$ where $E$ is a complex vector bundle on a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) $X$, it is usually possible to limit the definition to the case of a [Whitney sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whitney_sum) of [line bundles](wikipedia://en/361366), by means of a general device of characteristic class theory, the use of [Chern roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_roots) (aka, the [splitting principle](wikipedia://en/15958803)). For the definition, let[^o5]

$$Q(x) = \frac{x}{1 - e^{-x}}=\sum_{i=0}^\infty \frac{B_i}{i!}x^i = 1 +\dfrac{x}{2}+\dfrac{x^2}{12}-\dfrac{x^4}{720}+\cdots$$

be the [formal power series](wikipedia://en/60012) with the property that the coefficient of $x^n$ in $Q(x)^{n+1}$ is 1, where $B_i$ denotes the $i$-th [Bernoulli number](wikipedia://en/4964) (with $B_1 = +\frac{1}{2}$). Consider the coefficient of $x^j$ in the product

$$\prod_{i=1}^m Q(\beta_i x) \$$

for any $m > j$. This is symmetric in the $\beta_i$s and homogeneous of weight $j$: so can be expressed as a polynomial $\operatorname{td}_j(p_1,\ldots, p_j)$ in the [elementary symmetric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elementary_symmetric_function) $p$ of the $\beta_i$s. Then $\operatorname{td}_j$ defines the **Todd polynomials**: they form a [multiplicative sequence](wikipedia://en/39588081) with $Q$ as characteristic [power series](wikipedia://en/59958).

If $E$ has the $\alpha_i$ as its [Chern roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_roots), then the **Todd class**

$$\operatorname{td}(E) = \prod Q(\alpha_i)$$

which is to be computed in the [cohomology ring](wikipedia://en/1069723) of $X$ (or in its completion if one wants to consider infinite-dimensional manifolds).

The Todd class can be given explicitly as a formal power series in the Chern classes as follows:[^o6]

$$\operatorname{td}(E) = 1 + \frac{c_1}{2} + \frac{c_1^2 +c_2}{12} + \frac{c_1c_2}{24} + \frac{-c_1^4 + 4 c_1^2 c_2 + c_1c_3 + 3c_2^2 - c_4}{720} + \cdots$$

where the cohomology classes $c_i$ are the Chern classes of $E$, and lie in the cohomology group $H^{2i}(X)$. If $X$ is finite-dimensional then most terms vanish and $\operatorname{td}(E)$ is a polynomial in the Chern classes.

:::

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties of the Todd class"}

## Properties of the Todd class {#Properties_of_the_Todd_class}

The Todd class is multiplicative:[^o9]

$$\operatorname{td}(E\oplus F) = \operatorname{td}(E)\cdot \operatorname{td}(F).$$

Let $\xi \in H^2({\mathbb C} P^n)$ be the fundamental class of the hyperplane section. From multiplicativity and the Euler exact sequence for the tangent bundle of ${\mathbb C} P^n$

$$0 \to {\mathcal O} \to {\mathcal O}(1)^{n+1} \to T {\mathbb C} P^n \to 0,$$

one obtains [^o10]

$$\operatorname{td}(T {\mathbb C}P^n) = \left( \dfrac{\xi}{1-e^{-\xi}} \right)^{n+1}.$$

:::

::: {#o12 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Computations of the Todd class"}

## Computations of the Todd class {#Computations_of_the_Todd_class}

For any algebraic curve $C$ the Todd class is just $\operatorname{td}(C) = 1 + \frac{1}{2} c_1(T_C)$. Since $C$ is projective, it can be embedded into some $\mathbb{P}^n$ and we can find $c_1(T_C)$ using the normal sequence

> $$0 \to T_C \to T_\mathbb{P^n}|_C \to N_{C/\mathbb{P}^n} \to 0$$

and properties of chern classes. For example, if we have a degree $d$ plane curve in $\mathbb{P}^2$, we find the total chern class is

> $$\begin{aligned} c(T_C) &= \frac{c(T_{\mathbb{P}^2}|_C)}{c(N_{C/\mathbb{P}^2})} \\ &= \frac{1+3[H]}{1+d[H]} \\ &= (1+3[H])(1-d[H]) \\ &= 1 + (3-d)[H] \end{aligned}$$

where $[H]$ is the hyperplane class in $\mathbb{P}^2$ restricted to $C$.

:::

::: {#o14 .statement cue=named-heading name="Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch formula"}

## Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch formula {#Hirzebruch-Riemann-Roch_formula}

For any [coherent sheaf](wikipedia://en/603780) *F* on a smooth compact [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) *M*, one has

$$\chi(F)=\int_M \operatorname{ch}(F) \wedge \operatorname{td}(TM),$$

where $\chi(F)$ is its [holomorphic Euler characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_Euler_characteristic),

$$\chi(F):= \sum_{i=0}^{\text{dim}_{\mathbb{C}} M} (-1)^i \text{dim}_{\mathbb{C}} H^i(M,F),$$

and $\operatorname{ch}(F)$ its [Chern character](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chern_character).

:::
