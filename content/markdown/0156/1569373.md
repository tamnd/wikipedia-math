# Peter Simons (academic)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Simons"}

**Peter Murray Simons**, (born 23 March 1950) is a [British](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_people) retired [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher) and academic. From 2009 to 2016, he was [Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Moral Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moral_Philosophy) at [Trinity College Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College_Dublin);[^o3] he is now [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus).[^o4] He is known for his work with [Kevin Mulligan](wikipedia://en/11782633) and [Barry Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Smith_%28academic%29) on [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) and the history of [Austrian philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_philosophy).[^o5] Since 2018 he is visiting professor at the [University of Italian Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Italian_Switzerland).[^o6]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Simons studied mathematics and philosophy, being supervise by wolfe mays, at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Manchester). While at Manchester he co-founded the seminar for austro-german philosophy.[^o8] He held teaching posts at the [University of Bolton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bolton), from which he holds an honorary doctorate, the [University of Salzburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Salzburg), where he is Honorary Professor of Philosophy, and the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds). He has been President of the [European Society for Analytic Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Society_for_Analytic_Philosophy) and is current director of the [Franz Brentano Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Brentano_Foundation).

His research interests include [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) and [ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology), the history of logic, the history of Central European Philosophy, particularly in Austria and Poland in the 19th and 20th centuries, and the application of metaphysics to engineering and other non-philosophical disciplines. He is the author or co-author of five books and over 290 articles.

He is married with two children.[^o9]
