# Statistical graphics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Statistical graphics"}

**Statistical graphics**, also known as **statistical graphical techniques**, are [graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graphics) used in the field of [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) for [data visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization).

:::

## Overview {#Overview}

Whereas [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [data analysis](wikipedia://en/2720954) procedures generally yield their output in numeric or tabular form, graphical techniques allow such results to be displayed in some sort of pictorial form. They include [plots](wikipedia://en/19774918) such as [scatter plots](wikipedia://en/412544), [histograms](wikipedia://en/13266), [probability plots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probability_plot_%28disambiguation%29), [spaghetti plots](wikipedia://en/7075785), residual plots, [box plots](wikipedia://en/160960), block plots and [biplots](wikipedia://en/14306166).[^o3]

[Exploratory data analysis](wikipedia://en/416589) (EDA) relies heavily on such techniques. They can also provide insight into a data set to help with testing assumptions, [model selection](wikipedia://en/3664073) and [regression model validation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regression_model_validation), estimator selection, relationship identification, factor effect determination, and [outlier](wikipedia://en/160951) detection. In addition, the choice of appropriate statistical graphics can provide a convincing means of communicating the underlying message that is present in the data to others.[^o3]

Graphical statistical methods have four objectives:[^o4]

1. The exploration of the content of a data set
1. The use to find structure in data
1. Checking assumptions in statistical models
1. Communicate the results of an analysis.

If one is not using statistical graphics, then one is forfeiting insight into one or more aspects of the underlying structure of the data.

## History {#History}

Statistical graphics have been central to the development of science and date to the earliest attempts to analyse data. Many familiar forms, including [bivariate plots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bivariate_map), [statistical maps](wikipedia://en/1506652), [bar charts](wikipedia://en/393311), and [coordinate paper](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coordinate_paper) were used in the 18th century. Statistical graphics developed through attention to four problems:[^o6]

- Spatial organization in the 17th and 18th century
- Discrete comparison in the 18th and early 19th century
- Continuous distribution in the 19th century and
- Multivariate distribution and correlation in the late 19th and 20th century.

Since the 1970s statistical graphics have been re-emerging as an important analytic tool with the revitalisation of [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics) and related technologies.[^o6]

## Examples {#Examples}

*Image: Playfair TimeSeries-2.png.* [William Playfair](wikipedia://en/1411831)'s trade-balance time-series chart, published in his *Commercial and Political Atlas*, 1786

*Image: Snow-cholera-map-1.jpg.* [John Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Snow_%28physician%29)'s Cholera map in dot style, 1854

Famous graphics were designed by:

- [William Playfair](wikipedia://en/1411831) who produced what could be called the first [line](wikipedia://en/6879051), [bar](wikipedia://en/393311), [pie](wikipedia://en/728487), and [area charts](wikipedia://en/11731170). For example, in 1786 he published the well known diagram that depicts the evolution of [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England)'s imports and exports,[^o9]
- [James Watt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Watt) and his employee [John Southern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Southern_%28engineer%29), who around 1790 invented [the steam indicator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indicator_diagram), a device for plotting pressure variations within a steam engine cylinder through its stroke,[^o10]
- [Florence Nightingale](wikipedia://en/100127), who used statistical graphics to persuade the British Government to improve army hygiene,[^o11]
- [John Snow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Snow_%28physician%29) who plotted deaths from [cholera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cholera) in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London) in 1854 to detect the source of the disease,[^o12] and
- [Charles Joseph Minard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Joseph_Minard) who designed a large portfolio of maps of which the one depicting [Napoleon](wikipedia://en/69880)'s campaign in [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) is the best known.[^o13]

See the [plots](wikipedia://en/19774918) page for many more examples of statistical graphics.
