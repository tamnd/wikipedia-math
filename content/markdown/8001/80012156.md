# James Wallace (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="James Wallace"}

**James Wallace** (died 1850) was an Irish-born American mathematician and Catholic priest. After emigrating to the United States, he taught mathematics in New York City, at [Georgetown College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University_College_of_Arts_&_Sciences) in D.C., and finally at [South Carolina College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Carolina_College) in Columbia. Whilst in New York he published *A New Treatise on the Use of the Globes, and Practical Astronomy; or a Comprehensive View of the System of the World*. He was an occasional correspondent of [Thomas Jefferson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Jefferson).[^o3][^o4]
