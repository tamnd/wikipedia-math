# Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory

::: {#o1 .statement cue=infobox-mathematical-statement name="Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory"}

**Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory.**

:::

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Main conjecture of Iwasawa theory"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **main conjecture of [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150)** is a deep relationship between [*p*-adic *L*-functions](wikipedia://en/22059815) and [ideal class groups](wikipedia://en/150907) of [cyclotomic fields](wikipedia://en/30872462), proved by [Kenkichi Iwasawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenkichi_Iwasawa) for primes satisfying the [Kummer–Vandiver conjecture](wikipedia://en/2478058) and proved for all primes by [Barry Mazur](wikipedia://en/23087679) and [Andrew Wiles](wikipedia://en/2027).[^n1] The [Herbrand–Ribet theorem](wikipedia://en/1078637) and the [Gras conjecture](wikipedia://en/28337935) are both easy consequences of the main conjecture.

:::

There are several generalizations of the main conjecture, to [totally real fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_real_field),[^n2][^n3] [CM fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CM_field), [elliptic curves](wikipedia://en/10225), and so on.

## Motivation {#Motivation}

Iwasawa was partly motivated by an analogy with [Weil's description](wikipedia://en/244705) of the zeta function of an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) over a [finite field](wikipedia://en/11615) in terms of eigenvalues of the [Frobenius endomorphism](wikipedia://en/1088270) on its [Jacobian variety](wikipedia://en/489734).[^n4] In this analogy,

- The action of the Frobenius corresponds to the action of the group Γ.
- The Jacobian of a curve corresponds to a module *X* over Γ defined in terms of ideal class groups.
- The zeta function of a curve over a finite field corresponds to a *p*-adic *L*-function.
- Weil's theorem relating the eigenvalues of Frobenius to the zeros of the zeta function of the curve corresponds to Iwasawa's main conjecture relating the action of the [Iwasawa algebra](wikipedia://en/31746215) on *X* to zeros of the *p*-adic zeta function.

## History {#History}

The main conjecture of Iwasawa theory was formulated as an assertion that the two methods of defining *p*-adic *L*-functions (by [Galois modules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galois_module) or by interpolation of [special values of *L*-functions](wikipedia://en/25305882)) should coincide, as far as that was well-defined. This was proved by Mazur and Wiles for **Q**.[^n1] Wiles later proved it for all [totally real number fields](wikipedia://en/312853).[^n2] These proofs were modeled upon [Ken Ribet](wikipedia://en/30876850)'s proof of the converse to Herbrand's theorem (the [Herbrand–Ribet theorem](wikipedia://en/1078637)).

[Karl Rubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Rubin) found a more elementary proof of the Mazur–Wiles theorem by using [Thaine's method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thaine's_method) and Kolyvagin's [Euler systems](wikipedia://en/1052176),[^n5][^n6] and later proved other generalizations of the main conjecture for imaginary [quadratic fields](wikipedia://en/454781).[^n7]

In 2014, [Christopher Skinner](wikipedia://en/59264582) and [Eric Urban](wikipedia://en/63270274) proved several cases of the main conjectures for a large class of [modular forms](wikipedia://en/286000).[^n8] As a consequence, for a [modular elliptic curve](wikipedia://en/4559850) over the [rational numbers](wikipedia://en/19727024), they prove that the vanishing of the [Hasse–Weil *L*-function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse–Weil_L-function) *L*(*E*, *s*) of *E* at *s* = 1 implies that the *p*-adic [Selmer group](wikipedia://en/1970862) of *E* is infinite. Combined with theorems of [Gross](wikipedia://en/4326863)-[Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557) and [Kolyvagin](wikipedia://en/4559839), this gave a conditional proof (on the [Tate–Shafarevich conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tate–Shafarevich_conjecture)) of the conjecture that *E* has infinitely many rational points if and only if *L*(*E*, 1) = 0, a (weak) form of the [Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer_conjecture). These results were used by [Manjul Bhargava](wikipedia://en/5102088), Skinner, and [Wei Zhang](wikipedia://en/46408911) to prove that a positive proportion of elliptic curves satisfy the Birch–Swinnerton-Dyer conjecture.[^n9][^n10]

## Statement {#Statement}

- *p* is a prime number.
- *F*<sub>*n*</sub> is the field **Q**(ζ) where ζ is a root of unity of order *p*<sup>*n*+1</sup>.
- Γ is the largest subgroup of the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of *F*<sub>∞</sub> isomorphic to the [*p*-adic integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_integers).
- γ is a topological generator of Γ.
- *L*<sub>*n*</sub> is the *p*-[Hilbert class field](wikipedia://en/3021036) of *F*<sub>*n*</sub>.
- *H*<sub>*n*</sub> is the Galois group Gal(*L*<sub>*n*</sub>/*F*<sub>*n*</sub>), isomorphic to the subgroup of elements of the ideal class group of *F*<sub>*n*</sub> whose order is a power of *p*.
- *H*<sub>∞</sub> is the [inverse limit](wikipedia://en/15109) of the Galois groups *H*<sub>*n*</sub>.
- *V* is the vector space *H*<sub>∞</sub>⊗<sub>**Z**<sub>*p*</sub></sub>**Q**<sub>*p*</sub>.
- ω is the [Teichmüller character](wikipedia://en/31757958).
- *V*<sup>*i*</sup> is the ω<sup>*i*</sup> eigenspace of *V*.
- *h*<sub>*p*</sub>(ω<sup>*i*</sup>,*T*) is the characteristic polynomial of γ acting on the vector space *V*<sup>*i*</sup>.
- *L*<sub>*p*</sub> is the [p-adic L function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_L_function) with *L*<sub>*p*</sub>(ω<sup>*i*</sup>,1–*k*) = –B<sub>*k*</sub>(ω<sup>*i*–*k*</sup>)/*k*, where *B* is a [generalized Bernoulli number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generalized_Bernoulli_number).
- *u* is the unique p-adic number satisfying γ(ζ) = ζ<sup>u</sup> for all p-power roots of unity ζ.
- *G*<sub>*p*</sub> is the [power series](wikipedia://en/59958) with *G*<sub>*p*</sub>(ω<sup>*i*</sup>,*u*<sup>*s*</sup>–1) = *L*<sub>*p*</sub>(ω<sup>*i*</sup>,*s*).

The main conjecture of Iwasawa theory proved by Mazur and Wiles states that if *i* is an odd integer not congruent to 1 mod *p*–1 then the ideals of $\mathbf Z_p[[T]]$ generated by *h*<sub>*p*</sub>(ω<sup>*i*</sup>,*T*) and *G*<sub>*p*</sub>(ω<sup>1–*i*</sup>,*T*) are equal.

:::
