# Daqing Wan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daqing Wan"}

**Daqing Wan** (born 1964 in China) is a Chinese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), who after working in the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) for near 40 years, he moved to [Chongqing University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chongqing_University) in 2025.[^o3] He received his Ph.D. from the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) in 1991, under the direction of [Neal Koblitz](wikipedia://en/1587628).[^o4]

:::

Between 1997 and 2025 he was on the faculty of mathematics at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine). He also held visiting positions at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), [Pennsylvania State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pennsylvania_State_University), the [University of Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes), the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](wikipedia://en/401367), and the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o5]

His primary interests include [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and arithmetic [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), particularly [zeta functions](wikipedia://en/25809) over [finite fields](wikipedia://en/11615). He is known for his proof of [Dwork](wikipedia://en/1678313)'s conjecture [^o6] that the p-adic [unit root zeta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_root_zeta_function) attached to a family of varieties over a finite field of [characteristic p](wikipedia://en/1066621) is [p-adic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic) [meromorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meromorphic). [^o7] [^o8] [^o9]

In 2001 he received the [Morningside Silver Medal](wikipedia://en/39615068) awarded to exceptional mathematicians of Chinese descent under the age of 45.[^o10]

Wan, a renowned mathematician and former child prodigy, has left California to return to his hometown of Chongqing. Amid intense competition among Chinese universities, he has taken a new position in his home city.[^o11]
