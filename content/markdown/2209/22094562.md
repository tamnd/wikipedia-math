# E. Brian Davies

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="E. Brian Davies"}

**Edward Brian Davies** (13 June 1944 – 2 June 2025) was a British mathematician who was professor of Mathematics, [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) (1981–2010), and was the author of the [popular science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_science) book *Science in the Looking Glass: What do Scientists Really Know*.[^o4][^o5][^o6] In 2010, he was awarded a [Gauss Lecture](wikipedia://en/48440954) by the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973).[^o7]

:::

Davies died on 2 June 2025, at the age of 80.[^o8]
