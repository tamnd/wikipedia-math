# Catherine Doléans-Dade

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Catherine Doléans-Dade"}

**Catherine Doléans-Dade** (24 January 1942 – 19 September 2004) was a [French American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_American) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). She made significant contributions to the calculus of [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536), including a general change of variables formula, a theorem on [stochastic differential equations](wikipedia://en/1361454), and exponential processes of semimartingales.[^o3]

:::

After earning her doctorate from the [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg) in 1970, she became a professor in the Mathematics Department of the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign). She died of cancer in 2004.[^o4]
