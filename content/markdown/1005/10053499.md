# Oleg Lupanov

Borisovich

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Oleg Lupanov"}

**Oleg Borisovich Lupanov** (Оле́г Бори́сович Лупа́нов; 2 June 1932 – 3 May 2006) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [Russian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), dean of the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University)'s [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) (1980–2006), head of the Chair of [Discrete Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_Mathematics) of the [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) (1981–2006).[^o4]

:::

Together with his graduate school advisor, [Sergey Yablonsky](wikipedia://en/10039004), he is considered one of the founders of the Soviet school of [Mathematical Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cybernetics). In particular he authored pioneering works on synthesis and complexity of [Boolean circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_circuits), and of [control systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control_systems) in general (Управляющие системы), the term used in the [USSR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) and [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia) for a generalization of [finite-state automata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite-state_automata), [Boolean circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boolean_circuits) and [multi-valued logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multi-valued_logic) [circuits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logic_circuit).

[Ingo Wegener](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ingo_Wegener), in his book *The Complexity of Boolean Functions,*[^o5] credits O. B. Lupanov for coining the term *[Shannon](wikipedia://en/5693) effect* in his 1970 paper,[^o6] to refer to the fact that almost all Boolean functions have nearly the same circuit complexity as the hardest function.

O. B. Lupanov is best known for his (*k*, *s*)-Lupanov representation of Boolean functions[^o7] that he used to devise an asymptotically optimal method of Boolean circuit synthesis, thus proving the asymptotically tight upper bound on Boolean circuit complexity:

$$C(f)\le \frac{2^n}{n} + o\left(\frac{2^n}{n}\right).$$

## Biography {#Biography}

O. B. Lupanov graduated from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University)'s [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) in 1955. He received his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandidat) in 1958 from the [Academy of Sciences of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Soviet_Union) and his Doctorate degree in 1963. He began teaching at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1959 and became professor there in 1967. From 1955 he had appointment at the [Institute of Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984473) and he was a professor at [Faculty of Computational Mathematics and Cybernetics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Computational_Mathematics_and_Cybernetics) (1970–1980). He had served as the Dean of the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University)'s [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) (1980–2006), and as the founding head of the Chair of [Discrete Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Discrete_Mathematics) of the [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) (1981–2006).[^o4]

Lupanov became a corresponding member of the Academy of Sciences of the Soviet Union in 1972 and a full member of Russian Academy of Sciences in 2003. He was the lead scientist of the [Keldysh Institute of Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984473) since 1993 and was awarded the title of a distinguished professor of Moscow State University in 2002. He was a recipient of the prestigious [Lenin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize) (1966) and of the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University)'s [Lomonosov Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Award) (1993).

His students count more than 30 [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kandidat) degree holders and 6 holders of the [Soviet/Russian Doctorate degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate#Doctorates_in_Russia).[^o9] As a dean of the [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics), he was known as a democratic and accessible person.[^o10]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Lupanov died at around 7pm on 3 May 2006 in his office at the [Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University).
