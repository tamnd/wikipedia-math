# Maximal ergodic theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Maximal ergodic theorem"}

The **maximal ergodic theorem** is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) in [ergodic theory](wikipedia://en/258986), a discipline within [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831).

:::

Suppose that $(X, \mathcal{B},\mu)$ is a [probability space](wikipedia://en/43325), that $T : X\to X$ is a (possibly noninvertible) [measure-preserving transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure-preserving_transformation), and that $f\in L^1(\mu,\mathbb{R})$. Define $f^*$ by

$$f^* = \sup_{N\geq 1} \frac{1}{N} \sum_{i=0}^{N-1} f \circ T^i.$$

Then the maximal ergodic theorem states that

$$\int_{f^{*} > \lambda} f \, d\mu \ge \lambda \cdot \mu\{ f^{*} > \lambda\}$$

for any λ ∈ **R**.

This theorem is used to prove the point-wise [ergodic theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ergodic_theorem).
