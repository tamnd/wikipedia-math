# Polyphase sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Polyphase sequence"}

In mathematics, a **polyphase sequence** is a sequence whose terms are [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) [roots of unity](wikipedia://en/171950):

:::

$$a_n = e^{i\frac{2\pi}{q}x_n} \,$$

where *x*<sub>*n*</sub> is an [integer](wikipedia://en/14563).

Polyphase sequences are an important class of sequences and play important roles in synchronizing sequence design.
