# Tango bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tango bundle"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Tango bundle** is one of the indecomposable [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) of rank *n* − 1 constructed on *n*-dimensional projective space *P*<sup>*n*</sup> by (Tango 1976)

:::
