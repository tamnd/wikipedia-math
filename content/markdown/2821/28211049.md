# Ugo Morin

Ugo Morin

*Image: Ugo-morin.jpg.* Ugo Morin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ugo Morin"}

**Ugo Morin** (February 7, 1901 – January 1, 1968) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [antifascist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antifascist).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Morin was born in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste) in 1901. His family was originally from [Mali Lošinj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mali_Lošinj). He obtained his first degree at the [University of Padova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Padova) in 1926; in 1933 he was a lecturer in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). In 1935 he became a professor at the University of Padova, and from 1942 to 1945 he taught at the [University of Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence) (analytical geometry). From 1946 he was again a professor in Padova, where he died in 1968. He was also the author of many scientific articles about classical [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384).

While working in [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence), he was an active antifascist: he organized the [Partito d’Azione](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partito_d’Azione) and its militia, participated in the Resistance, organized the clandestine Partito d’Azione and [Giustizia e Libertà](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giustizia_e_Libertà); in 1945 he was chairman of the Tuscan CLN ([National Liberation Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Liberation_Committee)).
