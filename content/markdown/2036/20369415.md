# Victor Pan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Victor Pan"}

*Image: Victor Yakovlevich Pan 01.jpg.* Victor Pan in 1996

**Victor Yakovlevich Pan** (Пан Виктор Яковлевич) is a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/USSR) and [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [computer scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_scientist), known for his research on [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775) for [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000) and [matrix multiplication](wikipedia://en/125280).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Pan earned his Ph.D. at [Moscow University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_University) in 1964, under the supervision of [Anatoli Georgievich Vitushkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoli_Georgievich_Vitushkin), and continued his work at the [Soviet Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Academy_of_Sciences). During that time, he published a number of significant papers and became known informally as "polynomial Pan" for his pioneering work in the area of [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) [computations](wikipedia://en/5926). In late 1970s, he immigrated to the [United States](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) and held positions at several institutions including [IBM Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IBM_Research). Since 1988, he has taught at [Lehman College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehman_College) of the [City University of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_New_York).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Victor Pan is an expert in [computational complexity](wikipedia://en/2230) and has developed a number of new [algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algorithms). One of his notable early results is a proof that the number of multiplications in [Horner's method](wikipedia://en/14263) is optimal.CVP

In the theory of [matrix multiplication algorithms](wikipedia://en/21450030), Pan in 1978 published an algorithm with running time $O(n^{2.795})$. This was the first improvement over the [Strassen algorithm](wikipedia://en/771965) after nearly a decade, and kicked off a long line of improvements in fast matrix multiplication that later included the [Coppersmith–Winograd algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coppersmith–Winograd_algorithm) and subsequent developments.SNO He wrote the text *How to Multiply Matrices Faster* (Springer, 1984) surveying early developments in this area.HMM His 1982 algorithmP82 still held the record in 2020 for the fastest "practically useful" matrix multiplication algorithm (i.e., with a small base size and manageable hidden constants). In 1998, with his student Xiaohan Huang, Pan showed that matrix multiplication algorithms can take advantage of rectangular matrices with unbalanced [aspect ratios](wikipedia://en/1021), multiplying them more quickly than the time bounds one would obtain using square matrix multiplication algorithms.FRM

Since that work, Pan has returned to symbolic and numeric computation and to an earlier theme of his research, computations with polynomials. He developed fast algorithms for the numerical computation of polynomial [roots](wikipedia://en/264210),UP and, with Bernard Mourrain, algorithms for multivariate polynomials based on their relations to structured matrices.MPD He also authored or co-authored several more books, on matrix and polynomial computation,PMC structured matrices,SMP and on numerical root-finding procedures.NMR

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Pan was appointed [Distinguished Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_Professor) at Lehman College in 2000.

In 2013 he became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), for "contributions to the mathematical theory of computation".
