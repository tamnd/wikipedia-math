# Enea Bortolotti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Enea Bortolotti"}

**Enea Bortolotti** (28 September 1896 – 22 June 1942) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He graduated in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1920 at the University of Pisa, where he was a student of [Luigi Bianchi](wikipedia://en/701065). He taught [analytic](wikipedia://en/2202) and [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) at the [Universities of Cagliari](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cagliari) and [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florence). He was mainly involved in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625): he was a specialist in the [theory of linear connections](wikipedia://en/5760393).[^o5][^o6]
