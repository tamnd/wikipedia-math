# Mean value problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mean value problem"}

The **mean value problem** (also known as **Smale's mean value conjecture**) is a problem in the mathematical field of [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) first posed by [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294) in 1981.[^o2]

:::

The problem asks:

For a given [complex](wikipedia://en/5826) [polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) $P$ of [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652) $d \ge 2$[^o3] and a [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) $z$, is there a [critical point](wikipedia://en/1951424) $c$ of $P$ (i.e. $P'(c) = 0$) such that

$$\left| \frac{P(z) - P(c)}{z - c} \right| \le K|P'(z)| \text{ for }K=1 \text{?}$$

The question was initially resolved by Smale for $K=4$.[^o2] For a polynomial of degree $d$, the constant $K$ has to be at least $\frac{d-1}{d}$ due to the example $P(z) = z^{d} - d z$, so no constant bound better than $K=1$ can exist.

## Partial results {#Partial_results}

The [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) is known to hold in special cases; for other cases, the bound on $K$ could be improved depending on the degree $d$, although no absolute bound $K<4$ is known that holds for all $d$.

In 1989, David Tischler showed that the conjecture is true for the optimal bound $K = \frac{d-1}{d}$ if $P$ has only [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) [roots](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_of_a_polynomial), or if all roots of $P$ have the same [norm](wikipedia://en/990534).[^o5][^o6]

In 2007, Anthony Conte et al. proved that $K \le 4 \frac{d-1}{d+1}$,[^o3] slightly improving on the bound $K \le 4$ for fixed $d$. Also in 2007, Edward Crane showed that $K < 4-\frac{2.263}{\sqrt{d}}$ for $d \ge 8$.[^o7]

Considering the reverse [inequality](wikipedia://en/89489), Vladimir Dubinin and Toshiyuki Sugawa proved that (under the same conditions as above) there exists a critical point $c$ such that $\left| \frac{P(z) - P(c)}{z - c} \right| \ge \frac{|P'(z)|}{n 4^{n}}$.[^o8] The problem of optimizing this lower bound is known as the [dual mean value problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_mean_value_problem).[^o9]
