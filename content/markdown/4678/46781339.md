# Quillen spectral sequence

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quillen spectral sequence"}

In the area of mathematics known as [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), the **Quillen spectral sequence**, also called the Brown–Gersten–Quillen or BGQ spectral sequence (named after [Kenneth Brown](wikipedia://en/41104429), [Stephen Gersten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Gersten), and [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519)), is a [spectral sequence](wikipedia://en/803894) converging to the [sheaf cohomology](wikipedia://en/1055357) of a type of topological space that occurs in algebraic geometry.[^o2][^o3] It is used in calculating the [homotopy](wikipedia://en/212250) properties of a [simplicial group](wikipedia://en/39418646).

:::
