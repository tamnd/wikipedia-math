# Stacey Franklin Jones

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stacey Franklin Jones"}

**Stacey Franklin Jones** is an American computer scientist, and academic administrator who was the fifth chancellor of [Elizabeth City State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_City_State_University) from 2014 to 2015.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Jones is from [Boston](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston).[^o4] She earned a B.S. in mathematics, magna cum laude, from [Howard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_University) in 1982.[^o4] From 1982 to 1993, she worked in Maryland for [Northrop Grumman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Grumman) as a defense and electronic systems software engineer and [product development manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Product_Development_Management).[^o4] She completed M.S. degrees in numerical science (1986) and [technical management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_management) (1991) at [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University). In 1997, Jones earned a Ph.D. in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) from the [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University).[^o4] Her dissertation was titled, *Major Cost Categories for a Rough-Order-Magnitude Cost Model for Multimedia Computer-based Training*.[^o5] [C. Dianne Martin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._Dianne_Martin) was her doctoral advisor.[^o5]

In 1997, Jones joined the computer science department at Johns Hopkins University as a research scientist and adjunct faculty member.[^o4] She joined [Benedict College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benedict_College) in 2000 as the chair of the mathematics and computer science department.[^o4] She served as its dean of the school of science, technology, engineering and mathematics from 2002 to 2008.[^o4] Jones became the vice president of sponsored programs and research in 2007 and senior vice president in 2009.[^o4] In July 2010, she became the provost and vice president for academic affairs of [Bowie State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowie_State_University).[^o6] In the fall of 2011, she returned to the private sector.[^o4] On October 1, 2014, Jones became the fifth chancellor and tenth chief executive officer of [Elizabeth City State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_City_State_University).[^o7] Succeeding interim chancellor, [Charles Becton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Becton), she is the first female in the role.[^o7][^o4] Jones resigned on December 31, 2015.[^o7] From August 2016 to January 2018, she was the interim provost and vice president for academic affairs at [Norfolk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norfolk_State_University).[^o8] She became the special projects assistant to the interim president in February 2018.[^o8] In 2018, she joined [Fairmont State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairmont_State_University) as its vice president for institutional effectiveness and strategic operations.[^o9]

Jones has two children.[^o4]
