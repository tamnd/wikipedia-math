# UES (cipher)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="UES"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **UES** (**Universal Encryption Standard**) is a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) designed in 1999 by [Helena Handschuh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helena_Handschuh) and [Serge Vaudenay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Serge_Vaudenay). They proposed it as a transitional step, to prepare for the completion of the [AES process](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AES_process).

:::

UES was designed with the same interface as [AES](wikipedia://en/1260): a [block size](wikipedia://en/294065) of 128 bits and [key size](wikipedia://en/5749) of 128, 192, or 256 bits. It consists of two parallel [Triple DES](wikipedia://en/48375) encryptions on the halves of the block, with [key whitening](wikipedia://en/2990178) and key-dependent swapping of bits between the halves. The [key schedule](wikipedia://en/609603) is taken from [DEAL](wikipedia://en/663184).
