# Hu Hesheng

[Hu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hu_%28surname%29)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Hu Hesheng"}

**Hu Hesheng** (; 20 June 1928 – 2 February 2024) was a Chinese mathematician. She served as vice-president of [Chinese Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Mathematical_Society), president of the Shanghai Mathematical Society, and was an academician of [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o3] She gave the [Noether Lecture](wikipedia://en/11566873) in 2002.[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born in [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai), Hu studied mathematics at [National Chiao Tung University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Chiao_Tung_University) (now [Shanghai Jiaotong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Jiaotong_University)) and [Great China University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_China_University). She received her master's degree in mathematics from [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University) in 1952, under the supervision of [Su Buqing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Su_Buqing). From 1952 to 1956, she was a researcher at the Institute of Mathematics of the Chinese Academy of Sciences. In 1956, she began working as a lecturer at [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University) in Shanghai, later becoming an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) and eventually achieving full-time professorship.[^o6]

## Service and recognition {#Service_and_recognition}

Hu served as vice president of the Chinese Mathematical Society and president of the Shanghai Mathematical Society. In 2002, she was chosen as the [Emmy Noether Lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noether_Lecturer) for that year's [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Beijing, China.[^o8] She was elected as an academician of the Chinese Academy of Sciences in 1991 and as an academician of the [Third World Academy of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Third_World_Academy_of_Science) in 2003.[^o10]

## Research {#Research}

Her main academic interests were [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o10] She led a research group at Fudan University during the 1980s and 1990s.

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

Hu's husband was [Gu Chaohao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gu_Chaohao), also a mathematician, who served as the president of [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China).[^o12] They were married until his death in 2012.

Hu Hesheng died on 2 February 2024, at the age of 95.[^o13]
