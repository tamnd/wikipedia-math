# Mimi Kim

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mimi Kim"}

**Mimi Y. Kim** is the Harold and Muriel Block Chair in [epidemiology](wikipedia://en/66997) and population health at the [Albert Einstein College of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Einstein_College_of_Medicine), where she heads the division of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878).[^o2]

:::

Kim graduated from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) with a bachelor's degree in [bioengineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bioengineering) in 1986, and completed an Sc.D. in [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1990. She joined the [New York University School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University_School_of_Medicine) in 1990, becoming an assistant professor in 1994 and an associate professor in 2000. She moved to the Albert Einstein College of Medicine in 2003.[^o3] She was given the Block Professorship in 2013.[^o4]

In 2014 she was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) "for exemplary leadership of academic biostatistics programs; for extensive collaborative research in HIV/AIDS, cancer, and rheumatology; for innovative study designs and analyses applied to medical research; and for extensive service to the biostatistics profession."[^o5] She is the president-elect of the Korean International Statistical Society.[^o6]
