# Lurdes Inoue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lurdes Inoue"}

**Lurdes Yoshiko Tani Inoue** is a Brazilian-born statistician of Japanese descent, who specializes in [Bayesian inference](wikipedia://en/49571). She works as a professor of [biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in the [University of Washington School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington_School_of_Public_Health).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Inoue's grandparents emigrated from Japan to Brazil in the 1930s; she was born in [São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/São_Paulo), where she grew up.[^o3]

She earned bachelor's and master's degree from the [University of São Paulo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_São_Paulo) in 1992 and 1995,[^o5] and received a fellowship from the Brazilian government to continue her studies in the US.[^o3] She completed her Ph.D. in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) in 1999 from [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University),[^o5] under the supervision of [Don Berry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don_Berry_%28statistician%29).

After postdoctoral research at the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas), she joined the University of Washington in 2002.[^o3] In 2019, she became the chair of the biostatistics department.[^o7]

## Book {#Book}

With [Giovanni Parmigiani](wikipedia://en/45494990), she is the author of the book *Decision Theory: Principles and Approaches* (Wiley, 2009).[^o9] This book won the DeGroot Prize of the [International Society for Bayesian Analysis](wikipedia://en/6038312) for 2009.[^o10]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2014, Inoue was elected as a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) "for substantial and fundamental contributions to Bayesian decision theory and innovation in the statistical modeling of disease progression with applications to cancer research; for outstanding mentoring of junior researchers; and for exemplary service to the profession."[^o12]
