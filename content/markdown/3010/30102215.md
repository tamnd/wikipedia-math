# Harold Levine

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Harold Levine"}

**Harold I. Levine** was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who was professor at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University). He specialized in [wave motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_motion) and [optics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optics).[^o2]

:::

Levine was born in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). After he graduated from the [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York), he earned a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in Physics from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University). In 1954, he was awarded a [Guggenheim fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_fellowship).[^o3] Levine died on December 10, 2017, at the age of 95.[^o4][^o5]
