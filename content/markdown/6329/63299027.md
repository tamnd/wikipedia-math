# Frank Calegari

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Frank Calegari"}

**Francesco Damien "Frank" Calegari** is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and the [Langlands program](wikipedia://en/171905).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Frank Calegari was born on December 15, 1975.[^o4] He has both Australian and American citizenship.[^o4]

He won a bronze medal and a silver medal at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) while representing [Australia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Australia) in 1992 and 1993 respectively.[^o5] Calegari received his PhD in mathematics from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 2002 under the supervision of [Ken Ribet](wikipedia://en/30876850).

## Career {#Career}

Calegari was a [Benjamin Peirce](wikipedia://en/364270) [Assistant Professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) from 2002 to 2006.[^o4] He then moved to [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), where he was an assistant professor from 2006 to 2009, an associate professor from 2009 to 2012, and a professor from 2012 to 2015.[^o4] He has been a professor of mathematics at the University of Chicago since 2015.[^o4][^o8]

Calegari was a [von Neumann Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_Fellow) of mathematics at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 2010 to 2011.[^o9]

Calegari was an editor at [Mathematische Zeitschrift](wikipedia://en/7457514) from 2013 to 2021.[^o4] He has been an editor of *[Algebra & Number Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebra_&_Number_Theory)* and an associate editor of the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)* since 2019.[^o4][^o10][^o11]

## Research {#Research}

Calegari works in [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), including [Langlands reciprocity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Langlands_reciprocity) and torsion classes in the cohomology of [arithmetic groups](wikipedia://en/630741).[^o8]

With George Boxer, [Toby Gee](wikipedia://en/58462234), and [Vincent Pilloni](wikipedia://en/58194439), he proved that all abelian surfaces and genus two curves over totally real fields are potentially modular and satisfy the [Hasse-Weil conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hasse-Weil_conjecture).[^o13]

In collaboration with [Vesselin Dimitrov](wikipedia://en/81558886) and [Yunqing Tang](wikipedia://en/72206056), Calegari proved[^o14] the unbounded denominators conjecture of [A.O.L. Atkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A.O.L._Atkin) and [Swinnerton-Dyer](wikipedia://en/2105540):[^o15] if a modular form $f(\tau)$ is not modular for some [congruence subgroup](wikipedia://en/382694) of the [modular group](wikipedia://en/325019), then the Fourier coefficients of $f(\tau)$ have unbounded denominators. It has been known for decades[^o16] that if $f(\tau)$ is modular for some congruence subgroup, then its coefficients have bounded denominators.

Also in collaboration with Dimitrov and Tang, he proved the linear independence of $1$, $\zeta(2)$, and $L(2,\chi_{-3})$.[^o17] This work, along with their work on the Unbounded Denominators conjecture, adds context to the efforts made[^o18][^o19][^o20] to understand [Roger Apery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Apery)'s proof[^o21] of the irrationality of $\zeta(3)$.

## Awards {#Awards}

Calegari held a 5-year [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005) Fellowship from 2002 to 2006 and a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) from 2009 to 2012.[^o4][^o23] He was inducted as a [Fellow of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society) in 2013,[^o4] and elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2025.[^o24] He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2026.[^o25]

Calegari was a plenary speaker at the 2022 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), where he gave a lecture entitled "30 years of modularity since [Fermat's Last Theorem](wikipedia://en/19021953)."[^o26][^o27]

Calegari was awarded the 2026 [Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) in Number Theory jointly with [Vesselin Dimitrov](wikipedia://en/81558886) and [Yunqing Tang](wikipedia://en/72206056).[^o28]
