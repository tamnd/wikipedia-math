# Caloric polynomial

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Caloric polynomial"}

In [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), the *m*th-degree **caloric polynomial** (or **heat polynomial**) is a "parabolically *m*-homogeneous" polynomial *P*<sub>*m*</sub>(*x*, *t*) that satisfies the [heat equation](wikipedia://en/166415)

:::

$$\frac{\partial P}{\partial t} = \frac{\partial^2 P}{\partial x^2}.$$

"Parabolically *m*-homogeneous" means

$$P(\lambda x, \lambda^2 t) = \lambda^m P(x,t)\text{ for }\lambda > 0.\,$$

The polynomial is given by

$$P_m(x,t) = \sum_{\ell=0}^{\lfloor m/2 \rfloor} \frac{m!}{\ell!(m - 2\ell)!} x^{m - 2\ell} t^\ell.$$

It is unique [up to](wikipedia://en/44787) a factor.

With *t* = −1/2, this polynomial reduces to the *m*th-degree [Hermite polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermite_polynomial) in *x*.
