# Peixoto's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peixoto's theorem"}

In the theory of [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), **Peixoto's theorem**, proved by [Maurício Peixoto](wikipedia://en/7427536), states that among all smooth [flows](wikipedia://en/2619735) on [surfaces](wikipedia://en/27865), i.e. [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) two-dimensional [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470), [structurally stable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structurally_stable) systems may be characterized by the following properties:

:::

- The set of [non-wandering points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-wandering_point) consists only of periodic orbits and fixed points.
- The set of [fixed points](wikipedia://en/449738) is finite and consists only of [hyperbolic equilibrium points](wikipedia://en/3948604).
- Finiteness of attracting or repelling [periodic orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Periodic_orbit).
- Absence of [saddle](wikipedia://en/640249)-to-saddle connections.

Moreover, they form an open set in the space of all flows endowed with the [*C*<sup>1</sup>](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuously_differentiable) topology.
