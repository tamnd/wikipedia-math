# Eugenio Giuseppe Togliatti

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eugenio Giuseppe Togliatti"}

**Eugenio Giuseppe Togliatti** (3 November 1890 – 5 October 1977) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), the brother of politician [Palmiro Togliatti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Palmiro_Togliatti). He was a researcher at the [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) from 1924 to 1926. He discovered [Togliatti surfaces](wikipedia://en/20705037).[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6]

:::
