# Togliatti surface

*Image: Togliatti surface.png.* The surface with $w = 1$ (real points, bounded by a sphere with radius=6).

*Image: 3D model of Togliatti surface (w=1).stl.* 3D model of same surface as above ($w = 1$) bounded by the cube $[-10, 10]^{3}$

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Togliatti surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Togliatti surface** is a [nodal surface](wikipedia://en/46573101) of degree five with 31 [nodes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Node_%28algebraic_geometry%29). The first examples were constructed by (txt). (txt) proved that 31 is the maximum possible number of nodes for a surface of this degree, showing this example to be optimal.

:::
