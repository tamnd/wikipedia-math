# Donald M. Davis (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Donald M. Davis"}

**Donald M. Davis** (born 7 May 1945) is an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_of_America) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801).

:::

Davis received a B.S. from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 1967 and a PhD in mathematics at [Stanford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford) in 1972, directed by [R. James Milgram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._James_Milgram). After postdoctoral positions at [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego) and [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), he began a 50-year career at [Lehigh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lehigh_University) in 1974. In 2012 he was named an inaugural Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4][^o5] Since 2002, he has been Executive Editor of *[Homology, Homotopy and Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology,_Homotopy_and_Applications)*.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Davis has published in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), [topological robotics](wikipedia://en/36365888), and [combinatorial number theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combinatorial_number_theory).[^o8] He is an expert on [immersions](wikipedia://en/6993953) of [projective spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_spaces), and maintains a website[^o9] with all known results for [real projective spaces](wikipedia://en/1014694). He computed the $v_1$-periodic homotopy groups of all [compact simple Lie groups](wikipedia://en/292831).[^o10]

## Coaching {#Coaching}

In 1993 Davis started the Lehigh Valley Math Team. In 2005, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2024 and 2025, they were [national champions in the American Regions Math League (ARML)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Regions_Math_League#Past_team_winners). They have finished second or third in ARML seven other times.[^o12] They won the Harvard/MIT Math Tournament ([HMMT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HMMT)) in 2023 and 2024, and the Princeton University Math Competition (PUMaC) in 2009, 2010, 2012, 2023, 2024, and 2025.[^o13]

## Running {#Running}

From 1977 through 2009, Davis competed in marathon and ultramarathon races. He was the overall winner of ultramarathon races of 31 to 78 miles in the 1970s, 1980s, 1990s, and 2000s.[^o15]
