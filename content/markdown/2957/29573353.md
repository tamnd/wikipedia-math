# Brauer tree

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brauer tree"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the [theory of finite groups](wikipedia://en/41890#Finite_group_theory), a **Brauer tree** is a [tree](wikipedia://en/48560) that encodes the [characters](wikipedia://en/293418) of a [block](wikipedia://en/1110685#Blocks_and_the_structure_of_the_group_algebra) with [cyclic](wikipedia://en/52327) [defect group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Defect_group) of a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707). In fact, the trees encode the [group algebra](wikipedia://en/349223) up to [Morita equivalence](wikipedia://en/3296134). Such algebras coming from Brauer trees are called Brauer tree algebras.

:::

(Feit 1984) described the possibilities for Brauer trees.
