# Gorenstein–Walter theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gorenstein–Walter theorem"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), the **Gorenstein–Walter theorem**, proved by [Gorenstein](wikipedia://en/1569478) and [Walter](wikipedia://en/40423024),[^n1][^n2][^n3] states that if a [finite group](wikipedia://en/323707) *G* has a [dihedral](wikipedia://en/100267) [Sylow 2-subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_2-subgroup), and *O*(*G*) is the maximal [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of odd [order](wikipedia://en/493527), then *G*/*O*(*G*) is isomorphic to a [2-group](wikipedia://en/24664), or the [alternating group](wikipedia://en/49726) *A*<sub>7</sub>, or a subgroup of PΓL<sub>2</sub>(*q*) containing PSL<sub>2</sub>(*q*) for *q* an odd prime power. Note that A<sub>5</sub> ≈ PSL<sub>2</sub>(4) ≈ PSL<sub>2</sub>(5) and A<sub>6</sub> ≈ PSL<sub>2</sub>(9).

:::
