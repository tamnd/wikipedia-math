# Steganographia

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steganographia"}

***Steganographia*** is a book on [steganography](wikipedia://en/28733), written in c. 1499 by the German [Benedictine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Saint_Benedict) abbot and polymath [Johannes Trithemius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Trithemius).

:::

## General {#General}

Trithemius' most famous work, *Steganographia* (written c.1499; published [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt), 1606), was placed on the *[Index Librorum Prohibitorum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_Librorum_Prohibitorum)* in 1609[^o4] and removed in 1900.[^o5] This book is in three volumes, and appears to be about [magic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_%28paranormal%29)—specifically, about using spirits to communicate over long distances. However, since the publication of a decryption [key](wikipedia://en/53039) to the first two volumes in 1606, they were discovered to be actually concerned with [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) and steganography. Until 1996, the third volume was widely believed to be solely about magic, but the "magical" formulas have now been shown to be [covertexts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stegotext) for yet more material on cryptography.[^o6][^o7]

## Reception {#Reception}

References within the third book to magical works by such figures as [Agrippa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heinrich_Cornelius_Agrippa) and [John Dee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dee) still lend credence to the idea of a mystic-magical foundation of the third volume.[^o9][^o10] Additionally, while Trithemius's steganographic methods can be established to be free of the need for angelic–astrological mediation, an underlying theological motive for their contrivance remains. The preface to *[Polygraphia](wikipedia://en/48429909)* equally establishes the everyday practicability of cryptography, and was conceived by Trithemius as a "secular consequent of the ability of a soul specially empowered by God to reach, by magical means, from earth to Heaven".[^o11] [Robert Hooke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Hooke) suggested, in the chapter o*f Dr. Dee's Book of Spirits*, that John Dee used Trithemian steganography to conceal his communication with [Queen Elizabeth I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizabeth_I_of_England).[^o12]
