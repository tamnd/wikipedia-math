# House with two rooms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="House with two rooms"}

*Image: Bing's house.svg.* Bing's house with two rooms, with its front wall removed. Small panels connect each vertical tunnel to the nearby side wall.

In [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), the **house with two rooms** or **Bing's house** is a particular [contractible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contractible), 2-dimensional [simplicial complex](wikipedia://en/252686) that is not [collapsible](wikipedia://en/20814602). The name was given by [R. H. Bing](wikipedia://en/967353).[^o2]

:::

The house is made of 2-dimensional panels, and has two rooms. The upper room may be entered from the bottom face, while the lower room may be entered from the upper face. There are two small panels attached to the tunnels between the rooms, which make this simplicial complex contractible.
