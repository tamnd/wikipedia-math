# Domenico Griminelli

*Image: Griminelli\_-\_Novissima\_prattica\_d'aritmetica\_mercantile,\_1670\_-\_872726.jpg.* *Novissima prattica d'aritmetica mercantile*, title page (1670)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Domenico Griminelli"}

**Domenico Griminelli** was a [17th-century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17th-century) [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [Catholic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catholic_Church) [priest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Priest) from [Correggio, Emilia-Romagna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correggio,_Emilia-Romagna).[^o2]

:::

He published in 1656[^o13] a tutorial book on [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) for [shopkeepers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopkeeper) and [merchants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merchant),[^o2][^o3][^o4][^o5] *Novissima prattica d'aritmetica mercantile* ("Brand-new practice of mercantile arithmetic"), also known outside Italy[^o6][^o7][^o8] and reprinted for two centuries.[^o9] The book's introduction echoed [Plato](wikipedia://en/22954) in declaring numbers to be a divine gift, without which civilization would vanish, and dedicated the work to cardinal [Girolamo Gastaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Girolamo_Gastaldi), the General Treasurer of the [Apostolic Camera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apostolic_Camera).[^o10]
