# Guglielmo Gargiolli

*Image: Gargiolli, Gugliemo - Iride celeste, 1655 - BEIC 879943.jpg.* *Iride celeste*, title page (1655)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guglielmo Gargiolli"}

**Guglielmo Gargiolli** was a [17th-century](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/17th-century) [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer).[^o2]

:::

He was Lecturer of Mathematics at the [University of Siena](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Siena), and Lecturer of [Military Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Military_engineering) and Mathematics at the Court of [Florence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florence). His book, *Iride celeste* ("Celestial Iris"), was noted for its explanation of how to estimate from a [distance](wikipedia://en/39378) the [height](wikipedia://en/239972) of an object and its distance from the observer.[^o3]
