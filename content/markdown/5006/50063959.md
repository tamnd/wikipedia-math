# Albert Fathi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albert Fathi"}

**Albert Fathi** (born 27 October 1951, in [Egypt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egypt)) is an [Egyptian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Egyptian_Americans)-[French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) mathematician. He specializes in [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and is currently a professor at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology).

:::

Fathi attended the *Collège des frères Lasalle* in [Cairo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cairo) and grew up bilingual in [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_language) and [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic). At age ten, he came as a political refugee to [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) and studied at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) in [Saint-Cloud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint-Cloud). He received in 1980 his PhD from the [University of Paris 11](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris_11) under [Laurence Siebenmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Siebenmann) with thesis *Transformations et homéomorphismes préservant la mesure*.[^o3][^o4] From 1987 to 1992 he was a professor at the [University of Florida](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Florida). Since 1992 he has taught at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon) (unit of pure and applied mathematics). He has also taught at the [École polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_polytechnique).

He has been a visiting professor at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) (1986/87),[^o5] at the [Universidad Complutense de Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universidad_Complutense_de_Madrid) (Instituto de Matemática Interdisciplinar), in [Nanjing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nanjing), in [Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge) and at [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute). In 2013 he received the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384).[^o6] He is a member of the Institut Universitaire de France.

At the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2014 in [Seoul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seoul), Fathi was an Invited Speaker with talk *Weak KAM Theory: the connection between Aubry-Mather theory and viscosity solutions of the [Hamilton–Jacobi equation](wikipedia://en/897539)*.
