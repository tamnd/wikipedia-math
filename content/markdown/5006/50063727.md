# Francesco Barberino Benici

*Image: Barberino\_Benici\_-\_Aritmetica\_prattica,\_1697\_-\_4783464.tif.* *Aritmetica prattica* (1697)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Francesco Barberino Benici"}

**Francesco Barberino Benici** (3 December 1642 – 26 September 1702) was an [Italian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o2][^o3]

:::

He was among the first popularizers of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) for [shopkeepers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shopkeeper), along with [Elia Del Re](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elia_Del_Re), [Christopher Clavius](wikipedia://en/633645), and [Domenico Griminelli](wikipedia://en/50063794).[^o4]
