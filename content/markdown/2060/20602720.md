# Gerald Folland

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gerald Folland"}

**Gerald Budge Folland** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a professor of mathematics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington). He is the author of several textbooks on [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). His areas of interest include [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147) (on both [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) and [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups)), [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).

:::

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
