# Charles Epstein (mathematician)

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Epstein"}

**Charles L. Epstein** is a Senior Research Scientist in the Center for Computational Mathematics at the Flatiron Institute. He was the [Thomas A. Scott Professor of Mathematics Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_A._Scott_Professorship_of_Mathematics) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), Philadelphia.[^o4]

:::

## Research areas {#Research_areas}

Charles Epstein is an [analyst](wikipedia://en/48396) and [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969). His research interests include partial differential equations, [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779), [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology), [population genetics](wikipedia://en/219268), [nuclear magnetic resonance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_magnetic_resonance) and [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging), and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506); he has also worked in hyperbolic geometry, univalent function theory, several complex variables, [microlocal analysis](wikipedia://en/6108841) and [index theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Index_theory).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He was an undergraduate in mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and graduate student at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), where he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1983 under the direction of [Peter Lax](wikipedia://en/486365).[^o7]

He was a postdoc with [William Thurston](wikipedia://en/39190) before moving to the University of Pennsylvania, where he has been since. Epstein won a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 1988.[^o4]

He is currently a Senior Research Scientist in the Center for Computational Mathematics at the Flatiron Institute, New York City, and was the [Thomas A. Scott Professor of Mathematics Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_A._Scott_Professorship_of_Mathematics) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), Philadelphia.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2014, Charles Epstein became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to analysis, geometry, and applied mathematics including medical imaging, as well as for service to the profession".[^o9] He was a co-recipient of the [Stefan Bergman Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stefan_Bergman_Prize) in 2016.[^o10]
