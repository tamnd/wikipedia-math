# Jean-Louis Nicolas

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Louis Nicolas"}

**Jean-Louis Nicolas** is a French [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527).

:::

He is the namesake (with [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284)) of the [Erdős–Nicolas numbers](wikipedia://en/42260413),[^o2][^o3] and was a frequent co-author of Erdős,[^o4] who would take over the desk of Nicolas' wife Anne-Marie (also a mathematician) whenever he would visit.[^o5] Nicolas is also known for his research on [integer partitions](wikipedia://en/104790),[^o5] and for his unusual proof that there exist infinitely many *n* for which

$$\varphi(n) < e^{-\gamma}\frac {n} {\log \log n}$$

where $\varphi(n)$ is [Euler's totient function](wikipedia://en/53452) and γ is [Euler's constant](wikipedia://en/148594): he proved this bound unconditionally by providing two different proofs, one in the case that the [Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/19344125) holds and another in the case that it fails.[^o6]

Nicolas earned his Ph.D. in 1968 as a student of [Charles Pisot](wikipedia://en/10796294). He works at [Claude Bernard University Lyon 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_Bernard_University_Lyon_1).[^o8]

A conference in honor of Nicolas' 60th birthday was held on January 14–19, 2002 at the [Centre International de Rencontres Mathématiques](wikipedia://en/54507572) in [Marseille](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marseille). The proceedings of the conference were published as a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) in *The Ramanujan Journal*.[^o9]
