# Archetypal analysis

Archetypal literary criticism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Archetypal analysis"}

**Archetypal analysis** in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) is an [unsupervised learning](wikipedia://en/233497) method similar to [cluster analysis](wikipedia://en/669675) and introduced by [Adele Cutler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adele_Cutler) and [Leo Breiman](wikipedia://en/4909283) in 1994. Rather than "typical" observations (cluster centers), it seeks extremal points in the multidimensional data, the "[archetypes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archetype)". The archetypes are [convex combinations](wikipedia://en/794534) of observations chosen so that observations can be approximated by convex combinations of the archetypes.

:::
