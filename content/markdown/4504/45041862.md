# Manfredo do Carmo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Manfredo do Carmo"}

*Image: Manfredo do Carmo 1968 (de-bordered, auto-adjusted).jpg.* Manfredo do Carmo at [Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California), California in 1968

**Manfredo Perdigão do Carmo** (15 August 1928, [Maceió](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maceió) – 30 April 2018, [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro)) was a Brazilian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He spent most of his career at [IMPA](wikipedia://en/2334708) and is seen as the doyen of [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) in Brazil.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Do Carmo studied [civil engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_engineering) at the [University of Recife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Recife) from 1947 to 1951. After working a few years as engineer, he accepted a teaching position at the newly created Institute of Physics and Mathematics at Recife.[^o5]

On suggestion of [Elon Lima](wikipedia://en/13531757), in 1959 he went to [Instituto Nacional de Matemática Pura e Aplicada](wikipedia://en/2334708) (IMPA) to improve his background[^o5][^o6] and in 1960 he moved to the US to pursue a Ph.D. in mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under the supervision of [Shiing-Shen Chern](wikipedia://en/391905). He defended his thesis *The Cohomology Ring of Certain Kahlerian Manifolds* in 1963.

After working again at [University of Recife](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Recife) and at the [University of Brasilia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Brasília), in 1966 he became professor at IMPA in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro). From 2003 to his death he was [emeritus professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at the same institution.[^o5]

Do Carmo was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowships) in [1965](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Guggenheim_Fellowships_awarded_in_1965) and [1968](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Guggenheim_Fellowships_awarded_in_1968).[^o8][^o9] In 1978 he was [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065#1978,_Helsinki) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) held in [Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Helsinki).[^o10][^o11][^o12] In 1991 he obtained a Doctorate *[honoris causa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honoris_causa)* from [Federal University of Alagoas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Alagoas)[^o13][^o14] and in 2012 from [University of Murcia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Murcia)[^o15][^o16] and from [Federal University of Amazonas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_University_of_Amazonas).[^o17][^o18]

He served as president of the [Brazilian Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Mathematical_Society) in the term 1971–1973.[^o19] He was elected a member of the [Brazilian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brazilian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1970,[^o20] a member of [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) (TWAS) in 1997[^o21] and a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in 2013.[^o22]

Among his awards, he received the [Prêmio Almirante Álavaro Alberto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prêmio_Almirante_Álavaro_Alberto) from the [National Council for Scientific and Technological Development](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Council_for_Scientific_and_Technological_Development) in 1984,[^o23] the [TWAS Prize in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_TWAS_Prize_laureates#Mathematics) in 1992,[^o21][^o24] the [National Order of Scientific Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Order_of_Scientific_Merit#Mathematics) in 1995[^o25] and the *Comenda Graciliano Ramos* from the municipality of [Maceió](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maceió) in 2000.

Do Carmo died on 30 April 2018 at the age of 89.[^o26][^o27][^o28]

## Research {#Research}

Do Carmo's main research interests were [Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/144652) and the [differential geometry of surfaces](wikipedia://en/15513875).[^o6]

In particular, he worked on [rigidity](wikipedia://en/1458875) and convexity of [isometric immersions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isometric_immersion),[^o30][^o31] stability of [hypersurfaces](wikipedia://en/525101)[^o32][^o33] and of [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734),[^o34][^o35] [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) of [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470),[^o36] [isoperimetric problems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isoperimetric_problem),[^o37] [minimal submanifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minimal_submanifold) of a [sphere](wikipedia://en/27859),[^o38][^o39] and manifolds of constant [mean curvature](wikipedia://en/684210)[^o40][^o41][^o42][^o43] and vanishing [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622).[^o44]

Do Carmo published more than 100 papers in peer-reviewed journals;[^o45] in 2012 a selection of his works was published by [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media).[^o46] He is also known for his textbooks:[^o47][^o48] they were translated into many languages and used in courses from universities such as [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and [Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University).[^o49][^o5]

He supervised 27 PhD students, including [Celso Costa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celso_Costa), [Marcos Dajczer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcos_Dajczer) and [Keti Tenenblat](wikipedia://en/46444137).[^o7]
