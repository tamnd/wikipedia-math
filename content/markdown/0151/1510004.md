# CD-57

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="CD-57"}

*Image: CD-57-IMG 0540-0542-black.jpg.*

*Image: CD-57-IMG 0544.JPG.* Opened CD-57

The **(Hagelin) CD-57** was a portable, mechanical cipher machine manufactured by [Crypto AG](wikipedia://en/602428), first produced in 1957.[^o2] It was derived from the earlier **CD-55**, and was designed to be compatible with the larger [C-52](wikipedia://en/1513791) machines. Compact, the CD-57 measured merely 5 1/8in × 3 1/8in × 1 1/2in (13 × 8 × 3.8 cm) and weighed 1.5 pounds (680 gr). The CD-57 used six wheels.

:::

A variant is the **CD-57(RT)**, a similar device using a [one-time pad](wikipedia://en/22210) system rather than rotating wheels. The **STG-61** was a licensed copy of the CD-57 by Hell.[^o3]

Sullivan (2002) shows how the CD-57 can be attacked using a [hill climbing](wikipedia://en/364002) search technique.
