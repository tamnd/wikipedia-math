# Jonathan Lazare Alperin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jonathan Lazare Alperin"}

*Image: AlperinJL.jpg.* Alperin in 1981

**Jonathan Lazare Alperin** (; June 2, 1937 – June 22, 2025) was an American mathematician specializing in the area of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890). He is notable for his work in group theory which has been cited over 500 times according to the [Mathematical Reviews](wikipedia://en/1485646). The [Alperin–Brauer–Gorenstein theorem](wikipedia://en/8918642) is named after him.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Alperin attended [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and wrote his Ph.D. dissertation *On a Special Class of Regular p-Groups* (1961) under the direction of [Graham Higman](wikipedia://en/2600152). He was awarded a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1974. He has several times (1969, 1979, and 1983) been a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o3] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]

Alperin was a professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). He published over 60 papers; his work has been cited over 500 times.

He is also known for his conjecture, (Alperin 1987), a topic of current research in [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685), and for his work on the local control of fusion (Alperin 1967), part of [local group theory](wikipedia://en/24664#Local_control). In (Alperin Brauer Gorenstein 1970), the [Alperin–Brauer–Gorenstein theorem](wikipedia://en/8918642) was proven, giving the classification of [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group) with [quasi-dihedral](wikipedia://en/1050057) [Sylow 2-subgroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sylow_p-subgroup).

Alperin died on June 22, 2025, at the age of 88.[^o8]
