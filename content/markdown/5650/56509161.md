# Julia Silge

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Julia Silge"}

**Julia Silge** (born June 10, 1978) is an American [data scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_scientist) and [software engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineer). She has developed tools for [statistical modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28statistics%29) in the [R programming language](wikipedia://en/376707),[^o3][^o4] including the [text mining](wikipedia://en/318439) [package](wikipedia://en/65740838) tidytext.[^o5] Silge currently works for [Posit PBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posit_PBC), formerly known as RStudio PBC.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Silge studied physics at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University), graduating in 2000. She obtained her [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) (2002) and [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (2005) in [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) from the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin).[^o4][^o7] She was an [adjunct professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunct_professor) at [University of New Haven](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Haven) and [Quinnipiac University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quinnipiac_University) from 2006 to 2008.

Silge has worked as a [data scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_scientist) for several companies, most recently [Stack Overflow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stack_Overflow) and [Posit PBC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Posit_PBC).[^o3][^o4] At Stack Overflow, she researched the popularity of different programming languages[^o8] and skills for technologists.[^o9] She also began working on tidytext, an [R package](wikipedia://en/65740838) for [text mining](wikipedia://en/318439), with colleague [David Robinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_G._Robinson_%28data_scientist%29). Their book *Text Mining with R: A Tidy Approach* (2017) drew on examples of text analysis ranging from [Jane Austen novels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jane_Austen),[^o10] popular songs,[^o11] [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) metadata, and Twitter archives.[^o12]

In February 2017, Silge made the news when she used a note attached to a [pizza delivery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_delivery) to contact her senator [Orrin Hatch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orrin_Hatch) to object to the nomination of [Betsy DeVos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Betsy_DeVos) as Secretary of Education, after failing to reach Hatch by phone.[^o13][^o14]
