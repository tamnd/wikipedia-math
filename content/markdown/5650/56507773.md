# Jeffrey T. Leek

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jeffrey T. Leek"}

**Jeffrey Tullis Leek** is an American biostatistician and [data scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_scientist) working as a Vice President, Chief Data Officer, and Professor at [Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hutchinson_Cancer_Research_Center).[^o3][^o4] He is an author of the Simply Statistics blog, and runs several online courses through [Coursera](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coursera), as part of their Data Science Specialization.[^o5][^o6] His most popular course is The Data Scientist's Toolbox,[^o7] which he instructed along with [Roger Peng](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Peng) and [Brian Caffo](wikipedia://en/42148175). Leek is best known for his contributions to [genomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genomic) data analysis and critical view of research and the accuracy of popular statistical methods.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Leek graduated from [Utah State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utah_State_University) in 2003 with his Bachelors of Science. Then went on to study at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) achieving a [Master's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master's_degree) in 2005 and completed a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in 2007 with [John Storey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_D._Storey) as his doctoral advisor.[^o4][^o9]

## Research and career {#Research_and_career}

Leek joined [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) as an assistant professor in [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in 2009, working at the [Bloomberg School of Public Health](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_School_of_Public_Health). In 2014 he became an associate professor in [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) and [Oncology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oncology).

Leek works in The Center for Computational Biology[^o11] at Johns Hopkins University creating statistical packages[^o12][^o13] for analysis of genomes.

He also co-edits a blog, Simply Statistics[^o14] with Roger Peng and Rafa Irizarry, which contains a mix of articles on [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) and [meta-research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meta-research).

Leek has conducted several talks at prestigious universities and locations such as a colloquium series at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard)[^o15] and a lecture at the [New York Genome Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Genome_Center) titled “Building a Comprehensive Resource for the Study of Human Gene Expression with Machine Learning and Data Science”[^o16] as a part of their lecture series.

He is an expert in reproducibility, and his work and opinions have been published in notable scientific and medical journals such as *Nature*[^o17][^o18] and the *Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences.* Leek wrote a self-published book, *The Elements of Data Analytic Style* and is considered an expert on replication.[^o19][^o20]

He is currently Vice President and Chief Data Officer at Fred Hutchinson Cancer Center in Seattle, where he and his team are working on AI-supported cancer treatment discovery.[^o24][^o4]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Leek was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2020.[^o22] In 2021, Leek won the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483).[^o23] He was named one of the one hundred most influential people in AI in 2025 by Time Magazine.[^o24]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Leek's highly cited works include

- "Capturing Heterogeneity in Gene Expression Studies by Surrogate Variable Analysis"[^o26]
- "Tackling the Widespread and Critical Impact of Batch Effects in High-Throughput Data"[^o27]
