# Valeriy Oseledets

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valeriy Oseledets"}

**Valeriy Iustinovich Oseledets** (Валерий Иустинович Оселедец; 25 May 1940 – 13 March 2025) was a Soviet and Russian mathematician.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Oseledets was born on 25 May 1940, in the [Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union).[^o3] He completed his undergraduate program in 1962 from [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), where he studied probability theory.[^o4] In 1965, he proved the [Oseledets Theorem](wikipedia://en/2537958), named after himself. He received his Ph.D. under advisor [Yakov Sinai](wikipedia://en/1751626) in 1967.[^o5]

He was a faculty member and professor under the Department of Mechanics and Mathematics at Lomonosov Moscow State University.[^o6] His work primarily focused on [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481), [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis), [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087), and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934). Oseledets died on 13 March 2025, at the age of 84.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

- **[Kolmogorov Prize](wikipedia://en/73812406)**: he received this award in 2009.
