# Foundations of Algebraic Geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Foundations of Algebraic Geometry"}

***Foundations of Algebraic Geometry*** is a book by (txt) that develops [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) over [fields](wikipedia://en/10603) of any [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621). In particular it gives a careful treatment of [intersection theory](wikipedia://en/3148602) by defining the local intersection multiplicity of two [subvarieties](wikipedia://en/248808).

:::

Weil was motivated by the need for a rigorous theory of correspondences on [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260) in positive characteristic, which he used in his proof of the [Riemann hypothesis for curves over finite fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_hypothesis_for_curves_over_finite_fields).

Weil introduced [abstract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_variety) rather than [projective varieties](wikipedia://en/320469) partly so that he could construct the [Jacobian of a curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacobian_of_a_curve). (It was not known at the time that Jacobians are always projective varieties.) It was some time before anyone found any examples of [complete abstract varieties](wikipedia://en/1047627) that are not projective.

In the 1950s Weil's work was one of several competing attempts to provide satisfactory foundations for algebraic geometry, all of which were superseded by [Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042)'s development of [schemes](wikipedia://en/364754).
