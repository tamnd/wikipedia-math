# Three subgroups lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Three subgroups lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), more specifically [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **three subgroups lemma** is a result concerning [commutators](wikipedia://en/7193). It is a consequence of [Philip Hall](wikipedia://en/1655033) and [Ernst Witt](wikipedia://en/2619911)'s [eponymous identity](wikipedia://en/7193#Identities_%28group_theory%29).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition kind=definition/notation cue=heading name="Notation"}

## Notation {#Notation}

In what follows, the following notation will be employed:

- If *H* and *K* are [subgroups](wikipedia://en/28011) of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G*, the commutator of *H* and *K*, denoted by \[*H*, *K*\], is defined as the subgroup of *G* generated by [commutators](wikipedia://en/7193#Identities_%28group_theory%29) between elements in the two subgroups. If *L* is a third subgroup, the convention that \[*H*,*K*,*L*\] = \[\[*H*,*K*\],*L*\] will be followed.
- If *x* and *y* are elements of a group *G*, the [conjugate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conjugate_%28group_theory%29) of *x* by *y* will be denoted by $x^{y}$.
- If *H* is a subgroup of a group *G*, then the [centralizer](wikipedia://en/145661) of *H* in *G* will be denoted by **C**<sub>G</sub>(*H*).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let *X*, *Y* and *Z* be subgroups of a group *G*, and assume

$[X,Y,Z]=1$ and $[Y,Z,X]=1.$

Then $[Z,X,Y]=1$.[^o6]

More generally, for a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) $N$ of $G$, if $[X,Y,Z]\subseteq N$ and $[Y,Z,X]\subseteq N$, then $[Z,X,Y]\subseteq N$.[^o7]

:::

::: {#o9 .proof cue=heading name="Proof and the Hall–Witt identity"}

## Proof and the Hall–Witt identity {#Proof_and_the_Hall–Witt_identity}

**Hall–Witt identity**

If $x,y,z\in G$, then

$$[x, y^{-1}, z]^y\cdot[y, z^{-1}, x]^z\cdot[z, x^{-1}, y]^x = 1.$$

::: {#o10 .proof cue=inline-head name="Proof of the three subgroups lemma"}

**Proof of the three subgroups lemma**

Let $x\in X$, $y\in Y$, and $z\in Z$. Then $[x,y^{-1},z]=1=[y,z^{-1},x]$, and by the Hall–Witt identity above, it follows that $[z,x^{-1},y]^{x}=1$ and so $[z,x^{-1},y]=1$. Therefore, $[z,x^{-1}]\in \mathbf{C}_G(Y)$ for all $z\in Z$ and $x\in X$. Since these elements generate $[Z,X]$, we conclude that $[Z,X]\subseteq \mathbf{C}_G(Y)$ and hence $[Z,X,Y]=1$.

:::
