# Jeffrey Lagarias

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jeffrey Lagarias"}

**Jeffrey Clark Lagarias** (born November 16, 1949) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).

:::

## Education {#Education}

While in high school in 1966, Lagarias studied [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy) at the [Summer Science Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summer_Science_Program).

He completed an S.B. and S.M. in Mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1972.[^o4] The title of his thesis was "Evaluation of certain character sums".[^o4] He was a [Putnam Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Putnam_Fellow) at MIT in 1970.[^o5] He received his Ph.D. in Mathematics from MIT for his thesis "The 4-part of the class group of a quadratic field", in 1974.[^o4][^o6] His advisor for both his masters and Ph.D. was [Harold Stark](wikipedia://en/788807).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

In 1974, he joined [AT&T Bell Laboratories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT&T_Bell_Laboratories) and eventually became a member of technical staff. From 1995 to 2004, he was a Technology Consultant at AT&T Research Laboratories. In 2004, he moved to the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) as a professor of mathematics.[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

Lagarias originally worked in analytic [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705). His later work has been in [theoretical computer science](wikipedia://en/323392).

Lagarias discovered an elementary problem that is equivalent to the [Riemann hypothesis](wikipedia://en/19344125), namely whether for all *n* > 0, we have

$$\sigma(n) \le H_n + e^{H_n} \ln H_n$$

with equality only when *n* = 1. Here *H*<sub>*n*</sub> is the *n*th [harmonic number](wikipedia://en/214729), the sum of the reciprocals of the first $n$ positive integers, and *σ*(*n*) is the [divisor function](wikipedia://en/407249), the sum of the positive divisors of *n*.[^o9]

He disproved [Keller's conjecture](wikipedia://en/33297462) in dimensions at least 10. Lagarias has also done work on the [Collatz conjecture](wikipedia://en/37895) and [Li's criterion](wikipedia://en/4049772) and has written several highly cited papers in symbolic computation with [Dave Bayer](wikipedia://en/1442686).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Lagarias received in 1986 a [Lester R. Ford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford) award from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772)[^o11] and again in 2007.[^o12][^o13]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14]

In 2024 he was elected as a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o15]
