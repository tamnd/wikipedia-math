# Johann Hommel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Hommel"}

**Johann Hommel** (also *Johannes Homelius*, *Hummelius*, *Homilius*, *Hummel*; 2 February 1518 in [Memmingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memmingen) – 4 July 1562 in [Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leipzig)) was a German [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Work {#Work}

Hommel was appointed professor of mathematics at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig) in 1551.

In 1552 or 1553, [Richard Cantzlar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Cantzlar) introduced [transversal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transversal_%28instrument_making%29) dot lines in graduations. It was a variant of the [zigzag](wikipedia://en/972019) line system introduced by Hommel. [Tycho Brahe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tycho_Brahe) obtained the zigzag line system from Hommel.

The [lunar crater](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lunar_crater) [Hommel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hommel_%28crater%29) is named after him.
