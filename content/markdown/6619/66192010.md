# Topological Hochschild homology

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Topological Hochschild homology"}

In mathematics, **Topological Hochschild homology** is a topological refinement of [Hochschild homology](wikipedia://en/10499629) which rectifies some technical issues with computations in characteristic $p$. For instance, if we consider the $\mathbb{Z}$-algebra $\mathbb{F}_p$ then

> $$HH_k(\mathbb{F}_p/\mathbb{Z}) \cong \begin{cases} \mathbb{F}_p & k \text{ even} \\ 0 & k \text{ odd} \end{cases}$$

but if we consider the ring structure on

> $$\begin{aligned} HH_*(\mathbb{F}_p/\mathbb{Z}) &= \mathbb{F}_p\langle u \rangle \\ &= \mathbb{F}_p[u,u^2/2!, u^3/3!,\ldots] \end{aligned}$$

(as a [divided power algebra](wikipedia://en/8006138) structure) then there is a significant technical issue: if we set $u \in HH_2(\mathbb{F}_p/\mathbb{Z})$, so $u^2 \in HH_4(\mathbb{F}_p/\mathbb{Z})$, and so on, we have $u^p = 0$ from the resolution of $\mathbb{F}_p$ as an algebra over $\mathbb{F}_p\otimes^\mathbf{L}\mathbb{F}_p$,[^o2] i.e.

> $$HH_k(\mathbb{F}_p/\mathbb{Z}) = H_k(\mathbb{F}_p\otimes_{ \mathbb{F}_p\otimes^\mathbf{L}\mathbb{F}_p }\mathbb{F}_p)$$

This calculation is further elaborated on the [Hochschild homology](wikipedia://en/10499629) page, but the key point is the pathological behavior of the ring structure on the Hochschild homology of $\mathbb{F}_p$. In contrast, the Topological Hochschild Homology ring has the isomorphism

> $$THH_*(\mathbb{F}_p) = \mathbb{F}_p[u]$$

giving a less pathological theory. Moreover, this calculation forms the basis of many other THH calculations, such as for smooth algebras $A/\mathbb{F}_p$

:::

## Construction {#Construction}

Recall that the [Eilenberg–MacLane spectrum](wikipedia://en/12401224) can be embed ring objects in the [derived category](wikipedia://en/985897) of the integers $D(\mathbb{Z})$ into [ring spectrum](wikipedia://en/21118494) over the ring spectrum of the [stable homotopy group of spheres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy_group_of_spheres). This makes it possible to take a commutative ring $A$ and constructing a complex analogous to the Hochschild complex using the monoidal product in ring spectra, namely, $\wedge_\mathbb{S}$ acts formally like the derived tensor product $\otimes^\mathbf{L}$ over the integers. We define the Topological Hochschild complex of $A$ (which could be a commutative [differential graded algebra](wikipedia://en/2415863), or just a [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990)) as the [simplicial complex](wikipedia://en/252686),[^o5] <sup>pg 33-34</sup> called the [Bar complex](wikipedia://en/31594198)

> $$\cdots \to HA\wedge_\mathbb{S}HA\wedge_\mathbb{S}HA \to HA\wedge_\mathbb{S}HA \to HA$$

of spectra (note that the arrows are incorrect because of Wikipedia formatting...). Because simplicial objects in spectra have a realization as a spectrum, we form the spectrum

> $$THH(A) \in \text{Spectra}$$

which has homotopy groups $\pi_i(THH(A))$ defining the topological Hochschild homology of the ring object $A$.
