# Journal of Logic, Language and Information

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Logic, Language and Information"}

The ***Journal of Logic, Language and Information*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) covering research on "natural, formal, and programming languages". It is the official journal of the European [Association for Logic, Language and Information](wikipedia://en/22910426) and was established in 1974. It is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) and the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Natasha Alechina ([Utrecht University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utrecht_University)). According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2020 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.829.[^o3]
