# Igor Dolgachev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Igor Dolgachev"}

**Igor V. Dolgachev** (born 7 April 1944[^o3]) is a Russian–American mathematician specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He has been a professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) since 1978. He introduced [Dolgachev surfaces](wikipedia://en/22729603) in 1981.[^o4]

:::

Dolgachev completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) in 1970, with thesis *On the purity of the degeneration locus of families of curves* written under the supervision of [Igor Shafarevich](wikipedia://en/1091815).
