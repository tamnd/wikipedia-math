# Karel Hrbáček

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karel Hrbáček"}

**Karel Hrbáček** (born 1944) is professor emeritus of mathematics at [City College of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_College_of_New_York).[^o2] He specializes in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), and [non-standard analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-standard_analysis).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Karel studied at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) with [Petr Vopěnka](wikipedia://en/3105466), looking at large [cardinal numbers](wikipedia://en/6173). He was awarded the degree RNDr. Before his appointment at City College of New York he was an exchange fellow at [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and a research associate at [Rockefeller University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rockefeller_University). In 1980 he received an award from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) for his article on Non-standard Set Theory.[^o4]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- 1999: (with [Thomas Jech](wikipedia://en/6207903)) *Introduction to Set Theory*, Third edition. Monographs and Textbooks in Pure and Applied Mathematics, 220. [Marcel Dekker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marcel_Dekker) [^o6][^o7][^o8]
- 1992: (with David Ballard) "Standard foundations for nonstandard analysis", [Journal of Symbolic Logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Symbolic_Logic) 57(2): 741–748
- 1979: "Nonstandard set theory", [American Mathematical Monthly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Mathematical_Monthly) 86(8): 659–677
- 1978: "Axiomatic foundations for nonstandard analysis", [Fundamenta Mathematicae](wikipedia://en/1576701) 98(1): 1–19
