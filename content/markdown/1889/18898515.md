# Petrovsky lacuna

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Petrovsky lacuna"}

*Image: Schlierenfoto Mach 1-2 Pfeilflügel - NASA.jpg.* Petrovsky lacunas are similar to the spaces between [shock waves](wikipedia://en/146253) of a [supersonic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supersonic) object.

In mathematics, a **Petrovsky lacuna**, named for the Russian mathematician [I. G. Petrovsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._G._Petrovsky), is a region where the [fundamental solution](wikipedia://en/1820858) of a linear [hyperbolic partial differential equation](wikipedia://en/1575825) vanishes. They were studied by (Petrovsky 1945) who found [topological](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topological) conditions for their existence.

:::
