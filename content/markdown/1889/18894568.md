# Mathematical programming with equilibrium constraints

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mathematical programming with equilibrium constraints"}

**Mathematical programming with equilibrium constraints (MPEC)** is the study of [constrained optimization](wikipedia://en/4171950) problems where the constraints include [variational inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Variational_inequalities) or [complementarities](wikipedia://en/18104802). MPEC is related to the [Stackelberg game](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stackelberg_game).

:::

MPEC is used in the study of [engineering design](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineering_design), [economic equilibrium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economic_equilibrium), and [multilevel games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multilevel_games).

MPEC is difficult to deal with because its [feasible region](wikipedia://en/9157119) is not necessarily [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) or even [connected](wikipedia://en/6233).
