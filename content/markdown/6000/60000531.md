# Andor Kertész (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andor Kertész"}

Kertész Andor

**Andor Kertész** (; 19 February 1929 – 3 April 1974) was a [Hungarian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and professor of mathematics at the [Lajos Kossuth University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Debrecen) (KLTE), [Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debrecen). He is the father of [linguist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistics) [András Kertész](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/András_Kertész).

:::

## Biography and career {#Biography_and_career}

Kertész was born on 19 February 1929 in [Gyula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gyula_%28town%29), [Békés County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Békés_County), Hungary.

He graduated from the Roman Catholic Secondary Grammar School at Gyula in 1947.

He earned [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degrees at [Lajos Kossuth University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Debrecen) (KLTE), [Debrecen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debrecen) in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), [Physics](wikipedia://en/22939), and [Descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) in 1952.

During his academic years he also worked as a demonstrator and then as an [intern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internship) at the Institute of Mathematics of the KLTE and he became an [aspirant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspirant) of [professors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) [Tibor Szele](wikipedia://en/22826039) and [László Rédei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/László_Rédei) in [Modern algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) in 1951 and since his graduation.

In 1954 he was appointed to an [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the Institute of Mathematics, KLTE.

He took the [candidate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences) (C.Sc.) of Mathematics degree in 1954 and was awarded [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) (D.Sc.) degree in mathematics in 1957.[^o4]

From 1960 until 1968 he acted as head of the Department of Algebra and Number Theory, Institute of Mathematics but as an [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) ([docent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent), [reader](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reader_%28academic_rank%29)) just until 1963.

From 1961 until 1963, and from 1968 to 1971, he was a visiting professor at the [Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_University_of_Halle-Wittenberg), [East Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany).

In 1962, he lectured in the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom) and [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany).

In 1963 he received the title of [university (full) professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the KLTE.

He was elected to full membership of the [Academy of Sciences Leopoldina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_Leopoldina) in 1968.

In his memory and honour they established the Andor Kertész County Memorial Competition in Mathematics in 1988.

His papers were issued in both national and international prestigious professional research scientific journals, and numerous scientific articles and books were published.

He presented his talks at several conferences and at the different science forums and he developed significant research and professional relationships in Western, Central and Eastern Europe.

He died on 3 April 1974 in Budapest due to chronic and serious illness.

## Achievements {#Achievements}

His three main fields of interest are in [Algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923): theory of [Abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974), theory of [Modules](wikipedia://en/276410) and theory of [Rings](wikipedia://en/48404). He was also interested in the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220).

He revealed the [direct sum](wikipedia://en/2084687) of modules and developed the general theory of [linear equations](wikipedia://en/17570).

He also made big and important discoveries about [Radicals of Rings](wikipedia://en/372242).

He dealt with the problems of [cardinality](wikipedia://en/6174) in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553).[^o6]

During his visiting professorship in [Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Halle_%28Saale%29), [East Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_Germany) he contributed to the discovery of the mathematical achievements of [Georg Cantor](wikipedia://en/12216), too.

He was the important [scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholar) of the Debrecen algebraic school founded by [Tibor Szele](wikipedia://en/22826039).

At the [Martin Luther University of Halle-Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Luther_University_of_Halle-Wittenberg) he had a great role in the establishment of the [Modern algebraic](wikipedia://en/19616384) school.

## Family {#Family}

His father, Lajos Kertész (1899–1974) was a teacher of music. His mother was Mária Nyíri. He had three siblings. Andor Kertész's wife was Ilona Tóth, teacher of history and geography. They had two children, a daughter, physician Gabriella Kertész and a son, linguist András Kertész.
