# Ehud de Shalit

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ehud de Shalit"}

**Ehud de Shalit** (אהוד דה שליט; born 16 March 1955) is an Israeli [number theorist](wikipedia://en/21527) and professor at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ehud de Shalit was born in [Rehovot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rehovot). His father was [Amos de-Shalit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amos_de-Shalit). He completed his [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) at the Hebrew University in 1975, and his [Ph.D](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D). at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1984 under the supervision of [Andrew Wiles](wikipedia://en/2027).

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

De Shalit joined the faculty of Hebrew University in 1987 and was promoted to full professor in 2001.[^o5] He is an editor for the [Israel Journal of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Journal_of_Mathematics).[^o5][^o6]
