# Ilan Amit

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ilan Amit"}

**Ilan Amit** (אִילָן עָמִית; 1935-01-19 – 2013) was an Israeli [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [spiritual philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spiritual_philosophy), and defence consultant. He worked as a [strategist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategist) and senior advisor to Israel's defence establishment, including the [Mossad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Ilan Kroch (later Amit) was born in Haifa. His father, a [mathematics teacher](wikipedia://en/326471), was deputy principal of the [Hebrew Reali School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_Reali_School) and a founder of the [Hebrew Scouts Movement in Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_Scouts_Movement_in_Israel).[^o4][^o8] Amit studied at the Reali School, where he was a student of [Josef Schächter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josef_Schächter). In 1960, Amit was one of the founders of the [moshav shitufi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moshav_shitufi) [Yodfat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yodfat), where he became a proponent of the teachings of mystic [George Gurdjieff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Gurdjieff).[^o5][^o6]

After completing his undergraduate studies in mathematics at the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_–_Israel_Institute_of_Technology), Amit worked at [Mekorot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mekorot), soon becoming head of the company's [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) department.[^o7] He completed his Ph.D. in mathematics from the Technion in 1967, under the supervision of [Elisha Netanyahu](wikipedia://en/23745967).

Amit joined the military research department at [Rafael](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Advanced_Defense_Systems) in the late 1970s, not long after which he became blind as the result of illness. In the late 1980s Amit joined a team in [Mossad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mossad)'s intelligence division that aimed to engage in intelligence estimates and formulate recommendations in the area of policy and strategy.[^o8]

In 2009, he became a member of the Prime Minister's [National Security Council](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Security_Council_%28Israel%29). He died at the age of 78 following a stroke, survived by his wife and four children.[^o9]

## Published works {#Published_works}

Amit has translated [Kierkegaard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Søren_Kierkegaard) into Hebrew and published essays on [Emily Dickinson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emily_Dickinson) and on therapy of the absurd, along with many [classified](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classified_information) research papers.[^o14] His published books include:

- Amit, Ilan. (2013). "חידת הנוכחות". Sifrey Aliyat HaGag. isbn:978-965-545-667-7
- Amit, Ilan. (2009). "The Lamp: A (Not Quite) Spiritual Biography". Eureka Editions. isbn:978-90-72395-64-1. <https://books.google.com/books?id=tI07YgEACAAJ>
- Amit, Ilan. (2005). "גורדייף והעבודה הפנימית". Mapa Publishing. isbn:978-965-521-015-6
