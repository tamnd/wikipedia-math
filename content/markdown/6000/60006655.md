# Samuel Segun Okoya

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Samuel Segun Okoya"}

**Samuel Segun Okoya** (born 20 October 1958, in [Zaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zaria), [Kaduna State](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaduna_State), Nigeria) is an [academic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_staff) in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) at [Obafemi Awolowo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obafemi_Awolowo_University). He is the editor-in-chief of the *Journal* and *Notices* of the Nigerian Mathematical Society[^o5] and the First Occupier of Pastor E.A Adeboye Outstanding Professor of Mathematics (Endowed Professorial Chair) [University of Lagos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Lagos).[^o6] He is the [alumnus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alumnus) to attain the position of [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) and head of the [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) Department at [Obafemi Awolowo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Obafemi_Awolowo_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Okoya had his entire tertiary education at Obafemi Awolowo University. He graduated with his bachelor's degree in 1983, earned his master's degree in 1986 and Ph.D. in 1989. His PhD dissertation, supervised by Reuben O. Ayeni, was titled "A Mathematical Model for Explosions with Chain Branching and Chain Breaking Kinetics".[^o8] He is a Fellow of the Mathematical Association of Nigeria and Nigerian Mathematical Society. He regularly visits the [Abdus Salam International Centre for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abdus_Salam_International_Centre_for_Theoretical_Physics), [The World Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Academy_of_Sciences) and [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868).
