# Gyroelongated bipyramid

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gyroelongated bipyramid"}

The **gyroelongated bipyramids** are the [polyhedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polyhedra) constructed with a [bipyramid](wikipedia://en/4153) and an [antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851). Their [dual polyhedra](wikipedia://en/8815) are the [truncated trapezohedra](wikipedia://en/3521844). The bipyramid is sliced into two congruent pyramids and then attached to the bases of an antiprism in between; such a process of construction is known as gyroelongation. The resulting construction has triangular faces, classified as [simplicial polyhedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplicial_polyhedron). There are infinitely many members of gyroelongated bipyramids.[^o2]

:::

Some members are special cases for having [equilateral triangular](wikipedia://en/173285) faces, which are known as [deltahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deltahedra): the [gyroelongated square bipyramid](wikipedia://en/715707)[^o3] and [regular icosahedron](wikipedia://en/14968).[^o4][^o5] For a gyroelongated triangular bipyramid, it is a non-convex deltahedron because its faces are [coplanar](wikipedia://en/1508434), thereby it is not strictly convex. Considering that each pair of its triangles merged into [rhombi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombi), the resulting polyhedron can be seen as a [trigonal trapezohedron](wikipedia://en/3504388).
