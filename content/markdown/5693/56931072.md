# Boris Kashin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Kashin"}

*Image: Boris Kashin math lecture 2021.JPG.* Lecture by Boris Kashin at the [Karelian Scientific Center of the Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karelian_Research_Centre_of_RAS), 2021

**Boris Sergeevich Kashin** (Борис Сергеевич Кашин; born July 3, 1951, in [Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow)) is a Russian mathematician,[^o3] Academician of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) (since 2011),[^o3] [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doktor_nauk),[^o3] Professor at the [MSU Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics).[^o3] Member of the [Central Committee of the Communist Party of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_Committee_of_the_Communist_Party_of_the_Russian_Federation) since 2000.

:::

He graduated from the [MSU Faculty of Mechanics and Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MSU_Faculty_of_Mechanics_and_Mathematics) in 1973. And then entered to the [Steklov Mathematical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Mathematical_Institute), where he currently works.[^o3]

In 1976 he defended his [Candidate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)'s Dissertation. In 1977 he defended his doctoral dissertation. In 1990 he received the title of Professor.

Member of the [Communist Party of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Communist_Party_of_the_Soviet_Union) since 1980. He was elected a corresponding member of the [Russian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1997.[^o3]

He is currently Editor-in-Chief of the journal *[Matematicheskii Sbornik](wikipedia://en/11555472)*.[^o3]

In 2012, as a deputy of the [State Duma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Duma), Kashin was among the initiators of the bill of the [Dima Yakovlev law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dima_Yakovlev_Law).[^o4]
