# Encyclopedic Dictionary of Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Encyclopedic Dictionary of Mathematics"}

The ***Encyclopedic Dictionary of Mathematics*** (***EDM***) is a translation of the Japanese *Iwanami Sūgaku Jiten*. The editor of the first and second editions was [Shokichi Iyanaga](wikipedia://en/18357287); the editor of the third edition was [Kiyosi Itô](wikipedia://en/1329240); the fourth edition was edited by the [Mathematical Society of Japan](wikipedia://en/18376786).

:::
