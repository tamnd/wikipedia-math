# Burgess Davis

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Burgess Davis"}

**Burgess James Davis** is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) known for his research in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), particularly his work on [martingales](wikipedia://en/274536), [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436), and [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451). He is a professor emeritus in the Departments of Statistics and Mathematics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Davis earned his B.S. degree from [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) in 1965, followed by an M.S. and Ph.D. from the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois) in 1966 and 1968 respectively.[^o4] His doctoral advisor was [Donald Burkholder](wikipedia://en/25786492).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

He joined the faculty of [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) from 1968 to 1974. In 1974 he joined [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) where he served as a professor of Statistics and Mathematics until achieving emeritus status in 2014.[^o7]

From 1981 to 1987, Professor Davis served as associate editor of the [Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748), a publication of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), and then as editor from 1991 to 1993.[^o8] He also served as associate editor (from 1988 to 1990) of the [Transactions of the AMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transactions_of_the_AMS) and (from 1999 to 2002) of the [Illinois Journal of Math](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_Journal_of_Math).[^o4]

## Research contributions {#Research_contributions}

Davis's research focuses on the interconnections between classical analysis (particularly [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147)), probability theory, and [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895). His most notable contributions include:

- [Stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis) and [martingale theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martingale_theory): His work established key inequalities for [stochastic integrals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_integral) and martingales, which includes a strong version of the [Burkholder–Gundy inequalities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Burkholder–Davis–Gundy_inequalities), which are central to modern stochastic analysis.[^o10]
- [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) and [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342): He explored the relationship between Brownian motion and concepts from [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and potential theory, including the work on the moments of the lifetime and conditioned Brownian motion in various domains.[^o10]
- [Random walks](wikipedia://en/235451): Davis has published research on reinforced random walks, a class of self-interacting random processes where the probability of a step is proportional to the time already spent at the neighboring vertex.[^o11]

He has supervised at least seven doctoral students.[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- Davis was named a Top Ten Outstanding Teacher in the College of Science in 1999.[^o4]
- He was elected a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), a distinction recognizing individuals who have made "outstanding contributions to the development and application of statistics and probability."[^o13]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Davis has published over 50 peer-reviewed articles in major mathematical and statistical journals.[^o4] Among them are:

- Davis, B. (1969). Comparison tests for martingale inequalities. *[Annals of Mathematical Statistics](wikipedia://en/23772805)*, 40(2), 505–508.[^o4]
- Davis, B. (1976). On the $L^p$ norms of stochastic integrals and other martingales. *[Duke Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_Mathematical_Journal)*, 43(4), 697–704.[^o4]
- Davis, B. (1979). Brownian motion and analytic functions. *[Annals of Probability](wikipedia://en/34673748)*, 7(6), 913–932.[^o4]
- Davis, B., & Volkov, S. (2004). Vertex reinforced random jump processes on trees and finite graphs. *Probability Theory and Related Fields*, 128(1), 42–62.[^o10]
