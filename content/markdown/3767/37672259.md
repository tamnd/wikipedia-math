# Mazur's control theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mazur's control theorem"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), **Mazur's control theorem**, introduced by (txt), describes the behavior in **Z**<sub>*p*</sub> extensions of the [Selmer group](wikipedia://en/1970862) of an [abelian variety](wikipedia://en/247152) over a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field).

:::
