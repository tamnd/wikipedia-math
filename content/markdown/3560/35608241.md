# Michael Karinski

Ivanovich

*Image: Михаил Каринский.jpg.* Michael Ivanovich Karinski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Karinski"}

**Michael Ivanovich Karinski** (Михаил Иванович Каринский; 1840–1917) was a Russian [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher).[^o2] His *[Classification of Inferences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_of_Inferences)* (1880) has been called "the most important single work in [logical theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Logical_theory) that nineteenth-century Russia produced."[^o3]

:::
