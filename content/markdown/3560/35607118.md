# Dara Ó Briain: School of Hard Sums

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dara Ó Briain: School of Hard Sums"}

***Dara Ó Briain: School of Hard Sums*** is a British comedy game show about the subject of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), based on the Japanese show *Comaneci University Mathematics*.[^o3] The programme was broadcast on [Dave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dave_%28TV_channel%29), was presented by [Dara Ó Briain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dara_Ó_Briain), and starred [Marcus du Sautoy](wikipedia://en/3935943). Each episode was themed and Ó Briain, along with a guest or guests, attempted to solve various conundrums set by du Sautoy.[^o3] At the end of each episode, Ó Briain set homework questions for viewers; the answers could be checked on the show's website.[^o4]
