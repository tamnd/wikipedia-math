# Raghunatha Shiromani

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raghunatha Shiromani"}

**Raghunatha Shiromani** (রঘুনাথ শিরোমণি, [IAST](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IAST): Raghunātha Śiromaṇi) (c. 1477–1547[^o3]) was an Indian [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_philosophy) and [logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_logic). He was the head ( The Chancellor ) of the [Ancient Mithila University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Mithila_University) also known as [Mithila Vidyapeeth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ancient_Mithila_University).[^o4] He was born in a [brahmin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brahmin) family at [Nabadwip](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nabadwip), in the [Bengal Sultanate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bengal_Sultanate) (present-day [Nadia district](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nadia_district) of [West Bengal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Bengal) state). He was the grandson of Śulapāṇi (c. 14th century CE), a noted writer on [Smṛti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smṛti) from his mother's side. He was a pupil of [Vāsudeva Sārvabhauma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vasudeva_Sarvabhauma). He brought the new school of [Nyaya](wikipedia://en/59941), [Navya Nyāya](wikipedia://en/4286622), representing the final development of Indian [formal logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Formal_logic), to its zenith of analytic power.

:::

Raghunatha's analysis of relations revealed the true nature of number, inseparable from the abstraction of natural phenomena, and his studies of [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) dealt with the [negation](wikipedia://en/161019) or [nonexistence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonexistence) of a complex [reality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reality). His most famous work in logic was the [Tattvacintāmaṇidīdhiti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tattvachintamanididhiti), a commentary on the *[Tattvacintāmaṇi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tattvacintāmaṇi)* of [Gangeśa Upādhyāya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gangesha), founder of the *Navya Nyāya* school.
