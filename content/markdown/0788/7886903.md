# Lawrence D. Brown

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lawrence D. Brown"}

**Lawrence David (Larry) Brown** (16 December 1940 – 21 February 2018)[^o3][^o4][^o5] was Miers Busch Professor and Professor of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [Wharton School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School) of the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) in [Philadelphia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philadelphia), Pennsylvania. He is known for his groundbreaking work in a broad range of fields including [decision theory](wikipedia://en/446216), [recurrence](wikipedia://en/60876#Properties) and [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), nonparametric function estimation, minimax and adaptation theory, and the analysis of [census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/US_Census) data and [call-center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Call_centre) data.[^o17]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Brown was educated at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) and [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), where he earned his Ph.D. in 1964. He earned numerous honors, including election to the [United States National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences), and published widely, beginning with his Ph.D. research, which made major advances in [admissibility](wikipedia://en/1776726).[^o3] He was president of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) in 1992–93. He was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2013.[^o7]

After having been assistant professor at [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley), associate professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) – with the latter move entailing a change from a statistics to a mathematics department, allowing him to avoid being drafted for the [Vietnam War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnam_War)[^o3] – and professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) and [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), he was invited to join the Department of Statistics at the [Wharton School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School) of the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania).

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Brown was born in Los Angeles to parents [Louis M. Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_M._Brown) and Hermione Brown. He was married to [Linda Zhao](wikipedia://en/64225295), a fellow statistician at the Wharton School.[^o9]
