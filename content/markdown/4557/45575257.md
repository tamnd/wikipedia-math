# Lucy R. Wyatt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lucy R. Wyatt"}

**Lucy R. Wyatt** is an English mathematician and a professor in the School of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield), Yorkshire. She is a member of the Environmental Dynamics research group in the School of Mathematics.

:::

## Education {#Education}

Wyatt obtained a BSc in mathematics from the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) in 1972. In 1973 she was awarded an M.Sc. in fluid mechanics from the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol), and in 1976 she obtained her PhD in [physical oceanography](wikipedia://en/387440) from the [University of Southampton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southampton).

In 1981 she began working on the oceanographic applications of [HF radar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HF_radar) as a research assistant at the [University of Birmingham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Birmingham), and in 1987 she joined the Department of Applied Mathematics, the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield). Wyatt's research interests include high-frequency radar oceanography and ocean surface waves. She has been an associate editor of the *[IEEE Journal of Oceanic Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Journal_of_Oceanic_Engineering)*.[^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Wyatt was named to the 2025 class of [IEEE Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow) "for contributions to HF radar metocean inversion methods, measurement campaigns, and validation".[^o5]
