# Joseph Liberman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph Liberman"}

**Joseph Liberman** (1917 in [Henichesk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henichesk) – August 1941 in Иосиф Меерович Либерман) was a Soviet mathematician, a student of [Aleksandrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Danilovich_Aleksandrov), best known for [Liberman's lemma](wikipedia://en/25125675).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

In 1936 he entered [Leningrad State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University) as one of the winners of the first city Mathematical Olimpiad.

He worked in geometry and real analysis. In 1938 he published his first paper. In 1939 he entered graduate school.

He served in the army since June 1941. He defended Ph.D. thesis in June 1941. At this time he was already lieutenant of Navy anti-aircraft warfare.

In August 1941 he was sent to [Tallinn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tallinn), [Estonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Estonia) where he died.

Three geometric papers of Liberman appeared in 1941 and another in 1943.
