# Phyllis Chinn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Phyllis Chinn"}

**Phyllis Zweig Chinn** (née Zweig; born September 26, 1941) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who holds a professorship in mathematics, [women's studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_studies), and teaching preparation at [Cal Poly Humboldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_Polytechnic_University,_Humboldt) in [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California). Her publications concern [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), and the history of [women in mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women_in_mathematics).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Chinn was born in [Rochester, New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rochester,_New_York) and graduated in 1962 from [Brandeis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brandeis_University).[^o3] She earned her Ph.D. in 1969 from the [University of California, Santa Barbara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Santa_Barbara) with a dissertation on [graph isomorphism](wikipedia://en/247577) supervised by [Paul Kelly](wikipedia://en/45092620).[^o3] She taught at [Towson State College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Towson_University), a training school for teachers in [Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maryland), from 1969 to 1975, and earned tenure there in 1974, before moving to Cal Poly Humboldt.[^o3] At the time she joined the Cal Poly Humboldt faculty, she was the first female mathematics professor there; the only other female professor in the sciences was a [biologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biologist).[^o14]

In 1997 she became chair of the mathematics department at Cal Poly Humboldt.[^o6]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Chinn has written highly cited work on [graph bandwidth](wikipedia://en/27414778),[^o8] [dominating sets](wikipedia://en/1747972),[^o9] and on bandwidth.[^o14][^o10]

Chinn is also an avid [juggler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Juggling),[^o14] and founded a juggling club at Cal Poly Humboldt in the 1980s.[^o11]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Cal Poly Humboldt named Chinn as Outstanding Professor for 1988–1989.[^o13] She was the 2010 winner of the [Louise Hay Award](wikipedia://en/51270001) for Contributions to Mathematics Education, given by the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698), for her work in improving mathematics education at the middle and high school levels and encouraging young women to become mathematicians.[^o14]
