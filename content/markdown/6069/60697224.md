# Introduction to Statistical Pattern Recognition

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Introduction to Statistical Pattern Recognition"}

***Introduction to Statistical Pattern Recognition*** is a book by [Keinosuke Fukunaga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keinosuke_Fukunaga), providing an introduction to statistical [pattern recognition](wikipedia://en/126706). The book was first published in 1972 by [Academic Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_Press), with a 2nd edition being published in 1990.[^o3]

:::

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

- Chapter 1: Introduction
- Chapter 2: Random Vectors and Their Properties
- Chapter 3: Hypothesis Testing
- Chapter 4: Parametric Classifiers
- Chapter 5: Parameter Estimation
- Chapter 6: Nonparametric Density Estimation
- Chapter 7: Nonparametric Classification and Error Estimation
- Chapter 8: Successive Parameter Estimation
- Chapter 9: Feature Extraction and Linear Mapping for Signal Representation
- Chapter 10: Feature Extraction and Linear Mapping for Classification
- Chapter 11: Clustering

## Reception {#Reception}

The book has received reviews from publications including [Thomas M. Cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_M._Cover) in the journal *[IEEE Transactions on Information Theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Transactions_on_Information_Theory)*, [Anthony J. Duben](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anthony_J._Duben) in the journal *[ACM Computing Reviews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Computing_Reviews)*, and [John Clements Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Clements_Davis) in the journal *[Computers & Geosciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computers_&_Geosciences)*.[^o6][^o7][^o8]
