# Jacek Banasiak

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacek Banasiak"}

**Jacek Banasiak** (born 15 March 1959) is a Polish mathematician who is a Professor and [South African Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Research_Chair) in Mathematical Models and Methods in Biosciences and Bioengineering at the [University of Pretoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pretoria), South Africa.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Jacek Banasiak was born on 15 March 1959 in [Łódź](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łódź), Poland.[^o4] He obtained a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) (MSc) in Math from the [Łódź University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Łódź_University_of_Technology) in 1981,[^o23] and [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) (PhD) in mathematics on 15 March 1989 from the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde), Scotland.[^o5] He was [hablitated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) (PD) in Physics on 17 June 1999 from the Faculty of Mathematics, Informatics and Mechanics, [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) with a thesis titled *Singularly Perturbed Evolution Equations with Applications in Kinetic Theory and Other Branches of Mathematical Physics*.[^o6]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Banasiak became a professor on 21 December 2007,[^o8] and has been a Professor of Mathematical Sciences at the [University of KwaZulu-Natal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_KwaZulu-Natal) from 2011 until 2015.[^o9][^o10] After, he joined the [University of Pretoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pretoria) and became the [South African Research Chair](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Research_Chair) in Mathematical Models and Methods in Biosciences and Bioengineering at the Department of Mathematics and Applied Mathematics,[^o11] [extraordinary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraordinary_professor) at the Łódź University of Technology,[^o12] and a [visiting professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Visiting_scholar) of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the Strathclyde University.[^o13]

Banasiak research focuses on [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) especially [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) theory,[^o14] [asymptotic analysis](wikipedia://en/641995),[^o15][^o16] [mathematical biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_biology),[^o17][^o18][^o19] fragmentations coagulation equations,[^o20] and [difference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Difference_equation) and [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309).[^o21][^o22]

Banasiak served as the vice president of the [South African Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/52070946) (2001–2005).[^o9][^o23] He is a member of the [Academy of Science of South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Science_of_South_Africa) and the [African Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1575286) (AIMS).[^o24] He is also the Editor-in-Chief of [Afrika Matematika](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Afrika_Matematica) since 2010,[^o25][^o9] Associate Editor of Quaestiones Mathematicae[^o26] and Evolution Equations and Control Theory (EECT),[^o27] and a member of the Editorial Board of Mathematical Methods for the Applied Sciences[^o28] and Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems.[^o29]

## Awards and honours {#Awards_and_honours}

In 2012, Banasiak won the South African Mathematical Society (SAMS) award for Research Distinction.[^o31] In 2014, he was honoured with the [Polish Cross of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_of_Merit_%28Poland%29) (Silver) for his contributions to science.[^o23] Banasiak was elected a Fellow of the [African Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2016.[^o32]
