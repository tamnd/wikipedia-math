# Busemann's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Busemann's theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Busemann's theorem** is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) in [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417) and [geometric tomography](wikipedia://en/32445296). It was first proved by [Herbert Busemann](wikipedia://en/25491802) in 1949 and was motivated by his theory of area in [Finsler spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finsler_space).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let *K* be a [convex body](wikipedia://en/8652020) in *n*-[dimensional](wikipedia://en/8398) [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) **R**<sup>*n*</sup> containing the [origin](wikipedia://en/1313432) in its [interior](wikipedia://en/55610). Let *S* be an (*n* − 2)-dimensional [linear subspace](wikipedia://en/56357) of **R**<sup>*n*</sup>. For each [unit vector](wikipedia://en/167053) *θ* in *S*<sup>⊥</sup>, the [orthogonal complement](wikipedia://en/1058833) of *S*, let *S*<sub>*θ*</sub> denote the (*n* − 1)-dimensional [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) containing *θ* and *S*. Define *r*(*θ*) to be the (*n* − 1)-dimensional volume of *K* ∩ *S*<sub>*θ*</sub>. Let *C* be the curve {*θr*(*θ*)} in *S*<sup>⊥</sup>. Then *C* forms the boundary of a convex body in *S*<sup>⊥</sup>.
