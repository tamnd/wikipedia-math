# Skorokhod problem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Skorokhod problem"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), the **Skorokhod problem** is the problem of solving a [stochastic differential equation](wikipedia://en/1361454) with a reflecting boundary condition.[^o2]

:::

The problem is named after [Anatoliy Skorokhod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoliy_Skorokhod) who first published the solution to a stochastic differential equation for a [reflecting Brownian motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflecting_Brownian_motion).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

## Problem statement {#Problem_statement}

The classic version of the problem states[^o7] that given a [càdlàg](wikipedia://en/30862839) process {*X*(*t*), t ≥ 0} and an [M-matrix](wikipedia://en/7939066) *R*, then stochastic processes {*W*(*t*), t ≥ 0} and {*Z*(*t*), t ≥ 0} are said to solve the Skorokhod problem if for all non-negative *t* values,

1. $$W(t)=X(t)+R Z(t)\ge 0$$
1. $Z(0)=0$ and $dZ(t) \ge 0$
1. $$\int_0^t W_i(s)\text{d}Z_i(s)=0.$$

The matrix *R* is often known as the reflection matrix, *W*(*t*) as the reflected process and *Z*(*t*) as the regulator process.
