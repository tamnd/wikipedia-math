# String art

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="String art"}

\_\_notoc\_\_

*Image: StringArt.jpg.* String art, created with thread and paper

*Image: E8-with-thread.jpg.* A string art representing a projection of the 8-dimensional [4<sub>21</sub> polytope](wikipedia://en/19236362)

*Image: Quadratic Beziers in string art.svg.* Quadratic Béziers in string art: The end points (**•**) and control point (**×**) define the quadratic Bézier curve (**⋯**). The arc is a segment of a [parabola](wikipedia://en/23231).

**String art**, **pin and thread art**, or **filography** is characterized by an arrangement of colored thread strung between points to form [geometric patterns](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometric_pattern) or representational designs such as a ship's [sails](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sail), sometimes with other artist material comprising the remainder of the work. Thread, wire, or string is wound around a grid of nails hammered into a [velvet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Velvet)-covered wooden board. Though straight lines are formed by the string, the slightly different angles and metric positions at which strings intersect gives the appearance of [Bézier curves](wikipedia://en/4664) (as in the mathematical concept of [envelope](wikipedia://en/880235) of a family of straight lines). [Quadratic Bézier curves](wikipedia://en/4664#Quadratic_curves) are obtained from strings based on two intersecting segments. Other forms of string art include [Spirelli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spirelli), which is used for [cardmaking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardmaking) and [scrapbooking](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrapbooking), and curve stitching, in which string is stitched through holes.

:::

String art has its origins in the 'curve stitch' activities invented by [Mary Everest Boole](wikipedia://en/2770094) at the end of the 19th century to make mathematical ideas more accessible to children.[^o2] It was popularised as a decorative craft in the late 1960s through kits and books.[^o3]

A computational form of string art that can produce photo-realistic artwork was introduced by Petros Vrellis, in 2016.[^o4]
