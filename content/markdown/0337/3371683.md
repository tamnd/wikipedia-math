# ISO 31-11

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="ISO 31-11"}

**ISO 31-11:1992** was the part of [international standard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_standard) [ISO 31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ISO_31) that defines *mathematical signs and symbols for use in physical sciences and technology*. It was superseded in 2009 by ISO 80000-2:2009 and subsequently revised in 2019 as [ISO-80000-2:2019](wikipedia://en/9007520#Part_2:_Mathematics).[^o2]

:::

It included definitions for symbols for [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) and [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826), [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) and [special functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_function) and values, [matrices](wikipedia://en/20556859), [vectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vector_%28mathematics%29), and [tensors](wikipedia://en/29965), [coordinate systems](wikipedia://en/81931), and miscellaneous [mathematical relations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_relation).[^o3]
