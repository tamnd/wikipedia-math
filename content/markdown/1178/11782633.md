# Kevin Mulligan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kevin Mulligan"}

**Kevin Mulligan** (born 23 June 1951) is a British philosopher, working on [ontology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ontology), the [philosophy of mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_mind), and [Austrian philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_philosophy). He is currently Honorary Professor at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), Full Professor at the [University of Italian Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Italian_Switzerland),[^o3] Director of Research at the Institute of Philosophy of Lugano, and member of the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea)[^o4] and of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Letters,_History_and_Antiquities).[^o5] He is also known for his work with [Peter Simons](wikipedia://en/1569373) and [Barry Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Smith_%28academic%29) on [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics) and the history of [Austrian philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_philosophy).

:::

A major [Festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) on his work was published in 2014.[^o6]

Since 2016 he is academic director and professor at the [Università della Svizzera italiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Università_della_Svizzera_italiana) in Switzerland.
