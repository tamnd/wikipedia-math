# Monica VanDieren

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monica VanDieren"}

**Monica M. VanDieren** is an American mathematician specializing in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858). She is University Professor of Mathematics and Director of the University Honors Program at [Robert Morris University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Morris_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

VanDieren graduated from the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) in 1996, with a bachelor's degree in mathematics. She did her graduate studies in mathematical sciences at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University) through their Pure & Applied Logic Program, earning a master's degree in 1998 and completing her Ph.D. in 2002. Her dissertation was *Categoricity and Stability in Abstract Elementary Classes*, with [Rami Grossberg](wikipedia://en/5688623) as her [doctoral advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor).

After completing her doctorate, she was a Szegö Assistant Professor of Mathematics at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) for 2002–2003, and then T. H. Hildebrandt Assistant Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) from 2003 to 2006, before joining the Robert Morris University mathematics department in 2006. She was promoted to full professor in 2012 and University Professor in 2016; she was co-director of the University Honors Program from 2008 to 2018 and director from 2018 to 2022. In August 2022, she joined IBM's Quantum Industry and Technical Services.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Some of VanDieren's research has been directed towards [Shelah's categoricity conjecture](wikipedia://en/27344508#Shelah's_categoricity_conjecture) in [classification theory](wikipedia://en/15828771).

Beyond VanDieren's university work, she has also led mathematics enrichment workshops for middle school and high school students, for instance using [origami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Origami) to demonstrate the beauty of mathematics.

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

VanDieren is married to Rami Grossberg.
