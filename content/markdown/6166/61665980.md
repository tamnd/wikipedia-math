# Compound of six octahedra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of six octahedra"}

The **compound of six octahedra** has two forms. One form is a symmetric arrangement of 6 [octahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octahedra), considered as square bipyramid. It is a dual of a special case of the [compound of 6 cubes with rotational freedom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_six_cubes_with_rotational_freedom).

:::

Another form is a dual of another [compound of six cubes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_six_cubes).
