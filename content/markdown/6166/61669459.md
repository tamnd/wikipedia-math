# Gitta Kutyniok

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gitta Kutyniok"}

**Gitta Kutyniok** (born 1972) is a German applied mathematician known for her research in [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), [deep learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning), [compressed sensing](wikipedia://en/11403316), and [image processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Image_processing). She has a Bavarian AI Chair for "Mathematical Foundations of Artificial Intelligence" in the institute of mathematics at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Kutyniok was educated in [Detmold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detmold), and obtained a diploma in mathematics and computer science at [Paderborn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paderborn_University) in 1996. She then completed her doctorate ([Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) at Paderborn University in 2000. Her dissertation, *Time-Frequency Analysis on Locally Compact Groups*, was supervised by Eberhard Kaniuth.

From 2000 to 2008, she held short term positions at Paderborn University, the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology), the [University of Giessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Giessen), [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis), [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), and [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). In 2006, she earned her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in [Giessen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giessen), in 2008, she became a full professor at [Osnabrück University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Osnabrück_University), and in 2011, she was given the Einstein Chair at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin). In 2018, she added courtesy affiliations with computer science and electrical engineering at TU Berlin. In October 2020, she moved to [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich), where she holds a Bavarian AI chair.

Since taking her position in Berlin, she has also visited [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), and taken an adjunct faculty position at the [University of Tromsø](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tromsø).

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

Kutyniok became a member of the [Berlin-Brandenburg Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Brandenburg_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) in 2016. In 2019 she was named a [SIAM Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Fellow) "for contributions to applied [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), compressed sensing, and imaging sciences". She was named an [IEEE Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Fellow), in the 2024 class of fellows, "for contributions to the mathematical theory of artificial intelligence in signal processing and communication".

She was the Emmy-Noether Lecturer of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in 2013, and has been selected as a plenary speaker at the eighth [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846), in 2020. In 2021 she was elected Vice President-at-Large for SIAM with term running 1 January 2022 – 31 December 2023.[^o5]
