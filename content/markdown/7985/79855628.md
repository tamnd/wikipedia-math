# Diamond operation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Diamond operation"}

In [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **diamond operation** of [simplicial sets](wikipedia://en/1027784) is an [operation](wikipedia://en/4140245) taking two simplicial sets to construct another simplicial set. It is closely related to the [join of simplicial sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Join_%28simplicial_sets%29) and used in an alternative construction of the [twisted diagonal](wikipedia://en/79855645).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

*Image: Join.svg.* Visualization of the diamond $X\diamond Y$ with the blue part representing $X$ and the green part representing $Y$.

For simplicial set $X$ and $Y$, their *diamond* $X\diamond Y$ is the [pushout](wikipedia://en/582024) of the diagram:[^o4][^o5]

$$X\times Y\times\Delta^1\leftarrow X\times Y\times\partial\Delta^1\rightarrow X+Y.$$

One has a canonical map $X\diamond Y\rightarrow\Delta^0\diamond\Delta^0 \cong\Delta^1$ for which the fiber of $0$ is $X$ and the fiber of $1$ is $Y$.

:::

## Right adjoints {#Right_adjoints}

Let $Y$ be a simplicial set. The functor $Y\diamond -\colon \mathbf{sSet}\rightarrow Y\backslash\mathbf{sSet}, X\mapsto(Y\mapsto X\diamond Y)$ has a [right adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_adjoint) $Y\backslash\mathbf{sSet}\rightarrow\mathbf{sSet}, (t\colon Y\rightarrow W)\mapsto t\backslash\backslash W$ (alternatively denoted $Y\backslash\backslash W$) and the functor $-\diamond Y\colon \mathbf{sSet}\rightarrow Y\backslash\mathbf{sSet}, X\mapsto(Y\mapsto X\diamond Y)$ has a right adjoint $Y\backslash\mathbf{sSet}\rightarrow\mathbf{sSet}, (t\colon Y\rightarrow W)\mapsto W//t$ (alternatively denoted $W//Y$).[^o7][^o8] A special case is $Y=\Delta^0$ the [terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_object) simplicial set, since $\mathbf{sSet}_* =\Delta^0\backslash\mathbf{sSet}$ is the category of pointed simplicial sets.

::: {#o10 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- For simplicial sets $X$ and $Y$, there is a unique morphism $\gamma_{X,Y}\colon X\diamond Y\rightarrow X*Y$ from the [join of simplicial sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Join_%28simplicial_sets%29) compatible with the maps $X+Y\rightarrow X*Y,X\diamond Y$ and $X*Y,X\diamond Y\rightarrow\Delta^1$.[^o11] It is a weak categorical equivalence, hence a weak equivalence of the [Joyal model structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyal_model_structure).[^o12][^o13]
- For a simplicial set $X$, the functors $X\diamond -,-\diamond X\colon\mathbf{sSet}\rightarrow\mathbf{sSet}$ preserve weak categorical equivalences.[^o14][^o15]

:::
