# Join (category theory)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Join"}

In [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **join** of [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) is an [operation](wikipedia://en/4140245) making the [category of small categories](wikipedia://en/2072741) into a [monoidal category](wikipedia://en/330604). In particular, it takes two small categories to construct another small category. Under the [nerve](wikipedia://en/5127933) construction, it corresponds to the [join of simplicial sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Join_%28simplicial_sets%29).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

For small categories $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{D}$, their *join* $\mathcal{C}\star\mathcal{D}$ is the small category with:[^o4]

$$\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{C}\star\mathcal{D}) =\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{C})\sqcup\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{D});$$

$$\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}\star\mathcal{D}}(X,Y) :=\begin{cases} \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{C}}(X,Y); & X,Y\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) \\ \operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal{D}}(X,Y); & X,Y\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{D}) \\ \{*\}; & X\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{C}), Y\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{D}) \\ \emptyset; & X\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{D}), Y\in\operatorname{Ob}(\mathcal{C}) \end{cases}.$$

The join defines a [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) $-\star-\colon \mathbf{Cat}\times\mathbf{Cat}\rightarrow \mathbf{Cat}$, which together with the [empty category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empty_category_%28category_theory%29) as unit element makes the category of small categories $\mathbf{Cat}$ into a [monoidal category](wikipedia://en/330604).

For a small category $\mathcal{C}$, one further defines its *left cone* and *right cone* as:

$$\mathcal{C}^\triangleleft :=[0]\star\mathcal{C},$$

$$\mathcal{C}^\triangleright :=\mathcal{C}\star[0].$$

:::

## Right adjoints {#Right_adjoints}

Let $\mathcal{D}$ be a small category. The functor $\mathcal{D}\star-\colon \mathbf{Cat}\rightarrow \mathcal{D}\backslash\mathbf{Cat}, \mathcal{D}\mapsto(\mathcal{C}\mapsto\mathcal{D}\star\mathcal{C})$ has a [right adjoint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Right_adjoint) $\mathcal{D}\backslash\mathbf{sSet}\rightarrow\mathbf{sSet}, (F\colon\mathcal{D}\rightarrow\mathcal{E})\mapsto F\backslash\mathcal{E}$ (alternatively denoted $\mathcal{D}\backslash\mathcal{E}$) and the functor $-\star\mathcal{D}\colon \mathbf{Cat}\rightarrow \mathcal{D}\backslash\mathbf{Cat}, \mathcal{D}\mapsto(\mathcal{C}\mapsto\mathcal{C}\star\mathcal{D})$ also has a right adjoint $\mathcal{D}\backslash\mathbf{sSet}\rightarrow\mathbf{sSet}, (F\colon\mathcal{D}\rightarrow\mathcal{E})\mapsto\mathcal{E}/F$ (alternatively denoted $\mathcal{E}/\mathcal{D}$).[^o6] A special case is $\mathcal{D}=[0]$ the [terminal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Terminal_object) small category, since $\mathbf{Cat}_* =[0]\backslash\mathbf{Cat}$ is the category of pointed small categories.

::: {#o8 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- The join is associative. For small categories $\mathcal{C}$, $\mathcal{D}$ and $\mathcal{E}$, one has:[^o9]

$$(\mathcal{C}\star\mathcal{D})\star\mathcal{E} \cong\mathcal{C}\star(\mathcal{D}\star\mathcal{E}).$$

- The join reverses under the dual category. For small categories $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{D}$, one has:[^o4][^o10]

$$(\mathcal{C}\star\mathcal{D})^\mathrm{op} \cong\mathcal{C}^\mathrm{op}\star\mathcal{D}^\mathrm{op}.$$

- Under the [nerve](wikipedia://en/5127933), the join of categories becomes the [join of simplicial sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Join_%28simplicial_sets%29). For small categories $\mathcal{C}$ and $\mathcal{D}$, one has:[^o11][^o12]

$$N(\mathcal{C}\star\mathcal{D}) \cong N\mathcal{C}*N\mathcal{D}.$$

:::
