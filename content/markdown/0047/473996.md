# Gaston Tarry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gaston Tarry"}

*Image: GTarry.jpeg.* Gaston Tarry

**Gaston Tarry** (27 September 1843 – 21 June 1913) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Born in [Villefranche de Rouergue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Villefranche_de_Rouergue), [Aveyron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aveyron), he studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at high school before joining the [civil service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Civil_service) in [Algeria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algeria). He pursued mathematics as an [amateur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amateur).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

In 1895 Tarry published a systematic algorithm for traversing a maze without prior knowledge of its layout; this method is now recognized in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) as one of the earliest variants of a [depth-first search](wikipedia://en/97034) (DFS) algorithm.[^o3][^o4]

Tarry also made contributions to [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), including work on the [Prouhet–Tarry–Escott problem](wikipedia://en/16400356).[^o5]

In 1900 Tarry confirmed [Leonhard Euler](wikipedia://en/17902)'s conjecture that no 6×6 [Graeco-Latin square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graeco-Latin_square#Thirty-six_officers_problem) was possible (the [36 officers problem](wikipedia://en/473979#Thirty-six_officers_problem)).[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9]
