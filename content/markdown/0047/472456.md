# Pierre Boutroux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pierre Boutroux"}

**Pierre Léon Boutroux** (; 6 December 1880 – 15 August 1922) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and historian of science. Boutroux is chiefly known for his work in the history and [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) on 6 December 1880 into a well connected family of the French intelligentsia. His father was the philosopher [Émile Boutroux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Émile_Boutroux). His mother was Aline Catherine Eugénie Poincaré, sister of the scientist and mathematician [Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/48740). A cousin of Aline, [Raymond Poincaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_Poincaré) was to be [President of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/President_of_France).

He occupied the mathematics chair at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) from 1913 until 1914.[^o4] He occupied the History of sciences chair from 1920 to 1922.

Boutroux published his major work *Les principes de l'analyse mathématique* in two volumes; *Volume 1* in 1914 and *Volume 2* in 1919. This is a comprehensive view of the whole field of mathematics at the time.

He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1904 at Heidelberg, in 1908 at Rome, and in 1920 at Strasbourg.[^o5]

He died on 15 August 1922, aged 41 years.
