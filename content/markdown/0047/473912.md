# 800 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="800"}

**800** (**eight hundred**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [799](wikipedia://en/465001#790s) and preceding [801](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/801_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

It is the sum of four consecutive primes (193 + 197 + 199 + 211). It is a [Harshad number](wikipedia://en/641307), an [Achilles number](wikipedia://en/7894744) and the area of a [square](wikipedia://en/659939) with diagonal 40.

## Integers from 801 to 899 {#Integers_from_801_to_899}

#### 801 {#801}

801 is a [Harshad number](wikipedia://en/641307). 801 is the sum of a square and positive cube in more than one way, and a sum of distinct positive cubes in more than one way:

$$801 = 17^2 + 8^3 = 26^2 + 5^3 = 1^3 + 2^3 + 4^3 + 6^3 + 8^3 = 2^3 + 4^3 + 9^3.$$

In the [gematria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gematria) of 2nd century bishop [Irenaeus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irenaeus), 801 stands for both the [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language) word for a dove, and for [Alpha and Omega](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alpha_and_Omega), and therefore represents God in two ways.[^o11]

There are 801 club patterns in a 50x50 grid of coins.[^o12]
