# 900 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="900"}

**900** (**nine hundred**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [899](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/899_%28number%29) and preceding [901](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/901_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

It is the [square](wikipedia://en/213919) of [30](wikipedia://en/341668) and the sum of [Euler's totient function](wikipedia://en/53452) for the first 54 [positive integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_integer). In base 10, it is a [Harshad number](wikipedia://en/641307). It is also the first number to be the square of a [sphenic number](wikipedia://en/245203).

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

**900** is also:

- In Greek [number symbols](wikipedia://en/166697), the sign [Sampi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sampi) ("ϡ", literally "like a [pi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pi_%28letter%29)")
- A [skateboarding trick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/900_%28skateboarding_trick%29) in which the skateboarder spins two and a half times (360 degrees times 2.5 is 900)
- A [900 series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/900_series_%28bowling%29) refers to three consecutive [perfect games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_game_%28bowling%29) in [bowling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bowling)[^o5]
- [Yoda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoda)'s age in [Star Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Star_Wars)[^o6]
