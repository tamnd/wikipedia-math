# Cryptographie indéchiffrable

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cryptographie indéchiffrable"}

***Cryptographie indéchiffrable*** (English: ***Indecipherable Cryptography)*** (subtitle: *basée sur de nouvelles combinaisons rationelles* (*based on new rational combinations*)) is a French book on [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) written by [Émile Victor Théodore Myszkowski](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Émile_Victor_Théodore_Myszkowski), a retired [French Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Army) colonel, and published in 1902.[^o3][^o4]

:::

His book described a [cipher](wikipedia://en/5244) that the author had invented and claimed (incorrectly) was "[undecipherable](wikipedia://en/5715)" (i.e. secure against unauthorised attempts to read it). It was based on a form of [repeated-key transposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Repeated-key_transposition).
