# Wilkie investment model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilkie investment model"}

The **Wilkie investment model**, often just called **Wilkie model**, is a [stochastic asset model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_asset_model) developed by A. D. Wilkie that describes the behavior of various economics factors as [stochastic](wikipedia://en/47895) [time series](wikipedia://en/406624). These time series are generated by [autoregressive models](wikipedia://en/1434444). The main factor of the model which influences all asset prices is the [consumer price index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Consumer_price_index). The model is mainly in use for [actuarial](wikipedia://en/43405) work and [asset liability management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asset_liability_management). Because of the stochastic properties of that model it is mainly combined with [Monte Carlo methods](wikipedia://en/56098).

:::

Wilkie first proposed the model in 1986, in a paper published in the *Transactions of the Faculty of Actuaries*.[^o2] It has since been the subject of extensive study and debate.[^o3][^o4] Wilkie himself updated and expanded the model in a second paper published in 1995.[^o5] He advises to use that model to determine the "funnel of doubt", which can be seen as an interval of minimum and maximum development of a corresponding economic factor.

## Components {#Components}

- price inflation
- wage inflation
- share yield
- share dividend
- consols yield (long-term interest rate)
- bank rate (short-term interest rate)
