# Ivan Cherednik

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ivan Cherednik"}

**Ivan Cherednik** (Иван Владимирович Чередник) is a Russian-American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He introduced [double affine Hecke algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cherednik_algebra), and used them to prove [Macdonald's constant term conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macdonald's_constant_term_conjecture) in (Cherednik 1995). He has also dealt with [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [Soliton equations](wikipedia://en/51654). His research interests include [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), and [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582). He is currently the Austin M. Carr Distinguished Professor of mathematics at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill).

:::

In 1998 he was an Invited Speaker of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Berlin.[^o4]
