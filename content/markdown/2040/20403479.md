# Russo–Dye theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Russo–Dye theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Russo–Dye theorem** is a result in the field of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180). It states that in a [unital](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unital_algebra) [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184), the closure of the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of the [unitary elements](wikipedia://en/20402624) is the closed [unit ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_ball).[^o2] The theorem was published by B. Russo and H. A. Dye in 1966.[^o3]

:::

## Other formulations and generalizations {#Other_formulations_and_generalizations}

Results similar to the Russo–Dye theorem hold in more general contexts. For example, in a unital \*-Banach algebra, the closed [unit ball](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unit_ball) is contained in the closed [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of the [unitary elements](wikipedia://en/20402624).[^o2]

A more precise result is true for the [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) of all [bounded linear operators](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_linear_operator) on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932): If *T* is such an operator and ||*T*|| \< 1 − 2/*n* for some integer *n* > 2, then *T* is the mean of *n* [unitary operators](wikipedia://en/225999).[^o5]

::: {#o7 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

This example is due to Russo & Dye,[^o3] Corollary 1: If *U*(*A*) denotes the [unitary elements](wikipedia://en/20402624) of a [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) *A*, then the [norm](wikipedia://en/246722) of a [linear mapping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_mapping) *f* from *A* to a [normed linear space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_linear_space) *B* is

$$\sup_{U \in U(A)} ||f(U)||.$$

In other words, the norm of an operator can be calculated using only the unitary elements of the algebra.

:::
