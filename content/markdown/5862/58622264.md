# Williams number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Williams number"}

In [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **Williams number base *b*** is a [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) of the form $(b-1) \cdot b^n-1$ for integers *b* ≥ 2 and *n* ≥ 1.[^o2] The Williams numbers base 2 are exactly the [Mersenne numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mersenne_number).

:::

A **Williams prime** is a Williams number that is [prime](wikipedia://en/23666). They were considered by [Hugh C. Williams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hugh_C._Williams).[^o3]

It is conjectured that for every *b* ≥ 2, there are infinitely many Williams primes for base *b*.
