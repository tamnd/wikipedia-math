# David Wolfe (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Wolfe"}

**David Wolfe** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and amateur [Go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go_%28game%29) player.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wolfe graduated from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in 1985, with a bachelor's degree in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering).[^o4] He obtained a Ph.D. in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1994, with a dissertation *Mathematics of Go: Chilling Corridors* combining both subjects and supervised by [Elwyn Berlekamp](wikipedia://en/655893).

After working as a lecturer at the University of California, Berkeley from 1991 to 1996, as an associate professor at [Gustavus Adolphus College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustavus_Adolphus_College) from 1996 to 2008, and then as an adjunct faculty member at [Dalhousie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalhousie_University), he moved from academia to the software industry.[^o4] Wolfe was a fan of [Martin Gardner](wikipedia://en/20435) and in 2009 he teamed up with [Tom M. Rodgers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_Malin_Rodgers) to edit a Gardner tribute book.[^o6]

## Books {#Books}

Wolfe is the author of books on [combinatorial game theory](wikipedia://en/292231), including:

- *Mathematical Go: Chilling Gets the Last Point* (with [Elwyn Berlekamp](wikipedia://en/655893), [A K Peters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_K_Peters), 1994; also published as *Mathematical Go Endgames: Nightmares for the Professional Go Player*, Ishi Press, 1994)[^o8]
- *Lessons in Play: An Introduction to Combinatorial Game Theory* (with [Michael H. Albert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_H._Albert) and Richard Nowakowski, A K Peters, 2007; 2nd ed., [CRC Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRC_Press), 2019)[^o9]
