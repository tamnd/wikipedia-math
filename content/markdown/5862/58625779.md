# Ernest Preston Lane

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ernest Preston Lane"}

**Ernest Preston Lane** (November 28, 1886, [Russellville, Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russellville,_Tennessee) – October 1969) was an American mathematician, specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

In 1909, he received his bachelor's degree in from the [University of Tennessee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tennessee). Later in life, he went on to receive his master's degree from the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) in 1913.[^o4] He taught mathematics at several academic institutions before receiving in 1918 from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) his PhD under [Ernest Julius Wilczynski](wikipedia://en/30874595) with thesis *Conjugate systems with indeterminate axis curves*. Lane served as Instructor of Mathematics at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) from 1918 to 1919. At the [University of Wisconsin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin) Lane was from 1919 to 1923 an assistant professor. At the University of Chicago he was from 1923 to 1927 an assistant professor, from 1927 to 1928 an associate professor, and from 1928 to 1952 a full professor, retiring in 1952 as professor emeritus. He was the chair of the University of Chicago's mathematics department from 1941 to 1946.

Lane was a [Guggenheim Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) for the academic year 1926–1927.[^o4] His doctoral students included [Alice T. Schafer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alice_T._Schafer), [Sun Guangyuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Guangyuan) and [Abba Verbeck Newton](wikipedia://en/76096963).[^o6]
