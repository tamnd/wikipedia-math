# Adjacent-vertex-distinguishing-total coloring

*Image: Avd-total-coloring-of-complete-graph-K4.svg.* A proper AVD-total-coloring of the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) K<sub>4</sub> with 5 colors, the minimum number possible.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adjacent-vertex-distinguishing-total coloring"}

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a [total coloring](wikipedia://en/690702) is a coloring on the vertices and edges of a graph such that:

:::

(1). no adjacent vertices have the same color;

(2). no adjacent edges have the same color; and

(3). no edge and its endvertices are assigned the same color.

In 2005, Zhang et al.[^o2] added a restriction to the definition of total coloring and proposed a new type of coloring defined as follows.

Let *G* = (*V*,*E*) be a simple graph endowed with a total coloring φ, and let *u* be a vertex of *G*. The set of colors that **occurs** in the vertex *u* is defined as *C*(*u*) = {*φ*(*u*)} ∪ {*φ*(*uv*) | *uv* ∈ *E*(*G*)}. Two vertices *u*,*v* ∈ *V*(*G*) are **distinguishable** if their color-sets are distinct, i.e., *C*(*u*) ≠ *C*(*v*).

In [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), a total coloring is an **adjacent-vertex-distinguishing-total-coloring** (AVD-total-coloring) if it has the following additional property:

(4). for every two adjacent vertices *u*,*v* of a graph *G*, their colors-sets are distinct from each other, i.e., *C*(*u*) ≠ *C*(*v*).

The **adjacent-vertex-distinguishing-total-chromatic number** *χ*<sub>at</sub>(*G*) of a graph *G* is the fewest colors needed in an AVD-total-coloring of *G*.

The following lower bound for the AVD-total chromatic number can be obtained from the definition of AVD-total-coloring: If a simple graph *G* has two adjacent vertices of maximum degree, then *χ*<sub>*at*</sub>(*G*) ≥ Δ(*G*) + 2.[^o3] Otherwise, if a simple graph *G* does not have two adjacent vertices of maximum degree, then *χ*<sub>*at*</sub>(*G*) ≥ Δ(*G*) + 1.

In 2005, Zhang et al. determined the AVD-total-chromatic number for some classes of graphs, and based in their results they conjectured the following.

**AVD-Total-Coloring Conjecture.** (Zhang et al.[^o4])

*χ*<sub>*at*</sub>(*G*) ≤ Δ(*G*) + 3.

The AVD-Total-Coloring Conjecture is known to hold for some classes of graphs, such as [complete graphs](wikipedia://en/85816),[^o5] graphs with Δ=3,[^o6][^o7] and all [bipartite graphs](wikipedia://en/244431).[^o8]

In 2012, Huang et al.[^o9] showed that *χ*<sub>*at*</sub>(*G*) ≤ 2Δ(*G*) for any simple graph *G* with maximum degree Δ(*G*) > 2. In 2014, Papaioannou and Raftopoulou[^o10] described an algorithmic procedure that gives a 7-AVD-total-colouring for any 4-regular graph.
