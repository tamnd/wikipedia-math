# Spinor bundle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Spinor bundle"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), given a [spin structure](wikipedia://en/2844303) on an $n$-dimensional orientable [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) $(M, g),\,$ one defines the **spinor bundle** to be the [complex vector bundle](wikipedia://en/1538109) $\pi_{\mathbf S}\colon{\mathbf S}\to M\,$ associated to the corresponding [principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) $\pi_{\mathbf P}\colon{\mathbf P}\to M\,$ of spin frames over $M$ and the [spin representation](wikipedia://en/10160091) of its [structure group](wikipedia://en/411231) ${\mathrm {Spin}}(n)\,$ on the space of [spinors](wikipedia://en/29276) $\Delta_n$.

:::

A section of the **spinor bundle** ${\mathbf S}\,$ is called a **spinor field**.

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

Let $({\mathbf P},F_{\mathbf P})$ be a [spin structure](wikipedia://en/2844303) on a [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) $(M, g),\,$that is, an [equivariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equivariant) lift of the oriented [orthonormal frame bundle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthonormal_frame_bundle) $\mathrm F_{SO}(M)\to M$ with respect to the double covering $\rho\colon {\mathrm {Spin}}(n)\to {\mathrm {SO}}(n)$ of the [special orthogonal group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Special_orthogonal_group) by the [spin group](wikipedia://en/411231).

The **spinor bundle** ${\mathbf S}\,$ is defined [^o4] to be the [complex vector bundle](wikipedia://en/1538109)

$${\mathbf S}={\mathbf P}\times_{\kappa}\Delta_n\,$$

associated to the [spin structure](wikipedia://en/2844303) ${\mathbf P}$ via the [spin representation](wikipedia://en/10160091) $\kappa\colon {\mathrm {Spin}}(n)\to {\mathrm U}(\Delta_n),\,$ where ${\mathrm U}({\mathbf W})\,$ denotes the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) of [unitary operators](wikipedia://en/225999) acting on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) ${\mathbf W}.\,$ The spin representation $\kappa$ is a faithful and [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) of the group ${\mathrm {Spin}}(n).$[^o5]

:::
