# Center (algebra)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Center"}

The term **center** or **centre** is used in various contexts in [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) to denote the set of all those elements that [commute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutative_operation) with all other elements.

:::

- The [center of a group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_of_a_group) *G* consists of all those elements *x* in *G* such that *xg* = *gx* for all *g* in *G*. This is a [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of *G*.
- The similarly named notion for a [semigroup](wikipedia://en/27799) is defined likewise and it is a subsemigroup.[^o2][^o3]
- The [center](wikipedia://en/53796787) of a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) (or an [associative algebra](wikipedia://en/2112)) *R* is the subset of *R* consisting of all those elements *x* of *R* such that *xr* = *rx* for all *r* in *R*.[^o4] The center is a [commutative](wikipedia://en/61346) [subring](wikipedia://en/24873145) of *R*.
- The [center of a Lie algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_of_a_Lie_algebra) *L* consists of all those elements *x* in *L* such that \[*x*,*a*\] = 0 for all *a* in *L*. This is an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) of the Lie algebra *L*.
