# Chandrashekhar

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chandrashekhar"}

**Chandrasekhar**, **Chandrashekhar** or **Chandra Shekhar** is an Indian name and may refer to a number of individuals. The name comes from the name of an incarnation of the Hindu god [Shiva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shiva). In this form he married the goddess [Parvati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parvati). Etymologically, the name comes from the Sanskrit words "चन्द्र (*chandra*)", meaning "moon", and "शेखर (*śekhara*)", meaning "crest" or "crown", which is an epithet of the Shiva.

:::
