# Ludwig Immanuel Magnus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ludwig Immanuel Magnus"}

**Ludwig Immanuel Magnus** (March 15, 1790 – September 25, 1861) was a German Jewish [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who, in 1831, published a paper about the [inversion transformation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inversion_transformation), which leads to [inversive geometry](wikipedia://en/295844).

:::

His reputation as a mathematician was established by 1834 and an honorary [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) was conferred on him by the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn). His work appeared in [Gergonne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Diaz_Gergonne)'s *[Annales de mathématiques pures et appliquées](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annales_de_mathématiques_pures_et_appliquées)* vols. xi and xvi (1820–25); in *[Crelle's Journal](wikipedia://en/973547)*, vols. v, vii, viii, and ix (1830–32); in the third part (1833) of [Meier Hirsch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Meier_Hirsch)'s "Sammlung Geometrischer Aufgaben"; and in "Sammlung von Aufgaben und Lehrsätzen aus der Analytischen Geometrie des Raumes" (published in 1837, written earlier).

He studied [Euclid](wikipedia://en/9331) while working in his uncle's bank. From 1813 to 1815 he served as a [gunner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artillery) in the [Napoleonic Wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleonic_Wars). After the war he returned to banking and taught mathematics until 1834, when the founder of the [academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy) at which he was teaching died. He then left teaching and spent nine years as the head [revenue](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revenue) officer for the [Berliner Kassenverein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berliner_Kassenverein), retiring in 1843.
