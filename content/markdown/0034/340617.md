# List of complex analysis topics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of complex analysis topics"}

**[Complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759)**, traditionally known as the **theory of functions of a complex variable**, is the branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) that investigates [functions](wikipedia://en/185427) of [complex numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_numbers). It is useful in many branches of mathematics, including [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969); as well as in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), including [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics), [thermodynamics](wikipedia://en/29952), and [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering).

:::

See also: [glossary of real and complex analysis](wikipedia://en/77414703).
