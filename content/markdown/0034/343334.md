# 70 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="70"}

**70** (**seventy**) is the natural number following [69](wikipedia://en/378157) and preceding [71](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/71_%28number%29).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

70 is a [composite](wikipedia://en/82289) number, an [Erdős–Woods number](wikipedia://en/2616413),[^o4] a [Pell number](wikipedia://en/1355482), a [central binomial coefficient](wikipedia://en/2128244), and a [primitive abundant number](wikipedia://en/27077452). 70 is the smallest [weird number](wikipedia://en/321930), which is a natural number that is [abundant](wikipedia://en/321831) but not [semiperfect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiperfect).[^o6]

70 is also part of the only nontrivial solution pair to the [cannonball problem](wikipedia://en/28045698), along with [24](wikipedia://en/362201).

## In religion {#In_religion}

- In [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism) tradition, [Ptolemy II Philadelphus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ptolemy_II_Philadelphus) ordered 72 Jewish elders to translate the [Torah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Torah) into [Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language); the result was the [Septuagint](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Septuagint) (from the [Latin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latin_language) for "seventy"). The Roman numeral seventy, LXX, is the scholarly symbol for the Septuagint.
- In Islamic history and in Islamic interpretation the number 70 or 72 is most often and generally hyperbole for an infinite amount:
  - There are 70 dead among [Muhammad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muhammad)'s adversaries during the [Battle of Badr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Badr).
  - 70 of Muhammad's followers are martyred at the [Battle of Uhud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Uhud).
  - In [Shia Islam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shia_Islam), there are 70 martyrs among [Imam Hussein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imam_Hussein)'s followers during the [Battle of Karbala](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Karbala).

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

- In some traditions, 70 years of marriage is marked by a [platinum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Platinum) [wedding anniversary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_anniversary).
- Under [Social Security (United States)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_Security_%28United_States%29), the age at which a person can receive the maximum retirement benefits (and may do so and continue working without reduction of benefits).

## Number name {#Number_name}

Several languages, especially ones with [vigesimal](wikipedia://en/209196) number systems, do not have a specific word for 70: for example, soixante-dix; halvfjerds, short for halvfjerdsindstyve. (For French, this is true only in France, Canada and Luxembourg; other French-speaking regions such as [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium), [Switzerland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Switzerland), [Aosta Valley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aosta_Valley) and [Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jersey) use septante.)[^o10]
