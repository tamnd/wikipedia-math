# 30 (number)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="30"}

**30** (**thirty**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [29](wikipedia://en/362212) and preceding [31](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/31_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

*Image: Square pyramidal number.svg.* 30 is a square pyramidal number.

30 is a [composite](wikipedia://en/82289), a [regular number](wikipedia://en/5941535), a [pronic number](wikipedia://en/652733),[^o4] and a [primorial](wikipedia://en/422786).[^o5]

The [SI prefix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SI_prefix) for 10<sup>30</sup> is [Quetta-](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quetta-) (Q), and for 10<sup>−30</sup> (i.e., the reciprocal of 10<sup>30</sup>) [quecto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quecto-) (q). These numbers are the largest and smallest number to receive an SI prefix to date.

## In other fields {#In_other_fields}

**Thirty** is used (as [–30–](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/–30–)) to indicate the end of a newspaper (or broadcast) story, a copy editor's typographical notation.[^o7]

There are 30 days in the months April, June, September and November (and in unusual circumstances February—see [February 30](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/February_30)). Although the number of days in a month vary, 30 is used to estimate months elapsing.

30 years of marriage is marked by the pearl [wedding anniversary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wedding_anniversary).[^o8]

## History and literature {#History_and_literature}

- Age 30 is when Jewish priests traditionally start their service (according to Numbers 4:3).
- One of the rallying cries of the 1960s student/youth protest movement was the slogan, "[Don't trust anyone over thirty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jack_Weinberg)".
- In *[The Myth of Sisyphus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Myth_of_Sisyphus)* the French existentialist [Albert Camus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert_Camus) comments that the age of thirty is a crucial period in the life of a man, for at that age he gains a new awareness of the meaning of time.
