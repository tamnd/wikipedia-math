# 80 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="80"}

**80** (**eighty**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [79](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/79_%28number%29) and preceding [81](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/81_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

80 is:

- The sum of [Euler's totient function](wikipedia://en/53452) *φ*(*x*) over the first sixteen integers:

1 + 1 + 2 + 2 + 4 + 2 + 6 + 4 + 6 + 4 + 10 + 4 + 12 + 6 + 8 + 8 = 80.

- A [semiperfect number](wikipedia://en/321918), since adding up some subsets of its divisors (e.g., [1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1_%28number%29), [4](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4_%28number%29), [5](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5_%28number%29), [10](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/10_%28number%29), [20](wikipedia://en/208259), and [40](wikipedia://en/341671)) gives 80.[^o4]
- A [ménage number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ménage_number).[^o5]
- [Palindromic](wikipedia://en/105956) in bases 3 (2222<sub>3</sub>), 6 (212<sub>6</sub>), 9 (88<sub>9</sub>), 15 (55<sub>15</sub>), 19 (44<sub>19</sub>), and 39 (22<sub>39</sub>).
- A [repdigit](wikipedia://en/216551) in bases 3, 9, 15, 19, and 39.
- The sum of the first four [twin prime](wikipedia://en/41997) pairs:

(3 + 5) + (5 + 7) + (11 + 13) + (17 + 19) = 80.

The [Pareto principle](wikipedia://en/24562) (also known as the 80-20 rule) states that, for many events, roughly 80% of the effects come from 20% of the causes.[^o6][^o7]

Every solvable configuration of the [15 puzzle](wikipedia://en/432213) can be solved in no more than 80 single-tile moves.[^o8]

## In trade {#In_trade}

In Northern Europe, there have traditionally been words for eighty units - often eggs or herrings: *Wall (unit)* in German, *ol* in Danish, and *øll* in Norwegian (all cognates).
