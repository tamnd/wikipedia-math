## Terminology {#Terminology}

::: {#o4 .entry cue=list-entry name="Degree"}

- [Degree](wikipedia://en/5930652): The maximum exponents among the monomials.

:::

::: {#o5 .entry cue=list-entry name="Factor"}

- [Factor](wikipedia://en/3776351): An expression being multiplied.

:::

::: {#o6 .entry cue=list-entry name="Linear factor"}

- [Linear factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_factor): A factor of degree one.

:::

::: {#o7 .entry cue=list-entry name="Coefficient"}

- [Coefficient](wikipedia://en/51203): An expression multiplying one of the monomials of the polynomial.

:::

::: {#o8 .entry cue=list-entry name="Root (or zero) of a polynomial"}

- [Root (or zero) of a polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Root_of_a_polynomial): Given a polynomial *p*(*x*), the *x* values that satisfy *p*(*x*) = 0 are called roots (or zeroes) of the polynomial *p*.
