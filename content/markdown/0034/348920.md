# Eli Biham

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eli Biham"}

**Eli Biham** (אלי ביהם) is an Israeli [cryptographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cryptographer) and [cryptanalyst](wikipedia://en/5715) who is a professor at the [Technion - Israel Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion_-_Israel_Institute_of_Technology) Computer Science department. From 2008 to 2013, Biham was the [dean](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dean_%28education%29) of the Technion Computer Science department, after serving for two years as chief of CS graduate school. Biham invented (publicly) [differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/8536),[^o3] for which he received his Ph.D., while working under [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314).

:::

## Contributions to cryptanalysis {#Contributions_to_cryptanalysis}

section

- [Differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/8536) - publicly invented during his Ph.D. studies under [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314)
- Attacking all triple modes of operation.
- [Impossible differential cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/3329225) - joint work with [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314) and [Alex Biryukov](wikipedia://en/9189491)
- Breaking (together with [Lars Knudsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Ramkilde_Knudsen)) the ANSI X9.52 CBCM mode (few days before the final standardization)
- Breaking the [GSM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GSM) security mechanisms (with Elad Barkan and Nathan Keller)
- Co-invention of [related-key attacks](wikipedia://en/1516144).
- [Differential Fault Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_Fault_Analysis) - joint work with [Adi Shamir](wikipedia://en/57314)
- Conditional Linear Cryptanalysis - joint work with Stav Perle[^o5]
- Efficient [slide attacks](wikipedia://en/1821327) with reduced time complexity[^o6]

### New cryptographic primitives {#New_cryptographic_primitives}

Biham has taken part in the design of several new cryptographic primitives:

- [Serpent](wikipedia://en/385154) (with [Ross Anderson](wikipedia://en/61571) and [Lars Knudsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lars_Ramkilde_Knudsen)), a [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) which was one of the final five contenders to become the [Advanced Encryption Standard](wikipedia://en/99431)
- [Tiger](wikipedia://en/511596) (with [Ross Anderson](wikipedia://en/61571)), a [hash function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hash_function) fast on 64-bit machines, and
- [Py](wikipedia://en/3955084) (with [Jennifer Seberry](wikipedia://en/4546010)), one of a family of fast [stream ciphers](wikipedia://en/49007) (see article for more detail on their cryptanalytic status).
- SHAvite-3 (with [Orr Dunkelman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orr_Dunkelman)), a [hash function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hash_function) which was one of the 14 semifinalists in the [NIST hash function competition](wikipedia://en/20051952).
