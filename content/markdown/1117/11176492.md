# John Brillhart

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John Brillhart"}

**John David Brillhart** (November 13, 1930 – May 21, 2022) was a mathematician who worked in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) at the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Brillhart was born on November 13, 1930, in [Berkeley, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley,_California).[^o4][^o5]

He studied at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), where he received his A.B. in 1953, his M.A. in 1966, and his Ph.D. in 1967.[^o4] His doctoral thesis in mathematics was supervised by [D. H. Lehmer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._H._Lehmer), with assistance from [Leonard Carlitz](wikipedia://en/10356778).[^o4]

Before becoming a mathematician, he served in the [United States Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army).[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Brillhart joined the faculty at the [University of Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Arizona) in 1967 and retired in 2001.[^o4] He advised two Ph.D. students.[^o4]

## Research {#Research}

Brillart worked in [integer factorization](wikipedia://en/15491). His joint work with Michael A. Morrison in 1975 describes how to implement the [continued fraction factorization](wikipedia://en/1335392) method originally developed by Lehmer and [Ralph Ernest Powers](wikipedia://en/1560725) in 1931.[^o4][^o9] One consequence was the first factorization of the [Fermat number](wikipedia://en/91127) $F^7 = 2^{2^7}+1$.[^o4][^o9] Their ideas were influential in the development of the [quadratic sieve](wikipedia://en/582340) by [Carl Pomerance](wikipedia://en/2134159).[^o4]

Brillhart was a member of the [Cunningham Project](wikipedia://en/1572446), which factors [Mersenne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mersenne_number), Fermat, and related numbers.[^o4] He was also a founding member and financial contributor to the [Number Theory Foundation](wikipedia://en/13053993) started by [John L. Selfridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_L._Selfridge).[^o4]
