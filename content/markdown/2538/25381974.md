# Vague torus

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vague torus"}

In [classical mechanics](wikipedia://en/19555586), a **vague torus** is a region in [phase space](wikipedia://en/191101) that is characterized by approximate [constants of motion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constants_of_motion), as opposed to an actual torus defined by exact constants of motion. The concept of vague tori is used to describe regular ([quasiperiodic](wikipedia://en/1590390)) segments of otherwise [chaotic](wikipedia://en/6295) trajectories.[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::
