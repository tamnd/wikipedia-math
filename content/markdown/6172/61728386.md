# Krull's separation lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Krull's separation lemma"}

In [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), **Krull's separation lemma** is a [lemma](wikipedia://en/18634) in [ring theory](wikipedia://en/250424). It was proved by [Wolfgang Krull](wikipedia://en/964328) in 1928.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Statement of the lemma"}

## Statement of the lemma {#Statement_of_the_lemma}

Let $I$ be an [ideal](wikipedia://en/25977) and let $M$ be a multiplicative system (*i.e.* $M$ is closed under multiplication) in a [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) $R$, and suppose $I \cap M = \varnothing$. Then there exists a [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) $P$ satisfying $I \subseteq P$ and $P \cap M = \varnothing$.[^o5]

:::
