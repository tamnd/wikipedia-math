::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="5-simplex honeycomb"}

In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **5-simplex honeycomb** or **hexateric honeycomb** is a space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654) or pentacomb). Each vertex is shared by 12 [5-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-simplex), 30 [rectified 5-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_5-simplex), and 20 [birectified 5-simplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_5-simplex). These facet types occur in proportions of 2:2:1 respectively in the whole honeycomb.

:::

## A5 lattice {#A5_lattice}

This [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) is called the [A<sub>5</sub> lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A5_lattice) or **5-simplex lattice**. The 30 vertices of the [stericated 5-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stericated_5-simplex) vertex figure represent the 30 roots of the ${\tilde{A}}_5$ Coxeter group.[^o8] It is the 5-dimensional case of a [simplectic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simplectic_honeycomb).
