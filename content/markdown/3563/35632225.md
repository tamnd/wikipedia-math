# List of works by Averroes

*Image: AverroesColor.jpg.* [Ibn Rushd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_Rushd) was the preeminent philosopher in the history of [Al-Andalus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Andalus).

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of works by Averroes"}

According to a chronology over Averroes’ writings by Joseph Kenny OP, Averroes first writings date from his age of 31 (year 1157).[^o2]

:::

The following is a [bibliography](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bibliography) of [Averroes](wikipedia://en/47836)' works.
