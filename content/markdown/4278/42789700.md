# Giovanni Fagnano

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Fagnano"}

**Giovanni Francesco Fagnano dei Toschi** (born 31 January 1715 in [Senigallia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senigallia), died 14 May 1797 in Senigallia) was an Italian churchman and mathematician, the son of [Giulio Carlo de' Toschi di Fagnano](wikipedia://en/2415799), also a mathematician.[^o13]

:::

## Religious career {#Religious_career}

Fagnano was ordained as a priest. In 1752 he became [canon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canon_%28priest%29),[^o13] and in 1755[^o13] he was appointed [archdeacon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archdeacon) of the cathedral of Senigallia.[^o12]

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Fagnano is known for [Fagnano's problem](wikipedia://en/10783043), the problem of inscribing a minimum-[perimeter](wikipedia://en/23636) [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) within an [acute triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acute_triangle). As Fagnano showed, the solution is the [orthic triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthic_triangle), whose vertices are the points where the altitudes of the original triangle cross its sides.[^o5] Another property of the orthic triangle, also proven by Fagnano, is that its [angle bisectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angle_bisector) are the altitudes of the original triangle.[^o13]

Fagnano also partially solved the problem of finding the [geometric median](wikipedia://en/10324687) of sets of four points in the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410); this is the point minimizing the sum of its distances to the four given points. As Fagnano showed, when the four points form the vertices of a [convex](wikipedia://en/6292) [quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/25278), the geometric median is the point where the two diagonals of the quadrilateral cross each other. In the other possible case, not considered by Fagnano, one point lies within the triangle formed by the other three, and this inner point is the geometric median. Thus, in both cases, the geometric median coincides with the [Radon point](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon_point) of the four given points.[^o6][^o7][^o8][^o9]
