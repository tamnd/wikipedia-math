# Alan Reid (mathematician)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alan Reid"}

**Alan William Reid** (born June 14, 1962) is a Scottish-American mathematician working primarily with [arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/15239331). He is the [Edgar Odell Lovett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edgar_Odell_Lovett) Chair of mathematics at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University), 2017—present.[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Reid grew up in [Buckie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buckie), [Scotland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scotland).[^o5] He obtained his doctorate from the [University of Aberdeen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Aberdeen),[^o6] supervised by [Colin Maclachlan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colin_Maclachlan),[^o7] on the topic of *Arithmetic Kleinian Groups and their Fuchsian Subgroups*. He was a [Royal Society University Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_University_Research_Fellow) at Cambridge 1992–96. He was awarded the [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1997,[^o8] and became one of the (inaugural) in 2013.[^o9]

## Research {#Research}

Alan Reid's research primarily focusses on [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774), [hyperbolic manifolds](wikipedia://en/1589135) and [profinite groups](wikipedia://en/24266). He proved that the [figure-eight knot](wikipedia://en/59565) is the only knot whose complement is an [arithmetic hyperbolic 3-manifold](wikipedia://en/15239331).[^o11] With [Martin Bridson](wikipedia://en/36378173), Ben McReynolds and Ryan Spitler, he found the first examples of non-elementary [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) which are determined by their finite quotients among [finitely generated](wikipedia://en/963042) [residually finite groups](wikipedia://en/5780875).[^o12]

He has published more than 100 papers,[^o13] and supervised 21 PhD students to completion as of 2023.[^o14][^o15]
