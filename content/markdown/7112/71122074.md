# John D. P. Meldrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John D. P. Meldrum"}

**John David Philip Meldrum** (18 July 1940 in [Rabat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabat), Morocco; died 9 August 2018 in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh), Scotland) was a British mathematician. Meldrum was an [algebraist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_Algebra) and his research was mostly related to [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Meldrum was born in Rabat, Morocco.

In 1964 he was appointed as a supernumerary fellow and college lecturer in mathematics at [Emmanuel College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_College,_Cambridge). Meldrum received his PhD from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1967 on the topic of "*Central Series in Wreath Products*". His supervisor was [Derek Roy Taunt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Derek_Roy_Taunt).

In 1969 he became a lecturer for mathematics at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) and in 1982 he was appointed there as a senior lecturer.

He died on 9 August 2018 in Edinburgh after a battle with the [Parkinson's disease](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Parkinson's_disease).
