::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of five great icosahedra"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of 5 [great icosahedra](wikipedia://en/3029898), in the same arrangement as in the [compound of 5 icosahedra](wikipedia://en/14767374).

:::

Under the action of the symmetry group, the triangles in this compound decompose into two [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29): 40 of the triangles lie in coplanar pairs in icosahedral planes, while the other 60 lie in unique planes.
