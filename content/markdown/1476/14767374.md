The **compound of five icosahedra** is [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809). It's composed of 5 [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084), rotated around a common axis. It has [icosahedral](wikipedia://en/2877844) symmetry *I<sub>h</sub>*.

:::

The triangles in this compound decompose into two [orbits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orbit_%28group_theory%29) under action of the symmetry group: 40 of the triangles lie in coplanar pairs in icosahedral planes, while the other 60 lie in unique planes.

The compound of five icosahedra shares the same vertex arrangement of a nonuniform [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

(0, ±2, ±2τ)

(±τ<sup>−1</sup>, ±1, ±(1+τ<sup>2</sup>))

(±τ, ±τ<sup>2</sup>, ±(2τ−1))

where τ = (1+$\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) (sometimes written φ).
