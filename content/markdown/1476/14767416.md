::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Compound of five great dodecahedra"}

This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a composition of 5 [great dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/2860441), in the same arrangement as in the [compound of 5 icosahedra](wikipedia://en/14767374).

:::

It is one of only five polyhedral compounds (along with the [compound of six tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/14810194), the [compound of two great dodecahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compound_of_two_great_dodecahedra), the [compound of two small stellated dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14751130), and the [compound of five small stellated dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/14767417)) which is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) and [face-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Face-transitive) but not [edge-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge-transitive).
