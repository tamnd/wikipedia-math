# Regular modal logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regular modal logic"}

In [modal logic](wikipedia://en/333365), a **regular modal logic** is a modal logic containing (as axiom or theorem) the [duality](wikipedia://en/609737) of the modal operators:

:::

$$\Diamond A \leftrightarrow \lnot\Box\lnot A$$

and closed under the rule

$$\frac{(A\land B)\to C}{(\Box A\land\Box B)\to\Box C}.$$

Every [normal modal logic](wikipedia://en/911820) is regular, and every regular modal logic is [classical](wikipedia://en/14763077).
