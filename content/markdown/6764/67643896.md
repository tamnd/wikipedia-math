# Thomas Geisser

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas Geisser"}

**Thomas Hermann Geisser** (born February 28, 1966) is a German mathematician working at [Rikkyo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rikkyo_University) in Tokyo, Japan. He works in the field of [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), [motivic cohomology](wikipedia://en/809314) and [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500).

:::

## Education {#Education}

From 1985 Geisser studied at [Bonn University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn_University) under the supervision of Günther Harder and obtained a master's degree in 1990. He continued to obtain a PhD under the supervision of [Christopher Deninger](wikipedia://en/32349981) at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster); the title of his thesis is "A p-adic analog of Beilinson's conjecture for Hecke characters of imaginary quadratic fields".

## Career {#Career}

Geisser spent three years at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) as a visiting scholar and visiting fellow, respectively. After further stays in Essen, [University of Illinois, Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois,_Urbana-Champaign) and [Tokyo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_University), he became [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at the [University of Southern California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California), and was promoted to [associate professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associate_professor) in 2002 and [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in 2006.[^o6]

After visiting [Tokyo University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tokyo_University) again, he became a professor at [Nagoya University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nagoya_University) in 2010, and moved to Rikkyo University in 2015.[^o7]

He received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) (2000) and a [Humboldt Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) (2021).[^o8]

He is editor for *[Documenta Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Documenta_Mathematica)*[^o9] and managing editor for *Commentarii Mathematici Universitatis Sancti Pauli*.[^o10]
