# Network dynamics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Network dynamics"}

**Network dynamics** is a research field for the study of [networks](wikipedia://en/766409) whose status changes in time. The dynamics may refer to the structure of connections of the units of a network,[^o4][^o5] to the collective internal state of the network,[^o6][^o7] or both. The networked systems could be from the fields of [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology), [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [sociology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sociology), [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), etc. Networked systems are typically characterized as [complex systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_systems) consisting of many units coupled by specific, potentially changing, interaction topologies.

:::

For a [dynamical systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dynamical_systems)' approach to discrete network dynamics, see [sequential dynamical system](wikipedia://en/17590530).
