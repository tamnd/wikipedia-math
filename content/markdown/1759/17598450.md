# Wagner graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wagner graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Wagner graph** is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 8 [vertices](wikipedia://en/638899) and 12 edges.[^o3] It is the 8-vertex [Möbius ladder](wikipedia://en/7355278) graph.

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

As a Möbius ladder, the Wagner graph is [nonplanar](wikipedia://en/24314) but has [crossing number](wikipedia://en/18298594) one, making it an [apex graph](wikipedia://en/28228942). It can be embedded without crossings on a [torus](wikipedia://en/74800) or [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350), so it is also a [toroidal graph](wikipedia://en/1633057). It has girth 4, diameter 2, radius 2, [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 3 and is both 3-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 3-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph).

The Wagner graph has 392 [spanning trees](wikipedia://en/455770); it and the [complete bipartite graph](wikipedia://en/525320) $K_{3,3}$ have the most spanning trees among all cubic graphs with the same number of vertices.[^o6]

The Wagner graph is a [vertex-transitive graph](wikipedia://en/581175) but is not [edge-transitive](wikipedia://en/584406). Its full automorphism group is isomorphic to the [dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) $D_{8}$ of order 16, the group of symmetries of an [octagon](wikipedia://en/314575), including both rotations and reflections.

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Wagner graph is

$$(x-3)(x-1)^2(x+1)(x^2+2x-1)^2.$$

It is the only graph with this characteristic polynomial, making it a graph determined by its [spectrum](wikipedia://en/534914).

The Wagner graph is [triangle-free](wikipedia://en/7667996) and has [independence number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Independence_number) three, providing one half of the proof that the [Ramsey number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramsey_number) $R(3,4)$ (the least number $n$ such that any $n$-vertex graph contains either a triangle or a four-vertex independent set) is 9.[^o7]

:::

## Graph minors {#Graph_minors}

Möbius ladders play an important role in the theory of [graph minors](wikipedia://en/353042). The earliest result of this type is a 1937 theorem of [Klaus Wagner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klaus_Wagner) (part of a cluster of results known as [Wagner's theorem](wikipedia://en/3126130)) that graphs with no *K*<sub>5</sub> minor can be formed by using [clique-sum](wikipedia://en/17598460) operations to combine planar graphs and the Möbius ladder *M*<sub>8</sub>.[^o9] For this reason *M*<sub>8</sub> is called the Wagner graph.

The Wagner graph is also one of four minimal [forbidden minors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forbidden_minor) for the graphs of [treewidth](wikipedia://en/3987086) at most three (the other three being the [complete graph](wikipedia://en/85816) *K*<sub>5</sub>, the graph of the [regular octahedron](wikipedia://en/22458), and the graph of the [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659)) and one of four minimal forbidden minors for the graphs of [branchwidth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branchwidth) at most three (the other three being *K*<sub>5</sub>, the graph of the octahedron, and the [cube graph](wikipedia://en/6706815)).[^o10][^o11]

::: {#o13 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The Wagner graph is a [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508) [Hamiltonian graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) and can be defined by the [LCF notation](wikipedia://en/24186720) \[4\]<sup>8</sup>. It is an instance of an [Andrásfai graph](wikipedia://en/54284233), a type of [circulant graph](wikipedia://en/3498787) in which the vertices can be arranged in a cycle and each vertex is connected to the other vertices whose positions differ by a number that is 1 (mod 3). It is also isomorphic to the [circular clique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circular_clique) $K_{8/3}$.

It can be drawn as a [ladder graph](wikipedia://en/19657952) with 4 rungs made cyclic on a topological [Möbius strip](wikipedia://en/37817).
