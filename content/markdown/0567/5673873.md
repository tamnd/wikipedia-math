# Bruce C. Berndt

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bruce C. Berndt"}

**Bruce Carl Berndt** (born March 13, 1939) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). Berndt attended college at [Albion College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albion_College), graduating in 1961, where he also ran track. He received his master's and [doctoral](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) degrees from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). He lectured for a year at the [University of Glasgow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Glasgow) and then, in 1967, was appointed an assistant professor at the [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign), where he has remained since. In 1973–74, he was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton.[^o3] He is currently () [Michio Suzuki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michio_Suzuki_%28mathematician%29) Distinguished Research Professor of Mathematics at the University of Illinois.

:::

Berndt is an [analytic number theorist](wikipedia://en/251513) who is known for his work explicating the discoveries of [Srinivasa Ramanujan](wikipedia://en/47717).[^o4][^o5] He is a coordinating editor of *[The Ramanujan Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Ramanujan_Journal)* and, in 1996, received an expository [Steele Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steele_Prize) from the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for his work editing *Ramanujan's Notebooks*.[^o6][^o7] A [Lester R. Ford Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lester_R._Ford_Award) was given to Berndt, with Gert Almkvist, in 1989[^o8] and to Berndt, with S. Bhargava, in 1994.[^o9]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] In December 2012, he received an honorary doctorate[^o11] from [SASTRA University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SASTRA_University) in [Kumbakonam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kumbakonam), India.
