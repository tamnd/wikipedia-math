# Coble surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coble surface"}

In algebraic geometry, a **Coble surface** was defined by (Dolgachev Zhang 2001) to be a smooth rational projective surface with empty [anti-canonical linear system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anti-canonical_linear_system) |−K| and non-empty anti-bicanonical linear system |−2K|. An example of a Coble surface is the [blowing up](wikipedia://en/1817790) of the projective plane at the 10 nodes of a [Coble curve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coble_curve).[^o3]

:::
