# Igusa quartic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Igusa quartic"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Igusa quartic** (also called the **Castelnuovo–Richmond quartic** *CR*<sub>4</sub> or the **Castelnuovo–Richmond–Igusa quartic**) is a quartic [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) in 4-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135), studied by (txt). It is closely related to the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of genus 2 curves with level 2 structure. It is the dual of the [Segre cubic](wikipedia://en/23421399).

:::

It can be given as a codimension 2 variety in *P*<sup>5</sup> by the equations

$$\sum x_i=0$$

$$\big(\sum x_i^2\big)^2 = 4 \sum x_i^4$$
