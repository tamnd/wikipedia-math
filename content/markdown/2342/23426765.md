# Coble hypersurface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coble hypersurface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Coble hypersurface** is one of the hypersurfaces associated to the [Jacobian variety](wikipedia://en/489734) of a curve of [genus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Genus_%28topology%29) 2 or 3 by [Arthur Coble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Coble).

:::

There are two similar but different types of Coble hypersurfaces.

- The [Kummer variety](wikipedia://en/23431597) of the Jacobian of a genus 3 curve can be embedded in 7-dimensional [projective space](wikipedia://en/242135) under the 2-theta map, and is then the [singular locus](wikipedia://en/981915) of a 6-dimensional quartic hypersurface (Coble 1982), called a Coble hypersurface.
- Similarly the Jacobian of a genus 2 curve can be embedded in 8-dimensional projective space under the 3-theta map, and is then the singular locus of a 7-dimensional cubic hypersurface (Coble 1917), also called a Coble hypersurface.
