# Coble variety

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coble variety"}

In mathematics, the **Coble variety** is the [moduli space](wikipedia://en/361609) of ordered sets of 6 points in the [projective plane](wikipedia://en/24350), and can be represented as a double cover of the projective 4-space branched over the [Igusa quartic](wikipedia://en/23421089). It is a 4-dimensional [variety](wikipedia://en/1021753) that was first studied by [Arthur Byron Coble](wikipedia://en/17821492).

:::
