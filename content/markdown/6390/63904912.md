# Ultrabornological space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ultrabornological space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) $X$ is called **ultrabornological** if every [bounded linear operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_linear_operator) from $X$ into another TVS is necessarily [continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_map). A general version of the [closed graph theorem](wikipedia://en/497834) holds for ultrabornological spaces. Ultrabornological spaces were introduced by [Alexander Grothendieck](wikipedia://en/2042) (Grothendieck \[1955, p. 17\] "espace du type (β)").[^n1]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let $X$ be a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS).

### Preliminaries {#Preliminaries}

A **[disk](wikipedia://en/1734292)** is a convex and [balanced](wikipedia://en/1734238) set. A disk in a TVS $X$ is called **[bornivorous](wikipedia://en/64267682)**[^n2] if it [absorbs](wikipedia://en/1728179) every bounded subset of $X.$

A linear map between two TVSs is called **infrabounded**[^n2] if it maps [Banach disks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_disk) to bounded disks.

A disk $D$ in a TVS $X$ is called **infrabornivorous** if it satisfies any of the following equivalent conditions:

$D$ [absorbs](wikipedia://en/1728179) every [Banach disks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Banach_disk) in $X.$

while if $X$ locally convex then we may add to this list:

the [gauge](wikipedia://en/3510908) of $D$ is an infrabounded map;[^n2]

while if $X$ locally convex and Hausdorff then we may add to this list:

$D$ absorbs all compact disks;[^n2] that is, $D$ is "compactivorious".

### Ultrabornological space {#Ultrabornological_space}

A TVS $X$ is **ultrabornological** if it satisfies any of the following equivalent conditions:

every infrabornivorous disk in $X$ is a neighborhood of the origin;[^n2]

while if $X$ is a locally convex space then we may add to this list:

every bounded linear operator from $X$ into a complete [metrizable TVS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrizable_TVS) is necessarily continuous; every infrabornivorous disk is a neighborhood of 0; $X$ be the inductive limit of the spaces $X_D$ as $D$ varies over all compact disks in $X$; a seminorm on $X$ that is bounded on each Banach disk is necessarily continuous; for every locally convex space $Y$ and every linear map $u : X \to Y,$ if $u$ is bounded on each Banach disk then $u$ is continuous; for every Banach space $Y$ and every linear map $u : X \to Y,$ if $u$ is bounded on each Banach disk then $u$ is continuous.

while if $X$ is a Hausdorff locally convex space then we may add to this list:

$X$ is an inductive limit of Banach spaces;[^n2]

:::

::: {#o14 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Every [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) ultrabornological space is [barrelled](wikipedia://en/1733592),[^n2] [quasi-ultrabarrelled space](wikipedia://en/64274563), and a [bornological space](wikipedia://en/1804457) but there exist bornological spaces that are not ultrabornological.

Every ultrabornological space $X$ is the [inductive limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inductive_limit) of a family of [nuclear](wikipedia://en/2702957) [Fréchet spaces](wikipedia://en/233801), spanning $X.$ Every ultrabornological space $X$ is the inductive limit of a family of nuclear [DF-spaces](wikipedia://en/63703274), spanning $X.$

:::

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

The finite product of locally convex ultrabornological spaces is ultrabornological.[^n2] Inductive limits of ultrabornological spaces are ultrabornological.

Every Hausdorff sequentially complete [bornological space](wikipedia://en/1804457) is ultrabornological.[^n2] Thus every [complete](wikipedia://en/53695856) Hausdorff [bornological space](wikipedia://en/1804457) is ultrabornological. In particular, every [Fréchet space](wikipedia://en/233801) is ultrabornological.[^n2]

The [strong dual space](wikipedia://en/63951372) of a [complete](wikipedia://en/53695856) [Schwartz space](wikipedia://en/64266300) is ultrabornological.

Every Hausdorff [bornological space](wikipedia://en/1804457) that is [quasi-complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-complete) is ultrabornological.

**Counter-examples**

There exist [ultrabarrelled spaces](wikipedia://en/64268157) that are not ultrabornological. There exist ultrabornological spaces that are not ultrabarrelled.
