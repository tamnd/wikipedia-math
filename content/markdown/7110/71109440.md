# Ulisse Stefanelli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ulisse Stefanelli"}

**Ulisse Stefanelli** is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is currently professor at the Faculty of Mathematics of the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). His research focuses on [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), and [materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Stefanelli obtained his PhD under the guidance of Pierluigi Colli in 2003 at the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia). He holds a *Researcher* position at the Istituto di Matematica Applicata e Tecnologie Informatiche E. Magenes of the [National Research Council (Italy)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Research_Council_%28Italy%29) in [Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pavia) since 2001. In 2013 he has been appointed to the chair of Applied Mathematics and Modeling at the Faculty of Mathematics of the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). He has also conducted research at the [University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin), the [ETH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH) and the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich), the [Weierstrass Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute) in Berlin, and the Laboratoire de Mécanique et Génie Civil in Montpellier.

Since 2017 he is the *speaker* of the Spezialforschungsbereich F65 *Taming Complexity in Partial Differential Systems* funded by the [Austrian Science Fund](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Science_Fund).[^o5]
