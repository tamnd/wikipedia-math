# Dirk van Dalen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dirk van Dalen"}

*Image: Dirk van Dalen.jpg.* Dirk van Dalen

**Dirk van Dalen** (born 20 December 1932, [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam)) is a [Dutch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [historian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historian) of science.

:::

## Life {#Life}

Van Dalen studied mathematics and physics and astronomy at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam). Inspired by the work of [Brouwer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LEJ_Brouwer) and [Heyting](wikipedia://en/1180911), he received his Ph.D. in 1963 from the University of Amsterdam for the thesis *Extension problems in intuitionistic plane Projective geometry*.[^n1] From 1964 to 1966 Van Dalen taught [logic](wikipedia://en/19636) and mathematics at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and later [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). From 1967 he was professor at the [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utrecht). In 2003 Dirk van Dalen was awarded the Academy Medal 2003 of the [Royal Dutch Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Dutch_Academy_of_Sciences) for bringing the works of Brouwer to international attention.[^n2]
