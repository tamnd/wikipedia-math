# Ruth Etzioni

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ruth Etzioni"}

**Ruth D. Etzioni** is a biostatistician who develops statistical computer models to research cancer progression. She is the Rosalie and Harold Rea Brown endowed chair at the [Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hutchinson_Cancer_Research_Center).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Etzioni completed a B.S. in statistics at the [University of Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cape_Town) in 1985.[^o4] She earned a M.S. (1987) and Ph.D. (1990) in Statistics at [Carnegie Mellon University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carnegie_Mellon_University).[^o4] She was a postdoctoral researcher in the department of biostatistics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) from 1991 to 1992.[^o4]

Etzioni joined the faculty at the [Fred Hutchinson Cancer Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fred_Hutchinson_Cancer_Research_Center) in 1992 as an associate in the biostatistics program.[^o4] Her research interests include the development and implementation of statistical methods for prostate cancer studies.[^o4] She held multiple positions before her promotion to full member of the public health sciences division in 2002.[^o4] Etzioni was elected a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in April 2016.[^o4] As of 2016, her research focuses on the development of statistical computer models to learn about the latent process of cancer progression from observed data on disease incidence and mortality.[^o5] Etzioni became the inaugural recipient of the Rosalie and Harold Rea Brown endowed chair in 2020.[^o6]
