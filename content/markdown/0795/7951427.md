# Higher spin alternating sign matrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Higher spin alternating sign matrix"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **higher spin alternating sign matrix** is a generalisation of the [alternating sign matrix](wikipedia://en/1740155) (**ASM**), where the columns and rows sum to an integer *r* (the *spin*) rather than simply summing to 1 as in the usual alternating sign matrix definition. HSASMs are square matrices whose elements may be integers in the range −*r* to +*r*. When traversing any row or column of an ASM or HSASM, the partial sum of its entries must always be non-negative.[^o2]

:::

High spin ASMs have found application in [statistical mechanics](wikipedia://en/28481) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), where they have been found to represent [symmetry groups](wikipedia://en/28186) in [ice crystal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ice_crystal) formation.

Some typical examples of HSASMs are shown below:

$$\begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 &-1 & 1 \\ 2 &-1 & 2 &-1 \\ 0 & 1 &-1 & 2 \end{pmatrix};\quad \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 2 & 0&0 \\ 0 & 1 &-1 & 2 &0\\ 2 &-1 &-1 & 0 &2\\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 0 &0\\ 0&2&0&0&0 \end{pmatrix};\quad \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 0 & 0 & 2 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 2 &-2 & 2 & 0 \\ 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \end{pmatrix};\quad \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & 2 \\ 2 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 2 & 0 \end{pmatrix}.$$

The set of HSASMs is a [superset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Superset) of the ASMs. The [extreme points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extreme_points) of the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of the set of *r*-spin HSASMs are themselves integer multiples of the usual ASMs.
