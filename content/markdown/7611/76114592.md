# Benjamin Schlein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Benjamin Schlein"}

*Image: Benjamin Schlein.jpg.* Benjamin Schlein in 2017 in [Oberwolfach](wikipedia://en/1368669).

**Benjamin Schlein** (born 28 May 1975 in [Lugano](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lugano)) is a Swiss-born Italian[^o2] mathematician and professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich). Schlein's research is in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). He works in mathematical analysis of [many-body](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Many-body_problem) [quantum systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_system) and [random matrix theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Random_matrix_theory).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Schlein studied theoretical physics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) and received his PhD in 2002 under the supervision of [Jürg Fröhlich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jürg_Fröhlich). From 2004 to 2005 he was an NSF Postdoctoral Fellow at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) and from 2005 to 2006 at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). From 2006 to 2007 he was an assistant professor at [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis). During 2007-2008 he was a Research Fellow, supported by Kovalevskaja Award at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich). From 2007 to 2010 he was a lecturer at [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University).[^o5] From 2010 to 2014 he was a Hausdorff Chair at the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in Germany.[^o2][^o6] He has been a professor at the [University of Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zurich) since 2014.[^o7][^o8]

He was one of the four editors-in-chief of the [Journal of Functional Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Functional_Analysis) in 202224. As of 2025 he is still part of the editorial board.[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2006, Schlein received the Sofja Kovalevskaja Award from the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation).[^o11] In 2009, he was awarded the Young Scientist Prize of the [International Union of Pure and Applied Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Pure_and_Applied_Physics) (IUPAP) for outstanding results in mathematical analysis of many-body quantum systems, in particular, Bose gases.[^o12] In 2018, Schlein was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o13] In 2026 he was awarded the [Leroy P. Steele Prize for Seminal Contribution to Research](wikipedia://en/1252550) of the AMS.[^o14]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

He is the brother of Italian politician [Elly Schlein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elly_Schlein).[^o16]
