# Miklós Rédei

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Miklós Rédei"}

**Miklós Rédei** (born October 4, 1952, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest), [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary))[^o2] is a Hungarian [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) who specializes in the [philosophy of physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_physics).[^o3] He is professor of philosophy at the Department of Philosophy, Logic and Scientific Method at the [London School of Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics).[^o4]

:::

Rédei holds an MA in physics and MA in philosophy from [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), and a PhD in philosophy from the same university from 1982. He was awarded his doctorate ([Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)) in physics in 1989, his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in philosophy in 1997, and became [Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) in philosophy in 2001.[^o3] He has been an assistant professor at [Eötvös Loránd University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eötvös_Loránd_University), [Fulbright Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program) at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh), [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine), senior fellow at the [Dibner Institute for the History of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dibner_Institute_for_the_History_of_Science_and_Technology) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology), and visiting fellow at the Munich Centre for Mathematical Philosophy at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich).[^o5]

Rédei has published extensively on the logical foundations of [quantum mechanics](wikipedia://en/25202), [probabilistic causation](wikipedia://en/14474114), [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416), the [history of mathematics](wikipedia://en/14220), as well as the life and works of [John von Neumann](wikipedia://en/15942).[^o6]

## Books {#Books}

Rédei is the author or coauthor of:

- *Quantum Logic in Algebraic Approach* (Kluwer, 1998).[^o8]
- *The Principle of the Common Cause* (with Gábor Hofer-Szabó and László E. Szabó, Cambridge University Press, 2013).

His [edited volumes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edited_volume) include:

- *John von Neumann and the Foundations of Physics* (edited with Michael Stölzner, Kluwer, 2001)[^o9]
- *John von Neumann: Selected Letters* (American Mathematical Society, 2005).[^o10]
- *The Vienna Circle in Hungary* (edited with András Máté and Friedrich Stadler, Springer, 2011)[^o11]
