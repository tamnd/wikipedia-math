# Cynthia Vinzant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cynthia Vinzant"}

*Image: Cynthia Vinzant MFO 2017.jpg.* Vinzant at the [Oberwolfach Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1368669) in 2017

**Cynthia Vinzant** is an American mathematician specializing in [real algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/7252198); her research has also involved [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582), [matroid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid_theory), [Hermitian matrices](wikipedia://en/189682), and [spectrahedra](wikipedia://en/28733878) in [convex optimization](wikipedia://en/1674411). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Vinzant is a 2007 graduate of [Oberlin College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberlin_College) in Ohio, where she studied mathematics and neuroscience. She completed her Ph.D. in mathematics in 2011 at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), with the dissertation *Real Algebraic Geometry in Convex Optimization* supervised by [Bernd Sturmfels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernd_Sturmfels).

After working as a Hildebrandt Assistant Professor at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) and as a postdoctoral researcher at the [Simons Institute for the Theory of Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Institute_for_the_Theory_of_Computing) in Berkeley, Vinzant obtained a tenure-track assistant professor position at [North Carolina State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/North_Carolina_State_University) in 2015. She moved to the University of Washington in 2021 and was promoted to associate professor in 2023.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2020, Vinzant was named as a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellow) in mathematics and as a von Neumann Fellow at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). She was elected as a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2024 class of fellows. Vinzant and colleagues will receive[^o4] the 2025 *Michael and Sheila Held Prize*[^o5] from the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).
