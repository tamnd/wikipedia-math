# Lan-Hsuan Huang

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lan-Hsuan Huang"}

**Lan-Hsuan Huang** (黃籃萱) is a Taiwanese-American mathematician and [mathematical physicist](wikipedia://en/173416) specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), and their applications in the [theory of relativity](wikipedia://en/30001). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut). Huang serves on the editorial board of the *[Journal of Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Mathematical_Physics)*.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Huang majored in mathematics at [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University), graduating in 2004. She went to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) for graduate study in mathematics, and completed her Ph.D. there in 2009. Her doctoral dissertation, *Center of Mass and Constant Mean Curvature Foliations for Isolated Systems*, was supervised by [Richard Schoen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Schoen).

After three years at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) as Joseph F. Ritt Assistant Professor of Mathematics, she joined the Department of Mathematics [^o3] at the [University of Connecticut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Connecticut) in 2012 as a tenure-track assistant professor. She was promoted to associate professor in 2016, and to full professor in 2020.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2018, Huang was named a Simons Fellow[^o5] in mathematics and a von Neumann Fellow[^o6] at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). She was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) in the 2024 class of fellows. She was awarded a Simons Fellowship again in 2025.[^o5]
