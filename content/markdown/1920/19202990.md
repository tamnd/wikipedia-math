# Essential subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Essential subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially in the area of [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) studying the theory of [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974), an **essential subgroup** is a [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) that determines much of the structure of its containing group. The concept was generalized to [essential submodules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essential_submodule).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) $S$ of a (typically [abelian](wikipedia://en/2974)) [group](wikipedia://en/19447) $G$ is said to be **essential** if whenever *H* is a non-trivial subgroup of *G*, the intersection of *S* and *H* is non-trivial: here "non-trivial" means "containing an element other than the identity".

:::
