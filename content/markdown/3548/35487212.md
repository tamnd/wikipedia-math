# Joachim Weickert

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joachim Weickert"}

**Joachim Weickert** (born 15 March 1965 in [Ludwigshafen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwigshafen)) is a German professor of mathematics and computer science at [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University). In 2010, Weickert was awarded the [Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottfried_Wilhelm_Leibniz_Prize) for his work in image processing.[^o2][^o3]

:::

Weickert did his undergraduate studies at the [University of Kaiserslautern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kaiserslautern) and then stayed there as a graduate student, earning his doctorate in mathematics in 1996 under the supervision of Helmut Neunzert; his dissertation was titled *Anisotropic Diffusion in Image Processing*. After taking postdoctoral research positions at the [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utrecht) and the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen), he became an assistant professor at the [University of Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim), and earned a [habilitation degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 2001. In the same year, he took a faculty position as a full professor at [Saarland University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saarland_University).[^o5]
