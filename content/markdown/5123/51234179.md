# Todorov surface

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Todorov surface"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a **Todorov surface** is one of a class of surfaces of general type introduced by (txt) for which the conclusion of the [Torelli theorem](wikipedia://en/3002552) does not hold.

:::
