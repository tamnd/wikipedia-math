# Leticia Corral

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Leticia Corral"}

**Leticia Corral** (born March 16, 1959) is a Mexican [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), [astrophysicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysics) and [materials scientist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Corral was born in [Ciudad Cuauhtémoc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ciudad_Cuauhtémoc,_Chihuahua) in the northern state of [Chihuahua](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chihuahua_%28state%29).[^o4]

She earned a degree in industrial [chemical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_engineering) from the [Chihuahua Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chihuahua_Institute_of_Technology), a master's degree in mathematics from [CINVESTAV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CINVESTAV), and a Ph.D. in materials science from the Center for Advanced Materials Research (CIMAV) in Chihuahua. She also completed postdoctoral work at CIMAV's [Monterrey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monterrey) campus.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Corral is a research professor at the [National Technological Institute of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technological_Institute_of_Mexico) and the Institute of Technology of Ciudad Cuauhtémoc. Her current research focuses on [astrophysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysics), particularly the mathematics of [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), [black holes](wikipedia://en/4650) and [wormholes](wikipedia://en/34043), the [origins of the universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmogony), and Sir [Roger Penrose](wikipedia://en/26193)'s theories of [cosmology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmology).

She presented a paper titled "Matemáticas Aplicadas a los Materiales" at the School of Mechanical & Manufacturing Engineering in [Dublin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin) in 2003, and "Modelling heat transfer in work rolls of a continuous hot strip mill, Part I" in 2005 at [City, University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City,_University_of_London). The latter was published as a chapter of the book *Mathematical Modelling: Education, Engineering and Economics*. In [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul) in 2012, she presented research conducted with three generations of [mechatronics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechatronics) students at the Institute of Technology of Ciudad Cuauhtémoc, titled "Diseño de un robot mecatrónico para dar terapia y remover tumores". She received the 2013 Chihuahua Award in Technological Sciences for this work.

In 2009, the Scientific Committee of the 5th International Conference on Diffusion in Solids and Liquids gave her the [Joseph Fourier Award](wikipedia://en/25767401) for her research on [heat transfer](wikipedia://en/184726) into black holes. Since then, she has published several articles on Penrose's [cosmic censorship hypothesis](wikipedia://en/7609); on energy transfer and fluid flow around massive astrophysical objects; and on modelling the structure of wormholes, [virtual particles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virtual_particle) from the [Big Bang](wikipedia://en/4116), and mass transfer in the [Higgs boson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higgs_boson).

Her research on the Higgs boson, which she presented in [Vilamoura, Portugal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilamoura), in 2011, predicted with high accuracy the mass of the Higgs boson. Her prediction was identical to the value later reported on July 4, 2012, by the [ATLAS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ATLAS_experiment) and [CMS experiments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Muon_Solenoid) at the [Large Hadron Collider](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Large_Hadron_Collider).

At [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) on July 2, 2015, Corral presented a model that, using [relativistic equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_relativistic_equations), predicted very low [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891) at the Big Bang. Her theory disagreed with the [phase-space](wikipedia://en/191101) volume posited by [Stephen Hawking](wikipedia://en/19376148), who, Corral said, did not take into account the [asymmetrical component of time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Entropy_%28arrow_of_time%29) when calculating the entropy in the Big Bang [singularity](wikipedia://en/54244), that work was not published in a specialized scientific journal nor peer reviewed.[^o7] However, it matched Penrose's [Weyl curvature hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weyl_curvature_hypothesis), which does take that into account.[^o4] As such, she is a supporter of the [cyclic model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_model) of the Universe, in which the initial conditions match the final ones.[^o5] However there's not any academic paper published by her on a Astrophysics journal, also the claimed scientific achievements are not supported or recognized by the scientific community.

## Awards {#Awards}

Corral received various awards for her contributions to science including, notably, the [Joseph Fourier](wikipedia://en/25767401) Award in 2009, for her scientific contributions in the understanding of solid and liquid diffusion.[^o4]

## Industrial property {#Industrial_property}

In 2020, [Leticia Corral](wikipedia://en/51231314) was one of the holders of an industrial design registration granted by the [Mexican Institute of Industrial Property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Institute_of_Industrial_Property) (IMPI), under file number **MX/2020/84324**.[^o11] The design was developed in collaboration with students from the [National Technological Institute of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Technological_Institute_of_Mexico), Cuauhtémoc campus, including:

- [Hortencia Mendoza Olivas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hortencia_Mendoza_Olivas)
- [Andrea Aragonez Aguirre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Aragonez_Aguirre)
- [Francisco Alexis Cordero Zúñiga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francisco_Alexis_Cordero_Zúñiga)
- [Jorge Uriel Acosta Arévalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jorge_Uriel_Acosta_Arévalo)
- [Irvin Eduardo Parra Domínguez](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irvin_Eduardo_Parra_Domínguez)
- [Andrea Jaquelyn Lam Bencomo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andrea_Jaquelyn_Lam_Bencomo)
- [Álvaro Adrián Pavón González](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Álvaro_Adrián_Pavón_González)
- [José Ramón Acosta Cerda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/José_Ramón_Acosta_Cerda)
- [Paúl Eduardo Hernández Ríos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paúl_Eduardo_Hernández_Ríos)
- [Sergio Ruelas Ledezma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergio_Ruelas_Ledezma)

This registration is part of a technological innovation project in the medical field, focused on the functional design of devices related to oncology treatments.
