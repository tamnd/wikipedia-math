# Horseshoe (symbol)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Horseshoe"}

**Horseshoe**[^o2] (**⊃**, \\supset in [TeX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TeX)) is a [symbol](wikipedia://en/37673) used to represent:

:::

- [Material conditional](wikipedia://en/658808) in [propositional logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositional_calculus)
- [Superset](wikipedia://en/27631) in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553)

It was used by [Whitehead](wikipedia://en/43395) and [Russell](wikipedia://en/4163) in *[Principia Mathematica](wikipedia://en/24133)*. In [Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode) the symbol is encoded 2283.
