# Wedge (symbol)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wedge"}

**Wedge** (**∧**) is a [symbol](wikipedia://en/37673) that looks similar to an in-line [caret](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caret) (^). It is used to represent various [operations](wikipedia://en/4140245). In [Unicode](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicode), the symbol is encoded 2227 and by `\wedge` and `\land` in [TeX](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TeX). The opposite symbol (∨) is called a [vel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vel_%28symbol%29), or sometimes a (descending) wedge. Some authors who call the descending wedge *vel* often call the ascending wedge *ac* (the corresponding Latin word for "and", also spelled "atque"), keeping their usage parallel.

:::

::: {#o4 .application cue=heading name="Use"}

## Use {#Use}

Wedge is used to represent various [operations](wikipedia://en/4140245):

- [Logical conjunction](wikipedia://en/18152) in [propositional logic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Propositional_calculus) and [first-order logic](wikipedia://en/10983)[^o5]
- [Meet](wikipedia://en/3956618) in [lattice theory](wikipedia://en/469304)
- [Exterior product](wikipedia://en/221537) or wedge product in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625)
