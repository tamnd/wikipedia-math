# Charles Roy Henderson bibliography

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Roy Henderson bibliography"}

The **Charles Roy Henderson bibliography** contains a list of works published by American statistician [Charles Roy Henderson](wikipedia://en/18707316) (1911–1989). His corpus covers his 40-year professional career from his PhD thesis in 1948 until his death in 1989. It includes three of his most important scientific publications: *Estimation of Variance and Covariance Components* (1953),[^n1] *The estimation of environmental and genetic trends from records subject to culling* (1959, with [Oscar Kempthorne](wikipedia://en/23146939), [Shayle R. Searle](wikipedia://en/18409802), and C. M. von Krosigk),[^n2] and *A simple method for computing the inverse of a numerator relationship matrix used in prediction of breeding values* (1976).[^n3]

:::
