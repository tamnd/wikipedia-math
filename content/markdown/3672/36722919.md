# Torsion sheaf

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Torsion sheaf"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **torsion sheaf** is a [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) of abelian groups $\mathcal{F}$ on a [site](wikipedia://en/12910#Sites_and_sheaves) for which, for every object *U*, the space of sections $\Gamma(U, \mathcal{F})$ is a [torsion abelian group](wikipedia://en/3568695). Similarly, for a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666) *p*, we say a sheaf $\mathcal{F}$ is ***p*-torsion** if every section over any object is killed by a power of *p*.

:::

A torsion sheaf on an étale site is the union of its [constructible subsheaves](wikipedia://en/26131119).[^o2]
