# Locally acyclic morphism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Locally acyclic morphism"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), a morphism $f: X \to S$ of [schemes](wikipedia://en/364754) is said to be **locally acyclic** if, roughly, any [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) on *S* and its restriction to *X* through *f* have the same [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231), locally. For example, a [smooth morphism](wikipedia://en/7837041) is universally locally acyclic.

:::
