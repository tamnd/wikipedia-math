# Uwe Grimm

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uwe Grimm"}

*Image: Uwe Grimm.jpg.* Grimm at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2014

**Uwe Grimm** (1963–2021) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physicist). He was one of the pioneers in [aperiodic tilings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aperiodic_tilings) and their dynamical and spectral properties.[^o2] He also worked in [statistical physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_physics) and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).

:::

Grimm was born on March 9, 1963, in [Gütersloh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gütersloh), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germany). He earned his PhD from the [University of Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) in 1991 with a thesis in statistical mechanics on quantum spin chains and their symmetries, written under the supervision of Vladimir Rittenberg. After that he spent eight years as a postdoctoral researcher in [Melbourne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Melbourne), Amsterdam and [Chemnitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemnitz), where he completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) on the physics of aperiodic order. In 2000 he joined the [Open University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_University) in [Milton Keynes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milton_Keynes), where he became a professor in 2009. He was Head of the Department of Mathematics and Statistics from 2014 to 2016. He served as Co-editor of [Acta Crystallographica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Crystallographica) Section A and was Chair of the IUCr Commission on Aperiodic Crystals from 2014 to 2017. He died on October 28, 2021, in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford), [UK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom).
