# Romane Clark

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Romane Clark"}

**Romane Lewis Clark** (December 3, 1925 – August 17, 2007) was an American philosopher and Professor Emeritus of Philosophy at [Indiana University, Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University,_Bloomington). He is known for his works on [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065),[^o3][^o4][^o5] especially his eponymous paradox ([Clark's paradox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_object_theory#Overview)).[^o6][^o7]

:::
