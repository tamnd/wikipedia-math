# FK-AK space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="FK-AK space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180) and related areas of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), an **FK-AK space** or [FK-space](wikipedia://en/1725128) with the **AK property** is an [FK-space](wikipedia://en/1725128) which contains the [space of finite sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_of_finite_sequences) and has a [Schauder basis](wikipedia://en/3460662).[^o2]

:::

## Examples and non-examples {#Examples_and_non-examples}

- $c_0$ the [space of convergent sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_of_convergent_sequences) with the [supremum norm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supremum_norm) has the AK property.
- $\ell^p$ ($1 \leq p < \infty$) the [absolutely p-summable sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Absolutely_p-summable_sequences) with the $\|\cdot\|_p$ norm have the AK property.
- $\ell^\infty$ with the supremum norm does **not** have the AK property.

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

An FK-AK space $E$ has the property

$$E' \simeq E^\beta$$

that is the [continuous dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual) of $E$ is [linear isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_isomorphic) to the [beta dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_dual) of $E.$

FK-AK spaces are [separable spaces](wikipedia://en/27855).

:::
