# Result

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Result"}

A **result** (also called **upshot**) is the outcome or consequence of a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) of actions or events.[^o2] Possible results include [gain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gain_%28accounting%29), [injury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injury), [value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_%28economics%29), and [victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory). Some types of results include the outcome of an [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_%28philosophy%29), the final value of a [calculation](wikipedia://en/239121), and the outcome of a [vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vote).

:::

## Description {#Description}

A result is the final consequence of a [sequence](wikipedia://en/27838) of actions or events expressed [qualitatively](wikipedia://en/1901895) or [quantitatively](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_property). Possible results include advantage, disadvantage, [gain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gain_%28accounting%29), [injury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Injury), loss, [value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_%28economics%29), and [victory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victory).

There may be a range of possible outcomes associated with an event depending on the [point of view](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Point_of_view_%28philosophy%29), historical distance or relevance. Reaching no result can mean that actions are inefficient, ineffective, meaningless or flawed.

## Types {#Types}

Some types of result are as follows:

- in general, the outcome of any kind of [research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research), [action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Action_%28philosophy%29) or [phenomenon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phenomenon)[^o2]
- in [games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Game) (e.g. [cricket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_result_in_cricket), [lotteries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lottery)) or [wars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War), the result includes the identity of the victorious party and possibly the effects on the [environment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Environment_%28biophysical%29)
- in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the final value of a [calculation](wikipedia://en/239121) (e.g. arithmetic operation), [function](wikipedia://en/185427) or [statistical expression](wikipedia://en/26685), or the final statement of a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) that has been proven
- in [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), any [information](wikipedia://en/18985062) analyzed, extracted or interpolated from [polls](wikipedia://en/277315), [tests](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Test_method) or logs
- in [computer sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_sciences), the [return value](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Return_value) of a function, state of a system or list of records matching a query (e.g. web search). The *result type* is the [data type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_type) of the data returned by a function.
- in [science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science), the *outcome* and data concluded from an [experiment](wikipedia://en/59861) (e.g. see [null hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Null_hypothesis))
- in [forensics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forensics) and [justice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Justice), the *proof* of guilt or innocence of a suspect after evaluating evidence in a [criminal investigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Criminal_investigation)
- in [economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Economics) and [accounting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accounting), the profit or loss at the end of a fiscal period.
- in [democracy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Democracy), the [election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Election) of a [representative](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_democracy) or the outcome of a [vote](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vote) on a subject
- In [management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Management) and related fields, a result is a piece of [information](wikipedia://en/18985062) that has certain properties in absolute terms or in relation to previous results or settings
- In [chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemistry), the result is the final item of a [mix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mixture)
- In [Internet search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_search), the results are the list of pages supplied in response to a specific query[^o5]
