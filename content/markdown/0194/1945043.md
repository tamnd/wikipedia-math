# Whorl

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Whorl"}

*Image: Whorls.png.* Main whorled patterns.

A **whorl** ( or ) is an individual [circle](wikipedia://en/6220), [oval](wikipedia://en/2027249), volution or equivalent in a **whorled pattern**, which consists of a [spiral](wikipedia://en/61563) or multiple [concentric objects](wikipedia://en/19049087) (including [circles](wikipedia://en/6220), [ovals](wikipedia://en/2027249) and [arcs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arc_%28geometry%29)).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## In nature {#In_nature}

further examples

For [mollusc whorls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mollusc_whorls), the [body whorl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_whorl) in a mollusc shell is the most recently formed whorl of a spiral shell, terminating in the aperture.
