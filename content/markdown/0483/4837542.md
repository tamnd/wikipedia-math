# 2004 Transnistrian census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="2004 Transnistrian census"}

The **2004 Transnistrian census** was organized in [Transnistria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transnistria) at roughly the same time that [Moldova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldova) held [its own census](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldovan_Census_%282004%29), which Transnistria refused to participate in out of principle and deference to its September 2, 1990 [declaration of independence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Declaration_of_independence).

:::

## Census results {#Census_results}

Total population (including [Bender](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bender,_Moldova)): 555,347 (percentages below refer to this first figure)

Total population (excluding Bender): 450,337

- [Moldovans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moldovans): 31.9%
- [Russians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russians): 30.3%
- [Ukrainians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukrainians): 28.8%
- [Bulgarians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulgarians): 2%
- [Poles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people): 2%
- [Gagauz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gagauz_people): 1.5%
- [Jews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jews): 1.3%
- [Belarusians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belarusians): 1%
- [Germans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans): 0.6%
- Others: 0.5%[^o4][^o5]

Preliminary data, as shown here, was released forty days after the completion of the census. Final and more detailed results were released with a delay of nearly two years.

Compared with the 1989 census, the population decreased by 18% due to [war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_in_Transnistria), natural decrease and economically motivated emigration.[^o6]

In terms of religion, approximately 90% of Transnistria's population identified as Christian Orthodox. 6% chose not to respond, and 4% listed other religions (Catholic, Jewish, etc).[^o7]

Those residents who hold citizenship of Transnistria chose to identify themselves as citizens of Transnistria numbered 508,600 people (more than 90,0% of the permanent population of the republic). Meanwhile, 107,600 thousand people (19,4%) listed themselves as citizens of the Republic of Moldova, while 56,000 people (10,1%) claimed citizenship of the Russian Federation and 44,400 people (8,0%) citizenship of Ukraine.[^o8]

The cost of the census was estimated US \$550 thousand.[^o9]
