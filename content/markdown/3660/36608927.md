# Encyclopedia of Mathematics (James Tanton)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Encyclopedia of Mathematics"}

***Encyclopedia of Mathematics*** is a 2005 encyclopedic [reference work](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reference_work) by Australian–American mathematician [James Tanton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Tanton) that was published by Facts-on-File of New York.[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Synopsis {#Synopsis}

The book has over 1000 entries, which discuss various concepts, definitions, [people](wikipedia://en/80143), and [theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_theorem) that pertain to [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). The book also contains six [essays](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Essay) that discuss the various [branches of mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Branches_of_mathematics) and their [history](wikipedia://en/14220).

## Reception {#Reception}

[Booklist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Booklist) praised the book as a useful "basic resource for students who wish to have a better understanding of simple or not-so--simple mathematical concepts" and the *[School Library Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/School_Library_Journal)* called the encyclopedia "comprehensive".[^o7][^o8]
