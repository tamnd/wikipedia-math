# Bishop–Gromov inequality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bishop–Gromov inequality"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Bishop–Gromov inequality** is a [comparison theorem in Riemannian geometry](wikipedia://en/6626644#Riemannian_geometry), named after [Richard L. Bishop](wikipedia://en/43076291) and [Mikhail Gromov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mikhail_Gromov_%28mathematician%29). It is closely related to [Myers' theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Myers'_theorem), and is the key point in the proof of [Gromov's compactness theorem](wikipedia://en/6657292).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $M$ be a complete *n*-dimensional Riemannian manifold whose [Ricci curvature](wikipedia://en/276582) satisfies the lower bound

$$\mathrm{Ric} \geq (n-1) K$$

for a constant $K\in \mathbb{R}$. Let $M_K^n$ be the complete *n*-dimensional [simply connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply_connected) space of constant [sectional curvature](wikipedia://en/285618) $K$ (and hence of constant Ricci curvature $(n-1)K$); thus $M_K^n$ is the *n*-[sphere](wikipedia://en/27859) of radius $1/\sqrt{K}$ if $K>0$, or *n*-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697) if $K=0$, or an appropriately rescaled version of *n*-dimensional [hyperbolic space](wikipedia://en/318742) if $K<0$. Denote by $B(p,r)$ the ball of radius *r* around a point *p*, defined with respect to the [Riemannian distance function](wikipedia://en/144652#Riemannian_manifolds_as_metric_spaces).

Then, for any $p\in M$ and $p_K\in M_K^n$, the function

$$\phi(r) = \frac{\mathrm{Vol} \, B(p,r)}{\mathrm{Vol}\, B(p_K,r)}$$

is non-increasing on $(0,\infty)$.

As *r* goes to zero, the ratio approaches one, so together with the monotonicity this implies that

$$\mathrm{Vol} \,B(p,r) \leq \mathrm{Vol} \, B(p_K,r).$$

This is the version first proved by Bishop.[^o5][^o6]

:::
