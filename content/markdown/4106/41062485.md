# Ramón Verea

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ramón Verea"}

*Image: Busto de Ramón Verea, A Estrada.jpg.* Bust of Ramón Verea at [A Estrada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Estrada).

**Ramón Silvestre Verea Aguiar y García** ([Curantes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Estrada), 11 December 1833 – [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires), 6 February 1899) was a Galician journalist, engineer and writer, known as the inventor of a Calculadora Alicia with an internal multiplication table (1878).

:::
