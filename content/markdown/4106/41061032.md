# Samuel J. Lomonaco Jr.

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Samuel J. Lomonaco Jr."}

**Samuel John Lomonaco Jr.** is an American academic and mathematician who is a professor of computer science and electrical engineering at the [University of Maryland, Baltimore County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_Baltimore_County).

:::

## Background {#Background}

Lomonaco earned a Bachelor of Science degree in mathematics from [Saint Louis University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Louis_University_%28United_States%29) and a PhD in mathematics from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).[^o4] Lomonaco specializes in [quantum computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_computation), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [quantum information science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_science), [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), and [quantum algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_algorithm).[^o5][^o6][^o7]
