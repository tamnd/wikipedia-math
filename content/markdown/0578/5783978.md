# Paranormal space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Paranormal space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the realm of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a **paranormal space** (Nyikos 1984) is a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) in which every [countable](wikipedia://en/6026) discrete collection of [closed sets](wikipedia://en/47279) has a [locally finite](wikipedia://en/11611098) open expansion.

:::
