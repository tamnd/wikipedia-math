# Rational representation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rational representation"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), a [linear representation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_representation) of an algebraic group is said to be **rational** if, viewed as a map from the group to the [general linear group](wikipedia://en/113564), it is a [rational map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_map) of algebraic varieties.

:::

Finite direct sums and products of rational representations are rational.

A rational $G$ module is a module that can be expressed as a sum (not necessarily direct) of rational representations.
