# Rothe–Hagen identity

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rothe–Hagen identity"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Rothe–Hagen identity** is a [mathematical identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_identity) valid for all [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826) ($x, y, z$) except where its denominators [vanish](wikipedia://en/264210):

:::

$$\sum_{k=0}^n\frac{x}{x+kz}{x+kz \choose k}\frac{y}{y+(n-k)z}{y+(n-k)z \choose n-k}=\frac{x+y}{x+y+nz}{x+y+nz \choose n}.$$

It is a generalization of [Vandermonde's identity](wikipedia://en/916157), and is named after the independent work of [Heinrich August Rothe](wikipedia://en/35235049) in 1793 and of [Johann Georg Hagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johann_Georg_Hagen) in 1891.
