# Bach tensor

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bach tensor"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), the **Bach tensor** is a trace-free [tensor](wikipedia://en/29965) of rank 2 which is [conformally invariant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformally_invariant) in dimension *n* = 4.[^o2] Before 1968, it was the only known conformally invariant tensor that is [algebraically independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algebraically_independent) of the [Weyl tensor](wikipedia://en/1026848).[^o3] In [abstract indices](wikipedia://en/1674987) the Bach tensor is given by

:::

$$B_{ab} = P_{cd}{{{W_a}^c}_b}^d+\nabla^c\nabla_cP_{ab}-\nabla^c\nabla_aP_{bc}$$

where *$W$* is the [Weyl tensor](wikipedia://en/1026848), and *$P$* the [Schouten tensor](wikipedia://en/6129873) given in terms of the [Ricci tensor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricci_tensor) *$R_{ab}$* and [scalar curvature](wikipedia://en/285622) *$R$* by

$$P_{ab}=\frac{1}{n-2}\left(R_{ab}-\frac{R}{2(n-1)}g_{ab}\right).$$
