# Ambient construction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ambient construction"}

In [conformal geometry](wikipedia://en/295907), the **ambient construction** refers to a construction of [Charles Fefferman](wikipedia://en/502831) and [Robin Graham](wikipedia://en/65653497)[^o2] for which a [conformal manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_manifold) of dimension *n* is realized (*ambiently*) as the boundary of a certain [Poincaré manifold](wikipedia://en/1589135), or alternatively as the [celestial sphere](wikipedia://en/48239) of a certain [pseudo-Riemannian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudo-Riemannian) manifold.

:::

The ambient construction is canonical in the sense that it is performed only using the [conformal class](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_class) of the metric: it is conformally invariant. However, the construction only works [asymptotically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Asymptotic), up to a certain [order of approximation](wikipedia://en/346769). There is, in general, an [obstruction](wikipedia://en/2179639) to continuing this extension past the critical order. The obstruction itself is of tensorial character, and is known as the (conformal) **obstruction tensor**. It is, along with the [Weyl tensor](wikipedia://en/1026848), one of the two primitive invariants in conformal differential geometry.

Aside from the obstruction tensor, the ambient construction can be used to define a class of conformally invariant [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886) known as the [GJMS operators](wikipedia://en/25525269).[^o3]

A related construction is the [tractor bundle](wikipedia://en/5803388).

## Overview {#Overview}

The model flat geometry for the ambient construction is the future [null cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Null_cone) in [Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space), with the origin deleted. The celestial sphere at infinity is the conformal manifold *M*, and the null rays in the cone determine a [line bundle](wikipedia://en/361366) over *M*. Moreover, the null cone carries a metric which degenerates in the direction of the generators of the cone.

The ambient construction in this flat model space then asks: if one is provided with such a line bundle, along with its degenerate metric, to what extent is it possible to *extend* the metric off the null cone in a canonical way, thus recovering the ambient Minkowski space? In formal terms, the degenerate metric supplies a [Dirichlet boundary condition](wikipedia://en/423504) for the extension problem and, as it happens, the natural condition is for the extended metric to be [Ricci flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricci_flat) (because of the normalization of the [normal conformal connection](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normal_conformal_connection).)

The ambient construction generalizes this to the case when *M* is conformally curved, first by constructing a natural null line bundle *N* with a degenerate metric, and then solving the associated Dirichlet problem on *N* × (-1,1).

## Details {#Details}

This section provides an overview of the construction, first of the null line bundle, and then of its ambient extension.

### The null line bundle {#The_null_line_bundle}

Suppose that *M* is a conformal manifold, and that \[*g*\] denotes the conformal metric defined on *M*. Let π : *N* → *M* denote the tautological subbundle of T<sup>\*</sup>*M* ⊗ T<sup>\*</sup>*M* defined by all representatives of the conformal metric. In terms of a fixed background metric *g*<sub>0</sub>, *N* consists of all positive multiples ω<sup>2</sup>*g*<sub>0</sub> of the metric. There is a natural action of **R**<sup>\+</sup> on *N*, given by

$$\delta_\omega g = \omega^2 g$$

Moreover, the [total space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_space) of *N* carries a tautological degenerate metric, for if *p* is a point of the fibre of π : *N* → *M* corresponding to the conformal representative *g*<sub>p</sub>, then let

$$h_p(X_p,Y_p) = g_p(\pi_*X,\pi_*Y).$$

This metric degenerates along the vertical directions. Furthermore, it is homogeneous of degree 2 under the **R**<sup>\+</sup> action on *N*:

$$\delta^*_\omega h = \omega^2 h$$

Let *X* be the vertical vector field generating the scaling action. Then the following properties are immediate:

*h*(*X*,-) = 0

**L**<sub>X</sub>h = 2*h*, where **L**<sub>X</sub> is the [Lie derivative](wikipedia://en/294437) along the vector field *X*.

### The ambient space {#The_ambient_space}

Let *N*<sup>~</sup> = *N* × (-1,1), with the natural inclusion *i* : *N* → *N*<sup>~</sup>. The dilations δ<sub>ω</sub> extend naturally to *N*<sup>~</sup>, and hence so does the generator *X* of dilation.

An **ambient metric** on *N*<sup>~</sup> is a Lorentzian metric *h*<sup>~</sup> such that

- The metric is *homogeneous*: δ<sub>ω</sub><sup>\*</sup> *h*<sup>~</sup> = ω<sup>2</sup> *h*<sup>~</sup>
- The metric is an *ambient extension*: *i*<sup>\*</sup> *h*<sup>~</sup> = *h*, where *i*<sup>\*</sup> is the [pullback](wikipedia://en/494995) along the natural inclusion.
- The metric is *[Ricci flat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ricci_flat)*: Ric(*h*<sup>~</sup>) = 0.

Suppose that a fixed representative of the conformal metric *g* and a local coordinate system *x* = (*x*<sup>i</sup>) are chosen on *M*. These induce coordinates on *N* by identifying a point in the fibre of *N* with (*x*,*t*<sup>2</sup>*g*(*x*)) where *t* > 0 is the fibre coordinate. (In these coordinates, *X* = *t* ∂<sub>*t*</sub>.) Finally, if ρ is a defining function of *N* in *N*<sup>~</sup> which is homogeneous of degree 0 under dilations, then (*x*,*t*,ρ) are coordinates of *N*<sup>~</sup>. Furthermore, any extension metric which is homogeneous of degree 2 can be written in these coordinates in the form:

$$h^\sim = t^2 g_{ij}(x,\rho)dx^idx^j+2\rho dt^2+2tdtd\rho,\,$$

where the *g*<sub>ij</sub> are *n*<sup>2</sup> functions with *g*(*x*,0) = *g*(*x*), the given conformal representative.

After some calculation one shows that the Ricci flatness is equivalent to the following differential equation, where the prime is differentiation with respect to ρ:

$$\rho g_{ij}''-\rho g^{kl}g_{ik}'g_{jl}+\tfrac12\rho g^{kl}g_{kl}'g_{ij}'+\frac{2-n}{2}g_{ij}'-\tfrac12 g^{kl}g_{kl}'g_{ij}+\mathrm{Ric}(g)_{ij}=0.$$

One may then formally solve this equation as a power series in ρ to obtain the asymptotic development of the ambient metric off the null cone. For example, substituting ρ = 0 and solving gives

*g*<sub>ij</sub><sup>′</sup>(*x*,0) = 2*P*<sub>ij</sub>

where *P* is the [Schouten tensor](wikipedia://en/6129873). Next, differentiating again and substituting the known value of *g*<sub>ij</sub><sup>′</sup>(*x*,0) into the equation, the second derivative can be found to be a multiple of the [Bach tensor](wikipedia://en/5784784). And so forth.
