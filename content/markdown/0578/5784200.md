# Observable subgroup

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Observable subgroup"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) of [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), an **observable subgroup** is an algebraic [subgroup](wikipedia://en/28011) of a [linear algebraic group](wikipedia://en/395885) whose every finite-dimensional [rational representation](wikipedia://en/5784135) arises as the restriction to the subgroup of a finite-dimensional rational representation of the whole group.

:::

An equivalent formulation, in case the base field is closed, is that *K* is an observable subgroup of *G* if and only if the quotient variety *G*/*K* is a [quasi-affine variety](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-affine_variety).

Some basic facts about observable subgroups:

- Every [normal](wikipedia://en/21918) algebraic subgroup of an algebraic group is observable.
- Every observable subgroup of an observable subgroup is observable.
