# Parafree group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Parafree group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the realm of [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is said to be **parafree** if its quotients by the terms of its [lower central series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lower_central_series) are the same as those of a [free group](wikipedia://en/59735) and if it is [residually nilpotent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Residually_nilpotent_group) (the intersection of the terms of its lower central series is trivial).

:::

Parafree groups share many properties with [free groups](wikipedia://en/59735), making it difficult to distinguish between these two types. [Gilbert Baumslag](wikipedia://en/11449648) was led to the study of parafree groups in attempts to resolve the conjecture that a group of cohomological dimension one is free. One of his fundamental results is that there exist parafree groups that are not free. With [Urs Stammbach](wikipedia://en/58132112), he proved there exists a non-free parafree group with every countable subgroup being free.
