# A-paracompact space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="A-paracompact space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is said to be **a-paracompact** if every [open cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_cover) of the space has a [locally finite](wikipedia://en/11611098) [refinement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Refinement_of_an_open_cover). In contrast to the definition of [paracompactness](wikipedia://en/48631), the refinement is not required to be open.

:::

Every paracompact space is a-paracompact, and in [regular spaces](wikipedia://en/53058) the two notions coincide.
