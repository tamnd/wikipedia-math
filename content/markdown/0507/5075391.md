# Volterra space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Volterra space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is said to be a **Volterra space** if any finite intersection of [dense](wikipedia://en/23523594) [G<sub>δ</sub> subsets](wikipedia://en/252076) is dense. Every [Baire space](wikipedia://en/200550) is Volterra, but the converse is not true. In fact, any [metrizable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metrizable) Volterra space is Baire.

:::

The name refers to a paper of [Vito Volterra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vito_Volterra) in which he uses the fact that (in modern notation) the intersection of two dense G-delta sets in the [real numbers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_numbers) is again dense.
