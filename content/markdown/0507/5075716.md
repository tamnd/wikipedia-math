# Realcompact space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Realcompact space"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) is said to be **realcompact** if it is [completely regular Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/30554) and it contains every point of its [Stone–Čech compactification](wikipedia://en/52897) that is real (meaning that the [quotient field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quotient_field) at that point of the [ring](wikipedia://en/48404) of real functions is the reals). Realcompact spaces have also been called **Q-spaces**, **saturated spaces**, **functionally complete spaces**, **real-complete spaces**, **replete spaces** and **Hewitt–Nachbin spaces** (named after [Edwin Hewitt](wikipedia://en/7482590) and [Leopoldo Nachbin](wikipedia://en/19050267)). Realcompact spaces were introduced by (Hewitt 1948).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- A space is realcompact if and only if it can be embedded [homeomorphically](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) as a closed subset in some (not necessarily finite) [Cartesian power](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_power) of the reals, with the [product topology](wikipedia://en/24910). Moreover, a (Hausdorff) space is realcompact if and only if it has the [uniform topology](wikipedia://en/32339#Topology_of_uniform_spaces) and is [complete](wikipedia://en/32339#Completeness) for the [uniform structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_structure) generated by the continuous real-valued functions (Gillman, Jerison, p. 226).
- For example [Lindelöf spaces](wikipedia://en/310480) are realcompact; in particular all subsets of $\mathbb{R}^n$ are realcompact.
- The (Hewitt) realcompactification υ*X* of a topological space *X* consists of the real points of its [Stone–Čech compactification](wikipedia://en/52897) β*X*. A [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) *X* is realcompact if and only if it coincides with its Hewitt realcompactification.
- Write *C*(*X*) for the ring of continuous real-valued functions on a topological space *X*. If *Y* is a real compact space, then ring homomorphisms from *C*(*Y*) to *C*(*X*) correspond to continuous maps from *X* to *Y*. In particular the [category](wikipedia://en/53759) of realcompact spaces is dual to the category of rings of the form *C*(*X*).
- In order that a [Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/13637) *X* is **compact** it is necessary and sufficient that *X* is **realcompact** and **pseudocompact** (see Engelking, p. 153).
