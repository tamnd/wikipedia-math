# Jonathan Partington

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jonathan Partington"}

**Jonathan Richard Partington** (born 4 February 1955) is an English mathematician who is Emeritus Professor of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Professor Partington was educated at [Gresham's School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gresham's_School), [Holt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holt,_Norfolk), and [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), where he completed his PhD thesis entitled "Numerical ranges and the Geometry of Banach Spaces" under the supervision of [Béla Bollobás](wikipedia://en/614956).

## Career {#Career}

Partington works in the area of [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), sometimes applied to [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), and is the author of several books in this area. He was formerly editor-in-chief of the *[Journal of the London Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_London_Mathematical_Society)*, a position he held jointly with his Leeds colleague [John Truss](wikipedia://en/71668954).

Partington's extra-mathematical activities include the invention of the [March March march](http://www.marchmarchmarch.org.uk/), an annual walk starting at [March, Cambridgeshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/March,_Cambridgeshire). He is also known as a writer or co-writer of some of the earliest British [text-based computer games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_text_based_games), including Acheton, Hamil, Murdac, Avon, Fyleet, Crobe, Sangraal, and [SpySnatcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spy_Snatcher), which started life on the [Phoenix computer system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phoenix_%28computer%29) at the [University of Cambridge Computer Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge_Computer_Laboratory). These are still available on the [IF Archive](https://www.ifarchive.org/indexes/if-archive/phoenix/games/zcode/).
