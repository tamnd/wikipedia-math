# Robert Rosebrugh

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Rosebrugh"}

**Robert Rosebrugh** is a Canadian [category theorist](wikipedia://en/5869) and [database theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Database_theory). He earned his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in mathematics in 1977 at [Dalhousie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dalhousie_University) under the supervision of Robert Paré. He is a professor emeritus at [Mount Allison University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mount_Allison_University). He is a founding editor of the journal Theory and Applications of Categories and served as the managing editor from its founding in 1995 until 2020.[^o3][^o4]

:::

Prior to the journal, in 1990 Rosebrugh created Categories, an internet [bulletin board](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bulletin_board_system) for category theory.[^o3] In 2003, along with [William Lawvere](wikipedia://en/872760), Rosebrugh published a book using [categorical algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Categorical_Algebra) as a [foundation](wikipedia://en/169358) and described the [category of sets](wikipedia://en/26829) for a [structural set theory](wikipedia://en/78527273).[^o5] Rosebrugh connected two theoretical approaches to database [view](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/View_%28SQL%29) updates to lenses, a type of object studied in category theory, in a 2010 paper.[^o6]

The journal Theory and Applications of Categories had a [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) in his honor in 2021[^o3]
