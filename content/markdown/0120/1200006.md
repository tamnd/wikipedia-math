# Elongated pentagonal orthocupolarotunda

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated pentagonal orthocupolarotunda"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated pentagonal orthocupolarotunda** is one of the [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) ($J_{40}$). As the name suggests, it can be constructed by elongating a [pentagonal orthocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1196275) ($J_{32}$) by inserting a [decagonal prism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism) between its halves. Rotating either the [cupola](wikipedia://en/689743) or the [rotunda](wikipedia://en/35582167) through 36 degrees before inserting the prism yields an [elongated pentagonal gyrocupolarotunda](wikipedia://en/1200004) ($J_{41}$).

:::

## Formulae {#Formulae}

The following [formulae](wikipedia://en/164040) for [volume](wikipedia://en/32498) and [surface area](wikipedia://en/27725) can be used if all [faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Faces_%28geometry%29) are [regular](wikipedia://en/333306), with edge length *a*:[^o4]

$$V=\frac{5}{12}\left(11+5\sqrt{5}+6\sqrt{5+2\sqrt{5}}\right)a^3\approx16.936...a^3$$

$$A=\frac{1}{4}\left(60+\sqrt{10\left(190+49\sqrt{5}+21\sqrt{75+30\sqrt{5}}\right)}\right)a^2\approx33.5385...a^2$$
