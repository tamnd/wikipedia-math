# Augmented truncated tetrahedron

*Image: J65 augmented truncated tetrahedron.stl.* 3D model of an augmented truncated tetrahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Augmented truncated tetrahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **augmented truncated tetrahedron** is a polyhedron constructed by attaching a [triangular cupola](wikipedia://en/1128465) onto a [truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/279651). It is an example of a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034). Out of 19 modified [Archimedean solids](wikipedia://en/1847), it is the only one created by augmenting the [truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/279651).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

## Construction {#Construction}

The augmented truncated tetrahedron is constructed from a [truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/279651) by attaching a [triangular cupola](wikipedia://en/1128465). This cupola covers one of the truncated tetrahedron's four hexagonal faces, so that the resulting polyhedron's faces are eight [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), three [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), and three [regular hexagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hexagon). Since it has the property of [convexity](wikipedia://en/6292) and has [regular polygonal](wikipedia://en/333306) faces, the augmented truncated tetrahedron is a [Johnson solid](wikipedia://en/16034), denoted as the sixty-fifth Johnson solid $J_{65}$.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

The surface area of an augmented truncated tetrahedron is:

$$\frac{6 + 13 \sqrt{3}}{2}a^2 \approx 14.258a^2,$$

the sum of the areas of its faces. Its volume can be calculated by slicing it off into both truncated tetrahedron and triangular cupola, and adding their volume:

$$\frac{11 \sqrt{2}}{4}a^3 \approx 3.889a^3.$$

It has the same [three-dimensional symmetry group](wikipedia://en/2694525) as the triangular cupola, the [pyramidal symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pyramidal_symmetry) $C_{3 \mathrm{v}}$. Its [dihedral angles](wikipedia://en/338046) can be obtained by adding the angle of a triangular cupola and an augmented truncated tetrahedron in the following:

- its dihedral angle between triangle and hexagon is as in the truncated tetrahedron: 109.47°;
- its dihedral angle between adjacent hexagons is as in the truncated tetrahedron: 70.53°;
- its dihedral angle between triangle and square is as in the triangular cupola's angle: 125.3°
- its dihedral angle between triangle and square, on the edge where the triangular cupola and truncated tetrahedron are attached, is the sum of both triangular cupola's square-hexagon angle and the truncated tetrahedron's triangle-hexagon angle: approximately 164.17°; and
- its dihedral angle between triangle and hexagon, on the edge where triangular cupola and truncated tetrahedron are attached, is the sum of the dihedral angle of a triangular cupola and truncated tetrahedron between that: approximately 141.3°;
