# Linear range

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Linear range"}

The **linear range** of a function is the portion of its overall range where the input is [directly proportional](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Directly_proportional) to the output.[^o2] The concept of a linear range is important in analytical science where it can apply to measuring equipment or chemical reactions.
