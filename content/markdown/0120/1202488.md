# Prüfer rank

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Prüfer rank"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), especially in the area of [algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) known as [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890), the **Prüfer rank** of a [pro-p group](wikipedia://en/1842075) measures the size of a group in terms of the ranks of its [elementary abelian](wikipedia://en/4843202) [sections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Section_%28group_theory%29).[^o2] The rank is well behaved and helps to define analytic pro-p-groups. The term is named after [Heinz Prüfer](wikipedia://en/1655401).

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The Prüfer rank of [pro-p-group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pro-p-group) $G$ is

$$\sup\{d(H)|H\leq G\}$$

where $d(H)$ is the [rank](wikipedia://en/244541) of the abelian group

$$H/\Phi(H),$$

where $\Phi(H)$ is the [Frattini subgroup](wikipedia://en/540707) of $H$.

As the Frattini subgroup of $H$ can be thought of as the group of non-generating elements of $H$, it can be seen that $d(H)$ will be equal to the *size of any minimal generating set* of $H$.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

Those [profinite groups](wikipedia://en/24266) with finite Prüfer rank are more amenable to analysis.

Specifically in the case of finitely generated [pro-p groups](wikipedia://en/1842075), having finite Prüfer rank is equivalent to having an [open](wikipedia://en/39358) [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) that is [powerful](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Powerful_p-groups). In turn these are precisely the class of [pro-p groups](wikipedia://en/1842075) that are [p-adic](wikipedia://en/51423) analytic – that is groups that can be imbued with a [p-adic](wikipedia://en/51423) [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) structure.

:::
