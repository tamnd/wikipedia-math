# Tom Ilmanen

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tom Ilmanen"}

**Tom Ilmanen** (1961–2025[^o3]) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882). He was a professor at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o4] He obtained his PhD in 1991 at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) with [Lawrence Craig Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Craig_Evans) as supervisor. Ilmanen and [Gerhard Huisken](wikipedia://en/39508202) used [inverse mean curvature flow](wikipedia://en/15710792) to prove[^o6] the [Riemannian Penrose conjecture](wikipedia://en/15523181), which is the fifteenth problem in [Yau](wikipedia://en/303137)'s list of open problems,[^o7] and was resolved at the same time in greater generality by [Hubert Bray](wikipedia://en/47433672) using alternative methods.[^o8]

:::

In their 2001 paper,[^o6] Huisken and Ilmanen made a conjecture on the mathematics of [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024), about the [curvature](wikipedia://en/60770) in spaces with very little [mass](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mass): as the mass of the space shrinks to zero, the curvature of the space also shrinks to zero. This was proved in 2023 by Conghan Dong and [Antoine Song](wikipedia://en/64457708).[^o9][^o10]

Ilmanen received a [Sloan Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Fellowship) in 1996.[^o15]

He wrote the research monograph *Elliptic Regularization and Partial Regularity for Motion by Mean Curvature*.[^o16]
