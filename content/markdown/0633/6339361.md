# Multicomplex number

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Multicomplex number"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **multicomplex number** systems $\mathbb{C}_n$ are defined inductively as follows: Let C<sub>0</sub> be the [real number](wikipedia://en/20646438) system. For every *n* > 0 let *i*<sub>*n*</sub> be a square root of −1, that is, an [imaginary unit](wikipedia://en/52358). Then $\mathbb{C}_{n+1} = \lbrace z = x + y i_{n+1} : x,y \in \mathbb{C}_n \rbrace$. In the multicomplex number systems one also requires that $i_n i_m = i_m i_n$ ([commutativity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commutativity)). Then $\mathbb{C}_1$ is the [complex number](wikipedia://en/5826) system, $\mathbb{C}_2$ is the [bicomplex number](wikipedia://en/4802961) system, $\mathbb{C}_3$ is the tricomplex number system of [Corrado Segre](wikipedia://en/2422061), and $\mathbb{C}_n$ is the multicomplex number system of order *n*.

:::

Each $\mathbb{C}_n$ forms a [Banach algebra](wikipedia://en/4665). [G. Bayley Price](wikipedia://en/38454090) has written about the function theory of multicomplex systems, providing details for the bicomplex system $\mathbb{C}_2 .$

The multicomplex number systems are not to be confused with *Clifford numbers* (elements of a [Clifford algebra](wikipedia://en/45305)), since Clifford's square roots of −1 anti-commute ($i_n i_m + i_m i_n = 0$ when *m* ≠ *n* for Clifford).

Because the multicomplex numbers have several square roots of –1 that commute, they also have [zero divisors](wikipedia://en/51441): $(i_n - i_m)(i_n + i_m) = i_n^2 - i_m^2 = 0$ despite $i_n - i_m \neq 0$ and $i_n + i_m \neq 0$, and $(i_n i_m - 1)(i_n i_m + 1) = i_n^2 i_m^2 - 1 = 0$ despite $i_n i_m \neq 1$ and $i_n i_m \neq -1$. Any product $i_n i_m$ of two distinct multicomplex units behaves as the $j$ of the [split-complex numbers](wikipedia://en/893559), and therefore the multicomplex numbers contain a number of copies of the split-complex number plane.

With respect to [subalgebra](wikipedia://en/45239) $\mathbb{C}_k$, *k* = 0, 1, ..., *n* − 1, the multicomplex system $\mathbb{C}_n$ is of [dimension](wikipedia://en/8398) 2<sup>*n* − *k*</sup> over $\mathbb{C}_k .$
