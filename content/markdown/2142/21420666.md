# The Unscrambler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="The Unscrambler"}

**The Unscrambler X** is a commercial [software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software) product for [multivariate data analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_data_analysis), used for [calibration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Calibration) of multivariate data which is often in the application of analytical data such as [near infrared spectroscopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Near_infrared_spectroscopy) and [Raman spectroscopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raman_spectroscopy), and development of predictive models for use in real-time [spectroscopic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectroscopic_analysis) of materials. The software was originally developed in 1986 by [Harald Martens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harald_Martens)[^o3] and later by [CAMO Software](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAMO_Software). After the acquisition of CAMO by [Aspen Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aspen_Technology) in 2020, Unscrambler is now owned by Aspen.

:::

## Functionality {#Functionality}

The Unscrambler X was an early adaptation of the use of [partial least squares](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_least_squares) (PLS).[^o5] Other techniques supported include [principal component analysis](wikipedia://en/76340) (PCA),[^o6] 3-way PLS, [multivariate curve resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_curve_resolution), [design of experiments](wikipedia://en/9541), [supervised classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supervised_classification), [unsupervised classification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unsupervised_classification) and [cluster analysis](wikipedia://en/669675).[^o7]

The software is used in spectroscopy (IR, NIR, Raman, etc.), chromatography, and process applications in research and non-destructive [quality control](wikipedia://en/41613) systems in pharmaceutical manufacturing,[^o8][^o9] [sensory analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sensory_analysis)[^o10][^o11] and the [chemical industry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemical_industry).[^o12][^o13]
