::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 5-cell"}

*Image: Rectified 5-cell 10 3 configuration.gif.* The 10 vertices, and 10 of the triangle faces represent a self dual symmetric [Desargues configuration](wikipedia://en/7209841), (10<sub>3</sub>), seen here perspective projected into 3-dimensions, with 2 colors of vertices and triangles within the projected tetrahedral symmetry. It has the full 120 automorphisms of the rectified 5-cell.

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **[rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821)** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) composed of 5 regular tetrahedral and 5 regular octahedral [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29). Each edge has one tetrahedron and two octahedra. Each vertex has two tetrahedra and three octahedra. In total it has 30 triangle faces, 30 edges, and 10 vertices. Each vertex is surrounded by 3 octahedra and 2 tetrahedra; the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584).

:::

Topologically, under its highest symmetry, \[3,3,3\], there is only one geometrical form, containing 5 regular tetrahedra and 5 rectified tetrahedra (which is geometrically the same as a regular octahedron). It is also topologically identical to a tetrahedron-octahedron segmentochoron.

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the *rectified 5-cell* is a uniform [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584), formed by three [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) around the sides, and two [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) on the opposite ends.[^o9]

Despite having the same number of vertices as cells (10) and the same number of edges as faces (30), the rectified 5-cell is not self-dual because the vertex figure (a uniform triangular prism) is not a dual of the polychoron's cells.

## Structure {#Structure}

Together with the simplex and [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), this shape and its [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) (a polytope with ten vertices and ten [triangular bipyramid](wikipedia://en/646933) facets) was one of the first 2-simple 2-simplicial 4-polytopes known. This means that all of its two-dimensional faces, and all of the two-dimensional faces of its dual, are triangles. In 1997, Tom Braden found another dual pair of examples, by gluing two rectified 5-cells together; since then, infinitely many 2-simple 2-simplicial polytopes have been constructed.[^o14][^o15]

## Semiregular polytope {#Semiregular_polytope}

It is one of three [semiregular 4-polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_4-polytopes#Semiregular_polytopes) made of two or more cells which are [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905), discovered by [Thorold Gosset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorold_Gosset) in his 1900 paper. He called it a *tetroctahedric* for being made of [tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/30606) and [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) cells.[^o17]

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as tC<sub>5</sub>.

More simply, the vertices of the *rectified 5-cell* can be positioned on a [hyperplane](wikipedia://en/99862) in 5-space as permutations of (0,0,0,1,1) *or* (0,0,1,1,1). These construction can be seen as positive [orthant](wikipedia://en/1283725) facets of the [rectified pentacross](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_pentacross) or [birectified penteract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birectified_penteract) respectively.

## Related 4-polytopes {#Related_4-polytopes}

The rectified 5-cell is the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232), and the [edge figure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edge_figure) of the uniform [2<sub>21</sub> polytope](wikipedia://en/19232473).

### Compound of the rectified 5-cell and its dual {#Compound_of_the_rectified_5-cell_and_its_dual}

The convex hull the rectified 5-cell and its dual (of the same long radius) is a nonuniform polychoron composed of 30 cells: 10 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606), 20 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) (as triangular antiprisms), and 20 vertices. Its vertex figure is a [triangular bifrustum](wikipedia://en/3522824).

### Pentachoron polytopes {#Pentachoron_polytopes}

The rectified 5-cell is one of 9 [Uniform 4-polytopes](wikipedia://en/1645899) constructed from the \[3,3,3\] [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004).

### Semiregular polytopes {#Semiregular_polytopes}

The rectified 5-cell is second in a dimensional series of [semiregular polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_k21_polytope). Each progressive [uniform polytope](wikipedia://en/6842392) is constructed as the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the previous polytope. [Thorold Gosset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorold_Gosset) identified this series in 1900 as containing all [regular polytope](wikipedia://en/518693) facets, containing all [simplexes](wikipedia://en/39781) and [orthoplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex) ([tetrahedrons](wikipedia://en/30606) and [octahedrons](wikipedia://en/80177635) in the case of the rectified 5-cell). The [Coxeter symbol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_symbol) for the rectified 5-cell is 0<sub>21</sub>.
