::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 600-cell"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **[rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447)** or **rectified hexacosichoron** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) composed of 600 regular octahedra and 120 icosahedra [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29). Each edge has two octahedra and one icosahedron. Each vertex has five octahedra and two icosahedra. In total it has 3600 triangle faces, 3600 edges, and 720 vertices.

:::

Containing the cell [realms](wikipedia://en/99862#Notes) of both the regular [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358) and the regular [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447), it can be considered analogous to the polyhedron [icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023), which is a rectified [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) and rectified [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the rectified 600-cell is a uniform [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659).

## Semiregular polytope {#Semiregular_polytope}

It is one of three [semiregular 4-polytopes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semiregular_4-polytopes#Semiregular_polytopes) made of two or more cells which are [Platonic solids](wikipedia://en/23905), discovered by [Thorold Gosset](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thorold_Gosset) in his 1900 paper. He called it a *octicosahedric* for being made of [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635) and [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084) cells.

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope, labeling it as tC<sub>600</sub>.

A related [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) polytope can be constructed with equal edge lengths removes 120 vertices from the rectified 600-cell, but isn't uniform because it contains [square pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128455) cells,[^o16] discovered by George Olshevsky, calling it a *swirlprismatodiminished rectified hexacosichoron*, with 840 cells (600 square pyramids, 120 pentagonal prisms, and 120 pentagonal antiprisms), 2640 faces (1800 triangles, 600 square, and 240 pentagons), 2400 edges, and 600 vertices. It has a chiral *bi-diminished [pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659)* [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).

Each removed vertex creates a pentagonal prism cell, and diminishes two neighboring icosahedra into pentagonal antiprisms, and each octahedron into a square pyramid.

This polytope can be partitioned into 12 rings of alternating 10 pentagonal prisms and 10 antiprisms, and 30 rings of square pyramids.
