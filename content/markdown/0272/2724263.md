# Swain equation

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Swain equation"}

The **Swain equation** relates the [kinetic isotope effect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_isotope_effect) for the [protium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen-1)/[tritium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tritium) combination with that of the [protium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrogen-1)/[deuterium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deuterium) combination according to:

:::

$$\frac{k_H}{k_T} = (\frac{k_H}{k_D})^{1.442}$$

where k<sub>H,D,T</sub> are the [reaction rate constants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reaction_rate_constant) for the protonated, deuterated and tritiated reactants respectively.
