# Cross's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cross's theorem"}

*Image: Vecten-Figur erweitert.svg.* All of the red triangles have the same area.

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), **Cross's theorem**, also known as **Vecten's theorem**, [equates](wikipedia://en/90446) the [area](wikipedia://en/1209) of a [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) to the area of each of the triangles formed by [squares](wikipedia://en/659939) drawn along its [sides](wikipedia://en/12158034).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Theorem"}

## Theorem {#Theorem}

Let $\triangle ABC$ be a triangle in the [Euclidean plane](wikipedia://en/15575410). Suppose squares $DABE$, $FBCG$, and $HCAI$ are drawn on the outside of $\triangle ABC$. Then the areas of the four triangles $\triangle ABC$, $\triangle AID$, $\triangle BEF$, and $\triangle CGH$ are equal.[^o4][^o5]

:::

::: {#o7 .proof cue=heading name="Proofs"}

## Proofs {#Proofs}

*Image: Vecten Flankendreiecke Beweisfigur 3.svg.* After the outer triangles are rotated, each has an equal base and height to the central triangle

::: {#o9 .proof cue=heading name="Proof by rotation"}

### Proof by rotation {#Proof_by_rotation}

[Rotate](wikipedia://en/39789) $\triangle AID$ by a [right angle](wikipedia://en/76956), such that $D$ coincides with $B$, and let this new triangle be $\triangle AI'B$. It is clear that $|AC|=|AI'|$, and that $C$, $A$, and $I$ are [collinear](wikipedia://en/3189581). Therefore, the areas of triangles $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle AI'B$ are equal. Since $\triangle AI'B$ is simply a rotation of $\triangle AID$, it follows that $\triangle ABC$ and $\triangle AID$ have the same area. Similar arguments prove the equality of all four areas.

*Image: Vecten Flankendreiecke Beweisfigur 2.svg.* Angles of the same color are supplementary.

:::

::: {#o11 .proof cue=heading name="Proof by formula for area"}

### Proof by formula for area {#Proof_by_formula_for_area}

Observe that $\angle CAB$ and $\angle DAI$ are [supplementary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supplementary_angles). Therefore, we have

> $$\begin{aligned}[] [DAI] &= \frac12|AD||AI|\sin(\angle DAI) \\ &=\frac12|AB||AC|\sin(\angle CAB) \\ &= [ABC], \end{aligned}$$

as desired. Similar arguments show all four areas are equal.

:::

:::

## History {#History}

*Image: Point de Vecten.svg.* Construction of the outer Vecten point.

The theorem is named after David Cross, who discovered it around 2004.[^o4][^o13] This configuration was also studied independently by Vecten, and consequently the theorem may also be called Vecten's theorem.[^o4] However, the name "Vecten's theorem" is more commonly used for the theorem stating the existence of the [Vecten points](wikipedia://en/44019858) of a triangle.[^o14]
