::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="B8 polytope"}

In 8-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 255 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821715) with [B<sub>8</sub> symmetry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B8_symmetry) (to which this article adds for illustration the [8-demicube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-demicube) as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) with half symmetry). There are two regular forms, the [8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-orthoplex) and [8-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cube), with 16 and 256 vertices respectively.

:::

They can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the B<sub>8</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 256 polytopes can be made in the B<sub>8</sub>, B<sub>7</sub>, B<sub>6</sub>, B<sub>5</sub>, B<sub>4</sub>, B<sub>3</sub>, B<sub>2</sub>, A<sub>7</sub>, A<sub>5</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>k</sub> has *\[k+1\]* symmetry, and B<sub>k</sub> has *\[2k\]* symmetry.

Each of these 256 polytopes is shown in these 10 symmetry planes, with vertices and edges drawn, and vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position in progressive order: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, magenta, red-violet.
