::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="A8 polytope"}

In 8-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 135 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821715) with A<sub>8</sub> symmetry. There is one self-dual regular form, the [8-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-simplex) with 9 vertices.

:::

Each can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the A<sub>8</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 135 polytopes can be made in the A<sub>8</sub>, A<sub>7</sub>, A<sub>6</sub>, A<sub>5</sub>, A<sub>4</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, A<sub>2</sub> [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>*k*</sub> has \[*k*+1\] symmetry.

Each of these 135 polytopes is shown in these 7 symmetry planes, with vertices and edges drawn, and vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position in progressive order: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, magenta, red-violet.
