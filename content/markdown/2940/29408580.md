::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="B7 polytope"}

In 7-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 128 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821719) with B<sub>7</sub> symmetry. There are two regular forms, the [7-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-orthoplex), and [8-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cube) with 14 and 128 vertices respectively. The 7-demicube is added with half of the symmetry.

:::

They can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the B<sub>7</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 128 polytopes can be made in the B<sub>7</sub>, B<sub>6</sub>, B<sub>5</sub>, B<sub>4</sub>, B<sub>3</sub>, B<sub>2</sub>, A<sub>5</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>k</sub> has *\[k+1\]* symmetry, and B<sub>k</sub> has *\[2k\]* symmetry.

These 128 polytopes are each shown in these 8 symmetry planes, with vertices and edges drawn, and vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position in progressive order: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, magenta, red-violet.
