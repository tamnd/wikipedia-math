::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="A7 polytope"}

In 7-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), there are 71 [uniform polytopes](wikipedia://en/6821719) with A<sub>7</sub> symmetry. There is one self-dual regular form, the [7-simplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-simplex) with 8 vertices.

:::

Each can be visualized as symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) in [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane) of the A<sub>7</sub> Coxeter group, and other subgroups.

## Graphs {#Graphs}

Symmetric [orthographic projections](wikipedia://en/203052) of these 71 polytopes can be made in the A<sub>7</sub>, A<sub>6</sub>, A<sub>5</sub>, A<sub>4</sub>, A<sub>3</sub>, A<sub>2</sub> [Coxeter planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_plane). A<sub>k</sub> has *\[k+1\]* symmetry. For even *k* and symmetrically ringed-diagrams, symmetry doubles to *\[2(k+1)\]*.

These 71 polytopes are each shown in these 6 symmetry planes, with vertices and edges drawn, and vertices colored by the number of overlapping vertices in each projective position in progressive order: red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, purple, magenta, red-violet.
