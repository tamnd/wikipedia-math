# Continuous q-Jacobi polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Continuous q-Jacobi polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **continuous *q*-Jacobi polynomials** *P*${}^{(\alpha,\beta)}_{n}$(*x*|*q*), introduced by (Askey Wilson 1985), are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by

$$P_n^{(\alpha,\beta)}(x;q)=\frac{(q^{n+1};q)_n}{(q;q)_n}{}_4\phi_3\left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n},q^{n+\alpha+\beta+1},q^{\frac12\alpha+\frac14e^{i\theta}},q^{\frac12\alpha+\frac14e^{-i\theta}}\\ q^{n+1},-q^{\frac12(\alpha+\beta+1)},-q^{\frac12(\alpha+\beta+2)}\end{matrix} ;q,q\right]\qquad x=\cos\,\theta.$$

:::
