# Little q-Laguerre polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Little q-Laguerre polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **little *q*-Laguerre polynomials** *p*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*a*|*q*) or **Wall polynomials** *W*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*; *b*,*q*) are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081) closely related to a continued fraction studied by (txt). (The term "Wall polynomial" is also used for an unrelated [Wall polynomial](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wall_polynomial) in the theory of classical groups.) (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by

$$\displaystyle p_n(x;a|q) = {}_2\phi_1(q^{-n},0;aq;q,qx) = \frac{1}{(a^{-1}q^{-n};q)_n}{}_2\phi_0(q^{-n},x^{-1};;q,x/a)$$

:::
