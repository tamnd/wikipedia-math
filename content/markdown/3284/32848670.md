# Affine q-Krawtchouk polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Affine q-Krawtchouk polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **affine *q*-Krawtchouk polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081), introduced by Carlitz and Hodges. (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by [^o5]

$$K^{\text{aff}}_n (q^{-x};p;N;q) = {}_3\phi_2\left( \begin{matrix} q^{-n},0,q^{-x}\\ pq,q^{-N}\end{matrix};q,q\right), \qquad n=0,1,2,\ldots, N.$$

:::

## Relation to other polynomials {#Relation_to_other_polynomials}

affine q-Krawtchouk polynomials → [little q-Laguerre polynomials](wikipedia://en/32848683)：

$$\lim_{a \to 1}=K_n^\text{aff}(q^{x-N};p,N\mid q)=p_n(q^x;p,q).$$
