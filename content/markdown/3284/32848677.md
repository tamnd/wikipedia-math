# Continuous q-Laguerre polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Continuous q-Laguerre polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **continuous *q*-Laguerre polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by [^o5]。

$P_{n}^{(\alpha)}(x|q)=\frac{(q^{\alpha+1};q)_{n}}{(q;q)_{n}}$$_{3}\phi_{2}(q^{-n},q^{\alpha/2+1/4}e^{i\theta},q^{\alpha/2+1/4}e^{-i\theta};q^{\alpha+1},0|q,q)$

:::
