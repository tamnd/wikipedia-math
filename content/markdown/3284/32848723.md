# Q-Laguerre polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Q-Laguerre polynomials"}

In mathematics, the ***q*-Laguerre polynomials**, or **generalized Stieltjes–Wigert polynomials** *P*${}^{(\alpha)}_{n}$(*x*;*q*) are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081) introduced by (Moak 1981). (Koekoek Lesky Swarttouw 2010) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The *q*-Laguerre polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by

$$\displaystyle L_n^{(\alpha)}(x;q) = \frac{(q^{\alpha+1};q)_n}{(q;q)_n} {}_1\phi_1(q^{-n};q^{\alpha+1};q,-q^{n+\alpha+1}x).$$

:::

## Orthogonality {#Orthogonality}

Orthogonality is defined by the unimono nature of the polynomials' convergence at boundaries in integral form.
