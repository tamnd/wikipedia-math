# Q-Meixner–Pollaczek polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Q-Meixner–Pollaczek polynomials"}

In mathematics, the ***q*-Meixner–Pollaczek polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by ：[^o5]

$$P_{n}(x;a\mid q) = a^{-n} e^{in\phi} \frac{(a^2;q)_n}{(q;q)_n}{}_3\phi_2(q^{-n}, ae^{i(\theta+2\phi)}, ae^{-i\theta}; a^2, 0 \mid q; q),\quad x=\cos(\theta+\phi).$$

:::
