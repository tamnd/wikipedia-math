# Little q-Jacobi polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Little q-Jacobi polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **little *q*-Jacobi polynomials** *p*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*a*,*b*;*q*) are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081), introduced by (Hahn 1949).[^o3] (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o7 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The little *q*-Jacobi polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$\displaystyle p_n(x;a,b;q) = {}_2\phi_1(q^{-n},abq^{n+1};aq;q,xq)$$

:::
