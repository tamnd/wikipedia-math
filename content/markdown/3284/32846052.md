# Big q-Jacobi polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Big q-Jacobi polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **big *q*-Jacobi polynomials** *P*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*a*,*b*,*c*;*q*) are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081).[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$\displaystyle P_n(x;a,b,c;q)={}_3\phi_2(q^{-n},abq^{n+1},x;aq,cq;q,q)$$

:::
