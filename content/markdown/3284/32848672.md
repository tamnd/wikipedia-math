# Dual q-Krawtchouk polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dual q-Krawtchouk polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **dual *q*-Krawtchouk polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (txt, 14)[^o3] give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$K_n(\lambda(x);c,N|q)={}_3\phi_2(q^{-n},q^{-x},cq^{x-N};q^{-N},0|q;q),\quad n=0,1,2,...,N,$$

where $\lambda(x)=q^{-x}+cq^{x-N}.$

:::
