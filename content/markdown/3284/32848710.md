# Q-Meixner polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Q-Meixner polynomials"}

In mathematics, the ***q*-Meixner polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$M_n(q^{-x};b,c;q)={}_2\phi_1\left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n},q^{-x}\\ bq\end{matrix} ;q,-\frac{q^{n+1}}{c}\right].$$

:::
