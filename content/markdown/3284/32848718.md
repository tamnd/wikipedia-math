# Q-Krawtchouk polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Q-Krawtchouk polynomials"}

In mathematics, the ***q*-Krawtchouk polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081) (txt, 14). give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

(Stanton 1981) showed that the *q*-Krawtchouk polynomials are spherical functions for 3 different [Chevalley groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chevalley_group) over finite fields, and (Koornwinder Wong Koekoek Swarttouw 2010–2022) showed that they are related to representations of the quantum group SU(2).

::: {#o6 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$K_n(q^{-x};p,N;q)={}_3\phi_2\left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n},q^{-x},-pq^n\\ q^{-N},0\end{matrix} ;q,q\right],\quad n=0,1,2,...,N.$$

:::
