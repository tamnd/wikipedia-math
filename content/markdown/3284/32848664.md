# Big q-Laguerre polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Big q-Laguerre polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **big *q*-Laguerre polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (txt, 14) have given a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [q-Pochhammer symbol](wikipedia://en/11149717) by

$$P_n(x;a,b;q)=\frac{1}{(b^{-1}q^{-n};q)_n}{}_2\phi_1\left(q^{-n},aqx^{-1};aq;q,\frac{x}{b}\right)$$

:::
