# Continuous q-Hermite polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Continuous q-Hermite polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **continuous *q*-Hermite polynomials** are a family of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081). (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties.

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) by

$$H_n(x|q)=e^{in\theta}{}_2\phi_0\left[\begin{matrix} q^{-n},0\\ -\end{matrix} ;q,q^n e^{-2i\theta}\right],\quad x=\cos\,\theta.$$

:::

## Recurrence and difference relations {#Recurrence_and_difference_relations}

$$2x H_n(x\mid q) = H_{n+1} (x\mid q) + (1-q^n) H_{n-1} (x\mid q)$$

with the initial conditions

$$H_0 (x\mid q) =1, H_{-1} (x\mid q) = 0$$

From the above, one can easily calculate:

$$\begin{aligned} H_0 (x\mid q) & = 1 \\ H_1 (x\mid q) & = 2x \\ H_2 (x\mid q) & = 4x^2 - (1-q) \\ H_3 (x\mid q) & = 8x^3 - 2x(2-q-q^2) \\ H_4 (x\mid q) & = 16x^4 - 4x^2(3-q-q^2-q^3) + (1-q-q^3+q^4) \end{aligned}$$

## Generating function {#Generating_function}

$$\sum_{n=0}^\infty H_n(x \mid q) \frac{t^n}{(q;q)_n} = \frac{1} {\left( t e^{i \theta},t e^{-i \theta};q \right)_\infty}$$

where $\textstyle x=\cos \theta$.
