# Discrete q-Hermite polynomials

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Discrete q-Hermite polynomials"}

In mathematics, the **discrete *q*-Hermite polynomials** are two closely related families *h*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*q*) and *ĥ*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*q*) of basic hypergeometric [orthogonal polynomials](wikipedia://en/32811718) in the basic [Askey scheme](wikipedia://en/32649081), introduced by (txt). (txt, 14) give a detailed list of their properties. *h*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*q*) is also called discrete q-Hermite I polynomials and *ĥ*<sub>*n*</sub>(*x*;*q*) is also called discrete q-Hermite II polynomials.

:::

::: {#o5 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The discrete *q*-Hermite polynomials are given in terms of [basic hypergeometric functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basic_hypergeometric_function) and the [Al-Salam–Carlitz polynomials](wikipedia://en/32680463) by

$$\displaystyle h_n(x;q)=q^{\binom{n}{2}}{}_2\phi_1(q^{-n},x^{-1};0;q,-qx) = x^n{}_2\phi_0(q^{-n},q^{-n+1};;q^2,q^{2n-1}/x^2) = U_n^{(-1)}(x;q)$$

$$\displaystyle \hat h_n(x;q)=i^{-n}q^{-\binom{n}{2}}{}_2\phi_0(q^{-n},ix;;q,-q^n) = x^n{}_2\phi_1(q^{-n},q^{-n+1};0;q^2,-q^{2}/x^2) = i^{-n}V_n^{(-1)}(ix;q)$$

and are related by

$$h_n(ix;q^{-1}) = i^n\hat h_n(x;q)$$

:::
