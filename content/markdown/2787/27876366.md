# Ashraf Huseynov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ashraf Huseynov"}

**Ashraf Iskandar oglu Huseynov** (Əşrəf İskəndər oğlu Hüseynov, September 20, 1907, Amirvarli, [Jabrayil District](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jabrayil_District) — August 26, 1981, [Baku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baku)) was an [Azerbaijani](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijani_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) (professor from 1948, member of [Azerbaijan National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Azerbaijan_National_Academy_of_Sciences) from 1962). His area of contributions embraced nonlinear [singular integral equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Singular_integral_equations), [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342) and [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

Huseynov was the first to study the nonlinear [Hilbert problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_problem) as applied to [analytic functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytic_functions).[^o3] He also created the *H<sub>α, β, γ</sub>* [function space](wikipedia://en/340630) and proved some [theorems](wikipedia://en/30977) for nonlinear singular integral equations with [Cauchy kernel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy_kernel) within that space.[^o3] Huseynov held positions in some Azerbaijani scientific institutions.
