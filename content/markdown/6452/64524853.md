# Wei-Ming Ni

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wei-Ming Ni"}

**Wei-Ming Ni** (; born 23 December 1950)[^o3] is a Taiwanese mathematician. He is a Presidential Chair Professor at the [Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong,_Shenzhen) and a professor emeritus at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota). He was previously the director of the Center for PDE at the [East China Normal University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/East_China_Normal_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ni works in the field of elliptic and parabolic [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations).[^o5][^o6] He graduated from [National Taiwan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Taiwan_University) with a [B.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.S.) in mathematics in 1972 and earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1979 under [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725).[^o7]

Ni is an editor-in-chief of the *Journal of Differential Equations*, and was an [ISI Highly Cited Researcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Scientific_Information) in 2002.[^o8][^o9] As said by the journal *Discrete and Continuous Dynamical Systems*: \[Ni\] first became a household name in the PDE community when he published with Gidas and Nirenberg the seminal paper in 1979, “On the symmetry of positive solutions of nonlinear elliptic equations” \[...\] The research and expository work of Professor Ni has influenced the research directions and activities of a large number of mathematicians, many of whom are playing important roles in the field of partial differential equations today.[^o9]
