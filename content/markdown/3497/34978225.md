# Dieudonné's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dieudonné's theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Dieudonné's theorem**, named after [Jean Dieudonné](wikipedia://en/1070084), is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) on when the [Minkowski sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_sum) of [closed sets](wikipedia://en/47279) is closed.

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $X$ be a [locally convex space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex_space) and $A,B \subset X$ nonempty closed [convex sets](wikipedia://en/6292). If either $A$ or $B$ is [locally compact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_compact) and $\operatorname{recc}(A) \cap \operatorname{recc}(B)$ (where $\operatorname{recc}$ gives the [recession cone](wikipedia://en/34978152)) is a [linear subspace](wikipedia://en/56357), then $A - B$ is closed.[^o4][^o5]

:::
