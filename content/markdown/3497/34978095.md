# Benedict Freedman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Benedict Freedman"}

**Benedict Freedman** (December 19, 1919 – February 24, 2012) was an American [novelist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novelist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), the co-author of *[Mrs. Mike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mrs._Mike)* and a professor of mathematics at [Occidental College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Occidental_College) in [Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Angeles).[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

### Upbringing {#Upbringing}

Freedman was born to a Jewish family in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City).[^o5] His father, [David](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Freedman), emigrated to America from [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania). He studied at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) from ages 13 to 16, but dropped out without graduating after the death of his father. He took up his father's profession as a radio writer, and moved to the west coast where he worked for [MGM Studios](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer).[^o2]

### Career {#Career}

#### Writing {#Writing}

Freedman met his wife [Nancy Freedman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy_Freedman) in 1940, when she was trying to break into acting. They married in 1941 despite her poor health; during the war Freedman continued to write, but also worked as an aeronautical engineer for [Hughes Aircraft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hughes_Aircraft). The couple wrote their 1947 novel, *[Mrs. Mike](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mrs._Mike)*, based on the real-life adventures of their friend [Katherine Mary Flannigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Katherine_Mary_Flannigan) who married a [Mountie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Canadian_Mounted_Police) and moved from Boston to the Canadian wilderness. It became a bestseller and inspired a 1950 film adaptation. The two Freedmans wrote nine more novels together, and Freedman also continued to write for the entertainment industry, including credits in 1960 for the television show *[My Favorite Martian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Favorite_Martian)*.[^o2]

#### Mathematics {#Mathematics}

In his 40s, Freedman began studying mathematics. He earned a bachelor's degree and a Ph.D. from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1968 and 1970 respectively; his thesis, on the topic of [intuitionistic logic](wikipedia://en/169262), was supervised by [Yiannis N. Moschovakis](wikipedia://en/2291002). On earning his doctorate, he joined the Occidental faculty, where he also came to head the general studies program. He retired in 1995.[^o2]

### Family {#Family}

Freedman's son, [Michael Freedman](wikipedia://en/658970), is also a noted mathematician, and they have collaborated.[^o11] Freedman's two daughters also work in academia as a musician at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) and as the director of the medical humanities program at the [University of California, Irvine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Irvine).[^o2] He died in 2012 in [Corte Madera, California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corte_Madera,_California).
