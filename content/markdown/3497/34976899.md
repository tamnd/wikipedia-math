# Group contraction

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Group contraction"}

In theoretical physics, [Eugene Wigner](wikipedia://en/10101) and [Erdal İnönü](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdal_İnönü) have discussed[^o2] the possibility to obtain from a given [Lie group](wikipedia://en/17945) a different (non-isomorphic) Lie group by a **group contraction** with respect to a continuous subgroup of it. That amounts to a limiting operation on a parameter of the [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944), altering the [structure constants](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Structure_constant) of this Lie algebra in a nontrivial singular manner, under suitable circumstances.[^o3][^o4]

:::

For example, [the Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/157092) of the [3D rotation group](wikipedia://en/173965) $\operatorname{SO}(3)$, $[X_{1}, X_{2}] = X_{3}$, etc., may be rewritten by a change of variables $Y_{1} = \varepsilon X_{1}$, $Y_{2} = \varepsilon X_{2}$, $Y_{3} = X_{3}$, as

$$[Y_{1}, Y_{2}] = \varepsilon^{2} Y_{3}, [Y_{2}, Y_{3}] = Y_{1}, [Y_{3}, Y_{1}] = Y_{2}.$$

The contraction limit $\varepsilon \to 0$ trivializes the first commutator and thus yields the non-isomorphic algebra of the plane [Euclidean group](wikipedia://en/652164), $E_{2} \sim \operatorname{ISO}(2)$. (This is isomorphic to the cylindrical group, describing motions of a point on the surface of a cylinder. It is the [little group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_group), or [stabilizer subgroup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stabilizer_subgroup), of null [four-vectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-vectors) in [Minkowski space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_space).) Specifically, the translation generators $Y_{1}, Y_{2}$, now generate the Abelian [normal subgroup](wikipedia://en/21918) of $E_{2}$ (cf. [Group extension](wikipedia://en/345807)), the [parabolic Lorentz transformations](wikipedia://en/230489#Parabolic).

Similar limits, of considerable application in physics (cf. [correspondence principles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Correspondence_principle)), contract

- the [de Sitter group](wikipedia://en/301666) $\operatorname{SO}(4, 1) \sim \operatorname{Sp}(2, 2)$ to the [Poincaré group](wikipedia://en/230487) $\operatorname{ISO}(3, 1)$, as the de Sitter radius diverges: $R \to \infty$; or
- the super-[anti-de Sitter](wikipedia://en/301670) algebra to the [super-Poincaré algebra](wikipedia://en/1057955) as the AdS radius diverges $R \to \infty$; or
- the [Poincaré group](wikipedia://en/230487) to the [Galilei group](wikipedia://en/295183#Galilean_group), as the [speed of light](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speed_of_light) diverges: *c* → ∞;[^o5] or
- the [Moyal bracket](wikipedia://en/8875186) Lie algebra (equivalent to quantum commutators) to the [Poisson bracket](wikipedia://en/294340) Lie algebra, in the [classical limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classical_limit) as the [Planck constant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planck_constant) vanishes: *ħ* → 0.
