# Conway algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Conway algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Conway algebra**, introduced by (txt, 115–139) and named after [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807), is an [algebraic](wikipedia://en/19616384) structure with two [binary operations](wikipedia://en/3948) | and \* and an [infinite number](wikipedia://en/142679) of [constants](wikipedia://en/24758132) *a*<sub>1</sub>, *a*<sub>2</sub>,..., satisfying certain [identities](wikipedia://en/298428). Conway algebras can be used to construct invariants of [links](wikipedia://en/1161784) that are [skein invariant](wikipedia://en/314902).

:::
