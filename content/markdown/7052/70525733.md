# Opinion polling for the 2023 Dutch general election

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Opinion polling for the 2023 Dutch general election"}

In the run-up to the [2023 Dutch general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2023_Dutch_general_election), various organisations carried out [opinion polling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opinion_polling) to gauge voting intentions in the [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands). Results of such polls are displayed in this list.

:::

The date range for these opinion polls are from the [2021 Dutch general election](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2021_Dutch_general_election), held on 15–17 March, to election day. The next election was scheduled for March 2025, but, due to the fall of the [fourth Rutte cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fourth_Rutte_cabinet), took place on 22 November 2023.[^o2][^o3] Four main pollsters performed opinion polls on voting intentions in the Netherlands, these being: I&O Research, Ipsos, Kantar and Peil.nl. Peil.nl does not give percentages, only showing seats while the other pollsters do include percentages.
