# David R. Brillinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David R. Brillinger"}

**David Ross Brillinger** (born 1937)[^o5] is a statistician and [Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) Professor of [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o5] He received his PhD from Princeton in 1961 under [John Tukey](wikipedia://en/415974).[^o8] Brillinger's former doctoral students include [Peter Guttorp](wikipedia://en/68019056),[^o8] [Ross Ihaka](wikipedia://en/19954367),[^o6] [Rafael Irizarry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rafael_Irizarry_%28scientist%29)[^o7] and [Victor Panaretos](wikipedia://en/66912945).[^o9]

:::
