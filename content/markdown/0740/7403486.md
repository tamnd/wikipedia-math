# Jeff Dinitz

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jeff Dinitz"}

**Jeffrey Howard Dinitz** (born 1952) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who taught [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) at the [University of Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vermont). He is best known for proposing the [Dinitz conjecture](wikipedia://en/920110), which became a major theorem.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Dinitz was born in 1952 in [Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brooklyn), [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29).

## XFL scheduling {#XFL_scheduling}

Dinitz is also well known for scheduling the first season of the now-defunct [XFL](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XFL_%282001%29) football league. He and a colleague from the [Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic), Dalibor Froncek, offered the then-brand-new XFL league their expertise to draft complicated schedules.

The XFL administration quickly agreed, which "surprised" Dinitz greatly. After some time on the computer, Dinitz and Froncek sent the XFL a draft schedule, and the new league gratefully accepted. Although the XFL folded after only one season, Dinitz was happy that "(he and Froncek) got to go to the championship game in Los Angeles".[^o5]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Dinitz is married to Susan Dinitz and has three children.
