# Peter Lancaster

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Lancaster"}

**Peter Lancaster** (born 14 November 1929) is a British–Canadian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He is [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary), where he has worked since 1962. His research focuses on [matrix analysis](wikipedia://en/40956141) and related fields, motivated by problems from [vibration theory](wikipedia://en/20646772), [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [systems theory](wikipedia://en/29238), and [signal processing](wikipedia://en/29324).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Lancaster was born in [Appleby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Appleby-in-Westmorland), [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England), and attended [Sir John Deane's Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sir_John_Deane's_College) and the [Liverpool Collegiate School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Liverpool_Collegiate_School). After an unsuccessful year in the [University of Liverpool School of Architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool_School_of_Architecture), he joined the mathematics program in the same university, graduating with an [honours degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honours_degree) in 1952. Lancaster thereupon worked as an [aerodynamicist](wikipedia://en/2819) with the [English Electric Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_Electric) until 1957, completing a Master's degree at the same time under the supervision of [Louis Rosenhead](wikipedia://en/39261792).

He took a teaching post at the [University of Malaya](wikipedia://en/286880), from which he was granted a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1964, and moved to [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) in November 1962 to work at the [University of Calgary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Calgary). Lancaster held visiting positions at the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology) (1965–66), the [University of Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Basel) (1968–69), and the [University of Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dundee) (1975–76) as well as shorter stays at the Universities of [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster), [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University), [Konstanz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Konstanz), and [Ben-Gurion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ben-Gurion_University_of_the_Negev).

Lancaster served as Department Chairman from 1973 to 1977, and President of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) from 1979 to 1981. He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1984, and was the recipient of the [Jeffery%E2%80%93Williams Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeffery%E2%80%93Williams_Prize) in 1991. Lancaster received a [Humboldt Research Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_Prize) in 2000. In 2018 the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) listed him in their inaugural class of fellows.[^o4]
