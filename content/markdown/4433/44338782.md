# Serre group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Serre group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Serre group** *S* is the pro-algebraic group whose [representations](wikipedia://en/12695) correspond to CM-motives over the [algebraic closure](wikipedia://en/3129) of the rationals, or to polarizable rational [Hodge structures](wikipedia://en/10297305) with abelian [Mumford–Tate groups](wikipedia://en/27137708). It is a [projective limit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_limit) of finite dimensional tori, so in particular is abelian. It was introduced by (txt). It is a subgroup of the [Taniyama group](wikipedia://en/44338719).

:::

There are two different but related groups called the Serre group, one the [connected component of the identity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Connected_component_of_the_identity) in the other. This article is mainly about the connected group, usually called the Serre group but sometimes called the connected Serre group. In addition one can define Serre groups of [algebraic number fields](wikipedia://en/28730822), and the Serre group is the inverse limit of the Serre groups of [number fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field).

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

The Serre group is the projective limit of the Serre groups of *S*<sub>*L*</sub> of finite [Galois extensions](wikipedia://en/457064) of the rationals, and each of these groups *S*<sub>*L*</sub> is a torus, so is determined by its module of characters, a finite free **Z**-module with an action of the finite [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673) Gal(*L*/**Q**). If *L*\* is the algebraic group with *L*\*(*A*) the units of *A*⊗*L*, then *L*\* is a torus with the same dimension as *L*, and its characters can be identified with integral functions on Gal(*L*/**Q**). The Serre group *S*<sub>*L*</sub> is a quotient of this torus *L*\*, so can be described explicitly in terms of the module *X*\*(*S*<sub>*L*</sub>) of rational characters. This module of rational characters can be identified with the integral functions λ on Gal(*L*/**Q**) such that

(σ−1)(ι+1)λ = (ι+1)(σ−1)λ = 0

for all σ in Gal(*L*/**Q**), where ι is complex conjugation. It is acted on by the Galois group.

The full Serre group *S* can be described similarly in terms of its module *X*\*(*S*) of rational characters. This module of rational characters can be identified with the locally constant integral functions λ on Gal($\overline{\mathbf{Q}}$/**Q**) such that

(σ−1)(ι+1)λ = (ι+1)(σ−1)λ = 0

for all σ in Gal($\overline{\mathbf{Q}}$/**Q**), where ι is complex conjugation.

:::
