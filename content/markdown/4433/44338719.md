# Taniyama group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Taniyama group"}

In mathematics, the **Taniyama group** is a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) that is an [extension](wikipedia://en/345807) of the [absolute Galois group](wikipedia://en/2996781) of the [rationals](wikipedia://en/19727024) by the [Serre group](wikipedia://en/44338782). It was introduced by (txt)[^o3] using an observation by [Deligne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deligne), and named after [Yutaka Taniyama](wikipedia://en/1077843). It was intended to be the [group scheme](wikipedia://en/510523) whose representations correspond to the (hypothetical) [CM](wikipedia://en/537183) [motives](wikipedia://en/430680) over the field **Q** of rational numbers.

:::
