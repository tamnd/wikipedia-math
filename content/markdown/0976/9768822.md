# Johann Faulhaber

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johann Faulhaber"}

*Image: Porträt Johannes Faulhaber (1580-1635) Ulm.jpg.* Johannes Faulhaber (1625/1630) with a compass and his book *"Secreta"* from the year 1621.

**Johann Faulhaber** (5 May 1580 – 10 September 1635) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specifically, a calculator (*Rechenmeister*).

:::

*Image: Ioannes-Faulhaberus-Mathematicus-Imperialis-Ulmae-Natus.png.* Ioannes Faulhaberus Mathematicus Imperialis Ulmæ Natus

## Biography {#Biography}

Born in [Ulm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ulm), Faulhaber was a trained weaver who later took the role of a surveyor of the city of Ulm. He collaborated with [Johannes Kepler](wikipedia://en/15736) and [Ludolph van Ceulen](wikipedia://en/362612). In 1620, while in Ulm, [Descartes](wikipedia://en/25525) probably corresponded with Faulhaber to discuss algebraic solutions of polynomial equations.[^o3]

He worked as fortification engineer in various cities (notably [Basel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Basel), where he was fortification engineer from 1622 to 1624, and [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt)), and also worked under [Maurice, Prince of Orange](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maurice,_Prince_of_Orange) in the Netherlands. He also built [water wheels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Water_wheel) in his home town and geometrical instruments for the military.

Faulhaber made the first publication of [Henry Briggs's](wikipedia://en/210399) [Logarithm](wikipedia://en/17860) in Germany. He is also credited with the first printed solution of [equal temperament](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equal_temperament).[^o4] He died in Ulm.

Faulhaber's major contribution was in calculating the [sums of powers of integers](wikipedia://en/12537045); thus the standard formula for such sums is now called [Faulhaber's formula](wikipedia://en/6738151). [Jacob Bernoulli](wikipedia://en/64740) makes references to Faulhaber in his *Ars Conjectandi*.

Other than his mathematical work, he also worked on various mystical matters, such as alchemy, astrology and numerology. He was a member of the [Rosicrucians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosicrucianism). In 1619, after his involvement in the Ulm comet dispute, he published his work *Fama Syderea Nova*, containing prophecies in connection with the [Great Comet of 1618](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Comet_of_1618) and the beginning of the [Thirty Years' War](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thirty_Years'_War). The Ulm comet dispute concerned whether the comets appearing during 1618 were signs sent by God, or just natural phenomena.[^o5][^o6]
