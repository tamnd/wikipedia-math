# High (computability)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="High"}

In [computability theory](wikipedia://en/155414), a [Turing degree](wikipedia://en/764405) \[*X*\] is high if it is computable in 0, and the [Turing jump](wikipedia://en/1681091) \[*X*\] is 0, which is the greatest possible degree in terms of [Turing reducibility](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Turing_reducibility) for the jump of a set which is computable in 0.[^o2]

:::

Similarly, a degree is *high n* if its n'th jump is the (n+1)'st jump of 0. Even more generally, a degree **d** is *generalized high n* if its n'th jump is the n'th jump of the join of **d** with 0.
