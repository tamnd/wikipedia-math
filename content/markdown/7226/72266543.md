# Jan Nekovář

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jan Nekovář"}

**Jan Nekovář** (1963 – 14 November 2022) was a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czech_Republic) academic and mathematician who specialized in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Nekovář first studied at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague) and was an exchange student at [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) from 1984 to 1985. He obtained his doctorate from the [Czechoslovak Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechoslovak_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1991 with a thesis titled *Modulární formy necelé váhy*.[^o5] From 1991 to 1993, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). In 1993, he became an assistant professor at Charles University, where he became a lecturer in 1995. He taught at [Christ's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ's_College,_Cambridge) from 1995 to 2002 and subsequently became a professor at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University).[^o6]

Nekovář was a visiting researcher at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) from 1988 to 1989, at the [Max Planck Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1386960) from 1989 to 1990, at the [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837) in 1998, the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) in 1991, as well as the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota), the [Centre de Recerca Matemàtica](wikipedia://en/21193525), the [Fields Institute](wikipedia://en/2495768), and the [Erwin Schrödinger International Institute for Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erwin_Schrödinger_International_Institute_for_Mathematical_Physics).

Jan Nekovář died in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989) on 14 November 2022, at the age of 59.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

- [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) (1998)
- [G. de B. Robinson Award](wikipedia://en/906055#Awards) (2014)
- Neuron Prize for Important Scientific Discovery (2019)[^o9]

## Publications {#Publications}

- "Class numbers of quadratic fields and Shimura's correspondence." (1990)
- "On p-adic height pairings" (1991)
- *Selmer complexes* (2006)
- "The Euler system method for CM points on Shimura curves" (2007)
- "Eichler-Shimura relations and semisimplicity of étale cohomology of quaternionic Shimura varieties" (2018)
- "Semisimplicity of certain Galois representations occurring in étale cohomology of unitary Shimura varieties" (2019)
