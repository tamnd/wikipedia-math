# Peter J. Freyd

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Peter J. Freyd"}

**Peter John Freyd** (; born February 5, 1936) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), a professor at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania), known for work in [category theory](wikipedia://en/5869) and for founding the [False Memory Syndrome Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/False_Memory_Syndrome_Foundation).

:::

## Mathematics {#Mathematics}

Freyd obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1960; his dissertation, on *Functor Theory*,[^o5] was written under the supervision of [Norman Steenrod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norman_Steenrod) and [David Buchsbaum](wikipedia://en/30781131).

Freyd is best known for his [adjoint functor](wikipedia://en/53991) theorem. He was the author of the foundational book *Abelian Categories: An Introduction to the Theory of Functors* (1964). This work culminates in a proof of the [Freyd–Mitchell embedding theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freyd–Mitchell_embedding_theorem).

In addition, Freyd's name is associated with the [HOMFLYPT polynomial](wikipedia://en/1039260) of knot theory, and he and [Andre Scedrov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre_Scedrov) originated the concept of (mathematical) [allegories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allegory_%28category_theory%29).

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
