# Yuval Flicker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuval Flicker"}

**Yuval Zvi Flicker** (יוּבַל צְבִי פְלִיקֶר; born 1955 in [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel)) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His primary research interests include [automorphic representations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Automorphic_representation).[^o3]

:::

He received his PhD degree from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1978. His thesis advisor was [Alan Baker](wikipedia://en/18950800), in the area of [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698).[^o3][^o4]

He taught at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University), [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University), where he now has the title of Faculty Emeritus.[^o5] He also worked with [David Kazhdan](wikipedia://en/3052833)[^o6] and [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544).[^o3][^o7]

## Education {#Education}

Born 1955 in [Kfar-Saba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kfar-Saba), raised in [Ramat-Gan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ramat-Gan), Flicker studied Mathematics and Philosophy at [Tel-Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel-Aviv_University) gaining a BA in 1973, then he studied Mathematics at the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) gaining an MA in 1974. After that he studied [Part III of the Mathematical Tripos](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Part_III_of_the_Mathematical_Tripos) at [DPMMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DPMMS), [Cambridge University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University) in 1974-75, where he was awarded his PhD under the supervision of [Fields Medalist](wikipedia://en/10859) [Alan Baker](wikipedia://en/18950800) in 1978. His dissertation was "Linear forms on Abelian Varieties over Local Fields". He was a Post Doctoral scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) [Princeton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey) 1978-79, at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) 1979-81, at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) 1981-85, and at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) 1985-87. He worked as a member of the Mathematics Department at the [Ohio State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio_State_University) from 1987 to 2015.

## Research {#Research}

Flicker's research interests include Automorphic and Admissible Representations, Automorphic forms over function fields, Arithmetic Geometry, Lifting of Representations, Hecke-Iwahori algebras, p-adic automorphic forms, Galois Cohomology, Local-Global Principles, Motives, Algebraic Groups, Covering Groups, Shimura Varieties. He coauthored works with [David Kazhdan](wikipedia://en/3052833),[^o6] [Pierre Deligne](wikipedia://en/394544),[^o7] his students[^o10] and other scholars.[^o11] He acknowledges influence of [Joseph Bernstein](wikipedia://en/5815383)[^o12] and of [Vladimir Drinfeld](wikipedia://en/1053747).[^o13] He is the author of several books.

## Dissemination {#Dissemination}

Flicker visited and lectured at the Universities of [Mannheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Mannheim), [Bielefeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bielefeld_University), [Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster), [Essen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Duisburg-Essen), [Köln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cologne), [HU Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin) supported by a [Humboldt Stiftung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Fellow), [DAAD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Academic_Exchange_Service) and SFB; at [MPIM](wikipedia://en/1386960) in Bonn; at [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo); at [TIFR Bombay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tata_Institute_of_Fundamental_Research) (and later TIFR Mumbai); at [University of Santiago, Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Santiago,_Chile); at [University of Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Buenos_Aires) supported by a [Fulbright award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Program); at the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences); at [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880) supported by an NUS Senior Fellowship; at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) supported by a [Lady Davis Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Davis_Fellows) and Schonbrunn Professorship, and [Simons Fellowship](https://www.simonsfoundation.org/); at [IMPA Rio de Janeiro](wikipedia://en/2334708); at [Erzincan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erzincan_University) supported by [TÜBİTAK](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_and_Technological_Research_Council_of_Turkey).

Flicker endorsed [An Open Letter to Richard Riley](http://www.csun.edu/~vcmth00m/riley.html), [United States Secretary of Education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Secretary_of_Education).

## Books {#Books}

Yuval Flicker is the author of a number of books including:

- *Arthur's Invariant Trace Formula and Comparison of Inner Forms* (2016)[^o16]
- *Drinfeld Moduli Schemes and Automorphic Forms* (2013)[^o17]
- *Automorphic Representations of Low Rank Groups* (2006)[^o18]
- *Automorphic Forms and Shimura Varieties of* PGSp(2) (2005)[^o19]
- *Matching of Orbital Integrals on* GL(4) *and* GSp(2) (1999)[^o20]
