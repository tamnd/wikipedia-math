# Holomorphic separability

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Holomorphic separability"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), the concept of **holomorphic separability** is a measure of the richness of the set of [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) on a [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) or [complex-analytic space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex-analytic_space).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

A [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) or [complex space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_analytic_space) $X$ is said to be holomorphically separable, if whenever *x* ≠ *y* are two points in $X$, there exists a [holomorphic function](wikipedia://en/14110) $f \in \mathcal O(X)$, such that *f*(*x*) ≠ *f*(*y*).[^o4]

Often one says the [holomorphic functions](wikipedia://en/14110) *separate points*.

:::

## Usage and examples {#Usage_and_examples}

- All complex manifolds that can be mapped [injectively](wikipedia://en/45196) into some $\mathbb{C}^n$ are holomorphically separable, in particular, all [domains](wikipedia://en/50263) in $\mathbb{C}^n$ and all [Stein manifolds](wikipedia://en/1053909).
- A holomorphically separable complex manifold is not compact unless it is discrete and finite.
- The condition is part of the definition of a [Stein manifold](wikipedia://en/1053909).
