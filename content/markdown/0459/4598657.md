# Ivan Petrovsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ivan Petrovsky"}

Georgiyevich

**Ivan Georgiyevich Petrovsky** (Иван Георгиевич Петровский; 18 January 1901 – 15 January 1973) was a [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working mainly in the field of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564). He greatly contributed to the solution of [Hilbert](wikipedia://en/8302)'s [19th](wikipedia://en/2336226) and [16th](wikipedia://en/381750) problems, and discovered what are now called [Petrovsky lacunas](wikipedia://en/18898515). He also worked on the theories of [boundary value problems](wikipedia://en/436779), [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), and on the [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) of [algebraic curves](wikipedia://en/253260) and [surfaces](wikipedia://en/648311).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Petrovsky was a student of [Dmitri Egorov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Egorov). Among his students were [Olga Ladyzhenskaya](wikipedia://en/1914268), [Evgenii Landis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evgenii_Landis), [Olga Oleinik](wikipedia://en/3033629) and [Sergei Godunov](wikipedia://en/5665800).

Petrovsky taught at [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494). He was a member of the [Academy of Sciences of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Soviet_Union) since 1946 and was awarded [Hero of Socialist Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hero_of_Socialist_Labour) in 1969. He was the president of [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) (1951–1973) and the head of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) ([Moscow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow), 1966).

He died in Moscow, and was buried in the [Novodevichy Cemetery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novodevichy_Cemetery).
