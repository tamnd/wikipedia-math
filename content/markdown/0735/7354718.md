# Guy Terjanian

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guy Terjanian"}

**Guy Terjanian** is a [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who has worked on [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705). He achieved his Ph.D. under [Claude Chevalley](wikipedia://en/366040) in 1970, and at that time published a counterexample[^o3] to the original form of a conjecture of [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351), which suitably modified had just been proved as the [Ax-Kochen theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ax-Kochen_theorem).

:::

In 1977, he proved that if *p* is an odd prime number, and the natural numbers *x*, *y* and *z* satisfy $x^{2p} + y^{2p} = z^{2p}$, then *2p* must divide *x* or *y*.[^o4]
