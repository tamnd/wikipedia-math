# Eva Cantoni

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eva Cantoni"}

**Eva Cantoni** (born 1970) is a Swiss statistician whose research focuses on [robust statistics](wikipedia://en/2885691) and [generalized linear models](wikipedia://en/747122), with applications including [housing market prediction](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_estate_economics), health care, and [livestock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Livestock) production. She is a professor at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva), in the Geneva School of Economics and Management, and president of the [Swiss Federal Statistics Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Statistics_Committee_%28Switzerland%29).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Cantoni was born in 1970, and received a Ph.D. in mathematics in 1999 from the University of Geneva. Her doctoral dissertation, *Resistant Techniques for Nonparametric Regression, Generalized Linear and Additive Models*, was supervised by Elvezio Ronchetti.

She became an associate professor at the University of Geneva in 2011, and full professor in 2019. She was vice-dean of the faculty of economy and management from 2020 to 2023, and became president of the Swiss Federal Statistics Committee in 2024.

## Book {#Book}

Cantoni is a coauthor of the book *Robust Methods in Biostatistics* (with Stephane Heritier, Samuel Copt, and [Maria-Pia Victoria-Feser](wikipedia://en/62386165), Wiley, 2009).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2025, the Geneva School of Economics and Management gave Cantoni their inaugural GSEM Teaching Engagement Award.
