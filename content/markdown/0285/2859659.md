# Pentagonal prism

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Pentagonal prism"}

Prism polyhedron stat table

*Image: Pentagonal prism.stl.* 3D model of a uniform pentagonal prism

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **pentagonal prism** is a [prism](wikipedia://en/283007) with a [pentagonal](wikipedia://en/20741014) base. It is a type of [heptahedron](wikipedia://en/9367140) with seven [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), fifteen [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), and ten [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417).

:::

## As a semiregular (or uniform) polyhedron {#As_a_semiregular_(or_uniform)_polyhedron}

If faces are all regular, the pentagonal prism is a [semiregular polyhedron](wikipedia://en/1548894), more generally, a [uniform polyhedron](wikipedia://en/2537223), and the third in an [infinite set](wikipedia://en/142679) of prisms formed by square sides and two regular polygon caps. It can be seen as a *[truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) [pentagonal hosohedron](wikipedia://en/2316192)*, represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) t{2,5}. Alternately it can be seen as the [Cartesian product](wikipedia://en/24104095) of a regular pentagon and a [line segment](wikipedia://en/22634860), and represented by the product {5}×{}. The [dual](wikipedia://en/8815) of a pentagonal prism is a [pentagonal bipyramid](wikipedia://en/728038).

The [symmetry group](wikipedia://en/28186) of a right pentagonal prism is [*D<sub>5h</sub>*](wikipedia://en/100267) of order 20. The [rotation group](wikipedia://en/2694525#Rotation_groups) is *D<sub>5</sub>* of order 10.

## Volume {#Volume}

The volume, as for all prisms, is the product of the area of the pentagonal base times the height or distance along any edge perpendicular to the base. For a uniform pentagonal prism with edges *h* the formula is

$$\frac{h^3}{4}\sqrt{5(5 + 2\sqrt{5})} \approx 1.72h^3$$

## Use {#Use}

Nonuniform pentagonal prisms called [pentaprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pentaprism) are also used in optics to rotate an image through a [right angle](wikipedia://en/76956) without changing its [chirality](wikipedia://en/32703814).
