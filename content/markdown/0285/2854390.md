# Leray cover

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leray cover"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Leray cover** is a [cover](wikipedia://en/317552) of a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) which allows for easy calculation of its [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060). Such covers are named after [Jean Leray](wikipedia://en/245430).

:::

## Motivation {#Motivation}

[Sheaf cohomology](wikipedia://en/1055357) measures the extent to which a locally exact sequence on a fixed topological space, for instance the [de Rham sequence](wikipedia://en/221530), fails to be globally exact. Its definition, using [derived functors](wikipedia://en/433875), is reasonably natural, if technical. Moreover, important properties, such as the existence of a [long exact sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Long_exact_sequence) in cohomology corresponding to any [short exact sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Short_exact_sequence) of [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466), follow directly from the definition. However, it is virtually impossible to calculate from the definition. On the other hand, [Čech cohomology](wikipedia://en/856614) with respect to an [open cover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_cover) is well-suited to calculation, but of limited usefulness because it depends on the open cover chosen, not only on the sheaves and the space. By taking a direct limit of Čech cohomology over arbitrarily fine covers, we obtain a Čech cohomology theory that does not depend on the open cover chosen.

In reasonable circumstances (for instance, if the topological space is [paracompact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paracompact)), the derived-functor cohomology agrees with this Čech cohomology obtained by direct limits. However, like the derived functor cohomology, this cover-independent Čech cohomology is virtually impossible to calculate from the definition. The Leray condition on an open cover ensures that the cover in question is already "fine enough". The derived functor cohomology agrees with the Čech cohomology with respect to any Leray cover.

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $\mathfrak{U} = \{U_i\}$ be an open cover of the topological space $X$, and $\mathcal{F}$ a sheaf on $X$. We say that $\mathfrak{U}$ is a Leray cover with respect to $\mathcal{F}$ if, for every nonempty finite set $\{i_1, \ldots, i_n\}$ of indices, and for all $k > 0$, we have that $H^k(U_{i_1} \cap \cdots \cap U_{i_n}, \mathcal{F}) = 0$ in the derived functor cohomology.[^n1]

For example, if $X$ is a separated scheme and $\mathcal{F}$ is quasicoherent, then any cover of $X$ by open affine subschemes is a Leray cover.[^n2]

:::
