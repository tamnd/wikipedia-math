# Whitney disk

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Whitney disk"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), given two [submanifolds](wikipedia://en/525095) *A* and *B* of a [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) *X* intersecting in two points *p* and *q*, a **Whitney disc** is a mapping from the two-dimensional [disc](wikipedia://en/329542) *D*, with two marked points, to *X*, such that the two marked points go to *p* and *q*, one boundary arc of *D* goes to *A* and the other to *B*.[^o2]

:::

Their existence and [embeddedness](wikipedia://en/52119) is crucial in proving the [h-cobordism theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H-cobordism_theorem), where it is used to cancel the intersection points; and its failure in low dimensions corresponds to not being able to embed a Whitney disc. [Casson handles](wikipedia://en/7024176) are an important technical tool for constructing the embedded Whitney disc relevant to many results on topological [four-manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-manifold).

[Pseudoholomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoholomorphic) Whitney discs are counted by the differential in [Lagrangian](wikipedia://en/24585634) intersection [Floer homology](wikipedia://en/2672856).
