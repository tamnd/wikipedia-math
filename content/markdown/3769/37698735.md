# Central Bureau of Statistics (Aruba)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Central Bureau of Statistics"}

**The Central Bureau of Statistics** of [Aruba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aruba), is in charge of the collection, processing and publication of statistics and reports to the Minister charged with responsibility for the subject of Statistics.[^o3] It was created mainly to facilitate the development of a statistical system for Aruba which is also a component of the [CARICOM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CARICOM) regional statistical system together with other member countries of this regional institution.

:::

## Legislation {#Legislation}

Statistics Aruba is governed by the [Statistics Act, GT 1991](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistics_Act_inAruba).

## Organisation {#Organisation}

The Central Bureau of Statistics (CBS) is the institution officially assigned with the collection, processing and publication of statistics to be used by policymakers, in practice and for research in different areas. It is a government department that resorts under the jurisdiction of the Ministry of Finance and Economic Affairs.

### Leadership {#Leadership}

The director of Statistics Aruba is the *Chief Statistician of Aruba*. Since 1986, the former directors of the Central Bureau of Statistics Aruba were:

- Enrique Jaccopucci (1986–1994)
- Randolf Lee (1996-2009)
