# Richard Cleve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Cleve"}

**Richard Erwin Cleve** is a Canadian [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) at the [David R. Cheriton School of Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_R._Cheriton_School_of_Computer_Science) at the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo), where he holds the [Institute for Quantum Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Quantum_Computing) Chair in [quantum computing](wikipedia://en/25220), and an associate member of the [Perimeter Institute for Theoretical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perimeter_Institute_for_Theoretical_Physics).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

He obtained his [BMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BMath) and [MMath](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MMath) from the University of Waterloo,[^o5] and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1989 at the [University of Toronto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toronto) under the supervision of [Charles Rackoff](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Rackoff).[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

He was the recipient of the 2008 [CAP-CRM Prize in Theoretical and Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAP-CRM_Prize_in_Theoretical_and_Mathematical_Physics), awarded for "fundamental results in [quantum information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_theory), including the structure of [quantum algorithms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_algorithms) and the foundations of [quantum communication complexity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_communication_complexity)."[^o8] He has authored several highly cited papers in quantum information,[^o9][^o10][^o11] and is one of the creators of the field of quantum communication complexity.[^o8][^o12] He is also one of the founding managing editors of the journal *[Quantum Information & Computation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_Information_&_Computation)*,[^o13] a founding fellow of the Quantum Information Processing program at the [Canadian Institute for Advanced Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Institute_for_Advanced_Research), and a Team Leader at [QuantumWorks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/QuantumWorks).[^o8]
