# Biggs–Smith graph

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Biggs–Smith graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Biggs–Smith graph** is a 3-[regular graph](wikipedia://en/85821) with 102 vertices and 153 edges.

:::

It has chromatic number 3, chromatic index 3, radius 7, diameter 7 and girth 9. It is also a 3-[vertex-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and a 3-[edge-connected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph).

All the [cubic](wikipedia://en/980508) [distance-regular graphs](wikipedia://en/7768943) are known.[^o4] The Biggs–Smith graph is one of the 13 such graphs.

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The automorphism group of the Biggs–Smith graph is a group of order 2448[^o7] isomorphic to the [projective special linear group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projective_special_linear_group) PSL(2,17). It acts transitively on the vertices, on the edges and on the arcs of the graph. Therefore, the Biggs–Smith graph is a [symmetric graph](wikipedia://en/1577896). It has automorphisms that take any vertex to any other vertex and any edge to any other edge. According to the *Foster census*, the Biggs–Smith graph, referenced as F102A, is the only cubic symmetric graph on 102 vertices.[^o8]

The Biggs–Smith graph is also uniquely determined by its [graph spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graph_spectrum), the set of graph eigenvalues of its [adjacency matrix](wikipedia://en/244463).[^o9]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Biggs–Smith graph is : $(x-3) (x-2)^{18} x^{17} (x^2-x-4)^9 (x^3+3 x^2-3)^{16}$.
