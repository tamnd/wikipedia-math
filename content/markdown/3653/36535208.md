# Guido Weiss

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Guido Weiss"}

**Guido Leopold Weiss** (29 December 1928–25 December 2021 in [St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Louis)[^o2]) was an American mathematician, working in analysis, especially Fourier analysis and harmonic analysis.

:::

## Childhood {#Childhood}

Weiss was born in [Trieste](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trieste) Italy into a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Judaism) family. His parents, [Edoardo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edoardo_Weiss) and Vonda Weiss, were both psychiatrists. Weiss was forced out of school at the age of 9, upon the passage of Italy's [Italian Racial Laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Italian_racial_laws), which forbade all Jewish children from attending public school. He attended a Jewish school in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome) until the end of 1939 when his father was sponsored by members of the [Menninger family](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Menninger_Foundation) to emigrate to America. The family settled in [Topeka, Kansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topeka,_Kansas).

## Career {#Career}

Weiss studied at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), where he received in 1951 his master's degree and in 1956 under [Antoni Zygmund](wikipedia://en/362477) his PhD with thesis *On certain classes of function spaces and on the interpolation of sublinear operators*. At [DePaul University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/DePaul_University) he became an instructor in 1955, an assistant professor in 1956, and in 1959 an associate professor. in 1960 he was a visiting professor in [Buenos Aires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Buenos_Aires) and in the same year a postdoc at the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) in Paris. In 1961 Weiss became an associate professor and in 1963 a professor at [Washington University in St. Louis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_University_in_St._Louis), where from 1967 to 1970 he was also chair of the mathematics department. He was a visiting professor at several universities, including the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) (1964–65), the [Université Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Sud) (1970–71), the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) in [Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pisa) (1980), in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) and in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing). In the academic year 1987–88 he was at [MSRI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute) as the organizer of a program in classical analysis.

In 1967 Weiss won the [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize) for his article *Harmonic Analysis*. In 1994 he was given honorary doctorates in [Milan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milan) and [Barcelona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barcelona).[^o7] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8]
