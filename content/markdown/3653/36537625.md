# Cubic pyramid

*Image: Pyramid augmented cube.png.* [Net](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_%28geometry%29)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Cubic pyramid"}

In four-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **cubic pyramid** is bounded by one [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) on the base and 6 [square pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128455) [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29) which meet at the [apex](wikipedia://en/6022079). Since a cube has a circumradius divided by edge length less than one,[^o3] the square pyramids can be made with regular faces by computing the appropriate height.

:::

## Construction and properties {#Construction_and_properties}

A cubic pyramid has nine vertices, twenty edges, and eighteen faces (which include twelve triangles and six squares). It has seven [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29), six are [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455) and one is a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285). By the calculation of [Euler's characteristic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler's_characteristic) for a four-dimensional polytope, the cubic pyramid is $V - E + F - C = 0$; the letter $V$, $E$, $F$, and $C$ designates the number of vertices, edges, faces, and cells of a cubic pyramid.

Exactly eight regular cubic pyramids will fit together around a vertex in four-dimensional space (the apex of each pyramid). This construction yields a [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) with eight cubical bounding cells, surrounding a central vertex with 16 edge-length long radii. The tesseract tessellates four-dimensional space as the [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139). The 4-dimensional content of a unit-edge-length tesseract is 1, so the content of the regular cubic pyramid is 1/8.

The regular [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) has *cubic pyramids* around every vertex. Placing eight cubic pyramids on the cubic bounding cells of a tesseract is Gosset's construction of the 24-cell. Thus, the 24-cell is constructed from exactly 16 cubic pyramids. The 24-cell tessellates 4-dimensional space as the [24-cell honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9481422).

*Image: Octahedral pyramid.png.* Octahedral pyramid, the dual of a cubic pyramid

The dual four-dimensional polytope of a cubic pyramid is an [octahedral pyramid](wikipedia://en/41727746), seen as an [octahedral](wikipedia://en/80177635) base, and eight regular [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) meeting at an apex.

The cubic pyramid can be folded from a three-dimensional [net](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_%28geometry%29) in the form of a non-convex [tetrakis hexahedron](wikipedia://en/722554), obtained by gluing [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455) onto the faces of a cube, and folded along the squares where the pyramids meet the cube.
