# Symlet

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Symlet"}

In [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), **symlet wavelets** are a family of [wavelets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wavelets). They are a modified version of [Daubechies wavelets](wikipedia://en/554121) with increased [symmetry](wikipedia://en/53741).[^o2][^o3][^o4]

:::
