In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **truncated 16-cell honeycomb** (or **cantic tesseractic honeycomb**) is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 4-space. It is constructed by [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) and [truncated 16-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Truncated_16-cell) [facets](wikipedia://en/7186647).
