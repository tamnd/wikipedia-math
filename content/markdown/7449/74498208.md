# Ranjan Roy

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ranjan Roy"}

**Ranjan Roy** (1948 – 2020) was an Indian-born American mathematician. He spent most of his career as a professor at [Beloit College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beloit_College).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Roy received a BS degree from the [Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIT_Kharagpur), an MS in mathematics from the [Indian Institute of Technology Kanpur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIT_Kanpur), and a PhD at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University).

## Career {#Career}

After receiving his PhD, Roy taught at the [University of Kentucky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kentucky) for a short time, then returned to India where he took posts at the [Indian Institute of Technology Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IIT_Delhi) and [Himachal Pradesh University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Himachal_Pradesh_University), and received a fellowship at the [Indian Institute of Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_Institute_of_Advanced_Study). After spending two years at the institute, he joined the Mathematics Institute at Punjab University as a Reader. He returned to the U.S. at [SUNY Plattsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Plattsburgh).

In 1982, Roy joined Beloit College, spending the rest of his career there.[^o4] He became the Ralph C. Huffer Professor of Mathematics and Astronomy, and at the time of his death, he was the chair of the Mathematics and Computer Science Department.[^o5]

## Publications {#Publications}

Roy published many papers on differential equations, fluid mechanics, special functions, Fuchsian groups, and the history of mathematics. He authored three advanced mathematics books: *Sources in the Development of Mathematics* (2011), *Elliptic and Modular Functions from Gauss to Dedekind to Hecke* (2017) and the posthumously published *Series and Products in the Development of Mathematics* (2021) all published by [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press). He is a coauthor of the book *Special Functions* (with [G. E. Andrews](wikipedia://en/3356273) and [R. Askey](wikipedia://en/2836040)), published by Cambridge University Press in 1999.[^o7]

## Awards {#Awards}

Roy earned several recognitions for distinguished mathematics teaching, including the following:[^o7][^o5]

- In 2003, Roy was one of three professors to receive the [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics).[^o9]
- The MAA Carl B. Allendoerfer Award
- The MAA Wisconsin Section teaching award
- Teacher of the Year honors from Beloit College in 1986 & 2000.
