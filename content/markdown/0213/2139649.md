# Moore space (algebraic topology)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moore space"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), a branch of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Moore space** is the name given to a particular type of [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) that is the [homology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homology_theory) analogue of the [Eilenberg–Maclane spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eilenberg–Maclane_space) of [homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/61994831), in the sense that it has only one nonzero homology (rather than homotopy) group.

:::

The study of Moore spaces was initiated by [John Coleman Moore](wikipedia://en/13433862) in 1954.

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Formal definition"}

## Formal definition {#Formal_definition}

Given an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) *G* and an [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) *n* ≥ 1, let *X* be a [CW complex](wikipedia://en/353022) such that

$$H_n(X) \cong G$$

and

$$\tilde{H}_i(X) \cong 0$$

for *i* ≠ *n*, where $H_n(X)$ denotes the *n*-th [singular homology group](wikipedia://en/431041) of *X* and $\tilde{H}_i(X)$ is the *i*-th [reduced homology](wikipedia://en/3110036) group. Then *X* is said to be a **Moore space**. It's also sensible to require (as Moore did) that *X* be simply-connected if *n*>1.[^o4]

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- $S^n$ is a Moore space of $\mathbb{Z}$ for $n\geq 1$.
- $\mathbb{RP}^2$ is a Moore space of $\mathbb{Z}/2\mathbb{Z}$ for $n=1$.
