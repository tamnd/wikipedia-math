# List of geometers

*Image: Oxyrhynchus papyrus with Euclid's Elements.jpg.* One of the oldest surviving fragments of Euclid's *Elements*, found at [Oxyrhynchus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxyrhynchus) and dated to c. 100 AD ([P. Oxy. 29](wikipedia://en/33044326)). The diagram accompanies Book II, Proposition 5.[^o1]

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="List of geometers"}

A **geometer** or **geometrician** is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specializes in [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446).[^o3][^o4]

:::

Some notable geometers and their main fields of work, chronologically listed, are:
