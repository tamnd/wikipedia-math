# Circumscribed circle

*Image: Circumscribed circles.svg.* Four cyclic polygons, each with a circle circumscribed about it

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Circumscribed circle"}

In geometry, a **circumscribed circle** for a set of points is a circle passing through each of them. Such a circle is said to *circumscribe* the points or a polygon formed from them; such a polygon is said to be *inscribed* in the circle.

:::

- [Circumcircle](wikipedia://en/1926432), the circumscribed circle of a triangle, which always exists for a given triangle.[^o2]
- [Cyclic polygon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_polygon), a general polygon that can be circumscribed by a circle. The vertices of this polygon are [concyclic points](wikipedia://en/504404). All triangles are cyclic polygons.
- [Cyclic quadrilateral](wikipedia://en/194131), a special case of a cyclic polygon.
