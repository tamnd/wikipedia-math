# T. O. Engset

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="T. O. Engset"}

*Image: NOR-Engset-photo-OB-F05845b.jpg.* Engset in 1935.

**Tore Olaus Engset** (May 8, 1865 – October, 1943 in [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo)) was a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) mathematician and engineer who did pioneering work in the field of [telephone traffic queuing theory](wikipedia://en/50578).[^o2]

*Image: NOR-Carneles-farm-Stranda.png.* The farm at Engset in [Stranda Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranda_Municipality), where T. O. Engset grew up

*Image: NOR-Engset-and-his-wife.png.* T. O. Engset and his wife Marie Amalie (1856–1927).

:::

Tore Olaus Engset was born in [Stranda Municipality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranda_Municipality) in [Møre og Romsdal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Møre_og_Romsdal), Norway. After he graduated school at the age of 18, Engset was admitted to the telegraph school in [Stavanger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stavanger_%28city%29) in 1883. He received his certificate in a year and took employment. In his spare time he continued his studies, graduating in 1892 but continuing his university studies. Engset received an [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in physics and mathematics in 1894 at [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo), after which he worked at [Televerket](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Televerket_%28Norway%29) as office worker, traffic analyst and from 1921 to 1922 and 1930–35 [director general](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Director-general).[^o3]

He developed the [Engset formula](wikipedia://en/51657713) in 1915, before the breakthroughs of [A. K. Erlang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A._K._Erlang) from 1917. His 1915 manuscript "Om beregningen av vælgere i et automatisk telefonsystem" (1915) was not, however, published until 1918. That work was translated to German as "Die Wahrscheinlichkeitsrechnung zur Bestimmung der Wählerzahl in automatischen Fernsprechämtern", in *[Elektrotechnische Zeitschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elektrotechnische_Zeitschrift)*, Heft 31, 1918. An English translation appeared in [Arne Myskja](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arne_Myskja), "On the Calculation of Switches in an Automatic Telephone System" in *Telektronikk*, 94(2):99-142, 1998.

He also published a work on [nuclear physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuclear_physics) (1927): "Die Bahnen und die Lichtstrahlung der Wasserstoffelektronen. Ergänzende Betrachtungen über Bahnformen und Strahlungsfrequenzen" (3 parts) in *[Annalen der Physik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annalen_der_Physik)*, 82 (1927) 1017; 83 (1927) 903; 84 (1927) 880.
