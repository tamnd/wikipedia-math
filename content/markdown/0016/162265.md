# Chris Freiling

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chris Freiling"}

**Christopher Francis Freiling** is a mathematician responsible for [Freiling's axiom of symmetry](wikipedia://en/162267) in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553).[^o2] He has also made significant contributions to [coding theory](wikipedia://en/321869), in the process establishing connections between that field and [matroid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matroid_theory).[^o3]

:::

Freiling obtained his Ph.D. in 1981 from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) under the supervision of [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985). He is a member of the faculty of the Department of Mathematics at [California State University, San Bernardino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_State_University,_San_Bernardino).[^o5]
