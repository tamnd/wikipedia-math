# Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach"}

**Karl Wilhelm Feuerbach** (30 May 1800 – 12 March 1834) was a German [geometer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geometer) and the son of legal scholar [Paul Johann Anselm Ritter von Feuerbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Johann_Anselm_Ritter_von_Feuerbach), and the brother of philosopher [Ludwig Feuerbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig_Feuerbach). After receiving his doctorate at age 22, he became a professor of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at the [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29) at [Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erlangen). In 1822 he wrote a small book on mathematics noted mainly for a theorem on the [nine-point circle](wikipedia://en/161243), which is now known as [Feuerbach's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feuerbach's_theorem). In 1827 he introduced [homogeneous coordinates](wikipedia://en/243316), independently of [Möbius](wikipedia://en/37875).[^o3]

:::
