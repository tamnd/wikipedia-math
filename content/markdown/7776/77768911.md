# Mikaela Iacobelli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Mikaela Iacobelli"}

**Mikaela Iacobelli** is an Italian mathematician working in kinetic theory and partial differential equations. She has been an Associate Professor of Mathematics at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) since 2023.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Iacobelli earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics in 2009 and a master's degree *cum laude* in mathematics in 2012, both from the [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome).[^o5]

She received a joint Ph.D. from [Sapienza University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) and the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in 2015 under the supervision of Emanuele Caglioti and [François Golse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/François_Golse). Her thesis was titled *Dynamics of large particle systems*.[^o6]

From 2015 to 2017, she was a research associate at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge). She was an assistant professor at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) from 2017 to 2019, and a titular professor at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) from 2019 to 2023. In 2023, she was appointed associate professor at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich).[^o3]

During the 2024–2025 academic year, she was a [Von Neumann Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_Fellow) at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, New Jersey.[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Iacobelli's research lies at the intersection of kinetic theory, plasma physics, statistical mechanics, and nonlinear partial differential equations. Her work studies the mathematical structures underlying singular limits, long-time dynamics, and degenerate problems.[^o9]

Her research topics include [Vlasov-type equations](wikipedia://en/1991528), quasineutral limits, kinetic [Wasserstein distances](wikipedia://en/6704603), [Landau damping](wikipedia://en/698720), magnetic and electromagnetic plasma models, quantization of measures, and ultrafast diffusion.[^o9] Her SNSF-funded project, *Challenges and Breakthroughs in the Mathematics of Plasmas*, focuses on kinetic models and singular limits for magnetized and electromagnetic plasma systems.[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

- 2015: [L'Oréal-UNESCO For Women in Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/L'Oréal-UNESCO_For_Women_in_Science_Awards) (French edition)[^o12]
- 2021: Invited speaker at the [International Congress on Mathematical Physics](wikipedia://en/23897905)[^o13]
- 2024: Awarded an [SNSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_National_Science_Foundation) Starting Grant for the project *Challenges and Breakthroughs in the Mathematics of Plasmas* (2026–2030)[^o14]
- 2025: [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize)[^o15]

## Professional service {#Professional_service}

Iacobelli is a member of the Committee for Women in Mathematics of the [European Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/969470).[^o17] She is also a member of the standing committee of [European Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/58318372).[^o18]
