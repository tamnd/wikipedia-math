# UsefulCharts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="UsefulCharts"}

**UsefulCharts** is a chartmaking company founded by Canadian educator and designer **Matt Baker**. The company produces [infographic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infographic) posters both physically and digitally and has an associated storefront in [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver) and a [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) channel.

:::

## Founder {#Founder}

**Matthew James Baker**[^o5] is a Canadian[^o6] educator and designer[^o7] who was born in 1975.[^o8] In a YouTube video, Baker said that he was born into a family who were members of the [Worldwide Church of God](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace_Communion_International). He described the church as a [cult](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cult) and has since converted to [Progressive Judaism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reform_Judaism).[^o9] In 2003, he cofounded Beacon Hill Academy, a non-profit school in [Nuwara Eliya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuwara_Eliya), [Sri Lanka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sri_Lanka), where he had previously worked as a teacher.[^o8][^o10][^o11] When he moved to [Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vancouver) in 2009, he uploaded around 100 educational charts online covering various topics which earned him money through [Google AdSense](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Google_AdSense) advertisements on his website.[^o8] He later began selling physical posters on [Amazon.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon.com) and was able to rely on income from the UsefulCharts business by 2011.[^o8]

Baker has a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in education,[^o12] with his dissertation titled "Psychological type and atheism: why some people are more likely than others to give up God."[^o8]

## Business {#Business}

The company was started by Matthew Baker on February 2, 2018 with the purpose of turning Matt's hobby of making charts into a business.

### Store {#Store}

The storefront of the company is located at [Commercial Drive, Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commercial_Drive,_Vancouver), where physical posters and books by UsefulCharts are sold.[^o15] Posters can also be purchased online through its website.[^o7]

### YouTube {#YouTube}

The company has an associated [YouTube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/YouTube) channel where Baker narrates over videos presenting educational charts. Baker has said the revenue between physical chart sales and the channel is "about 50/50." In a [Global News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Global_News) segment covering UsefulCharts, a video detailing the [history of Vancouver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_Vancouver) was highlighted.[^o15] It recently reached 2,000,000 subscribers.

### Notable charts {#Notable_charts}

Timeline of Ancient History.png Charts designed by the company which have received media attention include:

- *Evolution of the Alphabet*: a visual timeline of all 26 [glyphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Glyph) in the [English alphabet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_alphabet).[^o7][^o18][^o19][^o20]
- *Writing Systems of the World*: a chart detailing [writing systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writing_system) of various languages.[^o7][^o21]

## Reception {#Reception}

Biblical scholar [Jacob L. Wright](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacob_L._Wright) commented on [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter) in 2024 that Baker was "an extraordinary pedagogue" while sharing a video by UsefulCharts on the [Twelve Tribes of Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twelve_Tribes_of_Israel).[^o28] Mark Wilson, writing for *[Fast Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fast_Company)*, said *Evolution of the Alphabet* "beautifully illustrated" the history of the alphabet.[^o18]
