# Encyclopedia of Statistical Sciences

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Encyclopedia of Statistical Sciences"}

The ***Encyclopedia of Statistical Sciences*** is an encyclopaedia of statistics published by [John Wiley & Sons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Wiley_&_Sons).[^o2][^o3]

:::

The first edition, in nine volumes, was published in 1982; it was edited by [Norman Lloyd Johnson](wikipedia://en/2590640) and [Samuel Kotz](wikipedia://en/26671314). The second edition, in 16 volumes, was published in 2006; the senior editor was Samuel Kotz.
