# Council for the Mathematical Sciences

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Council for the Mathematical Sciences"}

The **Council for the Mathematical Sciences** (**CMS**) is an organisation that represents all types of British mathematicians at a national level. It is not a professional institution, but a collaboration of them.

:::

## History {#History}

It was established in 2001 by the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139), the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) and the [Royal Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/552664) to provide a forum for mathematics.

## Purpose {#Purpose}

- to represent the interests of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) to government, [Research Councils](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UK_Research_Councils) and other public bodies;
- to promote good practice in the [mathematics curriculum](wikipedia://en/326471) and its teaching and learning at all levels and in all sectors of education;
- to respond coherently and effectively to proposals from government and other public bodies which may affect the mathematical community;
- to work with other bodies such as the [Joint Mathematical Council](wikipedia://en/7779526) and [HoDoMS](wikipedia://en/7786083).

## Structure {#Structure}

It is situated off the A4200 in [Russell Square](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Square), next to the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London) in the offices of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097). It is accessed via the [Russell Square tube station](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russell_Square_tube_station) on the Piccadilly Line.
