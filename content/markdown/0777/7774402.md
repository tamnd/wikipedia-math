# Birch–Tate conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Birch–Tate conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically in [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500), the **Birch–Tate conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) proposed by [Bryan John Birch](wikipedia://en/4949339) and [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933) relating the $K_2$ group of a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) to its [Dedekind zeta function](wikipedia://en/1131243).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Formally, the [group](wikipedia://en/19447) $K_2$ of a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field) $F$ is defined as the [center](wikipedia://en/7125) of the [Steinberg group](wikipedia://en/14994781) of the [ring of integers](wikipedia://en/460613) of $F$. $K_2$ is also known as the tame kernel of $F$. The Birch–Tate conjecture relates the [order](wikipedia://en/493527) of this group (its number of elements) to the value of the [Dedekind zeta function](wikipedia://en/1131243) $\zeta_F$.

More specifically, let $F$ be a [totally real number field](wikipedia://en/312853) and let $N$ be the largest [positive integer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Positive_integer) such that the [extension](wikipedia://en/11634) of $F$ by the $N$-th [root of unity](wikipedia://en/171950) has an [elementary abelian](wikipedia://en/4843202) [2-group](wikipedia://en/24664) as its [Galois group](wikipedia://en/12673). Then the conjecture states that

$$\#K_2 = |N\cdot\zeta_F(-1)|.$$

:::

## Status {#Status}

Progress on this conjecture has been made as a consequence of work on [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150), and in particular of the proofs given for the so-called "[main conjecture of Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/22091332)."
