# Evan Siegel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Evan Siegel"}

**Evan Siegel** is a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Evan Siegel received his PhD in Mathematics from the [CUNY Graduate Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CUNY_Graduate_Center) in 2000, his MSc in Mathematics from [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), and his BSc in Mathematics from [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT). He is currently an Associate Professor of Mathematics at [New Jersey City University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey_City_University). In addition to his interest in Mathematics, Siegel is interested in the [history of the Middle East](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Middle_East) and has numerous publications on this topic. Siegel does research in sources in Persian, French, Arabic, Turkish, Russian, German, and Georgian.[^o4]

In 1998-2000 he was an Editorial Board Member of the Journal of Azerbaijani Studies, and in 1994-2002 a Corresponding Secretary of the International Society for Azerbaijani Studies.
