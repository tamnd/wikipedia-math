# Abu al-Jud

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abu al-Jud"}

**Abū al-Jūd Muḥammad b. Aḥmad b. al-Layth** ابو جود محمد بن احمد بن لیث was an [Iranian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iranian_peoples) mathematician who lived during 10th century and was a contemporary of [al-Biruni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Al-Biruni). He used [conics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conic) to solve [quartic](wikipedia://en/308152) and cubic equations, a century before the more famous work of [Omar Khayyam](wikipedia://en/92550),[^o2] although his solution did not deal with all the cases.[^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Not much is known about his life. He seems to have lived in the east of [Khurasan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Khurasan), within [Samanid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Samanid_Empire) territory. [Sa'id al-Andalusi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sa'id_al-Andalusi) claimed that he lived in Valencia (Balansiya) and died in 1014 or 1015, but other sources didn't mention these information. It is likely that he became a scribe after acquiring basic knowledge on mathematics.[^o5]
