# Census in the Dutch East Indies

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Census in the Dutch East Indies"}

**Censuses in the Dutch East Indies** were [censuses](wikipedia://en/6889) in the [Dutch East Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_East_Indies) colony of the Netherlands. The last census was conducted in 1930.

:::

## History {#History}

[Sebastiaan Cornelis Nederburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sebastiaan_Cornelis_Nederburgh), the former Commissioner-General of the [Dutch Cape Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_Cape_Colony), organized the first formal census of [Java](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Java) in 1795.[^n1] Between 1880 and 1905, the [Dutch East Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_East_Indies) conducted partial population counts every five years, with most of the data being limited to Java.[^n2] This was later followed by full censuses in 1920 and 1930. A third full census was planned for 1940 but was cancelled because of [Japanese occupation of the Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_occupation_of_the_Dutch_East_Indies) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).[^n1]
