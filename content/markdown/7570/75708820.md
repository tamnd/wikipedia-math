# Mauricio Resende

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mauricio Resende"}

**Mauricio G. C. Resende** (born July 27, 1955 in Maceió, Brazil) is a Brazilian-American research scientist with contributions to the field of [mathematical optimization](wikipedia://en/52033). He is best known for the development of the metaheuristics [GRASP](wikipedia://en/1804034) (greedy randomized adaptive search procedures),[^o2] and BRKGA (biased random-key genetic algorithms)[^o3] as well as the first successful implementation of [Karmarkar’s interior point algorithm](wikipedia://en/3736667).[^o4]

:::

He published over 180 peer-reviewed papers, the book Optimization by GRASP[^o5] and co-edited five books, including the Handbook of Applied Optimization,[^o6] the Handbook of Optimization in Telecommunications,[^o7] the Handbook of Heuristics,[^o8] and the Handbook of Massive Datasets.[^o9] Additionally, he gave multiple plenary talks[^o10] in international conferences and is on the editorial boards of several scientific journals.

## Education {#Education}

In June 1978, Mauricio G. C. Resende graduated from [PUC-Rio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pontifical_Catholic_University_of_Rio_de_Janeiro) with an [Electrical Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) degree with concentration in [Systems Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_engineering).[^o12] In August 1979, he earned a M.Sc. in [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) at the [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech). Later, in August 1987, he earned a Ph.D. in operations research in at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).[^o13]

## Career {#Career}

Mauricio G. C. Resende is currently an [INFORMS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INFORMS) Fellow,[^o15] holds a permanent member position of [DIMACS](wikipedia://en/3684858)[^o16] at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) and is an affiliate professor at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington).[^o17] Until December 2022, he worked at [Amazon.com](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amazon_%28company%29) as a Principal Research Scientist in the Mathematical Optimization and Planning group.[^o18] Previously, he was Lead Inventive Scientist at [AT&T Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AT&T_Bell_Labs) where he worked for over a quarter century.
