# Alexander Paseau

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Paseau"}

**Alexander Christopher Paseau** is a British philosopher of Greek and Belgian origin. He is professor of mathematical philosophy at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and the Stuart Hampshire Fellow at [Wadham College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wadham_College,_Oxford).[^o3] He specialises in the [philosophy of logic](wikipedia://en/21368888), the [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439), [metaphysics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metaphysics), [epistemology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Epistemology), and the [philosophy of religion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy_of_religion).[^o4]

:::

As of January 2025, he is editor-in-chief of *[The Journal for the Philosophy of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Journal_for_the_Philosophy_of_Mathematics)*[^o5]. He has been an Associate Editor of the journal *[Mind](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_%28journal%29)* and has held Research Fellowships from the [Mind Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_Association) and the [Leverhulme Trust](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leverhulme_Trust).[^o6]

## Research work {#Research_work}

Paseau subscribes to a broadly realist conception of mathematical truth, stating in an interview that "mathematical truth is not tensed. Mathematicians discover mathematical truths; they don’t make them up".[^o8] In the same interview, Paseau also maintains that "inductive reasoning is crucial for mathematical knowledge" and that "we can know a mathematical truth without ever having proved it".

In their book *One True Logic*, Paseau and his co-author Griffiths argue that there is one correct foundational logic and that it is highly [infinitary](https://blogs.kent.ac.uk/thereasoner/files/2022/09/TheReasoner-167.pdf).

Paseau also works on the subtraction argument for metaphysical nihilism. Paseau's view is discussed and defended by [Gonzalo Rodriguez-Pereyra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gonzalo_Rodriguez-Pereyra) in "The Subtraction Arguments for Metaphysical Nihilism: Compared and Defended".[^o9]
