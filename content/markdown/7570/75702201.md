# How to Think Like a Mathematician

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="How to Think Like a Mathematician"}

***How to Think Like a Mathematician: A Companion to Undergraduate Mathematics*** is a book by Kevin Houston, a [senior lecturer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_lecturer) in mathematics at the [University of Leeds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leeds). It was published in 2009 by [Cambridge University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_University_Press).[^o3]

:::

The book targets current and incoming [undergraduate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undergraduate_education) students of mathematics, and focuses on the [mathematical language](wikipedia://en/373299) and techniques required for [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) writing.[^o4][^o5]

## Summary {#Summary}

The book is divided into five chapters:

1. *Study skills for mathematicians*
1. *How to think logically*
1. *Definitions, theorems and proofs*
1. *Techniques of proof*
1. *Mathematics that all mathematicians need*

## Reception {#Reception}

Michele Intermont of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) described the book as a "wonderful resource".[^o5] Franz Lemmermeyer remarked that "the book is clearly written and contains suggestions and exercises from which not only undergraduates, but also highschool students can benefit".[^o8]
