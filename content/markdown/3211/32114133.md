# Alexander Goncharov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alexander Goncharov"}

*Image: Alexander Goncharov.jpg.* Alexander Goncharov

**Alexander B. Goncharov** (born April 7, 1960) is a Soviet American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and the Philip Schuyler Beebe Professor of Mathematics at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). He won the [EMS Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EMS_Prize) in 1992.

:::

Goncharov won a gold medal at the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) in 1976. He attained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) at [Lomonosov Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lomonosov_Moscow_State_University) in 1987, under supervision of [Israel Gelfand](wikipedia://en/364643) with thesis *Generalized conformal structures on manifolds*.[^o2] Goncharov was an Invited Speaker at the 1994 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) and gave a talk *[Polylogarithms](wikipedia://en/482471) in arithmetic and geometry*.

In 2019, Goncharov was appointed the Philip Schuyler Beebe Professor of Mathematics at Yale University,[^o3] as well as the Gretchen and Barry Mazur Chair at the [Institut des hautes études scientifiques](wikipedia://en/14527).[^o4]

[Vesselin Dimitrov](wikipedia://en/81558886) was a PhD student of his.
