# Irwin Kra

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Irwin Kra"}

**Irwin Kra** (born January 5, 1937) is an American mathematician, who works on the [function](wikipedia://en/185427) theory in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Kra studied at [Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_Institute_of_Brooklyn) (bachelor's degree in 1960) and at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University), where he graduated in 1964 and received his doctorate in 1966 under supervision of [Lipman Bers](wikipedia://en/9335972) (Conformal Structure and Algebraic Structure). After that, he was from 1966 to 1968 a [C.L.E. Moore instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.L.E._Moore_instructor) at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) and then at the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook), where he chaired from 1975 to 1981 the Faculty of Mathematics. From 1991 to 1996, there, he was Dean of the Division of Physical Sciences and Mathematics. Since 2004 he has been Professor Emeritus. He was a visiting professor at the [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) in [Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jerusalem) (where he collaborated with [Hershel M. Farkas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hershel_M._Farkas)), the [University of Perugia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Perugia) in [Santiago de Chile](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Santiago_de_Chile), the [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University), the [Fudan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fudan_University) in [Shanghai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai) (1987). From 2004 to 2008 he was Director of Math for America, a private organization that is dedicated to the improvement of college education in mathematics in the United States. He currently lives in New York City. In 2010 he taught at the [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University).

From 1972 to 1973 he was a Guggenheim Fellow. In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]

Kra studies [Riemann surfaces](wikipedia://en/173181) and their moduli spaces ([Teichmüller spaces](wikipedia://en/1835661)) and their connection with [Kleinian groups](wikipedia://en/1362652) and associated automorphic forms and their applications for example in number theory.

With [Bernard Maskit](wikipedia://en/45153921) in 1998, he published the collected works of [Lipman Bers](wikipedia://en/9335972) on function theory.

## Family relations {#Family_relations}

Irwin Kra has three children. He is the father of mathematician [Bryna Kra](wikipedia://en/39522217),[^o6] climate tech venture capitalist Gabriel Kra, and Douglas Kra.
