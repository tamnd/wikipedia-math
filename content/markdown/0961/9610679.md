# Morse–Palais lemma

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Morse–Palais lemma"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Morse–Palais lemma** is a result in the [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882) and theory of [Hilbert spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hilbert_spaces). Roughly speaking, it states that a [smooth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_function) enough [function](wikipedia://en/185427) near a critical point can be expressed as a [quadratic form](wikipedia://en/251478) after a suitable change of coordinates.

:::

The Morse–Palais lemma was originally proved in the finite-dimensional case by the [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Marston Morse](wikipedia://en/856523), using the [Gram–Schmidt orthogonalization process](wikipedia://en/82361). This result plays a crucial role in [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560). The generalization to Hilbert spaces is due to [Richard Palais](wikipedia://en/34779708) and [Stephen Smale](wikipedia://en/40294).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/lemma cue=named-heading name="Statement of the lemma"}

## Statement of the lemma {#Statement_of_the_lemma}

Let $(H, \langle \cdot ,\cdot \rangle)$ be a [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) Hilbert space, and let $U$ be an [open neighbourhood](wikipedia://en/39358) of the origin in $H.$ Let $f : U \to \mathbb{R}$ be a $(k+2)$-times continuously [differentiable function](wikipedia://en/330206) with $k \geq 1;$ that is, $f \in C^{k+2}(U; \mathbb{R}).$ Assume that $f(0) = 0$ and that $0$ is a non-degenerate [critical point](wikipedia://en/1951424) of $f;$ that is, the second derivative $D^2 f(0)$ defines an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) of $H$ with its [continuous dual space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_dual_space) $H^*$ by

$$H \ni x \mapsto \mathrm{D}^2 f(0) (x, -) \in H^*.$$

Then there exists a subneighbourhood $V$ of $0$ in $U,$ a [diffeomorphism](wikipedia://en/8564) $\varphi : V \to V$ that is $C^k$ with $C^k$ inverse, and an [invertible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invertible_function) [symmetric operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symmetric_operator) $A : H \to H,$ such that

$$f(x) = \langle A \varphi(x), \varphi(x) \rangle \quad \text{ for all } x \in V.$$

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/corollary cue=named-heading name="Corollary"}

## Corollary {#Corollary}

Let $f : U \to \mathbb{R}$ be $f \in C^{k+2}$ such that $0$ is a non-degenerate critical point. Then there exists a $C^k$-with-$C^k$-inverse diffeomorphism $\psi : V \to V$ and an [orthogonal decomposition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthogonal_decomposition)

$$H = G \oplus G^{\perp},$$

such that, if one writes

$$\psi (x) = y + z \quad \mbox{ with } y \in G, z \in G^{\perp},$$

then

$$f (\psi(x)) = \langle y, y \rangle - \langle z, z \rangle \quad \text{ for all } x \in V.$$

:::
