# Hausdorff completion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hausdorff completion"}

In [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the **Hausdorff completion** $\widehat{G}$ of a [group](wikipedia://en/19447) *G* with [filtration](wikipedia://en/726014) $G_n$ is the [inverse limit](wikipedia://en/15109) $\varprojlim G/G_n$ of the [discrete group](wikipedia://en/468536) $G/G_n$. A basic example is a [profinite completion](wikipedia://en/24266). The image of the canonical map $G \to \widehat{G}$ is a [Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/13637) [topological group](wikipedia://en/42315) and its [kernel](wikipedia://en/45240) is the intersection of all $G_n$: i.e., the [closure](wikipedia://en/42309) of the identity element. The canonical [homomorphism](wikipedia://en/13564) $\operatorname{gr}(G) \to \operatorname{gr}(\widehat{G})$ is an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828), where $\operatorname{gr}(G)$ is a [graded module associated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Associated_graded_module) to the filtration.

:::

The concept is named after [Felix Hausdorff](wikipedia://en/10989).
