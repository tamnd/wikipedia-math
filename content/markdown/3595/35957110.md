# Mori–Nagata theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mori–Nagata theorem"}

In algebra, the **Mori–Nagata theorem** introduced by (txt) and (txt), states the following: let *A* be a [noetherian](wikipedia://en/56129) [reduced](wikipedia://en/539749) [commutative ring](wikipedia://en/61346) with the [total ring of fractions](wikipedia://en/4276418) *K*. Then the [integral closure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_closure) of *A* in *K* is a [direct product](wikipedia://en/8887) of *r* [Krull domains](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Krull_domain), where *r* is the number of [minimal prime ideals](wikipedia://en/16143832) of *A*.

:::

The theorem is a partial generalization of the [Krull–Akizuki theorem](wikipedia://en/35947857), which concerns a one-dimensional noetherian domain. A consequence of the theorem is that if *R* is a [Nagata ring](wikipedia://en/10092186), then every *R*-subalgebra of finite type is again a Nagata ring (Nishimura 1976).

The Mori–Nagata theorem follows from [Matijevic's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matijevic's_theorem). (McAdam 1990)
