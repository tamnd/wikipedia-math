# Christian Reiher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Reiher"}

**Christian Reiher** (born 19 April 1984 in [Starnberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Starnberg)) is a German mathematician. He is the [fifth most successful participant](wikipedia://en/15712699) in the history of the [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), having won four gold medals in the years 2000 to 2003 and a bronze medal in 1999.

:::

Just after finishing his *[Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)*, he proved [Kemnitz's conjecture](wikipedia://en/22407758), an important problem in the [theory of zero-sums](wikipedia://en/2546047).[^o4] He went on to obtain his [Diplom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplom) in mathematics at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich).

Reiher received his [Dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.) at the [University of Rostock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rostock) under supervision of Hans-Dietrich Gronau in February 2010 (Thesis: *A proof of the theorem according to which every prime number possesses property B*) and works now at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg).[^o6]
