# Jacques Deruyts

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jacques Deruyts"}

**Joseph Gustave Jacques Deruyts** (18 March 1862 – 5 July 1945)[^o2] was a Belgian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known as a pioneer of [group representation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_representation_theory). He is the elder brother of the mathematician François Deruyts.

:::

Deruyts received his doctorate in 1883 from the [University of Liège](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liège) and was appointed there as assistant to Louis Pérard in [experimental physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Experimental_physics). Deruyts joined the academic staff in mathematics and was appointed in 1883 a professor of geometry at the University of Liège, where he remained until retirement as professor emeritus.

He was elected in 1892 as a member of the [Royal Academy of Science, Letters and Fine Arts of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Science,_Letters_and_Fine_Arts_of_Belgium), which has been awarding since 1952 the [Jacques Deruyts Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jacques_Deruyts_Prize).

He published in 1892 a treatise *Essai d'une théorie générale des formes algébriques*[^o3] which was pioneering research in the representation theory of [linear groups](wikipedia://en/3983656) and [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924).[^o4] He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) with talk "Sur la théorie algébrique des formes à séries de *n* variables" in 1920 at [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg).[^o5]
