# Ferdinand Minding

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ferdinand Minding"}

**Ernst Ferdinand Adolf Minding** (Фердинанд Готлибович Миндинг; January 11 – May 13 1885) was a German-Russian mathematician known for his contributions to [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He continued the work of [Carl Friedrich Gauss](wikipedia://en/6125) concerning [differential geometry of surfaces](wikipedia://en/15513875), especially its intrinsic aspects. Minding considered questions of bending of surfaces and proved the invariance of [geodesic curvature](wikipedia://en/483526). He studied [ruled surfaces](wikipedia://en/705158), [developable surfaces](wikipedia://en/2878111) and [surfaces of revolution](wikipedia://en/329549) and determined geodesics on the [pseudosphere](wikipedia://en/60766). Minding's results on the geometry of geodesic triangles on a surface of constant curvature (1840) anticipated [Beltrami](wikipedia://en/543760)'s approach to the foundations of [non-Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/58610) (1868).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Minding was largely self-taught in mathematics. He attended lectures in the [University of Halle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Halle) and eventually graduated with a thesis "De valore intergralium duplicium quam proxime inveniendo" (1829).

Minding worked as a teacher in Elberfeld and as a university lecturer in Berlin. His work on [statics](wikipedia://en/28767) drew the attention of [Alexander von Humboldt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt). However, his 1842 bid for election to [Berlin Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prussian_Academy_of_Sciences), supported by [Peter Gustav Lejeune Dirichlet](wikipedia://en/240002), failed and in 1843 he relocated to the [Imperial University of Dorpat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_University_of_Dorpat), where he was a professor of mathematics for the next 40 years. In Dorpat he taught [Karl Peterson](wikipedia://en/53783102) and supervised his doctoral thesis that established the [Gauss–Bonnet theorem](wikipedia://en/139229) and derived [Gauss–Codazzi equations](wikipedia://en/9633335). Minding also worked on [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) (Demidov prize of the St Petersburg Academy in 1861), [algebraic functions](wikipedia://en/974169), [continued fractions](wikipedia://en/739199) and [analytical mechanics](wikipedia://en/454450). His list of publications consists of some 60 titles, including several books. Many of his scientific accomplishments were only recognized properly after his death.
