# Frank Grosshans

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Frank Grosshans"}

**Frank D. Grosshans** (born 1942)[^o2] is an American mathematician who works in [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), where he is known for the discovery of [Grosshans subgroups](wikipedia://en/5791910) and [Grosshans graded coefficients](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grosshans_graded_coefficient).[^o3] He is a professor of mathematics at [West Chester University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Chester_University_of_Pennsylvania), Pennsylvania.[^o4] Grosshans has been an invited speaker at meetings of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o5]

:::

He received his B.S. from the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois) and his Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o4] He taught at [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) and [Johns Hopkins University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johns_Hopkins_University) before joining the West Chester University.
