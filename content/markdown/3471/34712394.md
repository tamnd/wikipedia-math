# Robert Evert Stong

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Evert Stong"}

**Robert Evert Stong** (August 23, 1936, [Oklahoma City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oklahoma_City) – April 10, 2008, [Charlottesville, Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charlottesville,_Virginia)) was a mathematician at the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) who proved the [Hattori–Stong theorem](wikipedia://en/34712196).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Stong received a B.A. and M.A. in mathematics at the [University of Oklahoma](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oklahoma). He received a Ph.D. in mathematics from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) in 1962.[^o5] His Ph.D. dissertation, *Some relations among characteristic classes and numbers*, was written under the supervision of [Richard Lashof](wikipedia://en/26518637).[^o5] He served on active duty with the [United States Army Reserves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_Army_Reserve) from 1962 to 1965 and was stationed at [Fort Benjamin Harrison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Benjamin_Harrison) and [the Pentagon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Pentagon). He worked on computer development and rose to the rank of captain.

## Career {#Career}

After serving with the Army, he went to the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) as a post-doctoral fellow (1964–66), and then to [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) as faculty (1966–68). In 1968 he became Professor of Mathematics at the University of Virginia, where he taught until his retirement in 2007. With Landweber and Ravenel, Stong introduced [elliptic cohomology](wikipedia://en/10295395).[^o4]

His doctoral students included [Nelson Saiers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nelson_Saiers).
