# Hattori–Stong theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hattori–Stong theorem"}

In [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), the **Hattori–Stong theorem**, [proved](wikipedia://en/82285) by (txt) and (txt), gives an [isomorphism](wikipedia://en/14828) between the [stable homotopy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_homotopy) of a [Thom spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thom_spectrum) and the primitive elements of its [K-homology](wikipedia://en/3021282).

:::
