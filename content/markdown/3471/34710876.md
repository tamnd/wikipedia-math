# Goncharov conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Goncharov conjecture"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Goncharov conjecture** is a conjecture introduced by [Goncharov](wikipedia://en/32114133)[^n1] suggesting that the [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of certain [motivic complexes](wikipedia://en/809314) coincides with pieces of [K-groups](wikipedia://en/598500). It extends a conjecture due to [Zagier](wikipedia://en/1408557).[^n2]

:::

::: {#o5 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $F$ be a field. Goncharov defined the following complex called $\Gamma(F,n)$ placed in degrees $[1,n]$:

$$\Gamma_F(n): \mathcal B_n(F)\to \mathcal B_{n-1}(F)\otimes F^\times_\mathbb Q\to\dots\to \Lambda^n F^\times_\mathbb Q.$$

He conjectured that $i$-th cohomology of this complex is isomorphic to the motivic cohomology group $H^i_{mot}(F,\mathbb Q(n))$.

:::
