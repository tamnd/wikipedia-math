# Fröberg conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fröberg conjecture"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), the **Fröberg conjecture** is a [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) about the possible [Hilbert functions](wikipedia://en/6612596) of a set of forms. It is named after Ralf Fröberg, who introduced it in (txt, page 120). The **Fröberg–Iarrobino conjecture** is a generalization introduced by (txt).

:::

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/conjecture cue=named-heading name="Statement of Conjecture"}

## Statement of Conjecture {#Statement_of_Conjecture}

Given generic homogeneous polynomials $g_1,g_2,\ldots,g_k\in \mathbb{C}[x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n]$ of degrees $a_1,a_2,\ldots, a_k$, respectively. Then the Hilbert series of $\mathbb{C}[x_1,x_2,\ldots,x_n]/\langle g_1,g_2,\ldots, g_k\rangle$ is

$${(1+t+t^2+\cdots)^n}{(1-t^{a_1})(1-t^{a_2})\cdots (1-t^{a_k})},$$

truncated at its first negative term.

:::
