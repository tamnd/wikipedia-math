# Preradical

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Preradical"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **preradical** is a [subfunctor](wikipedia://en/2151745) of the [identity functor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Identity_functor) in the category of left modules over a ring with identity. The class of all preradicals over R-mod is denoted by R-pr. There is a natural order in R-pr given by, for any two preradicals $\sigma$ and $\tau$, $\sigma\leq\tau$, if for any left R-module M, $\sigma M\leq \tau M$. With this order R-pr becomes a big lattice.

:::
