# Leila Haaparanta

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Leila Haaparanta"}

**Leila Tuulikki Haaparanta** (née Taiminen, born 20 October 1954) is a Finnish philosopher who works in [analytic philosophy](wikipedia://en/159211) and the [philosophy of logic](wikipedia://en/21368888). She is retired from the [University of Tampere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tampere) as a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Haaparanta was born on 20 October 1954 in [Kalvola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kalvola). She studied philosophy at the [University of Helsinki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Helsinki), earning a bachelor's degree in 1976, a master's degree in 1978, a [licenciate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licenciate) in 1979, and a Ph.D. in 1985; her dissertation was *Frege's Doctrine of Being*.

She taught at the University of Helsinki from 1977 to 1994, becoming an assistant in 1987, and held an affiliation as a researcher at the Academy of Finland from 1985 to 1997. She became a professor at the University of Tampere in 1998, and retired as professor emerita in 2018.

## Books {#Books}

Haaparanta is the editor of:

- *Frege Synthesized: Essays on the Philosophical and Foundational Work of Gottlob Frege* (with [Jaakko Hintikka](wikipedia://en/1710903), D. Reidel Publishing, 1986)
- *Mind, Meaning and Mathematics: Essays on the Philosophical Views of Husserl and Frege* (Synthese Library 237, Springer, 1994)
- *Analytic Philosophy in Finland* (with [Ilkka Niiniluoto](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ilkka_Niiniluoto), Rodopi, 2003)
- *The Development of Modern Logic* ([Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press), 2009)
- *Categories of Being: Essays on Metaphysics and Logic* (with Heikki J. Koskinen, Oxford University Press, 2012)

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Haaparanta was elected to the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) in 2002, and to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea) in 2011.

A [festschrift](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Festschrift) in her honor, *Filosofisia tutkielmia: Philosophical Studies in honorem Leila Haaparanta*, was edited by Luoma Kaisa, Oesch Erna, and Vilkko Risto, and published in 2004.
