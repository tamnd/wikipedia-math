This [uniform polyhedron compound](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of 6 [decagrammic prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagrammic_prism), aligned with the axes of fivefold rotational symmetry of a [dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/8407).

:::

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

(±√(τ/√5), ±2τ<sup>−1</sup>, ±√(τ<sup>−1</sup>/√5))

(±(√(τ/√5)+τ<sup>−2</sup>), ±1, ±(√(τ<sup>−1</sup>/√5)−τ<sup>−1</sup>))

(±(√(τ/√5)−τ<sup>−1</sup>), ±τ<sup>−2</sup>, ±(√(τ<sup>−1</sup>/√5)+1))

(±(√(τ/√5)+τ<sup>−1</sup>), ±τ<sup>−2</sup>, ±(√(τ<sup>−1</sup>/√5)−1))

(±(√(τ/√5)−τ<sup>−2</sup>), ±1, ±(√(τ<sup>−1</sup>/√5)+τ<sup>−1</sup>))

where τ = (1+√5)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) (sometimes written φ).
