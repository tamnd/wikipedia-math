The **compounds of ten octahedra** UC<sub>15</sub> and UC<sub>16</sub> are two [uniform polyhedron compounds](wikipedia://en/8606809). They are composed of a symmetric arrangement of 10 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635), considered as triangular [antiprisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antiprisms), aligned with the axes of three-fold rotational symmetry of an [icosahedron](wikipedia://en/43856084). The two compounds differ in the orientation of their octahedra: each compound may be transformed into the other by rotating each octahedron by 60 degrees.

:::

For UC15, the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of this compound is a nonuniform [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486). For UC16, the convex hull would be a nonuniform [truncated icosahedron](wikipedia://en/31282).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

[Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) for the vertices of this compound are all the cyclic permutations of

(0, ±(φ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{2}$ + 2*s*φ), ±(φ$\sqrt{2}$ − 2sφ<sup>−1</sup>))

(±($\sqrt{2}$ − *s*τφ<sup>2</sup>), ±($\sqrt{2}$ + *s*(2τφ − 1)), ±($\sqrt{2}$ + *s*φ<sup>−2</sup>))

(±(φ<sup>−1</sup>$\sqrt{2}$ − *s*φ), ±(φ$\sqrt{2}$ + *s*φ<sup>−1</sup>), ±3*s*)

where φ = (1 + $\sqrt{5}$)/2 is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386) and *s* is either +1 or −1. Setting *s* = −1 gives UC<sub>15</sub>, while *s* = +1 gives UC<sub>16</sub>.
