::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Prismatic compound of prisms"}

Each member of this infinite family of [uniform polyhedron compounds](wikipedia://en/8606809) is a symmetric arrangement of [prisms](wikipedia://en/11064788) sharing a common axis of rotational symmetry.

:::

This infinite family can be enumerated as follows:

- For each positive integer *n*≥1 and for each rational number *p*/*q*>2 (expressed with *p* and *q* [coprime](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coprime)), there occurs the compound of *n* *p*/*q*-gonal prisms, with symmetry group **D<sub>*np*h</sub>**.
