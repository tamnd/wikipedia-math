# Rosenbrock methods

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rosenbrock methods"}

**Rosenbrock methods** refers to either of two distinct ideas in [numerical computation](wikipedia://en/21506), both named for [Howard H. Rosenbrock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Howard_Harry_Rosenbrock).

:::

::: {#o3 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Kaps-Rentrop methods"}

## Numerical solution of differential equations {#Numerical_solution_of_differential_equations}

**Rosenbrock methods** for [stiff differential equations](wikipedia://en/2091823) are a family of single-step methods for solving [ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753).[^o4][^o5] They are related to the [implicit Runge–Kutta methods](wikipedia://en/213671#Implicit_Runge–Kutta_methods)[^o6] and are also known as Kaps–Rentrop methods.[^o7]

:::

## Search method {#Search_method}

**Rosenbrock search** is a [numerical optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_optimization) algorithm applicable to optimization problems in which the [objective function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Objective_function) is inexpensive to compute and the derivative either does not exist or cannot be computed efficiently.[^o9] The idea of Rosenbrock search is also used to initialize some [root-finding](wikipedia://en/153299) routines, such as **fzero** (based on [Brent's method](wikipedia://en/1254323)) in [Matlab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matlab). Rosenbrock search is a form of [derivative-free search](wikipedia://en/27328823) but may perform better on functions with sharp ridges.[^o10] The method often identifies such a ridge which, in many applications, leads to a solution.[^o11]
