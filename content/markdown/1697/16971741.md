# Boettcher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Boettcher"}

**Bottcher** or **Böttcher** is a [German surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_surname). Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Albrecht Böttcher](wikipedia://en/31431593) (born 1954), German mathematician
- [Annabelle Boettcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annabelle_Boettcher) (born 1961), German political scientist
- [Arthur Böttcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arthur_Böttcher) (1831–1889), German pathologist and anatomist
- [August Friedrich Böttcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/August_Friedrich_Böttcher) (1825–1900), insect dealer in Berlin
- [Bas Böttcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas_Böttcher) (born 1974), German slam poet
- [Brendan Bottcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brendan_Bottcher) (born 1991), Canadian curler
- [Bryce Boettcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bryce_Boettcher) (born 2002), American football and baseball player
- [Champ Boettcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champ_Boettcher) (1900–1965), American football player
- [Charles Boettcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Boettcher) (1852–1948), German-born Colorado businessman and philanthropist
- [Curt Boettcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Curt_Boettcher) (1944–1987), American singer, songwriter, musician and record producer
- [Grit Boettcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grit_Boettcher) (born 1938), German actress
- [Günter Böttcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Günter_Böttcher) (1954–2012), West German handball player

- [Lucjan Böttcher](wikipedia://en/36448971) (1872–1937), Polish mathematician
- [Markus Böttcher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markus_Böttcher) (born 1964), German television actor
