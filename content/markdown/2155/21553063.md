# Transversal plane

*Image: Transversal planes.JPG.* Plane "t" is a transversal plane because it intersects parallel planes "p" and "q".

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Transversal plane"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **transversal plane** is a plane that intersects (not contains) two or more [lines](wikipedia://en/946975) or [planes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plane_%28geometry%29).[^o2] A transversal plane may also form [dihedral angles](wikipedia://en/338046).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Theorems"}

## Theorems {#Theorems}

Transversal plane theorem for lines: Lines that intersect a transversal plane are parallel if and only if their alternate interior angles formed by the points of intersection are congruent.

Transversal plane theorem for planes: Planes intersected by a transversal plane are parallel if and only if their alternate interior [dihedral angles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dihedral_angles) are congruent.

Transversal line containment theorem: If a [transversal line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transversal_line) is contained in any plane other than the plane containing all the lines, then the plane is a transversal plane.

:::
