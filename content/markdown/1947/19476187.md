# Peter Giblin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Giblin"}

**Peter John Giblin** (10 July 1943) is an English [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose primary research involves [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763) and its application to [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [computer vision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_vision), and [computer graphics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_graphics).[^o3] Giblin is an [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) professor of mathematics at the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool)[^o4] where he has served on staff for more than 40 years. His positions at Liverpool have included Head of Department (of mathematical sciences), and Head of Division (of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210)).

:::

He is the author or co-author of eight published books, some of which have been translated into Russian. The [foreword](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foreword) for the Russian translation of his book *Curves and Singularities* was written by [V. I. Arnold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V._I._Arnold). Giblin has also authored or co-authored over a hundred [peer reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) published articles.[^o5] The first of these was published in 1968.
