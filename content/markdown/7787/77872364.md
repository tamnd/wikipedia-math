# María Cumplido

Cumplido

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="María Cumplido"}

**María Cumplido Cabello** (25 April 1992) is a Spanish mathematician and researcher who in 2020 was awarded the Vicent Caselles Mathematical Research Prize of the [Royal Spanish Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/65501555).[^o3][^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Cumplido was born in the Santa Rosa-Valdeolleros neighborhood of Córdoba, Spain.[^o6] She graduated in [Mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) from the [University of Seville](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Seville)[^o7] and spent a year at the [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in Paris.[^o8]

She obtained a master's degree in Advanced Mathematics at the University of Seville and a Collaboration Scholarship from the Ministry of Education in the Department of Algebra.[^o8] He also received a scholarship from the Ministry of Education in the Department of Algebra and was awarded a scholarship to study mathematics at the University of Seville, where she studied for a year.[^o8]

She received her PhD from the University of Seville and from the [University of Rennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Rennes). She was recognized for her thesis.[^o7] Cumplido was a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Burgundy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Burgundy) in Dijon and spent a month research stay in [Oaxaca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oaxaca), Mexico.[^o8] In October 2019 she became a postdoctoral researcher at Heriot-Watt University in [Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edinburgh). She has also worked at the [Complutense University of Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complutense_University_of_Madrid) and is currently a postdoctoral researcher at the University of Seville.

She has focused her research in the field of [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and has succeeded in generalizing geometric and [topological](wikipedia://en/29954) results on [braid groups](wikipedia://en/550138) to the algebraic context of Artin-Tits groups of spherical type,[^o7] thus solving a mathematical problem that had been unsolved for 20 years.[^o9] This field of research is potentially applicable in [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432) and [computer security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_security).[^o3]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2018 she won the second prize of the Rennes 1 Foundation for the best thesis in Mathematics and Science and [Information and Communication Technologies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_and_communications_technology).[^o7][^o11]

In 2020 she was one of six young mathematicians to receive the Vicent Caselles Mathematical Research Award of the Spanish Royal Mathematical Society and the BBVA Foundation.[^o7][^o12]
