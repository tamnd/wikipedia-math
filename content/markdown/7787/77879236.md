# Gisèle Mophou

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gisèle Mophou"}

**Gisèle Massengo Mophou** (also published as **Gisèle Adélie Mophou Loudjom**) is a Cameroonian [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506) whose research involves [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039) and [fractional differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractional_differential_equation). She is a professor at the [University of the French Antilles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_French_Antilles) in [Pointe-à-Pitre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointe-à-Pitre), [Guadeloupe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guadeloupe), where she directs the Laboratoire de Mathématiques Informatique et Applications (LAMIA).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mophou is originally from Cameroon. She earned a bachelor's degree in 1987, and a [diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies) in numerical analysis in 1997 at [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in France. She completed her PhD in numerical analysis in 2000, at the University of the French Antilles.

She obtained a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2010, and became a full professor at the University of the French Antilles in 2013. From 2017 to 2019 she returned to Cameroon, with the support of the [Alexander von Humboldt Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation), as German Research Chair at the [African Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1575286) Cameroon.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Mophou was elected to the [African Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/African_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2013.
