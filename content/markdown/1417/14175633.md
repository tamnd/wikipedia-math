# Georg Nöbeling

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Georg Nöbeling"}

**Georg August Nöbeling** (12 November 1907 – 16 February 2008) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Born and raised in [Lüdenscheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lüdenscheid), Nöbeling studied [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen) between 1927 and 1929 and [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna), where he was a student of [Karl Menger](wikipedia://en/149431) and received his PhD in 1931 on a generalization of the [embedding theorem](wikipedia://en/3173612), which for one special case can be visualized by the [Menger sponge](wikipedia://en/208811).[^o4] Nöbeling worked and researched in Menger's Mathematical Colloquium with [Kurt Gödel](wikipedia://en/16736), [Franz Alt](wikipedia://en/3318467), [Abraham Wald](wikipedia://en/1239430), [Olga Taussky-Todd](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Olga_Taussky-Todd) and others.

In 1933, he moved to the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen), where he habilitated in 1935 under [Otto Haupt](wikipedia://en/31696723) and obtained a professorship at the same place in 1940. His work focused on [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). 1968/1969 he solved [Specker](wikipedia://en/22209158)'s theorem on [abelian groups](wikipedia://en/2974).

As [Rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) (1962–1963) of the University of Erlangen he oversaw the merge with the business college in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg).[^o5] He also served twice as the chairman of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) and is a member of the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities). He celebrated his 100th birthday in 2007.[^o6][^o7]
