# Filecoin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Filecoin"}

**Filecoin** (**⨎**) is a [cryptocurrency](wikipedia://en/36662188) intended to be a [blockchain](wikipedia://en/44065971)-based [cooperative digital storage](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cooperative_storage_cloud) and data retrieval method.[^o3][^o4][^o5][^o6] It was developed by [Protocol Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Protocol_Labs) and shares some ideas from [InterPlanetary File System](wikipedia://en/47785998) allowing users to rent unused hard drive space.[^o7][^o8] Filecoin is an open protocol and uses a blockchain to record participation in the network.[^o9] Transactions are made using the blockchain's currency, FIL.[^o10] The blockchain is based on both [proof of-replication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_of-replication) and [proof of space-time](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Proof_of_space-time).[^o11][^o12]

:::

Filecoins are paid to miners (termed "storage providers" - SPs) who provide storage for clients, as opposed to [Bitcoin](wikipedia://en/28249265) where miners secure the network and maintain consensus.[^o13] The payment amount depends on the network schedule, storage sectors quantity and size, adherence to FIL+ and deals made with clients, and participation in the network requires putting tokens in collateral.[^o14]

## History {#History}

Filecoin, as a cryptocurrency and file storage network, was defined in a white paper published in July 2014.[^o16]

The project was launched in August 2017, and raised over \$200 million within 30 minutes via an [initial coin offering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Initial_coin_offering).[^o9][^o17]

In 2020, the project set up the Filecoin Foundation under the leadership of founding officer [Megan Klimen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Megan_Klimen) to support the development of the protocol, and the Filecoin Foundation for the Decentralized Web to support other projects contributing to the ecosystem of the [decentralized web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralized_web).[^o18]

In April 2021, the storage capacity of the network crossed the Baseline Target, which was interpreted as a transition from a "capacity building" stage of the network to one on which scale was achieved.[^o19] However, the network growth in 2022 was slower than the Baseline Target,[^o20] which caused the capacity to become smaller than the target by February 2023.[^o21]

Their Board of Directors and advisors include [Brewster Kahle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brewster_Kahle) (Founder of [Internet Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Archive)), [Joe Lubin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Lubin_%28entrepreneur%29) (Founder of Consensys & Cofounder of Ethereum), and Denelle Dixon (CEO of [Stellar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellar_%28payment_network%29) Development Foundation).[^o22]

## Philanthropy {#Philanthropy}

In April 2021, the Filecoin Foundation donated 50,000 filecoins worth \$10,000,000 to the [Internet Archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_Archive).[^o24][^o25] In addition, Internet Archive's founder [Brewster Kahle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brewster_Kahle) and director of partnerships Wendy Hanamura joined the boards of advisors of Filecoin and the Filecoin Foundation for the [Decentralized Web](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decentralized_web).[^o26]

In 2020, content from Internet Archive started to be stored in Filecoin, with the purpose of creating a decentralized prototype of the digital library.[^o24] By October 2023, one petabyte of data had been uploaded to the Filecoin network.[^o27]
