# Nuclear C\*-algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nuclear C*-algebra"}

In the mathematical field of [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a **nuclear C\*-algebra** is a [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) $A$ such that for every C\*-algebra $B$ the [injective](wikipedia://en/63566619) and [projective](wikipedia://en/63566617) C\*-[cross norms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cross_norm) coincides on the algebraic tensor product $A\otimes B$ and the completion of $A\otimes B$ with respect to this norm is a C\*-algebra. This property was first studied by (Takesaki 1964) under the name "Property T", which is not related to [Kazhdan's property T](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kazhdan's_property_T).

:::

::: {#o4 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Characterizations"}

## Characterizations {#Characterizations}

Nuclearity admits the following equivalent characterizations:

- The identity map, as a [completely positive map](wikipedia://en/6083511), approximately factors through matrix algebras. By this equivalence, nuclearity can be considered a [noncommutative](wikipedia://en/662624) analogue of the existence of [partitions of unity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partitions_of_unity).
- The [enveloping von Neumann algebra](wikipedia://en/15374396) is [injective](wikipedia://en/294185#Amenable_von_Neumann_algebras).
- It is [amenable](wikipedia://en/11417634) as a [Banach algebra](wikipedia://en/4665).
- (**For separable algebras**) It is isomorphic to a C\*-subalgebra $B$ of the [Cuntz algebra](wikipedia://en/4463325) $\text{𝒪}_{2}$ with the property that there exists a [conditional expectation](wikipedia://en/435099) from $\text{𝒪}_{2}$ to $B$.

:::

::: {#o6 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

The commutative unital C\* algebra of (real or complex-valued) [continuous functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_functions) on a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) [Hausdorff space](wikipedia://en/13637) as well as the noncommutative unital algebra of $n\times n$ real or complex matrices are nuclear.[^o7]

:::
