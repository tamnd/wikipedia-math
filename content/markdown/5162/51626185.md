This is a **list of countries by population in 1939** (including any dependent, occupied or colonized territories for [empires](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Empire)), providing an approximate overview of the world population **before** [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).

:::

Estimate numbers are from the beginning of the year, and exact population figures are for countries that were having a census in the year 1939 (which were on various dates in that year).
