# Cartan–Kähler theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cartan–Kähler theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Cartan–Kähler theorem** is a major result on the [integrability conditions for differential systems](wikipedia://en/968734), in the case of [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478), for [differential ideals](wikipedia://en/8056148) $I$. It is named for [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274) and [Erich Kähler](wikipedia://en/383501).

:::

## Meaning {#Meaning}

It is not true that merely having $dI$ contained in $I$ is sufficient for integrability. There is a problem caused by [singular solutions](wikipedia://en/506289). The theorem computes certain constants that must satisfy an inequality in order that there be a solution.

::: {#o4 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $(M,I)$ be a real analytic [EDS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Exterior_differential_system). Assume that $P \subseteq M$ is a connected, *$k$*-dimensional, real analytic, regular [integral manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integral_manifold) of *$I$* with $r(P) \geq 0$ (i.e., the tangent spaces $T_p P$ are "extendable" to higher dimensional integral elements).

Moreover, assume there is a real analytic submanifold $R \subseteq M$ of codimension $r(P)$ containing $P$ and such that $T_pR \cap H(T_pP)$ has dimension $k+1$ for all $p \in P$.

Then there exists a (locally) unique connected, $(k+1)$-dimensional, real analytic integral manifold $X \subseteq M$ of $I$ that satisfies $P \subseteq X \subseteq R$.

:::

::: {#o6 .proof cue=heading name="Proof and assumptions"}

## Proof and assumptions {#Proof_and_assumptions}

The [Cauchy-Kovalevskaya theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy-Kovalevskaya_theorem) is used in the proof, so the analyticity is necessary.

:::
