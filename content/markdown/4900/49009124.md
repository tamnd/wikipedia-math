# Journal of Applied Statistics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Applied Statistics"}

The ***Journal of Applied Statistics*** or ***J.Appl.Stat.*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering [applied statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_statistics) that is published by [Taylor & Francis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taylor_&_Francis). Its *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)* [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) was 1.2 in 2023.[^o8]

:::

## Creation {#Creation}

The journal was founded by [Gopal Kanji](wikipedia://en/59111771) in 1974 as the *Bulletin in Applied Statistics (BIAS)*. Kanji remained as editor until the end of 2007.[^o4]

## Quality rankings {#Quality_rankings}

In 2020, the [Australian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4579582) listed *J.Appl.Stat.* with a ranking of *B* on a scale including *A\**, *A*, *B* and *C*.[^o9] *J.Appl.Stat.* states that its *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)* [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) was 0.699 in 2017[^o7] and 1.2 in 2023.[^o8]
