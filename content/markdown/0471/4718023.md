# David Emmanuel (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Emmanuel"}

**David Emmanuel** (31 January 1854 – 4 February 1941) was a [Romanian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania) [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_Jews_in_Romania) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [member of the Romanian Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_members_of_the_Romanian_Academy), considered to be the founder of the modern mathematics school in Romania.

:::

Born in [Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bucharest), Emmanuel studied at [Gheorghe Lazăr](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gheorghe_Lazăr_National_College_%28Bucharest%29) and [Gheorghe Șincai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gheorghe_Șincai_National_College_%28Bucharest%29) high schools.[^o3] In 1873 he went to [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989), where he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in mathematics from the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) (Sorbonne) in 1879 with a thesis on *Study of [abelian integrals of the third species](wikipedia://en/1138853)*, becoming the second Romanian to have a Ph.D. in mathematics from the Sorbonne (the first one was [Spiru Haret](wikipedia://en/1194762)). The thesis defense committee consisted of [Victor Puiseux](wikipedia://en/3868862) (advisor), [Charles Briot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Briot), and [Jean-Claude Bouquet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Claude_Bouquet).[^o4]

In 1882, Emmanuel became a professor of [superior algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) and [function theory](wikipedia://en/26478) at the Faculty of Sciences of the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest).[^o5] Here, in 1888, he held the first courses on [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) and on [Galois theory](wikipedia://en/61316), and introduced [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553) in Romanian education.[^o6] Among his students were [Anton Davidoglu](wikipedia://en/3421491), [Alexandru Froda](wikipedia://en/22289393), [Traian Lalescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Traian_Lalescu), [Grigore Moisil](wikipedia://en/4000394), Alexandru Myller, [Miron Nicolescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Miron_Nicolescu), [Octav Onicescu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octav_Onicescu), [Dimitrie Pompeiu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dimitrie_Pompeiu), [Simion Stoilow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simion_Stoilow), and [Gheorghe Țițeica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gheorghe_Țițeica). Emmanuel had an important role in the introduction of modern mathematics and of the rigorous approach to mathematics in Romania.

Emmanuel was the president of the first Congress of Romanian Mathematicians, held in 1929 in [Cluj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cluj-Napoca). He died in Bucharest in 1941.

A street in the [Dorobanți](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dorobanți) neighborhood of Bucharest is named after him.
