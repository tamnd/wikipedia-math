# Wilhelm von Freeden

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm von Freeden"}

**Wilhelm Ihno Adolf von Freeden** (12 May 1822 [Norden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norden,_Lower_Saxony), [German Confederation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Confederation) – 11 January 1894 [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bonn)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and expert on [navigation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Navigation). He was the founder of the North German Naval Observatory. [Freeden Bank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeden_Bank) bears his name.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was educated at the universities of [Bonn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bonn) and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen). He was director of the school of navigation at [Elsfleth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsfleth), [Oldenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Oldenburg), and later settled in [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg).

### North German Naval Observatory {#North_German_Naval_Observatory}

In Hamburg, in 1867, he founded the private North German Naval Observatory (*Norddeutsche Seewarte*), which he directed until 1875 when it became the German Naval Observatory (*Deutsche Seewarte*), an agency of the [German Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire). The purpose of the observatory was to promote and to facilitate maritime intercourse. It was composed of a department of maritime [meteorology](wikipedia://en/19904); a bureau of nautical, meteorological, and magnetic instruments; a department of coast meteorology and signal service; and a bureau for testing chronometers. In 1945, the tasks of the German Naval Observatory were turned over to the German Hydrographic Institute (*Deutsche Hydrographische Institut*), and in 1990 that agency in turn became part of the present day Federal Department of Shipping and Hydrography (*[Bundesamt für Seeschifffahrt und Hydrographie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bundesamt_für_Seeschifffahrt_und_Hydrographie)*).

### Other activities {#Other_activities}

Freeden was a member of the [Reichstag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reichstag_%28German_Empire%29) from 1871 to 1876. He founded, with H. Tecklenborg-Bremen, a publication entitled *Hansa, Zeitschrift für Seewesen*, which he edited until 1891.
