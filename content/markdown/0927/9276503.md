# Dan Segal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Segal"}

**Daniel Segal** (born 1947)[^o3] is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford). He specialises in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) and [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890).

:::

He studied at [Peterhouse, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peterhouse,_Cambridge), before taking a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) at [Queen Mary College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_Mary,_University_of_London), [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), in 1972, supervised by [Bertram Wehrfritz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertram_Wehrfritz), with a dissertation on [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890) entitled *Groups of [Automorphisms](wikipedia://en/1160) of Infinite [Soluble Groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soluble_Group)*. He is an Emeritus Fellow of [All Souls College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All_Souls_College) at Oxford, where he was sub-warden from 2006 to 2008.[^o5]

His postgraduate students have included [Marcus du Sautoy](wikipedia://en/3935943) and [Geoff Smith](wikipedia://en/10429971). He is the son of psychoanalyst [Hanna Segal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hanna_Segal) and brother of philosopher [Gabriel Segal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gabriel_Segal_%28philosopher%29) as well as Michael Segal, a senior civil servant.
