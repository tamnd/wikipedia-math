# Robert Goodell Brown

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Goodell Brown"}

**Robert Goodell Brown** (1923–2013) was renowned in field of [forecasting](wikipedia://en/246074), specifically with major contributions of work regarding [exponential smoothing](wikipedia://en/1890727). He was an [International Institute of Forecasters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Institute_of_Forecasters) member, and a past director. He contributed to the field of forecasting with practical books.[^o2]

:::
