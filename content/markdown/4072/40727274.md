# Sidorenko

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sidorenko"}

**Sidorenko** is an East Slavic [patronymic surname](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Patronymic_surname) derived from the Ukrainian surname [Sydorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sydorenko), meaning "descendant of Sydir/Sidor".

:::

- [Aleksandr Sidorenko (wrestler)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aleksandr_Sidorenko_%28wrestler%29) (born 1972), Belarusian wrestler
- [Alexandre Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexandre_Sidorenko) (born 1988), French tennis player
- [Alexey Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexey_Sidorenko) (born 1983), Kazakhstani beach volleyball player
- [Denis Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dzianis_Sidarenka), Belarusian diplomat
- [Dmitri Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Sidorenko) (born 1973), Russian footballer
- [Elizaveta Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elizaveta_Sidorenko) (born 2003), Russian Paralympic swimmer
- [Ivan Ilyich Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Ilyich_Sidorenko) (1907–1984), Red Army sergeant
- [Ivan Mikhailovich Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Sidorenko) (1919–1994), Red Army officer
- [Kirill Sidorenko](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kirill_Sidorenko) (born 1983), Russian ice hockey player
