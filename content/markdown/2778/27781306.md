# Denis Henrion

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denis Henrion"}

*Image: Henrion - Usage du compas de proportion, 1637 - BEIC 218820 F.jpg.* *Usage du compas de proportion*, 1637

**Denis** (sometimes Didier) **Henrion**, was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) born at the end of the 16th century in France.[^o2] He co-edited the works of [Viète](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viète). He died around 1632 in Paris.

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Henrion wrote a tract concerning [logarithms](wikipedia://en/17860).[^o4]

He translated [Euclid's Elements](wikipedia://en/244107) from Latin into French.

He published *Problemata nobilissima duo* (Paris, 1616), a book against [Marin Ghetaldi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marin_Ghetaldi) and attacking [Viète](wikipedia://en/310238) and [Regiomontanus](wikipedia://en/25929). Later reorganized, the book was republished by its author.
