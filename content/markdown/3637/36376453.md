# Tsen rank

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tsen rank"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Tsen rank** of a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) describes conditions under which a system of [polynomial equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polynomial_equations) must have a solution in the [field](wikipedia://en/10603). The concept is named for [C. C. Tsen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._C._Tsen), who introduced their study in 1936.

:::

We consider a system of *m* polynomial equations in *n* variables over a field *F*. Assume that the equations all have constant term zero, so that (0, 0, ... ,0) is a common solution. We say that *F* is a **T**<sub>***i***</sub>-**field** if every such system, of degrees *d*<sub>1</sub>, ..., *d*<sub>*m*</sub> has a common non-zero solution whenever

$$n > d_1^i + \cdots + d_m^i. \,$$

The *Tsen rank* of *F* is the smallest *i* such that *F* is a T<sub>*i*</sub>-field. We say that the Tsen rank of *F* is infinite if it is not a T<sub>*i*</sub>-field for any *i* (for example, if it is [formally real](wikipedia://en/1067223)).

::: {#o3 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- A field has Tsen rank zero [if and only if](wikipedia://en/14922) it is [algebraically closed](wikipedia://en/1018).
- A finite field has Tsen rank 1: this is the [Chevalley–Warning theorem](wikipedia://en/2934314).
- If *F* is algebraically closed then rational function field *F*(*X*) has Tsen rank 1.
- If *F* has Tsen rank *i*, then the rational function field *F*(*X*) has Tsen rank at most *i* + 1.
- If *F* has Tsen rank *i*, then an [algebraic extension](wikipedia://en/2125) of *F* has Tsen rank at most *i*.
- If *F* has Tsen rank *i*, then an extension of *F* of [transcendence degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transcendence_degree) *k* has Tsen rank at most *i* + *k*.
- There exist fields of Tsen rank *i* for every integer *i* ≥ 0.

:::

## Norm form {#Norm_form}

We define a *norm form of level i* on a field *F* to be a [homogeneous polynomial](wikipedia://en/1698977) of degree *d* in *n*=*d*<sup>*i*</sup> variables with only the trivial zero over *F* (we exclude the case *n*=*d*=1). The existence of a norm form on level *i* on *F* implies that *F* is of Tsen rank at least *i* − 1. If *E* is an extension of *F* of finite degree *n* > 1, then the field [norm form](wikipedia://en/7204666) for *E*/*F* is a norm form of level 1. If *F* admits a norm form of level *i* then the rational function field *F*(*X*) admits a norm form of level *i* + 1. This allows us to demonstrate the existence of fields of any given Tsen rank.

## Diophantine dimension {#Diophantine_dimension}

The **Diophantine dimension** of a field is the smallest natural number *k*, if it exists, such that the field of is class C<sub>*k*</sub>: that is, such that any homogeneous polynomial of degree *d* in *N* variables has a non-trivial zero whenever *N* > *d*<sup>*k*</sup>. Algebraically closed fields are of Diophantine dimension 0; [quasi-algebraically closed fields](wikipedia://en/2934213) of dimension 1.[^o6]

Clearly if a field is T<sub>*i*</sub> then it is C<sub>*i*</sub>, and T<sub>0</sub> and C<sub>0</sub> are equivalent, each being equivalent to being algebraically closed. It is not known whether Tsen rank and Diophantine dimension are equal in general.
