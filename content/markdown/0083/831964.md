# Shewhart Medal

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Shewhart Medal"}

The **Shewhart Medal**, named in honour of [Walter A. Shewhart](wikipedia://en/433679), is awarded annually by the [American Society for Quality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Society_for_Quality) for "...outstanding technical leadership in the field of modern [quality control](wikipedia://en/41613), especially through the development to its theory, principles, and techniques."[^o3] The first medal was awarded in 1948.[^o4]
