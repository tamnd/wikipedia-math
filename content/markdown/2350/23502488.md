# Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether theorem"}

In [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), the **Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether theorem** states that a [central simple algebra](wikipedia://en/404082) over an [algebraic number field](wikipedia://en/28730822) *K* which splits over every [completion](wikipedia://en/145375) *K*<sub>*v*</sub> is a [matrix algebra](wikipedia://en/487541) over *K*. The theorem is an example of a [local-global principle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Local-global_principle) in [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705) and leads to a complete description of finite-dimensional [division algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Division_algebras) over algebraic number fields in terms of their [local invariants](wikipedia://en/36426069). It was proved independently by [Richard Brauer](wikipedia://en/605134), [Helmut Hasse](wikipedia://en/727118), and [Emmy Noether](wikipedia://en/149896) and by [Abraham Adrian Albert](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Adrian_Albert).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement of the theorem"}

## Statement of the theorem {#Statement_of_the_theorem}

Let *A* be a [central simple algebra](wikipedia://en/404082) of rank *d* over an [algebraic number field](wikipedia://en/28730822) *K*. Suppose that for any [valuation](wikipedia://en/347049) *v*, *A* splits over the corresponding local field *K*<sub>*v*</sub>:

$$A\otimes_K K_v \simeq M_d(K_v).$$

Then *A* is isomorphic to the matrix algebra *M*<sub>*d*</sub>(*K*).

:::

::: {#o5 .application cue=heading name="Applications"}

## Applications {#Applications}

Using the theory of [Brauer group](wikipedia://en/404078), one shows that two central simple algebras *A* and *B* over an algebraic number field *K* are isomorphic over *K* if and only if their completions *A*<sub>*v*</sub> and *B*<sub>*v*</sub> are isomorphic over the completion *K*<sub>*v*</sub> for every *v*.

Together with the [Grunwald–Wang theorem](wikipedia://en/20901695), the Albert–Brauer–Hasse–Noether theorem implies that every central simple algebra over an algebraic number field is *cyclic*, i.e. can be obtained by an explicit construction from a [cyclic field extension](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_extension) *L*/*K* .

:::
