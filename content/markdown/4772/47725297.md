# Lisette de Pillis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lisette de Pillis"}

**Lisette G. de Pillis** is an American mathematician at [Harvey Mudd College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College) and holds the Norman F. Sprague, Jr. Professorship of Life Sciences at Harvey Mudd.[^o2] She chaired the Department of Mathematics in 2008-2009 and again from 2014 to 2019. She directed the Harvey Mudd College Global Clinic program from 2009 to 2014. She is also the co-director of the Harvey Mudd College Center for Quantitative Life Sciences.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

De Pillis earned her Ph.D. in 1993 from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) under the supervision of [Heinz-Otto Kreiss](wikipedia://en/2070195). Her dissertation was *Far Field Behavior of Slightly Compressible Flows*.

## Research {#Research}

Her early research concerned [computational fluid dynamics](wikipedia://en/305924). In around 2000, she became interested in [cancer immunology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cancer_immunology), and began doing research on [population models](wikipedia://en/11397469) and [cellular automata](wikipedia://en/54342) models featuring cells of three types: normal cells, cancer cells, and immune cells. By augmenting the model to include cancer treatments and applying [control theory](wikipedia://en/7039), she was able to devise techniques that could lead to more effective personalized treatments for cancer.[^o7] Her continuing research has produced mathematical models of newly developing immunotherapies, combination therapies, and the emergence of autoimmune diseases.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

The [Argonne National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory) named her their [Maria Goeppert-Mayer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maria_Goeppert-Mayer) Argonne Distinguished Scholar for 1999–2000.[^o9] In 2016 she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] In 2017 she was a [HERS-CBL Clare Boothe Luce](https://www.hersnetwork.org/programs/hers-institute/) Leadership in STEM Scholar. In 2019 she was awarded the [MAA Southern California-Nevada Section](wikipedia://en/198772) [Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics.](http://sections.maa.org/socalnv/) In 2020 she was made the [Intercollegiate Biomathematics Alliance Distinguished Fellow.](https://about.illinoisstate.edu/iba/awards/)
