# Nancy Farley Wood

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Nancy Farley Wood"}

**Nancy Farley "Nan" Wood** (12 July 1903 – 19 March 2003) was a physicist and businesswoman who was a member of the [Manhattan Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Project). She was the only daughter of Daniel Lee Farley and Minerva Jane Ross, and a lifelong [feminist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feminist) and proponent of the [Women's liberation movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Women's_liberation_movement) as a founding member of the Chicago [National Organization for Women](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Organization_for_Women).[^o3][^o4][^o5] As a business owner, she designed, developed and manufactured her own line of ionizing radiation detectors. During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Wood taught [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) to [U.S. Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Navy) [sailors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailor) in Chicago. Later, during World War II, she was recruited to the [Manhattan Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Project), where she designed and developed [ionizing radiation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ionizing_radiation) [detectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detector) with [John Alexander Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Alexander_Simpson) in the instrument division at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) [Metallurgical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgical_Laboratory) or [Met Lab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Met_Lab). In 1949, Wood founded the N. Wood Counter Laboratory.[^o4][^o5]

:::

## Early life {#Early_life}

Wood was born Nancy Lee Farley on a [farm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Farm) in 1903 at [La Monte](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/La_Monte,_Missouri), [Pettis County, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pettis_County,_Missouri) and died in 2003 at the home of her son, William in [Baroda](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baroda,_Michigan), [Berrien County, Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berrien_County,_Michigan).[^o7] Wood was the second child of four and the only daughter of Daniel Lee Farley and Minerva Jane Ross. In addition to her three brothers, she also had two half-brothers and a half-sister. In 1928, she married John Curtis Wood and the couple had 6 children, 5 of whom survived to adulthood. Nancy and John Curtis Wood and their daughter Shirley June (who died at the age of two) are buried at Green Ridge Cemetery in [Green Ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Green_Ridge,_Missouri), [Pettis County, Missouri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pettis_County,_Missouri).[^o8]

## Education {#Education}

Wood began her education in a [one room schoolhouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/One_room_schoolhouse) in Green Ridge, Missouri. The family moved to central Missouri so she could attend college.[^o4] Wood graduated from the [Warrensburg Teacher's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Central_Missouri) and taught [high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) and [physics](wikipedia://en/22939). She attended the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) and in 1927 received a [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) degree in [education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Education).

## World War II and the Manhattan Project {#World_War_II_and_the_Manhattan_Project}

Prior to and during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Wood taught [calculus](wikipedia://en/5176) to [U.S. Navy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._Navy) [sailors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailor) in [Chicago, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chicago,_Illinois). Toward the end of World War II she was recruited to the [Manhattan Project](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manhattan_Project) where she designed and developed [radiation detectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiation_detector) with [John Alexander Simpson](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Alexander_Simpson) in the instrument division at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) [Metallurgical Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metallurgical_Laboratory) or [Met Lab](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Met_Lab).[^o4][^o5][^o11][^o12][^o13]

## N. Wood Counter Laboratory {#N._Wood_Counter_Laboratory}

In 1949, Wood founded the N. Wood Counter Laboratory.[^o15] The company was located atop the [Hyde Park Bank building](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyde_Park-Kenwood_National_Bank_Building) in Chicago. The company was established to develop, manufacture and supply gas-filled gamma radiation detectors and neutron radiation detectors. The customer base was research laboratories and universities engaged in the development of the peaceful utilization of [atomic energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atomic_energy).[^o4][^o16][^o17] The N. Wood Counter Laboratory company remained in business for over 50 years to supply a range of BF<sub>3</sub> proportional [neutron detectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutron_detector), [Geiger-Mueller detectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geiger_counter), proportional gamma counters and gas flow counters. In March 1994, Wood sold the company to her daughter, Marjory Wood Crawford.

In 1957, during the [International Geophysical Year](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Geophysical_Year), the N. Wood model G-15-34A neutron detectors (Simpson Counters) were utilized to collect data worldwide.

### NASA satellite programs {#NASA_satellite_programs}

[NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) chose the N. Wood Counter Laboratory G-5-3 [neutron detectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutron_detector) to be deployed as the initial detectors to operate in the [polar orbiting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_orbit) [satellites](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Satellite). The N. Wood Counter Laboratory D-5-3L gamma detectors orbited in satellites as part of the following [NASA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA) satellite programs:

- [Pioneer V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pioneer_V) mission.[^o19][^o20][^o21]
- [Discoverer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corona_%28satellite%29) or [Corona satellite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corona_%28satellite%29) missions.[^o22][^o23][^o24]
- [Explorers program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Explorers_program).[^o25][^o26]
- [Mariner program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mariner_program).[^o27][^o28][^o29]
- [Ranger program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ranger_program).[^o30]
