# Emil Artin Junior Prize in Mathematics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Emil Artin Junior Prize in Mathematics"}

Established in 2001, the **Emil Artin Junior Prize in Mathematics** is presented usually every year by the [Armenian Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/77547916) to a former student of an Armenian university, not more than 10 years after having received a doctoral degree,[^o2] for outstanding contributions in [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), and [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). The award is announced in the [Notices of the American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/4977512). The prize is named after [Emil Artin](wikipedia://en/245351), who was of [Armenian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenians) descent. Although eligibility for the prize is not fully international, as the recipient has to have studied in [Armenia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armenia), awards are made only for specific outstanding publications in leading international journals.

:::
