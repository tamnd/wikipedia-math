# Konstanze Rietsch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Konstanze Rietsch"}

**Konstanze Rietsch** is a pure mathematician who was educated in Austria and the US. She works in the UK as Professor of Geometry at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London).

:::

## Research {#Research}

Rietsch's research concerns the geometry of [flag varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_variety) and [mirror symmetry](wikipedia://en/644671) in [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681).

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Rietsch received a master's degree in 1993 from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 1993 and completed a Ph.D. in 1998 at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology). Her doctoral dissertation, *Total Positivity and Real Flag Varieties*, was supervised by [George Lusztig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Lusztig).

Following her doctorate, she visited the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), and became a postdoctoral research fellow in [Newnham College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Newnham_College,_Cambridge). Next, she became a Violette and Samuel Glasstone Research Fellow at the [Mathematical Institute, University of Oxford](wikipedia://en/7905850).

She moved to King's College in 2002.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Rietsch is an invited speaker at the 2026 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842).
