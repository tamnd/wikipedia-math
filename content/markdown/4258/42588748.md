# Ole Peder Arvesen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ole Peder Arvesen"}

**Ole Peder Arvesen** (27 March 1895 – 23 January 1991) was a Norwegian [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

Arvesen was born in [Fredrikstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredrikstad). He was appointed professor of [descriptive geometry](wikipedia://en/484891) at the [Norwegian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Institute_of_Technology) from 1938 to 1965. He served as secretary general of the [Royal Norwegian Society of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Norwegian_Society_of_Sciences_and_Letters) from 1950 to 1966, having been a fellow since 1934, and was also a fellow of the [Norwegian Academy of Technological Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Technological_Sciences). Among his publications are *Under Duskens billedbok* (*Under the Dusk picture book*) from 1928, the textbook *Innføring i nomografi* (*Introduction to [nomography](wikipedia://en/271164)*) from 1932, *Mennesker og matematikere* (*People and mathematicians*) from 1940, *Glimt av den store karikatur* (*Glimpse of the great caricature*) from 1941, and the memoir book *Men bare om løst og fast* (*But just about this and that*) from 1976. He was decorated Knight, First Class of the [Order of St. Olav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_St._Olav) in 1965.

A portrait of Arvesen, painted by [Agnes Hiorth](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnes_Hiorth), is located at the [Student Society in Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Student_Society_in_Trondheim), where he was an active participant over many years.[^o3][^o4]
