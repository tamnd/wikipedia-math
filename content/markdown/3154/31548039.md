# Ravenel's conjectures

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ravenel's conjectures"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Ravenel conjectures** are a set of mathematical conjectures in the field of [stable homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/2946655) posed by [Douglas Ravenel](wikipedia://en/27877012) at the end of a paper published in 1984.[^o2] It was earlier circulated in preprint.[^o3] The problems involved have largely been resolved, with all but the "telescope conjecture" being proved in later papers by others.[^o4][^o5][^o3] Ravenel's conjectures exerted influence on the field through the founding of the approach of [chromatic homotopy theory](wikipedia://en/42245935).

:::

The first of the seven conjectures, then the *nilpotence conjecture*, was proved in 1988 and is now known as the [nilpotence theorem](wikipedia://en/31480741).

The **telescope conjecture**, which was fourth on the original list, remains of substantial interest because of its connection with the convergence of an [Adams–Novikov spectral sequence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adams–Novikov_spectral_sequence). While opinion has been generally against the truth of the original statement, investigations of associated phenomena (for a [triangulated category](wikipedia://en/1679700) in general) have become a research area in its own right.[^o6][^o7]

On June 6, 2023, Robert Burklund, Jeremy Hahn, Ishan Levy, and [Tomer Schlank](wikipedia://en/75230578) announced a disproof of the telescope conjecture.[^o8] Their preprint was submitted to the [arXiv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ArXiv) on October 26, 2023.[^o9]
