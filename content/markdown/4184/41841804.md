# Mutation (algebra)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mutation"}

In the theory of [algebras over a field](wikipedia://en/191788), **mutation** is a construction of a new [binary operation](wikipedia://en/3948) related to the multiplication of the algebra. In specific cases the resulting algebra may be referred to as a **homotope** or an [**isotope**](wikipedia://en/42900472) of the original.

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definitions"}

## Definitions {#Definitions}

Let *A* be an algebra over a [field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Field_%28algebra%29) *F* with multiplication (not assumed to be [associative](wikipedia://en/1335)) denoted by juxtaposition. For an element *a* of *A*, define the **left *a*-homotope** $A(a)$ to be the algebra with multiplication

$$x * y = (xa)y. \,$$

Similarly define the **left (*a*,*b*) mutation** $A(a,b)$

$$x * y = (xa)y - (yb)x. \,$$

Right homotope and mutation are defined analogously. Since the right (*p*,*q*) mutation of *A* is the left (−*q*, −*p*) mutation of the [opposite algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Opposite_algebra) to *A*, it suffices to study left mutations.[^o4]

If *A* is a [unital algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unital_algebra) and *a* is invertible, we refer to the **isotope** by *a*.

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- If *A* is associative then so is any homotope of *A*, and any mutation of *A* is [Lie-admissible](wikipedia://en/39299080).
- If *A* is [alternative](wikipedia://en/3160) then so is any homotope of *A*, and any mutation of *A* is [Malcev-admissible](wikipedia://en/39299327).[^o4]
- Any isotope of a [Hurwitz algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hurwitz_algebra) is isomorphic to the original.[^o4]
- A homotope of a [Bernstein algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bernstein_algebra) by an element of non-zero weight is again a Bernstein algebra.[^o7]

:::

## Jordan algebras {#Jordan_algebras}

A [Jordan algebra](wikipedia://en/936371) is a commutative algebra satisfying the *Jordan identity* $(xy)(xx) = x(y(xx))$. The [Jordan triple product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_triple_product) is defined by

$$\{a,b,c\}=(ab)c+(cb)a -(ac)b. \,$$

For *y* in *A* the *mutation*[^o9] or *homotope*[^o10] *A*<sup>*y*</sup> is defined as the vector space *A* with multiplication

$$a\circ b= \{a,y,b\}. \,$$

and if *y* is invertible this is referred to as an *isotope*. A homotope of a Jordan algebra is again a Jordan algebra: isotopy defines an equivalence relation.[^o11] If *y* is [nuclear](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nucleus_%28algebra%29) then the isotope by *y* is isomorphic to the original.[^o12]
