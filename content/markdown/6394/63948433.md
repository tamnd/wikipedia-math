# Quasi-complete space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasi-complete space"}

In [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), a [topological vector space](wikipedia://en/45752) (TVS) is said to be **quasi-complete** or **boundedly complete**[^n1] if every [closed](wikipedia://en/47279) and [bounded](wikipedia://en/1789812) subset is [complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_space).[^n2] This concept is of considerable importance for non-[metrizable TVSs](wikipedia://en/64278078).[^n2]

:::

::: {#o6 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Properties"}

## Properties {#Properties}

- Every quasi-complete TVS is [sequentially complete](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sequentially_complete_space).[^n2]
- In a quasi-complete [locally convex](wikipedia://en/518844) space, the closure of the [convex hull](wikipedia://en/40634) of a compact subset is again compact.[^n3]
- In a quasi-complete Hausdorff TVS, every [precompact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Totally_bounded) subset is relatively compact.[^n2]
- If $X$ is a [normed space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Normed_space) and $Y$ is a quasi-complete [locally convex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Locally_convex) TVS then the set of all [compact linear maps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_linear_map) of $X$ into $Y$ is a closed vector subspace of $L_b(X;Y)$.[^n4]
- Every quasi-complete [infrabarrelled space](wikipedia://en/63950387) is barreled.[^n5]
- If $X$ is a quasi-complete locally convex space then every weakly bounded subset of the continuous dual space is [strongly bounded](wikipedia://en/63951372).[^n5]
- A quasi-complete [nuclear space](wikipedia://en/2702957) then $X$ has the [Heine–Borel property](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heine–Borel_property).[^n6]

:::

## Examples and sufficient conditions {#Examples_and_sufficient_conditions}

Every complete TVS is quasi-complete.[^n7] The product of any collection of quasi-complete spaces is again quasi-complete.[^n2] The projective limit of any collection of quasi-complete spaces is again quasi-complete.[^n8] Every [semi-reflexive space](wikipedia://en/22124711) is quasi-complete.[^n9]

The quotient of a quasi-complete space by a closed vector subspace may *fail* to be quasi-complete.

### Counter-examples {#Counter-examples}

There exists an [LB-space](wikipedia://en/63848931) that is not quasi-complete.[^n10]
