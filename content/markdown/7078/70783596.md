# Cathryn S. Dippo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cathryn S. Dippo"}

**Cathryn S. Dippo** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).[^o2] She became a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 1989.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

She obtained a Ph.D. in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) from [George Washington University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Washington_University).[^o2]

## Work {#Work}

In the mid-1980s she began the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation)/[American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)/[BLS](wikipedia://en/72528) Senior Research Fellow Program. In the late 1980s she began the BLS Behavioral Science Research Center. She retired as associate commissioner of the Office of Survey Methods Research at the [Bureau of Labor Statistics](wikipedia://en/72528) in the first decade of the 2000s. While at the [Bureau of Labor Statistics](wikipedia://en/72528) she also chaired the Current Population Survey Redesign and the FedStats R&D Working Group.[^o2][^o6]

She also worked as a [referee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scholarly_peer_review) for statistical journals.[^o2]

## Affiliations {#Affiliations}

From 1985 to 1986 she was a chairperson of the [methodology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Methodology) section of the [Washington Statistical Society](wikipedia://en/68694607) (WSS); the WSS is a chapter of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988). From 1986 to 1988 she was a representative-at-large of the WSS. In 1989 she became a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/51140705), which she also became a lifetime member of at some point.[^o8][^o3] She served as president of the WSS from 1989 to 1990.[^o9] She was a Curtis Jacobs Memorial Award Coordinator for the WSS, and on their Curtis Jacobs Memorial Committee, from 1991 to 1993. In 1991 she also received the WSS Presidents' Award as part of the [ASA](wikipedia://en/341988)-150 Committee. From 1992 to 1993 she was on the WSS's Committee on [ASA](wikipedia://en/341988) Fellows.[^o9]

She also became a member of the [Caucus for Women in Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucus_for_Women_in_Statistics).[^o3]
