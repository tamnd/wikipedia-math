# Karl Gustav Jöreskog

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Gustav Jöreskog"}

**Karl Gustav Jöreskog** (born 25 April 1935) is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). Jöreskog is a professor emeritus at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), and a co-author (with Dag Sörbom) of the [LISREL](wikipedia://en/17249082) statistical program. He is also a member of the [Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Swedish_Academy_of_Sciences). Jöreskog received his bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University). He is also a former student of [Herman Wold](wikipedia://en/454834). He was a statistician at [Educational Testing Service (ETS)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Testing_Service) and a visiting professor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).

:::

## Research {#Research}

Jöreskog proposed a reliable numerical method for computing [maximum-likelihood](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maximum-likelihood) estimates in [factor analysis](wikipedia://en/253492); similarly reliable methods were also proposed by Gerhard Derflinger, Robert Jennrich, and Stephen M. Robinson at roughly the same time. Jöreskog's [Fortran](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fortran) codes helped to popularize factor analysis around the world. While working at the [Educational Testing Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_Testing_Service) and giving lectures at Princeton University, Jöreskog proposed a linear model for the analysis of covariance structures, a fundamental contribution to [structural equation modeling (SEM)](wikipedia://en/2007748).

His other research interests include [multivariate analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multivariate_analysis), [item response theory](wikipedia://en/420159), [statistical computing](wikipedia://en/15832717), and factor-analysis in [geology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geology).

## Awards {#Awards}

- In 1974 he was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o5]
- In 2007 Jöreskog received the Award for distinguished scientific applications of psychology[^o6] from the [American Psychological Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Psychological_Association) (APA).
- In 2004 he was awarded [The Rudbeck Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Rudbeck_Medal)[^o7] by Uppsala University.
