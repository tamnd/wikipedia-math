# Clemency Montelle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Clemency Montelle"}

**Clemency Montelle** (born 8 July 1977) is a New Zealand historian of mathematics known for her research on [Indian mathematics](wikipedia://en/1848052) and [Indian astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indian_astronomy). She is a professor of mathematics at the [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury), and a fellow of the New Zealand India Research Institute of the [Victoria University of Wellington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Wellington).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Montelle is originally from Christchurch. She earned first class honours in mathematics and classical studies at the University of Canterbury in 1999, and completed a master's degree there in 2000. It was not until the fourth year of her studies that, finding a copy of [Euclid](wikipedia://en/9331)'s *[Elements](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elements_%28Euclid%29)* in the original Greek, she realized that she could reconcile her two interests by working in the history of mathematics.

She became a [Fulbright Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Scholar) at [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University), where she learned [Cuneiform](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cuneiform), [Sanskrit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanskrit), and [Arabic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arabic). She completed a Ph.D. in the history of mathematics there in 2005; at Brown, her faculty mentors included [David Pingree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Pingree), [Alice Slotsky](wikipedia://en/65349651), and [Kim Plofker](wikipedia://en/40242283).

## Service {#Service}

Montelle was vice president of the Commission for the History of Ancient and Medieval Astronomy for the 2017–2021 term. In 2026, she was awarded the [Agnes Mary Clerke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agnes_Mary_Clerke) Medal for the History of Astronomy by the [Royal Astronomical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Astronomical_Society). [^o4]

## Books {#Books}

Montelle is the author of the book *Chasing Shadows: Mathematics, Astronomy, and the Early Reckoning of Eclipse theory* (Johns Hopkins Press, 2011). With [Kim Plofker](wikipedia://en/40242283), she is the coauthor of *Sanskrit Astronomical Tables* (Springer, 2019).
