# George Glauberman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="George Glauberman"}

**George Isaac Glauberman** (born 1941) is a [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) who works on [finite simple groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finite_simple_group). He proved the [ZJ theorem](wikipedia://en/9758699) and the [Z<sup>\*</sup> theorem](wikipedia://en/9757648).

:::

Born in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City) on March 3, 1941, Glauberman did his undergraduate studies at the [Polytechnic Institute of Brooklyn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polytechnic_Institute_of_New_York_University), graduating in 1961, and earned a master's degree from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1962.[^o3] He obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) degree from the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1965, under the supervision of [Richard Bruck](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Richard_Bruck). He has had 22 PhD students, including [Ahmed Chalabi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ahmed_Chalabi) and [Peter Landrock](wikipedia://en/46710302). He has co-authored with [J.&nbsp;L. Alperin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J.&nbsp;L._Alperin), [Simon P. Norton](wikipedia://en/1641166), [Zvi Arad](wikipedia://en/11423028), and Justin Lynd.

In 1970 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) at [Nice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nice). In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o5]
