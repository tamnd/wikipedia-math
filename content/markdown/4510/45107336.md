# Denis Serre

*Image: Denis Serre.png.* Denis Serre

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Denis Serre"}

**Denis Serre** (born 1 November 1954) is a French mathematician who works as a professor at the [École normale supérieure de Lyon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_de_Lyon), where he has chaired the mathematics department since 2012.[^o2] His research concerns [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [hydrodynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrodynamics), and [conservation laws](wikipedia://en/6956).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Serre was born in [Nancy, France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nancy,_France);[^o2] he is the nephew of mathematician [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483).[^o4] He obtained his [agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation) in 1977, and his doctorat de troisième cycle in 1978, before finishing a [D.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) in 1982 from the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) under the supervision of [Roger Temam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Temam).[^o2] He worked as a student teacher at the ENS de St-Cloud (a precursor institution to the ENS de Lyon) from 1974 to 1978, at the [Centre national de la recherche scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) in Orsay from 1978 to 1983, and at the [University of St. Etienne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Monnet_University) from 1983 to 1987, before returning to the ENS de Lyon in 1987.[^o2]

## Publications {#Publications}

Serre is the author of several books, including

- *Systems of Conservation Laws* (2 vols., Cambridge University Press, 1999 & 2000).[^o7]
- *Matrices: Theory and Applications* (Springer, [Graduate Texts in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/7392980) 216, 2002; 2nd ed., 2010).[^o8]
- *Multidimensional Hyperbolic Partial Differential Equations: First-Order Systems and Applications* (with Sylvie Benzoni-Gavage, Oxford University Press, 2007). [^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

From 1992 to 1997 Serre was a junior member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France).[^o2] He became a knight of the [Ordre des Palmes Académiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_des_Palmes_Académiques) in 2012.[^o2] In 2014, he was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to [hyperbolic conservation laws](wikipedia://en/1575825#Hyperbolic_system_and_conservation_laws) and mathematical exposition."[^o11] On November 7–9, 2014, a conference on conservation laws was held in honor of his 60th birthday.[^o12] He received the [Blaise Pascal](wikipedia://en/4068) Prize in 1990, the [Institut Henri Poincaré](wikipedia://en/10513595) Prize in 2000 and the [Jacques-Louis Lions](wikipedia://en/3735500) Prize of the Académie des Sciences in 2017.
