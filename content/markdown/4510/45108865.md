# John M. Sullivan (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John M. Sullivan"}

**John Matthew Sullivan** (born February 25, 1963) is an American mathematician who works in Germany as a professor at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin). His research includes work on [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008), [constant-mean-curvature surfaces](wikipedia://en/35544695), [mathematical foams](wikipedia://en/2795211), [scientific visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_visualization), and [mesh generation](wikipedia://en/3050716).[^o2]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Sullivan was born in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey), and graduated [summa cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Summa_cum_laude) from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) in 1985. He earned a master's degree from the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) in 1986, and a doctorate from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1990 under the supervision of [Frederick J. Almgren, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_J._Almgren,_Jr.)[^o2]

## Career {#Career}

After postdoctoral studies at [The Geometry Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Geometry_Center) and the [Mathematical Sciences Research Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Sciences_Research_Institute), Sullivan joined the faculty of the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign) in 1997. He moved to Berlin in 2003, and chaired the [Berlin Mathematical School](wikipedia://en/14614258) from 2012 to 2014.[^o2]

## Awards {#Awards}

In 2012, he became one of the inaugural [Fellows of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society).[^o7]
