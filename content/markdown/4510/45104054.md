# Gordana Matic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gordana Matic"}

**Gordana Matic** is a [Croatian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Croats)-American mathematician[^o2] who works as a professor at the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia).[^o3] Her research concerns [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774) and [contact geometry](wikipedia://en/476398).

:::

Matic earned her doctorate from the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) in 1986, under the supervision of [Ronald J. Stern](wikipedia://en/46991782), and worked as a [C.L.E. Moore instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C.L.E._Moore_instructor) at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) before joining the University of Georgia faculty.[^o5][^o6]

Matic was the Spring 2012 speaker in the [University of Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas) Distinguished Women in Mathematics Lecture Series.[^o7] In 2014, she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to low-dimensional and contact topology."[^o8]
