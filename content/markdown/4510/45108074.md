# Irina Mitrea

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Irina Mitrea"}

**Irina Mitrea** is a Romanian-American mathematician who works as professor and department chair at the Department of Mathematics of [Temple University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Temple_University).[^o3] She is known for her contributions to [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147), particularly on the interface of this field with [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694), [scattering theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scattering_theory), [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) and [validated numerics](wikipedia://en/60531670). She is also known for her efforts to promote mathematics among young women.[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mitrea earned a master's degree from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) in 1993,[^o13] and completed her doctorate in 2000 at the [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota) under the supervision of [Carlos Kenig](wikipedia://en/54764530) and Mikhail Safonov. Her dissertation was *Spectral Properties of Elliptic Layer Potentials on Non-Smooth Domains*. Her publications include over fifty research articles and three books published by [Springer‐Verlag](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), [Birkhäuser](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhäuser), and [De Gruyter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Gruyter).[^o11] After temporary positions at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) and [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University), she joined the faculty of the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia) in 2004, and earned tenure there in 2007.[^o13] She also taught at the [Worcester Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Worcester_Polytechnic_Institute) before moving to Temple.[^o10] She is the founder of the Girls and Mathematics Program at Temple University, a week-long summer camp in mathematics for middle-school girls.[^o4] She is a member of the National Alliance for Doctoral Studies in the Mathematical Sciences, an organization providing mentorship to "build a national community of students, faculty, and staff who will work together to transform our departments, colleges, and universities into institutions where all students are welcome."[^o11]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2008, Mitrea won the [Ruth I. Michler Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/62170910) of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](https://awm-math.org/awards/awm-fellows/).[^o13] In 2014, she was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to partial differential equations and related fields as well as outreach to women and under-represented minorities at all educational levels."[^o14] Also in 2014, Mitrea was awarded a [Von Neumann Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Neumann_Fellowship) at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey).[^o15] In 2015 she received the AWM Service Award from the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o7] She is part of the 2019 class of fellows of the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698).[^o16]
