# Andrei Suslin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Andrei Suslin"}

Mikhail Suslin

**Andrei Suslin** (Андре́й Алекса́ндрович Су́слин, sometimes transliterated **Souslin**) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who contributed to [algebraic K-theory](wikipedia://en/598500) and its connections with [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He was a Trustee Chair and Professor of mathematics at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University).[^o3]

:::

He was born on 27 December 1950 in [St. Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St._Petersburg), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia). As a youth, he was an "all Leningrad" gymnast.[^o4] He received his PhD from [Leningrad University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad_University) in 1974; his thesis was titled *Projective modules over polynomial rings*.[^o5]

In 1976 he and [Daniel Quillen](wikipedia://en/435519) independently proved [Serre's conjecture](wikipedia://en/539562) about the triviality of algebraic [vector bundles](wikipedia://en/276524) on [affine space](wikipedia://en/298834).

In 1982 he and [Alexander Merkurjev](wikipedia://en/31902178) proved the [Merkurjev–Suslin theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merkurjev–Suslin_theorem) on the norm residue homomorphism in Milnor K2-theory, with applications to the [Brauer group](wikipedia://en/404078).

Suslin was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 1978 and 1994, and he gave a plenary invited address at the Congress in 1986. He was awarded the [Frank Nelson Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) in Algebra in 2000 by the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) for his work on [motivic cohomology](wikipedia://en/809314).[^o6]

In 2010 special issues of *Journal of K-theory*[^o7] and of *Documenta Mathematica* [^o8] have been published in honour of his 60th birthday.

He died in July 2018.[^o9]
