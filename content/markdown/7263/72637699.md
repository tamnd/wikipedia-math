# Long Yiming

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Long Yiming"}

**Long Yiming** (born October 1948) is a Chinese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and professor at [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University). He is a fellow of the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o2][^o3]

:::

His research focuses on [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298), [nonlinear functional analysis](wikipedia://en/8546912), [celestial mechanics](wikipedia://en/102182), the [variation method](wikipedia://en/37487265), and the [Hamiltonian system](wikipedia://en/1197531).[^o2]

He is a distinguished professor of the [Changjiang Scholars Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Changjiang_Scholars_Program) and the Director-General of the [Chinese Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Mathematical_Society).[^o2]

## Education {#Education}

Between 1978 and 1981, he was a student at [Nankai University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nankai_University), where he obtained a master's degree. Between August 1983 and December 1987, he was a student at the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin-Madison), where he received a doctorate degree in mathematics.[^o2]
