# Surface subgroup conjecture

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Surface subgroup conjecture"}

*Image: Jeremy Kahn and Vladimir Markovic.jpg.* Jeremy Kahn and [Vladimir Markovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Markovic) who first proved the conjecture, Aarhus, 2012.

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **surface subgroup conjecture** of [Friedhelm Waldhausen](wikipedia://en/20163961) states that the [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) of every [closed](wikipedia://en/669475), [irreducible](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Irreducible_manifold) [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) with infinite [fundamental group](wikipedia://en/11004) has a surface subgroup. By "surface subgroup" we mean the fundamental group of a closed surface not the 2-sphere. This problem is listed as Problem 3.75 in [Robion Kirby](wikipedia://en/2932424)'s problem list.[^o2]

:::

Assuming the [geometrization conjecture](wikipedia://en/220642), the only open case was that of closed [hyperbolic 3-manifolds](wikipedia://en/1237700). A [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) of this case was announced in the summer of 2009 by [Jeremy Kahn](wikipedia://en/48625747) and [Vladimir Markovic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Markovic) and outlined in a talk August 4, 2009 at the FRG (Focused Research Group) Conference hosted by the University of Utah. A preprint appeared in the arxiv.org server in October 2009.[^o3] Their paper was published in the *[Annals of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/2785)* in 2012.[^o3] In June 2012, Kahn and Markovic were given the [Clay Research Awards](wikipedia://en/664167) by the [Clay Mathematics Institute](wikipedia://en/7655) at a ceremony in [Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford).
