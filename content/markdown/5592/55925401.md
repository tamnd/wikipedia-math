# Mariana Kotzeva

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mariana Kotzeva"}

**Mariana Mihaylova Kotzeva** (Мариана Михайлова Коцева, born 12 March 1967) is a Bulgarian [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) and [econometrician](wikipedia://en/10390). Since the beginning of 2017 she has been the acting director-general of [Eurostat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eurostat), the directorate-general of the [European Commission](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Commission) responsible for providing statistical information to the institutions of the [European Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Union) and for harmonizing statistical methods across the Union. She is also the former president of the [National Statistical Institute of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Statistical_Institute_%28Bulgaria%29).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Kotzeva was born in [Byala, Ruse Province](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Byala,_Ruse_Province). She earned a master's degree in economics in 1985 (specializing in [economic statistics](wikipedia://en/340363)) from the [University of National and World Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_National_and_World_Economy) in [Sofia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sofia), Bulgaria. She earned a second master's degree in economics in 1993, from the [Central European University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Central_European_University) in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague) under its charter from the [University of the State of New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_State_of_New_York). In 1995 she completed a Ph.D. in economics, specializing in statistics and [demography](wikipedia://en/45642), from the [University of National and World Economy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_National_and_World_Economy).

## Career {#Career}

Since 2003 Kotzeva has been on the faculty of the University of National and World Economy. Additionally, before joining Eurostat in 2014, she has worked for the [Serbian Ministry of Labour and Employment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Labour,_Employment,_Veteran_and_Social_Policy_%28Serbia%29), the [United Nations Development Programme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations_Development_Programme), the Bulgarian Ministry of Labour and Social Policy, and the [National Statistical Institute of Bulgaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Statistical_Institute_%28Bulgaria%29). She was the head of the National Statistical Institute from 2008 to 2012.

In 2012, she joined Eurostat as "adviser hors classe". She became deputy director-general at Eurostat in 2014, after the retirement of Marie Bohatá, and at that time became "the most senior Bulgarian civil servant in the European Union’s administration". She replaced [Walter Radermacher](wikipedia://en/75535195) as acting director-general in 2017.
