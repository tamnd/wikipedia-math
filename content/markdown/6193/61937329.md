# Margit Voigt

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Margit Voigt"}

**Margit Voigt** is a German mathematician specializing in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743). She is a professor of [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476) at the [University of Applied Sciences Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Applied_Sciences_Dresden).

:::

Voigt completed her Ph.D. in 1992 at the [Technische Universität Ilmenau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Ilmenau). Her dissertation, *Über die chromatische Zahl einer speziellen Klasse unendlicher Graphen* \[*On the chromatic number of a special class of infinite graphs*\] was jointly supervised by Rainer Bodendiek and [Hansjoachim Walther](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hansjoachim_Walther).

Her results include the first known [planar graph](wikipedia://en/24314) that requires five colors for [list coloring](wikipedia://en/690669), and a counterexample to a related conjecture that list coloring of planar graphs requires at most one more color than [graph coloring](wikipedia://en/426743) for the same graphs.
