# Stanislav Molchanov

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Stanislav Molchanov"}

**Stanislav Alexeyevich Molchanov** (Станислав Алексеевич Молчанов) is a Soviet and American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

From 1958 to 1963 he was a student at the Mathematical and Mechanical faculty, [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) (MSU), where he graduated in 1963 with a master's thesis *On one problem from the diffusion process theory* supervised by [Eugene Dynkin](wikipedia://en/1351540). At MSU Molchanov graduated in 1967 with Russian Candidate degree (Ph.D.) with thesis *Some problems in the Martin boundary theory* and in 1983 with Russian Doctor of Sciences degree (higher doctoral degree) with thesis *Spectral theory of random operators*. At MSU he was from 1966 to 1971 an assistant professor, from 1971 to 1988 an associate professor, and from 1988 to 1990 a full professor in the department of probability theory and mathematical statistics. He was a visiting professor from 1991 to 1992 at the University of California, Irvine and from 1992 to 1993 at the University of Southern California. In 1994 Molchanov became a full professor at the University of North Carolina at Charlotte.[^o3]

He has been a visiting professor at the International School for Probability Theory in St. Flour, the Ruhr-Universität Bochum, the ETH Zurich, the EPFL Lausanne, the TU Berlin, Paris (University Paris IV and VI), Ottawa, Rome, Santiago de Chile, Cambridge's [Isaac Newton Institute](wikipedia://en/736837), and Bielefeld.[^o3]

His research deals with geometrical approaches to [Markov processes](wikipedia://en/60876) ([Martin boundaries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_boundary) and diffusion on Riemannian manifolds) and with spectral theory (localization in random media and spectral properties of Riemannian manifolds). His research on applied mathematics includes physical processes and fields in disordered structures involving averaging and intermittency with applications to geophysics, astrophysics, oceanography. With regard to physical processes, he has done research on wave processes in periodic and random media, quantum graphs, and applications to optics.[^o3]

With Ilya Goldsheid and [Leonid Pastur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonid_Pastur) he proved in 1977 localization in the Anderson model in one dimension.[^o4] With [Michael Aizenman](wikipedia://en/22411175), Molchanov proved in 1993 localization for large coupling constants and energies near the edge of the spectrum.[^o5]

In 1990 he was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in Kyoto.[^o6] In 2012 he became a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).
