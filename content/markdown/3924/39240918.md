# Nu function

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Nu function"}

In mathematics, the **nu function** is a generalization of the [reciprocal gamma function](wikipedia://en/4017902) of the [Laplace transform](wikipedia://en/18610).

:::

Formally, it can be defined as

$$\begin{aligned} \nu(x) & \equiv \int_0^\infty \frac{x^t \, dt}{\Gamma(t+1)} \\[10pt] \nu(x,\alpha) & \equiv \int_0^\infty \frac{x^{\alpha+t} \, dt}{\Gamma(\alpha+t+1)} \end{aligned}$$

where $\Gamma(z)$ is the [Gamma function](wikipedia://en/12316).[^o2][^o3]
