# Guy David (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guy David"}

**Guy David** (born 1957) is a French mathematician, specializing in analysis.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

David studied from 1976 to 1981 at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure), graduating with *[Agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* and *[Diplôme d'études approfondies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diplôme_d'études_approfondies)* (DEA). At the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud) (Paris XI) he received in 1981 his doctoral degree (*Thèse du 3ème cycle*) and in 1986 his higher doctorate (*Thèse d'État*) with thesis *Noyau de Cauchy et opérateurs de Caldéron-Zygmund* supervised by Yves Meyer. David was from 1982 to 1989 an *attaché de recherches* (research associate) at the *Centre de mathématiques Laurent Schwartz* of the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS). At the University of Paris-Sud he was from 1989 to 1991 a professor and from 1991 to 2001 a professor first class, and is since 1991 a professor of the *Classe exceptionelle*.[^o5]

David is known for his research on [Hardy spaces](wikipedia://en/314780) and on singular integral equations using the methods of [Alberto Calderón](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alberto_Calderón). In 1998 David solved a special case of a problem of [Vitushkin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoli_Georgievich_Vitushkin).[^o6] Among other topics, David has done research on Painlevé's problem of geometrically characterizing removable singularities for bounded functions; [Xavier Tolsa](wikipedia://en/61641556)'s solution of Painlevé's problem is based upon David's methods. With [Jean-Lin Journé](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Lin_Journé) he proved in 1984 the [T(1) Theorem](wikipedia://en/24697098),[^o7] for which they jointly received the [Salem Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salem_Prize). The T(1) Theorem is of fundamental importance for the theory of singular integral operators of Calderón-Zygmund type. David also did research on the conjecture of [David Mumford](wikipedia://en/394594) and Jayant Shah in image processing and made contributions to the theory of Hardy spaces; the contributions were important for [Jones' traveling salesman theorem in $\mathbb{R}^2$](wikipedia://en/28177884). David has written several books in collaboration with [Stephen Semmes](wikipedia://en/31859356).[^o5]

- 2004 — *Prix Servant*
