# María Emilia Caballero

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="María Emilia Caballero"}

Caballero **María Emilia Caballero Acosta** is a Mexican mathematician specializing in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), including [Lévy processes](wikipedia://en/1025748), [branching processes](wikipedia://en/377202), [Markov processes](wikipedia://en/60876), and Lamperti representations (an exponential relation between Markov processes and Lévy processes). She is a professor in the Faculty of Sciences and Researcher in the Institute of Sciences of the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico) (UNAM).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After doing her undergraduate studies at the [National Autonomous University of Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Autonomous_University_of_Mexico), Caballero went to [Pierre and Marie Curie University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_and_Marie_Curie_University) in France for graduate study in mathematics. She completed a [doctorat de troisième cycle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorat_de_troisième_cycle) in 1973, with the dissertation *Quelques proprietes en theorie du potentiel* in [potential theory](wikipedia://en/1431342), jointly supervised by Marcel Brelot and [Paul Malliavin](wikipedia://en/10459388). Her interest in probability theory developed out of this work and the probabilistic theory of potential.

Already in 1964, she had begun working as an adjunct professor at the [Escuela Nacional Preparatoria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escuela_Nacional_Preparatoria) and as an assistant in the Faculty of Sciences at UNAM. On completing her doctorate in 1973, she took her present position at the Institute of Mathematics.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Caballero is a member of the [Mexican Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mexican_Academy_of_Sciences). She won UNAM's Juana de Asbaje Medal in 2004. In 2012 she won UNAM's National University Award, the first woman in the Institute of Mathematics to win this award.
