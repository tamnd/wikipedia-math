# Sergei Vasilyevich Kerov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sergei Vasilyevich Kerov"}

**Sergei Vasilyevich Kerov** (Russian: Сергей Васильевич Керов; born 21 June 1946 in [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad) died 30 July 2000) was a Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and university professor. His research included [operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987), [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [probability](wikipedia://en/23542) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200).[^o2][^o3]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Kerov was born in 1946 in [Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leningrad) (now St. Petersburg). His father Vasily Kerov was a teacher for [analytical chemistry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Analytical_chemistry) at a university in Leningrad and his mother Marianna Nikolayeva was an expert in [seed physiology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plant_physiology).[^o2]

Kerov studied at the [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University). He obtained a PhD in 1975 under the supervision of [Anatoly Vershik](wikipedia://en/9036441). He was then a professor at various universities in St. Petersburg, including the [Herzen Pedagogical University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herzen_State_Pedagogical_University) and the University of Saint Petersburg. From 1993 he did research at the [Steklov Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/984494) in St. Petersburg. In 1994 he received a [Sc.D. (Doctor of Science)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences) from the Steklov Institute for his work *Asymptotic Representation Theory of the Symmetric Group, with Applications to Analysis*. From 1995 he was a professor at the University of Saint Petersburg.[^o2]

In 2000 he died of a [brain tumor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brain_tumor).[^o2]

## Work {#Work}

A list of Kerov's scientific articles was published in the Journal of Mathematical Sciences.[^o6]

In 1977 he proved together with [Anatoly Vershik](wikipedia://en/9036441) a limit theorem for the [Plancherel measure](wikipedia://en/31244986) for the [symmetric group](wikipedia://en/28901) with a limiting shape which is now called *Vershik–Kerov curve*.[^o7][^o8] The same result was also independently proved by Logan and Shepp and thus is also called *Logan–Shepp curve*. The result was later improved by Kerov to a central limit theorem.[^o9]
