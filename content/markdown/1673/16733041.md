# Brooke Benjamin

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Brooke Benjamin"}

**Thomas Brooke Benjamin**, [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o3] (15 April 1929 – 16 August 1995) was an [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), best known for his work in [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988), especially in applications of [nonlinear differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_differential_equation).[^o4]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Benjamin was educated at [Wallasey Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Kingsway_Academy) on the Wirral, the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) (BEng. 1950) and [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) (MEng. 1952), before being awarded his doctorate at [King's College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College,_Cambridge) in 1955. He was a fellow of King's from 1955 to 1964.

From 1979 until his death in 1995 he was [Sedleian Professor of Natural Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sedleian_Professor_of_Natural_Philosophy) at the [Mathematical Institute](wikipedia://en/7905850), [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), and a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow#Oxford.2C_Cambridge_and_Dublin) of [The Queen's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen's_College,_Oxford).[^o9]

## Contributions {#Contributions}

The [Benjamin–Ono equation](wikipedia://en/16697638) describes one-dimensional [internal waves](wikipedia://en/900160) in deep water. It was introduced by Benjamin in 1967, and later studied also by Hiroaki Ono. Another equation named after Benjamin, the [Benjamin–Bona–Mahony equation](wikipedia://en/16703476), models long [surface gravity waves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surface_gravity_wave) of small amplitude. Benjamin studied it with [Jerry L. Bona](wikipedia://en/21528082) and J. J. Mahony in a 1972 paper.
