# Georges Reeb

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Georges Reeb"}

**Georges Henri Reeb** (12 November 1920 – 6 November 1993) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He worked in [differential topology](wikipedia://en/8562), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Differential_equations), topological [dynamical systems theory](wikipedia://en/990632) and [non-standard analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Non-standard_analysis).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Reeb was born in [Saverne](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saverne), [Bas-Rhin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bas-Rhin), [Alsace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alsace), to Theobald Reeb and Caroline Engel. He started studying mathematics at [University of Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strasbourg), but in 1939 the entire university was evacuated to [Clermont-Ferrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clermont-Ferrand) due to the [German occupation of France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_occupation_of_France).[^o4]

After the war, he completed his studies and in 1948 he defended his PhD thesis, entitled *Propriétés topologiques des variétés feuilletées* \[Topological properties of foliated manifolds\] and supervised by [Charles Ehresmann](wikipedia://en/605108).[^o5]

In 1952 Reeb was appointed professor at [Université Joseph Fourier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Joseph_Fourier) in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble) and in 1954 he visited the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179). From 1963 he worked at [Université Louis Pasteur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Louis_Pasteur) in [Strasbourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strasbourg).[^o4][^o6]

There, in 1965 he created with [Jean Leray](wikipedia://en/245430) and [Pierre Lelong](wikipedia://en/34789789) the series of meeting *Rencontres entre Mathématiciens et Physiciens Théoriciens*. in 1966 Reeb and [Jean Frenkel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Frenkel) founded the *Institute de Recherche mathématique Avancée*, the first university laboratory associated to the [Centre National de la Recherche Scientifique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique), which he directed between 1967 and 1972.[^o7]

In 1967 he was President of the [Société Mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205)[^o8] and in 1971 he was awarded the Prize Petit d'Ormoy.[^o4][^o6]

In 1991 Reeb received an honorary doctorate from [Albert-Ludwigs-Universität Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Albert-Ludwigs-Universität_Freiburg) and from [Université de Neuchâtel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Neuchâtel). He died in 1993 in Strasbourg when he was 72 years old.[^o4][^o6]

## Research {#Research}

*Image: Serre thom reeb 1949.jpg.* From left to right: [René Thom](wikipedia://en/61633), Jean Arbault, [Jean-Pierre Serre](wikipedia://en/205483), [Josiane Serre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Josiane_Serre), Jean Braconnier (mathematician) and Georges Reeb at the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) in 1949

Reeb was the founder of the topological theory of [foliations](wikipedia://en/371227), a geometric structure on [smooth manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifolds) which partition them in smaller pieces. In particular, he described what is now called the [Reeb foliation](wikipedia://en/2604269), a foliation of the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792), whose leaves are all [diffeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Diffeomorphic) to $\mathbb{R}^2$, except one, which is a 2-[torus](wikipedia://en/74800).[^o10]

One of its first significant result, [Reeb stability theorem](wikipedia://en/27512200), describes the local structure foliations around a [compact](wikipedia://en/6042) leaf with finite [holonomy group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holonomy_group).

His works on foliations had also applications in [Morse theory](wikipedia://en/348560). In particular, the [Reeb sphere theorem](wikipedia://en/26558892) says that a compact manifold with a function with exactly two [critical points](wikipedia://en/1951424) is [homeomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homeomorphic) to the sphere. In turn, in 1956 this was used to prove that the [Milnor spheres](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milnor_sphere), although not diffeomorphic, are homeomorphic to the sphere $S^7$.[^o11]

Other important geometric concepts named after him include the [Reeb graph](wikipedia://en/24126396)[^o12] and the [Reeb vector field](wikipedia://en/10761147) associated to a [contact form](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contact_form).

Towards the end of his career, Reeb become a supporter of the theory of [non-standard analysis](wikipedia://en/21708) by [Abraham Robinson](wikipedia://en/46024), coining the slogan "The naïve integers don't fill up $\mathbb{N}$"[^o13][^o14] and working on its applications to [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087).[^o15]
