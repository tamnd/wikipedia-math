# E. F. Robertson

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="E. F. Robertson"}

**Edmund Frederick Robertson** (born 1 June 1943) is a British mathematician who is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) of [pure mathematics](wikipedia://en/457210) at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews).[^o4]

:::

## Work {#Work}

Robertson is one of the creators of the [MacTutor History of Mathematics archive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MacTutor_History_of_Mathematics_archive), along with John J. O'Connor. Robertson has written more than 100 research articles, mainly on the theory of [groups](wikipedia://en/19447) and [semigroups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semigroups). He is also the author or co-author of 17 textbooks.[^o4]

Robertson obtained a [Bachelor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) degree at the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews) in 1965. He then went to the [University of Warwick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warwick), where he received a [Master of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) degree in 1966 and a [Doctor of Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) degree in 1968.[^o4]

In 1998, he was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh).[^o5]

In 2015, he received together with his colleague O'Connor, the [Hirst Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hirst_Prize_and_Lectureship) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) for his work on the MacTutor History of Mathematics archive.[^o6] His thesis on "Classes of Generalised Nilpotent Groups" was done with Stewart E. Stonehewer.[^o7]
