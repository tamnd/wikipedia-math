# Elongated bicupola

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated bicupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated bicupolae** are two infinite sets of [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed by adjoining two *n*-gonal [cupolas](wikipedia://en/689743) to an *n*-gonal [prism](wikipedia://en/283007). They have 2*n* [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654), 4*n* [squares](wikipedia://en/659939), and 2 *n*-gons. The ortho forms have the cupola aligned, while gyro forms are counter aligned.
