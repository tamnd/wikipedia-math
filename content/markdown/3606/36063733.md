# Moncef Ben Salem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Moncef Ben Salem"}

*Image: Moncef Ben Salem.jpg.* Moncef Ben Salem

**Moncef Ben Salem** (المنصف بن سالم; February 1, 1953 – March 24, 2015) was a Tunisian politician and university professor.[^o2][^o3] He served as minister of [higher education](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Higher_education) and [scientific research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_research) under Prime Minister [Hamadi Jebali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamadi_Jebali).[^o4][^o5][^o6][^o7]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

### Early life {#Early_life}

Moncef Ben Salem was born on 1 April 1953. He received a BA degree in Mathematics and Physics 1972 and a master's degree in Maths in 1974.[^o5][^o6][^o10] He received PhDs in Maths and Physics from the [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse) and [Supméca](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supméca) in Paris.[^o5][^o6][^o10] He was a member of the [Union of Arab Mathematicians and Physicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Union_of_Arab_Mathematicians_and_Physicians) from 1980 to 1987.[^o10] He co-founded the [Ecole Nationale d'Ingénieurs de Sfax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Nationale_d'Ingénieurs_de_Sfax) in 1983 [^o11]

### Political activism and career {#Political_activism_and_career}

He was a member of the [Ennahda Movement](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ennahda_Movement).[^o7] He was critical of Presidents [Habib Bourguiba](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habib_Bourguiba) and [Zine El Abidine Ben Ali](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zine_El_Abidine_Ben_Ali), calling Bourguiba a "[zionist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zionism)".[^o7] As a result of his political activism under Ben Ali, he was jailed for eighteen months from 1987 to 1989, and from 1990 to 1993.[^o7] In 1987, his passport was revoked, and he was forbidden from leaving Tunisia or travelling inside the country for twenty years, as were his children.[^o5][^o6] From 1993 to 2011, he was forced to live under [surveillance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Surveillance).[^o7] He was also forbidden to work as a university professor.[^o5][^o6] During those years, he was supported by the [Canadian Committee of Scientists and Scholars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadian_Committee_of_Scientists_and_Scholars) and the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] He later taught at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland) in the US, at the [French National Centre for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research) in France, Italy, Germany, Belgium, and at the [University of Sfax](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sfax) in Tunisia.[^o5][^o7][^o10]

### Minister {#Minister}

On 20 December 2011, after former President Ben Ali was deposed, he joined the [Jebali Cabinet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jebali_Cabinet) as Minister of Higher Education.[^o5][^o7][^o10]

### Death {#Death}

He died on March 24, 2015, leaving a widow and 4 children.[^o5][^o15][^o16]
