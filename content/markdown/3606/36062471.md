# Gyroelongated pyramid

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Gyroelongated pyramid"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **gyroelongated pyramids** (also called *augmented [antiprisms](wikipedia://en/1851)*) are an infinite set of [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed by adjoining an $n$-gonal [pyramid](wikipedia://en/3307045) to an $n$-gonal [antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851).[^o3]

:::

There are two *gyroelongated pyramids* that are [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) made from regular triangles, square, and pentagons. A triangular and hexagonal form can be constructed with [coplanar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coplanar) faces. Others can be constructed allowing for [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428).
