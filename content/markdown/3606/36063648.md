::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Elongated cupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **elongated cupolae** are an infinite set of polyhedra, constructed by adjoining an *n*-gonal [cupola](wikipedia://en/689743) to a 2*n*-gonal [prism](wikipedia://en/283007).

:::

There are three *elongated cupolae* that are [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) made from regular triangles, squares, and pentagons. Higher forms can be constructed with isosceles triangles. Adjoining a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) to a cube also generates a polyhedron, but has two pairs of coplanar faces, so is not a Johnson solid. Higher forms can be constructed without regular faces.
