# Gyroelongated bicupola

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gyroelongated bicupola"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **gyroelongated bicupolae** are an infinite sets of [polyhedra](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed by adjoining two *n*-gonal [cupolas](wikipedia://en/689743) to an *n*-gonal [antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851). The triangular, square, and pentagonal gyroelongated bicupola are three of five [Johnson solids](wikipedia://en/16034) which are [chiral](wikipedia://en/1170160), meaning that they have a "left-handed" and a "right-handed" form.

:::

Adjoining two [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) to a square antiprism also generates a polyhedron, but not a Johnson solid, as it is not convex. The hexagonal form is also a polygon, but has [coplanar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coplanar) faces. Higher forms can be constructed without regular faces.
