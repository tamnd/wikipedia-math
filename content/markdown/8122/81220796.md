# 10,000,000,000,000

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="10,000,000,000,000"}

**10,000,000,000,000** (**ten trillion** on the short scale; **ten billion** on the long scale; ten thousand [billion](wikipedia://en/1136363); ten million [million](wikipedia://en/1045999)) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [9,999,999,999,999](wikipedia://en/81145927#9,000,000,000,000_to_9,999,999,999,999) and preceding 10,000,000,000,001. It is known as 10000 arab, 100 kharab, 10 [lakh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lakh) [crore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crore), or 1 **nil** in the Indian numbering system.

:::
