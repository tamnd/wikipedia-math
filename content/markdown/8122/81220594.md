# Carol Alexander

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Carol Alexander"}

**Carol Olivia Alexander** is a British financial mathematician and academic. She is a professor of finance at the [University of Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sussex), known for her research on financial markets, derivatives, and risk management. She is the author of the four-volume textbook *Market Risk Analysis* (2008), which is widely cited in the field.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Alexander began her academic career as a postdoctoral fellow at the [University of Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Amsterdam) from 1981 to 1982,[^o4] before returning to the [University of Sussex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sussex) as a lecturer in mathematics and economics. From 1999 to 2012 she held the Chair of Financial Risk Management at the [ICMA Centre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICMA_Centre), part of the [Henley Business School at the University of Reading](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Henley_Business_School_at_the_University_of_Reading).[^o5] In 2012 she rejoined the University of Sussex as Professor of Finance and served as Head of Business and Management between 2013 and 2015, during which time the department transitioned to the Sussex Business School.[^o6]

She has also held visiting appointments, including a professorship at the [Peking University HSBC Business School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University_HSBC_Business_School).[^o7]

## Research {#Research}

Alexander’s research focuses on financial risk, derivatives,[^o9] volatility modelling,[^o10] and cryptocurrencies. Her recent publications have examined topics such as Bitcoin option pricing,[^o11] hedging with crypto futures, and the performance of risk measures including Value-at-Risk[^o12] and Expected Shortfall.[^o12]

Since 2013, Alexander has served as an editor of the *[Journal of Banking and Finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Banking_and_Finance)*.
