# Angela Mihai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Angela Mihai"}

*Image: Angela-25c.jpg.* L. Angela Mihai FIMA FLSW

**Loredana Angela Mihai** [FIMA](wikipedia://en/697139) [FLSW](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales) is a British [applied mathematician](wikipedia://en/24295969) and [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506). Originally from [Romania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romania), she is Professor of Applied Mathematics at [Cardiff University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardiff_University)[^o2] where her interdisciplinary work bridges [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) with [materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science) and [biomechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biomechanics).

:::

She contributed research on soft, smart, and biological materials through pioneering approaches like [stochastic](wikipedia://en/292222) [modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model) of [elasticity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elasticity_%28physics%29)[^o3] that bring mathematical rigour to real-world materials with complex behaviours.

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Mihai earned her PhD in numerical analysis at [Durham University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Durham_University) in 2005.[^o5] After postdoctoral research at the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford), she joined the Cardiff University academic staff as a lecturer in 2011.[^o2]

## Leadership and service {#Leadership_and_service}

From 2023 to 2025, she served as Vice President of the United Kingdom and Republic of Ireland Section of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Mihai is a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) and a Fellow of the [Learned Society of Wales](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Learned_Society_of_Wales).[^o9]
