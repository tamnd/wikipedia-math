# Robert Henry Risch

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Henry Risch"}

**Robert Henry Risch** (born 1939) is an American mathematician who worked on [computer algebra](wikipedia://en/23659805) and is known for his work on [symbolic integration](wikipedia://en/1652672), specifically the [Risch algorithm](wikipedia://en/289450).[^o3] This result was cited as a milestone in the development of mathematics:

:::

He is also known for results on algebraic properties of [elementary functions](wikipedia://en/10412).[^o5] He received his PhD from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1968[^o6] under the supervision of [Maxwell A. Rosenlicht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxwell_A._Rosenlicht).[^o7] After his PhD, he worked at the [Thomas J. Watson Research Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_J._Watson_Research_Center) Mathematics of AI group[^o8] and, between 1970 and 1972, the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o9]
