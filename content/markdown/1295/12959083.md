# Toshiyuki Kobayashi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Toshiyuki Kobayashi"}

*Image: Toshiyuki Kobayashi.jpg.* Toshiyuki Kobayashi

**Toshiyuki Kobayashi** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) known for his original work in the field of [Lie theory](wikipedia://en/1595681), and in particular for the theory of discontinuous groups ([lattice](wikipedia://en/469246) in [Lie groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_groups)) and the application of geometric analysis to [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200). He was a major developer in particular of the theory of discontinuous groups for non-Riemannian [homogeneous spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homogeneous_spaces) and the theory of discrete breaking symmetry in [unitary representation](wikipedia://en/292788) theory.

:::

He has been a member of the Science Council of Japan since 2006, Board of Trustees of the Mathematical Society of Japan (2003–2007), the Editor in Chief of the *Journal of the Mathematical Society of Japan* (2002–2006), and currently is the Managing Editor of the *Japanese Journal of Mathematics* since 2006.

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

- Ph.D./Doctor of Science, 1990, [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo)
- Assistant Professor, 1987–1991, Associate Professor, 1991–2001, [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo)
- Associate Professor, 2001–2003, [RIMS](wikipedia://en/1978962), [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University)
- Full Professor, 2003–2007, [RIMS](wikipedia://en/1978962), [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University)
- Full Professor, 2007-, [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo)
- Principal Investigator, 2011-, [Kavli](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kavli) [Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_the_Physics_and_Mathematics_of_the_Universe) ([IPMU](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_the_Physics_and_Mathematics_of_the_Universe))

He has held many invited positions including [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton, USA (1991–1992), [Mittag-Leffler Institute](wikipedia://en/10096452), Sweden (1995–1996), [Université de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Paris), [Université de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Paris) VI, France (1999), [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), USA (2000–2001, 2008), [Université de Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Paris) VII, France (2003), [Max-Planck-Institut für Mathematik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max-Planck-Institut_für_Mathematik) in Bonn, Germany (2007), [IHES](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IHES), France (2010; 2012; 2014; 2015; 2017; 2019; 2023; 2025), Jubilee Professorship, [Chalmers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chalmers_University), Sweden (2013) and [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) (2019)
