# Roger Maddux

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roger Maddux"}

**Roger Maddux** (born 1948) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [algebraic logic](wikipedia://en/7870034).

:::

He completed his B.A. at [Pomona College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomona_College) in 1969, and his Ph.D. in mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1978, where he was one of [Alfred Tarski](wikipedia://en/59920)'s last students. His career has been at [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University), where he fills a joint appointment in [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) and mathematics.

Maddux is primarily known for his work in [relation algebras](wikipedia://en/4363670) and [cylindric algebras](wikipedia://en/7852887), and as the inventor of [relational bases](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Relational_base).[^o2][^o3]
