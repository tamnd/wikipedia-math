# Universal algebraic geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Universal algebraic geometry"}

In [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), **universal algebraic geometry** generalizes the geometry of [rings](wikipedia://en/48404) to geometries of arbitrary [varieties of algebras](wikipedia://en/1021753), so that every variety of algebras has its own algebraic geometry. The two terms *[algebraic variety](wikipedia://en/248808)* and *variety of algebras* should not be confused.

:::
