# Kharitonov region

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kharitonov region"}

A **Kharitonov region** is a concept in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831). It arises in the study of the [stability of polynomials](wikipedia://en/2057699).

:::

Let $D$ be a [simply-connected set](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simply-connected_set) in the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628) and let $P$ be the polynomial family.

$D$ is said to be a **Kharitonov region** if

$$V_T^n(V_S^n)$$

is a subset of $P.$ Here, $V_T^n$ denotes the set of all [vertex polynomials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_polynomial) of complex interval polynomials $(T^n)$ and $V_S^n$ denotes the set of all vertex polynomials of real interval polynomials $(S^n).$
