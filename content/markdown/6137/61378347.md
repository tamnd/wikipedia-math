# David Crighton Medal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Crighton Medal"}

The **David Crighton Medal** is a biennial prize established in 2002 by the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139) (IMA) and the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) (LMS) for "services both to mathematics and to the mathematical community". The holders are usually fellows of both the IMA and LMS.[^o5][^o2]

:::

The award is issued on odd-numbered years, with its partner IMA-LMS prize, the [Christopher Zeeman Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christopher_Zeeman_Medal), issued on even-numbered years.[^o3]

## Rationale {#Rationale}

The medal was first awarded in 2003 and was established in memory of [David Crighton](wikipedia://en/1560570) FRS (1942–2000) "for services both to mathematics and to the mathematical community".[^o5][^o6]
