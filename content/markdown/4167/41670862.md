# Douglas Jones (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Douglas Jones"}

**Douglas Samuel Jones** (10 January 1922 – 29 November 2013) was a mathematician and electrical engineer known for his works in the field of [electromagnetism](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetism).[^o3]

:::

He was described by [The Scotsman](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Scotsman) as "one of the most outstanding British mathematicians of his generation".[^o3]

## Life {#Life}

Jones was born 10 January 1922 in [Corby](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corby) [Northamptonshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northamptonshire), and was educated at [Wolverhampton Grammar School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wolverhampton_Grammar_School).[^o3] He was the eldest of four children.[^o5]

In his spare time, Jones was known to enjoy golf, walking and photography.[^o6] He and his wife Ivy had two children.[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

In 1940, Jones began studying [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) at Clarendon Laboratory of the [Corpus Christi College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpus_Christi_College,_Oxford), [Oxford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University).[^o8][^o3]

Jones joined the [RAF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAF) in 1942 and graduated MA in applied mathematics from Oxford in 1947. He then went to [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) to study [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering), but switched to physics, studying under [Victor Weisskopf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victor_Weisskopf), [Herman Feshbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herman_Feshbach), and [Robley Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robley_D._Evans_%28physicist%29).[^o8][^o9] In the same years he led a research team looking at equipment for night fighter operations. Awarded MBE in 1945 for his work with the RAF.[^o3]

Jones then worked as a lecturer at [Manchester University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Victoria_University_of_Manchester). In 1957 he was appointed chair of Mathematics at the [University of Keele](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Keele).[^o3]

During his time at Keele, Jones wrote the book *The Theory of Electromagnetism* in 1964 which established him as a leader in this field.[^o3]

In 1965, Jones was appointed to the Ivory Chair of Applied Mathematics at Queen's College, Dundee, then part of the [University of St Andrews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_St_Andrews), but which became the [University of Dundee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Dundee) in 1967.[^o3][^o5]

Jones retired from the University of Dundee in 1992, gaining the title Emeritus Professor.[^o3]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

- 1945: Awarded [Most Excellent Order of the British Empire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_British_Empire)[^o8]
- 1964: Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematics and its Applications](wikipedia://en/697139)[^o8]
- 1967: Elected [Fellow of the Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellowship_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh)[^o8]
- 1968: Elected [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society)[^o8]
- 1973: Awarded [Keith Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keith_Medal) of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh)[^o8]
- 1975: Awarded Honorary [Doctor of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Science) by the [University of Strathclyde](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Strathclyde)[^o8]
- 1975: Awarded [Marconi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marconi_Prize) by the [Institution of Electrical Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institution_of_Electrical_Engineers)[^o8]
- 1980: Elected Honorary Fellow of [Corpus Christi College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Corpus_Christi_College,_Oxford)[^o8]
- 1981: Awarded van der Pol Gold Medal of the [International Union of Radio Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Radio_Science)[^o8]
- 1986: Awarded [Naylor Prize and Lectureship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naylor_Prize_and_Lectureship) of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097)[^o8]
- 1989: Fellow of the [Institution of Electrical Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institution_of_Electrical_Engineers)[^o8]
- 2013: Life member of the [Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Electrical_and_Electronics_Engineers)[^o8]

## Publications {#Publications}

- *Electrical and Mechanical Oscillations* (1961)
- *Theory of Electromagnetism* (1964)
- *Generalised Functions* (1966)
- *Introductory Analysis* (vol. 1, 1969; vol 2, 1970)
- *Methods in Electromagnetic Wave Propagation* (1979, 2nd edn 1994)
- *Elementary Information Theory* (1979)
- *The Theory of Generalised Functions* (1982)
- *Differential Equations and Mathematical Biology* (1983, 3rd edn 2010)
- *Acoustic and Electromagnetic Waves* (1986)
- *Assembly Programming and the 8086 Microprocessor* (1988)
- *80×86 Assembly Programming* (1991)
- *Introduction to Asymptotics* (1997)
