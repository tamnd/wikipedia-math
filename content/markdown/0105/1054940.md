# Edward Jan Habich

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Edward Jan Habich"}

**Edward Jan Habich** (Eduardo de Habich) (31 January 1835, [Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw) – 31 October 1909, [Lima](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lima), [Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru)) was a Polish [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3] In 1876, he founded the [National University of Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_University_of_Engineering) (Universidad Nacional de Ingeniería), a renowned engineering school in Lima, Peru.[^o4] He was a member of the Peruvian Geographic Society and an Honorary Citizen of [Peru](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peru). In his native Poland, he took part in the [January Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/January_Uprising) against the Russian Empire in 1863.

:::

## Burial {#Burial}

Edward Jan Habich is buried at the [Cementerio Presbítero Matías Maestro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presbitero_Maestro), Lima, Peru.
