# Chang's model

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Chang's model"}

In mathematical [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553), **Chang's model** is the smallest [inner model](wikipedia://en/1118686) of set theory closed under [countable](wikipedia://en/6026) sequences. It was introduced by (txt). More generally Chang introduced the smallest inner model closed under taking sequences of length less than κ for any infinite [cardinal](wikipedia://en/6173) κ. For κ countable this is the [constructible universe](wikipedia://en/343335), and for κ the first uncountable cardinal it is Chang's model.

:::

Chang's model is a model of [ZF](wikipedia://en/152214). [Kenneth Kunen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Kunen) proved in (txt) that the [axiom of choice](wikipedia://en/840) fails in Chang's model provided there are sufficient large cardinals, such as uncountably many [measurable cardinals](wikipedia://en/248102).
