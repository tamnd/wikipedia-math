# Beulah Russell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beulah Russell"}

**Beulah Russell**, christened **Beatrice Beulah Russell** and also known as **Bulah Russell**, (October 22, 1878 – February 22, 1940) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Beulah graduated from [Randolph–Macon Woman's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randolph_College) with a [Bachelor of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) in 1903, and graduated from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago) with a [Master of Arts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in 1919.[^o5][^o6]

## Career {#Career}

From 1903 to 1905 Beulah taught at [Lafayette College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette_College) as an instructor in mathematics. From 1905 to 1909 she taught at [Grenada College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenada_College) as a professor of mathematics. From 1909 to 1925 she taught at [Randolph–Macon Woman's College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Randolph_College) as an instructor of mathematics and an adjunct professor of mathematics. In 1925 she became an associate professor of mathematics at the [College of William & Mary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_of_William_&_Mary).[^o6][^o3]

In a 1923 edition of *[The American Mathematical Monthly](wikipedia://en/1179483)* it is recorded that Beulah was elected to membership in the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).[^o8]

In 1930 Beulah became the first female professor to attend the [Edinburgh Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/635940) Colloquium held in St Andrews, Scotland.[^o3]
