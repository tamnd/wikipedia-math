# Johannes Droste

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Droste"}

*Image: JohannesDroste.jpg.* Droste (1908)

**Johannes Droste** (28 May 1886 – 16 September 1963)[^n1] was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [theoretical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Theoretical_physicist), who was the second person after [Karl Schwarzschild](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Schwarzschild) to solve [Einstein's field equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Einstein's_field_equation). He was a professor of mathematics at [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) from 1930 to 1956.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

*Image: Johannes Droste. De huldiging van Professor Lorentz bij de Hollandse Maatschappij der Wetenschappen in 1925 (cropped).jpg.*

On 27 May 1916,[^n2] a student of [Hendrik Lorentz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Lorentz) at the University of Leiden,[^n1] Droste presented his solution of Einstein's equation to the Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences.[^n2] He obtained his doctorate in 1916.[^n1] From 1914 to 1919, Droste taught mathematics at the gymnasium in Gorinchem.[^n1] He then joined the University of Leiden and became professor of mathematics there in 1930.[^n1] Devoting himself to the teaching of mathematical analysis, he abandoned his research in physics, with the exception of a few contributions in elasticity and thermodynamics.[^n1]
