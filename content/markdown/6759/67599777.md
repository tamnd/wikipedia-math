# Christian Soize

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Christian Soize"}

**Christian Soize** (October 10, 1948) is a French engineer and applied mathematician known for his contributions in [computational mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_mechanics) and [uncertainty quantification](wikipedia://en/5987648). He is an emeritus Professor at the [Laboratoire Modélisation et Simulation Multi Echelle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laboratoire_Modélisation_et_Simulation_Multi_Echelle) at [Gustave Eiffel University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustave_Eiffel_University).

:::

## Contributions {#Contributions}

Soize is an expert in [uncertainty quantification](wikipedia://en/5987648) and [computational mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_mechanics), known for the development of several numerical methods and probabilistic methodologies to deal with uncertainties in [computational science and engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computational_science_and_engineering), standing out for two pioneering methods: the [nonparametric probabilistic approach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonparametric_probabilistic_approach),[^o4] to take into account model uncertainties in computational physical models, and, more recently, the [probabilistic learning on manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Probabilistic_learning_on_manifolds) (PLoM)[^o5] for statistical learning within small reduced datasets.

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Soize is the recipient of several academic awards, including: [Alfred M. Freudenthal Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_M._Freudenthal_Medal),[^o7] [IACM Computational Mechanics Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IACM_Computational_Mechanics_Award),[^o8] Officier dans l'[Ordre des Palmes académiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_des_Palmes_académiques),[^o9] Chevalier dans l'[Ordre national du Mérite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_national_du_Mérite),[^o10] [European Association of Structural Dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Association_of_Structural_Dynamics) (EASD) Senior Research Prize,[^o11] Fellow of the [Acoustical Society of America](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acoustical_Society_of_America),[^o12] [International Association for Structural Safety and Reliability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Association_for_Structural_Safety_and_Reliability) (IASSAR) Research Award,[^o13] Chevalier dans l'[Ordre des Palmes académiques](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordre_des_Palmes_académiques),[^o14] and the [Prix Madame-Victor-Noury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_Madame-Victor-Noury).[^o15]
