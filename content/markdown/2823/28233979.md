# Moritz Allé

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Moritz Allé"}

**Moritz Allé** (1837–1913)[^o3] was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) astronomer and mathematician, one of the teachers of [Nikola Tesla](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikola_Tesla).[^o4]

:::

## Scientific career {#Scientific_career}

Allé studied mathematics at the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna). After his university graduation, Allé startet his professional career as an assistant at the [Vienna Observatory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Observatory) in 1856.[^o6] He was appointed *Adjunkt* (senior position) at the observatory in [Kraków](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kraków) in 1859.[^o7] In 1860 he completed his PhD at the [University of Kiel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian-Albrechts-Universität_zu_Kiel). In 1862 he was appointed *Adjunkt* at the observatory in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague).[^o8] It was there where he completed his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in mathematics in 1863.[^o9] In 1867, Allé was appointed professor of mathematics at the [Joanneum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz_University_of_Technology) in [Graz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Graz)[^o10] and was elected as its [rector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rector_%28academia%29) in 1875/76.[^o11] In June 1882 Allé was appointed professor of mathematics at the *Deutsche Technische Hochschule* in Prague.[^o12] There, he was also dean for five periods[^o6] and different faculties[^o13][^o14] but declined serving as rector after his election in June 1887 for health reasons.[^o15] In 1896 he succeeded Josef Kolbe at the chair of mathematics at the [Polytechnic Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien) in [Vienna](wikipedia://en/55866), where he served as rector as well in 1900/01.[^o6] Upon his retirement in 1906, Allé was awarded the imperial title of a *[Hofrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geheimrat)*.[^o16]

## Private life {#Private_life}

Moritz Allé died on April 6, 1913, and was buried on April 8 in Baden.[^o6]

## Scientific contributions {#Scientific_contributions}

The early scientific work of Allé led to a number of publications in astronomy where he contributed on orbit determination of planets and comets.[^o19] He later gave up this field of research in favour of mathematics.[^o6]
