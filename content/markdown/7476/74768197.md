# 264 (number)

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="264"}

**264** (**two hundred \[and\] sixty-four**) is the [natural number](wikipedia://en/21474) following [263](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/263_%28number%29) and preceding [265](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/265_%28number%29).

:::

## In mathematics {#In_mathematics}

264 is:

- an even [composite number](wikipedia://en/82289).[^o4]
- a [Recontres number](wikipedia://en/3476722) with one fixed point.[^o5]
- the number of closed [self-avoiding walks](wikipedia://en/11186496) from the origin in [4](wikipedia://en/208161) steps.[^o6]
- the largest known number whose square is undulating: $264^2 = 69696$[^o7]
