# Lilly Yue

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lilly Yue"}

**Lilly Qinli Yue** is a US government statististician, known for her work on "real-world evidence" on health care from non-clinical sources such as billing data and product registries. She is deputy director of the Division of [Biostatistics](wikipedia://en/3878) in the Center for Devices and Radiological Health of the [Food and Drug Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Drug_Administration).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Yue has a bachelor's degree in mathematics, a master's degree in stochastic [operations research](wikipedia://en/43476), and a master's degree in mathematical statistics. She completed a Ph.D. at [Texas A&M University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_A&M_University) in 1996, with the dissertation *Chemometric Calibration and Partial Least Squares* supervised by [Michael Longnecker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Longnecker).

She was a senior statistician at [Eli Lilly and Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eli_Lilly_and_Company) before moving to the [Food and Drug Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Food_and_Drug_Administration) in 1998.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Yue was elected as a [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association) in 2014. In 2020, as part of the RWE Methods Group at the FDA, she was a recipient of the FDA's Excellence in [Data Science](wikipedia://en/35458904) Group Award, "for extraordinary achievements in the timely development and active promotion of novel statistical methods for leveraging real-world evidence to support regulatory decision-making".
