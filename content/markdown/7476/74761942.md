# David S. Stoffer

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David S. Stoffer"}

*Image: David-Stoffer.jpg.*

**David S. Stoffer** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), and [Professor Emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_Emeritus) of Statistics at the [University of Pittsburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh).[^o2] He is the author of several books on [time series analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Time_series_analysis), *Time Series Analysis and Its Applications: With R Examples*[^o3] with [Robert H. Shumway](wikipedia://en/82495744), *Nonlinear Time Series: Theory, Methods, and Applications with R Examples*[^o4] with R. Douc and E. Moulines, and *Time Series: A Data Analysis Approach Using R*[^o5] with Robert H. Shumway.

:::

Stoffer's research includes papers on the subject of time series analysis, for example, with missing data in *An Approach to Time Series Smoothing and Forecasting Using the EM Algorithm*[^o6] published in the *[Journal of Time Series Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Time_Series_Analysis)*, the spectral analysis of qualitative time series in *Spectral Analysis For Categorical Time Series: Scaling and the Spectral Envelope*[^o7] published in *[Biometrika](wikipedia://en/1734178),* and numerical methods for time series in *A Monte Carlo Approach to Nonnormal and Nonlinear State-Space Modeling*[^o8] and *Bootstrapping State-Space Models: Gaussian Maximum Likelihood Estimation and the Kalman Filter*[^o9] both published in the *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313).* Many of his contributions are explained with numerical examples in his [Springer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Nature) text, *Time Series Analysis and Its Applications: With R Examples* ().

In 1989 Stoffer, along with collaborators from the [University of Pittsburgh School of Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pittsburgh_School_of_Medicine), received the *[American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)'s Outstanding Statistical Application Award*[^o10] for the article *A Walsh-Fourier Analysis of the Effects of Moderate Maternal Alcohol Consumption on Neonatal Sleep-State Cycling*[^o11] published in the *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313).* The award was presented to Stoffer in person at the [Joint Statistical Meetings](wikipedia://en/15571662) (JSM) held in [Washington, D.C.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.).

Stoffer's research was continually supported by the [U.S. National Science Foundation (NSF)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation)[^o12] and in 2008, he became a Program Director for two years at the NSF Division of Mathematical Sciences (DMS) through the [Intergovernmental Personnel Act](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intergovernmental_Personnel_Act) (IPA) program.[^o13] He returned to NSF-DMS in the first part of 2018 as a Program Director.

In 2006, Stoffer was named [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/51140705),[^o14] and in 2020 he was named a *Wiley, [Journal of Time Series Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Time_Series_Analysis) Distinguished Author*.[^o15]

Stoffer has acted as Editor or Associate Editor for the *[Journal of Time Series Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Time_Series_Analysis), [Journal of Forecasting](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Forecasting), [Annals of the Institute of Statistical Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annals_of_the_Institute_of_Statistical_Mathematics), [Journal of Business and Economic Statistics](wikipedia://en/5854058),* and *[Journal of the American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/16179313)*.
