# Raegan Higgins

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Raegan Higgins"}

**Raegan J. Higgins** is an American mathematician and co-director of the [EDGE Program](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/EDGE_program). She is also one of the co-founders of the website [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006), which highlights the accomplishments of Black mathematicians.[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Higgins went to [Xavier University of Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_University_of_Louisiana) in [New Orleans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans), Louisiana.[^o5] She attended [University of Nebraska-Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln) for her graduate studies studying under the advisement of Lynn Erbe and Allan Clemens Peterson.[^o6] She graduated in 2008 and was one of the first two African-American women to earn a doctoral degree in Mathematics from University of Nebraska-Lincoln.[^o5] Higgins's doctoral dissertation was titled *Oscillation theory of dynamic equations on time scales.*[^o7]

## Career {#Career}

Higgins studies [time scales](wikipedia://en/276174) and its application to [mathematical biology](wikipedia://en/662088).[^o9]

Higgins, along with [Ami Radunskaya](wikipedia://en/52572510), is co-director of the EDGE program, which supports women who are pursuing graduate degrees and ultimately careers in the mathematical sciences. She participated in the EDGE program in 2002 as a graduate student. She was also a workshop facilitator from 2014 to 2017.[^o10] Professor Higgins became co-director of the program in 2017.[^o11]

In 2008, Higgins joined the faculty at [Texas Tech University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas_Tech_University) in the Department of Mathematics & Statistics.[^o12] She earned tenure and was promoted to Associate Professor, becoming the first tenured African American in the department.[^o5]

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

In 2020, Higgins received the [Association for Women in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/9858698) (AWM) [service award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AWM_Service_Award).[^o14] She has won several [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) grants for various programs in mathematical education.[^o15][^o16][^o17][^o18] She also earned the 2021 AWM [Gweneth Humphreys Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gweneth_Humphreys_Award).[^o19] She co-delivered an invited plenary address at the 2021 National Math Festival.[^o20]

Higgins' accomplishments earned her recognition by [Mathematically Gifted & Black](wikipedia://en/64552006) as a Black History Month 2018 Honoree.[^o21]
