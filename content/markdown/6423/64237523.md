# Christina Eubanks-Turner

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christina Eubanks-Turner"}

Academics

**Christina Eubanks-Turner** is a professor of mathematics in the Seaver College of Science and Engineering at [Loyola Marymount University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Loyola_Marymount_University) (LMU). Her academic areas of interest include graph theory, commutative algebra, [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471), and mathematical sciences diversification. She is also the Director of the Master's Program in Teaching Mathematics at LMU.[^o3]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Eubanks-Turner was born and raised in [New Orleans, Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Orleans) and enjoyed logic puzzles and creative thinking as a child.[^o5][^o6] She received her B.S. *cum laude* from [Xavier University of Louisiana](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xavier_University_of_Louisiana), a [historically black college](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Historically_black_colleges_and_universities), in 2002; she received her M.S. in 2004 and her Ph.D. in 2008—both from the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln).[^o5] Eubanks-Turner was one of the first two African Americans to receive a doctorate degree in mathematics from the University of Nebraska–Lincoln.[^o7][^o8] Her dissertation explored the topic of "Prime ideals in low-dimensional mixed polynomial/[power series](wikipedia://en/59958) rings."[^o9] Eubanks-Turner's [doctoral advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) was [Sylvia Wiegand](wikipedia://en/37495498).[^o10]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Eubanks-Turner was the first African American to receive tenure at LMU's College of Science and Engineering.[^o7]

Her research areas include specialized mathematical training that teachers need to teach math at the undergraduate and secondary levels.[^o3][^o12][^o13] Her pedagogy also includes the integration of equity issues[^o14] into teaching and an approach to mathematics education that addresses the whole student.[^o6] Her research in mathematics includes topics in [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) and [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990).[^o15]
