# T. Tony Cai

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="T. Tony Cai"}

[Cai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cai_%28surname%29) **Tianwen Tony Cai** (; born March, 1967) is a Chinese [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He is the Daniel H. Silberberg Professor of Statistics and Vice Dean at the [Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School_of_the_University_of_Pennsylvania). He is also professor of Applied Math & Computational Science Graduate Group, and associate scholar at the Department of Biostatistics, Epidemiology & Informatics, Perelman School of Medicine, University of Pennsylvania. In 2008 Cai received the [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Cai was born in [Rui'an](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rui'an), [Wenzhou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wenzhou), [Zhejiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang), China. In 1986, he graduated from the Department of Mathematics, [Hangzhou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_University) (previous and current Department of Mathematics, [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University)), at 18 years old.[^o4] In 1989, he received an M.Sc. from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Jiao_Tong_University) and in 1996, earned a PhD from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).[^o5]

## Career {#Career}

Cai was appointed the Dorothy Silberberg Professor at the [Wharton School of the University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wharton_School_of_the_University_of_Pennsylvania) from July 1, 2007 [^o7] to July 31, 2018 and has been the Daniel H. Silberberg Professor at Wharton since August 1, 2018. Cai has been the Vice Dean of the Wharton School since August 1, 2017.

Cai's research focuses on [high-dimensional statistics, statistical machine learning](wikipedia://en/19330658), [large-scale inference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiple_comparisons), [nonparametric function estimation](wikipedia://en/8771473), [functional data analysis](wikipedia://en/2141368), and [statistical decision theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_decision_theory), and applications to [genomics, compressed sensing](wikipedia://en/11403316), [chemical identification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microanalysis), [medical imaging](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medical_imaging), and financial engineering.[^o8]

In 2017, Cai was elected to the presidency of International Chinese Statistical Association (ICSA).

## Additional affiliations and memberships {#Additional_affiliations_and_memberships}

Cai was a co-editor of the [Annals of Statistics](wikipedia://en/22160768) from 2010 to 2012. He has also served on editorial boards of several other journals, including Journal of the American Statistical Association (JASA), Journal of the Royal Statistical Society Series B (JRSSB), *[Statistics Surveys](wikipedia://en/22965880)*, *and Statistica Sinica.[^o10]*

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- 2009 Medallion Lecturer, [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics)[^o12]
- 2008 [COPSS Presidents' Award](wikipedia://en/11690483)[^o13]
- 2006 Fellow, Institute of Mathematical Statistics[^o14][^o15]

## Personal life {#Personal_life}

Cai has four brothers and one sister. His sister [Tianxi Cai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tianxi_Cai) is the John Rock Professor of Population and Translational Data Sciences in the Department of Biostatistics at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health. She is also a professor at Harvard Medical School. His brother Tianwu Michael Cai, majored in [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) (PhD, Rochester) is a [vice-president](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vice-president) of [Goldman Sachs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goldman_Sachs). Tony Cai has two children, a son and daughter.
