# Architectural geometry

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Architectural geometry"}

**Architectural geometry** is an area of research which combines applied geometry and [architecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Architecture),[^o2] which looks at the design, analysis and manufacture processes. It lies at the core of architectural design[^o3] and strongly challenges contemporary practice, the so-called architectural practice of the digital age.[^o4]

:::

Architectural geometry is influenced by following fields: [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), [fractal geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal_geometry), and [cellular automata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellular_automata).

*Image: Approssimazione-offset-ciclide-sfere.jpg.* polyhedral approximation of a cyclide-offset.

Topics include:

- freeform curves and surfaces creation
- developable surfaces
- discretisation
- generative design
- digital prototyping and manufacturing
