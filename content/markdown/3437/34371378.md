# Holomorphic curve

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Holomorphic curve"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), in the field of [complex geometry](wikipedia://en/186101), a **holomorphic curve** in a [complex manifold](wikipedia://en/509742) *M* is a non-constant [holomorphic map](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holomorphic_map) *f* from the [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628) to *M*.[^o2]

:::

[Nevanlinna theory](wikipedia://en/393135) addresses the question of the distribution of values of a holomorphic curve in the complex [projective line](wikipedia://en/398578).[^o2][^o3]
