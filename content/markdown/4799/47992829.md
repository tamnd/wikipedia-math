# Boris Zilber

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Boris Zilber"}

**Boris Zilber** (Борис Иосифович Зильбер, born 1949) is a Soviet-British mathematician who works in [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), specifically [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858). He is an emeritus professor of mathematical logic at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford).

:::

He obtained his doctorate ([Candidate of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_Sciences)) from the [Novosibirsk State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Novosibirsk_State_University) in 1975 under the supervision of Mikhail Taitslin[^o3] and his habilitation ([Doctor of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Sciences)) from the [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University) in 1986.[^o4]

Zilber received the [Senior Berwick Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Senior_Berwick_Prize) (2004) and the [Pólya Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pólya_Prize_%28LMS%29) (2015) from the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097).[^o5] He also gave the [Tarski Lectures](wikipedia://en/54104355) in 2002.[^o6]

## Research {#Research}

Zilber is well known for his seminal work around several fundamental problems in mathematics, mostly in the broad area of geometric [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858).[^o8] In particular, his trichotomy conjecture on the nature of strongly minimal sets has been extremely influential in geometric stability theory.[^o9] Although it is false in full generality (refuted by [Ehud Hrushovski](wikipedia://en/3132981)), it holds in many important settings, e.g. [Zariski geometries](wikipedia://en/3133250), and has been successfully applied to several problems including the Mordell-Lang conjecture for function fields.[^o10] Zilber's work on model theory of complex exponentiation led him to propose several influential conjectures including the Quasiminimality conjecture,[^o11] the [Existential Closedness conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Existential_Closedness_conjecture),[^o12] and the [Conjecture on Intersections with Tori](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zilber-Pink_conjecture).[^o12]
