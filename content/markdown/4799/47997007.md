# Howard Garland

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Howard Garland"}

**Howard Garland** (born 27 October 1937, in [Detroit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Detroit))[^o2] is an American mathematician, who works on [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), [Lie algebras](wikipedia://en/17944) ([structure theory](wikipedia://en/17944#Structure_theory_and_classification) and [representation theory](wikipedia://en/318712)), and infinite-dimensional algebras.[^o3]

:::

Garland received in 1958 his bachelor's degree from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), in 1959 his master's degree from [Wayne State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wayne_State_University), and in 1964 his Ph.D. from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) under [S. S. Chern](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/S._S._Chern) with thesis *On the cohomology of lattices in [Lie groups](wikipedia://en/17945)*). As a postdoc he was in 1964–1965 an instructor at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and in 1965–1966 a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o5] At Yale University he became in 1966 an assistant professor, in 1969 an associate professor, and in 1973 a full professor. Garland was a visiting professor in 1969–1970 at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) and in 1972–1973 at the [State University of New York at Stony Brook](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_University_of_New_York_at_Stony_Brook).

In 1986 Garland, with his doctoral student [Igor Frenkel](wikipedia://en/5033014) and with [Gregg Zuckerman](wikipedia://en/10822168), applied [Feigin](wikipedia://en/53823921)'s theory of semi-infinite cohomology of graded Lie algebras to explain some aspects of the [BRST quantization](wikipedia://en/6769980) of string theory.[^o6]
