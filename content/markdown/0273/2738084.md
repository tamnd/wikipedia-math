# Syntractrix

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Syntractrix"}

A **syntractrix** is a [curve](wikipedia://en/89246) of the form

:::

$x+\sqrt{b^2-y^2}= a \ln \frac{b+\sqrt{b^2-y^2}}{y}.$[^o2]

*Image: Syntractrix a=0.5 b=1.png.* The syntractrix for $a=0.5$ and $b=1.$

*Image: Syntractrix a=1.5 b=1.png.* The syntractrix for $a=1.5$ and $b=1.$

It is the locus of a point on the tangent of a [tractrix](wikipedia://en/841685) at a constant distance from the point of tangency, as the point of tangency is moved along the curve.[^o3]
