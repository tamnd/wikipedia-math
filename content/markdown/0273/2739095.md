# Abraham Manie Adelstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abraham Manie Adelstein"}

**Abraham Manie Adelstein** (28 March 1916 – 18 October 1992) was a South African born medical doctor who became the [United Kingdom](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Kingdom)'s Chief Medical [Statistician](wikipedia://en/48661).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Adelstein was the fourth of five children (four boys and one girl) of Nathan Adelstein, a miller, and Rosie Cohen, [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jew) immigrants from [Latvia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Latvia) to [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa).[^o3]

After graduating from the [University of Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Witwatersrand) and doing military service, Adelstein worked as a Health Officer (research and medical statistics) at [South African Railways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_African_Railways), 1947–61. He spent 1951–53 studying at the [London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Hygiene_and_Tropical_Medicine). He returned to South African Railways as the Director of Research and Medical Statistics.

Moving to [England](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/England) in 1961, he became

- Senior Lecturer at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), 1961–67,
- medical statistician at the [Office of Population Censuses and Surveys](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Population_Censuses_and_Surveys) in 1967
- [Chief Medical Statistician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chief_Medical_Statistician) in 1975 (among his staff was [John Fox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Fox_%28statistician%29), who later held this post).
- Visiting professor at the [London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Hygiene_and_Tropical_Medicine) after retiring in 1981, until 1984.

[David Adelstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Adelstein), one of his sons, was an active student politician at [L.S.E.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics) during the 1960s; he was President of [LSE Students' Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LSE_Students'_Union) during the famous protests in 1967. Both father and son were active politically on the [left-wing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left-wing) and in [anti-Apartheid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Apartheid) politics.

## Honours {#Honours}

- Fellow of the [Royal College of Physicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_College_of_Physicians), 1977
- [Donald Reid Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donald_Reid_Medal), [London School of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Hygiene_and_Tropical_Medicine), 1979
- [Bisset Hawkins Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bisset_Hawkins_Medal), [Royal College of Physicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_College_of_Physicians), 1983
- Fellow, Faculty of Public Health Medicine
