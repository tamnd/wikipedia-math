# Bullet-nose curve

*Image: Bullet nose curve.svg.* Bullet-nose curve with $a = 1$ and $b = 1$

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bullet-nose curve"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **bullet-nose curve** is a [unicursal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unicursal_curve) quartic curve with three [inflection points](wikipedia://en/379845), given by the equation

:::

$a^2y^2-b^2x^2=x^2y^2 \,$[^o2]

The bullet curve has three [double points](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_point) in the [real projective plane](wikipedia://en/411325), at $x = 0$ and $y = 0$, $x = 0$ and $z = 0$, and $y = 0$ and $z = 0$, and is therefore a unicursal (rational) curve of [genus](wikipedia://en/3115807) zero.

If

$$f(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} {2n \choose n} z^{2n+1} = z+2z^3+6z^5+20z^7+\cdots$$

then

$$y = f\left(\frac{x}{2a}\right)\pm 2b\$$

are the two branches of the bullet curve at the origin.
