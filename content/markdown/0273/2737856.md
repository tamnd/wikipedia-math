# Cochleoid

*Image: Cochleoid with a=1.svg.* $r=\frac{\sin \theta}{\theta}, -20<\theta<20$

*Image: Kochleoide als inverse zur quadratrix.svg.* cochleoid (solid) and its polar inverse (dashed)

*Image: Cochleoid.gif.* A flexible pole is fixed upright at one end and bent over to always form a circular arc. The other end then traces out a Cochleoid.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cochleoid"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cochleoid** is a [snail](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Snail)-shaped curve similar to a [strophoid](wikipedia://en/2738060) which can be represented by the [polar equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polar_equation)

:::

$$r=\frac{a \sin \theta}{\theta},$$

the [Cartesian equation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_equation)

$$(x^2+y^2)\arctan\frac{y}{x}=ay,$$

or the [parametric equations](wikipedia://en/576108)

$$x=\frac{a\sin t\cos t}{t}, \quad y=\frac{a\sin^2 t}{t}.$$

The cochleoid is the [inverse curve](wikipedia://en/15040455) of [Hippias' quadratrix](wikipedia://en/40429150).[^o2]
