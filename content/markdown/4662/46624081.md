# Tian Ye (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tian Ye"}

[Tian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tian_%28surname%29)

**Tian Ye** or **Ye Tian** (; born November 1971) is a Chinese mathematician known for his research in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177). He is a professor of mathematics at the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) and is the deputy director of its Morningside Center of Mathematics.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Tian Ye was born in [Neijiang](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neijiang), [Sichuan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan), China in November 1971.[^o4] From 1989 to 1996, he studied at [Sichuan University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sichuan_University) where he received his B.S. and M.S.[^o7] He received his Ph.D. in mathematics under [Shou-Wu Zhang](wikipedia://en/3582724) at [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 2003. He was a member at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) from 2003 to 2004.[^o12]

## Career {#Career}

Tian was a post-doctoral researcher at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University) from 2004 to 2006.[^o7] He has been a professor at the [Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) since 2006.[^o7][^o12] He is the deputy director of the Morningside Center of Mathematics.[^o8]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Tian received the [ICTP Ramanujan Prize](wikipedia://en/13595525) (2013) and the [Morningside Medal](wikipedia://en/39615068) (Silver 2007, Gold 2013).[^o10][^o11] He was elected a [member of the Chinese Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_members_of_the_Chinese_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2023.[^o4][^o12]

He was an [invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2022.[^o12]
