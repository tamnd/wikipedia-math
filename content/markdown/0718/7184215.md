# 5-cube

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="5-cube"}

Uniform polyteron stat table In [five-dimensional](wikipedia://en/2851410) [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **5-cube** (or **penteract**) is a five-dimensional [hypercube](wikipedia://en/39783) with 32 [vertices](wikipedia://en/11308417), 80 [edges](wikipedia://en/12158034), 80 square [faces](wikipedia://en/149926), 40 cubic [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29), and 10 [tesseract](wikipedia://en/31112) [4-faces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-face).

:::

It is represented by [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) {4,3,3,3} or {4,3<sup>3</sup>}, constructed as 3 tesseracts, {4,3,3}, around each cubic [ridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ridge_%28geometry%29).

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

It is a part of an infinite [hypercube](wikipedia://en/39783) family. The [dual](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dual_polytope) of a penteract is the [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615), of the infinite family of [orthoplexes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthoplex).

Applying an *[alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117)* operation, deleting alternating vertices of the 5-cube, creates another [uniform 5-polytope](wikipedia://en/14415338), called a [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232), which is also part of an infinite family called the [demihypercubes](wikipedia://en/6833695).

The penteract can be seen as an *order-3 tesseractic honeycomb* on a [4-sphere](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/4-sphere). It is related to the Euclidean 4-space (order-4) [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139) and paracompact hyperbolic honeycomb [order-5 tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/41926110).

## Cartesian coordinates {#Cartesian_coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinates) of the vertices of a 5-cube centered at the origin and having edge length 2 are

(±1,±1,±1,±1,±1),

while this 5-cube's interior consists of all points (*x*<sub>0</sub>, *x*<sub>1</sub>, *x*<sub>2</sub>, *x*<sub>3</sub>, *x*<sub>4</sub>) with −1 \< *x*<sub>*i*</sub> \< 1 for all *i*.

## Projection {#Projection}

The 5-cube can be projected down to 3 dimensions with a [rhombic icosahedron](wikipedia://en/3691443) envelope. There are 22 exterior vertices, and 10 interior vertices. The 10 interior vertices have the convex hull of a [pentagonal antiprism](wikipedia://en/2860091). The 80 edges project into 40 external edges and 40 internal ones. The 40 cubes project into [golden rhombohedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_rhombohedra) which can be used to [dissect](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissection_%28geometry%29) the rhombic icosahedron. The projection vectors are u = {1, φ, 0, -1, φ}, v = {φ, 0, 1, φ, 0}, w = {0, 1, φ, 0, -1}, where φ is the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386), $\frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2}$.

## Symmetry {#Symmetry}

The *5-cube* has [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004#Properties) symmetry B<sub>5</sub>, abstract structure $C_{2}\wr S_{5}$, order 3840, containing 25 hyperplanes of reflection. The [Schläfli symbol](wikipedia://en/489394) for the 5-cube, {4,3,3,3}, matches the [Coxeter notation](wikipedia://en/32088502) symmetry \[4,3,3,3\].

### Prisms {#Prisms}

All [hypercubes](wikipedia://en/39783) have lower symmetry forms constructed as prisms. The 5-cube has 7 prismatic forms from the lowest 5-[orthotope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orthotope), { }<sup>5</sup>, and upwards as orthogonal edges are constrained to be of equal length. The vertices in a prism are equal to the product of the vertices in the elements. The edges of a prism can be partitioned into the number of edges in an element times the number of vertices in all the other elements.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The *5-cube* is 5th in a series of [hypercube](wikipedia://en/39783):

The [regular skew polyhedron](wikipedia://en/19193037#Finite_regular_skew_polyhedra) {4,5| 4} can be realized within the 5-cube, with its 32 vertices, 80 edges, and 40 square faces, and the other 40 square faces of the 5-cube become square *holes*.

This polytope is one of 31 [uniform 5-polytopes](wikipedia://en/14415338#Uniform_5-polytope) generated from the regular 5-cube or [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615).
