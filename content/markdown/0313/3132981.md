# Ehud Hrushovski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ehud Hrushovski"}

**Ehud Hrushovski** (אהוד הרושובסקי; born 30 September 1959) is a [mathematical logician](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_logician).[^o3] He is a Merton Professor of Mathematical Logic at the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) and a Fellow of [Merton College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merton_College,_Oxford).[^o4] He was also Professor of Mathematics at the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem).[^o5]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Hrushovski's father, [Benjamin Harshav](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benjamin_Harshav),[^o7] was a [literary theorist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literary_theorist), a Yiddish and Hebrew [poet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poet) and a translator, [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) and [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) in [comparative literature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Comparative_literature). Ehud Hrushovski earned his PhD from the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley) in 1986 under [Leo Harrington](wikipedia://en/5693354); his dissertation was titled *Contributions to Stable Model Theory*. He was a professor of mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) until 1994, when he became a professor at the Hebrew University of Jerusalem. Hrushovski moved in 2017 to the University of Oxford, where he is the Merton Professor of Mathematical Logic.[^o5][^o3]

## Career {#Career}

Hrushovski is well known for several fundamental contributions to [model theory](wikipedia://en/19858), in particular in the branch that has become known as geometric model theory, and its applications.[^o10] His PhD thesis revolutionized [stable model theory](wikipedia://en/15828771) (a part of model theory arising from the stability theory introduced by [Saharon Shelah](wikipedia://en/331211)). Shortly afterwards he found counterexamples to the [Trichotomy Conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trichotomy_Conjecture) of [Boris Zilber](wikipedia://en/47992829) and his method of proof has become well known as [Hrushovski constructions](wikipedia://en/18926594) and found many other applications since.[^o11]

One of his most famous results is his proof of the geometric [Mordell–Lang conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell–Lang_conjecture) in all characteristics using model theory in 1996. This deep proof was a landmark in logic and geometry. He has had many other famous and notable results in model theory and its applications to geometry, algebra, and combinatorics.[^o12]

### Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

He was an invited speaker at the 1990 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) and a plenary speaker at the 1998 [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842).[^o14][^o15] He is a recipient of the [Erdős Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erdős_Prize) of the [Israel Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/59341674) in 1994, the Rothschild Prize in 1998, the [Karp Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karp_Prize) of the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438) in 1993 (jointly with [Alex Wilkie](wikipedia://en/10343119)) and again in 1998,[^o16] In 2007, he was honored with holding the [Gödel Lecture](wikipedia://en/69216431). In his absence, a lecture on his work titled *Algebraic Model Theory* was given by Thomas Scanlon.[^o17] In 2019 he was awarded the [Heinz Hopf Prize](wikipedia://en/24560211)[^o3] and in 2022 the [Shaw Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shaw_Prize) in Mathematical Sciences.[^o18]

Hrushovski is a fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) (2007),[^o19] and [Israel Academy of Sciences and Humanities](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) (2008). He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2020.[^o20]
