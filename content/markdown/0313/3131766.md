# Coframe

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Coframe"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **coframe** or **coframe field** on a [smooth manifold](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Smooth_manifold) $M$ is a system of [one-forms](wikipedia://en/314692) or [covectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Covector) which form a [basis](wikipedia://en/32370) of the [cotangent bundle](wikipedia://en/221600) at every point.[^o2] In the [exterior algebra](wikipedia://en/221537) of $M$, one has a natural map from $v_k:\bigoplus^kT^*M\to\bigwedge^kT^*M$, given by $v_k:(\rho_1,\ldots,\rho_k)\mapsto \rho_1\wedge\ldots\wedge\rho_k$. If $M$ is $n$ dimensional, a coframe is given by a section $\sigma$ of $\bigoplus^nT^*M$ such that $v_n\circ\sigma\neq 0$. The inverse image under $v_n$ of the complement of the zero section of $\bigwedge^nT^*M$ forms a $GL(n)$ [principal bundle](wikipedia://en/310950) over $M$, which is called the [coframe bundle](wikipedia://en/366550).

:::
