# List of mathematicians who did research in prison

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of mathematicians who did research in prison"}

A number of people have done mathematical research while imprisoned. Some were put in [prisoner-of-war camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prisoner-of-war_camp) during various conflicts, including [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I) and [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II); others were forced into [labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_camp) or [concentration camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentration_camp). Several mathematicians were jailed as a result of their political views (at least in part), notably [Évariste Galois](wikipedia://en/9815), [Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163), and [Chandler Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandler_Davis).

:::

| Name | Prison | Time period | Notable work done in prison | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Jean-Victor Poncelet](wikipedia://en/764818) | [Saratov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saratov) prisoner of war camp[^o4] | 1813–1814[^o5] | Formulation of [projective](wikipedia://en/243849) properties of [conics](wikipedia://en/19008673)[^o5] | Captured during the [French invasion of Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_invasion_of_Russia).[^o5] |
| [Évariste Galois](wikipedia://en/9815) | [Sainte Pélagie Prison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sainte-Pélagie_Prison)[^o9] | 1831–1832[^o9] | Preface to *Deux mémoires d’analyse pur*[^o7][^o8] | Arrested during [Bastille Day](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bastille_Day) demonstrations; charged with wearing a police uniform and carrying a firearm.[^o8][^o9] |
| [André Bloch](wikipedia://en/11772883) | [Charenton mental asylum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charenton_%28asylum%29)[^o11] | 1917–1948[^o11] | Various pieces of [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759), including [Bloch's theorem](wikipedia://en/11831990) and [Bloch's principle](wikipedia://en/34529648)[^o11] | Committed after murdering several family members; all of his publications were written while in Charenton.[^o11] |
| [Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163) | [HM Prison Brixton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HM_Prison_Brixton)[^o14] | 1918[^o13] | *[Introduction to Mathematical Philosophy](wikipedia://en/5387218)*[^o13] | Imprisoned due to anti-war activities during [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I).[^o14] |
| [Ludwig Wittgenstein](wikipedia://en/17741) | Prisoner of war camps in [Como](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Como) and [Cassino](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cassino)[^o16] | 1918–1919[^o17] | *[Tractatus Logico-Philosophicus](wikipedia://en/30062)*[^o18] | Captured by Italian forces while serving in the [Austrian Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Armed_Forces).[^o17] |
| [André Weil](wikipedia://en/2019) | Rouen Prison[^o21] | 1940[^o20] | Proof of the [Riemann hypothesis for curves over finite fields](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann_hypothesis_for_curves_over_finite_fields)[^o20] | Jailed for avoiding military obligations;[^o21] also worked on his draft of a [Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394) volume on [integration](wikipedia://en/15532) in prison.[^o20] |
| [Pál Turán](wikipedia://en/614998) | Various [labour camps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labor_camp) in [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary)[^o23] | 1940–1944[^o23] | [Turán's brick factory problem](wikipedia://en/22039971) | Interned in [forced labour service as a Hungarian Jew](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Labour_service_in_Hungary_during_World_War_II) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II);[^o23] also published work relating to the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809).[^o24] |
| [Jean Leray](wikipedia://en/245430) | [Oflag XVII-A](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oflag_XVII-A)[^o26] | 1940–1945[^o26] | Work in [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), including [sheaves](wikipedia://en/245466) and [spectral sequences](wikipedia://en/803894)[^o26] | Became a [German prisoner of war](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_prisoner-of-war_camps_in_World_War_II); avoided research in [fluid mechanics](wikipedia://en/2684988) due to fear the Germans would require him to work for them.[^o27] |
| [Jakow Trachtenberg](wikipedia://en/567390) | [Sachsenhausen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sachsenhausen_concentration_camp) or [Auschwitz concentration camp](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auschwitz_concentration_camp)[^o29] | 194?–1945[^o30] | [Trachtenberg system](wikipedia://en/567391) of [mental arithmetic](wikipedia://en/567292)[^o30] | Taken by the [Gestapo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gestapo) after fleeing to [Yugoslavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yugoslavia).[^o30] |
| [Chandler Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chandler_Davis) | [Danbury Federal Correctional Institution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Federal_Correctional_Institution,_Danbury)[^o32] | 1960[^o33] |  | Imprisoned for refusal to testify before the [House Un-American Activities Committee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_Un-American_Activities_Committee).[^o34] |
| Christopher Havens | Currently [Monroe Correctional Complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monroe_Correctional_Complex)[^o35] | 2011–[^o36] |  | Sentenced to 25 years for murder; cofounded the [Prison Mathematics Project](wikipedia://en/81946711) in 2020.[^o37] |
