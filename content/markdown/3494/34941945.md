# Giovanni Sansone

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Giovanni Sansone"}

**Giovanni Sansone** (24 May 1888 – 13 October 1979) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his works on [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), on the [theory of orthogonal functions](wikipedia://en/453041) and on the [theory of ordinary differential equations](wikipedia://en/32742753).[^o3]

:::

He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in Bologna in 1928.[^o4]
