# Guido Ascoli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Guido Ascoli"}

**Guido Ascoli** (12 December 1887 – 10 May 1957) was an Italian mathematician, known for his contributions to the [theory of partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), and for his works on the teaching of mathematics in secondary high schools.

:::

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- Ascoli, G.; Burgatti, Pietro; Giraud, Georges. (1936). "Equazioni alle derivate parziali dei tipi ellittico e parabolico". Sansoni Editore. p. IV + 186. <https://matematicaitaliana.sns.it/opere/404/> (available from the "*[Edizione Nazionale Mathematica Italiana](http://mathematica.sns.it/)*"). A book collecting the winning papers of the 1935 prize of the Annali della Reale Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa. An English translation of the title reads as:-"*Partial differential equations of elliptic and parabolic type*".
