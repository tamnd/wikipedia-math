# Richard Beez

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Richard Beez"}

*Image: Das Fotoalbum für Weierstraß 009 (Richard Beez).jpg.* Richard Beez

**Richard Beez** (27 May 1827 – 28 March 1902) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who proved [Beez's theorem](wikipedia://en/34948394).

:::

He studied at the [University of Leipzig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leipzig), where in 1850 he obtained a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) Later, Beez was a Gymnasium teacher in [Plauen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plauen).[^o2]
