# Norbert A'Campo

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Norbert A'Campo"}

**Norbert A'Campo** (born 27 April 1941)[^o3] is a [Swiss](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_people) mathematician working on [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763). He earned a doctorate in 1972 from the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud). In 1974 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065),[^o5] and in 1988 he was elected president of the [Swiss Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swiss_Mathematical_Society).[^o6] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
