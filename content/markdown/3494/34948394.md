# Beez's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Beez's theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **Beez's theorem**, introduced by [Richard Beez](wikipedia://en/34948378) in 1875, implies that if *n* > 3 then in general an (*n* – 1)-dimensional [hypersurface](wikipedia://en/525101) [immersed](wikipedia://en/6993953) in **R**<sup>*n*</sup> cannot be deformed.

:::
