# Lawrence Lin

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lawrence Lin"}

**Lawrence I-Kuei Lin** is an American statistician.

:::

Lin earned his doctorate from the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) in 1979, where, advised by [Peter A. Lachenbruch](wikipedia://en/81317743), he authored the dissertation *The Effect of Non-Normality on Multiple Group Discrimination and Estimation of the Logistic Risk Function when Data Are Heavy-Tailed with Clumping at Zero*.

Lin worked for [Baxter Healthcare](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Baxter_Healthcare) and is also an adjunct professor at the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago).[^o3][^o4][^o5] He was elected a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2012.[^o6]
