# M. K. Fort Jr.

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="M. K. Fort Jr."}

**Marion Kirkland "Kirk" Fort Jr.** (1921–1964) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), specializing in [general topology](wikipedia://en/178649). The [topological spaces](wikipedia://en/30450) called [Fort space](wikipedia://en/10105571) and [Arens–Fort space](wikipedia://en/8588347) are named after him.

:::

Fort was born in 1921 in [Spartanburg, South Carolina](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spartanburg,_South_Carolina), where he graduated with an [A.B.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from [Wofford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wofford_College) in 1941. He received an [M.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Arts) in 1944 from the [University of Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Virginia), where he also received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1948, advised by [Gordon Thomas Whyburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gordon_Thomas_Whyburn). He was at the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois) from then until 1953, when he came to the [University of Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Georgia) (UGA). He later served as head of the UGA mathematics department, 1959–1963. In 1963 Fort became the first holder of the university's [David C. Barrow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_C._Barrow) Chair of Mathematics. He died in 1964 during a [leave of absence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leave_of_absence) at the [Institute for Defense Analyses](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Defense_Analyses) in [Princeton, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton,_New_Jersey).[^o3]
