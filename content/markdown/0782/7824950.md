# Computer-based mathematics education

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Computer-based mathematics education"}

**Computer-based mathematics education** (**CBME**) is an approach to [teaching mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teaching_mathematics) that emphasizes the use of computers and [mathematical software](wikipedia://en/1727027).

:::

## Computers in math education {#Computers_in_math_education}

Computers are used in education in a number of ways, such as interactive [tutorials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tutorials), [hypermedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hypermedia), [simulations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simulation) and [educational games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_games).[^o3] Tutorials are types of software that present information, check learning by question/answer method, judge responses, and provide feedback.[^o3] Educational games are more like simulations and are used from the elementary to college level.[^o3] [E learning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/E_learning) systems can deliver math lessons and exercises and manage homework assignments.
