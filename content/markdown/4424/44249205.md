# Thomas W. Tucker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Thomas W. Tucker"}

**Thomas William Tucker** (born July 15, 1945) is a retired[^o2] American mathematician, a Charles Hetherington Professor of Mathematics, Emeritus, at [Colgate University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colgate_University),[^o3] and an expert in the area of [topological graph theory](wikipedia://en/1631772).[^o4][^o5]

:::

Tucker did his undergraduate studies at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), graduating in 1967,[^o3] and obtained his Ph.D. from [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) in 1971, under the supervision of Edward Martin Brown.

Tucker's father, [Albert W. Tucker](wikipedia://en/935841), was also a professional mathematician, and his brother, [Alan Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Tucker), and son, [Thomas J. Tucker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_J._Tucker), are also professional mathematicians.
