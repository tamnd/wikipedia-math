# Corank

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Corank"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **corank** is complementary to the concept of the rank of a mathematical object, and may refer to the dimension of the left [nullspace of a matrix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nullspace), the dimension of the [cokernel](wikipedia://en/279715) of a linear transformation of a [vector space](wikipedia://en/32370), or the number of elements of a [matroid](wikipedia://en/244321) minus its [rank](wikipedia://en/37123010).[^o2]

:::

## Left nullspace of a matrix {#Left_nullspace_of_a_matrix}

The corank of an $m\times n$ [matrix](wikipedia://en/20556859) is $m-r$ where $r$ is the [rank](wikipedia://en/26561) of the matrix. It is the dimension of the [left nullspace](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Left_nullspace) and of the [cokernel](wikipedia://en/279715) of the matrix. For a square matrix $M$, the corank and nullity of $M$ are equivalent.

## Cokernel of a linear transformation {#Cokernel_of_a_linear_transformation}

Generalizing matrices to [linear transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_transformation) of [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370), the corank of a linear transformation is the dimension of the [cokernel](wikipedia://en/279715) of the transformation, which is the quotient of the [codomain](wikipedia://en/50264) by the image of the transformation.

## Matroid {#Matroid}

For a [matroid](wikipedia://en/244321) with $n$ elements and [matroid rank](wikipedia://en/37123010) $r$, the corank or nullity of the matroid is $n-r$. In the case of [linear matroids](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_matroid) this coincides with the matrix corank. In the case of [graphic matroids](wikipedia://en/745714) the corank is also known as the [circuit rank](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Circuit_rank) or cyclomatic number.
