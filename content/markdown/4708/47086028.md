# Yves Le Jan

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yves Le Jan"}

**Yves Le Jan** (born 15 April 1952 in [Grenoble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grenoble)) is a French mathematician working in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [stochastic processes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes).

:::

Le Jan studied from 1971 to 1974 at the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure), finishing with an Agrégation. 1975 he became a researcher (Attaché de Recherche) at the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) (from 1987 Directeur de Recherche) and in 1979 obtained his PhD (Doctorat d´Etat). Since 1993 he is professor at the [University of Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris-Sud). From 2001 to 2004 he was leading its group on probability theory and statistics.

In 2006 he was [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in [Madrid](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Madrid) (*New developments in stochastic dynamics*).[^o2] In 2008 he became Senior Member of the [Institut Universitaire de France](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_Universitaire_de_France). In 2011 he was Doob Lecturer at the 8th World Congress in Probability and Statistics in [Istanbul](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Istanbul).

In 2011 he was awarded the [Sophie Germain Prize](wikipedia://en/48485384) and in 1995 the [Poncelet Prize](wikipedia://en/20842828) of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).

From 2000 to 2006 he was Editor of [Annales Henri Poincaré](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annales_Henri_Poincaré).
