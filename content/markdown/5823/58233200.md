# Mihnea Popa

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mihnea Popa"}

*Image: Mihnea Popa Oberwolfach 2017.jpg.* Popa at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2017

**Mihnea Popa** (born 11 August 1973) is a [Romanian-American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Romanian_Americans) mathematician at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), specializing in algebraic geometry.[^o2] He is known for his work on complex [birational geometry](wikipedia://en/382733), [Hodge theory](wikipedia://en/501590), [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152), and vector bundles.

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

Popa received his bachelor's degree in 1996 from the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest). He studied mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) from 1996 to 1997, and then in 2001 he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) under the supervision of [Robert Lazarsfeld](wikipedia://en/29022720). His thesis was titled *Linear Series on Moduli Spaces of Vector Bundles on Curves*. From 2001 to 2005, Popa was a Benjamin Peirce Assistant Professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and from 2005 to 2007 an assistant professor at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago). He joined the [University of Illinois at Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Chicago) as an associate professor in 2007 and became a full professor in 2011. In 2014 he moved to [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), and in 2020 he became a professor at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University).[^o5]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Popa is an honorary member of the [Institute of Mathematics of the Romanian Academy](wikipedia://en/8609230).[^o7] He was an [AMS Centennial Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AMS_Centennial_Fellowship) in 2005–2007, a [Sloan Research Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 2007–2009, and a [Simons Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) in 2015–2016.[^o2] In 2015 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o8] In 2018 he was an Invited Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Rio de Janeiro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rio_de_Janeiro).[^o9]
