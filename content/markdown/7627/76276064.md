# John W. Dold

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John W. Dold"}

**John William Dold** (also known as **Bill Dold**) is an emeritus professor in the [department of mathematics](wikipedia://en/10387568) in the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester), a specialist in the field of [Fluid Mechanics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fluid_Mechanics) and [Combustion](wikipedia://en/5638).[^o3][^o4][^o5] He was the founder of the journal [Combustion Theory and Modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion_Theory_and_Modelling).

:::

## Biography and research {#Biography_and_research}

John attended school at the [Christian Brothers College, Bulawayo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christian_Brothers_College,_Bulawayo) in [Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zimbabwe). He completed his bachelors (1971–1974) at the [University of Zimbabwe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Zimbabwe) and his doctoral degree (1976–1979) at the [Cranfield University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cranfield_University) under the supervision of [John Frederick Clarke](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Frederick_Clarke).[^o7] He obtained a master's degree (1971–1974) in area studies at the [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London).[^o8]

He then joined the faculty of the mathematics department at the [University of Bristol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bristol) and then joined the faculty of mathematics department at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester) in 1995. In 1997, he along with Mitchell D. Smooke, founded the journal [Combustion Theory and Modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion_Theory_and_Modelling).[^o9] He also worked at [Greater Manchester Fire and Rescue Service](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greater_Manchester_Fire_and_Rescue_Service) from 2010 to 2013. He served as the vice president of [SIAM](wikipedia://en/1883376) United Kingdom and Republic of Ireland Section from 1998 to 1999.[^o10] He also served as the editor for [Combustion Theory and Modelling](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Combustion_Theory_and_Modelling) and [SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Journal_on_Applied_Mathematics).

John Dold has made significant contributions in theoretical combustion science, particularly in areas related to partially premixed combustion, [fire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fire), ignition, [detonations](wikipedia://en/306686) and also in the field of fluid mechanics and water waves. [Kerr–Dold vortex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kerr–Dold_vortex), an exact solution of [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395) is named after him,
