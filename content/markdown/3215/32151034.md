# Hermann Brunn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hermann Brunn"}

*Image: 6Loops-Brunnian-link.svg.* More complex Brunnian link of six loops, based on illustration in Brunn's 1892 paper

**Karl Hermann Brunn** (1 August 1862 – 20 September 1939) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), known for his work in [convex geometry](wikipedia://en/4165882) (see [Brunn–Minkowski inequality](wikipedia://en/7500545)) and in [knot theory](wikipedia://en/153008). [Brunnian links](wikipedia://en/2492788) are named after him, as his 1892 article "Über Verkettung" included examples of such links.

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Hermann Brunn was born in [Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rome), and grew up in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich).[^o4] He studied mathematics and physics at the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München), graduating in 1887 with the thesis *Über Ovale und Eiflächen* (About ovals and eggforms).[^o5] He habilitated in 1889.
