# David Allen Hoffman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="David Allen Hoffman"}

David Hoffman

**David Allen Hoffman** is an American mathematician whose research concerns [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625). He is an [adjunct professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunct_professor) at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o3] In 1985, together with [William Meeks](wikipedia://en/57967381), he proved that [Costa's surface](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Costa's_surface) was embedded.[^o4] He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) since 2018, for "contributions to differential geometry, particularly minimal surface theory, and for pioneering the use of computer graphics as an aid to research."[^o5] He was awarded the [Chauvenet Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chauvenet_Prize) in 1990 for his expository article "The Computer-Aided Discovery of New Embedded Minimal Surfaces".[^o6] He obtained his Ph.D. from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) in 1971 under the supervision of [Robert Osserman](wikipedia://en/19663336).

:::

## Technical contributions {#Technical_contributions}

In 1973, James Michael and [Leon Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Simon) established a [Sobolev inequality](wikipedia://en/4665038) for functions on submanifolds of [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697), in a form which is adapted to the [mean curvature](wikipedia://en/684210) of the submanifold and takes on a special form for minimal submanifolds.[^o9] One year later, Hoffman and [Joel Spruck](wikipedia://en/64474699) extended Michael and Simon's work to the setting of functions on immersed submanifolds of [Riemannian manifolds](wikipedia://en/144652).HS74 Such inequalities are useful for many problems in [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474) which deal with some form of prescribed mean curvature.[^o10][^o11] As usual for Sobolev inequalities, Hoffman and Spruck were also able to derive new [isoperimetric inequalities](wikipedia://en/326182) for submanifolds of Riemannian manifolds.HS74

It is well known that there is a wide variety of [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734) in the three-dimensional [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697). Hoffman and [William Meeks](wikipedia://en/57967381) proved that any minimal surface which is contained in a half-space must fail to be properly immersed.HM90 That is, there must exist a compact set in Euclidean space which contains a noncompact region of the minimal surface. The proof is a simple application of the [maximum principle](wikipedia://en/1436104) and unique continuation for minimal surfaces, based on comparison with a family of [catenoids](wikipedia://en/273329). This enhances a result of Meeks, [Leon Simon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leon_Simon), and [Shing-Tung Yau](wikipedia://en/303137), which states that any two complete and properly immersed minimal surfaces in three-dimensional Euclidean space, if both are nonplanar, either have a point of intersection or are separated from each other by a plane.[^o12] Hoffman and Meeks' result rules out the latter possibility.
