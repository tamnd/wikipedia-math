# Tasso J. Kaper

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tasso J. Kaper"}

**Tasso Joost Kaper** (born June 25, 1964) is an American mathematician at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University), where he chairs the Department of Mathematics and Statistics. His research concerns [dynamical systems](wikipedia://en/9087) and [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969).[^o3]

:::

Kaper's father is [Hans G. Kaper](wikipedia://en/45346467), a Dutch-born retired mathematician at [Argonne National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory).[^o6][^o4] Tasso Kaper did his undergraduate studies at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), graduating in 1986.[^o3] He earned a Ph.D. in 1992 from the [California Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California_Institute_of_Technology), under the supervision of [Stephen Wiggins](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stephen_Wiggins). On finishing his doctorate, he joined the faculty at Boston University, where he has remained. He was editor-in-chief of *SIAM Journal on Applied Dynamical Systems* from 2005 to 2011, when he became department chair.[^o3] He is currently an editor-in-chief of the journal [Nonlinearity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinearity_%28journal%29) for the [Institute of Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Physics).

In 2009, both Tasso and Hans Kaper were simultaneously honored as [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376).[^o6] In 2012, Kaper became one of the inaugural fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
