# Niky Kamran

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Niky Kamran"}

**Niky Kamran** FRSC (born May 22, 1959) is a [Belgian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgians) and [Canadian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canadians) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research concerns [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416).[^o3] He is a Distinguished James McGill Professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at [McGill University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/McGill_University).[^o4]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Kamran was born in [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels), Belgium. He earned a [licentiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Licentiate_%28degree%29) in mathematics from the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles) in 1980.[^o4] He moved to Canada for graduate studies, earning a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1984 from the [University of Waterloo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Waterloo); his dissertation, titled *Contributions to the Study of the Separation of Variables and Symmetry Operators for Relativistic Wave Equations on Curved Spacetime*, was jointly supervised by [Raymond G. McLenaghan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raymond_McLenaghan) and Robert Debever.

## Career {#Career}

In 1986 he became an assistant professor at Waterloo but then, after spending a year as a member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179), Princeton, New Jersey, he moved to McGill in 1989. He was promoted to full professor in 1995, was awarded a James McGill Professorship in 2003 which he held until 2024, after which he was named Distinguished James McGill Professor as of 2024.[^o4]

Kamran won the [Aisenstadt Prize](wikipedia://en/8534576) in 1992.[^o8] He was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada)[^o9] in 2002 and was awarded a [Killam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Killam_Trusts) Fellowship from 2006 to 2008.[^o10] In 2012, he became one of the inaugural Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11] In 2014, Kamran was the winner of the [CRM-Fields-PIMS prize](wikipedia://en/19284033),[^o3] and in 2019 he was elected a member of the [Royal Academy of Science, Letters and Fine Arts of Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Academy_of_Science,_Letters_and_Fine_Arts_of_Belgium).[^o12] That same academy had awarded him in 1988 the mathematics prize of its annual competition for a memoir on the equivalence problem of [Élie Cartan](wikipedia://en/386274) and its applications.[^o13]
