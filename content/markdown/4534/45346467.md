# Hans G. Kaper

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hans G. Kaper"}

**Hans Gerard Kaper** (born June 10, 1936)[^o4] is a Dutch-American mathematician who worked for many years at [Argonne National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Argonne_National_Laboratory) until his retirement in 2008. He continues to hold adjunct professorships in mathematics and statistics at [Georgetown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgetown_University) and in music at the [University of Illinois at Urbana–Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana–Champaign),[^o2] where he has been a long-term collaborator on the UIUC Computer Music Project.[^o3]

:::

Kaper was born in [Alkmaar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alkmaar), [Netherlands](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Netherlands),[^o4] and earned a PhD in 1965 from the [University of Groningen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Groningen) under the supervision of Adriaan Isak van de Vooren. He taught briefly at Groningen before joining Argonne.[^o4] At Argonne, he directed the Mathematics and Computer Science Division.[^o12] He is also the editor-in-chief of *SIAM News*, the newsletter of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM).[^o6] In 1989 he became correspondent of the [Royal Netherlands Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Netherlands_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o7]

Since retiring, Kaper has investigated the mathematics of [climate change](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Climate_change), and directs the [NSF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation)-funded Mathematics and Climate Research Network.[^o2] With Hans Engler of Georgetown, he is a co-author of the book *Mathematics and Climate* (SIAM, 2013),[^o8][^o9][^o10][^o11] which won the 2013 ASLI Choice Award of the Atmospheric Science Librarians International.[^o12]

In 2009, Kaper and his son [Tasso J. Kaper](wikipedia://en/45346156) (a mathematician at [Boston University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_University)) were simultaneously honored as [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of SIAM.[^o13]
