# Ruth Pfeiffer

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ruth Pfeiffer"}

**Ruth Maria Pfeiffer** is a biostatistician who researches risk prediction, molecular and [genetic epidemiology](wikipedia://en/16017459), and [electronic medical records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Medical_Records). She is a senior investigator in the biostatistics branch at the [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute). Pfeiffer is an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute) and the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).

:::

## Life {#Life}

Pfeiffer received an M.S. degree in [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969) from the [TU Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/TU_Wien).[^o4] She earned a M.A. in applied statistics and a Ph.D. (1998) in [mathematical statistics](wikipedia://en/888711) from the [University of Maryland, College Park](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park).[^o4] Her dissertation was titled, *Statistical problems for stochastic processes with hysteresis*.[^o5] [Mark Freidlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mark_Freidlin) was Pfeiffer's doctoral advisor.[^o5]

Pfeiffer is a tenured senior investigator in the biostatistics branch of the division of cancer epidemiology and genetics (DCEG), [National Cancer Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Cancer_Institute) (NCI).[^o4] Her research focuses on statistical methods for risk prediction, problems arising in molecular and [genetic epidemiologic](wikipedia://en/16017459) studies, and the analysis of data from [electronic medical records](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_Medical_Records).[^o4] Pfeiffer is the recipient of a [Fulbright Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fulbright_Fellowship) and an elected member of the [International Statistical Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Statistical_Institute).[^o4] In 2013, she became an elected [Fellow of the American Statistical Association](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Statistical_Association).[^o4]
