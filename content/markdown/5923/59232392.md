# Caitlin Buck

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Caitlin Buck"}

**Caitlin E. Buck** (born 1964)[^o2] is a British [archaeologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Archaeologist) and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) specialising the application of [Bayesian statistics](wikipedia://en/404412) to archaeology, and known for her work in [radiocarbon dating](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radiocarbon_dating). She is a professor in the Department of Mathematics and Statistics at the [University of Sheffield](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sheffield).[^o3]

:::
