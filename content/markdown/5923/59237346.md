# Heike Fassbender

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Heike Fassbender"}

**Heike Fassbender** is a German mathematician specializing in [numerical linear algebra](wikipedia://en/7330660). She is a professor in the Institute for Computational Mathematics at the [Technical University of Braunschweig](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Braunschweig), and the president for the 2017–2019 term of the [Gesellschaft für Angewandte Mathematik und Mechanik](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gesellschaft_für_Angewandte_Mathematik_und_Mechanik) (GAMM, the International Association of Applied Mathematics and Mechanics).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Fassbender earned a master's degree in computer science at the [University at Buffalo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_at_Buffalo), and completed her Ph.D. at the [University of Bremen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bremen) in 1992. Her dissertation, *Numerische Verfahren zur diskreten trigonometrischen Polynomapproximation*, was jointly supervised by [Angelika Bunse-Gerstner](wikipedia://en/58931123) and Ludwig F. Elsner.

Fassbender moved to the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) in 2000. She joined the Technical University of Braunschweig faculty in 2002, and was vice president for teaching, studies, and further education at the university from 2008 to 2012.

## Book {#Book}

Fassbender is the author of the book *Symplectic Methods for the Symplectic Eigenproblem* (Kluwer, 2002).

## Professional service {#Professional_service}

As president of GAMM, Fassbender became the first woman to lead GAMM. As well as her presidency of GAMM, Fassbender was appointed for a four-year term on the German Accreditation Council in 2018. Moreover, she is treasurer of the International Council for Industrial and Applied Mathematics (ICIAM) is a worldwide organisation for professional applied mathematics societies, and for other societies with a significant interest in industrial or applied mathematics. January 1st, 2023, Fassbender is elected a chair member of the [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM) Council.[^o5]
