# Baruch Solomon Löwenstein

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Baruch Solomon Löwenstein"}

**Baruch Solomon Löwenstein** (born in mid-nineteenth century, [Włodarka](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Włodarka), [Russia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Russia)) was a [Jewish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jewish) mathematician. He wrote *Bikkure ha-Limmudiyyot*, explanations of mathematical passages in the works of [Abraham ibn Ezra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_ibn_Ezra), [Moses Maimonides](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moses_Maimonides), and [Joseph Delmedigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joseph_Delmedigo). He also annotated and published in 1863 a second edition of *Shebile di-Reḳia*, by Elias ben Ḥayyim Kohen Höchheimer, on the rules of the calendar, with the elements of geometry, trigonometry, and astronomy.[^o2]

:::
