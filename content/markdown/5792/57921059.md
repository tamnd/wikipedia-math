# Abraham Joseph Menz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Abraham Joseph Menz"}

**Abraham Joseph ben Simon Wolf Menz** (אברהם יוסף בן שמעון וואָלף מענץ) was an eighteenth century [rabbi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rabbi) and [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt).

:::

He wrote an elementary textbook on mathematics entitled *Reshit Limmudim*, in three parts: *Kelale handasah*, the general rules of [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923); *Yesodot ha-gematriot*, the elements of [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446); and *Yesod ha-tekunah*, on [astronomy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomy). Only the first part was published (Berlin, 1775).
