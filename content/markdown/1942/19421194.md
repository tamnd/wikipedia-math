# David S. Moore

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="David S. Moore"}

**David Sheldon Moore** is an American [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661), who is known for his leadership of [statistics education](wikipedia://en/24985094) for many decades.

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

David S. Moore received his A.B. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and the Ph.D. from [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University) in mathematics.[^o4]

In statistics education, David S. Moore is the author of a series of influential textbooks in statistical science, which use only high school algebra: *Introduction to the Practice of Statistics* (with [George P. McCabe](wikipedia://en/82333357)), of *An Introduction to the Basic Practice of Statistics*, and of *Statistics: Concepts and Controversies*.

In statistical science, David S. Moore has done research in the [asymptotic](wikipedia://en/26024794) theory of [robust](wikipedia://en/2885691) and [nonparametric statistics](wikipedia://en/223369).

In 1995, Moore received the [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics).[^o5]

Professor Moore was the 1998 President of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).

David S. Moore is a retired (in 2004) [Shanti S. Gupta](wikipedia://en/82364891) Distinguished Professor of Statistics, Emeritus at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University).[^o6]

Professor Moore has served as the second president of the [International Association for Statistical Education](wikipedia://en/62609633). He was the content developer for the Annenberg/Corporation for Public Broadcasting college-level telecourse *Against All Odds: Inside Statistics*.
