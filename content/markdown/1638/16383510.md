# Bernstein's constant

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernstein's constant"}

**Bernstein's constant**, usually denoted by the Greek letter β ([beta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beta_%28letter%29)), is a [mathematical constant](wikipedia://en/48779551) named after [Sergei Natanovich Bernstein](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sergei_Natanovich_Bernstein) and is equal to 0.2801694990... .

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let *E*<sub>*n*</sub>(ƒ) be the error of the best [uniform approximation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_approximation) to a [real function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_function) *ƒ*(*x*) on the interval \[−1, 1\] by real polynomials of no more than degree *n*. In the case of *ƒ*(*x*) = |*x*|, Bernstein showed that the limit

$$\beta=\lim_{n \to \infty}2nE_{2n}(f),\,$$

called **Bernstein's constant**, exists and is between 0.278 and 0.286. His [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138) that the limit is:

$$\frac {1}{2\sqrt {\pi}}=0.28209\dots\,.$$

was disproven by Varga and Carpenter, who calculated

$$\beta=0.280169499023\dots\,.$$

:::
