# John Dossey

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="John Dossey"}

**John Arthur Dossey** (March 15, 1944 – February 5, 2024) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

## Life and career {#Life_and_career}

Dossey was born in [Decatur, Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decatur,_Illinois), the son of Arthur[^o4] and Mary Dossey. He attended [Illinois State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois_State_University), earning his [BS degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Science) in 1965 and his [MS degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in 1968. He also attended the [University of Illinois](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_Urbana-Champaign), earning his [PhD degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471) in 1971.[^o5]

Dossey served as a professor in the department of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) at Illinois State University from 1971 to 1999. During his years as a professor, in 1989, he was named a [distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor),[^o6] and was awarded the [NCTM Lifetime Achievement Award](wikipedia://en/1561691) in 1996.[^o7]

## Death {#Death}

Dossey died on February 5, 2024, in [Tucson, Arizona](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tucson,_Arizona), at the age of 79.[^o9]
