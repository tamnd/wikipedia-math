# Golden ellipse

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Golden ellipse"}

A **golden ellipse** is an [ellipse](wikipedia://en/9277) in which the [aspect ratio](wikipedia://en/1021) of its two [semi-axes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-axis) $a$ and $b$ corresponds to the [golden ratio](wikipedia://en/12386).

:::

## Equivalent characterization {#Equivalent_characterization}

*Image: Goldene Ellipse.svg.*

Given is a [annulus](wikipedia://en/356158) with outer [radius](wikipedia://en/1780815) $a$ and inner radius $b$ as well as an ellipse with semi-major axis $a$ and semi-minor axis $b$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438).

Then the ratio $\frac{a}{b}$ corresponds to the golden ratio $\Phi$ if and only if the annulus and the ellipse have the same area. [^o3]

The [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) results from the following equivalence chain:

$$\pi a^2-\pi b^2=\pi ab \Leftrightarrow a^2-ba-b^2=0 \Leftrightarrow a=\frac{b}{2}\ \pm\sqrt{\frac{b^2}{4}+b^2} \Leftrightarrow a=\frac{1}{2}(1 \pm \sqrt{5}) \cdot b$$

Since only the positive solution is possible, after division by $b$ we get:

$$\frac{a}{b}=\frac{1}{2}(1 + \sqrt{5})=\Phi$$

## Relationship to the golden rectangle {#Relationship_to_the_golden_rectangle}

*Image: Goldene Ellipse im Goldenen Rechteck.svg.*

The golden ellipse can be [inscribed](wikipedia://en/4470203) in a [golden rectangle](wikipedia://en/219374) with the side lengths $2a$ and $2b$.[^o5]
