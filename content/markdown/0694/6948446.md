# Jaroslav Nešetřil

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jaroslav Nešetřil"}

**Jaroslav Nešetřil** (; born 13 March 1946) is a [Czech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Czechs) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). His research areas include [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170) (structural combinatorics, [Ramsey theory](wikipedia://en/157175)), [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401) (coloring problems, sparse structures), [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923) (representation of structures, [categories](wikipedia://en/5869), [homomorphisms](wikipedia://en/676328)), [posets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partially_ordered_sets) (diagram and [dimension](wikipedia://en/3121852) problems), [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science) ([complexity](wikipedia://en/7543), [NP-completeness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P_=_NP_problem)). He works at [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in [Prague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prague).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nešetřil received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from Charles University in 1973 under the supervision of Aleš Pultr and [Gert Sabidussi](wikipedia://en/31554519). He is responsible for more than 300 publications.[^o5] Since 2006, he is chairman of the Committee of Mathematics of Czech Republic (the Czech partner of [IMU](wikipedia://en/14868)).

Jaroslav Nešetřil is Editor in Chief of *Computer Science Review*[^o6] and *INTEGERS: the Electronic Journal of Combinatorial Number Theory*.[^o7] He is also honorary editor of *Electronic Journal of Graph Theory and Applications*.[^o8] Since 2008, Jaroslav Nešetřil belongs to the Advisory Board of the [Academia Sinica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Sinica).[^o9]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

He was awarded the state prize (1985 jointly with [Vojtěch Rödl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vojtěch_Rödl)) for a collection of papers in Ramsey theory. The book *Sparsity - Graphs, Structures, and Algorithms* he co-authored with [Patrice Ossona de Mendez](wikipedia://en/47882601) was included in [ACM Computing Reviews](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACM_Computing_Reviews) list of *Notable Books and Articles of 2012*.[^o11]

Nešetřil is a corresponding member of the German Academy of Sciences since 1996 and has been declared Doctor Honoris Causa of the University of Alaska ([Fairbanks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fairbanks)) in 2002. He has also been declared Doctor Honoris Causa of the [University of Bordeaux 1](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bordeaux_1) in 2009; the speech he made in French at this occasion attracted a great deal of attention.[^o12] He received in 2010 the [Medal of Merit of Czech Republic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_decorations_of_the_Czech_Republic#The_Medal_of_Merit)[^o13] and the Gold medal of Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, Charles University in 2011. In 2012, he has been elected to the [Academia Europaea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academia_Europaea).[^o14] Also, he has been elected honorary member of the [Hungarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungarian_Academy_of_Sciences) in 2013.[^o15]

He was an invited speaker of the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846), in [Amsterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amsterdam), 2008,[^o16] and invited speaker (by both the Logic and Foundations and Combinatorics sections) at the Combinatorics session of the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad), 2010.[^o17]

In 2018, on the occasion of the 670th anniversary of the establishment of Charles University, Nešetřil has received from the rector of Charles university the [Donatio Universitatis Carolinae](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Donatio_Universitatis_Carolinae) prize “for his contribution to mathematics and for his leading role in establishing a world-renowned group in discrete mathematics at Charles University”.[^o18]

Together with [Patrice Ossona de Mendez](wikipedia://en/47882601), he received the 2025 [Nerode Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nerode_Prize) for his work on Sparsity for the papers *Grad and classes with bounded expansion I. Decompositions*, *Grad and classes with bounded expansion II. Algorithmic aspects*, *First order properties on nowhere dense structures*, and *On nowhere dense graphs*.[^o19]
