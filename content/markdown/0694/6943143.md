# Gordon Park Baker

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gordon Park Baker"}

**Gordon Park Baker** ([Englewood, New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Englewood,_New_Jersey), 20 April 1938 – [Woodstock, Oxfordshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Woodstock,_Oxfordshire), 25 June 2002) was an American-born English [philosopher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosopher). His topics of interest included [Ludwig Wittgenstein](wikipedia://en/17741), [Gottlob Frege](wikipedia://en/48416), [Friedrich Waismann](wikipedia://en/1724365), [Bertrand Russell](wikipedia://en/4163), the [Vienna Circle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_Circle), and [René Descartes](wikipedia://en/25525). He was noted for his collaboration with [Peter Hacker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Hacker) and his disagreements with [Michael Dummett](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Dummett).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Baker was educated at [Phillips Exeter Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Phillips_Exeter_Academy), [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (major in mathematics),[^o3] and, as a [Marshall Scholar](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marshall_Scholarship), at [The Queen's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Queen's_College,_Oxford), where he intended to read [Philosophy, Politics, and Economics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy,_Politics,_and_Economics) but transferred to [Literae Humaniores](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Literae_Humaniores) (1960). He pursued his doctorate (1963–70) while teaching at the [University of Kent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Kent) and latterly as a Fellow of [St John's College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_John's_College,_Oxford).

He was a Trustee of the Waismann Fund.

His other interests included [real tennis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real_tennis) and the [harpsichord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harpsichord).

He was married to Ann Pimlott (1964), with whom he had three sons: [Alan](wikipedia://en/39448532), Geoffrey, and Nicholas. [Alan](wikipedia://en/39448532) is currently a professor of philosophy at [Swarthmore College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Swarthmore_College). From 1992 until Gordon Baker's death the philosopher Katherine Morris ([Mansfield College, Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mansfield_College,_Oxford)) was his acknowledged companion.
