# Jean-Pierre Kahane

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Jean-Pierre Kahane"}

**Jean-Pierre Kahane** (11 December 1926 – 21 June 2017) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) with contributions to [harmonic analysis](wikipedia://en/14147).[^o3] He's also credited for introducing [sub-gaussian random variables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sub-Gaussian_Random_Variable).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

Kahane attended the [École normale supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure) and obtained the *[agrégation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agrégation)* of mathematics in 1949. He then worked for the [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centre_national_de_la_recherche_scientifique) from 1949 to 1954, first as an intern and then as a research assistant. He defended his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD) in 1954; his advisor was [Szolem Mandelbrojt](wikipedia://en/1070245).

He was assistant [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor), then professor of mathematics in [Montpellier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montpellier) from 1954 to 1961. Since then, he has been professor until his retirement in 1994,[^o6] then [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emeritus) at the [Université de Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_de_Paris-Sud) in [Orsay](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Orsay).

He was a Plenary Speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1962 in Stockholm[^o7] and an Invited Speaker at the 1986 ICM meeting in Berkeley, California.[^o8] He was elected corresponding member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1982 and full member in 1998.[^o9] He was president of the [Société mathématique de France](wikipedia://en/1691205), the French Mathematical Society from 1971 to 1973. In 2000 Kahane received an [honorary doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Honorary_degree) from the Faculty of Science and Technology at [Uppsala University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uppsala_University), [Sweden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden)[^o10] In 2002 he was elevated to the rank of commander in the order of the [Légion d'Honneur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Légion_d'Honneur).[^o11] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o12]

## Activism {#Activism}

Kahane was also known for his lifelong activism as part of the [French Communist Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Communist_Party).[^o14]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

- *Lectures on mean periodic functions* (Bombay, Tata Institute, 1959).
- *Séries de Fourier absolument convergentes*, Ergebnisse der Mathematik und ihrer Grenzgebiete, vol. 50, Springer Verlag 1970
- with [Raphaël Salem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raphaël_Salem): *Ensembles parfaits et séries trigonométriques*, Hermann, 1963,[^o16] 1994
- *Some random series of functions*, Lexington, Massachusetts, D. C. Heath 1968, 2nd edition Cambridge University Press 1985
- *Séries de Fourier aléatoires*, Presse de l’ Université de Montreal 1967
- editor with A. G. Howson: *The Popularization of Mathematics*, Cambridge University Press 1990
- *Des series de Taylor au movement brownien, avec un apercu sur le retour*, in Jean-Paul Pier, *Development of mathematics 1900–1950*, Birkhäuser 1994
