# Shmuel Weinberger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shmuel Weinberger"}

**Shmuel Aaron Weinberger** (born February 20, 1963) is an American [topologist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologist). He enrolled in a specially designed concurrent BA-PhD program at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), obtaining a BA in 1981 and a PhD in mathematics in 1982 under the direction of [Sylvain Cappell](wikipedia://en/17173914).[^o2] Weinberger was, from 1994 to 1996, the [Thomas A. Scott Professor of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_A._Scott_Professorship_of_Mathematics) at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania),[^o4] and he is currently the Andrew MacLeish Professor of Mathematics and chair of the Mathematics department at the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago).[^o5]

:::

His research interests include [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164), and, in recent years, applications of topology in other disciplines. He has written a book on [topologically stratified spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Topologically_stratified_space) and a book on the application of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636) to geometry.

He has given the Porter lectures at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) (2000), the Jankowski memorial lecture of the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences) (2000), the Zabrodsky Memorial lecture at [Hebrew University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University) (2001), the Cairns lectures at [University of Illinois at Urbana-Champaign](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Illinois_at_Urbana-Champaign) (2002), the Marker lectures in Mathematics at [Penn State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Penn_State_University) (2003), the Lewis Lectures at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) (2004), the Blumenthal Lectures at [Tel Aviv University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv_University) (2005), the Hardy Lectures of the [London Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/591097) (2008), the William Benter Lecture at the [City University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/City_University_of_Hong_Kong) (2010), the Clifford Lectures at [Tulane University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tulane_University) (2012), the Minerva Lectures at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) (2017) and the [Abraham Robinson](wikipedia://en/46024) lecture at Yale (2019). In addition he has given invited lectures at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich) (1994), a mini-symposium at the [European Congress of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/13505846) (2008), the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (1989), the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) (2006), the [Association for Symbolic Logic](wikipedia://en/3770438) (2001), and [Pembroke Pines Charter High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pembroke_Pines_Charter_High_School) (2021).

In 2012, he was elected to the first class of AMS fellows.[^o6] In 2013, he was elected as a fellow of AAAS.[^o7]
