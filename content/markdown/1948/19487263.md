# Tomio Kubota

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tomio Kubota"}

**Tomio Kubota** (6 December 1930 – 30 June 2020) was a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527). His contributions include works on [p-adic L functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_L_function) and [real-analytic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Real-analytic) [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146).[^o5]

:::

His work on p-adic L-functions, later recognised as an aspect of [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150), was done jointly with [Leopoldt](wikipedia://en/27700368).[^o7]

He extended the concept of [metaplectic group](wikipedia://en/2981795), in a way significant for arithmetic applications. This opened a field for later research on associated [Dirichlet series](wikipedia://en/393258) and [automorphic forms](wikipedia://en/782146), and was a major step in the solution of [Kummer's conjecture](wikipedia://en/6006062).[^o9]
