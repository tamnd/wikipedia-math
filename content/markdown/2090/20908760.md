# Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse"}

***Institut de Mathématiques de Toulouse*** (Toulouse Mathematics Institute; IMT) is a research laboratory of the [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) community of the [Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toulouse) area in France. It is partially supported by the French public research agency [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS) as unit [UMR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unité_mixte_de_recherche) 5129. In 2020 the research in IMT is organized into six main teams, with some overlap:

:::

- [Analyse](https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1023) (2021-04-18). <https://web.archive.org/web/20210418200454/https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1023>,
- [Dynamique et géométrie complexe](https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1024) (2021-04-18). <https://web.archive.org/web/20210418205455/https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1024>,
- [Équations aux dérivées partielles](https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1025) (2021-04-18). <https://web.archive.org/web/20210418170608/https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1025>,
- [Géométrie topologie algèbre](https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1026) (2021-04-18). <https://web.archive.org/web/20210418182835/https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1026>,
- [Probabilités](https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1027) (2021-04-18). <https://web.archive.org/web/20210418193227/https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1027>,
- [Statistiques et optimisation](https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1028) (2021-04-18). <https://web.archive.org/web/20210418181238/https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/spip.php?article1028>.

IMT is one of the largest French research centres in mathematics, and its scientific activities cover almost all domains of mathematics. It had approximately 200 permanent [researchers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Researcher) and 100 [PhD students](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PhD_student) in 2020, belonging to various institutions of the [University of Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Toulouse) and CNRS.

The main buildings of IMT are located on the [Paul Sabatier University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Sabatier_University) campus.

IMT is in charge of the [Fermat Prize](wikipedia://en/10919195) and of the publication *[Annales de la Faculté des Sciences de Toulouse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Annales_de_la_Faculté_des_Sciences_de_Toulouse)*.
