# Fabio Mercurio

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Fabio Mercurio"}

**Fabio Mercurio** (born 26 September 1966) is an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), internationally known for a number of results in [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680).

:::

## Main results {#Main_results}

Mercurio worked during his Ph.D. on [incomplete markets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Incomplete_markets) theory using dynamic mean-variance hedging techniques. With [Damiano Brigo](wikipedia://en/20519590) (2002–2003), he has shown how to construct [stochastic differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_differential_equations) consistent with [mixture models](wikipedia://en/871681), applying this to [volatility smile](wikipedia://en/1533070) modelling in the context of [local volatility](wikipedia://en/11548901) models.[^o4] He is also one of the main authors in inflation modelling.[^o5] Mercurio has also authored several publications in top journals and co-authored the book *Interest rate models: theory and practice* for Springer-Verlag,[^o6] that quickly became an international reference for stochastic dynamic interest rate modelling. He is the recipient of the 2020 Risk quant-of-the-year award[^o7] jointly with Andrei Lyashenko of QRM for their joint paper Lyashenko and Mercurio (2019).

## Affiliations {#Affiliations}

Currently, Mercurio is the global head of Quantitative Analytics at [Bloomberg L.P.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_L.P.), [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). He holds a [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in mathematical finance from the [Erasmus University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erasmus_University) in [Rotterdam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotterdam).
