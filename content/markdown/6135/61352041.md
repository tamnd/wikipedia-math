# Plumbing (mathematics)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Plumbing"}

*Image: Plumbing\_disk\_bundles.jpg.* Plumbing two 1-disk bundles to get a new 2-manifold.

In the mathematical field of [geometric topology](wikipedia://en/621732), among the techniques known as [surgery theory](wikipedia://en/5961115), the process of **plumbing** is a way to create new [manifolds](wikipedia://en/2073470) out of [disk bundles](wikipedia://en/48880896). It was first described by [John Milnor](wikipedia://en/61632) (1956) and subsequently used extensively in surgery theory to produce manifolds and [normal maps](wikipedia://en/21298614) with given [surgery obstructions](wikipedia://en/21298802).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Let $\xi_i=(E_i,M_i,p_i)$ be a [rank](wikipedia://en/26561) *n* [vector bundle](wikipedia://en/276524) over an *n*-dimensional [smooth manifold](wikipedia://en/2119219) $M_i$ for *i* = 1,2. Denote by $D(E_i)$ the total space of the associated (closed) disk bundle $D(\xi_i)$and suppose that $\xi_i, M_i$ and $D(E_i)$are oriented in a compatible way. If we pick two points $x_i\in M_i$, *i* = 1,2, and consider a ball neighbourhood of $x_i$ in $M_i$, then we get neighbourhoods $D^n_i\times D^n_i$ of the fibres over the ball in $D(E_i)$. Let $h:D^n_1\rightarrow D^n_2$ and $k:D^n_1\rightarrow D^n_2$ be two diffeomorphisms (either both orientation preserving or reversing). The **plumbing** (Browder, 1972) of $D(E_1)$ and $D(E_2)$ at $x_1$ and $x_2$ is defined to be the [quotient space](wikipedia://en/237213) $P=D(E_1)\cup_f D(E_2)$ where $f:D^n_1\times D^n_1\rightarrow D^n_2\times D^n_2$ is defined by $f(x,y)=(k(y),h(x))$. The smooth structure on the quotient is defined by "straightening the angles" (Browder, 1972).

### Plumbing according to a tree {#Plumbing_according_to_a_tree}

If the base manifold is an *n*-sphere $S^n$, then by iterating this procedure over several vector bundles over $S^n$ one can plumb them together according to a [tree](wikipedia://en/48560) (Hirzebruch et al., 1993, §8). If $T$ is a tree, we assign to each vertex a vector bundle *$\xi$* over $S^n$ and we plumb the corresponding disk bundles together if two vertices are connected by an edge. One has to be careful that neighbourhoods in the total spaces do not overlap.

:::

## Milnor manifolds {#Milnor_manifolds}

Let $D(\tau_{S^{2k}})$ denote the disk bundle associated to the [tangent bundle](wikipedia://en/211794) of the *2k*-sphere. If we plumb eight copies of $D(\tau_{S^{2k}})$ according to the [diagram $E_8$](wikipedia://en/277109), we obtain a *4k*-dimensional manifold which certain authors (Madsen and Milgram, 1979; López de Medrano, 1971) call the **Milnor manifold** $M^{4k}_B$ (see also [E<sub>8</sub> manifold](wikipedia://en/6091657)).

For $k>1$, the boundary $\Sigma^{4k-1}=\partial M^{4k}_B$ is a [homotopy sphere](wikipedia://en/1202074) which generates $\theta^{4k-1}(\partial \pi)$, the group of [*h*-cobordism](wikipedia://en/681049) classes of homotopy spheres which bound [π-manifolds](wikipedia://en/3108161) (see also [exotic spheres](wikipedia://en/1114931) for more details). Its signature is $sgn(M^{4k}_B)=8$ and there exists (Browder, 1972, V.2.9) a [normal map](wikipedia://en/21298614) $(f,b)$ such that the [surgery obstruction](wikipedia://en/21298802) is $\sigma(f,b)=1$, where $g:(M^{4k}_B,\partial M^{4k}_B)\rightarrow (D^{4k},S^{4k-1})$ is a map of degree 1 and $b:\nu_{M^{4k}_B} \rightarrow \xi$ is a [bundle map](wikipedia://en/7220589) from the [stable normal bundle](wikipedia://en/9901365) of the Milnor manifold to a certain [stable vector bundle](wikipedia://en/18909129).

## The plumbing theorem {#The_plumbing_theorem}

A crucial theorem for the development of surgery theory is the so-called *Plumbing Theorem* (Browder, 1972, II.1.3) (presented here in the [simply connected](wikipedia://en/523879) case):

For all $k>1, l\in \mathbb{Z}$, there exists a *2k*-dimensional manifold $M$ with boundary $\partial M$ and a normal map $(g,c)$ where $g:(M,\partial M)\rightarrow (D^{2k},S^{2k-1})$ is such that $g|_{\partial M}$ is a homotopy equivalence, $c$ is a bundle map into the [trivial bundle](wikipedia://en/276526) and the surgery obstruction is $\sigma(g,c)=l$.

The proof of this theorem makes use of the Milnor manifolds defined above.
