# Vladimir Kondrashov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Kondrashov"}

**Vladimir Iosifovich Kondrashov** (Владимир Иосифович Кондрашов; 2 February 1909 – 26 February 1971) was a Soviet mathematician most well known for proving the [Rellich–Kondrachov theorem](wikipedia://en/7907151) that shows that the embedding of certain [Sobolev spaces](wikipedia://en/611964) into [L<sup>*p*</sup> spaces](wikipedia://en/45194) is [compact](wikipedia://en/6746775). His name has also been transliterated as V.I. Kondrachoff, W. Kondrachov, or V.I. Kondrašov.[^o2]

:::

## Life {#Life}

Kondrashov was born on 2 February in Moscow.[^o4] He graduated with a PhD from [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University) under the supervision of [Sergei Sobolev](wikipedia://en/380798) in 1941. As a postdoc at [Steklov Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steklov_Institute), where he obtained a DSc in 1950, he organised the Moscow Seminar on the Theory of Functions of Several Variables.[^o5][^o6] For the final 20 years of his life until his death on 26 February 1971, he worked at the [Moscow Engineering and Physics Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_Engineering_and_Physics_Institute).[^o4]
