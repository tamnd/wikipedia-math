# First moment of area

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="First moment of area"}

The **first moment of area** is based on the mathematical construct [moments in metric spaces](wikipedia://en/368684#Central_moments_in_metric_spaces). It is a measure of the spatial distribution of a shape in relation to an axis.

:::

The first moment of area of a shape, about a certain axis, equals the sum over all the [infinitesimal](wikipedia://en/160990) parts of the shape of the area of that part times its distance from the axis \[Σ*ad*\].

First moment of area is commonly used to determine the [centroid](wikipedia://en/187926) of an area.

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

Given an area A of any shape and a division of that area into n very small elemental areas (dA<sub>i</sub>), let x<sub>i</sub> and y<sub>i</sub> be the distances (coordinates) to each elemental area measured from a given x–y axis. The first moment of area in the x and y directions are respectively given by:

$$S_x = A \bar y = \sum_{i=1}^n {y_i \, dA_i} = \int_A y \, dA$$

and

$$S_y= A \bar x = \sum_{i=1}^n {x_i \, dA_i} = \int_A x \, dA.$$

The [SI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_System_of_Units) unit for **first moment of area** is a cubic [metre](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metre) (m<sup>3</sup>). In the American Engineering and Gravitational systems the unit is a cubic [foot](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Foot_%28length%29) (ft<sup>3</sup>) or more commonly [inch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inch)<sup>3</sup>.

The **static** or **statical moment of area**, usually denoted by the symbol *Q*, is a property of a shape that is used to predict its resistance to [shear stress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shear_stress). By definition:

$$Q_{j,x} = \int y_i \, dA,$$

where

- *Q*<sub>*j,x*</sub> – the first moment of area "j" about the neutral *x* axis of the entire body (not the [neutral axis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neutral_axis) of the area "j");
- *dA* – an elemental area of area "j";
- *y* – the perpendicular distance to the centroid of element *dA* from the neutral axis *x*.

### Shear stress in a semi-monocoque structure {#Shear_stress_in_a_semi-monocoque_structure}

The equation for [shear flow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shear_flow) in a particular web section of the cross-section of a [semi-monocoque](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semi-monocoque) structure is:

$$q = \frac{V_y S_x}{I_x}$$

- *q* – the shear flow through a particular web section of the cross-section
- *V*<sub>*y*</sub> – the [shear force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shear_force) perpendicular to the neutral axis *x* through the entire cross-section
- *S*<sub>*x*</sub> – the first moment of area about the neutral axis *x* for a particular web section of the cross-section
- *I*<sub>*x*</sub> – the [second moment of area](wikipedia://en/1736264) about the neutral axis *x* for the entire cross-section

[Shear stress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shear_stress) may now be calculated using the following equation:

$$\tau = \frac{q}{t}$$

- $\tau$ – the shear stress through a particular web section of the cross-section
- *q* – the shear flow through a particular web section of the cross-section
- *t* – the thickness of a particular web section of the cross-section at the point being measured[^o5]
