# Arne Meurman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Arne Meurman"}

**Arne Meurman** (born 6 April 1956) is a [Swedish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sweden) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) working on [finite groups](wikipedia://en/323707) and [vertex operator algebras](wikipedia://en/1287577). He is a [professor emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at [Lund University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lund_University).[^o2]

:::

He is best known for constructing the [monster vertex algebra](wikipedia://en/5032859) together with [Igor Frenkel](wikipedia://en/5033014) and [James Lepowsky](wikipedia://en/5033025).

In his free time Meurman plays chess, and he has competed in the finals of the top division in the regional [Allskånskan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allskånskan) competition with Lunds ASK.[^o3][^o4]

## Publications {#Publications}

- Arne Meurman, Mirko Primc, "Annihilating fields of standard modules of sl (2,C) and combinatorial identities", Memoirs AMS 1999
