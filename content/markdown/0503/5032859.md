# Monster vertex algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monster vertex algebra"}

The **monster vertex algebra** (or **moonshine module**) is a [vertex algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex_algebra) acted on by the [monster group](wikipedia://en/47422) that was constructed by [Igor Frenkel](wikipedia://en/5033014), [James Lepowsky](wikipedia://en/5033025), and [Arne Meurman](wikipedia://en/5033047). [R. Borcherds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/R._Borcherds) used it to prove the [monstrous moonshine](wikipedia://en/728168) conjectures, by applying the [Goddard–Thorn theorem](wikipedia://en/730378) of [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) to construct the [monster Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/1682953), an infinite-dimensional [generalized Kac–Moody algebra](wikipedia://en/1646167) acted on by the monster.

:::

The [Griess algebra](wikipedia://en/728596) is the same as the degree 2 piece of the monster vertex algebra, and the Griess product is one of the vertex algebra products. It can be constructed as [conformal field theory](wikipedia://en/364774) describing 24 free bosons compactified on the torus induced by the [Leech lattice](wikipedia://en/344919) and [orbifolded](wikipedia://en/293504) by the two-element reflection group.
