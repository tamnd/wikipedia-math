# James Lepowsky

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="James Lepowsky"}

**James Lepowsky** (born July 5, 1944) is a professor of mathematics at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey). Previously he taught at [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University). He received his Ph.D. from [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1970 where his advisors were [Bertram Kostant](wikipedia://en/3520590) and [Sigurdur Helgason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigurdur_Helgason_%28mathematician%29). Lepowsky graduated from [Stuyvesant High School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stuyvesant_High_School) in 1961,[^o3] 16 years after Kostant.[^o4] His current research is in the areas of infinite-dimensional [Lie algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lie_algebras) and [vertex algebras](wikipedia://en/1287577). He has written several books on vertex algebras and related topics. In 1988, in a joint work with [Igor Frenkel](wikipedia://en/5033014) and [Arne Meurman](wikipedia://en/5033047), he constructed the [monster vertex algebra](wikipedia://en/5032859) (also known as the Moonshine module). His PhD students include Stefano Capparelli, Yi-Zhi Huang, Haisheng Li, Arne Meurman, and Antun Milas.

:::

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
