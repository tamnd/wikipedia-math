# Isabella Novik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Isabella Novik"}

**Isabella Novik** (איזבלה נוביק; born 1971)[^o3] is a mathematician who works at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington) as the [Robert R. and Elaine F. Phelps](wikipedia://en/31368502) professor in mathematics. Her research concerns [algebraic combinatorics](wikipedia://en/14835582) and [polyhedral combinatorics](wikipedia://en/19696519).[^o4]

:::

Novik earned her Ph.D. from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) in 1999, under the supervision of [Gil Kalai](wikipedia://en/19709056). Her [doctoral dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis), *Face Numbers of Polytopes and Manifolds*, won the Haim Nessyahu Prize in Mathematics, awarded by the [Israel Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/59341674) for the best annual doctoral dissertations in mathematics.[^o6]

She was an Alfred P. Sloan research fellow for 2006–2008,[^o7] and was elected as a member of the 2017 class of [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to algebraic and geometric combinatorics".[^o8]
