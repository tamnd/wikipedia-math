# Kawasaki's Riemann–Roch formula

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kawasaki's Riemann–Roch formula"}

In [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625), **Kawasaki's Riemann–Roch formula**, introduced by Tetsuro Kawasaki, is the [Riemann–Roch formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann–Roch_formula) for [orbifolds](wikipedia://en/293504). It can compute the [Euler characteristic of an orbifold](wikipedia://en/54504773).

:::

Kawasaki's original proof made a use of the [equivariant index theorem](wikipedia://en/35112066). Today, the formula is known to follow from the [Riemann–Roch formula](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Riemann–Roch_formula_for_stacks) for [quotient stacks](wikipedia://en/40579087).
