# Tomek Bartoszyński

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Tomek Bartoszyński"}

**Tomasz** "**Tomek**" **Bartoszyński** (born 16 May 1957) is a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland)-American mathematician who works in [set theory](wikipedia://en/27553).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Bartoszyński studied mathematics at the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) from 1976 to 1981, and worked there from 1981 to 1987. In 1984, he defended his Ph.D. thesis *Combinatorial aspects of measure and category*; his advisor was Wojciech Guzicki. In 2004, he received his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) from the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences).

From 1986 onward, he worked in the United States: he taught at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California) and [University of California, Davis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Davis). From 1990 to 2006, he was a professor (full professor from 1998-2006) at [Boise State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boise_State_University).

In 1990-1991, he visited the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) as a [fellow of the Lady Davis foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lady_Davis_Fellows), and in 1996/97 he visited the [Free University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_University_of_Berlin) as a [Humboldt fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_von_Humboldt_Foundation). Currently, he is one of the program directors at the [National Science Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation) (NSF), responsible for [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170), [foundations](wikipedia://en/169358), and [probability](wikipedia://en/22934).

## Family {#Family}

His father, Robert Bartoszyński, is a statistician.

His wife, Joanna Kania-Bartoszyńska, is the NSF program director for [topology](wikipedia://en/29954) and [geometric analysis](wikipedia://en/4325474).

## Scientific work {#Scientific_work}

Bartoszyński's work is mainly concerned with [forcing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forcing_%28set_theory%29), specifically with applications of forcing to the [set theory of the real line](wikipedia://en/4937732). He has written about 50 papers in this field, as well as a monograph:

- Tomek Bartoszyński and [Haim Judah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haim_Judah): *Set theory. On the structure of the real line.* A K Peters, Ltd., Wellesley, MA, 1995;
