# Burton Wendroff

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Burton Wendroff"}

**Burton Wendroff** (born March 10, 1930) is an American applied mathematician known for his contributions to the development of numerical methods for the solution of [hyperbolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/1575825). The [Lax–Wendroff method](wikipedia://en/9504881) for the solution of hyperbolic PDE is named for Wendroff (as well as for [Peter Lax](wikipedia://en/486365)).

:::

Wendroff is an adjunct professor at the Department of Mathematics and Statistics, [University of New Mexico](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_New_Mexico). He is also a retired fellow and associate at the [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory).

Wendroff is a primary author of the chess program Lachex. Together with co-author Tony Warnock, Lachex competed at two [World Computer Chess Championships](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_Computer_Chess_Championships) at Cologne (1986) and Madrid (1992).[^o3][^o4]

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Wendroff received his B.A. degree in mathematics and physics from the [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) in 1951 and M.S. degree in mathematics from the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1952. After his M.S., Burt joined [Los Alamos National Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Los_Alamos_National_Laboratory) as a staff member. While at Los Alamos, he went to New York University to do his Ph.D. and received the degree under the supervision of [Peter Lax](wikipedia://en/486365) in 1958.[^o6] The title of his Ph.D. thesis was "Finite Difference Approximations to the Solutions of Partial Differential Equations". During 1966 to 1973, he served as a professor at the [University of Denver](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Denver).[^o7]

His primary area of research involves the development of [numerical schemes](wikipedia://en/1678715) for [hyperbolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/1575825) using [finite difference method](wikipedia://en/6054681). Together with Peter Lax, he has developed the now classical [Lax–Wendroff method](wikipedia://en/9504881).[^o8][^o9] He has developed two-dimensional HLLE [Riemann solver](wikipedia://en/9461390) and associated [Godunov-type difference scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Godunov's_scheme) for [gas dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gas_dynamics) problems. Wendroff has also made contribution to the early development of convergence study of [finite element method](wikipedia://en/18233581).[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Wendroff was elected as SIAM Fellow of [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) for his "contributions to the numerical solution of [partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564)".[^o12]
