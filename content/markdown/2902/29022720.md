# Robert Lazarsfeld

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert Lazarsfeld"}

**Robert Kendall Lazarsfeld** (born April 15, 1953) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specializes in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). He is currently a distinguished professor of mathematics at [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University).[^o3] He was previously the [Raymond L. Wilder](wikipedia://en/4297377) Collegiate Professor of Mathematics at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan).[^o4] He is the son of two sociologists, [Paul Lazarsfeld](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Lazarsfeld) and Patricia Kendall.

:::

## Career {#Career}

Lazarsfeld received his B.A. from [Harvard College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_College) in 1975 and earned his Ph.D. from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1980 under supervision of [William Fulton](wikipedia://en/9642149).

From 1983 to 1998, Lazarsfeld was an assistant professor, associate professor, and professor of mathematics at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). He joined the faculty of the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1997 and served as the Raymond L. Wilder Collegiate Professor there from 2007 to 2013. In 2013 he joined the faculty of [Stony Brook University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stony_Brook_University), where he was the chair of the Mathematics Department from 2016-2019. Since 2015, he is a distinguished professor at Stony Brook.

From 2002–2009, Lazarsfeld was an editor at the *[Journal of the American Mathematical Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_the_American_Mathematical_Society)* (Managing Editor, 2007–2009).[^o7] In 2012–2013, he served as the Managing Editor of the *[Michigan Mathematical Journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan_Mathematical_Journal)*.[^o8]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2006, Lazarsfeld was elected as a Fellow of the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o10]

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]

In 2015 he was awarded the AMS [Leroy P. Steele Prize](wikipedia://en/1252550) for Mathematical Exposition.[^o12]
