# Christopher Hacon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christopher Hacon"}

**Christopher Derek Hacon** (born 14 February 1970) is a mathematician with British, Italian and US nationalities.[^o3] He is currently distinguished professor of mathematics at the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) where he holds a Presidential Endowed Chair. His research interests include [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

Hacon was born in [Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manchester), but grew up in Italy where he studied at the [Scuola Normale Superiore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scuola_Normale_Superiore) and received a degree in mathematics at the [University of Pisa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pisa) in 1992. He received his doctorate from the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1998, under supervision of [Robert Lazarsfeld](wikipedia://en/29022720).

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2007, he was awarded a Clay Research Award for his work, joint with [James McKernan](wikipedia://en/25690427), on "the birational geometry of algebraic varieties in dimension greater than three, in particular, for \[an\] inductive proof of the existence of flips." [^o5]

In 2009, he was awarded the [Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) for outstanding contribution to algebra, along with McKernan.[^o6]

He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) 2010 in [Hyderabad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyderabad), on the topic of "*Algebraic Geometry.*"[^o7]

In 2011, he was awarded the [Antonio Feltrinelli Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Feltrinelli_Prize) in Mathematics, Mechanics and Applications by Italy's prestigious [Accademia Nazionale dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).[^o8]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]

In 2012, he became a [Simons Investigator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Investigator).[^o10]

In 2015, he won the American Mathematical Society Moore Prize.[^o11]

In 2017, he was elected to the American Academy of Arts and Sciences.[^o12]

In 2017, he won the 2018 [Breakthrough Prize in Mathematics](wikipedia://en/43125933) (with [James McKernan](wikipedia://en/25690427)).[^o13]

In 2018, he was elected to the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Academy_of_Sciences).

In 2019, he was elected to the [Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society).[^o14]
