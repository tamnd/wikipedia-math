# Alfredo Capelli

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Alfredo Capelli"}

**Alfredo Capelli** (5 August 1855 – 28 January 1910) was an Italian mathematician who discovered [Capelli's identity](wikipedia://en/22069816).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Capelli earned his [Laurea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurea) from the [University of Rome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sapienza_University_of_Rome) in 1877 under [Giuseppe Battaglini](wikipedia://en/34349418), and moved to the [University of Pavia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pavia) where he worked as an assistant for [Felice Casorati](wikipedia://en/5697549). In 1881 he became the professor of Algebraic Analysis at the [University of Palermo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Palermo), replacing [Cesare Arzelà](wikipedia://en/1683941) who had recently moved to Bologna. In 1886, he moved again to the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples), where he held the chair in algebra. He remained at [Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Naples) until his death in 1910. As well as being professor there, Capelli was editor of the *Giornale di Matematiche di Battaglini* from 1894 to 1910, and was elected to the [Accademia dei Lincei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accademia_dei_Lincei).
