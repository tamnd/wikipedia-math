# Lorenzo Pareschi

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Lorenzo Pareschi"}

**Lorenzo Pareschi** is an Italian mathematician working in [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506), [applied mathematics](wikipedia://en/24295969), [kinetic theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kinetic_theory), and multiscale computational methods. He is Chair of Applied and Computational Mathematics at [Heriot-Watt University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heriot-Watt_University) and professor of numerical analysis at the [University of Ferrara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ferrara).[^o3][^o4][^o5]

:::

## Career and research {#Career_and_research}

Pareschi received his PhD in mathematics in 1996 from the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna). He has held visiting and research appointments at institutions including the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison), [Georgia Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Institute_of_Technology), [Université d'Orléans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_d'Orléans), [Université Paul Sabatier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paul_Sabatier), and the Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o7]

His research concerns numerical methods for [kinetic equations](wikipedia://en/64204), [implicit-explicit methods](wikipedia://en/3652485) for [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [uncertainty quantification](wikipedia://en/5987648), structure-preserving methods, and mathematical models of [collective behaviour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Collective_behaviour).[^o3][^o8] He is particularly known for work on the numerical analysis of the [Boltzmann equation](wikipedia://en/1026522) and related kinetic models, including fast [spectral methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Spectral_methods) for the computation of the Boltzmann collision operator,[^o9] and for contributions to implicit-explicit [Runge-Kutta methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge-Kutta_methods) for multiscale [hyperbolic systems](wikipedia://en/1575825).[^o10]

His later work has also included [optimisation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Optimisation), [control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Control), [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), [social dynamics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Social_dynamics), and [epidemic modelling](wikipedia://en/951614).[^o3][^o11]

## Publications {#Publications}

Pareschi is the author or co-author of research monographs and advanced texts in numerical analysis and kinetic theory. With Giuseppe Toscani, he co-authored *Interacting Multiagent Systems: Kinetic Equations and Monte Carlo Methods*, published by [Oxford University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oxford_University_Press) in 2013.[^o13] In 2024 he co-authored *Implicit-Explicit Methods for Evolutionary Partial Differential Equations* with Sebastiano Boscarino and Giovanni Russo, published by [SIAM](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM).[^o14]

Among his most cited survey works is the article *Numerical Methods for Kinetic Equations*, published in *[Acta Numerica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Acta_Numerica)* in 2014.[^o15]

## Editorial work {#Editorial_work}

Pareschi has served on the editorial boards of several journals in applied and computational mathematics.[^o7] In 2025 the Maxwell Institute announced his appointment as the next Editor-in-Chief of *[Multiscale Modeling & Simulation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Multiscale_Modeling_&_Simulation)*, with effect from January 2026, and SIAM lists him as Editor-in-Chief for the journal's 2026 volume.[^o17][^o18]

## Honours and awards {#Honours_and_awards}

Among his distinctions are the Nelder Visiting Fellowship at [Imperial College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College_London) in 2015, the John von Neumann Professorship at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) in 2019, and a [Royal Society Wolfson Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_Wolfson_Fellowship) awarded in 2023. [^o20][^o3][^o21][^o22]

In 2025 he was awarded an Advanced Grant from the Italian Science Fund of [MUR](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ministry_of_Education,_University_and_Research) for the ADAMUS project, devoted to advanced numerical methods for multiscale systems with uncertainty.[^o23][^o24][^o25][^o26]
