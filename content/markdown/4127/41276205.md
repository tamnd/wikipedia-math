# Module spectrum

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Module spectrum"}

In algebra, a **module spectrum** is a [spectrum](wikipedia://en/1033697) with an action of a [ring spectrum](wikipedia://en/21118494); it generalizes a [module](wikipedia://en/276410) in abstract algebra.

:::

The [∞-category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/∞-category) of (say right) module spectra is [stable](wikipedia://en/42081371); hence, it can be considered as either analog or generalization of the [derived category](wikipedia://en/985897) of modules over a ring.

## K-theory {#K-theory}

[Lurie](wikipedia://en/26663863) defines the [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748) of a ring spectrum *R* to be the [K-theory of the ∞-category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-theory_of_the_∞-category) of [perfect modules](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perfect_module) over *R* (a perfect module being defined as a [compact object](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_object) in the ∞-category of module spectra).
