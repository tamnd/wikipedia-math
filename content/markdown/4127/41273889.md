# Infinite loop space machine

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Infinite loop space machine"}

In topology, a branch of mathematics, given a topological monoid *X* up to homotopy (in a nice way), an **infinite loop space machine** produces a [group completion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Group_completion) of *X* together with [infinite loop space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_loop_space) structure. For example, one can take *X* to be the [classifying space](wikipedia://en/923556) of a [symmetric monoidal category](wikipedia://en/3865249) *S*; that is, $X = BS$. Then the machine produces the group completion $BS \to K(S)$. The space $K(S)$ may be described by the [K-theory spectrum](wikipedia://en/41274264) of *S*.

:::

In 1977 [Robert Thomason](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Robert_Wayne_Thomason) proved the equivalence of all infinite loop space machines[^o2] (he was just 25 years old at the time). He published this result next year in a joint paper with [Jon Peter May](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jon_Peter_May).
