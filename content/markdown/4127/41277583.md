# Alec Muffett

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Alec Muffett"}

**Alec David Edward Muffett** (born 22 April 1968) is an Anglo-American [internet security](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Internet_security) expert and [software engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Software_engineering).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Muffett joined [Sun Microsystems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_Microsystems) in 1992, working initially as a [systems administrator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Systems_administrator). He rose through the ranks to become the principal engineer for security, a position which he held until he was retrenched, with many others, in 2009[^o4] (shortly before [Oracle acquired Sun](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sun_acquisition_by_Oracle)). While at Sun he was one of the researchers who worked on the [factorization](wikipedia://en/82341) of the 512 bit [RSA Challenge](wikipedia://en/498851) Number; [RSA-155](wikipedia://en/511379) was successfully factorized in August 1999.[^o5] He created [Crack](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crack_%28password_software%29), the original [password cracker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Password_cracker) for [Unix](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unix), and for the CrackLib password-integrity testing library.

In 2015, Muffett was named as one of the top six influential security thinkers by *[SC Magazine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SC_Magazine)*.[^o6]

Muffett assisted in creating [.onion](wikipedia://en/3648703) websites for *[The New York Times](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_Times),*[^o7] Wikipedia,[^o8] [BBC News](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BBC_News),[^o9] [Brave](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brave_%28web_browser%29),[^o10] [Twitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twitter),[^o11] [The Guardian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Guardian),[^o12] and [Reddit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reddit).[^o13]
