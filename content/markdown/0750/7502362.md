# Cobra ciphers

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cobra ciphers"}

In [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432), **Cobra** is the general name of a family of data-dependent permutation based [block ciphers](wikipedia://en/4594): Cobra-S128, Cobra-F64a, Cobra-F64b, Cobra-H64, and Cobra-H128. In each of these names, the number indicates the cipher's [block size](wikipedia://en/294065), and the capital letter indicates whether it is optimized for implementation in software, firmware, or hardware.

:::
