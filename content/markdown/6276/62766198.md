# Ina Kersten

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ina Kersten"}

*Image: Ina Kersten.jpg.* Ina Kersten at the memorial colloquium for [Ernst Witt](wikipedia://en/2619911), Hamburg, 1991

**Ina Kersten** (born 1946) is a German mathematician and former president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973). Her research concerns [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384) including the theory of [field extensions](wikipedia://en/11634) and [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924). She is a [professor emerita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_emerita) at the [University of Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Göttingen).

:::

Kersten was born in [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg), and earned her Ph.D. at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) in 1977. Her dissertation, *p-Algebren über semilokalen Ringen*, was supervised by [Ernst Witt](wikipedia://en/2619911). She completed a [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) at the [University of Regensburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Regensburg) in 1983.

Kersten was president of the German Mathematical Society from 1995 to 1997, which meant she was the first woman to head the society. Under her leadership, the society founded the journal *Documenta Mathematica*.
