# Monica Nevins

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Monica Nevins"}

**Monica A. Nevins** (born 1973) is a Canadian mathematician, and a professor of mathematics and statistics at the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa). Her research interests include [abstract algebra](wikipedia://en/19616384), [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200), [algebraic groups](wikipedia://en/247924), and mathematical [cryptography](wikipedia://en/18934432).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Nevins went to high school in [Val-d'Or](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Val-d'Or), Quebec. She graduated from the [University of Ottawa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Ottawa) in 1994, and completed a PhD in mathematics at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 1998. Her dissertation, *Admissible Nilpotent Coadjoint Orbits of p-adic Reductive Lie Groups*, was supervised by [David Vogan](wikipedia://en/10229758).

After postdoctoral research at the [University of Alberta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Alberta), Nevins joined the faculty of the University of Ottawa, where she was promoted to full professor in 2014.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Nevins was the 2010–2011 winner of the University of Ottawa Award for Excellence in Teaching. She was elected as a fellow of the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) in 2019.

## Personal {#Personal}

Monica married Ralph Nevins, who is a computer scientist and mathematical artist.
