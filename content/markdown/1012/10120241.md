::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="5-demicubic honeycomb"}

The **5-demicube honeycomb** (or **demipenteractic honeycomb**) is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 5-space. It is constructed as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the regular [5-cube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/5-cube_honeycomb).

:::

It is the first tessellation in the [demihypercube honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternated_hypercubic_honeycomb) family which, with all the next ones, is not regular, being composed of two different types of [uniform](wikipedia://en/6842392) [facets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Facet_%28mathematics%29). The [5-cubes](wikipedia://en/7184215) become alternated into [5-demicubes](wikipedia://en/7186232) h{4,3,3,3} and the alternated vertices create [5-orthoplex](wikipedia://en/7594615) {3,3,3,4} facets.

::: {#o9 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="D5 lattice"}

## D5 lattice {#D5_lattice}

The [vertex arrangement](wikipedia://en/14308644) of the **5-demicubic honeycomb** is the **D<sub>5</sub> lattice** which is the densest known [sphere packing](wikipedia://en/368621) in 5 dimensions.[^o10] The 40 vertices of the [rectified 5-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rectified_5-orthoplex) [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) of the *5-demicubic honeycomb* reflect the [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) 40 of this lattice.[^o11]

The D+ packing (also called D2) can be constructed by the union of two D<sub>5</sub> lattices. The analogous packings form lattices only in even dimensions. The kissing number is 2<sup>4</sup>=16 (2<sup>n−1</sup> for n\<8, 240 for n=8, and 2n(n−1) for n>8).[^o12]

∪

The D\*[^o13] lattice (also called D4 and C2) can be constructed by the union of all four 5-demicubic lattices:[^o14] It is also the 5-dimensional [body centered cubic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Body_centered_cubic), the union of two 5-cube honeycombs in dual positions.

∪ ∪ ∪ = ∪ .

The [kissing number](wikipedia://en/408555) of the D\* lattice is 10 (*2n* for n≥5) and its [Voronoi tessellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_tessellation) is a [tritruncated 5-cubic honeycomb](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tritruncated_5-cubic_honeycomb), , containing all [bitruncated 5-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_5-orthoplex), [Voronoi cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Voronoi_cell).[^o15]

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are three uniform construction symmetries of this tessellation. Each symmetry can be represented by arrangements of different colors on the 32 [5-demicube](wikipedia://en/7186232) facets around each vertex.
