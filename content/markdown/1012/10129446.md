# Tsen's theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tsen's theorem"}

In mathematics, **Tsen's theorem** states that a function field *K* of an [algebraic curve](wikipedia://en/253260) over an [algebraically closed field](wikipedia://en/1018) is [quasi-algebraically closed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-algebraically_closed) (i.e., **C<sub>1</sub>**). This implies that the [Brauer group](wikipedia://en/404078) of any such field vanishes,[^o2] and more generally that all the [Galois cohomology](wikipedia://en/1111315) groups *H*<sup>*i*</sup>(*K*, *K*<sup>\*</sup>) vanish for *i* ≥ 1. This result is used to calculate the [étale cohomology](wikipedia://en/396231) groups of an algebraic curve.

:::

The theorem was published by [Chiungtze C. Tsen](wikipedia://en/10143482) in 1933.
