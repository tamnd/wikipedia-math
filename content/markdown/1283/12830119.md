# List of triangle topics

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of triangle topics"}

This list of [triangle](wikipedia://en/30654) topics includes things related to the geometric shape, either abstractly, as in idealizations studied by geometers, or in triangular arrays such as [Pascal's triangle](wikipedia://en/49497) or [triangular matrices](wikipedia://en/374222), or concretely in physical space. It does not include metaphors like [love triangle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_triangle) in which the word has no reference to the geometric shape.

:::
