# Bernt Øksendal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Bernt Øksendal"}

**Bernt Karsten Øksendal** (born 10 April 1945 in [Fredrikstad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fredrikstad)) is a [Norwegian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norway) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He completed his undergraduate studies at the [University of Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oslo), working under [Otte Hustad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Otte_Hustad). He obtained his [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) from [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles) in 1971; his [thesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thesis) was titled *Peak Sets and Interpolation Sets for Some Algebras of Analytic Functions* and was supervised by [Theodore Gamelin](wikipedia://en/53092669). In 1991, he was appointed as a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) at the University of Oslo. In 1992, he was appointed as an adjunct professor at the [Norwegian School of Economics and Business Administration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_School_of_Economics_and_Business_Administration), [Bergen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bergen), Norway.

:::

His main field of interest is [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_processes), including [stochastic control](wikipedia://en/17058216), optimal stopping, stochastic ordinary and partial [differential equations](wikipedia://en/1424309) and applications, particularly to [physics](wikipedia://en/22939), [biology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biology) and [finance](wikipedia://en/33589680). For his contributions to these fields, he was awarded the [Nansen Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nansen_Prize) in 1996. He has been a member of the [Norwegian Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters) since 1996. He was elected as a member of the [Norwegian Royal Society of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Royal_Society_of_Sciences) in 2002.

As of February 2003, Øksendal has over 130 published works, including nine books. In 1982 he taught a postgraduate course in stochastic calculus at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh) which led to the book Øksendal, Bernt K. (1982). "Stochastic Differential Equations: An Introduction with Applications". Springer, Berlin In 2005, he taught a course in stochastic calculus at the [African Institute for Mathematical Sciences](wikipedia://en/1575286) in [Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Town).

He resided at [Hosle](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hosle).[^o4] He married Eva Aursland in June 1968. They have three children: Elise (born 1971), Anders (1974) and Karina (1981).

## Awards and appointments {#Awards_and_appointments}

- Visiting research fellow at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh), Scotland. The fellowship was awarded by the Science and Engineering Research Council, UK (Jan.-June 1982).
- Norwegian Mathematical Society (chairman 1987-1989)
- Appointed VISTA Professor by The Norwegian Academy of Sciences (1992–1996)
- Awarded Nansen Prize (1996)
- Elected member of Royal Norwegian Science Society (2002)
- European Research Council's Advanced Grant for Innovations in Stochastic Analysis and Applications (INNOSTOCH) (2009–2014)
- Awarded University of Oslo Research Prize for excellent research (2014)
- Scientific leader of the research program Challenges in Stochastic Control, Information and Applications (STOCONINF) (2016–2020)
- Appointed honorary doctor at the Norwegian School of Economics
