# Yuri Prokhorov

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Yuri Prokhorov"}

**Yuri Vasilyevich Prokhorov** (Ю́рий Васи́льевич Про́хоров; 15 December 1929 – 16 July 2013) was a Soviet and Russian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), active in the field of [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542). He was a [PhD student](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) of [Andrey Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161) at the [Moscow State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moscow_State_University), where he obtained his PhD in 1956.

:::

Prokhorov became a corresponding member of the [Academy of Sciences of the Soviet Union](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_the_Soviet_Union) in 1966, a full member in 1972. He was a vice-president of the [IMU](wikipedia://en/14868). He received [Lenin Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lenin_Prize) in 1970, [Order of the Red Banner of Labour](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_the_Red_Banner_of_Labour) in 1975 and 1979. He was also an editor of the [Great Soviet Encyclopedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Soviet_Encyclopedia).
