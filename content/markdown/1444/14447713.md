# Valentinus Otho

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Valentinus Otho"}

**Valentinus Otho** (also **Valentin Otto**; born around 1545–46 possibly in [Magdeburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magdeburg) – 8 April 1603 in [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg)) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer).

:::

## Life {#Life}

In 1573 he came to [Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lutherstadt_Wittenberg), proposing to [Johannes Praetorius](wikipedia://en/14447712) an approximation of [pi](wikipedia://en/23601) as $\pi \approx \tfrac {355} {113}$ (now known as *[milü](wikipedia://en/7315901)*, as named by its first discover, the Chinese mathematician [Zu Chongzhi](wikipedia://en/151762)).

In 1575 he supported [Georg Joachim Rheticus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Joachim_Rheticus) in his [trigonometric tables](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trigonometric_tables). The next year they went to [Kaschau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Košice) in [Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hungary) where Rheticus died. Thus, Otho inherited the [De revolutionibus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_revolutionibus) manuscript of [Nicolaus Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus) that Rheticus had published in 1543 in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg).

Otho became Professor for mathematics in Wittenberg, but when the rulers of Saxony did not support the tables, he moved to [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg) where Elector [Friedrich IV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_IV,_Elector_Palatine) sponsored the '’Opus Palatinum de Triangulis’’ in 1596.
