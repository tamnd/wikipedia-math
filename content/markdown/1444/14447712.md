# Johannes Praetorius (mathematician)

*Image: JohannesPraetorius.jpg.* Johannes Praetorius

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Praetorius"}

**Johannes Praetorius** or **Johann Richter** (1537 – 27 October 1616) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [astronomer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astronomer).

:::

## Life {#Life}

*Image: Gregor Reisch, Margarita Philosophica, Astronomia.svg.* *Astronomia, ars liberalis*

Praetorius was born in [Jáchymov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jáchymov), [Bohemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_Bohemia). From 1557 he studied at the [University of Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wittenberg), and from 1562 to 1569 he lived in [Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nuremberg). His astronomical and mathematical instruments are kept at [Germanisches Nationalmuseum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germanisches_Nationalmuseum) in Nuremberg.

In 1571 he became Professor of mathematics (astronomy) at [Wittenberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wittenberg) where he met [Valentinus Otho](wikipedia://en/14447713) and [Joachim Rheticus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Joachim_Rheticus). He died in [Altdorf bei Nürnberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Altdorf_bei_Nürnberg), aged about 79.

He taught [Copernicus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copernicus)' theory of astronomy initially as a means of eliminating the [equant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equant) from [Ptolemy](wikipedia://en/23979)'s account, and later moving to a proto-[Tychonic system](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tychonic_system).[^o3]
