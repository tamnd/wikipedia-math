# Wim Cohen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Wim Cohen"}

**Jacob Willem "Wim" Cohen** (27 August 1923 [Leeuwarden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leeuwarden) – 12 November 2000) was a Dutch mathematician, well known for over hundred scientific publications and several books in [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578).

:::

Cohen was born in a Jewish family, as the son of Benjamin Cohen and Aaltje Klein. Having acquired an [autodidact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Autodidact) knowledge of mathematics while in hiding during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II), Cohen got an [Engineer's degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer's_degree) (1949) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) degree (1955) in [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering) at [Delft University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University), on a dissertation titled *Stress Calculations in Helicoidal Shells and Propeller Blades*. He worked as [teletraffic engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teletraffic_engineer) with the *Telecommunications group* at [Philips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philips) (1950–57), at the applied mathematics department at Delft (1957–73) and [University of Utrecht](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utrecht) (1973-1998). He was buried in [Haifa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haifa).[^o5][^o6]

## Books {#Books}

- *The single server queue* (1969)
- *Statistical Power Analysis for the Behavioural Sciences* ([Academic Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_Press), 1969)
- *On regenerative processes in queueing theory*
- *Boundary value problems in queueing systems* ([Elsevier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Elsevier), 1983). Editor with [Onno J. Boxma](wikipedia://en/14482464).
- *Analysis of [random walks](wikipedia://en/235451)* ([IOS Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IOS_Press), 1992)

## Awards {#Awards}

- *[Hollandsche Maatschappij der Wetenschappen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hollandsche_Maatschappij_der_Wetenschappen) AKZO prize* (1986)
- honorary doctorate from the [Technion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technion) (1988)
- honorary member of the International Advisory Committee of the [International Teletraffic Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Teletraffic_Congress).
- ITC Lifetime Achievement Award by the International Advisory Committee of the [International Teletraffic Congress](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Teletraffic_Congress) (1997).
