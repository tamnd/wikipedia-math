# Ryszard Syski

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Ryszard Syski"}

**Ryszard Syski** (April 8, 1924 in [Płock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Płock), [Poland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poland) - June 11, 2007 in [Silver Spring, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silver_Spring,_Maryland)) was a [Polish](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_people)-[American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) whose research was in [queueing theory](wikipedia://en/50578).[^o3]

:::

During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) he was in the [Armia Krajowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Armia_Krajowa) with his parents, partaking in the [Warsaw Uprising](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Warsaw_Uprising), being imprisoned in [Lamsdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stalag_VIII-B), [Silesia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silesia) and [Bavaria](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavaria) (1944), and joining the [Polish Second Corps](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Second_Corps) for fights in Italy (1945).

His studies in mathematics started in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London), at the [Polish University Abroad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_University_Abroad) (1946). He joined *Automatic Telephone and Electric Co.* in London (1952). He got his [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) (1954) and [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) (1961) at [University of London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_London), on the dissertation *Stochastic Process in [Banach space](wikipedia://en/3989) and its Applications to Congestion Theory*.[^o4]

Encouraged by [Thomas L. Saaty](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_L._Saaty) he moved to [College Park, Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College_Park,_Maryland), joining the mathematics faculty of [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) (1961–1999), founded the journal [Stochastic Processes and their Applications](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_Processes_and_their_Applications) (1973) and was fellow of the Institute of Mathematical Statistics. Syski wrote over forty journal articles, often collaborating with notables such as [Félix Pollaczek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Felix_Pollaczek), [Lajos Takács](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lajos_Takács), [Julian Keilson](wikipedia://en/14466539) and [Wim Cohen](wikipedia://en/14449263). Syski died of complications from a brain injury received during a fall.[^o5]
