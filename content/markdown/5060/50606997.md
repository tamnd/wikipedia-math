# Skorokhod

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Skorokhod"}

**Skorokhod** (Скороход is a Russian surname derived from the word *скороход* meaning *fast walker*. Notable people with the surname include:

:::

- [Anatoliy Skorokhod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anatoliy_Skorokhod)
- [Anna Skorokhod](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Anna_Skorokhod), Ukrainian politician
