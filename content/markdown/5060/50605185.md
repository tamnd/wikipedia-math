# Jim Agler

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Jim Agler"}

**Jim Agler** is a mathematician who is an emeritus professor at the [University of California, San Diego](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_San_Diego). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) since 2016, for "contributions to [operator theory](wikipedia://en/712675) and the theory of [analytic functions](wikipedia://en/61478) of [several complex variables](wikipedia://en/5759)".[^o2]

:::

He obtained his Ph.D. from the [Indiana University Bloomington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University_Bloomington) in 1980 under the supervision of [John B. Conway](wikipedia://en/3261836). His thesis was titled *Subjordan Operators*.[^o3] The thesis generalizes the notion of a [Jordan operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jordan_operator), originally studied by [J. W. Helton](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._W._Helton), which are of the form *A + Q* where $A^* = A, Q^2 = 0, AQ=QA$ (defined on a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932) over the complex numbers).[^o4] Restricting this operator to an [invariant subspace](wikipedia://en/585826) produces a [subjordan operator](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Subjordan_operator). All subjordan operators satisfy the equation ${T^*}^3 - 3{T^*}^2T + 3T^*T^2 - T^3 = 0$ .[^o4] The thesis also characterizes all subjordan operators in terms of the [C\* algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*_algebra) $\mathbb{C}(I, M_2(\mathbb{C}))$.[^o4]

Agler and [John E. McCarthy](wikipedia://en/59255552) are the authors of the book *Pick Interpolation and Hilbert Function Spaces* ([American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), 2002).[^o5]

Some efforts to extend the [Herglotz representation theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herglotz_representation_theorem) are described in *Classical function theory, Operator Dilation Theory, and Machine Computations on Multiply-Connected Domains*.[^o6]
