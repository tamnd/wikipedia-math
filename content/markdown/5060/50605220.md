# Eric Anders Carlen

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eric Anders Carlen"}

**Eric Anders Carlen** is a mathematician who is a [distinguished professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Distinguished_professor) [emeritus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emeritus) at [Rutgers University – New Brunswick](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University_–_New_Brunswick). He is a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) since 2016, for "contributions to [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), mathematical physics, and probability".[^o2]

:::

He obtained his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1984 under the supervision of [Edward Nelson](wikipedia://en/387561).
