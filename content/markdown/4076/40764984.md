# Notre Dame Journal of Formal Logic

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Notre Dame Journal of Formal Logic"}

The ***Notre Dame Journal of Formal Logic*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) covering the [foundations of mathematics](wikipedia://en/169358) and related fields of [mathematical logic](wikipedia://en/19636), as well as [philosophy of mathematics](wikipedia://en/46439). It was established in 1960 and is published by [Duke University Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University_Press) on behalf of the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame). The [editors-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editors-in-chief) are Curtis Franks and [Anand Pillay](wikipedia://en/69187512) (University of Notre Dame).

:::

The founder of the magazine was Boleslaw Sobocinski.[^o3]
