# Christina Birkenhake

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christina Birkenhake"}

**Christina Birkenhake** (born 1961) is a German mathematician specializing in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997). She is a lecturer at the [University of Erlangen–Nuremberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen–Nuremberg), in the research group on algebra and geometry.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After studying mathematics at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) beginning in 1982, Birkenhake earned her doctorate ([dr. rer. nat.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._rer._nat.)) in 1989 from the University of Erlangen–Nuremberg. Her dissertation was *Heisenberg-Gruppen ampler Geradenbündel auf abelschen Varietäten* \[*[Heisenberg groups](wikipedia://en/448518) of [ample line bundles](wikipedia://en/1186896) on [abelian varieties](wikipedia://en/247152)*\], and her [doctoral advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) was Herbert Lange.

She worked as a research assistant at the University Erlangen-Nürnberg, earning her [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) there in 1994, until in 2001 she was given a chair in [complex analysis](wikipedia://en/5759) at [Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Gutenberg_University_Mainz). She returned to Erlangen–Nuremberg as a lecturer in 2003.
