# Idempotent analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Idempotent analysis"}

In [mathematical analysis](wikipedia://en/48396), **idempotent analysis** is the study of [idempotent semirings](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Idempotent_semiring), such as the [tropical semiring](wikipedia://en/3892336). The lack of an additive inverse in the semiring is compensated somewhat by the idempotent rule $A \oplus A = A$.

:::
