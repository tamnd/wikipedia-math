# Journal of Formalized Reasoning

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Journal of Formalized Reasoning"}

The ***Journal of Formalized Reasoning*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access) [academic journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) that publishes research on the formalization of mathematics, algorithms, and software systems, including work in classical mathematics, [constructive mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Constructive_mathematics), formal [algorithms](wikipedia://en/775), and [program verification](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Program_verification).[^o3] Established in 2009, it is maintained by *AlmaDL*, the [digital library](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_library) of the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna).

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

The journal is abstracted and indexed in [Scopus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scopus), [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683), and *[Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH)*.
