# Blandford–Znajek process

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Blandford–Znajek process"}

*Image: Active Black Hole Squashes Star Formation.jpg.* An active galactic nucleus with relativistic jets.

The **Blandford–Znajek process** is a mechanism for the extraction of [energy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Energy) from a [rotating black hole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rotating_black_hole),[^o2][^o3] introduced by [Roger Blandford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roger_Blandford) and [Roman Znajek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Znajek) in 1977.[^o4] This mechanism is the most preferred description of how [astrophysical jets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Astrophysical_jet) are formed around spinning [supermassive black holes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Supermassive_black_hole). This is one of the mechanisms that power [quasars](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasar), or rapidly accreting supermassive black holes.[^o5] Generally speaking, it was demonstrated that the power output of the accretion disk is significantly larger than the power output extracted directly from the hole, through its [ergosphere](wikipedia://en/1508445).[^o6][^o7] Hence, the presence (or not) of a poloidal magnetic field around the black hole is not determinant in its overall power output. It was also suggested that the mechanism plays a crucial role as a central engine for a [gamma-ray burst](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gamma-ray_burst).[^o8]

:::

## Physics of the mechanism {#Physics_of_the_mechanism}

*Image: Black hole jet diagram.jpg.* Magnetic field lines around the jets of a quasar.

As in the [Penrose process](wikipedia://en/2975616), the [ergosphere](wikipedia://en/1508445) plays an important role in the Blandford–Znajek process. In order to extract energy and [angular momentum](wikipedia://en/2839) from the black hole, the [electromagnetic field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electromagnetic_field) around the hole must be modified by [magnetospheric currents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magnetospheric_currents). In order to drive such currents, the electric field needs to not be screened, and consequently the vacuum field created within the ergosphere by distant sources must have an unscreened component. The most favored way to provide this is an e<sup>±</sup> pair cascade in a strong electric and radiation field.[^o10] As the ergosphere causes the magnetosphere inside it to rotate, the outgoing flux of angular momentum results in extraction of energy from the black hole.

The Blandford–Znajek process requires an [accretion disc](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Accretion_disc) with a strong [poloidal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toroidal_and_poloidal) magnetic field around a spinning black hole. The [magnetic field](wikipedia://en/36563) extracts spin energy, and the power can be estimated as the energy density at the speed of light cylinder times area:

$$P = B^2\left(\frac{r}{r_c}\right)^4 r_c c = \frac{B^2 r^4 \omega^2}{c},$$

where *B* is the magnetic field strength, $r_c$ is the [Schwarzschild radius](wikipedia://en/206115), and *ω* is the [angular velocity](wikipedia://en/65927).[^o5]
