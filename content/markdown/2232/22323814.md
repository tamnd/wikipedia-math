# Daniel Ocone

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Daniel Ocone"}

**Daniel Leonard Ocone** (born 1953) is a Professor in the Mathematics Department at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University), where he specializes in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [stochastic processes](wikipedia://en/47895).[^o2] He obtained his Ph.D. at [MIT](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIT) in 1980 under the supervision of [Sanjoy K. Mitter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sanjoy_K._Mitter). He is known for the [Clark–Ocone theorem](wikipedia://en/6599348) in [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis). The continuous Ocone martingale is also named after him; it is a continuous [martingale](wikipedia://en/274536) that is conditionally Gaussian, given its [quadratic variation](wikipedia://en/2143889) process.[^o4]

:::
