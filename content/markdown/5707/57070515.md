# Andrew Putman

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Andrew Putman"}

**Andrew Putman** (born October 22, 1979) is an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) at the [University of Notre Dame](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame). His research fields include [geometric group theory](wikipedia://en/955164) and [low-dimensional topology](wikipedia://en/621774).

:::

Putman earned his bachelor's degree from [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University). In 2007, he obtained his doctorate from the [University of Chicago](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago), under the supervision of [Benson Farb](wikipedia://en/32518776). He was a [C. L. E. Moore Instructor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C._L._E._Moore_Instructor) at MIT from 2007-2010, and then served on the faculty at Rice from 2010-2016. He then moved to Notre Dame, where he is currently the Notre Dame Professor of Topology.

In 2018, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3] In 2014, there was a [Seminar Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/2904840) talk by Aurélien Djament on Putman's work.[^o4] Further, in 2013, Putman received the [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) and a [National Science Foundation CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards).[^o5]
