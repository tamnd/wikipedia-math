# Christophe Ley

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christophe Ley"}

**Christophe Ley** is a Luxembourgish statistician,[^o2] and an author and editor of books on [directional statistics](wikipedia://en/1444970) and [sports statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sports_statistics). He is president of the Luxembourg Statistical Society.[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Ley obtained his PhD from the [Université libre de Bruxelles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_libre_de_Bruxelles) under the supervision of Davy Paindaveine.

After his PhD, Christophe Ley was chargé de recherche of the [National Fund for Scientific Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Fund_for_Scientific_Research) at the Université libre de Brussels until 2015, when he became [assistant professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Assistant_professor) at [Ghent University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ghent_University).[^o7] He joined the [University of Luxembourg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Luxembourg) in 2021 as associate professor.[^o8]

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

Ley is the co-author, with Thomas Verdebout, of the book *Modern Directional Statistics* ([CRC Press](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CRC_Press), 2017)[^o10] and the co-editor with Verdebout of *Applied Directional Statistics: Modern Methods and Case Studies* (CRC Press, 2018).[^o11]

He is also the co-editor, with Yves Dominicy, of *Statistics Meets Sports: What We Can Learn from Sports Data* ([Cambridge Scholars Publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cambridge_Scholars_Publishing), 2023).[^o8]
