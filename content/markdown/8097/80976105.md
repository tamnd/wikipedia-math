# Adel Javanmard

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Adel Javanmard"}

**Adel Javanmard** (born 1987) is a professor in the fields of artificial intelligence, machine learning, statistics and data science. He has worked on [high-dimensional statistics](wikipedia://en/19330658),[^o3] foundations of [deep neural network](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deep_learning), [message passing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Message_passing), and [differential privacy](wikipedia://en/23473696).[^o4] He is a professor of Data Sciences and Operations at the Marshall School of Business at the [University of Southern California (USC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California).[^o5]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

While in high school, Javanmard earned a gold medal in the National Mathematical Olympiad in 2003 and a silver medal at the 45th [International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO)](wikipedia://en/82208) in 2004.[^o7] He completed his undergraduate studies with BSc degrees in electrical engineering and pure mathematics at [Sharif University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharif_University_of_Technology).[^o3] In 2014, he received his PhD in Electrical Engineering from [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o8] His PhD thesis, "*Inference and estimation in high-dimensional data analysis"* won the Thomas Cover dissertation award from [IEEE Information Theory Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/IEEE_Information_Theory_Society).[^o9]

## Academic biography {#Academic_biography}

After completing his doctorate, Javanmard was an NSF postdoctoral fellow at the Center for Science of Information with UC Berkeley, working with [David Tse](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Tse).[^o11] In 2015, he joined [University of Southern California (USC)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Southern_California) as a faculty member in the Data Sciences and Operations Department in the Marshall School of Business.[^o5]

## Research {#Research}

Javanmard has worked in high-dimensional statistics,[^o13] robust machine learning,[^o14] optimization,[^o15] network analysis,[^o16] personalized decision-making,[^o17] and private learning.[^o18]
