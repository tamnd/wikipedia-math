# Reese Prosser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Reese Prosser"}

*Image: Reese T. Prosser 1974 (portion A).jpg.* Prosser in 1974

**Reese Trego Prosser** (May 18, 1927 in [Minneapolis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minneapolis) – June 15, 1996) was an American mathematician.

:::

He studied at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (A.B., 1949) and [University of California at Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California_at_Berkeley) under [John L. Kelley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_L._Kelley) (Ph.D. on the thesis *Structure of [Operator algebras](wikipedia://en/455987)*, 1955), while working as numerical analyst at [Lawrence Radiation Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lawrence_Radiation_Laboratory) (1953–55). He then joined [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) (instructor, 1955–56) and [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) (instructor, 1956). He also worked at [Lincoln Laboratory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lincoln_Laboratory) (1958–66), among others contributing an early study on routing in [packet switching](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Packet_switching) computer networks,[^o3] before becoming associate professor of mathematics at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) (1966) and professor (1969). He served as a research associate at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) (1973) and at [University of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California) (1974 and 1980) while maintaining his professorship at Dartmouth College where he taught until his death in 1996.[^o4]

Prosser was also a [cellist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cellist) and [sailing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sailing) devotee. The mathematics department at [Dartmouth College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dartmouth_College) opened the *Prosser Memorial Lectures* in 2002.
