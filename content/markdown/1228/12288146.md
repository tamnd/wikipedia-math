# Dialectica space

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dialectica space"}

**Dialectica spaces** are a [categorical](wikipedia://en/5869) way of constructing models of [linear logic](wikipedia://en/579675).

:::

They were introduced by [Valeria de Paiva](wikipedia://en/47321570) in her doctoral thesis, as a way of modeling both linear logic and [Gödel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gödel)'s [Dialectica interpretation](wikipedia://en/16974776)—hence the name.

Given a [category](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category_%28category_theory%29) *C* and a specific object *K* of *C* with certain (logical) properties, one can construct the category of Dialectica spaces over *C*, whose objects are pairs of objects of *C*, related by a *C*-[morphism](wikipedia://en/27716891) into *K*. Morphisms of Dialectica spaces are similar to [Chu space](wikipedia://en/4362087) morphisms, but instead of an equality condition, they have an [inequality](wikipedia://en/89489) condition, which is read as a [logical implication](wikipedia://en/37531624): the first object implies the second.
