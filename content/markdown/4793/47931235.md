# Lecture Notes in Mathematics

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Lecture Notes in Mathematics"}

***Lecture Notes in Mathematics*** is a [book series](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_series) in the field of [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), including articles related to both [research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Research) and teaching. It was established in 1964 and was edited by A. Dold, [Heidelberg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg) and B. Eckmann, [Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zürich). Its publisher is [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) (formerly Springer-Verlag).

:::

The intent of the series is to publish not only lecture notes, but results from seminars and conferences, more quickly than the several-years-long process of publishing polished journal papers in mathematics. In order to speed the publication process, early volumes of the series (before [electronic publishing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electronic_publishing)) were reproduced photographically from [typewritten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Typewriter) manuscripts. According to [Earl Taft](wikipedia://en/61298003), it has been "enormously successful" and "is considered a very valuable service to the mathematical community".

As of 2025, there have been over 2380 volumes in the series.
