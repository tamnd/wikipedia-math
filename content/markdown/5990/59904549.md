# Aldona Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Aldona Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė"}

**Aldona Džiugaitė-Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė** (1 January 1936 – 24 February 2017) was a Lithuanian mathematician who specialized in [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė was born on 1 January 1936 in Džiugai, [Ukmergė County](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ukmergė_County), Lithuania.[^o4]

Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė graduated from [Vilnius University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius_University) as a Doctor of Physics and Mathematics in 1964.[^o4] She worked at the Institute of Mathematics and Informatics until 2010.[^o5] She gained a second PhD from the [Academy of Sciences of Uzbekistan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academy_of_Sciences_of_Uzbekistan).[^o5]

Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė died in 2017 in [Vilnius](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vilnius).[^o5]

## Scientific activity {#Scientific_activity}

Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė's most important work is on the limit theorems of probability theory, including work on [renewal theory](wikipedia://en/1945387), and related work involving [polynomials](wikipedia://en/23000), [multiple integrals](wikipedia://en/2757224), and [nonlinear functions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nonlinear_function).[^o7]

Aleškevičienė-Statulevičienė was the chief researcher at the institute of mathematics and informatics at the [Lithuanian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lithuanian_Academy_of_Sciences) from 1989 to 2010.[^o5]
