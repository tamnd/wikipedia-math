# Grothendieck local duality

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Grothendieck local duality"}

In [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), **Grothendieck local duality** is a [duality theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duality_theorem) for [cohomology](wikipedia://en/194060) of [modules](wikipedia://en/276410) over [local rings](wikipedia://en/153391), analogous to [Serre duality](wikipedia://en/359175) of [coherent sheaves](wikipedia://en/603780).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Suppose that *R* is a [Cohen–Macaulay](wikipedia://en/497407) local ring of dimension *d* with maximal ideal *m* and residue field *k* = *R*/*m*. Let *E*(*k*) be a [Matlis module](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matlis_module), an [injective hull](wikipedia://en/665821) of *k*, and let $\overline{\Omega}$ be the completion of its [dualizing module](wikipedia://en/38513558). Then for any *R*-module *M* there is an isomorphism of modules over the completion of *R*:

$$\operatorname{Ext}_R^i(M,\overline\Omega) \cong \operatorname{Hom}_R(H_m^{d-i}(M),E(k))$$

where *H*<sub>*m*</sub> is a [local cohomology](wikipedia://en/3212091) group.

There is a generalization to Noetherian local rings that are not Cohen–Macaulay, that replaces the dualizing module with a [dualizing complex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dualizing_complex).

:::
