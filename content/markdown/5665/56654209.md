# Suzanne Dorée

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Suzanne Dorée"}

**Suzanne Ingrid Dorée** is a [professor of mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor_of_mathematics) at [Augsburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Augsburg_University), where she is also chair of the Department of Mathematics, [Statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), and [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science),. She is chair of the Congress of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772) and, as such, serves on its board of directors and the Section Visitors Program (Invited Speakers). Her doctoral research concerned [group theory](wikipedia://en/41890); she has also published in [mathematics education](wikipedia://en/326471).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Dorée grew up near [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), and did her undergraduate studies at the [University of Delaware](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delaware). She joined the Augsburg university faculty in 1989, and did her graduate studies at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison). She completed her Ph.D. at the University of Wisconsin–Madison in 1996; her dissertation, supervised by [Martin Isaacs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Isaacs), was *Subgroups with the Character Restriction Property and Normal Complements*.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2004, Dorée won a Distinguished Teaching Award from the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772).

In 2019, she received a [Deborah and Franklin Haimo Award for Distinguished College or University Teaching of Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deborah_and_Franklin_Haimo_Awards_for_Distinguished_College_or_University_Teaching_of_Mathematics).[^o4]
