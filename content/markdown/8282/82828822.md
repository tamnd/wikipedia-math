# Games of mental skill

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Games of mental skill"}

**Games of mental skill** are [games of skill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Games_of_skill) in which success depends primarily on [intellectual ability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intellectual_ability) rather than [physical effort](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Physical_effort). They usually involve [strategy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Strategy), [logic](wikipedia://en/46426065), [memory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory), [concentration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concentration), [planning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Planning), [calculation](wikipedia://en/239121), [pattern recognition](wikipedia://en/126706), or [problem solving](wikipedia://en/1467948).

:::

Games of mental skill can be played individually or competitively and may include [board games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Board_games), [card games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Card_games), [abstract strategy games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abstract_strategy_games), [puzzle games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Puzzle), [memory competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_competitions), [word games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Word_games), and other forms of structured [mental activity](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mental_activity).[^o2][^o3]

## Overview {#Overview}

The term is broader than [mind sport](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mind_sport), which generally refers to organized competitive activities with formal governing bodies, rankings, and championships. While all mind sports are games of mental skill, not all games of mental skill are considered mind sports. For example, [crossword puzzles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossword_puzzles), [Sudoku](wikipedia://en/1365807), [escape-room puzzles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Escape-room_puzzles), [trivia games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trivia_games), and many [educational games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Educational_games) may require considerable intellectual ability without being recognized as formal sports.[^o5][^o6]

Games of mental skill may involve complete information, as in [chess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess) or [go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go_%28game%29), or hidden information, as in [poker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poker) and [contract bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_bridge). Some games emphasize long-term strategy, while others rely more on memory, deduction, or quick thinking. Competitive forms often develop formal rules, rating systems, and organized tournaments over time.[^o7][^o8]

Common examples of games of mental skill include [chess](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chess), [draughts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Draughts), [go](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Go_%28game%29), [contract bridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Contract_bridge), [poker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Poker), [shogi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shogi), [xiangqi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xiangqi), [mahjong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mahjong), *[Scrabble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scrabble)*, [Sudoku](wikipedia://en/1365807), [crossword puzzles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crossword_puzzles), [Rubik's Cube solving](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rubik's_Cube_solving), [memory competitions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Memory_competitions), and various logic-based [video games](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Video_games). Some of these activities have international federations and world championships, while others remain informal recreational activities.[^o9][^o10]
