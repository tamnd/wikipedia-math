# Shaul Foguel

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Shaul Foguel"}

**Shaul Reuven Foguel** (שאול פוגל, December 5, 1931 - December 19, 2020) was an Israeli mathematician. Shaul Foguel was born to one of the founding families of the City of [Tel Aviv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tel_Aviv)[^o3][^o4] and his mother Dora Malkin was a direct descendant of [Saul Wahl](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saul_Wahl).[^o5] He received his B.S. and M.S. in Mathematics from the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem) and his PhD in Mathematics from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1958. He wrote his dissertation under [Nelson Dunford](wikipedia://en/21022291) on "Studies in Spectral Operators and the Basis Problem". Shaul Foguel was Professor Emeritus of the [Hebrew University of Jerusalem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hebrew_University_of_Jerusalem), and a supporter of the Israeli Left. He ran in the [1969 Knesset elections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/1969_Israeli_legislative_election) on the Peace List along with [Gadi Yatziv](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gadi_Yatziv), although it failed to win a seat. He spent his retirement in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City), where his two sons, [Professor Tuval Foguel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tuval_Foguel) of [Adelphi University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adelphi_University), and Sy Foguel, the CEO of [Berkshire Hathaway GUARD Insurance Companies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkshire_Hathaway_GUARD_Insurance_Companies) live.[^o6]
