# Argand system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Argand system"}

In mathematics, an *n*th-order **Argand system** (named after [French](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/France) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Jean-Robert Argand](wikipedia://en/543727)) is a [coordinate system](wikipedia://en/81931) constructed around the *n*th [roots of unity](wikipedia://en/171950). From the [origin](wikipedia://en/1313432), *n* axes extend such that the angle between each axis and the axes immediately before and after it is 360/*n* [degrees](wikipedia://en/1195294). For example, the [number line](wikipedia://en/287188) is the 2nd-order Argand system because the two axes extending from the origin represent 1 and −1, the 2nd roots of unity. The [complex plane](wikipedia://en/217628) (sometimes called the Argand plane, also named after Argand) is the 4th-order Argand system because the 4 axes extending from the origin represent 1, *i*, −1, and −*i*, the 4th roots of unity.

:::
