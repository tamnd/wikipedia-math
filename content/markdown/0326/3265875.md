# Steve Jackson (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Steve Jackson"}

**Stephen Craig Jackson** is an American [set theorist](wikipedia://en/27553) at the [University of North Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Texas).[^o2] Much of his most notable work has involved the [descriptive set-theoretic](wikipedia://en/396619) consequences of the [axiom of determinacy](wikipedia://en/1002300).[^o3] In particular he is known for having calculated the values of all the projective ordinals (the [suprema](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Suprema) of the lengths of all [prewellorderings](wikipedia://en/2137523) of the [real numbers](wikipedia://en/20646438) at a particular level in the [projective hierarchy](wikipedia://en/1236542)) under the assumption that the axiom of determinacy holds.

:::

In recent years he has also made contributions to the theory of [Borel equivalence relations](wikipedia://en/2123692). With [Dan Mauldin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Mauldin) he solved the [Steinhaus lattice problem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steinhaus_lattice_problem).[^o4][^o5]

Jackson earned his PhD in 1983 at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) under the direction of [Donald A. Martin](wikipedia://en/2270985), with a [dissertation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dissertation) on *A Calculation of* **δ**<sup>1</sup><sub>5</sub>. In it, he proved that, under the axiom of determinacy,

$$\boldsymbol{\delta}^1_5=\aleph_{\omega^{\omega^{\omega}}+1}$$

thereby solving the first Victoria Delfino problem,[^o6] one of the notorious problems of the combinatorics of the [axiom of determinacy](wikipedia://en/1002300).
