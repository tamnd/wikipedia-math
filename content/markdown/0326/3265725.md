# John C. Butcher

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="John C. Butcher"}

*Image: John C. Butcher, CSSim 2009 (1).jpeg.* During International Conference on Computer Modelling and Simulation CSSim 2009

**John Charles Butcher** (born 31 March 1933) is a New Zealand [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who specialises in [numerical methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numerical_methods) for the solution of [ordinary differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_differential_equations).[^o2]

:::

Butcher works on multistage methods for [initial value problems](wikipedia://en/498383), such as [Runge-Kutta](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge-Kutta) and [general linear methods](wikipedia://en/2090865). The [Butcher group](wikipedia://en/23338010) and the [Butcher tableau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Butcher_tableau) are named after him. More recently, he is investigating a new type of method with stability identical to that of a [Runge-Kutta method](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runge-Kutta_method).

## Biography {#Biography}

::: {#o4 .table cue=table}

| Positions held by John C. Butcher |
|---|
| Lecturer, [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney), 1959–1961. Senior Lecturer, [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury), 1961–1964. Computer Scientist, [Stanford Linear Accelerator Center](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_Linear_Accelerator_Center), 1965–1966.[^o6] Professor of Mathematics, [University of Auckland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Auckland), 1966–1979. Head of Mathematics Department, University of Auckland, 1967–1973. Founded Department of Computer Science, University of Auckland, 1980. Professor of Computer Science, University of Auckland, 1980–1988. Head of Applied and Computational Mathematics Unit, University of Auckland, 1989–1994, 1997–1998. Professor of Mathematics, University of Auckland, 1989–1999. Emeritus Professor, University of Auckland, 1999–present. |

:::

Butcher studied mathematics at [Auckland University College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auckland_University_College) - BSc and MSc - and the [University of Sydney](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Sydney) - PhD (1961) and DSc. Positions held are as aside.

He was awarded the Jones Medal from the [Royal Society of New Zealand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_New_Zealand) in 2010, for his "exceptional lifetime work on numerical methods for the solution of differential equations and leadership in the development of New Zealand mathematical sciences."[^o2] In 2011, he received the [Van Wijngaarden Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Van_Wijngaarden_Award).[^o7]

In the [2013 Queen's Birthday Honours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2013_Birthday_Honours_%28New_Zealand%29), Butcher was appointed an [Officer of the New Zealand Order of Merit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Officer_of_the_New_Zealand_Order_of_Merit), for services to mathematics.[^o8]
