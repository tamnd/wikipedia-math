# Karl Friedrich Wilhelm Dieterici

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karl Friedrich Wilhelm Dieterici"}

**Karl Friedrich Wilhelm Dieterici** (23 August 1790 – 30 July 1859) was a German [political economist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Political_economist).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

Dieterici was born and died in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin). He was an [engineer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Engineer)-[geographer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Geographer) in [Blücher](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gebhard_Leberecht_von_Blücher)'s army from 1813 to 1815, was engaged in the Ministry of Public Instruction, became professor of political science in the [University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), and in 1844 was placed at the head of the statistical bureau. He published a number of important works on political economy and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), among which may be mentioned: *De Via et Ratione Œconomiam Politicam Docendi* and *Statistische Uebersicht der wichtigsten Gegenstände des Verkehrs und Verbrauchs im preussischen Staat und im deutschen Zollverband* (1838).
