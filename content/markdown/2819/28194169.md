# Tower of fields

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tower of fields"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), specifically [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527), a **tower of fields** is a sequence of [field extensions](wikipedia://en/11634)

:::

$$F_0 \subseteq F_1 \subseteq \cdots \subseteq F_n \subseteq \cdots$$

A tower of fields may be finite or [infinite](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Infinite_sequence).

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- $\mathbb{Q} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ is a finite tower of the [rational](wikipedia://en/19727024), [real](wikipedia://en/20646438) and [complex numbers](wikipedia://en/5826).
- The sequence obtained by setting $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}$ and letting

$$F_n = F_{n-1}\big(2^{1/2^n}\big)$$

for $n\geq 1$ (i.e. $F_n$ is obtained from $F_{n-1}$ by [adjoining](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adjunction_%28field_theory%29) a $2^n$-th [root](wikipedia://en/235029) of $2$) is an infinite tower.

- Similarly, if $p$ is a [prime number](wikipedia://en/23666), the ***p*th [cyclotomic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclotomic) tower** of $\mathbb{Q}$ is obtained by setting $F_0 = \mathbb{Q}$ and letting $F_n$ be the field obtained by adjoining to $\mathbb{Q}$ the $p^n$-th [roots of unity](wikipedia://en/171950). This tower is of fundamental importance in [Iwasawa theory](wikipedia://en/396150).
- The [Golod–Shafarevich theorem](wikipedia://en/8845470) shows that there are infinite towers obtained by iterating the [Hilbert class field](wikipedia://en/3021036) construction to a [number field](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Number_field).

:::
