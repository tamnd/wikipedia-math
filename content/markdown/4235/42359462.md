# Roland Sprague

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Roland Sprague"}

**Roland Percival Sprague** (11 July 1894, [Unterliederbach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unterliederbach) – 1 August 1967) was a German mathematician, known for the [Sprague–Grundy theorem](wikipedia://en/41372)[^o2] and for being the first mathematician to find [a perfect squared square](wikipedia://en/67903#Perfect_squared_squares).[^o3]

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

With two mathematicians, [Thomas Bond Sprague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Bond_Sprague) and [Hermann Amandus Schwarz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hermann_Amandus_Schwarz), as grandfathers, Roland Sprague was also a great-grandson of the mathematician [Ernst Eduard Kummer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ernst_Eduard_Kummer) and a great-grandson of the musical instrument maker [Nathan Mendelssohn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mendelssohn_family) (1781–1852).[^o5]

After graduation ([Abitur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abitur)) in 1912 from the Bismarck-Gymnasium in [Berlin-Wilmersdorf](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Wilmersdorf), Sprague studied from 1912 to 1919 in [Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin) and [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen) with an interruption by military service from 1915 to 1918. In 1921 in Berlin he passed the state test for teaching in mathematics, chemistry, and physics. He was Studienassessor (probationary teacher at a secondary school) from 1922 at the Paulsen-Realgymnasium in [Berlin-Steglitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Steglitz) and from 1924 at the Schiller-Gymnasium (temporarily named "Clausewitz-Schule") in [Berlin-Charlottenburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berlin-Charlottenburg), where he became in 1925 [Studienrat](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Studienrat) (teacher at a secondary school).[^o5][^o6]

In 1950 Sprague received a PhD under [Alexander Dinghas](wikipedia://en/16687363) at the [Freie Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freie_Universität_Berlin) with dissertation *Über die eindeutige Bestimmbarkeit der Elemente einer endlichen Menge durch zweifache Einteilung*.[^o7] At the Pädagogische Hochschule Berlin, Sprague was from 1949 [Dozent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Docent), from 1953 Oberstudienrat (senior teacher at a secondary school), and from 1955 Professor.[^o5]

Sprague is known for his contributions to [recreational mathematics](wikipedia://en/26446), especially the [Sprague–Grundy function](wikipedia://en/41372#Development) and its application to [combinatorial games](wikipedia://en/292231), which Sprague and [Patrick Michael Grundy](wikipedia://en/46968203) discovered independently in 1935 and 1939 respectively.[^o8] This result of Sprague's enabled mathematical strategies devised originally by [Emanuel Lasker](wikipedia://en/244649) to be completed,[^o9] and provided a method for calculating winning strategies for generalizations of the game of [Nim](wikipedia://en/21885).
