# Robert C. James

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robert C. James"}

**Robert Clarke James** (1918[^o2] – September 25, 2004)[^o5] was an [American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Americans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who worked in [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

James attended [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) as an undergraduate, where his father was a professor. As a devout [Quaker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quakers), he was a [conscientious objector](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conscientious_objector) during [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II).[^o5]

He obtained his PhD at [Caltech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caltech) in 1946 under the direction of Aristotle Demetrius Michal. He spent a year as a Benjamin Peirce Fellow at [Harvard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and joined the faculty at [UC Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UC_Berkeley). In 1950, during the [loyalty oath controversy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/History_of_the_University_of_California,_Berkeley#1950s), James refused to sign the oath and moved to [Haverford College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Haverford_College). Later he was the founding math department chair both at [Harvey Mudd College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvey_Mudd_College) and at the [Claremont Graduate University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claremont_Graduate_University).[^o5]

James constructed a number of counterexamples in the theory of [Banach spaces](wikipedia://en/3989), including [James' space](wikipedia://en/44496861) and [James' tree space](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James'_tree_space). He also characterized [reflexivity](wikipedia://en/210091) for Banach spaces with an [unconditional Schauder basis](wikipedia://en/3460662#Unconditionality) and proved an [eponymous compactness criterion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James'_theorem).

With his father Glenn James (mathematician) (a professor at [UCLA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/UCLA) and long-time editor of [Mathematics Magazine](wikipedia://en/4230329)) he published a mathematical dictionary which went through several editions.[^o6]

James was made a [Fellow of the American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science) in 1978.[^o7]
