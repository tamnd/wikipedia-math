# Claude Sabbah

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Claude Sabbah"}

**Claude Sabbah** (born 30 October 1954) is a French mathematician and researcher at [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Sabbah received his doctoral degree from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) in 1976 under the supervision of [Lê Dũng Tráng](wikipedia://en/42622216).

## Selected publications {#Selected_publications}

### Books {#Books}

- *Introduction to Stokes Structures*, Springer Verlag, 2012, [^o7]
- *Polarizable twistor D-modules*, Société Mathématique de France, 2005 [^o8]
- With [Jean-Michel Bony](wikipedia://en/50769397), [Bernard Malgrange](wikipedia://en/9170142) and [Laurent Schwartz](wikipedia://en/317485) : *Distributions. Dans le sillage de Laurent Schwartz*, Éditions de l'École polytechnique, 2003, [^o9]
- *Déformations isomonodromiques et variétés de Frobenius*, EDP Sciences, 2002, [^o10]
  - English translation : *Isomonodromic Deformations and Frobenius Manifolds*, Springer Verlag, 2008, [^o11]
- *Équations différentielles à points singuliers irréguliers et phénomène de Stokes en dimension 2*, Société mathématique de France, 2000, [^o12]
