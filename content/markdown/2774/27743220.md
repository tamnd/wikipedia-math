# Computational Statistics (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Computational Statistics"}

***Computational Statistics*** is a quarterly [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes applications and research in the field of [computational statistics](wikipedia://en/15832717), as well as reviews of hardware, software, and books. According to the *[Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports)*, the journal has a 2012 [impact factor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Impact_factor) of 0.482.[^o3] It was established in 1986 as *Computational Statistics Quarterly* and obtained its current title in 1992. The journal is published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media) and the [editor-in-chief](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Editor-in-chief) is Philippe Vieu.
