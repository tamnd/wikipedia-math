# Hiraku Nakajima

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Hiraku Nakajima"}

**Hiraku Nakajima** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language): 中島 啓 *Nakajima Hiraku*; born November 30, 1962) is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), and a professor of the [Kavli Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe](wikipedia://en/34880544) at the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo). He is [International Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/14868) president for the 2023–2026 term.

:::

He obtained his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo) in 1991. In 2002 he was plenary speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in [Beijing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beijing). He won the 2003 [Cole Prize](wikipedia://en/1372177) in algebra for his work on [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446). He proved [Nekrasov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nikita_Nekrasov)'s [conjecture](wikipedia://en/6138).

## Biography {#Biography}

- 1985 - BA from the [University of Tokyo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Tokyo)
- 1987 - MA from the University of Tokyo, and became a research associate at the University of Tokyo
- 1991 - PhD from the University of Tokyo
- 1992 - Associate professor at [Tohoku University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tohoku_University)
- 1995 - Associate professor at the University of Tokyo
- 1997 - Associate professor at [Kyoto University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kyoto_University)
- 2000 - Full professor at Kyoto University
- 2018 - Full professor at [Kavli Institute for the Physics and Mathematics of the Universe](wikipedia://en/34880544)

## Awards and prizes {#Awards_and_prizes}
