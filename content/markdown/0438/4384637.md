# Robert S. Boyer

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Robert S. Boyer"}

**Robert Stephen Boyer** is an American retired [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) of [computer science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_science), [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and [philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) at [The University of Texas at Austin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Texas_at_Austin). He and [J Strother Moore](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J_Strother_Moore) invented the [Boyer–Moore string-search algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boyer–Moore_string-search_algorithm), a particularly efficient [string searching algorithm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/String_searching_algorithm), in 1977. He and Moore also collaborated on the Boyer–Moore automated theorem prover, [Nqthm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nqthm), in 1992.[^o4] Following this, he worked with Moore and [Matt Kaufmann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Matt_Kaufmann) on another theorem prover called [ACL2](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ACL2). He was elected [AAAI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AAAI_Fellow) Fellow in 1991.[^o5]

:::

## Publications {#Publications}

Boyer has published extensively, including the following books:

- *A Computational Logic Handbook*, with J S. Moore. Second Edition. Academic Press, London, 1998.
- *Automated Reasoning: Essays in Honor of Woody Bledsoe*, editor. Kluwer Academic, Dordrecht, The Netherlands, 1991.
- *A Computational Logic Handbook*, with J S. Moore. Academic Press, New York, 1988.
- *The Correctness Problem in Computer Science*, editor, with J S. Moore. Academic Press, London, 1981.
- *A Computational Logic*, with J S. Moore. Academic Press, New York, 1979.
