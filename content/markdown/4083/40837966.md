In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **steric tesseractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 4-space.

:::
