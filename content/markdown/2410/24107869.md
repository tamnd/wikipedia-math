# Robertson graph

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Robertson graph"}

In the [mathematical](wikipedia://en/18831) field of [graph theory](wikipedia://en/12401), the **Robertson graph** or **(4,5)-cage**, is a 4-[regular](wikipedia://en/85821) [undirected graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undirected_graph) with 19 vertices and 38 edges named after [Neil Robertson](wikipedia://en/1575704).[^o5]

:::

The Robertson graph is the unique [(4,5)-cage graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cage_graph) and was discovered by Robertson in 1964.[^o6] As a cage graph, it is the smallest 4-regular graph with girth 5.

It has [chromatic number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_number) 3, [chromatic index](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chromatic_index) 5, diameter 3, radius 3 and is both 4-[vertex-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-vertex-connected_graph) and 4-[edge-connected](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K-edge-connected_graph). It has [book thickness](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Book_thickness) 3 and [queue number](wikipedia://en/46183215) 2.[^o7] The graph is neither planar nor [1-planar](wikipedia://en/38249632).[^o8]

The Robertson graph is also a [Hamiltonian graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamiltonian_graph) which possesses 5376 distinct directed Hamiltonian cycles.

The Robertson graph is one of the smallest graphs with [cop number](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cop_number) 4.[^o9]

::: {#o11 .statement kind=statement/fact cue=heading name="Algebraic properties"}

## Algebraic properties {#Algebraic_properties}

The Robertson graph is not a [vertex-transitive graph](wikipedia://en/581175); its full automorphism group is isomorphic to the [dihedral group](wikipedia://en/100267) of order 24, the group of symmetries of a regular [dodecagon](wikipedia://en/508486), including both rotations and reflections.[^o12]

The [characteristic polynomial](wikipedia://en/218268) of the Robertson graph is

$$(x-4)(x-1)^2(x^2-3)^2(x^2+x-5)$$

$$(x^2+x-4)^2(x^2+x-3)^2(x^2+x-1).\$$

:::
