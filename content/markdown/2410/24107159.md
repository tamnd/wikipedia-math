# Popoviciu's inequality on variances

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Popoviciu's inequality on variances"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542), **Popoviciu's inequality**, named after [Tiberiu Popoviciu](wikipedia://en/39854514), is an [upper bound](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Upper_bound) on the [variance](wikipedia://en/32344) *σ*<sup>2</sup> of any bounded [probability distribution](wikipedia://en/23543). Let *M* and *m* be upper and lower bounds on the values of any [random variable](wikipedia://en/25685) with a particular probability distribution. Then Popoviciu's inequality states:[^o2]

:::

$$\sigma^2 \le \frac14 ( M - m )^2.$$

This equality holds precisely when half of the probability is concentrated at each of the two bounds.

Sharma *et al*. have sharpened Popoviciu's inequality:[^o3]

$${\sigma^2 + \left( \frac {\mu_3} {2\sigma^2} \right)^2} \le \frac14 (M - m)^2,$$

Where $\mu_3$ is the third [central moment](wikipedia://en/19983).

If one additionally assumes knowledge of the expectation, then the stronger [Bhatia&ndash;Davis inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhatia&ndash;Davis_inequality) holds

$$\sigma^2 \le ( M - \mu )( \mu - m )$$

where *μ* is the expectation of the random variable.[^o4]

In the case of an independent sample of *n* observations from a bounded probability distribution, the [von Szokefalvi Nagy inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Von_Szokefalvi_Nagy_inequality)[^o5] gives a lower bound to the variance of the sample mean:

$$\sigma^2 \ge \frac{ ( M - m )^2} {2n}.$$

::: {#o7 .proof cue=heading name="Proof via the Bhatia&ndash;Davis inequality"}

## Proof via the [Bhatia&ndash;Davis inequality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bhatia&ndash;Davis_inequality) {#Proof_via_the_Bhatia&ndash;Davis_inequality}

Let $A$ be a random variable with mean $\mu$, variance $\sigma^2$, and $\Pr(m \leq A \leq M) = 1$. Then, since $m \leq A \leq M$,

$$0 \leq \mathbb{E}[(M - A)(A - m)] = -\mathbb{E}[A^2] - m M + (m+M)\mu.$$

Thus,

$$\sigma^2 = \mathbb{E}[A^2] - \mu^2 \leq - m M + (m+M)\mu - \mu^2 = (M - \mu) (\mu - m).$$

Now, applying the [Inequality of arithmetic and geometric means](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Inequality_of_arithmetic_and_geometric_means), $ab \leq \left( \frac{a+b}{2} \right)^2$, with $a = M - \mu$ and $b = \mu - m$, yields the desired result:

$$\sigma^2 \leq (M - \mu) (\mu - m) \leq \frac{\left(M - m\right)^2}{4}.$$

:::
