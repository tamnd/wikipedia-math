# Itay Neeman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Itay Neeman"}

**Itay Neeman** (איתי נאמן; born 1972) is an Israeli [set theorist](wikipedia://en/27553) working as a professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).[^o2] He has made major contributions to the theory of [inner models](wikipedia://en/1118686), [determinacy](wikipedia://en/2596030) and [forcing](wikipedia://en/152205).[^o10]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Neeman was born in 1972 in [Safed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Safed), [Israel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Israel). After studying mathematics at [King's College London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King's_College_London) and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford),[^o4] he earned his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley), in 1996, under the supervision of [John R. Steel](wikipedia://en/2290967).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Neeman won a [CAREER Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Science_Foundation_CAREER_Awards) in 2001.[^o7] He was an invited speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842) in 2006.[^o8] In 2012, the [Simons Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simons_Foundation) named Neeman as one of their Simons Fellows, in the inaugural year of the Simons Fellows program.[^o9]

In 2019 he was awarded the [Hausdorff Medal](wikipedia://en/62036856), by the European Set Theory Society. The award cited three of his papers for their work on "iterating forcing using side conditions and the tree property" as having been the most significant contribution to set theory in the previous five years.[^o10]
