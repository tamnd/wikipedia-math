::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Cantellated 24-cells"}

In four-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantellated 24-cell** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899), being a [cantellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation) (a 2nd order truncation) of the regular [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485).

:::

There are 2 unique degrees of cantellations of the 24-cell including permutations with truncations.

The **cantellated 24-cell** or **small rhombated icositetrachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). Acronym: **srico**.[^n1]

The boundary of the cantellated 24-cell is composed of 24 [truncated octahedral](wikipedia://en/279654) cells, 24 [cuboctahedral](wikipedia://en/6280) cells and 96 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584). Together they have 288 triangular faces, 432 square faces, 864 edges, and 288 vertices.

::: {#o12 .definition kind=definition/construction cue=heading name="Construction"}

### Construction {#Construction}

When the [cantellation](wikipedia://en/6317514) process is applied to [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), each of the 24 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) becomes a [small rhombicuboctahedron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_rhombicuboctahedron). In addition however, since each [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635)'s edge was previously shared with two other [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635), the separating edges form the three parallel edges of a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) - 96 triangular prisms, since the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) contains 96 edges. Further, since each vertex was previously shared with 12 faces, the vertex would split into 12 (24\*12=288) new vertices. Each group of 12 new vertices forms a [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280).

:::

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the vertices of the cantellated 24-cell having edge length 2 are all permutations of coordinates and sign of:

(0, $\sqrt{2}$, $\sqrt{2}$, 2+2$\sqrt{2}$)

(1, 1+$\sqrt{2}$, 1+$\sqrt{2}$, 1+2$\sqrt{2}$)

The permutations of the second set of coordinates coincide with the vertices of an inscribed [runcitruncated tesseract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcitruncated_tesseract).

The dual configuration has all permutations and signs of:

(0,2,2+$\sqrt{2}$,2+$\sqrt{2}$)

(1,1,1+$\sqrt{2}$,3+$\sqrt{2}$)

### Structure {#Structure}

The 24 [small rhombicuboctahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_rhombicuboctahedron) are joined to each other via their triangular faces, to the [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280) via their axial square faces, and to the [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) via their off-axial square faces. The cuboctahedra are joined to the triangular prisms via their triangular faces. Each triangular prism is joined to two cuboctahedra at its two ends.

::: {#o16 .pointer-item cue=pointer name="Cantic snub 24-cell"}

### Cantic snub 24-cell {#Cantic_snub_24-cell}

A half-symmetry construction of the cantellated 24-cell, also called a **cantic snub 24-cell**, as , has an identical geometry, but its triangular faces are further subdivided. The cantellated 24-cell has 2 positions of triangular faces in ratio of 96 and 192, while the cantic snub 24-cell has 3 positions of 96 triangles.

## Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The convex hull of two cantellated 24-cells in opposite positions is a nonuniform polychoron composed of 864 cells: 48 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280), 144 [square antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860045), 384 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) (as triangular antipodiums), 288 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) (as tetragonal disphenoids), and 576 vertices. Its vertex figure is a shape topologically equivalent to a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) with a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) attached to one of its square faces.

The **cantitruncated 24-cell** or **great rhombated icositetrachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) derived from the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485). Acronym: **grico**.[^n1]

It is bounded by 24 [truncated cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/285879) corresponding with the cells of a 24-cell, 24 [truncated cubes](wikipedia://en/280529) corresponding with the cells of the dual 24-cell, and 96 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) corresponding with the edges of the first 24-cell.

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of a cantitruncated 24-cell having edge length 2 are all permutations of coordinates and sign of:

(1,1+$\sqrt{2}$,1+2$\sqrt{2}$,3+3$\sqrt{2}$)

(0,2+$\sqrt{2}$,2+2$\sqrt{2}$,2+3$\sqrt{2}$)

The dual configuration has coordinates as all permutations and signs of:

(1,1+$\sqrt{2}$,1+$\sqrt{2}$,5+2$\sqrt{2}$)

(1,3+$\sqrt{2}$,3+$\sqrt{2}$,3+2$\sqrt{2}$)

(2,2+$\sqrt{2}$,2+$\sqrt{2}$,4+2$\sqrt{2}$)
