In four-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantellated 5-cell** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899), being a [cantellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation) (a 2nd order truncation, up to [edge](wikipedia://en/12158034)-planing) of the regular [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821).

The **[cantellated](wikipedia://en/6317514) [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821)** or **small rhombated pentachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It has 30 vertices, 90 edges, 80 faces, and 20 cells. The cells are 5 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280), 5 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635), and 10 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584). Each vertex is surrounded by 2 cuboctahedra, 2 triangular prisms, and 1 octahedron; the [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a nonuniform triangular prism.

- Rectified dispentachoron

This construction is from the positive [orthant](wikipedia://en/1283725) facet of the [cantellated 5-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellated_5-orthoplex).

### Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

The convex hull of two cantellated 5-cells in opposite positions is a nonuniform polychoron composed of 100 cells: three kinds of 70 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) (10 rectified tetrahedra, 20 triangular antiprisms, 40 triangular antipodiums), 30 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) (as tetragonal disphenoids), and 60 vertices. Its vertex figure is a shape topologically equivalent to a [cube](wikipedia://en/6285) with a [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584) attached to one of its square faces.

The **cantitruncated [5-cell](wikipedia://en/513821)** or **great rhombated pentachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It is composed of 60 vertices, 120 edges, 80 faces, and 20 cells. The cells are: 5 [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654), 10 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), and 5 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651). Each vertex is surrounded by 2 truncated octahedra, one triangular prism, and one truncated tetrahedron.

### Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

A double symmetry construction can be made by placing truncated tetrahedra on the truncated octahedra, resulting in a nonuniform polychoron with 10 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651), 20 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668) (as ditrigonal trapezoprisms), two kinds of 80 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584) (20 with *D<sub>3h</sub>* symmetry and 60 *C<sub>2v</sub>*-symmetric wedges), and 30 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) (as tetragonal disphenoids). Its vertex figure is topologically equivalent to the [octahedron](wikipedia://en/80177635).
