In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **truncated 120-cell** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) formed as the [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the regular [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358).

:::

There are three truncations, including a [bitruncation](wikipedia://en/6792316), and a tritruncation, which creates the *truncated 600-cell*.

The **truncated 120-cell** or **truncated hecatonicosachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899), constructed by a uniform [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429) of the [regular](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convex_regular_4-polytope) [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358) [4-polytope](wikipedia://en/24979).

It is made of 120 [truncated dodecahedral](wikipedia://en/376538) and 600 [tetrahedral](wikipedia://en/30606) cells. It has 3120 faces: 2400 being [triangles](wikipedia://en/30654) and 720 being [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393). There are 4800 edges of two types: 3600 shared by three truncated dodecahedra and 1200 are shared by two truncated dodecahedra and one tetrahedron. Each vertex has 3 truncated dodecahedra and one tetrahedron around it. Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is an equilateral triangular pyramid.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Truncated 120-cell ([Norman W. Johnson](wikipedia://en/715527))
  - Tuncated hecatonicosachoron / Truncated dodecacontachoron / Truncated polydodecahedron
- Truncated-icosahedral hexacosihecatonicosachoron (Acronym thi) (George Olshevsky, and Jonathan Bowers)[^o13]

The **bitruncated 120-cell** or **hexacosihecatonicosachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It has 720 cells: 120 [truncated icosahedra](wikipedia://en/31282), and 600 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a digonal [disphenoid](wikipedia://en/8476166), with two truncated icosahedra and two truncated tetrahedra around it.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Bitruncated 120-cell / Bitruncated 600-cell ([Norman W. Johnson](wikipedia://en/715527))
  - Bitruncated hecatonicosachoron / Bitruncated hexacosichoron / Bitruncated polydodecahedron / Bitruncated polytetrahedron
- Truncated-icosahedral hexacosihecatonicosachoron (Acronym Xhi) (George Olshevsky, and Jonathan Bowers)[^o23]

The **truncated 600-cell** or **truncated hexacosichoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It is derived from the [600-cell](wikipedia://en/501447) by [truncation](wikipedia://en/4224429). It has 720 cells: 120 [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084) and 600 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a [pentagonal pyramid](wikipedia://en/1128461), with one icosahedron on the base, and 5 truncated tetrahedra around the sides.

### Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Truncated 600-cell ([Norman W. Johnson](wikipedia://en/715527))
- Truncated hexacosichoron (Acronym tex) (George Olshevsky, and Jonathan Bowers)[^o33]
- Truncated tetraplex (Conway)

### Structure {#Structure}

The truncated 600-cell consists of 600 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651) and 120 [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084). The truncated tetrahedral cells are joined to each other via their hexagonal faces, and to the icosahedral cells via their triangular faces. Each icosahedron is surrounded by 20 truncated tetrahedra.
