::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated 24-cells"}

In four-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcinated 24-cell** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899), being a [runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination) (a 3rd order truncation) of the regular [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485).

:::

There are 3 unique degrees of runcinations of the 24-cell including with permutations truncations and cantellations.

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **[runcinated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination_%28geometry%29) [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485)** or **small prismatotetracontoctachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) bounded by 48 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) and 192 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584). The octahedral cells correspond with the cells of a [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485) and its dual.

[E. L. Elte](wikipedia://en/27806942) identified it in 1912 as a semiregular polytope.

## Alternate names {#Alternate_names}

- Runcinated 24-cell ([Norman W. Johnson](wikipedia://en/715527))
- Runcinated icositetrachoron
- Runcinated polyoctahedron

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of the runcinated 24-cell having edge length 2 is given by all permutations of sign and coordinates of:

(0, 0, $\sqrt{2}$, 2+$\sqrt{2}$)

(1, 1, 1+$\sqrt{2}$, 1+$\sqrt{2}$)

The permutations of the second set of coordinates coincide with the vertices of an inscribed [cantellated tesseract](wikipedia://en/3715231).

### Related regular skew polyhedron {#Related_regular_skew_polyhedron}

The [regular skew polyhedron](wikipedia://en/19193037), {4,8|3}, exists in 4-space with 8 square around each vertex, in a zig-zagging nonplanar vertex figure. These square faces can be seen on the runcinated 24-cell, using all 576 edges and 288 vertices. The 384 triangular faces of the runcinated 24-cell can be seen as removed. The dual regular skew polyhedron, {8,4|3}, is similarly related to the octagonal faces of the [bitruncated 24-cell](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bitruncated_24-cell).

The **runcitruncated 24-cell** or **prismatorhombated icositetrachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) derived from the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485). It is bounded by 24 [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654), corresponding with the cells of a [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485), 24 [rhombicuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/26213), corresponding with the cells of the dual 24-cell, 96 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), and 96 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668).

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of an origin-centered runcitruncated 24-cell having edge length 2 are given by all permutations of coordinates and sign of:

(0, $\sqrt{2}$, 2$\sqrt{2}$, 2+3$\sqrt{2}$)

(1, 1+$\sqrt{2}$, 1+2$\sqrt{2}$, 1+3$\sqrt{2}$)

The permutations of the second set of coordinates give the vertices of an inscribed [omnitruncated tesseract](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Omnitruncated_tesseract).

The dual configuration has coordinates generated from all permutations and signs of:

(1,1,1+$\sqrt{2}$,5+$\sqrt{2}$)

(1,3,3+$\sqrt{2}$,3+$\sqrt{2}$)

(2,2,2+$\sqrt{2}$,4+$\sqrt{2}$)

A related 4-polytope is the **runcic snub 24-cell** or **prismatorhombisnub icositetrachoron**, s<sub>3</sub>{3,4,3}, . It is not uniform, but it is [vertex-transitive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vertex-transitive) and has all regular polygon faces. It is constructed with 24 [icosahedra](wikipedia://en/43856084), 24 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651), 96 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), and 96 [triangular cupolae](wikipedia://en/1128465) in the gaps, for a total of 240 cells, 960 faces, 1008 edges, and 288 vertices. Like the [snub 24-cell](wikipedia://en/3704756), it has symmetry \[3<sup>\+</sup>,4,3\], order 576.

The [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) contains one icosahedron, two triangular prisms, one truncated tetrahedron, and 3 triangular cupolae.

The **omnitruncated 24-cell** or **great prismatotetracontoctachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899) derived from the [24-cell](wikipedia://en/293485). It is composed of 1152 vertices, 2304 edges, and 1392 faces (864 squares, 384 hexagons, and 144 octagons). It has 240 cells: 48 [truncated cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/285879), 192 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668). Each vertex contains four cells in a phyllic disphenoidal [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719): two [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668), and two [truncated cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/285879).

### Structure {#Structure}

The 48 truncated cuboctahedral cells are joined to each other via their octagonal faces. They can be grouped into two groups of 24 each, corresponding with the cells of a 24-cell and its dual. The gaps between them are filled in by a network of 192 hexagonal prisms, joined to each other via alternating square faces in alternating orientation, and to the truncated cuboctahedra via their hexagonal faces and remaining square faces.

### Coordinates {#Coordinates}

The [Cartesian coordinates](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cartesian_coordinate) of an omnitruncated 24-cell having edge length 2 are all permutations of coordinates and sign of:

(1, 1+$\sqrt{2}$, 1+2$\sqrt{2}$, 5+3$\sqrt{2}$)

(1, 3+$\sqrt{2}$, 3+2$\sqrt{2}$, 3+3$\sqrt{2}$)

(2, 2+$\sqrt{2}$, 2+2$\sqrt{2}$, 4+3$\sqrt{2}$)

### Related polytopes {#Related_polytopes}

Nonuniform variants with \[3,4,3\] symmetry and two types of truncated cuboctahedra can be doubled by placing the two types of truncated cuboctahedra on each other to produce a nonuniform polychoron with 48 [truncated cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/285879), 144 [octagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/3026569) (as ditetragonal trapezoprisms), 192 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668), two kinds of 864 [rectangular trapezoprisms](wikipedia://en/6285) (288 with *D<sub>2d</sub>* symmetry and 576 with *C<sub>2v</sub>* symmetry), and 2304 vertices. Its vertex figure is an irregular [triangular bipyramid](wikipedia://en/646933).

*Image: Biomnitruncatotetracontaoctachoron vertex figure.png.*

[Vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719)

This polychoron can then be alternated to produce another nonuniform polychoron with 48 [snub cubes](wikipedia://en/180249), 144 [square antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860045), 192 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635) (as triangular antiprisms), three kinds of 2016 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606) (288 tetragonal disphenoids, 576 phyllic disphenoids, and 1152 irregular tetrahedra), and 1152 vertices. It has a symmetry of \[\[3,4,3\]<sup>\+</sup>\], order 1152.

*Image: Alternated biomnitruncatotetracontaoctachoron vertex figure.png.*

### Full snub 24-cell {#Full_snub_24-cell}

*Image: Full\_snub\_24-cell\_verf.png.* Vertex figure for the **omnisnub 24-cell**

The uniform [snub 24-cell](wikipedia://en/3704756) is called a *semi-snub 24-cell* by [John Horton Conway](wikipedia://en/15807) with Coxeter diagram within the [F<sub>4</sub>](wikipedia://en/292877) family, although it is a full snub or omnisnub within the D<sub>4</sub> family, as .

In contrast a **full snub 24-cell** or **omnisnub 24-cell**, defined as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the omnitruncated 24-cell, cannot be made uniform, but it can be given Coxeter diagram , and [symmetry](wikipedia://en/32088502#Point_groups) \[\[3,4,3\]\]<sup>\+</sup>, order 1152, and constructed from 48 [snub cubes](wikipedia://en/180249), 192 [octahedrons](wikipedia://en/80177635), and 576 [tetrahedrons](wikipedia://en/30606) filling the gaps at the deleted vertices. Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) contains 4 tetrahedra, 2 octahedra, and 2 snub cubes. It has 816 cells, 2832 faces, 2592 edges, and 576 vertices.
