::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Runcinated 120-cells"}

In four-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **runcinated 120-cell** (or *runcinated 600-cell*) is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899), being a [runcination](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Runcination) (a 3rd order truncation) of the regular [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358).

:::

There are 4 degrees of runcinations of the 120-cell including with permutations truncations and cantellations.

The *runcinated 120-cell* can be seen as an [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070) applied to a regular 4-polytope, the 120-cell or 600-cell.

The **runcinated 120-cell** or **small disprismatohexacosihecatonicosachoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It has 2640 cells: 120 [dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/8407), 720 [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659), 1200 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), and 600 [tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/30606). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is a nonuniform triangular [antiprism](wikipedia://en/1851) (equilateral-triangular antipodium): its bases represent a dodecahedron and a tetrahedron, and its flanks represent three triangular prisms and three pentagonal prisms.

  - Runcinated hecatonicosachoron / Runcinated dodecacontachoron / Runcinated hexacosichoron / Runcinated polydodecahedron / Runcinated polytetrahedron
- Small diprismatohexacosihecatonicosachoron (acronym: sidpixhi) (George Olshevsky, Jonathan Bowers)[^o12]

The **runcitruncated 120-cell** or **prismatorhombated hexacosichoron** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It contains 2640 cells: 120 [truncated dodecahedra](wikipedia://en/376538), 720 [decagonal prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism), 1200 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), and 600 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280). Its [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719) is an irregular rectangular pyramid, with one truncated dodecahedron, two decagonal prisms, one triangular prism, and one cuboctahedron.

It is composed of 2640 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29): 120 [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486), 600 [truncated tetrahedra](wikipedia://en/279651), 720 [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659), and 1200 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668). The **omnitruncated 120-cell** or **great disprismatohexacosihecatonicosachoron** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899), composed of 2640 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29): 120 [truncated icosidodecahedra](wikipedia://en/286534), 600 [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654), 720 [decagonal prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Decagonal_prism), and 1200 [hexagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859668). It has 14400 vertices, 28800 edges, and 17040 faces (10800 squares, 4800 hexagons, and 1440 decagons). It is the largest nonprismatic convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899).[^o38]

The vertices and edges form the [Cayley graph](wikipedia://en/358277) of the [Coxeter group](wikipedia://en/297004) H<sub>4</sub>.

- Great diprismatohexacosihecatonicosachoron (Acronym gidpixhi) (George Olshevsky, Jonathan Bowers)[^o40]

### Models {#Models}

The first complete physical model of a 3D projection of the omnitruncated 120-cell was built by a team led by Daniel Duddy and David Richter on August 9, 2006 using the [Zome](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zome) system in the London Knowledge Lab for the 2006 [Bridges Conference](http://www.bridgesmathart.org/).[^o46]

### Full snub 120-cell {#Full_snub_120-cell}

*Image: Snub 120-cell verf.png.* Vertex figure for the **omnisnub 120-cell**

The **full snub 120-cell** or **omnisnub 120-cell**, defined as an [alternation](wikipedia://en/4349117) of the omnitruncated 120-cell, can not be made uniform, but it can be given Coxeter diagram , and [symmetry](wikipedia://en/32088502) \[5,3,3\]<sup>\+</sup>, and constructed from 1200 [octahedrons](wikipedia://en/80177635), 600 [icosahedrons](wikipedia://en/43856084), 720 [pentagonal antiprisms](wikipedia://en/2860091), 120 [snub dodecahedrons](wikipedia://en/287137), and 7200 [tetrahedrons](wikipedia://en/30606) filling the gaps at the deleted vertices. It has 9840 cells, 35040 faces, 32400 edges, and 7200 vertices.[^o49]

:::
