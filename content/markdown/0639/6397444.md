In four-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **cantellated 120-cell** is a convex [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899), being a [cantellation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cantellation) (a 2nd order truncation) of the regular [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358).

:::

There are four degrees of cantellations of the 120-cell including with permutations truncations. Two are expressed relative to the dual 600-cell.

The **cantellated 120-cell** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It is named by its construction as a [Cantellation](wikipedia://en/6317514) operation applied to the regular [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358). It contains 1920 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28geometry%29), including 120 [rhombicosidodecahedra](wikipedia://en/285486), 1200 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584), 600 [octahedra](wikipedia://en/80177635). Its vertex figure is a [wedge](wikipedia://en/2951640), with two rhombicosidodecahedra, two triangular prisms, and one octahedron meeting at each vertex.

The **[cantitruncated](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_polychoron#Geometric_derivations_for_46_Wythoffian_polychora) 120-cell** is a [uniform polychoron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uniform_polychoron).

This [4-polytope](wikipedia://en/24979) is related to the regular [120-cell](wikipedia://en/717358). The cantitruncation operation create new truncated tetrahedral cells at the vertices, and triangular prisms at the edges. The original dodecahedron cells are cantitruncated into [great rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534) cells.

The image shows the 4-polytope drawn as a [Schlegel diagram](wikipedia://en/7201595) which projects the 4-dimensional figure into 3-space, distorting the sizes of the cells. In addition, the [decagonal](wikipedia://en/327393) faces are hidden, allowing us to see the elemented projected inside.

The **cantellated 600-cell** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It has 1440 cells: 120 [icosidodecahedra](wikipedia://en/15023), 600 [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280), and 720 [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659). Its vertex figure is an isosceles [triangular prism](wikipedia://en/2859584), defined by one icosidodecahedron, two cuboctahedra, and two pentagonal prisms.

- Ambo-02 tetraplex ([John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807))

### Construction {#Construction}

This 4-polytope has cells at 3 of 4 positions in the fundamental domain, extracted from the Coxeter diagram by removing one node at a time:

::: {#o33 .table cue=table}

| Node | Order | [Coxeter diagram](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coxeter_diagram) | Cell | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 600 |  | Cantellated tetrahedron ([Cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280)) | *Image: Cantellated tetrahedron.png.* |
| 1 | 1200 |  | None (Degenerate triangular prism) |  |
| 2 | 720 |  | [Pentagonal prism](wikipedia://en/2859659) | *Image: Pentagonal prism.png.* |
| 3 | 120 |  | Rectified dodecahedron ([Icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/15023)) | *Image: Icosidodecahedron.png.* |

:::

There are 1440 pentagonal faces between the [icosidodecahedra](wikipedia://en/15023) and [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659). There are 3600 squares between the [cuboctahedra](wikipedia://en/6280) and pentagonal prisms. There are 2400 triangular faces between the icosidodecahedra and cuboctahedra, and 1200 triangular faces between pairs of cuboctahedra.

There are two classes of edges: 3-4-4, 3-4-5: 3600 have two squares and a triangle around it, and 7200 have one triangle, one square, and one pentagon.

:::

The **cantitruncated 600-cell** is a [uniform 4-polytope](wikipedia://en/1645899). It is composed of 1440 [cells](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cell_%28mathematics%29): 120 [truncated icosahedra](wikipedia://en/31282), 720 [pentagonal prisms](wikipedia://en/2859659) and 600 [truncated octahedra](wikipedia://en/279654). It has 7200 vertices, 14400 edges, and 8640 faces (3600 squares, 1440 pentagons, and 3600 hexagons). It has an irregular tetrahedral [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719), filled by one truncated icosahedron, one pentagonal prism and two truncated octahedra.

- Cantitruncated hexacosichoron / Cantitruncated polydodecahedron
- Great rhombated hexacosichoron (acronym *grix*) ([George Olshevsky](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Olshevsky) and Jonathan Bowers)[^o42]
- Ambo-012 polytetrahedron ([John Conway](wikipedia://en/15807))
