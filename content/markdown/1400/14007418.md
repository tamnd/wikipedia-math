# Rudolf Wille

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rudolf Wille"}

*Image: Wille Rudolf 29.06.2012 Darmstadt.jpg.* Rudolf Wille in 2012.

**Rudolf Wille** (2 November 1937 – 22 January 2017) was a German [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and was professor of General Algebra from 1970 to 2003 at [Technische Universität Darmstadt (TU Darmstadt)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Darmstadt_University_of_Technology). His most celebrated work is the invention of [formal concept analysis](wikipedia://en/313845), an unsupervised [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488) technique that applies mathematical lattice theory to organize data based on objects and their shared attributes.

:::

An accomplished musician and has also made contributions to Mathematics in Music, Mathematical Pedagogy and the Philosophy of Science, Wille played an active leadership role in the [concept lattice](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Concept_lattice) research community.

Wille was a member of the Board of Directors of the Institute for Philosophy at TU Darmstadt from 1976. From 1983, was leader of the research group on [Formal concept analysis](wikipedia://en/313845) and from 1993 Chairman of the "Ernst Schröder Center for Conceptual Knowledge Engineering". Wille was also a founding member of the Center for Inter-Disciplinary Research in Darmstadt and maintained a footprint in other research groups around the world as a visiting consulting/scholar.

Wille's research interests included algebra, order and [lattice theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lattice_theory), foundations of geometry, [discrete mathematics](wikipedia://en/8492), [measurement theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Measure_theory), mathematics in music, philosophy of science, conceptual knowledge engineering and contextual logic.

A significant international community of researchers follow Wille's work on [Formal concept analysis](wikipedia://en/313845), the main forums being the International Conferences on Formal Concept Analysis ([ICFCA](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ICFCA)), Conceptual Structures (see also [Conceptual graphs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conceptual_graphs)) (ICCS) and Concept Lattices and their Application (CLA) conferences. The first two are published in the [Lecture Notes in Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lecture_Notes_in_Computer_Science) and the latter is a multi-stage conference that produces journal papers.

Wille oversaw more than 100 German "Diplom- und Staatsexamenarbeiten" in Mathematics, 51 PhD dissertations, and 8 Postdoctoral "habilitation" qualifications.

## Work {#Work}

Wille authored more than 250 scientific publications and co-authored the highly cited and influential textbook on [Formal concept analysis](wikipedia://en/313845) with his longtime collaborator (and former PhD student) Bernhard Ganter who is now Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at TU Dresden:

- B. Ganter, R. Wille (1999) *Formal Concept Analysis: Mathematical Foundations*, Springer-Verlag, .
