# Max A. Woodbury

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Max A. Woodbury"}

**Max Atkin Woodbury** (1917–2010) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was born in [St George, Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_George,_Utah) to [Angus Munn Woodbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Angus_Munn_Woodbury) and Grace (Atkin) Woodbury.[^o2][^o3][^o4] He had three brothers and two sisters, including the biologists [Dixon Miles Woodbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dixon_M._Woodbury) and [John Walter Woodbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/J._Walter_Woodbury).[^o4]

:::

## Career {#Career}

He received his Bachelor of Science from the [University of Utah](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Utah) in 1939, Master of Science from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1941 and metrology at [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology).[^o6][^o3] He obtained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) at the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) in 1948 advised by [Arthur Herbert Copeland](wikipedia://en/2568946). His dissertation was entitled [Probability](wikipedia://en/22934) and [Expected Values](wikipedia://en/9653).

He was a member of the faculty, University of Michigan 1947-1949, [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179) in Princeton 1949-1950,[^o8] member of faculty [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) 1950-1952.[^o19] He moved to be an associate professor in statistics at the [University of Pennsylvania](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Pennsylvania) from 1952-1954.[^o9] After a brief leave at the [Office of Naval Research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Office_of_Naval_Research) 1954-1956,[^o10] he became faculty at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University) from 1956-1965,[^o11][^o12] then a professor of computer science and biomathematics at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University).[^o13][^o14] He became an emeritus professor at Duke, but continued to take an active role in research for many years.[^o15][^o16]

::: {#o18 .statement cue=named-heading name="Woodbury identity"}

## Woodbury identity {#Woodbury_identity}

The [Woodbury matrix identity](wikipedia://en/430493) used in [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422) is named after him.[^o19][^o20] The related [Sherman–Morrison formula](wikipedia://en/2556798) is a special case of the formula,[^o21][^o22][^o23] with the term **Sherman-Morrison-Woodbury** sometimes used. An early overview of some of its uses has been given by Hager,[^o24] see also the book "Woodbury Matrix Identity".[^o25] These methods are taught in many mathematics courses on [linear algebra](wikipedia://en/18422).

:::
