# Joseph L. Walsh

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Joseph L. Walsh"}

*Image: Hille Walsh Zurich1932.tif.* Walsh (right) 1932 in Zürich

**Joseph Leonard Walsh** (September 21, 1895 – December 6, 1973) was an American mathematician who worked mainly in the field of [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396). The [Walsh function](wikipedia://en/380839) and the [Walsh–Hadamard code](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Walsh–Hadamard_code) are named after him. The [Grace–Walsh–Szegő coincidence theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grace–Walsh–Szegő_coincidence_theorem) is important in the study of the location of the zeros of multivariate polynomials.[^o3][^o4]

:::

He became a member of the [National Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_States_National_Academy_of_Sciences) in 1936 and served 1949–51 as president of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822). Altogether he published 279 articles (research and others) and seven books, and advised 31 PhD students.

For most of his professional career he studied and worked at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University). He received a B.S. in 1916 and a PhD in 1920. The Advisor of his PhD was [Maxime Bôcher](wikipedia://en/3180285). Walsh started to work as lecturer in Harvard afterwards and became a full professor in 1935. He was an Invited Speaker of the [ICM](wikipedia://en/627842) in 1920 at Strasbourg.[^o5] With two different scholarships he was able to study in Paris under [Paul Montel](wikipedia://en/1796347) (1920–21) and in Munich under [Constantin Carathéodory](wikipedia://en/470625) (1925–26). From 1937 to 1942 he served as chairman of his department at Harvard. During [World War II](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_II) he served as an officer in the US navy and was promoted to captain right after end of the war. After his retirement from Harvard in 1966 he accepted a position at the [University of Maryland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Maryland,_College_Park) where he continued to work up to a few months before his death.
