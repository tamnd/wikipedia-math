# William Thomson (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="William Thomson"}

Sir **William Thomson** [FRSE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FRSE) [LLD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Legum_Doctor) (1856–1947) was a 19th/20th century Scottish mathematician and physicist primarily working as a university administrator in [South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Africa).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He was born on [New Year's Eve](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Year's_Eve), 31 December 1856, in the village of Kirkton of Mailler in [Perthshire](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perthshire). He was educated at [Perth Academy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Perth_Academy) then studied mathematics and physics at the [University of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Edinburgh). He graduated with a BSc and MA in 1878 and began assisting in lectures at the university.[^o4]

In 1882, aged 26, he was elected a Fellow of the [Royal Society of Edinburgh](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Edinburgh). His proposers were [George Chrystal](wikipedia://en/14008803), [Peter Guthrie Tait](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_Guthrie_Tait), [Alexander Crum Brown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alexander_Crum_Brown) and [Sir William Turner](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Turner_%28anatomist%29).[^o5]

In 1883 he succeeded Prof [George Gordon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Gordon_%28mathematician%29) as Professor of Mathematics at [Stellenbosch University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stellenbosch_University) in [Cape Colony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Colony).[^o6]

In 1895 he succeeded Rev James Cameron as University Registrar at the [University of the Cape of Good Hope](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Cape_of_Good_Hope). In 1918 he transferred to the same role in the newly created [University of South Africa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_South_Africa) and in 1922 moved to the [University of the Witwatersrand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_the_Witwatersrand).

He was knighted by King [George V](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_V) in 1922 for services to university education.

He retired in 1928 and died in [Simonstown](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Simon's_Town) near [Cape Town](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cape_Town) on 6 August 1947.

## Family {#Family}

In 1884 he married Annie Catherine van der Riet. They had two daughters.

## Publications {#Publications}

- *[Mensuration](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mensuration_%28mathematics%29)* in 9th edition of Encyclopædia Britannica (1878)
- *Introduction to Determinants* (1881)
- *Algebra for the Use of Schools and Colleges* (1886)
- *Textbook of Geometrical Deductions* (1891)
- *Elementary Algebra* (1901)
