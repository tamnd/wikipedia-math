# Karen Aardal

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Karen Aardal"}

**Karen I. Aardal** (born 1961) is a Norwegian and Dutch applied mathematician, theoretical computer scientist, and [operations researcher](wikipedia://en/43476). Her research involves [combinatorial optimization](wikipedia://en/420555), [integer programming](wikipedia://en/411215), [approximation algorithms](wikipedia://en/563105), and [facility location](wikipedia://en/10469986), with applications such as positioning emergency vehicles to optimize their response time. She is a professor in the Delft Institute of Applied Mathematics at the [Delft University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Delft_University_of_Technology), and the chair of the [Mathematical Optimization Society](wikipedia://en/5751407) for the 2016–2019 term.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Aardal is originally from Norway. She earned her Ph.D. in 1992 at the [Université catholique de Louvain](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_catholique_de_Louvain) in Belgium. Her dissertation, *On the Solution of One and Two-Level Capacitated Facility Location Problems by the Cutting Plane Approach*, was supervised by [Laurence Wolsey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Laurence_Wolsey). Her dissertation won the second-place SOLA Dissertation Award of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences) Section on Location Analysis.

Aardal was formerly a researcher at the Dutch [Centrum Wiskunde & Informatica](wikipedia://en/506063), and additionally affiliated with [Eindhoven University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eindhoven_University_of_Technology) since 2005. She moved to Delft in 2008.

She was elected to the 2019 class of [Fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [Institute for Operations Research and the Management Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Operations_Research_and_the_Management_Sciences).[^o4]
