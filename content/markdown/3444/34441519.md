# Konstantin Posse

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Konstantin Posse"}

\_\_notoc\_\_

**Konstantin Alexandrovich Posse** (Константин Александрович Поссе; September 29, 1847 – August 24, 1928) was a Russian mathematician known for contributions to [analysis](wikipedia://en/48396) and in particular [approximation theory](wikipedia://en/1092713).[^o3] [Veniamin Kagan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veniamin_Kagan) and [D. D. Morduhai-Boltovskoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D._D._Morduhai-Boltovskoi) were among his students.

:::
