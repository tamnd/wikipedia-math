# Qi-Man Shao

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Qi-Man Shao"}

**Qi-Man Shao** (; born 1962) is a Chinese probabilist and [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661) mostly known for his contributions to asymptotic theory in probability and statistics. He is currently a Chair Professor of Statistics and Data Science[^o2] at the [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_University_of_Science_and_Technology).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He earned a bachelor's degree in Mathematics and a master's degree in Statistics & Probability from [Hangzhou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_University) (now [Zhejiang University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhejiang_University)) in 1983 and 1986, respectively. He went to graduate school at the [University of Science and Technology of China](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Science_and_Technology_of_China) and received a Ph.D. degree in Statistics & Probability in 1989. He spent four years as lecturer and then associate professor at [Hangzhou University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hangzhou_University) from 1986 to 1990. In July 1990, he joined [Carleton University, Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carleton_University) as a visiting research fellow, working with [Miklós Csörgő](wikipedia://en/81252297). From September 1991 to August 1992, he worked as a Taft Postdoctoral Fellow at the [University of Cincinnati](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cincinnati). He joined the [National University of Singapore](wikipedia://en/286880) as a lecturer in 1992, and later became a senior lecturer.

He joined the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon) as an assistant professor in 1996, and was later promoted to associate professor and professor. From 2005 to 2012, he was a professor and Chair Professor at the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hong_Kong_University_of_Science_and_Technology). In 2012, he moved to the [Chinese University of Hong Kong](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chinese_University_of_Hong_Kong), where he served as Department Chair from 2013 to 2018[^o4] and became the Choh-Ming Li Professor of Statistics in 2015.[^o5] Starting March 2019, he moved to the [Southern University of Science and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Southern_University_of_Science_and_Technology), as a Chair Professor and the Founding Chairman of the Department of Statistics and Data Science.

His research interests include asymptotic theory in [probability](wikipedia://en/23542) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26024794), self-normalized limit theory, [Stein’s method](wikipedia://en/12933953), and [high-dimensional and large-scale statistical analysis](wikipedia://en/19330658). He is particularly well-known for his fundamental contributions to self-normalized large and moderate deviation theories, Stein’s method for normal and non-normal approximation, and the development of various probability inequalities for dependent random variables. He authored and co-authored over 180 articles on probability and statistics, and co-authored three well-known books (*Monte Carlo Methods in Bayesian Computation* (2000),[^o6] *Self-normalized Processes: Limit Theory and Statistical Applications* (2009),[^o7] *and Normal Approximation by Stein’s Method* (2011)[^o8])*.*

## Honors and awards {#Honors_and_awards}

- Fok Ying Tung Education Foundation Award, 1989
- The State Natural Science Award (the 3rd class), 1997 (Z.Y. Lin, C.R. Lu and Q.M. Shao)
- Elected Fellow, the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics),[^o10] 2001
- [Invited speaker](wikipedia://en/47738065) (45min) at the 2010 [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842)[^o11]
- IMS Medallion Lecturer, Keynote Speaker at the 2011 [Joint Statistical Meetings](wikipedia://en/15571662)[^o12]
- Plenary speaker, 36th Conference on Stochastic Processes and Their Applications, 2013[^o13]
- Plenary speaker, IMS-China International Conference on Statistics and Probability, 2013
- The State Natural Science Award (the 2nd class), 2015 (Q.-M. Shao and B.-Y. Jing)[^o14]

## Professional services {#Professional_services}

- [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (IMS) Committee on Fellows, Member in 2007–2009 and 2011, Chair in 2009
- IMS Committee on Nominations (2011, 2016, 2017), Institute of Mathematical Statistics[^o17]
- Council Member, [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics) (2019–2022)[^o18]
