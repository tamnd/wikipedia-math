# 1930 Dutch East Indies census

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="1930 Dutch East Indies census"}

The **1930 Dutch East Indies census** (Volkstelling 1930) was the last [census](wikipedia://en/6889) of the [Dutch East Indies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dutch_East_Indies). The total population was enumerated to be 60,727,233. Its population density in 1930 was 60727233 inhabitants/km<sup>2</sup>. [^n1]
