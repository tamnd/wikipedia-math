# Peter Orlik

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Orlik"}

**Peter Paul Nikolas Orlik** (born 12 November 1938, in [Budapest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Budapest))[^o2] is an American mathematician, known for his research on [topology](wikipedia://en/29954), algebra, and [combinatorics](wikipedia://en/5170).[^o3]

:::

Orlik earned in 1961 his bachelor's degree from the [Norwegian Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norwegian_Institute_of_Technology) in [Trondheim](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trondheim) and in 1966 his Ph.D. from the [University of Michigan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Michigan) under [Frank Raymond](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frank_Raymond_%28mathematician%29) with thesis *Necessary conditions for the homeomorphism of Seifert manifolds*. He became in 1966 an assistant professor and in 1973 a full professor at the [University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).

Orlik was in the academic year 1971/72 a visiting professor in [Oslo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oslo). From 1967 to 1969 he was a visiting scholar at the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179).[^o5]

Orlik is the author of over 70 publications. He works on [Seifert manifolds](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Seifert_manifold), [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), [braid theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Braid_theory), [reflection groups](wikipedia://en/2422023), [invariant theory](wikipedia://en/363762), and hypergeometric integrals.[^o3] He was, with [Louis Solomon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Solomon) and [Hiroaki Terao](wikipedia://en/56374155), a pioneer of the theory of [arrangements of hyperplanes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arrangements_of_hyperplanes) in complex space.

In 2012 he was elected a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o6]
