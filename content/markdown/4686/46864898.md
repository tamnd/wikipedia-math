# CoolaData

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="CoolaData"}

**CoolaData** is a [big data](wikipedia://en/27051151) [behavioral analytics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Behavioral_analytics) platform for online web and mobile applications. It was acquired by [Medallia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Medallia) in 2019.[^o3]

:::

Cooladata was founded with the vision of developing an advanced BI and analytics platform that would be able to store unlimited amounts of data events in the cloud, while providing advanced user behavioral analytics, or in other words, providing the analytical capabilities and agility of an enterprise, at a fraction of the cost.[^o4]

## Founders {#Founders}

CoolaData was founded by two veteran BI experts. CoolaData's CEO Tomer Ben Moshe spent several years at [Amdocs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amdocs) (NYSE:DOX), where he co-initiated and served as SVP, COO of Amdocs Cloud Services. Before Amdocs Tomer was the CTO of Microsoft Israel, where he worked closely with software vendors, startups and the developer community.

## Funding {#Funding}

The company is backed by 83North (formerly Greylock IL), [Salesforce Ventures](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Salesforce_Ventures) and Carmel Ventures.[^o7]
