# REVSTAT

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="REVSTAT"}

***REVSTAT*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access_%28publishing%29) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes papers related to [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). It is published in English by the [Instituto Nacional de Estatística](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Instituto_Nacional_de_Estatística_%28Portugal%29), the national statistical office of Portugal. The journal was established in 2003, when it replaced the journal *Revista de Estatística*. It publishes two issues each year, both in print (subscription) and online as open access.

:::

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

*REVSTAT* is abstracted and indexed in [Current Index to Statistics](wikipedia://en/13958910), [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index_Expanded), [MathSciNet](wikipedia://en/2580683), [Statistical Theory and Method Abstracts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Theory_and_Method_Abstracts), and [Zentralblatt MATH](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zentralblatt_MATH).
