# SORT (journal)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="SORT"}

***SORT*** or ***Statistics and Operations Research Transactions*** is a [peer-reviewed](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer_review) [open access](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Open_access_%28publishing%29) [scientific journal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Scientific_journal) that publishes papers related to [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685). It is published by the [Institut d'Estadística de Catalunya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institut_d'Estadística_de_Catalunya), the statistical office of [Catalonia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalonia), in [English](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/English_language) with a brief summary in [Catalan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Catalan_language).

:::

The journal was established in 2003, when it replaced the journal *Qüestiió* (*Quaderns d'Estadística i Investigació Operativa*, 1977–2002). It publishes two issues each year, and is available online as open access.

## Abstracting and indexing {#Abstracting_and_indexing}

*SORT* is indexed in the [Current Index to Statistics](wikipedia://en/13958910), [Science Citation Index Expanded](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Science_Citation_Index), and [Journal Citation Reports](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_Citation_Reports).
