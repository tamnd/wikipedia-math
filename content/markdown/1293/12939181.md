# Quasithin group

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Quasithin group"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **quasithin group** is a [finite](wikipedia://en/323707) [simple group](wikipedia://en/47600) that resembles a [group of Lie type](wikipedia://en/1691490) of rank at most 2 over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [characteristic](wikipedia://en/1066621) 2. The classification of quasithin groups is a crucial part of the [classification of finite simple groups](wikipedia://en/7445).

:::

More precisely it is a finite simple group of [characteristic 2 type](wikipedia://en/29668519) and width 2. Here characteristic 2 type means that its [centralizers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centralizer) of [involutions](wikipedia://en/293450#Group_theory) resemble those of [groups of Lie type](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Groups_of_Lie_type) over fields of characteristic 2, and the width is roughly the maximal rank of an [abelian group](wikipedia://en/2974) of [odd](wikipedia://en/143135) [order](wikipedia://en/493527) normalizing a non-trivial 2-subgroup of *G*. When *G* is a group of Lie type of characteristic 2 type, the width is usually the rank (the dimension of a [maximal torus](wikipedia://en/360136) of the algebraic group).

## Classification {#Classification}

The quasithin groups were classified in a [1221-page paper](wikipedia://en/33074893) by (txt). An earlier announcement by (txt) of the classification, on the basis of which the classification of finite simple groups was announced as finished in 1983, was premature as the unpublished manuscript (Mason 1981) of his work was incomplete and contained serious gaps.

According to (Aschbacher Smith 2004b, theorem 0.1.1), the finite simple quasithin groups of [even](wikipedia://en/143135) characteristic are given by

- Groups of Lie type of characteristic 2 and rank 1 or 2, except that U<sub>5</sub>(*q*) only occurs for *q* = 4
- PSL<sub>4</sub>(2), PSL<sub>5</sub>(2), Sp<sub>6</sub>(2)
- The [alternating groups](wikipedia://en/49726) on 5, 6, 8, 9 points
- PSL<sub>2</sub>(*p*) for *p* a [Fermat](wikipedia://en/91127) or [Mersenne prime](wikipedia://en/18908), L${}^{\varepsilon}_{3}$(3), L${}^{\varepsilon}_{4}$(3), G<sub>2</sub>(3)
- The [Mathieu groups](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathieu_groups) M<sub>11</sub>, M<sub>12</sub>, M<sub>22</sub>, M<sub>23</sub>, M<sub>24</sub>, The [Janko groups](wikipedia://en/380191) J<sub>2</sub>, J<sub>3</sub>, J<sub>4</sub>, the [Higman–Sims group](wikipedia://en/403178), the [Held group](wikipedia://en/1110818), and the [Rudvalis group](wikipedia://en/1196760).

If the condition "even characteristic" is relaxed to "even type" in the sense of the revision of the classification by [Daniel Gorenstein](wikipedia://en/1569478), [Richard Lyons](wikipedia://en/35256697), and [Ronald Solomon](wikipedia://en/35255960), then the only extra group that appears is the [Janko group J1](wikipedia://en/10796779).
