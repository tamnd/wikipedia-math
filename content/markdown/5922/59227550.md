# Florentina Bunea

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Florentina Bunea"}

*Image: ZhouHuibin RohdeAngelika BuneaFlorentina MFO22546.jpg.* *Left to right:* Huibin Zhou, Angelika Rohde, and Florentina Bunea organized the 2018 [MFO](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) Workshop *Matrix Estimation Meets Statistical Network Analysis*.

**Florentina Bunea** is a Romanian-American statistician, interested in [machine learning](wikipedia://en/233488), the theory of [empirical processes](wikipedia://en/3690433), and [high-dimensional statistics](wikipedia://en/19330658). She is a professor at [Cornell University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cornell_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Bunea earned Bachelor's and master's degrees at the [University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bucharest) in 1989 and 1991. After working as an assistant professor at the [Politehnica University of Bucharest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Politehnica_University_of_Bucharest) from 1991 to 1995, she returned to graduate study at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington). She earned her Ph.D. there in 2000; her dissertation, *A Model Selection Approach to Partially Linear Regression*, was supervised by [Jon A. Wellner](wikipedia://en/73537857).

She joined the statistics faculty at [Florida State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Florida_State_University) in 2000, and moved to Cornell University in 2011. At Cornell, she is a faculty member in the Department of Statistics and Data Science, a member of the Center for Applied Mathematics and the Machine Learning Group in Cornell Computing and Information Science (CIS).[^o3]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 2017, Bunea was elected as a Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics).
