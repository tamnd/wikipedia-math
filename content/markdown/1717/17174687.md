# ABC@Home

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="ABC@Home"}

**ABC@Home** was an educational and non-profit [network computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_network) project finding abc-triples related to the [abc conjecture](wikipedia://en/174108) in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) using the [Berkeley Open Infrastructure for Network Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Berkeley_Open_Infrastructure_for_Network_Computing) (BOINC) [volunteer computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volunteer_computing) platform.

:::

In March 2011, there were more than 7,300 active participants from 114 countries with a total [BOINC credit](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BOINC_Credit_System) of more than 2.9 billion, reporting about 10 [teraflops](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/FLOPS) (10 trillion operations per second) of processing power.[^o4]

In 2011, the project met its goal of finding all abc-triples of at most 18 digits. By 2015, the project had found 23.8 million triples in total, and ceased operations soon after.[^o5]
