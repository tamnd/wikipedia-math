# Sylvain Cappell

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sylvain Cappell"}

**Sylvain Edward Cappell** (born 1946) is a [Belgian American](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgian_American) mathematician specializing in [topology](wikipedia://en/29954). He is a former student of [William Browder](wikipedia://en/6646431) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and has spent most of his career at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) at [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), where he is now a Silver Professor of Mathematics.

:::

He was born in [Brussels](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brussels), [Belgium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Belgium) and immigrated with his parents to New York City in 1950 and grew up largely in this city.[^o3] In 1963, as a senior at the [Bronx High School of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronx_High_School_of_Science), he won first place in the [Westinghouse Science Talent Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Westinghouse_Science_Talent_Search) for his work on "The Theory of Semi-cyclical Groups with Special Reference to Non-Aristotelian Logic." He then graduated from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1966, winning the Van Amringe Mathematical Prize.[^o4]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) (AMS).[^o5] Cappell served as its vice president from February 2010 until January 2013.[^o6][^o7] He was elected to the [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences) in 2018.[^o8]
