# Heinrich Tietze

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Heinrich Tietze"}

**Heinrich Franz Friedrich Tietze** (August 31, 1880 – February 17, 1964) was an [Austrian](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), famous for the [Tietze extension theorem](wikipedia://en/31404) on functions from topological spaces to the real numbers. He also developed the [Tietze transformations](wikipedia://en/3412351) for [group presentations](wikipedia://en/99494), and was the first to pose the [group isomorphism problem](wikipedia://en/18054026). [Tietze's graph](wikipedia://en/20058152) is also named after him; it describes the boundaries of a subdivision of the [Möbius strip](wikipedia://en/37817) into six mutually-adjacent regions, found by Tietze as part of an extension of the [four color theorem](wikipedia://en/10949) to non-orientable surfaces.

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Tietze was the son of [Emil Tietze](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emil_Tietze) and the grandson of [Franz Ritter von Hauer](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Ritter_von_Hauer), both of whom were Austrian geologists. He was born in [Schleinz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schleinz), [Austria-Hungary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austria-Hungary), and studied mathematics at the [Technische Hochschule Wien](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Hochschule_Wien) beginning in 1898. After additional studies in Munich, he returned to Vienna, completing his doctorate in 1904 and his [habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 1908.

From 1910 until 1918, Tietze taught mathematics in [Brno](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brno), and was promoted to [ordinary professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ordinary_professor) in 1913. He served in the Austrian army during [World War I](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/World_War_I), and then returned to Brno, but in 1919, he took a position at the [University of Erlangen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Erlangen-Nuremberg), and then in 1925 moved again to the [Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität München](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ludwig-Maximilians-Universität_München), where he remained for the rest of his career. One of his doctoral students was [Georg Aumann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georg_Aumann). Tietze retired in 1950, and died in [Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Munich), [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany).[^o4]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Tietze was a fellow of the [Bavarian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities) and a fellow of the [Austrian Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Austrian_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o4]
