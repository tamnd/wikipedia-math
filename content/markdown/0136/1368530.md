# Adolf Kneser

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Adolf Kneser"}

**Adolf Kneser** (19 March 1862 – 24 January 1930) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

He was born in [Grüssow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grüssow), [Grand Duchy of Mecklenburg-Schwerin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grand_Duchy_of_Mecklenburg-Schwerin) and died in Breslau, Germany.

He is the father of the mathematician [Hellmuth Kneser](wikipedia://en/1368525) and the grandfather of the mathematician [Martin Kneser](wikipedia://en/17620650).

Kneser is known for the first proof of the [four-vertex theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Four-vertex_theorem) that applied in general to non-convex curves. [Kneser's theorem on differential equations](wikipedia://en/10144855) is named after him, and provides criteria to decide whether a [differential equation](wikipedia://en/1424309) is [oscillating](wikipedia://en/10144971). He is also one of the namesakes of the [Tait–Kneser theorem](wikipedia://en/62530331) on [osculating circles](wikipedia://en/1896705).
