# Noncentral F-distribution

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Noncentral F-distribution"}

In [probability theory](wikipedia://en/23542) and [statistics](wikipedia://en/26685), the **noncentral *F*-distribution** is a [continuous probability distribution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_probability_distribution) that is a [noncentral generalization](wikipedia://en/20829683) of the (ordinary) [*F*-distribution](wikipedia://en/332116). It describes the distribution of the quotient (*X*/*n*<sub>1</sub>)/(*Y*/*n*<sub>2</sub>), where the numerator *X* has a [noncentral chi-squared distribution](wikipedia://en/2601749) with *n*<sub>1</sub> degrees of freedom and the denominator *Y* has a central [chi-squared distribution](wikipedia://en/113424) with *n*<sub>2</sub> degrees of freedom. It is also required that *X* and *Y* are [statistically independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_independence) of each other.

:::

It is the distribution of the [test statistic](wikipedia://en/1416975) in [analysis of variance](wikipedia://en/634) problems when the [null hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Null_hypothesis) is false. The noncentral *F*-distribution is used to find the [power function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_power) of such a test.

## Occurrence and specification {#Occurrence_and_specification}

If $X$ is a [noncentral chi-squared](wikipedia://en/2601749) random variable with noncentrality parameter $\lambda$ and $\nu_1$ degrees of freedom, and $Y$ is a [chi-squared](wikipedia://en/113424) random variable with $\nu_2$ degrees of freedom that is [statistically independent](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_independence) of $X$, then

$$F=\frac{X/\nu_1}{Y/\nu_2}$$

is a noncentral *F*-distributed random variable. The [probability density function](wikipedia://en/43487) (pdf) for the noncentral *F*-distribution is[^o3]

$$p(f) =\sum\limits_{k=0}^\infty\frac{e^{-\lambda/2}(\lambda/2)^k}{ B\left(\frac{\nu_2}{2},\frac{\nu_1}{2}+k\right) k!} \left(\frac{\nu_1}{\nu_2}\right)^{\frac{\nu_1}{2}+k} \left(\frac{\nu_2}{\nu_2+\nu_1f}\right)^{\frac{\nu_1+\nu_2}{2}+k}f^{\nu_1/2-1+k}$$

when $f\ge0$ and zero otherwise. The degrees of freedom $\nu_1$ and $\nu_2$ are positive. The term $B(x,y)$ is the [beta function](wikipedia://en/245660), where

$$B(x,y)=\frac{\Gamma(x)\Gamma(y)}{\Gamma(x+y)}.$$

The [cumulative distribution function](wikipedia://en/5793) for the noncentral *F*-distribution is

$$F(x\mid d_1,d_2,\lambda)=\sum\limits_{j=0}^\infty\left(\frac{\left(\frac{1}{2}\lambda\right)^j}{j!}e^{-\lambda/2} \right)I\left(\frac{d_1x}{d_2 + d_1x}\bigg|\frac{d_1}{2}+j,\frac{d_2}{2}\right)$$

where $I$ is the [regularized incomplete beta function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regularized_incomplete_beta_function).

The mean and variance of the noncentral *F*-distribution are

$$\operatorname{E}[F] \quad \begin{cases} = \frac{\nu_2(\nu_1+\lambda)}{\nu_1(\nu_2-2)} & \text{if } \nu_2>2\\ \text{does not exist} & \text{if } \nu_2\le2\\ \end{cases}$$

and

$$\operatorname{Var}[F] \quad \begin{cases} = 2\frac{(\nu_1+\lambda)^2+(\nu_1+2\lambda)(\nu_2-2)}{(\nu_2-2)^2(\nu_2-4)}\left(\frac{\nu_2}{\nu_1}\right)^2 & \text{if } \nu_2>4\\ \text{does not exist} & \text{if } \nu_2\le4.\\ \end{cases}$$

::: {#o5 .statement kind=statement/generalization cue=heading name="Special cases"}

## Special cases {#Special_cases}

When *λ* = 0, the noncentral *F*-distribution becomes the [*F*-distribution](wikipedia://en/332116).

:::

## Related distributions {#Related_distributions}

*Z* has a [noncentral chi-squared distribution](wikipedia://en/2601749) if

$$Z=\lim_{\nu_2\to\infty}\nu_1 F$$

where *F* has a noncentral *F*-distribution.

See also [noncentral t-distribution](wikipedia://en/3287836).

A Doubly noncentral *F* distribution has a [noncentral chi-squared distribution](wikipedia://en/2601749) in the numerator and denominator.[^o7]

## Implementations {#Implementations}

The noncentral *F*-distribution is implemented in the [R](wikipedia://en/376707) language (e.g., pf function), in [MATLAB](wikipedia://en/20412) (ncfcdf, ncfinv, ncfpdf, ncfrnd and ncfstat functions in the statistics toolbox) in [Mathematica](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematica) (NoncentralFRatioDistribution function), in [NumPy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NumPy) (random.noncentral\_f), and in [Boost C++ Libraries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boost_C++_Libraries).[^o10]

A collaborative wiki page implements an interactive online calculator, programmed in the [R language](wikipedia://en/376707), for the noncentral t, [chi-squared](wikipedia://en/2601749), and F distributions, at the Institute of Statistics and Econometrics of the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin).[^o11]
