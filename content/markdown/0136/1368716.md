# Wilhelm Süss

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Wilhelm Süss"}

**Wilhelm Süss** (7 March 1895 – 21 May 1958) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_people) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). He was founder and first director of the [Oberwolfach Research Institute for Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1368669).

:::

## Biography {#Biography}

He was born in [Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt), [Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Empire), and died in [Freiburg im Breisgau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg_im_Breisgau), [West Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Germany).

Süss earned a Ph.D. degree in 1922 from [Goethe University Frankfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goethe_University_Frankfurt), for a thesis written under the direction of [Ludwig Bieberbach](wikipedia://en/616186). In 1928, he took a lecturing position at the [University of Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greifswald), and in 1934 he became a Professor at the [University of Freiburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Freiburg).

Wilhelm Süss was a member of the [Nazi Party](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nazi_Party) and the [National Socialist German Lecturers League](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Socialist_German_Lecturers_League); he joined [Stahlhelm](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Der_Stahlhelm,_Bund_der_Frontsoldaten) to avoid being automatically enrolled in [Sturmabteilung](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sturmabteilung) but later he, with all Stahlhelm members, became members of Sturmabteilung. The extent to which he worked with Nazis or only cooperated as little as possible is a matter of debate among historians.[^o4][^o5][^o6]

In 1936–1940, he was an editor of the journal *[Deutsche Mathematik](wikipedia://en/31018614)*.
