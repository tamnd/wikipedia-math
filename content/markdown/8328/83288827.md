# Ziyang Gao

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ziyang Gao"}

**Ziyang Gao** (in Chinese: *高紫阳*) is a Chinese mathematician. He is a professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles). His research concerns [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), [Diophantine geometry](wikipedia://en/502205), [Shimura varieties](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shimura_varieties) and [transcendental number theory](wikipedia://en/1157698).

:::

In 2022, along with [Vesselin Dimitrov](wikipedia://en/81558886), Gao won the [David Goss Prize](wikipedia://en/8422003) for his work on Shimura varieties and Diophantine geometry[.](wikipedia://en/21527)[^o3] His work on the uniform Mordell conjecture, joint with [Vesselin Dimitrov](wikipedia://en/81558886) and [Philipp Habegger](wikipedia://en/67011825) is considered a major breakthrough in diophantine geometry and is the resolution of a 40-year old conjecture.[^o4]

## Career {#Career}

Gao received his Bachelor of Science in mathemtics from [Peking University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peking_University) in 2010 and his PhD from [Université Paris-Sud](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Université_Paris-Sud) and [Leiden University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University) in 2015, under the supervision of [Emmanuel Ullmo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Emmanuel_Ullmo) and [Bas Edixhoven](wikipedia://en/69886727).[^o6]. He was a member at the [Institute of Advanced Study](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Advanced_Study) in 2015 [^o7] and an Instructor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) from 2016 to 2018. From 2015 to 2021, he also worked as a researcher at [CNRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_National_Centre_for_Scientific_Research). In 2021, he took up an appointment as a [W3 professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_ranks_in_Germany) in [Leibniz University Hannover](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leibniz_University_Hannover). As of 2024, he is a professor at the [University of California, Los Angeles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Los_Angeles).

## Research {#Research}

Gao extended results of Edixhoven, Bruno Klingler, Ullmo and Andrei Yafaev, and of [Jonathan Pila](wikipedia://en/31960316), [Jacob Tsimerman](wikipedia://en/48497888) and [Umberto Zannier](wikipedia://en/58056511) from Shimura varieties to mixed Shimura varieties, thereby extending Tsimerman's work on the [Andre-Oort conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andre-Oort_conjecture) to mixed Shimura varieties.[^o9]

In joint work with Habegger, Gao resolved the geometric [Bogomolov conjecture](wikipedia://en/18992933) for [function fields](wikipedia://en/447645) of curves in characteristic zero. [^o10] Together with Dimitrov and Habegger, Gao proved a uniform version of the [Mordell conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mordell_conjecture) (also known as [Faltings](wikipedia://en/1709479)' theorem), thereby proving [Mazur's Conjecture B](wikipedia://en/66851658#Mazur's_Conjecture_B).[^o11] The uniform Mordell conjecture is considered a major breakthrough in diophantine geometry and is the resolution of a 40-year old conjecture. [^o4] With Tangli Ge and Lars Kühne, Gao extended his result on uniform Mordell from curves in Jacobians to subvarieties of abelian varieties, hence proving a uniform version of the Mordell-Lang conjecture. [^o12] Together with Habegger, Gao also proved the relative [Manin-Mumford conjecture](wikipedia://en/18992933#Manin-Mumford_conjecture).[^o13]

## Awards {#Awards}

- In 2015, the Stieljtes prize for his PhD dissertation.[^o15]
- In 2022, along with [Vesselin Dimitrov](wikipedia://en/81558886), the [David Goss Prize](wikipedia://en/8422003) for his work on Shimura varieties and Diophantine geometry, which is awarded every two years to outstanding mathematicians below the age of 35 for their contributions to [number theory.](wikipedia://en/21527)[^o16]
- In 2024 the Frontiers in Science award for his joint paper with Dimitrov and [Philipp Habegger](wikipedia://en/67011825) on the uniform Mordell conjecture.[^o17]
- In 2025 the Compositio prize for his paper titled "Generic rank of Betti map and unlikely intersections".[^o18]
