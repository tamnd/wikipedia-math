# Mihalis Dafermos

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Mihalis Dafermos"}

**Mihalis Dafermos** ([Greek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Greek_language): Μιχάλης Δαφέρμος; born October 1976)[^o3] is a Greek mathematician. He is Professor of Mathematics at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and holds the [Lowndean Chair of Astronomy and Geometry](wikipedia://en/648990) at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge).[^o4]

:::

Dafermos studied mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University) and earned a bachelor's degree in 1997. He submitted his doctoral dissertation titled *Stability and Instability of the [Cauchy Horizon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cauchy_horizon) for the Spherically Symmetric Einstein-Maxwell-Scalar Field Equations* in 2001, under the supervision of [Demetrios Christodoulou](wikipedia://en/2407999) at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).

He has won the [Adams Prize](wikipedia://en/1098110) writing on the subject *Differential Equations* in 2004 and the [Whitehead Prize](wikipedia://en/10681913) in 2009 for "his work on the rigorous analysis of [hyperbolic partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/1575825) in [general relativity](wikipedia://en/12024)."[^o6] In 2015 he was elected as a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o7]
