# Caccioppoli Prize

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Caccioppoli Prize"}

The **Caccioppoli Prize** is awarded by the [Italian Mathematical Union](wikipedia://en/11969092) to an Italian mathematician not exceeding the age of 38 who established a wide international reputation.[^o2] The prize is entitled to the memory of the Italian mathematician [Renato Caccioppoli](wikipedia://en/4262313) and is awarded on the occasion of the Italian Mathematical Union conference every four years. In its early stages the prize was awarded every two years. The recipient currently receives 10,000 euros.

:::

Further prizes of the Italian Mathematical Union are the [Bartolozzi Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bartolozzi_Prize), the [Stampacchia Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stampacchia_Medal) and the [Vinti Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vinti_Prize).
