# Dedekind–Kummer theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dedekind–Kummer theorem"}

In [algebraic number theory](wikipedia://en/174705), the **Dedekind–Kummer theorem** describes how a [prime ideal](wikipedia://en/24928) in a [Dedekind domain](wikipedia://en/145132) factors over the domain's [integral closure](wikipedia://en/39944913).[^o2] It is named after [Richard Dedekind](wikipedia://en/23475106) who developed the theorem based on the work of [Ernst Kummer](wikipedia://en/319117).

:::

## Statement for number fields {#Statement_for_number_fields}

Let $K$ be a [number field](wikipedia://en/28730822) and $\mathcal O_K$ the [ring of algebraic integers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ring_of_algebraic_integers) in $K$. Let $\alpha \in \mathcal O_K$ be such that $\mathbb{Q}[\alpha]=K$ and $f$ be the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $\mathbb{Z}[x]$. For any prime $p$ not dividing the index $[\mathcal O_K : \mathbb{Z}[\alpha]]$, write

$$f(x) \equiv \pi_1 (x)^{e_1} \cdots \pi_g(x)^{e_g} \mod p,$$

where $\pi_i (x)$ are monic [irreducible polynomials](wikipedia://en/188725) in $\mathbb F_p[x]$. Then, the ideal $(p) = p \mathcal O_K$ factors into prime ideals as

$$(p) = \mathfrak p_1^{e_1} \cdots \mathfrak p_g^{e_g}$$

such that $N(\mathfrak p_i) = p^{\deg \pi_i}$, where $N$ is the [ideal norm](wikipedia://en/460672).[^o4]

## Statement for Dedekind domains {#Statement_for_Dedekind_domains}

The Dedekind-Kummer theorem holds more generally than in the situation of number fields: Let $\mathcal o$ be a Dedekind domain contained in its quotient field $K$, $L/K$ a finite, separable field extension with $L=K[\theta]$ for a suitable generator $\theta$ and $\mathcal O$ the integral closure of $\mathcal o$. The above situation is just a special case as one can choose $\mathcal o = \mathbb{Z}, K=\mathbb{Q}, \mathcal O = \mathcal O_L$).

If $(0)\neq\mathfrak p\subseteq\mathcal o$ is a prime ideal coprime to the conductor $\mathfrak F=\{a\in \mathcal O\mid a\mathcal O\subseteq\mathcal o[\theta]\}$ (i.e. their sum is $\mathcal O$). Consider the minimal polynomial $f\in \mathcal o[x]$ of $\theta$. The polynomial $\overline f\in(\mathcal o / \mathfrak p)[x]$ has the decomposition

$$\overline f=\overline{f_1}^{e_1}\cdots \overline{f_r}^{e_r}$$

with pairwise distinct irreducible polynomials $\overline{f_i}$. The factorization of $\mathfrak p$ into prime ideals over $\mathcal O$ is then given by

$$\mathfrak p=\mathfrak P_1^{e_1}\cdots \mathfrak P_r^{e_r}$$

where $\mathfrak P_i=\mathfrak p\mathcal O+(f_i(\theta)\mathcal O)$ and the $f_i$ are the polynomials $\overline{f_i}$ lifted to $\mathcal o[x]$.[^o2]
