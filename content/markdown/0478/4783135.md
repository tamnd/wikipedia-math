# Representation theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Representation theorem"}

Universal approximation theorem In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), **representation theorem** is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) that states that every abstract structure with certain properties is [isomorphic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isomorphic) to another (abstract or concrete) structure.

:::

::: {#o3 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

### Algebra {#Algebra}

- [Cayley's theorem](wikipedia://en/105967) states that every [group](wikipedia://en/19447) is [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/12397) to a [permutation group](wikipedia://en/24634).[^o5]
- [Representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) studies properties of abstract groups via their [representations](wikipedia://en/12695) as [linear transformations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_transformation) of [vector spaces](wikipedia://en/32370).
- [Stone's representation theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone's_representation_theorem) for [Boolean algebras](wikipedia://en/3959) states that every Boolean algebra is isomorphic to a [field of sets](wikipedia://en/1018676).[^o6]

A variant, [Stone's representation theorem for distributive lattices](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone's_representation_theorem_for_distributive_lattices), states that every [distributive lattice](wikipedia://en/310883) is isomorphic to a sublattice of the [power set](wikipedia://en/23799) lattice of some set.

Another variant, [Stone's duality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stone's_duality), states that there exists a duality (in the sense of an arrow-reversing equivalence) between the [categories](wikipedia://en/53759) of Boolean algebras and that of [Stone spaces](wikipedia://en/380219).

- The [Poincaré–Birkhoff–Witt theorem](wikipedia://en/762696) states that every [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) embeds into the commutator Lie algebra of its [universal enveloping algebra](wikipedia://en/363551).
- [Ado's theorem](wikipedia://en/3422249) states that every finite-dimensional [Lie algebra](wikipedia://en/17944) over a [field](wikipedia://en/10603) of [characteristic zero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Characteristic_zero) embeds into the Lie algebra of [endomorphisms](wikipedia://en/9569) of some finite-dimensional vector space.
- [Birkhoff's HSP theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Birkhoff's_HSP_theorem) states that every [model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_%28model_theory%29) of an algebra *A* is the homomorphic image of a [subalgebra](wikipedia://en/45239) of a [direct product](wikipedia://en/8887) of copies of *A*.[^o7]
- In the study of [semigroups](wikipedia://en/27799), the [Wagner–Preston theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wagner–Preston_theorem) provides a representation of an [inverse semigroup](wikipedia://en/1757814) *S*, as a homomorphic image of the set of [partial bijections](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_bijection) on *S*, and the semigroup operation given by [composition](wikipedia://en/7472170).

### Category theory {#Category_theory}

- The [Yoneda lemma](wikipedia://en/34359) provides a [full and faithful](wikipedia://en/876534) [limit](wikipedia://en/39248)-preserving embedding of any category into a category of [presheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presheaf).
- [Mitchell's embedding theorem](wikipedia://en/453755) for [abelian categories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abelian_categories) realises every [small](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Small_category) abelian category as a full (and exactly embedded) [subcategory](wikipedia://en/310959) of a [category of modules](wikipedia://en/11231812) over some [ring](wikipedia://en/48404).
- [Mostowski's collapsing theorem](wikipedia://en/1251604) states that every well-founded extensional structure is isomorphic to a transitive set with the ∈-relation.
- One of the fundamental theorems in [sheaf](wikipedia://en/245466) theory states that every sheaf over a [topological space](wikipedia://en/30450) can be thought of as a sheaf of [sections](wikipedia://en/367621) of some (étalé) bundle over that space: the categories of sheaves on a topological space and that of [étalé spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Étalé_space) over it are equivalent, where the equivalence is given by the [functor](wikipedia://en/10987) that sends a bundle to its sheaf of (local) sections.

### Functional analysis {#Functional_analysis}

- The [Gelfand–Naimark–Segal construction](wikipedia://en/297446) embeds any [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) in an algebra of [bounded operators](wikipedia://en/455961) on some [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932).
- The [Gelfand representation](wikipedia://en/364372) (also known as the commutative Gelfand–Naimark theorem) states that any commutative [C\*-algebra](wikipedia://en/7184) is isomorphic to an algebra of continuous functions on its [Gelfand spectrum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gelfand_spectrum). It can also be seen as the construction as a duality between the category of commutative [C\*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*-algebras) and that of [compact Hausdorff spaces](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_Hausdorff_space).
- The [Riesz representation theorem](wikipedia://en/26452) states that a [Hilbert space](wikipedia://en/20598932), such as the [square-integrable function](wikipedia://en/28680558) space *L*<sup>2</sup>(*X*) on a manifold *X*, any linear functional *F* is equal to the inner product with a fixed element $a\in H$, i.e. $F(v) = \langle a,v\rangle$ for all $v\in H$. The more general [Riesz–Markov–Kakutani representation theorem](wikipedia://en/38948504) has several versions, one of them identifying the dual space of *C*<sub>0</sub>(*X*) with the set of regular measures on *X*.

### Geometry {#Geometry}

- The [Whitney embedding theorems](wikipedia://en/477578) embed any abstract [manifold](wikipedia://en/2073470) in some [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697).
- The [Nash embedding theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nash_embedding_theorem) embeds an abstract [Riemannian manifold](wikipedia://en/144652) isometrically in a [Euclidean space](wikipedia://en/9697).[^o12]

### Order Theory {#Order_Theory}

- It is a basic result that every [partially ordered set](wikipedia://en/23572) is [isomorphic](wikipedia://en/23916899) to a collection of [sets](wikipedia://en/27553) ordered by [inclusion](wikipedia://en/27631) (containment).

### Economics {#Economics}

- A [preference representation theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Preference_representation_theorem) states conditions for the existence of a [utility function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utility_function) representing a [preference relation](wikipedia://en/28737247). Examples are [Von Neumann–Morgenstern utility theorem](wikipedia://en/26898094) and [Debreu's representation theorems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Debreu's_representation_theorems).
