# Regina (program)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Regina"}

**Regina** is a suite of mathematical software for [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) [topologists](wikipedia://en/29954). It focuses upon the study of 3-manifold [triangulations](wikipedia://en/1440976) and includes support for [normal surfaces](wikipedia://en/3141418) and angle structures.[^o3]

:::

## Features {#Features}

- Regina implements a variant of [Rubinstein's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyam_Rubinstein) [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792) recognition algorithm. This is an algorithm that determines whether or not a [triangulated](wikipedia://en/1440976) [3-manifold](wikipedia://en/1018257) is [homeomorphic](wikipedia://en/13660) to the [3-sphere](wikipedia://en/39792).
- Regina further implements the [connect-sum](wikipedia://en/490134) decomposition. This will decompose a [triangulated](wikipedia://en/1440976) 3-manifold into a [connect-sum](wikipedia://en/490134) of [triangulated](wikipedia://en/1440976) [prime](wikipedia://en/490134) 3-manifolds.
- [Homology](wikipedia://en/1060236) and [Poincaré duality](wikipedia://en/540732) for 3-manifolds, including the [torsion linking form](wikipedia://en/540732).
- Includes portions of the [SnapPea kernel](wikipedia://en/3025251) for some geometric calculations.
- Has both a [GUI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GUI) and [Python](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Python_%28programming_language%29) interface.
