# Vladimir Buslaev

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Vladimir Buslaev"}

**Vladimir Savel'evich Buslaev** (Владимир Савельевич Буслаев, 19 April 1937, Leningrad[^o2] – 14 March 2012) was a Russian [mathematical physicist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_physicist).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Buslajew received his Ph.D. (Russian [candidate degree](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Candidate_of_sciences)) in 1963 from the [University of Leningrad](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University) under [Olga Ladyzhenskaya](wikipedia://en/1914268) with thesis *Short-Wave Asymptotics of Diffraction Problems in Convex Domains*. He was a professor at [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Leningrad).

## Contributions {#Contributions}

He did research on mathematical problems of [diffraction](wikipedia://en/8603) and the [WKB method](wikipedia://en/700154).[^o7]

## Recognition {#Recognition}

In 1963 he received the prize of the [Leningrad Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/9103238).

In 1983 he was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Invited_speaker_at_the_International_Congress_of_Mathematicians) in Warsaw and gave a talk *Regularization of many particle scattering*. He received an honorary doctorate from the [Université Paris Nord](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_13_University). In 2000 he received the State Prize of the Russian Federation and he was an Honoured Scientist of the Russian Federation. In 2000 he gave a plenary lecture (*Adiabatic perturbations of linear periodic problems*) at the annual meeting of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) in Dresden.[^o9]

He was awarded a 2000 [State Prize of the Russian Federation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/State_Prize_of_the_Russian_Federation) for "Asymptotic Methods of Studying the Equations of Mathematical Physics", along with Mikhail Karasev and Arlen Ilyin. (see list)
