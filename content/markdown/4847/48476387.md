# Brian White (mathematician)

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Brian White"}

**Brian Cabell White** is an American mathematician who specializes in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and [geometric measure theory](wikipedia://en/12169694). He is a professor of mathematics and a former chair of the mathematics department at [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University).[^o2]

:::

## Achievements {#Achievements}

He played a key role in the solution of the [double bubble conjecture](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double_bubble_conjecture), that the minimum-area enclosure of two volumes is formed from three spherical patches meeting in a circle and forming [dihedral angles](wikipedia://en/338046) of 2$\pi$/3 with each other, by proving that the optimal solution to this problem is necessarily a [surface of revolution](wikipedia://en/329549).[^o4]

White graduated from [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University) in 1977, as the top student in the sciences at Yale.[^o8] He earned his Ph.D. from [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1982, with a dissertation on [minimal surfaces](wikipedia://en/276734) supervised by [Frederick J. Almgren, Jr.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_J._Almgren,_Jr.) After postdoctoral research at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) of [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University), he became a faculty member at Stanford in 1983.[^o8]

## Awards {#Awards}

He was awarded a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1985,[^o7] and a [Guggenheim Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guggenheim_Fellowship) in 1999.[^o8] He was an [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) in 2002, speaking in the differential geometry section on the [curve-shortening flow](wikipedia://en/44089758) and [mean curvature flow](wikipedia://en/13110176).[^o9][^o10] In 2012, he was selected as one of the inaugural [fellows](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o11]
