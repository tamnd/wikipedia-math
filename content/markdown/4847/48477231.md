# Robert V. Kohn

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Robert V. Kohn"}

**Robert Vita Kohn** (October 5, 1953 – January 12, 2026) was an American mathematician who worked on [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), mathematical [materials science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Materials_science) and [mathematical finance](wikipedia://en/33589680). He was a professor at the [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences), [New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University).[^o3]

:::

## Background {#Background}

Kohn studied mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), obtaining his bachelor's degree in 1974. He obtained his Ph.D. at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) in 1979, as a student of [Frederick Almgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frederick_Almgren).[^o6]

Kohn died of cancer on January 12, 2026, at the age of 72.[^o7]

## Work {#Work}

Kohn is best known for his work on [non-linear partial differential equations](wikipedia://en/52564), including work with [Louis Nirenberg](wikipedia://en/1814725) and [Luis Caffarelli](wikipedia://en/2070173) in which they obtained partial results about the regularity of weak solutions of the [Navier–Stokes equations](wikipedia://en/48395).[^o9]

## Honors {#Honors}

Kohn received a [Sloan Research Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sloan_Research_Fellowship) in 1984.[^o11] In 2006, he was a plenary speaker at the [International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/627842), in Madrid (*Energy driven pattern formation*).[^o12] He was a member of the inaugural class of Fellows of the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM)[^o13]. He was a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o14] He was an elected member of [American Academy of Arts and Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Academy_of_Arts_and_Sciences).[^o15]
