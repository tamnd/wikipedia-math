# Stefan Güttel

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stefan Güttel"}

**Stefan Dietrich Güttel** (born 27 November 1981) is a German [numerical analyst](wikipedia://en/21506). He is Professor of [Applied Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Applied_Mathematics) in the [Department of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/10387568) at the [University of Manchester](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Manchester).

:::

Güttel was born in [Dresden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dresden), and was educated at the [Freiberg University of Mining and Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiberg_University_of_Mining_and_Technology), from which he gained his MSc in Applied Mathematics (2006) and PhD in Applied Mathematics (2010). His PhD thesis *Rational Krylov Methods for Operator Functions* was supervised by [Michael Eiermann](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michael_Eiermann). He worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the [University of Geneva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Geneva) (2010–2011) and the [University of Oxford](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oxford) (2011–2012). In 2012 he was appointed lecturer in mathematics at the University of Manchester, and later promoted to Senior Lecturer and Reader. In 2021 he was promoted to Professor of Applied Mathematics.[^o5]

Güttel is best known for his work on numerical algorithms for large-scale problems arising with differential equations and in data science, in particular [Krylov subspace](wikipedia://en/2542615) methods. He worked with companies such as [Intel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel), [Schlumberger](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Schlumberger), and [Arup](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arup_Group).

Since 2018 Güttel is a Fellow of the [Alan Turing Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Turing_Institute),[^o6] the United Kingdom's national institute for data science and artificial intelligence. In 2018 he received a Teaching Excellence Award of the University of Manchester. In 2021 he was awarded the [James H. Wilkinson Prize in Numerical Analysis and Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_H._Wilkinson_Prize_in_Numerical_Analysis_and_Scientific_Computing) by the [Society for Industrial and Applied Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1883376) (SIAM) for *his contributions to the analysis, implementation, and application of rational and block Krylov methods* (laudatio),[^o7] which have become popular for the efficient numerical solution of large eigenvalue problems, matrix equations, and in model order reduction. In 2023 he received the [Taussky–Todd Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ILAS_Taussky–Todd_Prize) of the [International Linear Algebra Society](wikipedia://en/63337149).[^o8] In 2026 he was elected a SIAM Fellow.[^o9]

Güttel has served as the elected Secretary and Treasurer of the UK and Republic of Ireland section of SIAM (2016–2018) and is a member of SIAM's membership committee (since 2020). He served on the editorial boards of the [SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIAM_Journal_on_Scientific_Computing) (2015–2021) and [Electronic Transactions on Numerical Analysis](wikipedia://en/53219445) (since 2020).[^o5]
