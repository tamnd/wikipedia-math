# Shanti S. Gupta

::: {#o5 .subject cue=lead name="Shanti S. Gupta"}

**Shanti Swarup Gupta** (January 25, 1925 – January 11, 2002) was an Indian-American statistician who served as the founding head of the department of statistics at [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University). He is best known for his pioneering work in ranking and selection theory, a branch of statistics used to determine the "best" populations among a group of candidates.[^o6]

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Gupta was born in Saunasi, Uttar Pradesh, India. He attended the [University of Delhi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Delhi) where he earned Bachelor of Arts and Master of Arts degrees in mathematics in 1943 and 1946, respectively.[^o8] He then enrolled at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill), where he completed his Ph.D. in statistics in 1956 under the supervision of [Raj Chandra Bose](wikipedia://en/1162266).[^o9] His dissertation, titled *On a Decision Rule for a Problem in Ranking Means*, laid the groundwork for his future research in selection procedures.

## Career {#Career}

After briefly working at [Bell Labs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bell_Labs), Gupta joined the faculty of [Purdue University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University) in 1962. At the time, statistics was part of the department of mathematics. In 1968, he became the founding head of Purdue's newly established department of statistics, a position he held until 1995.[^o11] During his tenure, he transformed the department into a globally recognized center for statistical research. He authored or co-authored over 200 research papers and several influential books, most notably the 1979 Wiley publication *Multiple Decision Procedures: Theory and Methodology of Selecting and Ranking Populations*.[^o8]

Shanti S. Gupta was a pivotal figure for the *[Journal of Statistical Planning and Inference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Journal_of_Statistical_Planning_and_Inference)*, serving as an editor from its founding in 1977, then later as editor-in-chief during the late 1990s up until late 2001.[^o12]

## Professional honors {#Professional_honors}

Gupta was an active leader in the international statistics community. His contributions were recognized through several prestigious appointments, including:

- 1968: elected as Fellow of the [Institute of Mathematical Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_of_Mathematical_Statistics), and from 1989 to 1990 its president.[^o14]
- 1970: elected as Fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).
- 1985: elected as Fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).
- 1988: [Erskine Fellowship](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Erskine_Fellowship) at the [University of Canterbury](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Canterbury).[^o15][^o16]

## Legacy {#Legacy}

In honor of his contributions, Purdue University established the *Shanti S. Gupta Distinguished Professorship* in Statistics. He is credited with mentoring over 30 doctoral students, many of whom became prominent statisticians themselves.[^o18]
