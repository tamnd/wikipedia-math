# Uniformly hyperfinite algebra

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Uniformly hyperfinite algebra"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), particularly in the theory of [C\*-algebras](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/C*-algebras), a **uniformly hyperfinite**, or **UHF**, algebra is a C\*-algebra that can be written as the closure, in the [norm topology](wikipedia://en/246722), of an increasing union of finite-dimensional full [matrix algebras](wikipedia://en/487541).

:::

::: {#o3 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A UHF C\*-algebra is the [direct limit](wikipedia://en/211566) of an inductive system {*A<sub>n</sub>*, *φ<sub>n</sub>*} where each *A<sub>n</sub>* is a finite-dimensional full matrix algebra and each *φ<sub>n</sub>* : *A<sub>n</sub>* → *A*<sub>*n*+1</sub> is a unital embedding. Suppressing the connecting maps, one can write

$$A = \overline {\cup_n A_n}.$$

:::

## Classification {#Classification}

If

$$A_n \simeq M_{k_n} (\mathbb C),$$

then *rk*<sub>*n*</sub> = *k*<sub>*n* + 1</sub> for some integer *r* and

$$\phi_n (a) = a \otimes I_r,$$

where *I<sub>r</sub>* is the identity in the *r* × *r* matrices. The sequence ...*k<sub>n</sub>*|*k*<sub>*n* + 1</sub>|*k*<sub>*n* + 2</sub>... determines a formal product

$$\delta(A) = \prod_p p^{t_p}$$

where each *p* is prime and *t<sub>p</sub>* = sup {*m* | *p<sup>m</sup>* divides *k<sub>n</sub>* for some *n*}, possibly zero or infinite. The formal product *δ*(*A*) is said to be the [supernatural number](wikipedia://en/18668810) corresponding to *A*.[^o5] [Glimm](wikipedia://en/6901985) showed that the supernatural number is a complete invariant of UHF C\*-algebras.[^o6] In particular, there are uncountably many isomorphism classes of UHF C\*-algebras.

If *δ*(*A*) is finite, then *A* is the full matrix algebra *M*<sub>*δ*(*A*)</sub>. A UHF algebra is said to be of **infinite type** if each *t<sub>p</sub>* in *δ*(*A*) is 0 or ∞.

In the language of [K-theory](wikipedia://en/246748), each [supernatural number](wikipedia://en/18668810)

$$\delta(A) = \prod_p p^{t_p}$$

specifies an additive subgroup of **Q** that is the rational numbers of the type *n*/*m* where *m* formally divides *δ*(*A*). This group is the [*K*<sub>0</sub> group](wikipedia://en/13314265) of *A*. [^o5]

## CAR algebra {#CAR_algebra}

One example of a UHF C\*-algebra is the [CAR algebra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CAR_algebra). It is defined as follows: let *H* be a separable complex Hilbert space *H* with orthonormal basis *f<sub>n</sub>* and *L*(*H*) the bounded operators on *H*, consider a linear map

$$\alpha : H \rightarrow L(H)$$

with the property that

$$\{ \alpha(f_n), \alpha(f_m) \} = 0 \quad \mbox{and} \quad \alpha(f_n)^*\alpha(f_m) + \alpha(f_m)\alpha(f_n)^* = \langle f_m, f_n \rangle I.$$

The CAR algebra is the C\*-algebra generated by

$$\{ \alpha(f_n) \}\;.$$

The embedding

$$C^*(\alpha(f_1), \cdots, \alpha(f_n)) \hookrightarrow C^*(\alpha(f_1), \cdots, \alpha(f_{n+1}))$$

can be identified with the multiplicity 2 embedding

$$M_{2^n} \hookrightarrow M_{2^{n+1}}.$$

Therefore, the CAR algebra has supernatural number 2<sup>∞</sup>.[^o8] This identification also yields that its *K*<sub>0</sub> group is the [dyadic rationals](wikipedia://en/56263).
