# Peter Friz

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Peter Friz"}

**Peter K. Friz** (born 1974 in [Klagenfurt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Klagenfurt)) is a mathematician working in the fields of [partial differential equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Partial_differential_equations), [quantitative finance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantitative_finance), and [stochastic analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stochastic_analysis).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

He studied at the [Vienna University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Technology), [Ecole Centrale Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ecole_Centrale_Paris), [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge) and [Courant Institute of Mathematical Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute_of_Mathematical_Sciences) ([New York University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_University)), and obtained his PhD in 2004 under the supervision of [S. R. Srinivasa Varadhan](wikipedia://en/9638038).

He worked as a quantitative associate at [Merrill Lynch](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Merrill_Lynch), then held academic positions at the [University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Cambridge), and the Radon Institute. Since 2009, he is full professor at [Technische Universität Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technische_Universität_Berlin), and associated with the [Weierstrass Institute for Applied Analysis and Stochastics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weierstrass_Institute_for_Applied_Analysis_and_Stochastics) in Berlin.

In 2010 he has been awarded an [ERC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Research_Council) Starting Grant.[^o4] In 2016 he has been awarded an ERC Consolidator Grant.[^o5]
