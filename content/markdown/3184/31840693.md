# Martin T. Barlow

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Martin T. Barlow"}

*Image: Martin T. Barlow Oberwolfach 2016.jpg.* Barlow at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematisches_Forschungsinstitut_Oberwolfach) in 2016

**Martin Thomas Barlow** [FRS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society#Fellows) [FRSC](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Canada) (born 16 June 1953 in [London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London)) is a British [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) who is professor of mathematics at the [University of British Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_British_Columbia) in [Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Canada) since 1992.[^o4]

:::

## History {#History}

Barlow is the son of Andrew Dalmahoy Barlow (1916–2006) and his wife Yvonne.[^o3] He is thus the grandson of [Alan Barlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Barlow), and his wife [Nora (née Darwin)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nora_Barlow), through whom he is a great-great-grandson of [Charles Darwin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Darwin). He is the nephew of [Horace Barlow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Horace_Barlow) (also FRS and Fellow of Trinity). In 1994 he married Colleen McLaughlin.[^o3]

He was educated [Sussex House School](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sussex_House_School), [St Paul's School, London](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/St_Paul's_School,_London), [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge) (BA 1975, Diploma 1976, ScD 1993); [University College of Swansea](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_College_of_Swansea) (PhD).[^o4][^o3]

Barlow worked as a research fellow of the [University of Liverpool](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Liverpool) 1978–1980. He was a Fellow of [Trinity College, Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Trinity_College,_Cambridge), 1979–1992. He worked in the [Statistical Laboratory, University of Cambridge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_Laboratory,_University_of_Cambridge) 1981–1985 and was a Royal Society University Research Fellow 1985–1992.[^o4]

## Work {#Work}

His mathematical interests include [probability](wikipedia://en/22934), [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) and [fractal sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fractal_sets).

His doctoral students include [Steven N. Evans](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steven_Neil_Evans).

## Recognition {#Recognition}

He was awarded the [Rollo Davidson Prize](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rollo_Davidson_Prize) in 1984.[^o4][^o8] He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society of Canada](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society_of_Canada) in 1998.[^o4] He was elected a [Fellow of the Royal Society](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow_of_the_Royal_Society) in 2005.[^o9] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10] In 2018 the [Canadian Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/906055) listed him in their inaugural class of fellows.[^o11]
