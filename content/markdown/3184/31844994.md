# StatCounter

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="StatCounter"}

**StatCounter** is an Irish [web traffic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Web_traffic) analysis website started in 1999.[^o2] Access to basic services is free to use and advanced services can cost between 5 and US\$119 a month.[^o4] StatCounter is based in [Dublin, Ireland](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dublin,_Ireland). The statistics from StatCounter are used to compute web usage share for example.[^o5] , StatCounter is used on 0.9% of all websites.[^o6]

:::

StatCounter statistics are directly derived from [hits](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hit_%28Internet%29)—as opposed to [unique visitors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unique_user)—from 3 million sites, which use StatCounter, resulting in total hits of more than 15 billion per month.[^o7] No artificial weightings are used to correct for [sampling bias](wikipedia://en/17692), thus the numbers in the statistics can not be considered to be [representative samples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Representative_sample).

The company was founded by Aodhán Cullen at the age of 16. Cullen received the "Internet Hero" award at the 2008 Eircom [Golden Spider Awards](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Golden_Spider_Awards).[^o8][^o9] He was also named the 2007 *[BusinessWeek](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomberg_Businessweek)* "Young European Entrepreneur of the Year".[^o10][^o11]
