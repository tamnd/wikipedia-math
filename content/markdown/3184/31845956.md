::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Stericated 6-cubes"}

In six-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **stericated 6-cube** is a convex [uniform 6-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821728), constructed as a [sterication](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sterication) (4th order truncation) of the regular [6-cube](wikipedia://en/10083462).

:::

There are 8 unique sterications for the 6-cube with permutations of truncations, cantellations, and runcinations.
