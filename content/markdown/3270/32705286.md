# Christophe Soulé

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Christophe Soulé"}

**Christophe Soulé** (born 1951) is a French mathematician working in [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177).

:::

## Education {#Education}

Soulé started his studies in 1970 at [École Normale Supérieure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Normale_Supérieure) in [Paris](wikipedia://en/22989). He completed his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) at the [University of Paris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Paris) in 1979 under the supervision of [Max Karoubi](wikipedia://en/30942178) and [Roger Godement](wikipedia://en/971810), with a dissertation titled *K-Théorie des anneaux d'entiers de corps de nombres et cohomologie étale*.

## Awards and recognition {#Awards_and_recognition}

In 1979, he was awarded a [CNRS Bronze Medal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CNRS_Bronze_Medal). He received the [Prix J. Ponti](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prix_J._Ponti) in 1985 and the [Prize Ampère](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prize_Ampère) in 1993.[^o5]

Since 2001, he is member of the [French Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/French_Academy_of_Sciences).[^o6] In 1983, he was [invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematicians](wikipedia://en/47738065) (ICM) in Warsaw.[^o7]
