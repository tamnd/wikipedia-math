# Q-expansion principle

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Q-expansion principle"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the ***q*-expansion principle** states that a [modular form](wikipedia://en/286000) *f* has coefficients in a [module](wikipedia://en/276410) *M* if its *q*-expansion at enough [cusps](wikipedia://en/6851758) resembles the *q*-expansion of a modular form *g* with coefficients in *M*. It was introduced by (txt, corollaries 1.6.2, 1.12.2).

:::
