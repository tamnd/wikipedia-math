# Gelfand–Zeitlin integrable system

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Gelfand–Zeitlin integrable system"}

In mathematics, the **Gelfand–Zeitlin system** (also written **Gelfand–Zetlin system**, **Gelfand–Cetlin system**, **Gelfand–Tsetlin system**) is an [integrable system](wikipedia://en/2573213) on conjugacy classes of Hermitian matrices. It was introduced by (txt), who named it after the [Gelfand–Zeitlin basis](wikipedia://en/880754#Gelfand–Tsetlin_basis), an early example of [canonical basis](wikipedia://en/1028158), introduced by [Israel Gelfand](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I._M._Gelfand) and [Michael Tsetlin](wikipedia://en/37832091) in 1950s. (txt) introduced a complex version of this integrable system.

:::
