# Eighth power

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Eighth power"}

In [arithmetic](wikipedia://en/3118) and [algebra](wikipedia://en/18716923), the **eighth power** of a number *n* is the result of multiplying eight instances of *n* together. So:

:::

$$n^{8} = n \times n \times n \times n \times n \times n \times n \times n.$$

Eighth [powers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Power_%28algebra%29) are also formed by multiplying a number by its [seventh power](wikipedia://en/54558969), or the [fourth power](wikipedia://en/665027) of a number by itself.

The sequence of eighth powers of [integer](wikipedia://en/14563) is:

0, 1, 256, 6561, 65536, 390625, 1679616, 5764801, 16777216, 43046721, 100000000, 214358881, 429981696, 815730721, 1475789056, 2562890625, 4294967296, 6975757441, 11019960576, 16983563041, 25600000000, 37822859361, 54875873536, 78310985281, 110075314176, 152587890625 ...

In the [archaic notation](wikipedia://en/31228704) of [Robert Recorde](wikipedia://en/310247), the eighth power of a number was called the "[zenzizenzizenzic](wikipedia://en/499002)".[^o2]

## Algebra and number theory {#Algebra_and_number_theory}

[Polynomial](wikipedia://en/23000) equations of [degree](wikipedia://en/5930652) 8 are [octic equations](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octic_equation). These have the form

$$ax^8+bx^7+cx^6+dx^5+ex^4+fx^3+gx^2+hx+k=0.\,$$

The smallest known eighth power that can be written as a sum of eight eighth powers is[^o4]

$$1409^8 = 1324^8 + 1190^8 + 1088^8 + 748^8 + 524^8 + 478^8 + 223^8 + 90^8.$$

The sum of the reciprocals of the nonzero eighth powers is the [Riemann zeta function](wikipedia://en/25809) evaluated at 8, which can be expressed in terms of the eighth power of [pi](wikipedia://en/23601):

$\zeta(8) = \frac{1}{1^8} + \frac{1}{2^8} + \frac{1}{3^8} + \cdots = \frac{\pi^8}{9450} = 1.00407 \dots$ ()

This is an example of a more general expression for [evaluating the Riemann zeta function at positive even integers](wikipedia://en/2334098), in terms of the [Bernoulli numbers](wikipedia://en/4964):

$$\zeta(2n) = (-1)^{n+1}\frac{B_{2n}(2\pi)^{2n}}{2(2n)!}.$$

## Physics {#Physics}

In [aeroacoustics](wikipedia://en/2518272), [Lighthill's eighth power law](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lighthill's_eighth_power_law) states that the power of the sound created by a turbulent motion, far from the turbulence, is proportional to the eighth power of the characteristic turbulent velocity.[^o6][^o7]

The ordered phase of the two-dimensional [Ising model](wikipedia://en/292744) exhibits an inverse eighth power dependence of the [order parameter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_parameter) upon the [reduced temperature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reduced_temperature).[^o8]

The [Casimir–Polder force](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Casimir–Polder_force) between two molecules decays as the inverse eighth power of the distance between them.[^o9][^o10]
