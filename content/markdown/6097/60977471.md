# Sealedenvelope.com

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Sealedenvelope.com"}

**Sealedenvelope.com** is British collaboration that provides support services for [clinical trials](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_trials). They provide services such as [randomization](wikipedia://en/65462), [allocation concealment](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Allocation_concealment), [code-break services](wikipedia://en/60571575), and case report management through a web-based design. They also perform certain calculations such as [power](wikipedia://en/238695) calculations.[^o2]

:::
