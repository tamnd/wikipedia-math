# Xuejia Lai

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Xuejia Lai"}

**Xuejia Lai** () is a cryptographer, currently a professor at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shanghai_Jiao_Tong_University). His notable work includes the design of the [block cipher](wikipedia://en/4594) [IDEA](wikipedia://en/15076) based on the [Lai-Massey scheme](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lai-Massey_scheme), the theory of [Markov ciphers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Markov_cipher), and the [cryptanalysis](wikipedia://en/5715) of a number of [cryptographic hash functions](wikipedia://en/439526). His book *On the Design and Security of Block Ciphers* () is frequently cited in cryptography papers.

:::

Lai received a [B.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B.Sc.) in [electrical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineering) in 1982 and an [M.Sc.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/M.Sc.) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831) in 1984 at [Northwest Institute of Telecommunication Engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwest_Institute_of_Telecommunication_Engineering). About 1982 he met [James Massey](wikipedia://en/9543907), who was visiting the university to give lectures in cryptography. Lai served as interpreter for these lectures. He later became one of Massey's doctoral students at [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich), where he received his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) in 1992. Together they designed the IDEA cipher.
