# Leonida Tonelli

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Leonida Tonelli"}

**Leonida Tonelli** (19 April 1885 – 12 March 1946) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), noted for proving [Tonelli's theorem](wikipedia://en/255245#Tonelli's_theorem_for_non-negative_measurable_functions), a variation of [Fubini's theorem](wikipedia://en/255245), and for introducing [semicontinuity methods](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Semicontinuity) as a common tool for the [direct method in the calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/24499402).[^o3]

:::

## Education {#Education}

Tonelli graduated from the [University of Bologna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Bologna) in 1907; his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) thesis was written under the direction of [Cesare Arzelà](wikipedia://en/1683941).
