# Kirsten Wickelgren

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kirsten Wickelgren"}

**Kirsten Graham Wickelgren** is an American mathematician whose research interests range over multiple areas including [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997), [algebraic topology](wikipedia://en/38801), [arithmetic geometry](wikipedia://en/1973177), and [anabelian geometry](wikipedia://en/27119265). She is a professor of mathematics at [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Wickelgren was one of the finalists in the 1999 [Intel Science Talent Search](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Intel_Science_Talent_Search). She majored in mathematics at [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), graduating [magna cum laude](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magna_cum_laude) in 2003. After a year at the [École normale supérieure (Paris)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_normale_supérieure_%28Paris%29), she went to [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University) for doctoral study in mathematics, completing her Ph.D. in 2009. Her dissertation, *Lower Central Series Obstructions To Homotopy Sections of Curves Over Number Fields*, was supervised by [Gunnar Carlsson](wikipedia://en/35054518).

She returned to Harvard as a five-year postdoctoral research fellow, funded by the [American Institute of Mathematics](wikipedia://en/3248005), and in 2013 became an assistant professor at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech). In 2018 she was tenured as associate professor there, and in 2019 she moved to [Duke University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duke_University) as a full professor.

In 2017, Wickelgren and Jesse Kass used motivic homotopy theory to count the lines on a smooth cubic surface,[^o4] generalizing known results in [enumerative geometry](wikipedia://en/3069092), and stimulating[^o5] new research in the field.

## Recognition {#Recognition}

Wickelgren was named to the 2023 class of Fellows of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), "for contributions to algebraic topology, algebraic geometry, and number theory".

## Family {#Family}

Wickelgren is daughter of psychologists [Norma Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norma_Graham) and [Wayne Wickelgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wickelphone), sister of physicist [Peter W. Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peter_W._Graham), and half-sister of lawyer [Abraham Wickelgren](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abraham_Wickelgren). She is granddaughter of psychologist [Frances K. Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frances_K._Graham) and great-granddaughter of surgeon [Evarts Ambrose Graham](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evarts_Ambrose_Graham).
