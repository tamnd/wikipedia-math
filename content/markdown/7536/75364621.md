# Johannes Ledolter

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Johannes Ledolter"}

*Image: Ledolter-johannes.jpg.* Johannes Ledolter

**Johannes Ledolter** (August 26, 1950 – November 5, 2023)[^o2] was an American applied [statistician](wikipedia://en/48661). He was a chaired Professor of [Business Analytics](wikipedia://en/10147369) and Statistics/Actuarial Science at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa) as well as an emeritus Professor at the [Vienna University of Economics and Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vienna_University_of_Economics_and_Business) in Austria, and served as an associate investigator for the [Iowa City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_City,_Iowa) VA Health Care System.

:::

Ledolter was initially brought to the United States by the Fulbright Scholarship. He authored 10 books and more than 150 journal articles and chapters.

## Education {#Education}

Ledolter earned a [M.S.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Master_of_Science) in statistics from the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison) in 1972[^o4] and in Social and Economic Statistics from the [University of Vienna](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Vienna) in 1974.[^o4][^o5] In 1975, he earned a [PhD](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_of_Philosophy) in statistics from the [University of Wisconsin-Madison](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Wisconsin–Madison).

## Teaching and consulting {#Teaching_and_consulting}

Ledolter taught beginning and advanced courses on statistical methods at the University of Iowa, Vienna University of Economics and Business, [Institute for Advanced Studies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Institute_for_Advanced_Studies_%28Vienna%29), [Stanford University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stanford_University), [University of Chicago Booth School of Business](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Chicago_Booth_School_of_Business), [Yale University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yale_University), and [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University).[^o7][^o4][^o8]

He had consulted with the [state of California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California), [Ford Motor Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ford_Motor_Company), [Procter & Gamble](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Procter_&_Gamble), [Celanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Celanese), [American Express](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Express), [Bemis Company](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bemis_Company), and the [A1 Telekom Austria Group](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A1_Telekom_Austria_Group).[^o4]
