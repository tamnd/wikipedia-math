# Rational reciprocity law

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Rational reciprocity law"}

In number theory, a **rational reciprocity law** is a [reciprocity law](wikipedia://en/247622) involving residue symbols that are related by a factor of +1 or –1 rather than a general root of unity.

:::

As an example, there are rational [biquadratic](wikipedia://en/20131133) and [octic reciprocity laws](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Octic_reciprocity_law). Define the symbol (*x*|*p*)<sub>*k*</sub> to be +1 if *x* is a *k*-th power modulo the prime *p* and -1 otherwise.

Let *p* and *q* be distinct primes congruent to 1 modulo 4, such that (*p*|*q*)<sub>2</sub> = (*q*|*p*)<sub>2</sub> = +1. Let *p* = *a*<sup>2</sup> + *b*<sup>2</sup> and *q* = *A*<sup>2</sup> + *B*<sup>2</sup> with *aA* odd. Then

$$(p|q)_4 (q|p)_4 = (-1)^{(q-1)/4} (aB-bA|q)_2 \ .$$

If in addition *p* and *q* are congruent to 1 modulo 8, let *p* = *c*<sup>2</sup> + 2*d*<sup>2</sup> and *q* = *C*<sup>2</sup> + 2*D*<sup>2</sup>. Then

$$(p|q)_8 = (q|p)_8 = (aB-bA|q)_4 (cD-dC|q)_2 \ .$$
