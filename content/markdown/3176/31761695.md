::: {#o4 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified 8-cubes"}

In eight-dimensional [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), a **rectified 8-cube** is a convex [uniform 8-polytope](wikipedia://en/6821715), being a [rectification](wikipedia://en/2192366) of the regular [8-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cube).

:::

There are unique 8 degrees of rectifications, the zeroth being the [8-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-cube), and the 7th and last being the [8-orthoplex](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/8-orthoplex). Vertices of the rectified 8-cube are located at the edge-centers of the 8-cube. Vertices of the *birectified 8-cube* are located in the square face centers of the 8-cube. Vertices of the *trirectified 8-cube* are located in the [7-cube](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/7-cube) cell centers of the 8-cube.
