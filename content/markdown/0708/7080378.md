# Kolmogorov continuity theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Kolmogorov continuity theorem"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Kolmogorov continuity theorem** is a [theorem](wikipedia://en/30977) that guarantees that a [stochastic process](wikipedia://en/47895) that satisfies certain constraints on the [moments](wikipedia://en/368684) of its increments will be continuous (or, more precisely, have a "continuous version"). It is credited to the [Soviet](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Soviet_Union) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) [Andrey Nikolaevich Kolmogorov](wikipedia://en/91161).

:::

::: {#o3 .statement cue=heading name="Statement"}

## Statement {#Statement}

Let $(S,d)$ be some complete separable metric space, and let $X\colon [0, + \infty) \times \Omega \to S$ be a stochastic process. Suppose that for all times $T > 0$, there exist positive constants $\alpha, \beta, K$ such that

$$\mathbb{E} [d(X_t, X_s)^\alpha] \leq K | t - s |^{1 + \beta}$$

for all $0 \leq s, t \leq T$. Then there exists a modification $\tilde{X}$ of $X$ that is a continuous process, i.e. a process $\tilde{X}\colon [0, + \infty) \times \Omega \to S$ such that

- $\tilde{X}$ is [sample-continuous](wikipedia://en/6673551);
- for every time $t \geq 0$, $\mathbb{P} (X_t = \tilde{X}_t) = 1.$

Furthermore, the paths of $\tilde{X}$ are locally [$\gamma$-Hölder-continuous](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hölder_continuity) for every $0<\gamma<\tfrac\beta\alpha$.

:::

::: {#o5 .example cue=heading name="Example"}

## Example {#Example}

In the case of [Brownian motion](wikipedia://en/4436) on $\mathbb{R}^n$, the choice of constants $\alpha = 4$, $\beta = 1$, $K = n (n + 2)$ will work in the Kolmogorov continuity theorem. Moreover, for any positive integer $m$, the constants $\alpha = 2m$, $\beta = m-1$ will work, for some positive value of $K$ that depends on $n$ and $m$.

:::
