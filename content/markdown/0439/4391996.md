# List of U.S. states and territories by GDP

*Image: GDP by U.S. state 2024.png.* The GDP of each U.S. state and the District of Columbia in 2024 according to the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis

*Image: GDP per capita by U.S. state.svg.* The GDP per capita of each U.S. state and the District of Columbia in 2021 according to the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis and the U.S. Census Bureau

*Image: Real GDP Growth Rate by U.S. state.svg.* Real GDP growth rate by U.S. state in 2021 according to the U.S. Bureau of Economic Analysis

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of U.S. states and territories by GDP"}

This is a list of [U.S. states](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._state) and [territories](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Territories_of_the_United_States) by [gross domestic product](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gross_domestic_product) (GDP). This article presents the 50 U.S. states and the [District of Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington,_D.C.) and their nominal GDP at current prices.

:::

The data source for the list is the [Bureau of Economic Analysis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bureau_of_Economic_Analysis) (BEA) in 2025. The BEA defined GDP by state as "the sum of [value added](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Value_added) from all [industries](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Industry_%28economics%29) in the state."[^o2]

Overall, in the calendar year 2025, the United States' Nominal GDP at Current Prices totaled at \$30.762 trillion, as compared to \$29.298 trillion in 2024.

The three U.S. states with the highest GDPs were [California](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/California) (\$4.251 trillion), [Texas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Texas) (\$2.904 trillion), and [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29) (\$2.468 trillion). The three U.S. states with the lowest GDPs were [Vermont](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vermont) (\$48.350 billion), [Wyoming](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wyoming) (\$52.622 billion), and [Alaska](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alaska) (\$75.012 billion).

[GDP per capita](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/GDP_per_capita) also varied widely throughout the United States in 2025, with [New York](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_%28state%29) (\$123,369), [Massachusetts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts) (\$114,635), and [Washington (state)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Washington_%28state%29) (\$111,860) recording the three highest GDP per capita figures in the U.S., while [Mississippi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mississippi) (\$55,877), [West Virginia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/West_Virginia) (\$61,873), and [Arkansas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arkansas) (\$63,703) recorded the three lowest GDP per capita figures in the U.S. The [District of Columbia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/District_of_Columbia), though, recorded a GDP per capita figure far higher than any U.S. state in 2025 at \$277,689.
