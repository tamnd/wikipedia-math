# Julia Pevtsova

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Julia Pevtsova"}

**Julia Pevtsova** is a Russian-American mathematician who works as a professor of mathematics at the [University of Washington](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Washington). Her research concerns [representation theory](wikipedia://en/19378200) and in particular [modular representation theory](wikipedia://en/1110685).

:::

Pevtsova competed for Russia in the 1992 [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208), earning a silver medal. She earned a bachelor's degree in 1997 from [Saint Petersburg State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg_State_University), and completed her doctorate in 2002 at [Northwestern University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northwestern_University), under the supervision of [Eric Friedlander](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eric_Friedlander). After postdoctoral studies at the [University of Oregon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Oregon), she joined the University of Washington in 2005.

In 2017, she became a [fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822) "for contributions to modular representation theory". In 2018 she won the distinguished teaching award of the Pacific Northwest Section of the [Mathematical Association of America](wikipedia://en/198772). The award cited her work teaching problem-solving to undergraduates in preparation for the [William Lowell Putnam Mathematical Competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Lowell_Putnam_Mathematical_Competition) and her leadership of [math circles](wikipedia://en/5163815) and other activities for local secondary-school students.
