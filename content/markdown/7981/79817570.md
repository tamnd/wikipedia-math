# Cisinski model structure

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Cisinski model structure"}

In [higher category theory](wikipedia://en/5274013) in [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), a **Cisinski model structure** is a special kind of [model structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_structure) on [Grothendieck topoi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Grothendieck_topos). In homotopical algebra, the [category of simplicial sets](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Category_of_simplicial_sets) is of particular interest. Cisinski model structures are named after [Denis-Charles Cisinski](wikipedia://en/78103543), who introduced them in 2001. His work is based on unfinished ideas presented by Alexander Grothendieck in his script *[Pursuing Stacks](wikipedia://en/40783144)* from 1983.[^o2]

:::

::: {#o4 .definition cue=heading name="Definition"}

## Definition {#Definition}

A cofibrantly generated model structure on a topos, for the cofibrations are exactly the monomorphisms, is called a *Cisinski model structure*. Cofibrantly generated means that there are small sets $I$ and $J$ of morphisms, on which the [small object argument](wikipedia://en/79556759) can be applied, so that they generate all cofibrations and trivial cofibrations using the [lifting property](wikipedia://en/53659730):[^o5]

$$\operatorname{Cofib} ={}^\perp(I^\perp);$$

$$W\cap\operatorname{Cofib} ={}^\perp(J^\perp);$$

More generally, a small set generating the class of monomorphisms of a category of [presheaves](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Presheaves) is called *cellular model*:[^o6][^o7]

$$\operatorname{Mono} ={}^\perp(I^\perp).$$

Every topos admits a cellular model.[^o8]

:::

::: {#o10 .example cue=heading name="Examples"}

## Examples {#Examples}

- [Joyal model structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joyal_model_structure): Cofibrations (monomorphisms) are generated by the boundary inclusions $\partial\Delta^n\hookrightarrow\Delta^n$ and acyclic cofibrations (inner anodyne extensions) are generated by inner horn inclusions $\Lambda_k^n\hookrightarrow\Delta^n$ (with $n\geq 2$ and $0<k<n$).[^o11][^o12]
- [Kan–Quillen model structure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kan–Quillen_model_structure): Cofibrations (monomorphisms) are generated by the boundary inclusions $\partial\Delta^n\hookrightarrow\Delta^n$ and acyclic cofibrations (anodyne extensions) are generated by horn inclusions $\Lambda_k^n\hookrightarrow\Delta^n$ (with $n\geq 2$ and $0\leq k\leq n$).[^o11]

:::
