# Holly Shulman

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Holly Shulman"}

**Holly B. Shulman** is an American statistician in the Division of Reproductive Health of the [Centers for Disease Control and Prevention](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Centers_for_Disease_Control_and_Prevention) (CDC), a developer of the Pregnancy Risk Assessment Monitoring System for the CDC, and the former president of the [Caucus for Women in Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucus_for_Women_in_Statistics). As well as her work on the Pregnancy Risk Assessment Monitoring System, her publications include highly cited work on [abortion](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Abortion)-related deaths.

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Shulman is originally from [Atlanta, Georgia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Atlanta,_Georgia), where she was an honors student in mathematics. Her interest in statistics began in a high school mathematics summer program, part of the Georgia Governor's Honors Program. She majored in mathematics at [Georgia Tech](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Georgia_Tech), and went on to do a master's degree in statistics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley).

## Career and later life {#Career_and_later_life}

Shulman has worked for the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention for over 35 years. She was president of the [Caucus for Women in Statistics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caucus_for_Women_in_Statistics) for 1999, and chaired the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988)'s Committee on Women in Statistics in 2000.
