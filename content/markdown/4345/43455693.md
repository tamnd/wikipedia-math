::: {#o8 .subject cue=lead name="Expanded cuboctahedron"}

The **expanded cuboctahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470) constructed by [expansion](wikipedia://en/6341070) of the [cuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/6280). It has 50 faces: 8 triangles, 30 squares, and 12 rhombs. The 48 vertices exist at two sets of 24, with a slightly different distance from its center.

:::

It can also be constructed as a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366) [rhombicuboctahedron](wikipedia://en/26213).

## Dissection {#Dissection}

| Edges | 168 |

| [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) | \-20 |
| [genus](wikipedia://en/61891) | 11 |
| [Symmetry group](wikipedia://en/2871265) | *O*<sub>*h*</sub>, \[4,3\], (\*432) order 48 |

:::

This polyhedron can be dissected into a central [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631) surrounded by: 12 [rhombic prisms](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rhombic_prism), 8 [tetrahedra](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tetrahedra), 6 [square pyramids](wikipedia://en/1128455), and 24 [triangular prisms](wikipedia://en/2859584).

If the central rhombic dodecahedron and the 12 rhombic prisms are removed, you can create a [toroidal polyhedron](wikipedia://en/24804975) with all regular polygon faces.[^o17] This toroid has 86 faces (8 triangles and 78 squares), 168 edges, and 62 vertices. 14 of the 62 vertices are on the interior, defining the removed central [rhombic dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/607631). With [Euler characteristic](wikipedia://en/148420) χ = f + v - e = -20, its [genus](wikipedia://en/61891), g = (2-χ)/2 is 11.

*Image: Excavated\_expanded\_cuboctahedron.png.*
