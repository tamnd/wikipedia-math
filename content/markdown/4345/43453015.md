::: {#o7 .subject cue=lead name="Truncated rhombicosidodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **truncated rhombicosidodecahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed as a [truncated](wikipedia://en/4224429) [rhombicosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/285486). It has 122 faces: 12 decagons, 30 octagons, 20 hexagons, and 60 squares.

:::

## Other names {#Other_names}

- Truncated small rhombicosidodecahedron
- Beveled icosidodecahedron

## Zonohedron {#Zonohedron}

As a [zonohedron](wikipedia://en/669402), it can be constructed with all but 30 octagons as [regular polygons](wikipedia://en/333306). It is 2-uniform, with 2 sets of 120 vertices existing on two distances from its center.

This polyhedron represents the [Minkowski sum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minkowski_sum) of a [truncated icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534), and a [rhombic triacontahedron](wikipedia://en/665025).[^o10]

## Related polyhedra {#Related_polyhedra}

The [truncated icosidodecahedron](wikipedia://en/286534) is similar, with all regular faces, and 4.6.10 [vertex figure](wikipedia://en/1005719).
