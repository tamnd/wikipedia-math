# Rectified truncated tetrahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified truncated tetrahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rectified truncated tetrahedron** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed as a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366), [truncated tetrahedron](wikipedia://en/279651). It has 20 faces: 4 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), 12 [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428), and 4 [regular hexagons](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Regular_hexagon).

:::

Topologically, the triangles corresponding to the tetrahedron's vertices are always equilateral, although the hexagons, while having equal edge lengths, do not have the same edge lengths with the equilateral triangles, having different but alternating angles, causing the other triangles to be [isosceles](wikipedia://en/315428) instead.
