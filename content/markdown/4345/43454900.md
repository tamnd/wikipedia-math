# Rectified truncated cube

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified truncated cube"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rectified truncated cube** is a [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed as a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366), [truncated cube](wikipedia://en/280529). It has 38 faces: 8 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), 24 [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428), and 6 [octagons](wikipedia://en/314575).

:::

Topologically, the triangles corresponding to the cube's vertices are always equilateral, although the octagons, while having equal edge lengths, do not have the same edge lengths with the equilateral triangles, having different but alternating angles, causing the other triangles to be [isosceles](wikipedia://en/315428) instead.
