# Rectified truncated dodecahedron

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Rectified truncated dodecahedron"}

In [geometry](wikipedia://en/18973446), the **rectified truncated dodecahedron** is a convex [polyhedron](wikipedia://en/23470), constructed as a [rectified](wikipedia://en/2192366), [truncated dodecahedron](wikipedia://en/376538). It has 92 faces: 20 [equilateral triangles](wikipedia://en/173285), 60 [isosceles triangles](wikipedia://en/315428), and 12 [decagons](wikipedia://en/327393).

:::

Topologically, the triangles corresponding to the dodecahedrons's vertices are always equilateral, although the decagons, while having equal edge lengths, do not have the same edge lengths with the equilateral triangles, having different but alternating angles, causing the other triangles to be [isosceles](wikipedia://en/315428) instead.
