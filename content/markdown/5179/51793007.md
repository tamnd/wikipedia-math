# Myles Tierney

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Myles Tierney"}

**Myles Tierney** (September 1937 – 5 October 2017[^o2]) was an American [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and Professor at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) who founded the theory of elementary [toposes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toposes) with [William Lawvere](wikipedia://en/872760).

:::

Tierney obtained his [B.A.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bachelor_of_Arts) from [Brown University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brown_University) in 1959 and his [Ph.D.](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ph.D.) from [Columbia University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Columbia_University) in 1965. His dissertation, *On the classifying spaces for K-Theory mod p*, was written under the supervision of [Samuel Eilenberg](wikipedia://en/362501). Following positions at [Rice University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rice_University) (1965–66) and [ETH Zurich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zurich) (1966–68), he became an associate professor at Rutgers in 1968.

Tierney was named a [Fellow](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fellow) of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o3]
