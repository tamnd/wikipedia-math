# Nikolay Korobov

::: {#o3 .subject cue=lead name="Nikolay Korobov"}

**Nikolai Mikhailovich Korobov** (Коробов Николай Михайлович; November 23, 1917 – October 25, 2004) was a Soviet [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) specializing in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) and [numerical analysis](wikipedia://en/21506). He is best known for his work in [analytic number theory](wikipedia://en/251513), especially in [exponential](wikipedia://en/468937) and [trigonometric sums](wikipedia://en/59038).

:::
