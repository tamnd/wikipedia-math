# Agnieszka Malinowska

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Agnieszka Malinowska"}

**Agnieszka Barbara Malinowska** is a Polish mathematician known for her research and books on [fractional calculus](wikipedia://en/229939) and the fractional [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882). She is an associate professor of mathematics, in the faculty of computer science of [Bialystok University of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bialystok_University_of_Technology).

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

Malinowska earned a master's degree in mathematics from the [University of Warsaw](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Warsaw) in 1995. She joined Bialystok University of Technology as a teaching assistant in that year. After completing her doctorate in 2003 through the [Polish Academy of Sciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Polish_Academy_of_Sciences), she became an assistant professor at Bialystok University of Technology, and later an associate professor.

## Books {#Books}

Malinowska is the coauthor of:

- *Introduction To The Fractional Calculus Of Variations* (with Delfim F. M. Torres, World Scientific, 2012)
- *Quantum Variational Calculus* (with Delfim F. M. Torres, Springer, 2014)
- *Advanced Methods in the Fractional Calculus of Variations* (with Tatiana Odzijewic and Delfim F. M. Torres, Springer, 2015)
