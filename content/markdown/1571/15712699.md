# List of International Mathematical Olympiad participants

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="List of International Mathematical Olympiad participants"}

The [International Mathematical Olympiad](wikipedia://en/82208) (IMO) is an annual international [high school](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/High_school) [mathematics competition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematics_competition) focused primarily on pre-[collegiate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/College) [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), and is the oldest of the [international science olympiads](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_science_olympiad).[^o2] The awards for exceptional performance include medals for roughly the top half participants, and honorable mentions for participants whom solve at least one problem perfectly.[^o3]

:::

This is a list of participants who have achieved notability. This includes participants that went on to become notable [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902), participants who won medals at an exceptionally young age, or participants who scored highly.

## Notable participants {#Notable_participants}

### Mathematicians {#Mathematicians}

A number of IMO participants have gone on to become notable [mathematicians](wikipedia://en/18902). The following IMO participants have either received a [Fields Medal](wikipedia://en/10859), an [Abel Prize](wikipedia://en/159285), a [Wolf Prize](wikipedia://en/714278) or a [Clay Research Award](wikipedia://en/664167), awards which recognise groundbreaking research in mathematics; a [European Mathematical Society Prize](wikipedia://en/969470), an award which recognizes young researchers; or one of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822)'s awards (a [Blumenthal Award](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blumenthal_Award) in Pure Mathematics, [Bôcher Memorial Prize](wikipedia://en/1099872) in Analysis, [Cole Prize in Algebra](wikipedia://en/1372177), [Cole Prize in Number Theory](wikipedia://en/1372177), [Fulkerson Prize in Discrete Mathematics](wikipedia://en/1425916), [Steele Prize in Mathematics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steele_Prize), or [Veblen Prize in Geometry and Topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Veblen_Prize)) recognizing research in specific mathematical fields. [Grigori Perelman](wikipedia://en/225266) proved the [Poincaré conjecture](wikipedia://en/23798) (one of the seven [Millennium Prize Problems](wikipedia://en/27599234)), and [Yuri Matiyasevich](wikipedia://en/2627712) gave a negative solution of [Hilbert's tenth problem](wikipedia://en/101851).

**G** denotes an IMO gold medal, **S** denotes a silver medal, **B** denotes a bronze medal, and **P** denotes a perfect score.
