# Vincenzo Flauti

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Vincenzo Flauti"}

**Vincenzo Flauti** (4 April 1782 – 20 June 1863) was an Italian [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), professor in the [university of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples_Federico_II).

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Flauti studied at the *Liceo del Salvatore*, the school led by [Nicola Fergola](wikipedia://en/47472521). Although he began medical studies, he changed them to mathematics influenced by his master Fergola. He taught at the [University of Naples](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Naples) from 1803 to 1860, succeeding Fergola in his chair in 1812.

In 1860, when the [Kingdom of the Two Sicilies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_of_the_Two_Sicilies) was conquered by Giuseppe Garibaldi and was incorporated into the Kingdom of Italy, Flauti was excluded from the Academy of Sciences of Naples and from his docent duties, because he had been a supporter of the [Bourbon monarchy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Bourbon).

Flauti was the leader of the synthetic school of mathematics founded by Fergola.[^n1] In 1807, jointly with Felice Giannattasio, he was entrusted by the Bourbon government to write a mathematics textbook for all schoolchildren in the kingdom.[^n2]
