# Ilka Agricola

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Ilka Agricola"}

**Ilka Agricola** (born 8 August 1973 in [The Hague](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Hague))[^o3] is a German mathematician specialising in [differential geometry](wikipedia://en/8625) and its applications in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). She is the dean of mathematics and computer science at [Marburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marburg_University), where she has also been responsible for making the university's collection of mathematical models public.[^o4]

:::

## Life and work {#Life_and_work}

Agricola studied [physics](wikipedia://en/22939) at the [Technical University of Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Technical_University_of_Munich) and [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) from 1991 to 1996.[^o3] After a guest stay at [Rutgers University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rutgers_University) in [New Jersey](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_Jersey) (United States) that lasted until the end of 1997 she went to the [Humboldt University of Berlin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Humboldt_University_of_Berlin), where in 2000 she earned a mathematics doctorate under Thomas Friedrich (mathematican).

From 2003 to 2008, she led one of the [Volkswagen Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Volkswagen_Foundation) funded research groups at the Humboldt University of Berlin in the field of special geometries in [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). From 2004 to 2008, she was a [project manager](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_manager) in the priority program for [string theory](wikipedia://en/28305) at the [German Research Foundation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Research_Foundation) and the Collaborative Research Center 1080. Agricola took the [Habilitation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Habilitation) in 2004 at the [University of Greifswald](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Greifswald) in mathematics. In 2008, she was appointed full professor at [Marburg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marburg_University). From November 2014 until October 2018, she has been Dean of the Department of Mathematics and [Computer Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Computer_Science). She was president of the [German Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/30864973) from 2021 to 2022.[^o7] [^o8]

Agricola is Editor-in-Chief of two [academic journals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Academic_journal) in mathematics published by [Springer Science+Business Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Springer_Science+Business_Media), [*Annals of Global Analysis and Geometry*](https://www.springer.com/journal/10455) (since 2015) and [*Mathematische Semesterberichte*](https://www.springer.com/journal/591) (since 2021). She is an editor of the journal *Communications in Mathematics* published by [De Gruyter](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/De_Gruyter).[^o9]

As of September 2025, Agricola is chair of the Committee on Publishing of the International Mathematical Union. [^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

In 2003, Ilka Agricola received the Medal of Honor of [Charles University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_University) in Prague.[^o12] In 2016, she was awarded the Ars legendi faculty prize of the [Stifterverband für die Deutsche Wissenschaft](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stifterverband_für_die_Deutsche_Wissenschaft) and [German Rectors' Conference](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/German_Rectors'_Conference) for excellence in teaching mathematics.[^o13] She was named a Fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822), in the 2022 class of fellows, "for contributions to differential geometry, in particular manifolds with special holonomy and on non-integrable geometric structures and for service to the mathematical community".[^o14]
