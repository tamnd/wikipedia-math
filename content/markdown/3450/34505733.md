# András P. Huhn

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="András P. Huhn"}

**András P Huhn** ([Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szeged), 26 January 1947 – [Szeged](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Szeged), 6 June 1985) was a Hungarian mathematician. [Huhn's theorem](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Huhn's_theorem) on the representation of [distributive](wikipedia://en/616019) [semilattices](wikipedia://en/755647) is named after him.

:::
