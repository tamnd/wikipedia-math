# Willi Jäger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Willi Jäger"}

*Image: Willi Jäger.jpg.* Jäger at [Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Oberwolfach), 2010

**Willi Jäger** (born 15 August 1940 in [Kschellowitz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Křelovice_%28Plzeň-North_District%29), [Bohemia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bohemia)) is a German mathematician.

:::

## Education {#Education}

He completed his PhD in 1966 at [LMU Munich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/LMU_Munich) under the direction of [Erhard Heinz](wikipedia://en/31696540).

## Career {#Career}

From 1969 to 1970, Jäger was a visiting scientist at the [Courant Institute](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Courant_Institute) in [New York City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City). In 1970, he became professor of mathematics at the [University of Münster](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Münster) and in 1974, he became professor of applied mathematics at [Heidelberg University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Heidelberg_University). In 1987, Jäger was founding member of the [Interdisciplinary Center for Scientific Computing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Interdisciplinary_Center_for_Scientific_Computing) of Heidelberg University.[^o5] He is a board member of the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach).

In addition to problems of scientific computing, including the effective use of computers for the mathematical modeling of complicated, mostly scientific problems, Jäger deals with problems of [nonlinear differential equations](wikipedia://en/146103#Nonlinear_differential_equations), [calculus of variations](wikipedia://en/171882), [branching processes](wikipedia://en/377202), and the [spectral theory](wikipedia://en/506713) of [differential operators](wikipedia://en/263886), mostly with a view to specific applications such as [data visualization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Data_visualization).

Jäger had more than 100 PhD students, including [Florin Diacu](wikipedia://en/22640190), [Bernold Fiedler](wikipedia://en/56983960), [Stephan Luckhaus](wikipedia://en/31696418), [Hubert Mara](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hubert_Mara) and [Martin Vingron](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Martin_Vingron).[^o3]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Thus far, he has been honoured with 2 honorary doctorates, several prizes and the [Order of Merit of the Federal Republic of Germany](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Order_of_Merit_of_the_Federal_Republic_of_Germany).[^o7] He gave a [DMV](wikipedia://en/30864973) [Gauss Lecture](wikipedia://en/48440954) in 2007.
