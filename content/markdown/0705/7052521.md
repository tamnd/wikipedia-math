# Tonelli–Hobson test

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tonelli–Hobson test"}

In [mathematics](wikipedia://en/18831), the **Tonelli–Hobson test** gives sufficient criteria for a function *ƒ* on **R**<sup>2</sup> to be an [integrable function](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Integrable_function). It is often used to establish that [Fubini's theorem](wikipedia://en/255245) may be applied to *ƒ*. It is named for [Leonida Tonelli](wikipedia://en/10018991) and [E. W. Hobson](wikipedia://en/1077078).

:::

More precisely, the Tonelli–Hobson test states that if *ƒ* is a [real-valued](wikipedia://en/22284647) [measurable function](wikipedia://en/44055) on **R**<sup>2</sup>, and either of the two [iterated integrals](wikipedia://en/349053)

$$\int_\mathbb{R}\left(\int_\mathbb{R}|f(x,y)|\,dx\right)\, dy$$

or

$$\int_\mathbb{R}\left(\int_\mathbb{R}|f(x,y)|\,dy\right)\, dx$$

is finite, then *ƒ* is [Lebesgue-integrable](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lebesgue-integrable) on **R**<sup>2</sup>.[^o2]
