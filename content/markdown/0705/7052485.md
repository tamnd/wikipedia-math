# Statisticians' and engineers' cross-reference of statistical terms

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Statisticians' and engineers' cross-reference of statistical terms"}

The following terms are used by [electrical engineers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Electrical_engineer) in [statistical signal processing](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Statistical_signal_processing) studies instead of typical statistician's terms.

{| class="wikitable" ! Statistics ! Electrical engineering |- |[Null hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Null_hypothesis) |Noise only hypothesis |- |[Alternative hypothesis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alternative_hypothesis) |Signal + noise hypothesis |- |[Critical region](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_region) |Signal present decision region |- |[Type I error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_I_error) |False alarm (FA) (noted as $P_{FA}$) |- |[Type II error](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Type_II_error) | Miss [^o2] |}

:::

In other engineering fields, particularly [mechanical engineering](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mechanical_engineering), uncertainty analysis examines systematic and random components of variations in measurements associated with physical experiments.
