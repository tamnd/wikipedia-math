::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Bitruncated 16-cell honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **bitruncated 16-cell honeycomb** (or **runcicantic tesseractic honeycomb**) is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 4-space.

:::

## Symmetry constructions {#Symmetry_constructions}

There are 3 different symmetry constructions, all with 3-3 [duopyramid](wikipedia://en/36040636) vertex figures. The ${\tilde{B}}_4$ symmetry doubles on ${\tilde{D}}_4$ in three possible ways, while ${\tilde{F}}_4$ contains the highest symmetry.
