::: {#o6 .subject cue=lead name="Bitruncated tesseractic honeycomb"}

In [four-dimensional](wikipedia://en/1364622) [Euclidean geometry](wikipedia://en/9417), the **bitruncated tesseractic honeycomb** is a uniform space-filling [tessellation](wikipedia://en/321671) (or [honeycomb](wikipedia://en/6008654)) in Euclidean 4-space. It is constructed by a [bitruncation](wikipedia://en/6792316) of a [tesseractic honeycomb](wikipedia://en/9400139). It is also called a **cantic quarter tesseractic honeycomb** from its q<sub>2</sub>{4,3,3,4} construction.

:::
