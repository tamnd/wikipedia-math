# Charles Julien Brianchon

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Charles Julien Brianchon"}

**Charles Julien Brianchon** (19 December 1783 – 29 April 1864) was a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and [chemist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Chemist).

:::

## Life {#Life}

He entered into the [École Polytechnique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/École_Polytechnique) in 1804 at the age of eighteen, and studied under [Monge](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Monge), graduating first in his class in 1808, after which he took up a career as a [lieutenant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lieutenant) in [Napoleon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Napoleon_Bonaparte)'s [artillery](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Artillery). Later, in 1818, Brianchon became a [professor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Professor) in the Artillery School of the [Royal Guard](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Guard_%28France%29) at [Vincennes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vincennes).

## Work {#Work}

Brianchon is best known for his [proof](wikipedia://en/82285) of *[Brianchon's theorem](wikipedia://en/1554065)* (1810).

Brianchon's book *Mémoire sur les lignes du second ordre* (Paris, 1817) is available online <https://archive.org/download/mmoiresurlesli00bria/mmoiresurlesli00bria.pdf>.
