# Dan Nettleton

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dan Nettleton"}

**Daniel S. Nettleton** is an American statistical genomicist.[^o3]

:::

Nettleton was raised in [Algona, Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Algona,_Iowa). Both of his parents were high school teachers. Nettleton attended [Wartburg College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wartburg_College), where he played basketball,[^o3] and earned his master's degree (1993) and doctorate (1996) in statistics at the [University of Iowa](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Iowa).[^o2] Nettleton began his teaching career at the [University of Nebraska–Lincoln](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Nebraska–Lincoln), then was recruited to join [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University)'s faculty in 2000 by [Dean Isaacson](wikipedia://en/77208846), who had earlier tried to convince Nettleton to pursue graduate studies at Iowa State.[^o3] At ISU, Nettleton holds the Laurence H. Baker Endowed Chair in Biological Statistics,[^o4] and was appointed to a distinguished professorship in 2015.[^o5] The following year, Nettleton was appointed director of the Laurence H. Baker Center for Bioinformatics and Biological Statistics.[^o6] In 2019, Nettleton succeeded [Max Morris](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Morris_%28statistician%29) as chair of the Department of Statistics,[^o7] and accepted a second term in the role in 2024.[^o8][^o9] Nettleton stepped down as chair in June 2026, was replaced by [Zhengyuan Zhu](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zhengyuan_Zhu) on an interim basis,[^o10] and became associate dean for finance and operations the following month.[^o11]

Nettleton was elected a fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988) in 2008,[^o12] and began serving as chair-elect of the ASA's Caucus of Academic Representatives in 2022.[^o13] In 2024, Nettleton was elected a fellow of the [American Association for the Advancement of Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Association_for_the_Advancement_of_Science).[^o14][^o15]
