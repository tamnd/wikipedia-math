# Erdős–Delange theorem

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Erdős–Delange theorem"}

The **Erdős–Delange theorem** is a theorem in [number theory](wikipedia://en/21527) concerning the distribution of [prime numbers](wikipedia://en/23666). It is named after [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284) and Hubert Delange.

:::

Let $\omega(n)$ denote the number of prime factors of an integer $n$, counted with multiplicity, and $\lambda$ be any [irrational number](wikipedia://en/20647689). The theorem states that the real numbers $\lambda\omega(n)$ are asymptotically uniformly distributed modulo 1. It implies the [prime number theorem](wikipedia://en/23692).

The theorem was stated without proof in 1946 by [Paul Erdős](wikipedia://en/601284), with a remark that "the proof is not easy". Hubert Delange found a simpler proof and published it in 1958, together with two other ways of deducing it from results of Erdős and of [Atle Selberg](wikipedia://en/2021).
