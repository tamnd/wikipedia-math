# Dean Isaacson

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Dean Isaacson"}

**Dean Leroy Isaacson** (born 1941) is an American statistician.

:::

## Academic career {#Academic_career}

After graduating from Milaca High School in [Milaca, Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Milaca,_Minnesota), Isaacson earned a bachelor's degree in mathematics at [Macalester College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Macalester_College), then completed his master's and doctorate at [University of Minnesota](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Minnesota).[^o3] He joined the [Iowa State University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iowa_State_University) faculty in 1968, and served as chair of the Department of Statistics from 1984 to 2002,[^o8][^o4] when he was succeeded in the role by [Kenneth Koehler](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kenneth_Koehler).[^o5][^o6] Isaacson was granted emeritus status upon retirement in 2009.[^o7][^o8]

## Honors {#Honors}

Isaacson is a 1994 fellow of the [American Statistical Association](wikipedia://en/341988).[^o10] In 2013, he was elected to the Milaca High School Hall of Fame.[^o11]

## Selected books {#Selected_books}

- Isaacson, Dean L.; Madsen, Richard W. (1976). "Markov Chains: Theory and Applications". Wiley. isbn:9780471428626[^o14]
