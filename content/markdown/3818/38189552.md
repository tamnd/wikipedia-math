# Michael Schlessinger

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Michael Schlessinger"}

**Michael Schlessinger** (1937-2025) was a Professor Emeritus of Mathematics at the [University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_North_Carolina_at_Chapel_Hill) who worked in [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997).

:::

## Career {#Career}

Schlessinger obtained his Ph.D. in 1964 from [Harvard University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Harvard_University), under the supervision of [John Tate](wikipedia://en/245933).

He proved [Schlessinger's theorem](wikipedia://en/38179442) about representable functors of Artinian algebras and introduced [Lichtenbaum–Schlessinger functors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lichtenbaum–Schlessinger_functor) in [deformation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Deformation_theory).

In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o4]
