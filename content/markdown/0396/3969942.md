# Tatyana van Aardenne-Ehrenfest

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Tatyana van Aardenne-Ehrenfest"}

Ehrenfest Pavlovna Van Aardenne-Ehrenfest

**Tatyana Pavlovna van Aardenne-Ehrenfest** (**Ehrenfest**; October 28, 1905 – November 29, 1984) was a Dutch [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902). She is known for her contributions to [De Bruijn sequences](wikipedia://en/1565267), [low-discrepancy sequences](wikipedia://en/478601), and the [BEST theorem](wikipedia://en/1455603).

:::

## Early life and education {#Early_life_and_education}

Tatyana Ehrenfest was born in Vienna in 1905 and spent her childhood in [St Petersburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Saint_Petersburg). She is the daughter of physicists [Paul Ehrenfest](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paul_Ehrenfest) and [Tatyana Afanasyeva](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tatyana_Afanasyeva).

In 1912 the Ehrenfests moved to [Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden) where her father succeeded [Hendrik Lorentz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hendrik_Lorentz) as professor at the [University of Leiden](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leiden_University). Until 1917 she was home schooled; after that, she attended the [Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stedelijk_Gymnasium_Leiden) in Leiden and passed the [final exams](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gymnasium_%28school%29#Final_degree) in 1922. She studied mathematics and physics at the University of Leiden. In 1928 she went to [Göttingen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Göttingen) where she took courses from [Harald Bohr](wikipedia://en/638305) and [Max Born](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Born). On December 8, 1931, she obtained her Ph.D. in Leiden, advised by [Willem van der Woude](wikipedia://en/47912777).[^o4] After that, she was never employed and, in particular, never held any academic position.

## Contributions {#Contributions}

[De Bruijn sequences](wikipedia://en/1565267) are [cyclic sequences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyclic_sequence) of symbols for a given alphabet and parameter $k$ such that every length-$k$ subsequence occurs exactly once within them. They are named after [Nicolaas Govert de Bruijn](wikipedia://en/3166399), despite their earlier discovery (for binary alphabets) by Camille Flye Sainte-Marie. De Bruijn and Van Aardenne-Ehrenfest jointly published the first investigation into de Bruijn sequences for larger alphabets, in 1951.

The [BEST theorem](wikipedia://en/1455603), also known as the de Bruijn–van Aardenne-Ehrenfest–Smith–Tutte theorem, relates [Euler tours](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euler_tour) and [spanning trees](wikipedia://en/455770) in [directed graphs](wikipedia://en/19721986), and gives a product formula for their number. It is a variant of an earlier formula of [Cedric Smith](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cedric_Smith_%28statistician%29) and [W. T. Tutte](wikipedia://en/52021), and was published by de Bruijn and Ehrenfest in the same paper as their work on de Bruijn sequences.

Van Aardenne-Ehrenfest is also known for her proof of a lower bound on [low-discrepancy sequences](wikipedia://en/478601).
