# Bent Fuglede

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bent Fuglede"}

**Bent Fuglede** (8 October 1925 – 7 December 2023) was a Danish mathematician.

:::

## Early life and career {#Early_life_and_career}

Fuglede was known for his contributions to mathematical analysis, in particular [functional analysis](wikipedia://en/11180), where he proved [Fuglede's theorem](wikipedia://en/706271) and stated [Fuglede's conjecture](wikipedia://en/56754296).[^o4]

Fuglede graduated from [Skt. Jørgens Gymnasium](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Skt._Jørgens_Gymnasium) 1943 and received his mag. scient. og cand. mag. in 1948 at the [University of Copenhagen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Copenhagen) after which he studied in USA until 1951. In 1952 he was employed as scientific assistant at [Den Polytekniske Læreanstalt](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Den_Polytekniske_Læreanstalt) and in 1954 as amanuensis at Matematisk Institut University of Copenhagen, in 1958 associate professor, and in 1959 head of department.[^o5][^o6] Fuglede also spent one year in Lund (Sweden) as Nordic docent.[^o6] Fuglede received his dr.phil. (Ph.D.) in 1960 from the University of Copenhagen; his [doctoral advisor](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctoral_advisor) was [Børge Jessen](wikipedia://en/20754801), after which he became professor of math at [Danmarks tekniske Højskole](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danmarks_tekniske_Højskole). In 1965 he became professor of math at the University of Copenhagen, where he stayed until his retirement in 1992.

Fuglede was a member of the [Royal Danish Academy of Sciences and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Danish_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Letters),[^o8] the [Finnish Academy of Science and Letters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Finnish_Academy_of_Science_and_Letters).[^o9] and the [*Bayerische Akademie der Wissenschaften*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bavarian_Academy_of_Sciences_and_Humanities).[^o6] In 2012 he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o10]

## Personal life and death {#Personal_life_and_death}

Fuglede was married to [Ólafía Einarsdóttir](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ólafía_Einarsdóttir), an Icelandic archeologist and historian. They had one child together, Einar.[^o12]

Bent Fuglede died in Copenhagen on 7 December 2023, at the age of 98.[^o13]
