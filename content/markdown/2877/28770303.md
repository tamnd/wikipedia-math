# Masanori Ohya

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Masanori Ohya"}

**Masanori Ohya** is a Japanese [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902).

:::

After he received a Ph.D. in [Mathematical Physics](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Physics) and [Information Science](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Information_Science) and Dr.Sc., he continuously worked on [operator algebra](wikipedia://en/455987), [quantum](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum) [entropy](wikipedia://en/9891), [quantum information theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quantum_information_theory) and bio-information. He achieved results in the fields of [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) and [mathematical physics](wikipedia://en/173416). In particular, he proposed his version of **quantum mutual entropy**.[^o3] Note this quantity is not the same as Holevo's chi quantity or coherent information, though each of them plays important role in quantum information theory. The information theoretic meaning of Ohya's quantum mutual information is still obscure.

He also proposed 'Information Dynamics' and 'Adaptive Dynamics', which he applied to the study of [chaos theory](wikipedia://en/6295), [quantum information](wikipedia://en/25274) and [biosciences](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Biosciences) as related fields.

## Main research {#Main_research}

Ohya studied multiple topics for more than thirty years, relating to quantum entropy, quantum information, chaos dynamics and life science. His main accomplishments are as follows:

1. Elucidation of Mathematical Bases of Quantum Channels,
1. Formulation of Quantum Mutual Information (Entropy),
1. Information Dynamics,
1. Analysis of Quantum Teleportation,
1. Quantum Algorithm,
1. Proposal of Adaptive Dynamics,
1. Applications to the life sciences.

They are explained in this book (Watanabe 2007).
