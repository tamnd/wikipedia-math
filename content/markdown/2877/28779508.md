# Bernard Teissier

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Bernard Teissier"}

**Bernard Teissier** (; born 1945)[^o3] is a French [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902) and a member of the [Nicolas Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394) group. He has made major contributions to [algebraic geometry](wikipedia://en/1997) and [commutative algebra](wikipedia://en/245990), specifically to [singularity theory](wikipedia://en/581763), [multiplicity theory](wikipedia://en/42778302)[^o4][^o5] and [valuation theory](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valuation_theory).

:::

Teissier attained his [doctorate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctorate) from [Paris Diderot University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Paris_Diderot_University) in 1973, under supervision of [Heisuke Hironaka](wikipedia://en/13822). He was a member and a leading figure of [Nicolas Bourbaki](wikipedia://en/167394).[^o7] Along with [Alain Connes](wikipedia://en/340), he gave the 1975/1976 [Peccot Lectures](wikipedia://en/60897406). He was an invited speaker at the International Congress of Mathematics at Warsaw in 1983.[^o8]

In 2012, he became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o9]
