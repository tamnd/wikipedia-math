# Theodor Schneider

::: {#o1 .subject cue=lead name="Theodor Schneider"}

*Image: TheodorSchneider.jpg.* Theodor Schneider 1970

**Theodor Schneider** (7 May 1911 – 31 October 1988) was a [German](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Germans) [mathematician](wikipedia://en/18902), best known for providing proof of what is now known as the [Gelfond–Schneider theorem](wikipedia://en/609487), who was born in [Frankfurt am Main](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frankfurt_am_Main) and died in [Freiburg im Breisgau](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freiburg_im_Breisgau).

:::

Schneider studied from 1929 to 1934 in Frankfurt; he solved [Hilbert's 7th problem](wikipedia://en/392579) in his PhD thesis, which then came to be known as the [Gelfond–Schneider theorem](wikipedia://en/609487). Later, he became an assistant to [Carl Ludwig Siegel](wikipedia://en/716602) in Göttingen, where he stayed until 1953. Then, he became a professor in Erlangen (1953–1959) and finally until his retirement in Freiburg (1959–1976). During his time in Freiburg, he also served as the director of the [Mathematical Research Institute of Oberwolfach](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mathematical_Research_Institute_of_Oberwolfach) from 1959 to 1963. His doctoral students include [H. P. Schlickewei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/H._P._Schlickewei).
