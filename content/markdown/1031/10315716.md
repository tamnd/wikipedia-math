# Katrin Wehrheim

::: {#o2 .subject cue=lead name="Katrin Wehrheim"}

**Katrin Wehrheim** (born 1974) is an associate professor of mathematics at the [University of California, Berkeley](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_California,_Berkeley). Wehrheim's research centers around [symplectic topology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symplectic_topology) and [gauge theory](wikipedia://en/23912155), and they are known for work on [pseudoholomorphic quilts](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pseudoholomorphic_quilt). With [Dusa McDuff](wikipedia://en/2838322), they have challenged the foundational rigor of a classic proof in [symplectic geometry](wikipedia://en/294298).[^o3]

:::

## Education and career {#Education_and_career}

After attending school in [Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hamburg)[^o5] and studying at the [University of Hamburg](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Hamburg) until 1995 and [Imperial College](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperial_College) until 1996, Wehrheim went to [ETH Zürich](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ETH_Zürich) for graduate studies. After almost dropping out to become an [Olympic rower](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rowing_at_the_Summer_Olympics), Wehrheim completed their PhD in 2002, under the joint supervision of [Dusa McDuff](wikipedia://en/2838322) and [Dietmar Salamon](wikipedia://en/62247177).[^o6]

Wehrheim was an instructor at [Princeton University](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Princeton_University) and member of the [Institute for Advanced Study](wikipedia://en/184179)[^o7] before taking a tenure track position at the [Massachusetts Institute of Technology](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Massachusetts_Institute_of_Technology) in 2005.[^o8] While they were at MIT, Wehrheim—who is openly gay—co-headed the 2008 Celebration of Women in Mathematics conference.[^o8][^o9] Since 2013, Wehrheim has been teaching mathematics at the University of California, Berkeley.[^o10]

## Awards and honors {#Awards_and_honors}

Wehrheim's PhD thesis in mathematics *Anti-Self-Dual Instantons with Lagrangian Boundary Conditions* won the 2002 ETH medal.[^o6] In 2010, Wehrheim received the [Presidential Career Award PECASE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PECASE) from [Barack Obama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barack_Obama) in a ceremony at the White House.[^o12] In 2012, Wehrheim became a fellow of the [American Mathematical Society](wikipedia://en/198822).[^o13]
